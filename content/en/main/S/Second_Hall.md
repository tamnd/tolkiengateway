The **Second Hall** was one of many Halls of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm). Between the Second and the [First Hall](/wiki/First_Hall) — counting from the [entrance](/wiki/Great_Gates "Great Gates") of the city — lay the ill-fated [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) in the [First Deep](/wiki/First_Deep). The hall's pillars were notable for being smooth and black, carved like trees, with the boughs holding up the ceiling.

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) joined battle with the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Balin's Colony](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony) in the hall. During the [ensuing brawl,](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Second_Hall "Battle of the Second Hall") [Lóni](/wiki/L%C3%B3ni), [Náli](/wiki/N%C3%A1li) and [Frár](/wiki/Fr%C3%A1r) were killed while the rest of the Colony escaped to the [Seventh Level](/wiki/Seventh_Level), allowing the Orcs to reclaim the Hall.

It was the largest of the halls to be encountered by the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) as they traversed Moria from west to east. On their way to the First Hall from the Second Hall, they met [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) at the Bridge of Khazad-dûm, and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) fell into the chasm.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Second Hall is the centerpiece of the &#34;Nud-melek&#34;. In the present day its tree-shaped columns stand barren. However, during the flashback set in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_1981" title="Third Age 1981">1981</a>, the columns and the ceiling are seen to be covered in ornate leaves.</dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As the great crossroads throughout Moria, the Second Hall once served as a marketplace. It contained a monumental gold and silver staircase, the Stairs of Sun and Moon, which had been melted by the passage of Durin&#39;s Bane when the last Dwarves fled. In a small side chamber, the skull of <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a> was placed on a broken pillar. Though the Orcs did not venerate their dead, the lingering malice of the Great Orc made it a powerful monument to hatred.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>
