The **Second Hall** was one of many Halls of [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md). Between the Second and the [First Hall](../F/First_Hall.md) — counting from the [entrance](../G/Great_Gates.md) of the city — lay the ill-fated [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md) in the [First Deep](../F/First_Deep.md). The hall's pillars were notable for being smooth and black, carved like trees, with the boughs holding up the ceiling.

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2994](../T/Third_Age_2994.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) joined battle with the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Balin's Colony](../B/Balin's_Colony~64118.md) in the hall. During the [ensuing brawl,](../B/Battle_of_the_Second_Hall.md) [Lóni](../L/Lóni.md), [Náli](../N/Náli.md) and [Frár](../F/Frár.md) were killed while the rest of the Colony escaped to the [Seventh Level](Seventh_Level.md), allowing the Orcs to reclaim the Hall.

It was the largest of the halls to be encountered by the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) as they traversed Moria from west to east. On their way to the First Hall from the Second Hall, they met [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) at the Bridge of Khazad-dûm, and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) fell into the chasm.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Second Hall is the centerpiece of the &#34;Nud-melek&#34;. In the present day its tree-shaped columns stand barren. However, during the flashback set in <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1981.md" title="Third Age 1981">1981</a>, the columns and the ceiling are seen to be covered in ornate leaves.</dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="../M/Moria_-_Through_the_Doors_of_Durin.md" title="Moria - Through the Doors of Durin">Moria: Through the Doors of Durin</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As the great crossroads throughout Moria, the Second Hall once served as a marketplace. It contained a monumental gold and silver staircase, the Stairs of Sun and Moon, which had been melted by the passage of Durin&#39;s Bane when the last Dwarves fled. In a small side chamber, the skull of <a href="../A/Azog.md" title="Azog">Azog</a> was placed on a broken pillar. Though the Orcs did not venerate their dead, the lingering malice of the Great Orc made it a powerful monument to hatred.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>
