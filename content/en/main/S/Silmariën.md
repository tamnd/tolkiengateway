**Silmariën** was the first child of the fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), and mother of the first [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").

## History {#History}

Silmariën was the eldest child of King Tar-Elendil and elder sister of [Meneldur](/wiki/Meneldur). However, because of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") law of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnatic_primogeniture" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">agnatic primogeniture</a> that then existed in the Realm, she could not succeed her father as King of Númenor, and instead her brother took up the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) as [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur).<sup id="cite_ref-UTWife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTWife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Silmariën wed [Elatan](/wiki/Elatan) of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) and, in honor of their son [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)"), her father created the royal title of [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië"). Also, instead of giving this valuable heirloom to his heir, Tar-Elendil gave her the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Silmariën also was given a *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fillet_(clothing)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">fillet</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-gladden_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gladden-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Her heirs, coming of the royal line of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> led the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) to found the Kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) centuries later. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), the hero of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), is her direct descendant through *many* generations.

Her fillet would become part of the [Elendilmir](/wiki/Elendilmir), a sign of royalty in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and, later, the crown of the Kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-gladden_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gladden-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Silmariën's name is not given a translation, although its etymology is straightforward in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It may incorporate *silma,* "silver, shining white;<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> crystal (white)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>", and obviously ends in the feminine ending *-rien*, apparently a variant of *-ien* (see *-[riel](/wiki/Riel "Riel")*).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The meaning therefore would be \*"Maiden garlanded with shining light"

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Silmariën was definitely the eldest child of Tar-Elendil, and her birthdate is given in [The Line of Elros: Kings of Númenor](/wiki/The_Line_of_Elros:_Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor) as [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [521](/wiki/Second_Age_521 "Second Age 521").<sup id="cite_ref-UTKing_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTKing-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [Tale of Years](/wiki/Appendix_B "Appendix B"), it is given as 548, a date that goes back to the first drafts of that text whose revision was overlooked by Tolkien.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> This error was fixed in later editions of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
