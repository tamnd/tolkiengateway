The **South Road** was a major road in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) connecting [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/99/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg/180px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="180" height="206" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/99/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg/270px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/99/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg/360px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Minas-tirith.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2634" data-file-height="3011"/></a><figcaption><i>Map of part of Gondor</i> by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a></figcaption></figure>

It passed the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor), ran parallel to the Anduin, and crossed the river [Erui](/wiki/Erui) at the [Crossings of Erui](/wiki/Crossings_of_Erui) before it reached [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

It was the chief highway of Minas Tirith, well-paved and wide enough for three lines of wagons. A broad green riding-track ran along its eastern edge.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), before the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor), the captains of the [Outlands](/wiki/Southern_Fiefs "Southern Fiefs") were expected to arrive at Minas Tirith on the South Road.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>From <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, the road passes through <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Harlond" title="Harlond">Harlond</a> and continues to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Arnach" title="Arnach">Arnach</a>, the major town of <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a>. After crossing into <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a>, the road runs past a small farming village of &#34;Glaniath&#34; before arriving to the east gate of <a href="/wiki/Pelargir" title="Pelargir">Pelargir</a>.</dd></dl>
