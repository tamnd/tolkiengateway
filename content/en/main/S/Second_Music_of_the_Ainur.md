The **Second Music of the Ainur** is the great music the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) will make together with the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) before [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) after [the End](/wiki/The_End "The End"), during which [Arda](/wiki/Arda) will be [rebuilt](/wiki/Arda_Healed "Arda Healed"). The [history](/wiki/Time "Time") of Arda is but a learning process towards it.

It will be more splendid than the first [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur) as the Children will participate, and every participant will fully understand their intent in their part and be in harmony with the others. Ilúvatar will give to them the [Secret Fire](/wiki/Secret_Fire) and his themes shall take Being at the same time they are uttered.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
