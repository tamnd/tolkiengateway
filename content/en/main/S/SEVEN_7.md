<i><b>SEVEN 7</b></i> is the seventh issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [1986](../_/1986.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cbv_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cbv-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "George MacDonald: A Portrait from His Letters "
    - By: Rolland Hein
- "Being Somebody Else: Smith's 'Sympathy' and Chesterton's 'Secret'"
    - By: Norbert Waszek
- "Provocative Generalizations: *The Allegory of Love* in Retrospect"
    - By: Margaret P. Hannay
- "The Polemic Image: The Role of Metaphor and Symbol in the Fiction of C.S. Lewis"
    - By: Kath Filmer
- "Appeasing the Gods in C.S. Lewis's *Till We Have Faces*"
    - By: Peter W. Macky
- "Tolkien's Concept of Philology as Mythology"
    - By: [J.S. Ryan](../J/John_Ryan.md)
- "Dorothy L. Sayers and the Proper Work of the Playwright "
    - By: George Ralph
- "(I) Playwrights Are Not Evangelists, (II) Writing a Local Play"
    - By: Dorothy L. Sayers
- Review Essays:
    - "Scientism and the Flight from Reality"
        - By: Geoffrey Price
    - "Old Thunder: A Life of Hilaire Belloc"
        - By: Aidan Mackey<sup id="cite_ref-cbv_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cbv-1">[1]</a></sup>
