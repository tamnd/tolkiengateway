**Structural references** (also known as **Arda structural references**) is a term coined by [Beregond, Anders Stenström](../A/Anders_Stenström.md) in 1998, aimed at simplifying the usage of giving references to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) by employing abbreviations and some conventions developed in *[Arda](<../A/Arda_(annual).md>)*.

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>A structural reference identifies an element of the text by help of its place in the text&#39;s structure (rather than by its place in the layout of the book, which does not work when editions differ in layout).
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../A/Anders_Stenström.md" title="Anders Stenström">Beregond, Anders Stenström</a></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

A Spanish version of the Arda structural references, called the Ardarathorn Project, was presented by the [Spanish Tolkien Society](Sociedad_Tolkien_Española.md) in 2004.
