**Sorontil** is a mountain of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) (actually the only known mountain besides the [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma)) standing near the island's [North Cape](/wiki/North_Cape) of [Forostar](/wiki/Forostar), with its eastern flanks forming sheer sea-cliffs.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) built a tall tower in the vicinity of this mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Sorontil is not given a translation in the sources, but an apparent analysis would be *[soron](/wiki/Soron "Soron")* + *-[til](/wiki/Til "Til")*, thus meaning "Eagle peak". The translation would suggest that its craggy peaks were possibly populated by [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
