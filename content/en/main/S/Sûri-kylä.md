**Sûri-kylä** is a settlement of the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth) in [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.

It is the capital of the region [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel), and at the westernmost point of the settlement stands the Great Lodge. In the Great Lodge sits the Council of Elders and the Lossoth's current leader: Yrjänä.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sûri-kylä* is actually Finnish (normally written "suuri kylä") and means "Large Village".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
