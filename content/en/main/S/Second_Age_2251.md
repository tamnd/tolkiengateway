**Second Age 2251** (abbreviated to **S.A. 2251**) is the 2251st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- After thirty years of [Tar-Ancalimon's](../T/Tar-Ancalimon.md) reign, the rift between the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md) and [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) becomes wider.<sup id="cite_ref-Ancalimon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ancalimon-1">[1]</a></sup>
- About this time the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) or [Ringwraiths](../N/Nazgûl.md), slaves of the [Nine Rings](../N/Nine_Rings.md), first appear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Tale of Years](../A/Appendix_B.md)*, it said in 2251 "[Tar-Atanamir](../T/Tar-Atanamir.md) takes the [Sceptre](Sceptre_of_Númenor.md)", however, Atanamir died in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [2221](Second_Age_2221.md). The year 2221 is itself an emendation of 2251, and the former (2221) appears in the later tables, while the latter (2251) in the earlier tables: therefore 2251 (properly 2221) should have read "Death of Tar-Atanamir. [Tar-Ancalimon](../T/Tar-Ancalimon.md) takes the sceptre".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
