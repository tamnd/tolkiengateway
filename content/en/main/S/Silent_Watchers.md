The **Silent Watchers** were sentinels in [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) with the ability to notice movement on the road that led to and passed by the gate of Minas Morgul.

## History {#History}

[Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) told [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) and [Sam](Sam.md) that the Silent Watchers, things inside Minas Morgul knew of movement on the road that climbed under the shadow of the walls of Minas Morgul and passed the gate of Minas Morgul.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [13 March](../_/13_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Gorbag](../G/Gorbag.md), the leader of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from Minas Morgul, told [Shagrat](Shagrat.md), the leader of the Orcs from the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), that "our" Silent Watchers had been uneasy more than two days ago.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This corresponds with [10 March](../_/10_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> when Frodo, Sam and Gollum left the road that led to Minas Morgul before the bridge over the river [Morgulduin](../M/Morgulduin.md) to climb the [Stairs of Cirith Ungol](Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Silent Watchers were initially named the sentinels<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-6">[6]</a></sup> and were, in fact, early versions of the [Two Watchers](../T/Two_Watchers.md) of [Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md)'s mention of the *Silent Watchers* was added *after* Tolkien had already concieved of [the Tower of Kirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md) and removed the Sentinels from Minas Morgul. The term "Silent Watchers" is only ever used to refer to the Watchers of Minas Morgul, and never the Watchers of Cirith Ungol, so they are distinct entities, at least in the final book.<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-6">[6]</a></sup>
