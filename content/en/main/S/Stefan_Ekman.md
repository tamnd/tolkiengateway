**Stefan Ekman** (b. [1972](/wiki/1972)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a Swedish fantasy scholar with a PhD in English from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University" title="wikipedia:Lund University">Lund University</a>. His dissertation on settings in fantasy literature, *Writing Worlds, Reading Landscapes: An Exploration of Settings in Fantasy*, has substantial sections on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It was successfully defended on 16 October 2010.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Between 2002 and 2009, Ekman was employed by a Swedish publishing company as a fantasy specialist. He has also worked as translator of fantasy books, and taught English at various universities in Sweden.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
