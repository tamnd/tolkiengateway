<i><b>saur</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "bad", used in the sense "bad of food" and "putrid".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [SAWA](/wiki/SAWA)<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") suggested that the name *[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron#Etymology)* could be "a genuine Sindarin formation from *saur*", but stated that the name is "probably from [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}
