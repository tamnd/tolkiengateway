English actor **Shaun Mark "Sean" Bean** (born [17 April](/wiki/17_April) [1959](/wiki/1959)) played [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

Bean had previously played Richard Sharpe in the well known British TV series <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharpe_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Sharpe (TV series)">Sharpe</a></i>, based on Bernard Cornwell's novels about the Napoleonic Wars. Boromir's comment about the shards of [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring")* ("Still sharp!") is believed to be included as a nod to that role.

Bean reunited with [Orlando Bloom](/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy_(2004_film)" title="wikipedia:Troy (2004 film)">Troy</a></i> as Odysseus, whereas Bloom had the role of Paris.

He has also played in the series <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones" title="wikipedia:Game of Thrones">Game of Thrones</a>, which is adapted from the novel series *A Song of Ice and Fire* by [George R. R. Martin](/wiki/George_R._R._Martin).
