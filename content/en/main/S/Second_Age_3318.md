**Second Age 3318** (abbreviated to **S.A. 3318**) is the 3318th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md), the son of [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md), the last man born in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early manuscripts [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>) was named "Kiryandil" and his date of birth was 3318, which would have made him and not [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md) the last man born in Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
