<i><b>SEVEN 15</b></i> is the fifteenth issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [1998](../_/1998.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup> Volume 15 is also the *C.S. Lewis and Owen Barfield Centenary Issue*, celebrating the centenary year of the birth of British writers [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) and [Owen Barfield](../O/Owen_Barfield.md).<sup id="cite_ref-vii_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-3">[3]</a></sup>
