<i><b>sharkû</b></i> is an [Orkish](../O/Orkish.md) word meaning "old man".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> Modified to fit the [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md) (and anglicized in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*) it was rendered as "[Sharkey](Sharkey.md)", used by the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) as a nickname for their leader, the [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Saruman](Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Andreas Möhn has suggested that *sharkû* might be derived from a hypothetical [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) [root](Sundocarme.md) ZAR. The ending could be identified with the ending seen in *[Tharkûn](../G/Gandalf.md#Names)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
