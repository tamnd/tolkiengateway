<i><b>sharkû</b></i> is an [Orkish](/wiki/Orkish) word meaning "old man".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> Modified to fit the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") (and anglicized in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*) it was rendered as "[Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)", used by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) as a nickname for their leader, the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Andreas Möhn has suggested that *sharkû* might be derived from a hypothetical [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") ZAR. The ending could be identified with the ending seen in *[Tharkûn](/wiki/Gandalf#Names "Gandalf")*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
