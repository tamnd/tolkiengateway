**Skinbark** (also known as **Fladrif**) was, with [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) and [Leaflock](/wiki/Leaflock), one of the three oldest [Ents](/wiki/Ents) of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest).

## History {#History}

He lived on the slopes above and to the west of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), and so he and his people suffered the most at the hands of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s despoiling [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). After many of his folk and tree-herds had been destroyed and he had been wounded, Skinbark moved high up the slopes to be with the [birches](/wiki/Birches "Birches") he most loved and would not come down.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Skinbark* is the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") (or English) translation of *Fladrif*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It appears like *Fladrif* is intended to be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin);<sup id="cite_ref-DW_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-3">[3]</a></sup> the name is mentioned together with the Sindarin name *[Nindalf](/wiki/Nindalf)* as acquiring the /v/ sound by the final *-f*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) has suggested that *Fladrif* is composed of the otherwise unattested words \**flâd* ("skin") and \**rîf* ("bark").<sup id="cite_ref-DW_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-3">[3]</a></sup>
