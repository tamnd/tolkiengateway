**Skin-changers** were creatures who could take different forms at will. Little is known of their abilities; the only famous skin-changers were [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) and his [descendants](../B/Beornings.md) (who could take the shape of a [Man](../M/Men.md) or of a bear),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but evidently other skin-changers also existed.

It has been suggested that [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md), a "master of shapes and hues", was able to change his form.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
