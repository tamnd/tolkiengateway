**Second Age 3434** (abbreviated to **S.A. 3434**) is the 3434th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The host of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md) crosses the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md)<sup id="cite_ref-dddd_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dddd-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Battle of Dagorlad](../B/Battle_of_Dagorlad.md) and defeat of [Sauron](Sauron.md)<sup id="cite_ref-dddd_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dddd-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) and [Amdír](../A/Amdír.md) are slain.<sup id="cite_ref-Princes_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Princes-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Siege of Barad-dûr](Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md) begins<sup id="cite_ref-dddd_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dddd-1">[1]</a></sup>
