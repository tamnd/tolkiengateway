The **Second Fall of Minas Ithil** was the siege and capture of [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) by the forces of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) that occurred from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2000](../T/Third_Age_2000.md) to [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2002](../T/Third_Age_2002.md).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) killed a large number of people in Gondor, including king [Telemnar](../T/Telemnar.md) and all his children.<sup id="cite_ref-Telemnar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Telemnar-2">[2]</a></sup> The devastation was such that Minas Ithil was emptied of its population,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-3">[3]</a></sup> the fortresses that guarded the passes to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) were unmanned, and the watch on the borders of Mordor ceased due to a lack of troops.<sup id="cite_ref-Telemnar_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Telemnar-2">[2]</a></sup> As a result, evil beings were able enter Mordor secretly.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-3">[3]</a></sup> During the reign of king [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md), in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) reentered Mordor and gathered the other Nazgûl.<sup id="cite_ref-EarnilII_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EarnilII-5">[5]</a></sup>

Gondor must have sent a force to reoccupy Minas Ithil by the time the siege began in T.A. 2000, since there were people there to defend it, but the extent to which it was repopulated, and when these efforts began, is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Siege and capture {#Siege_and_capture}

Twenty years after the return of the Witch-king to Mordor, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2000](../T/Third_Age_2000.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> men, who had been dominated by [Sauron](Sauron.md) in his first strength and had wandered homeless and masterless after his fall, now led by the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> came out of Mordor by night<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-3">[3]</a></sup> over the Pass of [Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md),<sup id="cite_ref-EarnilII_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EarnilII-5">[5]</a></sup> and probably also over the [main pass](../M/Morgul_Pass.md) in the ravine at the end of the [valley](../M/Morgul_Vale.md) in which Minas Ithil was located,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-3">[3]</a></sup> and started a siege of Minas Ithil.<sup id="cite_ref-EarnilII_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EarnilII-5">[5]</a></sup> It is possible that the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md) was captured by the Nazgûl during the same night, because the Tower of Cirith Ungol was an eastern outpost of the defences of Minas Ithil<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and because the vigilance had failed at the Tower of Cirith Ungol and treachery had yielded it up to the Witch-king.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Two years later in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2002](../T/Third_Age_2002.md) the troops of the Nazgûl captured Minas Ithil and its *[palantír](../I/Ithil-stone.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-EarnilII_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EarnilII-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

After the capture of Minas Ithil by the Nazgûl, the city became a terrible place, too dreadful to look upon and was renamed [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md), the Tower of Sorcery.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-3">[3]</a></sup> Minas Anor was renamed [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), the Tower of the Guard and there was constant war between Minas Morgul and Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
