**Snails** were gastropods, noted for their low speed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> There were exceptionally large ones around [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), one of which fell victim to a [thrush](/wiki/Thrushes "Thrushes"), as observed by [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Balin](/wiki/Balin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Most of them did not survive the onset of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
