<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>

**Snails** were gastropods, noted for their low speed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> There were exceptionally large ones around [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), one of which fell victim to a [thrush](../T/Thrushes.md), as observed by [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Balin](../B/Balin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Most of them did not survive the onset of [Smaug](Smaug.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
