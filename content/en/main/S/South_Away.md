<i><b>South Away</b></i> is the second track of the album [Let Mortal Heroes Sing Your Fame](/wiki/Let_Mortal_Heroes_Sing_Your_Fame) by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The lyrics are based upon the poem [Roll-roll-roll-roll,](/wiki/Roll-roll-roll-roll,) which is found in [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Other references include words spoken by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the toast to the new [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan), [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Leave the halls and caverns deep <br/>
Were the forests wide and dim <br/>
Stoops in shadow grey and grim <br/>
<br/>
Float beyond the world of trees <br/>
Past the rushes, past the reeds <br/>
Past the marshes, weaving weeds <br/>
<br/>
&#34;[...] and the crowns of seven kings, and the rods of the Five Wizards.&#34; <br/>
<br/>
South away! South away now! <br/>
Far away seek the sunlight and the day. <br/>
<br/>
Hail, Éomer, king of the mark.
</p>
</div>
