**Stone Street Studios** (located in [Wellington](/wiki/Wellington), New Zealand) is the main film production studio used by [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) to film [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
