**Spark-dragons**, or <i><b>Fealóke</b></i>, were a breed of [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") of whom all that is known is from the nomenclature by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> No story tells of their appearance in the history of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). It is possible that they were somehow able to radiate "rays of light".<sup id="cite_ref-light_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-light-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore one can only speculate whether they were related in some way to, or were another name for, great [glow-worms](/wiki/Glow-worms "Glow-worms").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Fealóke* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "spark-dragon", consisting of *fea* ("spark"), from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") *PHAY-* ("radiate, send out rays of light"),<sup id="cite_ref-light_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-light-2">[2]</a></sup> and *lóke* ("dragon"), from the root *LOK-* ("great serpent, dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Light-drakes (Quenya <i>Kalalóki</i>) are rare, wingless Dragons. They prefer to fight with their tails, which hold an electrically charged fluid that delivers &#34;a &#39;sting&#39; analogous to the impact of a lightning bolt&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A &#34;Light-drake&#34; is a Creature of the Drake-kind, able to inflict two strikes.</dd></dl>
