<i><b>Stronghold</b></i> is the fourth full-length studio album by [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning) and was released in [1999](/wiki/1999). Unlike the previous three studio albums, the album name is not [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related. Only the song [Rhûn](</wiki/Rh%C3%BBn_(song)> "Rhûn (song)") has a title directly referring to Tolkien. Despite many of the lyrics still being adapted from Tolkien's works, this album was the first to incorporate lyrics and audio samples from other works.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
