<i><b>Stronghold</b></i> is the fourth full-length studio album by [Summoning](Summoning.md) and was released in [1999](../_/1999.md). Unlike the previous three studio albums, the album name is not [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related. Only the song [Rhûn](<../R/Rhûn_(song).md>) has a title directly referring to Tolkien. Despite many of the lyrics still being adapted from Tolkien's works, this album was the first to incorporate lyrics and audio samples from other works.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
