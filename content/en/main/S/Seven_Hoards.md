The **Seven Hoards of the Dwarf-kings** were legendary treasures of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). The name suggests that each hoard belonged to one of the seven [Dwarven clans](../../category/D/Dwarven_clans.md). As it is said, the foundation of each hoard was a golden ring, and they were probably gathered thanks to the [Seven Rings](Seven_Rings.md) given to the Dwarves by [Sauron](Sauron.md) in the [Second Age](Second_Age.md).

By the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the hoards had been plundered and devoured by [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Smaug](Smaug.md) had once claimed a dwarven-hoard, but it is not known if it was one of the Seven Hoards.
