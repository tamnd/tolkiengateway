The **Seven Hoards of the Dwarf-kings** were legendary treasures of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). The name suggests that each hoard belonged to one of the seven [Dwarven clans](/wiki/Category:Dwarven_clans "Category:Dwarven clans"). As it is said, the foundation of each hoard was a golden ring, and they were probably gathered thanks to the [Seven Rings](/wiki/Seven_Rings) given to the Dwarves by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

By the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the hoards had been plundered and devoured by [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) had once claimed a dwarven-hoard, but it is not known if it was one of the Seven Hoards.
