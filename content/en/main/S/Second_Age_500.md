**Second Age 500** (abbreviated to **S.A. 500**) is the 500th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- About this time, [Sauron](Sauron.md) begins to stir again in the East of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> This evil is soon perceived by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and the remnants of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), but they remain unaware of the source of the evil.<sup id="cite_ref-note7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-note7-3">[2]</a></sup>
