In *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")* roleplaying game, the Dwarf-mines of **Scowle Hill** were one of many abandoned mines in the northern foothills of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). Dwarves mined there for iron and coal in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), though they left when the nearby cities were destroyed at the end of that age.<sup id="cite_ref-Scowle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scowle-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974"), following the [Fall of Fornost](/wiki/Fall_of_Fornost), [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) fled north with whatever treasures his followers could carry. They took refuge inside the labyrinthine Mines of Scowle Hill to evade pursuit and await aid from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Scowle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scowle-1">[1]</a></sup>

Within the old mines, they were hunted by a blind bear-like monster known as the Beast of Angmar. They managed to barricade themselves safely, but were cut off from any food sources. This eventually forced them to abandon the mines and flee towards [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel), though they had to leave a lot of treasure behind.<sup id="cite_ref-Scowle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scowle-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
