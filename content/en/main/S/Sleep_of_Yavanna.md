The **Sleep of Yavanna** was a period of the very ancient times of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), marked by darkness and stillness of nature.

## Background {#Background}

The two great [Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) which were giving light to the World during the [Spring of Arda](Spring_of_Arda.md) were destroyed by [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) along with the Valar's dwellings on [Almaren](../A/Almaren.md), driving them away into the [West](../U/Uttermost_West.md).<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus the [Great Lands](../M/Middle-earth.md) were left in darkness, and [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) protected the creatures living there by setting them to sleep and they did not grow or age until light should come again.<sup id="cite_ref-c3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-c3-2">[2]</a></sup> However, some forests seem to keep growing even in that darkness: when [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) and [Melian](../M/Melian.md) fell in their enraptured spell, "the trees of [Nan Elmoth](../N/Nan_Elmoth.md) grew tall and dark before they spoke any word".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

While the Valar were preparing the bliss of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) bathed in the light of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md), [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) was in darkness and silence, and fell beasts of hideous form multiplied in [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md). It was [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) who did not forsake the Outer Lands and kept the earth alive. Yavanna also came healing the hurts of Melkor, and [Orome](../O/Orome.md) hunted monsters, and kept the shadows at bay while blowing the [Valaróma](../V/Valaróma.md), albeit temporarily.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was during the Sleep of Yavanna when [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) in his impatience created the [Seven Fathers](../F/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md) of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) under the mountains of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). As Middle-earth was dark and dangerous, Aulë made his children robust and sturdy.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Varda](../V/Varda.md) created new [stars](Stars.md) and the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) awoke. Only Dwarves and Elves roamed Middle-earth which was lit only by the stars, and most creatures were sleeping.<sup id="cite_ref-c3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-c3-2">[2]</a></sup> Most life could be found in [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) under the guidance of [Melian](../M/Melian.md) and in the North where [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and other creatures of the [Enemy](../M/Morgoth.md) multiplied.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Long Night](../L/Long_Night.md), it was the rising of the [Sun](Sun.md) that finally returned light to Middle-earth and a [Second Spring of Arda](Second_Spring_of_Arda.md).
