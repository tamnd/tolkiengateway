The **Sleep of Yavanna** was a period of the very ancient times of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), marked by darkness and stillness of nature.

## Background {#Background}

The two great [Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps "Two Lamps") of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) which were giving light to the World during the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda) were destroyed by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") along with the Valar's dwellings on [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren), driving them away into the [West](/wiki/Uttermost_West "Uttermost West").<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus the [Great Lands](/wiki/Middle-earth "Middle-earth") were left in darkness, and [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) protected the creatures living there by setting them to sleep and they did not grow or age until light should come again.<sup id="cite_ref-c3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-c3-2">[2]</a></sup> However, some forests seem to keep growing even in that darkness: when [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) and [Melian](/wiki/Melian) fell in their enraptured spell, "the trees of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth) grew tall and dark before they spoke any word".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

While the Valar were preparing the bliss of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) bathed in the light of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"), [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) was in darkness and silence, and fell beasts of hideous form multiplied in [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno). It was [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) who did not forsake the Outer Lands and kept the earth alive. Yavanna also came healing the hurts of Melkor, and [Orome](/wiki/Orome) hunted monsters, and kept the shadows at bay while blowing the [Valaróma](/wiki/Valar%C3%B3ma), albeit temporarily.<sup id="cite_ref-s1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s1-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was during the Sleep of Yavanna when [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) in his impatience created the [Seven Fathers](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves "Fathers of the Dwarves") of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) under the mountains of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). As Middle-earth was dark and dangerous, Aulë made his children robust and sturdy.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Varda](/wiki/Varda) created new [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") and the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) awoke. Only Dwarves and Elves roamed Middle-earth which was lit only by the stars, and most creatures were sleeping.<sup id="cite_ref-c3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-c3-2">[2]</a></sup> Most life could be found in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) under the guidance of [Melian](/wiki/Melian) and in the North where [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other creatures of the [Enemy](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") multiplied.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Long Night](/wiki/Long_Night), it was the rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) that finally returned light to Middle-earth and a [Second Spring of Arda](/wiki/Second_Spring_of_Arda).
