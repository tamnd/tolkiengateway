**Samuel Hazeldine**, also known as **Sam Hazeldine**, is an English actor. He portrayed [Adar](</wiki/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Adar (The Rings of Power)") in <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 2 (page does not exist)">the second season</a> of the *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series by [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon_Studios "Amazon Studios"), taking over the role from [Joseph Mawle](/wiki/Joseph_Mawle).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
