The **Serni** was a river in the south of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The Serni flowed from a source south of the eastern [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) southwestward through [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md) until it met the river [Gilrain](../G/Gilrain.md) just above [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Serni* is an "adjectival formation" deriving from [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) *[sarn](Sarn.md)* ("small stone", "pebble") (therefore probably meaning "stony"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> or "pebbly") or the equivalent of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) collective *[sarnie](Sar.md)* (*sarniye*) ("shingle", "pebble bank").<sup id="cite_ref-UTIndex_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIndex-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-vt_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vt-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-8">[8]</a></sup>

The name of the river is spelled *Serni* in the maps of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Rivers and Beacon-hills of Gondor](../T/The_Rivers_and_Beacon-hills_of_Gondor~21248.md)*. In the footnote 1 of the Preface of [The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md) it was spelled *Sernui*<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> until the 2014 edition, where the editors [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) and [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) have changed the name to *Serni*, considering *Sernui* "very likely a typographic error for *Serni*" even though they refer to a comment by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md) who informed them that *sernui* "would be possible as an unattested adjectival formation \*'stony' from *sarn* 'stone'".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

It is mentioned that the Serni continued to be called the Serni after its confluence with the Gilraen until it flowed into the sea and that its mouth was blocked with shingles in the entry *Serni* in the index of *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* that was written by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) in [1980](../_/1980.md) and in the etymological essay [The Rivers and Beacon-hills of Gondor](../T/The_Rivers_and_Beacon-hills_of_Gondor~21248.md) that was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) sometime after June [1969](../_/1969.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UTIndex_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIndex-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NM_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> In addition, the label "Serni" is placed downstream from the confluence of the two rivers not far upstream from where the river flows into the Bay of Belfalas on the map of [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md), which was drawn by Christopher Tolkien and published with *Unfinished Tales* in 1980.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

On the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md), which was drawn by Christopher Tolkien in late [1953](../_/1953.md) and published with older editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* the "R" of the label "River GILRAIN" is placed downstream from the confluence of the two rivers not far upstream where the river flows into the Bay of Belfalas. This is consistent with the location of the "R" in the label "R Gilrain" on [The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), which was drawn by J.R.R. Tolkien during the writing of *The Lord of the Rings* based on which Christopher Tolkien drew the General Map of Middle-earth. The text of a revised version of the draft manuscript for the chapter *[The Last Debate](../T/The_Last_Debate.md)* explicitly states that Linhir and the fords were on the shore of the Gilrain, that the men of Lamedon defended the passage over the Gilrain at Linhir and that the enemy had sailed up the mouth of the Gilrain.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

For the joint course of the Gilrain and the Serni from their confluence to the sea the name *Linhir* was also used.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The river Serni was found in Lower <a href="../L/Lebennin.md" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> in the region of Central <a href="../G/Gondor.md" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>. As its name indicated, it was strewn with rocks. There were two fords, with the western one being south of the town of &#34;Malbarth&#34; and the eastern one south of &#34;Ost Anglebed&#34;. The Serni was the domain of the stern River-maid Grey-eye, one of the Five Sisters, though she and her sisters were widely considered a myth by the time of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>.</dd></dl>
