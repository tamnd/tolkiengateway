**Sir Gawain and the Green Knight** is a [14th century Middle English manuscript](<Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(poem).md>). Here, it can refer to:

## Publications {#Publications}

- *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](<Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition).md>)*, the [1925](../_/1925.md) edition by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md).
- Stanza 32 of *Sir Gawain and the Green Knight*, translated by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), a frament included in [1961](../_/1961.md) in *[A Short History of English Poetry](../A/A_Short_History_of_English_Poetry.md)*.
- *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl and Sir Orfeo](Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo.md)* by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), a book published posthumously in [1975](../_/1975.md), with the full translation of the poem along with *Pearl* and *[Sir Orfeo](<Sir_Orfeo_(booklet).md>)*.
- [Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (lecture)](<Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(lecture).md>), delivered by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1953](../_/1953.md) as part of the [W.P. Ker Lecture](../W/W.P._Ker_Lecture.md) series and later reprinted in [The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays.md).
