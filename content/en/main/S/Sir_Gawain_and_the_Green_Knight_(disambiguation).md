**Sir Gawain and the Green Knight** is a [14th century Middle English manuscript](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(poem)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (poem)"). Here, it can refer to:

## Publications {#Publications}

- *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (edition)")*, the [1925](/wiki/1925) edition by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon).
- Stanza 32 of *Sir Gawain and the Green Knight*, translated by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), a frament included in [1961](/wiki/1961) in *[A Short History of English Poetry](/wiki/A_Short_History_of_English_Poetry)*.
- *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo)* by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), a book published posthumously in [1975](/wiki/1975), with the full translation of the poem along with *Pearl* and *[Sir Orfeo](</wiki/Sir_Orfeo_(booklet)> "Sir Orfeo (booklet)")*.
- [Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (lecture)](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(lecture)>), delivered by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") in [1953](/wiki/1953) as part of the [W.P. Ker Lecture](/wiki/W.P._Ker_Lecture) series and later reprinted in [The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays).
