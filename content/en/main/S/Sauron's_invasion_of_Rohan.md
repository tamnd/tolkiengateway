On [11 March](/wiki/11_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup>[<i>dubious — <a href="/wiki/Talk:Sauron%27s_invasion_of_Rohan" title="Talk:Sauron&#39;s invasion of Rohan">discuss</a></i>]</sup> invaded [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) as part of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). They were defeated by the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) [the next day](/wiki/12_March "12 March").<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Muster_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Prelude {#Prelude}

On [4 March](/wiki/4_March), after the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) called for a [full muster of Rohan](/wiki/Eohere "Eohere") to be assembled at [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) on [10 March](/wiki/10_March).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> On [9 March](/wiki/9_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> [Hirgon](/wiki/Hirgon) arrived at [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) requesting on behalf of [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) that the Rohirrim ride to the defense of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), expecting it to soon be [besieged](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> The assembled forces departed for Minas Tirith [the next day](/wiki/10_March "10 March"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> leaving Rohan relatively undefended.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup>

## The Battle {#The_Battle}

On 11 March,<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> orcs invaded [the Wold](/wiki/The_Wold "The Wold"),<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-4">[3]</a></sup> but the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) defeated them the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> Few of the invaders survived.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
