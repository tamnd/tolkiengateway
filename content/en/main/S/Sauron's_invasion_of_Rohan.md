On [11 March](../_/11_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), [orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup>[<i>dubious — <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Talk:Sauron%27s_invasion_of_Rohan" title="Talk:Sauron&#39;s invasion of Rohan">discuss</a></i>]</sup> invaded [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) as part of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). They were defeated by the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) [the next day](../_/12_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Muster_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Prelude {#Prelude}

On [4 March](../_/4_March.md), after the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) called for a [full muster of Rohan](../E/Eohere.md) to be assembled at [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) on [10 March](../_/10_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> On [9 March](../_/9_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> [Hirgon](../H/Hirgon.md) arrived at [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md) requesting on behalf of [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) that the Rohirrim ride to the defense of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), expecting it to soon be [besieged](Siege_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> The assembled forces departed for Minas Tirith [the next day](../_/10_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> leaving Rohan relatively undefended.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup>

## The Battle {#The_Battle}

On 11 March,<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> orcs invaded [the Wold](../T/The_Wold.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-4">[3]</a></sup> but the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) defeated them the next day.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> Few of the invaders survived.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>
