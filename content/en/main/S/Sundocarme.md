**Sundocarmë** or "Base-structure" is the title of a section in Tolkien's <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tengwesta_Qenderinwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tengwesta Qenderinwa (page does not exist)">Tengwesta Qenderinwa</a> (*Quendian Grammar*) dealing with the three main shapes of [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) etymological roots and their modifications.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> All [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) and [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) words (and in a sense, also [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md)) derive from a base root or [sundo](Sundo.md).

Roots are not actual words, but bases that contain the general meanings, and from which, during the process of evolution and times, real words were derived through affixes, suffixes, or modifications.

In the very first phase of the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md), roots were probably used as actual words; in that period the Elves could speak only through uninflected words, without any differentiation between parts of the speech (Tradition says that the very first word ever said by an Elf was *el-e!*, a primitive exclamation "look!", referring to a star).

The consonantal frame of the root was called [canta](../C/Canta.md) and the central radical vowel called [sundóma](Sundóma.md).

## Categories {#Categories}

Roots usually have the construction combinations:

- VC or CV (examples: K<sup>W</sup>E "speak"; this category is called *monoconsonantic*)
- (C)VCV (examples: <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=DELE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="DELE (page does not exist)">DELE</a>- "walk", [ABA](../A/ABA.md)- "deny"; this category was preferred for meanings related to actions/verbs)
- CVC(V) (examples: <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TATA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="TATA (page does not exist)">TATA</a>- "two", [TUR](../T/TUR~21398.md)- "power, control")
