**Second Age 443** (abbreviated to **S.A. 443**) is the 443rd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- The *de jure* end of [Vardamir Nólimon](/wiki/Vardamir "Vardamir")'s rule as [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
