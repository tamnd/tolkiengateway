The **Seat of Seeing** was an ancient chair built upon the summit of [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md) within a great circular stone-paved court surrounded by a battlement, all of which was in ruins when visited by [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md). The high seat rested upon four carven pillars and could be reached by a stair of many steps.<sup id="cite_ref-Amon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early History {#Early_History}

The high seat upon Amon Hen had been made in the days of the great kings, according to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later it is stated that the Seat of Seeing was on the Hill of the Eye of the Men of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Amon_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amon-1">[1]</a></sup> It is thus open to interpretation whether the chair was built during the early years of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) or sometime in the [Second Age](Second_Age.md). At the very least the high seat was built before the time of the [Stewards](Stewards_of_Gondor.md), which meant that when Frodo sat there it was at a minimum 969 years old. One likely possibility is that the Seat of Seeing was built at the same time as the [Argonath](../A/Argonath.md), which occurred after Minalcar defeated the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) (in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1248](../T/Third_Age_1248.md)) and took the name of [Rómendacil II](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) but before the end of his reign (he died in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1366](../T/Third_Age_1366.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Later History {#Later_History}

On 26 February [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) after Frodo had fled from [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) (part of the [Breaking of the Fellowship](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md)) he came to the summit of the hill and, while still wearing [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), sat in the high seat. At first he saw little but mist and shadows, but soon he perceived many visions, soundless but filled with bright living images. The visions he saw stretched far beyond the normal range of sight (for instance, he saw the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md), over 300 miles away and behind the [Ered Nimrais](../W/White_Mountains.md)) and everywhere he looked he saw signs of war. His glimpse of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) filled him with hope, which was crushed when he looked at [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md). <sup id="cite_ref-Amon_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amon-1">[1]</a></sup>

Frodo then became aware of the gaze of the [Eye](Sauron.md#Eye_of_Sauron), which felt like a finger trying to nail him down. He jumped out of the chair and cowered with his hood covering his head. At that moment from some other point of power came another thought: “Take it off! Take it off! Fool, take it off! Take off the Ring!” Balanced between Eye and Voice, Frodo regained his ability to choose and removed the Ring an instant before he was discovered. Frodo then realized what he must do, which was to go to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) alone, and he left the Seat of Seeing behind.<sup id="cite_ref-Amon_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amon-1">[1]</a></sup>

It has been speculated that the visions Frodo saw were due solely to the magical power of the Seat of Seeing. However, while searching for Frodo, Aragorn ran to the summit and sat on the chair.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Even though he was the heir of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) he saw no visions (although he was in a hurry and a bit distracted). It appears (since Frodo did not see far-off visions at other times while wearing the Ring) that it was a combination of the Seat of Seeing and the power of the Ring that gave Frodo this temporary effect.

The identity of the Voice that strove against the Eye was later revealed when Aragorn, [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), and [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) first meet [Gandalf the White](../G/Gandalf.md) in [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md). Gandalf informs the others that the Ring was nearly revealed to [Sauron](Sauron.md) but “I sat in a high place, and I strove with the Dark Tower; and the Shadow passed”.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
