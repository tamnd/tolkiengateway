**Second Age 442** (abbreviated to **S.A. 442**) is the 442nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) [Elros Tar-Minyatur](../E/Elros.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Elros's eldest son, [Vardamir Nólimon](../V/Vardamir.md), becomes the second [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) but immediately abdicates in favour of his son.<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Vardamir's son, [Tar-Amandil](../T/Tar-Amandil.md), becomes the third [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Kings2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings2-3">[3]</a></sup>
