**Sonny Melendrez** (born [1946](../_/1946.md)) is an American actor and radio host who voiced the part of [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) in [*The Return of the King* (1980 film)](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>).
