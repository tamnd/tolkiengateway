**Seventh Age 1** (abbreviated to **Se.A. 1**) is the 1st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Seventh Age](Seventh_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The beginning of the [Seventh Age](Seventh_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AD_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AD-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) enters [Eä](../E/Eä.md) and is [incarnated](../O/Old_Hope.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AD_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AD-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
