The second edition of the [*The One Ring* Roleplaying Game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") has had two **starter sets** so far, one simply titled <i><b>Starter Set</b></i> in [2021](/wiki/2021) and another titled <i><b>Over Hill and Under Hill Starter Set</b></i>. A version of the latter was also released for *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*, which was again simply titled <i><b>Starter Set</b></i>.

The material of the first starter set was later republished as *[Hobbit Tales](/wiki/Hobbit_Tales)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

### Original Starter Set {#Original_Starter_Set}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The One Ring™ Starter Set contains all you need to take your first step into Middle-earth and experience the adventures Hobbits get up to. With streamlined rules and ready made characters, it is easy to quickly sit down and actually play the game. The One Ring is set between the time Bilbo went on his journey and the events in The Lord of the Rings™, allowing you to explore the Shire by taking on the roles of those same Hobbits whose children will go on to do extraordinary things, such as Drogo Baggins, Rory Brandybuck and Esmeralda Took. Besides the Rules and The Adventures, there’s a full compendium covering the four Farthings of the Shire, all the way from Greenholm and the White Downs in the west to Buckland and the Old Forest in the east.
</p><p><b>Includes</b>
</p>
<ul><li>A 24-page volume of Rules.</li>
<li>A 52-page volume describing The Shire.</li>
<li>A 31-page volume containing The Adventures.</li>
<li>Eight double-sided pre-generated character sheets.</li>
<li>Two large maps showing the Shire and Eriador.</li>
<li>30 Wargear Cards that can also be used with The One Ring core rules.</li>
<li>Six double-sided Journey Role and Combat Stance Cards that can also be used with The One Ring core rules.</li>
<li>Two D12 <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_Dice_Sets" title="The One Ring Dice Sets">Feat Dice</a>.</li>
<li>Six D6 <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_Dice_Sets" title="The One Ring Dice Sets">Success Dice</a>.</li></ul>
</blockquote>

### Over Hill and Under Hill Starter Set {#Over_Hill_and_Under_Hill_Starter_Set}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>You are enjoying a rare, quiet evening at the inn in Bree when a hunter joins your table to share a tale about a dark threat looming over the North Downs. However, you are not merely simple guests at The Prancing Pony — you are members of a Company. It is your self-appointed duty to inquire about the bone-chilling dangers that might emerge from the houseless hills beyond. Are you ready to take the road to adventure?
</p><p>This new and beginner-friendly starter set for The One Ring™ tabletop RPG, based on the works of J.R.R. Tolkien, provides all you need to embark on your journeys in Middle-earth. Immerse yourself in a world set between the time of Bilbo’s first adventure and the events of The Lord of the Rings™, exploring the Lone-lands of Eriador and taking on the roles of characters whose stories are yet to be told. This boxed set contains:
</p>
<ul><li>Condensed rules for action resolution, combat, councils and journeys.</li>
<li>The complete adventure Over Hill and Under Hill, bringing the heroes deep into the wild lands of northern Eriador to face a dark threat.</li>
<li>A large full-colour map of Eriador, with a combat diagram on the back.</li>
<li>Beautifully illustrated cardboard stand-up figures for Player-heroes and Adversaries.</li>
<li>Five pre-generated and illustrated Player-heroes.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_Dice_Sets" title="The One Ring Dice Sets">Two D12 Feat Dice and six D6 Success Dice, all engraved with custom symbols.</a></li></ul>
</blockquote>
