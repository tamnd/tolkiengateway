**Snaga** was a contemptuous term for the lesser [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), used especially among the larger and stronger [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai). It comes from a word in the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) meaning 'slave' or 'servant'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

There are two orcs known to be named (or called) "Snaga":

- [Snaga (orc of Isengard)](</wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Isengard)>)
- [Snaga (orc of Mordor)](</wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor)>)
