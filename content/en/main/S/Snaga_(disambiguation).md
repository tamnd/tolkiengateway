**Snaga** was a contemptuous term for the lesser [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), used especially among the larger and stronger [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md). It comes from a word in the [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md) meaning 'slave' or 'servant'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

There are two orcs known to be named (or called) "Snaga":

- [Snaga (orc of Isengard)](<Snaga_(orc_of_Isengard).md>)
- [Snaga (orc of Mordor)](<Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor).md>)
