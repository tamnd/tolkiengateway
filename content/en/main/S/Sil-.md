<i><b>sil-</b></i> means "shine" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It implies a white or silver light, often used when describing [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") or the [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") SIL/THIL "shine silver".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- *See also:* [Letter to Peter Alford (1956-58)](</wiki/Letter_to_Peter_Alford_(1956-58)>) (letter)

## Forms {#Forms}

- *síla* ("3rd person singular present")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Silmaril" title="Silmaril"><b>Sil</b>maril</a></i> = "Shining Light"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Telperion#Other_names" title="Telperion"><b>Sil</b>pion</a></i> = "Shining Lights" (*Silpion* is another name of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion))
- <i><a href="/wiki/Elen_s%C3%ADla_l%C3%BAmenn%27_omentielvo" title="Elen síla lúmenn&#39; omentielvo">Elen <b>síla</b> lúmenn&#39; omentielvo</a></i> = "A star **shines** on the hour of our meeting"
