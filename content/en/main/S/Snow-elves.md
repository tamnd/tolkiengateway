The **Snow-elves** lived at the North Pole. Not much is known about the Snow-elves; apparently they dressed in white (unlike the [Red](/wiki/Red_Elves "Red Elves") and [Green Elves](/wiki/Green-elves#Other_writings "Green-elves")), since they blended with the snow.<sup id="cite_ref-1929b_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1929b-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Snow-elves only appear once in tales of the *[Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas "Letters from Father Christmas")*, at a celebration of the coming of winter. The festivities were organised by [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas), and included a Bonfire to make the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear) happy. To the surprise of Father Christmas and the Polar Bear, the Snow-elves let off all the rockets — hundreds of them — together.<sup id="cite_ref-1929b_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1929b-1">[1]</a></sup>
