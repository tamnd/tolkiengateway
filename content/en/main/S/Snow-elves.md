The **Snow-elves** lived at the North Pole. Not much is known about the Snow-elves; apparently they dressed in white (unlike the [Red](../R/Red_Elves.md) and [Green Elves](../G/Green-elves.md#Other_writings)), since they blended with the snow.<sup id="cite_ref-1929b_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1929b-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Snow-elves only appear once in tales of the *[Father Christmas Letters](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)*, at a celebration of the coming of winter. The festivities were organised by [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md), and included a Bonfire to make the [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) happy. To the surprise of Father Christmas and the Polar Bear, the Snow-elves let off all the rockets — hundreds of them — together.<sup id="cite_ref-1929b_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1929b-1">[1]</a></sup>
