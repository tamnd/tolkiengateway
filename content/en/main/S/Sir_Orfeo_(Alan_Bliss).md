<i><b>Sir Orfeo</b></i> is an edition of the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) poem <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Orfeo" title="Sir Orfeo">Sir Orfeo</a></i> by [Alan Bliss](/wiki/Alan_Bliss). It prints all three manuscripts and reconstructs a thirty-eight line prologue.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The edition was first published in the *Oxford English Monographs*, of which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was a general editor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- Note: This publication is included in [Pieter Collier](/wiki/Pieter_Collier)'s "List of all Tolkien books".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## From the Introduction {#From_the_Introduction}

"\[Bliss thanks Tolkien\] *whose penetrating scholarship is an inspiration to all who have worked with him*" (p. vi) <span></span>
