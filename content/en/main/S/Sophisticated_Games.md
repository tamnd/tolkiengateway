**Sophisticated Games** is an international board game publisher. It specializes in the development and packaging of all book-based [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) games under license from [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises). Sophisticated Games is based in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge), [United Kingdom](/wiki/United_Kingdom).

The company develops and packages all non-movie [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) games under license from [Middle-earth Enterprises](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises), and has published many titles in this area: Reiner Knizia’s [Lord of the Rings](</wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_(board_game)> "Lord of the Rings (board game)"), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit:_The_Defeat_of_the_Evil_Dragon_Smaug&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit: The Defeat of the Evil Dragon Smaug (page does not exist)">The Hobbit</a> and the award winning [War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2004_board_game)> "War of the Ring (2004 board game)") series in conjunction with [ARES Games](/wiki/ARES_Games) in Italy. Sophisticated Games has also, in partnership with [Free League Publishing](/wiki/Free_League_Publishing) in Sweden, published a new edition of [The One Ring Role Playing Game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)"),

The company has also published five The Lord of the Rings™ and The Hobbit jigsaws, four featuring the art of [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe) and [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), and one the art of [Francesco Mattioli](/wiki/Francesco_Mattioli).
