The **Shadowy Seas** were that part of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") that lay closest to the shores of the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Aman "Aman"), to the immediate east of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) and the [Bay of Eldamar](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar). After the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor) and the first rising of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) elected to shield their realm from the outside world. They enmeshed these seas with a bewildering shadow - the source of their name - and created a chain of [Enchanted Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles), each of which had the power to ensnare any mariner braving this region.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Throughout the centuries that followed, no ship reached the shores of [Aman](/wiki/Aman), until [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) finally succeeded in passing through the shadows of the Shadowy Seas.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

The Shadowy Seas are referenced in Tolkien's *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. In the story, written down in [1927](/wiki/1927), the great whale [Uin](/wiki/Uin) takes the enchanted dog [Roverandom](/wiki/Rover "Rover") (formerly known as Rover) on adventures through the seas: Uin takes Roverandom through the Shadowy Seas to the [Bay of Fairyland](/wiki/Bay_of_Eldamar "Bay of Eldamar") beyond the [Magic Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles "Enchanted Isles") where Rover saw the [Mountains of Elvenhome](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri "Pelóri") and the light of Faery. Roverandom thought he could see the white glint of [a city of Elves on a green hill far away in the distance](/wiki/Tirion "Tirion").<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-3">[3]</a></sup>
