The **Shadowy Seas** were that part of the [Great Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) that lay closest to the shores of the [Blessed Realm](../A/Aman.md), to the immediate east of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) and the [Bay of Eldamar](../B/Bay_of_Eldamar.md). After the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) and the first rising of the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) elected to shield their realm from the outside world. They enmeshed these seas with a bewildering shadow - the source of their name - and created a chain of [Enchanted Isles](../E/Enchanted_Isles.md), each of which had the power to ensnare any mariner braving this region.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Throughout the centuries that followed, no ship reached the shores of [Aman](../A/Aman.md), until [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) finally succeeded in passing through the shadows of the Shadowy Seas.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

The Shadowy Seas are referenced in Tolkien's *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)*. In the story, written down in [1927](../_/1927.md), the great whale [Uin](../U/Uin.md) takes the enchanted dog [Roverandom](../R/Rover.md) (formerly known as Rover) on adventures through the seas: Uin takes Roverandom through the Shadowy Seas to the [Bay of Fairyland](../B/Bay_of_Eldamar.md) beyond the [Magic Isles](../E/Enchanted_Isles.md) where Rover saw the [Mountains of Elvenhome](../P/Pelóri.md) and the light of Faery. Roverandom thought he could see the white glint of [a city of Elves on a green hill far away in the distance](../T/Tirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-3">[3]</a></sup>
