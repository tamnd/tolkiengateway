The **Silvan Elves** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") <i><b>Tawarwaith</b></i>), or the **Wood-elves**, were the reclusive, forest-dwelling [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). In the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Silvan Elves mostly belonged either to the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) or to the [Elves of Mirkwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-SE-UTa_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SE-UTa-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Nandor" title="Nandor">Nandor</a></b></div></div>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r398211">.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote{position:relative;margin:0.5rem 0;padding:0.75rem 1rem;box-shadow:var(--tg-border-color-base)0px 0px 0px 1px inset;border-radius:var(--tg-border-radius-medium);background:var(--tg-color-surface-2);font-size:var(--tg-font-size-small);line-height:var(--tg-line-height-xx-small);display:flex;gap:0.5rem;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon{display:flex;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element{display:block}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element[src$=".svg"]{filter:var(--filter-invert);opacity:0.5}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--red{background:var(--background-color-destructive-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--yellow{background:var(--background-color-warning-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--green{background:var(--background-color-success-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}</style>

The Silvan Elves were descendants of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) - and thus in origin part of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) - who had lingered in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) during the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days). The Silvan Elves hid themselves in their woodland realms beyond the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and became a scattered folk hardly distinguishable from [Avari](/wiki/Avari), the Dark Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-SE-UTa_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SE-UTa-1">[1]</a></sup> While some of the Nandor continued to [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and later entered [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), they remained in the Vales of Anduin, and from these latter originated the Silvan Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-SE-UTa_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SE-UTa-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is also told that the some of the western [Avari](/wiki/Avari), dwelling in Eriador and the Vales of Anduin, were friendly to the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), and came to merge with the Wood-elves. These were [Nelyarin](/wiki/Nelyar "Nelyar") Avari ([Penni](/wiki/Penni)), and therefore remotely akin to the Nandor and Sindar.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

In the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), many [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), survivors of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), left [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) to establish woodland realms among the Silvan Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-4">[3]</a></sup> It was before the building of the [Dark Tower](/wiki/Dark_Tower) in c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000") that the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) made this migration.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Greenwood the Great {#Greenwood_the_Great}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood" title="Elves of Mirkwood">Elves of Mirkwood</a></b></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

In the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) (father of [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) and grandfather of [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas)) along with few other [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) came to [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great), east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). There, he became the King of the Silvan Elves east of Anduin and established the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> The culture of the Silvan folk was rude and rustic in comparison to the [Beleriandic](/wiki/Beleriand "Beleriand") culture of the Sindar but soon enough, the Sindar had merged with the Silvan Elves and adopted their language, taking names of Silvan form and style.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

Although the Silvan Elves initially dwelt in the south of the Wood,<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> centred around [Amon Lanc](/wiki/Amon_Lanc),<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-8">[7]</a></sup> they constantly retreated northwards (thrice in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age))<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-8">[7]</a></sup> and after the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) had fallen upon [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) by the mid-[Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Silvan Elves dwelt in the far north-east of the forest. Their king, [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) (the son of [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher), who was slain in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance)), built an [underground fortress](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls "Elvenking's Halls") for himself and his people inspired by [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) in the far [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

### Lothlórien {#Lothlórien}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Galadhrim" title="Galadhrim">Galadhrim</a></b></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

Little is known of their early history save that in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), they were ruled by a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") prince named [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr). After the destruction of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697"), many of its exiles, both [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), escaped through [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) into the east, and had joined the Silvan Elves of Lórien and mingled with them.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-9">[8]</a></sup>

[Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr) was lost in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) so his son, [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), succeeded to his kingship. During his reign, [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) was unleashed in [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and many of Lórien's people fled south to [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond). [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) himself was among them, and he was drowned in the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas). It was at this time that [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) became the [Lord and Lady of Lórien](/wiki/Lord_and_Lady_of_L%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-10">[9]</a></sup>

### Later history {#Later_history}

Although both Silvan realms were invaded in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the fall of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) ushered in a long peace whence the Silvan Elves in the now-cleansed [Eryn Lasgalen](/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen) long remained untroubled. Now the Silvan Elves once again dwelt in the south of the Wood, but these were [Elves of Lórien](/wiki/Elves_of_L%C3%B3rien) who crossed the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to live in a new realm named [East Lórien](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien). Though the original land of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) faded after the passing of [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), the Silvan Elves of the Wood lived peacefully under their trees for many long years.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[note 2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The English adjective <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/silvan" title="wiktionary:silvan">silvan</a></i> means "of, characteristic of, or consisting of woods or forests" or "living or located in woods or forests".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

An [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) name for the Silvan Elves was <i><b>Tawarwaith</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup>. It has been suggested that *Tawarwaith* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, consisting of *tawar* (wood, forest) + *[gwaith](/wiki/Gwaith "Gwaith")*(people).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[14]</a></sup>
