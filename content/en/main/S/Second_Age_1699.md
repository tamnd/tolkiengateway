**Second Age 1699** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1699**) is the 1699th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Sauron](Sauron.md) overruns [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
