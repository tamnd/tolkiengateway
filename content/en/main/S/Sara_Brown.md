**Sara Brown** is a Tolkien scholar and Language &amp; Literature Department Chair at <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Signum_University&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Signum University (page does not exist)">Signum University</a>. She has a PhD from Salford University with a thesis entitled *From Abjection to Alchemy in J.R.R. Tolkien’s Middle-earth Legendarium*.

She also co-hosts [The Tolkien Experience](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Experience) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Rings_of_Power_Wrap-up&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Rings of Power Wrap-up (page does not exist)">The Rings of Power Wrap-up</a> podcasts and serves on the editorial board of [Mallorn (journal)](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)>).

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2023](/wiki/2023): **[Tolkien Society Award](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_Awards "Tolkien Society Awards"): Best Article**, "'All that glisters is not gold': The Ring of Power and the Deception of Simile" in [Mallorn 63](/wiki/Mallorn_63)
- [2024](/wiki/2024): **[Tolkien Society Award](/wiki/Tolkien_Society_Awards "Tolkien Society Awards"): Best Article**, "'The Tale of ‘Aldarion and Erendis': Not Just a Medieval Love Story" in [Journal of Tolkien Research](/wiki/Journal_of_Tolkien_Research) vol. 18 no. 1
