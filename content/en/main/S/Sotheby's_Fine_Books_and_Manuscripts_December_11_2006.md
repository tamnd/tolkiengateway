<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s Fine Books and Manuscripts December 11, 2006</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby%27s) (New York), which includes a photograph and transcribed excerpts from a letter from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to [Naomi Mitchison](/wiki/Naomi_Mitchison) (see [letter 122](/wiki/Letter_122 "Letter 122")). The letter is erroneously said in the catalogue to have been written on 12 december 1949 (correct date is 18 December 1949).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
