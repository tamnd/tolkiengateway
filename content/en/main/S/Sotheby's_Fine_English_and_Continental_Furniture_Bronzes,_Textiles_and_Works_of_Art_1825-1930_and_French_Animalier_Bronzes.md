The description for Lot 116, a sale of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s private desk, includes [a written statement on the desk](/wiki/Desk_certificate_27_July_1972 "Desk certificate 27 July 1972").
