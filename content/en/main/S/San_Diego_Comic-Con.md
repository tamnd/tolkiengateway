**San Diego Comic-Con** is a convention that is held annually in San Diego, in California, which features numerous collectibles inspired by [J.R.R. Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
