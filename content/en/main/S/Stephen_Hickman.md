**Stephen Hickman** ([9 April](/wiki/9_April) [1949](/wiki/1949) - [16 July](/wiki/16_July) [2021](/wiki/2021)) was an American science-fiction and fantasy artist.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> While generally known for book covers, he also created [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)-inspired illustrations, some of them for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).
