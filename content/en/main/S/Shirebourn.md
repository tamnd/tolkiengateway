The **Shirebourn** is a lesser river of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)'s [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md). Its source was in the uplands of the [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md), from where it flowed south and then southeast, where it matched the boundary with the [Southfarthing](Southfarthing.md). At [Willowbottom](../W/Willowbottom.md), it met the [Thistle Brook](../T/Thistle_Brook.md), and then flowed eastwards.

The Shirebourn flowed into the Brandywine beneath [Deephallow](../D/Deephallow.md), and at its mouth formed the boggy region known as the [Overbourn Marshes](../O/Overbourn_Marshes.md), opposite the [southern end](../H/Haysend.md) of the [High Hay](../H/High_Hay.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its outflow was the [Mithe](../M/Mithe.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Shirebourn* means "Bright-stream". The etymology must be [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *Scire-burna* from *scïr* "bright, clear, pure" + *burna* "stream".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the name has nothing to do with *Shire*, in most translations, it is translated "River of the Shire" instead.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Schirebourn or Shirebourne is the old name of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Sherbourne" title="wikipedia:River Sherbourne">Sherbourne</a> river by Coventry, having the same etymology.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
