The **Shirebourn** is a lesser river of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing). Its source was in the uplands of the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country), from where it flowed south and then southeast, where it matched the boundary with the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing). At [Willowbottom](/wiki/Willowbottom), it met the [Thistle Brook](/wiki/Thistle_Brook), and then flowed eastwards.

The Shirebourn flowed into the Brandywine beneath [Deephallow](/wiki/Deephallow), and at its mouth formed the boggy region known as the [Overbourn Marshes](/wiki/Overbourn_Marshes), opposite the [southern end](/wiki/Haysend "Haysend") of the [High Hay](/wiki/High_Hay).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Its outflow was the [Mithe](/wiki/Mithe).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Shirebourn* means "Bright-stream". The etymology must be [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Scire-burna* from *scïr* "bright, clear, pure" + *burna* "stream".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Although the name has nothing to do with *Shire*, in most translations, it is translated "River of the Shire" instead.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Schirebourn or Shirebourne is the old name of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Sherbourne" title="wikipedia:River Sherbourne">Sherbourne</a> river by Coventry, having the same etymology.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
