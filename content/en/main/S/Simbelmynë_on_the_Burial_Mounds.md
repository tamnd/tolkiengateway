**Simbelmynë on the Burial Mounds** is the sixteenth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and the twenty-second scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

With the tomb of [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md) sealed shut, a multitude of small white flowers — [simbelmynë](Simbelmynë.md) — is shown growing there. [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) remarks that the flowers, which cover the graves of his ancestors, now cover that of his son. He laments that he should rule [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) during evil times. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) consoles him as he is overcome by grief.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>No parent should have to bury their child.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Théoden</cite></p>
</blockquote>

As Gandalf leaves Théoden to mourn in privacy, he sees a horse approaching, carrying two children.
