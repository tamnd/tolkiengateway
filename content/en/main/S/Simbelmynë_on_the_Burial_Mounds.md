**Simbelmynë on the Burial Mounds** is the sixteenth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and the twenty-second scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

With the tomb of [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) sealed shut, a multitude of small white flowers — [simbelmynë](/wiki/Simbelmyn%C3%AB "Simbelmynë") — is shown growing there. [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) remarks that the flowers, which cover the graves of his ancestors, now cover that of his son. He laments that he should rule [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during evil times. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) consoles him as he is overcome by grief.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>No parent should have to bury their child.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Théoden</cite></p>
</blockquote>
