**Sadoc Brandybuck** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Sadoc was the son of [Gormadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gormadoc_Brandybuck) and [Malva Headstrong](/wiki/Malva_Headstrong). He was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1179](/wiki/Third_Age_2779 "Third Age 2779"), the middle child, between his brothers [Madoc](/wiki/Madoc_Brandybuck "Madoc Brandybuck") and [Marroc](/wiki/Marroc_Brandybuck "Marroc Brandybuck"). He has three children, of which the daughter, [Salvia](/wiki/Salvia_Brandybuck "Salvia Brandybuck"), was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1226](/wiki/Third_Age_2826 "Third Age 2826").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymolgy {#Etymolgy}

The meaning of the name *Sadoc* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

A Cornish knight named *Sadoc* who becomes a Knight of the Round Table and a knight of Orkney named *Sadoc* appear in Malory's *Le Morte Darthur*. There also exists the [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) name *Cadoc* that is derived from *cad* ("battle").<sup id="cite_ref-Allan_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Allan-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Two knights called *Sadoc* play a role in Malory's *Le Morte Darthur*: a *Sadoc* who is a Cornish knight who is friendly to Sir Tristram and a *Sadoc* who is a knight of Orkney.<sup id="cite_ref-Allan_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Allan-3">[3]</a></sup> *Cadoc* is also the name of a Welsh <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cadoc" title="wikipedia:Cadoc">saint</a>.
