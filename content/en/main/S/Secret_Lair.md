<i><b>Secret Lair</b></i> is a series of small *[Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)* sets featuring collector-oriented alternate art reprints, which come in both non-foil and foil versions. Several of these sets are based on *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-more_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-more-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-mathoms_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mathoms-2">[2]</a></sup>

## More Adventures in Middle-earth {#More_Adventures_in_Middle-earth}

<i><b>More Adventures in Middle-earth</b></i> was released on [26 June](../_/26_June.md) [2023](../_/2023.md). It consisted of cards reprinted from [The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md), with alternative artwork from the [1978 film](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-more_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-more-1">[1]</a></sup>

The reprinted cards were<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>:

- *Slip On the Ring*
- *Gandalf, Friend of the Shire*
- *Mirror of Galadriel*
- *Shire Terrace*

## Second Breakfast and Beyond {#Second_Breakfast_and_Beyond}

<i><b>Second Breakfast and Beyond</b></i> releases on [17 August](../_/17_August.md) [2026](../_/2026.md). It consists of 7 Food tokens, illustrated by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Raymond_Bonilla&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Raymond Bonilla (page does not exist)">Raymond Bonilla</a>, [Randy Gallegos](../R/Randy_Gallegos.md), and [Ben Wootten](../B/Ben_Wootten.md). Each of the cards is themed after a Hobbit meal.<sup id="cite_ref-mathoms_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mathoms-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Over the Edge of the Wild {#Over_the_Edge_of_the_Wild}

<i><b>Over the Edge of the Wild</b></i> releases on [17 August](../_/17_August.md) [2026](../_/2026.md). It consists of the following cards, all illustrated by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Jordan_Speer&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jordan Speer (page does not exist)">Jordan Speer</a>:<sup id="cite_ref-mathoms_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mathoms-2">[2]</a></sup>

- *Cloudshift*
- *Quite a Merry Gathering*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
- *Stony Silence*
- *Imp's Mischief*
- *Seize the Spoils*

<h2><span id="Smaug.27s_Spoils"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Smaug&#39;s_Spoils">Smaug&#39;s Spoils</span></h2>

<i><b>Smaug&#39;s Spoils</b></i> releases on [17 August](../_/17_August.md) [2026](../_/2026.md). The set's illustrators are <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=David_Astruga&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="David Astruga (page does not exist)">David Astruga</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Brandon_L._Hunt&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Brandon L. Hunt (page does not exist)">Brandon L. Hunt</a>, and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gaboleps&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gaboleps (page does not exist)">Gaboleps</a>. It consists of the following cards:<sup id="cite_ref-mathoms_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mathoms-2">[2]</a></sup>

- *Erebor Heirloom*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>
- *Lightning Greaves*
- *Liquimetal Torque*
- *Sol Ring*
- *Thought Vessel*
