A **Seer**, or **Prophet**, was one gifted with extreme [foresight](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight"). While many [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) and [Edain](/wiki/Edain) had foresight, the seers predicted important events far in the future:

- [Agaldor](/wiki/Agaldor), who foresaw the coming of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) and the salvation of [Man](/wiki/Men "Men").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Amnon](/wiki/Amnon) who predicted the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Glirhuin](/wiki/Glirhuin) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), who predicted that the [Stone of the Hapless](/wiki/Stone_of_the_Hapless) would never be defiled or sink beneath the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Malbeth](/wiki/Malbeth) of the mid-[Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), who predicted that [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) would be the last king of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and also foresaw the fate of the [People of the Dead](/wiki/Dead_Men "Dead Men").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
