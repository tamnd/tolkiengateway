During the [Quest of the Ring](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Ring), a mysterious **shadow** passed over the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) in the night, darkening the stars as it moved over [Hollin](/wiki/Eregion "Eregion"). The origin of this shadow is unclear, as it was moving fast and against the wind.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

The shadow was not one of the [fell beasts](/wiki/Fell_beasts "Fell beasts") of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) because, as the [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") [Grishnakh](/wiki/Grishnakh) had stated, the Ring Wraiths had been forbidden by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to cross the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) until after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) progressed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A similar shadow had passed over [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen) when Frodo wore the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Shadow passes over the Fellowship already in the earliest manuscript version of the story of their trip in Hollin, notably before Tolkien's conception of the fell-beasts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
