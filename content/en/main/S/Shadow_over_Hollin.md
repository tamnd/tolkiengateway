During the [Quest of the Ring](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md), a mysterious **shadow** passed over the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) in the night, darkening the stars as it moved over [Hollin](../E/Eregion.md). The origin of this shadow is unclear, as it was moving fast and against the wind.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

The shadow was not one of the [fell beasts](../F/Fell_beasts.md) of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) because, as the [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) [Grishnakh](../G/Grishnakh.md) had stated, the Ring Wraiths had been forbidden by [Sauron](Sauron.md) to cross the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) until after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) progressed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A similar shadow had passed over [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md) when Frodo wore the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Shadow passes over the Fellowship already in the earliest manuscript version of the story of their trip in Hollin, notably before Tolkien's conception of the fell-beasts.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
