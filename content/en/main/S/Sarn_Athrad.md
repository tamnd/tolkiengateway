**Sarn Athrad** was the stony ford of the River [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) across which the [Dwarf-road](/wiki/Dwarf-road_of_Beleriand "Dwarf-road of Beleriand") from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) entered [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was here that the [Battle of Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad) took place between the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Dwarves of Nogrod](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Nogrod).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sarn Athrad* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Ford of Stones"<sup id="cite_ref-silmindex_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmindex-3">[3]</a></sup> or "Stony-ford",<sup id="cite_ref-RC_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup> from *[sarn](/wiki/Sarn "Sarn")* ("small stone, pebble") + *[athrad](/wiki/Athrad "Athrad")* ("ford, crossing").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, this ford (<i><b>Sarnathrod</b></i>) is crossing the river [Aros](/wiki/Aros) south of Artanor ([Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> This is in agreement with [The First 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_First_%27Silmarillion%27_Map), where it is marked as <b><i>Athrasarn</i> (Stony Ford)</b>, but later crossed out<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>.

On the Eastern extension of [The First 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_First_%27Silmarillion%27_Map), the ford is named <i><b>Sarn Athra</b></i>. It is located first south of the confluence of the Gelion and the Duilwen (later the Legolin river), but crossed out, and then relocated south of the confluence of Gelion and Ascar.

Finally on [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map) <i><b>Sarn Athrad</b></i> is located not far north of the confluence of [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) and Ascar rivers. However, the *north road of Dwarves* is crossing further north<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>, through a ford identified as *northern ford* in contrast with *Harathrad* the "South Ford"<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> (see below).

In a late text dated to 1971, Tolkien wrote that the name *Sarn Athrad* must be changed to <i><b>Athrad i-Nogoth</b></i> \[&gt; <i><b>Negyth</b></i>\] ("Ford of the Dwarves"), or <i><b>Athrad Dhaer</b></i> ("Great Ford").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, a related text has <i><b>Harathrad</b></i> ("Southern Ford") in addition to <i><b>Athrad Daer</b></i> <sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>(these names were already revealed by Christopher Tolkien in *The War of the Jewels*, p.335).

<dl><dd>Sarn Athrad (S. &#34;Stony Ford&#34;) is an abandoned ford on what remains of the Gelion in Forlindon. It was replaced by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Little_Ford&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Little Ford (page does not exist)">Edenathrad</a> (S. &#34;New ford&#34;) a little north, and a small village has been constructed nearby.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>
