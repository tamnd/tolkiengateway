**Serpents** was a name used both for the [dragons](../D/Dragons.md) (properly, **great serpents**)<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> and as a synonym for ordinary [snakes](Snakes.md).

During his fight with [Huan](../H/Huan.md), [Sauron](Sauron.md) briefly took the form of a serpent.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The "[Black Serpent](../B/Black_Serpent~63231.md)" on scarlet was also the heraldic symbol of a chieftain of the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md), suggesting that there were serpents in the South.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), "snake, serpent" is *[lókë](../L/Lókë.md)* ([Sindarin](Sindarin.md), *[lhûg](../L/Lhûg.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name *Lhamthanc* ("forked tongue") is said to be a "serpent-name", consisting of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lham&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lham (page does not exist)">lham(b)</a></i> + *[thanc](../T/Thanc.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
