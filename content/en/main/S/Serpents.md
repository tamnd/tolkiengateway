**Serpents** was a name used both for the [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") (properly, **great serpents**)<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> and as a synonym for ordinary [snakes](/wiki/Snakes "Snakes").

During his fight with [Huan](/wiki/Huan), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) briefly took the form of a serpent.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The "[Black Serpent](/wiki/Black_Serpent)" on scarlet was also the heraldic symbol of a chieftain of the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim), suggesting that there were serpents in the South.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), "snake, serpent" is *[lókë](/wiki/L%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Lókë")* ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), *[lhûg](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBg "Lhûg")*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name *Lhamthanc* ("forked tongue") is said to be a "serpent-name", consisting of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lham&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lham (page does not exist)">lham(b)</a></i> + *[thanc](/wiki/Thanc "Thanc")*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
