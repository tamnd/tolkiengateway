**Sandyman** was the miller of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).

## History {#History}

Sandyman owned and ran the [Old Mill](/wiki/Old_Mill) in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) around [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") and well before.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [The Gaffer](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") was not very fond of Sandyman, or his habits of telling tall tales and having quick judgment of others.

Prior to [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party), while gossiping about [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and the death of his parents, Sandyman related the rumour that [his mother](/wiki/Primula_Brandybuck "Primula Brandybuck") had pushed [his father](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins "Drogo Baggins") from a boat and he pulled her down; and was scolded by Gaffer for believing such rumours. He wondered about Bilbo augmenting his wealth by going out for journeys, and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) visiting him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Some time before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Sandyman died and passed his mill on to his son [Ted](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman "Ted Sandyman").

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Possibly the miller at [Sarehole](/wiki/Sarehole) Mill, Warwickshire, England
