**Salgant** was a [Gnome](../G/Gnome.md)-lord of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

Salgant was the chief of the [house of the Harp](../H/House_of_the_Harp.md), who bore the emblem of a golden harp upon a sable field. Unlike his brave folk, he was a craven that "fawned upon [Meglin](../M/Meglin.md)". He was said to be heavy and squat, being the only one of the [Gondothlim](../G/Gondothlim.md) to ride at battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> He used to visit many times a day the house of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), loving the good wine and food he received there.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191</sup>

When [Melko](../M/Melko.md)'s armies approached the city, he and Meglin alone convinced King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) to hold the city and guard the treasures, and though Meglin spoke he backed him up. In the beginning of the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), Meglin's plan was to attack Tuor's house, and ordered Salgant to delay Tuor and his [folk of the Wing](../F/Folk_of_the_Wing.md) and take them into the deadliest of the battle. However, Salgant became frightened, rode home and "lay... aquake on his bed".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:175–7</sup>

As the battle went worse, Turgon ordered him to the [Great Market](../G/Great_Market.md) to reinforce [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) and the [house of the Golden Flower](../H/House_of_the_Golden_Flower.md). But in his treachery he concealed this to his folk, saying they had to garrison the [Lesser Market](../L/Lesser_Market.md), where he lived. There his men fretted, abandoning Salgant, and with their deeds in battle they could redeem the cowardice of their lord.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182</sup>

It is suggested that he either perished by fire upon his bed or became a buffoon of Melko, "an ill fate for a noble of the good race of the Gnomes". It is said later that the young [Eärendel](../E/Eärendel.md) mourned him, because he had "told him quaint tales or played drolleries with him at time".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:190–1</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name *Salgant* is not glossed, but in the later [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) it was changed to <i><b>Talagand</b></i> ("Harper").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate was <i><b>Salakanto</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
