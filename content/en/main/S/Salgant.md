**Salgant** was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome)-lord of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

Salgant was the chief of the [house of the Harp](/wiki/House_of_the_Harp "House of the Harp"), who bore the emblem of a golden harp upon a sable field. Unlike his brave folk, he was a craven that "fawned upon [Meglin](/wiki/Meglin)". He was said to be heavy and squat, being the only one of the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) to ride at battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> He used to visit many times a day the house of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), loving the good wine and food he received there.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191</sup>

When [Melko](/wiki/Melko)'s armies approached the city, he and Meglin alone convinced King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to hold the city and guard the treasures, and though Meglin spoke he backed him up. In the beginning of the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), Meglin's plan was to attack Tuor's house, and ordered Salgant to delay Tuor and his [folk of the Wing](/wiki/Folk_of_the_Wing "Folk of the Wing") and take them into the deadliest of the battle. However, Salgant became frightened, rode home and "lay... aquake on his bed".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:175–7</sup>

As the battle went worse, Turgon ordered him to the [Great Market](/wiki/Great_Market) to reinforce [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) and the [house of the Golden Flower](/wiki/House_of_the_Golden_Flower "House of the Golden Flower"). But in his treachery he concealed this to his folk, saying they had to garrison the [Lesser Market](/wiki/Lesser_Market), where he lived. There his men fretted, abandoning Salgant, and with their deeds in battle they could redeem the cowardice of their lord.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182</sup>

It is suggested that he either perished by fire upon his bed or became a buffoon of Melko, "an ill fate for a noble of the good race of the Gnomes". It is said later that the young [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) mourned him, because he had "told him quaint tales or played drolleries with him at time".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:190–1</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name *Salgant* is not glossed, but in the later [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) it was changed to <i><b>Talagand</b></i> ("Harper").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate was <i><b>Salakanto</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
