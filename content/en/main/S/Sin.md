<i><b>sin</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word either meaning "thus" (adverb) or "this" (as an independent word in the sentence, not modifying another word like *sina* does). Also a form of *[sí](/wiki/S%C3%AD "Sí")* "now" occurring before vowels, possibly replaced by *sín*. [Patrick Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_Wynne) argues that *sin* is an adverb "thus" derived from the stem *si-* plus *[-n](/wiki/-n)*, "this (by me)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- <i><b>sin</b> quentë Quendingoldo Elendilenna</i> = "**this** [Pengolodh](/wiki/Pengolodh) said to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)" or "**thus** spoke Pengolodh to Elendil"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
