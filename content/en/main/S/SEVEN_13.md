<i><b>SEVEN 13</b></i> is the thirteenth issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [1996](../_/1996.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "G.K. Chesterton: Myth, Paradox, and the Commonplace"
    - By: Rolland Hein
- "The Day Boy and the Night Girl"
    - By: Adrian Gunther
- "Higher Dimensions: C.S. Lewis and Mathematics"
    - By: David L. Neuhouser
- "Fantasy Set to Music: Donald Swann, C.S. Lewis, and J.R.R. Tolkien"
    - By: William Phemister
- "Dorothy L. Sayer's 'Worship in the Anglican Church': A Presbyterian Response"
    - By: Mark A. Noll
- "An Anglican Response"
    - By: Colin Buchanan
- Review Essay:
    - "Dorothy L.:A Dramatic Portrait of Dorothy L. Sayers"
        - By: Barbara Reynolds<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
