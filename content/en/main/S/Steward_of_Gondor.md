**Steward** was the traditional title of a chief councillor to The [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor).

## History {#History}

King [Rómendacil I](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_I) established the office of **Steward of Gondor**, chosen by the King as a man of high trust and wisdom; Stewards were advanced in years since they were not permitted to go to war or to leave the realm. They were never a member of the [Royal House](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion "House of Anárion").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Mardil Voronwë](/wiki/Mardil "Mardil") was the one who started ruling Gondor after the death of its last King [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur). He ruled on the behalf of the King until the throne would be claimed by one of the Royal House.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup> The office was in all respects and purposes that of the King.

The Steward to King [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil), [Húrin of Emyn Arnen](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_of_Emyn_Arnen), was the founder of the **[House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin)**.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup> They were of [royal](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion "House of Anárion") [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") blood, but were not descendants of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) in the ruling line.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> After him all stewards would be chosen from his descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the time of the Steward [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur), who ruled briefly after the death of King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) and his sons ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944")), this title became hereditary, passing the station of councillor from father to son, much like the Kingship.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup> This line of ruling stewards carried on through twenty-six generations to [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor"), Ruling Steward at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>

Soon after the death of Denethor II, [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") was crowned King of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom). The Steward [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), son of Denethor II, surrendered to the King his rod of office, but it was returned to him.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_and_King_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward_and_King-6">[6]</a></sup> Faramir nominally ruled briefly as Steward until Elessar's coronation, but since Faramir rested in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing), Prince [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) took his place much of that time.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Though Faramir became Steward a month and a half before Elessar became King, Faramir is not considered one of the Ruling Stewards. King Elessar confirmed in Faramir and his descendants the office of Steward of Gondor, and granted him in addition the title of [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien), ensuring his line a position as councillor of the King.<sup id="cite_ref-Steward_and_King_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Steward_and_King-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Duties and office {#Duties_and_office}

Stewards served as the chief councillor to the king in the [Great Council of Gondor](/wiki/Great_Council_of_Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-L244_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L244-8">[8]</a></sup> or ruled instead of the king<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> as the representative of the king when the king was absent or sick or between the death of the king and the accession of the heir of the king.<sup id="cite_ref-L244_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L244-8">[8]</a></sup> Each Steward swore an oath "to hold rod and rule until in the name of the king, until he shall return" when they took up their office. This oath became a mere ritual after the last king of Gondor [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) had disappeared, but many in Gondor continued to believe that a king would return and some even remembered the line of the heirs of Isildur in the North.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Stewards used a simple white rod for their badge of office.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Additionally, they sat on a small throne that was in front of the throne used by the old kings of Gondor, letting that throne go unused in honor of the fallen kings.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

"Steward" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) was <i><b>Arandur</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> literally "king's servant, minister",<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> a combination of *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* ("king") + *[-ndur](/wiki/-ndur)* ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
