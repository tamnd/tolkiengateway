<i><b>Sandstorm at the Well</b></i> is a song composed by [Bear McCreary](/wiki/Bear_McCreary) for the [second episode](/wiki/Where_the_Stars_are_Strange "Where the Stars are Strange") of the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*. It features lyrics sung in [Rhûnnic](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBnnic) by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Mystery_of_the_Bulgarian_Voices&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Mystery of the Bulgarian Voices (page does not exist)">The Mystery of the Bulgarian Voices</a>. According to Bear McCreary, the language the lyrics were sung in was crafted from some of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s unpublished notes which some Tolkien scholars had unearthed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Poem {#Poem}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>I lurg i bauvailun, j&#39;aišäd i raibo,</i>
</p>
</div>

## Translation {#Translation}

The [Rhûnnic](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBnnic) text above along with its following translation were published by Amazon's composer Bear McCreary on his personal website.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<div class="poem">
<p><i>The threat unseen, the shrine at the border.</i>
</p>
</div>

Only the first line was revealed on McCreary's blog.
