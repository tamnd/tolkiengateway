The **sack of Eregion**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the destruction of the [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) realm [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) by the armies of [Sauron](Sauron.md) in the [Second Age](Second_Age.md). Sauron invaded Eregion to claim the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md), including the [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md), which were crafted without his knowledge.

## Background {#Background}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="../R/Rings_of_Power.md" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a></div></div>

In [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [1200](Second_Age_1200.md), [Sauron](Sauron.md) came to [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) in disguise, claiming to be an emissary of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) sent to offer aid to the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md). Although rejected by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), he was able to win over [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) and the smiths of Eregion. The brotherhood of smiths, the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](../G/Gwaith-i-Mírdain.md), profited from his knowledge,<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup> and around [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [1500](Second_Age_1500.md) they began forging the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md), finishing them in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [1590](Second_Age_1590.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup> Unbeknownst to them, Sauron (who had returned to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md)) created a master [Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) to rule the others. However, when the Elves perceived the designs of Sauron, they took off their [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md) and gave them into the safekeeping of Elven bearers, preventing the use of the Three Rings which Sauron craved most.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

Infuriated by Celebrimbor's defiance, Sauron led his host out of Mordor and invaded Eriador in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [1695](Second_Age_1695.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup> In response to the attack, Gil-galad sent out a force under the command of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) to aid Celebrimbor, as well as send word to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) for assistance.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup> Sauron also sought to recruit some [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md), but because two [Wizards](../W/Wizards.md) had influence among them, Sauron's dominance was not entirely successful, and he took a long time to attack Eregion.<sup id="cite_ref-NM3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM3-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

[Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) led a sortie from Eregion and drove back the vanguard of Sauron's host, later combining forces with Elrond and the reinforcements from [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md). As Sauron's host drew closer, the defenders of Eregion could no longer hold him off, because their forces were greatly outnumbered. Sauron's forces surrounded Eregion, thus preventing Elrond and Celeborn from returning there.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [1697](Second_Age_1697.md),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup> [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md) fell, with [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md) himself making a last desperate stand on the steps of the [House of the Mírdain](../H/House_of_the_Mírdain.md). He was taken captive by the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). Sauron took the [Nine Rings](../N/Nine_Rings.md), and had Celebrimbor tormented into revealing the location of the [Seven Rings](Seven_Rings.md). Afterwards, Celebrimbor was shot through with Orc-arrows, and by some accounts, Sauron used Celebrimbor's body as a banner as he turned to face the host of Elrond.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>

Elrond would have been overwhelmed, but the host of Sauron was attacked from the rear by the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md) and the [Elves of Lórinand](../G/Galadhrim.md) led by [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md). Sauron halted his pursuit of Elrond and turned on the Dwarves and Elves at his rear, quickly defeating them, though this allowed Elrond and his forces to escape.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Elrond was able to gather what remained of the Elves of Eregion and retreated northward and established a refuge, [Imladris](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup> Other survivors escaped into [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup> and were granted passage by their friends the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), eventually coming through the [Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) to the woodlands that would long afterwards be called [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). The Gates of Moria were shut and remained so until the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) came there thousands of years later.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Sauron had obtained all the [Rings of Power](../R/Rings_of_Power.md) save the [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md), which he perceived were given to Elvish guardians: Gil-galad and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md). The host of Sauron ravaged much of Eriador, pressing west to Lindon, where Sauron hoped to seize one of the Three. Furthermore, Sauron sent a bulk of his forces to [besiege](../F/First_Siege_of_Imladris.md) Elrond, in order to prevent him from assailing his rear.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>

He is then killed by <a href="Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> after discovering the deceit. <a href="../E/Elrond.md" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> laments the destruction of ancient knowledge as he sees a group of orcs burning books inside the city.</dd></dl>
