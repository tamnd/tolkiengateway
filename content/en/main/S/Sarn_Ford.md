**Sarn Ford** was the stone ford on the River [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin), on the far southern borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was guarded by the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North). However, on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), the Rangers were driven off by the arrival in the evening of the [Ringwraiths](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A road beginning at [Waymeet](/wiki/Waymeet) crossed the ford, which met the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) further south. By this road [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl) the Ringwraith entered the Shire during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [63](/wiki/Fourth_Age_63 "Fourth Age 63"), [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) and [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) passed Sarn Ford on their way to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the last time they were seen in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sarn Ford* was so named by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), after [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name is a half-translation of the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) name *[Sarn-athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad "Sarn Athrad")*, meaning "stony-ford" (*[sarn](/wiki/Sarn "Sarn")* + *[athrad](/wiki/Athrad "Athrad")*).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspirations {#Inspirations}

Perhaps [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was inspired by the landscape of his childhood: "<i>Hall Green village was only a little distance away [from Sarehole] down a lane and <b>across a ford.</b></i>".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Sarn Ford (colloquial name for <i>Iach Sarn</i> or <i>Athrad Sarn</i>) is a ford maintained by an Arthedain garrison. The site is given a detailed description and history.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Sarn Ford is the starting location of the game, shortly after the Nazgûl attacked the Rangers who guarded the Shire. <a href="/wiki/Eradan_(video_game_character)" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Andriel" title="Andriel">Andriel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Farin_(video_game_character)" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a> meet <a href="/wiki/Halbarad" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a>, who sends them to <a href="/wiki/Bree" title="Bree">Bree</a> to warn <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Oxmonline_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oxmonline-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Sarn Ford is on the western edge of in-game <a href="/wiki/Cardolan" title="Cardolan">Cardolan</a> region. It is visited on <a href="/wiki/26_September" title="26 September">26 September</a> as the Rangers led by Halbarad are still recovering from the attack.</dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Ruins_of_the_Lost_Realm" title="Ruins of the Lost Realm">Ruins of the Lost Realm</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Two miles north of Sarn Ford was the ancient Elven refuge of Tindailin (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> “glinting lake”), which was initially a resting stop on the way to the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Havens" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a>. The lake at its centre was home to the water-spirit Herunen, who had befriended the Elf Thalion and his love Mirwen. When war came to Tindailin, Thalion was grievously injured in the battle, despite his promises to Mirwen to stay away from the fighting. Thalion had to depart Middle-earth, and had entrusted a heirloom bracelet to Herunen as a message to Mirwen. Mirwen, however, felt betrayed and vowed to never return to Tindailin. This in turn led to Herunen growing resentful over the many centuries he spent waiting. In <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, an expedition led by Mirwen&#39;s brother Aranwë sought to recover said heirloom, but was attacked by Herunen.<sup id="cite_ref-Tindailin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tindailin-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>
