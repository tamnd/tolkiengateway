**Second Age 1350** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1350**) is the 1350th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- After this time, [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md) leave [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) and go to [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-1">[1]</a></sup> [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) stays behind.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-1">[1]</a></sup>
