The **Southerners** were an ill-defined group of people, generally referred to as such by [Bree-landers](../M/Men_of_Bree.md). These men came up from the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) into [Bree](../B/Bree.md) out of lands to the south around [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md). They had come up into the [Bree-lands](../B/Bree-land.md) looking for a place of peace from the unrest in their homes, though a few such as the [squint-eyed southerner](Squint-eyed_southerner.md) were in fact spies of [Saruman](Saruman.md) and were not to be trusted.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

### *The End of Bovadium* {#The_End_of_Bovadium}

In the Foreword of *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-CGL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGL-2">[2]</a></sup>, [Doctor Sarevelk](../D/Doctor_Sarevelk.md) labeled the Vastians who dwelt south of the *[Via Maxima](../V/Via_Maxima.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-FII_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FII-3">[3]</a></sup> in *[Bovadium](../B/Bovadium.md)* as **A-people** since they were the speakers of [language A](../L/Language_A.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Foreword_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foreword-4">[4]</a></sup> In Fragment II of the text, the A-people<sup id="cite_ref-Foreword_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foreword-4">[4]</a></sup> are referred to as **Southerners**. During the takeover of the city by the *[Motores Bi](../M/Motores_Bi.md)*, the Southerners were not as affected because their lands were "guarded by great gates and iron bars" that the *Motores* could not pass. As a consequence, they were still able to enjoy a peace free from the *Motores*. However, after the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../N/Northerners.md" title="Northerners">Northerners</a> became jealous of them and proposed to the Minister that a new road should be built "through the southern meadows", the Southerners distracted the Minister with their own proposals. But this was futile since the Minister choose not to do anything proposed by the Southerners or the Northerners and would have proceeded with his own plan had he not gotten trapped in the <a class="mw-disambig" href="../C/Capital_City.md" title="Capital City">Capital City</a>. The debate of the Southerners and the Northerners never reached a resolution because they were all "slain by the poisonous fumes" of the *Motores*.<sup id="cite_ref-FII_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FII-3">[3]</a></sup>

In a [related poem](../D/Domine_defende_nos_contra_hos_Motores_bos!.md), the Southerners are referred to as the **Southern folk**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
