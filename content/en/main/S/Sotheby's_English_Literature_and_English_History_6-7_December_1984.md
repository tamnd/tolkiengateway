<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s English Literature and English History 6–7 December 1984</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby%27s) (London). The catalogue reproduces several writings by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien): [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](/wiki/Concerning_..._%27The_Hoard%27) and [Kinship of the Half-elven](/wiki/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven) (Lot 276) and several extracts from letters to Eileen Elgar (among them, [Letter to Eileen Elgar (5 March 1964)](</wiki/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(5_March_1964)>) and [Letter to Eileen Elgar (24 December 1971)](</wiki/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(24_December_1971)>)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
