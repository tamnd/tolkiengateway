The **Star of Eärendil** is the light that shines in the horizon both in the morning and the evening. It consists in a boat raised by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and led by [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), who carries a shining [Silmaril](Silmaril.md) while watching the [Doors of Night](../D/Doors_of_Night.md).

## History {#History}

After [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) rescued a Silmaril from [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md), this was later given to their descendant [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md), wife of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md). Both took it to [Aman](../A/Aman.md), and the Valar decided to rise it as a new star. In a vessel appointed by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Elbereth.md" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>, Eärendil rose in the horizon as a sign of hope for [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Now fair and marvellous was that vessel made, and it was filled with a wavering flame, pure and bright; and Eärendil the Mariner sat at the helm, glistening with dust of elven-gems, and the Silmaril was bound upon his brow. Far he journeyed in that ship, even into the starless voids; but most often was he seen at morning or at evening, glimmering in sunrise or sunset, as he came back to Valinor from voyages beyond the confines of the world.<br/>. . .<br/>Now when first Vingilot was set to sail in the seas of heaven, it rose unlooked for, glittering and bright; and the people of Middle-earth beheld it from afar and wondered, and they took it for a sign, and called it Gil-Estel, the Star of High Hope.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<a href="../O/Of_the_Voyage_of_Eärendil_and_the_War_of_Wrath.md" title="Of the Voyage of Eärendil and the War of Wrath">Of the Voyage of Eärendil and the War of Wrath</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

As it first rose in the West, it was recognised by the [Noldo](../N/Noldo.md)-lord [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) as the jewel of his [father](../F/Fëanor.md) that he had sought for so long, now placed out of his reach beyond the [Circles of the World](../C/Circles_of_the_World.md).

It was Eärendil and the light of his star that led the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) in the early [Second Age](Second_Age.md), and at that time the light of the Star was so brilliant that it hid all the other stars in the sky. From its guiding light came the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md)' name for their land, [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md), meaning "Starwards".

The light of Eärendil's Star played a crucial role much later in history as well. It was light from this star that was gathered in [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s [phial](../P/Phial_of_Galadriel.md), his gift from [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), which helped him survive his journey into [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). Even today the star still shines — the story of Eärendil and his Silmaril was the Elvish account of the "star" we know today as the planet <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus" title="wikipedia:Venus">Venus</a>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Earlier forms of the name *Gil-Estel* were *Gil-Orestel*, *Gil-[Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Sindarin_word).md>)* and *Gil-Orrain*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, this star was historically known as <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Elm%C5%8D&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elmō (page does not exist)">Elmō</a></i> in [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
