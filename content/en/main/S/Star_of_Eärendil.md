The **Star of Eärendil** is the light that shines in the horizon both in the morning and the evening. It consists in a boat raised by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and led by [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), who carries a shining [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) while watching the [Doors of Night](/wiki/Doors_of_Night).

## History {#History}

After [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) rescued a Silmaril from [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown), this was later given to their descendant [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing), wife of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil). Both took it to [Aman](/wiki/Aman), and the Valar decided to rise it as a new star. In a vessel appointed by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Elbereth" title="Elbereth">Elbereth</a>, Eärendil rose in the horizon as a sign of hope for [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men).

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Now fair and marvellous was that vessel made, and it was filled with a wavering flame, pure and bright; and Eärendil the Mariner sat at the helm, glistening with dust of elven-gems, and the Silmaril was bound upon his brow. Far he journeyed in that ship, even into the starless voids; but most often was he seen at morning or at evening, glimmering in sunrise or sunset, as he came back to Valinor from voyages beyond the confines of the world.<br/>. . .<br/>Now when first Vingilot was set to sail in the seas of heaven, it rose unlooked for, glittering and bright; and the people of Middle-earth beheld it from afar and wondered, and they took it for a sign, and called it Gil-Estel, the Star of High Hope.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<a href="/wiki/Of_the_Voyage_of_E%C3%A4rendil_and_the_War_of_Wrath" title="Of the Voyage of Eärendil and the War of Wrath">Of the Voyage of Eärendil and the War of Wrath</a>&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

As it first rose in the West, it was recognised by the [Noldo](/wiki/Noldo)-lord [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) as the jewel of his [father](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor "Fëanor") that he had sought for so long, now placed out of his reach beyond the [Circles of the World](/wiki/Circles_of_the_World).

It was Eärendil and the light of his star that led the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), and at that time the light of the Star was so brilliant that it hid all the other stars in the sky. From its guiding light came the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans)' name for their land, [Elenna](/wiki/Elenna), meaning "Starwards".

The light of Eärendil's Star played a crucial role much later in history as well. It was light from this star that was gathered in [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s [phial](/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel "Phial of Galadriel"), his gift from [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), which helped him survive his journey into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). Even today the star still shines — the story of Eärendil and his Silmaril was the Elvish account of the "star" we know today as the planet <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus" title="wikipedia:Venus">Venus</a>.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Earlier forms of the name *Gil-Estel* were *Gil-Orestel*, *Gil-[Amdir](</wiki/Amdir_(Sindarin_word)> "Amdir (Sindarin word)")* and *Gil-Orrain*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, this star was historically known as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Elm%C5%8D&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Elmō (page does not exist)">Elmō</a></i> in [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
