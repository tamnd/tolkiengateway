The **Stairs of Cirith Ungol** were a pair of steep stairs cut into the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath "Ephel Dúath") on the borders of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). They led upward and away from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul), the city that guarded the main pass through the mountains, to reach [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol), a higher and lesser pass guarded by the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol). The first of the stairs was the steepest, the [Straight Stair](/wiki/Straight_Stair), which led up to a narrow passage that seemed to go on for miles. After that came the second set of stairs, the [Winding Stair](/wiki/Winding_Stair), which led up to the tunnel through [Shelob's Lair](/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair) and then on to Cirith Ungol itself.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They formed part of the swiftest path over the mountains from [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) to Minas Morgul.<sup id="cite_ref-Choices_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Choices-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is unknown who made the Stairs: they may have been originally carved by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s servants, or perhaps later by the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) when they raised the Tower to guard the pass. Whatever their origin, they were known to the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that inhabited the Tower in the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), although [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") were still able to use them to find a way into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). <span></span>
