**Stephen Thorne** ([1935](/wiki/1935)-[2019](/wiki/2019)) was a renowned radio actor and audiobook narrator. His credits include [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) in BBC's *[The Lord of the Rings radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)")*, Aslan in [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley)'s radio version of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)' *The Chronicles of Narnia*, and several characters in Doctor Who.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [1981](/wiki/1981): *[The Lord of the Rings (radio series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)")* - [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard)
- [1992](/wiki/1992): *[Tales from the Perilous Realm (radio series)](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)")*
    - "Farmer Giles of Ham" - [Chrysophylax](/wiki/Chrysophylax)
