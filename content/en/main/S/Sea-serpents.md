**Sea-serpents**, also called **fish-dragons**, were a breed of [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) only mentioned fleetingly in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) linguistic writings.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> No story tells of their appearance in the history of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Lingwilókë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "fish-dragon, sea-serpent", being comprised of *lingwe* ("fish") and *lókë* ("dragon"). The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognate of *lingwilókë* is <i><b>lhimlug</b></i>, being comprised of *lhim* ("fish") and *lhûg* ("dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In other writings {#In_other_writings}

In *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*, an ancient **Sea-serpent** known only as the **[Worm](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons")** or the **[Serpent](/wiki/Serpents "Serpents")** lived in a cave known as <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pot&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pot (page does not exist)">Pot</a> at the bottom of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Deep_Blue_Sea&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Deep Blue Sea (page does not exist)">Deep Blue Sea</a>. He was an extremely enormous and powerful creature, more than one hundred miles long, allegedly causing storms just by passing through the world. The [Man-in-the-Moon](/wiki/Man-in-the-Moon) had once tried to seal the Sea-serpent with his magic, but took fifty years to create the proper magic, and succeeded, though accidentally causing an entire [continent](/wiki/Atlantis "Atlantis") to sink. During the time of the story, the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizard) [Artaxerxes](/wiki/Artaxerxes) received written <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Complaints&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Complaints (page does not exist)">Complaints</a></i> about the Sea-serpent allegedly waking up and moving to a new den that was in a cave that was very close to the mer-king's palace. Unfortunately, when Mr. Artaxerxes went to deal with him, [Roverandom](/wiki/Rover "Rover") followed him and bit the tail of a shark in the shell carriage, causing a domino effect which ended with the last shark in the carriage biting the Sea-serpent. The awakening was further accelerated when Mr. Artaxerxes tried to put it back to sleep, causing him to dream that he was being burnt alive by a volcano. Earthquakes and hurricanes followed as the Sea-serpent continuously rolled over until he spoke loudly that he would only go back to sleep if Mr. Artaxerxes was banished. The Mer-people accepted his terms and banished the Wizard.<sup id="cite_ref-rover4_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rover4-2">[2]</a></sup> The Sea-serpent presumably went back to sleep.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Kent_Burles_-_Water_Drakes.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/3b/Kent_Burles_-_Water_Drakes.png/300px-Kent_Burles_-_Water_Drakes.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="408" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/3/3b/Kent_Burles_-_Water_Drakes.png 1.5x" data-file-width="331" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption>Water Drake in <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">MERP</a></i>, by Kent Burles</figcaption></figure>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Water Drakes, or Water Serpents, are drakes who have adapted to a life in freshwater pools or in the ocean. The smaller varieties, who only live in fresh-water, are called Rain-drakes (or Lake-worms), while the larger (who prey in all types of water) are called True Water-drakes or Sea Serpents.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> An individual Water-drake, <i>Séahmatha</i> (meaning &#34;Lake Serpent&#34; in the tongue of the Northmen), inhabits the Long Lake.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Sea Serpent&#34; and &#34;Rain-drake&#34; are creatures of the Drake-kind. A unique dragon of the water is <a href="/wiki/E%C3%A4rcarax%C3%AB" title="Eärcaraxë">Eärcaraxë</a>, which may be played keyed to coastal regions and sites.</dd></dl>
