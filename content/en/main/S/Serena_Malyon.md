**Serena Malyon** is a Canadian illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, she created art for [The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit") expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Beyond her work in a professional capacity, she has also made fan illustrations of Tolkien's works.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
