<i><b>sîr</b></i> means "river, stream" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-App_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), Noldorin *sîr* is a descendant form of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)/[Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *[sīre](/wiki/S%C3%ADr "Sír")* ("river"), deriving from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [SIR](/wiki/SIR) ("flow").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
