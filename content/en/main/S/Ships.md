**Ships** were used by several peoples of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), most notably the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") and [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim).

[Elves](/wiki/Elves) are said to have been naturally skilled in the craft of shipbuilding.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
