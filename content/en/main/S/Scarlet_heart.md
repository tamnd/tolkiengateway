The **scarlet heart** was an emblem of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

Nólemë (later [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and then Turgon's father) was isolated by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) during the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad"), and they cut out his heart. The body and the heart were rescued by Turgon, and it became his emblem.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In *[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")*, the emblems of the [House of the King](/wiki/House_of_the_King) included the scarlet heart, the Moon and the Sun, and they wore the respective colours.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The death and desecration of Nólemë would evolve to the death of [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon), now Turgon's brother, in the Battle of Unnumbered Tears.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> However, there is also a great similarity with Fingolfin's death: Morgoth broke his body, but this was rescued by [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor) and taken to Turgon, who makes a [Cairn](/wiki/Fingolfin%27s_Cairn "Fingolfin's Cairn") for his father.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
