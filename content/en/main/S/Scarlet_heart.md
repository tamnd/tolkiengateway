The **scarlet heart** was an emblem of [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

Nólemë (later [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md) and then Turgon's father) was isolated by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) during the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), and they cut out his heart. The body and the heart were rescued by Turgon, and it became his emblem.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In *[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)*, the emblems of the [House of the King](../H/House_of_the_King.md) included the scarlet heart, the Moon and the Sun, and they wore the respective colours.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The death and desecration of Nólemë would evolve to the death of [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md), now Turgon's brother, in the Battle of Unnumbered Tears.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> However, there is also a great similarity with Fingolfin's death: Morgoth broke his body, but this was rescued by [Thorondor](../T/Thorondor.md) and taken to Turgon, who makes a [Cairn](../F/Fingolfin's_Cairn.md) for his father.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
