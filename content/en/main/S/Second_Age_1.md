**Second Age 1** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1**) is the 1st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- The [Second Age](Second_Age.md) begins after the [Great Battle](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) and overthrow of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Foundation of the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md), and of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
