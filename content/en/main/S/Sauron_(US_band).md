**Sauron** is an American blackened thrash metal band formed in 2000 at East Lansing, Michigan. It is one of many bands with the [same name](</wiki/Sauron_(band)> "Sauron (band)").

The lyrics of the song *Witch King*, from their [2004](/wiki/2004) album *Trash Assault*, heavily reference the [eponymous character](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
