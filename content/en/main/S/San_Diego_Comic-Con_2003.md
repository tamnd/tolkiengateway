**Comic-Con 2003** was the annual [Comic-Con](../C/Comic-Con.md) convention held in San Diego in [2003](../_/2003.md). As it was the same year [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md) was released, there were numerous Tolkien-related activities.
