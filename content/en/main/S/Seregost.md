**Seregost** is a place in [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) that appears in several adaptations.

## Chronology and Creation {#Chronology_and_Creation}

The name was originally created by cartographer and calligrapher [Daniel Reeve](../D/Daniel_Reeve.md) while working on licensed map assets for the film trilogy and related merchandise.

Its first appearance is in the [Maps of Middle-earth](../M/Maps_of_Middle-earth.md) map set for [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md)*, where it appears as a minor label below what seems to be a hill with two towers on the western side of the mountain spur that branches off southwestwards from the [Ered Lithui](../E/Ered_Lithui.md) east of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) on the map of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) without further exposition.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Reeve subsequently used the Decipher map as a template when tasked with designing the expanded merchandise map for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-3">[3]</a></sup>

## In further adaptations {#In_further_adaptations}

### The Lord of the Rings Online {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

In *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Mordor](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Mordor.md)*, Seregost appears as a fortress in the swamps of the Agarnaith region. In the game's narrative, it served as the primary stronghold of Lhaereth, a poison brewer who engineered the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Given the appropriateness of the name, it is possible that the developers coined the name independently from Reeve.

### Middle-earth: Shadow of War {#Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War}

In *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_War.md)*, Seregost is adapted as a snow-covered mountain region in Mordor, centred around the fortress Khargukor.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The developers likely followed the merchandise map, also adapting [Nan Ungol](../N/Nan_Ungol.md).

### Fan works {#Fan_works}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Seregost.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/0/02/Seregost.jpg/200px-Seregost.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="114" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/0/02/Seregost.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="299" data-file-height="171"/></a><figcaption>Illustration from Thelandofshadow</figcaption></figure>

The fansite [Thelandofshadow.com](../T/Thelandofshadow.com.md) took on several of the names introduced by Reeve, and expanded on them. Seregost was described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Seregost was a place of lava and red rocks. Named for the &#39;Blood Stone&#39; flower of ancient tales, it was the eastern gate to Lilithlad in a gap in the Mithram Spur. It has been said that the Black Uruks of Barad-dúr came from this forsaken place, hiding there after the ruin of their Master in the Second Age, awaiting his return. Seregost was a place of Dark Sorcery and upon its molten foundations a great fortress stood that guarded the Eastern Road.
</p>
</blockquote>

The fansite being more easily accessible than Decipher's sourcebooks, as well as being cited by [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) fans for the fan-made *Mordor Gazetteer*, likely caused it to be mistakenly thought of as the original source of Seregost and other new names.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Seregost* seems to be a [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) name, which means "Blood Fortress". It seems to contain the elements *[sereg](Sereg.md)* ("blood")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and *[ost](../O/Ost.md)* ("fortress", "fort", "citadel")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>.
