The **Shadow Folk**, also called the <i><b>Úvalear</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Fol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fol-1">[1]</a></sup> were the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") that inhabited [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

After the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) reached the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) during their [exile](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor "Flight of the Noldor"), they had dealings<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fol-1">[1]</a></sup> with the Shadow Folk in Hisilómë, of whom it is said that they were neither "of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) nor of [Melko](/wiki/Melko)", and that they came from the primeval [void](/wiki/Void "Void") "when the world was first fashioned".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of *The Book of Lost Tales*, the terms "Shadow Folk" and "shadow-folk" were names given in fear by [Men](/wiki/Men) to the [Lost Elves](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi") who inhabited Hisilómë before their arrival.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
