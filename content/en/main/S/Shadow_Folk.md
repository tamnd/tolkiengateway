The **Shadow Folk**, also called the <i><b>Úvalear</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Fol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fol-1">[1]</a></sup> were the [fays](../F/Fays.md) that inhabited [Hisilómë](../H/Hisilómë.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

After the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) reached the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md) during their [exile](../F/Flight_of_the_Noldor.md), they had dealings<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Fol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fol-1">[1]</a></sup> with the Shadow Folk in Hisilómë, of whom it is said that they were neither "of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) nor of [Melko](../M/Melko.md)", and that they came from the primeval [void](../V/Void.md) "when the world was first fashioned".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earlier versions of *The Book of Lost Tales*, the terms "Shadow Folk" and "shadow-folk" were names given in fear by [Men](../M/Men.md) to the [Lost Elves](../I/Ilkorindi.md) who inhabited Hisilómë before their arrival.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
