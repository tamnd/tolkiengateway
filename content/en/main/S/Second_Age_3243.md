**Second Age 3243** (abbreviated to **S.A. 3243**) is the 3243rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Death of [Gimilkhâd](../G/Gimilkhâd.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gimil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gimil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Line of Elros](../T/The_Line_of_Elros%253A_Kings_of_Númenor.md)*, Gimilkhâd's birth occurred in [S.A.](Second_Age.md) [3044](Second_Age_3044.md) and his death in 3243 (indicating an age of 199).<sup id="cite_ref-Gimil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gimil-1">[1]</a></sup> In *The Silmarillion* it is stated that "Gimilkhâd died two years before his two hundredth year"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
