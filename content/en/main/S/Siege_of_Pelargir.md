The **Siege of Pelargir** was the aftermath of the last battle of the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1447](../T/Third_Age_1447.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> the rightful King [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) slew [Castamir the Usurper](../C/Castamir.md) at the [Battle of the Crossings of Erui](../B/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Erui.md). Led by his sons, Castamir's kin and the people of the fleets retreated and held out for one year in the port city of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md). The siege could not be broken because Eldacar had no ships, which allowed the rebels to resupply and gather all of the support that they could.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup> In [1448](../T/Third_Age_1448.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> the rebels sailed away to [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) and established a lordship independent of Gondor. This refuge of enemies lasted for generations, until finally subdued by King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
