The **Siege of Pelargir** was the aftermath of the last battle of the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1447](/wiki/Third_Age_1447 "Third Age 1447")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> the rightful King [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") slew [Castamir the Usurper](/wiki/Castamir "Castamir") at the [Battle of the Crossings of Erui](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Erui). Led by his sons, Castamir's kin and the people of the fleets retreated and held out for one year in the port city of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). The siege could not be broken because Eldacar had no ships, which allowed the rebels to resupply and gather all of the support that they could.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup> In [1448](/wiki/Third_Age_1448 "Third Age 1448")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> the rebels sailed away to [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and established a lordship independent of Gondor. This refuge of enemies lasted for generations, until finally subdued by King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>
