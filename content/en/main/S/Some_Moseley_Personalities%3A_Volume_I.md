<i><b>Some Moseley Personalities: Volume I</b></i> is a booklet published by the Moseley Local History Society. It contains several articles on well-known personalities from the Moseley area of [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md). The booklet includes an article on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) (pp. 28–33) written by Roy Thomas, which describes Tolkien's years in Birmingham and features several photographs of local homes related to the Tolkien family.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Featuring: The Balden Family; Joseph Chamberlain; Percy Harrison; Sir John Holder; Sir Barry Jackson; Cyril Lander; Joseph, Harry &amp; Oliver Lucas; Sir Herbert Manzoni; Sidney H Meteyard &amp; Kate Eadie; Henry Morton, JRR Tolkien; William Dyke Williamson
