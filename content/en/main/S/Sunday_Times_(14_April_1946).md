Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) — a rather uncomplimentary review of *English Literature at the Close of the Middle Ages* by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.K._Chambers" title="wikipedia:E.K. Chambers">E.K. Chambers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
