**Swertings** is a name referring to the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim). The word was [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish), used among the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of the secluded [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Only legends about them reached the Hobbits: the Swertings lived in the [Sunlands](/wiki/Sunlands) and rode to war on [oliphaunts](/wiki/Oliphaunts "Oliphaunts").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Swerting* is a word related to the term '[Swarthy Men](/wiki/Swarthy_Men)' used in the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") (where 'swarthy' is an old word for 'dark-skinned').<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swerting" title="wikipedia:Swerting">Swerting</a> is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, referring to a legendary character, also mentioned in *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")*.
