**Swertings** is a name referring to the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md). The word was [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md), used among the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of the secluded [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Only legends about them reached the Hobbits: the Swertings lived in the [Sunlands](Sunlands.md) and rode to war on [oliphaunts](../O/Oliphaunts.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Swerting* is a word related to the term '[Swarthy Men](Swarthy_Men.md)' used in the [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md) (where 'swarthy' is an old word for 'dark-skinned').<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swerting" title="wikipedia:Swerting">Swerting</a> is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name, referring to a legendary character, also mentioned in *[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)*.
