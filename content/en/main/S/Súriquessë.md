<i><b>Súriquessë</b></i> is the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) name of a grass.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name likely means "wind feather".<sup id="cite_ref-AI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-1">[1]</a></sup> It has been suggested that the name is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> and would derive from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=S%C3%BAre&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Súre (page does not exist)">súre</a></i> and *[quesse](../Q/Quesse.md)*.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
