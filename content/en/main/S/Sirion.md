**Sirion** was a river of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the principal river of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). During most of its course it was the border between [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md) and [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md). From the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md) to its mouth, the river was 130 leagues in length.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Sources {#Sources}

The Sirion's source was at [Eithel Sirion](../E/Eithel_Sirion.md) on the eastern side of the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md), which lay in between [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md) (later Anfauglith) and [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md) and [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> It was guarded by the tower of [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Course {#Course}

Sirion flowed southwards along the border of the Ered Wethrin, passing through the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md) before entering the narrow steep-sided valley between the Ered Wethrin and the [Echoriath](../E/Echoriath.md) named the Pass of Sirion. Sirion then continued south into Beleriand, with the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md) to the west, and [Dimbar](../D/Dimbar.md) and then [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) to the east. After leaving Doriath at Aelin-uial (the [Fens of Sirion](../F/Fens_of_Sirion.md)) it plunged below ground in the [Falls of Sirion](../F/Falls_of_Sirion.md) at [Andram](../A/Andram.md) (the Long Wall), where the ground fell steeply. Three leagues southwards the Sirion exited the underground caves at the [Gates of Sirion](../G/Gates_of_Sirion.md). It then flowed southwards through [Nan-Tathren](../N/Nan-tathren.md) until it reached the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md), part of [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md), at the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Tributaries {#Tributaries}

In order from north to south, the principal tributaries of the Sirion were the [Rivil](../R/Rivil.md), flowing from Dorthonion until it met Sirion in the Fen of Serech; the former [Dry River](../D/Dry_River.md), the [Mindeb](../M/Mindeb.md), which had its source in [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md) and the [Ered Gorgoroth](../E/Ered_Gorgoroth.md), the [Teiglin](../T/Teiglin.md), the [Esgalduin](../E/Esgalduin.md) of Doriath; the [Aros](../A/Aros.md) flowing south from Dorthonion and west along the [Forest of Region](../F/Forest_of_Region.md) (and also containing the waters of the [Celon](../C/Celon.md) from the Hills of [Himring](../H/Himring.md) and [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md)) which met at Aelin-uial, and the [Narog](../N/Narog.md), which joined Sirion in Nan-Tathren.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Crossing Points {#Crossing_Points}

The main crossing point was the [Ford of Brithiach](../F/Ford_of_Brithiach.md) where the road from Himlad which crossed the Esgalduin at [Iant Iaur](../I/Iant_Iaur.md) crossed Sirion north of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md). There was also a bridge within Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md)'s [Marchwardens](../M/Marchwardens.md) also kept secret ferries near [Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md) to cross the river.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Settlements {#Settlements}

Barad Eithel, at the source of the Sirion in the Ered Wethrin, was a chief fortress of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and his son [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> which guarded the pass into their realm of Mithrim. Further south, in the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md), lay [Tol Sirion](../T/Tol_Sirion.md) (see below). After the Coming of Men, the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of the [House of Haleth](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) made their home in the Forest of Brethil,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the [Sindar](Sindar.md) ruled by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) lay secure within the Girdle of [Melian](../M/Melian.md) in their realm of Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> At the outflow of Sirion into Belegaer, after the destruction of [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) and [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md), the Havens of Sirion were built by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and his people.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Islands {#Islands}

The most important island in Sirion was Tol Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> The original [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>) was built here by [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) in the strategic location controlling the Pass of Sirion where it entered Beleriand between the Ered Wethrin and the Echoriath.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> Shortly after [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) it was captured by Sauron and the island became known as [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](../T/Tol-in-Gaurhoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sirion* is [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) for "Great River"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> or "Great Stream",<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-9">[9]</a></sup> from *[sîr](Sîr.md)* ("river")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> with the suffix *iaun* ("roomy, wide, extensive") simplified.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-9">[9]</a></sup>
