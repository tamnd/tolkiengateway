**Sirion** was a river of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the principal river of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). During most of its course it was the border between [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) and [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand). From the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion) to its mouth, the river was 130 leagues in length.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Sources {#Sources}

The Sirion's source was at [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion) on the eastern side of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), which lay in between [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) (later Anfauglith) and [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) and [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> It was guarded by the tower of [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Course {#Course}

Sirion flowed southwards along the border of the Ered Wethrin, passing through the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech) before entering the narrow steep-sided valley between the Ered Wethrin and the [Echoriath](/wiki/Echoriath) named the Pass of Sirion. Sirion then continued south into Beleriand, with the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil) to the west, and [Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar) and then [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) to the east. After leaving Doriath at Aelin-uial (the [Fens of Sirion](/wiki/Fens_of_Sirion)) it plunged below ground in the [Falls of Sirion](/wiki/Falls_of_Sirion) at [Andram](/wiki/Andram) (the Long Wall), where the ground fell steeply. Three leagues southwards the Sirion exited the underground caves at the [Gates of Sirion](/wiki/Gates_of_Sirion). It then flowed southwards through [Nan-Tathren](/wiki/Nan-tathren "Nan-tathren") until it reached the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar), part of [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer), at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Tributaries {#Tributaries}

In order from north to south, the principal tributaries of the Sirion were the [Rivil](/wiki/Rivil), flowing from Dorthonion until it met Sirion in the Fen of Serech; the former [Dry River](/wiki/Dry_River), the [Mindeb](/wiki/Mindeb), which had its source in [Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb) and the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth), the [Teiglin](/wiki/Teiglin), the [Esgalduin](/wiki/Esgalduin) of Doriath; the [Aros](/wiki/Aros) flowing south from Dorthonion and west along the [Forest of Region](/wiki/Forest_of_Region) (and also containing the waters of the [Celon](/wiki/Celon) from the Hills of [Himring](/wiki/Himring) and [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad)) which met at Aelin-uial, and the [Narog](/wiki/Narog), which joined Sirion in Nan-Tathren.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Crossing Points {#Crossing_Points}

The main crossing point was the [Ford of Brithiach](/wiki/Ford_of_Brithiach) where the road from Himlad which crossed the Esgalduin at [Iant Iaur](/wiki/Iant_Iaur) crossed Sirion north of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil). There was also a bridge within Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s [Marchwardens](/wiki/Marchwardens) also kept secret ferries near [Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial) to cross the river.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Settlements {#Settlements}

Barad Eithel, at the source of the Sirion in the Ered Wethrin, was a chief fortress of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and his son [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon),<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> which guarded the pass into their realm of Mithrim. Further south, in the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion), lay [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion) (see below). After the Coming of Men, the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth) made their home in the Forest of Brethil,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) ruled by [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) lay secure within the Girdle of [Melian](/wiki/Melian) in their realm of Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> At the outflow of Sirion into Belegaer, after the destruction of [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) and [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar), the Havens of Sirion were built by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and his people.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Islands {#Islands}

The most important island in Sirion was Tol Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> The original [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)") was built here by [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") in the strategic location controlling the Pass of Sirion where it entered Beleriand between the Ered Wethrin and the Echoriath.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> Shortly after [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) it was captured by Sauron and the island became known as [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sirion* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Great River"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> or "Great Stream",<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-9">[9]</a></sup> from *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> with the suffix *iaun* ("roomy, wide, extensive") simplified.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-9">[9]</a></sup>
