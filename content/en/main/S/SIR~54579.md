**SIR** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](Sundocarme.md) signifying "flow".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *sir-*, [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md) *sirya-*, [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *sirio* ("flow")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)/[Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md) *[sīre](Sír.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *[sîr](Sîr.md)* ("river"); [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[siril](Siril.md)* ("rivulet")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
