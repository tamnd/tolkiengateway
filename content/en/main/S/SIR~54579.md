**SIR** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "flow".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *sir-*, [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *sirya-*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *sirio* ("flow")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)/[Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *[sīre](/wiki/S%C3%ADr "Sír")*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river"); [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[siril](/wiki/Siril "Siril")* ("rivulet")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
