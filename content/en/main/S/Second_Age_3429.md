**Second Age 3429** (abbreviated to **S.A. 3429**) is the 3429th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Sauron](Sauron.md) attacks [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), [takes](../F/First_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil.md) [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) and burns the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) escapes down [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and goes to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) in the North,<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup> bearing a seedling of the White Tree.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) defends [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md) and [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-1">[1]</a></sup>
