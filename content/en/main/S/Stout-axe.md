The **Stout-axes** or the **Khirvísa** are a part of Drása's Folk, a House of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) created for [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online) game by [Standing Stone Games](/wiki/Standing_Stone_Games).

When they were lured into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) by their desire for their [Ring of Power](/wiki/Ring_of_Power), they were eventually enslaved and lost to history. During the War of the Ring, they were under the command of the [Black Númenórean](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) Zôreth. Their king Váskmun Greytooth was brought to [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr), where he was forced to wear Manthríf until he could no longer endure it. After the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) they called themselves the **Firehorns** and, according to the game, they became free and many joined in the various endeavors of the other [Free Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/Free_peoples "Free peoples").
