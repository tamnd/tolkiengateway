The **Stout-axes** or the **Khirvísa** are a part of Drása's Folk, a House of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) created for [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md) game by [Standing Stone Games](Standing_Stone_Games.md).

When they were lured into [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) by their desire for their [Ring of Power](../R/Ring_of_Power.md), they were eventually enslaved and lost to history. During the War of the Ring, they were under the command of the [Black Númenórean](../B/Black_Númenórean.md) Zôreth. Their king Váskmun Greytooth was brought to [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md), where he was forced to wear Manthríf until he could no longer endure it. After the defeat of [Sauron](Sauron.md) they called themselves the **Firehorns** and, according to the game, they became free and many joined in the various endeavors of the other [Free Peoples of Middle-earth](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md).
