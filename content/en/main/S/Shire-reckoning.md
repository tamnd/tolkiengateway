The **Shire-reckoning** ([Númenórean Sindarin](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean_Sindarin): <i><b>Genediad Drannail</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the calendar system used by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and (eventually) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

In Bree, Year 1 corresponded to [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300"), however the Shire-reckoning Year 1 corresponded to [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601"): the year that colonization of the Shire began by the [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) [Marcho](/wiki/Marcho) and [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco). Therefore, years of the Third Age can be converted to Shire-years by subtracting 1600.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The last year of the Third Age was year [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1421](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"). In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) of King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), the year [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [1](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1") began on [March 25](/wiki/March_25), old style. The Hobbits mostly ignored the change and so for them the first year of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) was just [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1422](/wiki/Fourth_Age_1 "Fourth Age 1").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
