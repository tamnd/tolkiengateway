The **Shire-reckoning** ([Númenórean Sindarin](../N/Númenórean_Sindarin.md): <i><b>Genediad Drannail</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the calendar system used by the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) and (eventually) in [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

In Bree, Year 1 corresponded to [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md), however the Shire-reckoning Year 1 corresponded to [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1601](../T/Third_Age_1601.md): the year that colonization of the Shire began by the [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) [Marcho](../M/Marcho.md) and [Blanco](../B/Blanco.md). Therefore, years of the Third Age can be converted to Shire-years by subtracting 1600.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The last year of the Third Age was year [S.R.](Shire_Reckoning.md) [1421](../T/Third_Age_3021.md). In the [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md) of King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md), the year [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [1](../F/Fourth_Age_1.md) began on [March 25](../M/March_25.md), old style. The Hobbits mostly ignored the change and so for them the first year of the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) was just [S.R.](Shire_Reckoning.md) [1422](../F/Fourth_Age_1.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
