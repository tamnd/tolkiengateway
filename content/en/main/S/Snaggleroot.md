**Snaggleroot** was an [Ent](../E/Ents.md) who appeared alongside the [Entwife](../E/Entwives.md) [Winterbloom](../W/Winterbloom.md). He was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* television series by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md), where he appeared in the second season of the show. He is voiced by Jim Broadbent.
