<i><b>Sagan om Tolkien</b></i> (Swedish: *The Fairy-tale of Tolkien* or *The Tolkien saga*) is a Swedish-language book by [Åke Ohlmarks](../_/Åke_Ohlmarks.md), afterwards claimed by publishers (or the author?) to be the first biography in the world of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In any case, it was the first biography on Tolkien to be published in Sweden.

## From the back cover {#From_the_back_cover}

*The Fairy-tale of Tolkien* tells the story of the peculiar creator of the tales of the Ring. Professor Åke Ohlmarks - the Swedish translator of Tolkien - presents a portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien, covering his life and works. The book also provides a commentary to the trilogy - about its creation, languages and sources. It is the first Swedish biography centered on the greatest story-teller of our century.

\[Freely translated from Swedish\]
