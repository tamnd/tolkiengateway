The **Stair of the Hold** was the name of the steep road that led from the deep valley of [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale) up to the refuge of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow). The road looped several times in tight switchbacks upwards for hundreds of feet and gave it the appearance of an ascending stair, hence the name. The loops also gave any defenders protection from any enemy below. At each turn in the road were large standing stone carved to resemble squatting men with stumpy arms and fat bellies. These ancient statues were called the [Púkel-men](/wiki/P%C3%BAkel-men) by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Stair of the Hold was constructed by long-forgotten [Men](/wiki/Men_of_the_Mountains "Men of the Mountains") of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) during the [Dark Years](/wiki/Dark_Years).

The first men of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) to climb the Stair of the Hold were King [Brego](/wiki/Brego) and his son [Baldor](/wiki/Baldor).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup> Brego returned in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2569](/wiki/Third_Age_2569 "Third Age 2569") to attempt the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) and never returned.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) ascended the Stair of the Hold on [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") but did not descend, taking instead the Paths of the Dead. [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and his riders (with [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck")) went up the road on [9 March](/wiki/9_March) and rode down on [the 10th](/wiki/10_March "10 March").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
