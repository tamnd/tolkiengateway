**Second Age 877** (abbreviated to **S.A. 877**) is the 877th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) departs for [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) aboard his ship *[Hirilondë](../H/Hirilondë.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
