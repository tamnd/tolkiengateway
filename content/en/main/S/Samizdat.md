**Samizdat** (Russian: **самиздат**) is a term from Russian, describing a self-published book. It found its origin during the Soviet era, when dissident works were published and distributed privately.

[Mark T. Hooker](/wiki/Mark_T._Hooker) first used the term to describe the many unauthorized or "pirate" translations of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s work in Russian, but over time the term has been adopted for translations in all languages. According to Olga Markova, there were at least ten unauthorized translations of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) between [1990](/wiki/1990) and [2005](/wiki/2005).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
