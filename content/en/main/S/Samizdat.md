**Samizdat** (Russian: **самиздат**) is a term from Russian, describing a self-published book. It found its origin during the Soviet era, when dissident works were published and distributed privately.

[Mark T. Hooker](../M/Mark_T._Hooker.md) first used the term to describe the many unauthorized or "pirate" translations of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work in Russian, but over time the term has been adopted for translations in all languages. According to Olga Markova, there were at least ten unauthorized translations of [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) between [1990](../_/1990.md) and [2005](../_/2005.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
