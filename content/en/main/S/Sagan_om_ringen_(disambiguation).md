**Sagan om ringen** can refer to:

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/H%C3%A4rskarringen" title="Härskarringen">Sagan om ringen</a></b></i>, the title in Swedish of [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks)' translation of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- *[Sagan om Ringen (album)](</wiki/Sagan_om_Ringen_(album)>)*, [Bo Hansson](/wiki/Bo_Hansson)'s album of music inspired by *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- *Sagan om Ringen: Rollspelet*, the Swedish edition of *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* .
- *[Sagan om Ringen (1971 film)](</wiki/Sagan_om_Ringen_(1971_film)>)*, the 1971 Swedish television film adaption of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*
