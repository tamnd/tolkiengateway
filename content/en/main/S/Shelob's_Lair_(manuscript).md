**Shelob's Lair** is a manuscript page by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) for *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*. It is kept at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library) (MS. Tolkien Drawings 81). The manuscript, featuring a sketch of [Shelob's Lair](/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair) and the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol), was first written in pencil, before Tolkien went over it in ink.<sup id="cite_ref-Bodleian_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bodleian-1">[1]</a></sup>

A cropped version of the manuscript was first published in *[The Lord of the Rings 1977 Calendar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_1977_Calendar)*, together with the following note by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien):

"*The writing accompanying this picture is a fragment of an early draft of the passage in [The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers) (Book IV, Chapter 8, [The Stairs of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/The_Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol)) in which is described [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s first sight of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol). In this draft [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) had already disappeared at this point in the story.*"

In [1995](/wiki/1995), a full reproduction of the manuscript appeared in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator "J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator")* (p. 175, drawing 171).

In [2012](/wiki/2012), the manuscript was displayed at the exhibition *The Romance of the Middle Ages* (also reproduced in the [accompanying exhibition book](/wiki/The_Romance_of_the_Middle_Ages "The Romance of the Middle Ages")).<sup id="cite_ref-Bodleian_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bodleian-1">[1]</a></sup>
