**Shelob's Lair** is a manuscript page by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*. It is kept at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md) (MS. Tolkien Drawings 81). The manuscript, featuring a sketch of [Shelob's Lair](Shelob's_Lair.md) and the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md), was first written in pencil, before Tolkien went over it in ink.<sup id="cite_ref-Bodleian_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bodleian-1">[1]</a></sup>

A cropped version of the manuscript was first published in *[The Lord of the Rings 1977 Calendar](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_1977_Calendar.md)*, together with the following note by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md):

"*The writing accompanying this picture is a fragment of an early draft of the passage in [The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md) (Book IV, Chapter 8, [The Stairs of Cirith Ungol](../T/The_Stairs_of_Cirith_Ungol.md)) in which is described [Sam](Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s first sight of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md). In this draft [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) had already disappeared at this point in the story.*"

In [1995](../_/1995.md), a full reproduction of the manuscript appeared in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Artist_and_Illustrator.md)* (p. 175, drawing 171).

In [2012](../_/2012.md), the manuscript was displayed at the exhibition *The Romance of the Middle Ages* (also reproduced in the [accompanying exhibition book](../T/The_Romance_of_the_Middle_Ages.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Bodleian_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bodleian-1">[1]</a></sup>
