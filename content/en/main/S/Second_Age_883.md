**Second Age 883** (abbreviated to **S.A. 883**) is the 883rd [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) surrenders the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Sceptre of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), son of Tar-Meneldur, becomes [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Tar-Aldarion departs to [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond) to revisit [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the same year or the next.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>
- No records are left of the later voyages of Tar-Aldarion, but it is known that he went much on land and sea and went up the river [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) as far as [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) and met [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) there. His works at the harbour of [Vinyalondë](/wiki/Vinyalond%C3%AB) were swept away. The works that he began again there were never completed and the sea gnawed at them.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>
