**Second Age 883** (abbreviated to **S.A. 883**) is the 883rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md) surrenders the [Sceptre](Sceptre_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md), son of Tar-Meneldur, becomes [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Tar-Aldarion departs to [Mithlond](../M/Mithlond.md) to revisit [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) in the same year or the next.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>
- No records are left of the later voyages of Tar-Aldarion, but it is known that he went much on land and sea and went up the river [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md) as far as [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) and met [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) there. His works at the harbour of [Vinyalondë](../V/Vinyalondë.md) were swept away. The works that he began again there were never completed and the sea gnawed at them.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>
