**Summerfilth** was a non-existent month used jocularly among [Shire-hobbits](Shire-hobbits.md).

Proverbially, Summerfilth would begin on the day of [Friday](../H/Highday.md); there was no actual month in the [Shire Calendar](Shire_Calendar.md) that ever started on a Friday (since after the reform of c. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2670](../T/Third_Age_2670.md), each weekday always fell on the same date) and the Hobbits would say "*on Friday the first of Summerfilth*" to refer to something that would never happen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name 'Summerfilth' is taken from the real month of [Winterfilth](../W/Winterfilth.md) (approximately equivalent to our October).
