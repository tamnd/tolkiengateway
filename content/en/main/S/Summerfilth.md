**Summerfilth** was a non-existent month used jocularly among [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits).

Proverbially, Summerfilth would begin on the day of [Friday](/wiki/Highday "Highday"); there was no actual month in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) that ever started on a Friday (since after the reform of c. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2670](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670"), each weekday always fell on the same date) and the Hobbits would say "*on Friday the first of Summerfilth*" to refer to something that would never happen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name 'Summerfilth' is taken from the real month of [Winterfilth](/wiki/Winterfilth) (approximately equivalent to our October).
