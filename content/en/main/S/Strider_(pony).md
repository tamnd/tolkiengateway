**Strider** was a [pony](/wiki/Ponies "Ponies") acquired by [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). He named it "[Strider](/wiki/Strider)" after [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s own nickname from [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

Strider rode with Frodo back to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") at the end of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Two years later he carried him westwards in the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings) to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) on the coast of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
