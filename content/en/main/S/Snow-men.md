The **Snow-men** are a people in the North Pole, the only people who live close to [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md). His gardener, the [Snow Man](Snow_Man.md), is the eldest of them. They aren't snowmen made of snow of course, as Father Christmas clarifies in one letter; however in another letter he says that the Snow Man broke himself and he used what was left of him to draw a white picture.

Their children, the Snow-boys often help Father Christmas, as one time when they battled the [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md). One time the [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) was too lazy packing the presents and he called the Snow-boys for help to the cellars (although they aren't allowed there) and started playing, causing his fur to be burned by the crackers.
