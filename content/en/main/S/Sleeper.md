The **Sleeper** was a mysterious figure who dwelt within the [Tower of Pearl](/wiki/Tower_of_Pearl) located in the westernmost cape of the [Twilit Isles](/wiki/Twilit_Isles) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In his travel to [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r), [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) woke up the Sleeper with the [gong](/wiki/Gong_of_the_Children "Gong of the Children") of [Littleheart](/wiki/Littleheart).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:254</sup>

As [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) never finished "[The Tale of Eärendel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_E%C3%A4rendel)", the Sleeper's identity was never specified. In an outline of the Tale, Tolkien wrote: "The Sleeper is [Idril](/wiki/Idril) but he \[Eärendel\] does not know", but this was struck out.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:276</sup> In other note, the Sleeper was a messenger of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) who was cursed and unable to leave the Tower; after being awakened by Littleheart's gong, the Sleeper warned the travellers of the magic.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:263</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Before writing any narrative of the Lost Tales, Tolkien wrote *[Tha Eadigan Saelidan: The Happy Mariners](/wiki/Tha_Eadigan_Saelidan:_The_Happy_Mariners)*, which [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) identifies with 'the Song of the Sleeper of the Tower of Pearl' mentioned in the Lost Tale of "[The Hiding of Valinor](/wiki/The_Hiding_of_Valinor)".<sup id="cite_ref-Earendel_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendel-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:274</sup> Although the poem gives some details about the Tower, it does not include any information about the Sleeper.
