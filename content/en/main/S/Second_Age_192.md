**Second Age 192** (abbreviated to **S.A. 192**) is the 192nd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Amandil](../T/Tar-Amandil.md), eldest son and child of [Vardamir Nólimon](../V/Vardamir.md) and third [King](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-1">[1]</a></sup>
