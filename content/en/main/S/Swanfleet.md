The **Swanfleet** or **Nîn-in-Eilph** was a marshy area in eastern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) through which the lower reaches of the [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin) (or "Swanfleet river") flowed before it joined the [Mitheithel](/wiki/Mitheithel) (Hoarwell).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Swanfleet was effectively an inland delta, with uncertain streams and a very uncertain difference between land and water.

It strongly resembled the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) on the other side of [Hithaeglir](/wiki/Misty_Mountains "Misty Mountains"), and as such many [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) felt right at home here after their migration into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). These Stoors remained here until the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) nearly wiped them out, and the remainder then went to the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

At the east end of the Swanfleet was a waterfall, beyond which was a ford over the Glanduin,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> from where led a road to the ruins of [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil), the ruined city of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nîn-in-Eilph* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "water-lands of the Swans".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first printings of *[A Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/A_Map_of_Middle-earth)*, [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) wrongly labels "R\[iver\] Swanfleet" as the lower part of the R. Glanduin; Swanfleet is not a river. The mistake was corrected in later prints.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The name Swanfleet is applied not only to the delta, which is specifically called &#34;Wadewater&#34;, but to the entire surrounding region east of the <a href="/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3" title="Gwathló">Gwathló</a>. This includes &#34;Western <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>&#34; and the lands about the <a href="/wiki/Old_South_Road" title="Old South Road">Old South Road</a> on the edge of <a href="/wiki/Enedwaith" title="Enedwaith">Enedwaith</a>. Several villages of <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> and <a href="/wiki/Stoors" title="Stoors">Stoor</a> <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a> can be found here, as can the ruins of &#34;<a href="/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil" title="Ost-in-Edhil">Caras Gelebren</a>&#34;, capital of Eregion.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Swanfleet is presented as an optional starting location available to new characters of all races, as opposed to the default starting regions of <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, <a href="/wiki/Blue_Mountains" title="Blue Mountains">Ered Luin</a>, and <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, to which new characters are sent depending on their race.</dd></dl>
