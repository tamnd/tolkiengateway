The **Swanfleet** or **Nîn-in-Eilph** was a marshy area in eastern [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) through which the lower reaches of the [Glanduin](../G/Glanduin.md) (or "Swanfleet river") flowed before it joined the [Mitheithel](../M/Mitheithel.md) (Hoarwell).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The Swanfleet was effectively an inland delta, with uncertain streams and a very uncertain difference between land and water.

It strongly resembled the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md) on the other side of [Hithaeglir](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), and as such many [Stoors](Stoors.md) felt right at home here after their migration into [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). These Stoors remained here until the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) nearly wiped them out, and the remainder then went to the [Southfarthing](Southfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

At the east end of the Swanfleet was a waterfall, beyond which was a ford over the Glanduin,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> from where led a road to the ruins of [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md), the ruined city of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nîn-in-Eilph* is [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) for "water-lands of the Swans".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first printings of *[A Map of Middle-earth](../A/A_Map_of_Middle-earth.md)*, [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md) wrongly labels "R\[iver\] Swanfleet" as the lower part of the R. Glanduin; Swanfleet is not a river. The mistake was corrected in later prints.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The name Swanfleet is applied not only to the delta, which is specifically called &#34;Wadewater&#34;, but to the entire surrounding region east of the <a href="../G/Gwathló.md" title="Gwathló">Gwathló</a>. This includes &#34;Western <a href="../E/Eregion.md" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>&#34; and the lands about the <a href="../O/Old_South_Road.md" title="Old South Road">Old South Road</a> on the edge of <a href="../E/Enedwaith.md" title="Enedwaith">Enedwaith</a>. Several villages of <a href="../M/Men.md" title="Men">Men</a> and <a href="Stoors.md" title="Stoors">Stoor</a> <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a> can be found here, as can the ruins of &#34;<a href="../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md" title="Ost-in-Edhil">Caras Gelebren</a>&#34;, capital of Eregion.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Swanfleet is presented as an optional starting location available to new characters of all races, as opposed to the default starting regions of <a href="../B/Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, <a href="../B/Blue_Mountains.md" title="Blue Mountains">Ered Luin</a>, and <a href="../T/The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, to which new characters are sent depending on their race.</dd></dl>
