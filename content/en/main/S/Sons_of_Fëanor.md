The **Sons of Fëanor** were the seven [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elves](/wiki/Elves) [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros), [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm), [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir), [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin), [Amrod and Amras](/wiki/Amrod_and_Amras).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They took the [Oath of Fëanor](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor) with their father,<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> and pursued it after his death in the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath).<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup> They led a [scarce part](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor") of the [exiled Noldor](/wiki/Exiled_Noldor "Exiled Noldor") and held lands in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand), with Maedhros and Maglor holding the immediate passes south of [Lothlann](/wiki/Lothlann), and the other brothers ruling lands behind their guard to the south. Their [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) names are the ones usually given.

## History {#History}

After the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and his sons swore the terrible Oath of Fëanor, vowing to retrieve the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) from anyone that would withhold them. The Sons of Fëanor followed their father [out of Valinor](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor "Flight of the Noldor") and into [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) along with their following of [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> They took part in the Dagor-nuin-Giliath, in which Fëanor was mortally wounded. Soon after, Maedhros was captured by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). Following his rescue by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon), Maedhros waived his claim to kingship over the Noldor, and it passed to the [House of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin). Thereafter the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor) was called the Dispossessed, as [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) had [predicted](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos "Doom of Mandos").<sup id="cite_ref-Return_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Sons of Fëanor and their followers settled in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) defending the open region which was named the [March of Maedhros](/wiki/March_of_Maedhros). Maedhros made his fortress upon [Himring](/wiki/Himring), Celegorm and Curufin held the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon), Maglor defended the [land](/wiki/Maglor%27s_Gap "Maglor's Gap") between the northern arms of [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), Caranthir and his folk moved to [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) which was thenceforth also called Dor Caranthir and Amrod and Amras dwelt in the woods south of the March.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Fëanor's sons were in general allied with the other [Elves of Beleriand](/wiki/Elves_of_Beleriand) against the power of Morgoth and helped [besiege](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband "Siege of Angband") [Angband](/wiki/Angband) until the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).

After the recovery of one of the Great Jewels by [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) the Oath of their father came into action. Maedhros saw that Morgoth was indeed assailable, and set out to unite the peoples of Beleriand under the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros). Little aid came from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) because of the earlier deeds of the Sons of Fëanor The Union was ended in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), leaving the Sons of Fëanor scattered and defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Oath of Fëanor was roused once more when the Fëanorians learned that the Silmaril of Beren and Lúthien was now in the possession of their son [Dior](/wiki/Dior). They [attacked](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying "Second Kinslaying") Dior in Doriath, and Celegorm, Curufin, and Caranthir were slain in the conflict, but they gained not the Silmaril, for Elwing escaped with it and fled to the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After a time, tidings of Elwing's dwelling reached Maedhros, and, tormented by their Oath, he and his remaining brothers sent messages to the Havens requesting the Silmaril. But Elwing refused to surrender the Jewel, and the Sons of Fëanor [attacked](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying "Third Kinslaying") her and the Elves of Sirion. Maedhros and Maglor had the victory, but both Amrod and Amras were slain. Elwing flung herself into the [Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea "Great Sea") with the Silmaril and brought it to [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), who bore it into the West. The sons of Eärendil and Elwing, [Elros](/wiki/Elros) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), were captured by the two brothers but Maglor grew to love them and fostered them.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-7">[7]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), only Maedhros and Maglor remained. They stole the last two Silmarils from the camp of [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB), but their evil deeds in recovering them meant that the hallowed Jewels rejected their claimants, and their hands were burned. Maedhros in anguish threw himself into a fiery fissure, and Maglor in despair cast his Jewel into the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea).<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-7">[7]</a></sup>

## The Sons {#The_Sons}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/53/L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg/300px-L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="302" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/53/L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg/450px-L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/5/53/L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg/600px-L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1_-_Feanor_and_his_family.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="1031"/></a><figcaption><i>Feanor and his family</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_L%C3%ADda_Holubov%C3%A1" title="Category:Images by Lída Holubová">Lída Holubová</a></figcaption></figure>

1. **[Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) the Tall** - his [father-name](/wiki/Father-name "Father-name") was *Nelyafinwë* ("Finwë the Third") and his [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") was *Maitimo* ("Well-shaped One"). He gave up the High Kingship because of his rescue by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) son of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin).
2. **[Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) the Minstrel** - his father-name was *Kanafinwë* ("Strong-voiced Finwë") and his mother-name was *Makalaurë* ("Forging Gold" meant in the poetic sense).
3. **[Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) the Fair** - his father-name was *Turcafinwë* ("Strong Bodied Finwë") and his mother-name was *Tyelcormo* ("Hasty-Riser").
4. **[Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) the Dark** - his father-name was *Morifinwë* ("Dark\[-haired\] Finwë") because of his dark hair, and his mother name was *Carnistir* ("Red-faced") because of his ruddy complexion.
5. **[Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) the Crafty** - his father-name was *Curufinwë* ("Skillful Finwë"), the same name as Fëanor. His mother-name was *Atarinkë* ("Little Father"). He was the father of [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), maker of the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power).
6. **[Amrod](/wiki/Amrod)** - his father-name was *Pityafinwë* ("Little Finwë") and his mother name was *Ambarussa* ("Top-russet").
7. **[Amras](/wiki/Amras)** - his father-name was *Telufinwë* ("Last Finwë") and though his mother-name was originally *Ambarussa*, his father insisted on naming him separately. His mother gave him the name *Umbarto* ("The Fated"), but Fëanor changed it to *Ambarto* ("Upwards-exalted"), disturbed by the ominous name.
