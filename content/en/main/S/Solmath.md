**Solmath** was the second month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), with thirty days approximately corresponding to modern [22 January](/wiki/22_January) through [20 February](/wiki/20_February).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for February, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/solmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:solmonaþ">Solmónaþ</a></i>, meaning "mud-month" (cf. <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/soil#Etymology" title="wiktionary:soil">soil</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In colloquial hobbitish, the month was pronounced, and sometimes spelled, *Somath*.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
