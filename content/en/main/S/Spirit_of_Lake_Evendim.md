A **spirit of [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim)** appears in [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) and [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)

## In *Middle-earth Role Playing* {#In_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

**Linyuilë**, also known as the ”Lady of the Lake” and the ”Cold Ghost,” was the water spirit of Lake Evendim.

In form and dress she resembled the Elves who migrated to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), but she adopted the languages and manners of the lake's later populations. This allowed her to walk among them if she so pleased, often for weddings. She similarly attended every coronation and birth at the palace of [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) until its destruction.

While she often helped those in need, she was fickle and held long grudges against those who wronged her (or resembled them).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## In *The Lord of the Rings Online* {#In_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

**Gwindeth**, the “Blue Lady of Evendim”, was a spirit who may have been the River-maiden of the Swiftbrook, a river that fed into Lake Evendim.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> She dwelt in the cave Gwindethrond ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Gwindeth's Cave”) and had been an adviser to the Kings of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The last of the Silithair, shining adamants from the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor), was placed into her care by [Amlaith](/wiki/Amlaith). It was retrieved at the behest of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) to reforge [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
