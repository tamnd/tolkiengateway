In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Siragalë** was an Elven realm which later became [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Some of the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) who fled [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) settled in a woodland whose rivers drained into the [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin). They named this land *Siremyn-galador* ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Flowing-hill Tree-land”), and named themselves Sirannar accordingly. The warrior Merethorn united this people against the servants of Morgoth who roamed the lands. After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), he swore allegiance to [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and married a Noldo woman named Elindiel.

The Sirannar never built any towns, and Merethorn traveled around his realm, setting up court wherever he was needed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> Men started simplifying the name to “Sirigalë”, and its inhabitants were the first of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) to be called [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).

Two major routes, the [Eastern Trail](/wiki/East_Road "East Road") ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Len Romen*) and the Red-Dwarf Trail ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Len Caraug*), met at [Michinnec](/wiki/Michel_Delving "Michel Delving") in Sirigalë. It is from there that Merethorn dealt with Dwarves and Men.

Merethorn was slain by a Sand Drake during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), and Elindiel held Lindon responsible. She rejected the later rule of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [10](/wiki/Third_Age_10 "Third Age 10") Sirigalë became an Elvish province of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) when she swore fealty to its king.<sup id="cite_ref-taleofyears_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-taleofyears-3">[2]</a></sup> The loss of Merethorn led many of the Sirannar to sail westward, and thus Sirigalë never truly flourished. Their dwindling numbers led to an increased need for mannish lords to keep the province free from brigands. <sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

When Arnor was divided, Elindiel went with [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain). Many of the remaining Elves perished in the [Second Northern War](/wiki/Angmar_conflict "Angmar conflict") of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1408&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1408 (page does not exist)">1408</a>-<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1410&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1410 (page does not exist)">1410</a>. Ten years later, the final orcs are finally driven from Sirigalë and the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-taleofyears_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-taleofyears-3">[2]</a></sup>

The reign of [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II) saw political conflict between the king and the Tarma family, who wished to devolve power to the nobility. The Tarmas saw potential in Siragalë, acquiring more and more foresting and hunting rights in the [north of the province](/wiki/Northfarthing "Northfarthing"). Deeming it the *Tarmaladen* ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") “Tarma-fiefdom”), they made new refugees swear loyalty to the family rather than to the king.

[Marcho and Blanco](/wiki/Marcho_and_Blanco), two Hobbit refugees, had become aware of these tensions, and through giving their oaths to the king directly were able to bypass the Tarmas. While the few remaining Elves were not happy to see farmers in their old lands, they eventually came to prefer them over Men. The brothers also eventually found friends in [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) and received permission to properly settle in the region as a ways of curbing the power the Tarma family.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>
