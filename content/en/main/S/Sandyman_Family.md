The **Sandyman Family** was a family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) from [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton). They operated the [Old Mill](/wiki/Old_Mill).

## History {#History}

The Sandyman family did not get along well with some of the other Hobbiton families, especially the [Gamgees](/wiki/Gamgee_Family "Gamgee Family"). [The Gaffer](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") did not like [the miller](/wiki/Sandyman "Sandyman"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> nor did his son [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") like miller's son [Ted](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman "Ted Sandyman"), who collaborated with [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) and [Sharkey](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
