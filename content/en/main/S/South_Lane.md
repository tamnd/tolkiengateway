**South Lane** was a short lane in the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) that led from the [Bywater Road](../B/Bywater_Road.md) southward a quarter of a mile to the farmlands of the [Cotton Family](../C/Cotton_Family~66362.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup> On [2 November](../_/2_November.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) when the [Travellers](../T/Travellers.md) were on the Bywater Road<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and had dispersed the first small knot of [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md), [Sam](Samwise_Gamgee.md) suggested that they set up a defence at the Cotton's home down South Lane. [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) rejected this idea because the Ruffians could too easily hem them in and then smoke them out. Eventually the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) decided on more direct action.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the manuscript for *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* Sam's suggestion to take the South Lane to the Cotton's farm was accepted. The Travellers went to the farm and the wary Cottons let them in. A fight broke out and six ruffians were killed. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) rewrote this episode so that no ruffians were killed at the farm, instead the hobbits were trapped and planned to burst out. At this point [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) said his father realized that he had taken a wrong turning; there was no credible way for the hobbits to burst out unscathed.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup>
