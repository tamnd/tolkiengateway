**South Lane** was a short lane in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") that led from the [Bywater Road](/wiki/Bywater_Road) southward a quarter of a mile to the farmlands of the [Cotton Family](/wiki/Cotton_Family).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup> On [2 November](/wiki/2_November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") when the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) were on the Bywater Road<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and had dispersed the first small knot of [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") suggested that they set up a defence at the Cotton's home down South Lane. [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") rejected this idea because the Ruffians could too easily hem them in and then smoke them out. Eventually the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) decided on more direct action.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the manuscript for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* Sam's suggestion to take the South Lane to the Cotton's farm was accepted. The Travellers went to the farm and the wary Cottons let them in. A fight broke out and six ruffians were killed. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) rewrote this episode so that no ruffians were killed at the farm, instead the hobbits were trapped and planned to burst out. At this point [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) said his father realized that he had taken a wrong turning; there was no credible way for the hobbits to burst out unscathed.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup>
