<i><b>sí</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "now".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><b>sí</b> man i yulma nin enquantuva?</i> ('Who now shall refill the cup for me?')<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- <i><b>sí</b> ar lúmesse ya firuvamme</i> ('now and at the hour of our death')<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>
