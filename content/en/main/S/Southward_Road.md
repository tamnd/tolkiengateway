The **Southward Road** was a road in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).

## Course {#Course}

The Southward Road ran from the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) southwards through the narrow strip of land between the mountains of the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) and the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The road had been constructed a long time ago and ran southwards in a straight and level course, sometimes cutting through the slopes of hillsides or across streams on stone bridges in the form of a wide shapely arch until at last it became a straight country cart-road.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Just north of the Cross-roads the Southward Road wound its way near the feet of the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) until it descended to the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads) of the Fallen King, where it met the [road](/wiki/Morgul-road "Morgul-road") that went from [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) to [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Interpretations {#Interpretations}

It is not known if the name *Southward Road* was another name for the **North Road**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> of Ithilien or for the [Harad Road](/wiki/Harad_Road), which means *South Road*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> or if the name *Southward Road* was only used for a part of the road, which continued under the name *Harad Road* or *South Road* after the Cross-roads<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 2]</a></sup> or after a point lying further to the south, such as after the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup>
