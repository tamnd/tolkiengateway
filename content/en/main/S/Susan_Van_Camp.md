**Susan Van Camp** (born [11 June](../_/11_June.md) [1959](../_/1959.md)) is an American fantasy and comic book artist. In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*. She also illustrated for [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md) and early [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) sets.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
