The **Sirith** was a river in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that rose in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in eastern [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin), and flowed southward. South of the last outliers of the mountains it was joined by a tributary, the River [Celos](/wiki/Celos). At the point where it met the [Great River Anduin](/wiki/Anduin "Anduin"), the port of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) was built.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Sirith* means "a flowing" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Sirith was in the area of Upper <a href="/wiki/Lebennin" title="Lebennin">Lebennin</a> in the Eastern <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> region. It flowed through the fortified valley of <a href="/wiki/Tumladen_(Gondor)" title="Tumladen (Gondor)">Tumladen</a> before turning west to meet the <a href="/wiki/Celos" title="Celos">Celos</a> below the <a href="/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor" title="Warning beacons of Gondor">beacon</a> &#34;Sirthanc&#34;. The town of &#34;Erynos&#34; was located on the western shore across from where the two rivers met. South of the juncture, the two joined rivers flowed south from the beacon to the <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>, forming the boundary with Lower Lebennin and the region of Central Gondor. The combined river also bisected the port city of <a href="/wiki/Pelargir" title="Pelargir">Pelargir</a> where it joined the <a href="/wiki/Anduin" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>. The Sirith was the domain of the honest River-maid Truetongue, one of the Five Sisters, though she and her sisters were widely considered a myth by the time of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>.</dd></dl>
