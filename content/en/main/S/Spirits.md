**Spirits** are the essential beings who generally dwell in the [Unseen](../U/Unseen.md), and whose nature generally does not include a corporeal body.

[Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) creates spirits from nothing through the [Flame Imperishable](../F/Flame_Imperishable.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Groups {#Groups}

### Ainur {#Ainur}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../A/Ainur.md" title="Ainur">Ainur</a></b></div></div>

Before [Eä](../E/Eä.md), [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md) [created](../C/Creation_of_the_Ainur.md) the spirits known as the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md), the Holy Ones. The greater were called [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and the lesser [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SII-2">[2]</a></sup>

Some of the spirits were associated with a certain <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_element" title="wikipedia:Classical element">element</a>, or physical phenomenon:

- *Air spirits*: Spirits shaped like [hawks](../H/Hawks.md) and [eagles](../E/Eagles.md) delivered messages to [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) as he sat in his throne in [Ilmarin](../I/Ilmarin.md) upon [Taniquetil](../T/Taniquetil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *Fire spirits*: The Maia [Arien](../A/Arien.md) is said to have been "*from the beginning a spirit of fire*".<sup id="cite_ref-S11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-4">[4]</a></sup> Melkor seduced some of the fire spirits, which became known as the [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- *Water spirits*: A host of spirits followed [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) to maintain the waters of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), of whom the Maiar [Ossë](../O/Ossë.md) and [Uinen](../U/Uinen.md) were the greatest.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Perhaps [Salmar](Salmar.md), and [Goldberry](../G/Goldberry.md) and the [River-woman](../R/River-woman.md) were such water spirits.

### Evil spirits {#Evil_spirits}

All Ainur originally adhered to Eru, but the Vala [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) became corrupted and fell from the grace of [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SII_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SII-2">[2]</a></sup> Upon the creation of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), Melkor "*gathered to himself spirits out of the voids of Eä that he had perverted to his service*", and these became known as the [Úmaiar](../_/Úmaiar.md) or [demons](../D/Demons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

It is told that [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) sent "*[spirits of shadows](Spirits_of_shadows.md)*" against [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md) and the vessel of the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), as he hated the light of the [Sun](Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S11_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-4">[4]</a></sup> There was a strife in [Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md), but Tilion was victorious against these evil spirits.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-4">[4]</a></sup> They may possibly have been among the spirits Morgoth gathered to his service.

In the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), spirits of "*evil vigilance*" are said to have abode in the [Two Watchers](../T/Two_Watchers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The [Barrow-wights](../B/Barrow-wights.md) are furthermore said to be evil spirits who had entered in deserted mounds, stirring the bones of the dead.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Other {#Other}

[Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) was probably a "spirit" (of some sorts) of the earth.<sup id="cite_ref-coghill_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-coghill-10">[10]</a></sup> [Goldberry](../G/Goldberry.md) and the [River-woman](../R/River-woman.md) would perhaps belong to such an order.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The *[fëar](../F/Fëar.md)* are the spirits (souls) that inhabit the bodies of the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md); Some fëar also, and "spirits from afar", were sent to inhabit the [Ents](../E/Ents.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [Unbodied](../U/Unbodied.md) fëar, could be controlled by necromancy<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> (See also: [undead](../U/Undead.md)).

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), a collective name for the spirits is *[ëalar](../_/Ëalar.md)*, actually meaning "beings".<sup id="cite_ref-MR_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-15">[15]</a></sup> Another word was [*thúlë*, later *súlë*](Súlë.md), from the [root](Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=TH%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THŪ (page does not exist)">THŪ</a>, related to words for "wind".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> It was also the name for one of the [tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[An Evening in Tavrobel](../A/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel.md)*, an early poem from Tolkien's youth, tiny, gleaming spirits appear dancing in [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In a draft of *[In the House of Tom Bombadil](../I/In_the_House_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*, Tom explains that [Old Man Willow](../O/Old_Man_Willow.md) is a "grey thirsty earth-bound spirit" that had "become imprisoned in the greatest Willow of the [Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>
