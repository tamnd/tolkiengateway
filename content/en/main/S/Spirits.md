**Spirits** are the essential beings who generally dwell in the [Unseen](/wiki/Unseen), and whose nature generally does not include a corporeal body.

[Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) creates spirits from nothing through the [Flame Imperishable](/wiki/Flame_Imperishable).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Groups {#Groups}

### Ainur {#Ainur}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Ainur" title="Ainur">Ainur</a></b></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

Before [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4), [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") [created](/wiki/Creation_of_the_Ainur "Creation of the Ainur") the spirits known as the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur), the Holy Ones. The greater were called [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and the lesser [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar).<sup id="cite_ref-SII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SII-2">[2]</a></sup>

Some of the spirits were associated with a certain <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_element" title="wikipedia:Classical element">element</a>, or physical phenomenon:

- *Air spirits*: Spirits shaped like [hawks](/wiki/Hawks "Hawks") and [eagles](/wiki/Eagles "Eagles") delivered messages to [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) as he sat in his throne in [Ilmarin](/wiki/Ilmarin) upon [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- *Fire spirits*: The Maia [Arien](/wiki/Arien) is said to have been "*from the beginning a spirit of fire*".<sup id="cite_ref-S11_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-4">[4]</a></sup> Melkor seduced some of the fire spirits, which became known as the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
- *Water spirits*: A host of spirits followed [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) to maintain the waters of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), of whom the Maiar [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) and [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen) were the greatest.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Perhaps [Salmar](/wiki/Salmar), and [Goldberry](/wiki/Goldberry) and the [River-woman](/wiki/River-woman) were such water spirits.

### Evil spirits {#Evil_spirits}

All Ainur originally adhered to Eru, but the Vala [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") became corrupted and fell from the grace of [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar").<sup id="cite_ref-SII_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SII-2">[2]</a></sup> Upon the creation of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), Melkor "*gathered to himself spirits out of the voids of Eä that he had perverted to his service*", and these became known as the [Úmaiar](/wiki/%C3%9Amaiar) or [demons](/wiki/Demons "Demons").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

It is told that [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) sent "*[spirits of shadows](/wiki/Spirits_of_shadows "Spirits of shadows")*" against [Tilion](/wiki/Tilion) and the vessel of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), as he hated the light of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-S11_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-4">[4]</a></sup> There was a strife in [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen), but Tilion was victorious against these evil spirits.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-4">[4]</a></sup> They may possibly have been among the spirits Morgoth gathered to his service.

In the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), spirits of "*evil vigilance*" are said to have abode in the [Two Watchers](/wiki/Two_Watchers).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The [Barrow-wights](/wiki/Barrow-wights) are furthermore said to be evil spirits who had entered in deserted mounds, stirring the bones of the dead.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Other {#Other}

[Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) was probably a "spirit" (of some sorts) of the earth.<sup id="cite_ref-coghill_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-coghill-10">[10]</a></sup> [Goldberry](/wiki/Goldberry) and the [River-woman](/wiki/River-woman) would perhaps belong to such an order.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The *[fëar](/wiki/F%C3%ABar "Fëar")* are the spirits (souls) that inhabit the bodies of the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar); Some fëar also, and "spirits from afar", were sent to inhabit the [Ents](/wiki/Ents).<sup id="cite_ref-L247_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L247-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [Unbodied](/wiki/Unbodied) fëar, could be controlled by necromancy<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> (See also: [undead](/wiki/Undead "Undead")).

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), a collective name for the spirits is *[ëalar](/wiki/%C3%8Balar "Ëalar")*, actually meaning "beings".<sup id="cite_ref-MR_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-15">[15]</a></sup> Another word was [*thúlë*, later *súlë*](/wiki/S%C3%BAl%C3%AB "Súlë"), from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=TH%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THŪ (page does not exist)">THŪ</a>, related to words for "wind".<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> It was also the name for one of the [tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[An Evening in Tavrobel](/wiki/An_Evening_in_Tavrobel)*, an early poem from Tolkien's youth, tiny, gleaming spirits appear dancing in [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In a draft of *[In the House of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/In_the_House_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, Tom explains that [Old Man Willow](/wiki/Old_Man_Willow) is a "grey thirsty earth-bound spirit" that had "become imprisoned in the greatest Willow of the [Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest "Old Forest")".<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>
