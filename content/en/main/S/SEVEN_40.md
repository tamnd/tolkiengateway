<i><b>SEVEN 40</b></i> is the fourtieth and most current volume of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2024](../_/2024.md).<sup id="cite_ref-vii_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Joy Davidman's Unpublished Letters: August 16 to December 19, 1952"
    - By: Joy Davidman Lewis and Don W. King
- "Language, Consciousness, and the Recovery of Human Meaning"
    - By: Owen Barfield and Sarah O'Dell
- "Shakespeare on (and in) the Margins: C.S. Lewis's Engagement with Shakespeare"
    - By: Sarah R.A. Waters
- "The Poetry of Conduct: Sanctification in George Herbert and C.S. Lewis"
    - By: Kristine A. Wolberg
- "A 'Diagram of Love Himself': Reading C.S. Lewis's The Four Loves as Spiritual Practice"
    - By: Toby F. Coley
- "The Historical Perspective: Gleanings from C.S. Lewis's Personal Library"
    - By: K. Alan Snyder and Jamin Metcalf
- Remembrances
    - "Father Ian Boyd, CSB"
        - By: Father Daniel Callam, CSB, and Adam Schwartz
    - "Aidan Mackey"
        - By: Elise Peterson and Marjorie Lamp Mead
    - "Peter Schakel"
        - By: Elise Peterson and Charles Huttar
- Book Reviews
    - "Review of What Barfield Thought by Landon Loftin and Max Leyf"
        - By: David Willey
    - "Review of Freud's Last Session (Film), directed by Matt Brown"
        - By: Harry Lee Poe
    - "Review of the Wade Annotated Edition of The Man Born to Be King by Dorothy L. Sayers, annotations by Kathryn Wehr"
        - By: Carolyn Weber
    - "Review of Gifted Amateurs by David Bratman"
        - By: Giovanni Carmine Costabile
    - "Review of The Battle of Maldon Together with The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth by J.R.R. Tolkien, edited by Peter Grybauskas"
        - By: David Llewellyn Dodds
    - "Review of Tolkien's Faith by Holly Ordway"
        - By: Joel Scandrett
- Booknotes
    - "Something Has Gone Crack": New Perspectives on J.R.R. Tolkien in the Great War
        - By: Chloe DuBois
    - The Fall of Númenor
        - By: Joel D. Ruark<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
