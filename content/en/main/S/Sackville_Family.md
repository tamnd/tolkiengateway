The **Sackville Family** was a hobbit family of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

[Camellia Sackville](/wiki/Camellia_Sackville) is the only known member of the family;<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> apart from her almost nothing is known about them, except that they are described as being one of the Shire's wealthier families.

For more of them see [Sackville-Baggins Family](/wiki/Sackville-Baggins_Family)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name Sackville is intended to be of more aristocratic association than [Baggins](/wiki/Baggins). [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted that it was intentional that "bag" and "sack" have similar meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sackville is a real-life English surname.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth)* Sackville appears as a locality in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing). <span></span>
