The **Sackville Family** was a hobbit family of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

[Camellia Sackville](../C/Camellia_Sackville.md) is the only known member of the family;<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> apart from her almost nothing is known about them, except that they are described as being one of the Shire's wealthier families.

For more of them see [Sackville-Baggins Family](Sackville-Baggins_Family.md)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name Sackville is intended to be of more aristocratic association than [Baggins](../B/Baggins.md). [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that it was intentional that "bag" and "sack" have similar meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

Sackville is a real-life English surname.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](../T/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)* Sackville appears as a locality in the [Southfarthing](Southfarthing.md). <span></span>
