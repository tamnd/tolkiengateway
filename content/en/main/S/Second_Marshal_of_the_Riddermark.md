The **Second Marshal of the Riddermark** was the second such Marshal, the highest military rank among the [Riders of Rohan](../_/Éored.md), and title of the lieutenants of the [King of Rohan](../K/King_of_Rohan.md).

The Second Marshal’s duties, like those of the [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md), were not fixed, but assigned by the King as the times demanded.

## During the War of the Ring {#During_the_War_of_the_Ring}

Prior to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) was under [Saruman](Saruman.md)’s power and the influence of [Grima Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md), through whose voice the King’s orders were made. At this time, the Second Marshal was Théoden's son [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md), whose command was based at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md). Théodred, exceeding the authority of his post as Second Marshall, assumed general command and made orders concerning the [Muster of Edoras](../M/Muster_of_Edoras.md), normally under the command of the [First Marshal](../F/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md), the bulk of which he placed under the command of [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md).

Théodred's men were defeated by [Saruman](Saruman.md)'s forces at the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md), in which Théodred was slain. After that time, the traditional ranks were not strictly adhered to.  At the Full Muster ahead of the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), Elfhelm became a Marshall of the Riddermark, commanding the Muster of the East-mark, however the title of Second Marshall was not given. After the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) (formerly the [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md)), became King and revised the old tradition of the three marshals, giving Elfhelm the new title of [Marshal of the East-mark](../M/Marshal_of_the_East-mark.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><span></span>
