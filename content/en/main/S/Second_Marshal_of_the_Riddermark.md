The **Second Marshal of the Riddermark** was the second such Marshal, the highest military rank among the [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored"), and title of the lieutenants of the [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).

The Second Marshal’s duties, like those of the [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark), were not fixed, but assigned by the King as the times demanded.

## During the War of the Ring {#During_the_War_of_the_Ring}

Prior to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) was under [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)’s power and the influence of [Grima Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), through whose voice the King’s orders were made. At this time, the Second Marshal was Théoden's son [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), whose command was based at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). Théodred, exceeding the authority of his post as Second Marshall, assumed general command and made orders concerning the [Muster of Edoras](/wiki/Muster_of_Edoras), normally under the command of the [First Marshal](/wiki/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "First Marshal of the Riddermark"), the bulk of which he placed under the command of [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm).

Théodred's men were defeated by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s forces at the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), in which Théodred was slain. After that time, the traditional ranks were not strictly adhered to.  At the Full Muster ahead of the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), Elfhelm became a Marshall of the Riddermark, commanding the Muster of the East-mark, however the title of Second Marshall was not given. After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) (formerly the [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark)), became King and revised the old tradition of the three marshals, giving Elfhelm the new title of [Marshal of the East-mark](/wiki/Marshal_of_the_East-mark).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><span></span>
