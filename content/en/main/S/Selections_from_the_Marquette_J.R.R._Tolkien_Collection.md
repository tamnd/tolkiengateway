<i><b>Selections from the Marquette J.R.R. Tolkien Collection</b></i> is an exhibition catalogue from the [Marquette University Library](../M/Marquette_University.md). The catalogue includes three essays on works by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md):

- "Mr Bliss: Notes on the Manuscript and Story" (by [Jared C. Lobdell](../J/Jared_Lobdell.md))
- "The Boundaries of the Little Kingdom" (by [Taum Santoski](../T/Taum_Santoski.md))
- "A Tale that Grew in the Telling" (by [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md))

Of note is the inclusion of a sentence by Tolkien that was removed from the typescript of *[Farmer Giles of Ham](../F/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham.md)* prior to typesetting (p. 15).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
