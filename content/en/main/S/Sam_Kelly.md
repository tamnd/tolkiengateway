**Sam Kelly** ([April 17](/wiki/17_April "17 April") [1981](/wiki/1981) - )<sup id="cite_ref-Questionnaire_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Questionnaire-2">[2]</a></sup> is a Kiwi actor<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup> who portrayed [Silinde](/wiki/Silinde)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and an Elf Warrior of [the Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, an Elf Warrior during [the Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg") and an [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") in [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") Soldier in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and Elf at [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s Coronation in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

As of [February 17th](/wiki/17_February "17 February") [2004](/wiki/2004), Kelly was represented by Gail Cowan Management.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He considers himself good at table tennis.<sup id="cite_ref-Questionnaire_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Questionnaire-2">[2]</a></sup> Silinde has a brief speaking part in the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), which was cut.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
