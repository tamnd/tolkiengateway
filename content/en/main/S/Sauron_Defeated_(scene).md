**Sauron Defeated (scene)** is the fifty-sixth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the seventy-first scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Having reclaimed the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md), [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) cradles it with joy, seemingly unaware that he is falling into the fires of [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md). As he is consumed in the furnace, [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) hangs onto the precipice of the [Sammath Naur](Sammath_Naur.md). [Sam](Sam.md) struggles to reach Frodo, who looks down into the furnaces below. *"Don't you let go!"*, Sam cries, and with an effort, Frodo clasps his hand.

The ring sits on the surface of the furnace for a short time before being unmade in the fires. Immediately, [Sauron](Sauron.md) howls, and the armies of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) flee from battle at the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md). The tower of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) crumbles, and the [Eye of Sauron](../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md) shrinks into nothingness, before sending a shockwave across the land. A look of realisation dawns on the face of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) who gazes towards Mordor with tears in his eyes, and [Merry](../M/Merry.md) shouts Frodo's name in jubilation. The [Towers of the Teeth](../T/Towers_of_the_Teeth.md) collapse, and the land surrounding the [Host of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) disintegrates.

But as Mount Doom undergoes a huge eruption — spewing vast amounts of lava into the air and destroying the nearby [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) — the celebrations of the members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) are cut short as they fear Frodo's fate. Gandalf, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) look on in concern, and [Pippin](../P/Pippin.md) weeps for Frodo.
