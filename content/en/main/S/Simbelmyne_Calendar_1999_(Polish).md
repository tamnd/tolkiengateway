The **Simbelmyne Calendar 1999** was produced by the [Polish Tolkien Society](/wiki/Polish_Tolkien_Society) and features 13 black and white illustrations by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aleksandra_Jagiellowicz&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aleksandra Jagiellowicz (page does not exist)">Aleksandra Jagiellowicz</a>, [Małgorzata Pudlik](/wiki/Ma%C5%82gorzata_Pudlik), [Karolina Stopa](/wiki/Karolina_Stopa), [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Arkadiusz_Kubala&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arkadiusz Kubala (page does not exist)">Arkadiusz Kubala</a>, and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maciej_Wygnanski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maciej Wygnanski (page does not exist)">Maciej Wygnanski</a>. Gregorian calendar compared with the reckonings of [Imladris](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell "Reckoning of Rivendell"), [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). Moon phases are also displayed.

- Dimensions: 8 1/4 x 11 3/4 inches
- Stapled in upper left corner
