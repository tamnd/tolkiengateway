**Silmo**, an [Ainu](../A/Ainur.md) only appearing in the earliest versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), was appointed guardian of the tree [Silpion](../T/Telperion.md) — to be "ever mindful" of the watering of the tree. For this task, he was chosen by the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Lórien](../I/Irmo.md), who loved the youthful Silmo.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

He wanted to steer the [Moon](../M/Moon.md) but could not do it and so [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) appointed [Ilinsor](../I/Ilinsor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has suggested that the [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md) (a later creation) "perhaps owes something to the figure of Silmo".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Silmo* is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name. His name in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) was <i><b>Thilim</b></i> (containing the masculine ending *-im*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
