**Silmo**, an [Ainu](/wiki/Ainur "Ainur") only appearing in the earliest versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), was appointed guardian of the tree [Silpion](/wiki/Telperion "Telperion") — to be "ever mindful" of the watering of the tree. For this task, he was chosen by the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo"), who loved the youthful Silmo.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

He wanted to steer the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) but could not do it and so [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) appointed [Ilinsor](/wiki/Ilinsor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has suggested that the [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") [Tilion](/wiki/Tilion) (a later creation) "perhaps owes something to the figure of Silmo".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Silmo* is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name. His name in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) was <i><b>Thilim</b></i> (containing the masculine ending *-im*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
