**Sundóma** is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of the central radical vowel in a [sundo](/wiki/Sundo "Sundo"), an etymological root.

In the process of a language, the sundóma took part in the following phenomena:

<dl><dt>Intensification</dt>
<dd>prefixing to a sundo its sundóma, usually together with its lengthening either in the first or in the last position.</dd></dl>

<dl><dt><ul><li><a href="/wiki/LAK" title="LAK">LAK</a>- &gt; Á-LAK</li>
<li><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NAR (page does not exist)">NAR</a>- &gt; A-NÁR &gt; <a href="/wiki/Sun" title="Sun">Anar</a></li>
<li><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=THIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="THIL (page does not exist)">THIL</a>- &gt; I-THIL &gt; <a href="/wiki/Moon#Other_names" title="Moon">Isil</a></li>
<li><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MAN&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MAN (page does not exist)">MAN</a>- &gt; A-MAN &gt; <a href="/wiki/Aman" title="Aman">Aman</a></li></ul></dt></dl>

<dl><dt><i>A</i> infixion</dt>
<dd>adding an a before the sundóma I creating an <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ohlon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ohlon (page does not exist)">ohlon</a> (diphthong):</dd></dl>

<dl><dt><ul><li><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SLIW&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SLIW (page does not exist)">SLIW</a>- &gt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Slaiw%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Slaiwâ (page does not exist)">slaiwâ</a></i></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/THUS" title="THUS">THUS</a>- &gt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Thaus%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Thausâ (page does not exist)">thausâ</a></i></li></ul></dt></dl>

<dl><dt>I/Y infixion</dt>
<dd>putting an i/y after the sundóma creating an <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ohlon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ohlon (page does not exist)">ohlon</a> (less frequently occurred than the previous one):</dd></dl>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *sundóma* means "root-vowel",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> from *[sundo](/wiki/Sundo "Sundo")* "root" and *[óma](/wiki/%C3%93ma "Óma")* "voice".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
