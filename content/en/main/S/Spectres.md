**Spectres** were evil creatures only mentioned fleetingly in linguistic writings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Likely *spectre* was just another name for [undead](/wiki/Undead "Undead") beings such as [ghosts](/wiki/Ghosts "Ghosts") or [wraiths](/wiki/Wraiths "Wraiths").

Perhaps spectres were related to the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) concept *[auth](/wiki/Auth "Auth")* ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ausa](/wiki/Ausa "Ausa")*), referring to a spectral apparition.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Spectre* derives from Latin *spectrum*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Specters</i> are rare, incorporeal Undead monsters. They are included among the minions of the Balrog of Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
