The **Summerdays** were the extra days between the months of [Lithe](/wiki/Lithe) and [Mede](/wiki/Mede) in the [Bree Calendar](/wiki/Bree_Calendar), equivalent to the *[enderi](/wiki/Enderi "Enderi")*. In the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), they were called [Lithedays](/wiki/Lithedays).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
