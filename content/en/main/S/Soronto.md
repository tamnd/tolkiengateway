**Soronto** was a [Man](../M/Man.md) of the [royal lineage](../L/Line_of_Elros.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), the grandson of [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md) and son of [Ailinel](../A/Ailinel.md), a sister of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md). He lived during the later years of the first millennium of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> His father was [Orchaldor](../O/Orchaldor.md), a descendant of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before Tar-Aldarion changed the [Law of Succession in Númenor](../L/Law_of_Succession_in_Númenor.md) only the son of the King could be the King's heir or, if he had no son, then the nearest male kinsman of male descent from [Elros](../E/Elros.md) Tar-Minyatur would be King. This meant that [Malantur](../M/Malantur.md) (grandson of [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md)'s younger brother [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil).md>)) was in line for the Sceptre. After Tar-Aldarion changed the law, his daughter [Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md) could become the ruler of Númenor. But because of a clause in the changed law that said that the [Queen](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md) had to marry in order to keep her position and could not die childless, Soronto apparently remained hopeful of receiving the [Sceptre](Sceptre.md). However, despite [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md)'s reluctance to wed, she did eventually marry, to spite Soronto and kept him from taking her place. Once her son [Anárion](../T/Tar-Anárion.md) was born Soronto's hopes were completely dashed.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Soronto* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests the first element seems to be *[soron](Soron.md)* ("eagle").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
