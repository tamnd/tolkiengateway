**Soronto** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) of the [royal lineage](/wiki/Line_of_Elros "Line of Elros") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the grandson of [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) and son of [Ailinel](/wiki/Ailinel), a sister of [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion). He lived during the later years of the first millennium of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> His father was [Orchaldor](/wiki/Orchaldor), a descendant of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before Tar-Aldarion changed the [Law of Succession in Númenor](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor) only the son of the King could be the King's heir or, if he had no son, then the nearest male kinsman of male descent from [Elros](/wiki/Elros) Tar-Minyatur would be King. This meant that [Malantur](/wiki/Malantur) (grandson of [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil)'s younger brother [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(son_of_Tar-Amandil)> "Eärendur (son of Tar-Amandil)")) was in line for the Sceptre. After Tar-Aldarion changed the law, his daughter [Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB "Tar-Ancalimë") could become the ruler of Númenor. But because of a clause in the changed law that said that the [Queen](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queen of Númenor") had to marry in order to keep her position and could not die childless, Soronto apparently remained hopeful of receiving the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre). However, despite [Tar-Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB)'s reluctance to wed, she did eventually marry, to spite Soronto and kept him from taking her place. Once her son [Anárion](/wiki/Tar-An%C3%A1rion "Tar-Anárion") was born Soronto's hopes were completely dashed.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Soronto* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests the first element seems to be *[soron](/wiki/Soron "Soron")* ("eagle").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
