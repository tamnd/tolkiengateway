**STAK** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "split, insert".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> {#Derivatives[1]}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *stankā*, *staknā*
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *sanka* ("cleft, split")
    - [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *sthanka*
        - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[thanc](/wiki/Thanc "Thanc")*

- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *nestak-* ("insert, stick in")
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *[nestegi](/wiki/Nestegi "Nestegi")*
