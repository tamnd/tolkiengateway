**Second Age 820** (abbreviated to **S.A. 820**) is the 820th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) returns from the first [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) voyage with the ship *[Palarran](../P/Palarran.md)* and brings back a diamond as a gift for [Erendis](../E/Erendis.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
