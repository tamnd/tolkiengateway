**Sting** was an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) knife that was probably made in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). Its maker and original bearer are unknown, but it became the main weapon borne by [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md).

## History {#History}

During the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md), in [May](../M/May.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-1">[1]</a></sup> [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) happened upon a trio of [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) named <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Tom.md" title="Tom">Tom</a>, [Bert](../B/Bert.md), and [William](../W/William.md) who nearly ate them. Only the intervention of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) saved the company from such a horrible fate.<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the scuffle, William dropped the key to their [Trolls' cave](../T/Trolls'_cave~67749.md), which contained all manner of treasure plundered from the trolls' victims. [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) recovered the key, allowing the company to claim the treasure in its entirety, including several fine [Elven](../E/Elves.md) blades. [Glamdring](../G/Glamdring.md) went to Gandalf, while its mate [Orcrist](../O/Orcrist.md) was claimed by [Thorin Oakenshield](../T/Thorin.md). Bilbo, too, claimed a blade: merely a dagger by the standards of [Men](../M/Men.md) or its original Elvish makers, and but a tiny pocket-knife for the trolls whose hoard it was found in, but it made an excellent short sword for a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mutton_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mutton-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_(sketch).jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/f2/John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_%28sketch%29.jpg/150px-John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_%28sketch%29.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="158" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/f2/John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_%28sketch%29.jpg/225px-John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_%28sketch%29.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/f/f2/John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_%28sketch%29.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_Riddles_in_the_Dark_%28sketch%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="531" data-file-height="559"/></a><figcaption><i>Riddles in the Dark</i> (sketch) by <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

When Bilbo was captured by [goblins](../O/Orcs.md) while crossing the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), the goblins failed to notice the weapon simply because Bilbo wore it inside his breeches.<sup id="cite_ref-Riddles_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riddles-2">[2]</a></sup> When Bilbo was separated from the others during their escape, this allowed him to rely on the dagger's glowing blade to light his way, as well as use it to keep a hungry [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) at bay while he tried to find his way out of the labyrinthine tunnels in the roots of the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Riddles_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riddles-2">[2]</a></sup>

The weapon remained nameless until the company reached [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) and, deep in the darkness, Bilbo and his companions were beset by the forest's [Spiders](Spiders.md). Awakening to find himself half-cocooned, Bilbo used the dagger to free himself and slay his attacker, the first time he had used it in battle. The act prompted him to name the weapon with the words, "I will give you a name, and I will call you *Sting*." <sup id="cite_ref-Flies_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-3">[3]</a></sup> Bilbo used the newly-christened Sting to cut the spiders' silk to free his companions, and during the ensuing battle, utilized it in combination with the [magic ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) he had found in Gollum's cave, thrown rocks, and hurled insults to help the venom-weakened dwarves fight their way out of the spiders' nest.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-3">[3]</a></sup>

When Bilbo returned to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2942](../T/Third_Age_2942.md), he brought his little dagger with him. He displayed it above the mantleplace,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> where it most likely was when Gandalf and [Balin](../B/Balin.md) visited him seven years later in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2949](../T/Third_Age_2949.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> After the [Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) on [September 22](../_/22_September.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md), Bilbo took Sting with him when he left [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), and it resided with the aged hobbit in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) for the next 17 years. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), just before the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) set off from Rivendell, Bilbo gave Sting to his nephew [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), as well as his wondrous [Mithril-coat](../M/Mithril-coat.md), as a gift to protect him on the perilous journey to [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) that lay ahead.

As a consequence of his centuries of corruption under the One Ring's influence, Gollum feared weapons of Elvish make like Sting, which made the knife a factor in aiding Frodo in temporarily taming the wretched creature. It also helped Frodo and [Samwise Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md) escape from the lair of the terrible [Shelob](Shelob.md) in [Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md), and during the ensuing battle, even after paralyzing Frodo, the massive spider met its match in a determined Samwise Gamgee and learned how the Elvish weapon earned its name. Still armed with Sting, Sam stormed the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](../T/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol.md) in order to rescue Frodo, though he found little in the way of a fight, as the tower had seen a battle between its own forces over Frodo's [Mithril-coat](../M/Mithril-coat.md).

After the defeat of Sauron at the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), when both Bilbo and Frodo sailed across the sea to the [Undying Lands](../U/Undying_Lands.md), Sting remained with Sam. He may have taken it with him when he, at last, followed them as was his right as the last remaining [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearers.md), or it may have remained as an heirloom of the [Gardner Family](../G/Gardner_Family~66367.md).

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Sting had the (apparently) magic ability to detect the presence of goblins in its vicinity. When Orcs were nearby, it glowed blue, as Bilbo noticed when alone in the dark just prior to meeting Gollum.<sup id="cite_ref-Riddles_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riddles-2">[2]</a></sup> This ability was used several times by Frodo and the Fellowship such as when the group encountered Orcs in the mines of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) and by [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) upon the Fellowship's arrival at [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Sting also performed better than ordinary weapons in combat and against odd materials; it cut through the webs of giant spiders easily, whereas the [blade](../D/Daggers_of_Westernesse.md) that Sam brought from the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) failed to cut through Shelob's webs.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> During the [Battle of the Chamber of Mazarbul](../B/Battle_of_the_Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md), Boromir's sword glanced off of the hide of a troll, but Sting was able to deal a painful wound.

<b><a href="../_/1977.md" title="1977">1977</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)">The Hobbit</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Sting has a distinctive shape, with a vaguely leaf-shaped blade, round, ball-shaped pommel, featureless black hilt, and a unique guard sporting a ring in the front that the wielder&#39;s finger slips into when grasping the hilt. This version of Sting glows bright white, not blue as in the books and most other adaptations. Notably, following the Rankin/Bass adaptations&#39; pattern of not explicitly showing violence, Sting is not shown stabbing or slashing its victims; instead, a strange fly-eye like effect is shown when Bilbo slays the giant spiders in Mirkwood, an effect that is oddly repeated when he knocks another spider off of a branch with a rock.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/1980.md" title="1980">1980</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)">The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Sting&#39;s appearance is broadly similar to that in Rankin/Bass&#39; earlier film, with some alterations; notably, the shape of the blade is changed from vaguely leaf-shaped to stout and straight-bladed, and the hilt is no longer a featureless black, but is now adorned with intricate designs.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>-<a href="../_/2003.md" title="2003">3</a>: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Sting is leaf-shaped. Upon its crossguard are engraved the <a href="Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">Sindarin</a> words:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><span class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Tengwar_Sting_engraved.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/b9/Tengwar_Sting_engraved.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="434" height="39" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="434" data-file-height="39"/></a></span>
<dl><dd><i>Maegnas aen estar nin dagnir in yngyl im</i></dd></dl></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Which is translated &#34;Maegnas is my name, I am the spider&#39;s bane&#34; (Maegnas is <a class="mw-redirect" href="../N/Neo-Sindarin.md" title="Neo-Sindarin">neo-Sindarin</a> for &#34;Sting&#34;)</dd></dl>

Since the sword was given a name by Bilbo and the inscription mentions Bilbo's adventure, it can be supposed that the Elves engraved the letters on the ancient sword while Bilbo was in Rivendell. This is supported by [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) in which Sting appears plain, without any writing.

<b><a href="../_/2003.md" title="2003">2003</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)">The Hobbit (video game)</a></i></b><i> </i>

<dl><dd>Unlike the book, Sting is first seen in the Dream World stage, a sort of tutorial wherein Bilbo, after fainting during the Unexpected Party, dreams he is at the Battle of Five Armies and must fight his way through a few waves of goblins before being overwhelmed by <a href="../B/Bolg.md" title="Bolg">Bolg</a> and his guard, startling awake, and continuing the story.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> After Bilbo awakens, Sting is not seen again until the end of the Troll-Hole stage, which takes place after the encounter with Tom, Bill, and Bert like in the book. Unlike in the book, where Bilbo does not name Sting until he is in <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, he gives it a name almost immediately.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>By contrast, the GBA version stays faithful to the book, where Bilbo does not name Sting until Mirkwood. Before then, the weapon is simply called &#34;Small Dagger&#34; in the inventory.</dd></dl>
