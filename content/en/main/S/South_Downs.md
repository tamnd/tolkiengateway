The **South Downs** was a range of hills that ran through the central regions of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). They were a dreary, empty land with scarce vegetation.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Walking along the [East-West Road](/wiki/East-West_Road) from [Bree](/wiki/Bree) to the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills), one could see the Downs to the south. The [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway), running through the [Andrath](/wiki/Andrath), separated the South Downs from the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>South Downs is an area in the <a href="/wiki/Cardolan" title="Cardolan">Cardolan</a> region. It is dotted with ruins of grand ancient keeps while the only current inhabitants are scattered farmers.</dd></dl>
