<i><b>Seventeen</b></i> **January 1967**, is an issue of the American magazine *Seventeen*. The article "J.R.R. Tolkien Talks about the Discovery of Middle-earth, the Origins of Elvish" by [Richard Plotz](/wiki/Richard_Plotz) includes extracts (pp. 92–93, 118) from an [interview with Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
