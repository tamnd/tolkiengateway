**Sala Baker** is a New Zealand-born stuntman, whose first role as an actor was [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*. He also played [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers")* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*; in the former, he is billed as "Man Flesh Uruk". Baker also appeared in [Ringers: Lord of the Fans](/wiki/Ringers:_Lord_of_the_Fans), as himself.
