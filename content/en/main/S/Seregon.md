**Seregon** was a plant that grew in rocky soil and produced blood-red flowers, which gave it its name. *Seregon* was noted as the one plant that mantled the otherwise barren head of [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md) was the captain of the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md), an outlaw band, that was seeking a new lair when they captured [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) the [Petty-dwarf](../P/Petty-dwarves.md). In return for his life Mîm led the [Men](../M/Men.md) to his home, which was carved into the summit of [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md). As they approached the secret hideout from the north, sunlight from the west illuminated the hill and its rich, red flowers, causing the outlaw [Andróg](../A/Andróg.md) to exclaim, "See! There is blood on the hill-top".<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

Mîm later betrayed Túrin and his followers to a party of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). The Orcs attacked the hideout and killed all but Túrin and his elf-friend [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md). The blood of the rest of the outlaws flowed out upon the *seregon*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *seregon* means "blood of stone" in [Sindarin](Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> (from *[sereg](Sereg.md)* = "blood" and *[gond](../G/Gond.md)* = "stone").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
