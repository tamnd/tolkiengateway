**Seregon** was a plant that grew in rocky soil and produced blood-red flowers, which gave it its name. *Seregon* was noted as the one plant that mantled the otherwise barren head of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh).<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") was the captain of the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith), an outlaw band, that was seeking a new lair when they captured [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) the [Petty-dwarf](/wiki/Petty-dwarves "Petty-dwarves"). In return for his life Mîm led the [Men](/wiki/Men) to his home, which was carved into the summit of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh). As they approached the secret hideout from the north, sunlight from the west illuminated the hill and its rich, red flowers, causing the outlaw [Andróg](/wiki/Andr%C3%B3g) to exclaim, "See! There is blood on the hill-top".<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

Mîm later betrayed Túrin and his followers to a party of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). The Orcs attacked the hideout and killed all but Túrin and his elf-friend [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg). The blood of the rest of the outlaws flowed out upon the *seregon*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *seregon* means "blood of stone" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> (from *[sereg](/wiki/Sereg "Sereg")* = "blood" and *[gond](/wiki/Gond "Gond")* = "stone").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
