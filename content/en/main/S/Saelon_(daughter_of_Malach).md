## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name *Saelon* is an emendation from an earlier name *Sael..th* (which wasn't completely legible).<sup id="cite_ref-Wise_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wise-2">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) speculates that in this characters are seen the roots of a story which would eventually become *[Athrabeth Finrod ah Andreth](/wiki/Athrabeth_Finrod_ah_Andreth)* - especially since *Saelon* was an earlier name of [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth) in the drafts of that story.<sup id="cite_ref-Wise_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wise-2">[2]</a></sup>
