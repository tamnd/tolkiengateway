**Second Age 3316** (abbreviated to **S.A. 3316**) is the 3316th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Amandil](../A/Amandil.md), [Lord of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md), breaks the [Ban of the Valar](../B/Ban_of_the_Valar.md) by sailing to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) to plead for the [Valar](../V/Valar.md)'s help.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
