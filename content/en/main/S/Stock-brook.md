**The Stock-brook** or **Stockbrook** was a brook of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), and a minor tributary of the River [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine). It rose in the forested hills above [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End), and from there flowed north-eastwards out into the fields of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish). It passed through the southern end of the village of [Stock](/wiki/Stock), from which it took its name, before emptying into the Brandywine a few miles north of [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [25 September](/wiki/25_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") crossed the Stock-brook on their journey to [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot)'s house.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggested the name represents a possible "[Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English")" *Se Stocbroc*.

As it gets its name from the village of Stock, its meaning is "the brook running through Stock".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
