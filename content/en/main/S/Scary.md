**Scary** was a small settlement that lay in the hilly region of caves and rock holes that formed the northern parts of the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md), which also gave its name to the surrounding [stony hills](../H/Hills_of_Scary.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

From among the rocky lands around Scary much of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)'s stone was quarried.

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Scary quarries were used by [Sharkey's Men](../R/Ruffians.md) to store their loot, and after Sharkey was defeated, much of this was recovered by the [Shire-hobbits](Shire-hobbits.md). It was around these hills that [Fredegar Bolger](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md) organized his band of rebels against the Ruffians, until they were caught.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) notes that the name was meaningless in [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). However he notes that since it was in a rocky region and of a stone-quarry, it may be supposed to contain English dialectal *scar*, "rocky cliff".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Based on the above, [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) has suggested that Scary represents a speculative [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) \**Skerig* "precipitous (place)". Salo also noted that the form of this dialectal word, specifically the existence of sk-, reveals a [Norse](../N/Norse.md) origin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md) suggests an etymology from Old English *scearu* "a boundary", marking the Shire's northern boundary; the retaining (or reversal) of /sk/ might reflect the peculiarity of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_English" title="wikipedia:Northern English">Northern English</a> (in such names as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skipton" title="wikipedia:Skipton">Skipton</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarborough,_North_Yorkshire" title="wikipedia:Scarborough, North Yorkshire">Scarborough</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
