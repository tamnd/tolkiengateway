<i><b>sereg</b></i> is a noun meaning "blood" in [Sindarin](Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*sereg* derives from the [root](Sundocarme.md) [SEREK](SEREK.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[serke](Serke.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
