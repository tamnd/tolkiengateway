**Southern Star** was a variety of pipe-weed grown in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and apparently originating in the district around [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom). The 'star' of its name presumably refers to the star-shaped flowers of the [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") plant.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the second (out of seven) drafts of [Prologue](/wiki/Prologue_Concerning_Hobbits,_and_other_matters "Prologue Concerning Hobbits, and other matters"), "Southern Star" was originally named "Hornpipe Twist".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
