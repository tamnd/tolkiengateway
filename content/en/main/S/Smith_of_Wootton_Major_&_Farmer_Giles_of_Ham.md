<i><b>Smith of Wootton Major &amp; Farmer Giles of Ham</b></i> is an anthology, first published in [1969](/wiki/1969) by [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books), collecting the books *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)* and *[Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham)* under the same covers.
