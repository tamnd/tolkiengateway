**Dr. Stephen Yandell** is Assistant Professor of English at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_University_(Cincinnati)" title="wikipedia:Xavier University (Cincinnati)">Xavier University</a> in Cincinnati, Ohio. He has written several articles on the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings) [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), and gives an undergraduate class of Tolkien.
