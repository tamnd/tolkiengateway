**Sancho Proudfoot** (b. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2990](/wiki/Third_Age_2990 "Third Age 2990")<sup id="cite_ref-AppC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppC-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

The son of [Olo Proudfoot](/wiki/Olo_Proudfoot), at the age of eleven, Sancho was present at the [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") of his uncle [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins"), along with his father and grandfather [Odo](/wiki/Odo_Proudfoot "Odo Proudfoot").<sup id="cite_ref-AppC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppC-1">[1]</a></sup>

The day after, on [23 September](/wiki/23_September), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"), he went to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) to seek for a treasure he heard rumours about. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") could just stop him, but not after Sancho had already started excavating the pantry, where he thought he heard an echo.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Sancho is thrown out by Frodo's (at that stage referred to as Bingo) lawyers, [Iago Grubb](/wiki/Iago_Grubb) and [Folco Burrowes](/wiki/Folco_Burrowes) eject him when he tries to loot the pantry.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Jim Allan recognizes the name as one of the short meaningless [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) names<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in which case it probably represents genuine Hobbitish *Sancha*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Sancho" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Sancho</a> is a real Spanish name which derives from Latin *sanctius* "holy". This makes Sancho one of the very few Spanish names appearing in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>A young Hobbit named Sancho Proudfoot - or, the &#34;dread highwayman Sancho Proudfoot &#34;, as he prefers to call himself - causes quite some mischief in Hobbiton and <a href="/wiki/Bywater" title="Bywater">Bywater</a>. Several of the early missions the player - as Frodo - has to do involve solving problems caused by Sancho, such as retrieving a metal pin of the <a href="/wiki/Old_Mill" title="Old Mill">Hobbiton Mill</a> and <a href="/wiki/Old_Noakes" title="Old Noakes">Old Noakes</a>&#39; medicinal herbs.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
