The **South Undeep** was one of two westward bends of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) located between [The Wold](/wiki/The_Wold) and the [Brown Lands](/wiki/Brown_Lands) (the other being the [North Undeep](/wiki/North_Undeep)). The South Undeep was the smaller of the two bends.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both of the Undeeps had many shallows and wide shoals that afforded an easy crossing for armies,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which occurred a number of times during the history of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The South Undeep is located in the northern Wold, connecting two riverbanks through a small island in the middle of the river. Due to the unstable political situation in Rohan, a party of <a href="/wiki/Easterlings" title="Easterlings">Easterlings</a> has been able to take the crossing and establish a fortified camp on the western bank, from which they attack and burn the Rohirrim town of <i>Langhold</i>.</dd></dl>
