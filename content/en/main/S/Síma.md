**síma** or **isima** means "mind, imagination, conception" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Perhaps a possible [PQ](../P/PQ.md) [Root](Sundocarmë.md) \*I-SIM or \*SÍ.

It is not clear whether it contains the ending *[-ma](../_/-ma.md)* (*sí-ma*) or if *m* is a part of the root (*sím-a*).

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- **símaryassen** "in their conception"
