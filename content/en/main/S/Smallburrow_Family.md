The **Smallburrow** family was a family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) from the [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) area. They were amongst the lower classes of the society, and were acquainted with families like the [Gamgees](/wiki/Gamgee_Family "Gamgee Family") and [Cottons](/wiki/Cotton_Family "Cotton Family").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
