The **Smallburrow** family was a family of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) from the [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) area. They were amongst the lower classes of the society, and were acquainted with families like the [Gamgees](../G/Gamgee_Family~66365.md) and [Cottons](../C/Cotton_Family~66362.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
