The **Sea of Windless Storm** was a great body of water that bordered the land of [Faery](/wiki/Faery) in the story *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*. In the wanderings of [Smith](/wiki/Smith_Smithson "Smith Smithson") about the land he once came upon the desolate shore of this sea and beheld the great blue waves that rolled out of [Unlight](/wiki/Unlight) to the strand. The sea bore white ships that returned from battles on the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Dark_Marches&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dark Marches (page does not exist)">Dark Marches</a>. Smith saw one ship brought in on a silent wave whose [elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Mariners&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mariners (page does not exist)">mariners</a> sang a song of triumph which shook Smith's heart with fear. After this incident Smith never returned to this shore.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
