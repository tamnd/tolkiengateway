**Silver** is a highly reflective grey or white metal referenced often by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") in his [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

One of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), was silver in colour if not in material.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> *[Mithril](/wiki/Mithril)* is said to highly resemble silver.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari) prized silver above gold, and their skill as silversmiths was esteemed even by the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Several examples of objects made of silver are the crown of [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the silver harp of the [House of the Harp](/wiki/House_of_the_Harp) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), the Tree of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), according to [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) the [habergeon](/wiki/Uncommon_words "Uncommon words") of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) (which, if true, suggests that silver was stronger in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) than in the real world), and one of the gates of Gondolin. As well, it was often referenced in titles and names (for example [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), *silver-tree*, [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), *silver-hand*, and [Celebrindal](/wiki/Celebrindal), *silver-foot*).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition)" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>In the book, silver was said to have been so plentiful in <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a> that even Dwarves, who like to hoard it, had more than enough to export it to the Elves. While the Dwarves used it widely, for chalices, plates, cups, mugs, horns, and decorative inlays, the Elves tended to use it primarily for common purposes like utensils, cups, and plates.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Silver ore can be mined by journeyman prospectors in <a href="/wiki/Blue_Mountains" title="Blue Mountains">Ered Luin</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Ancient silver ore can be mined by master prospectors in the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>, <a href="/wiki/Forochel" title="Forochel">Forochel</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
