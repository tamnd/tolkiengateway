<i><b>SEVEN 30</b></i> is the thirtieth issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2013](../_/2013.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup> This issue commemorates the 50th anniversary of [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s death.<sup id="cite_ref-vii_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Early Prose Joy: A Brief Introduction"
    - By: Andrew Lazo
- "Early Prose Joy: C.S. Lewis's Early Draft of an Autobiographical Manuscript"
    - By: C.S. Lewis
- "A Grief Observed: A Study of C.S. Lewis's Thoughts on the Subject"
    - By: Walter Hooper
- "C.S. Lewis's and the BBC's Brains Trust: A Study in Resiliency"
    - By: Bruce R. Johnson
- "'Shifting Change': Liminality and Gender in Till We Have Faces"
    - By: Suzanne Rosenthal Shumway
- Review Essay:
    - "The Hopeless Hope of G.K. Chesterton"
        - By: Adam Schwartz<sup id="cite_ref-vii_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-3">[3]</a></sup>
