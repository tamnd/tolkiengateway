**Squirrels** were nut-eating rodents often found in trees.

[Númenor](/wiki/Elenna "Elenna") in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) had many squirrels in its woods, mostly red. Like all the animals in the isle, they were friendly and easy to tame. [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") women were fond of them, and sometimes squirrels lived nearby the houses to visit them.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), the squirrels were black in colour.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During their journey through Mirkwood, [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and his company killed a black squirrel for food, but found it to be inedible.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Nature of the black squirrels {#Nature_of_the_black_squirrels}

[John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) has suggested that the black squirrels are "*dark in coloration*" (like the other animals the company meet in Mirkwood), "*no doubt from protective camouflage in a dimly lit environment*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Henry Gee, discussing the topic of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanism" title="wikipedia:Melanism">melanism</a> in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), accepts the complexity of the subject (as black creatures often can be said to having been created or used by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)), but concludes that dark creatures *per se* are not "evil" in the works of Tolkien: "*black creatures, whether swans, butterflies or squirrels, are remarkable only inasmuch as there are light-colored forms with which they can be contrasted.*"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1995: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Black squirrels are described and given role-playing statistics. The creature is portrayed as an ally of the Wood-elves and the Woodmen.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

**2004:** <i><b>The Hall of Fire</b></i>

<dl><dd>Black squirrels are described and given statistics in the RPG magazine <i>The Hall of Fire</i> (intended for use with <a href="/wiki/Decipher" title="Decipher">Decipher</a>&#39;s <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>). Black squirrels are conceived as having been created by the &#39;dark magics&#39; of, and act as spy for, the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Necromancer" title="Necromancer">Necromancer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Squirrels are found throughout Middle-earth. Lore-masters can also have a non-combat squirrel pet.</dd></dl>
