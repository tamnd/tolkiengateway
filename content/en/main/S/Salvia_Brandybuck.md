**Salvia Bolger, née Brandybuck**, was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Salvia was the third child and first daughter of [Sadoc Brandybuck](Sadoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Gundabald Bolger](../G/Gundabald_Bolger.md) and had one son, [Theobald Bolger](../T/Theobald_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/salvia#Latin" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Salvia</a> is the latin name for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvia" title="wikipedia:Salvia">Sage</a>.
