**Salvia Bolger, née Brandybuck**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Salvia was the third child and first daughter of [Sadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Sadoc_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Gundabald Bolger](/wiki/Gundabald_Bolger) and had one son, [Theobald Bolger](/wiki/Theobald_Bolger).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/salvia#Latin" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Salvia</a> is the latin name for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salvia" title="wikipedia:Salvia">Sage</a>.
