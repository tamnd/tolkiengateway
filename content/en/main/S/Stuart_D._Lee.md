**Stuart D. Lee** is a member of the English Faculty and Merton College, and Deputy <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_information_officer" title="wikipedia:Chief information officer">CIO</a> at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). His research and teaching focus on Old English, World War One literature, and the fiction of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
