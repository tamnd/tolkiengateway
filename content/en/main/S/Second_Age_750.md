**Second Age 750** (abbreviated to **S.A. 750**) is the 750th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) founded by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) arrive at [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) in [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") is founded.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) is taken as a lord of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Construction of [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") founds the [Guild of Venturers](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers).<sup id="cite_ref-Aldarion_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldarion-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* it is stated that "*[Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) therefore went eastwards, about the year 700 of the Second Age, and established the (primarily but by no means solely) [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") realm of Eregion.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup> This contradicts the information published in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* which gives the founding of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in the year 750.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible to reconcile the texts if 700 is seen as the year of their moving eastwards with 750 as the year of the foundation of Eregion; the canonicity of this, however, is unknown. <span></span>
