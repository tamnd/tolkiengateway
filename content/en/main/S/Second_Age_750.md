**Second Age 750** (abbreviated to **S.A. 750**) is the 750th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) founded by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) arrive at [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>
- The [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) in [Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md) is founded.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) is taken as a lord of the [Silvan Elves](Silvan_Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- Construction of [Ost-in-Edhil](../O/Ost-in-Edhil.md) begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) founds the [Guild of Venturers](../G/Guild_of_Venturers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aldarion_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldarion-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* it is stated that "*[Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) therefore went eastwards, about the year 700 of the Second Age, and established the (primarily but by no means solely) [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) realm of Eregion.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Galadriel_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galadriel-2">[2]</a></sup> This contradicts the information published in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* which gives the founding of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) in the year 750.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB1-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible to reconcile the texts if 700 is seen as the year of their moving eastwards with 750 as the year of the foundation of Eregion; the canonicity of this, however, is unknown. <span></span>
