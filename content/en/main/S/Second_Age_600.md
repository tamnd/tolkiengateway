**Second Age 600** (abbreviated to **S.A. 600**) is the 600th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Spring {#Spring}

- The first [ships](Ships.md) of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) begin to appear on the coasts of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md): [Entulessë](../E/Entulessë.md), a ship headed by [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md), [Captain of the King's Ships](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md), arrives in [Mithlond](../G/Grey_Havens.md) where the Númenóreans are welcomed by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-B1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Island_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Island-2">[2]</a></sup>
- A meeting takes place at [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md) between the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) and the [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md) of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md); the two groups recognise their common heritage.<sup id="cite_ref-Aldarion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldarion-3">[3]</a></sup> It is noted that it was a millennium or more since the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) had separated from their kin in Eriador to reach [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
