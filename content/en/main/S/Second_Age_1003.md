**Second Age 1003** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1003**) is the 1003rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Tar-Anárion](../T/Tar-Anárion.md), son of [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Hallacar](../H/Hallacar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
