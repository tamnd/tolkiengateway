**Simonne Rosalie Thérèse Odile d'Ardenne** ([1899](/wiki/1899)-[1986](/wiki/1986); *S.R.T.O.* or sometimes *S.T.R.O.*) was a Belgian medieval philologist who studied [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) during the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and became Professor of Comparative Grammar at the University of Liège in 1938.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

D'Ardenne became a close friend of the Tolkien family, and J.R.R. visited her in Belgium in the 1950s.
