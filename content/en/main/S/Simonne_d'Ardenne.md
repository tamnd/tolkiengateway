**Simonne Rosalie Thérèse Odile d'Ardenne** ([1899](../_/1899.md)-[1986](../_/1986.md); *S.R.T.O.* or sometimes *S.T.R.O.*) was a Belgian medieval philologist who studied [Middle English](../M/Middle_English.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) during the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and became Professor of Comparative Grammar at the University of Liège in 1938.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

D'Ardenne became a close friend of the Tolkien family, and J.R.R. visited her in Belgium in the 1950s.
