The **Star of the Dúnedain** was a gift that was given by [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) to [Samwise Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [15](../F/Fourth_Age_15.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is very probable that the Star of the Dúnedain was the same as the silver brooch that was shaped like a rayed star that was worn on their cloaks on their left shoulder by the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) who came to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) to aid their [Chieftain](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Dúnedain.md) [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the silver star that was worn by Aragorn on his cloak when he served [Steward](Steward_of_Gondor.md) [Ecthelion II](../E/Ecthelion_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
