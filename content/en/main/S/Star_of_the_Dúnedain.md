The **Star of the Dúnedain** was a gift that was given by [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") to [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is very probable that the Star of the Dúnedain was the same as the silver brooch that was shaped like a rayed star that was worn on their cloaks on their left shoulder by the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North) who came to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) to aid their [Chieftain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftain of the Dúnedain") [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the silver star that was worn by Aragorn on his cloak when he served [Steward](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor "Steward of Gondor") [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
