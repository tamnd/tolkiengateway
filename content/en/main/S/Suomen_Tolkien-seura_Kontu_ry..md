**Suomen Tolkien-seura Kontu ry.**, the **Finnish Tolkien Society Kontu** ("Kontu" is the Shire in Finnish) is the Finnish Tolkien society, founded in 1992.

The society publishes *Hobittilan Sanomat* ("Hobbiton News") and *Legolas* (both with an English summary), which are well recognized [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) publications in Finland.
