**Stonewain Valley** was a long narrow straight valley in the eastern [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), deep within the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest). Its eastern end opened just to the north-west of the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor) that encircled [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

A road called the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gondrant&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gondrant (page does not exist)">Gondrant</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> four horses wide, had been made in ancient days by the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor),<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-3">[3]</a></sup> as a route from the quarries beneath [Min-Rimmon](/wiki/Min-Rimmon) to [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor).<sup id="cite_ref-Parma_17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Parma_17-4">[4]</a></sup>

By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), it was overgrown and all but forgotten. However, on [13 March](/wiki/13_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) met with [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n) in the Drúadan Forest<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the headman of the [Woses](/wiki/Woses) revealed the existence of the old road. During the next day the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) used the road in Stonewain Valley to avoid the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) blocking the main road to come to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name was given because of the "*wains (sleds or drays) passed to and fro from the stone-quarries*" along the long, narrow defile.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) it was called <i><b>Nand&#39; Ondolunkava</b></i> or <i><b>Ondolunkanan</b>(<b>do</b>)</i>, in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) <i><b>Nan Gondresgion</b></i> and in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) <i><b>Stānwægna Dæl</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Parma_17_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Parma_17-4">[4]</a></sup> Another Sindarin name, a translation of "Stonewain Valley", was <i><b>Imrath Gondraich</b></i>, where *imrath* meant "a long narrow valley with a road or watercourse running through it lengthwise."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Another Sindarin translation was <i><b>Tum Gondregain</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
