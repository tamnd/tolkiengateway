<b><i>Silver Leaves</i> 1</b> ("Inaugural Issue") is the first issue of *[Silver Leaves](Silver_Leaves.md)*, the journal of [The White Tree Fund](../T/The_White_Tree_Fund.md), published [2007](../_/2007.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md): "Tolkien Connections"
- Robert Hierholzer: "Post Traumatic Stress Disorder in a Hobbit?"
- Steven Duec\]: "Middle-earth Crossword"
- Kathryn E. Darden: "A Man Like You"
- [Dimitra Fimi](../D/Dimitra_Fimi.md): "A Note on Turin and Oedipus"
- Kerri-Anne Cundill: "Hope"
- Kerri-Anne Cundill: "Candle"
- N.C. Graham: "Firelace"
- Charity Yang and Laura Hwee: "Wellinghall Smial: Tolkien Day Report"
- Gerda Marz and [Jo-Anna Dueck](../J/Jo-Anna_Dueck.md): "Tolkien Lecture Series 2007'
- Sunny Timmons and [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md): "Remembering Professor Daniel Timmons"
- Rebecca Kirkland and Bruce Hopkins: "The Journey Continues for Reclaiming Our Youth"
- John Adcox: "Can fantasy be myth? Mythopoeia and The Lord of the Rings"
- L. Lara Sookoo: "Defending Middle - earth From Charges of Racism"
- [Janet Brennan Croft](../J/Janet_Brennan_Croft.md): "How I Learned More About Hobbits"
- [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md): "Some thoughts on reading The Lord of the Rings aloud"
- Amy L. Timco: "Weavers, Witches, and Warriors: The Women of The Lord of the Rings"
- Kerri-Anne Cundill: "When Fear is Near"
- Steven Dueck: "Phrase to Phrase"

- Patricia Scovil: "The Lord of the Rings, The Musical: A Review'
