**Second Age 729** (abbreviated to **S.A. 729**) is the 729th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Almiel](../A/Almiel.md), youngest daughter and child of [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-1">[1]</a></sup>
