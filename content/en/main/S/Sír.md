<i><b>sír</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> (also rendered <i><b>sīr</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "river".

The dual form <i><b>siryat</b></i> appears in the sentence *[imbe](/wiki/Imb%C3%AB "Imbë") siryat* ("between two rivers"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> yielding an unattested singular form \**sirya* ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)/[Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") <i><b>sīre</b></i> ("river") derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [SIR](/wiki/SIR) ("flow").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
