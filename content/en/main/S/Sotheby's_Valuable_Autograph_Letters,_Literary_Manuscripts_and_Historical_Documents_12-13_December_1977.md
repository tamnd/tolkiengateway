<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s Valuable Autograph Letters, Literary Manuscripts and Historical Documents 12–13 December 1977</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](/wiki/Sotheby%27s) (London). It includes several letters by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien):

- Lot 363 (p. 181): [Letter 122](/wiki/Letter_122).
- Lot 364 (p. 181): [Letter 144](/wiki/Letter_144).
- Lot 365 (p. 182): [Letter 154](/wiki/Letter_154). The final page of the letter is reproduced.
- Lot 366 (p. 182): [Letter 164](/wiki/Letter_164).
- Lot 367 (p. 183): [Letter 176](/wiki/Letter_176).
- Lot 368 (p. 183): [Letter 220](/wiki/Letter_220).
- Lot 369 (p. 183): [Letter 220a](/wiki/Letter_220a).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
