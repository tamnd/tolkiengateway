**South Ithilien** was the southern lands of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), from [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin) in the north to the River [Poros](/wiki/Poros) in the south.

The land was a strip of land between [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to the west and [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) to the east. It was traversed by a portion of the [Harad Road](/wiki/Harad_Road) from the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros) to the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads); there was another road to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). Its most notable feature was the hills of [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>South Ithilien was an area in the Eastern <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> region. It consisted of <a href="/wiki/Emyn_Arnen" title="Emyn Arnen">Emyn Arnen</a> and surrounding lands, directly across the river from <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Pelennor_Fields" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a>. It was connected to Lossarnach in the south-west by crossing the river and to <a href="/wiki/Osgiliath" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a> in the north-west. A cliff in the south prevented access to Ithilien south of <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien" title="Rangers of Ithilien">Rangers of Ithilien</a> in the area had their main camp in the ruins of &#34;Bâr Húrin&#34;, the abandoned estate of the <a href="/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin" title="House of Húrin">House of the Stewards</a>, and a smaller camp in the north nearer to Osgiliath.</dd></dl>
