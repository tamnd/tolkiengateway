**Second Age 829** (abbreviated to **S.A. 829**) is the 829th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) returns from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) with nine ships loaded with fine timber from the forests of the coasts of Middle-earth, after having built two additional ships at the haven of [Vinyalondë](../V/Vinyalondë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-1">[1]</a></sup>
- Aldarion departs again towards Middle-earth with a fleet of three ships for a fourteen-year long voyage. As [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md) had ordered the shipyards of [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md) to be closed and had forbidden the felling of trees for shipbuilding, Aldarion's ships needed refitment because they had been damaged by rough winds.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-1">[1]</a></sup>
