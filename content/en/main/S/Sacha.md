**Sacha** was the name of a "fire-[fay](../F/Fay.md)", according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Say_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Say-1">[1]</a></sup>

While nothing else is known about the character specifically, in an addendum to the text <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Creatures_of_the_Earth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Creatures of the Earth (page does not exist)">The Creatures of the Earth</a></i>, the fays/spirits associated with the greater [Valar](../V/Valar.md) are broken down into four elemental groups:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- Air (consisting of the [manir](../M/Manir.md) and the [súruli](Súruli.md))
- Earth (consisting of the [tavari](../T/Tavari.md), the [nermir](../N/Nermir.md), etc.)
- Water (consisting of the [wingildi](../W/Wingildi.md), the [oarni](../O/Oarni.md), etc.)
- **Fire** - for which no group of spirits is given in the text

It is possible that Sacha belonged to some category of fays/spirits associated with the above mentioned **Fire** group.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Sacha* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md). According to [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md), it is probably a derivative of the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SAH(Y)A&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SAH(Y)A (page does not exist)">SAH(Y)A</a> ("be hot").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate is <i><b>Sáya</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Say_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Say-1">[1]</a></sup>
