**Sharku** was the leader of the [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") [Warg](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs")[-riders](/wiki/Wolf-riders "Wolf-riders") of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). Sharku was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [Jed Brophy](/wiki/Jed_Brophy).

## History {#History}

Sharku was the veteran chief of a group of marauding Warg-riders who ranged deep into the realms of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), sowing panic and fear among [Saruman's](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") enemies.

By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), he had thrown his support behind the corrupted [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards"), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). He was tasked by him with ambushing the column of [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) women and children retreating from [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) to the safety of [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and his guard, with the help of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), fended off the attack. However, during the battle, Aragorn got tangled up in the harness of Sharku's Warg and both the beast and the ranger fell over a cliff. Sharku, mortally wounded, survived just long enough to gloat about Aragorn's apparent death to Legolas and Gimli.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[Sharkû](/wiki/Shark%C3%BB)* is the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) word meaning "Old Man",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> most likely referring to [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey), the name Saruman used when he controlled [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
