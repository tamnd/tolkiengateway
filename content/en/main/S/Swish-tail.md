**Swish-tail** was a [pony](/wiki/Ponies "Ponies") belonging to [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck).

## History {#History}

Swish-tail was one of five ponies that Merry had ready for the Hobbits' journey at [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the home of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil), the ponies became friends with [Fatty Lumpkin](/wiki/Fatty_Lumpkin), a pony of Tom's. At the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) they became so frightened that they ran off to find Fatty. Tom called them all back with the names he had given them: [Sharp-ears](/wiki/Sharp-ears), [Bumpkin](/wiki/Bumpkin), [Wise-nose](/wiki/Wise-nose), Swish-tail, and [White-socks](/wiki/White-socks). He returned them to the hobbits, along with the baggage they had been carrying.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During their stay at the *[Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony)* the Hobbits' ponies were let out to delay their departure from [Bree](/wiki/Bree). They ran out in the Downs to find Fatty. They stayed with Tom for a while, until he had heard news of the events at Bree. He sent the ponies to [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur), and they stayed there working for Barliman, where they were well cared for by [Bob](/wiki/Bob).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
