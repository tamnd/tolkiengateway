**Sharon L. Baird** (born [August 16](/wiki/16_August "16 August"), [1943](/wiki/1943)) is an American actress. She originally shot to fame in [1955](/wiki/1955) as a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mouseketeer" title="wikipedia:Mouseketeer">Mouseketeer</a>, and remained one until adulthood. In [1978](/wiki/1978), she participated in [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi)'s [film of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"), as the rotoscoping actor of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).
