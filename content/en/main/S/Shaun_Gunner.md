**Shaun Gunner** is the Chief Executive Officer of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society).<sup id="cite_ref-CEO_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CEO-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Twitter_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Twitter-5">[5]</a></sup> Gunner previously served for over 12 years as the Chair of the Society, making him the longest-serving Chair in the Society's history.

Gunner has also served as the Society's publicity officer, was the Deputy Chair of [The Return of the Ring: Celebrating Tolkien in 2012](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Ring "The Return of the Ring"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and was responsible for the [Tolkien 2019](/wiki/Tolkien_2019) event. He has also contributed to the journals *[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)")*, *[Hither Shore](</wiki/Hither_Shore_(journal)> "Hither Shore (journal)")*, and *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*.

He is an administrator ([User:Mith](/wiki/User:Mith)) of the [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) website, and an advisor and assistant to [Emil Johansson](/wiki/Emil_Johansson)'s [The Lord of the Rings Project](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Project).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Gunner is also a regular commentator on Tolkien and adaptations in the media, and was a guest co-host of [The Prancing Pony Podcast](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony_Podcast) in the 2022-3 season.<sup id="cite_ref-PPP_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PPP-8">[8]</a></sup>

Gunner is a politics graduate and lives in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rustington" title="wikipedia:Rustington">Rustington</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sussex" title="wikipedia:Sussex">Sussex</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Twitter_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Twitter-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> He is an elected councillor for his area - serving as Leader of the Council between 2021 and 2023<sup id="cite_ref-ADC_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ADC-10">[10]</a></sup> - and formerly chaired the mental health charity <a class="external text" href="https://mkcharity.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Mankind</a>.
