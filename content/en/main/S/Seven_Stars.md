**Seven Stars** was a common symbol throughout [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

It refers to the constellation that turns through the northern skies every night of the year. To the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) this was the [Valacirca](/wiki/Valacirca) that signalled the downfall of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but the [Men](/wiki/Men) of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Northern_lands&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Northern lands (page does not exist)">Northern lands</a> called it simply '[The Wain](/wiki/Valacirca "Valacirca")'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Seven Stars are today known as the Plough, or the Big Dipper.

The [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) also displayed seven stars with a crown, probably representing [Durin's Crown](/wiki/Durin%27s_Crown).

The Seven Stars were also a symbol of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil): The [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) escaped the [Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth) on [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)'s nine ships.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Of those nine, each of the seven which carried one of the [Palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri) had a single five-rayed star on its sail.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> When [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil) was reforged, in [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril)'s blade were traced seven stars, set between a crescent Moon and a rayed Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In the [Rhyme of Lore](/wiki/Rhymes_of_Lore "Rhymes of Lore") remembered by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), they are mentioned along with the seven *[Palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Crown of Gondor](/wiki/Crown_of_Gondor) bore seven jewels of adamant set in the circlet.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
