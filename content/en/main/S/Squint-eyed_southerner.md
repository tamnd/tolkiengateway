The **squint-eyed southerner** was a nameless brigand driven from [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md), rumored to have a strain of [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)-blood within his veins. He initially acted as one of the corrupted [Wizard](../W/Wizards.md) [Saruman](Saruman.md)'s most trusted agents and spies, before being compelled into the service of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) as an informant.

## History {#History}

Hailing from Dunland, the squint-eyed southerner was banished by his fellow [hillmen](../D/Dunlendings.md) due to a rumor that he had Orc-blood coursing through his veins (implying he may have been one of the [Half-orcs](../H/Half-orcs.md) or [Goblin-men](../G/Goblin-men.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-UT10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT10-2">[2]</a></sup>

Having entered the employ of the Wizard Saruman, the southerner was at one point stationed on the borders of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) to negotiate the purchase of "[leaf](../P/Pipe-weed.md)" and other supplies which were stored in [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) in preparation for the [war](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). The squint-eyed southerner had been instructed by his master to infiltrate the Shire and learn of any [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) who had departed recently in hopes of finding the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md). The southerner was well supplied with maps, lists of names, and notes concerning the Shire.

In September of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), the Dunlending was traveling back to the Shire when the black-robed [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) came upon him at the [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) crossing. When questioned, the southerner told the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) everything, betraying the White Wizard; this proved Saruman was withholding information from his own master, [Sauron](Sauron.md). The Lord of [Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) was made aware of the name [Baggins](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md) and [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) was singled out for immediate visit and inquiry.

The Witch-king put the [Shadow](Shadow.md) of Fear on the southerner and sent him to [Bree](../B/Bree.md) as a spy, warning him he now answered to the [Red Eye](../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md) and not the [White Hand](../W/White_Hand.md). The Dunlending met up with [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md) and at the [Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md) on [September 29th](September_29.md), the two of them saw [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) vanish into thin air. After reporting the information to the Nazgûl, the Nine Black Riders came to attack that night.<sup id="cite_ref-UT10_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT10-2">[2]</a></sup>

Aragorn kept the Hobbits safe through the night. The next morning, the squint-eyed southerner was spotted inside Bill Ferny's house when the company set out to [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FRI11_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRI11-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nothing else is recorded of the squint-eyed southerner. However in [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), a large number of [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) occupied the Shire and oppressed the Hobbits.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible the southerner was among them as many of Saruman's agents there resembled him. The Men were driven out of the Shire in the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md) on [3 November](../_/3_November.md). He either met his death in the battle, or survived and fled away.

## Description {#Description}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Just what my father meant to convey by the &#39;squint-eyed Southerner&#39; at Bree I&#39;m not sure. I don&#39;t think that he can possibly have meant that the man had &#39;slit-eyes&#39; (goblin-like). He may have meant that he actually had a squint (optical disorder), but that seems unnecessarily particular. So the likeliest meaning, I think, is that the man didn&#39;t look straight, but obliquely, watchfully, sideways, suggesting craftiness and crookedness.
</p>

</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i> card identifies one of the Men at <i><a href="../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md" title="The Prancing Pony">The Prancing Pony</a></i> as the squint-eyed southerner<b>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></b></dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2011.md" title="2011">2011</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A character called <b>The Southerner</b> (loosely based on the novel character) attempts to talk Harley Bramble into joining with a gang of outlaws. After Bramble is convinced not to by <a href="../E/Eradan_(video_game_character).md" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="../A/Andriel.md" title="Andriel">Andriel</a> and <a href="../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a>, the Southerner threatens that his &#34;<a href="../M/Mordor.md" title="Mordor">powerful</a> <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">friends</a>&#34; will trouble the trio. If the player returns to Bree after the mission to <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Mount_Gundabad.md" title="Mount Gundabad">Mount Gundabad</a>, it will be revealed that Ruffians have attacked the town but have been defeated, with Bramble claiming he himself made sure the Southerner will trouble Bree-folk no more.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2013.md" title="2013">2013</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a>:</i></b><i></i>

<dl><dd>A character credited as <b>Squint</b> appears in the prologue in Bree. Along with another character credited as &#34;Bill Ferny Sr.&#34; (loosely based on Bill Ferny), Squint has seemingly tracked <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> to the Prancing Pony (presumably to collect on the bounty put out on Thorin&#39;s head). The two Men are preparing to confront Thorin when <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> goes over and sits and starts talking with the <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarf</a>. When it becomes apparent the <a href="../W/Wizards.md" title="Wizards">Wizard</a> is not going anywhere, Bill and Squint get up and leave the inn.</dd></dl>
