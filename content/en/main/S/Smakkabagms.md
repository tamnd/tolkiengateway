<i><b>Smakkabagms</b></i> is an unpublished poem that was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) possibly around [1924](/wiki/1924).<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-1">[1]</a></sup> While the poem is not known to have been published anywhere, or whether any of its manuscripts survive today, it may have been among the poems that were rejected for inclusion in the collection of poems, *[Songs for the Philologists](/wiki/Songs_for_the_Philologists)*, in [1936](/wiki/1936). [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) claimed in their commentary to the poem *[From One to Five](/wiki/From_One_to_Five)* that they "have not seen" this poem.<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Smakkabagms* is a [Gothic](/wiki/Gothic) word meaning "fig tree".<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-2">[2]</a></sup>
