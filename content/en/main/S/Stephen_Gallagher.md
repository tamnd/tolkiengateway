**Stephen Gallagher** is a New Zealand composer and music editor. He worked as a music editor for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). For *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*, Gallagher also composed music for [Blunt the Knives](/wiki/Blunt_the_Knives) sung by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") and The Torture Song sung by the [Goblin King](/wiki/Great_Goblin "Great Goblin").<sup id="cite_ref-SGAbout_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SGAbout-1">[1]</a></sup> He composed the score for the animated film *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
