<i><b>Symboliek van Tolkien&#39;s In de ban van de ring</b></i> (*Symbolism of Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings*) is a Dutch-language book by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mellie_Uyldert" title="wikipedia:Mellie Uyldert">Mellie Uyldert</a>, an esotericist practitioner of alternative medicine. The book was first published in [1974](/wiki/1974)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>, and was last reprinted in [2002](/wiki/2002).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also released in German in [2001](/wiki/2001) as *Die Entdeckung von Mittelerde: Die Symbolik von Tolkiens »Der Herr der Ringe«*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Content {#Content}

Uydlert drew parallels between [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s conquest and various modern processes she thought negatively of, such as taxation, fluoridation of water, internationalization, digital banking, and television viewing.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Back cover {#Back_cover}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This booklet is meant for those, who feel or perceive more in Tolkien&#39;s work than just a story. For those who can think in backgrounds, symbols and analogies. The author sees in Tolkien&#39;s book a highly current message for us in our time. Because of the enthusiastic response to speeches and courses by the author on Tolkien&#39;s book <i>The Lord of the Rings</i>, this booklet was published.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
