**STEL** is a [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "remain firm". A derivative of STEL is said to be contained as the "\[l\]atter element" in the names *[Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion#Etymology)* and *Aegthelion*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
