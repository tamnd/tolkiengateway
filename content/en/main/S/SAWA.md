**SAWA** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "disgusting, foul, vile",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> or "bad, unhealthy, ill, wretched".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17b_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17b-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[saura](/wiki/Saura "Saura")* ("foul, vile"); *sau-* (prefix found in *saucare* "doing or making things very badly"); *söa* ("filth")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[saur](/wiki/Saur "Saur")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
