**Soni Alcorn-Hender**, also known by the pseudonym **Bohemian Weasel**, is an English artist currently living in Portugal who has created artwork for numerous [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien)-related publications and products.

## History {#History}

Alcorn-Hender grew up in southern England and received a two-year diploma from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Portsmouth#Portsmouth_Municipal_Technical_Institute_and_the_College_of_Art" title="wikipedia:University of Portsmouth">Portsmouth College of Art &amp; Design</a> before moving to Scotland to complete a degree in illustration at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_School_of_Art" title="wikipedia:Glasgow School of Art">Glasgow School of Art</a>. She later moved to Portugal to be with family and continues to pursue her artistic endeavors there.

The influence of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) on Alcorn-Hender's art began at the age of four, when her parents began to read the author's works to her.<sup id="cite_ref-BW-About_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BW-About-1">[1]</a></sup> In addition to Tolkien, she also cites <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illustration#The_.22Golden_Age.22" title="wikipedia:Illustration">Golden Age</a> illustrators such as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Rackham" title="wikipedia:Arthur Rackham">Arthur Rackham</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Bauer_(illustrator)" title="wikipedia:John Bauer (illustrator)">John Bauer</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Dulac" title="wikipedia:Edmund Dulac">Edmund Dulac</a>, and contemporary illustrator <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshitaka_Amano" title="wikipedia:Yoshitaka Amano">Yoshitaka Amano</a> as influences.

Following the recommendation of fellow artist [Grant Gould](/wiki/Grant_Gould), in November [2007](/wiki/2007) she was commissioned by Topps to produce cards for the forthcoming [The Lord of the Rings Masterpieces](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Masterpieces) II series.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In [2013](/wiki/2013), Alcorn-Hender also worked on the licensed cards of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* for Cryptozoic Entertainment.

Alcorn-Hender's artwork has been featured in numerous issues of [*Mallorn*](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)") and [*Amon Hen*](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)"), both of which are published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). She has also exhibited at many [Tolkien exhibitions](/wiki/Tolkien_exhibitions) including several [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) conventions, "Evil in the Shining Light" in [2015](/wiki/2015), "Illuminating Tolkien" in [2016](/wiki/2016), and "Far from the Shire Art, Art in the Mill" in [2017](/wiki/2017). In [2018](/wiki/2018), her work was chosen for the Tolkien Society annual Christmas card, which was shipped out with copies of *Amon Hen*. Her work has also been displayed in the [*Beyond Bree*](</wiki/Beyond_Bree_(calendar)> "Beyond Bree (calendar)") calendar, having appeared in the [2021](/wiki/2021), [2022](/wiki/2022), [2023](/wiki/2023), and [2026](/wiki/2026) editions.<sup id="cite_ref-BW-About_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BW-About-1">[1]</a></sup>
