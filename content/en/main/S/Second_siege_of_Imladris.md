The **second siege of Imladris** took place sometime between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1356](/wiki/Third_Age_1356 "Third Age 1356") and [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) in the War between [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). At some point the siege was broken, and Lord [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) was able to help subdue the forces of Angmar for a time by bringing reinforcements from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
