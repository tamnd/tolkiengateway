**Siriondil** means "Devoted to [Sirion](Sirion.md)" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from *[Sirion](Sirion.md)* and *[-ndil](../_/-ndil.md)*, "friend, lover, devoted to"), and can refer to:

- [Siriondil](<Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor).md>) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [570](../T/Third_Age_570.md) - [830](../T/Third_Age_830.md)), the eleventh [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).
- [Siriondil](<Siriondil_(son_of_Calimmacil).md>) (died before [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1945](../T/Third_Age_1945.md)), son of [Calimmacil](../C/Calimmacil.md) and the father of [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md).
