**Siriondil** means "Devoted to [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion)" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[Sirion](/wiki/Sirion)* and *[-ndil](/wiki/-ndil)*, "friend, lover, devoted to"), and can refer to:

- [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Siriondil (King of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [570](/wiki/Third_Age_570 "Third Age 570") - [830](/wiki/Third_Age_830 "Third Age 830")), the eleventh [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
- [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(son_of_Calimmacil)> "Siriondil (son of Calimmacil)") (died before [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1945](/wiki/Third_Age_1945 "Third Age 1945")), son of [Calimmacil](/wiki/Calimmacil) and the father of [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II).
