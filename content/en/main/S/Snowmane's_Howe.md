**Snowmane's Howe** was the place on the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) where the horse of [King Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden"), [Snowmane](/wiki/Snowmane), fell and was subsequently buried by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim). Upon that hollow, a stone was erected and there the grass grew the most green and long ever after.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
