<i><b>Saturday Evening Post</b></i> **(2 July 1966)** contains the article "[The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)" (pp. 90–94) by Henry Resnik (illustrated by Paula Powers). The article includes extracts from an interview with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) conducted on 2\[?\] March 1966.
