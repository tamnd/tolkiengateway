<i><b>Sandastan</b></i> was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for a defensive formation used by the soldiers of the [Númenórean Realms](../N/Númenórean_Realms.md), better known by the [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) <i><b><a href="../T/Thangail.md" title="Thangail">thangail</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The final element in *sandastan* is derived from the stem *stama-* meaning bar or exclude, so the literal translation is probably 'shield-barrier'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
