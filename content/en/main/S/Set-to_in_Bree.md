There was a minor **set-to**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> **in [Bree](../B/Bree.md)** between the [Bree-landers](../B/Bree-landers.md) and the [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md), that happened early in [T.A. 3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

During the autumn of [T.A, 3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) more and more Men came to Bree up the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) from the south. While some of them were refugees, many were bad men.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> Before the end of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) the Bree-landers expelled the new-comers from Bree. Two treacherous Bree-landers, [Harry Goatleaf](../H/Harry_Goatleaf.md) and [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md), joined with the ruffians and went off with them.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

Early in the [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) after a heavy snowfall these expelled Men attacked Bree at night. [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md) suspected that [Harry Goatleaf](../H/Harry_Goatleaf.md) and [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md) might let the ruffians in.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

In a resulting set-to Bree-landers suffered some causalities. Three of the [Men of Bree](../M/Men_of_Bree.md) ([Mat Heathertoes](../M/Mat_Heathertoes.md), [Rowlie Appledore](../R/Rowlie_Appledore.md) and little [Tom Pickthorn](../T/Tom_Pickthorn.md) from over the [Hill](../B/Bree-hill.md)), and two of the [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) ([Willie Banks](../W/Willie_Banks.md) from up-away and a member of [Underhill Family](../U/Underhill_Family.md) from [Staddle](Staddle.md)) were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> However, the the ruffians were defeated.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

After this skirmish some of these ruffians retreated to the woods beyond [Archet](../A/Archet.md), where they lived as robbers,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> while other, like Bill Ferny, joined their comrades in the [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Despite their victory, the Bree-landers continued to be troubled by the ruffians, since it wasn't safe on the [road](../E/East_Road.md), nobody could travel far and people locked their homes early. The Bree-landers had to keep watchers all round the fence and many men on the gates at night to protect themselves from further attacks.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup>
