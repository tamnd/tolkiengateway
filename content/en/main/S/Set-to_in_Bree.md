There was a minor **set-to**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> **in [Bree](/wiki/Bree)** between the [Bree-landers](/wiki/Bree-landers) and the [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians"), that happened early in [T.A. 3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

During the autumn of [T.A, 3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") more and more Men came to Bree up the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) from the south. While some of them were refugees, many were bad men.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> Before the end of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") the Bree-landers expelled the new-comers from Bree. Two treacherous Bree-landers, [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf) and [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny), joined with the ruffians and went off with them.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

Early in the [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") after a heavy snowfall these expelled Men attacked Bree at night. [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) suspected that [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf) and [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) might let the ruffians in.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

In a resulting set-to Bree-landers suffered some causalities. Three of the [Men of Bree](/wiki/Men_of_Bree) ([Mat Heathertoes](/wiki/Mat_Heathertoes), [Rowlie Appledore](/wiki/Rowlie_Appledore) and little [Tom Pickthorn](/wiki/Tom_Pickthorn) from over the [Hill](/wiki/Bree-hill "Bree-hill")), and two of the [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) ([Willie Banks](/wiki/Willie_Banks) from up-away and a member of [Underhill Family](/wiki/Underhill_Family) from [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle)) were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> However, the the ruffians were defeated.

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

After this skirmish some of these ruffians retreated to the woods beyond [Archet](/wiki/Archet), where they lived as robbers,<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup> while other, like Bill Ferny, joined their comrades in the [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Despite their victory, the Bree-landers continued to be troubled by the ruffians, since it wasn't safe on the [road](/wiki/East_Road "East Road"), nobody could travel far and people locked their homes early. The Bree-landers had to keep watchers all round the fence and many men on the gates at night to protect themselves from further attacks.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">[3]</a></sup>
