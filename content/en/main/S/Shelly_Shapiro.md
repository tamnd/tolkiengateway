**Shelly Shapiro** is an editor of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Del_Rey&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Del Rey (page does not exist)">Del Rey</a> Books specializing in the Star Wars line.

In the past she worked as an illustrator and cartographer for works of fiction. In [1988](/wiki/1988) she reproduced some maps of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth): *[Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](/wiki/Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor)*, *[The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](/wiki/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age)* and *[Númenórë‎](</wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3r%C3%AB_(map)> "Númenórë (map)")*.
