**Sûza** is the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) word for "province, sphere of occupation, division of a realm",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> visible in **Sûza-t**, "[The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Andreas Möhn](../A/Andreas_Möhn.md) suggested that perhaps the second element *-za* was related to the [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Z%C3%A2yan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Zâyan (page does not exist)">zâyan</a></i> ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

> *"\[...\] actually the [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) and mod. Icelandic* <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/s%C3%BDsla" title="wikipedia:sýsla">sýsla</a> *(Swed.* <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/syssla#Swedish" title="wiktionary:syssla">syssla</a>*, Dan.* <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/syssel#Danish" title="wiktionary:syssel">syssel</a>*, now obsolete in sense* amt*, but occurring in place-names) was in mind when I said that the real untranslated name of the Shire was* Súza *\[...\] hence it was also said \[...\] that it was a 'district of well-ordered* business'*."*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
