The **Slag-hills**,<sup id="cite_ref-Hills_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hills-1">[1]</a></sup> also called the **slag-mounds**<sup id="cite_ref-Mounds_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mounds-2">[2]</a></sup> and the **slag-heaps of Mordor**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> were endless rows of high mounds of debris, composed of crushed and powdered rock and fire-blasted and poison-stained earth,<sup id="cite_ref-Marshes_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marshes-4">[4]</a></sup> that had been piled over many years by toiling [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) in the [Desolation of the Morannon](../D/Desolation_of_the_Morannon.md) in front of the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Opens_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Opens-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Disaster of the Morannon {#Disaster_of_the_Morannon}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md), King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) positioned himself with his Guard and banner on a low natural hill when the main force of his army, which had just travelled north from [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md), was suddenly [attacked](../D/Disaster_of_the_Morannon.md). War-chariots of the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) and an unexpectedly large force of cavalry from the east had approached on a road that went eastward from north of the Black Gate to a point north of the [Ered Lithui](../E/Ered_Lithui.md) and north of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md). King Ondoher and his son [Artamir](../A/Artamir.md), and almost all of his Guard, were killed in battle.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Note_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During unknown later years Orcs piled up blasted stone and earth on the hill on which King Ondoher had died.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Frodo approaches the Desolation of the Morannon {#Frodo_approaches_the_Desolation_of_the_Morannon}

On [4 March](../_/4_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Mounds_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mounds-2">[2]</a></sup> [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) came to the Slag-hills and hid in a hollow.<sup id="cite_ref-Marshes_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marshes-4">[4]</a></sup> The next day Frodo prepared to enter [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) by the Black Gate but was persuaded by Gollum to head south to seek a more [secret way](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md) through the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Battle of the Morannon {#Battle_of_the_Morannon}

Approaching the Black Gate on [25 March](../_/25_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Hills_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hills-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) arrayed his meager forces on two of the Slag-hills behind a "moat" of reeking mud and foul-smelling pools. It was upon these low hills that the [Army of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) was surrounded and attacked by the forces of [Sauron](Sauron.md) in the [Battle of the Morannon](../B/Battle_of_the_Morannon.md), and it was here that they witnessed the downfall of Sauron when Frodo completed his [quest](../Q/Quest_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Opens_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Opens-5">[5]</a></sup> It was assumed by historians that King Aragorn placed his troops on the same hill on which King Ondoher had made his stand 1,075 years before.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-7">[7]</a></sup>
