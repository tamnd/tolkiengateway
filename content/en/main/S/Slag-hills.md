The **Slag-hills**,<sup id="cite_ref-Hills_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hills-1">[1]</a></sup> also called the **slag-mounds**<sup id="cite_ref-Mounds_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mounds-2">[2]</a></sup> and the **slag-heaps of Mordor**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> were endless rows of high mounds of debris, composed of crushed and powdered rock and fire-blasted and poison-stained earth,<sup id="cite_ref-Marshes_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marshes-4">[4]</a></sup> that had been piled over many years by toiling [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) in the [Desolation of the Morannon](/wiki/Desolation_of_the_Morannon) in front of the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-Opens_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Opens-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Disaster of the Morannon {#Disaster_of_the_Morannon}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"), King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) positioned himself with his Guard and banner on a low natural hill when the main force of his army, which had just travelled north from [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), was suddenly [attacked](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Morannon "Disaster of the Morannon"). War-chariots of the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) and an unexpectedly large force of cavalry from the east had approached on a road that went eastward from north of the Black Gate to a point north of the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui) and north of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr). King Ondoher and his son [Artamir](/wiki/Artamir), and almost all of his Guard, were killed in battle.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Note_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During unknown later years Orcs piled up blasted stone and earth on the hill on which King Ondoher had died.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Frodo approaches the Desolation of the Morannon {#Frodo_approaches_the_Desolation_of_the_Morannon}

On [4 March](/wiki/4_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Mounds_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mounds-2">[2]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) came to the Slag-hills and hid in a hollow.<sup id="cite_ref-Marshes_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marshes-4">[4]</a></sup> The next day Frodo prepared to enter [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) by the Black Gate but was persuaded by Gollum to head south to seek a more [secret way](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol "Cirith Ungol") through the mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Battle of the Morannon {#Battle_of_the_Morannon}

Approaching the Black Gate on [25 March](/wiki/25_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Hills_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hills-1">[1]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) arrayed his meager forces on two of the Slag-hills behind a "moat" of reeking mud and foul-smelling pools. It was upon these low hills that the [Army of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West) was surrounded and attacked by the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon), and it was here that they witnessed the downfall of Sauron when Frodo completed his [quest](/wiki/Quest_of_the_Ring "Quest of the Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-Opens_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Opens-5">[5]</a></sup> It was assumed by historians that King Aragorn placed his troops on the same hill on which King Ondoher had made his stand 1,075 years before.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-7">[7]</a></sup>
