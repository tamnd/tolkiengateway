<i><b>SEVEN 38</b></i> is the thirty-eighth volume of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2022](../_/2022.md). Note that the issue's year is "2021/2022," for an issue was not published in [2021](../_/2021.md).<sup id="cite_ref-vii_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-1">[1]</a></sup>
