The **Southfarthing** was the southmost and warmest [farthing](../F/Farthings.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

Its northernmost point was the [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md), its eastern border was the [Brandywine](../B/Baranduin.md) River, and much of its border with the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) ran along the River [Shirebourn](Shirebourn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Other features include:

- Hills: Part of the [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md)
- Swamps: [Marshes in the south](../M/Marshes_in_the_south.md)
- Roads: An unnamed road crossed the Southfarthing to [Sarn Ford](Sarn_Ford.md).
- Towns: Longbottom, [Pincup](../P/Pincup.md).

## History {#History}

About [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1630](../T/Third_Age_1630.md) many [Stoors](Stoors.md) from [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) entered and settled in the Southfarthing,<sup id="cite_ref-third_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-2">[2]</a></sup> and thereafter [Stoorish](Stoors.md) characteristics and appearance were common among the Hobbits in that region.

[Tobold Hornblower](../T/Tobold_Hornblower.md) of Longbottom first grew pipe-weed in the Southfarthing about the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2670](../T/Third_Age_2670.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Saruman](Saruman.md), having received reports on [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)’s movements and noting his interest in the Shire, began keeping [agents](../R/Ruffians.md) in the Southfarthing in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2953](../T/Third_Age_2953.md).<sup id="cite_ref-third_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-2">[2]</a></sup> By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) there were rumors of trouble with the "[Big People](../B/Big_People.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Southfarthing was where [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) began his rise to power during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), based on the substantial property in the region that he inherited from his father.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Presumably it was he who began exporting pipe-weed to Saruman.

## Culture {#Culture}

This was the region where most of the Shire's [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) production was concentrated, in warm sheltered places like [Longbottom](../L/Longbottom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Another noted product of the Southfarthing was the strong red wine called [Old Winyards](../O/Old_Winyards.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](../T/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)* (page 71), [Hardbottle](../H/Hardbottle.md) is shown in the Southfarthing rather than the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-8">[8]</a></sup> and Sackville, shown in the Southfarthing, is entirely invented (compare the [Sackville Family](Sackville_Family.md)).
