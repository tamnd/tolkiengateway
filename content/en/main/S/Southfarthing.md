The **Southfarthing** was the southmost and warmest [farthing](/wiki/Farthings "Farthings") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## Geography {#Geography}

Its northernmost point was the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone), its eastern border was the [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") River, and much of its border with the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) ran along the River [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Other features include:

- Hills: Part of the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country)
- Swamps: [Marshes in the south](/wiki/Marshes_in_the_south)
- Roads: An unnamed road crossed the Southfarthing to [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford).
- Towns: Longbottom, [Pincup](/wiki/Pincup).

## History {#History}

About [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1630](/wiki/Third_Age_1630 "Third Age 1630") many [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) from [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) entered and settled in the Southfarthing,<sup id="cite_ref-third_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-2">[2]</a></sup> and thereafter [Stoorish](/wiki/Stoors "Stoors") characteristics and appearance were common among the Hobbits in that region.

[Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower) of Longbottom first grew pipe-weed in the Southfarthing about the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2670](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), having received reports on [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)’s movements and noting his interest in the Shire, began keeping [agents](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") in the Southfarthing in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2953](/wiki/Third_Age_2953 "Third Age 2953").<sup id="cite_ref-third_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-2">[2]</a></sup> By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") there were rumors of trouble with the "[Big People](/wiki/Big_People)".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Southfarthing was where [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) began his rise to power during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), based on the substantial property in the region that he inherited from his father.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Presumably it was he who began exporting pipe-weed to Saruman.

## Culture {#Culture}

This was the region where most of the Shire's [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") production was concentrated, in warm sheltered places like [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Another noted product of the Southfarthing was the strong red wine called [Old Winyards](/wiki/Old_Winyards).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Atlas_of_Middle-earth)* (page 71), [Hardbottle](/wiki/Hardbottle) is shown in the Southfarthing rather than the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing)<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-8">[8]</a></sup> and Sackville, shown in the Southfarthing, is entirely invented (compare the [Sackville Family](/wiki/Sackville_Family)).
