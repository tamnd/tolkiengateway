**Siriondil** was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") nobleman.

## History {#History}

Siriondil was the son of [Calimmacil](/wiki/Calimmacil), and a nephew of King [Narmacil II](/wiki/Narmacil_II) (d. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856")) and as such, a distant cousin of King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of Gondor. He was probably named after his ancestor [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Siriondil (King of Gondor)"), a former King of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1883](/wiki/Third_Age_1883 "Third Age 1883") he had a son, [Earnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

After [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) and his sons were slain in a battle against the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944"), the [next year](/wiki/Third_Age_1945 "Third Age 1945") Eärnil was elected the thirty-second King of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
