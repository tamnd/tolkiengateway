The **Stiffbeards** were one of the seven houses of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) that dwelt in [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn). They were originally paired with the [Ironfists](/wiki/Ironfists).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The locations of the four [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") clans, including the Stiffbeards, who lived in the [East](/wiki/East), are unknown. The distance between their mansions in the East and the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), specifically [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad), was said to be as great or greater than that of Gundabad's distance from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) in the West.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

It is possible that the Stiffbeards were among the "*Houses of other Fathers*" who sent troops to help [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) in the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Late in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), when war and terror grew in the [East](/wiki/East) itself, considerable numbers of Stiffbeards and dwarves of the Eastern clans left their ancient homelands. They sought refuge in Middle-earth's western lands, where some of them may have met [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The unnamed Dwarven tribes were given invented names in an early supplement.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> While these were published before the publication of Tolkien&#39;s late note on the name of the Dwarven houses of the East, it has been suggested that the Stiffbeards could correspond to <b>Bávor&#39;s tribe</b> (as portrayed in <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">MERP</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The House corresponding to the Stiffbeards is called <b>Thulin&#39;s Folk</b>, said to inhabit the uttermost North of Middle-earth (dwelling so far north that &#34;<i>their beards grow stiff from cold</i>&#34;, according to Khazâd legend).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Dwalin" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> mentioned Thorin, the heir of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin</a> having a meeting with his <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">kin</a>, with &#34;envoys from the Seven Kingdoms&#34;, the same number as the Houses of the Dwarves, and also <a href="/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills" title="Dwarves of the Iron Hills">Dwarves of the Iron Hills</a>. It&#39;s unspecified on why Thorin had a meeting with all houses of the Dwarves, however it&#39;s likely for the purpose of declaring war on <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> and to reclaim his homeland.</dd></dl>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/eb/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/eb/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/eb/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Stiffbeards.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="1080"/></a><figcaption>The &#34;Kámbrada&#34; in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></figcaption></figure>

<b>2017: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The House corresponding to the Stiffbeards is called the <b>Kámbrada</b>, also known as <b>Drása&#39;s Folk</b>. Their &#34;outer&#34; names are mostly based on old Baltic, with some old Slavonic and Norse. Their bannes show a white beard under a hammer and chisel. They refused <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>&#39;s demand to return their <a href="/wiki/Seven_Rings" title="Seven Rings">Dwarf-ring</a>, &#34;Sindya&#34;, also known as &#34;Manthríf&#34;, and so Sauron reclaimed the Ring by force and enslaved many of Drása&#39;s Folk. At some point in history, the Kámbrada warred with the <a href="/wiki/Ironfists#Portrayal_in_adaptations" title="Ironfists">Zhélruka</a>, but during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs" title="War of the Dwarves and Orcs">War of the Dwarves and Orcs</a> their King &#34;Usk&#34; befriended his Zhélruka counterpart &#34;King Vélat&#34;. Usk fell in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a> and was succeeded by his son &#34;Ugún&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Those of the Kámbrada enslaved in <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> lost much of their culture and history and came to be known as &#34;Stout-axes&#34;, or &#34;Khirvísa&#34; in their language. After Sauron&#39;s defeat, the Stout-axes were discovered by <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> and at his behest many came to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Erebor" title="Erebor">Erebor</a> as refugees. Stout-axes were introduced as a playable race with the release of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Minas_Morgul" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Minas Morgul">The Lord of the Rings Online: Minas Morgul</a></i> expansion pack. In the introduction for Stout-axe player characters, the player escapes Mordor when <a href="/wiki/Gollum" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> is set free from <a href="/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a>, and then takes refuge among the Longbeards of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls" title="Thorin&#39;s Halls">Thorin&#39;s Hall</a>.</dd></dl>
