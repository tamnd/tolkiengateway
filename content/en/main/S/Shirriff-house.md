**Shirriff-houses** were crude building used by the [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs) after [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) took control of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and expanded the [Watch](/wiki/Watch).

At the west end of [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton) was a poorly constructed one-story brick building with narrow windows, which served as the barracks of the [First Eastfarthing Troop](/wiki/First_Eastfarthing_Troop).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

All the Shirriff-houses were torn down before the end of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1419](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
