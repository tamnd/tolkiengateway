<i><b>Silver Leaves</b></i> is a journal for fans of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and fantasy literature. It was formerly the official journal of [The White Tree Fund](/wiki/The_White_Tree_Fund), but is now published by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Oloris_Publishing&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oloris Publishing (page does not exist)">Oloris Publishing</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1.1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1.1-1">[1]</a></sup>
