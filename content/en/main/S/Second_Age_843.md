**Second Age 843** (abbreviated to **S.A. 843**) is the 843rd [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) returns from a fourteen year long voyage from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) with ships that were battered and broken by the seas. He had first sailed to the haven of [Vinyalondë](../V/Vinyalondë.md) and then sailed along the coasts southwards, far beyond any place that had been reached by the ships of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) before. On his return northwards, he had encountered great storms, scarcely escaped shipwreck in the [Harad](../H/Harad.md) and found Vinyalondë overthrown by great seas and plundered by hostile men.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
