**Second Age 521** (abbreviated to **S.A. 521**) is the 521st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Silmariën](Silmariën.md), eldest child and daughter of [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md) and ancestor of the [Lords of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md).<sup id="cite_ref-RC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Kings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
