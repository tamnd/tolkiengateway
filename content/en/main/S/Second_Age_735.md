**Second Age 735** (abbreviated to **S.A. 735**) is the 735th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) leaves [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) on his third voyage to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). No longer satisfied just to visit [Mithlond](../G/Grey_Havens.md), Aldarion also explores the coasts southwards past the mouths of the rivers [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md), [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md) and [Isen](../I/Isen.md), rounding [Andrast](../A/Andrast.md) to visit the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md) and to see the mountains in the country of Amroth.<sup id="cite_ref-Aldarion_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aldarion-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>
