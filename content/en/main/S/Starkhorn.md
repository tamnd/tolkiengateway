The **Starkhorn** was one of the largest of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains). It was a snow-clad peak that rose in the inner regions of the mountain range. From its feet sprang the [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn), whose widening valley ran northwards through the mountains to emerge onto the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). The [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) called that valley [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale), and built their courts of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) at its mouth, from where the lonely white peak of the Starkhorn could be seen along the straight length of the valley. On a cliff above the valley they found the refuge of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) which had been built by [Pre-Númenórean Men](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") of the White Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It means "horn standing up stiff like a spike" and represents [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

As [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") notes, to an English reader *stark* now has implications of nakedness and grimness, not originally present in the word.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
