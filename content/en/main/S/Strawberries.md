**Strawberries** were wild berries found in several regions of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) tried to satisfy his hunger with wild strawberries growing near a stream in the upper Vales, as [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) escaped the [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> during early [July](/wiki/July).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the last years of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), strawberries were among the many fruits produced by [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire), and were enjoyed by the young [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s adventure in [Switzerland](/wiki/Switzerland) in [1911](/wiki/1911) was his inspiration for the journey from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to the other side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and it is quite possible he consumed strawberries during this time. <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Michael_Coren&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Coren (page does not exist)">Michael Coren</a> notes, "For dessert he liked a trifle or, in summer, strawberries with lots of cream."<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)*, No. 23 contains an illustration by J.R.R. Tolkien of a strawberry.
