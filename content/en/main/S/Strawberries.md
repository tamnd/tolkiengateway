**Strawberries** were wild berries found in several regions of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

[Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) tried to satisfy his hunger with wild strawberries growing near a stream in the upper Vales, as [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) escaped the [Goblin-town](../G/Goblin-town.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> during early [July](../J/July.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the last years of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), strawberries were among the many fruits produced by [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), and were enjoyed by the young [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s adventure in [Switzerland](Switzerland.md) in [1911](../_/1911.md) was his inspiration for the journey from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) to the other side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and it is quite possible he consumed strawberries during this time. <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Michael_Coren&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Coren (page does not exist)">Michael Coren</a> notes, "For dessert he liked a trifle or, in summer, strawberries with lots of cream."<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Artist_and_Illustrator.md)*, No. 23 contains an illustration by J.R.R. Tolkien of a strawberry.
