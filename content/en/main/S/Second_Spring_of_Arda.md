The **Second Spring of Arda** was a period that followed the [Sleep](/wiki/Sleep_of_Yavanna "Sleep of Yavanna") of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) after the destruction of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) and the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Τhe [Valar](/wiki/Valar) sent out two great vessels carry the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) and [Sun](/wiki/Sun) into the sky and bring light to all of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> With the first coming of sunlight to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the Sleep of Yavanna ended, and the sleeping things across the world came to life once more and began to grow. Thus, this reawakening of life at the first rising of the Sun is the time known as the Second [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
