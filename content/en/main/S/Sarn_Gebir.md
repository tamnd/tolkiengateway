**Sarn Gebir** were the **Rapids** of the Great River [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). They appeared as the river's course passed between the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md), above the [Falls of Rauros](../R/Rauros.md). As they were unnavigable rocks and stony eyots, a portage-way was built on the western bank of the river, facilitating travel with light boats from [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md) to [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), until the [Orcs of Mordor](../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md) began to multiply.

While the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) navigated the Anduin, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) was preparing for the Rapids, but they reached them sooner than he reckoned, on midnight of [23 February](../_/23_February.md). The stream pulled them towards the eastern bank, where they were ambushed by [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) archers from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). The Fellowship paddled away from the rapids. During the [next day](../_/24_February.md) they portaged a few miles to the foot of the rapids and resumed their paddling on the morning of [25 February](../_/25_February.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sarn Gebir* is a [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) name meaning "stone-spiked".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early manuscripts, Sarn Gebir was a name for the highlands which would later be named [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The ambush at the rapids of Sarn Gebir scene was intended to be in the film, to the point that an entire on-location set was built, but was not included in the final cut for one reason: before filming could begin on the set, record-breaking torrential rains and 20 foot high floodwaters washed away the entire set. So complete was the destruction that not even any wreckage of the set was <i>ever</i> found.  Without the time or funding to construct a new set, the scene had to be cut from the script.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Rapids of Sarn Gebir can be accessed from the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../W/Wold.md" title="Wold">Wold</a> region of <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>.</dd></dl>
