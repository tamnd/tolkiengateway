**Sarn Gebir** were the **Rapids** of the Great River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). They appeared as the river's course passed between the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), above the [Falls of Rauros](/wiki/Rauros "Rauros"). As they were unnavigable rocks and stony eyots, a portage-way was built on the western bank of the river, facilitating travel with light boats from [Wilderland](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion") to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), until the [Orcs of Mordor](/wiki/Orcs_of_Mordor) began to multiply.

While the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) navigated the Anduin, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was preparing for the Rapids, but they reached them sooner than he reckoned, on midnight of [23 February](/wiki/23_February). The stream pulled them towards the eastern bank, where they were ambushed by [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") archers from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). The Fellowship paddled away from the rapids. During the [next day](/wiki/24_February "24 February") they portaged a few miles to the foot of the rapids and resumed their paddling on the morning of [25 February](/wiki/25_February).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sarn Gebir* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "stone-spiked".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early manuscripts, Sarn Gebir was a name for the highlands which would later be named [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The ambush at the rapids of Sarn Gebir scene was intended to be in the film, to the point that an entire on-location set was built, but was not included in the final cut for one reason: before filming could begin on the set, record-breaking torrential rains and 20 foot high floodwaters washed away the entire set. So complete was the destruction that not even any wreckage of the set was <i>ever</i> found.  Without the time or funding to construct a new set, the scene had to be cut from the script.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Rapids of Sarn Gebir can be accessed from the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Wold" title="Wold">Wold</a> region of <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>.</dd></dl>
