The **sword of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB)** was a [weapon](/wiki/Category:Weapons "Category:Weapons") that the said [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") carried with him during the [War of the Powers](/wiki/War_of_the_Powers), and which was later used by his herald [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB) during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).

## History {#History}

The first mention of such a weapon occurred during the [War of the Powers](/wiki/War_of_the_Powers), where it is said:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>It came to pass that at last the gates of <a href="/wiki/Utumno" title="Utumno">Utumno</a> were broken and its halls unroofed, and <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Melkor" title="Melkor">Melkor</a> took refuge in the uttermost pit. Thence, seeing that all was lost (for that time), he sent forth on a sudden a host of <a href="/wiki/Balrogs" title="Balrogs">Balrogs</a>, the last of his servants that remained, and they assailed the standard of Manwë, as it were a tide of flame. But they were withered in the wind of his wrath and <b>slain with the lightning of his sword</b>; and Melkor stood at last alone.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring" title="Morgoth&#39;s Ring">Morgoth&#39;s Ring</a></i>, &#34;<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Annals_of_Aman" title="Annals of Aman">Annals of Aman</a>&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

Afterwards, millennia later, it is said that Manwë gave his sword to [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB) his herald,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> who led the [Host of the Valar](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar) during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), where [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) was finally defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
