**Stephen Walsh** is an English fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

He illustrated a piece of alternate art for *[Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
