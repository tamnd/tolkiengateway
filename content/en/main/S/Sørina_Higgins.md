**Sørina Higgins** is an author, editor, writing consultant, English teacher, and scholar of British modernist literature, particularly the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She has taught courses in literature, creative writing, and academic writing for over two decades in a variety of settings and now works as a <a class="external text" href="https://wyrdhoard.com/about/sorina-higgins/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">writing coach</a>. In [2018](../_/2018.md), her edited collection *[The Inklings and King Arthur](../T/The_Inklings_and_King_Arthur.md)* won the Mythopoeic Scholarship Award for Inkling Studies.
