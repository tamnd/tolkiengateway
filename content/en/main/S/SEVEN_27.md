<i><b>SEVEN 27</b></i> is the twenty-seventh issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](/wiki/SEVEN)*, published in [2010](/wiki/2010).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the article "Woodland Prisoner" (delivered as a speech at the [1983](/wiki/1983) Tolkien Conference at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University)), [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) quotes from several letters from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). These letters are:

- [Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (11 November 1964)](</wiki/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(11_November_1964)>)
- [Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (18 December 1965)](</wiki/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(18_December_1965)>)
- [Letter 289b](/wiki/Letter_289b)
- [Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (4 June 1968)](</wiki/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(4_June_1968)>)
- [Letter 348a](/wiki/Letter_348a)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The volume also includes the first publication of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)'s manuscript "[Language and Human Nature](/wiki/Language_and_Human_Nature)", to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) contributed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "C.S. Lewis on Language and Meaning"
    - By: Steven A. Beebe (includes transcription of Lewis manuscript fragment entitled "[Language and Human Nature](/wiki/Language_and_Human_Nature)")
- "In Search of Lucy: The Life of Lucy Barfield"
    - By: Owen A. and Adelene Barfield
- "C.S. Lewis Remembered: Cambridge, 1957-1960"
    - By: Tom McAlindon
- "Woodland Prisoner"
    - By: [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) with introduction and annotations by [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff)
- "Rust" and "She"
    - By: [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield)
- "C.S. Lewis and the Art of the Apologue"
    - By: Samuel Joeckel
- Review Essays:
    - "G.K. Chesterton as Thinker and Theologian"
        - By: [Ralph C. Wood](/wiki/Ralph_C._Wood)
    - "Dearborn's The Baptized Imagination"
        - By: Kirstin Jeffrey Johnson
- "Some Planets in Narnia: A Quantitative Investigation of the Planet Narnia Thesis"
    - Justin L. Barrett<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
