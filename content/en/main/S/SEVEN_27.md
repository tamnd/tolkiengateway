<i><b>SEVEN 27</b></i> is the twenty-seventh issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2010](../_/2010.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the article "Woodland Prisoner" (delivered as a speech at the [1983](../_/1983.md) Tolkien Conference at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md)), [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md) quotes from several letters from [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). These letters are:

- [Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (11 November 1964)](<../L/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(11_November_1964).md>)
- [Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (18 December 1965)](<../L/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(18_December_1965).md>)
- [Letter 289b](../L/Letter_289b.md)
- [Letter to Clyde S. Kilby (4 June 1968)](<../L/Letter_to_Clyde_S._Kilby_(4_June_1968).md>)
- [Letter 348a](../L/Letter_348a.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The volume also includes the first publication of [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s manuscript "[Language and Human Nature](../L/Language_and_Human_Nature.md)", to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "C.S. Lewis on Language and Meaning"
    - By: Steven A. Beebe (includes transcription of Lewis manuscript fragment entitled "[Language and Human Nature](../L/Language_and_Human_Nature.md)")
- "In Search of Lucy: The Life of Lucy Barfield"
    - By: Owen A. and Adelene Barfield
- "C.S. Lewis Remembered: Cambridge, 1957-1960"
    - By: Tom McAlindon
- "Woodland Prisoner"
    - By: [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md) with introduction and annotations by [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md)
- "Rust" and "She"
    - By: [Owen Barfield](../O/Owen_Barfield.md)
- "C.S. Lewis and the Art of the Apologue"
    - By: Samuel Joeckel
- Review Essays:
    - "G.K. Chesterton as Thinker and Theologian"
        - By: [Ralph C. Wood](../R/Ralph_C._Wood.md)
    - "Dearborn's The Baptized Imagination"
        - By: Kirstin Jeffrey Johnson
- "Some Planets in Narnia: A Quantitative Investigation of the Planet Narnia Thesis"
    - Justin L. Barrett<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
