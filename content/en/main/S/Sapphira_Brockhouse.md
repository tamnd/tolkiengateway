**Sapphira Boffin, née Brockhouse**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Sapphira married [Uffo Boffin](/wiki/Uffo_Boffin) and had two children: [Gruffo](/wiki/Gruffo_Boffin "Gruffo Boffin") and [Gerda](/wiki/Gerda_Boffin "Gerda Boffin").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapphire" title="wikipedia:Sapphire">Sapphire</a></i> is a blue gemstone. The name derives from Greek *σάπφειρος* through Latin and Greek.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
