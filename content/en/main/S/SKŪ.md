**SKŪ** and **KHŪ** are [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "curse".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

The root of opposite meaning is [AYA](/wiki/AYA).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *hūna* ("cursed, accursed"); *húta* ("to curse")<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>
