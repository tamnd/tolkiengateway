The **Seven Gates of Gondolin** were seven mighty gates guarding the [Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor), a ravine by which [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) was approached via [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen).

## History {#History}

The Seven Gates were built by [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondolin "King of Gondolin") [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to protect Gondolin from invasion. It was through these gates that [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) passed to enter into Tumladen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> However, it was not through these gates that the host of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) entered and [laid seige](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin "Fall of Gondolin") to Gondolin, as after hearing [Ulmo's warning](/wiki/Ulmo%27s_warning), Turgon closed the doors.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Seven Gates each had their own divisions and units of guards, such as the [Dark Guard](/wiki/Dark_Guard),<sup id="cite_ref-SilmMaeglin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilmMaeglin-3">[3]</a></sup> that were led by different captains or chieftains, such as [Elemmakil](/wiki/Elemmakil). The gates, listed from the end of the Orfalch furthest from Gondolin to the end opening into Tumladen, were:

- [Gate of Wood](/wiki/Gate_of_Wood)
- [Gate of Stone](/wiki/Gate_of_Stone)
- [Gate of Bronze](/wiki/Gate_of_Bronze)
- [Gate of Writhen Iron](/wiki/Gate_of_Writhen_Iron)
- [Gate of Silver](/wiki/Gate_of_Silver)
- [Gate of Gold](/wiki/Gate_of_Gold)
- [Gate of Steel](/wiki/Gate_of_Steel)
