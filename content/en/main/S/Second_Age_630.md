**Second Age 630** (abbreviated to **S.A. 630**) is the 630th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>), son of [Silmariën](Silmariën.md) and [Elatan](../E/Elatan.md) and the first [Lord of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-1">[1]</a></sup>
