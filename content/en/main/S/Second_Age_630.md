**Second Age 630** (abbreviated to **S.A. 630**) is the 630th [year of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Notable events in this year include:

- Birth of [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)"), son of [Silmariën](/wiki/Silmari%C3%ABn) and [Elatan](/wiki/Elatan) and the first [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-1">[1]</a></sup>
