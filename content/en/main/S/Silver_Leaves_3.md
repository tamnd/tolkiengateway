<b><i>Silver Leaves</i> 3</b> ("Tolkien and Fantasy Art &amp; Artists") is the third issue of *[Silver Leaves](/wiki/Silver_Leaves)*, the journal of [The White Tree Fund](/wiki/The_White_Tree_Fund), published [2010](/wiki/2010).
