**Silvan Elvish** was a name for the tongue spoken by the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves).

## History {#History}

Silvan Elvish descended from **Nandorin/Danian**<sup id="cite_ref-pe18_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe18-1">[1]</a></sup> (from their leader [Lenwë/Dân](/wiki/Lenw%C3%AB "Lenwë")), which was the language of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor), those [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who broke from the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey) east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). As such, Nandorin was a branch of the [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin).

A branch of the Nandor eventually completed the Journey, settling in [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) and became known as the [Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi); those spoke *West-Danian* (also called *Ossiriandrin* or *Ossiriandish*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and *Laiquenderin*). Those who remained in [the East](/wiki/Rhovanion "Rhovanion") spoke *East-Danian*.<sup id="cite_ref-pe18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe18-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the surviving Elves of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) carried their speech back into the east. In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the Nandor became known as [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves), with their language being known as Silvan Elvish, heard both in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) and the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)-speakers settled in both the main Silvan realms, and in Lórien, at least, Sindarin quickly overtook Silvan Elvish in common use. The history of the tongue in Mirkwood is more difficult to determine - some sources suggest that it, too, was quickly overtaken by Sindarin, but other sources dispute this. Sindarin had become the dominant [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) tongue of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands), with Nandorin little-spoken; it was represented mainly in old place-names and Silvan dialects developed under its influence.

By the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the only remnants of Silvan were a few names, like **[Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon)**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> **[Amroth](/wiki/Amroth)**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or **[Legolas](/wiki/Legolas)**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> which sound like Sindarin, but actually betray the influences of Silvan words.

## Other names {#Other_names}

[Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) calls the dialect the "*woodland tongue*".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In one note [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") calls the language just Silvan.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Attributes {#Attributes}

In a note where Tolkien describes the languages types exemplified by the ranges of consonant sounds and combinations found in then, *Danian* is described as being of *Germanic* type. This is contrasted against Telerin ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)) which is of *Latin* type, and Noldorin ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) which is of *Welsh* type.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Lhammas](/wiki/Lhammas "Lhammas")* (ca. [1937](/wiki/1937)-[38](/wiki/1938 "1938")), Tolkien used the names *Ossiriandic* or *Ossiriandeb*; and *Leikvian* for *East Danian*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
