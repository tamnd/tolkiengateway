The **Seventh Level** of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) or [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) was a level of chambers, corridors and rooms, being six levels above the [Great Gates](../G/Great_Gates.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> The term *level* or *levels* in the ancient [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) kingdom generally refer to the eastern area of Moria, nearest the eastern gate which was most populated when Khazad-dûm was a mighty city of the Dwarves.

The [Chamber of Mazarbul](../C/Chamber_of_Mazarbul.md) was located on the Seventh Level of Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
