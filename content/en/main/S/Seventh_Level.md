The **Seventh Level** of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) or [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") was a level of chambers, corridors and rooms, being six levels above the [Great Gates](/wiki/Great_Gates).<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> The term *level* or *levels* in the ancient [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") kingdom generally refer to the eastern area of Moria, nearest the eastern gate which was most populated when Khazad-dûm was a mighty city of the Dwarves.

The [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul) was located on the Seventh Level of Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
