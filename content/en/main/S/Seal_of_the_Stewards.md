The **seal of the Stewards** was the seal of the [Stewards](Steward_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), consisting of the letters R·ND·R below three stars. The letters represent the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word *[Arandur](Steward_of_Gondor.md)* ("king's servant"). This is the actual [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) title that [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) renders into English as 'Steward'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the twelfth Steward, [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md), sent six riders of endurance and great courage north to [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) for aid in his desperate wars, he sent the seal incised upon a small stone with each of then. Only one of the riders, [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md), succeeded in reaching the lands of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), but from this came the famous [Ride of Eorl](../R/Ride_of_Eorl.md) and the foundation of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
