The **seal of the Stewards** was the seal of the [Stewards](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor "Steward of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), consisting of the letters R·ND·R below three stars. The letters represent the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[Arandur](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor "Steward of Gondor")* ("king's servant"). This is the actual [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) title that [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") renders into English as 'Steward'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the twelfth Steward, [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion), sent six riders of endurance and great courage north to [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) for aid in his desperate wars, he sent the seal incised upon a small stone with each of then. Only one of the riders, [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir), succeeded in reaching the lands of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), but from this came the famous [Ride of Eorl](/wiki/Ride_of_Eorl) and the foundation of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
