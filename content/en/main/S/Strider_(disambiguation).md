**Strider** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- "Strider", a sobriquet of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in the north.
    - [Telcontar](/wiki/Telcontar), the House of [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), which translates as "Strider".
- [Strider](</wiki/Strider_(pony)> "Strider (pony)"), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins)'s pony after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) which was named after Aragorn.

## Other {#Other}

- *[Strider (chapter)](</wiki/Strider_(chapter)>)*, the tenth chapter of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
