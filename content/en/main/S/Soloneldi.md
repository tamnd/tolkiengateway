**Soloneldi** is a [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) word meaning "surf-singers", a term for [themselves](/wiki/Falmari "Falmari") (along with <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fallinelli&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fallinelli (page does not exist)">Fallinelli</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first part derives from the [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SOL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SOL (page does not exist)">SOL</a> (\*<i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Solo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Solo (page does not exist)">solo</a></i>) and the second from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NYEL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NYEL (page does not exist)">NYEL</a> (\*<i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Neldi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neldi (page does not exist)">neldi</a></i>).

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

[Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi) was Tolkien's older name of the Teleri. <span></span>
