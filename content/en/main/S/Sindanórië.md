**Sindanórie** is a said to be the name of a "mythical region of shadows", situated at the outer feet of the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) Mountains in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [her lament](/wiki/Nam%C3%A1ri%C3%AB "Namárië"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) said that out of a Grey Country (*sindanóriello*) darkness lay on the waves that separated herself and [Varda](/wiki/Varda).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name *Sindanórie* consists of *[sinda](/wiki/Sindar#Etymology "Sindar")* ("grey") and *[nōre](/wiki/N%C3%B3re "Nóre")* ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
