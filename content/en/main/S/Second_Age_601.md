**Second Age 601** (abbreviated to **S.A. 601**) is the 601st [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

## Autumn {#Autumn}

- [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md), [Captain of the King's Ships](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md), returns to [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) after completing the first [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) voyage to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Island_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Island-1">[1]</a></sup>
