**Some Contributions to Middle-English Lexicography** is the title of an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). In Part I Tolkien discusses aspects of a variety of [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) words and their Modern English meanings. Part II consists of a series of notes on the glossary to the Early English Text Society Edition of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Group" title="wikipedia:Katherine Group">Hali Meiðhad</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The essay was first published in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1 No. 2](</wiki/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1_No._2)> "The Review of English Studies (Vol. 1 No. 2)") (April 1925), and was reprinted in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1](</wiki/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1)> "The Review of English Studies (Vol. 1)") (1969).
