**Some Contributions to Middle-English Lexicography** is the title of an essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). In Part I Tolkien discusses aspects of a variety of [Middle English](../M/Middle_English.md) words and their Modern English meanings. Part II consists of a series of notes on the glossary to the Early English Text Society Edition of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Group" title="wikipedia:Katherine Group">Hali Meiðhad</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The essay was first published in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1 No. 2](<../T/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1_No._2).md>) (April 1925), and was reprinted in [*The Review of English Studies*, Vol. 1](<../T/The_Review_of_English_Studies_(Vol._1).md>) (1969).
