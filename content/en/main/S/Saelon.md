**Saelon** was one of the main characters in *[The New Shadow](/wiki/The_New_Shadow)*, the abandoned sequel to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## History {#History}

Saelon was caught by [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas) as a child when he was stealing an apple in Borlas' garden. Borlas' anger and description of Saelon's behaviour as doing *orc's work* struck in to Saelon's heart - he felt deeply aggrieved and held this as a deep grudge.

Some years later, Borlas and Saelon sat in the garden across from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). After Borlas described the [Dark Tree](/wiki/Dark_Tree), it is Saelon who later mentions that there were men who have grown *under the shadow* in recent times and whispered the name [Herumor](</wiki/Herumor_(The_New_Shadow)> "Herumor (The New Shadow)"), the possible leader of a rebellious faction in Gondor, to Borlas.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Saelon* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), however its meaning is unclear. According to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), the name might contain the element <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sael&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sael (page does not exist)">sael</a></i> ("wise") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=On&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="On (page does not exist)">on</a></i> ("masculine suffix").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the earliest version (text A), the character was named <i><b><a href="/wiki/Egalmoth" title="Egalmoth">Egalmoth</a></b></i>. In the second version (text B), the character was named <i><b>Arthael</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-1">[1]</a></sup>
