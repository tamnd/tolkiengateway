**Stella Marie Mills** ([1903](/wiki/1903)-[1989](/wiki/1989))<sup id="cite_ref-DAA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DAA-1">[1]</a></sup> was a student of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds). Tolkien offered her assistance in becoming employed by the *[Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*. In [1933](/wiki/1933), Mills published *[The Saga of Hrolf Kraki](/wiki/The_Saga_of_Hrolf_Kraki)*, a translation of the [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) *[Hrólfs saga kraka](/wiki/Hr%C3%B3lfs_saga_kraka)*, which she dedicated to [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon), Tolkien, and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Talbut_Onions" title="wikipedia:Charles Talbut Onions">C.T. Onions</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Mills would become a good friend of the [Tolkien family](/wiki/Tolkien_Family "Tolkien Family"), and Priscilla Tolkien recorded her reminiscences of Mills in a Foreword published in the [2012](/wiki/2012) edition of *The Saga of Hrolf Kraki*.<sup id="cite_ref-DAA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DAA-1">[1]</a></sup> She was among the recipients to whom Tolkien sent inscribed editions of the newly published *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, on or close after [21 September](/wiki/21_September) [1937](/wiki/1937).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
