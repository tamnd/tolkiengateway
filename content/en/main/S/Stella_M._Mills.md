**Stella Marie Mills** ([1903](../_/1903.md)-[1989](../_/1989.md))<sup id="cite_ref-DAA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DAA-1">[1]</a></sup> was a student of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md). Tolkien offered her assistance in becoming employed by the *[Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md)*. In [1933](../_/1933.md), Mills published *[The Saga of Hrolf Kraki](../T/The_Saga_of_Hrolf_Kraki.md)*, a translation of the [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) *[Hrólfs saga kraka](../H/Hrólfs_saga_kraka.md)*, which she dedicated to [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md), Tolkien, and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Talbut_Onions" title="wikipedia:Charles Talbut Onions">C.T. Onions</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Mills would become a good friend of the [Tolkien family](../T/Tolkien_Family.md), and Priscilla Tolkien recorded her reminiscences of Mills in a Foreword published in the [2012](../_/2012.md) edition of *The Saga of Hrolf Kraki*.<sup id="cite_ref-DAA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DAA-1">[1]</a></sup> She was among the recipients to whom Tolkien sent inscribed editions of the newly published *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, on or close after [21 September](../_/21_September.md) [1937](../_/1937.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
