<i><b>The Stapeldon Magazine</b></i>, first published in [1901](../_/1901.md), was a magazine produced by Exeter College.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Several issues contain poems by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## December 1913 {#December_1913}

Vol. 4. No. 20. Pg. 345. Oxford: Exeter college.

This issue contains the first stanza of the poem *[Valedictory](../V/Valedictory.md)* (p. 11).

## June 1920 {#June_1920}

Vol. 5. no. 26. June 1920. Pg. 345.

This issue contains the poem '[The Happy Mariners](../T/The_Happy_Mariners.md)' (pp. 69–70).

The poem was reprinted in a revised form in *[A Northern Venture](../A/A_Northern_Venture.md)* in 1923.

## June 1927 {#June_1927}

Vol. 7. no. 40.

This issue contains two poems published under one overall title: *[Adventures in Unnatural History and Medieval Metres, being the Freaks of Fisiologus](../A/Adventures_in_Unnatural_History_and_Medieval_Metres,_being_the_Freaks_of_Fisiologus.md)*: (i) Fastitocalon, (ii) Iumbo, or ye Kinde of ye Oliphaunt.

Both poems were later reprinted in revised forms, as [Fastitocalon](<../F/Fastitocalon_(poem).md>) and [Oliphaunt](<../O/Oliphaunt_(poem).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
