In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*, **Scorba** the Rubine was a younger brother of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).

Fiercely jealous of his more powerful brother, he sought to establish his own lair in imitation Smaug's [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Zarak Dûm proved to be ideal: it was a Dwarven colony bordering [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), that had succumbed to a plague after the Dwarves had delved into an alchemy chamber of the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) in the [Under-deeps](/wiki/Under-deeps).

Scorba hunted in [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), often feasting on orcs and trolls, though he avoided the ruins Carn Dûm out of fear.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> His presence was tolerated by the Witch-king, since he effectively guarded the border of his realm.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

By the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), Scorba had perished and his lair was inhabited by goblins.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
