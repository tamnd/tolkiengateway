**Sterday** (archaic form *Sterrendei*) was the first day of the week in the [Shire Calendar](Shire_Calendar.md). It was dedicated to the [stars](Stars.md), representing the [Númenórean](../N/Númenórean.md) day *[Orgilion](../O/Orgilion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the time of [Isengrim Took II](../I/Isengrim_Took_II.md), Sterday was also the first day of each year, falling always on 2 [Yule](../Y/Yule.md) (and 2 [Lithe](../L/Lithe.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup> As such, [1 April](../_/1_April.md) and [1 October](../_/1_October.md) always fell on Sterday.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sterday* represents an archaic [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) form "Sterrendei", a word preserved in the [Yellowskin](../Y/Yellowskin.md). It is dedicated to the [stars](Stars.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is translated as "Saturday" in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* but as it was the first day of the week, it corresponded more to our Monday. For this reason, The "Saturday" referred in the poem *[The Man in the Moon Stayed Up Too Late](../T/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Stayed_Up_Too_Late.md)*, actually refers to the Hobbitish [Thursday](../M/Mersday.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
