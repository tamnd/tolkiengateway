**Salkinórë** was the name of a continent in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), later known as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africa" title="wikipedia:Africa">Africa</a>, according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Salkinórë* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md). It is glossed as "Grass Land" by *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)*, consisting of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Salki&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Salki (page does not exist)">salki</a></i> or <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Salk%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Salkë (page does not exist)">salkë</a></i> ("grass") + *[nórë](../N/Nórë.md)* ("land, nation, family, country").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Another name for the place was <i><b>Andesalkë</b></i>, consisting of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ande(a)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ande(a) (page does not exist)">ande(a)</a></i> ("long") + *salkë*, meaning "Long-grass".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> An earlier form of *Andesalkë* was *Andisalkë*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
