<i><b>Silmë Nuquerna</b></i> is the name in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) of the thirtieth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In form it is an inversion of the twenty-ninth letter ([Silmë](Silmë.md)). It is mostly used as a variation, especially in modes employing superimposed *[tehtar](../T/Tehtar.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and it represents **S**. However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](../M/Mode_of_Beleriand.md), as seen as in the inscription on the [West-gate](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) of [Moria](../M/Moria.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *Silmë Nuquerna* represents vocalic **Y**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ós</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
