<i><b>Silmë Nuquerna</b></i> is the name in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) of the thirtieth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In form it is an inversion of the twenty-ninth letter ([Silmë](/wiki/Silm%C3%AB)). It is mostly used as a variation, especially in modes employing superimposed *[tehtar](/wiki/Tehtar "Tehtar")*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and it represents **S**. However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand), as seen as in the inscription on the [West-gate](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin") of [Moria](/wiki/Moria),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *Silmë Nuquerna* represents vocalic **Y**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ós</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
