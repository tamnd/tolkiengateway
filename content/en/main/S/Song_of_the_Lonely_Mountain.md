**Song Of The Lonely Mountain** is a song, written by [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore) and performed by [Neil Finn](/wiki/Neil_Finn), that features in the end credits of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*. It is also the twelfth track of the second disc of both *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)* and *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition)* (in an extended form).
