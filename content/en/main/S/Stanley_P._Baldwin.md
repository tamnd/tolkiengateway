**Stanley P. Baldwin** (born [1942](/wiki/1942)) is a writer and teacher, currently living in Nebraska. He is a frequent contributor to scholarly series as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SparkNotes" title="wikipedia:SparkNotes">SparkNotes</a> and [CliffsNotes](/wiki/Cliffs_Notes_on_Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_The_Hobbit "Cliffs Notes on Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit"). He wrote about *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")* and many others in the latter series, and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in the former.
