**Stanley P. Baldwin** (born [1942](../_/1942.md)) is a writer and teacher, currently living in Nebraska. He is a frequent contributor to scholarly series as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SparkNotes" title="wikipedia:SparkNotes">SparkNotes</a> and [CliffsNotes](../C/Cliffs_Notes_on_Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_and_The_Hobbit.md). He wrote about *[Beowulf](<../B/Beowulf_(poem).md>)* and many others in the latter series, and [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in the former.
