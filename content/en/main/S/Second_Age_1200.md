**Second Age 1200** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1200**) is the 1200th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Sauron](Sauron.md) endeavours to seduce the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md). [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) refuses to treat with him; but the smiths of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) are won over.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- The [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) begin to make permanent havens.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
