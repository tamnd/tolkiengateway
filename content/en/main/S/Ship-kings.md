The **Ship-kings** was a title given to four great [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) during its "golden age", starting with King [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur), and his three successors:

- **[Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur)** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [830](/wiki/Third_Age_830 "Third Age 830")-[913](/wiki/Third_Age_913 "Third Age 913"))
- **[Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I)** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [913](/wiki/Third_Age_913 "Third Age 913")-[936](/wiki/Third_Age_936 "Third Age 936"))
- **[Ciryandil](/wiki/Ciryandil)** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [936](/wiki/Third_Age_936 "Third Age 936")-[1015](/wiki/Third_Age_1015 "Third Age 1015"))
- **[Ciryaher Hyarmendacil I](/wiki/Hyarmendacil_I "Hyarmendacil I")** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1015](/wiki/Third_Age_1015 "Third Age 1015")-[1149](/wiki/Third_Age_1149 "Third Age 1149"))

The Ship-kings did much to expand [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)'s naval and military might. Between them, they enormously extended the South-kingdom's control over the southern lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), including the lands west of the [Mouths of Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin"), and as far south as [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and the [Harad](/wiki/Harad).

Their influence was such that, at the end of the reign of Hyarmendacil I, Gondor had reached a peak of strength and power that it would never again surpass. Indeed, the period of the ship-kings was followed by a period of stagnation, disasters and wars, that led to its decline.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
