The **Ship-kings** was a title given to four great [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) during its "golden age", starting with King [Tarannon Falastur](../T/Tarannon_Falastur.md), and his three successors:

- **[Tarannon Falastur](../T/Tarannon_Falastur.md)** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [830](../T/Third_Age_830.md)-[913](../T/Third_Age_913.md))
- **[Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md)** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [913](../T/Third_Age_913.md)-[936](../T/Third_Age_936.md))
- **[Ciryandil](../C/Ciryandil.md)** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [936](../T/Third_Age_936.md)-[1015](../T/Third_Age_1015.md))
- **[Ciryaher Hyarmendacil I](../H/Hyarmendacil_I.md)** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1015](../T/Third_Age_1015.md)-[1149](../T/Third_Age_1149.md))

The Ship-kings did much to expand [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)'s naval and military might. Between them, they enormously extended the South-kingdom's control over the southern lands of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), including the lands west of the [Mouths of Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md), and as far south as [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) and the [Harad](../H/Harad.md).

Their influence was such that, at the end of the reign of Hyarmendacil I, Gondor had reached a peak of strength and power that it would never again surpass. Indeed, the period of the ship-kings was followed by a period of stagnation, disasters and wars, that led to its decline.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
