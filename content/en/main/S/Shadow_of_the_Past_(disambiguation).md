**Shadow of the Past** can refer to:

- **[The Shadow of the Past (chapter)](/wiki/The_Shadow_of_the_Past "The Shadow of the Past")**, the second chapter of [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).
- **[Shadow of the Past (film)](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Shadow_of_the_Past "The Lord of the Rings: Shadow of the Past")**, an upcoming film.
- **[The Shadow of the Past (scene)](</wiki/The_Shadow_of_the_Past_(scene)>)**, a scene in [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).
- **[The Shadow Of The Past (soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Shadow_Of_The_Past_(soundtrack)>)**, the third track in [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack).
- **[A Shadow of the Past](/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past)**, the first episode of [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios)'s television series, [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power).
