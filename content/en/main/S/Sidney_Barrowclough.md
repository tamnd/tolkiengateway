**Sidney Barrowclough** ([1894](../_/1894.md) - [1 February](../_/1_February.md) [1969](../_/1969.md)) was a member of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md). A few years younger than the core members, Barrowclough (and [Ralph Payton](../R/Ralph_Payton.md)) continued the T.C.B.S. after the "big four"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> left King Edward's School.

Barrowclough was born in 1894 to John and Emily Barrowclough. He had a younger sister Agnes, born in [1889](../_/1889.md).<sup id="cite_ref-lives_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lives-2">[1]</a></sup> In October of either [1911](../_/1911.md) or [1913](../_/1913.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 2]</a></sup> Barrowclough, along with Ralph Payton, joined the T.C.B.S.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup> While at [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md), he played as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-quarter_back" title="wikipedia:Three-quarter back">three-quarter back</a> on the school rugby team.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[3]</a></sup> Barrowclough is described by [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md) as "\[affecting\] a cold cynicism, robing his sarcasm in verbal elegance".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup> Barrowclough would serve in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Field_Artillery" title="wikipedia:Royal Field Artillery">Royal Field Artillery</a> at the rank of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Lieutenant#United_Kingdom_and_other_Commonwealth_countries" title="wikipedia:Second Lieutenant">Second Lieutenant</a> during [World War I](../W/World_War_I.md). Barrowclough married Constance Marian Page in [1920](../_/1920.md) and died on [1 February](../_/1_February.md), 1969.<sup id="cite_ref-lives_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lives-2">[1]</a></sup>
