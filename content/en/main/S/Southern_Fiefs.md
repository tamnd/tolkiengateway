The **Southern Fiefs** or **Outlands** of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) were the seabord lands south of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the fiefs of Gondor outside the [Pelennor](/wiki/Pelennor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

These seaboard lands along the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) provided much of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith)'s needs: [fish](/wiki/Fish "Fish") from the [Ethir](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin"), [flowers](/wiki/Category:Flowers "Category:Flowers") from [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach), and at earlier times, stone from [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien). The fiefs also provided skilled soldiers.

## History {#History}

After the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) raided the Gondorian coasts up to [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas), until [Umbardacil](/wiki/Umbardacil) avenged retook [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1810](/wiki/Third_Age_1810 "Third Age 1810").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Years later the Corsairs once more harried the coasts of Gondor during the time of [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), when Gondor and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) were plagued by the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter), three great fleets sailed from Umbar and [Harad](/wiki/Harad) and raided and landed on the Gondorian coasts, as north as the mouth of [Isen](/wiki/Isen). Travelling up [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui) and Isen they even attacked [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Before the spring of [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759"), [Beregond](</wiki/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beregond (Steward of Gondor)") overcame the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

By the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), most regions of Gondor were either deserted or overrun and only the Southern Fiefs remained. During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the Outlands are under attack from the south by a large army of [Corsairs from Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar"), allies of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), and they sent small reinforcements to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Known fiefs {#Known_fiefs}

The Southern Fiefs were:

- [Dor-en-Ernil](/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil)
- [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach)
- [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin)
- [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas)
- [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin)
- [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon)
- [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas) and the [Green Hills](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin "Pinnath Gelin")
- [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale)
- [Ringló Vale](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3_Vale)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiefdom" title="wikipedia:Fiefdom">fief</a> is, in feudalism, a piece of inheritable land given to a vassal. The vassal returned this gift with (military) allegiance and/or revenue.
