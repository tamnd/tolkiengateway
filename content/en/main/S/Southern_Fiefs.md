The **Southern Fiefs** or **Outlands** of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) were the seabord lands south of [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the fiefs of Gondor outside the [Pelennor](../P/Pelennor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

These seaboard lands along the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md) provided much of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md)'s needs: [fish](../F/Fish.md) from the [Ethir](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md), [flowers](../../category/F/Flowers.md) from [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md), and at earlier times, stone from [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md). The fiefs also provided skilled soldiers.

## History {#History}

After the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) raided the Gondorian coasts up to [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md), until [Umbardacil](../U/Umbardacil.md) avenged retook [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1810](../T/Third_Age_1810.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Years later the Corsairs once more harried the coasts of Gondor during the time of [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md), when Gondor and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) were plagued by the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md), three great fleets sailed from Umbar and [Harad](../H/Harad.md) and raided and landed on the Gondorian coasts, as north as the mouth of [Isen](../I/Isen.md). Travelling up [Lefnui](../L/Lefnui.md) and Isen they even attacked [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Before the spring of [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md), [Beregond](<../B/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) overcame the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

By the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), most regions of Gondor were either deserted or overrun and only the Southern Fiefs remained. During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the Outlands are under attack from the south by a large army of [Corsairs from Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md), allies of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), and they sent small reinforcements to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Known fiefs {#Known_fiefs}

The Southern Fiefs were:

- [Dor-en-Ernil](../D/Dor-en-Ernil.md)
- [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md)
- [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md)
- [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md)
- [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md)
- [Lamedon](../L/Lamedon.md)
- [Anfalas](../A/Anfalas.md) and the [Green Hills](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md)
- [Blackroot Vale](../B/Blackroot_Vale.md)
- [Ringló Vale](../R/Ringló_Vale.md)

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiefdom" title="wikipedia:Fiefdom">fief</a> is, in feudalism, a piece of inheritable land given to a vassal. The vassal returned this gift with (military) allegiance and/or revenue.
