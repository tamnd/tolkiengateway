The **Sociedad Tolkien Peruana** (Spanish: "Peruvian Tolkien Society"), or **STP** is a [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") based in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru" title="wikipedia:Peru">Peru</a>. It was founded on [January 3](/wiki/3_January "3 January"), [2001](/wiki/2001), on the 118th birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It has two [smials](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society#Smials "The Tolkien Society"), one for the North (Tumbes) and one for the South (Arequipa). The Society's main activities include the yearly *Máratulda Vinyë Meldorinna*, *Bilbo &amp; Frodo Rally* and *Gran Concilio*, of which the fourth was held on [April 4](/wiki/4_April "4 April"), [2009](/wiki/2009).

As off 2023 it appears to be active. Although it's official website has not been updated since 2009 it's Facebook activity page shows activity in 2023.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
