The **Sociedad Tolkien Peruana** (Spanish: "Peruvian Tolkien Society"), or **STP** is a [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) based in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru" title="wikipedia:Peru">Peru</a>. It was founded on [January 3](../_/3_January.md), [2001](../_/2001.md), on the 118th birthday of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). It has two [smials](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md#Smials), one for the North (Tumbes) and one for the South (Arequipa). The Society's main activities include the yearly *Máratulda Vinyë Meldorinna*, *Bilbo &amp; Frodo Rally* and *Gran Concilio*, of which the fourth was held on [April 4](../_/4_April.md), [2009](../_/2009.md).

As off 2023 it appears to be active. Although it's official website has not been updated since 2009 it's Facebook activity page shows activity in 2023.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
