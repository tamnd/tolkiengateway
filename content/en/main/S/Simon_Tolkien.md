**Simon Mario Reuel Tolkien** (born [12 January](../_/12_January.md) [1959](../_/1959.md)) is the eldest son of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and grandson of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He is the only child of Christopher's first marriage, to [Faith Faulconbridge](../F/Faith_Tolkien.md). Since [2011](../_/2011.md), he has acted as a director of the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) along with [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md), [Michael George Tolkien](../M/Michael_George_Tolkien.md), and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Steven_Maier&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Steven Maier (page does not exist)">Steven Maier</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Biography {#Biography}

Simon Tolkien studied modern history at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Oxford" title="wikipedia:Trinity College, Oxford">Trinity College</a> in the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). After which, he embarked on a fifteen-year career as a criminal lawyer and became a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrister" title="wikipedia:Barrister">barrister</a> in [1994](../_/1994.md).

In [1984](../_/1984.md), he married Tracy Steinberg, who was Jewish.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Steinberg owned and operated a vintage clothing store in Chelsea, London, called *Steinberg &amp; Tolkien*, which shut down in [September](September.md) of [2007](../_/2007.md). Later, Tolkien moved to Southern California with his wife and their two children.<sup id="cite_ref-life_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-life-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [January](../J/January.md) of [2000](../_/2000.md), Simon Tolkien began writing fiction.<sup id="cite_ref-life_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-life-3">[3]</a></sup> Tolkien's first novel, *The Stepmother* (published in the United States as *Final Witness: A Novel*), was published in 2003. *The Inheritance: A Novel*, his second book, was published on April 13, 2010 by Minotaur Books.

Simon Tolkien disagreed with the then policy of his [grandfather's estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) over the adaptations of his grandfather's works. As a result, he had a strained relationship with his father over that point,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> however they subsequently reconciled.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

He was consulted on the development of the television series [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md) and he helped develop its story and the character arcs. As a result, he is credited as a "series consultant".<sup id="cite_ref-SimonTolkien_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SimonTolkien-6">[6]</a></sup>
