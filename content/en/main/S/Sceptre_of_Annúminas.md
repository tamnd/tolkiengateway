The **Sceptre of Annúminas** was a silver rod, originally the symbol of office of the [Lords of Andúnië](../../category/L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md) in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). It survived the [Downfall](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), and was held by the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) while that realm lasted. After the division of the [North-kingdom](../A/Arnor.md), it was handed down through the generations of the [Kings of Arthedain](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) until the death of the last king, [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md). After that the sceptre was kept in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ancient_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ancient-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [2951](../T/Third_Age_2951.md), when [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) was twenty years old, [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) gave him the other heirlooms of the House of Elendil, but he withheld the Sceptre saying that Aragorn had yet to earn it.<sup id="cite_ref-tale_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tale-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), when Elrond arrived in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) with his daughter [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), he handed the sceptre to Aragorn, who had been crowned as King Elessar.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> By this time the sceptre was likely the most ancient work of [Men’s](../M/Men.md) hands preserved in Middle-earth, being more than five thousand years old.<sup id="cite_ref-ancient_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ancient-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the reign of Elessar, some lands of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) (former Arnor) such as [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), were said to be under the protection of the **Northern Sceptre**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md), on his deathbed, Elessar gave the sceptre of Arnor and the [Crown of Gondor](../C/Crown_of_Gondor.md) into the hands of his son, [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-tale_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tale-2">[2]</a></sup>
