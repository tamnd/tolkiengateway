The **Sceptre of Annúminas** was a silver rod, originally the symbol of office of the [Lords of Andúnië](/wiki/Category:Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Category:Lords of Andúnië") in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). It survived the [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor"), and was held by the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor) while that realm lasted. After the division of the [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor"), it was handed down through the generations of the [Kings of Arthedain](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain) until the death of the last king, [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"). After that the sceptre was kept in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-ancient_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ancient-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [2951](/wiki/Third_Age_2951 "Third Age 2951"), when [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was twenty years old, [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) gave him the other heirlooms of the House of Elendil, but he withheld the Sceptre saying that Aragorn had yet to earn it.<sup id="cite_ref-tale_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tale-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), when Elrond arrived in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) with his daughter [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), he handed the sceptre to Aragorn, who had been crowned as King Elessar.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> By this time the sceptre was likely the most ancient work of [Men’s](/wiki/Men "Men") hands preserved in Middle-earth, being more than five thousand years old.<sup id="cite_ref-ancient_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ancient-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the reign of Elessar, some lands of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) (former Arnor) such as [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), were said to be under the protection of the **Northern Sceptre**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120"), on his deathbed, Elessar gave the sceptre of Arnor and the [Crown of Gondor](/wiki/Crown_of_Gondor) into the hands of his son, [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion).<sup id="cite_ref-tale_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tale-2">[2]</a></sup>
