The **Seven Dwarf-rings** were the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power) given to seven [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") Lords by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in the guise of [Annatar](/wiki/Annatar).

## History {#History}

Apparently the Lords were the Kings of the Seven Houses, as [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) mentions that the Rings were given to the "Dwarf-kings".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The most famous was the [Ring of Thrór](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r): in Dwarven tradition it was said [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) gave the Ring to [Durin III](/wiki/Durin_III) king of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) before the Downfall of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion),<sup id="cite_ref-a_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-a-2">[2]</a></sup> but this seems unlikely as Celebrimbor was said to have yielded the Seven - all the Seven - to Sauron after torture.<sup id="cite_ref-ring_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ring-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Dwarf Lords proved resistant to the malevolent magic of the rings, as they are hard to tame, and the thoughts of their hearts are hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-ring_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ring-3">[3]</a></sup> The rings, used only for the gaining of wealth, amplified their wearer's natural skills and desire for dominion which as a consequence, made them greedy and exceedingly rich; the Rings gave them the power to multiply whatever they mined.<sup id="cite_ref-a_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-a-2">[2]</a></sup> It is said that thanks to them the [Seven Hoards](/wiki/Seven_Hoards) were made. But also the Rings kindled greed and wrath in the Dwarves, bringing evils that in the long-term benefitted Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-ring_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ring-3">[3]</a></sup>

Four were consumed and destroyed by [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons), but Sauron succeeded in finding two.<sup id="cite_ref-b_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b-5">[5]</a></sup> The last one was worn by [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin"), but in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2845](/wiki/Third_Age_2845 "Third Age 2845") he was imprisoned by Sauron in the dungeons of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), and the ring was taken from him.<sup id="cite_ref-a_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-a-2">[2]</a></sup>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Each of the seven rings has its own card, released in two versions. The first in  <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">Middle-earth: The Wizards</a></i> and then the second in <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Lidless_Eye" title="Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye">Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye</a></i> or <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Against_the_Shadow" title="Middle-earth: Against the Shadow">Middle-earth: Against the Shadow</a></i>):
<ul><li>Dwarven Ring of Dwálin&#39;s Tribe</li>
<li>Dwarven Ring of Barin&#39;s Tribe</li>
<li>Dwarven Ring of Bávor&#39;s Tribe</li>
<li>Dwarven Ring of Thélor&#39;s Tribe</li>
<li>Dwarven Ring of Durin&#39;s Tribe</li>
<li>Dwarven Ring of Thrár&#39;s Tribe</li>
<li>Dwarven Ring of Drúin&#39;s Tribe.</li></ul></dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Seven appear in the introduction of the movie, worn by Dwarf kings. They are designed consistently to the general &#39;Dwarven&#39; aesthetics of the movie, with angular, crystalline shapes. However nowhere in Tolkien&#39;s books is mentioned that the Seven were designed or made for the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Several rings are named:
<ul><li>Ring of Accretion<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></li>
<li>Ring of Fury<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></li>
<li>Ring of Guile<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></li>
<li>Ring of Retribution<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></li>
<li>Ring of Artifice<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></li></ul></dd></dl>

**2012: [The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey):**

<dl><dd>During a meeting of the <a href="/wiki/White_Council" title="White Council">White Council</a>, Gandalf mentions that of the Seven Rings, four were consumed by <a href="/wiki/Dragons" title="Dragons">Dragons</a>, two were taken by <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r" title="Ring of Thrór">the last</a> had vanished with <a href="/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>When <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> encounters Thráin in <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>, he grabs Thrains hand and discovers his ring finger has been cut off. A flashback reveals <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog the Defiler</a> cut off <a href="/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>&#39;s finger and took <a href="/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r" title="Ring of Thrór">his ring</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2017: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Dwarf-rings consumed by dragons were not destroyed, but Sauron had those dragons imprisoned and tortured in an attempt to recover the Rings. The &#34;Ring-drakes&#34;, as they were called, were &#34;Hrímil Frost-heart&#34;, who consumed &#34;Tínya&#34; and the king of the Eastern &#34;Zhélruka&#34; dwarves who bore it; &#34;Thostír the Rank&#34;, who consumed &#34;Tulcya&#34;, the ring of another House in <a href="/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn" title="Rhûn">Rhûn</a>; and &#34;Fingar the Greedy&#34;, a Great Worm of <a href="/wiki/Angband" title="Angband">Angband</a> who consumed &#34;Úrya&#34; and &#34;Vanya&#34;, the Rings of the Ered Luin kingdoms.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Two other rings of the eastern Houses were reclaimed by Sauron: &#34;Taurya&#34; and &#34;Sindya&#34;, the Ring of &#34;Drása&#39;s Folk&#34; (later dubbed &#34;Stout-axes&#34;), who called it &#34;Manthríf&#34;. &#34;Angya&#34; was the name of the Ring of Durin, which was called &#34;Handórm&#34; by the Dwarves.</dd></dl>
