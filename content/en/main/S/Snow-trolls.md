**Snow-troll** was a term used to describe [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan") [Helm](/wiki/Helm) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) when he stalked into the camps of his enemies in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759") during the siege of the [Suthburg](/wiki/Hornburg "Hornburg") in the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter).<sup id="cite_ref-appendix_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appendix-1">[1]</a></sup>

There is no other known reference to the term snow-troll in the works of J.R.R. Tolkien, so it cannot be known whether they existed within [Arda](/wiki/Arda), even as folklore. The passage in which the term appears is noted as being an extract from one of [the sources](/wiki/Category:Books_and_documents_within_the_legendarium "Category:Books and documents within the legendarium") that comprise the [Shire records](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch "Red Book of Westmarch")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

### Television {#Television}

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><b>September 1: <i><a href="/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past" title="A Shadow of the Past">A Shadow of the Past</a></i>:</b>
<dl><dd>While investigating a <a href="/wiki/D%C3%BArnost" title="Dúrnost">fortress</a> located within the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Forodwaith" title="Forodwaith">Forodwaith</a>, <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel&#39;s company</a> is ambushed by a stalking <b>Hristorco</b> (<a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Snow-troll</i>),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-twitter_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-twitter-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> a variation of <a href="/wiki/Trolls" title="Trolls">Trolls</a> that had long ago adapted to live within the harsh and cold climates of the northern realms that they call home.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl></dd></dl>

### Games {#Games}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Snow Trolls are large (10-12 ft tall), grey-white and rare creatures, who prey the <a href="/wiki/Northern_Waste" title="Northern Waste">northern regions</a> of Middle-earth. They turn into pillars of icy slag if exposed to the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Snow Trolls are a playable unit in the <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> faction. They are one of the strongest units, not only in that faction, but in the game overall. They are equipped with pickaxes, helmets and small shields. Snow trolls can be used also in the <i>Create-a-hero</i> section.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar">The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A Snow-troll fought in the army of <a href="/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm_Dourhand" title="Skorgrím Dourhand">Skorgrím Dourhand</a> during the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Battle_of_Edhelion&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Battle of Edhelion (page does not exist)">Battle of Edhelion</a>. He destroyed the city&#39;s gate and tried to kill <a href="/wiki/Dorong%C3%BAr_Whitethorn" title="Dorongúr Whitethorn">Dorongúr Whitethorn</a> and the player, but was slain himself by <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> before he could complete his attempt.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> Other Snow-trolls are found in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Luin" title="Ered Luin">Ered Luin</a> region.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Snow-trolls are encountered on the slopes of Mount <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>. They are implied to be hostile, even to the various Orcs and Trolls gathering at the mountain.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/Lego_The_Hobbit:_The_Video_Game" title="Lego The Hobbit: The Video Game">Lego The Hobbit: The Video Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Big Little Character Pack DLC includes a Snow-Troll as a playable character.</dd></dl>
