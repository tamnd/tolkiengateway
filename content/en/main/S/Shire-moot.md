A **Shire-moot** was a gathering of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) summoned by the [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of the Shire, who was also the master of the Shire-moot. By the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) a Shire-moot was only held in times of emergency.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
