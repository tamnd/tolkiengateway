A **Shire-moot** was a gathering of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) summoned by the [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of the Shire, who was also the master of the Shire-moot. By the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) a Shire-moot was only held in times of emergency.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
