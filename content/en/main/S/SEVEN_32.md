<i><b>SEVEN 32</b></i> is the thirty-second issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2015](../_/2015.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup> This issue celebrates the 50th anniversary of the Wade Center.<sup id="cite_ref-vii_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-3">[3]</a></sup>

Beginning with this issue, the journal is now subtitled "Journal of the Marion E. Wade Center." It was previously "An Anglo-American Literary Review."
