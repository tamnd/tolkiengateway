**Standelf** was a small village in the southern regions of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In a draft of *[A Conspiracy Unmasked](/wiki/A_Conspiracy_Unmasked)*, Standelf is mentioned once (while it is never mentioned in the published text of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*) as traversed by the road "from the [Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge "Brandywine Bridge") to Standelf and [Haysend](/wiki/Haysend)". In the final map, the road stops at Standelf and doesn't continue to Haysend.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "Stone-delving", from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *stán* "stone" and *delf* "a delving or burrow".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests the "Old Hobbitish" form of the name was *Stangedelf*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

There is a place called Stonydelph in Warwickshire of the same etymology.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
