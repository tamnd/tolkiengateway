**Seredic Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).

## History {#History}

Seredic was the son of [Saradas Brandybuck](/wiki/Saradas_Brandybuck). He married [Hilda Bracegirdle](/wiki/Hilda_Bracegirdle) and had three children: [Doderic](/wiki/Doderic_Brandybuck "Doderic Brandybuck"), [Ilberic](/wiki/Ilberic_Brandybuck "Ilberic Brandybuck") and [Celandine](/wiki/Celandine_Brandybuck "Celandine Brandybuck"). They all attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Seredic* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

In an earlier version of the family tree for the [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybucks), the name of Seredic was <i><b>Ceredic</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Ceredic* is a real [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) name and a variant of the name *Caradoc*. <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceredig" title="wikipedia:Ceredig">Ceredic Guletic</a></i> was the son of *Cunedag Guletic*, a chief from the fifth century who drove the Goidels/Gaels from North Wales. *Ceredic Guletic* ruled a region that was named *Ceredigion* after him in Welsh and Cardiganshire in English.<sup id="cite_ref-Allan_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Allan-4">[4]</a></sup>
