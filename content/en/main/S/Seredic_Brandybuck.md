**Seredic Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md).

## History {#History}

Seredic was the son of [Saradas Brandybuck](Saradas_Brandybuck.md). He married [Hilda Bracegirdle](../H/Hilda_Bracegirdle.md) and had three children: [Doderic](../D/Doderic_Brandybuck.md), [Ilberic](../I/Ilberic_Brandybuck.md) and [Celandine](../C/Celandine_Brandybuck.md). They all attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Seredic* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

In an earlier version of the family tree for the [Brandybucks](../B/Brandybucks.md), the name of Seredic was <i><b>Ceredic</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Ceredic* is a real [Welsh](../W/Welsh.md) name and a variant of the name *Caradoc*. <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceredig" title="wikipedia:Ceredig">Ceredic Guletic</a></i> was the son of *Cunedag Guletic*, a chief from the fifth century who drove the Goidels/Gaels from North Wales. *Ceredic Guletic* ruled a region that was named *Ceredigion* after him in Welsh and Cardiganshire in English.<sup id="cite_ref-Allan_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Allan-4">[4]</a></sup>
