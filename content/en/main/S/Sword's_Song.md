<i><b>Sword&#39;s Song</b></i> is the second full-length album released by the Epic metal band [Battlelore](/wiki/Battlelore). The cover features *The Slaying of Glaurung* by [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), used with permission. It was released in July 22, [2003](/wiki/2003).
