<i><b>Sword&#39;s Song</b></i> is the second full-length album released by the Epic metal band [Battlelore](../B/Battlelore.md). The cover features *The Slaying of Glaurung* by [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md), used with permission. It was released in July 22, [2003](../_/2003.md).
