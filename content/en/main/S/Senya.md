<i><b>senya</b></i> or <i><b>senwa</b></i> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) means "usual".<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology of *senya*/*senwa* is unknown. [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) has suggested that it contains the verbal stem <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sen (page does not exist)">sen</a></i> "let loose, free, let go".<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-1">[1]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- *[sanya](Sanya.md)* ("regular, normal")<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<i><b>senya</b></i> also appears in the chapter *[The Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../T/The_Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md)*, although unglossed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It has been suggested that it could mean "my son", as it is spoken by [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) in response to his son [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>)'s use of the word *[atarinya](../A/Atarinya.md)*, or "my father".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
