**South Gondor**, known in [Sindarin](Sindarin.md) as **Harondor**, was a region south of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-West_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The borders of South Gondor were the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-West_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-1">[1]</a></sup> and the river [Poros](../P/Poros.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-3">[3]</a></sup> in the north, the river [Harnen](../H/Harnen.md) in the south,<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md)<sup id="cite_ref-West_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-1">[1]</a></sup> and the river Harnen<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> in the east and the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md)<sup id="cite_ref-West_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-1">[1]</a></sup> in the west. It was described as a "desert land" at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-West_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-1">[1]</a></sup> However, it is possible that this meant that it was a land that was deserted by its inhabitants because of the wars between [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) and the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> It is possible that South Gondor had a climate that was fluctuating between mild winters and very hot and dry summers.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

South Gondor was absorbed by Gondor during the height of its power, probably the expansions of [Tarannon Falastur](../T/Tarannon_Falastur.md), who extended the sway of Gondor along the coasts west and south of the Mouths of Anduin<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

South Gondor remained a part of Gondor until the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md) after which it became a debatable land between the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) and the [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>

During the days of [Steward](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md) [Túrin II](../T/Túrin_II.md), the Haradrim occupied South Gondor and there was much fighting along the [Poros](../P/Poros.md). When they started invading [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md), many [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) came to the support of Gondor so that Túrin won against the Haradrim at the [Crossings of Poros](../C/Crossings_of_Poros.md)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup> in ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2885](../T/Third_Age_2885.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[10]</a></sup> name *Harondor* means "South Gondor",<sup id="cite_ref-RC_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-13">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup> being a compound of the prefixal form *har-* of *[harn](../H/Harn.md)* ("south") and the [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) form of *Gondor*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

J.R.R. Tolkien wrote in notes for the Dutch translator of The Lord of the Rings that the label "South Gondor (Harondor)" on the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) was an error and that the label should have been "Harondor (South Gondor)".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-13">[11]</a></sup>
