**Shiro No Norite** (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kanji" title="wikipedia:Kanji">Kanji</a>: **白の乗手**, "The White Rider") is the Japanese [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md). Founded in [1981](../_/1981.md), they have three publications: *Elanor*, the monthly bulletin; *Shiro No Norite*, which publishes research, and *Kuro No Norite* ("The Black Rider"), a journal discussing other fantasy. Unlike some other societies' publications, *Shiro No Norite* publishes solely in Japanese. It has one smial, *Cuiviénen*.

In 2011 it showed activity at Shinshu University.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> A(n) (expected) member wrote about the society on their blog in 2017.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The smial website, which is the only website found, shows no activity after 2018. It is unclear whether this society is now defunct.
