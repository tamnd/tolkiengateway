The **Straight Road** was the route that left the [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth)'s curvature through sky and space to the ethereal land of [Aman](/wiki/Aman).

## History {#History}

The Straight Road, so called because it followed the old path across [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) from before the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) when the Flat World was [made Round](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World "Changing of the World"), was only kept open to [Elves](/wiki/Elves), who were allowed to sail to it on their ships by a special grace of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

By the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the Elves would take ships to follow the Straight Road and return to Aman from the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) or [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Some mortals were allowed to take the Straight Road to Aman. [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) sailed into the West on the [White Ship](/wiki/White_Ship).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) were also permitted to depart for Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In earlier versions of the legendarium, [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) of England found the Straight Road and visited [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) Ages later.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

[Smith](/wiki/Smith_Smithson "Smith Smithson") appears to have found a conceptually similar road, leading him to the land of "[Faery](/wiki/Faery)".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
