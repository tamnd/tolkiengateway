The **Straight Road** was the route that left the [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)'s curvature through sky and space to the ethereal land of [Aman](../A/Aman.md).

## History {#History}

The Straight Road, so called because it followed the old path across [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) from before the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) when the Flat World was [made Round](../C/Changing_of_the_World.md), was only kept open to [Elves](../E/Elves.md), who were allowed to sail to it on their ships by a special grace of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

By the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) the Elves would take ships to follow the Straight Road and return to Aman from the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) or [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Some mortals were allowed to take the Straight Road to Aman. [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) sailed into the West on the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later [Samwise Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) were also permitted to depart for Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In earlier versions of the legendarium, [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) of England found the Straight Road and visited [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) Ages later.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

[Smith](Smith_Smithson.md) appears to have found a conceptually similar road, leading him to the land of "[Faery](../F/Faery.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
