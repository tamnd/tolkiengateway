**Stock** was a town in the north of the [Marish](../M/Marish.md), lying on the [Stock-brook](Stock-brook.md) stream near to the banks of the River [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) and south of the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md) in the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). The [Causeway](../C/Causeway.md) traversed Stock, and above the town it branched to a road<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> that led to [Tuckborough](../T/Tuckborough.md) to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 88</sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> *[The Golden Perch](../T/The_Golden_Perch.md)* in Stock was said to have the finest beer in the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md). The [Puddifoots](../P/Puddifoot_Family.md) of Stock were house-dwellers, as most folk of the Marish.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 92</sup>

During [Frodo's](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) journey to [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md), Frodo announced that he wished to straight across country to the [Bucklebury Ferry](../B/Bucklebury_Ferry.md). [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) objected to the short cut, and mentioned the *Golden Perch*. One more reason to avoid Stock was in order to keep Pippin away from there and prevent their journey from being delayed.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 88</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name possibly represents a modernization of [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *Stoc* "place, outlying farm or hamlet".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
