**Stock** was a town in the north of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish), lying on the [Stock-brook](/wiki/Stock-brook) stream near to the banks of the River [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) and south of the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). The [Causeway](/wiki/Causeway) traversed Stock, and above the town it branched to a road<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> that led to [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough) to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 88</sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> *[The Golden Perch](/wiki/The_Golden_Perch)* in Stock was said to have the finest beer in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing). The [Puddifoots](/wiki/Puddifoot_Family "Puddifoot Family") of Stock were house-dwellers, as most folk of the Marish.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 92</sup>

During [Frodo's](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") journey to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), Frodo announced that he wished to straight across country to the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry). [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") objected to the short cut, and mentioned the *Golden Perch*. One more reason to avoid Stock was in order to keep Pippin away from there and prevent their journey from being delayed.<sup id="cite_ref-short_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-2">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:p. 88</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name possibly represents a modernization of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Stoc* "place, outlying farm or hamlet".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
