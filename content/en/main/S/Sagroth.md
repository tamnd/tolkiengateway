**Sagroth** was one of the guards that [Hardang](../H/Hardang.md), the [Warden of Brethil](../H/Halad.md), had assigned to keep watch at the [Haudh-en-Elleth](../H/Haudh-en-Elleth.md). He was the first to spot the sleeping [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) when he had come to that place. A fellow guard, [Forhend](../F/Forhend.md), said that Sagroth could be trusted to always find trouble, for he knew that Hardang would not be pleased with the discovery of Húrin within Brethil.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later, when Hardang wanted to send [Manthor](../M/Manthor.md) away while he dealt with Húrin, he told him to go until Captain [Avranc](../A/Avranc.md) came to take command (this was an on-the-spot promotion of Avranc). Manthor immediately quit his service to Hardang and stated that he had left Sagroth, "a woodman older and wiser" than Avranc in command.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

When "[The Wanderings of Húrin](../T/The_Wanderings_of_Húrin.md)" was in draft form, in the second appearance of Sagroth's name it was emended to *[Galhir](../G/Galhir.md)* but then returned to *Sagroth*. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) speculates that this wasn't a change of name, but an introduction of a new character in the story.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
