<i><b>Sotheby&#39;s English Literature, History, Children&#39;s Books, Illustrations and Photographs 8 July 2004</b></i> is an auction catalogue from [Sotheby's](Sotheby's.md) (London). The catalogue includes several [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related lots, the most notable being:

- 379 (p. 232) - This lot consists of [twelve letters from Tolkien to Dorothy "Ding" Wood](../L/Letters_to_Dorothy_Wood.md), dating from 1960 to 1973. The lot description includes extracts from many of the letters. The lot also includes letters from [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) and [Priscilla Tolkien](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md).
- 381 (p. 234) - A proof copy of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* dating from [1953](../_/1953.md). The lot description quotes an earlier version of the Ring verse ("...and in the shadow bind them") and goes on to record a number of differences between the proof copy and the published book. The title page is reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- 383 (p. 236) - The lot consists of copies of the first authorized paperback edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (published by [Ballantine Books](../B/Ballantine_Books.md)), from the library of J.R.R. Tolkien. The lot description briefly describes Tolkien's annotations in the books, and quotes a key written by Tolkien.
