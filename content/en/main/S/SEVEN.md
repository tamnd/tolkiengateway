<i><b>SEVEN: An Anglo-American Literary Review</b></i> (also spelled <i><b>VII: An Anglo-American Literary Review</b></i>) is an annual journal of scholarship on seven British authors: [Owen Barfield](../O/Owen_Barfield.md), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.K._Chesterton" title="wikipedia:G.K. Chesterton">G.K. Chesterton</a>, [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), [George MacDonald](../G/George_MacDonald.md), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_L._Sayers" title="wikipedia:Dorothy L. Sayers">Dorothy L. Sayers</a>, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The journal was founded in [1980](../_/1980.md) by Dr. [Clyde S. Kilby](../C/Clyde_S._Kilby.md), Dr. Barbara Reynolds and Dr. Beatrice Batson, and is published by the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The journal has published 40 issues.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

While most of the volumes include at least one article related to Tolkien, two issues are noteworthy for containing formerly [unpublished quotes](../../index/U/Unpublished_material.md) from [writings by Tolkien](../../index/W/Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md):

- [VII: An Anglo-American Literary Review, Volume 17](SEVEN_17.md) ([2000](../_/2000.md))
- [VII: An Anglo-American Literary Review, Volume 27](SEVEN_27.md) ([2010](../_/2010.md))
