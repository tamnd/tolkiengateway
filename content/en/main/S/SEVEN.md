<i><b>SEVEN: An Anglo-American Literary Review</b></i> (also spelled <i><b>VII: An Anglo-American Literary Review</b></i>) is an annual journal of scholarship on seven British authors: [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G.K._Chesterton" title="wikipedia:G.K. Chesterton">G.K. Chesterton</a>, [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), [George MacDonald](/wiki/George_MacDonald), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_L._Sayers" title="wikipedia:Dorothy L. Sayers">Dorothy L. Sayers</a>, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), and [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The journal was founded in [1980](/wiki/1980) by Dr. [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby), Dr. Barbara Reynolds and Dr. Beatrice Batson, and is published by the [Marion E. Wade Center](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The journal has published 40 issues.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

While most of the volumes include at least one article related to Tolkien, two issues are noteworthy for containing formerly [unpublished quotes](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material") from [writings by Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Writings by J.R.R. Tolkien"):

- [VII: An Anglo-American Literary Review, Volume 17](/wiki/SEVEN_17 "SEVEN 17") ([2000](/wiki/2000))
- [VII: An Anglo-American Literary Review, Volume 27](/wiki/SEVEN_27 "SEVEN 27") ([2010](/wiki/2010))
