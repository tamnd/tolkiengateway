**Stephen A. Daniele** (born [3 October](../_/3_October.md) [1966](../_/1966.md)) is an American fantasy artist who illustrated for [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md) and other [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) publications. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

While generally working in traditionally media, he also began working digitally after [2000](../_/2000.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
