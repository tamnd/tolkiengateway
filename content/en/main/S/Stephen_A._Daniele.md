**Stephen A. Daniele** (born [3 October](/wiki/3_October) [1966](/wiki/1966)) is an American fantasy artist who illustrated for [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine) and other [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons) publications. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

While generally working in traditionally media, he also began working digitally after [2000](/wiki/2000).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
