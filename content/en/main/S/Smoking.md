**Smoking** was an activity or custom begun by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) (who referred to it as an 'art'). It consisted of inhaling the smoke of the burning leaves of a herb called *[Pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed)*.

The [Hobbits of](/wiki/Bree-hobbits "Bree-hobbits") [Bree](/wiki/Bree) claimed to be the first to have smoked the Pipe-weed, but the first who cultivated it was [Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower) of [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), around the year [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1070](/wiki/Third_Age_2670 "Third Age 2670"). Smoking had spread from the Hobbits to [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"), and [Wizards](/wiki/Wizards).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was an avid pipe smoker. He attributed his addiction to his childhood, when he watched Father [Francis Xavier Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan) smoking his pipe in a specific location each time.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

People who had met Tolkien noticed that a pipe was almost always in his hand or mouth, often making difficult to understand his speech.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In a [1966 interview](/wiki/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits "The Man Who Understands Hobbits"), Tolkien said: "Every morning I wake up thinking, 'Good, another 24 hours of smoking'".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

An [auctioned cigar bill](</wiki/Cigar_bill_(3_March_1972)> "Cigar bill (3 March 1972)") from early [1972](/wiki/1972) shows that Tolkien favoured *Capstan Medium Navy Cut*.
