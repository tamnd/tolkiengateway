**Saint Brendan**, also known as *Brendan of Clonfert* and *Brendanus*, was an Irish monk who made a legendary journey to the western sea and found utopian lands. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was aware of this legend and made references to it.

The concept of [Aman](/wiki/Aman) as the Paradise in the West, and the voyage of [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), are inspired from the tale of St. Brendan. Tolkien even wrote a poem *[Imram](/wiki/Imram)* about him.

Brendan's voyage is referenced again in *[The Notion Club Papers](/wiki/The_Notion_Club_Papers)* as well as the search for the West.
