**Saint Brendan**, also known as *Brendan of Clonfert* and *Brendanus*, was an Irish monk who made a legendary journey to the western sea and found utopian lands. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was aware of this legend and made references to it.

The concept of [Aman](../A/Aman.md) as the Paradise in the West, and the voyage of [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md), are inspired from the tale of St. Brendan. Tolkien even wrote a poem *[Imram](../I/Imram.md)* about him.

Brendan's voyage is referenced again in *[The Notion Club Papers](../T/The_Notion_Club_Papers.md)* as well as the search for the West.
