<i><b>SEVEN 12</b></i> is the twelfth issue of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [1995](../_/1995.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup> Volume 12 celebrates the 30th anniversary of the Wade Center.<sup id="cite_ref-vii_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Wade Center 30th Anniversary"
    - By: Christopher W. Mitchell
- "*Lilith*: A Dark Labyrinth Towards the Light"
    - By: Giorgio Spina
- "Worship in the Anglican Church"
    - By: Dorothy L. Sayers
- "Under the Mercy: An Introduction to Charles Williams"
    - By: Nancy E. Topolewski
- "Creating and Re-creating Worlds with Words: The Religion and the Magic of Language in *The Lord of the Rings*"
    - By: Mary Zimmer
- "'Making the Poor Best of Dull Things': C.S. Lewis as Poet"
    - By: Don W. King
- "The Image in the Mirror: Americans in Wimsey's Acquaintance"
    - By: Petra-Angela Wacker
- Review Essay:
    - "The History of Middle-earth"
        - By: [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md)<sup id="cite_ref-vii_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vii-3">[3]</a></sup>
