**Sigismond Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Sigismond was the son of [Hildibrand Took](/wiki/Hildibrand_Took). He had two children, [Rosamunda](/wiki/Rosamunda_Took "Rosamunda Took") and [Ferdinand](/wiki/Ferdinand_Took "Ferdinand Took").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Took family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The name <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigismund" title="wikipedia:Sigismund">Sigismond</a></i> contains *sigis* ("victory") and *mund* ("hand" "protection").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
