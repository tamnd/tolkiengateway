An [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), addressed as **Snaga**, was part of the garrison of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol). He was one of the few survivors of the [Battle of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol)<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-1">[1]</a></sup> on [14 March](/wiki/14_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Snaga was running down the stairs of the tower when he encountered what he thought was a "[Great Elf-warrior](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")" on the stairs. Spinning around, he fled back the way he came. The "Great Elf-warrior" was actually [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) who followed the Orc, although being tired he fell behind his foe.

Snaga came upon [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat), who ordered him to send word to [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) that they captured a [Halfling](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"). Snaga refused, and after a short chase, made his way to the top of the tower. There, he lashed the prisoner, [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), with a whip. He was quickly overcome by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), and fell through the trap door to his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Tower_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tower-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Snaga was a contemptuous term for the lesser [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), used especially among the larger and stronger [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai). It comes from a word in the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) meaning 'slave' or 'servant'.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1980: <a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Two Orcs appear in the role of Snaga, though none are called or credited as such. Sam encounters one on the stairs, who runs, trips and falls to his doom. A second orc is lashing Frodo. After a short fight, he too falls to his doom.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Snaga was voiced by <a href="/wiki/Gordon_Reid" title="Gordon Reid">Gordon Reid</a>.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1989" title="1989">1989</a>: <a href="/wiki/Crack_of_Doom_Software_Adventure" title="Crack of Doom Software Adventure"><i>Crack of Doom</i> Software Adventure<b></b></a></b>

<dl><dd>Snaga is simply referred to in-game as a &#34;small rat-faced Orc&#34;, and is armed with a short knife. Like in the book, he will flee upon first seeing Sam, leading the player on a short chase through the tower until he reaches the &#34;<a href="/wiki/Shagrat" title="Shagrat">large angry Orc</a>&#34;. He can be killed by Sam.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Like in the animated film, Snaga&#39;s role is seemingly filled by more than one Orc. First, Sam encounters a trio of Uruk-hai on the stairs, before slaying all three of them. Later, <a href="/wiki/Gorbag" title="Gorbag">Gorbag</a> (who survives the battle in this adaptation) fulfills the latter role as the Orc that threatens Frodo before being killed by Sam.</dd></dl>
