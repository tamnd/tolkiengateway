The **Sirannon**, or **Gate-stream**, was a stream in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

The Sirannon flowed from its springs near the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin), the West-gate of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) (hence its name) along the highroad to [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> to the city [Ost-in-Edhil](/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It originally ran through a shallow valley that lay in front of the West-gate and then dropped through a wide cleft down the [Stair Falls](/wiki/Stair_Falls) over a thirty-foot high cliff. It then continued as a swift and noisy river through a deep, narrow channel over a river bed consisting of brown and red stained stones, with the highroad on its northern side.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-4">[4]</a></sup> It is possible that the Sirannon flowed into the nearest river, [Glanduin](/wiki/Glanduin), which flowed from the western side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) south of the Doors of Durin to the river [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At some point in time, probably between the time that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had been to Moria to search for [Thráin](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in) after his disappearance in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2845](/wiki/Third_Age_2845 "Third Age 2845")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the time that [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) had been there,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> maybe during his great journey and errantries from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2957](/wiki/Third_Age_2957 "Third Age 2957") until [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and the end of the failed attempt of the dwarven expedition of [Balin](/wiki/Balin) to resettle Moria in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA2994_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2994-8">[8]</a></sup> someone or something had dammed the river so that a dark, still lake filled the entire valley before the West-gate of Moria, and hardly a trickle of water flowed down the Stair Falls into the channel.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-4">[4]</a></sup>

At the time of the expedition of Balin to Moria between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989")<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and [2994](/wiki/Third_Age_2994 "Third Age 2994"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA2994_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2994-8">[8]</a></sup> the lake reached the wall of the West-gate and the [Watcher in the Water](/wiki/Watcher_in_the_Water) in the lake took the dwarf [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in), which prevented the dwarves from getting out of Moria through the West-gate.<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-10">[10]</a></sup>

On [13 January](/wiki/13_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) reached the channel with the river bed and were surprised that the river was reduced to a trickle. They travelled upriver on the road next to the river, climbed the stairs cut into the rock beside the Stair Falls, and discovered that the Sirannon had been dammed. There was now a lake so wide that the great roots of two very large holly trees next to the West-gate reached from the wall of the cliff to the water of the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-4">[4]</a></sup> After [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) threw a stone into the lake, the Watcher in the Water nearly caught the Ring-bearer [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins). The Fellowship of the Ring fled from his attack into Moria, but the Watcher in the Water, with a noise of rending and crashing, closed the doors of the West-gate with its tentacles, thus blocking the exit through the Doors of Durin.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

Moria was eventually resettled by [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Fourth_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fourth-13">[13]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that the dam was removed and that the original course of the Sirannon was restored.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Sirannon is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-14">[14]</a></sup> It literally means "stream of the Gate". The [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name **Gate-stream** is its translation.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-2">[2]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that the name is a combination of *[sîr](/wiki/S%C3%AEr "Sîr")* ("river", "stream") and *[annon](/wiki/Annon "Annon")* ("(great) gate", "door").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-14">[14]</a></sup>

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The waters of Sirannon still flow into the Black Pool before the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Door_of_Durin" title="Door of Durin">Door of Durin</a> but not out - instead the water seeps through its bottom into the Water-works of <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a>. The dry riverbed of Sirannon&#39;s former course can be followed to the point of meeting with <a href="/wiki/Glanduin" title="Glanduin">Glanduin</a>.</dd></dl>
