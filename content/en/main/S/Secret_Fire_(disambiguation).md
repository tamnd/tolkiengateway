**Secret Fire** refers to more than one concept:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Secret Fire](/wiki/Secret_Fire)**, Ilúvatar's creative power, also known as the **Flame Imperishable**.
- [Flame of Anor](/wiki/Flame_of_Anor), the pure light of the Sun, named by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) along with the Secret Fire.
