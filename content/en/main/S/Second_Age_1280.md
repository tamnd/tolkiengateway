**Second Age 1280** (abbreviated to **S.A. 1280**) is the 1280th [year of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) of the [Second Age](Second_Age.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Notable events in this year include:

- [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md) surrenders the [Sceptre](Sceptre_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Tar-Anárion](../T/Tar-Anárion.md), son of Tar-Ancalimë, becomes [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
