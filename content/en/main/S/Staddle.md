**Staddle** was a village in [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land).<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located on the gentler south-eastern slopes of [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill).<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup> Staddle was the main settlement of the [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> Pipe-weed grew on the south slopes of Bree-hill.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> there were some hobbits of the the [Underhill Family](/wiki/Underhill_Family) from Staddle in *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony)* in [Bree](/wiki/Bree). That night [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) arrived at the inn and Frodo introduced himself as "[Mr. Underhill](/wiki/Mr._Underhill)" to [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur), the innkeeper. When Barliman introduced "Mr. Underhill" to the other guests in the common-room, he attracted their interest.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Frodo and his companions left with [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") the [next morning](/wiki/30_September "30 September")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and most of the inhabitants from Staddle were crowded on the road in Bree to watch their departure.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Having walked for some miles on the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road), Aragorn and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) could see later the houses and [hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-holes "Hobbit-holes") north of the East Road on the south-eastern slopes of Bree-hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Bree was raided early in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") by [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") from the south during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). Three Men and two Hobbits from Bree-land, among them an Underhill from Staddle, were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Staddle* is a dialectical English name, which is derived from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *stathol* (*staðol*), which means "foundation" (of buildings, sheds, ricks, etc.) and occurs in real-life English place-names.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
