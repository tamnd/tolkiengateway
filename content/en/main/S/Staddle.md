**Staddle** was a village in [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> It was located on the gentler south-eastern slopes of [Bree-hill](../B/Bree-hill.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup> Staddle was the main settlement of the [Bree-hobbits](../B/Bree-hobbits.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> Pipe-weed grew on the south slopes of Bree-hill.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [29 September](../_/29_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> there were some hobbits of the the [Underhill Family](../U/Underhill_Family.md) from Staddle in *[The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md)* in [Bree](../B/Bree.md). That night [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Samwise Gamgee](Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) arrived at the inn and Frodo introduced himself as "[Mr. Underhill](../M/Mr._Underhill.md)" to [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md), the innkeeper. When Barliman introduced "Mr. Underhill" to the other guests in the common-room, he attracted their interest.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Frodo and his companions left with [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) the [next morning](../_/30_September.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and most of the inhabitants from Staddle were crowded on the road in Bree to watch their departure.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Having walked for some miles on the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md), Aragorn and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) could see later the houses and [hobbit-holes](../H/Hobbit-holes.md) north of the East Road on the south-eastern slopes of Bree-hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Bree was raided early in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) by [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) from the south during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). Three Men and two Hobbits from Bree-land, among them an Underhill from Staddle, were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Staddle* is a dialectical English name, which is derived from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *stathol* (*staðol*), which means "foundation" (of buildings, sheds, ricks, etc.) and occurs in real-life English place-names.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
