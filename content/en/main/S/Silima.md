<i><b>Silima</b></i> was the crystaline substance invented by [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) to create the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils). Only he could make it.<sup id="cite_ref-Notes_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notes-1">[1]</a></sup>

"Like the crystal of diamonds it appeared, and yet was more strong than adamant, so that no violence could mar it or break it within the Kingdom of Arda." The Silmarils held the merged [light](/wiki/Light "Light") of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), so this crystal was like the [body](/wiki/Hr%C3%B6a "Hröa") which holds the [spirit](/wiki/F%C3%ABa "Fëa") in the case of the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar). It was said that the secret of the substance from which it was made shall be only known when Fëanor returns in [the End](/wiki/The_End "The End"), after the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon) have passed away.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Silima* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and contains the element *[sil-](/wiki/Sil- "Sil-")* ("shine (with white or silver light)").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is an element in both *[silma-ril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils")* and *[silivren](/wiki/Silivren "Silivren")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Notes_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notes-1">[1]</a></sup>
