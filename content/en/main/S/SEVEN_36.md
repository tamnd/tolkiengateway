<i><b>SEVEN 36</b></i> is the thirty-sixth volume of the [Marion E. Wade Center](../M/Marion_E._Wade_Center.md)'s annual journal *[SEVEN](SEVEN.md)*, published in [2019](../_/2019.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jstor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jstor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Through the Screen: Dorothy L. Sayers's Journey into New Worlds"
    - By: Crystal Downing
- "The (Revised) Clinical Imagination: An Unpublished Appendix to The Problem of Pain"
    - By: Sarah O'Dell
- "Pain and Behaviour in Medical Practice": Robert E. Havard's Draft of the Appendix to The Problem of Pain"
    - By: Robert E. Havard
- "The Conflicted Jewish Imagination of Joy Davidman"
    - By: Andrew Barron
- "Introduction to Joy Davidman's Sermon 'Chosen for What?'"
    - By: Andrew Barron
- "Chosen for What? (The Problem of the Christian Jew)"
    - By: Joy Davidman
- "Repenting of Scientism: The Critical Function of Dante in George MacDonald's Lilith"
    - By: Michael Wayne Wilhelm
- "C. S. Lewis the Churchman: His Work on the Anglican Commission to Revise the Psalter"
    - By: Joel D. Heck
- "Is Mary Jane?--Mary Neylan as a Model for Jane Studdock in That Hideous Strength"
    - By: David C. Downing
- Review Essays:
    - "Something to Chew On: Rethinking G.K. Chesterton"
        - By: Crystal Downing
- Book Reviews:
    - "Review of The Fame of C.S. Lewis: A Controversialist's Reception in Britain and America by Stephanie Derrick"
        - By: Suzanne Bray
    - "Review of Doors In: The Fairy Tale World of George MacDonald by Rolland Hein"
        - By: Monika B. Hilder
    - "Review of Further Up and Further In: Orthodox Conversations with C.S. Lewis on Scripture and Theology by Edith M. Humphrey"
        - By: Andrew Cuneo
    - "Review of George MacDonald in the Age of Miracles: Incarnation, Doubt, and Reenchantment by Timothy Larsen"
        - By: Olga Lukmanova
    - "Review of C.S. Lewis's Mere Christianity by George M. Marsden"
        - By: Grayson Carter
    - "Review of "Esotericism and Narrative: The Occult Fiction of Charles Williams by Aren Roukema"
        - By: David Llewellyn Dodds
    - "Review of The Downstretched Hand: Individual Development in George MacDonald's Major Fantasies for Children by Lesley Willis Smith"
        - By: Bethany Bear Hebbard
    - "Review of C.S. Lewis on Christian Life: Becoming Truly Human in the Presence of God by Joe Rigney"
        - By: Michael Tofte
    - "Review of Systematic Mythology: Imaging the Invisible" by Jennifer Agee"
        - By: Aaron M. Hill
    - "Review of Pagan Saints in Middle-earth by Claudio A. Testi"
        - By: Laura Schmidt
- Booknotes
    - Sudden Heaven: The Collected Poems of Ruth Pitter, a Critical Edition
        - By: Don W, King, ed.
    - The Lion in the Waste Land: Fearsome Reputation in the Work of C.S. Lewis, Dorothy L. Sayers, and T.S. Eliot
        - By: Janice Brown
    - The Metaphysical Mysteries of G.K. Chesterton: A Critical Study of the Father Brown Stories and Other Detective Fiction
