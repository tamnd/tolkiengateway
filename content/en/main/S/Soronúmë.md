**Soronúmë** was one of the constellations that [Varda](/wiki/Varda) traced in the sky with the dews of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), before the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The name means "Eagle of the West" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[soron](/wiki/Soron "Soron")*, "[eagle](/wiki/Eagle "Eagle")" and *[númë](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen "Númen")*, "<a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West_(disambiguation)" title="West (disambiguation)">West</a>").

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The translation suggests a connection with the constellation we know today as <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquila_%28constellation%29" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Aquila</a>, but this was never confirmed by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"). The constellation now known as Lyra may also be considered, as its former name in Arabic was *an-nasr al-wāqi‘* "falling eagle" (giving its name to its main star <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vega" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Vega</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
