The **San Diego Comic-Con: International** is the largest comic convention in the world and always delights [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) fans by having numerous Tolkien-related collectibles and events. This 2001 convention was prior to the release of [The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) and showcased a surge of movie-related collectibles.
