<i><b>Winter&#39;s Tales for Children 1</b></i>, illustrated by Hugh Marshall, is a children's book published in [1965](/wiki/1965), in which two of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poems were first published: <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Dragon%27s_Visit" title="The Dragon&#39;s Visit">The Dragon&#39;s Visit</a></b></i> (pp. 84–87) and <i><b><a href="/wiki/Once_upon_a_Time" title="Once upon a Time">Once upon a Time</a></b></i> (pp. 44–45).

A similar edition was published in the United States by St. Martin's Press.

Tolkien also submitted a third piece (a [poem for Rosalind Ramage](/wiki/Rosalind_Ramage "Rosalind Ramage")) for inclusion in the anthology, which eventually was omitted due to lack of space.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
