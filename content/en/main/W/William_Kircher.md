**William Kircher** (born 23 May, 1958) is a New Zealand actor who portrayed [Bifur](/wiki/Bifur) in the [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> as well as <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tom" title="Tom">Tom</a> the [Troll](/wiki/Troll) in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*. Kircher attended the Toi Whakaari New Zealand Drama School, graduating with a Diploma at the age of 18 in 1976. He later married Nicole Chesterman Kircher, whom he shares four daughters with.

## Trivia {#Trivia}

Kircher lied about his age when applying to Toi Whakaari, being only 16 at the time of his admission.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A prolific stage actor, Kircher has acted in more than 100 plays and at least 30 screen projects.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
