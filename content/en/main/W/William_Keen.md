**William Walter Maurice Keen**, also known as **Will Keen**, is an English actor. He portrayed Lord Belzagar in the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
