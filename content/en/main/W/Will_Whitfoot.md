**Will Whitfoot** was the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Whitfoot was elected Mayor at the [Free Fair](/wiki/Free_Fair) late in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), at least as late as [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1413](/wiki/Third_Age_3013 "Third Age 3013").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

He was renowned as being the fattest hobbit in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing), and was affectionately referred to as "Flourdumpling" after he emerged from the collapse of the [Town Hole](/wiki/Town_Hole) covered in chalk. [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) later caused a great deal of laughter at the [Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony) when he related that incident to the [Bree-folk](/wiki/Bree-folk).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

He was one of the first victims of the new rule of [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) and [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey). He went to protest against Lotho's felling of trees, but was apprehended by [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) on his way to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). He was locked up in the [Lockholes](/wiki/Lockholes), and Lotho usurped his title.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> He was freed after the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), but was at first unfit for duty as Mayor, so [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) served as Deputy Mayor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> He was officially restored as Mayor on the next Free Fair, on [Midyear's Day](/wiki/Midyear%27s_Day) of [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1420](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020").<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[4]</a></sup>

Will Whitfoot resigned in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1427](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6"), and was succeeded by [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name "Whitfoot" is derived from "White foot".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
