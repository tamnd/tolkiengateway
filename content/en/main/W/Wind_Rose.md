**Wind Rose** is an Italian power metal band founded in [2009](/wiki/2009) in Pisa, Tuscany. The band uses pickaxes, dwarven axes and dwarven costumes as their aesthetics. Because of the aesthetics and the band's sound, the genre they perform is often referred to as *dwarf* or *dwarven metal*.<sup id="cite_ref-newsroom_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-newsroom-1">[1]</a></sup>

Before focusing on Dwarves in particular, the aim of their first album, *Shadows over Lothadruin* ([2012](/wiki/2012)), was to create *“a Lord of the Rings-like story”*. While the band now themes itself after fantasy Dwarves in the broadest sense, some of their songs contain more explicit references to Tolkien's Dwarves in particular. Their first dwarf-centered song, *The Breed of Durin*, on their second album *Wardens of the West Wind* ([2015](/wiki/2015)), refers to the [retaking of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor").<sup id="cite_ref-newsroom_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-newsroom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*To Erebor*, from their [2017](/wiki/2017) album, *Stonehymn*, goes a step further and is explicitly written from the perspective of the Dwarven army in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), whereas *The Returning Race* on the same album seems to be written from the perspective of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Having some Tolkien-themed songs per album continued in [2019](/wiki/2019)'s *Wintersaga* with the songs *There and Back Again* and *The King Under the Mountain*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, as well as in [2022](/wiki/2022)'s *Warfront* with *The Battle of the Five Armies*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
