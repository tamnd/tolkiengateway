**Wolfe Morris** ([January 5](../_/5_January.md), [1925](../_/1925.md) - [July 21](../_/21_July.md), [1996](../_/1996.md)) was an English actor. His major roles were Padmasambhava in the Dr. Who story arch <i><a class="external text" href="http://tardis.wikia.com/wiki/The_Abominable_Snowmen" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Abominable Snowmen</a></i> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Cromwell,_1st_Earl_of_Essex" title="wikipedia:Thomas Cromwell, 1st Earl of Essex">Thomas Cromwell</a> in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Six_Wives_of_Henry_VIII_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:The Six Wives of Henry VIII (TV series)">The Six Wives of Henry VIII</a></i>. He also provided the voice of [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) in [BBC 4](../B/BBC.md)'s 1968 radio series *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>)*.
