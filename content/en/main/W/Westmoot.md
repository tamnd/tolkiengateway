<b><a class="mw-selflink selflink">Westmoot</a></b> is a convention, inspired by the success of [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot), aimed at US members of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). The inaugural event took place in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri" title="wikipedia:Kansas City, Missouri">Kansas City, Missouri</a> on Friday [May 9](/wiki/May_9) - Sunday [May 11](/wiki/May_11), [2025](/wiki/2025) at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_World_War_I_Museum_and_Memorial" title="wikipedia:National World War I Museum and Memorial">National World War I Museum and Memorial</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The event features a range of presentations, including talks, quizzes, lectures, workshops, performances, papers, discussions, and entertainment, providing a rich and engaging experience for all attendees.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg/300px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="337" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/c/c8/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg/450px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/c/c8/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg/600px-Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil_-_Westmoot_2026_design.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="898"/></a><figcaption>Westmoot 2026 t-shirt design by <a href="/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil" title="Matěj Čadil">Matěj Čadil</a></figcaption></figure>

Westmoot 2026 will be held on May 22nd-24th in Minneapolis, Minnesota at the Royal Sonesta Minneapolis Downtown.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The official t-shirt for Westmoot 2026 was designed by [Matěj Čadil](/wiki/Mat%C4%9Bj_%C4%8Cadil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
