**Werewolves** were servants of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), fell beasts ([wolves](Wolves.md)) inhabited by dreadful spirits that Sauron had imprisoned in their bodies.<sup id="cite_ref-S19_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Werewolves were created (or a least corrupted from some other form) by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), who was their master, and who took the shape of a great [wolf](Wolves.md) himself at least once.<sup id="cite_ref-S19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S19-1">[1]</a></sup>

The first werewolf was [Draugluin](../D/Draugluin.md), and the greatest was [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md), the guardian of [Angband](../A/Angband.md), a descendant of Draugluin as all other werewolves were.

In the *[Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)* it is told that "creatures that walked in wolf-shapes" entered [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) in the [Valian Year](../V/Valian_Years.md) 1330.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Although not appearing as such in the known written records of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) during the [Second](../S/Second_Age.md) and [Third](../T/Third_Age.md) Ages, Gandalf mentioned the werewolves as being among Sauron's servants in the late Third Age, along with [orcs](../O/Orcs.md), [trolls](../T/Trolls.md), [wargs](Wargs.md), and [wraiths](Wraiths.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), werewolves were called *gaurhoth* (sing. *[gaur](../G/Gaur.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name was *[nauro](../N/Nauro.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
