**Wootton Minor** was the name of a village in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s story, *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*. It lay a few miles away from [Wootton Major](/wiki/Wootton_Major), which was named as such because it was larger than Wootton Minor (although neither was very large). When [Nan Smithson](/wiki/Nan_Smithson) grew up she moved to Wootton Minor, married <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tom_Wright&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tom Wright (page does not exist)">Tom Wright</a>, and had a son named [Tomling](/wiki/Tomling).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
