**Widow Rumble** was a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Widow Rumble looked after [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) when his son [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") moved from [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row) to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") mentions that "rumble" is not a meaningful word in the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) of that era.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
