The **West-mark** was the military division of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) that lay to the west of the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) and [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) rivers (excepting the lands around the capital at [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras)), commanded by a [Marshal](/wiki/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark "First Marshal of the Riddermark") based at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Bordering on Rohan's enemies in [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), the West-mark was historically the scene of much strife. The worst troubles in this region were seen during the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter), when the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) forced the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) to abandon [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) and flee to the West-mark, where they sheltered in Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) also lay on its borders, so that the West-mark was of great importance during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). The King's son [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) was given the rank of Second Marshal of the Mark, and given the command of this region in the wars with [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). He went to battle with the enemy at the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen), where he lost his life. The command of the West-mark was then taken by the lord [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand), who fought and lost a second battle at the Fords, but was able to rally enough of his forces to help in the desperate defence of Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
