The **West-mark** was the military division of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) that lay to the west of the [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md) and [Snowbourn](../S/Snowbourn.md) rivers (excepting the lands around the capital at [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md)), commanded by a [Marshal](../F/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md) based at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Bordering on Rohan's enemies in [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md), the West-mark was historically the scene of much strife. The worst troubles in this region were seen during the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md), when the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) forced the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) to abandon [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and flee to the West-mark, where they sheltered in Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) also lay on its borders, so that the West-mark was of great importance during the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md). The King's son [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md) was given the rank of Second Marshal of the Mark, and given the command of this region in the wars with [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md). He went to battle with the enemy at the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md), where he lost his life. The command of the West-mark was then taken by the lord [Erkenbrand](../E/Erkenbrand.md), who fought and lost a second battle at the Fords, but was able to rally enough of his forces to help in the desperate defence of Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
