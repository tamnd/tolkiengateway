"**When the black breath blows**" is a poem of herblore spoken by the [herb-master](../H/Herb-master.md) of the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md) in the chapter "[The Houses of Healing](../T/The_Houses_of_Healing.md)". It is an old rhyme telling of the healing properties of [athelas](../A/Athelas.md), though it is considered by some to be little more than an old wives' tale despite the truth of its words.

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>When the <a href="../B/Black_Breath.md" title="Black Breath">black breath</a> blows<br/>
and death&#39;s shadow grows<br/>
and all lights pass,<br/>
come athelas! come athelas!<br/>
Life to the dying<br/>
In the king&#39;s hand lying!
</p>
</div>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i> </b>

The poem is recounted by one of the healers at [Halbarad](../H/Halbarad.md)'s encampment at [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md), though "athelas" is here mispronounced as "ah-THAY-lus" rather than "ATH-e-lass" as the poem would suggest.
