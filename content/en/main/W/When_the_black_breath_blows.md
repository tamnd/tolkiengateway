"**When the black breath blows**" is a poem of herblore spoken by the [herb-master](/wiki/Herb-master "Herb-master") of the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing) in the chapter "[The Houses of Healing](/wiki/The_Houses_of_Healing)". It is an old rhyme telling of the healing properties of [athelas](/wiki/Athelas "Athelas"), though it is considered by some to be little more than an old wives' tale despite the truth of its words.

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>When the <a href="/wiki/Black_Breath" title="Black Breath">black breath</a> blows<br/>
and death&#39;s shadow grows<br/>
and all lights pass,<br/>
come athelas! come athelas!<br/>
Life to the dying<br/>
In the king&#39;s hand lying!
</p>
</div>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i> </b>

The poem is recounted by one of the healers at [Halbarad](/wiki/Halbarad)'s encampment at [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford), though "athelas" is here mispronounced as "ah-THAY-lus" rather than "ATH-e-lass" as the poem would suggest.
