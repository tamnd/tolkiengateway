**White Lady** is an epithet that referred to multiple women within the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

During the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel) ([Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1362](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1362 "Year of the Trees 1362") - [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400")), an Elven princess and the sister of King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), was known as "the White Lady of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup> and "the White Lady of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin)"<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup>.

During the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis), a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") Queen and wife of [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), was known as the "White Lady of [Emerië](/wiki/Emeri%C3%AB)" and the "White Lady".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) (b. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2995](/wiki/Third_Age_2995 "Third Age 2995")), the niece of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and slayer of the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king), was called the "White Lady of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)" or the "White Lady" by [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Lady of [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien"), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) (b. [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1362](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1362 "Year of the Trees 1362")), the bearer of [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya), was also referred to as the "White Lady" by Faramir<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and by [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.
