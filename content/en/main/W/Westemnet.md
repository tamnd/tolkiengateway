The **Westemnet** was the western plains of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> west of the river [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It probably extended to the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The most important of Rohan's settlements and fortifications lay to the south of the Westemnet, where the [White Mountains](White_Mountains.md) rose up from the plain. [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md) was among these, and to the south-east was the seat of the [Lords of the Mark](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md), [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) itself.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Emnet* means "plain" in [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>
