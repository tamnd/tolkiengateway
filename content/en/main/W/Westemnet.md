The **Westemnet** was the western plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> west of the river [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It probably extended to the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The most important of Rohan's settlements and fortifications lay to the south of the Westemnet, where the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) rose up from the plain. [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) was among these, and to the south-east was the seat of the [Lords of the Mark](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan"), [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) itself.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Emnet* means "plain" in [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>
