The **Woodmen** were those [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) who dwelt in [Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md) in the [Third](../T/Third_Age.md) and [Fourth Ages](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

Despite being troubled by evil creatures of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) such as Orcs and [Spiders](../S/Spiders.md), they survived until the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md) and the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

## Origins {#Origins}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="../N/Northmen.md" title="Northmen">Northmen</a></div></div>

The Woodmen were descended from the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) who migrated to the [West](Westlands.md) or from [Men](../M/Men.md) who were closely related to the Edain. As a consequence, their language was related to [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the end of the First Age some of those Men had settled in the northern and eastern borders of Greenwood.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Some migrated from the east of the Greenwood along its southern edge and up the vales of the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) or between its northern edge and the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md). During the [First](../F/First_Age.md) and [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) the Northmen, such as the ancestors of the Woodmen, had been allies of the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md) and the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of the hills surrounding the Greenwood, and were enemies of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Woodmen maintained friendly relations with the [Elves of the Greenwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [4 October](../_/4_October.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the Woodmen noticed the [disastrous battle](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md) between the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) and the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) at the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md). They sent runners to [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) and assembled a force to ambush the Orcs in order to rescue the Dúnedain, but by the time they arrived at the site of the battle it was too late: [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and almost all his Guards had been killed. All the Woodmen could do was to drive away the surviving Orcs, before they could mutilate the bodies of the dead. It was probably those Woodmen who found the stunned [Estelmo](../E/Estelmo.md) alive under his master [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>)'s dead body.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-4">[4]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md) a shadow fell on Greenwood and men began to call it Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md) the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) spread north from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> but it is not recorded how the Woodmen fared.

The [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md) used to feed on the Northmen's [sheep](../S/Sheep.md), who defended them with their [bows](../B/Bows.md) of yew; thus they were afraid to fly anywhere near where men lived. The [Wargs](Wargs.md) and the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) also usually did not dare to approach as they were brave and well-armed.<sup id="cite_ref-H6_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H6-10">[10]</a></sup>

At the time of the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> the Woodmen settled in Mirkwood south of the [Old Forest Road](../O/Old_Forest_Road.md) near the western edges of the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Around this time some bold Woodmen and their families were attempting to make their way back to the north, cutting down trees, and building settlements among the woods in the valleys and along the river-shores. This time Orcs and Wargs started planning a joint raid against them to capture slaves. When the Orcs discovered [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md), they thought they were spies of those Woodmen and hunted them down, fearing they would warn their people.<sup id="cite_ref-H6_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H6-10">[10]</a></sup>

Obviously the raid never took place, thanks to the subsequent events, including the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) where Orcs and Wargs were devastated. After the Battle, Men could travel without fear and many came to [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md)'s home to celebrate [Yule](../Y/Yule.md), and some gathered under him as a chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

A couple of years later, [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) traversed Mirkwood in search for [his Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) and [its new bearer](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md); this terrified the Woodmen who talked about "a [ghost](../G/Ghosts.md) that drank blood", that even crept into houses to steal babies.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

It is possible that the Woodmen fought against forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) in Mirkwood during the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md), because [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) had a vision on the Seat of Seeing on [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md) of a fight of Elves and Men and fell beasts under the branches of Mirkwood<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> and some of the Woodmen were living in the western edge of Mirkwood north of Dol Guldur and east of the Gladden Fields.

After the cleansing of the forest, the Woodmen and the [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md) were given the central portion of [Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md), between the [Narrows of the Forest](../N/Narrows_of_the_Forest.md) and the [Woodland Realm](Woodland_Realm.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Messengers from the Woodmen of Mirkwood<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> came to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) after they had received a message that all who hate [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) should come there<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> in three manuscript versions of what would later become the chapter *[The Muster of Rohan](../T/The_Muster_of_Rohan.md)*.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> mentions at the meeting of the <a href="White_Council.md" title="White Council">White Council</a> that <a class="mw-redirect" href="../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md" title="Greenwood the Great">Greenwood the Great</a> is now called &#34;<a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>&#34; by the Woodmen. In the writings of J.R.R. Tolkien men began to call it Mirkwood in T.A. 1050, almost 2000 years before this meeting of the White Council.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Woodmen live in the Vales on both sides of the river <a href="../A/Anduin.md" title="Anduin">Anduin</a>. They are self-reliant and very isolated people, with even the nearby villages having very little contact with one another. Woodmen settlements include the larger town of Hultvís, the smaller villages of Blómgard and Arhaim, the recently-destroyed Waldfast and the long-abandoned Audaghaim. During the closing weeks of the <a href="War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> the Woodmen were attacked by the <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> and <a href="Wargs.md" title="Wargs">Wargs</a> of the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, but a united force of <a href="../B/Beornings.md" title="Beornings">Beornings</a> and Woodmen pushed back and eventually defeated them at the Battle of the <a href="../O/Old_Ford.md" title="Old Ford">Old Ford</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Woodmen are deeply superstitious due to the long years of living under the darkness of <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, but many of their folk tales bear a seed of truth in them. For example, the real events of <a href="../G/Gollum.md" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> stealing Woodmen babies have been attributed to &#34;Úbil the Snatcher&#34;, who over the years have transformed into the hunger-crazed &#34;Old Mad Ubb&#34; used to scare the children.</dd></dl>
