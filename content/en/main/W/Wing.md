<i><b>wing</b></i> means "foam, spume, spindrift" in the tongue of the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), as interpreted by the [Elven loremasters](/wiki/Lambengolmor "Lambengolmor") after the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-PMXI4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMXI4-1">[1]</a></sup>

Another conception is that *wing* was a [Bëorian](/wiki/Taliska "Taliska") word.<sup id="cite_ref-PMRos_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMRos-2">[2]</a></sup>
