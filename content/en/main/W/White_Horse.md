The **White Horse** was the emblem of the kingdom of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup> It was depicted on a green field on the banner of Rohan<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-2">[2]</a></sup> and emblazoned on a shield of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) that was given to [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck")<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

On March 10, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) gave Merry a round shield with the device of the white horse on it<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-3">[3]</a></sup>.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)_-_Rohan_flag.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/0/0c/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Rohan_flag.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Rohan_flag.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="188" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/0/0c/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Rohan_flag.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Rohan_flag.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/0/0c/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_%28film_series%29_-_Rohan_flag.jpg 2x" data-file-width="452" data-file-height="283"/></a><figcaption>As designed for the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> film series</figcaption></figure>

[Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) rode under this banner to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields)<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> on March 15, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and after his death it was flown by his nephew and successor [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) during the rest of the battle<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and on March 25, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> during the battle before the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) itself<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup>. It continued to be carried by [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) when he accompanied king [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") with the cavalry of the Mark beyond the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn) or to the far fields of the South after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) ended.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

According to [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey)'s *[The Road to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_to_Middle-earth)*, the emblem may have been inspired from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uffington_White_Horse" title="wikipedia:Uffington White Horse">Uffington White Horse</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

The White Horse is a recurring motif in mythology and prehistoric English culture. See <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leucippotomy" title="wikipedia:Leucippotomy">leucippotomy</a>.
