The **Walls of the Sun** or **Mountains of the Sun** was a great, curve-shaped mountain range in the [Land of the Sun](/wiki/Land_of_the_Sun), in the far East of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). It corresponded symmetrically to the mountain range [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) on the land of [Aman](/wiki/Aman), but not as tall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier writings, a great mountain [Kalormë](/wiki/Kalorm%C3%AB) was in the Land, which seemed to correspond to the [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB) in the [Aman](/wiki/Aman)<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-2">[2]</a></sup> although it is not specified in a mountain range.
