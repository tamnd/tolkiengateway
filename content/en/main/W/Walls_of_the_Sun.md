The **Walls of the Sun** or **Mountains of the Sun** was a great, curve-shaped mountain range in the [Land of the Sun](../L/Land_of_the_Sun.md), in the far East of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). It corresponded symmetrically to the mountain range [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md) on the land of [Aman](../A/Aman.md), but not as tall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier writings, a great mountain [Kalormë](../K/Kalormë.md) was in the Land, which seemed to correspond to the [Oiolossë](../O/Oiolossë.md) in the [Aman](../A/Aman.md)<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-2">[2]</a></sup> although it is not specified in a mountain range.
