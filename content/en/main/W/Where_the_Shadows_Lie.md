**Where the Shadows Lie** is the second track on [*The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power* (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One:_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)"). It plays during the credits of *[Alloyed](/wiki/Alloyed)*. Its lyrics are derived from the "[verse long known in Elven-lore](/wiki/Ring-inscription#Ring-verse "Ring-inscription")" from *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
