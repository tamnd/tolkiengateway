**Wulf** (fl. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2754](/wiki/Third_Age_2754 "Third Age 2754")—[T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759")) was a young nobleman who dwelt in the [West-march](/wiki/West-march). He was the son of [Freca](/wiki/Freca) and it was said that their family had a measure of [dunlendish](/wiki/Dunlendings "Dunlendings") blood.

## History {#History}

Freca attempted to force a marriage between Wulf and the daughter of King [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm "Helm"). When Freca's plan failed, he was slain, and Wulf found himself declared the King's enemy. He fled across the borders into [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), where he seems to have been warmly received. For four years, Wulf built up his power in Dunland, and allied himself with Rohan's and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)'s enemies.

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), with Rohan's armies drawn into the east to repel an attack there, Wulf and his allies invaded from the west. They devastated Helm's forces at the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen) and overran the country and took [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), where Wulf killed Prince [Haleth](</wiki/Haleth_(son_of_Helm)> "Haleth (son of Helm)"); he sat in the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Golden_Hall) and claimed the Kingship of Rohan.

The exiled King, Helm, was driven into hiding in the mountains. During the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter) Wulf's armies besieged him at [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg), and Helm's nephew, [Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f) at [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow). Helm and his second son died of the cold. In the spring, Helm was avenged by Fréaláf, who led a small party into Edoras and killed 'King' Wulf in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759"). With help from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), Fréaláf cleared Wulf's followers from Rohan, and succeeded his uncle Helm to become its tenth rightful King.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
