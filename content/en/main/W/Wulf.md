**Wulf** (fl. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2754](../T/Third_Age_2754.md)—[T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md)) was a young nobleman who dwelt in the [West-march](West-march.md). He was the son of [Freca](../F/Freca.md) and it was said that their family had a measure of [dunlendish](../D/Dunlendings.md) blood.

## History {#History}

Freca attempted to force a marriage between Wulf and the daughter of King [Helm Hammerhand](../H/Helm.md). When Freca's plan failed, he was slain, and Wulf found himself declared the King's enemy. He fled across the borders into [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md), where he seems to have been warmly received. For four years, Wulf built up his power in Dunland, and allied himself with Rohan's and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)'s enemies.

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md), with Rohan's armies drawn into the east to repel an attack there, Wulf and his allies invaded from the west. They devastated Helm's forces at the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md) and overran the country and took [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md), where Wulf killed Prince [Haleth](<../H/Haleth_(son_of_Helm).md>); he sat in the [Golden Hall](../G/Golden_Hall.md) and claimed the Kingship of Rohan.

The exiled King, Helm, was driven into hiding in the mountains. During the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md) Wulf's armies besieged him at [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md), and Helm's nephew, [Fréaláf](../F/Fréaláf.md) at [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md). Helm and his second son died of the cold. In the spring, Helm was avenged by Fréaláf, who led a small party into Edoras and killed 'King' Wulf in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md). With help from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), Fréaláf cleared Wulf's followers from Rohan, and succeeded his uncle Helm to become its tenth rightful King.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
