The **White Company** was the guard of the [Princes of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien "Prince of Ithilien") during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The company was created by King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") to settle the complex case of [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond), as he had abandoned his post and spilled blood, yet saved [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) from an early [death](/wiki/The_Pyre_of_Denethor "The Pyre of Denethor") upon doing so. Elessar exiled Beregond from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), but appointed him to be the first **Captain of the Guard** of Prince Faramir<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> to dwell within the hills of [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen) in sight of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas) and two old servants retired to [Pen-arduin](/wiki/Pen-arduin) from the **Prince's guard**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>After King Elessar charges Steward <a href="/wiki/Faramir" title="Faramir">Faramir</a> with cleansing the evil of <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a>, Faramir leads the White Company into the <a href="/wiki/Morgul_Vale" title="Morgul Vale">Morgul Vale</a>, where they and the <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien" title="Rangers of Ithilien">Rangers of Ithilien</a> establish several camps outside the Dead City.</dd></dl>
