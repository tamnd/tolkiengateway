**Winterfilth** was the tenth month of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), approximately corresponding to modern [22 September](../_/22_September.md) through [21 October](../_/21_October.md).

In older times, Winterfilth was the last month of the Hobbit year, and the harvest occurred during it. However after the Hobbits adopted the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md), Winterfilth became the tenth month.

This month was called [Wintring](Wintring.md) in [Bree](../B/Bree.md). It was a jest in Bree to speak of "Winterfilth in the (muddy) Shire"<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name referred to the completion of the year ("filling") before Winter in their old calendar (before they adopted the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md)) when their new year began after harvest.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name actually represents a [modernization](../H/Hobbitish.md) of the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for October, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/winterfylle%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:winterfylleþ">Winterfylleþ</a></i>, meaning "Winter-filling" or "Winter-full moon".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
