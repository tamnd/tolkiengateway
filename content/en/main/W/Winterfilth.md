**Winterfilth** was the tenth month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), approximately corresponding to modern [22 September](/wiki/22_September) through [21 October](/wiki/21_October).

In older times, Winterfilth was the last month of the Hobbit year, and the harvest occurred during it. However after the Hobbits adopted the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning), Winterfilth became the tenth month.

This month was called [Wintring](/wiki/Wintring) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree). It was a jest in Bree to speak of "Winterfilth in the (muddy) Shire"<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name referred to the completion of the year ("filling") before Winter in their old calendar (before they adopted the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning)) when their new year began after harvest.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name actually represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for October, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/winterfylle%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:winterfylleþ">Winterfylleþ</a></i>, meaning "Winter-filling" or "Winter-full moon".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
