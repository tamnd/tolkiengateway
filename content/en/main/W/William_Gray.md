**William "Bill" Gray** is Professor of Literary History and Hermeneutics at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chichester" title="wikipedia:University of Chichester">University of Chichester</a>, UK. He studied literature, philosophy and theology at the Universities of Oxford, Edinburgh and Princeton, and has published widely in these areas.
