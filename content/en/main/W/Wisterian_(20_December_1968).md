<b><i>Wisterian</i> (20 December 1968)</b> is an issue of a magazine published by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Salle_College_High_School" title="wikipedia:La Salle College High School">La Salle College High School</a>. The issue (p. 3) reproduces a letter from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to Billy Callahan (see [Letter to Billy Callahan](/wiki/Letter_to_Billy_Callahan)). Also includes a slightly inaccurate transcription of the letter.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>
