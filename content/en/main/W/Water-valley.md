The **Water-valley** was a gentle valley in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

The Water-valley was the valley of [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) was constructed in the wide valley, and the towns of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton) and [Whitfurrows](/wiki/Whitfurrows) were located in it.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) took [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") for many a long walk through the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
