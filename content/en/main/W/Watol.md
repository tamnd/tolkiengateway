**Watol** or **Watla** was an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglo-Saxons" title="wikipedia:Anglo-Saxons">Anglo-Saxon</a> king, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

He commissioned a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") called [Íring](/wiki/%C3%8Dring), the son of an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_people" title="wikipedia:Irish people">Irish</a> king [Ír](/wiki/%C3%8Dr), to build a road through his lands, which he completed in seven years. However, Watol's sons, the [Wætlingas](/wiki/W%C3%A6tlingas), were angered by Íring's arrogance, and so they wagered that they could build an even better road in only three years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The character of Watol is based upon the legendary King Weatla, whose sons, according to the English chronicler <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_of_Worcester" title="wikipedia:Florence of Worcester">Florence of Worcester</a>, built the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watling_Street" title="wikipedia:Watling Street">Watling Street</a> in the year <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1013" title="wikipedia:1013">1013 AD</a>; Florence of Worcester also said that the name *Watling Street* was afterwards applied to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milky_Way" title="wikipedia:Milky Way">Milky Way</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
