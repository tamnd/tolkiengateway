**William E. Wilson** is an American author who wrote *[Greater Harad](/wiki/Greater_Harad)* and *[Nazgûl's Citadel](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl%27s_Citadel)* for [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing). After his work in an official capacity, he maintained several resources for the game on his personal website.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Despite his work on Middle-earth Role Playing, he himself found the system of [Lord of the Rings Adventure Game](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_Adventure_Game) to suit the setting better.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
