**William "Bill" Cater** (1924 - 2020)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a British journalist who interviewed [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) on [2 August](/wiki/2_August) [1966](/wiki/1966) for the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Express" title="wikipedia:Daily Express">Daily Express</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Cater later became a friend of Tolkien '<b><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Manual_of_Style#Redlinks" title="Tolkien Gateway:Manual of Style"><i>former link</i></a>]</sup> </b>and they exchanged several letters. In 1977, Cater made an interview with [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), concerning the publication of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

In a 1972 article celebrating Tolkien's upcoming 80th birthday, Cater coined a famous expression which was to be used on the dustwrappers of an edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*:

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The English-speaking world is divided into those who have read The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings and those who are going to read them.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>William Cater, The Sunday Times (UK)</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395753"/></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference) in 1992, but it was not submitted for later publication in [the conference's proceedings](/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference "Proceedings of the J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
