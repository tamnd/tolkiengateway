**William "Bill" Cater** (1924 - 2020)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a British journalist who interviewed [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) on [2 August](../_/2_August.md) [1966](../_/1966.md) for the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Express" title="wikipedia:Daily Express">Daily Express</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Cater later became a friend of Tolkien '<b><sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../project/M/Manual_of_Style.md#Redlinks" title="Tolkien Gateway:Manual of Style"><i>former link</i></a>]</sup> </b>and they exchanged several letters. In 1977, Cater made an interview with [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), concerning the publication of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

In a 1972 article celebrating Tolkien's upcoming 80th birthday, Cater coined a famous expression which was to be used on the dustwrappers of an edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*:

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The English-speaking world is divided into those who have read The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings and those who are going to read them.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>William Cater, The Sunday Times (UK)</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

He presented a paper called *"Interviewing Tolkien"* at the [J.R.R. Tolkien Centenary Conference](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md) in 1992, but it was not submitted for later publication in [the conference's proceedings](../P/Proceedings_of_the_J.R.R._Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
