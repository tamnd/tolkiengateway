The **Wild Hills** were part of the Wide World outside of the village of [Ham](../H/Ham.md), the home of [Farmer Giles](../F/Farmer_Giles.md). They lay to the west and north of Ham, near the dubious marches of the mountain-country. [The Giant](../T/The_Giant.md) who came blundering into Farmer Giles’ fields usually went walking in the Wild Hills (it was only by making a bad guess when he was lost that the Giant came to lands inhabited by men). <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
