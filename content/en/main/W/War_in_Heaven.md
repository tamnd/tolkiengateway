<i><b>War in Heaven</b></i> was the first novel published by [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams). The graphic novel *[Heaven's War](/wiki/Heaven%27s_War)*, a fictional story about [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), was a play on the title of Williams's book.
