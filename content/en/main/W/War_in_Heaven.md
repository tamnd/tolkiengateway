<i><b>War in Heaven</b></i> was the first novel published by [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md). The graphic novel *[Heaven's War](../H/Heaven's_War.md)*, a fictional story about [Charles Williams](../C/Charles_Williams.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), was a play on the title of Williams's book.
