**Walking with Bilbo** is a devotional book by Sarah Arthur.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The author of Walking with Frodo takes readers on an adventure of faith with a new devotional that relates themes from <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a> to living the <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian life</a>. Unlike the fateful quest <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> was asked to carry out, <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>&#39;s journey came as an unexpected adventure. Readers will be reminded that God chooses us to be unlikely heroes in the adventure of life.
</p>
</blockquote>
