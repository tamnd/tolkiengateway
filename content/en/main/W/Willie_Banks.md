**Willie Banks** was a [Hobbit](../B/Bree-hobbits.md) of [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

## History {#History}

Willie was called to arms when the [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) tore through [Bree](../B/Bree.md). [Sharkey](../S/Saruman.md)'s men were repelled, but Willie and a few others did not survive.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
