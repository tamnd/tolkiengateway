The **Woodland Realm**<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-5">[4]</a></sup> was the great kingdom of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium">#Other versions of the legendarium</a></div></div>

The Woodland Realm was established in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md) by [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md), a [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) [Elf of Doriath](../I/Iathrim.md), after the [War of Wrath](War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-date_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-date-4">[3]</a></sup> Unlike most [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) and his household declined the [Valar](../V/Valar.md)'s offer to depart from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). Instead, he migrated eastward from [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and became the King of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) east of [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup> The few Sindar who had come with him soon merged with the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md), adopting their customs and language and taking names of [Silvan form and style](../S/Silvan_Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> Oropher and his household wished to return to the simple existence natural to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) before they had been disturbed by the Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

Originally, Oropher's realm encompassed the south of [Greenwood](../G/Greenwood.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> with dwellings about the hill of [Amon Lanc](../A/Amon_Lanc.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup> However, throughout the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), he and his people migrated north three times.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup> According to one tradition, the first movement was northward beyond the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md), due to Oropher's desire to distance himself from the increasing encroachments of the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](../D/Dwarves_of_Khazad-dûm.md) and his resentment of the intrusions of [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> However, his people did maintain constant intercourse with their [kin](../G/Galadhrim.md) west of the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

Oropher was also disturbed by the reports of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s rising power, and by the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) he dwelt in the western glens of the [Emyn Duir](../M/Mountains_of_Mirkwood.md), and his numerous people lived and roamed in the woods and vales westward as far as Anduin, north of the ancient [Dwarf-Road](../M/Men-i-Naugrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3430](../S/Second_Age_3430.md), Oropher led the forces of the Woodland Realm against [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) as part of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md), and was slain during battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anke_Eißmann_-_Thranduil.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg/200px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg/400px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg 2x" data-file-width="462" data-file-height="464"/></a><figcaption><a href="../A/Anke_Eißmann.md" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a> - <i>Thranduil</i></figcaption></figure>

Oropher's son, [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md), succeeded him as the lord of the Woodland Realm<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> and ruled the Woodland Realm in peace for many years.

Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1100](../T/Third_Age_1100.md), an evil entity known as the [Necromancer](../N/Necromancer.md) (later identified as Sauron) created a stronghold on Amon Lanc called [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> As the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) fell upon [Greenwood the Great](../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md) and spread ever northward, Thranduil's realm retreated before it, until at last Thranduil established his realm in the north-east of the forest and delved there a [fortress and great halls underground](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> His realm was also protected by the [Enchanted River](../E/Enchanted_River.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> which could cause one to sleep and dream deeply, whilst the Elvenking's Halls served as the chief fortress of the realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-10">[9]</a></sup>

Thranduil's realm is said to have extended into the woods surrounding the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and growing along the west shores of the [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md), before the coming of the [Dwarves exiled from Moria](../D/Dwarves_of_Moria.md) and the invasion of the [dragon](../D/Dragon.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> The Woodland Realm did trade with the neighbouring realms of Erebor and [Dale](../D/Dale.md), and imported wine from [Dorwinion](../D/Dorwinion.md) via the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md). The former traffic came to an end with the destruction of Erebor by Smaug in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> but again resumed after the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> [Thorin's band of Dwarves](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) intruded in the Woodland Realm and were held prisoner in the [Elvenking's Halls](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-10">[9]</a></sup> Upon hearing the death of Smaug, Thranduil immediately assembled the army of the Woodland Realm and marched towards [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md). In the ensuing [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md), many [Wood-elves](Wood-elves.md) were slain <sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> and many of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) that survived wandered into the Woodland Realm were slain or lost in the trackless dark of Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/C/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg/200px-Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="148" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg/300px-Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg/400px-Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg 2x" data-file-width="721" data-file-height="535"/></a><figcaption><a href="../C/Capucine_Mazille.md" title="Capucine Mazille">Capucine Mazille</a> - <i>The Battle of Five Armies</i></figcaption></figure>

On [20 June](../_/20_June.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), a force of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) attacked the Woodland Realm (orchestrated by Sauron),<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[1]</a></sup> the purpose of this raid being to provide a distraction and facilitate the escape of [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md),<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup> who had been entrusted to Thranduil's care by the [Ranger](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[1]</a></sup>

In the wake of this, Thranduil sent his son, [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), to deliver news of Gollum's escape to Aragorn and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). Upon arriving, Legolas participated in the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) as an ambassador of the Woodland Realm where the full details of Sauron's resurgence were revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[2]</a></sup>

Meanwhile, on [15 March](../_/15_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), Sauron's forces invaded the Woodland Realm in force and there was a long [battle under the trees](../B/Battle_under_the_trees.md) and great ruin of fire; but in the end Thranduil had the victory. On the [Elven New Year](../E/Elven_New_Year.md) ([6 April](../_/6_April.md)), Thranduil met [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md), [Lord of Lórien](../L/Lord_of_Lórien.md), and the two agreed to rename the forest [Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md). Thranduil took the area from the northern eaves of the forest south to the [Emyn Duir](../E/Emyn_Duir.md) for the Woodland Realm.<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup>

### Later History {#Later_History}

After the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md), the [Silvan Elves of the Greenwood](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md), including those of the Woodland Realm, remained untroubled.<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup> Many folk of the realm removed south to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) to restore the beauty of the [South-kingdom](../S/South-kingdom.md), especially [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-durin_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-durin-15">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RK-MinasTirith_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-MinasTirith-16">[15]</a></sup>

The eventual fate of the Woodland Realm is unknown.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Foundation {#Foundation}

The foundation of the Woodland Realm is disputed:

- According to [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) was among the wave of [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) who, before the building of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) c. [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md), established the Woodland Realm among the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) and became the king in the north of [Greenwood the Great](../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-17">[16]</a></sup>

- An alternate origin story is given in the article [The History of Galadriel and Celeborn](../T/The_History_of_Galadriel_and_Celeborn.md) in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*. [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md), along with a handful of [Sindar from Doriath](../I/Iathrim.md) (alongside his son Thranduil), took up the rule of the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) of Greenwood the Great sometime after the [ruin of Doriath](../R/Ruin_of_Doriath.md). He established his realm in the south of the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

- In one draft from the article [The Tale of Years of the Second Age](../T/The_Tale_of_Years_of_the_Second_Age.md) from *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*, [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) was among the wave of [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md) from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) who established the Woodland Realm in the north of Greenwood the Great in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md).<sup id="cite_ref-date_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-date-4">[3]</a></sup>

This article and the wiki reconcile these three stories as: [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) and his household of [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) (including his son [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md)) from [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) established the Woodland Realm in the south of [Greenwood the Great](../G/Greenwood_the_Great.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md).

The quests in the area involve bringing stability to the realm so that trade may resume.</dd></dl>
