The **Woodland Realm**<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-5">[4]</a></sup> was the great kingdom of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium">#Other versions of the legendarium</a></div></div>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r398211">.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote{position:relative;margin:0.5rem 0;padding:0.75rem 1rem;box-shadow:var(--tg-border-color-base)0px 0px 0px 1px inset;border-radius:var(--tg-border-radius-medium);background:var(--tg-color-surface-2);font-size:var(--tg-font-size-small);line-height:var(--tg-line-height-xx-small);display:flex;gap:0.5rem;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon{display:flex;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element{display:block}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element[src$=".svg"]{filter:var(--filter-invert);opacity:0.5}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--red{background:var(--background-color-destructive-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--yellow{background:var(--background-color-warning-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--green{background:var(--background-color-success-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}</style>

The Woodland Realm was established in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750") by [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher), a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") [Elf of Doriath](/wiki/Iathrim "Iathrim"), after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).<sup id="cite_ref-date_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-date-4">[3]</a></sup> Unlike most [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) and his household declined the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)'s offer to depart from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Instead, he migrated eastward from [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and became the King of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) east of [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup> The few Sindar who had come with him soon merged with the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves), adopting their customs and language and taking names of [Silvan form and style](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish "Silvan Elvish").<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> Oropher and his household wished to return to the simple existence natural to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) before they had been disturbed by the Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

Originally, Oropher's realm encompassed the south of [Greenwood](/wiki/Greenwood),<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> with dwellings about the hill of [Amon Lanc](/wiki/Amon_Lanc).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup> However, throughout the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), he and his people migrated north three times.<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup> According to one tradition, the first movement was northward beyond the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields), due to Oropher's desire to distance himself from the increasing encroachments of the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) and his resentment of the intrusions of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> However, his people did maintain constant intercourse with their [kin](/wiki/Galadhrim "Galadhrim") west of the Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

Oropher was also disturbed by the reports of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s rising power, and by the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) he dwelt in the western glens of the [Emyn Duir](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood "Mountains of Mirkwood"), and his numerous people lived and roamed in the woods and vales westward as far as Anduin, north of the ancient [Dwarf-Road](/wiki/Men-i-Naugrim "Men-i-Naugrim").<sup id="cite_ref-Note_14_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note_14-6">[5]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430"), Oropher led the forces of the Woodland Realm against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) as part of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men), and was slain during battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg/200px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg/300px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/e5/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg/400px-Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann_-_Thranduil.jpg 2x" data-file-width="462" data-file-height="464"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann" title="Anke Eißmann">Anke Eißmann</a> - <i>Thranduil</i></figcaption></figure>

Oropher's son, [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil), succeeded him as the lord of the Woodland Realm<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> and ruled the Woodland Realm in peace for many years.

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1100](/wiki/Third_Age_1100 "Third Age 1100"), an evil entity known as the [Necromancer](/wiki/Necromancer) (later identified as Sauron) created a stronghold on Amon Lanc called [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> As the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) fell upon [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great) and spread ever northward, Thranduil's realm retreated before it, until at last Thranduil established his realm in the north-east of the forest and delved there a [fortress and great halls underground](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls "Elvenking's Halls").<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> His realm was also protected by the [Enchanted River](/wiki/Enchanted_River),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> which could cause one to sleep and dream deeply, whilst the Elvenking's Halls served as the chief fortress of the realm.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-10">[9]</a></sup>

Thranduil's realm is said to have extended into the woods surrounding the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and growing along the west shores of the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake), before the coming of the [Dwarves exiled from Moria](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Moria "Dwarves of Moria") and the invasion of the [dragon](/wiki/Dragon "Dragon") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup> The Woodland Realm did trade with the neighbouring realms of Erebor and [Dale](/wiki/Dale), and imported wine from [Dorwinion](/wiki/Dorwinion) via the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running). The former traffic came to an end with the destruction of Erebor by Smaug in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> but again resumed after the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> [Thorin's band of Dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") intruded in the Woodland Realm and were held prisoner in the [Elvenking's Halls](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls).<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-10">[9]</a></sup> Upon hearing the death of Smaug, Thranduil immediately assembled the army of the Woodland Realm and marched towards [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor). In the ensuing [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), many [Wood-elves](/wiki/Wood-elves) were slain <sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup> and many of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that survived wandered into the Woodland Realm were slain or lost in the trackless dark of Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg/200px-Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="148" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg/300px-Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/8d/Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg/400px-Capucine_Mazille_-_The_Battle_of_Five_Armies.jpg 2x" data-file-width="721" data-file-height="535"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Capucine_Mazille" title="Capucine Mazille">Capucine Mazille</a> - <i>The Battle of Five Armies</i></figcaption></figure>

On [20 June](/wiki/20_June) [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), a force of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attacked the Woodland Realm (orchestrated by Sauron),<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[1]</a></sup> the purpose of this raid being to provide a distraction and facilitate the escape of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum),<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup> who had been entrusted to Thranduil's care by the [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[1]</a></sup>

In the wake of this, Thranduil sent his son, [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), to deliver news of Gollum's escape to Aragorn and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). Upon arriving, Legolas participated in the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) as an ambassador of the Woodland Realm where the full details of Sauron's resurgence were revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-council_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-council-3">[2]</a></sup>

Meanwhile, on [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), Sauron's forces invaded the Woodland Realm in force and there was a long [battle under the trees](/wiki/Battle_under_the_trees "Battle under the trees") and great ruin of fire; but in the end Thranduil had the victory. On the [Elven New Year](/wiki/Elven_New_Year) ([6 April](/wiki/6_April)), Thranduil met [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), [Lord of Lórien](/wiki/Lord_of_L%C3%B3rien), and the two agreed to rename the forest [Eryn Lasgalen](/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen). Thranduil took the area from the northern eaves of the forest south to the [Emyn Duir](/wiki/Emyn_Duir) for the Woodland Realm.<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup>

### Later History {#Later_History}

After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the [Silvan Elves of the Greenwood](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood"), including those of the Woodland Realm, remained untroubled.<sup id="cite_ref-great_14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-14">[13]</a></sup> Many folk of the realm removed south to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) to restore the beauty of the [South-kingdom](/wiki/South-kingdom), especially [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-durin_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-durin-15">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RK-MinasTirith_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-MinasTirith-16">[15]</a></sup>

The eventual fate of the Woodland Realm is unknown.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Foundation {#Foundation}

The foundation of the Woodland Realm is disputed:

- According to [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) was among the wave of [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) who, before the building of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000"), established the Woodland Realm among the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) and became the king in the north of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-17">[16]</a></sup>

- An alternate origin story is given in the article [The History of Galadriel and Celeborn](/wiki/The_History_of_Galadriel_and_Celeborn) in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*. [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher), along with a handful of [Sindar from Doriath](/wiki/Iathrim "Iathrim") (alongside his son Thranduil), took up the rule of the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) of Greenwood the Great sometime after the [ruin of Doriath](/wiki/Ruin_of_Doriath "Ruin of Doriath"). He established his realm in the south of the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindarin_Princes_7-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindarin_Princes-7">[6]</a></sup>

- In one draft from the article [The Tale of Years of the Second Age](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Years_of_the_Second_Age) from *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) was among the wave of [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) who established the Woodland Realm in the north of Greenwood the Great in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-date_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-date-4">[3]</a></sup>

This article and the wiki reconcile these three stories as: [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) and his household of [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) (including his son [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil)) from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) established the Woodland Realm in the south of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Greenwood_the_Great) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").
