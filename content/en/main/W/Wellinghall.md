**Wellinghall** was one of the [Ent-houses](/wiki/Ent-houses) of [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard), formed by a narrow space in the flanks of [Methedras](/wiki/Methedras), guarded and flanked by evergreen trees. The roofless 'hall' came to an end in a sheer rock wall, down which flowed a stream welling - hence the name 'Wellinghall' - from the springs of the River [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash). The stream flowed down the rocky cliff, and fell across the arched entrance to an inner hall, where [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) kept his meagre furniture. From there, it flowed between the trees that lined the main space, and on to join again with the main flow of the Entwash.

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg/250px-Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="171" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/a3/Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg/375px-Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/a3/Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg/500px-Cor_Blok_-_At_the_Enthouse.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1097"/></a><figcaption>&#34;At the Enthouse&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Cor_Blok" title="Cor Blok">Cor Blok</a></figcaption></figure>

Wellinghall is best known as the place where [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") stayed with Treebeard on the night before [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot), on [29 February](/wiki/29_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was here that they were first given [Ent-draughts](/wiki/Ent-draughts) by Treebeard, and so grew to become the two tallest [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) recorded in the histories of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard)'s translation (into the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron")) for part of the name of his dwelling. It meant "hall (under or behind) the outflow of the spring of Entwash".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
