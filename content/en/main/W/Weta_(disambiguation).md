**Weta** is a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weta" title="wikipedia:Weta">subspecies of insects from New Zealand</a>, but can refer to:

- [Sideshow Collectibles](/wiki/Sideshow_Collectibles), sometimes Sideshow Weta, a California-based company which deals in collectibles.
- [Wētā FX](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_FX), formerly known as Weta Digital, a [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) company which created the special effects for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
    - "<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Weta_Digital_(documentary)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Weta Digital (documentary) (page does not exist)">Weta Digital (documentary)</a>", a series of extras on the Extended Edition DVDs.
- [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop), a related company which created all the props.
    - "<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Weta_Workshop_(documentary)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Weta Workshop (documentary) (page does not exist)">Weta Workshop (documentary)</a>", a series of extras on the Extended Edition DVDs.
