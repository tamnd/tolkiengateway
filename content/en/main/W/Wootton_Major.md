**Wootton Major** was a town found in *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)*, the primary location in the story. It was named such because it was "larger than [Wootton Minor](Wootton_Minor.md)". It was well known in the country around it, because of the skill of its craftsmen. Especially the [Master Cook](../M/Master_Cook.md), who was held in high esteem, and every twenty-four years baked a magnificent cake for [The Feast of Good Children](../T/The_Feast_of_Good_Children.md). The village held meetings and debates in the old [Great Hall](../G/Great_Hall_of_Wootton_Major.md). From this village came [Smith](../S/Smith_Smithson.md), the hero of the short tale, and his ancestors.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
