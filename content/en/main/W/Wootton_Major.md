**Wootton Major** was a town found in *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*, the primary location in the story. It was named such because it was "larger than [Wootton Minor](/wiki/Wootton_Minor)". It was well known in the country around it, because of the skill of its craftsmen. Especially the [Master Cook](/wiki/Master_Cook), who was held in high esteem, and every twenty-four years baked a magnificent cake for [The Feast of Good Children](/wiki/The_Feast_of_Good_Children). The village held meetings and debates in the old [Great Hall](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Wootton_Major "Great Hall of Wootton Major"). From this village came [Smith](/wiki/Smith_Smithson "Smith Smithson"), the hero of the short tale, and his ancestors.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
