The **White Towers** were three tall towers located in the [Emyn Beraid](/wiki/Tower_Hills "Tower Hills") west of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). The westernmost tower, called [Elostirion](/wiki/Elostirion),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> had a *[palantír](/wiki/Elostirion-stone "Elostirion-stone")* which looked ever westward towards [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) could see them shining far off in the moonlight; they said that from the top of Elostirion one could view the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer").<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The White Towers were built on the [Emyn Beraid](/wiki/Tower_Hills "Tower Hills") by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) as a gift to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> perhaps after the founding of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>).

They were sometimes the destination of pilgrimage by [Wandering Companies](/wiki/Wandering_Companies) of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who would gaze to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) through the *palantír*. Such a band, led by [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor"), met [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") in the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End) of the Shire on [24 September](/wiki/24_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

With time, the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) grew estranged from the Elves and the concept of the Sea became a word of terror and death, and they avoided looking upon the towers on the west.<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-4">[3]</a></sup>

In [September](/wiki/September) [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021")<sup id="cite_ref-App|Later_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Later-8">[7]</a></sup> the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings), including Frodo and Sam, passed the White Towers on their way to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to J.R.R. Tolkien's [Unfinished index](/wiki/Unfinished_index) for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* the towers on the Hills were reputed to have been built by the elves, but they were actually built by Elendil.<sup id="cite_ref-RC1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC1-10">[9]</a></sup>
