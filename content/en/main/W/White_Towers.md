The **White Towers** were three tall towers located in the [Emyn Beraid](../T/Tower_Hills.md) west of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). The westernmost tower, called [Elostirion](../E/Elostirion.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> had a *[palantír](../E/Elostirion-stone.md)* which looked ever westward towards [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) could see them shining far off in the moonlight; they said that from the top of Elostirion one could view the [Sea](../B/Belegaer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The White Towers were built on the [Emyn Beraid](../T/Tower_Hills.md) by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) as a gift to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> perhaps after the founding of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) ([S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3320](../S/Second_Age_3320.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>).

They were sometimes the destination of pilgrimage by [Wandering Companies](Wandering_Companies.md) of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) who would gaze to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) through the *palantír*. Such a band, led by [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor.md), met [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) in the [Woody End](Woody_End.md) of the Shire on [24 September](../_/24_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

With time, the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) grew estranged from the Elves and the concept of the Sea became a word of terror and death, and they avoided looking upon the towers on the west.<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-4">[3]</a></sup>

In [September](../S/September.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md)<sup id="cite_ref-App|Later_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Later-8">[7]</a></sup> the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](../L/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings.md), including Frodo and Sam, passed the White Towers on their way to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to J.R.R. Tolkien's [Unfinished index](../U/Unfinished_index.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* the towers on the Hills were reputed to have been built by the elves, but they were actually built by Elendil.<sup id="cite_ref-RC1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC1-10">[9]</a></sup>
