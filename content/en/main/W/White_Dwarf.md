<i><b>White Dwarf</b></i> (founded [1977](/wiki/1977)) is a game magazine published by [Games Workshop](/wiki/Games_Workshop).

## History {#History}

Various issues of *White Dwarf* have material related to game adaptations of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth): during the 1980s many issues included articles about both role-playing game settings and miniature war games.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadel-1">[1]</a></sup> After releasing *The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game* in [2001](/wiki/2001), each issue of *White Dwarf* featured a special section about this line of miniature war game (apparently dropping this regular section during the latter half of [2010](/wiki/2010)).<sup id="cite_ref-GW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GW-2">[2]</a></sup> Similarly, *White Dwarf* has introduced a new regular feature with the introduction of *[The Hobbit Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Strategy_Battle_Game)* in [2012](/wiki/2012).
