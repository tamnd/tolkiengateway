The **White Downs** were a range of chalk downs in the [Westfarthing](Westfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md), the chief town of the Shire, was located there.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Every seven years the [Free Fair](../F/Free_Fair.md) was held on the White Downs at [Lithe](../L/Lithe.md), during which the [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) was elected.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
