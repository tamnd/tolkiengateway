The **White Downs** were a range of chalk downs in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), the chief town of the Shire, was located there.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Every seven years the [Free Fair](/wiki/Free_Fair) was held on the White Downs at [Lithe](/wiki/Lithe), during which the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving) was elected.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
