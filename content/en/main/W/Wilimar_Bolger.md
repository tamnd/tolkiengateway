**Wilimar Bolger** was a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Wilimar was the son of [Wilibald Bolger](/wiki/Wilibald_Bolger) and [Prisca Baggins](/wiki/Prisca_Baggins). Wilimar Bolger attended the [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
