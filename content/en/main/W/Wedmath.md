**Wedmath** is the eighth month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), following [Afterlithe](/wiki/Afterlithe) and approximately corresponding to modern [24 July](/wiki/24_July) through [22 August](/wiki/22_August).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for August, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/weodmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:weodmonaþ">Wéodmónaþ</a></i>, meaning "grass-month" (cf. *weed*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
