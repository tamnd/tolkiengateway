**Wi Kuki Kaa** ([December 16](/wiki/16_December "16 December"), [1938](/wiki/1938) - [February 19](/wiki/19_February "19 February"), [2006](/wiki/2006)), a New Zealand actor, was originally chosen to play [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*, but his character did not make the shooting phase.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> He does, however, appear as Ghân on the [Decipher card](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game "The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game") "[Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/LotR_TCG:_Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n "LotR TCG: Ghân-buri-Ghân")".
