**Wulf**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a Chieftain of the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings). He was present when [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) rallied the Dunlendings against the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) at [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). When Saruman reminded them that the Rohirrim took Dunlending lands, Wulf yelled "murderers!". Saruman ordered them "take back the lands they stole from you" and to "Burn every village!" and the Dunlendings charged.

Wulf is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* film.
