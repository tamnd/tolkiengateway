**Wilcome "Will" Cotton** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Will was the youngest son of [Holman Cotton](/wiki/Holman_Cotton). His brother [Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior") named his third child, [Wilcome](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II "Wilcome Cotton II"), after him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *willa* "pleasure" + *cuma* "guest"; *Wilcuma* is a welcome guest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
