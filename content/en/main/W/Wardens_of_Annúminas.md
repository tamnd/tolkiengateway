The **Wardens of Annúminas** were a group of [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) in *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* that protected the ancient city of [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) from Angmarim, Gertheryg, Kergrim and tomb-robbers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> By the late Third Age, they operated from an encampment outside the old keep of [Tinnudir](/wiki/Tinnudir) on an island in [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game)* featured a “Warden of Annúminas” card.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
