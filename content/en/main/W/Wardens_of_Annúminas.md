The **Wardens of Annúminas** were a group of [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)* that protected the ancient city of [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md) from Angmarim, Gertheryg, Kergrim and tomb-robbers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> By the late Third Age, they operated from an encampment outside the old keep of [Tinnudir](../T/Tinnudir.md) on an island in [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*[The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Card_Game.md)* featured a “Warden of Annúminas” card.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
