Winterbloom was an [Entwife](/wiki/Entwives "Entwives") who appeared alongside the [Ent](/wiki/Entwives "Entwives") [Snaggleroot](/wiki/Snaggleroot). She was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* television series by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where she appeared in the second season of the show. She is voiced by Olivia Williams.
