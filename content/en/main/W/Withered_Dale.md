The **Withered Dale** was the name of the valley where [Tevildo](../T/Tevildo.md) encountered [Huan](../H/Huan.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2IN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2IN-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Withered Dale was a wide valley that was shaped "*like a bowl among the rocks*". Nothing grew there except for withered grass and some low bushes with only a few leaves.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:48</sup> The valley lay slightly less than a mile westwards of the [halls of Tevildo](../H/Halls_of_Tevildo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:15-6</sup> [Tinúviel](../T/Tinúviel.md) told Tevildo of Huan lying injured in the woods nearby. Tevildo's fell for Tinúviel's ruse and he followed her with two of his thanes, one of which was [Oikeroi](../O/Oikeroi.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:41-5</sup> It was here where [Tevildo](../T/Tevildo.md) had encountered [Huan](../H/Huan.md), who was pretending to be wounded, lying in a bank of fern. Huan killed Oikeroi, the other thane fled, and Tevildo himself fled up a tall and smooth tree near the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2I_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2I-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:27-8</sup>
