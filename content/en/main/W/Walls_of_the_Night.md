The **Walls of the Night** were the outer **walls of the world** beyond [Ekkaia](/wiki/Ekkaia) that surrounded [Arda](/wiki/Arda) as its boundaries in the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).

In the North and South, Ekkaia was narrower and the Walls were closer to the north and south lands of Arda.

In [East](/wiki/East) and [West](/wiki/Uttermost_West "Uttermost West"), Ekkaia was wider and the distance from the land was greater; the [Door of Night](/wiki/Door_of_Night) was an opening in the west. The [Gates of Morning](/wiki/Gates_of_Morning) are also mentioned, but it is not known if these were an equivalent eastern opening on the same Walls.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Presumably Melkor fled through the Walls after he retreated from the [First War](/wiki/First_War). During the [Spring of Arda](/wiki/Spring_of_Arda), he came over the Walls of Night into the north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and secretly built [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally, they were called the Wall of Things, and conceived as "the walls of terrestrial cities, or gardens - walls with a top: a "ring-fence".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In the [Ambarkanta](/wiki/Ambarkanta) they're called [Ilurambar](/wiki/Ilurambar), and they're as a shell that fence [Vaiya](/wiki/Vaiya) and the world from the [Void](/wiki/Void) that are totally impervious and their only opening is the [Door of Night](/wiki/Door_of_Night).
