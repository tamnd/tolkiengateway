The <i><b>Writing Magazine</b></i> issue of August 2014 includes the article "Tolkien on writing and me" by Paula Coston (pp. 12–14). The article is about:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>a series of letters between <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien</a> and Paula Coston née Iley on writing. Paula had links with Tolkien because her grand-parents lived next door to him, and she met him and <a href="/wiki/Edith_Tolkien" title="Edith Tolkien">Edith</a> as a child.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
