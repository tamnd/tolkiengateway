A **white [cow](/wiki/Cows "Cows")** "belonged to the [fairies](/wiki/Fairies "Fairies")" dwelling alone in [Artanor](/wiki/Artanor), according to a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Further_specimens_of_Angloquenya_Revised&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Further specimens of Angloquenya Revised (page does not exist)">poem</a> that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote in [English](/wiki/Modern_English "Modern English") with the *Angloquenya* alphabet. Although nothing else is known about this creature, it is possible that the cow itself was a [Faërie](/wiki/Fa%C3%ABrie) creature.<sup id="cite_ref-PE18_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE18-1">[1]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p><span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">Once upon a time long ago in a far distant</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">country where no foot of mortal has ever even</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">unto this day set foot, there lived a white cow</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">and it belonged to the fairies and the fairies dwelt</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">?alone in that land of mystery and of sorrow. Did</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">I say sorrow? Yes, I did, for many tears</span><br/>
<span class="mw-poem-indented" style="display: inline-block; margin-inline-start: 1em;">were spilt there since the beginning of the world.</span>
</p>
</div>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Transcription adapted to normal English</cite></p>
</blockquote>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As noted by [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith), "nothing about a white cow in Artanor or [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) appears elsewhere in the published [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), but white cows as denizens of the fairy world are a common motif in [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) mythology".<sup id="cite_ref-PE18_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE18-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
