The **White Ship** was the ship that sailed from the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) into the [Uttermost West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"), carrying the [Ring-bearers](/wiki/Ring-bearers) to Aman. Its departure on [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") marked the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Along with [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearers "Ring-bearers") and [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) the [Ring-finder](/wiki/Ring-finder) the ship also carried [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), who had been the [Keepers of the Three Rings](/wiki/Keepers_of_the_Three_Rings). They were accompanied to the Grey Havens by [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor") and many other Elves besides, but whether any of these others went aboard the ship with them is not told.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It does seem, though, that Gandalf's horse [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax) did make the journey for he was waiting at the quayside.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The White Ship was one of the last ships to have set sail from the Grey Havens.

<b>1980: <a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol" title="Tower of Cirith Ungol">Tower of Cirith Ungol</a>, <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> remarks &#34;We can&#39;t escape, for evil is everywhere&#34;. He explains that only <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> can escape <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> by boarding the &#34;White Ships&#34; at the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Havens" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a> and sailing to &#34;<a href="/wiki/Valinor" title="Valinor">the lands beyond</a>&#34;. Frodo has a vision of himself, <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> setting sail. At Bilbo&#39;s 129th birthday, he tells Frodo that he will depart from the Grey Havens on the White Ship tomorrow. Gandalf explains that they are old and have completed their job; The <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a> is over and the Age of <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Man</a> has begun. Bilbo asks why Bilbo can join him, believing only Elves can board the ships. Elrond explains he has served them well and there is &#34;always room for a friend&#34;. Frodo asks whether he can join and Gandalf obliges. The next day, <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a> and <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>  watch as the ship departs from the Havens.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the film, Frodo&#39;s narration claims that the White Ship is &#34;the last ship to leave Middle-earth,&#34; despite it being separate from the later <a href="/wiki/Last_Ship" title="Last Ship">Last Ship</a> in the book.</dd></dl>
