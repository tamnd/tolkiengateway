<i><b>winyamo</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "youngster".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) has suggested that the word consists of *[winya](../V/Vinya.md)* ("young") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mo (page does not exist)">mo</a></i> ("somebody, one").<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>
