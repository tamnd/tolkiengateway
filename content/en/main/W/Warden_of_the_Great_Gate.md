The **Warden of the Great Gate** was the title given to the keeper of the [Gate of Steel](/wiki/Gate_of_Steel), who held ultimate authority over all the [Seven Gates](/wiki/Seven_Gates) of the [Orfalch Echor](/wiki/Orfalch_Echor). At the time of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor)'s arrival at [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), this was [Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
