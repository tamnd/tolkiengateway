The **Warden of the Great Gate** was the title given to the keeper of the [Gate of Steel](../G/Gate_of_Steel.md), who held ultimate authority over all the [Seven Gates](../S/Seven_Gates.md) of the [Orfalch Echor](../O/Orfalch_Echor.md). At the time of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md)'s arrival at [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), this was [Ecthelion](../E/Ecthelion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
