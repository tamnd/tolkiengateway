**War of Wrath** is the opening track for [Blind Guardian](../B/Blind_Guardian.md)'s Tolkien themed album, *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](../N/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth.md)*. It is a conversation between [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).
