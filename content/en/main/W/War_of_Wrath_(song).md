**War of Wrath** is the opening track for [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s Tolkien themed album, *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)*. It is a conversation between [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).
