**Westmarch** can refer to:

- **[Westmarch of the Shire](/wiki/Westmarch "Westmarch"), land added to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1452](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31").**
    - [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch), a hereditary position held by the [Fairbairn Family](/wiki/Fairbairn_Family).
    - [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch), the basis for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and the *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## See also {#See_also}

- The [West-march](/wiki/West-march) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), including a wedge of land between the rivers [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn) and the [Isen](/wiki/Isen)
- [West-mark](/wiki/West-mark), the military division of Rohan, covering the lands west of [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash) and [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn).
- The [West March](/wiki/West_March), an alternative name for the forest of [Nivrim](/wiki/Nivrim), that laid west of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)
