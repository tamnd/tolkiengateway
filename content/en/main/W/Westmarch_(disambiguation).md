**Westmarch** can refer to:

    - [Warden of Westmarch](Warden_of_Westmarch.md), a hereditary position held by the [Fairbairn Family](../F/Fairbairn_Family~66364.md).
    - [Red Book of Westmarch](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md), the basis for *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and the *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
