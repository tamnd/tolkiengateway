The **whetting spell** was a [magic song](../M/Magic.md) that was sung by [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*. The spell mentions several and mysterious concepts that would never appear in any other works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md); the spell itself will not appear in later versions of the story.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) had been captured by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), his friend [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) and the [Gnome](../G/Gnome.md) [Flinding](../F/Flinding.md) rescued him from the enemy's camp while he was still chained and unconscious. Once they took him nearby, Beleg sought [his sword](../A/Anglachel.md) to cut Túrin's chains, and whispered a magic song to sharpen it:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>There wondrous wove he  words of sharpness,<br/>
and the names of knives  and Gnomish blades<br/>
he uttered o&#39;er it:  even Ogbar&#39;s spear<br/>
and the glaive of Gaurin  whose gleaming stroke<br/>
did rive the rocks  of Rodrim&#39;s hall;<br/>
the sword of Saithnar,  and the silver blades<br/>
of the enchanted children  of chains forged<br/>
in their deep dungeon;  the dirk of Nargil,<br/>
the knife of the North  in Nogrod smithied;<br/>
the sweeping sickle  of the slashing tempest,<br/>
the lambent lightning&#39;s  leaping falchion<br/>
even Celeg Aithorn  that shall cleave the world.<br/>
Then whistling whirled he  the whetted sword-blade<br/>
and three times three  it threshed the gloom,<br/>
till flame was kindled  flickering strangely<br/>
like licking firelight  in the lamp&#39;s glimmer<br/>
blue and baleful  at the blade&#39;s edges.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><i><a href="../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md" title="The Lay of the Children of Húrin">The Lay of the Children of Húrin</a></i>, &#34;II. Beleg&#34;, p. 45, vv. 1207-1223</cite></p>
</blockquote>

## List of terms {#List_of_terms}

Below is a list of terms that are used within the whetting spell.

- **Ogbar**, whose gleaming spear tore the rocks of Rodrim's hall.
- **Gaurin**, whose gleaming glaive tore the rocks of Rodrim's hall.
- **Rodrim**, whose hall was torn apart by the weapons of Ogbar and Gaurin.
- **Saithnar**, whose sword was invoked.
- **Nargil**, whose knife was forged in the north in [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md). (In a note, *Loruin* was written as an alternative name to *Nargil*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>).
- **Celeg Aithorn**, a mythological sword or lightning bolt that will cleave the world.
