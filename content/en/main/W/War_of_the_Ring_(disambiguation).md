The **War of the Ring** may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring)**, a major war in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* which ended following the destruction of [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring).

## Books {#Books}

- *[The War of the Ring](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring)*, the eighth book in *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series, and the third of *[The History of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
- *The War of the Ring*, the name of Book V in some editions of *The Lord of the Rings*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*, a [2004](/wiki/2004) video game by [Sierra Entertainment](/wiki/Sierra_Entertainment).
- *[War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(1977_board_game)> "War of the Ring (1977 board game)")*, a [1979](/wiki/1979) board game.
- *[War of the Ring](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2004_board_game)> "War of the Ring (2004 board game)")*, a [2004](/wiki/2004) board game.
- *[War of the Ring: Second Edition](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_Second_Edition)*, a [2011](/wiki/2011) board game by [ARES Games](/wiki/ARES_Games).
- *[War of the Ring: The Card Game](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_The_Card_Game)*, a [2023](/wiki/2023) board game by ARES Games.
- *The Appendices Part 5: The War of the Ring*, disc three of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").
- *War of the Ring*, a game mode in [The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II") and [The Rise of the Witch-King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king").
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_War_of_the_Ring_(Eamon_series)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The War of the Ring (Eamon series) (page does not exist)">The War of the Ring</a></i>, a series of games made by [Eamon](</wiki/Eamon_(video_game)> "Eamon (video game)").
- [*War of the Ring* (2009 book)](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring_(2009_book)> "War of the Ring (2009 book)"), a [2009](/wiki/2009) supplement for *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)*.
- *[War of the Ring: Battlehosts](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_Battlehosts)*, a [2010](/wiki/2010) supplement for *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)*.
