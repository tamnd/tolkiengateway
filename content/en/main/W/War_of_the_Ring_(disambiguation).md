The **War of the Ring** may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md)**, a major war in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* which ended following the destruction of [The One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).

## Books {#Books}

- *[The War of the Ring](../T/The_War_of_the_Ring.md)*, the eighth book in *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series, and the third of *[The History of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_History_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
- *The War of the Ring*, the name of Book V in some editions of *The Lord of the Rings*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)*, a [2004](../_/2004.md) video game by [Sierra Entertainment](../S/Sierra_Entertainment.md).
- *[War of the Ring](<War_of_the_Ring_(1977_board_game).md>)*, a [1979](../_/1979.md) board game.
- *[War of the Ring](<War_of_the_Ring_(2004_board_game).md>)*, a [2004](../_/2004.md) board game.
- *[War of the Ring: Second Edition](War_of_the_Ring%253A_Second_Edition.md)*, a [2011](../_/2011.md) board game by [ARES Games](../A/ARES_Games~73366.md).
- *[War of the Ring: The Card Game](War_of_the_Ring%253A_The_Card_Game.md)*, a [2023](../_/2023.md) board game by ARES Games.
- *The Appendices Part 5: The War of the Ring*, disc three of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>).
- *War of the Ring*, a game mode in [The Battle for Middle-earth II](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md) and [The Rise of the Witch-King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II%253A_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king.md).
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_War_of_the_Ring_(Eamon_series)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The War of the Ring (Eamon series) (page does not exist)">The War of the Ring</a></i>, a series of games made by [Eamon](<../E/Eamon_(video_game).md>).
- [*War of the Ring* (2009 book)](<War_of_the_Ring_(2009_book).md>), a [2009](../_/2009.md) supplement for *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game.md)*.
- *[War of the Ring: Battlehosts](War_of_the_Ring%253A_Battlehosts.md)*, a [2010](../_/2010.md) supplement for *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game.md)*.
