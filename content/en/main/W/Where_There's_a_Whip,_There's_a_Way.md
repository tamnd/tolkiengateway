**Where There's a Whip, There's a Way** is a song, written by [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough) for the [1980](/wiki/1980) [animated movie *The Return of the King*](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)"). The title is a pun on the proverb "Where there's a will, there's a way" and the [slave-driver](/wiki/Slave-drivers "Slave-drivers")'s comment "Where there's a whip, there's a will".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The tune and tone are very similar to *[Down, Down, To Goblin Town](/wiki/Down,_Down,_To_Goblin_Town)*, which had appeared in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)").

The song is sung during the trip [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") make through [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).
