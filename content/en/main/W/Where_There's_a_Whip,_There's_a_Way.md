**Where There's a Whip, There's a Way** is a song, written by [Glenn Yarbrough](../G/Glenn_Yarbrough.md) for the [1980](../_/1980.md) [animated movie *The Return of the King*](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>). The title is a pun on the proverb "Where there's a will, there's a way" and the [slave-driver](../S/Slave-drivers.md)'s comment "Where there's a whip, there's a will".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The tune and tone are very similar to *[Down, Down, To Goblin Town](../D/Down,_Down,_To_Goblin_Town.md)*, which had appeared in [*The Hobbit* (1977 film)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>).

The song is sung during the trip [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) make through [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).
