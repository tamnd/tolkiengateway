**Woodridings**, or **19 Lakeside Road**, was a bungalow owned by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It was situated in Branksome, Poole near [Bournemouth](/wiki/Bournemouth). He and [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") moved there in [1968](/wiki/1968) to escape Tolkien's fame in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) after his retirement, and lived there until Edith's death in November 1971.<sup id="cite_ref-TL838_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL838-1">[1]</a></sup> The widowed professor moved back to Oxford in March 1972.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The house was sold some time after September 2007 by the person who bought it from Tolkien<sup id="cite_ref-TL838_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL838-1">[1]</a></sup> and subsequently demolished to be replaced with two "superb contemporary houses", which at some point were given the names "Luthien House" and "Beren House".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Various "treasures" recovered from Tolkien's house were put up for exhibition in November 2008.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
