The **White Mountains**, or <i><b><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Etymology">Ered Nimrais</a></b></i> was a great mountain range that lay between [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon)/[Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in the North and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the South. They ran 600 miles (965 kilometers) from [Thrihyrne](/wiki/Thrihyrne) in the north-west to [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) and [Amon Tirith](/wiki/Hill_of_Guard "Hill of Guard") in the east. A low spur sprung off south-west, and ended at [Ras Morthil](/wiki/Ras_Morthil).

## Geographic features {#Geographic_features}

### Important peaks {#Important_peaks}

The mountain range’s notable peaks were, from west to east, [Thrihyrne](/wiki/Thrihyrne), [Starkhorn](/wiki/Starkhorn), [Dwimorberg](/wiki/Dwimorberg), [Írensaga](/wiki/%C3%8Drensaga), [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien), [Calenhad](/wiki/Calenhad), [Min-Rimmon](/wiki/Min-Rimmon), [Erelas](/wiki/Erelas), [Nardol](/wiki/Nardol), [Eilenach](/wiki/Eilenach), [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn), [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) and [Amon Tirith](/wiki/Amon_Tirith).

At the foot of Thrihyrne was the valley known as [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), and the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves) of Aglarond were underneath its spurs.

The three mountains Starkhorn, Dwimorberg and Irensaga enclosed the valley of [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow), and the Dwimorberg was also the home of the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).

Halifirien, Calenhad, Min-Rimmon, Erelas, Nardol, Eilenach, and Amon Dîn were strategically chosen hilltops on the northern side of the range. They were the [Warning beacons of Gondor](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor). There was probably a beacon-row on the south side too.

### Passes {#Passes}

There were passes over the White Mountains, and many of the kin of [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel) are said to have been lost there.<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Rivers {#Rivers}

There are multiple rivers which have their sources issuing forth from the White Mountains.

On the Western facing (from North to South);

- [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn)
- [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui)

On the Northern facing (from West to East);

- [Deeping-stream](/wiki/Deeping-stream)
- [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn)
- [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream)

On the Southern facing (from West to East);

- [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond)
- [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril)
- [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3)
- [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain)
- [Serni](/wiki/Serni)
- [Celos](/wiki/Celos)
- [Erui](/wiki/Erui)

## History {#History}

### Origins {#Origins}

A local legend among the [indigenous people](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) told of [giants](/wiki/Giants "Giants") making the mountains to keep [Men](/wiki/Men) out of their lands by the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). One of them, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Stiffneck&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Stiffneck (page does not exist)">Stiffneck</a> or [Tarlang](/wiki/Tarlang) died there and some peaks are his [neck](/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck "Tarlang's Neck") and [head](/wiki/Dol_Tarlang "Dol Tarlang"); the stones he was carrying were known as [Cûl Veleg](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_Veleg) and [Cûl Bîn](/wiki/C%C3%BBl_B%C3%AEn).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Inhabitants {#Inhabitants}

Throughout history the White Mountains were inhabited primarily by Men, including the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain), [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings), [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), and [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor"); but also at times by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and in the Fourth Age by [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).

The Drúedain lived along its northern edges, leaving [Púkel-men](/wiki/P%C3%BAkel-men) at [Starkhorn](/wiki/Starkhorn) and [Drúwaith Iaur](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur). They resettled to a forest that would later be named after them: [Tawar in-Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest "Drúadan Forest").

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the White Mountains were populated by a people related to the Dunlendings who had been servants of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). They swore allegiance to [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), but betrayed him and were cursed: they became known as the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers).

The valley on the southern side were under the rule of the [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor"), and the lords of [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale), [Ringló Vale](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3_Vale), [Tumladen](</wiki/Tumladen_(Gondor)> "Tumladen (Gondor)") and [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach) were loyal fiefs.<sup id="cite_ref-rkmap_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rkmap-3">[3]</a></sup>

From [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), the northern vales and hills became under the rule of the Rohirrim, mighty horse-lords from the North. They settled in [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) and [Grimslade](/wiki/Grimslade), and built their capital [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) on an outlying hill.<sup id="cite_ref-rkmap_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rkmap-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799"), thousands of [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") fled south and troubled Rohan for two generations. Although the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) fought them off, some Orcs managed to reach the Mountains; King [Walda](/wiki/Walda) was killed by an Orc at [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the early [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), son of [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) led a colony of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and settled in the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves), of which he had discovered the value during the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of the White Mountains, <i><b>Ered Nimrais</b></i>, consists of *[ered](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* ("mountains") and *nimrais* ("white-peaks; pale-horns").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, these mountains are initially referred to as the **South Mountains**<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:400</sup> and **Black Mountains**.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:472</sup>
