The **Woods of Oromë**, dominions of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), were in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), in the southern part of the land. To the west lay the [Pastures of Yavanna](/wiki/Pastures_of_Yavanna) while to the east rose the [Hyarmentir](/wiki/Hyarmentir), and beyond was the narrow land of [Avathar](/wiki/Avathar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") [Tilion](/wiki/Tilion) is told to have lived in the woods of Oromë from where he went to [Lórien](</wiki/L%C3%B3rien_(Valinor)> "Lórien (Valinor)") when he would rest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
