The **Woods of Oromë**, dominions of the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), were in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), in the southern part of the land. To the west lay the [Pastures of Yavanna](../P/Pastures_of_Yavanna.md) while to the east rose the [Hyarmentir](../H/Hyarmentir.md), and beyond was the narrow land of [Avathar](../A/Avathar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Maia](../M/Maiar.md) [Tilion](../T/Tilion.md) is told to have lived in the woods of Oromë from where he went to [Lórien](<../L/Lórien_(Valinor).md>) when he would rest.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
