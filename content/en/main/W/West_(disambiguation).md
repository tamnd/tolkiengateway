**West** is a relative term in [Arda](../A/Arda.md), usually associated with the sunset. It was known in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) as *[annûn](../A/Annûn.md)* or *[dûn](../D/Dûn.md)*, in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) as *[andúnë](../A/Andúnë.md)* or *[númen](../N/Númen.md)*, and in [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) as *[adûn](../A/Adûn.md)*.

In an abstract sense, "the West" was honored as the home of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md), and the legacy of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). Politically, it encompassed the [realms and peoples](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md) of the [Westlands](Westlands.md) who belonged to the cultural sphere of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and were allied to them against [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).

*West* may refer to:

## Arda {#Arda}

### Location {#Location}

- **The [Uttermost West](../U/Uttermost_West.md) or Far West, [Aman](../A/Aman.md). There, the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) dwell, and the Elves go when they "sail West".**
- [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), the most western land east of Belegaer. Elves who dwelt here were occasionally known as <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=D%C3%BAnedhil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dúnedhil (page does not exist)">Dúnedhil</a>, West-elves, to distinguish them from the [Avari](../A/Avari.md).
- The West, or [Westernesse](../N/Númenor.md), lies in Belegaer, to the West of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), or Men of the West, take their name after this sense.
- **After the drowning of both Beleriand and Númenor, "the West" generally referred to the north-western portions of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), or [Westlands](Westlands.md), specifically [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md); or at least [those who opposed Sauron](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md).**
