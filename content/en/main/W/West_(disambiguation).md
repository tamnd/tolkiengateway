**West** is a relative term in [Arda](/wiki/Arda), usually associated with the sunset. It was known in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as *[annûn](/wiki/Ann%C3%BBn "Annûn")* or *[dûn](/wiki/D%C3%BBn "Dûn")*, in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) as *[andúnë](/wiki/And%C3%BAn%C3%AB "Andúnë")* or *[númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen "Númen")*, and in [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) as *[adûn](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBn "Adûn")*.

In an abstract sense, "the West" was honored as the home of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar), and the legacy of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Politically, it encompassed the [realms and peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples "Free peoples") of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) who belonged to the cultural sphere of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and were allied to them against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).

*West* may refer to:

## Arda {#Arda}

### Location {#Location}

- **The [Uttermost West](/wiki/Uttermost_West) or Far West, [Aman](/wiki/Aman). There, the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) dwell, and the Elves go when they "sail West".**
- [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), the most western land east of Belegaer. Elves who dwelt here were occasionally known as <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=D%C3%BAnedhil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dúnedhil (page does not exist)">Dúnedhil</a>, West-elves, to distinguish them from the [Avari](/wiki/Avari).
- The West, or [Westernesse](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor"), lies in Belegaer, to the West of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), or Men of the West, take their name after this sense.
- **After the drowning of both Beleriand and Númenor, "the West" generally referred to the north-western portions of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), or [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands), specifically [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador); or at least [those who opposed Sauron](/wiki/Free_peoples "Free peoples").**
