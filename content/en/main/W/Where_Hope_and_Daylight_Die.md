<i><b>Where Hope and Daylight Die</b></i> is the fifth track of the album [Stronghold](../S/Stronghold.md) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The lyrics are influenced by an earlier version of the [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) poem [In Western Lands beneath the Sun](../I/In_Western_Lands_beneath_the_Sun.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Still here I wake and I think of you<br/>
I see you far away<br/>
Answer my call<br/>
Can you hear my voice<br/>
I hear you<br/>
<br/>
For we are gone and forever lost<br/>
Broken here I lie<br/>
Beneath the shadow sink<br/>
Where daylight dies<br/>
I wake for you<br/>
<br/>
In better lands the sun may shine<br/>
And green leaves on trees spring<br/>
Their opening and blossoming<br/>
But here the raven sing<br/>
<br/>
But still I stand and think of<br/>
Days when grass was green<br/>
And my heart was so young<br/>
They &#39;ve never been past<br/>
Forever lost
</p>
</div>
