**Wolf-riders**,<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-deep_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-deep-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-road_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-road-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-battle_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle-5">[4]</a></sup> or **wolfriders**,<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> were groups of "[Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) or Orclike beings" that rode on [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves").<sup id="cite_ref-index_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-7">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) used [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") as steeds during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-first_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-first-8">[7]</a></sup> Wolfriders partook in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears) alongside wolves and [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons).<sup id="cite_ref-jewels_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jewels-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), there were alliances that were made between the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs) in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) and the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) against the [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen). The Orcs sometimes rode on wolves.<sup id="cite_ref-alliance_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-alliance-11">[10]</a></sup> During the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), the swiftest wolf-riders were used by the Orcs and Wargs as a vanguard to attack [Dale](/wiki/Dale), where they killed many [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and [Lake-men](/wiki/Lake-men) before being defeated on the eastern slopes of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle-5">[4]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) employed Wolf-riders alongside Orcs and [evil](/wiki/Category:Evil "Category:Evil") [Men](/wiki/Men) to harass [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup> These Wolf-riders<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup> were mostly used in tracking fugitives or destroying isolated groups.<sup id="cite_ref-fords_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fords-12">[11]</a></sup>

On [25 February](/wiki/25_February), "a great pack of the dreaded Orcish wolfriders" were part of the vanguard of the eastern force that Saruman had sent to slay [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) during the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> These wolfriders<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> were "feared by [horses](/wiki/Horses "Horses")"<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> as they passed through gaps between horsemen and slashed at the bellies of the horses.<sup id="cite_ref-fords_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fords-12">[11]</a></sup> After Théodred's death,<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> two wolfriders were reported by [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm)'s outriders, causing him to ride to the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen) instead of to the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup>

On [2 March](/wiki/2_March), "several troops of the dreaded wolfriders" were sent along the eastern bank of the [Isen](/wiki/Isen) as forerunners to the main division of Saruman's forces. These wolfriders tried to attack Elfhelm's companies and prevent him from reinforcing [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold).<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen), it was reported by a swift scout "that wolf-riders were abroad in the [valley](/wiki/Westfold_Vale "Westfold Vale")" about [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-deep_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-deep-3">[2]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had sent Elfhelm "with many Riders to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras)" because he was afraid of "wolf-riders and plunderers" riding to [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld) when no-one was there to defend it.<sup id="cite_ref-road_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-road-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, [Beren](/wiki/Beren) slew many "wolf-rider Orcs"<sup id="cite_ref-notes_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-13">[12]</a></sup> during his adventures prior to entering [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-tinuviel_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tinuviel-14">[13]</a></sup>

In an early version of the [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad) in the tale of "[Turambar and the Foalókë](/wiki/Turambar_and_the_Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB)", some of the Orcs in [Glorund](/wiki/Glaurung "Glaurung")'s army were "mounted upon wolves".<sup id="cite_ref-turambar_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turambar-15">[14]</a></sup>

In the immediate aftermath of the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) in the [first version](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)") of the tale, [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) and six of the [Exiles of Gondolin](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin) were chased by a score of wolfriders in [Tumladin](/wiki/Tumladin) before being rescued by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and the [folk of the Wing](/wiki/Folk_of_the_Wing "Folk of the Wing"). Two of the wolfriders escaped. After going through the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Echoriad "Echoriad"), the Exiles of Gondolin became fearful of encountering any wolfriders.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-16">[15]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, wolfriders roamed freely alongside "wandering [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and the [Things of Morgoth](/wiki/Things_of_Morgoth)" throughout [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-roaming_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-roaming-17">[16]</a></sup>

After the fall of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, wolf-riders were among the Orcs and Wolves that took [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) captive.<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-18">[17]</a></sup>

In a very early draft of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) took [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) across Rohan to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), but after smelling some war, they all witnessed "a battle of **Wolfriders** (Saruman) and the [Horsemasters](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim")" on the way.<sup id="cite_ref-early_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-early-19">[18]</a></sup> It is very likely that this battle was the earliest version of the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen) since those are the only battles in *The Lord of the Rings* in which Wolf-riders<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup> fight against the Rohirrim.

In an early draft of the chapter "[The Road to Isengard](/wiki/The_Road_to_Isengard)", Gandalf's fear of "wolf-riders and plunderers"<sup id="cite_ref-road_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-road-4">[3]</a></sup> riding to Meduseld was originally a fear of "wolf-riders and cruel plunderers" being "sent swiftly" to the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Golden_Hall).<sup id="cite_ref-cruel_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cruel-20">[19]</a></sup>

In an early draft of the chapter "[Flotsam and Jetsam](/wiki/Flotsam_and_Jetsam)", Merry reported that Saruman emptied Isengard of all his remaining forces, which included groups of Orcs "mounted on great wolves" and regiments of [Dunlanders](/wiki/Dunlanders).<sup id="cite_ref-forces_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-forces-21">[20]</a></sup> He also saw the [Ents](/wiki/Ents) deal "with a pack of wolf-riders" that were escorting [Gríma](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma) into [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-isengard_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isengard-22">[21]</a></sup>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The card <i>Wolf-riders</i> can be played either as a creature or as a short-event, the latter increasing the prowess and strikes of a Wolf attack.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Saruman refers to the pack of Wolf-riders under the command of <a href="/wiki/Sharku" title="Sharku">Sharku</a> as <b>Warg-riders</b> and sends them to attack the Rohan refugees of Edoras on their way to Helm&#39;s Deep. During the skirmish with Warg-riders, <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> falls from a cliff, and is later found by <a href="/wiki/Brego_(horse)" title="Brego (horse)">Brego</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Orc lieutenant <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gothmog" title="Gothmog">Gothmog</a> rides a Warg during one scene. Two Warg-riders are seen riding alongside the <a href="/wiki/Easterlings" title="Easterlings">Easterlings</a> after the <a href="/wiki/Olog-hai" title="Olog-hai">Olog-hai</a> breach the <a href="/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith" title="Great Gate of Minas Tirith">Great Gate of Minas Tirith</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the expansion <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king">The Rise of the Witch-king</a></i>, Thrall Masters can summon <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a> Wolf-Riders.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the film trilogy, <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bolg" title="Bolg">Bolg</a>, as well as many orcs under their command, are shown to ride Wargs as they pursue <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a>, and later take part in the Battle of the Five Armies.</dd></dl>
