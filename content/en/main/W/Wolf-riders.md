**Wolf-riders**,<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-deep_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-deep-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-road_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-road-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-battle_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle-5">[4]</a></sup> or **wolfriders**,<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> were groups of "[Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) or Orclike beings" that rode on [wolves](Wolves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-index_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-7">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) used [wolves](Wolves.md) as steeds during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-first_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-first-8">[7]</a></sup> Wolfriders partook in the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../B/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears.md) alongside wolves and [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-jewels_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jewels-9">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

During the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), there were alliances that were made between the [Wargs](Wargs.md) in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) and the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) against the [Woodmen](Woodmen.md). The Orcs sometimes rode on wolves.<sup id="cite_ref-alliance_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-alliance-11">[10]</a></sup> During the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md), the swiftest wolf-riders were used by the Orcs and Wargs as a vanguard to attack [Dale](../D/Dale.md), where they killed many [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and [Lake-men](../L/Lake-men.md) before being defeated on the eastern slopes of the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle-5">[4]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md), [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) employed Wolf-riders alongside Orcs and [evil](../../category/E/Evil.md) [Men](../M/Men.md) to harass [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup> These Wolf-riders<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup> were mostly used in tracking fugitives or destroying isolated groups.<sup id="cite_ref-fords_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fords-12">[11]</a></sup>

On [25 February](../_/25_February.md), "a great pack of the dreaded Orcish wolfriders" were part of the vanguard of the eastern force that Saruman had sent to slay [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md) during the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> These wolfriders<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> were "feared by [horses](../H/Horses.md)"<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> as they passed through gaps between horsemen and slashed at the bellies of the horses.<sup id="cite_ref-fords_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fords-12">[11]</a></sup> After Théodred's death,<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup> two wolfriders were reported by [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md)'s outriders, causing him to ride to the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md) instead of to the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup>

On [2 March](../_/2_March.md), "several troops of the dreaded wolfriders" were sent along the eastern bank of the [Isen](../I/Isen.md) as forerunners to the main division of Saruman's forces. These wolfriders tried to attack Elfhelm's companies and prevent him from reinforcing [Grimbold](../G/Grimbold.md).<sup id="cite_ref-isen_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isen-6">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md), it was reported by a swift scout "that wolf-riders were abroad in the [valley](Westfold_Vale.md)" about [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-deep_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-deep-3">[2]</a></sup>

[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) had sent Elfhelm "with many Riders to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md)" because he was afraid of "wolf-riders and plunderers" riding to [Meduseld](../M/Meduseld.md) when no-one was there to defend it.<sup id="cite_ref-road_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-road-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, [Beren](../B/Beren.md) slew many "wolf-rider Orcs"<sup id="cite_ref-notes_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-13">[12]</a></sup> during his adventures prior to entering [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-tinuviel_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tinuviel-14">[13]</a></sup>

In an early version of the [Battle of Tumhalad](../B/Battle_of_Tumhalad.md) in the tale of "[Turambar and the Foalókë](../T/Turambar_and_the_Foalókë.md)", some of the Orcs in [Glorund](../G/Glaurung.md)'s army were "mounted upon wolves".<sup id="cite_ref-turambar_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-turambar-15">[14]</a></sup>

In the immediate aftermath of the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) in the [first version](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>) of the tale, [Eärendel](../E/Eärendel.md) and six of the [Exiles of Gondolin](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) were chased by a score of wolfriders in [Tumladin](../T/Tumladin.md) before being rescued by [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and the [folk of the Wing](../F/Folk_of_the_Wing.md). Two of the wolfriders escaped. After going through the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Echoriad.md), the Exiles of Gondolin became fearful of encountering any wolfriders.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-16">[15]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](../B/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears.md) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, wolfriders roamed freely alongside "wandering [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and the [Things of Morgoth](../T/Things_of_Morgoth.md)" throughout [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-roaming_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-roaming-17">[16]</a></sup>

After the fall of [Dor-Cúarthol](../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, wolf-riders were among the Orcs and Wolves that took [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) captive.<sup id="cite_ref-beleg_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beleg-18">[17]</a></sup>

In a very early draft of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) took [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) across Rohan to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), but after smelling some war, they all witnessed "a battle of **Wolfriders** (Saruman) and the [Horsemasters](../R/Rohirrim.md)" on the way.<sup id="cite_ref-early_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-early-19">[18]</a></sup> It is very likely that this battle was the earliest version of the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](../B/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) since those are the only battles in *The Lord of the Rings* in which Wolf-riders<sup id="cite_ref-riders_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-riders-1">[1]</a></sup> fight against the Rohirrim.

In an early draft of the chapter "[The Road to Isengard](../T/The_Road_to_Isengard.md)", Gandalf's fear of "wolf-riders and plunderers"<sup id="cite_ref-road_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-road-4">[3]</a></sup> riding to Meduseld was originally a fear of "wolf-riders and cruel plunderers" being "sent swiftly" to the [Golden Hall](../G/Golden_Hall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-cruel_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cruel-20">[19]</a></sup>

In an early draft of the chapter "[Flotsam and Jetsam](../F/Flotsam_and_Jetsam.md)", Merry reported that Saruman emptied Isengard of all his remaining forces, which included groups of Orcs "mounted on great wolves" and regiments of [Dunlanders](../D/Dunlanders.md).<sup id="cite_ref-forces_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-forces-21">[20]</a></sup> He also saw the [Ents](../E/Ents.md) deal "with a pack of wolf-riders" that were escorting [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md) into [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-isengard_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-isengard-22">[21]</a></sup>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The card <i>Wolf-riders</i> can be played either as a creature or as a short-event, the latter increasing the prowess and strikes of a Wolf attack.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Saruman refers to the pack of Wolf-riders under the command of <a href="../S/Sharku.md" title="Sharku">Sharku</a> as <b>Warg-riders</b> and sends them to attack the Rohan refugees of Edoras on their way to Helm&#39;s Deep. During the skirmish with Warg-riders, <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> falls from a cliff, and is later found by <a href="../B/Brego_(horse).md" title="Brego (horse)">Brego</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Orc lieutenant <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../G/Gothmog.md" title="Gothmog">Gothmog</a> rides a Warg during one scene. Two Warg-riders are seen riding alongside the <a href="../E/Easterlings.md" title="Easterlings">Easterlings</a> after the <a href="../O/Olog-hai.md" title="Olog-hai">Olog-hai</a> breach the <a href="../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md" title="Great Gate of Minas Tirith">Great Gate of Minas Tirith</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the expansion <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II%253A_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king">The Rise of the Witch-king</a></i>, Thrall Masters can summon <a href="../G/Gundabad.md" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a> Wolf-Riders.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the film trilogy, <a href="../A/Azog.md" title="Azog">Azog</a> and <a href="../B/Bolg.md" title="Bolg">Bolg</a>, as well as many orcs under their command, are shown to ride Wargs as they pursue <a href="../T/Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a>, and later take part in the Battle of the Five Armies.</dd></dl>
