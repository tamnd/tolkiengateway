The **white pillar** was a monument erected upon the highest hill of the headland overlooking [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar). This great pillar was white with a crystal globe at its peak. The globe absorbed the rays of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) or the [Moon](/wiki/Moon) and shone like a star. In clear weather, this star-like glow could be seen far out to sea and even upon the south coasts of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3261](/wiki/Second_Age_3261 "Second Age 3261"), the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) King [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) landed at Umbar and humbled the might of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> Although Ar-Pharazôn's reign marked the darkest times of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), culminating in [disaster](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor"), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his exiled followers remembered the landing with pride. They erected the white pillar in memory of the event, commemorating the power of their race and the humiliation of the Dark Lord.

At the end of the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife) between the factions of [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") and [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir), the sons of the usurper sailed with their followers from [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) to [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1448](/wiki/Third_Age_1448 "Third Age 1448"), wresting control of the city from Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After Umbar fell under the domination of Sauron's servants, the memorial to his humiliation was destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>
