**Wolvercote Cemetery** is a municipal cemetery in north [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md). It is the final resting place of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He was buried with his wife, [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md). [John Tolkien's](../J/John_Tolkien.md) grave is not far away from his parents' grave. [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md) is also buried in Wolvercote Cemetery.

<h2><span id="The_Tolkiens.27_grave"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Tolkiens&#39;_grave">The Tolkiens&#39; grave</span></h2>

Edith Tolkien died on [28 November](../_/29_November.md), [1971](../_/1971.md), at the age of eighty-two, and Tolkien had the name *Lúthien* engraved on her stone.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> On sundays after church he would visit her grave.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When Tolkien died 21 months later of pneumonia on [2 September](../_/2_September.md), [1973](../_/1973.md), at the age of 81, he was buried in the same grave, with *Beren* added to his name,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> so that the engraving now reads:

<dl><dd><i>Edith Mary Tolkien,</i> <i>Lúthien,</i> <i>1889 – 1971</i></dd>
<dd><i>John Ronald Reuel Tolkien,</i> <i>Beren</i> , <i>1892 – 1973</i></dd></dl>
