**Whales** were large aquatic creatures of the seas of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). A famous ship of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md), the [Hirilondë](../H/Hirilondë.md), was also given a name after this creature: *Turuphanto*, meaning the "Wooden Whale".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) appears the whale [Uin](../U/Uin.md), the "Primeval Whale",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> a vassal of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *Gnomish Lexicon*, the word <i><b>uin</b></i> means "whale". Another [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) word for whale was <i><b>uimoth</b></i> ("sheep of the waves"), incorporating *[moth](../S/Sheep.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other fiction {#Other_fiction}

In the story *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)* appears the large whale Uin, said to be the "*oldest of the Right Whales*".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Whales are animals included among the Water Beasts. Races include humpback whales, killer whales, and the Cherethrynd. A related creature is the Demon whale of Forochel, which is included among the Water Monsters.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
