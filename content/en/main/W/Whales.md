**Whales** were large aquatic creatures of the seas of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). A famous ship of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), the [Hirilondë](/wiki/Hirilond%C3%AB), was also given a name after this creature: *Turuphanto*, meaning the "Wooden Whale".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") appears the whale [Uin](/wiki/Uin), the "Primeval Whale",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> a vassal of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *Gnomish Lexicon*, the word <i><b>uin</b></i> means "whale". Another [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) word for whale was <i><b>uimoth</b></i> ("sheep of the waves"), incorporating *[moth](/wiki/Sheep "Sheep")*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other fiction {#Other_fiction}

In the story *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)* appears the large whale Uin, said to be the "*oldest of the Right Whales*".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Whales are animals included among the Water Beasts. Races include humpback whales, killer whales, and the Cherethrynd. A related creature is the Demon whale of Forochel, which is included among the Water Monsters.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
