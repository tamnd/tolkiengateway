**Waymeet** or **Waymoot** was a town in the [Westfarthing](Westfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). The [East Road](../E/East_Road.md), the main road through the Shire, ran through the town on its way to [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md). Two smaller roads also led to Waymeet: one from [Little Delving](../L/Little_Delving.md) to the north and one from [Tookland](../T/Tookland.md) and the [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md) beyond.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Where these roadways came together, a town had grown up, appropriately known as Waymeet.

## History {#History}

In the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md), the ruffians who took over the Shire had a staging post or barracks in Waymeet,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and it was from here that [Sharkey's Men](../S/Sharkey's_Men.md) set out for the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md), in which they were soundly defeated by the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name refers to the village being at the meeting of three ways.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name *Waymeet* is a modernised spelling of the older form, [Waymoot](Waymoot.md), which was the spelling preferred by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in early drafts of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*. Though he ultimately modernised the name within the book itself, the map of the Shire in the prologue of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* was never corrected, and still carries the older spelling of the name.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-4">[4]</a></sup>
