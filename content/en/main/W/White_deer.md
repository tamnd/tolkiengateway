**White deer** were a type of [deer](/wiki/Deer "Deer") found in the forest of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). Along with two fawns, such a hind surprised [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) on the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor) when they first entered [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) forest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

An encounter with a white doe occurs in "[The Lay of Aotrou and Itroun](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Aotrou_and_Itroun)", preceding Autrou's meeting with a witch.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Magical animals of white colour were common in [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) and [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) mythology. The legends of King Arthur include a story about the pursuit of a large, <a class="external text" href="https://mortedarthur.fandom.com/wiki/White_hart" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">white deer</a>, leading to the arrival at the magical Sir Pellinore's well.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) has noted that the appearance of white deer in *The Hobbit* forebodes the approach by Bilbo and company to the [Wood-elven](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood") area of Mirkwood, as "*\[i\]n Celtic tradition, encounters with white animals (especially white deer) usually prefigure an encounter with beings from the Otherworld ([Faërie](/wiki/Faerie "Faerie"))*".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The White Hart is the largest breed of deer found in northwestern Middle-earth (rumoured to have <a href="/wiki/Fairies" title="Fairies">Faerie</a> blood). The suggested Sindarin name of this type of deer was <i>Nimfiara</i> in the early publications from MERP, later revised to <i>Nimmerais</i> (sing. <i>Nimmaras</i>).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Extended Edition includes a scene in which a white deer appears as the dwarves try to cross the river in Mirkwood. <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield" title="Thorin Oakenshield">Thorin</a> tries to shoot it down with an arrow but he misses. The deer later flees.</dd></dl>
