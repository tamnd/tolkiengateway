**Woody End** was an upland wooded region of the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), lying between the [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md) on the west and the [Marish](../M/Marish.md) on the east. Its woods were the sources of the [Stock-brook](../S/Stock-brook.md) and the [Thistle Brook](../T/Thistle_Brook.md). One the northern eaves of the region was [Woodhall](Woodhall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the evening of [24 September](../_/24_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) met [Gildor Inglorion](../G/Gildor_Inglorion.md) and his party of wandering elves in the Woody End.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [22 September](../_/22_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3021](../T/Third_Age_3021.md) the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](../L/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings.md) passed from the Woody End on their way to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md), and they were joined by Frodo and Sam.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) the name means "place at one end of a wood", representing a possible [Old Hobbitish](../O/Old_English.md) *Wudig Ende*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
