**Woody End** was an upland wooded region of the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), lying between the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country) on the west and the [Marish](/wiki/Marish) on the east. Its woods were the sources of the [Stock-brook](/wiki/Stock-brook) and the [Thistle Brook](/wiki/Thistle_Brook). One the northern eaves of the region was [Woodhall](/wiki/Woodhall).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the evening of [24 September](/wiki/24_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") met [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor_Inglorion) and his party of wandering elves in the Woody End.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021") the [Last Riding of the Keepers of the Rings](/wiki/Last_Riding_of_the_Keepers_of_the_Rings) passed from the Woody End on their way to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens), and they were joined by Frodo and Sam.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) the name means "place at one end of a wood", representing a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *Wudig Ende*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
