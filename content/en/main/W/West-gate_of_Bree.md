The **West-gate of Bree** was the portal into [Bree](/wiki/Bree) on the west side of the settlement.

## Description {#Description}

The West-gate was one of the two openings of the hedge that surrounded Bree, at the points where the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) entered and exited the village. Being the westernmost opening to Bree, it looked towards [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was guarded by one [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring),<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup> though he later joined with [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and the followers of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Since it is mentioned that the gate clanged when shut it apparently was made of metal.<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") arrived at the West-gate on [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> well after dark. Harry Goatleaf was sitting in a lodge beyond the gate but when the hobbits arrived he jumped up, fetched a lantern, and looked over the gate. He questioned the travelers before allowing them entry. After the hobbits left a dark figure climbed over the gate without Harry being aware of his entry.<sup id="cite_ref-Pony_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pony-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") told the hobbits that [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") had visited Harry Goatleaf at the West-gate on Monday<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> ([27 September](/wiki/27_September)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>).

After the War of the Ring the four hobbits returned to Bree on their journey home. They passed through the West-gate heading for the Shire on [30 October](/wiki/30_October) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
