"**When evening in the Shire was grey**" is the first line of a poem that was recited by [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md). It is a lament for [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), who had fallen in [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Poem {#Poem}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><audio src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/a/af/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_When_evening_in_the_Shire_was_grey.mp3" width="300" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 300px;" controls=""><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_When_evening_in_the_Shire_was_grey.mp3">https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_When_evening_in_the_Shire_was_grey.mp3</a></audio><figcaption><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> reads When evening in the Shire was grey</figcaption></figure>

<br><br><br><br><br>

<div class="poem">
<p><i>When evening in <a href="../T/The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">the Shire</a> was grey<br/></i>
<i>his footsteps on <a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Hill.md" title="The Hill">the Hill</a> were heard;<br/></i>
<i>before the dawn he went away<br/></i>
<i>on journey long without a word.<br/></i>
<br/>
<i>From  <a href="../R/Rhovanion.md" title="Rhovanion">Wilderland</a> to Western shore,<br/></i>
<i>from northern waste to southern hill,<br/></i>
<i>through <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">dragon-lair</a> and <a href="../B/Back_Door.md" title="Back Door">hidden door</a><br/></i>
<i>and <a href="../M/Mirkwood.md" title="Mirkwood">darkling woods</a> he walked at will.<br/></i>
<br/>
<i>With <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarf</a> and <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbit</a>, <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a> and <a href="../M/Men.md" title="Men">Men</a>,<br/></i>
<i>with mortal and <a href="../M/Maiar.md" title="Maiar">immortal folk</a>,<br/></i>
<i>with <a href="../G/Great_Eagles.md" title="Great Eagles">bird</a> on bough and <a href="../B/Beorn.md" title="Beorn">beast</a> in den,<br/></i>
<i>in their own secret tongues he spoke.<br/></i>
<br/>
<i>A deadly <a href="../G/Glamdring.md" title="Glamdring">sword</a>, a healing hand,<br/></i>
<i>a back that bent beneath his load;<br/></i>
<i>a trumpet-voice, a burning brand,<br/></i>
<i>a weary <a href="../G/Gandalf.md#Other_names" title="Gandalf">pilgrim</a> on the road.<br/></i>
<br/>
<i>A lord of wisdom throned he sat,<br/></i>
<i>swift in anger, quick to laugh;<br/></i>
<i>an old man in a battered hat<br/></i>
<i>who leaned upon a thorny staff.<br/></i>
<br/>
<i>He stood upon the <a href="../B/Bridge_of_Khazad-dûm.md" title="Bridge of Khazad-dûm">bridge</a> alone<br/></i>
<i>and <a href="../D/Durin&#39;s_Bane.md" title="Durin&#39;s Bane">Fire and Shadow</a> both defied;<br/></i>
<i>his staff was broken on the stone,<br/></i>
<i>in <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Khazad-dûm</a> his wisdom died.</i>
</p>
</div>

<dl><dd>Upon hearing this poem, <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Samwise Gamgee</a> suggested another stanza:</dd></dl>

<div class="poem">
<p><i>The finest rockets ever seen:<br/></i>
<i>they burst in stars of blue and green,<br/></i>
<i>or after thunder golden showers<br/></i>
<i>came falling like a rain of flowers.</i>
</p>
</div>
