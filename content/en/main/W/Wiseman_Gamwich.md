**Wiseman Gamwich** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Wiseman was the son of [Hamfast of Gamwich](/wiki/Hamfast_of_Gamwich). He moved to [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield), where his descendants became the [Gamgees](/wiki/Gamgee_Family "Gamgee Family").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
