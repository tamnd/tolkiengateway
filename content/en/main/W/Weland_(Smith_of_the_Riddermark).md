**Weland**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a smith and [horse](../H/Horses.md) tender of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)

Weland is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* film.

## History {#History}

Weland was a [Man](../R/Rohirrim.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) during the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md). He worked as a smith and [horse](../H/Horses.md) tender in [the Riddermark](../R/Rohan.md).

Anticipating an attack on the capital of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) fled with the Rohirrim to the fortress of [the Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) in [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md). Weland found much work at Helm's Deep, tending to many Riders' horses. When [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) reported that [Saruman the White](../S/Saruman.md) had sent a force of over ten thousand [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) to assail the Hornburg, Théoden ordered every man and strong boy able to bear arms to prepare for battle. Weland armoured himself and when the Uruks arrived, he fought in [the Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md).
