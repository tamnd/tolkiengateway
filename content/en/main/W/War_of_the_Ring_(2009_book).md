<i><b>The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: War of the Ring</b></i> is a rulebook for [Games Workshop](/wiki/Games_Workshop)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)*. It provides an expansion to the original game rules published in *[The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game: Rulebook](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game:_Rulebook)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

As the Third Age draws to a close, Middle-earth is ravaged by the War of The Ring. Great battles are fought across the lands as Sauron's fell armies invade from Mordor to bring the Dark Lord's dominion to all.

This 328-page rules manual for War of The Ring is packed with inspiring pictures, both standard and historical scenarios, hobby advice, extensive profiles and army organisation for every model in the range, and all the rules you'll need to play out massive legendary battles in Middle-earth. This is the ultimate guide and essential purchase for playing games of War of The Ring.
