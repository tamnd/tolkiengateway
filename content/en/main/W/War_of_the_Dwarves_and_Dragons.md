The **War of the Dwarves and Dragons** is a name given to the conflict that took place between [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) and the [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons) of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), which took place from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2570](/wiki/Third_Age_2570 "Third Age 2570") to around [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589"). It brought about the death of King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I) and resulted in [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) returning to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

Following the defeat of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), many Dragons were left in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Between the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") and the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the [Seven Rings](/wiki/Seven_Rings) helped the Dwarves increase their treasure troves and established their legendary [Seven Hoards](/wiki/Seven_Hoards), which however drew the mightiest of dragons and opponents; and over the years they plundered the works of the Dwarves and devoured some of the Rings.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Many centuries before the beginning of the war, the [Long-worm](/wiki/Long-worm) [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha) had plundered treasure from the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and kept it in his hoard. Scatha was slain by [Fram](/wiki/Fram) of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), who kept the treasure for himself and refused to yield a penny of it to the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

Following the loss of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") to [the Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane"), in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1999](/wiki/Third_Age_1999 "Third Age 1999") [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) at [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). However, in [2210](/wiki/Third_Age_2210 "Third Age 2210") Thráin's son, [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I), abandoned Erebor and removed his people to the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) to join the rest of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Conflicts {#Conflicts}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But there were dragons in the wastes beyond; and after many years they became strong again and multiplied, and they made war on the Dwarves, and plundered their works.
</p>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

The Dragons in the North eventually multiplied and became strong, and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2570](/wiki/Third_Age_2570 "Third Age 2570")<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> they made war on [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves"), sacking and plundering their halls. The Dwarves held out for around twenty years, but finally in [2589](/wiki/Third_Age_2589 "Third Age 2589")<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> the Dragons attacked the halls of King [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I). King Dáin, and his second son [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r), were killed by a great [Cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drake) outside the doors to his halls.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Following the death of their king, most of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) abandoned the Grey Mountains. In [2590](/wiki/Third_Age_2590 "Third Age 2590"),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> King [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) and his uncle [Borin](/wiki/Borin) returned to Erebor with the [Arkenstone](/wiki/Arkenstone) to re-establish the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain). However, Thrór's younger brother [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r) led others to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

The [Dwarves of the Iron Hills](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills) and the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) managed to prosper for the following couple of centuries.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> However the threat of Dragons would return: in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770") the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug), hearing of the wealth of Erebor, flew south from the North "like a hurricane" and [sacked](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor") the wealthy [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Smaug dwelt in the Lonely Mountain until the [Quest for Erebor](/wiki/Quest_for_Erebor) in [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") which resulted in Smaug being killed by the [Black Arrow](/wiki/Black_Arrow) shot by [Bard](/wiki/Bard).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Although [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) was killed in the subsequent [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), [Dáin](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") of the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) (grandson of [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r)) became [King of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") and [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
