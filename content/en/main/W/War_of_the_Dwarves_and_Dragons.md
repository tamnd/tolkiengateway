The **War of the Dwarves and Dragons** is a name given to the conflict that took place between [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) and the [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) of the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md), which took place from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2570](../T/Third_Age_2570.md) to around [2589](../T/Third_Age_2589.md). It brought about the death of King [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md) and resulted in [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) returning to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background {#Background}

Following the defeat of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) in the [War of Wrath](War_of_Wrath.md), many Dragons were left in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Between the [Second](../S/Second_Age.md) and the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the [Seven Rings](../S/Seven_Rings.md) helped the Dwarves increase their treasure troves and established their legendary [Seven Hoards](../S/Seven_Hoards.md), which however drew the mightiest of dragons and opponents; and over the years they plundered the works of the Dwarves and devoured some of the Rings.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Many centuries before the beginning of the war, the [Long-worm](../L/Long-worm.md) [Scatha](../S/Scatha.md) had plundered treasure from the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and kept it in his hoard. Scatha was slain by [Fram](../F/Fram.md) of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), who kept the treasure for himself and refused to yield a penny of it to the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

Following the loss of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) to [the Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md), in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1999](../T/Third_Age_1999.md) [Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md) established the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md) at [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md). However, in [2210](../T/Third_Age_2210.md) Thráin's son, [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md), abandoned Erebor and removed his people to the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) to join the rest of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Conflicts {#Conflicts}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>But there were dragons in the wastes beyond; and after many years they became strong again and multiplied, and they made war on the Dwarves, and plundered their works.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>, <a href="../A/Appendix_A.md" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a>, &#34;Durin&#39;s Folk&#34;</cite></p>
</blockquote>

The Dragons in the North eventually multiplied and became strong, and in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2570](../T/Third_Age_2570.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> they made war on [Durin's Folk](../D/Dwarves.md), sacking and plundering their halls. The Dwarves held out for around twenty years, but finally in [2589](../T/Third_Age_2589.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> the Dragons attacked the halls of King [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md). King Dáin, and his second son [Frór](../F/Frór.md), were killed by a great [Cold-drake](../C/Cold-drake.md) outside the doors to his halls.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Following the death of their king, most of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) abandoned the Grey Mountains. In [2590](../T/Third_Age_2590.md),<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-5">[5]</a></sup> King [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) and his uncle [Borin](../B/Borin.md) returned to Erebor with the [Arkenstone](../A/Arkenstone.md) to re-establish the [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md). However, Thrór's younger brother [Grór](../G/Grór.md) led others to the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

The [Dwarves of the Iron Hills](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills.md) and the [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md) managed to prosper for the following couple of centuries.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup> However the threat of Dragons would return: in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md) the dragon [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md), hearing of the wealth of Erebor, flew south from the North "like a hurricane" and [sacked](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md) the wealthy [Kingdom under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Smaug dwelt in the Lonely Mountain until the [Quest for Erebor](../Q/Quest_for_Erebor.md) in [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md) which resulted in Smaug being killed by the [Black Arrow](../B/Black_Arrow~68980.md) shot by [Bard](../B/Bard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Although [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) was killed in the subsequent [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md), [Dáin](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) of the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) (grandson of [Grór](../G/Grór.md)) became [King of Durin's Folk](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) and [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
