**Wilibald Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Wilibald was the son of [Theobald Bolger](/wiki/Theobald_Bolger) and [Nina Lightfoot](/wiki/Nina_Lightfoot). He married [Prisca Baggins](/wiki/Prisca_Baggins), and had three children, [Wilimar](/wiki/Wilimar_Bolger "Wilimar Bolger"), [Heribald](/wiki/Heribald_Bolger "Heribald Bolger") and [Nora](/wiki/Nora_Bolger "Nora Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The name <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willibald" title="wikipedia:Willibald">Wilibald</a></i> contains *wilja* ("wish", "desire") and *bald* ("bold").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
