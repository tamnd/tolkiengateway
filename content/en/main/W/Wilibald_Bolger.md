**Wilibald Bolger** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Wilibald was the son of [Theobald Bolger](../T/Theobald_Bolger.md) and [Nina Lightfoot](../N/Nina_Lightfoot.md). He married [Prisca Baggins](../P/Prisca_Baggins.md), and had three children, [Wilimar](Wilimar_Bolger.md), [Heribald](../H/Heribald_Bolger.md) and [Nora](../N/Nora_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The name <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willibald" title="wikipedia:Willibald">Wilibald</a></i> contains *wilja* ("wish", "desire") and *bald* ("bold").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
