The **Woods of Núath** was a wooded area extending westward from the upper waters of the river [Narog](/wiki/Narog).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Woods of Núath was a wooded area in the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> It was bordered in the west by the hills lying north-west in [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) south of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), and in the east by [Eithel Ivrin](/wiki/Eithel_Ivrin), the source of [Narog](/wiki/Narog). The river [Ginglith](/wiki/Ginglith) began in it.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

When [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) led [Túor](/wiki/T%C3%BAor) eastward to go to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) they passed through the Woods of Núath. The journey was through bitter winds and took weeks to accomplish.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
