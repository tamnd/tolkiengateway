**Wild Berries** grew in the upper [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md). In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> as [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [the dwarves](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md), and [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) sought to escape from the [goblins](../O/Orcs.md) in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), famished Bilbo looked for food as they marched. Although the blackberries were still in flower and he could not find any hawthorn-berries, Bilbo did manage to find three wild strawberries.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
