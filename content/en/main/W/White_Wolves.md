The **white Wolves** were a type of wolf native to the frozen lands in the north of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). They were not normally seen in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), but seem to have remained among the snows of the [Forodwaith](../N/Northern_Waste.md) and perhaps beyond.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2911](../T/Third_Age_2911.md), one of the harshest winters in Middle-earth's history descended, the so-called [Fell Winter](<../F/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The ice and snow of the north came at least as far south as [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). With them came the white Wolves, invading Eriador and roaming as far south as the Shire. The frozen [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) River meant that they were even able to cross into the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md). This was the last time the [Horn-call of Buckland](../H/Horn-call_of_Buckland.md) was used before the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md), one hundred and seven years later.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
