The **white Wolves** were a type of wolf native to the frozen lands in the north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). They were not normally seen in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), but seem to have remained among the snows of the [Forodwaith](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste") and perhaps beyond.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2911](/wiki/Third_Age_2911 "Third Age 2911"), one of the harshest winters in Middle-earth's history descended, the so-called [Fell Winter](</wiki/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age)> "Fell Winter (Third Age)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The ice and snow of the north came at least as far south as [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). With them came the white Wolves, invading Eriador and roaming as far south as the Shire. The frozen [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) River meant that they were even able to cross into the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing). This was the last time the [Horn-call of Buckland](/wiki/Horn-call_of_Buckland) was used before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), one hundred and seven years later.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
