**Weather Hills** was the name among [Men](../M/Men.md) for the north-south range of hills that lay in central [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), specifically between [Bree-land](../B/Bree-land.md) and the [Hoarwell](../H/Hoarwell.md). The most significant peak was [Weathertop](Weathertop.md)/Amon Sûl, lay at the southern end of the range adjacent to the [Great East Road](../G/Great_East_Road.md).

## History {#History}

In the early [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) the Weather Hills were occupied by [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md), as well as the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) and [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the year [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md) [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md) first returned to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Upon hearing that some of their distant kin had come, these [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md) petitioned [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) to meet the mariners. Twelve men came and rejoiced to meet the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It marked part of the border between the lands of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md). When [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) was formed and Rhudaur became hostile to Arthedain, [Argeleb I](../A/Argeleb_I.md) fortified the range but after the enemies laid siege to the Hills, Argeleb fell ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1356](../T/Third_Age_1356.md)).

With the help of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) and [Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md), [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md) drove out the enemies from the Hills and Arnor kept a guarded border along the Weather Hills, the East Road and the lower Hoarwell.

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md), Angmar and Rhudaur crossed the Hoarwell, occupied Cardolan, and closed in on Weathertop.

Marks of this fortification still existed in the path [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) led the hobbits on to reach Weathertop.

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Weather Hills, called <i>Emyn Sûl</i> (&#34;Hills of the Wind&#34;) in Sindarin, run some ninety miles north-west to south-east. Local inhabitants work a few small mines or keep grazing sheep.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Weather Hills form the boundary of <a href="../B/Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a> with the <a href="../L/Lone-lands.md" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>. A small river originated in the dell of Bleakrift a short distance north-west of <a href="Weathertop.md" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a> and flowed south-west into the <a href="../M/Midgewater_Marshes.md" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a>. The Midgewater Pass crossed this river, allowing passage into the hills north of the <a href="../E/East_Road.md" title="East Road">East Road</a>. During the <a href="War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the hills were infested with <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">orcs</a>, goblins, <a href="../H/Half-orcs.md" title="Half-orcs">half-orcs</a> and <a href="Wargs.md" title="Wargs">wargs</a> both from <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> and from <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>. The <a href="../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md" title="Rangers of the North">Ranger</a> Candaith watched over the hills before travelling south as part of the <a href="../G/Grey_Company.md" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a>.</dd></dl>
