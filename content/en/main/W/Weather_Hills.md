**Weather Hills** was the name among [Men](/wiki/Men) for the north-south range of hills that lay in central [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), specifically between [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land) and the [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell). The most significant peak was [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop)/Amon Sûl, lay at the southern end of the range adjacent to the [Great East Road](/wiki/Great_East_Road).

## History {#History}

In the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the Weather Hills were occupied by [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men), as well as the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) and [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the year [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600") [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur) first returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Upon hearing that some of their distant kin had come, these [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men) petitioned [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) to meet the mariners. Twelve men came and rejoiced to meet the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It marked part of the border between the lands of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur). When [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) was formed and Rhudaur became hostile to Arthedain, [Argeleb I](/wiki/Argeleb_I) fortified the range but after the enemies laid siege to the Hills, Argeleb fell ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1356](/wiki/Third_Age_1356 "Third Age 1356")).

With the help of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon "Elves of Lindon"), [Arveleg I](/wiki/Arveleg_I) drove out the enemies from the Hills and Arnor kept a guarded border along the Weather Hills, the East Road and the lower Hoarwell.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409"), Angmar and Rhudaur crossed the Hoarwell, occupied Cardolan, and closed in on Weathertop.

Marks of this fortification still existed in the path [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) led the hobbits on to reach Weathertop.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Weather Hills, called <i>Emyn Sûl</i> (&#34;Hills of the Wind&#34;) in Sindarin, run some ninety miles north-west to south-east. Local inhabitants work a few small mines or keep grazing sheep.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Weather Hills form the boundary of <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a> with the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>. A small river originated in the dell of Bleakrift a short distance north-west of <a href="/wiki/Weathertop" title="Weathertop">Weathertop</a> and flowed south-west into the <a href="/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a>. The Midgewater Pass crossed this river, allowing passage into the hills north of the <a href="/wiki/East_Road" title="East Road">East Road</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the hills were infested with <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">orcs</a>, goblins, <a href="/wiki/Half-orcs" title="Half-orcs">half-orcs</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wargs" title="Wargs">wargs</a> both from <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> and from <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North" title="Rangers of the North">Ranger</a> Candaith watched over the hills before travelling south as part of the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Company" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a>.</dd></dl>
