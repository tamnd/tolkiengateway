The "**wolf-language**"<sup id="cite_ref-wolflanguage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wolflanguage-1">[1]</a></sup> was a language that was used by certain [dogs](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs"), [wargs](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs"), and [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") in [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

## Description {#Description}

While not much is known about this language, it is described as queer, sounding "like animal noises turned into talk".<sup id="cite_ref-queer_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-queer-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [wargs](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs") over the [Edge of the Wild](/wiki/Edge_of_the_Wild) used a dreadful dialect of this tongue which [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) understood, though it "sounded terrible" to [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-fire_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fire-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor), when [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) were found by the Wargs at their <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Meeting-place&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Meeting-place (page does not exist)">meeting-place</a> in the pine-woods above the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin), their chief posted wolf-guards beneath the trees before he spoke to the other wolves in a circle "about cruel and wicked things" in the wolf-language. Occasionally, the other wolves would speak in unison in answer to their chief. Gandalf overheard them discussing how the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were late for their planned raid on the [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen) that night. After Gandalf and the Company threw burning pine-cones at the wolves, the wolf-guards began "cursing the dwarves in" this language.<sup id="cite_ref-fire_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fire-3">[3]</a></sup>

Later, when Gandalf and Thorin and Company arrived at [Beorn's Hall](/wiki/Beorn%27s_Hall), [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) spoke to his long-bodied grey dogs in this language, asking them to retrieve torches for illuminating the hall.<sup id="cite_ref-queer_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-queer-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the early hours of [14 January](/wiki/14_January) in the year [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-chronology_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-chronology-4">[4]</a></sup> the wolf-language was used by the "[Hound of Sauron](/wiki/Hound_of_Sauron)" to signal his pack to attack the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) after surrounding the the ring of boulder-stones where the Fellowship chose to shelter at.<sup id="cite_ref-history_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-5">[5]</a></sup>
