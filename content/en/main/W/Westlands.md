**Westlands** was a large part of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), perhaps referring to the entire north-western region of the wide continent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Occasionally it may also be used more specifically as a name of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The Westlands were the most well known and documented part of Middle-earth, the north-western part of the continent facing [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer). It consisted of huge regions of land, such as [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)/[Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). The most extreme regions of the Westlands (that perhaps did not belong to them) were alien lands such as [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn), [Harad](/wiki/Harad), and [Khand](/wiki/Khand). Beyond them, unknown lands lay to the [East](/wiki/East) and <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/South_(disambiguation)" title="South (disambiguation)">South</a>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Of the Westlands, the western portion called Beleriand was drowned at the end of the First Age, and survivors relocated to Lindon and Eriador. Eriador, now the westernmost part of Middle-earth, was bordered by the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to the East which stretched down south to the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Across the Misty Mountains from Eriador was another region of the Westlands, Rhovanion. It extended east to the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn). Within Rhovanion, there were the great forest of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest), and [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). To the south was the region of Mordor, encircled on three sides by mountains. To the far north of Rhovanion and Eriador alike was the icy [Forodwaith](</wiki/Forodwaith_(lands)> "Forodwaith (lands)").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

The southern part of the Westlands was around the Bay of Belfalas, including the area of Gondor.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## History {#History}

In the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), most of the Westlands were covered by an immense forest.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Westlands were populated by almost all known races of Middle-earth: [Elves](/wiki/Elves) ([Eldar](/wiki/Eldar)) and [Men](/wiki/Men) who stayed behind on their way to the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>, and who later escaped the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Ents](/wiki/Ents) also made their realms all over the Westlands.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

During the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), many [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) came and established their settlements. It was then when the huge forests begen to shrink as trees were used to build their ships.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) and the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) were the remnants of those days.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

The Númenórean Realms in Exile, [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), dominated the Westlands during the Second and [Third Ages](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age"). Eventually, most of the Mannish peoples of the Westlands were brought together under the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom). Their first King, [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), was titled "Lord of the Westlands" in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-KL_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-KL-7">[7]</a></sup>
