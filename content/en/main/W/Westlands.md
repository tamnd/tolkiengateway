**Westlands** was a large part of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), perhaps referring to the entire north-western region of the wide continent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Occasionally it may also be used more specifically as a name of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The Westlands were the most well known and documented part of Middle-earth, the north-western part of the continent facing [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md). It consisted of huge regions of land, such as [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)/[Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md), [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), and [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). The most extreme regions of the Westlands (that perhaps did not belong to them) were alien lands such as [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md), [Harad](../H/Harad.md), and [Khand](../K/Khand.md). Beyond them, unknown lands lay to the [East](../E/East.md) and <a class="mw-disambig" href="../S/South_(disambiguation).md" title="South (disambiguation)">South</a>.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Of the Westlands, the western portion called Beleriand was drowned at the end of the First Age, and survivors relocated to Lindon and Eriador. Eriador, now the westernmost part of Middle-earth, was bordered by the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) to the East which stretched down south to the [White Mountains](White_Mountains.md) and the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Across the Misty Mountains from Eriador was another region of the Westlands, Rhovanion. It extended east to the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md). Within Rhovanion, there were the great forest of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md), and [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). To the south was the region of Mordor, encircled on three sides by mountains. To the far north of Rhovanion and Eriador alike was the icy [Forodwaith](<../F/Forodwaith_(lands).md>).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

The southern part of the Westlands was around the Bay of Belfalas, including the area of Gondor.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## History {#History}

In the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md), most of the Westlands were covered by an immense forest.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Westlands were populated by almost all known races of Middle-earth: [Elves](../E/Elves.md) ([Eldar](../E/Eldar.md)) and [Men](../M/Men.md) who stayed behind on their way to the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="West.md" title="West">West</a>, and who later escaped the destruction of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Ents](../E/Ents.md) also made their realms all over the Westlands.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

During the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), many [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) came and established their settlements. It was then when the huge forests begen to shrink as trees were used to build their ships.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md) and the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) were the remnants of those days.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

The Númenórean Realms in Exile, [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), dominated the Westlands during the Second and [Third Ages](../T/Third_Age.md). Eventually, most of the Mannish peoples of the Westlands were brought together under the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md). Their first King, [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md), was titled "Lord of the Westlands" in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-KL_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-KL-7">[7]</a></sup>
