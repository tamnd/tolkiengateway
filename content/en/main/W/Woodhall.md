**Woodhall** was a village in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") within the woods of [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") met [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor") and his people on the road near the Woody End above Woodhall,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the three passed close by the village on the last stages of their journey to [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) the name means "hall in a wood", representing a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *Wuduheall*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The latter element (see: <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/haugh" title="wiktionary:haugh">haugh</a></i>) means "recess, a piece of land half-enclosed by slopes or wood" is also seen in "[Deephallow](/wiki/Deephallow)".
