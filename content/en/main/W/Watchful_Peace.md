The **Watchful Peace** was a relatively peaceful 4-century period during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2063](/wiki/Third_Age_2063 "Third Age 2063"), when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), then known as "the Necromancer", fled from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) to hide himself from the [Wise](/wiki/Wise) until his return in [2460](/wiki/Third_Age_2460 "Third Age 2460").

## Background {#Background}

When [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) reestablished relations with [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), Kings [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) and [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) realised that a single force was co-ordinating the attacks on both of their kingdoms and that they should work together to combat the evil. However it was already too late; the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) managed to overthrow [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), the last remnant of the Northern Kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor). Angmar was in turn destroyed by [the forces of Gondor](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") and the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) gathered back in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to prepare for Sauron's return;<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> they subsequently [captured](/wiki/Second_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "Second Fall of Minas Ithil") [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2002](/wiki/Third_Age_2002 "Third Age 2002"), and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) suffered the end of the line of the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) with the loss of [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2050](/wiki/Third_Age_2050 "Third Age 2050"). The [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) also suffered a major loss with the awakening of a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrog) and the subsequent evacuation of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), which caused many [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) to seek to leave [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup> By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2060](/wiki/Third_Age_2060 "Third Age 2060") the shadow of Dol Guldur grew so much that [the Wise](/wiki/The_Wise "The Wise") realised that Sauron was returning there.<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

But Sauron's plans were to change; in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2063](/wiki/Third_Age_2063 "Third Age 2063") [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) went there to investigate; the "Necromancer" fled to the [East](/wiki/East).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>

During his absence, the Nazgûl remained quiet in [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup> The Shadow on [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) had lessened, but the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) had used this period to prepare for Sauron's return, who, in the meantime, was corrupting the [Men](/wiki/Men) of the East.

## History {#History}

### Gondor {#Gondor}

The first ten [Ruling Stewards](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") had relative calm in which to renew their strength. These included [Mardil Voronwë](/wiki/Mardil "Mardil"), [Eradan](/wiki/Eradan), [Herion](/wiki/Herion), [Belegorn](/wiki/Belegorn), [Húrin I](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_I), [Túrin I](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_I), [Hador](</wiki/Hador_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Hador (Steward of Gondor)"), [Barahir](</wiki/Barahir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Barahir (Steward of Gondor)"), [Dior](</wiki/Dior_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Dior (Steward of Gondor)"), and [Denethor I](/wiki/Denethor_I).

During the Peace Gondor strengthened its borders, keeping a watchful eye on the east, as [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul) was still a threat on their flank and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) was still occupied with [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). But no major escalations occurred except for minor skirmishes with the [Haven of Umbar](/wiki/Umbar "Umbar").

During the Peace the garrisons of the forts of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) (which was depopulated during the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague)) were not renewed, and local chieftains (whose subjects were increasingly of mixed blood) gained more control.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Eriador {#Eriador}

After the fall of Angmar the [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls) of NE Eriador had fled to the east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and didn't trouble the area for some centuries.<sup id="cite_ref-NM_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-5">[5]</a></sup>

The first seven [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) ruled their people, the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North), during the Watchful Peace: [Aranarth](/wiki/Aranarth), [Arahael](/wiki/Arahael), [Aranuir](/wiki/Aranuir), [Aravir](/wiki/Aravir), [Aragorn I](/wiki/Aragorn_I), [Araglas](/wiki/Araglas), and [Arahad I](/wiki/Arahad_I). Even before the Peace ended, evil things such as [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) secretly invaded or attacked Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-6">[6]</a></sup>

The [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") prospered and even expanded and appointed a [Thain](/wiki/Thain) to take the role of the King, the first being [Bucca](/wiki/Bucca),<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-6">[6]</a></sup> and [Isumbras I](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_I "Isumbras Took I") became the first [Tookish](/wiki/Took "Took") Thain later; [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) was first occupied by [Oldbucks](/wiki/Oldbucks) at this time.

### Longbeards {#Longbeards}

At the beginning of the Watchful Peace, the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) dwelt in both the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) and in [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) made his abode at Erebor, but the majority of his people inhabited the Grey Mountains and his son [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) moved there as well.

He was followed as [King of Durin's Folk](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") by [Glóin](</wiki/Gl%C3%B3in_(King_of_Durin%27s_Folk)> "Glóin (King of Durin's Folk)") and [Óin](</wiki/%C3%93in_(King_of_Durin%27s_Folk)> "Óin (King of Durin's Folk)").

### North {#North}

The [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) dwelt in the north vales of [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) during the Watchful Peace.

Further south, some [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) inhabited the areas near the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields), having settled there some centuries ago to flee the shadow of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), and formed a matriarchal society.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-shadow_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-7">[7]</a></sup> [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol) was born and spent his youth there toward the end of the Watchful Peace.<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-shadow_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-7">[7]</a></sup>

## End of the Peace {#End_of_the_Peace}

A new race of Orcs was bred in Mordor, the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> But Sauron was not idle in the east either: he created a strong alliance between the various tribes of [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings), so that when he returned he was more powerful and had many [Men](/wiki/Men) in his service.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2460](/wiki/Third_Age_2460 "Third Age 2460"), Sauron returned with new strength. His return coincided with [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring")'s discovery and Sméagol claiming it three years later. In c. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2480](/wiki/Third_Age_2480 "Third Age 2480") Orcs began to spread again in the Misty Mountains in order to block all passes into Eriador, whilst Sauron's creatures took Moria as their own. In the next years [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) and [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) were devastated by the Uruk-hai.<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup>
