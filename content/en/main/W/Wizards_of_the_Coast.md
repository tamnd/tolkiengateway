**Wizards of the Coast LLC** is a gaming company, founded in [1990](../_/1990.md). While they initially produced role-playing games, they rose to fame when they published [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) in [1993](../_/1993.md). Magic was a collectible trading card game (the first of its kind) with a fantasy setting containing many aesthetic elements inspired by Lord of the Rings among other fiction. Since [1997](../_/1997.md) they also publish [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) and for several periods they also published its official periodical [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md).

In 1999 the company was acquired by Hasbro.

On June 23, 2023, Wizards released [The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md) a new Magic: The Gathering set with 451 cards that were new renditions of various characters and events from the Lord of the Rings trilogy.

In August 2026, Wizards will release a second Tolkien-related Magic: The Gathering Universes Beyond product, [Magic: The Gathering \| The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md).
