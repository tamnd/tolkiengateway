**Wizards of the Coast LLC** is a gaming company, founded in [1990](/wiki/1990). While they initially produced role-playing games, they rose to fame when they published [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) in [1993](/wiki/1993). Magic was a collectible trading card game (the first of its kind) with a fantasy setting containing many aesthetic elements inspired by Lord of the Rings among other fiction. Since [1997](/wiki/1997) they also publish [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons) and for several periods they also published its official periodical [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine).

In 1999 the company was acquired by Hasbro.

On June 23, 2023, Wizards released [The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth) a new Magic: The Gathering set with 451 cards that were new renditions of various characters and events from the Lord of the Rings trilogy.

In August 2026, Wizards will release a second Tolkien-related Magic: The Gathering Universes Beyond product, [Magic: The Gathering \| The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit").
