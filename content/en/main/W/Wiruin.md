**Wiruin** was a great whirlpool located in a bay near where the [Helkaraksë](/wiki/Helkaraks%C3%AB) jutted out from the [western mainland](/wiki/Araman "Araman").<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup> It only appears within *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was a fearsome eddy which shrieked with a loud wailing noise that was terrible to hear; anything that approached was sucked down into its depths and crushed upon ice and rock.<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) on their [Exile](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") reached the Helkaraksë, some elected to attempt to sail across the [sea](/wiki/Great_Sea "Great Sea") rather than cross the frozen isthmus. In this, a great ship was lost with all aboard by being sucked into Wiruin. The Noldoli were therefore forced to cross by land.<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup>
