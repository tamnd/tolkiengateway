**Wiruin** was a great whirlpool located in a bay near where the [Helkaraksë](../H/Helkaraksë.md) jutted out from the [western mainland](../A/Araman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup> It only appears within *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It was a fearsome eddy which shrieked with a loud wailing noise that was terrible to hear; anything that approached was sucked down into its depths and crushed upon ice and rock.<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup>

When the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) on their [Exile](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) reached the Helkaraksë, some elected to attempt to sail across the [sea](../G/Great_Sea.md) rather than cross the frozen isthmus. In this, a great ship was lost with all aboard by being sucked into Wiruin. The Noldoli were therefore forced to cross by land.<sup id="cite_ref-flight_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flight-1">[1]</a></sup>
