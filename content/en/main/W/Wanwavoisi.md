**Wanwavoisi** is a name for both **[Varda](/wiki/Varda)** and **[Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB)** together, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name, but its etymology is unclear, although it probably contains the element *wanwa* ("great gale").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
