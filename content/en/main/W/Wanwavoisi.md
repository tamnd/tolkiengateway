**Wanwavoisi** is a name for both **[Varda](../V/Varda.md)** and **[Manwë](../M/Manwë.md)** together, according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) name, but its etymology is unclear, although it probably contains the element *wanwa* ("great gale").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
