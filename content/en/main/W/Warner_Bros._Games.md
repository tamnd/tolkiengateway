**Warner Bros. Games** (formerly known as **Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment**, also known as **WB Games**) is a world-wide distributor and developer of video games and currently, it is owned by Warner Bros. Discovery. In [2009](/wiki/2009) it entered into a partnership with the [Saul Zaentz Company](/wiki/Middle-earth_Enterprises "Middle-earth Enterprises") and [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) (itself now a subsidiary of [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.)) to make video games based on both the *The Lord of the Rings* [books](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings") and [films](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* films](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") and [book](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit"). As a result, it is the current company that has the video game rights for both *The Lord of the Rings* and *The Hobbit* and their adaptations.

## *The Lord of the Rings* video games {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings_video_games}

After the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Entertainment_Expo" title="wikipedia:Electronic Entertainment Expo">Electronic Entertainment Expo</a> [2009](/wiki/2009), Martin Tremblay, President of Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment, said:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The Lord of the Rings franchise is world renowned for its captivating adventures, and we are creating new experiences in the game space designed to appeal to a wide array of consumer tastes from core to casual. The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest is the first of many different types of games we will be developing with the film and literary rights. It is also the first game allowing kids and their families to interact in their favorite locations and stories from The Lord of the Rings with accessible gameplay.<sup id="cite_ref-IGN_Announce_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IGN_Announce-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
