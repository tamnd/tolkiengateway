The **White Hand** was an emblem of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) during the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md). After [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) had been captured, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) came upon the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) slain by [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md). Four of the goblin-soldiers bore shields with a small white hand centered in a field of black - these were warriors of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the road to Isengard there was a tall black pillar upon which was a great stone carved in the likeness of a long white hand with a finger pointing north, the nails of which were painted red.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
