The **White Hand** was an emblem of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). After [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") had been captured, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) came upon the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) slain by [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir). Four of the goblin-soldiers bore shields with a small white hand centered in a field of black - these were warriors of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the road to Isengard there was a tall black pillar upon which was a great stone carved in the likeness of a long white hand with a finger pointing north, the nails of which were painted red.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
