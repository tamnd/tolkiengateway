The title **Wise-woman** was applied to women of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) who had great knowledge of the history and lore of their people. Some married, while others remained unwed, they were greatly esteemed among Men. All of them passed their knowledge on to a chosen successor, as was usual among the [Wise](Wise.md) of the Edain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Two Wise-women are known by name: [Adanel](../A/Adanel.md) and [Andreth](../A/Andreth.md). <span></span>
