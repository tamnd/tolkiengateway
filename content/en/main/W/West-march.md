The **West-march** or the **West Marches** was a part of the realm of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). It was a triangular peninsula laying between the rivers [Isen](../I/Isen.md) and [Adorn](../A/Adorn.md), and represented the furthest western boundary of Rohan. It was declared to be part of Rohan at the [Oath of Eorl](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md), but by the time of the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md) its people's loyalty to Edoras was dubious, given their mixed Dunlending and Rohirric heritage.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2754](../T/Third_Age_2754.md) the region was ruled by [Freca](../F/Freca.md). He had constructed a stronghold near the source of the Adorn and paid little heed to the king of Rohan at the time, [Helm Hammerhand](../H/Helm_Hammerhand.md). After a disagreement over the proposal that Freca's son [Wulf](Wulf.md) should marry Helm's daughter, Helm killed Freca with a single blow. This precipitated a war between the West-march (supported by the Dunlendings) and the rest of Rohan. Though Wulf was able to take [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) for a time, he was eventually slain by Helm's nephew, [Fréaláf](../F/Fréaláf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

The region was only fully reconquered for Rohan by King [Folcwine](../F/Folcwine.md) nearly a hundred years later.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup> However, the people of the region had not forgotten the slaying of their lord by the time of the War of the Ring, and many sided with [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_(territory)" title="wikipedia:March (territory)">March</a></i> here is used in the sense of "borderland, especially one serving as a defence of the inner lands of a realm", similar to the [Riddermark](../R/Riddermark.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
