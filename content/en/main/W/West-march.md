The **West-march** or the **West Marches** was a part of the realm of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). It was a triangular peninsula laying between the rivers [Isen](/wiki/Isen) and [Adorn](/wiki/Adorn), and represented the furthest western boundary of Rohan. It was declared to be part of Rohan at the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl), but by the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) its people's loyalty to Edoras was dubious, given their mixed Dunlending and Rohirric heritage.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2754](/wiki/Third_Age_2754 "Third Age 2754") the region was ruled by [Freca](/wiki/Freca). He had constructed a stronghold near the source of the Adorn and paid little heed to the king of Rohan at the time, [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm_Hammerhand). After a disagreement over the proposal that Freca's son [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) should marry Helm's daughter, Helm killed Freca with a single blow. This precipitated a war between the West-march (supported by the Dunlendings) and the rest of Rohan. Though Wulf was able to take [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) for a time, he was eventually slain by Helm's nephew, [Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

The region was only fully reconquered for Rohan by King [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine) nearly a hundred years later.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup> However, the people of the region had not forgotten the slaying of their lord by the time of the War of the Ring, and many sided with [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March_(territory)" title="wikipedia:March (territory)">March</a></i> here is used in the sense of "borderland, especially one serving as a defence of the inner lands of a realm", similar to the [Riddermark](/wiki/Riddermark).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
