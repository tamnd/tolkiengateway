**Wisconsin Ring Game** is a semiannual live action role-playing game hosted twice a year at <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_Dodge_State_Park" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Governor Dodge State Park</a> in southern Wisconsin. First played on [11 June](../_/11_June.md) [1977](../_/1977.md), the game was revived in the fall of 1987 and re-enacts the events of the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md). The game has continued to this day after a short break due to the <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">global pandemic</a>.

## Game Play {#Game_Play}

Players are divided into three teams taking on the roles of characters in the story with the following win objectives:

- The forces of Good led by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) need to take the [Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) to [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md) in the center of the park to destroy it.
- The forces of Evil led by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) need to capture the Ring and return it to Sauron.
- The forces of the White Hand led by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) need to capture the Ring and claim it at Mount Doom.

In addition teams can also attempt to take citadels such as [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) and [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) for points with the forces of evil winning by default if all good citadels are captured before the Ring is destroyed. Evil also wins if Saruman claims the Ring for himself at Mount Doom. Certain citadels and objectives are time gated such as Gondor and Mount Doom to help control the pacing of the game.

## Origin {#Origin}

Wisconsin Ring Game was conceived by Jeff McKinnon and members of the <a class="external text" href="https://lafollette.madison.k12.wi.us/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Robert M. La Follette High School</a> Chess &amp; Strategy Club when they decided test out the roleplaying guide <a class="external text" href="https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2147484002/Rules-for-the-Live-Ring-Game" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Rules for the Live Ring Game</i></a> by [Wendell Lariston Hill Jr.](Wendell_Lariston_Hill,_Jr..md) published in 1973.
