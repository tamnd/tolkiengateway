The **Watch** was a body of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) responsible for maintaining the peace in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Its members wore caps with feathers to distinguish themselves.

It consisted of the [Shirriffs](../S/Shirriffs.md), a police force of twelve hobbits (three in each [Farthing](../F/Farthings.md)) responsible for 'Internal Work', and the [Bounders](../B/Bounders.md), a larger body that guarded the borders of the Shire. The head of the Watch was the [First Shirriff](../F/First_Shirriff.md), a post attached to that of the [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s occupation, the body was greatly increased by him and the [Chief Shirriff](../C/Chief_Shirriff.md). This expanded force was organized into companies, one of which was the [First Eastfarthing Troop](../F/First_Eastfarthing_Troop.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
