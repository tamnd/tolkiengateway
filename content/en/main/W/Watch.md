The **Watch** was a body of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) responsible for maintaining the peace in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Its members wore caps with feathers to distinguish themselves.

It consisted of the [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs), a police force of twelve hobbits (three in each [Farthing](/wiki/Farthings "Farthings")) responsible for 'Internal Work', and the [Bounders](/wiki/Bounders), a larger body that guarded the borders of the Shire. The head of the Watch was the [First Shirriff](/wiki/First_Shirriff), a post attached to that of the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s occupation, the body was greatly increased by him and the [Chief Shirriff](/wiki/Chief_Shirriff). This expanded force was organized into companies, one of which was the [First Eastfarthing Troop](/wiki/First_Eastfarthing_Troop).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
