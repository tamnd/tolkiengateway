**Wídfara** was a man of Rohan during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

His home was in [the Wold](/wiki/The_Wold "The Wold"), the wild grasslands of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)'s northern border, and in these northern lands he had acquired a special weather-sense. He followed [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and it was he who first reported a change in the wind during the long ride, a change that augured the end of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s looming darkness.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He fought in the ensuing [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Wídfara* is [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) for "far, wide traveller".

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Wídfara comes from the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Wold" title="Wold">Wold</a>. When a remote region of Wildermore stops sending messengers, <a href="/wiki/Harding_(rider_of_Rohan)" title="Harding (rider of Rohan)">Harding</a> sends a group of riders to check on them, including Wídfara. The group is ambushed and the few survivors are scattered. Wídfara&#39;s horse Windfoot is killed and he himself sustains a wound, after which he slowly makes it to the village of Scylfig on foot. There he is found by the party of the player, <a href="/wiki/Gl%C3%A9owine" title="Gléowine">Gléowine</a> and <a href="/wiki/Horn_(rider_of_Rohan)" title="Horn (rider of Rohan)">Horn</a>.</dd>
<dd>Wídfara later recovers from his wounds and returns to the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Wold" title="Wold">Wold</a> just in time to answer the muster of the Rohirrim. He rides with Harding&#39;s men to the summons and is met again by the player in the <a href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest" title="Drúadan Forest">Drúadan Forest</a>. He survives the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>.</dd></dl>
