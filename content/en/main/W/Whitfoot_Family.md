**Whitfoot** was the [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") surname of [Will Whitfoot](/wiki/Will_Whitfoot) and, presumably, any of his kin.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Although they didn't seem to be of the upper class, they had some prominence as one of them was [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name "Whitfoot" is derived from "White foot".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
