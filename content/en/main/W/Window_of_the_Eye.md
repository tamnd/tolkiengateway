The **Window of the [Eye](/wiki/Eye_of_Sauron "Eye of Sauron")** was a window in the topmost tower of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It faced westwards in the direction of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is possible that it was one of the windows from which [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) gazed on the northwestern lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).
