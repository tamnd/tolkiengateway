**Windfola** was a grey horse of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).

Windfola was the horse that carried [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) (in the guise of [Dernhelm](/wiki/Dernhelm)) the long miles from [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) to the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields), secretly accompanied by [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Windfola was in the charge of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), but at the onset of the [Lord of the Nazgûl](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king"), the horse went mad with fear, threw his riders, and fled in panic. Left behind by their steed, Éowyn and Merry brought down the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), but by then Windfola had left them far behind on the battlefield.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Windfola means "wind-foal" in the [language](/wiki/Rohan_language "Rohan language") of the Rohirrim.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
