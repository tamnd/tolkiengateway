**Wilcome Cotton** refers to two members of the [Cotton Family](/wiki/Cotton_Family):

- [Wilcome ("Will") Cotton](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_I "Wilcome Cotton I") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1346](/wiki/Third_Age_2946 "Third Age 2946")), son of [Holman ("Long Holm") Cotton](/wiki/Holman_Cotton "Holman Cotton") and uncle of [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).
- [Wilcome ("Jolly") Cotton](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II "Wilcome Cotton II") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1384](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984")), son of [Tolman ("Tom") Cotton Senior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior") and brother of [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).
