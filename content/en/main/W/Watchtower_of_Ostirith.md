The **Watchtower of Ostirith**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a tower located within the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and watched over villages such as [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad). The Watchtower of Ostirith was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

During the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), the Watchtower of Ostirith was originally built by the [Men](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") who followed [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), of which, it is said that the tower still possesses a power fashioned for those tainted by the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow). The watchtower also housed the stone-engraven likeness of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), Morgoth's lieutenant. This sculpture concealed an elaborate device that could unleash the fires of [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom").

Sometime after the [Wars of Beleriand](/wiki/Wars_of_Beleriand), a garrison of [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan) [Elves](/wiki/Elves) under the command of the [Watchwarden Revion](/wiki/Revion "Revion") used the tower for their seventy nine year vigilance over the many villages of the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor"). This garrison included [Elves](/wiki/Elves) such as [Arondir](/wiki/Arondir) and [Médhor](/wiki/M%C3%A9dhor).

Late into the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the elves stationed at Ostirith were captured by a group of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) operating within the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). At the same time, [Bronwyn](/wiki/Bronwyn) led the villagers of [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad) to seek shelter at the tower. Many other refugees fled there as well, such as the villagers of [Iorbad](/wiki/Iorbad).

Eventually, Arondir is let out of the Orc camp by leave of the Orcs' leader, [Adar](/wiki/Adar), to tell the [Men](/wiki/Men) at the Watchtower to either swear fealty to him or to leave the Southlands.

When Arondir delivered the message, some of the villagers, including [Rowan](</wiki/Rowan_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Rowan (The Rings of Power)") and [Waldreg](/wiki/Waldreg), leave the old Watchtower to bend the knee to Adar. The remaining villagers under Bronwyn make up a battle plan for when the Orcs inevitably come for them.

Hours later, Adar led his army directly into a trap in which Arondir caused the collapse of the Watchtower. The collapse annihilated much of the army, including <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bazur&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bazur (page does not exist)">Bazur</a>, but did not succeed in killing Adar.

Later, at the behest of Adar, Waldreg returns to the ruins of the Watchtower of Ostirith and uses the [Orc Sigil Hilt](/wiki/Orc_Sigil_Hilt) as a key to activate a mechanism which opened the dam holding back lake waters. These waters coursed their way to a cavern within [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom"), awakening it from its dormancy. The ensuing eruption left Tirharad and what remained of the Watchtower of Ostirith in ruin.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ostirith* is a combination of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) words *[ost](/wiki/Ost "Ost")* ("enclosure; fort, citadel; fortified town; fortress or stronghold")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and *[tirith](/wiki/Tirith "Tirith")* ("watch, ward, guard; watching, guarding")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.
