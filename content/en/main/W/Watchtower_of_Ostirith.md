The **Watchtower of Ostirith**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a tower located within the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and watched over villages such as [Tirharad](../T/Tirharad.md). The Watchtower of Ostirith was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

During the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md), the Watchtower of Ostirith was originally built by the [Men](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) who followed [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), of which, it is said that the tower still possesses a power fashioned for those tainted by the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md). The watchtower also housed the stone-engraven likeness of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), Morgoth's lieutenant. This sculpture concealed an elaborate device that could unleash the fires of [Orodruin](../M/Mount_Doom.md).

Sometime after the [Wars of Beleriand](Wars_of_Beleriand.md), a garrison of [Silvan](../S/Silvan.md) [Elves](../E/Elves.md) under the command of the [Watchwarden Revion](../R/Revion.md) used the tower for their seventy nine year vigilance over the many villages of the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md). This garrison included [Elves](../E/Elves.md) such as [Arondir](../A/Arondir.md) and [Médhor](../M/Médhor.md).

Late into the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the elves stationed at Ostirith were captured by a group of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) operating within the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). At the same time, [Bronwyn](../B/Bronwyn.md) led the villagers of [Tirharad](../T/Tirharad.md) to seek shelter at the tower. Many other refugees fled there as well, such as the villagers of [Iorbad](../I/Iorbad.md).

Eventually, Arondir is let out of the Orc camp by leave of the Orcs' leader, [Adar](../A/Adar.md), to tell the [Men](../M/Men.md) at the Watchtower to either swear fealty to him or to leave the Southlands.

When Arondir delivered the message, some of the villagers, including [Rowan](<../R/Rowan_(The_Rings_of_Power).md>) and [Waldreg](Waldreg.md), leave the old Watchtower to bend the knee to Adar. The remaining villagers under Bronwyn make up a battle plan for when the Orcs inevitably come for them.

Hours later, Adar led his army directly into a trap in which Arondir caused the collapse of the Watchtower. The collapse annihilated much of the army, including <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Bazur&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bazur (page does not exist)">Bazur</a>, but did not succeed in killing Adar.

Later, at the behest of Adar, Waldreg returns to the ruins of the Watchtower of Ostirith and uses the [Orc Sigil Hilt](../O/Orc_Sigil_Hilt.md) as a key to activate a mechanism which opened the dam holding back lake waters. These waters coursed their way to a cavern within [Orodruin](../M/Mount_Doom.md), awakening it from its dormancy. The ensuing eruption left Tirharad and what remained of the Watchtower of Ostirith in ruin.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ostirith* is a combination of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words *[ost](../O/Ost.md)* ("enclosure; fort, citadel; fortified town; fortress or stronghold")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and *[tirith](../T/Tirith.md)* ("watch, ward, guard; watching, guarding")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.
