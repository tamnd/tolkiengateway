***Welcome to My Worlds: The Art of Rob Alexander*** is a book from 2005 containing artworks by [Rob Alexander](/wiki/Rob_Alexander), including his work for *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*. The cover shows [his painting](/wiki/:File:Rob_Alexander_-_The_Witch-king_Unleashed.jpg) of the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king).

## From the publisher

{{Blockquote|Welcome to My Worlds is an intimate, personal collection of book cover illustration, card game art and conceptual design, from the brush of acclaimed artist Rob Alexander.

Rob works in watercolours, but the results can seem like oils or acrylics. He constantly sprinkles touches of the realistic and unusual into traditional themes like Chinese tombstones in a beautiful mountainscape, like a demon hoping for a chance at Heaven, like a monster grieving for the people he has killed.

His work covers a broad and varied spectrum: from high fantasy and landscapes, to action adventure and gothic horror, plus book covers, children's stories and the Magic: the Gathering phenomenon for which he has created more than 130 images. All Rob's pieces are unified by a strong sense of subtlety, of poetry and of beauty. The worlds he creates, reproduced here for the first time in book form, invite and enchant, drawing viewers in, making them want to know more.

His artwork is truly something different. So sit back, curl up, and enjoy.}}
