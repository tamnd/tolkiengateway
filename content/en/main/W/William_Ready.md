**William Bernard Ready** ([16 September](../_/16_September.md) [1914](../_/1914.md) - [12 September](../_/12_September.md) [1981](../_/1981.md)<sup id="cite_ref-McMaster1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-McMaster1-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a University Librarian and Professor of Bibliography at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University" title="wikipedia:McMaster University">McMaster University</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario" title="wikipedia:Ontario">Ontario</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> taking up the position in [1966](../_/1966.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Montreal_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Montreal-3">[3]</a></sup>

Born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff" title="wikipedia:Cardiff">Cardiff</a>, Wales,<sup id="cite_ref-McMaster1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-McMaster1-1">[1]</a></sup> Ready studied bibliography in England and Wales, and after the Second World War he became an acquisitions librarian at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University" title="wikipedia:Stanford University">Stanford University</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Montreal_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Montreal-3">[3]</a></sup> Between [1956](../_/1956.md) to [1963](../_/1963.md) he was Director of Libraries at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ready foresaw the cultural value of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings and, while at [Marquette University](../M/Marquette_University.md), he managed to purchase a large selection of original literary manuscripts by Tolkien in 1956-57, including manuscripts of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CG_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-4">[4]</a></sup>

According to his own account, Ready had arranged a lecture tour for Tolkien at American universities, but Tolkien was forced to decline because of illness. In order to make amends, Tolkien therefore offered Ready to buy his manuscripts.<sup id="cite_ref-Montreal_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Montreal-3">[3]</a></sup> However, [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) and [Wayne G. Hammond](Wayne_G._Hammond.md) have stated (deriving from archived correspondence) that Tolkien "*was directly approached to sell his papers, and did not himself suggest their sale.*"<sup id="cite_ref-CG_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-4">[4]</a></sup>
