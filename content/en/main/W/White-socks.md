**White-socks** was a [pony](../P/Ponies.md) belonging to [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md).

## History {#History}

White-socks was one of five ponies that Merry had ready for the Hobbits' journey at [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md). At the home of [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md), the ponies became friends with [Fatty Lumpkin](../F/Fatty_Lumpkin.md), a pony of Tom's. At the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) they became so frightened that they ran off to find Fatty. Tom called them all back with the names he had given them: [Sharp-ears](../S/Sharp-ears.md), [Bumpkin](../B/Bumpkin.md), [Wise-nose](Wise-nose.md), [Swish-tail](../S/Swish-tail.md), and White-socks. Eventually they all returned with all the bagge, and went back on their journey with the Hobbits.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During their stay at the *[Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md)* the Hobbits' ponies were let out to delay their departure from [Bree](../B/Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They ran out in the Downs to find Fatty. They stayed with Tom for a while, until he had heard news of the events at Bree. He sent the ponies to [Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md), and they stayed there working for Barliman, they were taken well cared for by [Bob](../B/Bob.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
