**Wolf** is the popular name of several animals in the Canidae family. They are related to [dogs](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs") and [hounds](/wiki/Hounds "Hounds").

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Wolf](</wiki/Wolf_(dog)> "Wolf (dog)"), one of [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot)'s dogs.**
- [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith), or Wolf-men, a group of outlaws.
- [Wolf-Sauron](/wiki/Wolf-Sauron), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in Wolf-form.
- [Wolves](/wiki/Wolves), creatures of evil:
    - [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs), or Wild Wolves, creatures of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) and part of the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).
    - [Werewolves](/wiki/Werewolves), of whom the greatest was [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth).
    - [White Wolves](/wiki/White_Wolves), animals from [Forodwaith](</wiki/Forodwaith_(lands)> "Forodwaith (lands)") who attacked [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1311](/wiki/Third_Age_2911 "Third Age 2911").
- [Wolf-language](/wiki/Wolf-language), the language of wolves and dogs
- [Wolf-riders](/wiki/Wolf-riders), "Orcs or Orclike beings"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that rode on wolves.
- [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf), a Man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) of [Dunlendish](/wiki/Dunland "Dunland") ancestry.
