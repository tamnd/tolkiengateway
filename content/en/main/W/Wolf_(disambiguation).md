**Wolf** is the popular name of several animals in the Canidae family. They are related to [dogs](../D/Dogs.md) and [hounds](../H/Hounds.md).

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Wolf](<Wolf_(dog).md>), one of [Farmer Maggot](../F/Farmer_Maggot.md)'s dogs.**
- [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md), or Wolf-men, a group of outlaws.
- [Wolf-Sauron](Wolf-Sauron.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in Wolf-form.
- [Wolves](Wolves.md), creatures of evil:
    - [Wargs](Wargs.md), or Wild Wolves, creatures of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) and part of the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md).
    - [Werewolves](Werewolves.md), of whom the greatest was [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md).
    - [White Wolves](White_Wolves.md), animals from [Forodwaith](<../F/Forodwaith_(lands).md>) who attacked [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1311](../T/Third_Age_2911.md).
- [Wolf-language](Wolf-language.md), the language of wolves and dogs
- [Wolf-riders](Wolf-riders.md), "Orcs or Orclike beings"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that rode on wolves.
- [Wulf](Wulf.md), a Man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) of [Dunlendish](../D/Dunland.md) ancestry.
