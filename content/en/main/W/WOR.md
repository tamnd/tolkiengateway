**WOR** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "express, cause to exude (by pressure)" and is an element in the word *[miruvor](/wiki/Miruvor "Miruvor")*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Note that the root appears to have been rejected by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), who wrote that "this is <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/false_etymology" title="wikipedia:false etymology">false etymology</a> \[...\] there was not in fact any word *wor-* in Elvish, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) or [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)."<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> One can interpret Tolkien's note in several ways:

- The Elves falsely thought that the latter elements of *[miruvórë/miruvor](/wiki/Miruvor "Miruvor")* derived from a presumed root WOR.
- Tolkien altogether removed the root WOR (and its relation to *miruvórë/miruvor*) from the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") .
