The **W.P. Ker lecture** was a yearly lecture at the University of Glasgow. It is now a full lectureship. It was instated in 1938 in honour of William Paton Ker, of whom [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") spoke with reverence in *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)*.

On April 15, [1953](/wiki/1953), Tolkien was given the honour of providing this lecture. It was a discussion of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a></i> and the religious themes temptation, adultery and repentance.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On various websites with a "Tolkien Timeline" (a selection:<a class="external autonumber" href="http://tolkien.cro.net/tolkien/tolktmln.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a><a class="external autonumber" href="http://gollum.usask.ca/tolkien/era3.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[2]</a><a class="external autonumber" href="http://mural.uv.es/jojise/narratime.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[3]</a><a class="external autonumber" href="http://profile.myspace.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=user.viewprofile&amp;friendid=54131761" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[4]</a><a class="external autonumber" href="http://brokenwillsandsavinggrace.blogspot.com/2006/06/timeline-of-jrr-tolkien.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[5]</a>), the date of this lecture is incorrectly given as [1933](/wiki/1933) (or "circa 1933"). It seems to be based on a misreading and ignorant copy-pasting. Only one site cites a source <a class="external autonumber" href="http://gollum.usask.ca/tolkien/era3.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[6]</a>, [William Ready](/wiki/William_Ready)'s *[The Tolkien Relation](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Relation)*, in which it is recorded erronously.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Publication {#Publication}

In [1983](/wiki/1983), the lecture was included as the chapter "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight" in *[The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)*, reprinted several times.

In [2020](/wiki/2020), the lecture was included in the deluxe edition of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo)*.
