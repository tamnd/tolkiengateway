<i><b>Winds of Change</b></i> is an album by [Soundestiny](../S/Soundestiny.md) that was released in 2010.

All words and music were written by Darryl Grant Potter. This CD is Part II of two albums that constitute the rock musical **RingLord** – a rock opera or rock musical inspired by [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md). The songs themselves contain no references to any [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) characters such as [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) or [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), nor place-names like [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) or [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). As the project consisted of 25 songs in total, the band decided to divide the tracks into two separate *[TLOTR](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*-inspired album releases. *Winds of Change* (RingLord Part II) was released in 2010 whereas [Shadow Rising](../S/Shadow_Rising.md) (RingLord Part I) had been released six years prior by [Soundestiny](../S/Soundestiny.md) in 2004. The time lag was the result of live concerts, music video filming and recording of two other CDs during the intervening period.
