**Willow-wren**, also known as **Little Bird**, was one of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Forest-folk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Forest-folk (page does not exist)">Forest-folk</a><sup id="cite_ref-Bombadil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bombadil-1">[1]</a></sup> in the poem *[Bombadil Goes Boating](/wiki/Bombadil_Goes_Boating)*.

## History {#History}

Willow-wren was a bird that overheard Tom Bombadil's decision to sail down the Withywindle, and threatened to tell [Old Man Willow](/wiki/Old_Man_Willow) of this. Tom countered the bird by saying that if the bird did tell Old Man Willow, he would burn her and roast her on a willow-spit to end her prying. Willow-wren responded by teasing Bombadil that he would need to catch her first before she reaches Old Man Willow. After Willow-wren flew away from the area, Tom decided to go in that direction anyway to visit [Hays-end](/wiki/Hays-end).<sup id="cite_ref-Bombadil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bombadil-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later, when the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Otter-folk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Otter-folk (page does not exist)">Otter-folk</a> found Tom's boat docked at Hays-end, she helped them loose it alongside [Fisher Blue](/wiki/Fisher_Blue) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Old_Swan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Old Swan (page does not exist)">Old Swan</a>. They waited for Tom to come and look for it, but he was already heading for home by the time they loosed his boat, and so they waited for a long time.<sup id="cite_ref-Bombadil_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bombadil-1">[1]</a></sup>
