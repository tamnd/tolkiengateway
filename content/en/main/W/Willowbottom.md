**Willowbottom** was a small village near [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End) in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It stood on the banks of the stream called [Thistle Brook](/wiki/Thistle_Brook), just above the place where that stream flowed into the River [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The second element of the name is frequent in English placenames, and often appears as *-botham* ([Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *botm* "deep valley"). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) gives the meaning of '"steep-sided valley" or especially a "deeplying flat"'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> See also [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom). <span></span>
