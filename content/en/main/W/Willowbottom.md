**Willowbottom** was a small village near [Woody End](Woody_End.md) in the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). It stood on the banks of the stream called [Thistle Brook](../T/Thistle_Brook.md), just above the place where that stream flowed into the River [Shirebourn](../S/Shirebourn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The second element of the name is frequent in English placenames, and often appears as *-botham* ([Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *botm* "deep valley"). [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) gives the meaning of '"steep-sided valley" or especially a "deeplying flat"'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> See also [Longbottom](../L/Longbottom.md). <span></span>
