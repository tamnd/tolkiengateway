**Wilcome "Jolly" Cotton** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).

## History {#History}

Wilcome was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1384](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984"), named after his uncle [Will](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_I "Wilcome Cotton I").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Jolly and his brother helped their father fend off [Sharkey](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman")'s [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians"), and actively participated in the revolt and [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *willa* "pleasure" + *cuma* "guest"; *Wilcuma* is a welcome guest. *Jolly* comes from Old French *jolif* "merry, happy", which is cognate to the word *[yule](/wiki/Yule "Yule")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
