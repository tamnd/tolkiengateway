[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) served in the British Army during **World War I** (or the "**Great War**"), most notably in the bloody [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme). The earliest works of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium")—collected in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)*—were begun during the conflict. The extent to which the war and Tolkien's experience of it are reflected in his written work is a matter of much interest in Tolkien scholarship.

## Timeline {#Timeline}

<dl><dd><b>See also <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_World_War_I" title="wikipedia:Timeline of World War I">Timeline of World War I</a></i> at Wikipedia</b></dd></dl>

### 1914 {#1914}

- **[June 28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Archduke_Franz_Ferdinand_of_Austria" title="wikipedia:Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria">The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria</a> in Sarajevo.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[July 28](/wiki/28_July "28 July")** - Austria-Hungary declares war on Serbia. Russia mobilizes. The Great War begins.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[August 4](/wiki/4_August "4 August")** - The British Empire declares war on Germany, following tension and conflict on the mainland in July.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[August](/wiki/August)** - [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien) joins the army as a bugler. [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith) and [Robert Gilson](/wiki/Robert_Gilson) join the army. [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman) joins the navy instead. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) plans to finish his education.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1915 {#1915}

- **[June 10](/wiki/10_June "10 June")** - Exams start at Oxford University. Because of the war, only Tolkien and 24 others remain in the entire University. Tolkien passes.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - Tolkien applies at the Oxford recruiting office to join the army.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - Tolkien becomes a second lieutenant, and is appointed to the 13th Service Battalion of the Lancashire Fusilliers.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[September 25](/wiki/25_September "25 September")** - The Big Four of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.) - Tolkien, Smith, Wiseman and Gilson - have their last meeting.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 21](/wiki/21_November "21 November")** - G.B. Smith is shipped to France.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1916 {#1916}

- **[January 2](/wiki/2_January "2 January")** - Wiseman reports for duty at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Superb_(1907)" title="wikipedia:HMS Superb (1907)">HMS Superb</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invergordon" title="wikipedia:Invergordon">Invergordon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[January 8](/wiki/8_January "8 January")** - Robert Gilson is promoted to Lieutenant and shipped to France.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[March 16](/wiki/16_March "16 March")** - Tolkien receives his degree at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford).<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[March 22](/wiki/22_March "22 March")** - Tolkien marries [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Carpenter_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carpenter-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **[May 31](/wiki/31_May "31 May") - [June 1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - Christopher Wiseman fights in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Jutland" title="wikipedia:Battle of Jutland">Battle of Jutland</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 2](/wiki/2_June "2 June")** - Tolkien receives orders to report in Folkestone, and prepare for embarking.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 4](/wiki/4_June "4 June")** - Tolkien is shipped to Étaples, France.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 27](/wiki/27_June "27 June")** - Tolkien leaves Étaples, and goes to the Somme front.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 1](/wiki/1_July "1 July")** - One of the bloodiest battles in recent history, the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme), starts. Robert Gilson is one of the first of the 360,000 British casualties when he is hit by a shell at La Boiselle.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 14](/wiki/14_July "14 July")** - Tolkien's C-company is sent into action at the Somme front. This is Tolkien's first 5-day front-line duty.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilbert_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilbert-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **[July 22](/wiki/22_July "22 July")** - [Ralph "the Baby" Payton](/wiki/Ralph_Payton "Ralph Payton"), one of the T.C.B.S., is killed in the Battle of the Somme. His body is never identified.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 24](/wiki/24_July "24 July")** - Tolkien's second five day front-line duty begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilbert_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilbert-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **[August 19](/wiki/19_August "19 August")** - Tolkien and G.B. Smith meet at Acheux.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 26](/wiki/26_October "26 October")** - While in reserve, Tolkien's battalion is inspected by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Haig,_1st_Earl_Haig" title="wikipedia:Douglas Haig, 1st Earl Haig">Sir Doulgas Haig</a>, the British commander at the Battle of the Somme.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 27](/wiki/27_October "27 October")** – J.R.R. Tolkien is struck by trench fever, which ends his active service after just 5 months.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 28](/wiki/28_October "28 October")** – Tolkien is hospitalized.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 8](/wiki/8_November "8 November")** - Tolkien is repatriated. He now stays at a hospital in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 18](/wiki/18_November "18 November")** - The Battle of the Somme ends. Neither side wins. Tolkien is officially stricken off as a casualty by his Battalion.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 29](/wiki/29_November "29 November")** - G.B. Smith is hit by shrapnel, and wounded on his right arm and thigh.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[December 3](/wiki/3_December "3 December")** - G.B. Smith dies of gas-gangrene in his wounds. The news does not reach Tolkien until the 16th.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[December 16](/wiki/16_December "16 December") - [25](/wiki/25_December "25 December")** - Tolkien recovers, and he and Edith visit <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Great_Haywood&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Great Haywood (page does not exist)">Great Haywood</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1917 {#1917}

- **[January 12](/wiki/12_January "12 January")** - Tolkien's leave of absence (to recover from his trench fever) ends, but he is far from recovered.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[January 29](/wiki/29_January "29 January")** - Tolkien faces a Medical Board in Birmingham, but he is not deemed fit to return to war.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[February](/wiki/February)** - Tolkien faces his second Medical Board, but is again deemed unfit for duty.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[February 27](/wiki/27_February "27 February")** - Tolkien faces his third Medical Board, and again his leave is extended.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[April 18](/wiki/18_April "18 April")** - Tolkien and Wiseman, the Great Twin Brethren, meet in Harrogate, and discuss the fate of the [Barrovians](/wiki/T.C.B.S. "T.C.B.S.") and the future of the Society.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[April 19](/wiki/19_April "19 April")** - Tolkien, in revalidation, is assigned to the Humber Garrison, but is not fit enough.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 1](/wiki/1_June "1 June")** - Tolkien is considered fit for service again, but he is not reassigned to the Lancashire Fusilliers.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 29](/wiki/29_June "29 June")** - J.R.R. Tolkien succumbs to gastritis while at Brocton Camp.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[August 1](/wiki/1_August "1 August")** - ["Tea Cake" Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley "Thomas Kenneth Barnsley") is killed in Ypres.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 13](/wiki/13_October "13 October")**- Tolkien is found unfit for duty for six months, but able to do a desk job.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November](/wiki/November)** – Tolkien moves into <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=50_St._John%27s_Street&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="50 St. John&#39;s Street (page does not exist)">50 St. John&#39;s Street</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 16](/wiki/16_November "16 November")** - The Tolkien's first son, [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien"), is born.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1918 {#1918}

- **[October 11](/wiki/11_October "11 October")** - Tolkien is officially released from Brooklands Hospital, Birmingham.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 11](/wiki/11_November "11 November")** - The War comes to an end in a cease-fire.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>

### 1919 {#1919}

- **[February 17](/wiki/17_February "17 February")** - [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien) returns home.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- **[June 28](/wiki/28_June "28 June")** - The war officially ends, with the signing of the Treaty of Versailles.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[July 15](/wiki/15_July "15 July")** - Tolkien is released from service.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 16](/wiki/16_July "16 July")** - Tolkien is officially demobilized.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
