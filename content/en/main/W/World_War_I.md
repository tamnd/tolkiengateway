[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) served in the British Army during **World War I** (or the "**Great War**"), most notably in the bloody [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md). The earliest works of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md)—collected in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md)*—were begun during the conflict. The extent to which the war and Tolkien's experience of it are reflected in his written work is a matter of much interest in Tolkien scholarship.

## Timeline {#Timeline}

<dl><dd><b>See also <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_World_War_I" title="wikipedia:Timeline of World War I">Timeline of World War I</a></i> at Wikipedia</b></dd></dl>

### 1914 {#1914}

- **[June 28](../_/28_June.md)** - <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Archduke_Franz_Ferdinand_of_Austria" title="wikipedia:Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria">The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria</a> in Sarajevo.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[July 28](../_/28_July.md)** - Austria-Hungary declares war on Serbia. Russia mobilizes. The Great War begins.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[August 4](../_/4_August.md)** - The British Empire declares war on Germany, following tension and conflict on the mainland in July.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[August](../A/August.md)** - [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) joins the army as a bugler. [Geoffrey Bache Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) and [Robert Gilson](../R/Robert_Gilson.md) join the army. [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) joins the navy instead. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) plans to finish his education.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1915 {#1915}

- **[June 10](../_/10_June.md)** - Exams start at Oxford University. Because of the war, only Tolkien and 24 others remain in the entire University. Tolkien passes.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 28](../_/28_June.md)** - Tolkien applies at the Oxford recruiting office to join the army.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 15](../_/15_July.md)** - Tolkien becomes a second lieutenant, and is appointed to the 13th Service Battalion of the Lancashire Fusilliers.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[September 25](../_/25_September.md)** - The Big Four of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md) - Tolkien, Smith, Wiseman and Gilson - have their last meeting.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 21](../_/21_November.md)** - G.B. Smith is shipped to France.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1916 {#1916}

- **[January 2](../_/2_January.md)** - Wiseman reports for duty at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Superb_(1907)" title="wikipedia:HMS Superb (1907)">HMS Superb</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invergordon" title="wikipedia:Invergordon">Invergordon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[January 8](../_/8_January.md)** - Robert Gilson is promoted to Lieutenant and shipped to France.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[March 16](../_/16_March.md)** - Tolkien receives his degree at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[March 22](../_/22_March.md)** - Tolkien marries [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Carpenter_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carpenter-5">[5]</a></sup>
- **[May 31](../_/31_May.md) - [June 1](../_/1_June.md)** - Christopher Wiseman fights in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Jutland" title="wikipedia:Battle of Jutland">Battle of Jutland</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 2](../_/2_June.md)** - Tolkien receives orders to report in Folkestone, and prepare for embarking.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 4](../_/4_June.md)** - Tolkien is shipped to Étaples, France.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 27](../_/27_June.md)** - Tolkien leaves Étaples, and goes to the Somme front.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 1](../_/1_July.md)** - One of the bloodiest battles in recent history, the [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md), starts. Robert Gilson is one of the first of the 360,000 British casualties when he is hit by a shell at La Boiselle.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 14](../_/14_July.md)** - Tolkien's C-company is sent into action at the Somme front. This is Tolkien's first 5-day front-line duty.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilbert_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilbert-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **[July 22](../_/22_July.md)** - [Ralph "the Baby" Payton](../R/Ralph_Payton.md), one of the T.C.B.S., is killed in the Battle of the Somme. His body is never identified.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 24](../_/24_July.md)** - Tolkien's second five day front-line duty begins.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilbert_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilbert-6">[6]</a></sup>
- **[August 19](../_/19_August.md)** - Tolkien and G.B. Smith meet at Acheux.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 26](../_/26_October.md)** - While in reserve, Tolkien's battalion is inspected by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Haig,_1st_Earl_Haig" title="wikipedia:Douglas Haig, 1st Earl Haig">Sir Doulgas Haig</a>, the British commander at the Battle of the Somme.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 27](../_/27_October.md)** – J.R.R. Tolkien is struck by trench fever, which ends his active service after just 5 months.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 28](../_/28_October.md)** – Tolkien is hospitalized.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 8](../_/8_November.md)** - Tolkien is repatriated. He now stays at a hospital in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 18](../_/18_November.md)** - The Battle of the Somme ends. Neither side wins. Tolkien is officially stricken off as a casualty by his Battalion.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 29](../_/29_November.md)** - G.B. Smith is hit by shrapnel, and wounded on his right arm and thigh.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[December 3](../_/3_December.md)** - G.B. Smith dies of gas-gangrene in his wounds. The news does not reach Tolkien until the 16th.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[December 16](../_/16_December.md) - [25](../_/25_December.md)** - Tolkien recovers, and he and Edith visit <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Great_Haywood&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Great Haywood (page does not exist)">Great Haywood</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1917 {#1917}

- **[January 12](../_/12_January.md)** - Tolkien's leave of absence (to recover from his trench fever) ends, but he is far from recovered.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[January 29](../_/29_January.md)** - Tolkien faces a Medical Board in Birmingham, but he is not deemed fit to return to war.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[February](../F/February.md)** - Tolkien faces his second Medical Board, but is again deemed unfit for duty.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[February 27](../_/27_February.md)** - Tolkien faces his third Medical Board, and again his leave is extended.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[April 18](../_/18_April.md)** - Tolkien and Wiseman, the Great Twin Brethren, meet in Harrogate, and discuss the fate of the [Barrovians](../T/T.C.B.S..md) and the future of the Society.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[April 19](../_/19_April.md)** - Tolkien, in revalidation, is assigned to the Humber Garrison, but is not fit enough.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 1](../_/1_June.md)** - Tolkien is considered fit for service again, but he is not reassigned to the Lancashire Fusilliers.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[June 29](../_/29_June.md)** - J.R.R. Tolkien succumbs to gastritis while at Brocton Camp.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[August 1](../_/1_August.md)** - ["Tea Cake" Barnsley](../T/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley.md) is killed in Ypres.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[October 13](../_/13_October.md)**- Tolkien is found unfit for duty for six months, but able to do a desk job.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November](../N/November.md)** – Tolkien moves into <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=50_St._John%27s_Street&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="50 St. John&#39;s Street (page does not exist)">50 St. John&#39;s Street</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 16](../_/16_November.md)** - The Tolkien's first son, [John](../J/John_Tolkien.md), is born.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>

### 1918 {#1918}

- **[October 11](../_/11_October.md)** - Tolkien is officially released from Brooklands Hospital, Birmingham.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[November 11](../_/11_November.md)** - The War comes to an end in a cease-fire.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>

### 1919 {#1919}

- **[February 17](../_/17_February.md)** - [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) returns home.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- **[June 28](../_/28_June.md)** - The war officially ends, with the signing of the Treaty of Versailles.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-4">[4]</a></sup>
- **[July 15](../_/15_July.md)** - Tolkien is released from service.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[July 16](../_/16_July.md)** - Tolkien is officially demobilized.<sup id="cite_ref-Garth_1-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Garth-1">[1]</a></sup>
