<i><b>War of the Ring: The Card Game</b></i> is an re-imagined, streamlined and condensed version of the [larger board game of the same name](<War_of_the_Ring%253A_Second_Edition_(2011_board_game).md>).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Inspired by the best-selling and award-winning War of the Ring board game, War of the Ring - The Card Game allows players to journey to the world of J.R.R. Tolkien’s [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) and create their own version of the dramatic conflict between the Dark Lord, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), and the [Free Peoples](../F/Free_Peoples.md) of Middle-earth.

In War of the Ring - The Card Game, up to 4 players compete in two teams, the Shadow against the Free Peoples, each player using a specific and different card deck representing the strengths and weaknesses of the different factions involved in the war.

The Free Peoples desperately try to complete their quest to destroy the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md), while at the same time defending their homelands from the encroaching hordes of Sauron and from his evil allies. The Shadow players must strike quickly and decisively, before the [Ring-bearers](../R/Ring-bearers.md) can complete their quest; or try to slowly corrupt [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md), burdening him with wounds, toil, and the sorrow for the loss of his Companions.

## Expansions {#Expansions}

- *Against the Shadow* (2023) - Adds a co-operative and solo mode to the game, enabling the player to take on an automated Shadow player. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *Fire and Swords* (2024) - Expands the card pool with new battlegrounds, introduces new factions, skirmishes as well as unique Scenarios that can be undertaken.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *Hope and Despair* (2026) - Adds Story cards to the game, representing key turning points in the *Lord of the Rings* story as gameplay inflection points.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
