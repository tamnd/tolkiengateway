The **White Tree of Gondor** stood as a symbol of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the [Court of the Fountain](</wiki/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith)> "Court of the Fountain (Minas Tirith)") in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). Its predecessor was a seedling of [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor"), planted in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil), that was destroyed before the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

The White Tree also appears on the [livery of the heirs of Elendil](/wiki/Livery_of_the_heirs_of_Elendil "Livery of the heirs of Elendil"), which was worn by the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel) of Minas Tirith,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> on the [standard of Elendil](/wiki/Standard_of_Elendil "Standard of Elendil"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and on the royal banner of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### First White Tree {#First_White_Tree}

The first White Tree came from a fruit that [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), at great personal risk, managed to steal from [Nimloth](</wiki/Nimloth_(tree)> "Nimloth (tree)") the Fair, the White Tree of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), before that one was destroyed at [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s insistence. He suffered many wounds during this mission, and he came near death, but when the first leaf opened in the spring, Isildur was healed.

This sapling was brought to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) on Isildur's ship, and it was eventually planted in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) before his house. But when Sauron returned to Middle-earth, he launched a [sudden attack](/wiki/First_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "First Fall of Minas Ithil") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") that captured Minas Ithil, and he burned the White Tree. Isildur escaped, taking with him again a sapling.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Second White Tree {#Second_White_Tree}

In the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2"), Isildur planted the seedling of the White Tree in [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) in memory of his brother, [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup>

This White Tree lasted until [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636"), when the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) struck [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), killing King [Telemnar](/wiki/Telemnar) and his children among many others.<sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third White Tree {#Third_White_Tree}

A third sapling was planted in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640") by King [Tarondor](</wiki/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor)> "Tarondor (King of Gondor)"). This tree died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2872](/wiki/Third_Age_2872 "Third Age 2872") with the death of the [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) [Belecthor II](/wiki/Belecthor_II).<sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-7">[7]</a></sup>

Since no seedling was found, the tree was left standing after its death "until the King returns".<sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-7">[7]</a></sup> When eventually replaced in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), it was laid in the [Tombs of the Kings](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen "Rath Dínen") with royal honours.

### Fourth White Tree {#Fourth_White_Tree}

After [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) became king, he feared that he would not marry [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) and found a dynasty, and he connected his unmarried state with the continued lack of a living White Tree. Then he discovered (with [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s help) a sapling of the White Tree in the high hallow upon the slopes of [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin), above Minas Tirith. This sapling he reverently planted in its place, and he took it as a sign that Arwen would arrive soon. In June of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") the sapling bloomed.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 1]</a></sup>
