The **White Tree of Gondor** stood as a symbol of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the [Court of the Fountain](<../C/Court_of_the_Fountain_(Minas_Tirith).md>) in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). Its predecessor was a seedling of [Nimloth](../N/Nimloth_of_Númenor.md), planted in [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md), that was destroyed before the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

The White Tree also appears on the [livery of the heirs of Elendil](../L/Livery_of_the_heirs_of_Elendil.md), which was worn by the [Guards of the Citadel](../G/Guards_of_the_Citadel.md) of Minas Tirith,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> on the [standard of Elendil](../S/Standard_of_Elendil.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and on the royal banner of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### First White Tree {#First_White_Tree}

The first White Tree came from a fruit that [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md), at great personal risk, managed to steal from [Nimloth](<../N/Nimloth_(tree).md>) the Fair, the White Tree of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), before that one was destroyed at [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s insistence. He suffered many wounds during this mission, and he came near death, but when the first leaf opened in the spring, Isildur was healed.

This sapling was brought to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) on Isildur's ship, and it was eventually planted in [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) before his house. But when Sauron returned to Middle-earth, he launched a [sudden attack](../F/First_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3429](../S/Second_Age_3429.md) that captured Minas Ithil, and he burned the White Tree. Isildur escaped, taking with him again a sapling.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Second White Tree {#Second_White_Tree}

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md), Isildur planted the seedling of the White Tree in [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md) in memory of his brother, [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup>

This White Tree lasted until [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md), when the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) struck [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), killing King [Telemnar](../T/Telemnar.md) and his children among many others.<sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third White Tree {#Third_White_Tree}

A third sapling was planted in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md) by King [Tarondor](<../T/Tarondor_(King_of_Gondor).md>). This tree died in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2872](../T/Third_Age_2872.md) with the death of the [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) [Belecthor II](../B/Belecthor_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-7">[7]</a></sup>

Since no seedling was found, the tree was left standing after its death "until the King returns".<sup id="cite_ref-B2_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B2-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stew_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-7">[7]</a></sup> When eventually replaced in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), it was laid in the [Tombs of the Kings](../R/Rath_Dínen.md) with royal honours.

### Fourth White Tree {#Fourth_White_Tree}

After [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) became king, he feared that he would not marry [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) and found a dynasty, and he connected his unmarried state with the continued lack of a living White Tree. Then he discovered (with [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)'s help) a sapling of the White Tree in the high hallow upon the slopes of [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md), above Minas Tirith. This sapling he reverently planted in its place, and he took it as a sign that Arwen would arrive soon. In June of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) the sapling bloomed.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 1]</a></sup>
