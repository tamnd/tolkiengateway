**West Beleriand** covered the lands of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to the west of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md); they were for the most part under the lordship of [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) in [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), and [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) the Shipwright in the [Falas](../F/Falas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

West Beleriand was the region of Beleriand facing [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) to the west with the coast of [Falas](../F/Falas.md) and the havens of [Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) and the seaward tower of [Barad Nimras](../B/Barad_Nimras.md). To the north the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) separated [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) from the rest of Beleriand. The forested strip of [Nivrim](../N/Nivrim.md) was in West Beleriand, separated from the rest of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) by Sirion. To the south, [Nan Tathren](../N/Nan_Tathren.md) was the small forested area between Narog and Sirion. Further south, the land of [Arvernien](../A/Arvernien.md) with the forest of [Nimbrethil](../N/Nimbrethil.md) formed the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md), ending to the [Cape Balar](../C/Cape_Balar.md).

Rivers included [Brithon](../B/Brithon.md) and [Nenning](../N/Nenning.md) and the tributaries to Sirion, [Narog](../N/Narog.md) (with [Ginglith](../G/Ginglith.md) and [Ringwil](../R/Ringwil.md)) and [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md) (with [Malduin](../M/Malduin.md) and other minor rivers). Between Ginglith and Narog was the field of [Tumhalad](../T/Tumhalad.md). The [Ravines of Taeglin](../R/Ravines_of_Taeglin.md) surrounded the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md).

Between Narog and Sirion lay [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md), a part of the Realm of Nargothrond with the hill of [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md). To that realm also belonged [Taur-en-Faroth](../T/Taur-en-Faroth.md) to the west of Narog.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

At its widest this region was ninety-nine leagues across.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
