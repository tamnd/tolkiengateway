**West Beleriand** covered the lands of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to the west of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion); they were for the most part under the lordship of [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) the Shipwright in the [Falas](/wiki/Falas).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

West Beleriand was the region of Beleriand facing [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) to the west with the coast of [Falas](/wiki/Falas) and the havens of [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) and the seaward tower of [Barad Nimras](/wiki/Barad_Nimras). To the north the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) separated [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) from the rest of Beleriand. The forested strip of [Nivrim](/wiki/Nivrim) was in West Beleriand, separated from the rest of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) by Sirion. To the south, [Nan Tathren](/wiki/Nan_Tathren) was the small forested area between Narog and Sirion. Further south, the land of [Arvernien](/wiki/Arvernien) with the forest of [Nimbrethil](/wiki/Nimbrethil) formed the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar), ending to the [Cape Balar](/wiki/Cape_Balar).

Rivers included [Brithon](/wiki/Brithon) and [Nenning](/wiki/Nenning) and the tributaries to Sirion, [Narog](/wiki/Narog) (with [Ginglith](/wiki/Ginglith) and [Ringwil](/wiki/Ringwil)) and [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) (with [Malduin](/wiki/Malduin) and other minor rivers). Between Ginglith and Narog was the field of [Tumhalad](/wiki/Tumhalad). The [Ravines of Taeglin](/wiki/Ravines_of_Taeglin) surrounded the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil).

Between Narog and Sirion lay [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen), a part of the Realm of Nargothrond with the hill of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh). To that realm also belonged [Taur-en-Faroth](/wiki/Taur-en-Faroth) to the west of Narog.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

At its widest this region was ninety-nine leagues across.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
