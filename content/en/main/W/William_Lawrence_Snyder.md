**William Lawrence Snyder** ([14 February](../_/14_February.md), [1918](../_/1918.md) – [3 June](../_/3_June.md), [1998](../_/1998.md)) was an academy award winning American film producer. He was the producer of the [first animated film](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* in 1967. The film was directed by [Gene Deitch](../G/Gene_Deitch.md) and was made in under a month. The running time for the film was a minuscule twelve minutes and was produced simply so that Snyder could extend his license for *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, and sell it back to Tolkien and his publishers for $100,000.
