**William Lawrence Snyder** ([14 February](/wiki/14_February), [1918](/wiki/1918) – [3 June](/wiki/3_June), [1998](/wiki/1998)) was an academy award winning American film producer. He was the producer of the [first animated film](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* in 1967. The film was directed by [Gene Deitch](/wiki/Gene_Deitch) and was made in under a month. The running time for the film was a minuscule twelve minutes and was produced simply so that Snyder could extend his license for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, and sell it back to Tolkien and his publishers for $100,000.
