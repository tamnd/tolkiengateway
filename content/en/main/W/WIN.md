**WIN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "young".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[vinya](/wiki/Vinya "Vinya")*; *[víne](/wiki/V%C3%ADne "Víne")* ("youth")<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the deleted root GWIN ("new, fresh"), with the derivatives Quenya *[winya](/wiki/Vinya "Vinya")* ("new, fresh, young"), [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *bîn*. [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") also experimented with a derivative ?[Avarin](/wiki/Avarin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gwinarn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gwinarn (page does not exist)">Gwinarn</a></i>. The root GWIN was linked to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=GWEN&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GWEN (page does not exist)">GWEN</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In a manuscript associated with the essay *Eldarin Hands, Fingers &amp; Numerals* appears another deleted stem: WINI ("little"), perhaps intending to explain Quenya *wine*, *winimo* ("baby").<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>
