**WIN** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "young".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[vinya](../V/Vinya.md)*; *[víne](../V/Víne.md)* ("youth")<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appears the deleted root GWIN ("new, fresh"), with the derivatives Quenya *[winya](../V/Vinya.md)* ("new, fresh, young"), [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *bîn*. [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) also experimented with a derivative ?[Avarin](../A/Avarin.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gwinarn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gwinarn (page does not exist)">Gwinarn</a></i>. The root GWIN was linked to <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=GWEN&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GWEN (page does not exist)">GWEN</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In a manuscript associated with the essay *Eldarin Hands, Fingers &amp; Numerals* appears another deleted stem: WINI ("little"), perhaps intending to explain Quenya *wine*, *winimo* ("baby").<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>
