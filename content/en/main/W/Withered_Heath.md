The **Withered Heath** was a [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons")-infested valley in the eastern [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) in northern [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion).

## Description {#Description}

The Withered Heath was a long narrow valley in the eastern part of the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), where they forked into two thin ranges.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On the floor of this long east-west valley between the mountains was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_(habitat)" title="wikipedia:Heath (habitat)">heath</a>, burned and blackened by its inhabitants, being the breeding-ground of [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At one time the dragons of the Heath had infested the Grey Mountains and the lands beyond, and in the closing decades of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) terrorised the inhabitants of the north.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Smaug was the last of the great dragons, but lesser members of his kind remained.

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the earliest versions of the legendarium, the *Withered Heath* was the name of the [Heath of the Sky-roof](/wiki/Heath_of_the_Sky-roof), a location of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) near [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel), after a great battle between [Men](/wiki/Men) who came to the island.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the manuscript of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, Bladorthin (the pre-cursor to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)) says that the Withered Heath is "*where the Great Dragons used to live*".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

It is a frigid but geologically active area with sulphur deposits and geysers, and a river running eastward through the middle. In the north-west is the Anvil of Winterstith, a glacier that houses the Frost-horde, an army of dragon-spawn led by Hrímil Frost-Heart, a great Cold-<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dragon" title="Dragon">dragon</a> of the ancient days. A servant of <a href="/wiki/Morgoth" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a> during the First Age, Hrímil rejected Sauron and earned his enmity by consuming one of the Dwarven <a href="/wiki/Rings_of_Power" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a>.</dd></dl>
