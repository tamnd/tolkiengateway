The **Withered Heath** was a [dragon](../D/Dragons.md)-infested valley in the eastern [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) in northern [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md).

## Description {#Description}

The Withered Heath was a long narrow valley in the eastern part of the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md), where they forked into two thin ranges.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On the floor of this long east-west valley between the mountains was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_(habitat)" title="wikipedia:Heath (habitat)">heath</a>, burned and blackened by its inhabitants, being the breeding-ground of [dragons](../D/Dragons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At one time the dragons of the Heath had infested the Grey Mountains and the lands beyond, and in the closing decades of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Dragon [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) terrorised the inhabitants of the north.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Smaug was the last of the great dragons, but lesser members of his kind remained.

## Other version of the legendarium {#Other_version_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the earliest versions of the legendarium, the *Withered Heath* was the name of the [Heath of the Sky-roof](../H/Heath_of_the_Sky-roof.md), a location of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) near [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md), after a great battle between [Men](../M/Men.md) who came to the island.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the manuscript of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, Bladorthin (the pre-cursor to [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)) says that the Withered Heath is "*where the Great Dragons used to live*".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Withered Heath is an area of <a href="../G/Grey_Mountains.md" title="Grey Mountains">Ered Mithrin</a> where players can find many <a href="../D/Dragons.md" title="Dragons">Drakes</a>. It is a frigid but geologically active area with sulphur deposits and geysers, and a river running eastward through the middle. In the north-west is the Anvil of Winterstith, a glacier that houses the Frost-horde, an army of dragon-spawn led by Hrímil Frost-Heart, a great Cold-<a class="mw-redirect" href="../D/Dragon.md" title="Dragon">dragon</a> of the ancient days. A servant of <a href="../M/Morgoth.md" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a> during the First Age, Hrímil rejected Sauron and earned his enmity by consuming one of the Dwarven <a href="../R/Rings_of_Power.md" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a>.</dd></dl>
