**Walter Hooper** was a visitor to [the Inklings](/wiki/The_Inklings "The Inklings"), holded by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and their friends. Walter was invited into a reading meet-up, in which *[The Horse and His Boy](/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia "The Chronicles of Narnia")*, *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*, and other books in progress (at the time) were being written. He is mainly known by his work as literary trustee for C.S. Lewis and [Owen Barfield](/wiki/Owen_Barfield).
