The **Waste** was a region that was located somewhere within [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Stage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stage-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), [Bard](/wiki/Bard) gave a large amount of gold to the [Master of Lake-town](/wiki/Master_of_Lake-town) to help the [Lake-men](/wiki/Lake-men). However, the Master caught the dragon-sickness, stole most of the gold and fled with it from [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town).<sup id="cite_ref-Stage_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stage-1">[1]</a></sup> After his companions had deserted him, he starved to death in the Waste,<sup id="cite_ref-Stage_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stage-1">[1]</a></sup> sometime between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") and [2949](/wiki/Third_Age_2949 "Third Age 2949").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the [The Last Stage](/wiki/The_Last_Stage) chapter, the Master was murdered by his companions for the gold and thrown into the waste afterwards. In a modification of this earlier version, the Master's companions simply left him and the Master died of starvation in the waste.<sup id="cite_ref-Back_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Back-4">[3]</a></sup> In a slightly later version, the Master died of starvation within the waste without any companions being mentioned.<sup id="cite_ref-End_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-End-5">[4]</a></sup> The word *waste* was uncapitalized in all of those versions.<sup id="cite_ref-Back_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Back-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-End_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-End-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Speculation {#Speculation}

It is possible that the capitalization of the word *Waste* within the published book may have been a mistake or a last minute change by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) or by the publisher.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) has suggested that the *Waste* may possibly have been another name for the [Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/Desolation_of_the_Dragon "Desolation of the Dragon"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> though how he came to this conclusion is unknown.
