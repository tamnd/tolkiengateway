The **Wise** were a group of people among the various kindreds of the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) who had great knowledge and understanding of history and other lore. They were greatly respected, but always few in number. The Wise were both female and male; those who were female were sometimes referred to as [Wise-women](/wiki/Wise-woman "Wise-woman").

In the context of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Wise mostly refers to the Istari and the highest [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) who formed the [White Council](/wiki/White_Council) against the [shadow](/wiki/Shadow "Shadow").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The following people were often considered to be among the Wise:

- [Adanel](/wiki/Adanel)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor)
- [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn)
- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)
- The [Istari](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards")
- [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)
- [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel)
- [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad)
- [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil)
- [Nerdanel](/wiki/Nerdanel)
