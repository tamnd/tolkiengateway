The **Wise** were a group of people among the various kindreds of the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) who had great knowledge and understanding of history and other lore. They were greatly respected, but always few in number. The Wise were both female and male; those who were female were sometimes referred to as [Wise-women](Wise-woman.md).

In the context of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the Wise mostly refers to the Istari and the highest [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) who formed the [White Council](White_Council.md) against the [shadow](../S/Shadow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The following people were often considered to be among the Wise:

- [Adanel](../A/Adanel.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Andreth](../A/Andreth.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Bëor](../B/Bëor.md)
- [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md)
- [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md)
- The [Istari](Wizards.md)
- [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)
- [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md)
- [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md)
- [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md)
- [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md)
- [Nerdanel](../N/Nerdanel.md)
