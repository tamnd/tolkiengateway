The **Westfold** was a region of slopes and fields<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the west of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), between the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen) in the west and the region called the [Folde](/wiki/Folde) in the east,<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup> the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) in the south<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> and the walls of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and the eaves of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The [Great West Road](/wiki/Great_West_Road) ran through the Westfold north of the White Mountains from the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen) to the Folde and [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> In the south of the Westfold lay the Westfold Vale, a great bay in the northernmost arm of the White Mountains with the [Deeping-coomb](/wiki/Deeping-coomb) and the [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), the stronghold of the Westfold,<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup> at its southern end.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The Men who lived in the Westfold were called [Westfold-men](/wiki/Westfold-men)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> or Westfolders.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Westfold was originally part of the realm of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) until in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510") the fief of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) was granted to [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) the Young by the [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion). Thereafter the Westfold was part of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) led by [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) overran Rohan and King [Helm](/wiki/Helm) took refuge in Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> During the subsequent [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter) first prince [Háma](/wiki/H%C3%A1ma) and then Helm made sorties and forays into the Westfold for food and to attack their besiegers. Both men died in the snow.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/69/Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png/200px-Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="138" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/69/Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png/300px-Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/69/Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png/400px-Mark_Fisher_-_Westfold.png 2x" data-file-width="736" data-file-height="506"/></a><figcaption>Map of the Westfold</figcaption></figure>

In the beginning of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") when the forces of the wizard [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) invaded Rohan, [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred), the son of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), was the [Second Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Second_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark) with his base at Helm's Deep and was in command of the forces of the [West-mark](/wiki/West-mark). When actual war began Théodred assumed the overall command, ordered the summoning of a [Muster of Edoras](/wiki/Muster_of_Edoras) and ordered [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm) to reinforce the Muster of Westfold with four companies of riders from the Muster of Edoras<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-13">[13]</a></sup> to defend against the invading forces.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

On [25 February](/wiki/25_February) the [First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen) was fought.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> In this fight the sole objective of Saruman was to slay Théodred; had his forces invaded the Westfold immediately after this battle Rohan would have suffered grievously.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-13">[13]</a></sup> Instead, Saruman's army withdrew and did not return until [2 March](/wiki/2_March) to fight the [Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

Following the fall of Théodred, command in the West-mark was assumed by [Erkenbrand](/wiki/Erkenbrand), who was Lord of [Deeping-coomb](/wiki/Deeping-coomb) and much of the rest of the Westfold. In the Second Battle, the forces of Erkenbrand were scattered and Saruman's host marched through the Westfold to attack the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_13-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-13">[13]</a></sup> However, in the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) all of the enemies were crushed.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

After the fall of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) the funeral escort of King Théoden rode to Edoras. Subsequently, the remaining members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) travelled through the Westfold from [14 August](/wiki/14_August) to [22 August](/wiki/22_August) when King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") took his leave.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Westfold* is a name in the language of Rohan. It is a combination of "West" and *fold* from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *folde* ("earth", "land", "country").<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [Unfinished index](/wiki/Unfinished_index) for [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)'s index of [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales) the Westfold are "the slopes and fields" between [Thrihyrne](/wiki/Thrihyrne) and [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> However, the river Isen is located west<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> of the *Thrihyrne*, which is at the head of the valley of Helm's Deep,<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> and although the western boundary of the *Folde* is not indicated, the *Folde* was the centre of the kingdom of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-N_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-2">[2]</a></sup>

On the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings) a label called *Westfold* was placed in a western valley in the south of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) where Dunland is now located on the final published maps, but it was struck out afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> In an earlier manuscript version of the chapter Helm's Deep the men in the service of Saruman who attacked the Rohirrim at the Fords of Isen<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> and at Helm's Deep came from this *Westfold* and cried in the tongue of Westfold.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>
