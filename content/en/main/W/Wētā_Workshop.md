**Wētā Workshop** is a special effects and prop company that created sets and props for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), and [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was founded in 1987 by [Richard Taylor](/wiki/Richard_Taylor) and Tania Rodger.
