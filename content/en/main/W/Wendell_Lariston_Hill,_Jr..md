**Wendell Lariston Hill, Jr.** ([December 28](/wiki/28_December "28 December"), [1952](/wiki/1952) - [August 18](/wiki/18_August "18 August"), [2023](/wiki/2023)) was a commercial artist who founded the company Land of Legend early in his career. This company produced the board game <a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkienguide.com/modules/wiwimod/index.php?page=QuestMagicRing" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Quest of the Magic Ring</i></a> and the live action role-playing game <a class="external text" href="https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2147484002/Rules-for-the-Live-Ring-Game" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Rules for the Live Ring Game</i></a> in the 1970s. A modern version of *Rules for the Live Ring Game* is being used in the semi-annual [Wisconsin Ring Game](/wiki/Wisconsin_Ring_Game) held in <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governor_Dodge_State_Park" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Governor Dodge State Park</a>.
