**Wild Wood** is mentioned in lore as an ancient forest where [elf-children](/wiki/Elves "Elves") wondered at their first Dawn.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

If taken literally, the Wild Wood should refer to some woods near [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen) and the [Orocarni](/wiki/Orocarni).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
