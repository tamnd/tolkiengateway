The **Wandering Days** was the term used by [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to refer to the centuries-long period of their westward migration from their races' ancestral home somewhere in the wild East (probably in the upper [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin)).<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>

The oral history and memory of the Hobbits did not extend further back than their "Wandering Days", with their exact origins having been lost. So the first known historical fact is that they were alerted by the rise of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), so they drifted westward. Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050") many [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) settled in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and about [1150](/wiki/Third_Age_1150 "Third Age 1150") the [Fallohides](/wiki/Fallohides) followed, while many [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) who emerged from the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Pass) settled near [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) in [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), specifically [the Angle](/wiki/Angle "Angle"). In [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300") the Kingdom of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) was founded, so the northern Hobbits had to flee from further westwards, reaching [Bree](/wiki/Bree). Some Stoors (in [1356](/wiki/Third_Age_1356 "Third Age 1356")) returned to the [Gladden](/wiki/Gladden_Fields "Gladden Fields"). The others continued westward and their first settlements were in Bree and [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>

Eventually, in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601") a large population of [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits) was granted permission by King [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup> one of the last few Kings of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), to colonize the region beyond the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine), and were later joined by the Stoors of Dunland. This would become [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire), the main homeland of most of Hobbit-kind.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
