**Waldreg** was the barkeeper of [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad) during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Waldreg was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where he was portrayed by [Geoff Morrell](/wiki/Geoff_Morrell).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Waldreg worshipped [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) secretly, and wished for his return to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Under the instruction of [Adar](</wiki/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Adar (The Rings of Power)"), he activated a mechanism at the [Watchtower of Ostirith](/wiki/Watchtower_of_Ostirith), which caused a flood of water to flow into the volcano [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom"). As a result, the volcano erupted, destroying the Southlands and effectively creating the land of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Waldreg later taunted a captive Sauron, whom he believed to be [Halbrand](/wiki/Halbrand). After leaving Sauron with a [Warg](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs"), Sauron spoke to the Warg using the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) and ordered it to kill Waldreg. The old man was later devoured by the Warg.
