**Wolves** were animals that could be found within [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during all three ages.

## History {#History}

### First age {#First_age}

Wolves were of old allied with the [Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord),<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> and were often used as steeds by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup> Wolves, or creatures in wolf-shapes, first came to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) over mountain passes or through dark forests alongside other fell creatures during [Melkor's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") third age of captivity.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During [Fingolfin's](/wiki/Fingolfin "Fingolfin") suicidal duel with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), his body was broken and cast to great wolves of [Angband](/wiki/Angband), only for [Thorondor](/wiki/Thorondor) to take the body away for burial.<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-4">[4]</a></sup> [Rochallor](/wiki/Rochallor) stayed with Fingolfin until his death. Yet when he was assailed by the great wolves, the great steed escaped an immediate death by outrunning them due to his swiftness only to die when his heart broke upon coming to [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

When [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) took over the isle of [Tol Sirion](/wiki/Tol_Sirion), he built a watch-tower upon it and renamed the isle to [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth), and becoming the *"Lord of [Werewolves](/wiki/Werewolves)"*.<sup id="cite_ref-Fingolfin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingolfin-4">[4]</a></sup> These werewolves of Angband were bred from the great wolf [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin). Of which, [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth) was said to be the greatest of those descended from him.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim) was deceived into betraying [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), he heard wolf howls when the deception was unmasked and Sauron revealed himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>

When Sauron had sent many wolves into the [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen), [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) took their [wolf-hounds](/wiki/Hounds "Hounds"), of whom [Huan](/wiki/Huan) was their chief, and hunted the wolves down. It was said that Huan would meet his end upon encountering the [greatest wolf](/wiki/Carcharoth "Carcharoth") to walk upon [Arda](/wiki/Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>

When Huan and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) assailed Sauron's watch-tower, they were met with an army of wolves which Sauron sent to attack them on the bridge one by one before sending [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin) himself, only for him to be also be killed by Huan. After his death, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) decided to take on the form of the [mightiest wolf](/wiki/Wolf-Sauron "Wolf-Sauron") in order to fulfill the prophecy of Huan's death, but failed and yielded [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-2">[2]</a></sup>

Wolves were among the creatures that fought for Morgoth during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), after which, they moved freely alongside Orcs into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) as far southwards as [Nan-tathren](/wiki/Nan-tathren) and [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>

Wolves were among the Orcs that were holding [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) captive. Four of these Wolf-sentinels were shot by the [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg), to free his friend.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Wolves were among the forces that Morgoth used to assail the city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Third age {#Third_age}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Quinton_Hoover_-_Orc-mounts.jpg"><img src="/w/images/4/45/Quinton_Hoover_-_Orc-mounts.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="298" height="253" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="298" data-file-height="253"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Quinton_Hoover" title="Quinton Hoover">Quinton Hoover</a> - <i>Orc-mounts</i></figcaption></figure>

During the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace), [Aragorn I](/wiki/Aragorn_I) ruled as the fifth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) for only eight years before his life was cut short in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2327](/wiki/Third_Age_2327 "Third Age 2327"), when he was killed by wild wolves in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

After [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had escaped [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) sent out wolves and orcs in pursuit.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

On [13 January](/wiki/13_January) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) camped at [Hollin](/wiki/Hollin) and were attacked by wolves.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The next morning no remains of Wolves were found around.

## Other names {#Other_names}

**Wolf** is also used as a short form of **[Werewolf](/wiki/Werewolves "Werewolves")**.

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), one of the words for "wolf" is *ñarmo* or *narmo*. Cognates of the same meaning are [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) *garm*, and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *garaf*. (A "wolf-howl" is *naule* in Quenya, and *gaul* in Noldorin.)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Another Quenya word for "wolf" is *ráka*. Cognates, also meaning "wolf", are [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin") *draug* and Doriathrin *drôg*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, "wolf" is *harog* or *harw*; "a she-wolf" is *harach*;<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) has *ulku* "wolf".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One), the discarded [Valar](/wiki/Valar) siblings, [Makar](/wiki/Makar) and [Meássë](/wiki/Me%C3%A1ss%C3%AB), hunted wolves and [Bears](/wiki/Bears) within the black mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

In [The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two), it was said that poisonous snake dwelled where wolves roamed.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i></b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins" title="Lobelia Sackville-Baggins">Lobelia Sackville-Baggins</a> believes that wolves are in the Shire, and demands that <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a> alert the local <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Shirriff" title="Shirriff">Shirriff</a>, <a href="/wiki/Robin_Smallburrow" title="Robin Smallburrow">Robin Smallburrow</a> about it by ringing Bywater&#39;s town bell. Robin initially dismisses Lobelia and Frodo&#39;s warning, but this changes when he is attacked by a white wolf the following night, and Frodo must save him. More wolves are encountered outside Bywater, then in the <a href="/wiki/Old_Forest" title="Old Forest">Old Forest</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Barrow-downs" title="Barrow-downs">Barrow-downs</a>, and the streets of <a href="/wiki/Bree" title="Bree">Bree</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Wolves are found in nearly every region of Middle-earth. The term specifically applies to the standard variety, which are most often found wild, occasionally tamed by <a href="/wiki/Ruffians" title="Ruffians">brigands</a>, but never ridden. <a href="/wiki/Wargs" title="Wargs">Wargs</a> have their own distinct classification and appearances, and are the creatures used as mounts by <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs and Goblins</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past" title="A Shadow of the Past">first episode</a> of the first season, wolf-tracks are spotted by <a href="/wiki/Dilly_Brandyfoot" title="Dilly Brandyfoot">Dilly Brandyfoot</a>, causing <a href="/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot" title="Elanor Brandyfoot">Elanor Brandyfoot</a> to spot a wolf atop a hill and then order the <a href="/wiki/Harfoots" title="Harfoots">Harfoot</a> children back to the community&#39;s current dwelling place. In the <a href="/wiki/Adar_(episode)" title="Adar (episode)">third episode</a>, it is revealed during <a href="/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows" title="Sadoc Burrows">Sadoc Burrows&#39;s</a> speech of remembrance that one Harfoot, <a href="/wiki/Daffodil_Burrows" title="Daffodil Burrows">Daffodil Burrows</a>, was killed and taken by wolves during a previous migration.</dd></dl>
