**War Office form 22 November 1916** is a form from the service record of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_Office" title="wikipedia:War Office">War Office</a>, in which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) confirms his return to England from France due to trench fever (dated [22 November](/wiki/22_November) [1916](/wiki/1916)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
