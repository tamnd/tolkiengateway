The **White Council**, also referred to as the **Council of the Wise**, was a gathering of the [Wise](/wiki/Wise) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). At least two different Councils were known to have existed in the history of Middle-earth: one which met at least once after [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) was driven out of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1701](/wiki/Second_Age_1701 "Second Age 1701"),<sup id="cite_ref-UTConcerning_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTConcerning-5">[5]</a></sup> and another which first met in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2463](/wiki/Third_Age_2463 "Third Age 2463")<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> and went on to meet irregularly throughout the course of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). The purpose of the Council was "to unite and direct the forces of the [West](/wiki/Westlands "Westlands"), in resistance to the shadow."<sup id="cite_ref-PMThird_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMThird-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Members {#Members}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

No members are explicitly identified as being part of the first White Council during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), however it is implied that at least [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) were members as Gil-galad gave [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya) to Elrond at the Council. [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) were present in [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") at the same time as the Council, making them potential candidates as well.<sup id="cite_ref-UTConcerning_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTConcerning-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

The following individuals were clearly stated to have been members of the later iteration of the White Council during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age):

- [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), the appointed leader of the Council
- [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)
- [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), who first summoned it
- [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)
- [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)

Other lords of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) were said to have joined the Council at some point in time.<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

A "White Council" first met in c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1701](/wiki/Second_Age_1701 "Second Age 1701").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[note 1]</a></sup> At this meeting it was decided that [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") should become the stronghold of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), rather than [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion). It seems likely that the "White Council" of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) echoed this "White Council" of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-UT|Concerning_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT|Concerning-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2063](/wiki/Third_Age_2063 "Third Age 2063")<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) entered [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), thus daunting the concealed [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) into fleeing [eastwards](/wiki/East "East"), beginning the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace); although this was before the formation of the Council, the feat was attributed to them.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Angus_McBride_-_Dol_Guldur.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7a/Angus_McBride_-_Dol_Guldur.jpg/300px-Angus_McBride_-_Dol_Guldur.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="289" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/7/7a/Angus_McBride_-_Dol_Guldur.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="400" data-file-height="385"/></a><figcaption><i>Dol Guldur</i> by <a href="/wiki/Angus_McBride" title="Angus McBride">Angus McBride</a></figcaption></figure>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2463](/wiki/Third_Age_2463 "Third Age 2463"),<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> following Sauron's return to Dol Guldur and the end of the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace) in [2460](/wiki/Third_Age_2460 "Third Age 2460"),<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> Galadriel summoned the first meeting of the White Council.<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-4">[4]</a></sup>

There are only four known meetings of the White Council:<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2463](/wiki/Third_Age_2463 "Third Age 2463"): There were [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), Gandalf, Elrond, Galadriel and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), and other lords of the Eldar. It was mooted that Gandalf be the head of the Council, but to Galadriel's dismay he refused the office as he preferred his independence. Saruman was chosen as their chief instead, because of his deep knowledge on Sauron's devices, and he begrudged Gandalf for being the desired candidate.<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851"): The Council met at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-UTGandalf_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTGandalf-10">[8]</a></sup> The Grey Pilgrim urged an attack on [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) following his discovery in the [previous year](/wiki/Third_Age_2850 "Third Age 2850") that its master was indeed the returned [Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord).<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup> Gandalf also wished to simultaneously prevent a union between Sauron and [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) - the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragon) that had sacked [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) not long ago.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup> Saruman overruled him, however, because in secret he had begun to covet Sauron's power for himself.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> The White Wizard thus insisted that there was no reason to attack the Dark Lord, claiming therefore that, the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) had fallen into the Anduin, and by now it had been flown to the [Sea](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas "Bay of Belfalas").<sup id="cite_ref-repetition_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-repetition-12">[note 3]</a></sup> In reality Saruman wanted to win himself some time, knowing that the Ring would sooner or later reveal itself if it felt the growing presence of its [master](</wiki/Lord_of_the_Ring_(title)> "Lord of the Ring (title)"), Sauron; moving against him, it would make the Ring hide again.<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup> Unusually for a White Council meeting, Gandalf sat apart from the others, in silence and smoking, whilst Saruman spoke against the attack on Dol Guldur. This irritated Saruman and he spoke to Gandalf afterwards, asking him why he did not join in the discussion, and mocked his smoking. Gandalf replied that [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed"), a practice of the [Halflings](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"), gave him 'patience'. Saruman mocked him again and in response Gandalf sent out many rings of smoke into the air and grasped them in his hand before they vanished. Saruman read this gesture as suggesting that Gandalf suspected him of wanting to possess the One Ring, or that there was a connection between the rings of power and the Halflings.<sup id="cite_ref-UTGandalf_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTGandalf-10">[8]</a></sup>

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"): Gandalf said that although the One Ring was lost, its existence alone allowed Sauron to still manifest - the Enemy, who now had the [Nine Rings](/wiki/Nine_Rings) and three of the [Seven](/wiki/Seven_Rings "Seven Rings") - and repeated his call to attack Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup> Saruman finally conceded to the assault for he had been alarmed by Sauron's searching for the One Ring in the Anduin<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> and believed that thrusting him from Dol Guldur would allow him to search freely.<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup> The White Council launched an [attack on Dol Guldur](/wiki/Attack_on_Dol_Guldur "Attack on Dol Guldur"), but the Dark Lord, having already made plans, fled to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2953](/wiki/Third_Age_2953 "Third Age 2953"): Following Sauron's open declaration in [2951](/wiki/Third_Age_2951 "Third Age 2951"),<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> there was a discussion on the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power). Saruman once again repeated his claim that the One Ring had been lost down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and into the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer")<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-repetition_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-repetition-12">[note 3]</a></sup> this quieting Gandalf's worries about Bilbo's Ring. Afterwards, the Wizard retreated to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and isolated himself from the others<sup id="cite_ref-SRings_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SRings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup> and would be ensnared by the Dark Lord in [3000](/wiki/Third_Age_3000 "Third Age 3000"), becoming his power-lusting vassal.<sup id="cite_ref-AppBTA_3-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBTA-3">[3]</a></sup>

However Gandalf had already arranged for <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> to leave Rivendell during the meeting, knowing already that Saruman would hinder him out of spite and pride.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gandalf has been imprisoned and tormented by Sauron and <a href="/wiki/Keeper_of_the_Dungeons" title="Keeper of the Dungeons">his minion</a> after a failed mission to the stronghold of Dol Guldur. As Gandalf telepathically makes contact with Radagast, Galadriel arrives at the fortress to rescue him, vanquishing the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> jailor in the process. She and Gandalf are then surrounded by the spectres of the Nazgûl, but Elrond and Saruman arrive on the scene and attack them. Galadriel heals Gandalf while their companions battle the Nine. After Gandalf is swiftly taken to safety by Radagast, Sauron himself manifests before the council, forcing Galadriel to assume a dark, ethereal form in order to banish him, assisted by the power of <a href="/wiki/Nenya" title="Nenya">Nenya</a> and her <a href="/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel" title="Phial of Galadriel">phial</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Dark_Lord" title="Dark Lord">Dark Lord</a>&#39;s spirit then flees into the East, which Elrond suggests should be kept under watch by <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, who should be warned of the Enemy&#39;s return. Both awed and shaken by Sauron&#39;s display of strength, Saruman plays down on Galadriel and Elrond&#39;s worries, professedly vowing that he himself would deal with Sauron, and ultimately foreshadowing his later corruption.</dd></dl>
