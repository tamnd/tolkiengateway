**Warren Mahy** (born [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [1969](/wiki/1969)) is a designer, illustrator and sculptor from [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand). He has worked with [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop) for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), contributed to the *[Isengard](</wiki/Isengard_(LOTRRPG)> "Isengard (LOTRRPG)")* supplement by [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher) and appeared as [Halbarad](/wiki/Halbarad) on a [Decipher Card](/wiki/Decipher_Card).

For [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering), he illustrated several cards for the *[Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth")* and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit")* expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
