The **Way of Running Waters** was a street in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It connected the [Road of Pomps](../R/Road_of_Pomps.md) to the walls on the south. Along it were the [Fountains of the South](../F/Fountains_of_the_South.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
