**Warren Special Edition: The Lord of the Rings** is a picture book by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Publishing" title="wikipedia:Warren Publishing">Warren Publishing</a> as a tie-in to [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>)*. It features several interviews and short articles, as well as a retelling of the story with artwork from the movie.

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Realm of the Rings: A Legend for Our Time": an introduction into modern mythology and fantasy
- "Warriors of the Ring: Leading Characters of the Odyssey": short profiles on [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md)
- "J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings: A Fantasy Film": the story of the film with images
- "Ward of the Rings: J.R.R. Tolkien, the Mayor of Middle-earth": short biographical sketch of Tolkien
- "Zaentz Perserve Us! The Producer of The Lord of the Rings": biographical sketch and interview with [Saul Zaentz](../S/Saul_Zaentz.md)
- "Lord of the Rotoscope: Bakshi's Brainchild": biographical sketch and interview with [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md)
