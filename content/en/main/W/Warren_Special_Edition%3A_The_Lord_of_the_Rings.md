**Warren Special Edition: The Lord of the Rings** is a picture book by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Publishing" title="wikipedia:Warren Publishing">Warren Publishing</a> as a tie-in to [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)")*. It features several interviews and short articles, as well as a retelling of the story with artwork from the movie.

## Contents {#Contents}

- "Realm of the Rings: A Legend for Our Time": an introduction into modern mythology and fantasy
- "Warriors of the Ring: Leading Characters of the Odyssey": short profiles on [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)
- "J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings: A Fantasy Film": the story of the film with images
- "Ward of the Rings: J.R.R. Tolkien, the Mayor of Middle-earth": short biographical sketch of Tolkien
- "Zaentz Perserve Us! The Producer of The Lord of the Rings": biographical sketch and interview with [Saul Zaentz](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz)
- "Lord of the Rotoscope: Bakshi's Brainchild": biographical sketch and interview with [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi)
