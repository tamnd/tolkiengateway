The **Westmarch** of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") was land that lay between the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs) and the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), west of and originally outside of the Shire. The only known town in this new district was [Undertowers](/wiki/Undertowers).<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1452](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31"), the Westmarch was given to the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) as a gift from [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the request of Mayor [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Peregrin I](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") made [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) the first [Warden of Westmarch](/wiki/Warden_of_Westmarch) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1462](/wiki/Fourth_Age_41 "Fourth Age 41"). Fastred and [Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner) moved to [Undertowers](/wiki/Undertowers); many other hobbits migrated to the Westmarch.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Warden kept the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch) as an heirloom of the [Fairbairn Family](/wiki/Fairbairn_Family). It was long preserved at Undertowers and was the most important source for the history of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
