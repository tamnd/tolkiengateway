<i><b>W.H. Auden: A Legacy</b></i> is a collection of twenty-six essays on [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) covering his life and art.

[Unpublished letters](/wiki/Letters_not_published_in_%22The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien%22 "Letters not published in \"The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien\"") from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to Roger Verhulst and [Rod Jellema](/wiki/Rod_Jellema) are quoted and described by Jellema in an essay recounting Auden's withdrawal from his contract for writing a booklet on Tolkien in the Eerdmans series. The essay is titled "Auden on Tolkien: The Book that Isn't, and the House that Brought it Down".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
