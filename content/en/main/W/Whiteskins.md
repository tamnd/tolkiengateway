**Whiteskins** was a name among [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) for the [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/Riders_of_Rohan),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> whose far northern origins would have given them a much paler skin colour than the [Men](/wiki/Men) who lived around them.
