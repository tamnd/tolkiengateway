<i><b>War of Words: Soldier-Poets of the Somme</b></i> is a documentary, first aired on [BBC Two](../B/BBC.md) on [15 November](../_/15_November.md) [2014](../_/2014.md) as part of the centenary anniversary of [World War I](World_War_I.md).

The documentary features vignettes on poets who fought in the [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md): <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegfried_Sassoon" title="wikipedia:Siegfried Sassoon">Siegfried Sassoon</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Graves" title="wikipedia:Robert Graves">Robert Graves</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Jones_(artist-poet)" title="wikipedia:David Jones (artist-poet)">David Jones</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Rosenberg" title="wikipedia:Isaac Rosenberg">Isaac Rosenberg</a> and [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). Tolkien's vignette, with commentary from [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md), recounts his involvement in the battles around Thiepval Wood. It also highlights the influences the war had on the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the [Dead Marshes](../D/Dead_Marshes.md), and the eternal conflict of good versus evil.
