<i><b>War of Words: Soldier-Poets of the Somme</b></i> is a documentary, first aired on [BBC Two](/wiki/BBC "BBC") on [15 November](/wiki/15_November) [2014](/wiki/2014) as part of the centenary anniversary of [World War I](/wiki/World_War_I).

The documentary features vignettes on poets who fought in the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme): <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegfried_Sassoon" title="wikipedia:Siegfried Sassoon">Siegfried Sassoon</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Graves" title="wikipedia:Robert Graves">Robert Graves</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Jones_(artist-poet)" title="wikipedia:David Jones (artist-poet)">David Jones</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Rosenberg" title="wikipedia:Isaac Rosenberg">Isaac Rosenberg</a> and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Tolkien's vignette, with commentary from [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth), recounts his involvement in the battles around Thiepval Wood. It also highlights the influences the war had on the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes), and the eternal conflict of good versus evil.
