**William Johnson** (May [1924](/wiki/1924) – [23 September](/wiki/23_September) [2016](/wiki/2016)) was a New Zealand actor born in Hebden Bridge, England. He played [Old Noakes](/wiki/Old_Noakes) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. He only appeared in the [extended edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)").

In June [2007](/wiki/2007), Johnson and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
