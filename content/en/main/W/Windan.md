**Windan** was a term used by some [Avari](/wiki/Avari) to describe themselves. It is unknown if the name "Windan" was applied o a particular group of Avari specifically or to all Avari as a whole.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is an [Avarin](/wiki/Avarin) word meaning "the People". It derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *[kwendī](/wiki/Kwende "Kwende")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup>
