The **White Tree** can refer to any of these trees:

- [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion), the White Tree of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), one of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor).
- [Galathilion](/wiki/Galathilion), The White Tree of [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion).
- [Celeborn](</wiki/Celeborn_(White_Tree)> "Celeborn (White Tree)"), the White Tree of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).
- [Nimloth](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor"), the White Tree of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).
- **The [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor), four different seedlings.**

- It can also refer to [The White Tree Fund](/wiki/The_White_Tree_Fund), a charitable organisation.
