**WATH** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "shade".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aw%27tha&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aw&#39;tha (page does not exist)">aw&#39;tha</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[auþa, ausa](/wiki/Ausa "Ausa")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[auth](/wiki/Auth "Auth")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i>waþar, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Vasar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vasar (page does not exist)">vasar</a></i> ("a veil"); *[vasarya-](/wiki/Vasarya "Vasarya")* ("to veil")<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") ([Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin)): *watha*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[gwath](/wiki/Gwath "Gwath")*; *gwathra-* ("overshadow, dim, veil, obscure"); *[gwathren](/wiki/Gwathren "Gwathren")*; *[gwathui](/wiki/Gwathui "Gwathui")*;<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin): *[gwath](/wiki/Gwath "Gwath")*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
