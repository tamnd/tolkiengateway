**Walking Tree Publishers** is the publishing company of *[Eredain](/wiki/Eredain)*, the Swiss [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies"). It publishes the [Cormarë Series](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB_Series) and Tales of Yore Series.
