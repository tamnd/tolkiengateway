**Walking Tree Publishers** is the publishing company of *[Eredain](../E/Eredain.md)*, the Swiss [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md). It publishes the [Cormarë Series](../C/Cormarë_Series.md) and Tales of Yore Series.
