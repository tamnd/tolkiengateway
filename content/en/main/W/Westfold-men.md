**Westfold-men**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> also known as **Westfolders**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or men of Westfold<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> were [Men](../M/Men.md) who lived in the [Westfold](Westfold.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## History {#History}

Some of the original Westfold-men may have been [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) until it was given to the Rohirrim in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Those people likely built or helped build Helm's Deep which was located in Westfold.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](War_of_the_Ring.md) their commander was [Erkenbrand](../E/Erkenbrand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> They participated in the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](../B/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) and suffered many losses.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Erkenbrand then led his men to [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Some Westfold-men were already at Helm's Deep,<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> but Erkenbrand and the men under his command had not arrived by the time the battle had begun and it was feared that they has been overtaken and would not come.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Erkenbrand and his men (1000 in total) arrived in a dire hour of need during the battle led by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Westfold is a combination of "West" and fold from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) folde ("earth", "land", "country").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](../F/First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) a label called *Westfold* was placed in a western valley in the south of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) where Dunland is now located on the final published maps, but it was struck out afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> In an early draft of the chapter [Helm's Deep](<../H/Helm's_Deep_(chapter).md>) the men who served [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) were from Westfold and spoke the "Tongue of Westfold".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [Helmingas](../H/Helmingas.md)

The Index of 50th Anniversary Edition of The Lord of the Rings (edited by Wayne G. Hammond and Christina Scull) seems to interpret Helmingas and Westfold-men as somehow synonymous, and connects the two.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
