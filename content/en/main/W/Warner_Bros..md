**Warner Bros.** is a world-wide film and television American company based in Burbank, California, currently owned by Warner Bros. Discovery.

Currently, the company has the distribution rights of [almost](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md>) all licensed film adaptations of [J.R.R. Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) works.

## Subsidiaries {#Subsidiaries}

- [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md), film studio of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)
- [Warner Bros. Games](Warner_Bros._Games.md), publisher of every Middle-earth related videogame since 2010.
- [Turbine](../T/Turbine.md), former developers and publishers of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*.
- [Snowblind Studios](../S/Snowblind_Studios.md), developer of *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)*.
- [Monolith Productions](../M/Monolith_Productions.md), developer of *[Guardians of Middle-earth](../G/Guardians_of_Middle-earth.md)*, *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md)* and *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_War.md)*.
