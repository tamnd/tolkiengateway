**Warner Bros.** is a world-wide film and television American company based in Burbank, California, currently owned by Warner Bros. Discovery.

Currently, the company has the distribution rights of [almost](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_film)> "The Hobbit (1967 film)") all licensed film adaptations of [J.R.R. Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") works.

## Subsidiaries {#Subsidiaries}

- [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema), film studio of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")
- [Warner Bros. Games](/wiki/Warner_Bros._Games), publisher of every Middle-earth related videogame since 2010.
- [Turbine](/wiki/Turbine), former developers and publishers of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.
- [Snowblind Studios](/wiki/Snowblind_Studios), developer of *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)*.
- [Monolith Productions](/wiki/Monolith_Productions), developer of *[Guardians of Middle-earth](/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth)*, *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* and *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War)*.
