**Withy-weir** was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weir" title="wikipedia:Weir">weir</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> in the [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle) river above the [Windle-reach](/wiki/Windle-reach). [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) passed it during his boat-trip along the river.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
