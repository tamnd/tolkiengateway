The **Wells of Varda** were the great vats beneath the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) created by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) to store the dew that fell from [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) and [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When the Valië knew that the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves) was near, she used the light from the vats of Telperion to create renowned [stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars") and constellations.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Wells were drained by [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) in the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), and while drinking she grew to such a size that even [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") feared her.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the gold and silver light collected following the destruction of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) were collected in the great cauldrons [Kulullin and Silindrin](/wiki/Kulullin_and_Silindrin). These further collected the dew of the trees when they waxed, and from them the water was provided to refresh the trees when they waned. The watering of Laurelin was tended by Urwen ([Arien](/wiki/Arien)), a maiden of [Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na); and the watering of [Silpion](/wiki/Silpion) by [Silmo](/wiki/Silmo), a youth beloved by [Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
