**Whitwell** was a settlement in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Whitwell was located on the [Green Hills](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country "Green Hill Country"), not far from [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Paladin Took II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II), had a farm there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Whit* is a shortening of "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/white" title="wikipedia:white">white</a>", referring to the colour of the soil (presumably pertaining to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/chalk" title="wikipedia:chalk">chalk</a> in the soil of the Green Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[2]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that the name means "white spring", representing an [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *Hwitwielle*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitwell" title="wikipedia:Whitwell">Whitwell</a> is a real-life English place-name.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[2]</a></sup>
