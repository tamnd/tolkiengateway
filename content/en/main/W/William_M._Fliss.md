**William M. Fliss** is the Tolkien archivist at [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University)'s Raynor Library.

## History {#History}

In [2022](/wiki/2022), Fliss co-curated the exhibition [J.R.R. Tolkien: The Art of the Manuscript](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_The_Art_of_the_Manuscript) with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sarah_C._Schaefer&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sarah C. Schaefer (page does not exist)">Sarah C. Schaefer</a>, and co-edited the accompannying exhibition cataloge. He is currently working on an "oral history of Tolkien fandom" project, expecting to complete 6000 short interviews with Tolkien fans from around the world (one for each of the [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/Riders_of_Rohan) summoned by [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) to aid [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
