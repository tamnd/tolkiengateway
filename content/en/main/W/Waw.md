In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Waw** was an island archipelago off the southeast coast of [Lóchas Drûs](../L/Lóchas_Drûs.md), inhabited by descendants of [Wôlim](../L/Lóchas_Drûs.md#Wôl) settlers.<sup id="cite_ref-waw_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-waw-1">[1]</a></sup> The island was traditionally governed by Elder Councils. Since its rule by the Dog-king [Dwar](../T/Third_of_the_Nazgûl.md), it was also known as the Isle of Dogs.<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Wôlim of Waw were described as typically having hazel eyes and a ruddy complexion.<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

Horm was a fishing village on the island, which was later expanded into a port. The island also had a citadel named Alk Waw on its southeastern peninsula.<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Between the second and third centuries of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the island was settled by the Wôlim.<sup id="cite_ref-waw_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-waw-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1965&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1965 (page does not exist)">1965</a>, the K'prur of Hent attacked Waw. The village of Horm was burned, many people died, and most fishing boats were sank. A K'pur overseer was installed in the citadel of Alk Waw. A survivor of the attack, Dendra Dwar, returned in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1980&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1980 (page does not exist)">1980</a> to enact vengeance. Having trained the years before, he took the citadel. The K'prur besieged the citadel for two years, until Dwar overcame them in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1982&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1982 (page does not exist)">1982</a> with two-thousand warhounds. He reconquered the island, but took power instead of reconvening the Elder Councils.<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-2">[2]</a></sup>

Turning his hometown of Horm into a massive port, he had a fleet constructed. He conquered nearby islands, including Címóníemor, an Elven island to the south. Waw ultimately conquered Hent, but Dwar had seemingly disappeared, leaving his nephew Dendra Swip as the new Lord of Dogs. In truth, Dendra, now a servant of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), secretly remained in Alk Waw for 252 years. From there he pushed his heirs to more pillaging and conquest, until he had to depart to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2250&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2250 (page does not exist)">2250</a>. He briefly returned to Waw during the capture of Sauron by [Ar-Pharazon](../A/Ar-Pharazon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1051&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1051 (page does not exist)">1051</a>, a reawakened Dwar returned to Waw. By then, the island had become part of [Lóchas Drûs](../L/Lóchas_Drûs.md). He manipulated a rebellion against their Lochan overlords. The people of Waw reconquered Címóníemor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1507&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1507 (page does not exist)">1507</a>, and under Dendra Dwar II Waw finally declared itself independent again in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1617&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1617 (page does not exist)">1617</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-dwar_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwar-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
