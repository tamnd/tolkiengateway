**Wayne Gordon Hammond** (born [11 February](/wiki/11_February), [1953](/wiki/1953)) is a librarian known for his research and writings on the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He earned a Bachelor of Arts degree with Honors as an English major at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldwin%E2%80%93Wallace_College" title="wikipedia:Baldwin–Wallace College">Baldwin-Wallace College</a> in 1975 and Master of Arts in Library Science from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan" title="wikipedia:University of Michigan">University of Michigan</a> in 1976.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> From 1976 onward he worked as Assistant Librarian at the Chapin Library of Rare Books at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_College" title="wikipedia:Williams College">Williams College</a> and became its director in 2015. Hammond retired in 2022.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In 1994 Hammond married fellow Tolkien scholar [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) and the two have since collaborated on several projects. He is a regular contributor to publications of the [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society).
