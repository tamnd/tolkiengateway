**War of the Ring** is a 2004 large scale strategy board game set during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), simulating the [Free Peoples](/wiki/Free_Peoples) struggles against the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) across a large map of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). It features the art of [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

The long awaited masterpiece from three Italian inventors, di Meglio, Maggi and Nepitello. The oversized box contains no less than 204 plastic figures as well as a massive 70x100cm board. Basic and advanced rules enable a wide range of players to tackle this extraordinary game. There are editions in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch and Greek.

Artwork by the world's number one Tolkien illustrator, John Howe.

## Battles of the Third Age {#Battles_of_the_Third_Age}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/49/Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg/250px-Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="248" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/49/Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg/375px-Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/49/Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg/500px-Battles_of_the_Third_Age.jpg 2x" data-file-width="604" data-file-height="600"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

War of the Ring - Battles of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) was an expansion released in 2006, it introduces new armies and figures, including the [Ents](/wiki/Ents), [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings), Siege Towers, Catapults, [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs), [Southron](/wiki/Haradrim "Haradrim") Cavalry, and more! [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") take the field, with brand-new rules and new strategies.

You will have a chance to add these new figures to make your War of the Ring battles even more fun ... and to re-fight the battles of [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) and [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on a new gameboard, with new rules which move the game from the grand-strategy to the tactical scale and add a whole new dimension to War of the Ring!

## Editions {#Editions}

In 2011, [a second edition of the game](</wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_Second_Edition_(2011_board_game)> "War of the Ring: Second Edition (2011 board game)") was published by [Ares Games](/wiki/Ares_Games) with the same designers. It featured such refinements as making the map and cards larger and thus easier to read, improvements to miniatures with improved sculpts and rules optimizations to help balance out gameplay. The rules changes between editions were significant enough that the three expansions from the second edition are not compatible with the first one.
