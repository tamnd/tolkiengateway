**WĀ** (WAWA, WAIWA) is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "blow; sound of, noise of wind".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Waiwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Waiwa (page does not exist)">waiwa</a> &gt; vaiwa</i> ("wind")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): *[gwaew](/wiki/Gwaew "Gwaew")* ("wind")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gwau&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gwau (page does not exist)">gwau</a></i> ("wind")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root GWĀ-, with the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) derivative *wā* ("wind").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
