**WĀ** (WAWA, WAIWA) is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "blow; sound of, noise of wind".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Waiwa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Waiwa (page does not exist)">waiwa</a> &gt; vaiwa</i> ("wind")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)): *[gwaew](../G/Gwaew.md)* ("wind")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gwau&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gwau (page does not exist)">gwau</a></i> ("wind")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety397_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety397-1">[1]</a></sup>
