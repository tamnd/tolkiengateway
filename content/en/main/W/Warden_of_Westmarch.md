**Warden of [Westmarch](Westmarch.md)** was a title granted by [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Peregrin I](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), to [Fastred of Greenholm](../F/Fastred_of_Greenholm.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1462](../F/Fourth_Age_41.md). This was done at the request of Fastred's father-in-law, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), who was the [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The title was hereditary, passed by Fastred to his son [Elfstan Fairbairn](../E/Elfstan_Fairbairn.md), and associated from then on with the [Fairbairns of the Towers](../F/Fairbairn_Family~66364.md). They held the heirloom of the [Red Book](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is unknown if the Warden was just a title, or also had any offices of responsibilities. <span></span>
