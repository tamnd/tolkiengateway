**Warden of [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch)** was a title granted by [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Peregrin I](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), to [Fastred of Greenholm](/wiki/Fastred_of_Greenholm) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1462](/wiki/Fourth_Age_41 "Fourth Age 41"). This was done at the request of Fastred's father-in-law, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), who was the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The title was hereditary, passed by Fastred to his son [Elfstan Fairbairn](/wiki/Elfstan_Fairbairn), and associated from then on with the [Fairbairns of the Towers](/wiki/Fairbairn_Family "Fairbairn Family"). They held the heirloom of the [Red Book](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch "Red Book of Westmarch").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is unknown if the Warden was just a title, or also had any offices of responsibilities. <span></span>
