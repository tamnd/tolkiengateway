The **Westfarthing** was one of the four [Farthings](../F/Farthings.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Its easternmost point was the [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup> and on its western border were the [Far Downs](../F/Far_Downs.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (and later, from [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [31](../F/Fourth_Age_31.md) on, the region of [Westmarch](Westmarch.md)). Within its bounds was the "capital" of the Shire, [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md) and the ancestral homeland of the [Tooks](../T/Took_Family.md) (and thus the seat of the [Thain](../T/Thain.md)), known as [Tookland](../T/Tookland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Also, in the Westfarthing was [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), the home of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and his nephew, [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

A considerable portion of this farthing was included on the map called [A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md) so many features of this section were shown:

- Hills: A portion of the [Green Hill Country](../G/Green_Hill_Country.md), [Hobbiton Hill](../H/Hobbiton_Hill.md), [White Downs](White_Downs.md), [Far Downs](../F/Far_Downs.md)
- Lakes: [Bywater Pool](../B/Bywater_Pool.md)
- Rivers: [The Water](../T/The_Water.md), possibly a portion of the [Norbourn](../N/Norbourn.md)
- Roads: [East Road](../E/East_Road.md), [South Lane](../S/South_Lane.md)
- Swamps: [Rushock Bog](../R/Rushock_Bog.md)
- Towns: [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md), [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), [Needlehole](../N/Needlehole.md), [Nobottle](../N/Nobottle.md), [Overhill](../O/Overhill.md), [Tookbank](../T/Tookbank.md), [Tuckborough](../T/Tuckborough.md), [Waymeet](Waymeet.md), [Little Delving](../L/Little_Delving.md), [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md), [Whitwell](Whitwell.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Greenholm](../G/Greenholm.md) in the Far Downs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Perhaps the most significant event regarding Westfarthing was when [Isumbras Took I](../I/Isumbras_Took_I.md) became a [Thain](../T/Thain.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2340](../T/Third_Age_2340.md), beginning the hereditary Thainship of the Took line.<sup id="cite_ref-order_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-order-6">[6]</a></sup> Thus Westfarthing was the centre of Hobbit "authority".

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), the Unexpected Party occurred in the home of [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md)<sup id="cite_ref-third_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-7">[7]</a></sup> in the West Farthing, and from the *[Green Dragon](../G/Green_Dragon~72852.md)* in [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md), the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md) began.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> When Bilbo returned the next year he had to deal with an auction of his belongings since he had been presumed dead.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

To celebrate his 111th and [Frodo's](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) 33rd birthdays as well as his departure from the Shire, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md) Bilbo held a [Farewell Party](../F/Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-third_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-7">[7]</a></sup>

On [23 September](../_/23_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) left [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) heading for [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

Upon their return from their adventures in the south, Frodo, Sam, Pippin, and [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) returned to the Shire and in the Westfarthing they were the leaders in the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md), on [1 November](../_/1_November.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

Every seven years the [Mayor of Michel Delving](../M/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving.md) was elected at the [Free Fair](../F/Free_Fair.md) on the [White Downs](White_Downs.md) at [Lithe](../L/Lithe.md).<sup id="cite_ref-order_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-order-6">[6]</a></sup>

As Westfarthing was the centre of Hobbit authority, the dialect of the Westfarthing (in scattered parts) had retained the deferential pronoun of the proper [Westron](Westron.md) which was lost in other forms of [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) language. It was used more as a light-hearted term of endearment.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
