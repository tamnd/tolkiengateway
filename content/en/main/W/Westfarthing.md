The **Westfarthing** was one of the four [Farthings](/wiki/Farthings) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Its easternmost point was the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone)<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup> and on its western border were the [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (and later, from [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [31](/wiki/Fourth_Age_31 "Fourth Age 31") on, the region of [Westmarch](/wiki/Westmarch)). Within its bounds was the "capital" of the Shire, [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving) and the ancestral homeland of the [Tooks](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family") (and thus the seat of the [Thain](/wiki/Thain)), known as [Tookland](/wiki/Tookland).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Also, in the Westfarthing was [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), the home of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and his nephew, [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-Part_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Part-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

A considerable portion of this farthing was included on the map called [A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire) so many features of this section were shown:

- Hills: A portion of the [Green Hill Country](/wiki/Green_Hill_Country), [Hobbiton Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill), [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs), [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs)
- Lakes: [Bywater Pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool)
- Rivers: [The Water](/wiki/The_Water), possibly a portion of the [Norbourn](/wiki/Norbourn)
- Roads: [East Road](/wiki/East_Road), [South Lane](/wiki/South_Lane)
- Swamps: [Rushock Bog](/wiki/Rushock_Bog)
- Towns: [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), [Needlehole](/wiki/Needlehole), [Nobottle](/wiki/Nobottle), [Overhill](/wiki/Overhill), [Tookbank](/wiki/Tookbank), [Tuckborough](/wiki/Tuckborough), [Waymeet](/wiki/Waymeet), [Little Delving](/wiki/Little_Delving), [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving), [Whitwell](/wiki/Whitwell),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Greenholm](/wiki/Greenholm) in the Far Downs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Perhaps the most significant event regarding Westfarthing was when [Isumbras Took I](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_I) became a [Thain](/wiki/Thain) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2340](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340"), beginning the hereditary Thainship of the Took line.<sup id="cite_ref-order_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-order-6">[6]</a></sup> Thus Westfarthing was the centre of Hobbit "authority".

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), the Unexpected Party occurred in the home of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End)<sup id="cite_ref-third_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-7">[7]</a></sup> in the West Farthing, and from the *[Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon)* in [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor) began.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> When Bilbo returned the next year he had to deal with an auction of his belongings since he had been presumed dead.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

To celebrate his 111th and [Frodo's](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") 33rd birthdays as well as his departure from the Shire, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") Bilbo held a [Farewell Party](/wiki/Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-third_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-7">[7]</a></sup>

On [23 September](/wiki/23_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") left [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) heading for [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

Upon their return from their adventures in the south, Frodo, Sam, Pippin, and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") returned to the Shire and in the Westfarthing they were the leaders in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater), on [1 November](/wiki/1_November) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Culture {#Culture}

Every seven years the [Mayor of Michel Delving](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving) was elected at the [Free Fair](/wiki/Free_Fair) on the [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs) at [Lithe](/wiki/Lithe).<sup id="cite_ref-order_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-order-6">[6]</a></sup>

As Westfarthing was the centre of Hobbit authority, the dialect of the Westfarthing (in scattered parts) had retained the deferential pronoun of the proper [Westron](/wiki/Westron) which was lost in other forms of [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) language. It was used more as a light-hearted term of endearment.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
