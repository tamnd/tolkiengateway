**William O’Connor** ([21 May](/wiki/21_May) [1951](/wiki/1951) - [31 January](/wiki/31_January) [2018](/wiki/2018)) was an American author and fantasy artist.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was contracted by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create artwork for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).
