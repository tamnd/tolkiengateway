**William O’Connor** ([21 May](../_/21_May.md) [1951](../_/1951.md) - [31 January](../_/31_January.md) [2018](../_/2018.md)) was an American author and fantasy artist.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was contracted by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create artwork for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).
