The **War of the Dwarves and Orcs** was a great war fought between the two races.

## Background {#Background}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2770](../T/Third_Age_2770.md), [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) was [sacked](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md) by the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> Many of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) were rendered homeless and wandered through western [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), trying to make as best a living as they could. After some years of wandering they settled down in [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md). Among these Dwarves were [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md), formerly the [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md), his son [Thráin II](../T/Thráin.md), and his grandson [Thorin II](../T/Thorin_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

Thrór fell into despair after living many years in poverty, or perhaps the [Ring](../R/Rings_of_Power.md) he wore was ultimately working evil upon its bearer.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> Whatever the case, he decided to leave his people in [2790](../T/Third_Age_2790.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> to seek out and look upon the ancestral halls of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md). Accompanied by his friend [Nár](../N/Nár.md), they crossed the [Redhorn Pass](../R/Redhorn_Pass.md) and came down to the [East-gate of Moria](../G/Great_Gates.md) where Thrór took it upon himself to enter alone, despite the warnings of Nár who stayed behind in the dale.

Thrór was caught and slain by [Azog](../A/Azog.md) the [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)-chieftain of Moria, and after a few days Thrór's head was branded with Azog's name and the corpse was thrown out of the gate where it was found by Nár. He was left alive to serve as a messenger to the Dwarves that Azog now claimed to be the King of Moria, and to stay away.

When Nár returned to the king's son Thráin, he told him of his <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Murder_of_Thr%C3%B3r&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Murder of Thrór (page does not exist)">father&#39;s murder</a> and the Orc's warning. For seven days without eating or sleeping the new king sat, until he stood and cried "This cannot be borne!"<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

From 2790 to [2793](../T/Third_Age_2793.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md) responded to this tragedy by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mustering_of_the_Dwarves&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mustering of the Dwarves (page does not exist)">gathering an army</a>, and calling on the other Houses of the Dwarves for war.

### Early Conflicts {#Early_Conflicts}

In 2793, the Dwarven host was ready, and departed for war; assailing and sacking all the Orc-holds they could find from [Mount Gundabad](../G/Gundabad.md) in the north, to the [Gladden](../G/Gladden_River.md) in the south one by one.

Little is actually known about the six year-long war; however, it is known that most of it was fought underground, in the great mines and tunnels of the Misty Mountains, where Dwarves excelled in combat. Both sides fought without mercy, and the Dwarves are said to have acted as cruelly as the Orcs they battled.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Battle of Azanulbizar {#Battle_of_Azanulbizar}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a></b></div></div>

The war came to its climax in [2799](../T/Third_Age_2799.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> when the final battle was fought in the Dimrill Dale below the East-gate of [Moria](../M/Moria.md), the [Battle of Azanulbizar](../B/Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md), the same gate the Fellowship of the Ring would exit 220 years later. The Dwarves gained victory in this notoriously bloody encounter when reinforcements arrived late on the scene from the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md). Azog was slain by [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md).

In its aftermath, King Thráin wanted to enter reclaim the city, but the Dwarves not of Durin's folk refused, saying that the city was not their Fathers' House; they had honored Thrór's memory by fighting and that was enough. [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) also warned the king that [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) still dwelt within Khazad-dûm.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

The war was very costly for the Dwarves and Orcs: nearly half of those involved in the final battle were killed. [Náin](<../N/Náin_(son_of_Grór).md>), [Frerin](../F/Frerin.md), and [Fundin](../F/Fundin.md) were among the more notable casualties. Thráin II himself lost an eye and gained a limp, and Thorin was wounded when his shield broke and he had to use an oak branch to defend himself-hence the epithet "[Oakenshield](../O/Oakenshield.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Orcs suffered irreparable damage to their numbers by war's end; ten thousand alone were killed in the Dimrill Dale. Those that survived the final battle fled south, seeking refuge in the [White Mountains](White_Mountains.md), where they troubled the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) for two generations.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2851](../T/Third_Age_2851.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> King [Walda](Walda.md) was slain with all his companions by Orcs near [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md). His son [Folca](../F/Folca.md) hunted down and eliminated the last Orc-hold in the White Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

As a result of such losses, the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) virtually disappeared as a threat to [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [Wilderland](../R/Rhovanion.md). One and a half centuries later the Orcs of the North were recovering, but their numbers were permanently depleted to severely low levels in the aftermath of the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md) in [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup> It was here that [Bolg](../B/Bolg.md) son of Azog tried to avenge his father and the [Great Goblin](../G/Great_Goblin.md), but in his failure, three fourths of his people were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2020: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The conflict is referred to as the &#34;<i>Sixth</i> War of Dwarves and Orcs&#34;. A &#34;Fifth War of the Dwarves and Orcs&#34; is mentioned as having been triggered by the slaying of King <a href="../T/Thorin_I.md" title="Thorin I">Thorin I</a> by Orcs in the <a href="../G/Grey_Mountains.md" title="Grey Mountains">Grey Mountains</a>. The alliance of multiple Houses is known as the &#34;Gabil&#39;akkâ, the Unified Army of Dwarves&#34;, and the host assembled by Thráin in particular as the &#34;Haban&#39;akkâ&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Battle of Azanulbizar is portrayed in an extended flashback sequence in which the player assumes the role of &#34;Hermáth Stormhammer&#34;, a close friend of <a href="../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a> who composed a detailed account of the war on the King&#39;s request. All seven of the Dwarven Houses, including their kings, participate in the conflict, despite some coming from very far to the East and not having any contact with the Longbeards since the fall of <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Khazad-dûm</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>A potential seventh war, known at the time as the &#34;War of Three Peaks&#34;, begins after the downfall of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> in <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">Third Age 3019</a>, when King <a href="../T/Thorin_Stonehelm.md" title="Thorin Stonehelm">Thorin Stonehelm</a>&#39;s son Prince Durin assembles a dwarven host to reclaim <a href="../G/Gundabad.md" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>.</dd></dl>
