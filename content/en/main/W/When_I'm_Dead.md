**When I'm Dead** is a song by [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md). It is published as the 27th song in *[Songs for the Philologists](../S/Songs_for_the_Philologists.md)* in [1936](../_/1936.md).

It is presented in three languages: [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), Scots, and [Gothic](../G/Gothic.md).

## In Old English {#In_Old_English}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Þa ic beo dead.<br/>
no byrgaþ lic min,<br/>
at doð min ban<br/>
on blod-read win.<br/>
<br/>
Sete fullne belg<br/>
æt heafd&#39; and fet,<br/>
wel þæt mines lices<br/>
brosnung bett.
</p>
</div>

## In Scots {#In_Scots}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Quhan deid me tais,<br/>
O biri nocht me,<br/>
bot sett ilk a bain <br/>
in cleir clarré.<br/>
<br/>
Sett ane bottel o bouse<br/>
at mi heid and mi foit,<br/>
and in rest and in ro<br/>
everma i moit.
</p>
</div>

## In Gothic {#In_Gothic}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Gadauþna&#39; ic þan,<br/>
ni allis filh mik,<br/>
ak daupiþs in wein<br/>
fagino ik.<br/>
<br/>
Satei fairnana balg<br/>
and haubiþ mein —<br/>
ganisand auk baina<br/>
daupida, in wein.
</p>
</div>
