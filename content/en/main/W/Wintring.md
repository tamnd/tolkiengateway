**Wintring** in [Bree](/wiki/Bree) was the tenth month of the [Bree Calendar](/wiki/Bree_Calendar), corresponding to [Winterfilth](/wiki/Winterfilth) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately corresponding to modern [22 September](/wiki/22_September) through [21 October](/wiki/21_October)).<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Bree name *Wintring* means "Wintry".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) believed that Wintring was a Bree alteration of the older name.<sup id="cite_ref-cal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cal-1">[1]</a></sup>
