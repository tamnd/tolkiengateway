The **Wandering Companies** were traveling groups of [High Elves](/wiki/High_Elves) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

In the [First](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") and [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the people of Eriador included [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) from [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), who came west but never passed the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) to join [their brethren](/wiki/Laegrim "Laegrim"). In the Second Age some of these Nandor were nomadic "wandering companies", and they acknowledged [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) as "[Lord and Lady](/wiki/Lord_and_Lady) of the Eldar in Eriador", ruling from [Nenuial](/wiki/Nenuial).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In later ages wandering companies set out from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to various destinations, such as the [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) had contact with [Elves](/wiki/Elves) around Eriador, and the [Fallohides](/wiki/Fallohides) were friendlier to them than other Hobbits. Along with [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), they were a source of news from the outside world. It is possible that the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) learned the art of building directly from them. However as time passed in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), they had less contact with the Elves, growing some fear about them, and distrust for those that had dealings with them, coupled with their fear of the [Sea](/wiki/Sea).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), and later [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) were often wandering far from home, in the hills and woods at night, and they were suspected meeting Elves who seldom walked in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-4">[4]</a></sup> Indeed some Elves like [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor_Inglorion) had seen them many times while journeying, although the hobbits did not notice them.<sup id="cite_ref-three_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-5">[5]</a></sup> In the years leading to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), some [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon) traversed the Shire in <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Spring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Spring (page does not exist)">Spring</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Autumn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Autumn (page does not exist)">Autumn</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-three_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-5">[5]</a></sup> while others passed on their way to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens), leaving [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-shadow_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shadow-4">[4]</a></sup>

One such Wandering Company, led by [Gildor Inglorion](/wiki/Gildor "Gildor"), having gazed in the *[Palantír](/wiki/Elendil%27s_Stone "Elendil's Stone")* of the [White Towers](/wiki/White_Towers), seeking a glimpse of [Elbereth](/wiki/Varda "Varda"), were returning to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). On [24 September](/wiki/24_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), their chants drove away a [Ringwraith](/wiki/Ringwraith), and soon were encountered by Frodo, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).

Gildor promised to inform other Wandering Companies of Frodo's journey, so that those who could, should help him.<sup id="cite_ref-three_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Customs {#Customs}

During their wandering the Elves were silent and could move unseen, and seldom took other travellers with them.

At nights they camped in places around Eriador. One such place was a hill in [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End), above [Woodhall](/wiki/Woodhall) (eighteen miles west of the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry)<sup id="cite_ref-short_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-6">[6]</a></sup>); a greensward and a natural opening like a "hall" formed by the trees as pillars. They sang watching the stars rising, especially [Menelvagor](/wiki/Menelvagor), which was a sign for merriment. Then they lit a wood-fire, and torches with lights of gold and silver set upon the trees, and feasted sitting on the grass or old sawn trunks. They shared cool gold-coloured and fragrant draughts; and dishes heaped with food, more than a hobbit's birthday feast (although just a small fare compared to how they treated guests in their halls), and fruits sweeter and richer than those of the Shire gardens.<sup id="cite_ref-three_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-three-5">[5]</a></sup> They rested on fragrant beds made of fern and grass, set on bowers made by laced tree-branches.<sup id="cite_ref-short_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-short-6">[6]</a></sup>
