**Westernesse** was a name in the [Common Speech](Westron.md) for the [Isle of Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The ending *-ess* (also in [Elvenesse](../E/Elvenesse.md)) was used in romance literature for fictional lands that had partly francized names (as in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyonesse" title="wikipedia:Lyonesse">Lyonesse</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matter_of_Britain" title="wikipedia:Matter of Britain">Arthurian legends</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the name is Middle English, rare and of vague meaning, taken to mean "Western lands" (in contrast to the [East](../E/East.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the 13th century chivalric romance <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Horn" title="wikipedia:King Horn">King Horn</a></i>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westernesse" title="wikipedia:Westernesse">Westernesse</a> is a kingdom ruled by "King Ailmar" and it is reached by ship by Horn.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
