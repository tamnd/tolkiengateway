**Walda** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2780](/wiki/Third_Age_2780 "Third Age 2780") – [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851"), aged 71 years) was the twelfth [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Walda_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walda-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Walda was the son of [Brytta Léofa](/wiki/Brytta "Brytta") and was born in a time when his country was still plagued by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) who had invaded Rohan after the [War of Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_Dwarves_and_Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-Brytta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brytta-2">[2]</a></sup>

He became king after the death of his father in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2842](/wiki/Third_Age_2842 "Third Age 2842"). At the time of his father's death it was believed Rohan had been freed of the Orcs. However, some Orcs remained, and in [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851") after ruling just nine years he and all his companions were killed by Orcs when they rode from [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow) on mountain paths.<sup id="cite_ref-Walda_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walda-1">[1]</a></sup>

Walda was buried in the third mound on the east-side of the Barrow-field<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> on the left side of the road that approached Edoras<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> from the north-west.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He was succeeded and avenged by his son [Folca](/wiki/Folca) who cleared Rohan of all Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-Folca_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folca-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Walda* is derived from the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name *wealda*, which means "ruler".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

His death date is recorded as [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851")<sup id="cite_ref-Walda_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walda-1">[1]</a></sup> in the list of the Kings of the Mark in [Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A), but as as [2861](/wiki/Third_Age_2861 "Third Age 2861") in The Tale of Years of the Third Age in [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The death date [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2851](/wiki/Third_Age_2851 "Third Age 2851") is probably correct, because the entry for king Walda in the list of Kings of the Mark mentions that he "was king only nine years"<sup id="cite_ref-Walda_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walda-1">[1]</a></sup> and the entry for his father mentions that his father died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2842](/wiki/Third_Age_2842 "Third Age 2842").<sup id="cite_ref-Brytta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brytta-2">[2]</a></sup>

There was no note about the events during the life of Walda in the list of Kings of the Mark in the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
