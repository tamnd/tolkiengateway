In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Hathor** was a kingdom in the [Mûmakan](/wiki/M%C3%BBmakan), founded sometime after [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_100&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 100 (page does not exist)">100</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup> Its monarch ruled from the capital city Tanith.<sup id="cite_ref-hathor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hathor-2">[2]</a></sup> Its inhabitants had blond hair and blue eyes, with fair skin tanned by the sun, leading scholars to believe they migrated from elsewhere. Their deep fear of the open sea meant that this likely happened over land.<sup id="cite_ref-hathorians_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hathorians-3">[3]</a></sup>

On 8 February [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1703&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1703 (page does not exist)">1703</a>, the King of Hathor was assassinated by the Winds of Taurang, a group which acted on behalf of the [Court of Ardor](/wiki/Court_of_Ardor).<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
