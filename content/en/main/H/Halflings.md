**Halflings** ([S](../S/Sindarin.md). <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Periannath.md" title="Periannath">Periannath</a></b></i>) was a name for [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) used by [Men](../M/Men.md); it was originally given to them by the tall [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) who had stood two [rangar](../R/Ranga.md) tall, making the average Hobbit about half their height. The term first applied to the [Harfoots](Harfoots.md) who became known in [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md) and later to the [Fallohides](../F/Fallohides.md) and the [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md).<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name reached the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md), which they learned from their [northern cousins](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) although they never had seen a halfling in person until the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word halfling appeared in the final line of the [rhyme](../S/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken.md) from [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) and [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md)'s dreams, "*And the Halfling forth shall stand.*" Boromir claimed to have understood little of the rhyme, but recognized [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) as the Halfling when he stood up with [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, by that time the Gondorians were shorter than their ancestors since the line of the Kings ended, and the halflings were more than half of their height.<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup>

<br> It became known that a halfling bore the Ring, and [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) gave orders to the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) to capture any halflings alive and bring them back without plundering them. This led to the capture of [Pippin](../P/Pippin.md) and [Merry](../M/Merry.md), who were halflings, although neither of them was the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) became the first halfling who visited [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md),<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup> whom the Gondorians recognised as a "[Prince of the Halflings](../E/Ernil_i_Pheriannath.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name "Halfling" is a translation from the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) *[banakil](../B/Banakil.md)* which means "half-man". It was translated into both [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) as **[Perian](../P/Perian.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
