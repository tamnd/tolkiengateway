**Halflings** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Periannath" title="Periannath">Periannath</a></b></i>) was a name for [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) used by [Men](/wiki/Men); it was originally given to them by the tall [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) who had stood two [rangar](/wiki/Ranga "Ranga") tall, making the average Hobbit about half their height. The term first applied to the [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) who became known in [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050") and later to the [Fallohides](/wiki/Fallohides) and the [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors).<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name reached the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians), which they learned from their [northern cousins](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor "Dúnedain of Arnor") although they never had seen a halfling in person until the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word halfling appeared in the final line of the [rhyme](/wiki/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken "Seek for the Sword that was broken") from [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) and [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)'s dreams, "*And the Halfling forth shall stand.*" Boromir claimed to have understood little of the rhyme, but recognized [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) as the Halfling when he stood up with [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, by that time the Gondorians were shorter than their ancestors since the line of the Kings ended, and the halflings were more than half of their height.<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup>

<br> It became known that a halfling bore the Ring, and [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) gave orders to the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) to capture any halflings alive and bring them back without plundering them. This led to the capture of [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) and [Merry](/wiki/Merry), who were halflings, although neither of them was the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) became the first halfling who visited [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith),<sup id="cite_ref-linear_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-linear-1">[1]</a></sup> whom the Gondorians recognised as a "[Prince of the Halflings](/wiki/Ernil_i_Pheriannath "Ernil i Pheriannath")".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name "Halfling" is a translation from the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) *[banakil](/wiki/Banakil "Banakil")* which means "half-man". It was translated into both [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) as **[Perian](/wiki/Perian)**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
