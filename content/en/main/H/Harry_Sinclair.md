New Zealand director, actor and musician **Harry Sinclair** played [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*. He is a friend of [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson), and was offered the role because he was the most corrupt-looking person Jackson knew. He had only one line, but because he did not sound as corrupt, it was dubbed by [Hugo Weaving](/wiki/Hugo_Weaving).
