<i><b>helke</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "ice".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested the spelling *helcë*, to conform with the Quenya spelling used in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KHELEK](/wiki/KHELEK) ("ice")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[helka](/wiki/Helka "Helka")* ("ice-cold")
