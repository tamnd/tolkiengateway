**Hob "Old Gammidgy" Gammidge** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hob was the son of [Wiseman Gamwich](/wiki/Wiseman_Gamwich). He made his living as a roper in [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> a tradition which was passed along to his son [Hobson](/wiki/Hobson) and his grandson, [Andy](/wiki/Andwise_Roper "Andwise Roper"), who adopted the surname [Roper](/wiki/Roper_family "Roper family").

Hob was married to [Rowan](/wiki/Rowan), the daughter of [Holman the Greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded "Holman the greenhanded"). Their son Hobson was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1285](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
