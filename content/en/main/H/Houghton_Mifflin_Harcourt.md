**Houghton Mifflin Harcourt**, until 2007 known as **Houghton Mifflin Company**, long held the rights for publishing works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in the United States. [Harper Collins](/wiki/Harper_Collins) (the European publisher) eventually acquired it.

In the late summer of [1965](/wiki/1965) Tolkien sent revisions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* to Houghton Mifflin in order to renew the legal copyright in the United States. The second edition was released in [1967](/wiki/1967).<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1987](/wiki/1987) the company was encouraged by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) to synchronise its standard ([1966](/wiki/1966)) edition of *The Lord of the Rings* with the standard British edition of [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin), and incorporate additional corrections.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>

After around [1994](/wiki/1994) the editions of *The Lord of the Rings* had some new errors introduced by the digitalization process by HarperCollins; some of them had been subsequently corrected, and published by HM in [2004](/wiki/2004).<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [16 January](/wiki/16_January) [2001](/wiki/2001), Houghton Mifflin <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkien-movies.com/words/2001/01-16-01e.shtml" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">announced</a> they had acquired rights to publish books based on [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). The company combined with parts of **Harcourt Brace** in 2007.

In [2021](/wiki/2021) HarperCollins purchased the trade book divisions of HMH (including the American Tolkien rights) and in 2022 the remainder of the company was purchased by Veritas Capital. New printings of hardcover and trade paperback editions of Tolkien works in America transitioned to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=William_Morrow&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="William Morrow (page does not exist)">William Morrow</a>, an imprint of HarperCollins, in 2022. Some Houghton Mifflin copies remain on bookstore shelves until exhausted.

## Publication history {#Publication_history}

- [21 October](/wiki/21_October) [1954](/wiki/1954) - *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*
- [21 April](/wiki/21_April) [1955](/wiki/1955) - *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*
- [5 January](/wiki/5_January) [1956](/wiki/1956) - *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*
- [1966](/wiki/1966) - Standard edition of *The Lord of the Rings*, remaining unchanged thereafter.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [1967](/wiki/1967) - Second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [1987](/wiki/1987) - Corrected edition of *The Lord of the Rings*, synchronized with the British edition.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [2004](/wiki/2004) - Edition including some corrections introduced by [Harper Collins](/wiki/Harper_Collins) ten years earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [17 April](/wiki/17_April) [2007](/wiki/2007) - *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*
- [16 May](/wiki/16_May) [2007](/wiki/2007) - [*The Children of Húrin*: Deluxe Edition](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin:_Deluxe_Edition "The Children of Húrin: Deluxe Edition")
