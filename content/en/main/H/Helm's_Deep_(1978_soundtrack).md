<i><b>Helm&#39;s Deep</b></i> is sixteenth track of [*The Lord Of The Rings - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack*](<../T/The_Lord_Of_The_Rings_(Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack).md>). The track features diagetic chanting from the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md). It contains the soundtrack's main motif.
