**Holman "Long Hom" Cotton** was a Hobbit of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).

## History {#History}

Holman was the son of [Cotman](/wiki/Cotman). He moved to [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater), and took up the family name "[Cotton](/wiki/Cotton_Family "Cotton Family")". He became a farmer, which passed to his son, [Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior"). He also had another son, [Will](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_I "Wilcome Cotton I").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Holman means "hole-man", and is distinct from the real-life English surname<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> which contains *holm* "river island" rather than *hol* "hole".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
