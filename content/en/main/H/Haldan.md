**Haldan** was the son of [Haldar](/wiki/Haldar), grandson of [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad), and nephew to [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth) herself, who went to [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) where she settled her People. Haleth left no heir of her own, and so as grandson of Haldad her father, Haldan took up the [lordship](/wiki/Lord_of_Brethil "Lord of Brethil") of the [Men of Brethil](/wiki/Men_of_Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
