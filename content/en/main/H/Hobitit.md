**Hobitit** (Finnish: **Hobbits**) was a Finnish TV mini series based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* which aired in [1993](/wiki/1993) for 9 episodes on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YLE" title="wikipedia:YLE">YLE</a>.

The series was written and directed by Timo Torikka. Toni Edelmann composed the soundtrack.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> *Hobitit* featured nine episodes of 30 minutes runtime that were aired from 29 March to 24 May 1993. Filming locations included the *Ryhmäteatteri* theatre in Helsinki and Yle's production facilities.

2. Road ("Tie")
3. Old Forest ("Vanha metsä")
4. Prancing Pony ("Pomppiva poni")
5. Strider ("Konkari")
6. Lórien
7. Mordor
8. Mount Doom ("Tuomiovuori")
9. Liberation ("Vapautus")

## Plot {#Plot}

The series follows the story of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) as they head for Mount Doom to destroy the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring). Most of the other story - the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) raging in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) - has been omitted.
