<i><b>hyarmen</b></i> is a noun meaning "south" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Hyarmen.gif"><img src="/w/images/1/14/Hyarmen.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Hyarmen* is also the name in Quenya of the thirty-third letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> In origin it was a variation on *[Harma](/wiki/Harma)* (letter 11) and represented **HY**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but by the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) its most frequently represents **H**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was one of the most widely known and used of all the Tengwar since it indicated "south" even in languages that used a very different term.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Há</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<br>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the West-lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) the four cardinal directions were *[Númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen), Hyarmen, [Rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men),* and *[Formen](/wiki/Formen)*, and they were named in this order, beginning with (and facing) west. *Hyarmen* meant a left-hand region and *Formen* meant a right-hand region.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus the word for "south" derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KHYAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHYAR (page does not exist)">KHYAR</a>, meaning "left hand", plus [MEN](/wiki/MEN) indicating direction.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
