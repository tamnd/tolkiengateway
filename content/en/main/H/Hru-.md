<i><b>hru-</b></i>, <i><b>hrú-</b></i> or <i><b>ru-</b></i> (from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SRUGU&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SRUGU (page does not exist)">SRUGU</a>) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) prefix meaning evil.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> An example would be **hrúcare** = "evil-doing".
