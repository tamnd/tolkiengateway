**Horn of Gondor** is a [fan film](/wiki/Category:Fan_films "Category:Fan films") based on [a short story](/wiki/Cirion_and_Eorl_and_the_Friendship_of_Gondor_and_Rohan "Cirion and Eorl and the Friendship of Gondor and Rohan") in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*. The film was made available on YouTube on [18 September](/wiki/18_September) [2020](/wiki/2020).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

After the invasion of the fierce hordes of [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth), [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) is on the brink of destruction. The [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) places his hope on the half-forgotten nation of the Horselords in the north. Will the knight [Borondir](/wiki/Borondir) succeed and convey Cirion's message?
