**Helluin** was the name in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for the blue star known today as <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sirius" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Sirius</a>, the "Dog Star".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The second element in *Helluin* is most probably [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[luinë](/wiki/Luin%C3%AB "Luinë")* ("blue").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It's possible that the first element refers to "ice" (see: *[Helcar](/wiki/Helcar)*) in which case the name means "ice-blue".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the star was called <i><b>Nielluin</b></i> ("Azure Bee" or "Blue Bee") in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Another Qenya variants were *Nierninwa* and *Niellúnë*, and its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name was <i><b>Niothluimi</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
