**Hemlocks** were a type of wildflower<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> strongly associated with the meeting of [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md), most famously in the [poem](../S/Song_of_Beren_and_Lúthien.md) sung by [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

They also grew in the Old Forest.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tolkien's choice for hemlocks in the story of Beren and Lúthien was based on a time during [World War I](../W/World_War_I.md), when he was stationed at Yorkshire. Near the village of Roos there was was a woodland glade filled with hemlocks.<sup id="cite_ref-flora_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flora-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

There is debate around the exact type of hemlock that inspired Tolkien, since while the plant most commonly known as hemlock (*Conium maculatum*) matches the description, its unpleasant odours possibly don't.<sup id="cite_ref-flora_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-flora-4">[3]</a></sup> [The Plants of Middle-earth](../T/The_Plants_of_Middle-earth.md) posits that it is cow parsley (*Anthriscus sylvestris*) <sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>, which [Tolkien and the Great War](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War.md) agrees with. In any case, according to his son Christopher, Tolkien himself “regarded the restriction of a vernacular name to this or that species within a large group of plants not easily distinguishable to the eye as the pedantry of popularizing botanists—who ought to content themselves with the Linnean names.”<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1984-1997: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i></b>

<dl><dd><i><b>Water Hemlocks</b></i> (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Ulucthond</i>, <a href="../Q/Quenya.md" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Ulcasunda</i>) were a type of hemlock described in the game&#39;s books, which grew in Wetlands. They were compared to parsnips, having a tuber like it and turnips, though this one was poisonous. The juice from its root could be mixed with its antidote, the curoloth flower, to make a powerful poison to apply on blades, fittingly called <i>blade hemlock</i>. <sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>
