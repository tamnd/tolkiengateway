**HAN** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "add to, increase, enhance, honour (espec. by gift)".<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *han* ("beyond")<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup>

Other possible derivatives are Quenya *[hantalë](Hantalë.md)* and \**[hanta-](Hantalë.md#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-1">[1]</a></sup>
