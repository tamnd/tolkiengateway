**Hirluin the Fair**<sup id="cite_ref-MT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-2">[2]</a></sup> or **Hirluin of the Green Hills**<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-3">[3]</a></sup> was the leader of the troops from [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin), the Green Hills, that came to aid [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

He was one of the many leaders of Men who brought companies to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith); in his contingent were three hundred gallant green-clad men.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-2">[2]</a></sup> A week later, he fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), rushing to the aid of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), in the van with [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil) of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), [Húrin of the Keys](</wiki/H%C3%BArin_(Warden_of_the_Keys)> "Húrin (Warden of the Keys)") and [Forlong of Lossarnach](/wiki/Forlong "Forlong").<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-3">[3]</a></sup> He was slain on the fields before the City.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/48/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg/150px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="264" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/48/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg/225px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/48/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Hirluin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="580" data-file-height="1020"/></a><figcaption>Hirluin in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></figcaption></figure>

**Hirluin** is probably a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Blue Lord". [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that it is composed of the two elements *[hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* ("lord", "master") and *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")* ("blue").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Hirluin is first met in <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> shortly before the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, drinking in a tavern and remembering the days of their youth with his good friend <a href="/wiki/Forlong" title="Forlong">Forlong</a>. Later, both of them lead the defence of the White City, fighting on the wall alongside other defenders. When the Rohirrim army arrives, both of them rush outside the city gates and faces their foes together before Hirluin is slain before his friend.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Hirluin&#39;s seat in <a href="/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin" title="Pinnath Gelin">Pinnath Gelin</a> is called <i>Ost Arndír</i> and has been inherited by his daughter Lady <i>Nauriel</i>, whose consort is the brother of Lord <a href="/wiki/Duinhir" title="Duinhir">Duinhir</a>. Hirluin&#39;s young grandson is named <a href="/wiki/Thorongil" title="Thorongil">Thorongil</a>, after <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">a great hero</a> with whom Hirluin fought side-by-side in <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbar</a>.</dd></dl>
