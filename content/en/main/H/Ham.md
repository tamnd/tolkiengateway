**Ham** was the village where [Farmer Giles](../F/Farmer_Giles.md) lived. It sat some twenty leagues from the capital of the [Middle Kingdom](../M/Middle_Kingdom.md). Besides the redoubtable farmer and his wife [Agatha](../A/Agatha.md), notable people of the village were the parson, the miller, and [Fabricius Cunctator](../F/Fabricius_Cunctator.md) the blacksmith. In the middle of town was a church where the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md#Other_fiction) [Chrysophylax](../C/Chrysophylax.md) surrendered to Giles. There was a bridge over the river on one side of the village, where Giles met King [Augustus Bonifacius](../A/Augustus_Bonifacius.md) and where the [Battle of the Bridge](../B/Battle_of_the_Bridge.md) was "fought".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
