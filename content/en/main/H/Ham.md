**Ham** was the village where [Farmer Giles](/wiki/Farmer_Giles) lived. It sat some twenty leagues from the capital of the [Middle Kingdom](/wiki/Middle_Kingdom). Besides the redoubtable farmer and his wife [Agatha](/wiki/Agatha), notable people of the village were the parson, the miller, and [Fabricius Cunctator](/wiki/Fabricius_Cunctator) the blacksmith. In the middle of town was a church where the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons#Other_fiction "Dragons") [Chrysophylax](/wiki/Chrysophylax) surrendered to Giles. There was a bridge over the river on one side of the village, where Giles met King [Augustus Bonifacius](/wiki/Augustus_Bonifacius) and where the [Battle of the Bridge](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Bridge) was "fought".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
