**Heden**, "the leather and fur clad", was the father of [Eoh](/wiki/Eoh) and [Beorn](</wiki/Beorn_(son_of_Heden)> "Beorn (son of Heden)"), and a grandfather of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

He was said to be a descendant of the god <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odin" title="wikipedia:Odin">Wóden</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Hed_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hed-1">[1]</a></sup>
