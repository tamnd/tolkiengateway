**Hanstovánen**, formerly **Amnos**, was the beaching place of the ship [Mornië](</wiki/Morni%C3%AB_(ship)> "Mornië (ship)") in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

Mornië, the ship that ferried the dead, was often beached at Amnos, a northern harbour at the end of the path that led there from [Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos"). As the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) travelled along the coasts of [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar) during their [exile](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), they came to Amnos before they reached the [Helkaraksë](/wiki/Helkaraks%C3%AB). A servant of [Vefántur](/wiki/Vef%C3%A1ntur) saw them there and asked that they turn back. When they refused, he stood on a tall rock and spoke the [Prophecies of Amnos](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos "Doom of Mandos"), which warned of hardships to come. The prophecy was named for Amnos, as that was the place where it was spoken. Thus, this place was later called *Hanstovánen*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

*Hanstovánen* was also written as *Mornielta* and *Vane Hansto*, but some of these were struck out, and *Hanstovánen* appears to be the final form, being the one used in the narrative.<sup id="cite_ref-notes_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:170</sup>

The name *Amnos* was emended from *Emnon*, which was an emendation from *Morniento*.<sup id="cite_ref-notes_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:170</sup> In the following Lost Tale, the location is referred as the strands of <i><b>Amnor</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

A prophecy by [Amnon](/wiki/Amnon) referenced by [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in *[The Tale of the Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")* resembled the Prophecies of Amnos, but here Amnon was a prophet rather than a location. A few other notes for Lost Tales mention a Prophecy of Amnon, but these do not specify whether Amnon was a person or place.<sup id="cite_ref-notes_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:172</sup>

In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the [Doom of the Noldor](/wiki/Doom_of_the_Noldor) is given in the lands of [Araman](/wiki/Araman).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
