<i><b>hoth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "host, horde (nearly always in a bad sense)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) (an early version of Sindarin) the word *hoth* means "host, crowd" and is "frequent in people-names". It derives from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *khotsē* "assembly" ([root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KHOTH&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHOTH (page does not exist)">KHOTH-</a> "gather").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
