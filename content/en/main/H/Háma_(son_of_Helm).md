**Háma** was the second son of [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm "Helm").

## History {#History}

When the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) invaded [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), Háma took refuge in [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) with his father. During the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter) Helm was besieged, the Rohirrim's food ran low, and the people began to starve. After [Yule](/wiki/Yule), Háma led a group out of the fortress on a sortie to find food, but they got lost in a snow storm and never returned.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Háma in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) means "of home".
