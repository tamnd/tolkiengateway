**Hardang** was a [Chieftain of the Haladin](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## History {#History}

He descended from [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir) of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth), and was the son of [Hundad](/wiki/Hundad) son of [Hundar](/wiki/Hundar).<sup id="cite_ref-Hardang_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hardang-1">[1]</a></sup> After the death of the Chieftain of Brethil, [Handir](/wiki/Handir), Hardang tried to have his cousin, [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir) dismissed of the throne in favor of himself. Many believed Brandir was unfit to rule and supported Hardang.

After the death of Brandir a new Chieftain had to be elected. As Brandir had no children the [Men of Brethil](/wiki/Men_of_Brethil) wished for his cousin [Manthor](/wiki/Manthor) to take up the line, however they were connected by a daughter, and thus Hardang won the election. Hardang promoted Manthor to become the Captain of the Guards at the [Crossings of Taeglin](/wiki/Crossings_of_Taeglin).

Within two years of Hardang's rule, rumors of the return of [Húrin Thalion](/wiki/H%C3%BArin "Húrin") began spreading. Hardang disliked the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) for he had no blood relation, and upon Húrin's return, at the [Folkmoot](/wiki/Folkmoot_for_Judgement "Folkmoot for Judgement"), Manthor defended Húrin against Hardang.

The Folk of Brethil were angered by Hardang's actions and burned the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hall_of_the_Chieftains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall of the Chieftains (page does not exist)">Hall of the Chieftains</a>. Hardang attempted to escape but was hit with a spear in his back and he perished.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the story, Hardang's name was Harathor, and it was he, not Manthor, who was the brother of Túrin's companion Hunthor.
