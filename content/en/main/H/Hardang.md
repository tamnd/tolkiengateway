**Hardang** was a [Chieftain of the Haladin](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

He descended from [Halmir](Halmir.md) of the [House of Haleth](House_of_Haleth.md), and was the son of [Hundad](Hundad.md) son of [Hundar](Hundar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hardang_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hardang-1">[1]</a></sup> After the death of the Chieftain of Brethil, [Handir](Handir.md), Hardang tried to have his cousin, [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md) dismissed of the throne in favor of himself. Many believed Brandir was unfit to rule and supported Hardang.

After the death of Brandir a new Chieftain had to be elected. As Brandir had no children the [Men of Brethil](../M/Men_of_Brethil.md) wished for his cousin [Manthor](../M/Manthor.md) to take up the line, however they were connected by a daughter, and thus Hardang won the election. Hardang promoted Manthor to become the Captain of the Guards at the [Crossings of Taeglin](../C/Crossings_of_Taeglin.md).

Within two years of Hardang's rule, rumors of the return of [Húrin Thalion](Húrin.md) began spreading. Hardang disliked the [House of Hador](House_of_Hador.md) for he had no blood relation, and upon Húrin's return, at the [Folkmoot](../F/Folkmoot_for_Judgement.md), Manthor defended Húrin against Hardang.

The Folk of Brethil were angered by Hardang's actions and burned the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hall_of_the_Chieftains&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall of the Chieftains (page does not exist)">Hall of the Chieftains</a>. Hardang attempted to escape but was hit with a spear in his back and he perished.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the story, Hardang's name was Harathor, and it was he, not Manthor, who was the brother of Túrin's companion Hunthor.
