The **Hornrock** was a black spur of rock that stood out southward from the northern rock-wall of [Helm's Deep](Helm's_Deep.md). It was on the crest of the Hornrock that the castle of the [Hornburg](Hornburg.md) was built, and from it the [Deeping Wall](../D/Deeping_Wall.md) ran across the Deep's mouth to the southern wall.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
