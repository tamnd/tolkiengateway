The **"Hound of Sauron"** was the name given by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to one of the leaders of the [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs) that attacked the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship) in [Hollin](/wiki/Hollin).

## History {#History}

On [13 January](/wiki/13_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), after Gandalf confronted him, the wargs under him leapt upon the Fellowship with their leader. While the so called "Hound of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)" was killed, it is unclear who he was killed by as, during the attack, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) stabbed “one huge leader”, while [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) decapitated another leader, and “the heart of a great wolf-chieftain” was pierced by [Legolas's](/wiki/Legolas "Legolas") last arrow, kindled by fire.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
