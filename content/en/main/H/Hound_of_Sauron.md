The **"Hound of Sauron"** was the name given by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to one of the leaders of the [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md) that attacked the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship.md) in [Hollin](Hollin.md).

## History {#History}

On [13 January](../_/13_January.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), after Gandalf confronted him, the wargs under him leapt upon the Fellowship with their leader. While the so called "Hound of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)" was killed, it is unclear who he was killed by as, during the attack, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) stabbed “one huge leader”, while [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) decapitated another leader, and “the heart of a great wolf-chieftain” was pierced by [Legolas's](../L/Legolas.md) last arrow, kindled by fire.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
