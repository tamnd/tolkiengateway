The **House of the Lord** was a place of evil worship in the land of [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien).

## History {#History}

According to the *[Tale of Adanel](/wiki/Tale_of_Adanel)* that was told to [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth), the House of the Lord was constructed under the orders of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) shortly after the awakening of [Men](/wiki/Men), whom he had in fair form seduced into his servitude with promises of great knowledge and wealth.

The House was said to have been great in size, having a high seat where Morgoth would sit in the times when he visited Hildórien or appeared unto them from afar. Men would come into the House to pray to Morgoth and to offer gifts. Morgoth would speak from afar to those who prayed in the House, giving them commands to do his bidding. To the cruelest and strongest men who came most often to the House, Morgoth gave them great gifts.

From the House of the Lord, all of Mankind at one point had pledged their service to Morgoth, thus bringing about the [Fall of Man](/wiki/Darkness "Darkness"). When [some men](/wiki/Edain "Edain") began to reject Morgoth's rule, his servants would take them into the House of the Lord to be sacrificed in fire.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is unknown what became of the House of the Lord after Morgoth left.
