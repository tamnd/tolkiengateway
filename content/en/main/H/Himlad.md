**Himlad** was a cold land in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand), a plain lying between the Rivers [Aros](/wiki/Aros) and [Celon](/wiki/Celon), right below [Himring](/wiki/Himring); it communicated with [Lothlann](/wiki/Lothlann) through the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon) to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Himlad rose to a swelling highland at its centre, approximately 300 feet high at its flat top. It often felt the chill northern airs through Aglon. It had no trees except in its southern part near the rivers. It was from these features that its name was derived.<sup id="cite_ref-maeglin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

This region was not inhabited by Sindar before the coming of the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-maeglin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) it was held by [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin), two of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor), who guarded the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Their dwelling was located atop a low hill at the south eastern corner of the Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Dwelling_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwelling-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name comes from *[him](/wiki/Him "Him")* "cool"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and *[lad](/wiki/Lad "Lad")* "plain".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the draft of the [Of Maeglin](</wiki/Maeglin_(chapter)> "Maeglin (chapter)") chapter, the dwelling of Celegorm and Curufin was located at the south western corner of the Pass. However, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) Believed that "*this was a slip of the pen*".<sup id="cite_ref-Dwelling_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwelling-4">[4]</a></sup>
