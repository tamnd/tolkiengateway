The **Headstrong**s were a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") family, probably well-to-do,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> although they were not included in Tolkien's list of wealthier Hobbit families.<sup id="cite_ref-L|25_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L|25-2">[2]</a></sup> The only known member of the family, [Malva Headstrong](/wiki/Malva_Headstrong), married [Gormadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gormadoc_Brandybuck), a notable [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
