**Henry Gee** is a biologist, palaeontologist, and author of *[The Science of Middle-earth](</wiki/The_Science_of_Middle-earth_(2004_book)> "The Science of Middle-earth (2004 book)")*.

## About {#About}

Henry Gee was awarded a BA in zoology and genetics from the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in [1984](/wiki/1984) before going on to complete a PhD in zoology at the [University of Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge "Cambridge") in [1991](/wiki/1991);<sup id="cite_ref-about_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-about-1">[1]</a></sup> before finishing his doctorate, he joined the staff of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature(journal)" title="wikipedia:Nature(journal)">Nature</a></i> in [1987](/wiki/1987) where he is now a senior editor of biological sciences.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> At *Nature* he devised and edits the "Futures" science-fiction series for which he won the Best Publisher award from the <a class="external text" href="https://www.esfs.info/esfs-awards/2000-2009/5" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">European Science Fiction Society</a> in [2005](/wiki/2005).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Aside from his work in biology, Gee has gained notoriety in the Tolkien community as the author of *[The Science of Middle-earth](</wiki/The_Science_of_Middle-earth_(2004_book)> "The Science of Middle-earth (2004 book)")*, the editor of *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")* from [issues 45-54](</wiki/Category:Mallorn_(journal)> "Category:Mallorn (journal)"), and the special correspondent on science matters - under the pseudonym of "[Olog-Hai](/wiki/Olog-hai "Olog-hai")"<sup id="cite_ref-about_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-about-1">[1]</a></sup> - at <a class="external text" href="http://www.theonering.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TheOneRing.net</a>.
