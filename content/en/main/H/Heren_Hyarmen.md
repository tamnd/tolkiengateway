**Heren Hyarmen** (**Order of the South**) is the first official [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md)-affiliated Smial in Latin America. It was founded on [19 March](../_/19_March.md) [1989](../_/1989.md) by [Ronald Kyrmse](../R/Ronald_Kyrmse.md), in São Paulo, Brazil. The name was changed on [9 January](../_/9_January.md) [2003](../_/2003.md) to "Heren Hyarmeno", the correct Quenya genitive to mean "Order of the South".<sup id="cite_ref-Marq_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Marq-1">[1]</a></sup>

This Tolkien Society smial is defunct as off 2023 it is no longer listed as being active.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The official correspondence with [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) elicited the following reply:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>As far as I know, the Heren Hyarmen is the first Smial in Latin America. However, you cannot lay claim to being the first in the Southern Hemisphere as there have been two in Australia over the past 12 years. Both have not survived to this day, so at the moment your smial is the ONLY one in the Southern Hemisphere!
</p>

</blockquote>
