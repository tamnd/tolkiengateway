The **Hornblower Family** was an old and important family of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md)'s [Southfarthing](../S/Southfarthing.md). Its most famous member was [Tobold Hornblower](../T/Tobold_Hornblower.md) of [Longbottom](../L/Longbottom.md), who introduced [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) into the Shire, and his family remained associated with the industry.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Their [Longbottom Leaf](../L/Longbottom_Leaf.md) variety was famous, and indeed barrels of Leaf bearing the Hornblower mark were found as far afield as the ruins of [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

From Tobold's time, and perhaps earlier, the Hornblowers were one of the Shire's more important families. The importance of the family is also seen in the jewel-name of [Amethyst](../A/Amethyst_Hornblower.md), and her marriage into the prominent [Bolger Family](../B/Bolger_Family~66348.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Tanta Hornblower](../T/Tanta_Hornblower.md) married into the [Baggins Family](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md) by marrying [Largo Baggins](../L/Largo_Baggins.md); they were the great-grandparents of [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of this old family name is occupational; perhaps one of Tobold's ancestors, or himself, actually did blow a horn of some kind.<sup id="cite_ref-N_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-5">[5]</a></sup> Perhaps the name is a punning reference to pipe-smoking.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Hornblow and Hornblower are real-life English surnames.<sup id="cite_ref-N_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
