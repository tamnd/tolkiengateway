The **Hornblower Family** was an old and important family of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing). Its most famous member was [Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower) of [Longbottom](/wiki/Longbottom), who introduced [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") into the Shire, and his family remained associated with the industry.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Their [Longbottom Leaf](/wiki/Longbottom_Leaf) variety was famous, and indeed barrels of Leaf bearing the Hornblower mark were found as far afield as the ruins of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

From Tobold's time, and perhaps earlier, the Hornblowers were one of the Shire's more important families. The importance of the family is also seen in the jewel-name of [Amethyst](/wiki/Amethyst_Hornblower "Amethyst Hornblower"), and her marriage into the prominent [Bolger Family](/wiki/Bolger_Family).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Tanta Hornblower](/wiki/Tanta_Hornblower) married into the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family) by marrying [Largo Baggins](/wiki/Largo_Baggins); they were the great-grandparents of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of this old family name is occupational; perhaps one of Tobold's ancestors, or himself, actually did blow a horn of some kind.<sup id="cite_ref-N_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-5">[5]</a></sup> Perhaps the name is a punning reference to pipe-smoking.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Hornblow and Hornblower are real-life English surnames.<sup id="cite_ref-N_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
