The **Hobbitry-in-arms** was the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/militia" title="wikipedia:militia">militia</a> of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). The captain of the Hobbitry-in-arms was the [Thain](../T/Thain.md), who summoned it at a [Shire-muster](../S/Shire-muster.md) in times of emergency.<sup id="cite_ref-OrderingShire_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OrderingShire-1">[1]</a></sup>

The last battle to take place within the borders of the Shire before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), and so presumably the last time the Hobbitry-in-arms was mobilised, was the [Battle of Greenfields](../B/Battle_of_Greenfields.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2747](../T/Third_Age_2747.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) 1147). Hobbits did not fight amongst themselves, and for the next centuries they had been sheltered from the dangers of the outside world by the efforts of the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md), of which they were largely unaware. By the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) the existence of an armed hobbitry was largely a relic of harder times. What store of weapons remained in the Shire were trophies hanging on walls or displayed in the [Mathom-house](../M/Mathom-house.md) at [Michel Delving](../M/Michel_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ConcerningHobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ConcerningHobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) a hastily-assembled hobbitry under the command of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) ejected the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) from the Shire in the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md). It is not clear whether this constituted an official mobilisation of the Hobbitry-in-arms, although the contingent from [Tookland](../T/Tookland.md) was raised by Thain [Paladin Took II](../P/Paladin_Took_II.md) and led by his son and heir [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>
