The **Hobbitry-in-arms** was the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/militia" title="wikipedia:militia">militia</a> of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). The captain of the Hobbitry-in-arms was the [Thain](/wiki/Thain), who summoned it at a [Shire-muster](/wiki/Shire-muster) in times of emergency.<sup id="cite_ref-OrderingShire_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OrderingShire-1">[1]</a></sup>

The last battle to take place within the borders of the Shire before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), and so presumably the last time the Hobbitry-in-arms was mobilised, was the [Battle of Greenfields](/wiki/Battle_of_Greenfields) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2747](/wiki/Third_Age_2747 "Third Age 2747") ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") 1147). Hobbits did not fight amongst themselves, and for the next centuries they had been sheltered from the dangers of the outside world by the efforts of the [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"), of which they were largely unaware. By the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the existence of an armed hobbitry was largely a relic of harder times. What store of weapons remained in the Shire were trophies hanging on walls or displayed in the [Mathom-house](/wiki/Mathom-house) at [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-ConcerningHobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ConcerningHobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") a hastily-assembled hobbitry under the command of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) ejected the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) from the Shire in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater). It is not clear whether this constituted an official mobilisation of the Hobbitry-in-arms, although the contingent from [Tookland](/wiki/Tookland) was raised by Thain [Paladin Took II](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II) and led by his son and heir [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-3">[3]</a></sup>
