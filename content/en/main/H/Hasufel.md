**Hasufel** was the grey-coated steed of [Gárulf](/wiki/G%C3%A1rulf) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). After the loss of his master in the [Ambush at Fangorn Forest‎](/wiki/Ambush_at_Fangorn_Forest "Ambush at Fangorn Forest"), Hasufel followed the [éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored") of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) until he lent it to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) when they met in the northern fields of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The hardy creature saw many adventures, including the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), before Aragorn was brought his own horse, [Roheryn](/wiki/Roheryn), by the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster), *hasufel* means "dark skin" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *Hasu* means "grey".

Sigurd's horse in Morris' "Sigurd the Volsung" is named "Grayfell," although the Norse name is "Grani."

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Hasufel is loaned to Aragorn as in the book, but they become separated in a battle with warg riders. Aragorn is then found by the films-exclusive horse <a href="/wiki/Brego_(horse)" title="Brego (horse)">Brego</a>, who then becomes his chosen mount.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Arod" title="Arod">Arod</a> and Hasufel, led by <a href="/wiki/Shadowfax" title="Shadowfax">Shadowfax</a>, appear in a mission in which the player has to stop <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">orcs</a> from stealing Black Horses of <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>.</dd></dl>
