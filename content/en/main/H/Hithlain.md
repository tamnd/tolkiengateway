<i><b>Hithlain</b></i> was a strong fibre made from the inner bark of *[mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn "Mallorn")* trees.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was grey and felt silky. The [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) used it to make [ropes](/wiki/Elven_rope "Elven rope"), which were flexible and light, yet extremely robust. It may also have been used in the creation of the grey [Elven cloaks](/wiki/Elven_cloaks) of Lothlórien.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") and [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") climbed down a cliff of the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) with the help of a *hithlain* rope, which Sam tied to a stump at the top. When Sam tugged the rope in annoyance that they'd have to leave it and it would help Gollum get down, it came loose and fell on him. Frodo thought Sam had tied it badly, but Sam thought it came because he called it.<sup id="cite_ref-Taming_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Taming-3">[3]</a></sup>

When Frodo and Sam tied their Elven rope around [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)'s ankle he screamed in pain. Apparently the fact that [Elves](/wiki/Elves) made the material, or as Gollum claimed, caused him agony when it touched him.<sup id="cite_ref-Taming_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Taming-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
