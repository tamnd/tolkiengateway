**Helkar** was one of the two pillars held one of the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) created to light [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) in an early conception of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

## History {#History}

Helkar was a great pillar, raised by [Melko](../M/Melko.md) to house the *southern* of the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) fashioned by [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) to illuminate [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The pillar was extremely tall, rising to the stars, and shone like pale blue crystal. Melko claimed that Helkar and its northern twin [Ringil](<../R/Ringil_(tower).md>) were made of a strong, imperishable substance that he had devised; in reality he lied for they were made of ice (a material unknown to the other [Valar](../V/Valar.md)). Thus, when Aulë's lamps were placed upon them (silver in the north and gold in the south) the ice melted and caused great floods to pour into the seas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Helka* is [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) for "ice-cold". The [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) cognate was <i><b>Helchor</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) noted that the story of the Lamps in its early stage was very different from the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*. It is Melko, not Aulë, who built the two pillars. The name *Helkar* disappeared as the name for the southern pillar; eventually the [Sea of Helcar](../S/Sea_of_Helcar.md) would be the body of water formed at the roots of [Illuin](../I/Illuin.md), the *northern* pillar and lamp. The only remaining influence from the older version of the story was that the fall of the pillars created inland seas, a vestige of the notion that the pillars had been made of ice that melted.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
