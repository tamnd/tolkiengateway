**Helkar** was one of the two pillars held one of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) created to light [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in an early conception of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## History {#History}

Helkar was a great pillar, raised by [Melko](/wiki/Melko) to house the *southern* of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) fashioned by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) to illuminate [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The pillar was extremely tall, rising to the stars, and shone like pale blue crystal. Melko claimed that Helkar and its northern twin [Ringil](</wiki/Ringil_(tower)> "Ringil (tower)") were made of a strong, imperishable substance that he had devised; in reality he lied for they were made of ice (a material unknown to the other [Valar](/wiki/Valar)). Thus, when Aulë's lamps were placed upon them (silver in the north and gold in the south) the ice melted and caused great floods to pour into the seas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Helka* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) for "ice-cold". The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate was <i><b>Helchor</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted that the story of the Lamps in its early stage was very different from the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*. It is Melko, not Aulë, who built the two pillars. The name *Helkar* disappeared as the name for the southern pillar; eventually the [Sea of Helcar](/wiki/Sea_of_Helcar) would be the body of water formed at the roots of [Illuin](/wiki/Illuin), the *northern* pillar and lamp. The only remaining influence from the older version of the story was that the fall of the pillars created inland seas, a vestige of the notion that the pillars had been made of ice that melted.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
