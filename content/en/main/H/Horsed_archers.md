**Horsed archers** were warriors armed with [bows](/wiki/Bows "Bows") that shot riding on [horses](/wiki/Horses "Horses"). They existed throughout the history of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) among different peoples and cultures.

## History {#History}

### First Age {#First_Age}

The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) used horsed archers during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon), the prince of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), rode against the young [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) with a company of horse archers and forced him to flee back to [Angband](/wiki/Angband).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later horsed archers of Fingon pursued [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), that had been defeated in the battle in Hithlum, into the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Fingon_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingon-2">[2]</a></sup> [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) also had many horsed archers. They were defeated in the [battle of the Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon "Battle of the Pass of Aglon"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but the hosts of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) suffered great casualties in that battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Fingon_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingon-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

The [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) shot bows from horseback as a sport and pastime during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but they did not ride on horses to war. However, units of light-armed horsed archers (often of non-Númenórean origin) existed within Númenórean armies.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the same time period [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen), who were skilled and fearless riders, well-armed due to a close [alliance](/wiki/Alliance_of_Dwarves_and_Men "Alliance of Dwarves and Men") with [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), used great forces of horsed archers to fight against [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") raids.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) horsed archers were used by the [Éotheod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od "Éothéod") and by their descendants, the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim). The great [*éoherë*](/wiki/%C3%89oher%C3%AB "Éoherë") led by [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) to the [battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant "Battle of the Field of Celebrant") included several hundreds of horsed archers. After the battle some companies of horsed archers remained in [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) to deal with bands of enemies that might still lurk there.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> During the [First battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen "First Battle of the Fords of Isen") [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) had at least one company of horsed archers.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Some riders of [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s *[éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored")* were skilled at shooting from a running horse. They were able to shoot many Orcs during the pursuit of [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk)'s company.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) saw a vision from [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen) of horsed archers among the armies of the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
