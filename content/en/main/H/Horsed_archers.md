**Horsed archers** were warriors armed with [bows](../B/Bows.md) that shot riding on [horses](Horses.md). They existed throughout the history of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) among different peoples and cultures.

## History {#History}

### First Age {#First_Age}

The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) used horsed archers during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md), the prince of [Hithlum](Hithlum.md), rode against the young [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) with a company of horse archers and forced him to flee back to [Angband](../A/Angband.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later horsed archers of Fingon pursued [orcs](../O/Orcs.md), that had been defeated in the battle in Hithlum, into the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fingon_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingon-2">[2]</a></sup> [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md) and [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) also had many horsed archers. They were defeated in the [battle of the Pass of Aglon](../B/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but the hosts of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) suffered great casualties in that battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Fingon_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fingon-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

The [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) shot bows from horseback as a sport and pastime during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but they did not ride on horses to war. However, units of light-armed horsed archers (often of non-Númenórean origin) existed within Númenórean armies.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the same time period [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md), who were skilled and fearless riders, well-armed due to a close [alliance](../A/Alliance_of_Dwarves_and_Men.md) with [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), used great forces of horsed archers to fight against [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) raids.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) horsed archers were used by the [Éotheod](../_/Éothéod.md) and by their descendants, the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md). The great [*éoherë*](../_/Éoherë.md) led by [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) to the [battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md) included several hundreds of horsed archers. After the battle some companies of horsed archers remained in [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) to deal with bands of enemies that might still lurk there.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> During the [First battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md) had at least one company of horsed archers.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Some riders of [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)'s *[éored](../_/Éored.md)* were skilled at shooting from a running horse. They were able to shoot many Orcs during the pursuit of [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md)'s company.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) saw a vision from [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md) of horsed archers among the armies of the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
