<i><b>halla</b></i> is an adjective meaning "tall" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-footnote_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-footnote-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Halla.png"><img src="/w/images/0/02/Halla.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="14" height="55" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="14" data-file-height="55"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Halla* is also the name in Quenya of a letter that was originally used to represent "breath" **H**. It might be placed before a consonant to indicated that it was unvoiced and breathed, such as expressing voiceless *l* or *r*.<sup id="cite_ref-footnote_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-footnote-1">[1]</a></sup> Over time though the letter *[Harma](/wiki/Harma)*, when used at the beginning of a word, came to represent "breath" *h* (and is then called *[Aha](/wiki/Aha)*).
