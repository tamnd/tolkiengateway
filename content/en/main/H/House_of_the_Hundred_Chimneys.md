The **House of the Hundred Chimneys**, only mentioned in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") (*[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*), was the ancient house of [Gilfanon](/wiki/Gilfanon). It was located near the bridge of [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel) on [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As the village of Tavrobel was based on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Haywood" title="wikipedia:Great Haywood">Great Haywood</a>, G. L. Elkin, the Acting Director of the Shugborough Estate, has suggested to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) that the House of Hundred Chimneys may have been based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shugborough_Hall" title="wikipedia:Shugborough Hall">Shugborough Hall</a>, the home of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_of_Lichfield" title="wikipedia:Earl of Lichfield">Earl of Lichfield</a>, which features prominent chimneys.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) have suggested that [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") was perhaps inspired by a house named Gipsy Green nearby <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penkridge" title="wikipedia:Penkridge">Penkridge</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staffordshire" title="wikipedia:Staffordshire">Staffordshire</a> (where Tolkien stayed at a camp in [1918](/wiki/1918) recovering from "trench fever"). As can be seen in an illustration by Tolkien of the house, it features several, prominent chimneys.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
