The **House of the Hundred Chimneys**, only mentioned in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) (*[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*), was the ancient house of [Gilfanon](../G/Gilfanon.md). It was located near the bridge of [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md) on [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As the village of Tavrobel was based on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Haywood" title="wikipedia:Great Haywood">Great Haywood</a>, G. L. Elkin, the Acting Director of the Shugborough Estate, has suggested to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) that the House of Hundred Chimneys may have been based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shugborough_Hall" title="wikipedia:Shugborough Hall">Shugborough Hall</a>, the home of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earl_of_Lichfield" title="wikipedia:Earl of Lichfield">Earl of Lichfield</a>, which features prominent chimneys.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) have suggested that [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was perhaps inspired by a house named Gipsy Green nearby <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penkridge" title="wikipedia:Penkridge">Penkridge</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staffordshire" title="wikipedia:Staffordshire">Staffordshire</a> (where Tolkien stayed at a camp in [1918](../_/1918.md) recovering from "trench fever"). As can be seen in an illustration by Tolkien of the house, it features several, prominent chimneys.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
