**Haerast**, the **Far Shore**, referred to the north-eastern coast of [Aman](/wiki/Aman) (specifically [Araman](/wiki/Araman)) as seen from [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) across the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer). Haerast was the opposite of Nevrast, the Hither Shore of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Haerast* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The meaning "Far Shore"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> is its translation<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> analyzed as \**hae* "far" + \**rast* "shore".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

These words have not been attested in [canon](/wiki/Canon "Canon") by themselves, but can be deduced etymologically. *Hae* represents [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KHAYA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHAYA (page does not exist)">KHAYA</a>, also seen in words *na-chaered*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> *haeron*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> cf. also [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *háya* "far"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>). While *rast* is seen in the names *[Andrast](/wiki/Andrast)*, *Nevrast*, cf. also Q. *hresta* "shore, beach".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
