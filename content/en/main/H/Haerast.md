**Haerast**, the **Far Shore**, referred to the north-eastern coast of [Aman](../A/Aman.md) (specifically [Araman](../A/Araman.md)) as seen from [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) across the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md). Haerast was the opposite of Nevrast, the Hither Shore of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Haerast* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The meaning "Far Shore"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> is its translation<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> analyzed as \**hae* "far" + \**rast* "shore".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

These words have not been attested in [canon](../C/Canon.md) by themselves, but can be deduced etymologically. *Hae* represents [root](../R/Root.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KHAYA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHAYA (page does not exist)">KHAYA</a>, also seen in words *na-chaered*,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> *haeron*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> cf. also [Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) *háya* "far"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>). While *rast* is seen in the names *[Andrast](../A/Andrast.md)*, *Nevrast*, cf. also Q. *hresta* "shore, beach".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
