**Haudh-en-Elleth** was the grave of [Finduilas](../F/Finduilas.md) of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) that stood near the [Crossings of Teiglin](../C/Crossings_of_Teiglin.md) on the western borders of the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) would not allow [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) to draw near to the mound, so they soon feared and avoided the place.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On one of his forays to battle Orcs near the Crossings, Túrin discovered [Niënor](../N/Niënor.md) his sister (whom he did not know) upon the mound, naked and cowering from a storm.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later Túrin met [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) at the mound and learned that Niënor was indeed his sister.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Haudh-en-Elleth* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name glossed as "Mound of the Elf-maid" or "mound (grave) of the Noldorin maid", from *[haudh](Haudh.md)* ("funeral mound") + *[en](../E/En.md)* ("of the") + *Elleth* ("Elf-maid").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:141</sup> An early form was *Haudh-en-Ellas*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:97</sup>
