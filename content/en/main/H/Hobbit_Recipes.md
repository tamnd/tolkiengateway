<i><b>Hobbit Recipes or Cooking with Hobbits</b></i> is a cookbook inspired by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [Hobbits](Hobbits.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This fine collection of Hobbit culinary delights has been collected and annotated by that renowned Hobbitish scholar; Gamling Wort. Herein he leads us ‘big folk’ through some wonderful and mouth-watering dishes. Mushrooms and rabbit, naturally, abound ~ enjoy!Book includes; 36 pages, 32 recipes, 35 line illustrations by Cornelius Clifford, colour cover. Size approx: 8.25 by 5.5 inches (210 x 145 mm)
</p>
</blockquote>
