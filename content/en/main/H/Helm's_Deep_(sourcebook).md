<i><b>Helm&#39;s Deep</b></i> is a sourcebook of [Decipher](../D/Decipher.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Featuring 96 pages of information about the fortress of [Helm's Deep](Helm's_Deep.md) and it surrounding lands, the newest addition to the [Lord of the Rings RPG](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md) gives players a brand new mass-combat system for conducting warfare in the [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) "chapters" (campaigns). Fans of the Lord of the Rings property will find many familiar images from the films as well as extensive analysis of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s fantastic stronghold of Helm's Deep.
