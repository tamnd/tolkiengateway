<i><b>Helm&#39;s Deep</b></i> is a sourcebook of [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Featuring 96 pages of information about the fortress of [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) and it surrounding lands, the newest addition to the [Lord of the Rings RPG](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game "The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game") gives players a brand new mass-combat system for conducting warfare in the [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) "chapters" (campaigns). Fans of the Lord of the Rings property will find many familiar images from the films as well as extensive analysis of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s fantastic stronghold of Helm's Deep.
