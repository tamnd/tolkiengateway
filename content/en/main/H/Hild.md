**Hild** was a noble woman of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

Hild was the daughter of [King Gram](/wiki/Gram "Gram"), and sister of [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm "Helm"). Her son [Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f) took the crown after the death of Helm and his two sons.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hild* is a poetic word in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) for "battle".
