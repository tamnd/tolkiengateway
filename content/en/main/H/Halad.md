**Halad** or "Warden" was an was an elected title given to the leader of the [Folk of Haleth](/wiki/Folk_of_Haleth) in the Forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:263</sup> The Halad's residence was called [Obel Halad](/wiki/Obel_Halad) and their duties included the command of the armed men of the land and leadership of the [Folkmoot for Judgement](/wiki/Folkmoot_for_Judgement), the gatherings of the people called to make important decisions.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:283</sup>

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [390](/wiki/First_Age_390 "First Age 390"),<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup> [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth), the founder of the [Folk of Haleth](/wiki/Folk_of_Haleth) led her people from [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad) to the relative safety of the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil) in the lands of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-3">[3]</a></sup> In exchange for defending the [Crossing of the Teiglin River](/wiki/Crossing_of_Teiglin "Crossing of Teiglin"), King [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) made use of his friendship with Thingol to allow the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) to remain free men.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-3">[3]</a></sup> Years later, when Haleth died, the Haladin chose to establish a ruling line from the descendants of Haldad. Because Haleth did not marry and had no children, her brother's son [Haldan](/wiki/Haldan) became the next Halad Lord of Brethil.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-3">[3]</a></sup> The position of the Halad became elective, chosen by the [Men of Brethil](/wiki/Men_of_Brethil), but by custom all chieftains had come from the noble [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth).<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:263</sup>

After the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), and the capture of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith_in_Beleriand "Minas Tirith in Beleriand"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) began to roam unhindered across the lands of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand). [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir), son of [Haldan](/wiki/Haldan), called on the aid of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), who sent many stout [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") warriors under the command of [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) to his aid. Together, Halmir and Beleg destroyed a legion of invading Orcs, and gave the southern lands a respite from the defeats in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Haldir joined the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) and led a force of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) in the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) attempted a retreat across [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) covered by Haldir, but he was slain, with most of his Men.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After this, the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) had relative peace in their woods for more than twenty years, until a sudden assault by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). [Handir](/wiki/Handir) set out to defeat them, but his men were defeated and he himself was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Soon after [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir)'s succession, [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) came to [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil), and began before long to take control of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) himself. After the death of [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung), Brandir was slain in anger by Túrin in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [499](/wiki/First_Age_499 "First Age 499").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Then there was some contention in Brethil concerning who should become the next Halad. Many had wished to elect [Manthor](/wiki/Manthor) but Hardang came from the senior branch of the family under [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir) and obtained the title.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:270</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Since the chieftains of Brethil were elected from the family of [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) it was natural to used the term "Halad" for the position. Haldad's name meant "watchdog" and was derived from *hal(a)*, meaning "watch" or "guard".<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:270</sup>

When spoken of in a general way this official was called "The Halad";<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:275</sup> when name and title were spoken the title came second, as in "[Hardang](/wiki/Hardang) Halad".<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:276</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

All of the information about the office of the Halad comes from *[The Wanderings of Húrin](/wiki/The_Wanderings_of_H%C3%BArin)* in *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*. This was a late-written story that [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) regretfully chose not to use in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

In a section not included in the story it was stated that [Avranc](/wiki/Avranc), not of the House of Haleth, was chosen for the next Halad but that the authority and reverence of the office was gone.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:308</sup>

In an isolated note [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) had written possible name changes. One would have substituted *Halbar* for *Halad*.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:309</sup>
