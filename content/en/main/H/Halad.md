**Halad** or "Warden" was an was an elected title given to the leader of the [Folk of Haleth](../F/Folk_of_Haleth.md) in the Forest of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:263</sup> The Halad's residence was called [Obel Halad](../O/Obel_Halad.md) and their duties included the command of the armed men of the land and leadership of the [Folkmoot for Judgement](../F/Folkmoot_for_Judgement.md), the gatherings of the people called to make important decisions.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:283</sup>

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [390](../F/First_Age_390.md),<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup> [Haleth](Haleth.md), the founder of the [Folk of Haleth](../F/Folk_of_Haleth.md) led her people from [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md) to the relative safety of the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md) in the lands of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-3">[3]</a></sup> In exchange for defending the [Crossing of the Teiglin River](../C/Crossing_of_Teiglin.md), King [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) made use of his friendship with Thingol to allow the [Haladin](Haladin.md) to remain free men.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-3">[3]</a></sup> Years later, when Haleth died, the Haladin chose to establish a ruling line from the descendants of Haldad. Because Haleth did not marry and had no children, her brother's son [Haldan](Haldan.md) became the next Halad Lord of Brethil.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-3">[3]</a></sup> The position of the Halad became elective, chosen by the [Men of Brethil](../M/Men_of_Brethil.md), but by custom all chieftains had come from the noble [House of Haleth](House_of_Haleth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:263</sup>

After the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), and the capture of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith_in_Beleriand.md), [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) began to roam unhindered across the lands of [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md). [Halmir](Halmir.md), son of [Haldan](Haldan.md), called on the aid of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), who sent many stout [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) warriors under the command of [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) to his aid. Together, Halmir and Beleg destroyed a legion of invading Orcs, and gave the southern lands a respite from the defeats in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Haldir joined the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) and led a force of the [Haladin](Haladin.md) in the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md). [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) attempted a retreat across [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md) covered by Haldir, but he was slain, with most of his Men.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After this, the [Haladin](Haladin.md) had relative peace in their woods for more than twenty years, until a sudden assault by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). [Handir](Handir.md) set out to defeat them, but his men were defeated and he himself was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Soon after [Brandir](../B/Brandir.md)'s succession, [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) came to [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md), and began before long to take control of the [Haladin](Haladin.md) himself. After the death of [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md), Brandir was slain in anger by Túrin in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [499](../F/First_Age_499.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Then there was some contention in Brethil concerning who should become the next Halad. Many had wished to elect [Manthor](../M/Manthor.md) but Hardang came from the senior branch of the family under [Halmir](Halmir.md) and obtained the title.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:270</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Since the chieftains of Brethil were elected from the family of [Haldad](Haldad.md) it was natural to used the term "Halad" for the position. Haldad's name meant "watchdog" and was derived from *hal(a)*, meaning "watch" or "guard".<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:270</sup>

When spoken of in a general way this official was called "The Halad";<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:275</sup> when name and title were spoken the title came second, as in "[Hardang](Hardang.md) Halad".<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:276</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

All of the information about the office of the Halad comes from *[The Wanderings of Húrin](../T/The_Wanderings_of_Húrin.md)* in *[The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md)*. This was a late-written story that [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) regretfully chose not to use in *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.

In a section not included in the story it was stated that [Avranc](../A/Avranc.md), not of the House of Haleth, was chosen for the next Halad but that the authority and reverence of the office was gone.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:308</sup>

In an isolated note [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) had written possible name changes. One would have substituted *Halbar* for *Halad*.<sup id="cite_ref-Wanderings_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wanderings-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:309</sup>
