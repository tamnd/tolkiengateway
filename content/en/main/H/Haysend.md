**Haysend** or **Hays-end** was a location at the southern tip of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md), at the place where the River [Withywindle](../W/Withywindle.md) flowed out of the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) into the [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was named for the fact that it stood at the end of the [High Hay](High_Hay.md), the Hedge raised to protect the [Bucklanders](../B/Bucklanders.md) from the strange things that lived in the Forest and beyond it.

According to the poem "[Bombadil Goes Boating](../B/Bombadil_Goes_Boating.md)", the inhabitants of the Hays-end and [Breredon](../B/Breredon.md) had a more fierce and protective attitude than most [Hobbits](Hobbits.md), which is hardly surprising given the perilous location of their village.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "hay's end", the end of the hedge or boundary-hedge.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

It is not clear what is at the Haysend. [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes that in *[A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md)* map the main road of Buckland stops right at [Standelf](../S/Standelf.md) and doesn't continue to Haysend, although a draft of *[A Conspiracy Unmasked](../A/A_Conspiracy_Unmasked.md)* mentions that road running "to Standelf and Haysend". No habitations are marked on the Map, nor in its previous drafts,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> although the poem *Bombadil Goes Boating* mentions inhabitants, as well as the village of Breredon. <span></span>
