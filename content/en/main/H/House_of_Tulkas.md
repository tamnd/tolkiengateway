The **house of Tulkas** was the dwelling of [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md) in [Valmar](../V/Valmar.md), the city of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

The house is said to have sprung "*high into the air with many storeys*", and to sport a "*tower of bronze and pillars of copper in a wide arcade*". Tulkas's house also had a court, where champions engaged in athletic games. There would often [Nessa](../N/Nessa.md), wife of Tulkas, appear to bring goblets of wine and drinks to the players.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
