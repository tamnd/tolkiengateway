The **House of Telcontar** was the Royal House of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md). The House was founded by [Aragorn II](../A/Aragorn.md) after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), who was the Heir of Elendil, and took the throne under the name Elessar.

## History {#History}

During the War of the Ring, [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), a companion of Aragorn's in the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), became a member of the elite Citadel Guard at [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), capital of Gondor. When Aragorn entered the city, Peregrin persisted in crudely referring to Aragorn by the nickname he had garnered as a Ranger of the North: "Strider", even in front of foreign dignitaries such as King [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Aragorn was amused and decided that the name of the lineage he founded would be "Telcontar", which means "Strider" in Quenya. Aragorn then took the royal name "Elessar".<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-2">[1]</a></sup>

With Queen [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md), King Elessar Telcontar reigned for 120 years, before finally surrendering his life. This long reign is attributed to Elessar's descent from the Men of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), who are blessed with long life (he was 88 when he took the throne). He rebuilt the old capital [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md) in Arnor and later ruled from this northern capital, although the old capital Minas Tirith was still of great political importance. Elessar had one known son, [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md), and an unspecified number of daughters.

After Aragorn's death in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-3">[2]</a></sup> his son [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md) took up the High Kingship.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-3">[2]</a></sup> One hundred years into his reign, he encountered a [new threat](../T/The_New_Shadow.md), known only as the "[Dark Tree](../D/Dark_Tree.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

Of Eldarion, it was foretold that he should rule a great realm, and that it should endure for a hundred generations of [Men](../M/Men.md) after him, that is until a new age brought in again new things; and from him should come the kings of many realms in long days after. But if this foretelling spoke truly, none now can say, for [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) are no more; and even the chronicles of the House of Telcontar and all their deeds and glory are lost.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Telcontar* (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[telˈkontar]</span>) was a translation into [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) of Aragorn's common nickname in the north, "[Strider](<../S/Strider_(Aragorn).md>)".<sup id="cite_ref-Houses_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Houses-2">[1]</a></sup>
