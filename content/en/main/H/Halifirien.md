**Halifirien**, originally known as **Eilenaer** and later known as **Amon Anwar** by the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians), **Halifirien** by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim)<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup> and the Whispering Wood" in [Westron](/wiki/Westron)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>, was the seventh and westernmost of the [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") north of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) which ran westwards from the city of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to close to the border between [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) (formerly the Gondorian provnice of *[Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Appearance {#Appearance}

Halifirien was the highest of the seven [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") north of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and the highest point of a long northward spur of the White Mountains. *Halifirien* appeared to stand alone out of the [Firien Wood](/wiki/Firienholt "Firienholt") due to a deep cleft, the Firien-dale, that separated it from the northward spur of the White Mountains. On the cleft side it was a sheer wall, but its outer slopes, especially to the north, were long and nowhere steep. Trees grew densely on the base of its outer slopes, becoming thinner almost up to the summit, which was bare. When [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) visited, the summit was leveled and a memorial mound was raised on the eastward side, with a stairway and then a pathway marked by standing stones descending to the great West Road. After the hallowing by Isildur the hill and the surrounding woods were known for their profound silence.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

Besides the trees on the slopes and the green grass at the summit (which was surrounded by a belt of white birches), the memorial mound was covered with white flowers of *[alfirin](/wiki/Alfirin "Alfirin")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the end of the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441")) and before he departed for [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2")), Isildur journeyed with his nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil) and a party of trusted friends about the borders of all the lands claimed by [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). Near the center of these lands they came to a hill then called [Eilenaer](/wiki/Eilenaer). The party made a path to the treeless summit, created a level space, and raised a mound at its eastern end. Within the mound Isildur laid a casket and hallowed it as the [Tomb of Elendil](/wiki/Tomb_of_Elendil). After that time the hill was renamed Amon Anwar, the “Hill of Awe”.<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup>

No beacon was built on Amon Anwar as long as communication was still maintained between [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Morgul "Minas Morgul"), [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Tirith "Minas Tirith") and [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) with the [Palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri) in the great days of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

Following the victory at the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant), [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) the [Stewards of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor), brought [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young), the [Lord of the Éothéod](/wiki/Lord_of_the_%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), to the summit of Amon Anwar in August, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"). There they spoke the [Oath](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl "Oath of Eorl") by which Calenardhon was ceded to the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od). Cirion also declared that Amon Anwar was now a hallowed place of both peoples and that the Eorlings and the Stewards should henceforward share in its guard and maintenance. After Eorl had returned to the North to bring back all his people, Cirion removed the tomb of Elendil and took the casket to [Rath Dínen](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) had settled in [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) (later known as [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan)), a warning beacon was built<sup id="cite_ref-Note35_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note35-4">[4]</a></sup> on the crown of the hill<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup> at the beacon-site, a wide circle and lodges were constructed for the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Beacon-wardens&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beacon-wardens (page does not exist)">Beacon-wardens</a> in the trees near the summit<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>. The Beacon-wardens had turns of duty and did not stay long, unless they were forced to stay there by bad weather. Initially Gondor and Rohan shared the guarding and maintenance of Amon Anwar.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

In later days as Gondor declined and the Rohirrim grew in power and numbers, the wardens of Anwar were provided entirely by the people of [Eastfold](/wiki/Eastfold) from Rohan. The hill was then named the **Halifirien** by them.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the ride of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) (on March 7, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")) they saw the beacons of Gondor, including the one on the Halifirien, being lit.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After King Elessar returned, the bond of Cirion and Eorl was renewed in the same place with [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), king of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Halifirien* means "Holy Mount"<sup id="cite_ref-Tradition_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tradition-1">[1]</a></sup> or "Holy Mountain" in the [language of Rohan](/wiki/Rohanese "Rohanese")<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VTHalifirien_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VTHalifirien-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NMHalifirien_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NMHalifirien-8">[8]</a></sup>. The name is a modernized spelling of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Háligfirgen*.<sup id="cite_ref-Note33_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note33-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VTHalifirien_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VTHalifirien-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NMHalifirien_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NMHalifirien-8">[8]</a></sup> The second element *firgen* means "mountain" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-Note33_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note33-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

*Eilenaer* is a name of [pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean") origin, which is related to the name *[Eilenach](/wiki/Eilenach)*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> It was not a name in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), [Númenórean](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic "Adûnaic") or [Westron](/wiki/Westron).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

*Amon Anwar* means "Hill of Awe" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> from *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* "hill" and *[anwar](/wiki/Anwar "Anwar")* "awe"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>.
