**Helm’s Deep** was a deep gorge<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> below the [Thrihyrne](../T/Thrihyrne.md) near the end of the northernmost arm of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Thrihyrne_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrihyrne-2">[2]</a></sup> on the far side of the [Westfold Vale](../W/Westfold_Vale.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> which was named after [Helm](Helm.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the ninth [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Geography and description {#Geography_and_description}

The deep gorge was located below the Thrihyrne at the head<sup id="cite_ref-Thrihyrne_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thrihyrne-2">[2]</a></sup> of the Westfold Vale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> Caves,<sup id="cite_ref-Dike_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dike-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> such as the [Glittering Caves](../G/Glittering_Caves.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> were located in the cliff walls of the gorge. The lower entrance of the gorge, [Helm's Gate](Helm's_Gate.md),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> was fortified by the [Deeping Wall](../D/Deeping_Wall.md) and the [Hornburg](Hornburg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> A quarter of a mile below Helm's Dike, a high rampart made from earth behind a trench ran across the [Deeping-coomb](../D/Deeping-coomb.md) from one side of the valley to the other side.<sup id="cite_ref-Dike_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dike-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Deeping-stream](../D/Deeping-stream.md) flowed through the gorge through a culvert at the base of the Deeping Wall, turned to flow along the feet of the Hornburg down to Helm's Dike<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> where it flowed through a wide breach in the rampart<sup id="cite_ref-Dike_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dike-6">[6]</a></sup> down the Deeping-coomb and into the Westfold Vale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> The distance from the mouth of the Deeping-coomb to the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md) was approximately five leagues (15 miles)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> for the crows of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TT|Road_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT|Road-12">[12]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the long distant days of the glory of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> at the height of their power,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> the [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) [sea-kings](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) of Gondor built a fortress at the entrance to the gorge,<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-3">[3]</a></sup> which they called the [fortress of Aglarond](Hornburg.md),<sup id="cite_ref-UT11c_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT11c-14">[14]</a></sup> which means "Glittering Caves"<sup id="cite_ref-TT|Road_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT|Road-12">[12]</a></sup> in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is probable that [Men](../M/Men.md) from Gondor had already explored the gorge and its caves and named the fortress after the Glittering Caves.

When [Steward](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md) [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) granted the province of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) to King [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) (later referred to as the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> the fortress of Aglarond was repaired with the help of stonemasons from Gondor and handed over to the Rohirrim and the old hereditary guards of the fortress were transferred to the fortress of [Angrenost](../I/Isengard.md), which remained under the control of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-UT11c_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT11c-14">[14]</a></sup> The Rohirrim called the fortress the *Súthburg* and the caves *Glǽmscrafu* in their own [language](../R/Rohanese.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UTFootnote_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTFootnote-17">[17]</a></sup>

During the war against the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) and [Wulf's](../W/Wulf.md) usurpation of Rohan, [Helm Hammerhand](Helm.md) took refuge in the keep, where they held out under siege during the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md)–[2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> After Helm’s death, the gorge was called Helm's Deep<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> and the fortification was called the Hornburg.<sup id="cite_ref-UTFootnote_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTFootnote-17">[17]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Helm’s Deep became the refuge of some of the Rohirrim under [King Théoden](../T/Théoden.md). The keep fell under siege and was breached by [Saruman's](../S/Saruman.md) Uruk-hai, and subsequently the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) was fought.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> After the battle, someone, probably the [Huorns](Huorns.md),<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> laid the slain [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) in a mound known after as the [Death Down](../D/Death_Down.md).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> The [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) allied with Saruman were buried more honourably in a separate mound below the Dike.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element *Helm* in the name *Helm's Deep* refers to Helm,<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> the King of Rohan who sought refuge in the Hornburg when his realm was invaded by the Dunlendings.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) admits that he probably had not placed Helm's Deep on his [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) and his [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md) map at precisely the point where J.R.R. Tolkien intended it to be.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>
