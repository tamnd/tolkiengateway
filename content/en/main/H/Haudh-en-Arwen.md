**Haudh-en-Arwen**, **Ladybarrow** (also called **Tûr Haretha**), was the funeral mound of [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth). The <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Garth_of_the_Graves&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Garth of the Graves (page does not exist)">Garth of the Graves</a> was possibly built around it.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Haleth was the daughter of [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), who led her people westward across [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and founded the settlement of [Men](/wiki/Men) in the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil). After her death, the [People of Haleth](/wiki/People_of_Haleth) - as they had become known - raised a [barrow](/wiki/Barrows "Barrows") (known as Tûr Haretha or Haudh-en-Arwen) for her in the Forest.<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) term *Haudh-en-Arwen*<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup> can roughly be translated as "**Ladybarrow**". The actual translation would be "Mound of the Noble Maiden". The element *Arwen* had no direct connection with [Arwen Evenstar](/wiki/Arwen "Arwen"), who lived millennia after Haleth's time, but it does give an insight into the origin of her name. [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *arwen* means literally "high maiden", and is translated here as the "lady" in 'Ladybarrow'.

**Tûr Haretha** is the Mannish name of "The Ladybarrow". <span></span>
