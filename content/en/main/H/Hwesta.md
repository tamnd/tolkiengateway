<i><b>hwesta</b></i> is a noun meaning "breeze" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Hwesta.gif"><img src="/w/images/e/e1/Hwesta.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Hwesta* is also the name in Quenya of the twelfth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the fourth letter of the third grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiceless spirants" and it is the third letter in the fourth series of consonants. For written Quenya or [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the fourth series is used for the labialized or *kw*-series, the *[Quessetéma](/wiki/Quesset%C3%A9ma)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup>

In Quenya, which does not need a distinct sign for *chw*, this letter represents **HW**. In Sindarin, which does distinguish between *chw* and *hw*, *Hwesta* represents **CHW** while *[Hwesta Sindarinwa](/wiki/Hwesta_Sindarinwa)* (the thirty-fourth letter) represents **HW**.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> In [Westron](/wiki/Westron) the fourth series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](/wiki/Calmat%C3%A9ma)*, and this letter represents spirant **CH** as in Scottish "loch".<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Aha</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> (not to be confused with the name in Quenya of [letter 11](/wiki/Aha "Aha")).
