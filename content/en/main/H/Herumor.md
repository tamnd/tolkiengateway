**Herumor** was a [Black Númenórean](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) who lived in the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> Herumor was among those [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) who sailed east from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) to establish fortresses and dwellings along the coasts during Sauron's stay in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) (from [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3262](../S/Second_Age_3262.md) to [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md)) and were already bent to Sauron's will. Herumor rose to power among the [Haradrim](Haradrim.md). It is possible that Herumor was among those Númenórean servants from the south that Sauron gathered to him when he prepared to attack [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3429](../S/Second_Age_3429.md) after he had taken shape again after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

*Herumor* seems to be a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name and seems to mean "Black Lord".<sup id="cite_ref-eldamo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eldamo-3">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) suggests that the initial element of this name is Q. *[heru](Heru.md)* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md) suggests that the second element is *[morë](../M/Morë.md)* ("dark, black").<sup id="cite_ref-eldamo_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eldamo-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

In the defunct [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) game from the 1980s, Herumor is given an extended history. [Fuinur](../F/Fuinur.md) is portrayed as his older brother.
