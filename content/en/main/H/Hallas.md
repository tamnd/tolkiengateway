**Hallas** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) 2480<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> – 2605,<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 125 years) was the thirteenth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

Hallas was the heir of [Steward](../S/Stewards.md) [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md), who committed the rule of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to his son while Cirion led an army north to fight the [Balchoth](../B/Balchoth.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md). Following the unexpected victory at the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md), Hallas was a witness when Cirion gifted [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) to [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl.md) and his people.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In fact, it was Hallas who devised the names *[Rohan](../R/Rohan.md)* and *[Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Hallas died in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2605](../T/Third_Age_2605.md) and was succeeded by his son, who became Steward [Húrin II](Húrin_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hallas* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) suggests that it means "tallness" and is a combination of *hall* ("tall") and the abstract ending *-as*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
