The **Hermit's Hat** was a [constellation](../C/Constellations.md) far to the east of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) in [Arda](../A/Arda.md). The Hermit's Hat was invented by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*.

## History {#History}

The Hermit's Hat was a constellation recognized by the [Mystics](../M/Mystics.md) that could only be seen somewhere deep within [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md), "where the stars are strange".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[Note 1]</a></sup>

During the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), a group of three Mystics came west from Rhûn seeking [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). When they found [a being](../T/The_Stranger.md) they believed to be Sauron, they told him of the Hermit's Hat. They were later defeated by him after realizing that he was an [Istar](../O/Order_of_Wizards.md) rather than Sauron. Accompanied by [Elanor Brandyfoot](../E/Elanor_Brandyfoot.md), the Istar then departed the [Grove](../G/Grove.md) for [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) in order to find out more about the constellation.
