The **Hermit's Hat** was a [constellation](/wiki/Constellations "Constellations") far to the east of [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) in [Arda](/wiki/Arda). The Hermit's Hat was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.

## History {#History}

The Hermit's Hat was a constellation recognized by the [Mystics](/wiki/Mystics) that could only be seen somewhere deep within [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn), "where the stars are strange".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[Note 1]</a></sup>

During the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), a group of three Mystics came west from Rhûn seeking [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). When they found [a being](/wiki/The_Stranger "The Stranger") they believed to be Sauron, they told him of the Hermit's Hat. They were later defeated by him after realizing that he was an [Istar](/wiki/Order_of_Wizards "Order of Wizards") rather than Sauron. Accompanied by [Elanor Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot), the Istar then departed the [Grove](/wiki/Grove) for [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) in order to find out more about the constellation.
