**Hundar** was one of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), younger son of [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir), and brother to [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)"), [Hareth](/wiki/Hareth),<sup id="cite_ref-Haladin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Haladin-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Hiril](/wiki/Hiril).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Hundar had two children: a daughter [Hunleth](/wiki/Hunleth) and a son [Hundad](/wiki/Hundad).<sup id="cite_ref-Haladin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Haladin-1">[1]</a></sup>

He was later slain in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early genealogical table for the Haladin, Halmir had a son named *Haldor* which became *Hundar* and finally *Haldir*. With the final change a second son named Hundar was added to the table and both brothers fought and died in the Nirnaeth Arnoediad.<sup id="cite_ref-Haladin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Haladin-1">[1]</a></sup>
