**Hending** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hending was the fourth child of [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name in Middle English means "courteous, skillful one".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In *[Gerald of Wales on the Survival of Welsh](/wiki/Gerald_of_Wales_on_the_Survival_of_Welsh)*, Tolkien equates the name "Hending" — nominally the author of a compilation of traditional proverbs that end with "quoth Hending" — with the Welsh "henddyn", the typical wise old man.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
