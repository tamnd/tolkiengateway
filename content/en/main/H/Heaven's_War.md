<i><b>Heaven&#39;s War</b></i> is a fantasy graphic novel by Micah Harris and Michael Gaydos.

The story is set in [1938](/wiki/1938) and features a fictional history in the lives of three [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings): [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams) (the title "Heaven's War" likely is a play on Williams's first novel, *[War in Heaven](/wiki/War_in_Heaven)*), [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") and [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis).

Williams who had been once a member of a Hermetic society, is the main protagonist of the story.

## Editorial synopis {#Editorial_synopis}

<dl><dd><i><a href="/wiki/1938" title="1938">1938</a>: As the world moves toward global war, a secret angelic battle is waged in the heavenly realms to determine mankind&#39;s fate. The infamous Aleister Crowley plans to manipulate those angelic struggles and thus shape the world according to his will. Only &#34;<a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Inklings" title="The Inklings">The Inklings</a>&#34; - fantasy authors <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a>, <a href="/wiki/C.S._Lewis" title="C.S. Lewis">C.S. Lewis</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Charles_Williams" title="Charles Williams">Charles Williams</a> - oppose him. They must decipher a landscape of sacred geometry to intercept Crowley at the threshold of heaven. And, for one of the Inklings, the pursuit will reach outside time itself.</i></dd></dl>
