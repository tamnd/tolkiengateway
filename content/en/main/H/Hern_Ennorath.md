**Hern Ennorath** (Sindarin for *Southern Lands of Middle-earth*) is the Tolkien Fellowship of Australia and New Zealand. It is a [smial](/wiki/Smials "Smials") of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) and consists of the separate fellowships of Canberra, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Adelaide along with members in other parts of Australia and friends from New Zealand. Hern Ennorath meets every month via Zoom, featuring presentations and quiz nights. The fellowships also meet in-person in their local area.

## Ozmoot {#Ozmoot}

Since [2023](/wiki/2023), Hern Ennorath has organised Ozmoot, the regional Australian Tolkien conference in collaboration with The Tolkien Society and [Signum University](/wiki/Corey_Olsen "Corey Olsen"). Ozmoots are held in-person and also online for participants all over Australia and anywhere else.

Past Ozmoots:

- Ozmoot [2023](/wiki/2023): University of Queensland, Brisbane
- Ozmoot [2024](/wiki/2024): Harold Park Community Hall, Sydney
- Ozmoot [2025](/wiki/2025): Richmond Neighbourhood Centre, Melbourne
- Ozmoot [2026](/wiki/2026): Holt Neighbourhood Centre, Canberra
