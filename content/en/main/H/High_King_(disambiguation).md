**High King** was a title used by a ruler who had at least nominal overlordship over other realms:

- [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)"), a title used by the Kings of all the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain).
- [High King of Arda](/wiki/King_of_Arda "King of Arda"), a title of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), the greatest of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).
- [High King of Arnor](/wiki/King_of_Arnor "King of Arnor"), a title used by the rulers of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).
- [High King of the Elves](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Elves), the title of [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB) as the noblest of all the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).
- [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor), a title used by the four leaders of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).
