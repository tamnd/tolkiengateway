**High King** was a title used by a ruler who had at least nominal overlordship over other realms:

- [High King](<High_King_(Dúnedain).md>), a title used by the Kings of all the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md).
- [High King of Arda](../K/King_of_Arda.md), a title of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md), the greatest of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).
- [High King of Arnor](../K/King_of_Arnor.md), a title used by the rulers of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).
- [High King of the Elves](High_King_of_the_Elves.md), the title of [Ingwë](../I/Ingwë.md) as the noblest of all the [Elves](../E/Elves.md).
- [High King of the Noldor](High_King_of_the_Noldor.md), a title used by the four leaders of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).
