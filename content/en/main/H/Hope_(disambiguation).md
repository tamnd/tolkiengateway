**Hope** can refer to several concepts or people:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- *[Estel](../E/Estel.md)*, the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "hope", a name of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and a philosophical concept.
- *[Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Sindarin_word).md>)*, a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "hope", but another concept.
- The [Old Hope](../O/Old_Hope.md), a belief among some [Men](../M/Men.md) after their Fall.
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Harthad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harthad (page does not exist)">Harthad</a></i>, a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) term for "hope".
