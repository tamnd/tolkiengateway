**Hope** can refer to several concepts or people:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- *[Estel](/wiki/Estel)*, the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "hope", a name of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and a philosophical concept.
- *[Amdir](</wiki/Amdir_(Sindarin_word)> "Amdir (Sindarin word)")*, a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "hope", but another concept.
- The [Old Hope](/wiki/Old_Hope), a belief among some [Men](/wiki/Men) after their Fall.
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Harthad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harthad (page does not exist)">Harthad</a></i>, a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) term for "hope".

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[Hope and Memory](/wiki/Hope_and_Memory)*, a scene in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.
    - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hope_and_Memory_(Soundtrack)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hope and Memory (Soundtrack) (page does not exist)">Hope and Memory (Soundtrack)</a></i>, the accompanying music track.
- *[Hope Fails](/wiki/Hope_Fails)*, a scene in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.
    - <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hope_Fails_(Soundtrack)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hope Fails (Soundtrack) (page does not exist)">Hope Fails (Soundtrack)</a></i>, the accompanying music track.
- "The Hidden Hope", the seventh book of *[The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Shadows_of_Angmar)*.
- *[Born of Hope](/wiki/Born_of_Hope)*, a fan film.
