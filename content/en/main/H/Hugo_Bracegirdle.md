**Hugo Bracegirdle** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hugo was the eldest child of [Bruno Bracegirdle](/wiki/Bruno_Bracegirdle), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1350](/wiki/Third_Age_2950 "Third Age 2950").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In his lifetime, he was notorious for borrowing books from [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), though he rarely returned them. In [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") he was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party), after which Bilbo left Hugo an empty bookcase in which he could store all those books.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) likened himself to Hugo Bracegirdle.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
