**Henneth Annûn** was a hidden [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") outpost north-east of [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) in North [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) son of Steward [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") had his base of operations there, and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) were taken there by his company.

This secret dwelling consisted of a cave behind a west-facing waterfall, the Window-curtain, overlooking an oval [pool](/wiki/Forbidden_Pool "Forbidden Pool"). The cave had been excavated by the stream feeding the waterfall, which originally fell from the hole in the cliff constituting the window in the name, but that stream had since been diverted and the tunnel sealed, save for a concealed entrance.<sup id="cite_ref-Window_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Window-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the days of [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) most of the remaining people of Ithilien departed due to the influx of [Mordor-orcs](/wiki/Mordor-orcs). In response, he had many secret outposts and refuges established for [his soldiers](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2901](/wiki/Third_Age_2901 "Third Age 2901")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Henneth Annûn was constructed. It remained the longest guarded and manned.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [1 March](/wiki/1_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") Faramir left [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to go to Henneth Annûn to prepare a raid against enemy soldiers marching north through Ithilien. When the raid occurred on [7 March](/wiki/7_March) Faramir also met Frodo and Sam, and afterwards brought them to the hidden cave.<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/18/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="222" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/18/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/18/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Window_on_the_West.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="946"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a> - <i>The Window on the West</i></figcaption></figure>

Frodo and Sam were led blindfolded into the cave and had their eyes uncovered just as sunset shone through the curtain of water. Faramir fed and spoke with the two travellers, gradually gaining their trust until Sam accidentally revealed Frodo's possession of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"). However, Faramir resisted any temptation to seize it for himself. Later in the night, [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) came to the Forbidden Pool beneath the cave. In order to save his life, Frodo had to draw in the creature until Faramir's men could capture him. Frodo convinced Faramir to spare Gollum even though Faramir had his doubts.

On the morning of [8 March](/wiki/8_March), Frodo, Sam, and Gollum were allowed to depart from Henneth Annûn to continue their journey into [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). The [next day](/wiki/9_March "9 March"), Faramir and his men vacated the refuge;<sup id="cite_ref-Great_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-5">[5]</a></sup> three followed their captain to Minas Tirith while the remainder of the company rode to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The refuge remained abandoned during the rest of the War of the Ring.
