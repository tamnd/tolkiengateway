**Hundad** was the son of [Hundar](Hundar.md), who was of the [House of Haleth](House_of_Haleth.md), and the father of [Hardang](Hardang.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was said that neither Hundad nor his son Hardang were men of mark or gallantry.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
