The **house of the Fountain** was one of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](/wiki/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim "Twelve houses of the Gondothlim"), the kindreds of the elven city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Their story is recounted in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

Their lord was [Ecthelion of the Fountain](/wiki/Ecthelion "Ecthelion"), and they lived in the southern part of the city, near the [Fountains of the South](/wiki/Fountains_of_the_South).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:181</sup> They were said to find delight in silver and diamonds, and their swords were long, bright, and pale. They went into battle accompanied by the sweet music of their flutes.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), they were held in reserve by King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), who watched the battle from [his tower](/wiki/Tower_of_the_King "Tower of the King"). After the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) made a breach in the wall and other houses had been defeated near the northern gate, the enemy sent messengers to its troops, informing them to hold what they had won. Then a sweet music sounded amid the host of the Gondothlim, as the people of the Fountain came to battle playing their flutes, led by their lord. The music ceased suddenly and the army attacked at the shout of Ecthelion.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:180</sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>&#39;Tis said that Ecthelion&#39;s folk there slew more of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Goblins" title="Goblins">goblins</a> than fell ever in all the battles of the <a href="/wiki/Eldali%C3%AB" title="Eldalië">Eldalië</a> with that race, and that his name is a terror among them to this latest day, and a warcry to the Eldar.
</p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

[Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and the [house of the Wing](/wiki/House_of_the_Wing "House of the Wing") joined the fight alongside them, and they harried the attacking Orcs until they almost reached the gate. However, some [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") opened a path from [Amon Gwareth](/wiki/Amon_Gwareth) and knocked down walls and ward-towers, opening a gap in the walls of that part of the city. Tuor gathered bands of other houses to fight against the oncoming enemies, while on his right Ecthelion rallied his people. Tuor and Ecthelion killed many Balrogs and Orc captains, but the enemy outnumbered them; Ecthelion was injured and they had to retreat.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:180–1</sup>

Tuor carried Ecthelion to the [Square of the Folkwell](/wiki/Square_of_the_Folkwell), where the guard of the Fountain and other houses were scattered, forming a good battalion. They were led by Tuor to the [Square of the King](/wiki/Square_of_the_King), and there they resisted with the remnants of all the other kindreds (except of the [Hammer of Wrath](/wiki/House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath "House of the Hammer of Wrath"), who had been cut down in a charge outside the city).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182</sup> After Ecthelion died defeating the balrog [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") at the [Fountain of the King](/wiki/Fountain_of_the_King), there is no mention of what happened to his house, who either were dispersed or joined the [exiles](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin").

## Other names {#Other_names}

Their [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name was <i><b>Thlim Ecthel</b></i>, while in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) they were simply known as <i><b>Kapalindar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
