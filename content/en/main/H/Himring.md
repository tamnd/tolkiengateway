**Himring** was a hill in the northeast of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). It was east of the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon) and west of [Maglor's Gap](/wiki/Maglor%27s_Gap).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The greatest hill of that region, it was wide-shouldered, treeless, with a flattened summit, and surrounded by numerous lesser hills.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [drowning of](/wiki/War_of_Wrath "War of Wrath") [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the peak of Himring remained above the waves.<sup id="cite_ref-UTIMap_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIMap-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### The fortress of Maedhros {#The_fortress_of_Maedhros}

The fortress on Himring was built when the Sons of Fëanor went east shortly after [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros), eldest of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) waived his claim as [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) in favor of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It was the chief fortress of Maedhros, from which he guarded the north-eastern border region that became known as the [March of Maedhros](/wiki/March_of_Maedhros).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-2">[2]</a></sup>

The fortress stood firm through the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), and many survivors from the surrounding regions, including Maedhros' brother [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), rallied there. In the battle the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) had taken the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon), but Maedhros was later able to retake the Pass using the forces from Himring.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Himring's fortress remained for centuries, until the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad); where the sons of Fëanor forsook it.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After the Nirnaeth, the hill of Himring became garrisoned by soldiers of Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

### The Island {#The_Island}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png/300px-Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="128" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png/450px-Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png 1.5x, /w/images/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Location_of_Tol_Morwen.png 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="214"/></a><figcaption>Remnants of sunken Beleriand: the islands west of Forlindon, Tol Himling, <a href="/wiki/Tol_Fuin" title="Tol Fuin">Tol Fuin</a> and (speculatively) <a href="/wiki/Tol_Morwen" title="Tol Morwen">Tol Morwen</a>.</figcaption></figure>

After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), when the western lands were flooded, the plains about the hill were drowned and the top of the hill was all that remained of Himring. Left standing as an island, Himring lay off the north-west coast, about twenty-five miles out from the shores of northern [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).<sup id="cite_ref-UTIMap_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIMap-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Literally, the name means "cool-cold".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Himring is translated as "Ever-cold".<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On the original maps that accompany *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the island was labelled *Himling*, since this was Tolkien's early name for what later would become *Himring* in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-UTIMap_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTIMap-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
