**Haudh in Gwanûr** was the burial mound of [Folcred](/wiki/Folcred) and [Fastred](</wiki/Fastred_(son_of_Folcwine)> "Fastred (son of Folcwine)"), erected at their death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885")<sup id="cite_ref-TA2885_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2885-1">[1]</a></sup> high upon the shore of the river [Poros](/wiki/Poros).<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the days of [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards "Ruling Stewards") [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) had occupied [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor) and there were many armed conflicts along the river Poros.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885") emissaries of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) stirred up the Haradrim who crossed the Poros invaded Ithilien<sup id="cite_ref-TA2885_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2885-1">[1]</a></sup> with a large army.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> When King [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) heard of the invasion he fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl)<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> and sent many men led by his twin sons Folcred and Fastred to help Túrin II.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-3">[3]</a></sup>

Túrin II defeated the Haradrim invaders with the help of the troops from Rohan in a [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Crossings_of_Poros "Battle of the Crossings of Poros") at the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros),<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup> but Folcred and Fastred fell side by side in battle in Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-3">[3]</a></sup>

They were buried together high upon the shore of the river Poros according to the customs of their [people](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") in a burial mound, which was called *Haudh in Gwanûr*. The mound stood there for a long time and the enemies of Gondor were afraid to pass the mound.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Haudh in Gwanûr* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-4">[4]</a></sup> Its meaning is not glossed, but it probably means "Burial Mound of the Twins".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[haudh](/wiki/Haudh "Haudh")* ("mound in sward"),<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-4">[4]</a></sup> the plural *in* of the definite article *i*<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> and *[gwanûr](/wiki/Gwan%C3%BBr "Gwanûr")* ("pair of twins").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-4">[4]</a></sup> In editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* prior to the [2004 edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)"), the circumflex (^) was omitted.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Haudh-in-Gwanûr</i> is a card, where players face an attack by the undead. Harondor is written on the right hand side below the bottom of the drawing of Haudh-in-Gwanûr.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1987" title="1987">1987</a>-<a href="/wiki/1996" title="1996">96</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <i><a href="/wiki/Sea-Lords_of_Gondor" title="Sea-Lords of Gondor">Sea-Lords of Gondor</a> - Pelargir and Lebennin</i> campaign module contains a detailed map that includes Ithilien. This map shows that Haudh-in-Gwanûr is on the north bank of the river Poros.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In <i><a href="/wiki/Southern_Gondor:_The_Land" title="Southern Gondor: The Land">Southern Gondor: The Land</a></i> it is mentioned that Haudh-in-Gwanur is on the north bank of the river Poros at Athrad Poros and that the river Poros forms the border between Harondor and Ithilien. As a consequence, Haudh-in-Gwanur is probably in Ithilien rather than in Harondor. In addition, it is mentioned that orcs from <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> cast down the tall stone that crowned the mound in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2954" title="Third Age 2954">2954</a> and that Princess <a href="/wiki/%C3%89owyn" title="Éowyn">Éowyn</a> of Ithilien caused the standing stone of Haudh-in-Gwanur to be restored soon after the War of the Ring.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In <i><a href="/wiki/Southern_Gondor:_The_People" title="Southern Gondor: The People">Southern Gondor: The People</a></i> it is mentioned that the men of the city of <i>Amrûn</i> cast down the standing stone of Haudh-in-Gwanur at Athrad Poros in the year <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2954" title="Third Age 2954">2954</a>.</dd></dl>
