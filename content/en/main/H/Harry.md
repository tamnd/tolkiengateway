**Harry Cooper**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was one of the children who attended [The Feast of Good Children](/wiki/The_Feast_of_Good_Children) when [Smith Smithson](/wiki/Smith_Smithson) ate the [Fay-star](/wiki/Fay-star) that had been put in the [Great Cake](/wiki/Great_Cake). Long after he had retired, the [Master Cook](/wiki/Master_Cook) at that occasion, [Nokes](/wiki/Nokes), was trying to figure out who had swallowed the Fay-star and he remembered Harry, the Cooper's son. Nokes claimed that Harry was "a barrel of a boy" who had a fat mouth similar to a frog. [Alf](/wiki/Alf), who had been Nokes' apprentice at the time, reprovingly said that Harry was a nice boy with a large friendly grin. He had not eaten the star because he had carefully cut his slice of the Cake to pieces before eating and had found nothing.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

### Roverandom {#Roverandom}

In the novella, *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*, Harry was the manager of the toy shop who told an employee to mark [Rover](/wiki/Rover) for sixpence before putting on the window display.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Tales and Songs of Bimble Bay {#Tales_and_Songs_of_Bimble_Bay}

In the poem, *[William and the Bumpus](/wiki/William_and_the_Bumpus)*, "Harry and [his father](/wiki/Harry%27s_father "Harry's father")" were two inhabitants of [Bimble Bay](/wiki/Bimble_Bay) who fled for "their lives" after howling in fear upon seeing the [Bumpus](/wiki/Bumpus) peeping over the walls of the market-place. In early [1961](/wiki/1961),<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-4">[4]</a></sup> when Tolkien revised the poem for inclusion within *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)*, the characters of "Harry and his father" were merged into [Grip](</wiki/Grip_(Bill_Butcher%27s_dog)> "Grip (Bill Butcher's dog)"), [Bill Butcher](/wiki/Bill_Butcher)'s [dog](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs").<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup>
