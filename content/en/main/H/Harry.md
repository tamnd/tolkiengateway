**Harry Cooper**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was one of the children who attended [The Feast of Good Children](../T/The_Feast_of_Good_Children.md) when [Smith Smithson](../S/Smith_Smithson.md) ate the [Fay-star](../F/Fay-star.md) that had been put in the [Great Cake](../G/Great_Cake.md). Long after he had retired, the [Master Cook](../M/Master_Cook.md) at that occasion, [Nokes](../N/Nokes.md), was trying to figure out who had swallowed the Fay-star and he remembered Harry, the Cooper's son. Nokes claimed that Harry was "a barrel of a boy" who had a fat mouth similar to a frog. [Alf](../A/Alf.md), who had been Nokes' apprentice at the time, reprovingly said that Harry was a nice boy with a large friendly grin. He had not eaten the star because he had carefully cut his slice of the Cake to pieces before eating and had found nothing.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

### Roverandom {#Roverandom}

In the novella, *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)*, Harry was the manager of the toy shop who told an employee to mark [Rover](../R/Rover.md) for sixpence before putting on the window display.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Tales and Songs of Bimble Bay {#Tales_and_Songs_of_Bimble_Bay}

In the poem, *[William and the Bumpus](../W/William_and_the_Bumpus.md)*, "Harry and [his father](Harry's_father.md)" were two inhabitants of [Bimble Bay](../B/Bimble_Bay.md) who fled for "their lives" after howling in fear upon seeing the [Bumpus](../B/Bumpus.md) peeping over the walls of the market-place. In early [1961](../_/1961.md),<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-4">[4]</a></sup> when Tolkien revised the poem for inclusion within *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)*, the characters of "Harry and his father" were merged into [Grip](<../G/Grip_(Bill_Butcher's_dog).md>), [Bill Butcher](../B/Bill_Butcher.md)'s [dog](../D/Dogs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-commentary_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-commentary-5">[5]</a></sup>
