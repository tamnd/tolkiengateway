<i><b>half</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning "seashell".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Derived from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *syalmā*, root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SYAL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SYAL (page does not exist)">SYAL-</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
