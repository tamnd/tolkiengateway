**Helevorn** was the deep, mountain-shaded lake<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in northern [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) at the foot of [Mount Rerir](/wiki/Mount_Rerir). A stream flowed from its southwest corner into the [Greater Gelion](/wiki/Greater_Gelion).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the return of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the people of [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) dwelt about the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Its waters were defiled by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) ("Battle of Sudden Flame").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It was destroyed in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) along with [Mount Rerir](/wiki/Mount_Rerir), though it partially survived.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Helevorn* means "Black Glass" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It was probably translated from an earlier [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) name such as "*Narag-zâram*".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
