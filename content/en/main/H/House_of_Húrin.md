The **House of Húrin**, also known as the **Hurinionath**,<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> were the descendants of [Húrin of Emyn Arnen](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_of_Emyn_Arnen), the [Steward](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") to King [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

This ancient family of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) used (beside the [Common Speech](/wiki/Common_Speech)) the [Sindarin tongue after the fashion of Gondor](/wiki/Gondor_Sindarin "Gondor Sindarin"). Their official names (after [Mardil Voronwë](/wiki/Mardil "Mardil")) were in that tongue and drawn mostly from the ancient legends of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Húrin and his House were noted as being of high [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") blood,<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-2">[2]</a></sup> for they were ultimately of [royal origin](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion "House of Anárion") (they had some share in the actual blood of Elendil and Anarion), and had kept their blood more pure than most other families in the later ages.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> Húrin himself was of ultimately royal descent and a kinsman to his King, Minardil.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Húrin's time it became traditional for the Steward to be chosen from among his descendants, and in the time of [Vorondil](/wiki/Vorondil), who succeeded his father [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur), the post became hereditary. For this, the House was also known as **House of the Stewards**.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-2">[2]</a></sup> Vorondil's [horn](/wiki/Great_Horn "Great Horn") became a hereditary heirloom, carried by the heir to the House.

After the loss of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)'s last King, [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur), the Stewards of the House of Húrin [took up the rule](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") of the [South-kingdom](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor"). That was until after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) when [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), the heir of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor_II), became [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien) and [Lord of Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Lord_of_Emyn_Arnen) (the lands of the House's patriarch).<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

Showing the descendants of Húrin of Emyn Arnen: First the hereditary Stewards of the Kings of Gondor, then the Ruling Stewards, and lastly the Princes of Ithilien (who again served as Stewards to the restored line of Kings). All information in the tree is from Appendix A ("The Southern Line: Heirs of Anarion"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or "The Stewards"<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-2">[2]</a></sup>) or (mostly for dates of birth) *The Peoples of Middle-earth*, "The Heirs of Elendil",<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> unless otherwise specified. The figures after the names are those of birth and death where recorded. A dagger symbol, '†' signifies those who did not die of old age. A name preceded by '\*' indicates one of the Ruling Stewards; an *italicized* name denotes a [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien). A dashed line indicates marriage or a line of descent with some members missing.
