**Hannar** was one of the four Dwarves that stayed at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) after [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md)' birthday party.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the early writings of the chapter "[A Long-expected Party](../A/A_Long-expected_Party.md)", Hannar was given some dialogue and a bit more development. However, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) later changed his mind, and did not disclose his name in the book nor gave him any dialogue.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hanarr* and *Hannarr* are dwarfs from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. Both their names mean "Skilled".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
