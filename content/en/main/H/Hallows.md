The **Hallows** or **the Tombs** were a place in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) where [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor), [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor"), and other important men of the realm were buried, containing the [House of the Kings](/wiki/House_of_the_Kings) and the [House of the Stewards](/wiki/House_of_the_Stewards).

They were built on a mountain shoulder that rose to the height of the fifth wall of the city and connected Mount [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) with the [Hill of Guard](/wiki/Hill_of_Guard)<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> on which Minas Tirith was built<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

[Borondir](/wiki/Borondir), a soldier of Gondor whose deeds in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant) earned him great renown, was laid in tomb in the Hallows after his death in that battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

Sometime later, after the foundation of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) relocated the remains of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) to the Hallows (until then being buried on [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien)).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the Steward [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") brought his son [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) to the Hallows where he intended to burn both of them alive. The guard [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) tried to save Faramir but was held up by Denethor's servants. Beregond had killed two of the servants in the Hallows before [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), alerted by [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), came and rescued Faramir, though Denethor succeeded in taking his own life.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) was temporarily placed in the Hallows after his death until his body was brought back to Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and Peregrin Took of the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") were also given a place there after their deaths, beside the tomb of King [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hallows* means "sacred place" and is a translation of an unknown [Gondor Sindarin](/wiki/Gondor_Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
