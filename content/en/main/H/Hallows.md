The **Hallows** or **the Tombs** were a place in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) where [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md), and other important men of the realm were buried, containing the [House of the Kings](House_of_the_Kings.md) and the [House of the Stewards](House_of_the_Stewards.md).

They were built on a mountain shoulder that rose to the height of the fifth wall of the city and connected Mount [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md) with the [Hill of Guard](Hill_of_Guard.md)<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> on which Minas Tirith was built<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

[Borondir](../B/Borondir.md), a soldier of Gondor whose deeds in the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md) earned him great renown, was laid in tomb in the Hallows after his death in that battle.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

Sometime later, after the foundation of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) relocated the remains of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) to the Hallows (until then being buried on [Halifirien](Halifirien.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [15 March](../_/15_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the Steward [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md) brought his son [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) to the Hallows where he intended to burn both of them alive. The guard [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md) tried to save Faramir but was held up by Denethor's servants. Beregond had killed two of the servants in the Hallows before [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), alerted by [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), came and rescued Faramir, though Denethor succeeded in taking his own life.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) was temporarily placed in the Hallows after his death until his body was brought back to Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and Peregrin Took of the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) were also given a place there after their deaths, beside the tomb of King [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hallows* means "sacred place" and is a translation of an unknown [Gondor Sindarin](../G/Gondor_Sindarin.md) name.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
