**Haleth** was the daughter of [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) and the twin sister of [Haldar](/wiki/Haldar). In time she became the leader of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), one of the [Three Houses of the Edain](/wiki/Edain#The_Three_Houses "Edain").

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [312](/wiki/First_Age_312 "First Age 312"), the Haladin crossed the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), the second group of [Men](/wiki/Men) to do so. They settled in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion), the land of the [Elf](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") Lord [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir). Though they dwelled in Caranthir's land, they did not swear allegiance to him and remained free men. After her father and her twin brother were slain in [a raid](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade") by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), Haleth became [Chieftain of the Haladin](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin). She kept her people alive for seven days while the Orcs laid siege to the encampment, until Caranthir finally arrived with reinforcements. Impressed with her bravery, he offered her a fiefdom in his land and the protection of his soldiers. Haleth, however, was proud and refused his offer.

Haleth instead led her people to the relative safety of the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil) in the lands of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol). In exchange for defending the [Crossing of Teiglin](/wiki/Crossing_of_Teiglin), King [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) made use of his friendship with Thingol to allow the Haladin to remain freemen. Years later, when Haleth died, her people raised a monument to her, the *[Haudh-en-Arwen](/wiki/Haudh-en-Arwen)*. While her father had been elected Chief of his people, the Haladin chose to establish a ruling line from the descendants of Haldad. Because Haleth did not marry and had no children, her brother's son [Haldan](/wiki/Haldan) became Chieftain of the Haladin.

The Haladin remained in the Forest of Brethil for the duration of the First Age, and in time were known as the **[House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Haleth* has no known meaning. In the language of the Haladin, *hal* means "chief" or "guard". [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) also has the feminine ending *-eth*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [The Quenta](/wiki/The_Quenta), **Haleth the Hunter** was the son of [Hador](/wiki/Hador) who first appears as one of the Fathers of Men.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, he appeared as the leader of one of the Three Houses of Men,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until his character was reworked into Lady Haleth.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
