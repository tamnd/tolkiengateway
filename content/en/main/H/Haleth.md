**Haleth** was the daughter of [Haldad](Haldad.md) and the twin sister of [Haldar](Haldar.md). In time she became the leader of the [Haladin](Haladin.md), one of the [Three Houses of the Edain](../E/Edain.md#The_Three_Houses).

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [312](../F/First_Age_312.md), the Haladin crossed the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), the second group of [Men](../M/Men.md) to do so. They settled in [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md), the land of the [Elf](../N/Noldor.md) Lord [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md). Though they dwelled in Caranthir's land, they did not swear allegiance to him and remained free men. After her father and her twin brother were slain in [a raid](../B/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade~66779.md) by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), Haleth became [Chieftain of the Haladin](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin.md). She kept her people alive for seven days while the Orcs laid siege to the encampment, until Caranthir finally arrived with reinforcements. Impressed with her bravery, he offered her a fiefdom in his land and the protection of his soldiers. Haleth, however, was proud and refused his offer.

Haleth instead led her people to the relative safety of the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md) in the lands of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md). In exchange for defending the [Crossing of Teiglin](../C/Crossing_of_Teiglin.md), King [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) made use of his friendship with Thingol to allow the Haladin to remain freemen. Years later, when Haleth died, her people raised a monument to her, the *[Haudh-en-Arwen](Haudh-en-Arwen.md)*. While her father had been elected Chief of his people, the Haladin chose to establish a ruling line from the descendants of Haldad. Because Haleth did not marry and had no children, her brother's son [Haldan](Haldan.md) became Chieftain of the Haladin.

The Haladin remained in the Forest of Brethil for the duration of the First Age, and in time were known as the **[House of Haleth](House_of_Haleth.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Haleth* has no known meaning. In the language of the Haladin, *hal* means "chief" or "guard". [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) also has the feminine ending *-eth*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [The Quenta](../T/The_Quenta.md), **Haleth the Hunter** was the son of [Hador](Hador.md) who first appears as one of the Fathers of Men.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, he appeared as the leader of one of the Three Houses of Men,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> until his character was reworked into Lady Haleth.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
