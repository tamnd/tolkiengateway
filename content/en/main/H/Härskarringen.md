<i><b>Härskarringen</b></i> ("The Ruling Ring") is the name of [Åke Ohlmarks](/wiki/%C3%85ke_Ohlmarks)'s translation into Swedish of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## History {#History}

While the work as a whole is popularly known as <i><b>Sagan om ringen</b></i> in Sweden (and sometimes also titled so by the publishers), this is properly the title of the translation (published in [1959](/wiki/1959)) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. In [1960](/wiki/1960) followed <i><b>Sagan om de två tornen</b></i> (*[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*), and in [1961](/wiki/1961) <i><b>Sagan om konungens återkomst</b></i> (*[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*).<sup id="cite_ref-Beregond_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beregond-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Härskarringen* was the second translation of *The Lord of the Rings* (the first being the Dutch translation by [Max Schuchart](/wiki/Max_Schuchart), *In de Ban van de Ring*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A second Swedish translation of *The Lord of the Rings* came in 2004-5: *Ringarnas herre*, translated by [Erik Andersson](/wiki/Erik_Andersson).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
