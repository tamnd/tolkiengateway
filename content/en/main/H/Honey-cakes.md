The **honey-cakes of the Beornings**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> were a type of waybread baked by [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) himself<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> and later by the [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> Although their exact recipe was a secret, it is known that they were twice-baked and made with honey. They remained good to eat for a long time and were very nutritious and quite tasty, although they caused thirst.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Beorn provided [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) for their journey through [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) with honey-cakes, alongside other provisions.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the Beornings were not very willing to share their cakes with travellers, although [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) had tasted their honey-cakes and considered the Beornings the best bakers that he knew of. However, after tasting [Elvish](../E/Elves.md) *[lembas](../L/Lembas.md)* he decided that *lembas* was even better.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
