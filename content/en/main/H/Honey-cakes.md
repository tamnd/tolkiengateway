The **honey-cakes of the Beornings**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> were a type of waybread baked by [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) himself<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup> and later by the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> Although their exact recipe was a secret, it is known that they were twice-baked and made with honey. They remained good to eat for a long time and were very nutritious and quite tasty, although they caused thirst.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Beorn provided [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) for their journey through [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) with honey-cakes, alongside other provisions.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the Beornings were not very willing to share their cakes with travellers, although [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) had tasted their honey-cakes and considered the Beornings the best bakers that he knew of. However, after tasting [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") *[lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas")* he decided that *lembas* was even better.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
