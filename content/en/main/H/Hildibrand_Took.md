**Hildibrand Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hildibrand was a son of [the Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"). He also had a son, [Sigismond](/wiki/Sigismond_Took "Sigismond Took"), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1290](/wiki/Third_Age_2890 "Third Age 2890").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name contains *hild* "battle" and *brand* "sword".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hildebrand" title="wikipedia:Hildebrand">Hildebrand</a> is a figure from German legends. <span></span>
