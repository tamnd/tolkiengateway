**Halfast "Hal" Gamgee** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Halfast was the son of [Halfred](/wiki/Halfred_of_Overhill "Halfred of Overhill"), an uncle of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He worked for [Mr. Boffin](/wiki/Boffin_Family "Boffin Family") in [Overhill](/wiki/Overhill), and often went up in the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) to hunt.

He had the reputation for his wild stories of things he saw. He reportedly encountered a "[Tree-man](/wiki/Tree-men "Tree-men")" while hunting beyond the [North Moors](/wiki/North_Moors).

Sam mentioned this gossip to [Ted Sandyman](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman) while discussing the queer news heard in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name likely comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *hālfæst* meaning "hale and firm".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
