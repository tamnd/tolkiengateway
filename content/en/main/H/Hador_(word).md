<i><b>hador</b></i> or <i><b>hadron</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "thrower of spears or darts". The word could also appear as a personal name, <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Hador_(disambiguation)" title="Hador (disambiguation)">Hador</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") continued to use the name *Hador* in the writings of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") after the evolution from Noldorin to [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> although it is unknown if he intended the Noldorin meaning to be retained.

In both [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo)'s and [Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis)'s [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish") wordlists, *hador* and *hadron* ("thrower") appear as valid Sindarin forms.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
