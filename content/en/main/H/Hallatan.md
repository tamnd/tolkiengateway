**Hallatan** was the second child and only son of [Cemendur](</wiki/Cemendur_(son_of_Axantur)> "Cemendur (son of Axantur)"), a minor noble of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). He was recorded as owning land in the region of [Hyarastorni](/wiki/Hyarastorni), near the borders of [Emerië](/wiki/Emeri%C3%AB).

Hallatan was respected by the people of [Mittalmar](/wiki/Mittalmar) and represented the region in the [Council of the Sceptre](/wiki/Council_of_the_Sceptre).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was the only Council member who supported [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) in his decision to resign the Sceptre to his son [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) in 883, because of his high esteem for the king's son.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, he served as the first [Regent of Númenor](/wiki/Regent_of_Gondor "Regent of Gondor") when [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion) traveled to Middle-earth in 883 or 884.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Hallatan would become a member of the royal family when his son [Hallacar](/wiki/Hallacar) married [Tar-Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB), the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Due to his ownership of herds of sheep Hallatan acquired the epithet "Sheep-lord".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hallatan* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Tall Man" from *[halla](/wiki/Halla "Halla")* ("tall") and *[Atan](/wiki/Atani "Atani")* ("[Man](/wiki/Man)").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
