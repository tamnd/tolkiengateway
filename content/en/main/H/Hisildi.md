The **Hisildi** was the name of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who chose to stay in [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor) near [Koivië-néni](/wiki/Koivi%C3%AB-n%C3%A9ni), and became followers of the [fay](/wiki/Fay "Fay") [Tû](</wiki/T%C3%BB_(king_of_the_Hisildi)> "Tû (king of the Hisildi)"), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

They were a subset of [Ilkorindi](/wiki/Ilkorindi), the Elves that had never reached [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r).<sup id="cite_ref-Nuin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nuin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Tû and the Hisildi {#Tû_and_the_Hisildi}

The [fay](/wiki/Fay "Fay") [Tû](</wiki/T%C3%BB_(king_of_the_Hisildi)> "Tû (king of the Hisildi)"), a wizard more capable of performing acts of [magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic") than any other being, save except those living in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), was also a wanderer, and in one of his wanderings he came across the [Ilkorindi](/wiki/Ilkorindi), the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor). The Elves living there were immediately drawn to him, as he had taught them, and instructed them in deep knowledge - eventually, he became a mighty king among them.

The Ilkorin tales later called him the *[Lord of Gloaming](/wiki/Lord_of_Gloaming)*, and all the Ilkorindi under his rule were called <i><b>Hisildi</b></i> or the *twilight people*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nuin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nuin-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Nuin and the Awakening of Men {#Nuin_and_the_Awakening_of_Men}

One particularly famous Hisildë was a wise Elf named [Nuin](/wiki/Nuin). He was the one who stumbled upon the first [Men](/wiki/Men) in [Murmenalda](/wiki/Murmenalda) while they were still asleep, awaiting their [Awakening](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men").<sup id="cite_ref-Nuin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nuin-1">[1]</a></sup>

He played an important role in the development of humanity, since he was the one who thought them speech - hence his nickname the *Father of Speech*. He was the one who awoke [Ermon](/wiki/Ermon) and [Elmir](/wiki/Elmir), the first Men to awaken, after which the rest of Men learned to speak; and they spread all over Palisor, gaining the knowledge of the Hisildi, and from there went in all directions of the world - and a great age passed.<sup id="cite_ref-Gil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gil-2">[2]</a></sup>

### The War of Palisor {#The_War_of_Palisor}

However, a servant of [Melko](/wiki/Melko) called [Fankil](/wiki/Fankil), along with [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins) and [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> alienated most of the Men against the Elves. And in the first war between Goblins and Elves, called the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=War_of_Palisor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="War of Palisor (page does not exist)">War of Palisor</a>, of Men only the folk of Ermon sided with the Elves. The famed Nuin also fought there, but in the end he died at the hands of Goblins "through the treachery of Men".<sup id="cite_ref-Gil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gil-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Fortunately, one Ilkorin called [Tareg](/wiki/Tareg) evidently managed to escape, and hearing a rumour of the returned [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) fighting Melko in the North-west, he immediately decided to march there.

Finally, of Tû, the fay-king of the Hisildi, it is said that after the proliferation of Men and their growth in stature, he "faded before the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and hid in the bottomless caverns".<sup id="cite_ref-Gil_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gil-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Hisildi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). It is glossed as "Twilight People, Dark Elves", possibly being an elaboration on <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=H%C3%ADs%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hísë (page does not exist)">hísë</a></i> ("dusk").<sup id="cite_ref-His_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-His-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In some of the earlier outlines of the story of the Hisildi, several other alternative names were given for them: *Humarni*, *Kaliondi* and *Lómëarni*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

The names *Humarni* and *Kaliondi* are in Qenya, but their meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-His_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-His-4">[3]</a></sup>

The name *Lómëarni* is likewise in Qenya. The first part of the name, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%B3mear&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lómear (page does not exist)">lómear</a></i>, is glossed as "Child of Gloom" in the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*, a combination of *[lómë](/wiki/L%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Lómë")* ("gloom") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ar_(Qenya_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ar (Qenya word) (page does not exist)">ar</a></i> ("child"); with *Lómëarni* being the plural form of *Lómëar*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
