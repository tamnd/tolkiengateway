**Horn** can refer to:

- [Horn](<Horn_(rider_of_Rohan).md>) (died [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)), a Rider of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) who fell in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).
- <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Horn.md" title="Horn">Horns</a>, musical instruments:
    - [Great Horn](../G/Great_Horn.md), the horn of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md).
    - [Horn-call of Buckland](Horn-call_of_Buckland.md), a signal to [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) that there were invaders.
    - [Horn of the Mark](Horn_of_the_Mark.md) given to [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md).
    - [Valaróma](../V/Valaróma.md), the horn of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md).
    - [Windbeam](../W/Windbeam.md), the Horn of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) in an early draft.
- [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), one of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), whose name means "horn-blowing".
- *[ras](../R/Ras.md)*, or "horn", a type of mountain:
    - [Starkhorn](../S/Starkhorn.md), a mountain to the south of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).
    - [Redhorn](../C/Caradhras.md), or *Caradhras*, the largest of the [Mountains of Moria](../M/Mountains_of_Moria.md).
    - [Methedras](../M/Methedras.md), the most southerly peak of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).
