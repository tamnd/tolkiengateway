**Horn** can refer to:

- [Horn](</wiki/Horn_(rider_of_Rohan)> "Horn (rider of Rohan)") (died [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), a Rider of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) who fell in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).
- <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Horn" title="Horn">Horns</a>, musical instruments:
    - [Great Horn](/wiki/Great_Horn), the horn of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir).
    - [Horn-call of Buckland](/wiki/Horn-call_of_Buckland), a signal to [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) that there were invaders.
    - [Horn of the Mark](/wiki/Horn_of_the_Mark) given to [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck).
    - [Valaróma](/wiki/Valar%C3%B3ma), the horn of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB).
    - [Windbeam](/wiki/Windbeam), the Horn of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) in an early draft.
- [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), one of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), whose name means "horn-blowing".
- *[ras](/wiki/Ras "Ras")*, or "horn", a type of mountain:
    - [Starkhorn](/wiki/Starkhorn), a mountain to the south of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).
    - [Redhorn](/wiki/Caradhras "Caradhras"), or *Caradhras*, the largest of the [Mountains of Moria](/wiki/Mountains_of_Moria).
    - [Methedras](/wiki/Methedras), the most southerly peak of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains).
