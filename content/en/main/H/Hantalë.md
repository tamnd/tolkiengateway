<i><b>hantalë</b></i> (or *hantale*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "Thanksgiving". Note that the word only appears in the compound *[Eruhantalë](/wiki/Eruhantal%C3%AB)* ("Thanksgiving to [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar")").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT43_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word obviously contains the suffix <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-le&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-le (page does not exist)">-le</a></i> which is typically used to derive nouns from verbs.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> If so, the core of the word is a possible verb \**hanta-* "give thanks" to which *-le* is suffixed.

The words *hantale* and \**hanta-* possibly derive from the [root](/wiki/Root "Root") [HAN](/wiki/HAN) "increase, honor".<sup id="cite_ref-VT43_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT43-3">[3]</a></sup>
