**Hildifons Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hildifons was the sixth of the twelve children of [the Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"). He went on a journey outside [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and never returned.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) was young he used to pester [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) for information about [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) who had "gone off", such as his uncle Hildifons.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name contains *hild* "battle" and *fons* "ready".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
