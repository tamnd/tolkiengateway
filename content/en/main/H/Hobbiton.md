**Hobbiton** was a village in the central regions of [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), within the borders of the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

Hobbiton was located on both sides of the [Water](../T/The_Water.md), approximately a mile north-west of the neighbouring village of [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md). The [Bywater Road](../B/Bywater_Road.md) passed through both villages and connected them to the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The dwellings were standard of [Hobbits](Hobbits.md), referred to as [smials](../S/Smials.md) or [Hobbit-holes](Hobbit-holes.md) but there were also buildings of wood, brick, or stone. One of the more prominent buildings in Hobbiton was the [Old Mill](../O/Old_Mill.md) which stood on the north side of the Water, near the bridge, which eventually was demolished. On the south of The Water there were several other dwellings. The [Old Grange](../O/Old_Grange.md) was on the west side of the lane which ran from the bridge to [Hobbiton Hill](Hobbiton_Hill.md).

The Hill overlooked the village and the neighbourhood called <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Underhill_(village)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Underhill (village) (page does not exist)">Underhill</a>. In it was [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md), the ancestral smial of the [Baggins Family](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md) and the famous [Ring-bearers](../R/Ring-bearer.md) [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md). On the southern side of the Hill were three (later more) smaller Hobbit-holes along [Bagshot Row](../B/Bagshot_Row.md).

The village also has their own Post Office.

## History {#History}

Hobbiton was built on the both sides of the Water. [Bungo Baggins](../B/Bungo_Baggins.md) built Bag End in the Hill. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), the Unexpected Party occurred there<sup id="cite_ref-third_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-2">[2]</a></sup> When Bilbo returned the next year he had to deal with an auction of his belongings since he had been presumed dead.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

To celebrate his 111th and [Frodo's](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) 33rd birthdays as well as his departure from the Shire, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md) Bilbo held a [Farewell Party](../F/Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-third_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-third-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Hobbiton was devastated. [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) had named himself Chief and rough-looking [Men](../M/Men.md) had come to Hobbiton on Lotho's invitation. Frodo and his companions were stunned to see their homes, trees and hedges all torn up.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [Scouring of the Shire](../S/Scouring_of_the_Shire.md), Bag End was restored and new holes were dug along Bagshot Row. The new mill was removed. [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) spread [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md)'s [gift of earth](../S/Sam's_garden_box.md) from her orchard around the Shire, paying special attention to Hobbiton and Bywater. He planted the [mallorn](../M/Mallorn.md) [seed](../G/Golden_Tree.md) in the [Party Field](../P/Party_Field.md) by the Hill where the [Party Tree](../P/Party_Tree.md) had once stood. Soon Hobbiton was a peaceful and beautiful village once again.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The ending *-ton*, frequent in English place-names means "town, village".

[David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) has suggested it represents a speculative [Old Hobbitish](../O/Old_English.md) \**[Holbytlatun](Holbytla.md)* "town of hobbits". He also notes that the name suggests two things: It was probably the first or more distinctive settlement of the area (making "town of hobbits" a distinctive name); and that it was given to it by the [Big Folk](../B/Big_Folk.md) (perhaps the [Dúnedain of the North](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
