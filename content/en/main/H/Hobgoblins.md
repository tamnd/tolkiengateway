**Hobgoblin** was a name "*for the larger kinds*"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) found in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). In the [quest](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor "Quest of Erebor") for the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) warns [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) that the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) are "*simply stiff with goblins, hobgoblins, and orcs of the worst description*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The term appears only once in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. It has been speculated that "*the term perhaps, but doubtfully, refers to the large soldier-orcs known as [Uruks](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai")*",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> possibly since Tolkien had not created the latter name by the time of his writing of *The Hobbit*.

In a 1971 letter to [Roger Lancelyn Green](/wiki/Roger_Lancelyn_Green), Tolkien commented that "*the statement that hobgoblins were 'a larger kind' is the reverse of the original truth.*"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in adaptations {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<b>1996, 1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The card &#34;Hobgoblins&#34; was released in two editions: one for the expansion set <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Dragons" title="Middle-earth: The Dragons">Middle-earth: The Dragons</a></i> (card art by Ron Chironna) and one for <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Lidless_Eye" title="Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye">Middle-earth: The Lidless Eye</a></i> (card art by Heather Hudson).</dd></dl>

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Hobgoblins are a breed of large, tusked <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc-kind</a> found in the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Mountains" title="Grey Mountains">Grey Mountains</a>. They are not servants of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> and instead belong to the <b>Frost-horde</b>, followers of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Dragon" title="Dragon">dragon</a> Hrímil Frost-heart. Hrímil was imprisoned in the dungeons of <a href="/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Barad-dûr">Barad-dûr</a> for refusing to give up one of <a href="/wiki/Seven_Rings" title="Seven Rings">Seven Rings</a> that she had swallowed. For this, the Frost-horde and all hobgoblins detest Sauron and his followers, only allying with them briefly as part of deception to further their own plans.</dd></dl>
