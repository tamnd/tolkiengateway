The **House of the Stewards** was a building in the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), situated near the [House of the Kings](/wiki/House_of_the_Kings) on [Rath Dínen](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen).

It was capped by a great dome, and within was a wide vaulted chamber with shrouded walls. This was filled with marble tables upon which were carved forms of sleeping men - the tombs of the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Pyre_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pyre-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was the location where [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") immolated himself on [15 March](/wiki/15_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> causing the building's dome to collapse.<sup id="cite_ref-Pyre_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pyre-2">[2]</a></sup>
