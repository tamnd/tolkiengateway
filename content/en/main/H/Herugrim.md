**Herugrim** was the sword of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) King of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## History {#History}

When Gandalf and his companions arrived at [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld) on March 2, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the sword was in the custody of Théoden's traitorous counsellor [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"). After being healed by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), Théoden reclaimed the sword,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and rode with it to the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) and the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Herugrim* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name meaning "Very fierce or cruel, savage".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> It has been noted that *herugrim* is in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercian_dialect" title="wikipedia:Mercian dialect">Mercian dialect</a> of [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) (the spelling of the "standard" <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Saxon_dialect_(Old_English)" title="wikipedia:West Saxon dialect (Old English)">West Saxon dialect</a> would have been *heorugrim*).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> *Heorugrim* occurs in line 1564 of *Beowulf,* as an adjective applied to the hero in his fight with Grendel's mother; Tolkien translated it as "fell".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the original draft of [Helm's Deep](</wiki/Helm%27s_Deep_(chapter)> "Helm's Deep (chapter)"), Théoden is specifically noted as having used Herugrim in the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg). It is said "Theoden dismounted and fought among his guard. Herugrim sang a song that has long been silent."<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
