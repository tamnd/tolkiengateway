**Horses** were beasts of burden and transport used by [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men) through the ages. Famous among horses were [Nahar](/wiki/Nahar), the steed of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), [Rochallor](/wiki/Rochallor), the warhorse of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), and [Felaróf](/wiki/Felar%C3%B3f), the steed of [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young). The breeding and riding of horses were arts developed to their greatest height, among Men, by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## History {#History}

Horses existed as native creatures in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth); however, there is evidence that some of the breeds in Middle-earth, particularly in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), were enhanced by sires brought from [Aman](/wiki/Aman). Although horses may have roamed freely when [Men](/wiki/Men) awoke with the rising of the Sun, it is unclear if the Elves who stayed in Middle-earth - the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor), and [Avari](/wiki/Avari) - had horses while "Middle-earth lay for the most part in the Sleep of Yavanna."<sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the early days of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), Oromë was heard sounding his horn [Valaróma](/wiki/Valar%C3%B3ma), and riding through Beleriand and over the mountains hunting the fell beasts of Morgoth. He had been riding and hunting there with his folk since before the Firstborn awoke and, of all the Valar, came the most reluctantly to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> But, the Sindar were awed by his splendour and feared him and the "great noise and onrush of Nahar."<sup id="cite_ref-Sindar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindar-1">[1]</a></sup> This does not mean that the early Elves feared horses, as they would have no reason to fear any natural and uncorrupted beast,<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-3">[3]</a></sup> or that they did not keep horses themselves.

In [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), the Elves did ride. [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor)'s son, [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm), "in Valinor was a friend of Oromë, and often followed the Vala's horn."<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamar_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamar-4">[4]</a></sup> [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel), while still in Eldamar, was also mentioned as one who loved to ride and hunt in the forests.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamar_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamar-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) brought some of their horses, which flourished in [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen), from Valinor with them on the ships that sailed to [Losgar](/wiki/Losgar):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>From few their horses had increased swiftly, and the grass of Ard-galen was rich and green. Of these horses many of the sires came from Valinor, and they were given to Fingolfin by Maedhros in atonement of his losses, for they had been carried by ship to Losgar.
</p>

Tolkien</a>, <a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a> (ed.), <i><a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, &#34;<i><a href="/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></i>: <a href="/wiki/Of_Beleriand_and_its_Realms" title="Of Beleriand and its Realms">Of Beleriand and its Realms</a>&#34;</cite></p>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

The Rohirrim believed their noble horse line, the *[Mearas](/wiki/Mearas)*, must have had a sire that was brought by the hunter [Béma](/wiki/B%C3%A9ma) (Oromë) from the West.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-5">[5]</a></sup>

The horses of Elves were ridden bitless, with only decorative headstalls and saddles, or wholly without tack,<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Flight_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L211_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L211-7">[7]</a></sup> and the *Mearas*, like elf horses, also seemed to prefer being ridden thus by the riders they allowed.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the word for "horse" is <i><b>roch</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-9">[9]</a></sup>

- Examples: *[Rochallor](/wiki/Rochallor#Etymology)*, *[Rohan](/wiki/Rohan#Etymology)*, *[Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim#Etymology)*, *[Roheryn](/wiki/Roheryn#Etymology)*<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), the word for "horse" is <i><b>rokko</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-SApp_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-9">[9]</a></sup> or <i><b>rocco</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-L211_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L211-7">[7]</a></sup> (pl. *rokkor*<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>). In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), these forms derive from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ROK](/wiki/ROK).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> An intermediate form was [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian "Primitive Quendian") *rokkō* ("swift horse for riding").<sup id="cite_ref-L211_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L211-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L297_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-12">[12]</a></sup>

Words in [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early tongues included:

- [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *olombo* ("horse") <sup id="cite_ref-VT45a_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45a-13">[13]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *lobor* ("horse")<sup id="cite_ref-VT45a_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45a-13">[13]</a></sup>
- [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) *Brog* ("horse"); *bros* (*brossa*) or *broch* ("mare"); *lobros* ("steed, horse"); *mair* ("horse", poetic).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

[Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) also had its own terms for a horse, specifically *karab* in "common" gender singular ("horse", as a species/creature), the masculine singular *karbû* ("male horse", "stallion"), the feminine singular *karbî* ("female horse", "mare"), and the plural *karîb* ("horses").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>
