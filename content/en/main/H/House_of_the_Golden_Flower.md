The **house of the Golden Flower** was one of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](/wiki/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim "Twelve houses of the Gondothlim"), the kindreds of the elven city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Their story is recounted in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

[Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) was the chief of this house. They bore the device of a rayed sun upon their shields. Their garments and weapons are not described in great detail, but it is said that their lord wore a mantle embroidered with celandines in golden threads, and his arms were damascened with gold.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the balrog [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") led [Melko](/wiki/Melko)'s armies to the city's northern gate, which was soon knocked down.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup> The Golden Flower's strongest troops were stationed in the [Great Market](/wiki/Great_Market), in the eastern part of the city, and at some point they decided to march to the fight about that gate. They marched there by a circuitous way with the idea of surprising the foe upon his left flank, but instead they were ambushed by a host of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) led by [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs). They fought for hours, until a [fire-drake](/wiki/Fire-drakes "Fire-drakes") came and overwhelmed them. They had to cut their way out with great difficulty, as the dragon began to burn the entire market.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182</sup>

Fortunately, Glorfindel had previously sent messengers to King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) explaining the urgency of the situation, so the [house of the Harp](/wiki/House_of_the_Harp "House of the Harp") was sent to aid the Golden Flower. However, [Salgant](/wiki/Salgant), the lord of the Harp, betrayed his men, sending them to the [Lesser Market](/wiki/Lesser_Market) in the southern part of the city. The men of the Harp fretted thereat, and broke from their lord to come before the [king's hall](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace "Square of the Palace") right in the moment that Glorfindel and his folk were chased by a press of enemies. The troops of the Harp saved them, driving the enemy back into the market, but there they were trapped, burned to death or poisoned by the breath of the [dragon](/wiki/Dragon "Dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182–3</sup>

Thus Glorfindel and the last of his soldiers, along with all the remaining houses, barricaded themselves in the [Square of the King](/wiki/Square_of_the_King). After Turgon proclaimed the fall of the city and ordered the survivors to follow [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), Glorfindel held the rear for those who retreated towards [Idril's secret way](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way), and many more of the Golden Flower fell there.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:186</sup>

Further on, while crossing [Cristhorn](/wiki/Cristhorn), Glorfindel was behind the [Exiles](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin "Exiles of Gondolin").<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:192</sup> The rearguard was attacked by a band of Orcs and a Balrog, and there happened the duel between Glorfindel and the Balrog, in which both fell into the abyss. After the body of Glorfindel had been recovered, Tuor had a cairn raised for him.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:194–5</sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Yellow flowers have fared thither and blow ever now about that mound in those unkindly places; but the folk of the Golden Flower wept at its building and might not dry their tears.
</p>
</blockquote>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Their [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name was <i><b>Thlim Losglóriol</b></i>, while in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) they were simply known as <i><b>Lóter Kuluinai</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Los Glóriol* was later emended to <i><b>Los&#39;lóriol</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

This is the only [house of the Gondothlim](/wiki/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim "Twelve houses of the Gondothlim") properly named in the legendarium following the Lost Tales: in the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)*, Glorfindel is mentioned as "chief of the House of the Golden Flower of Gondolin".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> This text was used by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) for the [twenty-third chapter](/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_the_Fall_of_Gondolin "Of Tuor and the Fall of Gondolin") of the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
