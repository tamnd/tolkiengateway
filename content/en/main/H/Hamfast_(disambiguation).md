**Hamfast** was the name of several [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits):

- [Hamfast of Gamwich](/wiki/Hamfast_of_Gamwich), the forefather of the [Gamgee Family](/wiki/Gamgee_Family)
- [Gaffer Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee), father of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)
- [Hamfast Gardner](/wiki/Hamfast_Gardner), Gaffer's grandson
- [Baravorn](/wiki/Baravorn), the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) translation of the name
