**Hamfast** was the name of several [Hobbits](Hobbits.md):

- [Hamfast of Gamwich](Hamfast_of_Gamwich.md), the forefather of the [Gamgee Family](../G/Gamgee_Family~66365.md)
- [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md), father of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)
- [Hamfast Gardner](Hamfast_Gardner.md), Gaffer's grandson
- [Baravorn](../B/Baravorn.md), the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) translation of the name
