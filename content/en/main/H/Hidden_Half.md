The **Hidden Half** is a note placed below the edge of the world on the Diagram I of the [Ambarkanta maps](/wiki/Ambarkanta_maps), to reflect the conception of the "round world". The [Mountains of the West](/wiki/Mountains_of_the_West) and [East](/wiki/Walls_of_the_Sun "Walls of the Sun") prevented anyone from finding the Hidden Half of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
