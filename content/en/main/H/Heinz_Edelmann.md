**Heinz Edelmann** ([June 20](/wiki/20_June "20 June"), [1934](/wiki/1934)– [July 21](/wiki/21_July "21 July"), [2009](/wiki/2009)) was a German illustrator and graphic designer.

He studied printmaking at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kunstakademie_D%C3%BCsseldorf" title="wikipedia:Kunstakademie Düsseldorf">Düsseldorf Academy of Fine Arts</a>. Since [1958](/wiki/1958) he worked as a freelance artist and designer for theatre posters, and German advertising. From 1961-1969 he was a regular illustrator and cover designer for the internationally renowned youth magazine *twen*. During 1967-1968 he worked as art director on the Beatles-movie <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Submarine_(film)" title="wikipedia:Yellow Submarine (film)">Yellow Submarine</a></i>.

For the German publishing company Klett-Cotta, he designed some book covers, like the cover of the first German edition of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) ([1969](/wiki/1969)-[70](/wiki/1970 "1970")) and the cover of *Fabelhafte Geschichten* ([1975](/wiki/1975); including [Farmer Giles of Ham](/wiki/Farmer_Giles_of_Ham), [Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major) and [Leaf by Niggle](/wiki/Leaf_by_Niggle)).

Also known are his numerous illustrations for Kenneth Grahame's children's book <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wind_in_the_Willows" title="wikipedia:The Wind in the Willows">The Wind in the Willows</a>.

Heinz Edelmann taught from 1972 to 1976 industrial graphic design in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fachhochschule_D%C3%BCsseldorf" title="wikipedia:Fachhochschule Düsseldorf">Department of Applied Sciences Düsseldorf</a>. After that he was Lecturer of Art and Design at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne_University_of_Applied_Sciences" title="wikipedia:Cologne University of Applied Sciences">Cologne University of Applied Sciences</a> (Cologne factory schools) and in 1999, he became professor of illustration at the State Academy of Fine Arts in Stuttgart.
