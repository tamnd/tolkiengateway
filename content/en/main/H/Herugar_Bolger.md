**Herugar Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Herugar was the only child of [Rudigar Bolger](/wiki/Rudigar_Bolger) and [Belba Baggins](/wiki/Belba_Baggins). He married [Jessamine Boffin](/wiki/Jessamine_Boffin) and they had one child together, [Odovacar](/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger "Odovacar Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name *Herugar* could be an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "sword-spear". It could be a combination of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) elements *heoru* ("sword")<sup id="cite_ref-BT1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT1-3">[3]</a></sup>, which is spelled *heru* in the Mercian dialect of Old English,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and *gar* ("spear")<sup id="cite_ref-BT2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT2-5">[5]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the Old English poem <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beowulf" title="wikipedia:Beowulf">Beowulf</a> a brother of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hrothgar" title="wikipedia:Hrothgar">Hrothgar</a> is called <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heorogar" title="wikipedia:Heorogar">Heorogar</a>. <span></span>
