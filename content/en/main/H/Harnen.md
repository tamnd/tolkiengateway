The **Harnen** was a river in the south of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## Course {#Course}

The approximately 500 mile course of the Harnen began in the southern slopes of the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md), it flowed south-west for about 250 miles, after that it bent west and then sped for another 250 miles to the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md), which it entered in a wide delta by the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md). The mouth of the Harnen is located about halfway between the mouths of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md). The [Harad Road](Harad_Road.md) crosses the Harnen about 65 miles upstream from the point where the river turns west.<sup id="cite_ref-utmap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-utmap-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1050](../T/Third_Age_1050.md), [Ciryaher](../C/Ciryaher.md) crossed the Harnen with his army and defeated the Men of the Harad. Thereafter their kings acknowledged the overlordship of Gondor and their sons lived as hostages in the court of the king of Gondor. In the time of King Ciryaher the realm of Gondor extended south to the river Harnen and beyond it along the coast to the peninsula and haven of Umbar.<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the wars of the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md), Castamir's sons and their followers sailed from [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) in the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1448](../T/Third_Age_1448.md) and established an independent realm in Umbar,<sup id="cite_ref-Eldacar_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldacar-3">[3]</a></sup> which had been a part of Gondor since its conquest by [Eärnil I](../E/Eärnil_I.md) in the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [933](../T/Third_Age_933.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> After that date the river formed the southern border of the fief [South Gondor](../S/South_Gondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-utmap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-utmap-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> which was contested between Gondor and the Corsairs of Umbar.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldacar_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldacar-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that the northern portion of the river Harnen upstream from the point where the Harad road crossed the river Harnen was the border between Gondor and [Near Harad](../N/Near_Harad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-utmap_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-utmap-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Harnen seems to be a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Guide_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-8">[8]</a></sup> meaning "South Water"<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Guide_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-8">[8]</a></sup> or "South(ern) River".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-7">[7]</a></sup> It seems to be a compound of the prefixal form *har-* of *[harn](Harn.md)* ("south") and *[nen](../N/Nen.md)* ("water", "lake", "pool", "(lesser) river").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-7">[7]</a></sup>
