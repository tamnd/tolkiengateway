The **Hobbiton Road** was a road in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It started in [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) branching off the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) and passed through [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was lined with trees.

During [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s occupations the trees were replaced by ugly houses.

## Identification {#Identification}

The Road is mentioned in the narrative but it is not marked in *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)* and therefore it is not known which portion of which road is the Hobbiton Road. [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) identifies this Road as passing through Hobbiton and then around [the Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill "Hobbiton Hill") and on to [Overhill](/wiki/Overhill).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Encyclopedia of Arda](/wiki/Encyclopedia_of_Arda) identifies the road as "at least" the portion between Bywater and Hobbiton.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
