**Hengest** and **Horsa** were the elder sons of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), and one of the [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") conquerors of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) following the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Hen_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

Hengest and Horsa were born during the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_century" title="wikipedia:5th century">5th century AD</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heligoland" title="wikipedia:Heligoland">Heligoland</a>, an island off the coast of the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), to Eriol and his wife [Cwén](/wiki/Cw%C3%A9n).

While Hengest and Horsa were still children, their mother died, and their father went to seek [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), the island of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>.

After the brothers grew up, they sought vengeance on their uncle [Beorn](</wiki/Beorn_(son_of_Heden)> "Beorn (son of Heden)") for the murder of their grandfather [Eoh](/wiki/Eoh), and afterwards became great chieftains among their people.<sup id="cite_ref-Hen_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hen-1">[1]</a></sup>

### The Faring Forth {#The_Faring_Forth}

Following the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), the disastrous attempt by the Elves of Tol Eressëa to save their [kin](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi") in the Great Lands which resulted in their total defeat and eventual fading, evil Men invaded Tol Eressëa, which was now anchored near the coast of the Great Lands.<sup id="cite_ref-War_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

However, some time after, Hengest and Horsa (alongside their half-brother [Heorrenda](/wiki/Heorrenda)) invaded Tol Eressëa as well, which would in later days become known as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-War_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-2">[2]</a></sup>

There, Hengest established his capital at what was once the city of [Kortirion](/wiki/Kortirion), which would later be known as *Warwíc* (in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English)), and eventually as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick" title="wikipedia:Warwick">Warwick</a></i>; meanwhile Horsa established himself at what was once the town of [Taruithorn](/wiki/Taruithorn), which would later be known as *Oxenaford* (in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English)), and eventually as *[Oxford](/wiki/Oxford)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

Unlike all other Men that invaded the island, however, the sons of Eriol (Hengest, Horsa, and Heorrenda) were friendly to the Elves, and it is through them that the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_people" title="wikipedia:English people">English people</a> retained the "true tradition of the [fairies](/wiki/Elves "Elves")".<sup id="cite_ref-War_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-War-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The names *Hengest* and *Horsa* are in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), with *Hengest* meaning "stallion",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> and *Horsa* meaning "horse".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>
