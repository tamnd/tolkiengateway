**Hey dol! merry dol! ring a dong dillo!** is one of the songs sung by [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) in the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The poem {#The_poem}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><audio src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/b6/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Hey_dol%21_merry_dol%21_ring_a_dong_dillo%21.mp3" width="300" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 300px;" controls=""><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Hey_dol!_merry_dol!_ring_a_dong_dillo!.mp3">https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Hey_dol!_merry_dol!_ring_a_dong_dillo!.mp3</a></audio><figcaption><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> reads an extract of the poem</figcaption></figure>

<div class="poem">
<p><i>Hey dol! merry dol! ring a dong dillo!</i><br/>
<i>Ring a dong! hop along! fal lal the willow!</i><br/>
<i>Tom Bom, jolly Tom, Tom Bombadillo!</i><br/><br/>
<i>Hey! Come merry dol! derry dol! My darling!</i><br/>
<i>Light goes the weather-wind and the feathered starling.</i><br/>
<i>Down along under Hill, shining in the sunlight,</i><br/>
<i>Waiting on the doorstep for the cold starlight,</i><br/>
<i>There my pretty lady is, <a href="../R/River-woman.md" title="River-woman">River-woman</a>&#39;s daughter,</i><br/>
<i>Slender as the willow-wand, clearer than the water.</i><br/>
<i>Old Tom Bombadil water-lilies bringing</i><br/>
<i>Comes hopping home again. Can you hear him singing?</i><br/>
<i>Hey! Come merry dol! derry dol! and merry-o,</i><br/>
<i>Goldberry, Goldberry, merry yellow berry-o!</i><br/>
<i>Poor old <a href="../O/Old_Man_Willow.md" title="Old Man Willow">Willow-man</a>, you tuck your roots away!</i><br/>
<i>Tom&#39;s in a hurry now. Evening will follow day.</i><br/>
<i>Tom&#39;s going home again water-lilies bringing.</i><br/>
<i>Hey! Come derry dol! Can you hear me singing?</i>
</p>
</div>
