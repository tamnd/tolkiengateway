**Heritage Models** was an American company which produced miniature models.<sup id="cite_ref-appelcline_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appelcline-1">[1]</a></sup>

Jim Oden had begun importing miniatures from the British [Miniature Figurines](../M/Miniature_Figurines.md) in [1972](../_/1972.md). After that company had set up its own American foundry, Oden started Heritage Models to make manufacture models. Heritage launched its first line of fantasy models in 1975, and also expanded towards role-playing books and products meant for use with [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md).<sup id="cite_ref-appelcline_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appelcline-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1978](../_/1978.md), Heritage Models released a line of miniatures based on the [1978 adaptation of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-appelcline_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appelcline-1">[1]</a></sup> A [painting guide](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Painting_Guide.md) was also published. William A. Barton, in a review for *The Space Gamer*, noted that the line's faithfulness to the movie might “turn off some fantasy afficionados”, though also found it curious that omitted characters like the [barrow-wights](../B/Barrow-wights.md) were covered in the line.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Like they had done with previous licensed products, Heritage Models had also intended to release a role-playing game along with the miniatures line. Steve Perrin and Steve Herrin, who had co-authored <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneQuest" title="wikipedia:RuneQuest">RuneQuest</a>, worked on *Heroes of Middle Earth*. Contractual issues meant the end of the project.<sup id="cite_ref-appelcline_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appelcline-1">[1]</a></sup>

The company went bankrupt in [1983](../_/1983.md) and the rights to its models ended up with various other companies. <sup id="cite_ref-appelcline_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appelcline-1">[1]</a></sup>
