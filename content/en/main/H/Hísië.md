<i><b>hísië</b></i> is a noun meaning "mist" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Compare with *[hîth](/wiki/H%C3%AEth "Hîth")* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB)* = "(Land of) Misty Night"
- *[Hísimë](/wiki/H%C3%ADsim%C3%AB)* = "November"
