**Hildor** was one of the many names among the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) for the race of [Men](../M/Men.md), meaning "the Followers" or "the Aftercomers".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hildor* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word, and contains an influence from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [KHILI](../K/KHILI.md) ("follow").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup> In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md), and in the manuscript *[Words Phrases and Passages](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_17.md)*, appears the Quenya form <i><b>hildi</b></i> ("followers = mortal men"; "after-comers"), derived from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [KHIL-](../K/KHILI.md#Other_versions).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that the unattested singular form of plural *Hildor* should be *Hildo*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
