**Hildor** was one of the many names among the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) for the race of [Men](/wiki/Men), meaning "the Followers" or "the Aftercomers".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hildor* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word, and contains an influence from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KHILI](/wiki/KHILI) ("follow").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-2">[2]</a></sup> In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), and in the manuscript *[Words Phrases and Passages](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17")*, appears the Quenya form <i><b>hildi</b></i> ("followers = mortal men"; "after-comers"), derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KHIL-](/wiki/KHILI#Other_versions "KHILI").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the unattested singular form of plural *Hildor* should be *Hildo*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
