**Hithlum** was a region north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) near the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB).

## Description {#Description}

Hithlum was separated from Beleriand proper by the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) mountain chain, and was named after the sea mists which formed there at times.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup> The Ered Wethrin ("Mountains of Shadow") formed the southern and eastern wall, and had only a few passes; as such they formed a natural defensive line. The western wall was formed by the [Ered Lómin](/wiki/Ered_L%C3%B3min) or "Echoing Mountains", which curved north-westward to [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB). The land of [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth) lay west of the Ered Wethrin and was not part of Beleriand or Hithlum, and the land of [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) was separated from Hithlum by the southern part of the Ered Lómin.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> Hithlum was cold and rainy, but quite fertile.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup>

Hithlum was subdivided in [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim), where the High Kings of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) had their halls,<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min), which later became a fief of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The northern highlands, such as the mountains about Hithlum, were raised during the battles of the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) during the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Hithlum was populated by the [Elves of Mithrim](/wiki/Elves_of_Mithrim), who were the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), since the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). The Noldor entered Hithlum via the [Firth of Drengist](/wiki/Firth_of_Drengist) and first camped at the shores of [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim). The land was covered by mists and vapours from the [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim), and thus they named it **Hísilómë** or **Land of Mist**.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup> [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) gave leave to the Noldor to settle it and was ruled by [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) from its northern lands.<sup id="cite_ref-return_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-5">[5]</a></sup>

Later in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), Hithlum was [continually under attack](/wiki/Assaults_on_Hithlum "Assaults on Hithlum") by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), but its natural defences, fortified by fortresses such as [Barad Eithel](/wiki/Barad_Eithel), proved impassable.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> Almost a century after the [Dagor Aglareb](/wiki/Dagor_Aglareb), a small number of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) came from the [Firth of Drengist](/wiki/Firth_of_Drengist) from the west, but once they were spotted, Fingon pushed them back into the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-return_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [416](/wiki/First_Age_416 "First Age 416") Fingolfin granted [Hador](/wiki/Hador) a hereditary fiefdom in [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> where he gathered most of the people of his kin, thus forming the noble **[House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador)**.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The mountains protected Hithlum during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), after which [Edain](/wiki/Edain) of the [First House](/wiki/House_of_Beor "House of Beor") came from [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) as refugees and joined those of Hador.<sup id="cite_ref-ruin_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ruin-8">[8]</a></sup>

Seven years after the Dagor Bragollach, Morgoth sent a great force against Hithlum, attacking the passes of the Ered Wethrin. [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)") died defending the [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion); in a second attempt the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim) sailed up the Firth of Drengist, then assisted Fingon by striking the unsuspecting orcs from the west.<sup id="cite_ref-ruin_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ruin-8">[8]</a></sup>

Hithlum eventually was lost after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). The Hadorians were scattered, killed, or enslaved, the Noldor who could not flee in time were enslaved in Morgoth's mines. Morgoth feigned to gave the [Swarthy Men](/wiki/Swarthy_Men) the lands of Hithlum as a reward, but in truth they were actually trapped there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Hithlum was completely destroyed during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

*Hithlum* is a [North Sindarin](/wiki/North_Sindarin) word,<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-11">[11]</a></sup> meaning "Mist-shadow" (*[hith](/wiki/Hith "Hith")* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lum (page does not exist)">lum</a></i>), a translation from [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <i><b>Hísilómë</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌhisiˈloːme]</span>, stem *Hisilómi-*).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Its classical [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name is said to be <i><b>Hithlũ</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-11">[11]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") initially marked the word as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin); its second element was cognate to Quenya <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lumbe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lumbe (page does not exist)">lumbe</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an [early poem](/wiki/A_Song_of_Aryador "A Song of Aryador") and in the [Lost Tale of Turambar](/wiki/Turambar_and_the_Foal%C3%B3k%C3%AB "Turambar and the Foalókë"), the region was called *Aryador* in the language of [Men](/wiki/Men), although according to the *[Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon)*, it was an [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) word. [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognates are given: *Areandor*, *Areanóre*, *Areanor*.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
