**Hithlum** was a region north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) near the [Helcaraxë](Helcaraxë.md).

## Description {#Description}

Hithlum was separated from Beleriand proper by the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) mountain chain, and was named after the sea mists which formed there at times.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup> The Ered Wethrin ("Mountains of Shadow") formed the southern and eastern wall, and had only a few passes; as such they formed a natural defensive line. The western wall was formed by the [Ered Lómin](../E/Ered_Lómin.md) or "Echoing Mountains", which curved north-westward to [Helcaraxë](Helcaraxë.md). The land of [Lammoth](../L/Lammoth.md) lay west of the Ered Wethrin and was not part of Beleriand or Hithlum, and the land of [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) was separated from Hithlum by the southern part of the Ered Lómin.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> Hithlum was cold and rainy, but quite fertile.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup>

Hithlum was subdivided in [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md), where the High Kings of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) had their halls,<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md), which later became a fief of the [House of Hador](House_of_Hador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The northern highlands, such as the mountains about Hithlum, were raised during the battles of the [Ainur](../A/Ainur.md) during the [Battle of the Powers](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Hithlum was populated by the [Elves of Mithrim](../E/Elves_of_Mithrim.md), who were the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), since the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). The Noldor entered Hithlum via the [Firth of Drengist](../F/Firth_of_Drengist.md) and first camped at the shores of [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md). The land was covered by mists and vapours from the [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md), and thus they named it **Hísilómë** or **Land of Mist**.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-1">[1]</a></sup> [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) gave leave to the Noldor to settle it and was ruled by [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) from its northern lands.<sup id="cite_ref-return_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-5">[5]</a></sup>

Later in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), Hithlum was [continually under attack](../A/Assaults_on_Hithlum.md) by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), but its natural defences, fortified by fortresses such as [Barad Eithel](../B/Barad_Eithel.md), proved impassable.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> Almost a century after the [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md), a small number of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) came from the [Firth of Drengist](../F/Firth_of_Drengist.md) from the west, but once they were spotted, Fingon pushed them back into the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-return_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [416](../F/First_Age_416.md) Fingolfin granted [Hador](Hador.md) a hereditary fiefdom in [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> where he gathered most of the people of his kin, thus forming the noble **[House of Hador](House_of_Hador.md)**.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The mountains protected Hithlum during the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), after which [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of the [First House](House_of_Beor.md) came from [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) as refugees and joined those of Hador.<sup id="cite_ref-ruin_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ruin-8">[8]</a></sup>

Seven years after the Dagor Bragollach, Morgoth sent a great force against Hithlum, attacking the passes of the Ered Wethrin. [Galdor](<../G/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-lómin).md>) died defending the [Eithel Sirion](../E/Eithel_Sirion.md); in a second attempt the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md) sailed up the Firth of Drengist, then assisted Fingon by striking the unsuspecting orcs from the west.<sup id="cite_ref-ruin_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ruin-8">[8]</a></sup>

Hithlum eventually was lost after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md). The Hadorians were scattered, killed, or enslaved, the Noldor who could not flee in time were enslaved in Morgoth's mines. Morgoth feigned to gave the [Swarthy Men](../S/Swarthy_Men.md) the lands of Hithlum as a reward, but in truth they were actually trapped there.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Hithlum was completely destroyed during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

*Hithlum* is a [North Sindarin](../N/North_Sindarin.md) word,<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-11">[11]</a></sup> meaning "Mist-shadow" (*[hith](Hith.md)* + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lum (page does not exist)">lum</a></i>), a translation from [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) <i><b>Hísilómë</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌhisiˈloːme]</span>, stem *Hisilómi-*).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Its classical [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name is said to be <i><b>Hithlũ</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-11">[11]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) initially marked the word as [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md); its second element was cognate to Quenya <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lumbe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lumbe (page does not exist)">lumbe</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an [early poem](../A/A_Song_of_Aryador.md) and in the [Lost Tale of Turambar](../T/Turambar_and_the_Foalókë.md), the region was called *Aryador* in the language of [Men](../M/Men.md), although according to the *[Gnomish Lexicon](../G/Gnomish_Lexicon.md)*, it was an [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) word. [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognates are given: *Areandor*, *Areanóre*, *Areanor*.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
