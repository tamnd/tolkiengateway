**Haldir** was the eldest son of [Halmir](Halmir.md) and a [Chieftain of Brethil](../C/Chieftain_of_Brethil.md).

## History {#History}

He married [Glóredhel](../G/Glóredhel.md) of the [House of Hador](House_of_Hador.md) and they had a son named [Handir](Handir.md). He also fostered their nephews [Húrin](Húrin.md) and [Huor](Huor.md) during their childhood years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Haldir joined the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) and led a force of the [Haladin](Haladin.md) in the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md). [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) attempted a retreat across [Anfauglith](../A/Anfauglith.md) covered by Haldir, but he was slain, with most of his Men.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

News of his death caused Glóredhel to die of grief.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He was succeeded by his son [Handir](Handir.md) as the Chieftain of the Haladin.
