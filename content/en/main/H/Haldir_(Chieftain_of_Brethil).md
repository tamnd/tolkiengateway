**Haldir** was the eldest son of [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir) and a [Chieftain of Brethil](/wiki/Chieftain_of_Brethil).

## History {#History}

He married [Glóredhel](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3redhel) of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) and they had a son named [Handir](/wiki/Handir). He also fostered their nephews [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Huor](/wiki/Huor) during their childhood years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Haldir joined the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) and led a force of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) in the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad). [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) attempted a retreat across [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) covered by Haldir, but he was slain, with most of his Men.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

News of his death caused Glóredhel to die of grief.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He was succeeded by his son [Handir](/wiki/Handir) as the Chieftain of the Haladin.
