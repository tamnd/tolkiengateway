**Heir of Isildur** was the title of thirty-nine lords of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) descended in right line from [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) the son of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), from his son [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)") to [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who [reunited the Kingdoms](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom "Reunited Kingdom") of the Dúnedain in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

[Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was the eldest son of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), and so by right he and his heirs should have inherited the title of [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") and ruled the [Two Kingdoms](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom "Reunited Kingdom") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). Soon after the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Isildur and his three elder sons all perished in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Isildur's only remaining Heir was [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)"), kept safe in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) but only eleven years old at that time, and unable to take up his rule. So, Isildur's nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil) took control of the kingdom of Gondor, and the Two Kingdoms split from one another as Gondor's rule went to the [House of Anárion](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion). In the south, Gondor remained strong under the rule of Meneldil's descendants, but the [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") was not so fortunate. The sons of [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eärendur (King of Arnor)") quarreled, and while [Amlaith](/wiki/Amlaith) was the Heir of Isildur and his father, the other sons broke the kingdom into three separate realms. Of these, Amlaith ruled [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), the only that [was ruled](/wiki/Kings_of_Arthedain "Kings of Arthedain") by the Heir of Isildur. The other kingdoms were slowly overcome by dark forces of the evil land of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). By the time [Argeleb I](/wiki/Argeleb_I) of Arthedain came to power, the line of kings had failed in those kingdoms, so Argeleb claimed sovereignty over all of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor)<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup> (taking the royal prefix *[ar(a)-](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* as a token of this<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>).

[Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), Isildur's Heir through twenty-three generations, was the last King to rule in the north; he was also notable for marrying [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel), daughter of the [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher), thus uniting the [House of Isildur](/wiki/House_of_Isildur) and [House of Anárion](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion). He later attempted to reclaim the [High Kingship](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain"), but failed,<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-5">[5]</a></sup> and the last remnant of royal Arnor was overrun by its enemies.

Though their royal cities were wasted, and their people dwindled, the line of Isildur's Heirs continued for another sixteen generations under the title '[Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain")', who were Arvedui's descendants and ruled the remnants of the [Dúnedain of Arnor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor). When the line of the Kings of Gondor also failed and the [Ruling Stewards](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") ruled the Kingdom, it was the Chieftains who held the line of Anárion alive, but they were hidden in the north, forgotten by all except in [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") where heirs of Isildur were harboured as well as their heirlooms were kept.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Heir of Isildur was a target of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), if any remained in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth); when [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II) died, the [Wise](/wiki/Wise) decided to protect his son [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) by raising him without telling him of his name and heritage.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The last Chieftain was [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), who famously came to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and reclaimed the High Kingship of all the western lands as the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
