"**Half-trolls**" was a term used to describe the appearance of the men from [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad) who fought for [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). These [Men](/wiki/Men) were said to be black skinned with white eyes and red tongues.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later in the battle, the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Swan-knights&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Swan-knights (page does not exist)">knights</a> of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) were said to have driven before them "troll-men and Variags and orcs."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

There is no other known reference to "half-trolls" in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), so it cannot be known whether they ever truly existed within [Arda](/wiki/Arda), even as folklore. If "half-trolls" did exist, whether their origins had any similarity to those of [half-orcs](/wiki/Half-orcs "Half-orcs") can only be a matter for speculation.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

According to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), a character in the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legends_about_Theodoric_the_Great#The_Thidrekssaga" title="wikipedia:Legends about Theodoric the Great">Thiðrekssaga</a></i> (a 13th century work which influenced *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)*), who is an off-spring of a woman and an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/incubus" title="wikipedia:incubus">incubus</a>, appears "troll-like" and is "all over dark, with black hair and black beard".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Half-trolls (Sindarin <i>Pirtereg</i>, sing. <i>Pertorog</i>) were bred by Sauron, from a union of Olog-hai and Variags or Men from Khand. They are about seven feet tall and have black skin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Half-trolls of Far Harad&#34; is a creature, able to inflict two strikes on a player. &#34;Half-trolls&#34; is a Troll Faction, playable at Cirith Ungol or Barad-dûr.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game">The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <i>Half Trolls</i> are &#34;Mahûd men&#34; of Far Harad who were altered to the size and strength of the Olog-hai of Sauron. During the end of the Third Age, when Sauron was destroyed, their powers diminished and they too fell into darkness.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-6">[6]</a></sup> Units include &#34;Half Troll Warband&#34; and &#34;Half Troll of Far Harad&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Half-Troll Marauders</i> are crossbreeds of Trolls and an Orcs. These creatures serve as the pikemen unit for the Goblin faction.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king">a later expansion pack</a>, <i>Half Troll Swordsmen</i> are available.<sup id="cite_ref-WP_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Half-trolls appear as a type of Troll-kind creature crossed with the blood of <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a>. They have the strength and appearance of <a href="/wiki/Trolls" title="Trolls">Trolls</a> yet are also cunning and intelligent. <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a> bestowed Half-trolls onto the Haradrim as a gift. Half-trolls can be found throughout the various regions of <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> and <a href="/wiki/An%C3%B3rien" title="Anórien">Anórien</a>, fighting alongside Men of <a href="/wiki/Harad" title="Harad">Harad</a> and the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Corsairs" title="Corsairs">Corsairs</a> of <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbar</a>.</dd></dl>
