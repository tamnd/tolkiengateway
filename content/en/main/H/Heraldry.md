**Heraldic devices**, **emblems** and **flags** are a minor element throughout of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

In the first phase of his [mythology](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) described the heraldic devices with vivid and figurative images, as can be seen particularly in the description of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](#twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim); but in later narratives, the devices became few and simple. However, around [1960](/wiki/1960)-[61](/wiki/1961 "1961"), Tolkien also drew several devices for characters of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), which are more complex, but also ornamental and abstract.<sup id="cite_ref-Illustrator_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Illustrator-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191</sup> This article deals with illustrations and textual descriptions in separated sections.

## Drawings {#Drawings}

After finishing his drawings in the 1960s, Tolkien defined some rules for [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") heraldry:<sup id="cite_ref-Illustrator_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Illustrator-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191</sup>

- Women's personal designs are set within a circle.
- Men's personal designs are set within a lozenge.
- General (impersonal) designs or emblems of a [family](/wiki/Category:Elven_noble_houses "Category:Elven noble houses") are set within a square.
- The rank was usually held to be shown by number of points which reached the outer rim:
    - Four points were for princes.
    - Six to eight points for kings.
    - The great ancestors sometimes had as many as 16, as in the [House of Finwë](#Elves).

### Elves {#Elves}

Since the personal emblems are set in a lozenge and house emblems are set in a square, it is likely that some of the Elvish designs below (for example, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor)) could be used by a noble family, having them tipped forty-five degrees. Tolkien only made this variation with the emblem of the [House of Finwë](/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB). Elvish designs are symmetrical on all sides, unlike those of [Men](/wiki/Men), so they can be used in any position in a circular movement that may have reflected Elvish [immortality](/wiki/Immortality "Immortality").<sup id="cite_ref-Illustrator_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Illustrator-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:192, 194-5</sup>

### Early locations {#Early_locations}

In the early phase of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* ([1916](/wiki/1916)-[20](/wiki/1920 "1920")), Tolkien made three heraldic devices for towns from England (then the core of his mythology), which he laveled with the title "<i><b>i·glin grandin a·Dol Erethin Airi</b></i>" ("the fair towns of Holy [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa)"). As noted by the editors, these towns were special for Tolkien and his wife [Edith](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") in those years.<sup id="cite_ref-Erethrin_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Erethrin-19">[17]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:93</sup>

### Twelve houses of the Gondothlim {#Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim}

The array of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](/wiki/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim "Twelve houses of the Gondothlim") in the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* is quite detailed. Some of these folks have their emblems mentioned with capitalized letters (the White Wing, the Stricken Anvil). However, from some of the houses no details about their heraldry is given, but like the others, most probably they had their devices based on their names and reflecting their raiments:

- The [houses of the Pillar](/wiki/House_of_the_Pillar "House of the Pillar") and [of the Tower of Snow](/wiki/House_of_the_Tower_of_Snow "House of the Tower of Snow") were both under the lordship of [Penlod](/wiki/Penlod), so they probably had similar raiments.
- The [house of the Tree](/wiki/House_of_the_Tree "House of the Tree") had green raiment.
- The [people of the Fountain](/wiki/People_of_the_Fountain "People of the Fountain") were fond of silver and diamonds, and bore pale and bright swords.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-49">[47]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

### Gnomish banners {#Gnomish_banners}

In some "[Prose Fragments Following the Lost Tales](/wiki/Prose_Fragments_Following_the_Lost_Tales)", Tolkien wrote a short narrative about the coming of the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) into the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) (the later [Return of the Noldor](/wiki/Return_of_the_Noldor)), in which their leader and their sons hold several banners:<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_51-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-51">[49]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Now the colours of the Gnomes were gold and white in those ancient days in memory of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Two_Trees" title="Two Trees">Two Trees</a>, but <a href="/wiki/Finw%C3%AB" title="Finwë">Gelmir</a>&#39;s standard bore upon a silver field with a crown of gold, and each captain had a fair banner; and the sign of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Golfin" title="Golfin">Golfin</a> in those days was upon gold a silver sword, and of <a href="/wiki/Delin" title="Delin">Delin</a> a green beech leaf upon silver diapered with golder flowers, and of <a href="/wiki/L%C3%BAthien_(son_of_Gelmir)" title="Lúthien (son of Gelmir)">Lúthien</a> a golden swallow that winged through an azure field as it were the sky set with silver stars...

The [sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor") (here unrelated to the royal house) are said to be the ones who wrought the standard and banners, which shore either under the sun, moon, darkness or mist, thanks to their [magical](/wiki/Magic "Magic") gems sewed in them.<sup id="cite_ref-Prose_51-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prose-51">[49]</a></sup>
