**Hill-trolls** were a variety of [troll](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") adapted for life among hills and moorland.

## History {#History}

[Aragorn's](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") grandfather [Arador](/wiki/Arador) was slain by hill-trolls in the [Coldfells](/wiki/Coldfells), north of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

A company of these creatures were among the armies that [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) loosed from the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon) in the [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon).<sup id="cite_ref-RKV10_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKV10-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

In appearance they were man-like, but larger, and seem to have been covered with hard scales. They were fearsome and vicious, bellowing like beasts as they stormed into battle, and beating their enemies down with great hammers.<sup id="cite_ref-RKV10_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKV10-2">[2]</a></sup>
