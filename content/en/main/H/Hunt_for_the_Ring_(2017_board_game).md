**Hunt for the Ring** is a hidden movement/deduction game published by [ARES Games](/wiki/ARES_Games) in 2017, retelling the journey of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) as they make their way to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) with [The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring), as told in the [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

The game is a battle of wits and takes you upon a new journey across Middle–earth. One player must bring Frodo Baggins — the [Ringbearer](/wiki/Ringbearer) — and his Hobbit friends from their home in the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) to the sanctuary of Rivendell, where the fate of the One Ring will be decided. Using secrecy and the help of powerful allies, the Ringbearer must escape the chase of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl), the terrible Ringwraiths, controlled by up to four other players. The Ringwraith players work together to find Frodo and recover the Ring for their [Dark Lord](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"), while the Ringbearer secretly tracks his path first from [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) to [Bree](/wiki/Bree), then from Bree to Rivendell, on a double–sided game board.
