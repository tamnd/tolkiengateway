**Hunt for the Ring** is a hidden movement/deduction game published by [ARES Games](../A/ARES_Games~73366.md) in 2017, retelling the journey of [Frodo](../F/Frodo.md) and the [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) as they make their way to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) with [The One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), as told in the [The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

The game is a battle of wits and takes you upon a new journey across Middle–earth. One player must bring Frodo Baggins — the [Ringbearer](../R/Ringbearer.md) — and his Hobbit friends from their home in the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) to the sanctuary of Rivendell, where the fate of the One Ring will be decided. Using secrecy and the help of powerful allies, the Ringbearer must escape the chase of the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md), the terrible Ringwraiths, controlled by up to four other players. The Ringwraith players work together to find Frodo and recover the Ring for their [Dark Lord](../S/Sauron.md), while the Ringbearer secretly tracks his path first from [Hobbiton](Hobbiton.md) to [Bree](../B/Bree.md), then from Bree to Rivendell, on a double–sided game board.
