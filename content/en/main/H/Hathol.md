**Hathol** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [365](/wiki/First_Age_365 "First Age 365")) was an [Adan](/wiki/Edain "Edain"), belonging to the tribes of [Men](/wiki/Men) who crossed the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) from the eastern lands. He was the grandson of [Malach Aradan](/wiki/Malach "Malach").<sup id="cite_ref-hador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hador-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the genealogical tree of the folk of Marach, *Hathol* is translated as "the Axe",<sup id="cite_ref-hador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hador-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) form of the old [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *Hathel*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, Hathol is the son of [Magor](/wiki/Magor) and the father of [Hador](/wiki/Hador).

In the earlier conception, Men entered Beleriand in the year [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, and the House of Hador comprised three generations by the time of the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach): [Hador](/wiki/Hador), his son Galion (<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Galdor" title="Galdor">Galdor</a>), and Galion's sons [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) &amp; [Huor](/wiki/Huor). In the [Later Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Later_Quenta_Silmarillion) Tolkien revised the entry date of Men into Beleriand to be nearly a century earlier; this led to a massive overhauling of the chronology and the genealogies of the Edain.<sup id="cite_ref-hador_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hador-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the House of Hador, four additional generations were added above Hador: [Marach](/wiki/Marach), Malach, [Magor](/wiki/Magor), and <a class="mw-selflink selflink">Hathol</a>. Hador remained the first [Lord of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min), while Magor and Hathol were noted as having served no Elf-lord and dwelt near the sources of [Teiglin](/wiki/Teiglin).<sup id="cite_ref-hador_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hador-1">[1]</a></sup>

In a later emendation, Tolkien struck those notes out and switched the positions of Magor and Hador, thus having the House of Hador established two generations earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-hador_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hador-1">[1]</a></sup>
