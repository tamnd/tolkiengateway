<i><b>hîr</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "[lord](../L/Lord.md), master".<sup id="cite_ref-L_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *hîr* ("master, ?lord, ?mastery") derives from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) KHER ("rule, govern, possess").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *[hiril](Hiril.md)* female form ("lady")

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Gwaihir](../G/Gwaihir.md)*: "Wind-lord"
- *[Barahir](../B/Barahir.md)*: "Fierce lord"
- <i><a href="../R/Rohirrim.md#Etymology" title="Rohirrim">Ro<b>hir</b>rim</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-L_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *[Gonnhirrim](../G/Gonnhirrim.md)*: "Masters of Stone"
