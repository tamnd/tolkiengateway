<i><b>hîr</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "[lord](/wiki/Lord "Lord"), master".<sup id="cite_ref-L_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *hîr* ("master, ?lord, ?mastery") derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") KHER ("rule, govern, possess").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *[hiril](/wiki/Hiril "Hiril")* female form ("lady")

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir)*: "Wind-lord"
- *[Barahir](/wiki/Barahir)*: "Fierce lord"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Rohirrim#Etymology" title="Rohirrim">Ro<b>hir</b>rim</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-L_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup>
- *[Gonnhirrim](/wiki/Gonnhirrim)*: "Masters of Stone"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[heru](/wiki/Heru "Heru")*
