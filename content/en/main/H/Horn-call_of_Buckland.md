The **Horn-call** or **Horn-cry of Buckland** was the alarm signal of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), calling them to action against invaders and incursions into their land.

## History {#History}

The Horn-call of Buckland was an emergency signal and as such had not often been used. One sounding had been to warn of [White Wolves](/wiki/White_Wolves) crossing the frozen [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) in the [Fell Winter](</wiki/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age)> "Fell Winter (Third Age)")<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-1">[1]</a></sup> of [2911](/wiki/Third_Age_2911 "Third Age 2911").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After that, it had not been blown again for one hundred and seven years; the [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybucks) used the horn on [30 September](/wiki/30_September) [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> when [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger) gasped out that invaders, the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl"), had intruded into Buckland. The alarm caused the Black Riders to depart from [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) and ride through the [North Gate](/wiki/Buckland_Gate "Buckland Gate").<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) was the next to sound the Horn-cry of Buckland on [2 November](/wiki/2_November) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> using the [Horn of the Mark](/wiki/Horn_of_the_Mark), rousing the Shire-folk against the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) who had taken control of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
