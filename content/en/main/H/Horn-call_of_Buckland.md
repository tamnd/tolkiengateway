The **Horn-call** or **Horn-cry of Buckland** was the alarm signal of the [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md), calling them to action against invaders and incursions into their land.

## History {#History}

The Horn-call of Buckland was an emergency signal and as such had not often been used. One sounding had been to warn of [White Wolves](../W/White_Wolves.md) crossing the frozen [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) in the [Fell Winter](<../F/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age).md>)<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-1">[1]</a></sup> of [2911](../T/Third_Age_2911.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After that, it had not been blown again for one hundred and seven years; the [Brandybucks](../B/Brandybucks.md) used the horn on [30 September](../_/30_September.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> when [Fredegar Bolger](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md) gasped out that invaders, the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md), had intruded into Buckland. The alarm caused the Black Riders to depart from [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md) and ride through the [North Gate](../B/Buckland_Gate.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) was the next to sound the Horn-cry of Buckland on [2 November](../_/2_November.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> using the [Horn of the Mark](Horn_of_the_Mark.md), rousing the Shire-folk against the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) who had taken control of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
