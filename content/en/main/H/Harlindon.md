**Harlindon** was a green and fair elvish land on the north-western shores of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). It was located west of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) and south of the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune), which divided Lindon into the northern [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon) and the southern Harlindon. At the head of the Gulf lay the seaport [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).

There were woods at the foot of the Blue Mountains and a haven in a small inlet on the southern shores of the Gulf called [Harlond](</wiki/Harlond_(Lindon)> "Harlond (Lindon)").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

At the beginning of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), refugees of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) came to Lindon, with [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) being the most of those concentrating to Harlindon, along with [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). Celeborn ruled the [Elves of Harlindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon "Elves of Lindon"), until the couple moved to [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750").<sup id="cite_ref-gal2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gal2-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Harlindon* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) from *[har](/wiki/Harn "Harn")* ("south") and *[Lindon](/wiki/Lindon)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
