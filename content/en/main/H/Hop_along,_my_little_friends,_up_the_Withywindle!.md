**Hop along, my little friends, up the Withywindle!** is one of the songs sung by [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md). He sang it when [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam Gamgee](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md), [Merry Brandybuck](../M/Merry_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin Took](../P/Pippin_Took.md) were following him to his house. He and [Goldberry](../G/Goldberry.md) finished it when the hobbits got to his house.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The poem {#The_poem}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>Hop along, my little friends, up the <a href="../W/Withywindle.md" title="Withywindle">Withywindle</a>!</i><br/>
<i>Tom&#39;s going on ahead candles for to kindle.</i><br/>
<i>Down west sinks the Sun: soon you will be groping.</i><br/>
<i>When the night-shadows fall, then the door will open,</i><br/>
<i>Out of the window-panes light will twinkle yellow.</i><br/>
<i>Fear no alder black! Heed no hoary willow!</i><br/>
<i>Fear neither root nor bough! Tom goes on before you.</i><br/>
<i>Hey now! merry dol! We&#39;ll be waiting for you!</i><br/><br/>
<i>Hey! Come derry dol! Hop along, my hearties!</i><br/>
<i>Hobbits! Ponies all! We are fond of parties.</i><br/>
<i>Now let the fun begin! Let us sing together!</i><br/><br/>
<i>Now let the song begin! Let us sing together</i><br/>
<i>Of sun, stars, moon and mist, rain and cloudy weather,</i><br/>
<i>Light on the budding leaf, dew on the feather,</i><br/>
<i>Wind on the open hill, bells on the heather,</i><br/>
<i>Reeds by the shady pool, lilies on the water:</i><br/>
<i>Old Tom Bombadil and the River-daughter!</i>
</p>
</div>
