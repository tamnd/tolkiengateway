<i><b>Hobbit Tales</b></i> is an expansion for the second edition of the [*The One Ring* Roleplaying Game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)"). A version for *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*, titled <i><b>Shire Adventures</b></i>, was also released.

The material of the book was originally part of *The One Ring*’s [first Starter Set](</wiki/Starter_Set_(The_One_Ring)> "Starter Set (The One Ring)"), together with a simplified ruleset, dice, some maps, and cards depicting gear.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><i>Hobbit™ Tales</i> is a supplement for <i>The One Ring™</i>, the official tabletop roleplaying game based on the works of J.R.R. Tolkien, offering all you need to take your first steps into Middle-earth and experience the adventures Hobbits get up to (when they cannot avoid it!).
</p><p>Explore the Shire with a thorough description of its four farthings and five exciting ready-to-play adventures, fully playable also by taking on the roles of some Hobbits whose descendants will go on to do extraordinary things, such as Primula and Rory Brandybuck, Paladin Took, and Bilbo Baggins himself!
</p><p>Note that the material in this expansion has been published previously in the first starter set for the second edition of <i>The One Ring™</i>.
</p>
</blockquote>
