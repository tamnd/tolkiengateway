<i><b>haran</b></i> (pl. *harni*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word. In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, its meaning is given as "king, chieftain", related to [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")*, "king".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> In later appearances, it seems to have been replaced by *aran*, like in [Aranwë](/wiki/Aranw%C3%AB).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
