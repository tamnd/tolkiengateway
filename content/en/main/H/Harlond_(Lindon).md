**Harlond** was a haven of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) on the southern shores of the [Gulf of Lhûn](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune "Gulf of Lune"). It was situated in a small inlet about 150 miles south-west of [Mithlond](/wiki/Grey_Havens "Grey Havens"), on the side of [Harlindon](/wiki/Harlindon).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is told that the fleet of [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur), arriving in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) during the [Angmar War](/wiki/Angmar_War), filled the harbours of Harlond, [Forlond](/wiki/Forlond), and the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> name *Harlond* is given the translation "Southhaven".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> *Harlond* is apparently composed of *[harn](/wiki/Harn "Harn")* ("south") + *[lond](/wiki/Lond "Lond")* ("haven").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The name used by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") on the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings") was *Harlorn*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
