**Harper** was the apprentice of [Alf](/wiki/Alf) after he became the new [Master Cook](/wiki/Master_Cook) for the village of [Wootton Major](/wiki/Wootton_Major). When Alf was ready to leave the village he said that Master Harper was a very good cook (and he was also from the village, unlike Alf).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
