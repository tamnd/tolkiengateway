**Húrin** means "Battle-ready mood" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[hûr](/wiki/H%C3%BBr "Hûr")*, "readiness, vigour, fiery spirit" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ind (page does not exist)">ind</a></i>, "mood"), and can refer to:

- **[Húrin Thalion](/wiki/H%C3%BArin "Húrin") ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [441](/wiki/First_Age_441 "First Age 441") - c. [500](/wiki/First_Age_500 "First Age 500")), the last [Lord of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min) and father of [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin"), hero of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).**
- [Húrin of Emyn Arnen](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_of_Emyn_Arnen) (fl. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1634](/wiki/Third_Age_1634 "Third Age 1634")), the first [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") whose name is recorded in history.
    - [House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin), the dynastic house of the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), named after Húrin of [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen).
- [Húrin I](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2124](/wiki/Third_Age_2124 "Third Age 2124") - [2244](/wiki/Third_Age_2244 "Third Age 2244")), fifth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of Gondor.
- [Húrin II](/wiki/H%C3%BArin_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2515](/wiki/Third_Age_2515 "Third Age 2515") - [2628](/wiki/Third_Age_2628 "Third Age 2628")), fourteenth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of Gondor.
- [Húrin the Tall](</wiki/H%C3%BArin_(Warden_of_the_Keys)> "Húrin (Warden of the Keys)") (fl. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), [Warden of the Keys](/wiki/Warden_of_the_Keys) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).
