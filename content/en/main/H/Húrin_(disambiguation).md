**Húrin** means "Battle-ready mood" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[hûr](Hûr.md)*, "readiness, vigour, fiery spirit" and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ind (page does not exist)">ind</a></i>, "mood"), and can refer to:

- **[Húrin Thalion](Húrin.md) ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [441](../F/First_Age_441.md) - c. [500](../F/First_Age_500.md)), the last [Lord of Dor-lómin](../L/Lord_of_Dor-lómin.md) and father of [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md), hero of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).**
- [Húrin of Emyn Arnen](Húrin_of_Emyn_Arnen.md) (fl. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1634](../T/Third_Age_1634.md)), the first [Steward of Gondor](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) whose name is recorded in history.
    - [House of Húrin](House_of_Húrin.md), the dynastic house of the [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), named after Húrin of [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md).
- [Húrin I](Húrin_I.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2124](../T/Third_Age_2124.md) - [2244](../T/Third_Age_2244.md)), fifth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of Gondor.
- [Húrin II](Húrin_II.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2515](../T/Third_Age_2515.md) - [2628](../T/Third_Age_2628.md)), fourteenth [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of Gondor.
- [Húrin the Tall](<Húrin_(Warden_of_the_Keys).md>) (fl. [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)), [Warden of the Keys](../W/Warden_of_the_Keys.md) of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).
