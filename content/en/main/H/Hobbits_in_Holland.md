<i><b>Hobbits in Holland: Leven en werk van JRR Tolkien (1892-1973)</b></i> is a Dutch-language exhibition catalogue. Apart from a richly commented catalogue section there appears the following articles: "Een biographische schets van Ronald Tolkien" (pp. 5–14), and "Tolkien en Nederland" (pp. 15–35), the latter treats [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s visit to Holland in [1958](/wiki/1958).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The catalogue also includes a [letter from Tolkien to a Mr Joukes](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Joukes "Letter to Mr Joukes").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
