**Harold Eric Riddett** ([1905](/wiki/1905)-[1990](/wiki/1990)), commonly known as **H.E. Riddett**, was a British artist. He is noteworthy for adding colour to many black-and-white maps and drawings in several posthumous [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) publications, most notably in many editions of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and illustrated editions of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
