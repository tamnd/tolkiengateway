**Harold Eric Riddett** ([1905](../_/1905.md)-[1990](../_/1990.md)), commonly known as **H.E. Riddett**, was a British artist. He is noteworthy for adding colour to many black-and-white maps and drawings in several posthumous [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) publications, most notably in many editions of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* and illustrated editions of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.
