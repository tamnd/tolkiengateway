The **Hills of Evendim**, or **Emyn Uial** in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), were a series of hills in northern [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).

The hills began about a hundred miles north of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) in northern Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the southern end was the city of [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md), the first capital of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), at [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md). The [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) or Brandywine River flowed out of this lake. The river [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) also had a tributary beginning in the hills.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) crossed the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) and settled in the Hills of Evendim. However, after the year [700](../S/Second_Age_700.md) Galadriel and Celeborn left and established the realm of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

When the [Men](../M/Men.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) first returned to Middle-earth, the Hills of Evendim were also populated by Men related to the ancestors of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md). The Númenóreans settled nearby, and the hills formed the core of the kingdom of Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> After the establishments of the Kingdoms in Exile by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and his sons, the Men of Evendim merged with the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), becoming the people of Arnor together with non-Edain Men like the Men of [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md) the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) conquered [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> His victory was short-lived, however, for in [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md) the forces of [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) descended from the Hills of Evendim and on the plain between [Nenuial](../L/Lake_Evendim.md) and the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) crushed the forces of Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, *Emyn Uial*, means "Hills of Dusk", from *[emyn](../E/Emyn.md)* ("hills", plural of *[amon](../A/Amon.md)*)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and *[uial](../U/Uial.md)* ("[twilight](../T/Twilight.md)").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one version of the history of Galadriel and Celeborn, their son [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) is born in the Hills of Evendim some time between [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [350](../S/Second_Age_350.md) and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_400&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 400 (page does not exist)">400</a>. However, in the later legendarium, Amroth was established as the son of [Amdír](../A/Amdír.md).
