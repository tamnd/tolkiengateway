The **Hills of Evendim**, or **Emyn Uial** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), were a series of hills in northern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

The hills began about a hundred miles north of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in northern Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the southern end was the city of [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas), the first capital of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), at [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim). The [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) or Brandywine River flowed out of this lake. The river [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) also had a tributary beginning in the hills.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) crossed the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) and settled in the Hills of Evendim. However, after the year [700](/wiki/Second_Age_700 "Second Age 700") Galadriel and Celeborn left and established the realm of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

When the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) first returned to Middle-earth, the Hills of Evendim were also populated by Men related to the ancestors of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain). The Númenóreans settled nearby, and the hills formed the core of the kingdom of Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> After the establishments of the Kingdoms in Exile by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons, the Men of Evendim merged with the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain), becoming the people of Arnor together with non-Edain Men like the Men of [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974") the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) conquered [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> His victory was short-lived, however, for in [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") the forces of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) descended from the Hills of Evendim and on the plain between [Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Evendim "Lake Evendim") and the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) crushed the forces of Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, *Emyn Uial*, means "Hills of Dusk", from *[emyn](/wiki/Emyn "Emyn")* ("hills", plural of *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")*)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and *[uial](/wiki/Uial "Uial")* ("[twilight](/wiki/Twilight "Twilight")").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one version of the history of Galadriel and Celeborn, their son [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) is born in the Hills of Evendim some time between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [350](/wiki/Second_Age_350 "Second Age 350") and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_400&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 400 (page does not exist)">400</a>. However, in the later legendarium, Amroth was established as the son of [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The name &#34;Emyn Uial&#34; is given the entirety of the area west of <a href="/wiki/Lake_Evendim" title="Lake Evendim">Lake Evendim</a>. Its northern half is covered in forested hills and ravines, but the southern half is narrower and more flat.</dd></dl>
