The **Host of the West** refers to several armies during the history of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md):

- The first [Host of the West](Host_of_the_Valar.md) refers to the great alliance that fought in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) at the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).
- The second [Host of the West](<Host_of_the_West_(Arnor).md>) refers to the allied army of [Elves](../E/Elves.md), [Men](../M/Men.md), and [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) under [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) and [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md) that fought in the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md).
- The last [Host of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) refers to the allied forces of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) at the close of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).
