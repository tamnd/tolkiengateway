The **Host of the West** refers to several armies during the history of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth):

- The first [Host of the West](/wiki/Host_of_the_Valar "Host of the Valar") refers to the great alliance that fought in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).
- The second [Host of the West](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") refers to the allied army of [Elves](/wiki/Elves), [Men](/wiki/Men), and [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) under [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) and [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan) that fought in the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost).
- The last [Host of the West](/wiki/Army_of_the_West "Army of the West") refers to the allied forces of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) at the close of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).
