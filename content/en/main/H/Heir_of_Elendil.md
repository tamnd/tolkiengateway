**Heir of Elendil** was a title granted to those descended from [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), the founder of the [Realms in Exile](../R/Realms_in_Exile.md) in the late years of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). This title signified a direct claim to the leadership of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) and the legacy of Elendil, the last Lord of [Andúnië](../A/Andúnië.md) in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).

In the North-kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), whose rulers were descended through Elendil's elder son [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md), its definition was simple: all rulers came from Elendil's direct male line, unbroken down to [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) himself.<sup id="cite_ref-NK_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NK-1">[1]</a></sup> The political situation in the South-kingdom of Gondor was more complicated, because its first independent King, [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md), was not a direct heir of Elendil in the strictest sense, being the son of Isildur's younger brother [Anárion](../A/Anárion_son_of_Elendil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-NK_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NK-1">[1]</a></sup> In [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), therefore, the title was taken to refer to any descendants of Elendil who ruled in their own stead, a convenience that allowed Gondor's Kings to represent themselves as true Heirs of Elendil. Even this situation was tenuous, as the first line of Kings would fail with the death of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/Siriondil.md" title="Siriondil">Siriondil</a>, who left no direct heir. Eventually, the line of Kings would fail altogether with the disappearance of the last king, [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After this point, with there being no descendant of Elendil strong enough to make a serious claim and for fear of another [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md), the [Stewards](../S/Stewards.md) who took up the rule of Gondor acted in place of the heirs of Elendil, and took on the powers associated with the title.

These complexities were resolved at the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) with the enthronement of [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn_Elessar.md), both a direct heir of Isildur and an heir of Anárion through his ancestor [Firiel](../F/Firiel.md), as [High King](High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) of Arnor and Gondor.

The title Heir of Elendil represented more than a genealogical claim - it incorporated the stewardship of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md)’s destiny and their role as protectors of the [Free peoples](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). It was through the heirs of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) that the hope of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) endured.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
