**Hyarmendacil I**, born **Ciryaher** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[kiˈrʲaher]</span>; [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [899](/wiki/Third_Age_899 "Third Age 899")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [1149](/wiki/Third_Age_1149 "Third Age 1149"), aged 250 years<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) was the fifteenth [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor) and the fourth and last of the [Ship-kings](/wiki/Ship-king "Ship-king").<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ciryaher was the son of King [Ciryandil](/wiki/Ciryandil), one of the [Ship-kings](/wiki/Ship-kings).<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ciryaher ascended the throne after his father Ciryandil was slain during a siege of [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1015](/wiki/Third_Age_1015 "Third Age 1015")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> by the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim), which were led by lords that had been driven from Umbar after it had been conquered by his grandfather [Eärnil I](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_I).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The siege of Umbar lasted for many years, but the Haradrim could not take Umbar, because of the power of the ships of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ciryaher began his reign by building up Gondor's army and navy, When he had assembled enough forces, Ciryaher came from Gondor by sea and by land. His armies crossed the river [Harnen](/wiki/Harnen) and completely defeated the Haradrim. The kings of the Men of Harad were forced to acknowledge the overlordship of Gondor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050") and their sons lived as hostages in the court of the King of Gondor. After this victory he took the name *Hyarmendacil*, which means "South-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-4">[4]</a></sup>

During his reign [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) reached its greatest extent and power. Gondor extended north to the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant) and to the southern end of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood), west to the river [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood), east to the inland [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn), south to the river Harnen and along the coast to the peninsula and haven of Umbar. The Men of the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) acknowledged the authority of Gondor. For the rest of his reign Gondor was at peace, because no enemies dared to attack Gondor. The watch on [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) was maintained through great fortresses that guaraded the passes to Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-4">[4]</a></sup>

Hyarmendacil reigned for one hundred and thirty-four years, which was the longest reign of all the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) of the line of [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), except <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tarondor" title="Tarondor">one</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-4">[4]</a></sup> He was the last of the [Ship-kings](/wiki/Ship-kings) and was succeeded by his lazy son [Atanatar II Alcarin](/wiki/Atanatar_II "Atanatar II").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ciryaher* is a name [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it probably means "Ship Lord" and be a compound of [cirya](/wiki/Cirya "Cirya") ("ship") and [heru](/wiki/Heru "Heru") ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

*Hyarmendacil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It means "South-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-Ciryaher_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ciryaher-4">[4]</a></sup> It is a compound of [hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen "Hyarmen") ("south") and [-(n)dacil](/wiki/-ndacil "-ndacil") ("victor").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A late manuscript of what would later become Appendix A also mentioned that after his victory over the kings of Harad, Gondor occupied all the land south of the mouths of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) up to the river [Harnen](/wiki/Harnen) and the borders of [Near Harad](/wiki/Near_Harad) and all the coastland as far as Umbar. It also mentions that Umbar became a great fortress and haven for the ships of Gondor and that only king <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tarondor" title="Tarondor">Tarondor</a> reigned longer than Hyarmendacil.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
