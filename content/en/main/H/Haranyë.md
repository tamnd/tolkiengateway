**Haranyë** is a calendar term used in the context of the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md). It is probably the name of the last year of a century.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In every fourth year, except the last of a century (<i>haranyë</i>)...
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a><sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

The word is obviously [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), but of uncertain etymology.

[Helge Fauskanger](Helge_Fauskanger.md) notes that Tolkien's wording does not make clear whether the word describes the "last \[year\] of a century" or a translation of "century" itself.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The word's <a class="external text" href="http://eldamo.org/content/words/word-2462020639.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">entry</a> at *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)* glosses it as "century". <span></span>
