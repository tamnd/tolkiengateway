**Haranyë** is a calendar term used in the context of the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning). It is probably the name of the last year of a century.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

The word is obviously [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), but of uncertain etymology.

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) notes that Tolkien's wording does not make clear whether the word describes the "last \[year\] of a century" or a translation of "century" itself.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The word's <a class="external text" href="http://eldamo.org/content/words/word-2462020639.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">entry</a> at *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)* glosses it as "century". <span></span>
