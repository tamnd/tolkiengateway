The **House of Bëor**, also known as the **First House of the Edain**, was the name given to the first of the three Houses of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) to cross the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) after travelling west, driven forth by the terrors wrought by Morgoth in the east. From the House of Bëor would come many famous lords and heroes, most notably [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren") who claimed a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) from [Morgoth's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown).

The followers of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor) and his descendants would become the **Bëorians**, **Men of Dorthonion**, and the **People of Bëor**.

## Origin {#Origin}

The [Men](/wiki/Men) of the House of Bëor descended from the tribes of Men who abandoned [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien) for the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"). They were at war with the [Men](/wiki/Men_of_Darkness "Men of Darkness") who fell under the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow), and were pursued by them.<sup id="cite_ref-DM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DM-1">[1]</a></sup> They followed a road around northern [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) where they met some [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> At the shores of the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn) a small part was separated, which would later become the House of Bëor. The Greater Folk, the ancestors of the [House of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador"), came later in the north-east woods near the shores. They crafted boats and could sail the inland Sea, and seemed to had been related to the ancestors of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Folk of Beor didn't have beasts of burden so they were hardened by toilsome journeys carrying great loads. A group of 2000 poor and ill-equipped adult men found a way to pass the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-DM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Under the leadership of [Balan](/wiki/Balan) (later called [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor)), they travailed the Blue Mountains into [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) through a valley below the springs of [Thalos](/wiki/Thalos).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§2</sup> in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [310](/wiki/First_Age_310 "First Age 310"). There were discovered by the Noldorin King [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"), eldest son of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), thus they were the first men to encounter one of the Eldar. Finrod befriended the House of Bëor (the First House of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain)) and persuaded [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), overlord of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to grant the new arrivals lands which to inhabit. Thingol gave to them (and the other two Edain Houses that followed) land in the realm of the Noldorin Elf-lords [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amras](/wiki/Amras), the youngest sons of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), of which this land was thereafter known as [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad), the Encampment.

In [402](/wiki/First_Age_402 "First Age 402"), there was fighting in the northern marches of Beleriand where the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attempted to break through the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon). They were repelled by forces from the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor) under [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), and the <a class="mw-selflink selflink">House of Bëor</a>, who become the first among Men to draw swords on behalf of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) of Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In [410](/wiki/First_Age_410 "First Age 410"), the region of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros) in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) was granted to the House of Bëor, with their chief, [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros)> "Boromir (Lord of Ladros)"), becoming the first [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> This fiefdom was short-lived, however, as it was overtaken by Morgoth's forces during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) in [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"); during this assault [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas), the third Lord of Ladros, was slain, leaving the leadership of the ruined house to his brother [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir).

Barahir did what he could to ensure the House's survival, sending the women and children to safety under the leadership of his wife [Emeldir](/wiki/Emeldir). They went to the forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) where some merged with the [Haladin](/wiki/House_of_Haleth "House of Haleth"), but others (including [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan) and [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen)) continued on to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and joined with the people of [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)"). Meanwhile Barahir remained in Dorthonion with his son [Beren](/wiki/Beren), Bregolas' sons [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund) and [Belegund](/wiki/Belegund), and a dwindling company of men. Pressed by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) from [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth) this company was eventually betrayed by one of their members [Gorlim the Unhappy](/wiki/Gorlim_the_Unhappy), revealing Barahir's hideout at [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin). All the company were destroyed save Beren who was away scouting when the attack happened. None the less, from that point on the House of Bëor ceased to be an independent entity and its people joined the surviving Houses of Men.

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png/300px-Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="237" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png/450px-Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/ac/Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png/600px-Juliana_Pinho_-_Beorians.png 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="810"/></a><figcaption><i>Beorians</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Juliana_Pinho" title="Category:Images by Juliana Pinho">Juliana Pinho</a></figcaption></figure>

The Bëorians were noted as being very akin to the Noldor in their appearance. They had dark or brown hair with grey or brown eyes, and some among them were fair-haired; their skin ranged from fair to even swarthy. Most were broad and of stocky build; those as tall as those of the Folk of Hador were rare.<sup id="cite_ref-DM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:308</sup><sup id="cite_ref-Men_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-7">[7]</a></sup>

They were eager, smart and quick in learning, cunning-handed and long in memory. They were moved sooner to pity than to laughter.<sup id="cite_ref-Men_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-7">[7]</a></sup> They are also described to be more steadfast even than the Folk of Hador, fortituously enduring hardships and sorrows, slow to cry and to laugh.<sup id="cite_ref-DM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DM-1">[1]</a></sup>

Many of those descended from Bëorian stock had the traits most akin to those of the Noldor, such as [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") whose mother [Morwen Eledhwen](/wiki/Morwen "Morwen") was Bëorian. He was mistaken for one of the Noldor, inspiring his name *Adanedhel*, meaning 'Man-elf'.

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for "House of Bëor" was <i><b>Bar Bëora</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Genealogies_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Genealogies-8">[8]</a></sup>
