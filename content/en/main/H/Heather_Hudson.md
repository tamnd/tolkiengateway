**Heather Hudson** (born [30 September](../_/30_September.md) [1962](../_/1962.md)) is an American fantasy artist. She often illustrated for [Wizards of the Coast](../W/Wizards_of_the_Coast.md) properties such as [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) and [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md). In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> She also created art for [Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

While originally painting in oils, she transitioned to digital painting in the 2000s. She is married to fellow artist [Daniel Gelon](../D/Daniel_Gelon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
