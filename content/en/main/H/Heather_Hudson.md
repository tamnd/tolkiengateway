**Heather Hudson** (born [30 September](/wiki/30_September) [1962](/wiki/1962)) is an American fantasy artist. She often illustrated for [Wizards of the Coast](/wiki/Wizards_of_the_Coast) properties such as [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) and [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons). In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> She also created art for [Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

While originally painting in oils, she transitioned to digital painting in the 2000s. She is married to fellow artist [Daniel Gelon](/wiki/Daniel_Gelon).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
