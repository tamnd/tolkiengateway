<b><i>Hither Shore</i> Band 6: <i>Gewalt, Konflikt Und Krieg Bei Tolkien / Violence, Conflict, and War in Tolkien</i></b> is the sixth volume of the journal *[Hither Shore](</wiki/Hither_Shore_(journal)> "Hither Shore (journal)")*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Foreword

- Tolkien Seminar 2009
    - "Violence in *The Lord of the Rings*" by Frank Weinreich
    - "Perspectives on Just War in Tolkien’s *Legendarium*" by Annie Birks
    - "Gibt es Macht ohne Gewalt?" by [Thomas Fornet-Ponse](/wiki/Thomas_Fornet-Ponse)
    - "Gewalt und Gewaltdarstellung bei Tolkien im Vergleich mit zeitgenössischen Gewalt- und Aggressionstheorien" by Friedhelm Schneidewind
    - "Der Sängerkrieg: Gesang und Gewalt in J.R.R. Tolkiens Mittelerde" by Julian T.M. Eilmann
    - "Von kühner Recken Streiten? Höfische Akteure und Heroische Gewalt in Tolkiens *Farmer Giles of Ham*" by Patrick Brückner
    - "Dagor dagorath &amp; Ragnarök: Tolkien &amp; the Apocalypse" by [Michaël Devaux](/wiki/Micha%C3%ABl_Devaux)
    - "lean Earth to Till: A Tolkienian Vision of War" by Anna Slack
    - "The Legacy of Swords: Animate Weapons and the Ambivalence of Heroic Violence" by Judith Klinger
    - "Language and Violence: The Orcs, the Ents, and Tom Bombadil" by Martin G.E. Sternberg
    - "The Problem of Closure: War and Narrative in *The Lord of the Rings*" by Margaret Hiley
    - "The Legends of the Trojan War in J.R.R. Tolkien" by Guglielmo Spirito
    - "'contraria contrariis curantur' – Krankheitsheilung als Kampf in Tolkiens *The Lord of the Rings*" by Petra Zimmermann

- Summaries of the German Essays
- Rezensionen / Reviews
