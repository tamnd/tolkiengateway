**Halmir** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [390](/wiki/First_Age_390 "First Age 390") - [471](/wiki/First_Age_471 "First Age 471")) was the son of [Haldan](/wiki/Haldan) and the lord of the [People of Haleth](/wiki/Men_of_Brethil "Men of Brethil") in [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> at the time of the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) ("Battle of Sudden Flame").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was the father of [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)"), [Hundar](/wiki/Hundar), [Hareth](/wiki/Hareth) and [Hiril](/wiki/Hiril).

## History {#History}

After the Dagor Bragollach, and the capture of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith_in_Beleriand "Minas Tirith in Beleriand"), [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) began to roam unhindered across the lands of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand). Halmir called on the aid of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), who sent many stout [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") warriors under the command of [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) to his aid. Together, Halmir and Beleg destroyed a legion of invading Orcs, and gave the southern lands a respite from the defeats in the north.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Halmir joined the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) but died before the [next battle](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad"). He was succeeded as leader of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) by his son, [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
