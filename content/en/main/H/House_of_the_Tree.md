The **house of the Tree** was one of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](../T/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim.md), the kindreds of the Elven city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). Their story is recounted in an early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

This folk was under the leadership of [Galdor of the Tree](../G/Galdor_of_the_Tree.md). Their garments were green, and they carried iron-studded clubs and slings.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

The [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) began with the balrog [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) leading his armies to the city's northern gate, where the folks of the Tree and [of the Hammer of Wrath](House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath.md) were positioned with their lords. When the [Iron Dragons](../I/Iron_Dragons.md) knocked down the gate, [Rog](../R/Rog.md), the leader of the Hammer, shouted his war-cry and both houses leapt at the foe, and "there the blows of their great hammers and the dint of their clubs rang to the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Encircling_Mountains.md) and [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) fell like leaves". From the walls, the [House of the Swallow](House_of_the_Swallow.md) and the [House of the Heavenly Arch](House_of_the_Heavenly_Arch.md) rained arrows down on the invaders, but there was still great confusion.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup>

After the [folk of the Hammer](House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath.md) were completely destroyed outside the walls, the folk of the Tree retreated to the [Square of the Folkwell](../S/Square_of_the_Folkwell.md), and guarded its western entrance by the [Arch of Inwë](../A/Arch_of_Inwë.md) against a horde of [goblins](../G/Goblins.md). [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and his soldiers entered the Square from the north, Tuor carrying a wounded [Ecthelion](../E/Ecthelion.md). Here Galdor was his salvation, as he rushed to kill the pursuers with his club. The guard of the Tree and many other houses were able to form a good battalion, which was led to the [Square of the Palace](../S/Square_of_the_Palace.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:181–2</sup>

After the death of King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md), Galdor and many others escaped, following Tuor through [Idril's secret way](../I/Idril's_secret_way.md). A member of this house, [Legolas](<../L/Legolas_(elf_of_Gondolin).md>), led the [Exiles of Gondolin](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) through the plain of [Tumladin](../T/Tumladin.md), as he knew it very well and was night-sighted.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:189</sup> When the survivors reached [Cristhorn](../C/Cristhorn.md), Galdor, Legolas and other men went ahead of the women and children,<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191</sup> and they fought against an ambush of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) with the help of the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:193</sup> Later they dwelt at the [mouth of Sirion](../M/Mouth_of_Sirion.md), and when their lord went to [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), still some of them named themselves as the People of Galdor.<sup id="cite_ref-names_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-names-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Their [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) names were <i><b>Nos Galdon</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-names_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-names-2">[2]</a></sup> and <i><b>Thlim Galdon</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Names_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-3">[3]</a></sup> while in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) they were simply known as <i><b>Aldar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Nos Galdon* was later ammended to <i><b>Nos nan Alwen</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-names_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-names-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
