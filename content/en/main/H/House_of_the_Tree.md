The **house of the Tree** was one of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](/wiki/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim "Twelve houses of the Gondothlim"), the kindreds of the Elven city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Their story is recounted in an early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

This folk was under the leadership of [Galdor of the Tree](/wiki/Galdor_of_the_Tree). Their garments were green, and they carried iron-studded clubs and slings.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

The [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) began with the balrog [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)") leading his armies to the city's northern gate, where the folks of the Tree and [of the Hammer of Wrath](/wiki/House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath "House of the Hammer of Wrath") were positioned with their lords. When the [Iron Dragons](/wiki/Iron_Dragons) knocked down the gate, [Rog](/wiki/Rog), the leader of the Hammer, shouted his war-cry and both houses leapt at the foe, and "there the blows of their great hammers and the dint of their clubs rang to the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains) and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) fell like leaves". From the walls, the [House of the Swallow](/wiki/House_of_the_Swallow) and the [House of the Heavenly Arch](/wiki/House_of_the_Heavenly_Arch) rained arrows down on the invaders, but there was still great confusion.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176</sup>

After the [folk of the Hammer](/wiki/House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath "House of the Hammer of Wrath") were completely destroyed outside the walls, the folk of the Tree retreated to the [Square of the Folkwell](/wiki/Square_of_the_Folkwell), and guarded its western entrance by the [Arch of Inwë](/wiki/Arch_of_Inw%C3%AB) against a horde of [goblins](/wiki/Goblins "Goblins"). [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and his soldiers entered the Square from the north, Tuor carrying a wounded [Ecthelion](/wiki/Ecthelion). Here Galdor was his salvation, as he rushed to kill the pursuers with his club. The guard of the Tree and many other houses were able to form a good battalion, which was led to the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:181–2</sup>

After the death of King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), Galdor and many others escaped, following Tuor through [Idril's secret way](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way). A member of this house, [Legolas](</wiki/Legolas_(elf_of_Gondolin)> "Legolas (elf of Gondolin)"), led the [Exiles of Gondolin](/wiki/Exiles_of_Gondolin) through the plain of [Tumladin](/wiki/Tumladin), as he knew it very well and was night-sighted.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:189</sup> When the survivors reached [Cristhorn](/wiki/Cristhorn), Galdor, Legolas and other men went ahead of the women and children,<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:191</sup> and they fought against an ambush of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) with the help of the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:193</sup> Later they dwelt at the [mouth of Sirion](/wiki/Mouth_of_Sirion "Mouth of Sirion"), and when their lord went to [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), still some of them named themselves as the People of Galdor.<sup id="cite_ref-names_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-names-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Their [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) names were <i><b>Nos Galdon</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-names_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-names-2">[2]</a></sup> and <i><b>Thlim Galdon</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Names_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-3">[3]</a></sup> while in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) they were simply known as <i><b>Aldar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Nos Galdon* was later ammended to <i><b>Nos nan Alwen</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-names_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-names-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
