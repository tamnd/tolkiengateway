**Haeren** was a location in the western part of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), just below the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md).

The location is not mentioned in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, and its nature is unknown. The name of the location only appears as a handwritten addition by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) on the final version of the *[General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name is unknown, but the beginning *hae-* may contain the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) adjective <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hae&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hae (page does not exist)">hae</a></i> "very far away" (compare *[Haerast](Haerast.md)* "Far Shore").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
