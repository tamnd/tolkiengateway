**Huorns** were creatures much like [Ents](/wiki/Ents). However, although they were sentient and capable of feeling and perceiving things, they did not appear to be fully sapient.

## History {#History}

The origin of the Huorns is unknown. It is not clear if Huorns were Ents that had become tree-like, or trees that had become Entish. Perhaps both varieties existed, though the [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) believed the former was true.<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-1">[1]</a></sup> Either way it is obvious they were part way between trees and Ents.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Huorns lived in [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest),<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-1">[1]</a></sup> though it is possible that the trees of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest), especially [Old Man Willow](/wiki/Old_Man_Willow), were Huorns as well.

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Huorns were aroused by [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) to destroy [Nan Curunír](/wiki/Nan_Curun%C3%ADr).<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-1">[1]</a></sup> Gandalf saw the shadow of these Huorns, as he rode with the host from [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) to [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Directed by the Ents, the Huorns also helped the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) to fight the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) at the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg).<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Huorns had great power and could wrap themselves in shadow. When angry, they were able to move with great speed. The Huorns further had voices, as they could speak with the Ents. When no Ents looked after them, the Huorns were "*queer and wild*", a threat to the [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples).<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Huorns* is a name in the Ent's "short language", given to those creatures because "they still have voices, and can speak with the Ents", according to Merry's account.<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-1">[1]</a></sup>

In drafts texts they received other names such as *Galbedirs*, *Lamorni* and *Ornómar* or *Ornómi*, with the meaning of "Talking Trees"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>. But it is not certain whether the name *Huorns* carried that meaning on.

It does have the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) element *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* "tall tree", and in an etymological Tolkien gave its first element as derived from *hō*, with a gloss of uncertain reading: it might be "speak, show" or "spirit, shadow", and could be related to other words associated with "heart".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> So, *Huorn* could mean "speaking tree" or "tree with a spirit" or "with a heart". The first element might also be related to the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") *[KHUG](/wiki/KHUG)* "bark, bay".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>.
