**Harlequin Miniatures** was a division under the British company [Harlequin Games](/wiki/Harlequin_Games) which in the late 1990s produced a range of metal miniatures based on the books by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) (in 32 mm size, unpainted). They lost their license to [Games Workshop](/wiki/Games_Workshop), who opted for the same size in the *[Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings_Strategy_Battle_Game)* so the figures could be mingled.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_One_Ring_Magazine_1.png"><img src="/w/images/f/f5/The_One_Ring_Magazine_1.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="412" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="412"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Harlequin Games also published *The One Ring* magazine (in total 6 issues were released, supporting their miniature ranges.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
