**Helm's Deep** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), the valley in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).
    - The [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg), the fortress of Rohan which protected the valley.
    - The [Battle of Helm's Deep](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg"), a conflict of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) between the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) and [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai).
