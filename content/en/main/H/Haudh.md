<i><b>haudh</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "funeral mound".<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *haudh* derives from [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) *khabdā* ("pile, (artificial) mound"), itself deriving from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KHAB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHAB (page does not exist)">KHAB</a> ("heap up, pile up"). Since *haudh* also carried a connotation of a funeral mound "in which weapons and other valuables were also buried", the word shows an apparent influence from root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=KHAW&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KHAW (page does not exist)">KHAW</a> ("cover up, hide away, lay in store").<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>
