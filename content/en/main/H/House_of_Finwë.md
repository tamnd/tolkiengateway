The **House of Finwë** was a large [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") royal house founded by [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor). Its device was that of a great golden sun, even though Finwë was slain before the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) was made.

The House of Finwë had three main branches, one for each of Finwë's sons: the [House of Fëanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor), the [House of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin) and the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin).

### Lineage {#Lineage}

Finwë had two wives. His first was [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel), who passed away soon after bearing their only child, [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor). His second wife was [Indis](/wiki/Indis), of the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), who bore him two sons: [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), and two daughters: [Findis](/wiki/Findis) and [Írimë](/wiki/%C3%8Drim%C3%AB).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Finwë's two daughters don't appear in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*.

In earlier versions of the legendarium, Finwë and Indis had three daughters: **Findis**, **Faniel** and **Finvain**. It remains unclear if the third daughter survived in the final conception of Finwë's family tree.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
