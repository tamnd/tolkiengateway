The **House of Finwë** was a large [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) royal house founded by [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md), [King of the Noldor](../K/King_of_the_Noldor.md). Its device was that of a great golden sun, even though Finwë was slain before the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) was made.

The House of Finwë had three main branches, one for each of Finwë's sons: the [House of Fëanor](House_of_Fëanor.md), the [House of Fingolfin](House_of_Fingolfin.md) and the [House of Finarfin](House_of_Finarfin.md).

### Lineage {#Lineage}

Finwë had two wives. His first was [Míriel](../M/Míriel.md), who passed away soon after bearing their only child, [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md). His second wife was [Indis](../I/Indis.md), of the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md), who bore him two sons: [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md), and two daughters: [Findis](../F/Findis.md) and [Írimë](../_/Írimë.md).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Finwë's two daughters don't appear in the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*.

In earlier versions of the legendarium, Finwë and Indis had three daughters: **Findis**, **Faniel** and **Finvain**. It remains unclear if the third daughter survived in the final conception of Finwë's family tree.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
