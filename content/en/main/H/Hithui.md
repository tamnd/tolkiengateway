**Hithui** was the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the eleventh month of the year, equivalent to [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) [Hísimë](/wiki/H%C3%ADsim%C3%AB) and comparable to the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits)' [Blotmath](/wiki/Blotmath) and modern November. Its name comes from the Elvish word for 'mist', and it marked the descent of winter on [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). After the beginning of Hithui, no long journeys were attempted in the [Northlands](/wiki/Northlands) until the arrival of the following spring.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
