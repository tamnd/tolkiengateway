**Hithui** was the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name for the eleventh month of the year, equivalent to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) [Hísimë](Hísimë.md) and comparable to the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md)' [Blotmath](../B/Blotmath.md) and modern November. Its name comes from the Elvish word for 'mist', and it marked the descent of winter on [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). After the beginning of Hithui, no long journeys were attempted in the [Northlands](../N/Northlands.md) until the arrival of the following spring.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
