**Hither Shore** was a term used especially by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) for the mortal lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), a reminder of the far distant shores of [Aman](/wiki/Aman).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), it also had a more specific meaning, since it translates into that language as the name [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), a cold seaward region in the northwest of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
