The **House of Fëanor** were the cursed descendants and followers of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), the eldest son of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and his spouse [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel).

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a7/Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg/300px-Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="196" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/a7/Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg/450px-Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/a7/Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg/600px-Angel_Falto_-_Feanor_%26_Sons.jpg 2x" data-file-width="700" data-file-height="457"/></a><figcaption><i>Feanor &amp; Sons</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Angel_Falto" title="Category:Images by Angel Falto">Angel Falto</a></figcaption></figure>

Fëanor was one of the greatest, and certainly the most skilled,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), but his burning anger at the loss of his [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) led him to commit acts that would greatly diminish the influence of his house among his people. As the heir of Finwë, his descendants (specifically [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros), his eldest son) should have inherited the [Kingship of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor "King of the Noldor"), but because of Fëanor's rash acts, the lordship of the Noldor passed to his brothers: [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) in [Aman](/wiki/Aman),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> and [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[3]</a></sup> For this reason, and for the loss of the Silmarils, the members of the House of Fëanor are referred to as the *Dispossessed*.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[3]</a></sup>

The history of the House was a woeful one, and most of its members met an early end. Fëanor and six of his seven sons perished before the end of the First Age. His second son [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) survived beyond the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), wandering the shores of Middle-earth, but his fate is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> The last of the House was Fëanor's grandson [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), the son of [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> who had a great part in the making of the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), and was tortured to death by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name of the House of Fëanor was <i><b>Nos Fëanor</b></i> (cf. *[nosse](/wiki/Noss%C3%AB#Quenya "Nossë")*).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) used the term "**[Fëanorians](/wiki/F%C3%ABanorians)**" for the followers of Fëanor many times in his notes and comments along *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, probably just for practical reasons.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> It is used like this by the [fandom](/wiki/Fandom "Fandom").

In the fourth stanza of the final form of the *[Eärendillinwë](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendillinw%C3%AB)*, Tolkien uses the word **Fëanorians**, though it is unclear if the term refers to the followers of Fëanor or the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> Tolkien also uses the word **Fëanorian**, when discussing linguistic matters of Old [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), to refer to the people of the House of Fëanor.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>
