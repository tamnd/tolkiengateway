**Hannah Blessing Wood** (born [1983](../_/1983.md)) is a poet, and also the sister of [Elijah Wood](../E/Elijah_Wood.md). She appeared in minor roles without lines in two of her brother's movies, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Flyer_(film)" title="wikipedia:Radio Flyer (film)">Radio Flyer</a></i> and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*, where she played a girl sheltering in the [Glittering Caves](../G/Glittering_Caves.md).
