**Half-orcs** were among [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s servants in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-TTIII9_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTIII9-3">[3]</a></sup>

Saruman's servants also included creatures known as [Goblin-men](/wiki/Goblin-men) who appeared to blend the traits of [Men](/wiki/Men) and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), but they were a different breed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The origin and nature of the half-orcs is unknown. In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), apparently Saruman learned that it was possible to mate [Men](/wiki/Men) with Orcs. Thus he created half-orcs and goblin-men who served him. Unlike ordinary Orcs, half-orcs were completely unbothered by sunlight.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [Squint-eyed southerner](/wiki/Squint-eyed_southerner), an outlaw driven from [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland) to [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and subservient to Saruman, was possibly a half-orc.<sup id="cite_ref-Knife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Knife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT10_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT10-6">[6]</a></sup>

Half-orcs were among the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) who came to Saruman's banner of the [White Hand](/wiki/White_Hand) in [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-TTIII9_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTIII9-3">[3]</a></sup> Many were counted among the strongest servants of Saruman. The half-orcs mostly perished at the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), either before the fortress walls or by the [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns).

More Half-orcs later followed [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) into exile, and were among his [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) who occupied [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Concerning the origin of the half-orcs, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) suggested late in his life that:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>It became clear in time that undoubted Men could under the domination of <a href="/wiki/Morgoth" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a> or his agents in a few generations be reduced almost to the Orc-level of mind and habits; and then they would or could be made to mate with Orcs, producing new breeds, often larger and more cunning. There is no doubt that long afterwards, in the <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>, Saruman rediscovered this, or learned of it in lore, and in his lust for mastery committed this, his wickedest deed: the interbreeding of Orcs and Men, producing both Men-orcs large and cunning, and Orc-men treacherous and vile.<sup id="cite_ref-myths_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-myths-8">[8]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Half-orcs (Sindarin <i>Piryrch</i>, sing. <i>Perorch</i>) are a crossbeed between Men and Orcs, being a distinct race in their own. Game statistics are given for players choosing to be a Half-orc.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Half-orcs and Greater Half-orcs are Orc Factions, playable by Fallen Wizards.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007-: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Half-orcs were categorized as one of the varieties of Orc-kind, along with Orcs, Goblins, <a href="/wiki/Hobgoblins" title="Hobgoblins">Hobgoblins</a> and Uruks. Half-orc Bandits were found in the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> They were affiliated with <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a> and could be found in almost every region he had influence in. His half-orcs in <a href="/wiki/Eriador" title="Eriador">Eriador</a> included many in <a href="/wiki/Dunland" title="Dunland">Dunland</a>, <a href="/wiki/Enedwaith" title="Enedwaith">Enedwaith</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>, the Lone-lands, and <a href="/wiki/Andrath" title="Andrath">Andrath</a> in Bree-land. A small number of half-orcs could be found as far as Nan Gurth in <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. His half-orcs also could be found in <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> as far east as the Sutcrofts in the <a href="/wiki/Eastemnet" title="Eastemnet">Eastemnet</a>.</dd></dl>
