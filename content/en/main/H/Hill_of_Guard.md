The **Hill of Guard** or **Amon Tirith** was the outlying hill on which [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) was built.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> On its western side it was joined with Mount [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) by a narrow shoulder which rose to the height of the fifth wall of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was originally called *Amon Anor* ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") 'Hill of the Sun').<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Tirith* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-4">[4]</a></sup> rendition of "Hill of Guard".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that it is a combination of *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") and *[tirith](/wiki/Tirith "Tirith")* ("watch", "guard").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-4">[4]</a></sup>
