**Havens of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas)** (called **Havens of Círdan** by [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) were the two great walled [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") cities: **[Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest)** at the mouth of the River [Nenning](/wiki/Nenning), and to the north **[Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar)** at the mouth of the River [Brithon](/wiki/Brithon).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Built on the western shores of the Falas, the Havens were the realm of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) the Shipwright and his people, the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim), who were considered [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Havens were besieged during the [First Battle of Beleriand](/wiki/First_Battle_of_Beleriand), but during the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath) the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that besieged the cities turned north to fight the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) and were all slain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> When [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") established himself in [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) his rule extended over all of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) excepts for the coasts, which continued to be ruled by Círdan, Felagund's ally. Thanks to the alliance between the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim) and the [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond), the Havens were built as fair towns and fortified behind great walls.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Havens of the Falas held out during the later [Battles of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand) until they were finally destroyed in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [473](/wiki/First_Age_473 "First Age 473"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and Círdan's people fled to the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) and the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
