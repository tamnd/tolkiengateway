**Havens of the [Falas](../F/Falas.md)** (called **Havens of Círdan** by [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) were the two great walled [Elven](../E/Elves.md) cities: **[Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md)** at the mouth of the River [Nenning](../N/Nenning.md), and to the north **[Brithombar](../B/Brithombar.md)** at the mouth of the River [Brithon](../B/Brithon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Built on the western shores of the Falas, the Havens were the realm of [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) the Shipwright and his people, the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md), who were considered [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Havens were besieged during the [First Battle of Beleriand](../F/First_Battle_of_Beleriand.md), but during the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md) the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) that besieged the cities turned north to fight the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) and were all slain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> When [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) established himself in [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) his rule extended over all of [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) excepts for the coasts, which continued to be ruled by Círdan, Felagund's ally. Thanks to the alliance between the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md) and the [Elves of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md), the Havens were built as fair towns and fortified behind great walls.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Havens of the Falas held out during the later [Battles of Beleriand](../B/Battles_of_Beleriand.md) until they were finally destroyed in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [473](../F/First_Age_473.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and Círdan's people fled to the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md) and the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
