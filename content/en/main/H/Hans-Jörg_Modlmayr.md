**Hans-Jörg Modlmayr** (born [6 March](/wiki/6_March) [1940](/wiki/1940)) is a German author and broadcaster. He studied history and English at Peterhouse College in [Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge). After his degree he taught German at Gonville &amp; Caius College and in the German Department of Cambridge University.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

While studying at Cambridge, Modlmayr became acquainted with the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in [1964](/wiki/1964).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> On [27 July](/wiki/27_July) [1966](/wiki/1966), Modlmayr and [J.S. Ryan](/wiki/John_Ryan "John Ryan") visited [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) without being invited. Nevertheless, Tolkien spent some time talking with them and showing them translations of his works.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

A collection of letters from Tolkien to Modlmayr came up [for sale](/wiki/J.A._Stargardt_Katalog_698 "J.A. Stargardt Katalog 698") in June 2012.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
