**Hendwine** was a son of a [Man](../M/Men.md) [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) and an [Elf](../E/Elf.md) [Nelmir](../N/Nelmir.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. He also had a brother called [Heorrenda](Heorrenda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Hen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hen-4">[3]</a></sup>

He is only mentioned in one note to *[The Cottage of Lost Play](../T/The_Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md)*, and nothing else is known about this specific character, since he doesn't appear in any of the narratives. However, see <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium">below</a> for more information on the later developments of the character.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Hendwine* is in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), probably meaning "close friend", from *hende* ("near, at hand") + *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-Hen_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hen-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Hendwine was also called <i><b>Lúthien</b></i> in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), with <i><b>Helúsion</b></i> being its [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) cognate.<sup id="cite_ref-Hen_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hen-4">[3]</a></sup>

Both *Lúthien* and *(He)Lúsion* here mean "friend", deriving from the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=LUSU&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LUSU (page does not exist)">LUSU</a> ("foment, cherish, warm, bathe").<sup id="cite_ref-Hen_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hen-4">[3]</a></sup> The element *He-* in *Helúsion* is most likely a non-significant syllable added to preserve the alliteration with its Old English form *Hendwine* and that of his brother *Heorrenda*.<sup id="cite_ref-Win_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Win-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to later notes, the character was called **Hlúdwine**, and was the son of [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md) and [Earissë](../E/Earissë.md). Here too, he also had a brother called Heorrenda.<sup id="cite_ref-Win_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Win-5">[4]</a></sup>

Hlúdwine appears in two of the notes in the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Notebook_C&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Notebook C (page does not exist)">Notebook C</a></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Win_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Win-5">[4]</a></sup> a small pocket-book which [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) used for notes and suggestions while writing *The Book of Lost Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Judging by the name of Hlúdwine's father: *Ælfwine*, this version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) probably belonged to the period where the mariner [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) (Ottor Wǽfre) from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_century" title="wikipedia:5th century">5th century AD</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglia_(peninsula)" title="wikipedia:Anglia (peninsula)">Anglia</a> was replaced by Ælfwine of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/11th_century" title="wikipedia:11th century">11th century AD</a><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

### Later etymology {#Later_etymology}

The name *Hlúdwine* is in Old English, consisting of the elements *hlúd* ("loud, sonorous") + *wine* ("friend").<sup id="cite_ref-Win_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Win-5">[4]</a></sup>
