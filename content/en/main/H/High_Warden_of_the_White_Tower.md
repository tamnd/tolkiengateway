**High Warden of the White Tower** is a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") title borne at least by [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), son of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor_II). Boromir's brother [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) told to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) that Boromir 'was the High Warden of the [White Tower](/wiki/Tower_of_Ecthelion "Tower of Ecthelion"), and our Captain-General'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is not known what responsibilities belonged to the office of the High Warden, or was it only a honorific title. No other bearers of the title are known. It is also possible that the title means the same as <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Captain_of_the_White_Tower&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Captain of the White Tower (page does not exist)">Captain of the White Tower</a>, a title borne by Boromir,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and Faramir after him.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
