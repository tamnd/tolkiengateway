**He chanted a song of wizardry,**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> is the first line of the first out of two poems in the Silmarillion, and is an excerpt for the [Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian) according to the last line before the poem. It is the battle between Sauron and Finrod Felagund.

[Clamavi De Profundis](/wiki/Clamavi_De_Profundis) made a song out of it. and added some elvish in-between some of the lines. They titled the song: **The Battle of Sauron and Finrod Felagund**.

## Poem excerpt {#Poem_excerpt}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><audio src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/Christopher_Tolkien_-_He_chanted_a_song_of_wizardry%2C.wav" width="300" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 300px;" controls=""><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_He_chanted_a_song_of_wizardry,.wav">https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_He_chanted_a_song_of_wizardry,.wav</a></audio><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien" title="Christopher Tolkien">Christopher Tolkien</a> reads <i>He chanted a song of wizardry,</i></figcaption></figure>

<br><br><br><br><br>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>He chanted a song of wizardry, <br/>
Of piercing, opening, of treachery, <br/>
Revealing, uncovering, betraying. <br/>
Then sudden Felagund there swaying, <br/>
Sang in a song of staying, <br/>
Resisting, battling against power, <br/>
Of secrets kept, strength like a tower, <br/>
And trust unbroken, freedom, escape; <br/>
Of changing and shifting shape, <br/>
Of snares eluded, broken traps, <br/>
The prison opening, the chain that snaps.
</p>
</div>
