<i><b>hröa</b></i> (pl. <i><b>hröar</b></i>) is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "body of an incarnate being".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The union of [*fëa* and *hröa*](../F/Fëa_and_hröa.md) was the essential characteristic of the [Incarnates](../I/Incarnates.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Unlike the *[fëa](../F/Fëa.md)* ("spirit"), it was made of the substances of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) and therefore susceptible to the evils of the world. An old form of the word was <i><b>hrondo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word came from <i>*srawâ</i>, the same as *hrávë* ("flesh"). The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) cognate for both *hröa* and *hrávë* was <i><b>rhaw</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
