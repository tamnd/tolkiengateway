<i><b>hröa</b></i> (pl. <i><b>hröar</b></i>) is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "body of an incarnate being".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The union of [*fëa* and *hröa*](/wiki/F%C3%ABa_and_hr%C3%B6a "Fëa and hröa") was the essential characteristic of the [Incarnates](/wiki/Incarnates).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Unlike the *[fëa](/wiki/F%C3%ABa "Fëa")* ("spirit"), it was made of the substances of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) and therefore susceptible to the evils of the world. An old form of the word was <i><b>hrondo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word came from <i>*srawâ</i>, the same as *hrávë* ("flesh"). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) cognate for both *hröa* and *hrávë* was <i><b>rhaw</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
