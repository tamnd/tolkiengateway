**Helm's Gate** was the entrance to the deep gorge of [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> on the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was fortified by the [Deeping Wall](/wiki/Deeping_Wall),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which ran from the southern side of the cliff wall of the gorge to the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg), a fortress on an heel of rock that was thrust outward from the northern side of the cliff wall, to block the entrance to Helm's Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-4">[4]</a></sup>

The [Deeping-stream](/wiki/Deeping-stream) flowed out of Helm's Deep through a wide culvert at the bottom of the Deeping Wall into the [Westfold Vale](/wiki/Westfold_Vale).<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-4">[4]</a></sup>

On the [3](/wiki/3_March "3 March")-[4 March](/wiki/4_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") the forces of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), the [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan), defended the Deeping Wall and the Hornburg against the army of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> during this battle the wall guarding the gate was partially destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The element *Helm* in the name *Helm's Gate* refers to the name of a man called Helm.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The gorge behind the Hornburg was called Helm's Deep after King [Helm](/wiki/Helm) of Rohan sought refuge in the Hornburg when his realm was invaded by the ursurper [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) and the Dunlendings.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is probable that the element *Helm* in *Helm's Gate* also refers to King Helm of Rohan.
