**Halbarad** was a [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

## History {#History}

Halbarad spent his early life watching the borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Halbarad was sent to the aid of his [Chieftain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain") [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). From [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), he led a thirty-man strong group of Rangers, calling themselves the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company). With them rode [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), the sons of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond). In the night between the [5th](/wiki/5_March "5 March") and the [6th of March](/wiki/6_March "6 March"), the Company met Aragorn at [Dol Baran](/wiki/Dol_Baran). Halbarad gave his kinsman a standard, which was a gift from [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), and a message, summoning him to take the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Grey Company, now with [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) part of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s comitatus, passed through the Paths of the Dead, to the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech), over [Tarlang's Neck](/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck), and on to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). He captained a [Corsair](/wiki/Corsair) ship and they sailed toward [Harlond](/wiki/Harlond_in_Gondor "Harlond in Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-Debate_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Debate-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [March 15](/wiki/15_March "15 March") they arrived on the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) and fought in the [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields"). Halbarad unfurled the standard, and it showed the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the signs of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). He was killed in this battle, and would never return to the North.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Though *Halbarad* is not glossed, it is suggested by [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) to possibly be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name comprised of the element *[barad](/wiki/Barad "Barad")* ("tower, great towering building") and the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hall&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hall (page does not exist)">hall</a></i> ("exalted, high").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Halbarad is in command of <a href="/wiki/Esteld%C3%ADn" title="Esteldín">Esteldín</a>, a hidden <a href="/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain" title="Dúnedain">Dúnedain</a> city in the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>. After receiving orders from Elrond to seek out Aragorn in Rohan, Halbarad gathered a large company of Rangers to head south. After suffering short delays in Enedwaith, Halbarad managed to form an alliance of sorts with the Falcon Clan in Dunland. However, Halbarad and his company were soon betrayed and imprisoned by the Falcon Clan. Some of the Rangers with Halbarad managed to escape and soon the Grey Company was free, at the cost of many lives. By the time they reached the borders of Rohan, only 30 of their number remained. At the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, Halbarad sacrifices himself to protect Aragorn from being killed by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gothmog" title="Gothmog">Gothmog</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Halbarad appears at the beginning of the game at the <a href="/wiki/Sarn_Ford" title="Sarn Ford">Sarn Ford</a>. Shortly after the <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Nazgûl</a> attacked the <a href="/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor" title="Dúnedain of Arnor">Dúnedain</a> who guarded <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">the Shire</a>, he sends <a href="/wiki/Eradan_(video_game_character)" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Andriel" title="Andriel">Andriel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Farin_(video_game_character)" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a> to <a href="/wiki/Bree" title="Bree">Bree</a> to warn <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
