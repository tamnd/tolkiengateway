**Hildórien** was the land in the far [east](/wiki/East "East") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in which the first [Men](/wiki/Men) or [Hildor](/wiki/Hildor) [awoke](/wiki/Awakening_of_Men "Awakening of Men") near the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was said among the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) that [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) went there and put a darkness in the hearts of the Men.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Some Men, who were at war with those who fell under the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow), fled Hildórien seeking the [Light](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor "Light of Valinor") of the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman").<sup id="cite_ref-DM_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DM-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)* appendix confirms that *Hildórien* is of the same [root](/wiki/Root "Root") as the word *[Hildor](/wiki/Hildor)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It can be therefore understood as [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *hildor* + *[-ien](/wiki/-nd "-nd")*, therefore "Land of the Followers".

## Geography {#Geography}

Hildorien was far to the east of [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), where the first Elves awoke.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It may have been located on Middle-earth's shore with the [East Sea](/wiki/East_Sea) and surrounded by the [Mountains of the Wind](/wiki/Mountains_of_the_Wind).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, Men awoke in a hidden valley called the **Vale of Murmenalda**, the **Vale of Sleep**, or **Slumbrous Dale**. It was enclosed by a wall of encircling mountains, east of [Palisor](/wiki/Palisor), described as "hallowed" and full of sweet fragrances and odours, and songs of [nightingales](/wiki/Nightingales "Nightingales"), its beauty being comparable to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). It was full of sleeping figures.

It was discovered by the [Dark Elf](/wiki/Dark_Elves "Dark Elves") [Nuin](/wiki/Nuin) after finding a passage through the mountain wall. Nuin almost swooned at its loveliness and was puzzled by the Sleepers. Despite [Tû](</wiki/T%C3%BB_(king_of_the_Hisildi)> "Tû (king of the Hisildi)")'s warnings, Nuin visited it often to watch the Sleepers until, overcome with curiosity, he woke the first pair of them.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

*Murmenalda* is [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), composed of *murme* "sleep" ([root](/wiki/Root "Root") MURU) and *nalda* (root NLDL).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the "[Myths Transformed](/wiki/Myths_Transformed)" section of *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*, the awakening of Men is proposed to be much earlier, sometime between the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves) and the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers) ([Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1050](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1050 "Year of the Trees 1050") - [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1100](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1100 "Year of the Trees 1100")).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> In *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, the date is given as [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1075](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1075 "Year of the Trees 1075") (which is of course by no means final desision).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
