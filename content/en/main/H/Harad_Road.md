The **Harad Road** was the long road that connected [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) and [Harad](Harad.md).

## Course {#Course}

The Harad Road ran from the [Cross-roads](../C/Cross-roads.md) southward through [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> crossing the river [Poros](../P/Poros.md) at the [Crossings of Poros](../C/Crossings_of_Poros.md), and then across [South Gondor](../S/South_Gondor.md) to the ford across the river [Harnen](Harnen.md) and beyond to the far lands of [Harad](Harad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-General_Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-General_Map-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that the Road was built or first used by the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md), as [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) extended its power to the south in the early [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-5">[4]</a></sup>

The southernmost part of the Road is not clear,<sup id="cite_ref-General_Map_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-General_Map-4">[3]</a></sup> perhaps indicating that it later fell into disuse and had disappeared by the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Harad* means "south" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> so Harad Road means "South Road".

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The road can be followed from the <a href="../C/Cross-roads.md" title="Cross-roads">Cross-roads</a> until the border of <a href="../S/South_Gondor.md" title="South Gondor">South Gondor</a>. There, the road is blocked by a <a href="Haradrim.md" title="Haradrim">haradrim</a> war-camp blocking the path, as Harondor is not present in the game.</dd></dl>
