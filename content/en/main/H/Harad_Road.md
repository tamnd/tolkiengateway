The **Harad Road** was the long road that connected [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) and [Harad](/wiki/Harad).

## Course {#Course}

The Harad Road ran from the [Cross-roads](/wiki/Cross-roads) southward through [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> crossing the river [Poros](/wiki/Poros) at the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros), and then across [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor) to the ford across the river [Harnen](/wiki/Harnen) and beyond to the far lands of [Harad](/wiki/Harad).<sup id="cite_ref-General_Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-General_Map-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is possible that the Road was built or first used by the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians), as [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) extended its power to the south in the early [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-5">[4]</a></sup>

The southernmost part of the Road is not clear,<sup id="cite_ref-General_Map_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-General_Map-4">[3]</a></sup> perhaps indicating that it later fell into disuse and had disappeared by the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Harad* means "south" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> so Harad Road means "South Road".

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The road can be followed from the <a href="/wiki/Cross-roads" title="Cross-roads">Cross-roads</a> until the border of <a href="/wiki/South_Gondor" title="South Gondor">South Gondor</a>. There, the road is blocked by a <a href="/wiki/Haradrim" title="Haradrim">haradrim</a> war-camp blocking the path, as Harondor is not present in the game.</dd></dl>
