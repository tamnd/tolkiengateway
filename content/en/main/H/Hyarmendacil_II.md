**Hyarmendacil II**, born with the name **Vinyarion** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1391](../T/Third_Age_1391.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1621](../T/Third_Age_1621.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Vinyarion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vinyarion-2">[2]</a></sup>), was the twenty-fourth [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Vinyarion ascended the throne after his father [Aldamir](../A/Aldamir.md) was killed in a battle with the Harad and the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1540](../T/Third_Age_1540.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Eleven years later, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1551](../T/Third_Age_1551.md), he defeated the Men of the Harad<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and took the name Hyarmendacil II<sup id="cite_ref-Vinyarion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vinyarion-2">[2]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vinyarion* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it means "New Son" and is a compound of [vinya](../V/Vinya.md) ("new") and the suffix [ion](../I/Ion.md) ("son").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

*Hyarmendacil* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. It means "South-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is a compound of [hyarmen](Hyarmen.md) ("south") and [-(n)dacil](../_/-ndacil.md) ("victor").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a late manuscript of what would later become Appendix A, it is mentioned that Vinyarion took the name Hyarmendakil II in 1551 after a great victory over Harad in revenge for the death of his father Aldamir.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>
