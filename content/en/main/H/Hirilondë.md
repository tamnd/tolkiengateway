**Hirilondë**, the "Haven-finder", was a ship built by [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) and the [Guild of Venturers](../G/Guild_of_Venturers.md) in the shipyards of [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md), a seaport in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). People wondered at its great size and called it **Turuphanto**, the 'Wooden Whale'.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Aldarion sailed with it to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for the first time in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [877](../S/Second_Age_877.md) and returned after five years, [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [882](../S/Second_Age_882.md).<sup id="cite_ref-notes_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-1">[1]</a></sup>

There was a custom among the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md): when one of their ships set sail over the sea, a woman, usually a relative to the captain, placed a branch of [oiolairë](../O/Oiolairë.md) as the [Green Bough of Return](../G/Green_Bough_of_Return.md) at the prow, but [Erendis](../E/Erendis.md), Aldarion's wife, refused to either set the bough or send one for Hirilondë. Instead, the wife of his captain at that time placed one on the ship.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

After Aldarion became [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md) and left towards Middle-earth once again, in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [883](../S/Second_Age_883.md), there was no Green Bough of Return on Hirilondë's prow. There stood the image of an [eagle](../E/Eagle.md) with golden beak and jewelled eyes, given to him by [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) during his previous journeys to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
