**Hirilondë**, the "Haven-finder", was a ship built by [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") and the [Guild of Venturers](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers) in the shipyards of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna), a seaport in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). People wondered at its great size and called it **Turuphanto**, the 'Wooden Whale'.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

Aldarion sailed with it to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for the first time in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [877](/wiki/Second_Age_877 "Second Age 877") and returned after five years, [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [882](/wiki/Second_Age_882 "Second Age 882").<sup id="cite_ref-notes_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-notes-1">[1]</a></sup>

There was a custom among the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans): when one of their ships set sail over the sea, a woman, usually a relative to the captain, placed a branch of [oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB "Oiolairë") as the [Green Bough of Return](/wiki/Green_Bough_of_Return) at the prow, but [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis), Aldarion's wife, refused to either set the bough or send one for Hirilondë. Instead, the wife of his captain at that time placed one on the ship.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

After Aldarion became [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor) and left towards Middle-earth once again, in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [883](/wiki/Second_Age_883 "Second Age 883"), there was no Green Bough of Return on Hirilondë's prow. There stood the image of an [eagle](/wiki/Eagle "Eagle") with golden beak and jewelled eyes, given to him by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) during his previous journeys to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
