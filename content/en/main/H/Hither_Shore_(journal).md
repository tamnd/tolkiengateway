<i><b>Hither Shore: Interdisciplinary Journal on Modern Fantasy Literature</b></i> is the title of a bilingual (German and English) peer-reviewed annual published by the [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](/wiki/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft) (the German [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies")). Overviews of the first five issues can be found in *[Tolkien Studies](</wiki/Tolkien_Studies_(journal)> "Tolkien Studies (journal)")*: [Volume 5](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_5 "Tolkien Studies: Volume 5") (2008) pp. 221–9, and [Volume 8](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_8 "Tolkien Studies: Volume 8") (2011) pp. 114–20.
