<i><b>Hither Shore: Interdisciplinary Journal on Modern Fantasy Literature</b></i> is the title of a bilingual (German and English) peer-reviewed annual published by the [Deutsche Tolkien Gesellschaft](../D/Deutsche_Tolkien_Gesellschaft.md) (the German [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md)). Overviews of the first five issues can be found in *[Tolkien Studies](<../T/Tolkien_Studies_(journal).md>)*: [Volume 5](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_5.md) (2008) pp. 221–9, and [Volume 8](../T/Tolkien_Studies%253A_Volume_8.md) (2011) pp. 114–20.
