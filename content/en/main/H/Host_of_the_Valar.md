The **host of the Valar**<sup id="cite_ref-silchap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silchap-1">[1]</a></sup>, also called the **Host of Valinor**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-silchap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silchap-1">[1]</a></sup> or <b><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West_(disambiguation)" title="Host of the West (disambiguation)">Host of the West</a></b><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, was the mighty army that overthrew [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and his legions at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

The army itself was composed of [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB), the herald of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB); possibly other [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) and some of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar);<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) under [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin); and the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) under [Ingwion](/wiki/Ingwion).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

They were brought to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) by the ships of [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari), who took pity on the words of [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing); however the Falmari refused to fight beside the Noldor, remembering the [First Kinslaying](/wiki/First_Kinslaying). Once they landed, the Host of the Valar drove away the Orcs of the shores of the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) in the [Battle of Eglarest](/wiki/Battle_of_Eglarest).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

The Host of the West met Morgoth's host of the North in the titanic clash of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). Assisted by the [Great Eagles](/wiki/Great_Eagles) and [Earendil](/wiki/Earendil), they were victorious.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
