The **host of the Valar**<sup id="cite_ref-silchap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silchap-1">[1]</a></sup>, also called the **Host of Valinor**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-silchap_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silchap-1">[1]</a></sup> or <b><a class="mw-disambig" href="Host_of_the_West_(disambiguation).md" title="Host of the West (disambiguation)">Host of the West</a></b><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>, was the mighty army that overthrew [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and his legions at the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

The army itself was composed of [Eönwë](../E/Eönwë.md), the herald of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md); possibly other [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) and some of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md);<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) under [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md); and the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) under [Ingwion](../I/Ingwion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

They were brought to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) by the ships of [Falmari](../F/Falmari.md), who took pity on the words of [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md); however the Falmari refused to fight beside the Noldor, remembering the [First Kinslaying](../F/First_Kinslaying.md). Once they landed, the Host of the Valar drove away the Orcs of the shores of the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) in the [Battle of Eglarest](../B/Battle_of_Eglarest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

The Host of the West met Morgoth's host of the North in the titanic clash of the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). Assisted by the [Great Eagles](../G/Great_Eagles.md) and [Earendil](../E/Earendil.md), they were victorious.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>
