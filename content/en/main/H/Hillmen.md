The **Hillmen** were a group of [Men](../M/Men.md) from whom came an evil lord who seized power in [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

## Origins {#Origins}

Almost nothing can be said about the origin of the Hillmen, except that they were Men and that they probably lived in or near Rhudaur.

It is possible that they were [Pre-Númenóreans](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) related to the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) and [Men of Bree](../M/Men_of_Bree.md) whose ancestors had lived in the valleys of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and moved to the southern valleys of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and then as far north as the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) during the [Dark Years](../D/Dark_Years.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is also possible that they were Men of evil kinds who were not related to the [Atani](../A/Atani.md) who were hostile to the Kings of the [Dúnedain of Arnor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They mave have been descendants of [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) who fled over the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) back to the east after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) at the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) from [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) or descendants of Easterlings who had not crossed over the Blue Mountains into Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Third Age {#Third_Age}

It is possible that the Hillmen lived in the [Ettenmoors](../E/Ettenmoors.md) or in the western Misty Mountains near the [Angle](../A/Angle.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> at the time of King [Argeleb I](../A/Argeleb_I.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md).

In the days of King Argeleb I an evil lord of the Hillmen who was secretly allied with the realm of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) seized power in Rhudaur where there were few [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> a great army came from Angmar, invaded and ravaged [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md), surrounded [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md), burned and destroyed the [Tower of Amon Sûl](../T/Tower_of_Amon_Sûl.md), defeated the Dúnedain and killed King [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md) of Arthedain. Evil Men who were subject to Angmar occupied Rhudaur and the Dúnedain of Rhudaur were killed or fled to the west.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The fate of the Hillmen is not known. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> troops from the elven realm of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), from [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), an expeditionary force from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) led by their prince [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) and a force from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) led by [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md) defeated the forces from Angmar so that no man and no Orc of the realm of Angmar remained west of the Misty Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Rhudaur was uninhabited.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a late manuscript version of what would later become Appendix A it is mentioned that power had been seized by "men in secret league with Angmar" during the time of King Malvegil of Arthedain<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and that "an evil folk, workers of sorcery, subjects of Angmar" kill the Dúnedain in Rhudaur and construct dark forts in the hills during the time of King Arveleg I of Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> In the latest manuscript version of what would become Appendix B it is mentioned that the Witch-king came from the North, overwhelmed the realms of Cardolan and Rhudaur, destroyed the Númenoreans that lived there, that Cardolan was abandoned and filled with deadly spirits and that "an evil people out of the North, much given to sorecry" lived in Rhudaur "for long".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007-: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the game&#39;s version of <a href="../M/Middle-earth.md" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, many tribes of Hillmen still roam the land in the late Third Age. These include the Corcur of the <a href="../T/Trollshaws.md" title="Trollshaws">Trollshaws</a> and the <a href="../M/Misty_Mountains.md" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a> (descendants of the Men of Rhudaur), the Créoth of the <a href="../L/Lone-lands.md" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a>, and the Trév Duvardain of <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. A second tribe of Angmar, the Trév Gállorg, are shown to be friendly towards the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../F/Free_Peoples.md" title="Free Peoples">Free Peoples</a>. These tribal names are original to the game and do not exist in the works of Tolkien.</dd></dl>
