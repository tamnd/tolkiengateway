The **Hall of Lore** is a building that was located within the [island](/wiki/Elenna "Elenna") kingdom of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). The Hall of Lore was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

The Hall of Lore was built by [Elros](/wiki/Elros) himself. According to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), the only reason why it still stands was because of [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir).

During the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Elendil is tasked by [Queen Regent Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel") to guard [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), making sure that she does not do anything to harm Númenor. Elendil takes Galadriel to the Hall of Lore, travelling a quarter day’s ride to get there.

Upon arriving at the Hall of Lore, Galadriel learns from Elendil that their last king, [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir), is still alive and loyal to the [Elves](/wiki/Elves). She also learns that the sigil that she sought the meaning of is actually a map of the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor"). The parchment tells of a plan to be enacted after the defeat of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).

## Deviation from Lore {#Deviation_from_Lore}

There is no reference to the Hall of Lore in [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s writings. It may have been inspired by the Gondorian Archives shown early on in [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s [movie adaptation](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring") of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring),* which depicts a series of rooms filled with scrolls and dusty old manuscripts.

None of the writings of J.R.R Tolkien mention Galadriel having personally visited [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). She was travelling with her husband [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and establishing a kingdom of her own at the time when the series is set. In Tolkien's work, [Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel") was also never Queen Regent because her father, [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir), was never deposed or forced to resign from his throne.
