The **Hall of Lore** is a building that was located within the [island](../E/Elenna.md) kingdom of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). The Hall of Lore was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

The Hall of Lore was built by [Elros](../E/Elros.md) himself. According to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), the only reason why it still stands was because of [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md).

During the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), Elendil is tasked by [Queen Regent Míriel](../T/Tar-Míriel.md) to guard [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), making sure that she does not do anything to harm Númenor. Elendil takes Galadriel to the Hall of Lore, travelling a quarter day’s ride to get there.

Upon arriving at the Hall of Lore, Galadriel learns from Elendil that their last king, [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md), is still alive and loyal to the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). She also learns that the sigil that she sought the meaning of is actually a map of the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md). The parchment tells of a plan to be enacted after the defeat of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).

## Deviation from Lore {#Deviation_from_Lore}

There is no reference to the Hall of Lore in [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings. It may have been inspired by the Gondorian Archives shown early on in [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s [movie adaptation](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md),* which depicts a series of rooms filled with scrolls and dusty old manuscripts.

None of the writings of J.R.R Tolkien mention Galadriel having personally visited [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). She was travelling with her husband [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and establishing a kingdom of her own at the time when the series is set. In Tolkien's work, [Míriel](../T/Tar-Míriel.md) was also never Queen Regent because her father, [Tar-Palantir](../T/Tar-Palantir.md), was never deposed or forced to resign from his throne.
