The **hills of Scary** were a small range of hills in the northern part of the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), near to the town of [Scary](/wiki/Scary), from which they took their name.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) a band of rebel [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), including [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger), had hidden in the [Brockenbores](/wiki/Brockenbores) near the hills of Scary until the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) smoked them out.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
