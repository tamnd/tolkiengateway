**Húrin the Tall**, also referred to as "Lord Húrin" was a [Gondorian](../G/Gondorian.md) noble during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Lord Húrin was assigned the office of the [Warden of the Keys](../W/Warden_of_the_Keys.md) of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and for that he was also known as **Húrin of the Keys**.

Húrin fought in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), during which the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) were outnumbered and hard-pressed. Húrin, along with [Forlong](../F/Forlong.md), [Hirluin](Hirluin.md), and [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md) came to their aid.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the battle, the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Host_of_the_West.md" title="Host of the West">armies</a> of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) marched to the [Black Gate](../B/Black_Gate.md) while Lord Húrin himself stayed in the city to command the small garrison that stayed behind.

A barricade was laid in place of the destroyed [Great Gate](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md) at the gatehouse of the city. After the [Fall of Sauron](../F/Fall_of_Sauron.md), at the coronation of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), he ceremoniously opened that barricade for the entry of the new king.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
