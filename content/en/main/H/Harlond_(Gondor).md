**Harlond** was the port and docks of the city of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), used by river traffic from the southern regions of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). It lay 3–4 miles<sup id="cite_ref-Guide_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-1">[1]</a></sup> to the south of the city, where its fortifications ran alongside the great outlying wall of the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and his followers in the fleet from the south landed at Harlond and swept north into the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Harlond* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Guide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup> It means "Southhaven".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that it is a compound of the prefixal form *har-* of *[harn](/wiki/Harn "Harn")* ("south", "southern") and *[lond](/wiki/Lond "Lond")* (\[land locked\] "haven").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the original version of <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, Harlond was added as part of <a href="/wiki/Lossarnach" title="Lossarnach">Lossarnach</a> with the Eastern <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a> region. It was seized by <a href="/wiki/Minas_Morgul" title="Minas Morgul">Morgul</a>-<a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">orcs</a> who blockaded the road as the <a href="/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor" title="Siege of Gondor">Siege of Gondor</a> began. This version of Harlond is filled with enemy tents and siege engines, with the quests focusing on undermining the enemy forces. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, the player meets <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Company" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a> as they disembark from the ships and fights alongside them throughout the city. After the war, Harlond is in the process of being rebuilt, with the quests focusing on aiding in restoration and renewal.</dd></dl>
