**Halimath** or **[Harvestmath](Harvestmath.md)** in [Bree](../B/Bree.md), was the ninth month of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), following [Wedmath](../W/Wedmath.md) and approximately corresponding to modern [23 August](../_/23_August.md) through [21 September](../_/21_September.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](Hobbitish.md) of the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for September, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/haligmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:haligmonaþ">hāliġmōnaþ</a></i> meaning "holy month" (referring to pagan sacrifices).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
