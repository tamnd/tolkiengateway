**Híri** was a stream in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It started to the north of the hill of [Kôr](../K/Kôr.md), and then flowed across the plain of Valinor until it plunged underground north of the roots of [Silpion](../S/Silpion.md) near [Sirnúmen](../S/Sirnúmen.md). It is possible that it eventually reached the [Outer Sea](../O/Outer_Sea.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
