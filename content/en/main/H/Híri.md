**Híri** was a stream in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It started to the north of the hill of [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r), and then flowed across the plain of Valinor until it plunged underground north of the roots of [Silpion](/wiki/Silpion) near [Sirnúmen](/wiki/Sirn%C3%BAmen). It is possible that it eventually reached the [Outer Sea](/wiki/Outer_Sea).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
