The **Hill of the Slain**, or **Haudh-en-Ndengin** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), was a huge green mound that stood alone in the middle of the burned and ruined land of [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith). It had been raised by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) after the victory in the terrible battle of [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), and beneath it lay the bodies of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Edain](/wiki/Edain) slain in that battle, along with their gear and weaponry.<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup>

Among the many slain heroes beneath the mound was [Huor](/wiki/Huor), the brother of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and great-grandfather of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond). According to some tales, Morgoth went there to taunt his prisoner Húrin after the Nirnaeth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Huor's wife [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan) also went to the mound seeking her lost husband. When she came to the Hill of the Slain, she laid down in grief and died.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Haudh-en-Ndengin was also known as **Hill of Tears** (**Haudh-en-Nirnaeth** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)).<sup id="cite_ref-Fifth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fifth-1">[1]</a></sup> In [Dor-Lómin](/wiki/Dor-L%C3%B3min) it was known simply as the **Great Mound**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In one manuscript, the mound was called <i><b>Hauð-na-Dengin</b></i> ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Mound of the Slain"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
