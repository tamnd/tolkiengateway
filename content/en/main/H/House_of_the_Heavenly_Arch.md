The **house of the Heavenly Arch** was one of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](../T/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim.md), the kindreds of the elven city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). Their story is recounted in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

Their leader was [Egalmoth](../E/Egalmoth.md), and they were a folk of "unaccounted wealth"; they arrayed themselves in many bright colours, for the "heavenly arch" referred to a rainbow, and their weapons were set with jewels that flamed in the light over the sky. Their shields were the blue of the heavens, and the boss of each one contained an arrangement of seven individual gems: rubies, amethysts, sapphires, emeralds, chrysoprases, topazes, and amber. An opal of great size was set in each of their helms. They were skilled swordsmen, but they also had the best archers of the twelve houses, along with the [folk of the Swallow](../F/Folk_of_the_Swallow.md), so they were positioned in the broad places along the walls of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), the balrog [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) gathered his armies at the northern gate and knocked it down. The folks of the Arch and the Swallow defended the northern walls, pouring rains of arrows over the hosts of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), helping the houses that fought below.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:176–7</sup> When the northern gate was lost, [goblins](../G/Goblins.md) held a great part of the walls, so many elves of both folks were thrust to their doom. The [House of the Fountain](House_of_the_Fountain.md) arrived to help, but a great gap was opened in the western wall, and bands of the Arch and the Swallow battled amid the wreck against oncoming enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:179–80</sup> Slowly the battle in that part of the city was lost and the elves retreated towards the [Square of the Folkwell](../S/Square_of_the_Folkwell.md), where the guard of the Arch, the Swallow and others gathered together, forming a good battalion. Led by [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), they marched to the [Square of the King](../S/Square_of_the_King.md), where they met their lord Egalmoth. He had been in charge of the engines of the wall in the south part of the city, and when he saw they had to fight in the streets, he had taken some men of the Arch and of the Swallow and marched to the Square, rescuing captives and wandering troops on the way.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:182–3</sup>

After the fall of the city, many of them probably survived as [exiles](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md), as it is said that their lord dwelt after in the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Their [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name was <i><b>Thlim Quing lion</b></i>, while in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) they were simply known as <i><b>Ilqingi</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
