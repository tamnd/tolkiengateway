**Hounds** were [dogs](../D/Dogs.md) bred for hunting.

Hounds were among the favorite animals of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> The greatest of all hounds in the histories of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) was [Huan](Huan.md) the Hound of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), one of the Hounds of Oromë, who defeated [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in single combat.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *huan* means "great dog, hound".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *ronyo* and [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *rhŷn* mean "'chaser', hound of chase".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), one of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s early conceptions of an [Elven](../E/Elvish.md) language, the word for "large hound" is *gobi* or *gôbi*. The word *saur* means "hound, wild dog".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Hound most often refers to &#34;<i>the fierce wolfhound  (S. &#39;huadraug&#39;)</i>&#34;, used by Men or Hobbits, and sometimes found in the wild. In the Elder Days, the Hounds were used by noble Men to hunt Morgoth&#39;s wargs and werewolves.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Wild Hounds&#34;, portrayed attacking an orc, is a Resource Faction. &#34;Hounds of Sauron&#34; is a Resource Event, which can be played against hero companies. And the &#34;Noble Hound&#34; is Resource Ally, playable by Hero companies.</dd></dl>
