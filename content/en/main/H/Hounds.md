**Hounds** were [dogs](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs") bred for hunting.

Hounds were among the favorite animals of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> The greatest of all hounds in the histories of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) was [Huan](/wiki/Huan) the Hound of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), one of the Hounds of Oromë, who defeated [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in single combat.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *huan* means "great dog, hound".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *ronyo* and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *rhŷn* mean "'chaser', hound of chase".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), one of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s early conceptions of an [Elven](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") language, the word for "large hound" is *gobi* or *gôbi*. The word *saur* means "hound, wild dog".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Hound most often refers to &#34;<i>the fierce wolfhound  (S. &#39;huadraug&#39;)</i>&#34;, used by Men or Hobbits, and sometimes found in the wild. In the Elder Days, the Hounds were used by noble Men to hunt Morgoth&#39;s wargs and werewolves.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Wild Hounds&#34;, portrayed attacking an orc, is a Resource Faction. &#34;Hounds of Sauron&#34; is a Resource Event, which can be played against hero companies. And the &#34;Noble Hound&#34; is Resource Ally, playable by Hero companies.</dd></dl>
