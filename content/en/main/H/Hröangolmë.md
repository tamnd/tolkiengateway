**Hröangolmë** or the "lore of the body" was one of the matters most studied by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md), concerning the arts of healing. Númenóreans had a self-healing power superior to the [Men](../M/Men.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), and sickness was rare among them until the decay of the isle. However, they had learned much of this lore from the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) and they found practical to keep a continued study of medicine in case sickness appeared. Thus, in their first travels to Middle-earth, Númenóreans were most welcomed by native Men, as they shared their knowledge of these skills.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hröangolmë* is clearly [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), from *[hröa](Hröa.md)* ("body") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C3%91olm%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ñolmë (page does not exist)">ñolmë</a></i> ("lore, knowledge").
