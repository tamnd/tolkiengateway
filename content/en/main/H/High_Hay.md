The **High Hay** (also called **The Hedge**) was a barrier hedge planted by the [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) to protect their land from the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md).

## History {#History}

The High Hay was planted sometime after the foundation of [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2340](../T/Third_Age_2340.md)) but many generations before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

When [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and his [travelling hobbits](../T/Travellers.md) journeyed from [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) told that long ago the trees attacked the Hedge by planting themselves right by it and leaning over it. In response, the Bucklanders cut down hundreds of trees to make a great bonfire in [Bonfire Glade](../B/Bonfire_Glade.md) and burned a long strip next to the Hay on its east side.<sup id="cite_ref-Old_Forest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Old_Forest-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Appearance {#Appearance}

Constantly tended, it was thick and tall, and ran for over twenty miles. At the north end the Hay ran down to the river-bank close to the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md). Nearby was an opening in the barrier, the [North Gate](../B/Buckland_Gate.md), where gate-guards were posted day and night.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From there the Hay wound southwards to the confluence of the [Brandywine River](../B/Baranduin.md) and the [Withywindle](../W/Withywindle.md). East of [Crickhollow](../C/Crickhollow.md) there was a private entrance that the [Brandybucks](../B/Brandybuck_Family~66350.md) used, which was a brick-lined tunnel under the Hay with a gate of thick-set iron bars on the forest side.<sup id="cite_ref-Old_Forest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Old_Forest-1">[1]</a></sup> At the south end there was either another gate or the hedge ended before the meeting of the two rivers because the town of [Breredon](../B/Breredon.md) was on the Old Forest side of the barrier.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
