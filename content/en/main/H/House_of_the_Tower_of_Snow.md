The **house of the Tower of Snow** was one of the [twelve houses of the Gondothlim](../T/Twelve_houses_of_the_Gondothlim.md), the kindreds of the elven city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). Their story is recounted in the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

This house is only briefly mentioned as being united with the [house of the Pillar](House_of_the_Pillar.md) under [Penlod](../P/Penlod.md), tallest of the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:173</sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), they were stationed in the northern part of the city, where the balrog [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>) had led the main attack. When the [house of the Hammer of Wrath](House_of_the_Hammer_of_Wrath.md) was completely destroyed outside the walls, the elves of the Tower of Snow and the Pillar suffered the next wave of attacks, receding further into the city. Finally, "Penlod perished there in a lane with his back to the wall, and about him many of the men of the Pillar and many of the Tower of the Snow".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:179</sup> There is no other mention of them, but later it is said that survivors of all the kindreds were reunited in the [Square of the King](../S/Square_of_the_King.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:183</sup> so probably some of them survived as [exiles](../E/Exiles_of_Gondolin.md) following the destruction of the city.

## Other names {#Other_names}

Their [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) name was <i><b>Thlim Ith Mindon</b></i>, while in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) they were simply known as <i><b>Issemindon</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
