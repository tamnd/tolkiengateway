**Hyarmendacil** means "South-victor" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[Hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen)*, "South" and *[dacil](/wiki/Dacil "Dacil")*, "victor"), and can refer to:

- [Hyarmendacil I](/wiki/Hyarmendacil_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [899](/wiki/Third_Age_899 "Third Age 899") - [1149](/wiki/Third_Age_1149 "Third Age 1149")), born Ciryaher, the fifteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who ruled during the height of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)'s power.
- [Hyarmendacil II](/wiki/Hyarmendacil_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1391](/wiki/Third_Age_1391 "Third Age 1391") - [1621](/wiki/Third_Age_1621 "Third Age 1621")), born Vinyarion, the twenty-fourth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) who defeated a [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) invasion.
