**Hyarmendacil** means "South-victor" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (from *[Hyarmen](Hyarmen.md)*, "South" and *[dacil](../D/Dacil.md)*, "victor"), and can refer to:

- [Hyarmendacil I](Hyarmendacil_I.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [899](../T/Third_Age_899.md) - [1149](../T/Third_Age_1149.md)), born Ciryaher, the fifteenth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) who ruled during the height of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)'s power.
- [Hyarmendacil II](Hyarmendacil_II.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1391](../T/Third_Age_1391.md) - [1621](../T/Third_Age_1621.md)), born Vinyarion, the twenty-fourth [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) who defeated a [Haradrim](Haradrim.md) invasion.
