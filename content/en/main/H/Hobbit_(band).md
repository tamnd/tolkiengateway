**Hobbit** originating from Tyler, Texas is one of the oldest [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") themed bands who sprouted back to the 70's. They paved the way for the Fantasy Rock genre and have become forged in the definition of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) [Music](/wiki/Music).

Over the years, members of the band have been Paul "Turk" Henry (Bass/Vocals), Gene Fields (co-founder, guitarist, singer and songwriter), Keith Young (Drums), Richard Hill (Keyboard/L. Guitar), Andy Stevens, Tony Marsh and Tim Elliston.

Gene Fields passed away in 2019.

*Hobbit* has also been featured in [Amon Hen 194](/wiki/Amon_Hen_194) as well as *[Ringers: Lord of the Fans](/wiki/Ringers:_Lord_of_the_Fans)*.
