**Hobbit** originating from Tyler, Texas is one of the oldest [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) themed bands who sprouted back to the 70's. They paved the way for the Fantasy Rock genre and have become forged in the definition of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) [Music](../M/Music.md).

Over the years, members of the band have been Paul "Turk" Henry (Bass/Vocals), Gene Fields (co-founder, guitarist, singer and songwriter), Keith Young (Drums), Richard Hill (Keyboard/L. Guitar), Andy Stevens, Tony Marsh and Tim Elliston.

Gene Fields passed away in 2019.

*Hobbit* has also been featured in [Amon Hen 194](../A/Amon_Hen_194.md) as well as *[Ringers: Lord of the Fans](../R/Ringers%253A_Lord_of_the_Fans.md)*.
