**Hobbits vs. Models** is the ninth episode of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America%27s_Next_Top_Model,_Cycle_14" title="wikipedia:America&#39;s Next Top Model, Cycle 14">fourteenth cycle</a> of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/America%27s_Next_Top_Model" title="wikipedia:America&#39;s Next Top Model">America&#39;s Next Top Model</a></i>. It originally aired on [May 5](../_/5_May.md), [2010](../_/2010.md), and was the second of three episodes filmed in [New Zealand](../N/New_Zealand.md).

As the title suggests, New Zealand's role in the production of *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* films is touched upon; the five remaining contestants pose on the construction site of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Matamata&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Matamata (page does not exist)">Matamata</a> set of [Hobbiton](Hobbiton.md). [Sarah McLeod](../S/Sarah_McLeod.md) appears as a guest host.
