**Helmut W. Pesch** (born [August 30](../A/August_30.md), [1952](../_/1952.md)) is a German author, translator, editor, illustrator, philologist and literary scholar.

## Biography {#Biography}

Helmut W. Pesch was born and raised in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%B6nchengladbach" title="wikipedia:Mönchengladbach">Mönchengladbach</a>. He studied English philology, history of art and archaeology in Cologne and later in Glasgow. In [1981](../_/1981.md), he completed his doctorate to become Doctor of Philosophy. His doctoral thesis, *Fantasy - Theorie und Geschichte einer literarischen Gattung* ("Fantasy - theory and history of a literary genre"), was the first German work about fantasy literature and was published in [1982](../_/1982.md).

Between [1984](../_/1984.md) and [1986](../_/1986.md) he was subeditor for Science-Fiction and Fantasy at the German publishing company Bastei-Lübbe, since then he works there as editor for fiction. He is also a member of the Board of Academic Advisors for [Walking Tree Publishers](../W/Walking_Tree_Publishers.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Pesch wrote some novels, inspired by mythological themes. During his research for fantasy and [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), Helmut W. Pesch published several essays and a critical anthology about Tolkien's stories and its inspirations. With his knowledge of [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) languages, he wrote a two-part elvish-dictionary.

Currently Pesch lives with his wife and his daughter near Cologne.
