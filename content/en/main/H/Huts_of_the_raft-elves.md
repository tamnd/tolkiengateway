The **huts of the raft-elves** was a collection of dwellings in which the [raft-elves](/wiki/Raft-elves "Raft-elves") lived in.

## History {#History}

These huts were located on a protruding bank of the [Forest River](/wiki/Forest_River), close to the eastern edge of the forest of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is stated that the subjects of [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) "had houses or huts on the ground and in the branches",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> so it is possible that these huts were situated within the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm).

The raft-elves collected empty barrels from the [Elvenking's Halls](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls) in this village, brought by the current of the Forest River. The barrels were then tied together and left to float to [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town) (from where barrels filled with goods had originally been brought up the river to the Woodland Realm).<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), hanging on to a barrel, spent a night hiding outside the village of the raft-elves while pursuing [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) (who were escaping the imprisonment of the [Wood-elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood") inside empty barrels).<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup>
