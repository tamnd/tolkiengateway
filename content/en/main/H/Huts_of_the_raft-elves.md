The **huts of the raft-elves** was a collection of dwellings in which the [raft-elves](../R/Raft-elves.md) lived in.

## History {#History}

These huts were located on a protruding bank of the [Forest River](../F/Forest_River.md), close to the eastern edge of the forest of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is stated that the subjects of [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) "had houses or huts on the ground and in the branches",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> so it is possible that these huts were situated within the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md).

The raft-elves collected empty barrels from the [Elvenking's Halls](../E/Elvenking's_Halls.md) in this village, brought by the current of the Forest River. The barrels were then tied together and left to float to [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md) (from where barrels filled with goods had originally been brought up the river to the Woodland Realm).<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), hanging on to a barrel, spent a night hiding outside the village of the raft-elves while pursuing [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) (who were escaping the imprisonment of the [Wood-elves](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md) inside empty barrels).<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup>
