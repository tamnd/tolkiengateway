**Huan**, the **Hound of Valinor**, was a great [wolfhound](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs"), one of the hunting dogs of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) the Hunter.

Huan had been granted special powers by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), he was as large as a small horse, immortal, tireless and sleepless, and was allowed to speak three times before he died. It was also prophesied that he could not be killed unless it was by the greatest [wolf](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") that ever lived; in this case a [werewolf](/wiki/Werewolf "Werewolf").

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/10/Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg/300px-Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="233" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/10/Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg/450px-Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/10/Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg/600px-Tuuliky_-_Celegorm_the_Fair.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3447" data-file-height="2680"/></a><figcaption><i>Celegorm the Fair</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Tuuliky" title="Category:Images by Tuuliky">Tuuliky</a></figcaption></figure>

Huan was given by Oromë to his friend [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm), one of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) and accompanied him on his huntings in the regions of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). When the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) under [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) rebelled, Huan went with his master to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Presumably he was with his master while he dwelt in [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad), and then when he retreated to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) by the late 5th century after their arrival.

Huan was with Celegorm and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) who were hunting when he smelled [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) and captured and brought the maid before Celegorm. She had set out to rescue [Beren](/wiki/Beren), who had gone with [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") to [Angband](/wiki/Angband) but had been captured in [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth). Celegorm and Curufin did not reveal to her that they had sent Beren to his death by the hand of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), and took her as a prisoner to Nargothrond, "for her own protection", secretly plotting to wed her to Celegorm and thereby force an alliance with Lúthien's father [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="203" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/7f/Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/7f/Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_L%C3%BAthien_Escapes_Upon_Huan.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1084"/></a><figcaption><i>Lúthien escapes upon Huan</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

Huan felt pity for Lúthien, and sought her out and so he became involved with the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril). Speaking for the first time, he told her of a way to escape, and then accompanied her to [Tol-in-Gaurhoth](/wiki/Tol-in-Gaurhoth) where, with the help of Lúthien's [magic](/wiki/Magic "Magic"), he killed all of Sauron's [Werewolves](/wiki/Werewolves) until Sauron himself came out, taking the shape of the greatest wolf that had ever lived. Huan nevertheless managed to defeat him, and Sauron was forced to flee. Huan went back to his master, while Lúthien and Beren went on their way back to [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). They came across Celegorm and Curufin, who had been exiled from Nargothrond by [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) when he learned of their deeds. Curufin tried to kill Lúthien, but Huan turned against his master, defending Beren and Lúthien.

Speaking for the second time, Huan told Beren and Lúthien of his plan to gain entrance to Angband, bringing them the skin of the werewolf [Draugluin](/wiki/Draugluin) and the bat-skin of Sauron's messenger [Thuringwethil](/wiki/Thuringwethil). Using these skins as disguise Beren and Lúthien went to Angband, while Huan hunted in the wild.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/7a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="201" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/7/7a/Ted_Nasmith_-_Huan%27s_Leap_2.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="301"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Huan&#39;s Leap&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After Beren and Lúthien had won the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) but Beren had lost his hand to the werewolf [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth), Huan joined Beren, Thingol, [Beleg Cúthalion](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg") and [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) in the [Hunting of the Wolf](/wiki/Hunting_of_the_Wolf). Huan and Beren managed to kill Carcharoth, but Huan was mortally wounded, as was Beren. Speaking for the third and last time, he wished Beren and Lúthien farewell, and died, with Beren's palm upon his head.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Origin and nature {#Origin_and_nature}

In a c. [1959](/wiki/1959) essay on the nature of Orcs, Tolkien speculated on whether speaking creatures such as Huan and the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) could be of [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar")<sup id="cite_ref-MR409-11_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR409-11-6">[5]</a></sup> origin.

> *But true 'rational' creatures, 'speaking peoples', are all of human / 'humanoid' form. Only the Valar and Maiar are intelligences that can assume forms of Arda at will. Huan and [Sorontar](/wiki/Thorondor "Thorondor") could be Maiar - emissaries of Manwe. But unfortunately in The Lord of the Rings [Gwaihir](/wiki/Gwaihir) and [Landroval](/wiki/Landroval) are said to be descendants of Sorontar.*

In the same c. 1959 essay he would speculate that Huan and the Eagles may not possess [fëar](/wiki/F%C3%ABa "Fëa"), but are instead beasts elevated by the Valar:

> *In summary: I think it must be assumed that 'talking' is not necessarily the sign of the possession of a 'rational soul' or fëa. (...) The same sort of thing may be said of Huan and the Eagles: they were taught language by the Valar, and raised to a higher level - but they still had no fëar*

However, in a note written on the paper containing the essay, Tolkien includes Huan as an example of a Maia clad in beast form<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>:

> *...As the Valar would robe themselves like the Children, many of the Maiar robed themselves like other lesser living things, as trees, flowers, beasts. (Huan.)*

A later text dating from c. [1970](/wiki/1970) would state that the Eagles were Maiar<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>, leaving Huan's spiritual status inconclusive.
