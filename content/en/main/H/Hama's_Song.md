**Hama's Song** is the second track on [*The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim* (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim_(Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)> "The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack)") by [Stephen Gallagher](/wiki/Stephen_Gallagher).

In the film, it is a song sung by [Háma](</wiki/H%C3%A1ma_(son_of_Helm)> "Háma (son of Helm)"), son of [Helm](/wiki/Helm), about his sister [Héra](/wiki/Helm%27s_daughter "Helm's daughter"). The song is prematurely ended by Háma upon Héra's entrance. It is sung by Háma's voice actor, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Yazdan_Qafouri&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yazdan Qafouri (page does not exist)">Yazdan Qafouri</a>.

The first line derives from *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*, *[Where now the horse and the rider? Where is the horn that was blowing?](/wiki/Lament_for_the_Rohirrim "Lament for the Rohirrim")*, more specifically its adaptation from *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*, where is it rendered as "Where is the horse and the Rider?".
