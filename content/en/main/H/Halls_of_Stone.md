**Halls of Stone** is the thirteenth episode of [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios)'s television series, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*. It is the fifth episode of the second season and it was released on [September 12](/wiki/12_September "12 September"), [2024](/wiki/2024).
