**Haldad** was a [Man](../M/Men.md) of the Second House of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md), and the first person to unite the [Haladin](Haladin.md) since their settlement in southern [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md).

He was said to be a brave, strong man, and was most famous as the father of [Haleth](Haleth.md).

## History {#History}

During the early years after they crossed the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), the [Haladin](Haladin.md) dwelt happily in [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md)'s lands in [Thargelion](../T/Thargelion.md). They lived a scattered, pastoral existence until one day a swarm of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) descended on them, without warning, out of the Blue Mountains to the east.

Until that time, the Haladin had no single leader, but upon the coming of the orc-assault Haldad, a man of renown among his kin, took on the role. He gathered what remnant he could of his people, and built a [defensive stockade](../G/Gelion-Ascar_Stockade.md) between the rivers [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md) and [Ascar](../A/Ascar.md), south of [Sarn Athrad](../S/Sarn_Athrad.md). There the Orcs [besieged them](../B/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade~66779.md) until their food was exhausted. Haldad led a sortie against the attacking Orcs, and was slain; his son [Haldar](Haldar.md) rushed to recover his father's body, and was slain in turn.

Haldad's death was not in vain; Haldad's daughter [Haleth](Haleth.md) led the Haladin through another week of starvation and siege until Caranthir's army appeared out of the north, and drove the attacking Orcs to their deaths.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Haldad* is a [Halethian](Halethian.md) name, meaning "watch-dog".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
