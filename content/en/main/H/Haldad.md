**Haldad** was a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of the Second House of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), and the first person to unite the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) since their settlement in southern [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion).

He was said to be a brave, strong man, and was most famous as the father of [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth).

## History {#History}

During the early years after they crossed the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) dwelt happily in [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir)'s lands in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion). They lived a scattered, pastoral existence until one day a swarm of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) descended on them, without warning, out of the Blue Mountains to the east.

Until that time, the Haladin had no single leader, but upon the coming of the orc-assault Haldad, a man of renown among his kin, took on the role. He gathered what remnant he could of his people, and built a [defensive stockade](/wiki/Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Gelion-Ascar Stockade") between the rivers [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion) and [Ascar](/wiki/Ascar), south of [Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Sarn_Athrad). There the Orcs [besieged them](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade") until their food was exhausted. Haldad led a sortie against the attacking Orcs, and was slain; his son [Haldar](/wiki/Haldar) rushed to recover his father's body, and was slain in turn.

Haldad's death was not in vain; Haldad's daughter [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth) led the Haladin through another week of starvation and siege until Caranthir's army appeared out of the north, and drove the attacking Orcs to their deaths.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Haldad* is a [Halethian](/wiki/Halethian) name, meaning "watch-dog".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
