<br> **Harvestmath** is the name of the ninth month in the [Bree Calendar](/wiki/Bree_Calendar) (used by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [Bree](/wiki/Bree)), following [Wedmath](/wiki/Wedmath). In the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) the month was known as [Halimath](/wiki/Halimath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for September, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/h%C3%A6rfestmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:hærfestmonaþ">hærfestmōnaþ</a></i> "Harvest-month".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
