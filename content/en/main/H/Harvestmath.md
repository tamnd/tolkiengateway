<br> **Harvestmath** is the name of the ninth month in the [Bree Calendar](../B/Bree_Calendar.md) (used by the [Hobbits](Hobbits.md) of [Bree](../B/Bree.md)), following [Wedmath](../W/Wedmath.md). In the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) the month was known as [Halimath](Halimath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](Hobbitish.md) of an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for September, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/h%C3%A6rfestmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:hærfestmonaþ">hærfestmōnaþ</a></i> "Harvest-month".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
