**Hildigard Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hildigard was the second child of [the Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took") and [Adamanta Chubb](/wiki/Adamanta_Chubb). Hildigard died at a young age.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Hildigard" come from the Germanic roots *hild* meaning "battle" and *gard* meaning "protection".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The hobbit's gender is not mentioned ([Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster), *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth)* for example treats Hildigard as a male hobbit), however <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hildegard" title="wikipedia:Hildegard">Hildegard</a> is a Germanic female name.
