The **Hollin ridge** was a low ridge on the western side of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> forty-five leagues (135 miles<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) south of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md). The ridge was topped by ancient holly-trees, and marked the northern border of the long-lost [Elven](../E/Elves.md) realm<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) (also known as Hollin).<sup id="cite_ref-South_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [8 January](../_/8_January.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) reached the Hollin ridge after a long night-march. From its heights the travelers could see the [Mountains of Moria](../M/Mountains_of_Moria.md) in the distance. They camped in a deep hollow beneath the ridge, which [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) re-climbed when he sensed the silence and watchfulness of the land. After the *[Crebain](../C/Crebain.md)* flew over their camp the Fellowship remained in hiding near the ridge until they could leave under cover of night.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Hollin ridge runs through the northern <a href="../E/Eregion.md" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>. At its westernmost point stands an Elven watchtower. Reaching it is difficult, requiring several jumping puzzles, and awards a hidden achievement and an in-game title.</dd></dl>
