The **halls of Nienna** lay at the westernmost border of the world, and it is said that the windows of her house looked "outward from the [walls of the world](../W/Walls_of_the_World.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> however it is said to be also near the [halls of Mandos](Halls_of_Mandos.md), which is westward to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). The [Valie](../V/Valar.md) [Nienna](../N/Nienna.md) dwelt mainly in her halls, and didn't visit often the gladness of [Valimar](../V/Valimar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

As described in an early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), Fui Nienna chose for her dwelling a wide and dark hall in the [courts of Mandos](Halls_of_Mandos.md) (in this version, her husband). This hall she named **Fui**, and therein:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>before her black chair burnt a brazier with a single flickering coal, and the roof was of <a href="../B/Bats.md" title="Bats">bats</a>&#39; wings, and the pillars that upheld it and the walls about were made of basalt.
</p>
</blockquote>

At Fui was summoned the [Men](../M/Men.md) after death, to await their doom (some of them stay there, some go to Melko's [Angamandi](../A/Angamandi.md), many to [Arvalin](../A/Avathar.md), and few to Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> As Christopher Tolkien also noted, in later versions it is [Mandos](../M/Mandos.md) who appears as the Doomsman of the Valar <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
