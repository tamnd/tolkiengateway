The **halls of Nienna** lay at the westernmost border of the world, and it is said that the windows of her house looked "outward from the [walls of the world](/wiki/Walls_of_the_World "Walls of the World")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> however it is said to be also near the [halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos"), which is westward to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). The [Valie](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Nienna](/wiki/Nienna) dwelt mainly in her halls, and didn't visit often the gladness of [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

As described in an early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), Fui Nienna chose for her dwelling a wide and dark hall in the [courts of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos "Halls of Mandos") (in this version, her husband). This hall she named **Fui**, and therein:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>before her black chair burnt a brazier with a single flickering coal, and the roof was of <a href="/wiki/Bats" title="Bats">bats</a>&#39; wings, and the pillars that upheld it and the walls about were made of basalt.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

At Fui was summoned the [Men](/wiki/Men) after death, to await their doom (some of them stay there, some go to Melko's [Angamandi](/wiki/Angamandi), many to [Arvalin](/wiki/Avathar "Avathar"), and few to Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> As Christopher Tolkien also noted, in later versions it is [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) who appears as the Doomsman of the Valar <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
