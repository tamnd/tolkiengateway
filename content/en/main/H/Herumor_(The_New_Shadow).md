**Herumor** was a term used by a growing [dark cult](../D/Dark_Tree.md) in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) during the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), over one hundred years into the reign of [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [220](../F/Fourth_Age_220.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarion-2">[2]</a></sup> a secret cult worshipping the [Dark Lord](../D/Dark_Lord.md) used the term Herumor to denote something of significance. Whether Herumor was the name of a person, an alias, a concept, or even a code word is not known. It is known, however, that the term was used in whispers in opposition against [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md) in the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

Sometime in the early months of the year 220<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarion-2">[2]</a></sup>, [Borlas](../B/Borlas.md) of [Pen-arduin](../P/Pen-arduin.md) picked up on the whispers of Herumor from a man named [Othrondir](../O/Othrondir.md). Later on, during a discussion with [Saelon](../S/Saelon.md) in [June](../J/June.md), he briefly pondered how Saelon had heard the name.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>
