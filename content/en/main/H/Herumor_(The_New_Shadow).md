**Herumor** was a term used by a growing [dark cult](/wiki/Dark_Tree "Dark Tree") in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), over one hundred years into the reign of [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [220](/wiki/Fourth_Age_220 "Fourth Age 220"),<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarion-2">[2]</a></sup> a secret cult worshipping the [Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord) used the term Herumor to denote something of significance. Whether Herumor was the name of a person, an alias, a concept, or even a code word is not known. It is known, however, that the term was used in whispers in opposition against [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion) in the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

Sometime in the early months of the year 220<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarion-2">[2]</a></sup>, [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas) of [Pen-arduin](/wiki/Pen-arduin) picked up on the whispers of Herumor from a man named [Othrondir](/wiki/Othrondir). Later on, during a discussion with [Saelon](/wiki/Saelon) in [June](/wiki/June), he briefly pondered how Saelon had heard the name.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>
