**Herubrand** was a man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Herubrand was presumably one of the [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") who rode with their [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan") [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) from Rohan to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). He fought and died in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields)<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup> on [15 March](/wiki/15_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>. His name is remembered in the poem [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Herubrand* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name, which means "sword-brand".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The first element is derived from *heoru* ("sword"). The second element is *brand* ("firebrand", "torch", metaphorically "sword").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Herubrand comes from the town of Woodhurst in the Stonedeans, a province in western <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> where many men have a mix of Rohirrim and Dunlending ancestry. His parents are Briant and Reeve Heremond; his wife is named Cynethryth and their son is <a href="/wiki/Herefara" title="Herefara">Herefara</a>. Under the influence of <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>, in early March <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a> a conspiracy of men attacks the Reeve&#39;s family in the Mead Hall of Woodhurst. Heremond and Cynethryth are slain but Herubrand, who is wounded, is saved by his loyalists who hide him and his mother outside the city. Unaware of how many men are involved in the conspiracy, the men loyal to the new Reeve bar the doors of the Mead Hall and keep the attack secret from the people. Herubrand and his advisors enlist the player, an outsider who could not have been involved the attack, to help them in uncovering the conspiracy and eventually take back the control of his city. <a href="/wiki/Herefara" title="Herefara">Herefara</a> is not present during these events, as he is leading the defence of the village of Brockbridge to the south from the forces of <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>.</dd>
<dd>Herubrand and Herefara are later met in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Druadan_Forest" title="Druadan Forest">Druadan Forest</a> during the march of Rohirrim towards <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> the father and son fight side by side, but are slain by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gothmog" title="Gothmog">Gothmog</a>&#39;s orcs.</dd></dl>
