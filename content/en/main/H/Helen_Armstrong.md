**Helen Armstrong** is an author and member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). She designed the logo of the Tolkien Society, and is a frequent contributor to *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")*. She wrote two entries in the *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia:_Scholarship_and_Critical_Assessment "J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia: Scholarship and Critical Assessment")* ([Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) and [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)), and compiled *[The History of Middle-earth Index](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index)*.
