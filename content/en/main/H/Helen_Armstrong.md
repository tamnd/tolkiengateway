**Helen Armstrong** is an author and member of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). She designed the logo of the Tolkien Society, and is a frequent contributor to *[Mallorn](<../M/Mallorn_(journal).md>)*. She wrote two entries in the *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia%253A_Scholarship_and_Critical_Assessment.md)* ([Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)), and compiled *[The History of Middle-earth Index](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth_Index.md)*.
