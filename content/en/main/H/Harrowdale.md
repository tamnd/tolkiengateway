**Harrowdale** was the name given to the deep north-south valley cut out of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) by the [Snowbourn](/wiki/Snowbourn) River. At its southern end, beneath the mountain [Starkhorn](/wiki/Starkhorn), stood [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow), an ancient structure of the [Men of the Mountains](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers"), later used as a refuge of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim). On its east side stood the saw-toothed peak [Írensaga](/wiki/%C3%8Drensaga), while on its far south-east there stood [Dwimorberg](/wiki/Dwimorberg), the Haunted Mountain, where lay the entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).

Hamlets in the dale were [Underharrow](/wiki/Underharrow) and [Upbourn](/wiki/Upbourn). At its northern end, where the Snowbourn issued onto the plains of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), stood Rohan's capital, [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the Lord of Harrowdale was [Dúnhere](/wiki/D%C3%BAnhere). It was there that the muster of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) took place on [10 March](/wiki/10_March)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> before [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) led his riders to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), where Théoden and Dúnhere were killed.
