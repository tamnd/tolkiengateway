**Harrowdale** was the name given to the deep north-south valley cut out of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) by the [Snowbourn](../S/Snowbourn.md) River. At its southern end, beneath the mountain [Starkhorn](../S/Starkhorn.md), stood [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md), an ancient structure of the [Men of the Mountains](../O/Oathbreakers.md), later used as a refuge of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md). On its east side stood the saw-toothed peak [Írensaga](../_/Írensaga.md), while on its far south-east there stood [Dwimorberg](../D/Dwimorberg.md), the Haunted Mountain, where lay the entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md).

Hamlets in the dale were [Underharrow](../U/Underharrow.md) and [Upbourn](../U/Upbourn.md). At its northern end, where the Snowbourn issued onto the plains of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), stood Rohan's capital, [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the Lord of Harrowdale was [Dúnhere](../D/Dúnhere.md). It was there that the muster of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) took place on [10 March](../_/10_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> before [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) led his riders to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), where Théoden and Dúnhere were killed.
