**Hírilorn** was an immense [beech-tree](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches") that grew over the gates of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), the underground city of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol). The tree was originally named <i><b>Neldor</b></i>, meaning "Triple-tree", because of its three massive trunks.

## History {#History}

During the time of the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril), Thingol feared his daughter [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) would risk her life to seek out her love [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and help him in his quest. He had a house built among the branches of the great beech and imprisoned Lúthien there. Because of this the tree was then called **Hírilorn**, meaning "Tree of the Lady"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), though it did not hold Lúthien for long. Using her magical arts, she escaped from Hírilorn and set out to aid Beren, eventually helping him recover a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") from [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s [Iron Crown](/wiki/Iron_Crown).

Just as the Quest for the Silmaril began near Hírilorn the quest ended there as well. During the [Hunting of the Wolf](/wiki/Hunting_of_the_Wolf), Beren received a mortal wound from [Carcharoth](/wiki/Carcharoth) and died in Lúthien's arms beneath the branches of the *Tree of the Lady*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) was seventeen years old news from [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) ceased due to the vigilance of the servants of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). Túrin went looking for King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) to ask for mail, sword, and shield; he found the king with [Melian](/wiki/Melian) sitting under Hírilorn. There they advised him and his wish for arms was granted.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) translated *Hírilorn* as "Tree of the Lady": *[hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* + *[-il](/wiki/-il)* ("lady") + *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

An obsolete name of the tree, used in the early [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) stage of the language, was *Golosbrindi* (glossed as "Queen of the Forest"), replaced with *Hirilorn* ("Queen of the Trees".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
