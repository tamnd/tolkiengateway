**Hírilorn** was an immense [beech-tree](../B/Beeches.md) that grew over the gates of [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md), the underground city of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md). The tree was originally named <i><b>Neldor</b></i>, meaning "Triple-tree", because of its three massive trunks.

## History {#History}

During the time of the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md), Thingol feared his daughter [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) would risk her life to seek out her love [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and help him in his quest. He had a house built among the branches of the great beech and imprisoned Lúthien there. Because of this the tree was then called **Hírilorn**, meaning "Tree of the Lady"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), though it did not hold Lúthien for long. Using her magical arts, she escaped from Hírilorn and set out to aid Beren, eventually helping him recover a [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) from [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md).

Just as the Quest for the Silmaril began near Hírilorn the quest ended there as well. During the [Hunting of the Wolf](Hunting_of_the_Wolf.md), Beren received a mortal wound from [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md) and died in Lúthien's arms beneath the branches of the *Tree of the Lady*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) was seventeen years old news from [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) ceased due to the vigilance of the servants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). Túrin went looking for King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) to ask for mail, sword, and shield; he found the king with [Melian](../M/Melian.md) sitting under Hírilorn. There they advised him and his wish for arms was granted.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) translated *Hírilorn* as "Tree of the Lady": *[hîr](Hîr.md)* + *[-il](../_/-il.md)* ("lady") + *[orn](../O/Orn.md)* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

An obsolete name of the tree, used in the early [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) stage of the language, was *Golosbrindi* (glossed as "Queen of the Forest"), replaced with *Hirilorn* ("Queen of the Trees".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
