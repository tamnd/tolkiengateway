The **House of the Kings** in the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) was the building wherein the [kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") were buried.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [1 March](/wiki/1_March) [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120")<sup id="cite_ref-Later_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-2">[2]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) laid down and died, after bidding farewell to [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion) and giving unto him the Winged [Crown of Gondor](/wiki/Crown_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is said that the beds of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) were set beside the bed of Aragorn in the House of the Kings.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-2">[2]</a></sup>
