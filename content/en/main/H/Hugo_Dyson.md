**Hugo Victor Dyson Dyson** was a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He helped Tolkien persuade [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) to (active) [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity).

He much preferred talk to readings at Inklings meetings and is remembered by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) as "lying on the couch, and lolling and shouting and saying, 'Oh God, no more Elves'" during reading of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
