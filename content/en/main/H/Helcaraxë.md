**Helcaraxë** was the perilous icy wastes that formerly lay between [Araman](/wiki/Araman) (a region in the north of [Aman](/wiki/Aman)) and [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the far north of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Here met the [Encircling Sea](/wiki/Ekkaia "Ekkaia") and the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer), creating "*vast fogs and mists of deathly cold, and the sea-streams were filled with clashing hills of ice and the grinding of ice deep-sunken*."<sup id="cite_ref-S9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Fingolfin_Leads_the_Host_Across_the_Helcarax%C3%AB.jpg"><img src="/w/images/4/46/Ted_Nasmith_-_Fingolfin_Leads_the_Host_Across_the_Helcarax%C3%AB.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="297" height="450" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="297" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption><i>Fingolfin Leads the Host Across the Helcaraxë</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After the beginning of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), when the [Elves awoke](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves"), [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB) decided not to lead the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) of the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March) there, on their way to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), because of the ice and extreme cold, but rather took them by the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-s5_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-s5-3">[3]</a></sup>

Escaping the pursuit of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) after destroying the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor), [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) fled to Middle-earth across the treacherous wastes of the Helcaraxë. Later, [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and his people also made their way into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) across the Helcaraxë. Many died while crossing it, including [Elenwë](/wiki/Elenw%C3%AB), [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon)'s wife.<sup id="cite_ref-S9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Helcaraxë was also referred to as the **Ice of the North**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the **Grinding Ice**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Another name for this region was perhaps also the **Narrow Ice**, used by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in his poem *[Eärendillinwë](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendillinw%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), the second element in *Helkarakse* is said to be the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[karakse](/wiki/Karakse "Karakse")* ("jagged hedge of spikes").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-7">[7]</a></sup> [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the first element (*hel-*) derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KHELEK](/wiki/KHELEK) ("ice").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) suggests the translation "ice fangs".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Also in the *Etymologies*, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") experimented with [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) translations of *Helkarakse*: *elcharaes*, *helcharaes* or *Helcharach*.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
