In *[The One Ring](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>)* roleplaying game, the **Halls of Harmelt** were a Dwarf-hold in [Harlindon](Harlindon.md), at the southern end of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md), within the top of a hill called the Stone of Fire. By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2965](../T/Third_Age_2965.md), the Dwarf-road which led to the Halls had been swallowed by the undergrowth of the forest as a security measure.<sup id="cite_ref-Harmelt_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Harmelt-1">[1]</a></sup>

The mines of Harmelt were initially renowned for their gold, and much of its decoration reflected that. By the time of Lord Mjolin, the last known master of Harmelt, the gold mines had run dry and much of the decoration was fake or shaved thin. This charade even extended to the mines, with bought gold nuggets planted there to trick travellers.<sup id="cite_ref-Harmelt_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Harmelt-1">[1]</a></sup> One of the avenues for new wealth Mjolin considered was sending burglars into [Moria](../M/Moria.md). Setting up an actual expedition was impossible, since even if [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) had not forbidden it, it could expose the issues his clan faced.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Dunlending raiders, well-armed by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md), attacked Harmelt in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2975](../T/Third_Age_2975.md), looting many of the remaining treasures.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
