**Hildigrim Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hildigrim was the third son of [the Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"). He married [Rosa Baggins](/wiki/Rosa_Baggins), with whom he had one son, [Adalgrim](/wiki/Adalgrim_Took "Adalgrim Took").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Hildigrim" is of Germanic origin, coming from *hild* meaning "battle", and *grim* meaning "fierce".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hildegrim_of_Ch%C3%A2lons" title="wikipedia:Hildegrim of Châlons">Hildegrim</a> was the name of a Bishop of Châlons who lived from ca. 750 until 827 and spread Christianity in Germany after the Saxon Wars of Charlemagne. He received a part of his education in York under <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcuin" title="wikipedia:Alcuin">Alcuin</a>.
