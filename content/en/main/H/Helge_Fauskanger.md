**Helge Kåre Fauskanger** (born [17 August](../_/17_August.md) [1971](../_/1971.md)) is a Norwegian Tolkien linguist. He is the creator of the *[Ardalambion](../A/Ardalambion.md)* website.

Fauskanger is acclaimed for his version of [Neo-Quenya](../N/Neo-Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
