**Helge Kåre Fauskanger** (born [17 August](/wiki/17_August) [1971](/wiki/1971)) is a Norwegian Tolkien linguist. He is the creator of the *[Ardalambion](/wiki/Ardalambion)* website.

Fauskanger is acclaimed for his version of [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
