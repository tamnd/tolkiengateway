The **House of Fingolfin** were the descendants and followers of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), second son of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB). They held the [High Kingship of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## History {#History}

Fingolfin was accounted [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor) after [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) bestowed the Kingship to him, in gratitude for his son [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) rescuing him from [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) and as compensation for the acts of [his father](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor "Fëanor") against him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the early years of the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor), the House of Fingolfin held the lands to the northwest of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand): [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum), [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) and [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Of these, the city of Gondolin held until [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [510](/wiki/First_Age_510 "First Age 510"), but Hithlum was overrun by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), where Fingon was slain (Fingolfin had already met his end in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The remnant of the House, including Fingon's son [Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad"), were succoured by [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) in the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).

After the Nirnaeth, [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) son of [Huor](/wiki/Huor) was sent as a messenger to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), and there wedded Turgon's daughter [Idril](/wiki/Idril). Thus was the blood of [Men](/wiki/Men) introduced into the House, and the descendants of their line were named half-elven, given the choice of whether they were to considered [Men](/wiki/Men) or [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>All these descendants chose to be considered among the [Firstborn](/wiki/Firstborn), except [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond)'s brother [Elros](/wiki/Elros) (who became the first King of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and was a direct ancestor of [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn")), and Elrond's daughter [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) (Queen to Aragorn at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age)).

After the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), the survivors of the House lived as exiles at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) or in the [Falas](/wiki/Falas), until [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), descendant of Fingolfin's second son Turgon, sailed into the West to seek the aid of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), who overthrew Morgoth in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) in c. [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=First_Age_583&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="First Age 583 (page does not exist)">583</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The House of Fingolfin was probably no more as an entity; the only known descendants were the two [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven) sons of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil); [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Elros](/wiki/Elros), the patriarch of the [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") [House of Elros](/wiki/House_of_Elros) and the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The last High King of the Noldor, Ereinion Gil-galad, remained in Middle-earth. Despite having been listed as the son of Fingon in the published *Silmarillion*, Christopher Tolkien later commented that it would have been better if he would have left Gil-Galad's parentage obscure in the published *Silmarillion*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
