**Harding** was a man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

## History {#History}

Harding fought and died in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). His name is remembered in the poem [Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](../S/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Harding* is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name, which means "hard one"<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> or "strong-one"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. It contains the Old English element *heard* ("hard").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Riders_of_Rohan.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan">The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Harding is the &#34;Aldor of the <a href="../E/Eastemnet.md" title="Eastemnet">Eastemnet</a>&#34;, governing all of East Rohan on behalf of the King. He dwells in the village of &#34;Harwick&#34;, the main settlement of <a href="../T/The_Wold.md" title="The Wold">the Wold</a>, of which he is also the Reeve. His son &#34;Léodwig&#34; along with <a href="../W/Wídfara.md" title="Wídfara">Wídfara</a> had disappeared in the remote province of &#34;Wildermore&#34; and Harding sends the player and his companions <a href="../G/Gléowine.md" title="Gléowine">Gléowine</a> and <a href="Horn_(rider_of_Rohan).md" title="Horn (rider of Rohan)">Horn</a> to look for them. The player finds both wounded but alive and they eventually return to Harwick. Harding later dies on the <a href="../P/Pelennor_Fields.md" title="Pelennor Fields">Pelennor Fields</a> after slaying many foes.</dd></dl>
