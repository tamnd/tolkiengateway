**Holman Greenhand** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), noted for being a fine gardener.

## History {#History}

Holman's grandfather and namesake, [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded), initiated both the family name and the family profession: gardening.<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-1">[1]</a></sup> Holman became a gardener in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), and one of the gardens he tended was that of [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), he took on his second cousin once-removed, [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Hamfast_Gamgee), as an apprentice.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

One morning of that spring, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) visited Bag End, but found its master, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), not at home. Holman informed him that Bilbo had gone on one of his walks, as it was the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)' [new year](/wiki/Yestare "Yestare"). Holman's description of Bilbo convinced Gandalf he had found the perfect man for his [mission](/wiki/Quest_for_Erebor "Quest for Erebor"), to re-take [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").<sup id="cite_ref-Quest_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quest-3">[3]</a></sup>

When Bilbo went missing - that is, on his mission - Holman and Hamfast had their hands full in making sure the garden wasn't trampled as the place was swarming with Hobbits, hoping to get a piece of the Baggins estate.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Gaffer took over the job around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2961](/wiki/Third_Age_2961 "Third Age 2961"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> about which time Holman retired or died.

Holman was mentioned by the Gaffer to [Ted Sandyman](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman), in a conversation which took place at a small inn on the Bywater road called [The Ivy Bush](/wiki/The_Ivy_Bush), in the year <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=T.A._3001&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="T.A. 3001 (page does not exist)">T.A. 3001</a>; it was during this conversation that the Gaffer mentioned his relation to Holman ("him being my dad's cousin").<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Holman means "hole-man", and is distinct from the real-life English surname<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup> which contains *holm* "river island" rather than *hol* "hole".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
