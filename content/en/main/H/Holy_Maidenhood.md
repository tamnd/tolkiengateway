"**Holy Maidenhood**" is a review by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) of the E.E.T.S. edition of *Hali Meidenhad: An Alliterative Prose Homily of the Thirteenth Century* edited by F.J. Furnivall.

The review was published on [26 April](/wiki/26_April) [1923](/wiki/1923) in *[The Times Literary Supplement](/wiki/The_Times_Literary_Supplement)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

On June 28, 2017, an <a class="external text" href="http://www.the-tls.co.uk/articles/public/tolkien-from-the-archives/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">edited version of Tolkien&#39;s review</a> (now entitled "In her maiden bliss") was published online in *The Times Literary Supplement*, appearing in the print version of the magazine on 30 June 2017 (page 34).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
