<i><b>hlothram(a)</b></i> is a [Westron](/wiki/Westron) word meaning "cottager". The word contains the element *hloth* ("a two-roomed dwelling or hole").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
