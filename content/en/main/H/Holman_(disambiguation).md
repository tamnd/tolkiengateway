**Holman** is a [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name which has been used by three [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"):

- [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded) (b. [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1210](/wiki/Third_Age_2810 "Third Age 2810")), great-great-grandfather of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).
- [Holman Greenhand](/wiki/Holman_Greenhand) (b. [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1292](/wiki/Third_Age_2892 "Third Age 2892")), grandson of Holman the greenhanded.
- [Holman Cotton](/wiki/Holman_Cotton) (b. [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1302](/wiki/Third_Age_2902 "Third Age 2902"), grandfather of [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton) and grandson of Holman the greenhanded.
