**Halfred Greenhand** was the son of [Holman the greenhanded](Holman_the_greenhanded.md).

## History {#History}

Halfred was a gardener like his father, and the first to use his father's nickname as a family name. He had one son, whom he named after his father: [Holman](Holman_Greenhand.md) was born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1292](../T/Third_Age_2892.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name comes from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *healfrēad* "half-red, reddish" or from *healfrǣd* "half counsel".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
