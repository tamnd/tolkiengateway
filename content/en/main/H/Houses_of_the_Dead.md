The **Houses of the Dead**<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> was the name given to the tombs of the [Kings](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) and selected heroes of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the city of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Pyre_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pyre-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The Houses of the Dead were located in **the Hallows**,<sup id="cite_ref-Pyre_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pyre-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Theoden_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theoden-4">[4]</a></sup> the Sacred Places of the tombs,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> on the western side of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) along the [Rath Dínen](../R/Rath_Dínen.md) (the "Silent Street" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md))<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup> at the height of the fifth wall of the city on a mountain shoulder that connected Mount [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md) with the [Hill of Guard](Hill_of_Guard.md)<sup id="cite_ref-MT_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-6">[6]</a></sup> on which Minas Tirith was built<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>. The mountain shoulder was hedged with great ramparts that extended back to the mountain wall of Mount Mindolluin that overhung its westend end.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-6">[6]</a></sup> Among these tombs were the [House of the Kings](House_of_the_Kings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> (the tombs of the [Kings](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)) and the [House of the Stewards](House_of_the_Stewards.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> (the tombs of the [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) of Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>.

They were accessed through the [Fen Hollen](../F/Fen_Hollen.md) (the "Closed Door" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) on the western side of the sixth wall of the city and then descending on a winding road that went down in many curves to the narrow land under the shadow of Mount Mindolluin's precipice at the height of the fifth wall. Fen Hollen was kept closed except during funerals. Only the Lord of the City and the employees who tended the Houses of the Dead were allowed to enter through it.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Houses of the Dead were pale domes with empty halls and images of the deceased men. The House of the Stewards contained a wide vaulted chamber and many rows of tables that were carved from marble. On each table lay the seemingly sleeping statue of a dead person with its hands folded and its head pillowed on the stone.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the time of the [Ruling Stewards](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:1_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-10">[10]</a></sup> from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2050](../T/Third_Age_2050.md)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> to [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Crowning_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Crowning-12">[12]</a></sup>, the [crown](../C/Crown_of_Gondor.md) of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) lay in the lap of King [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) in the Houses of the Dead <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>. It was placed there by Eärnil's son, [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md), the last king of the line of [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md)<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>, when he accepted the challenge of the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) to single combat, after which he rode to [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md) and was never seen again.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

After he had succeeded in obtaining the help of an army of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) of King [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) in a daring ride north to their land in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) and died defending his lord Steward [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) of Gondor, in the Battle of the Field of Celebrant<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md)<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> the Gondorian messenger [Borondir](../B/Borondir.md) was buried in the Houses of the Dead<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>. After [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) had sworn the [Oath of Eorl](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md) at the [tomb](../T/Tomb_of_Elendil.md) of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) on [Amon Anwar](Halifirien.md), Cirion had the casket of Elendil moved to the Houses of the Dead in Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

On [15 March](../_/15_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), as the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md) took place,<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) followed the steward [Denethor](../D/Denethor_II.md) into the Houses of the Dead<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup>. Despairing at the siege, the death of his son [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), and the wounding of his son [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), Denethor laid on a table beside the still alive (though unconscious) Faramir and asked Pippin to gather oil and dry wood and set them aflame. Pippin fled the house in terror to seek the aid of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-2">[2]</a></sup> On his way to find the [Wizard](../W/Wizard.md), he came upon the Gondorian soldier [Beregond](../B/Beregond.md), who he asked to stop anything dreadful from happening.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

Pippin later returned with Gandalf to find that Beregond had slain two servants of Denethor in order to stop them from lighting the funeral pyre. Gandalf, Pippin, and Beregond were able to save Faramir, but Denethor set flame to the pyre and was consumed in the ensuing fire.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

After the defeat of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), Faramir, who had been appointed as the last Ruling Steward, retrieved the crown of Gondor from its resting place in the Houses of the Dead for the coronation of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) as King Elessar [Telcontar](House_of_Telcontar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup> on [1 May](../_/1_May.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Crowning_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Crowning-12">[12]</a></sup>.

After his death in the Battle of the Pelennor Fields, King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) was placed in a tomb in the Houses of the Dead among the Kings of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-Theoden_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Theoden-4">[4]</a></sup> but on [22 July](../_/22_July.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> his body was taken back to Rohan to be buried in a mound among the [Kings of Rohan](../K/King_of_Rohan.md)<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup>.

[Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and Pippin were buried in the Houses of the Dead after [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [63](../F/Fourth_Age_63.md).<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> Their beds were placed beside the bed of King Elessar when he died on [1 March](../_/1_March.md) [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md).<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>
