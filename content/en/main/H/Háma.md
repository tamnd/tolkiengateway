**Háma** was the Doorward of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and Captain of the King's Guard of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). He was a loyal and devoted man, though he often anticipated his orders.

## History {#History}

Háma served as the doorguard of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), the abode of the [King of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan"). On [2 March](/wiki/2_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), four guests arrived at his post: [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He stood by the rules of the house, and ordered them all to surrender their weapons. He entered into a long argument with Aragorn who refused to hand [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril) to anyone else until he agreed to do so at the wish of Gandalf. He permitted Gandalf to keep his staff, a deliberate decision which would prove decisive in the healing of his king.

For this oversight, Háma was reprimanded by [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and was briefly relieved of his position as the doorguard. Háma did his Lord's bidding, and also released [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) from captivity. The King's sister-son had been imprisoned by [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), who had been overthrown with the use of Gandalf's staff shortly before. Háma arrested Gríma, and found various missing items in his possession, including [Herugrim](/wiki/Herugrim), the King's sword.<sup id="cite_ref-King_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-King-1">[1]</a></sup>

Háma rode with Théoden to the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg), and fought in the [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg") that ensued shortly after. He fell before the great gate, and his body was hacked to pieces by a horde of [Uruks](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai").<sup id="cite_ref-Voice_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Voice-3">[3]</a></sup> He was given the honour of being buried in a grave of his own under the shadow of the Hornburg. Despite his annoyance with his doorguard, Théoden obviously had an affection for Háma, and grieved at his fall, so much so that he personally cast first earth on his grave.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [5 March](/wiki/5_March) when Théoden rejected the words of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), the hewing of Háma's body before the gate of the Hornburg was among the grievances he cited.<sup id="cite_ref-Voice_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Voice-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Háma* comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), and means as much as "home, house, dwelling". The same element is used in <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Hamfast_(disambiguation)" title="Hamfast (disambiguation)">Hamfast</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
