**Haldir** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), probably a [Silvan Elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves"), and a marchwarden who guarded the forest's northern borders.

## History {#History}

Haldir was one of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim); unlike his brothers, [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_L%C3%B3rien)> "Rúmil (elf of Lórien)") and [Orophin](/wiki/Orophin), Haldir had traveled outside Lórien and knew [Westron](/wiki/Westron). He and his companions are described as wearing grey hooded cloaks and living on *[flets](/wiki/Telain "Telain")* in the trees.

Haldir was accompanied by his brothers between [15](/wiki/15_January "15 January") and [16 January](/wiki/16_January), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> when they encountered the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) who arrived in Lórien. His brothers interacted little with the Fellowship because they spoke little of the Common Speech. He treated [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) rather poorly due to bad relations between Elves and Dwarves. He made him go blindfolded through the wood until [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) said he did not have to.

He had heard of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), but there is no indication that they had met before.<sup id="cite_ref-LL_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LL-2">[2]</a></sup> Haldir became their guide to the city of [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon).

After [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) had nourished their guests, and they were set to depart, Haldir led them to their Elven boats.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Haldir leads a regiment of Elven archers from Lórien to the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep</a>, where he is eventually slain, along with most of his archers.</dd></dl>

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Haldir is first met on a talan on the western border of <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a>. He  frequently serves as the intermediary between the player and <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> during the epic story.</dd></dl>
