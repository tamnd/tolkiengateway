The **House of Elwë** [Singollo](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") was the royal family that ruled the land of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) for millennia.

## History {#History}

### Origins {#Origins}

The ancient line was started by **Elwë** Singollo (also known as [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol")) in the [Y.T. 1152](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1152 "Year of the Trees 1152"), when upon waking from his trance he appeared before his people (the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar)) with [Melian](/wiki/Melian) and gathered them together to form [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the mighty woodland kingdom in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Death of Thingol {#Death_of_Thingol}

Thingol, after being given the golden necklace ([Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr)) by [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), employed skilled dwarven smiths of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) to restore the necklace to its former magnificence.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> But the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), being enamoured of the beautiful treasure and the [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils") it contained, sought to have it to themselves. After completing their work they refused to hand Nauglamír over, claiming he had no right to the craftsmanship of the dwarves. Thingol, perceiving that they wanted the Silmaril, bade them leave his land without payment. But the smiths slew him, took the mighty jewel, and fled towards the mountains.
