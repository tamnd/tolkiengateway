**Mount Hyarmentir** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[çarˈmentir]</span>) was a mighty mountain to the south and east of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), perhaps of the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was one of the tallest in [Aman](../A/Aman.md), but not as [Taniquetil](../T/Taniquetil.md) to the north, which was the tallest of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-darkening_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-darkening-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) climbed it, as Ungoliant wrought her webs between clefts and pinnacles. From its summit, they looked out over the Tree-lit land of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), before they descended Mount Hyarmentir's long, shallow western slopes and set out to destroy the [light](../L/Light_of_Valinor.md) of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-darkening_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-darkening-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hyarmentir* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). The first element in the name is *[hyarmen](Hyarmen.md)* ("south").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The second element is likely *[tir](../T/Tir.md)* ("watch").

It probably means "south-watch".<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Mount *Hyarmentir* was originally named <i><b>Hyarantar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-early_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-early-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-annals_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-3">[3]</a></sup>
