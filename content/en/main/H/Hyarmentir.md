**Mount Hyarmentir** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[çarˈmentir]</span>) was a mighty mountain to the south and east of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), perhaps of the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was one of the tallest in [Aman](/wiki/Aman), but not as [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil) to the north, which was the tallest of the world.<sup id="cite_ref-annals_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-darkening_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-darkening-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) climbed it, as Ungoliant wrought her webs between clefts and pinnacles. From its summit, they looked out over the Tree-lit land of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), before they descended Mount Hyarmentir's long, shallow western slopes and set out to destroy the [light](/wiki/Light_of_Valinor "Light of Valinor") of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees).<sup id="cite_ref-annals_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-darkening_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-darkening-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hyarmentir* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). The first element in the name is *[hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen "Hyarmen")* ("south").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The second element is likely *[tir](/wiki/Tir "Tir")* ("watch").

It probably means "south-watch".<sup id="cite_ref-guide_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Mount *Hyarmentir* was originally named <i><b>Hyarantar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-early_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-early-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-annals_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annals-3">[3]</a></sup>
