**Halfred Gamgee** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Halfred was the second son of [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee"). He moved to the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *healfrēad* "half-red, reddish" or from *healfrǣd* "half counsel".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
