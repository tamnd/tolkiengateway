**Hans Conried** ([15 April](/wiki/15_April) [1917](/wiki/1917) - [5 January](/wiki/5_January) [1982](/wiki/1982)) was an American actor who provided the voice of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) in the [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) animated version of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)")* released in [1977](/wiki/1977).
