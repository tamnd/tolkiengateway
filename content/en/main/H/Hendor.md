**Hendor** was a servant of [Idril](/wiki/Idril) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

## History {#History}

He was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housecarl" title="wikipedia:Housecarl">house-carl</a> of [Idril](/wiki/Idril) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), and was aged.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) began, Idril sent most men of her guard down the [secret way](/wiki/Idril%27s_secret_way "Idril's secret way") with young [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:187</sup> Hendor had to carry the child down the tunnel, and while crossing [Tumladin](/wiki/Tumladin) these men were persecuted by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) upon [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves"). However, they were saved by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and Hendor was relieved of his burden, although Eärendel claimed he did not need to be beared.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:190</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hendor* is never glossed, but it could be related with [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish)/[Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *[hen](/wiki/Hen "Hen")* ("eye").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
