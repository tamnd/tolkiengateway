**Harley Bramble** is a [man](../M/Men_of_Bree.md) of [Bree](../B/Bree.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)*. People in Bree didn't like Harley Bramble and they didn't treat him well. When Harley met a [Southerner](../S/Southerners.md), the southerner used this to stir him up against the other [Bree-landers](../B/Bree-landers.md). If Harley would help the Southerners, he would be running things in Bree at the end. However, when the two were confronted by [Eradan](<../E/Eradan_(video_game_character).md>), [Andriel](../A/Andriel.md) and [Farin](<../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md>), Harley did choose to stay on the good side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
