**Haleth** was the oldest child of [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm "Helm") and brother of [Háma](</wiki/H%C3%A1ma_(son_of_Helm)> "Háma (son of Helm)").

## History {#History}

Haleth tried to defend [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) from the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) who had invaded in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758"), but he was killed at the doors of [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld), the last of its defenders. [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) son of [Freca](/wiki/Freca) usurped the throne while Helm and Háma fled to [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Haleth* means "man", "warrior" or "hero" in poetry in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Not to be confused with [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth).
