<i><b>hû</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun, meaning "spirit, shadow".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Huorn](/wiki/Huorn) = "*Tall tree*"*
- [Hûr](/wiki/H%C3%BBr) = "Fiery spirit"
- *[Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin)*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) it is meaning "[dog](/wiki/Dog "Dog")"; the plural is *hui*.<span></span>
