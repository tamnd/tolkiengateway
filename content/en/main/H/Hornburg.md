The **Hornburg** was a great Gondorian fortress later used by the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md).

It was said among the Rohirrim that no enemy had ever taken the Hornburg by assault and it was considered the strongest fortress in Rohan, stronger than [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) and [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The fortress was built by Gondor near the western border of its province of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) on a stone promontory, later called the [Hornrock](Hornrock.md) by the Rohirrim, at one end of the [Deeping Wall](../D/Deeping_Wall.md). This wall blocked the entrance to a deep valley, later called by the Rohirrim [Helm's Deep](Helm's_Deep.md), south-east of the northern end of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md), below the peaks known as the [Thrihyrne](../T/Thrihyrne.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-2">[2]</a></sup> It guarded the southern end of the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aglarond_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aglarond-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The fortress was built by [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md), at a date not known to the Rohirrim, who said that it was constructed in "far-off days of the glory of Gondor \[by\] the sea-kings ... with the hands of giants".<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-2">[2]</a></sup> It was originally called [Aglarond](../A/Aglarond.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Aglarond_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aglarond-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> a name used also for the caves located at the head of the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-Road_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-5">[5]</a></sup>

In later years whole regions of Gondor, including [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md), were depopulated or evacuated. Steward [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) gave the province to the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) as a reward for their assistance at the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md). Masons of Gondor helped to repair the fortress of Aglarond. After that it was handed over to the Rohirrim and the old hereditary Gondorian guards of Aglarond were stationed at the fortress of [Angrenost](../I/Isengard.md) (called *Isengard* by the Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Aglarond_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aglarond-3">[3]</a></sup> The Rohirrim called the fortress of Aglarond the *Súthburg*,<sup id="cite_ref-Fords_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fords-6">[6]</a></sup> probably because it guarded the southern end of the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md), as Isengard guarded the northern end.

When [Wulf](../W/Wulf.md) and the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) overran Rohan, [Helm Hammerhand](Helm.md), ninth King of Rohan, sought refuge in the fortress during the [Long Winter](../L/Long_Winter.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md) where he was besieged. The fortress was called the *Hornburg* after the days of King Helm,<sup id="cite_ref-Fords_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fords-6">[6]</a></sup> because of Helm's great horn,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> which he had blown before his lone raids into the camp of the enemies during the siege<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and because a horn sounded from its tower echoed in the Deep behind.<sup id="cite_ref-Deep_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deep-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the period [Battles of the Fords of Isen](../B/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md) leading up to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Erkenbrand](../E/Erkenbrand.md), the Lord of Deeping-coomb and of much other land in Westfold,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> resided at the Hornburg.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The name *Aglarond* was used for the caves and for the fortress at the entrance to Helm’s Deep.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> *Aglarond* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which is translated as "Glittering Caves"<sup id="cite_ref-Road_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-5">[5]</a></sup> or "Caves of Radiance.<sup id="cite_ref-Fords_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fords-6">[6]</a></sup> It contains the elements *[aglar](../A/Aglar.md)* ("brilliance",<sup id="cite_ref-N_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-12">[12]</a></sup> "radiance", glory"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup>) and *[rond](../R/Rond.md)* ("vault", "high roofed cavern"),<sup id="cite_ref-N_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Since the word *aglar* is derived from the stem KAL- ("light")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-13">[13]</a></sup>"Glittering Caves" can be the exact translation.

The Rohirrim called the caves of Aglarond *Glǽmscrafu*<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[note 1]</a></sup> (‘Caves of Radiance’<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>) and the fortress itself *Súthburg* ("South Fortress")<sup id="cite_ref-BT1_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT1-17">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BT2_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT2-18">[17]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-Fords_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fords-6">[6]</a></sup> Both *Glǽmscrafu*<sup id="cite_ref-Fords_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fords-6">[6]</a></sup> and *Súthburg*<sup id="cite_ref-BT1_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT1-17">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-BT2_18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BT2-18">[17]</a></sup> are [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) names.
