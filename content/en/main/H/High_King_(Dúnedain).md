**High King** was a title used by the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) for their ultimate ruler. Normally, "High King" would refer to a king who ruled over other lesser kings and lords, but this is not necessarily the case with the High King of the Dúnedain.

In [Gil-galad's letter](/wiki/Gil-galad%27s_letter), [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) the fifth King of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), was referred to as "High King",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> though he had no other rulers in his service.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Realms in Exile {#Realms_in_Exile}

The more common use of the title, though, came in the later [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), with the establishment of the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). In their earliest years, the Two Kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) were formed under the ultimate rule of one man, [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), who dwelt in [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) as the High King. The South-kingdom of Gondor was ruled jointly by his sons, but only under his suzerainty.

With Elendil's death against Sauron in the [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr), his elder son [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) inherited the High Kingship. Isildur ruled for only two years, though, before he too was lost in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields) before he could truly take up the rule of his twin realms.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

### Decline {#Decline}

After Isildur's death, historical events conspired to separate the Two Kingdoms, each of which took Kings of their own. The High Kingship should in principle have fallen on the [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur) [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)"), but in practice Valandil became King of Arnor only, and had no power over the South-kingdom.

In the mid-[Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), Gondor was left kingless; [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), as Isildur's heir, attempted to invoke the [Law of Succession in Númenor](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor) to claim the throne of Gondor, thus becoming High King; the [Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor) voted against his claim.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-4">[3]</a></sup> Shortly after, both realms would lose their kingship.

## Reign of Elessar {#Reign_of_Elessar}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/10/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg/300px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="284" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/10/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg/450px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/10/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg/600px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1277" data-file-height="1210"/></a><figcaption><i>The Last Steward of Gondor</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Rowena_Morrill" title="Category:Images by Rowena Morrill">Rowena Morrill</a></figcaption></figure>

Before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), there had been no [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") for 969 years,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> and no [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of a united [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) for 2,158 years.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> After [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s passing [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) the direct descendant and [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur), as well as [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), through many generations, ascended to the throne of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

Aragorn took the title of <b>King of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a></b><sup id="cite_ref-great_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[6]</a></sup> also **King of Gondor and Arnor** and **Lord of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands)** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") <i><b>Hîr i Mbair Annui</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>) signifying his Kingship over all the [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples) of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands), from the north to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-great_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[6]</a></sup> His rule of the West is therefore referred to as the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

Under his crown and protection were other kingdoms, like the [Kingdom of Dale](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Dale) and the [Kingdom Under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_Under_the_Mountain).<sup id="cite_ref-great_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[6]</a></sup>
