**High King** was a title used by the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) for their ultimate ruler. Normally, "High King" would refer to a king who ruled over other lesser kings and lords, but this is not necessarily the case with the High King of the Dúnedain.

In [Gil-galad's letter](../G/Gil-galad's_letter.md), [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md) the fifth King of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), was referred to as "High King",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> though he had no other rulers in his service.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Realms in Exile {#Realms_in_Exile}

The more common use of the title, though, came in the later [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), with the establishment of the [Realms in Exile](../R/Realms_in_Exile.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). In their earliest years, the Two Kingdoms of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) were formed under the ultimate rule of one man, [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md), who dwelt in [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md) as the High King. The South-kingdom of Gondor was ruled jointly by his sons, but only under his suzerainty.

With Elendil's death against Sauron in the [Siege of Barad-dûr](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md), his elder son [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) inherited the High Kingship. Isildur ruled for only two years, though, before he too was lost in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md) before he could truly take up the rule of his twin realms.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

### Decline {#Decline}

After Isildur's death, historical events conspired to separate the Two Kingdoms, each of which took Kings of their own. The High Kingship should in principle have fallen on the [Heir of Isildur](Heir_of_Isildur.md) [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor).md>), but in practice Valandil became King of Arnor only, and had no power over the South-kingdom.

In the mid-[Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), Gondor was left kingless; [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), as Isildur's heir, attempted to invoke the [Law of Succession in Númenor](../L/Law_of_Succession_in_Númenor.md) to claim the throne of Gondor, thus becoming High King; the [Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md) voted against his claim.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-4">[3]</a></sup> Shortly after, both realms would lose their kingship.

## Reign of Elessar {#Reign_of_Elessar}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/R/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/10/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg/300px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="284" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/10/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg/450px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/10/Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg/600px-Rowena_Morrill_-_The_Last_Steward_of_Gondor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1277" data-file-height="1210"/></a><figcaption><i>The Last Steward of Gondor</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Rowena_Morrill.md" title="Category:Images by Rowena Morrill">Rowena Morrill</a></figcaption></figure>

Before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), there had been no [King of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) for 969 years,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> and no [King](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of a united [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) for 2,158 years.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> After [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s passing [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) the direct descendant and [Heir of Isildur](Heir_of_Isildur.md), as well as [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md), through many generations, ascended to the throne of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

Aragorn took the title of <b>King of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a></b><sup id="cite_ref-great_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[6]</a></sup> also **King of Gondor and Arnor** and **Lord of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md)** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) <i><b>Hîr i Mbair Annui</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>) signifying his Kingship over all the [Free peoples](../F/Free_peoples~19934.md) of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md), from the north to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-great_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[6]</a></sup> His rule of the West is therefore referred to as the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

Under his crown and protection were other kingdoms, like the [Kingdom of Dale](../K/Kingdom_of_Dale.md) and the [Kingdom Under the Mountain](../K/Kingdom_Under_the_Mountain~32174.md).<sup id="cite_ref-great_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-7">[6]</a></sup>
