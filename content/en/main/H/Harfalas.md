**Harfalas** was the southern half of the region of [Falas](../F/Falas.md), located southeast of [Eglamar](<../E/Eglamar_(Falas).md>), and northwest of [Arvernien](../A/Arvernien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Har_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Har-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The region of Harfalas was marked on *[the Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-Har_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Har-1">[1]</a></sup> which formed the basis for the [map of Beleriand](../M/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North.md) in the published *[Silmarillion](../S/Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, apart from being labeled on the aforementioned map, Harfalas was never mentioned again, nor did it appear in any of the narratives, annals or essays set in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Harfalas* is in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), meaning "South [Falas](../F/Falas.md)". It is a combination of the prefixal form *[har-](Harn.md)* ("south") + *[falas](../F/Falas.md#Etymology)* ("shore").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
