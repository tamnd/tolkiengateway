**Harfalas** was the southern half of the region of [Falas](/wiki/Falas), located southeast of [Eglamar](</wiki/Eglamar_(Falas)> "Eglamar (Falas)"), and northwest of [Arvernien](/wiki/Arvernien).<sup id="cite_ref-Har_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Har-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The region of Harfalas was marked on *[the Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map")*,<sup id="cite_ref-Har_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Har-1">[1]</a></sup> which formed the basis for the [map of Beleriand](/wiki/Map_of_Beleriand_and_the_Lands_to_the_North "Map of Beleriand and the Lands to the North") in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, apart from being labeled on the aforementioned map, Harfalas was never mentioned again, nor did it appear in any of the narratives, annals or essays set in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Harfalas* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "South [Falas](/wiki/Falas)". It is a combination of the prefixal form *[har-](/wiki/Harn "Harn")* ("south") + *[falas](/wiki/Falas#Etymology "Falas")* ("shore").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
