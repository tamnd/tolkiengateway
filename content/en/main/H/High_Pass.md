The **High Pass**, also known as the **Pass of Imladris**<sup id="cite_ref-DisasterNote14_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DisasterNote14-1">[1]</a></sup> or <i><b>Cirith Forn en Andrath</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup>,<sup id="cite_ref-Index_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-3">[3]</a></sup> was a pass over the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) east of the elven refuge of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Somewhere close to Rivendell<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-2">[2]</a></sup> a long, hard and dangerous path climbed into the mountains until it reached the mountain under which laid [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town) on its way to the High Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-5">[5]</a></sup> From the High Pass the ancient [Old Forest Road](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road) descended to the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) to the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and then on into [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) forest.<sup id="cite_ref-DisasterNote14_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DisasterNote14-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

There were two passes near Goblin-town: the High Pass and another pass that was easier to travel. The main gate of Goblin-town was located near the easier pass. Because the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of Goblin-town had often caught travellers who spent the night near the main gate at the easier pass, travellers stopped using this pass and used the High Pass instead. However, the Orcs created a new entrance in a cave near the High Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Pan_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pan-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the main part of the army of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), along with [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and part of the army of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), used the High Pass, as well as the [pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Gate "Redhorn Gate") of [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras), to cross the Misty Mountains on their march to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) in the War of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>. After this war [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was slain on his way back from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) to Rivendell at the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) that had been sent there before the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to watch the passes.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the High Pass became dangerous again because of the Orcs. Only after the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs), which nearly wiped out all of the Orcs of the mountains, did it become safe again. Nevertheless, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2940](/wiki/Third_Age_2940 "Third Age 2940"), the goblins of [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town) had burrowed their way back to it, and [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and the Dwarves of [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) were captured there the next year during the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Grimbeorn the Old](/wiki/Grimbeorn "Grimbeorn") and the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) kept the High Pass open,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> but the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) ignored the High Pass because the eastern side of the Misty Mountains had become too dangerous.

## Other names {#Other_names}

The High Pass was also known as the <i><b>Pass of Imladris</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Index_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[12]</a></sup> the pass was called <i><b>Cirith Forn en Andrath</b></i> ("The High-climbing Pass of the North").<sup id="cite_ref-Index_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-3">[3]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that the name is a combination of *[cirith](/wiki/Cirith "Cirith")* ("pass"), *[forn](/wiki/Forod "Forod")* ("north"), *[en](/wiki/En "En")* ("of the") and *[andrath](/wiki/Andrath#Etymology "Andrath")* ("long climb").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The translation of *Cirith Forn en Andrath* as "the high-climbing pass of the north" seems to be either a loose gloss or even an error, since elsewhere in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* *andrath* is definitively translated as "long climb",<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> and *an(d)* is definitively stated to mean "long".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

In the draft for the chapter, "[The Ring Goes South](/wiki/The_Ring_Goes_South)", [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) gave a name for both the High Pass and the [Goblin Gate](/wiki/Front_Porch "Front Porch"), which was *Annerchin*.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
