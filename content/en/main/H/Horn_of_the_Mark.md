The **Horn of the Mark** was a silver horn made by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). It was among their treasure that the [Long-worm](/wiki/Long-worms "Long-worms") [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha) took from them.<sup id="cite_ref-partings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Fram](/wiki/Fram) killed [Scatha](/wiki/Scatha) and took the horn<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoemsnote_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoemsnote-2">[2]</a></sup>, his<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-3">[3]</a></sup> hoard became a matter of dispute between the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) and the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Nonetheless, the Horn was taken by the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) and apparently became an heirloom of the [House of Eorl](/wiki/House_of_Eorl), even when they settled [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

More than a thousand years later, at the end of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Lady [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) gave the Horn to [Holdwine](/wiki/Holdwine) ([Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck)) as a farewell gift for his services to Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-partings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

On their return to [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), Merry used the Horn to sound the [Horn-call of Buckland](/wiki/Horn-call_of_Buckland) on [2 November](/wiki/2_November) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, rousing the Shire-folk against the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) who had taken control of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In commemoration of those events and the liberation of the Shire, the Horn was sounded ceremonially every year at sundown on 2 November in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), commencing celebrations with bonfires and feasts.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
