The **Horn of the Mark** was a silver horn made by the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). It was among their treasure that the [Long-worm](../L/Long-worms.md) [Scatha](../S/Scatha.md) took from them.<sup id="cite_ref-partings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Fram](../F/Fram.md) killed [Scatha](../S/Scatha.md) and took the horn<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoemsnote_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoemsnote-2">[2]</a></sup>, his<sup id="cite_ref-collectedpoems_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collectedpoems-3">[3]</a></sup> hoard became a matter of dispute between the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) and the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Nonetheless, the Horn was taken by the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) and apparently became an heirloom of the [House of Eorl](House_of_Eorl.md), even when they settled [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

More than a thousand years later, at the end of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Lady [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) gave the Horn to [Holdwine](Holdwine.md) ([Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md)) as a farewell gift for his services to Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-partings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

On their return to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), Merry used the Horn to sound the [Horn-call of Buckland](Horn-call_of_Buckland.md) on [2 November](../_/2_November.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>, rousing the Shire-folk against the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) who had taken control of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In commemoration of those events and the liberation of the Shire, the Horn was sounded ceremonially every year at sundown on 2 November in [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md), commencing celebrations with bonfires and feasts.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
