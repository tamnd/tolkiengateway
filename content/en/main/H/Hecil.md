<i><b>hecil</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning *abandoned one*, *outcast*. See prefixes *[hehta-](Hehta-.md)* and *[avarta-](../A/Avarta-.md)*. Compare with [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[awarth](../A/Awarth.md)/[awartha-](../A/Awartha-.md)*.
