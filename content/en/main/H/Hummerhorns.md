**Hummerhorns** were a legendary race of winged insects mentioned only in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") verse. The *hummerhorns* were said to have battled a knight on a quest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

*[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*:<b></b>

<dl><dd><i>Hummerhorns</i>, described and given statistics in various supplements, are portrayed as aggressive creatures, weighing 6-8 lbs and resembling gigantic black and grey wasps.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
