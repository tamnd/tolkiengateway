**Hardbottle** was a settlement of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) located in the stony northern section of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 2]</a></sup>

The [Bracegirdle Family](/wiki/Bracegirdle_Family) had their roots in Hardbottle. When [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins) was released from the [Lockholes](/wiki/Lockholes) of [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving) after the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) she (having been a Bracegirdle before her marriage) returned to her family in Hardbottle. She did so because she had lost the last of her family, her son [Lotho](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins "Lotho Sackville-Baggins"), and she gave [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) back to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "rocky (hard) large dwelling", because it is excavated in or built of stone of the rocky North Farthing. The element *bottle* and *bold* ([Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *botl*, from which the verb "build" is derived) is a real-life English place-name element meaning "(large) dwelling".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[2]</a></sup>

Based on this, [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that the name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") *Heardbotl* "hard (stony) dwelling".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[3]</a></sup>
