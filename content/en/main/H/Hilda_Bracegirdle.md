**Hilda Brandybuck, née Bracegirdle** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hilda married [Seredic Brandybuck](/wiki/Seredic_Brandybuck), and together they had three children: [Doderic](/wiki/Doderic_Brandybuck "Doderic Brandybuck"), [Ilberic](/wiki/Ilberic_Brandybuck "Ilberic Brandybuck") and [Celandine](/wiki/Celandine_Brandybuck "Celandine Brandybuck"). She was a guest at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
