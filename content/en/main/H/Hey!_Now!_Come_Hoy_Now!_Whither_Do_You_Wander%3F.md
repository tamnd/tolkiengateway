**Hey! Now! Come Hoy Now! Whither Do You Wander?** is one of the songs sung by [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The poem {#The_poem}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>Hey! now! Come hoy now! Whither do you wander?</i><br/>
<i>Up, down, near or far, here, there or yonder?</i><br/>
<i><a href="/wiki/Sharp-ears" title="Sharp-ears">Sharp-ears</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wise-nose" title="Wise-nose">Wise-nose</a>, <a href="/wiki/Swish-tail" title="Swish-tail">Swish-tail</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bumpkin" title="Bumpkin">Bumpkin</a>,</i><br/>
<i><a href="/wiki/White-socks" title="White-socks">White-socks</a> my little lad, and old <a href="/wiki/Fatty_Lumpkin" title="Fatty Lumpkin">Fatty Lumpkin</a>!</i>
</p>
</div>
