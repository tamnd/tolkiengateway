**Helmingas** was probably a name for the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), indicating the followers of and descendants of King [Helm](/wiki/Helm), just as the name [Eorlingas](/wiki/Eorlingas) referred to the people of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) who followed the first King of Rohan, [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl "Eorl").

## History {#History}

On [4 March](/wiki/4_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") during the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> when [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) managed to penetrate the [Deeping Wall](/wiki/Deeping_Wall), [Gamling](/wiki/Gamling) cried, "Helm! Helm! Forth Helmingas! " to rally the Rohirrim who defended [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) to repel the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Helmingas* is a name in the language of Rohan, represented by [Old English](/wiki/Old_English). It consists of *[Helm](/wiki/Helm#Etymology)* and *[-ingas](/wiki/-ings "-ings")* ("people").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Helmingas appears only once in the text, spoken by Gamling, and when he does, [Westfold-men](/wiki/Westfold-men) gather with him. The Index of [50th Anniversary Edition of *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)") (edited by [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull)) seems to interpret Helmingas and Westfold-men as somehow synonymous, and connects the two. <span></span>
