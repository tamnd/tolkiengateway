**Helmingas** was probably a name for the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md), indicating the followers of and descendants of King [Helm](Helm.md), just as the name [Eorlingas](../E/Eorlingas.md) referred to the people of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) who followed the first King of Rohan, [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl.md).

## History {#History}

On [4 March](../_/4_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> when [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) managed to penetrate the [Deeping Wall](../D/Deeping_Wall.md), [Gamling](../G/Gamling.md) cried, "Helm! Helm! Forth Helmingas! " to rally the Rohirrim who defended [Hornburg](Hornburg.md) to repel the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Helmingas* is a name in the language of Rohan, represented by [Old English](../O/Old_English.md). It consists of *[Helm](Helm.md#Etymology)* and *[-ingas](../_/-ings.md)* ("people").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Helmingas appears only once in the text, spoken by Gamling, and when he does, [Westfold-men](../W/Westfold-men.md) gather with him. The Index of [50th Anniversary Edition of *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) (edited by [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md)) seems to interpret Helmingas and Westfold-men as somehow synonymous, and connects the two. <span></span>
