**Holly** was a thorny evergreen species of ilex. In [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), it was especially abundant in the land of [Hollin](Hollin.md), which took its name from this tree.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "holly" was *[ereg](../E/Ereg.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
