**Harry Goatleaf** was a [Man of Bree](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") and a member of the [Goatleaf Family](/wiki/Goatleaf_Family).

## History {#History}

Harry Goatleaf worked as a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gate-keepers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gate-keepers (page does not exist)">Gate-keeper</a> stationed at the [western gate](/wiki/West-gate_of_Bree "West-gate of Bree") in the great hedge that surrounded the township of [Bree](/wiki/Bree).<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup> On [23 September](/wiki/23_September), [Strider](/wiki/Strider) observed the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) having a conversation with Goatleaf before leaving him white and shaking.<sup id="cite_ref-Monday_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Monday-2">[2]</a></sup> When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and his [companions](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") arrived at the town, he got a lantern and questioned them gruffly about their intentions, silently observing that they came from the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) before letting them in. Before wishing them good night, he warned them that there would be more folk besides him at *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony)* who would ask them questions.<sup id="cite_ref-Gate_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gate-1">[1]</a></sup>

After [Strider](/wiki/Strider) advised the four [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to be more careful as the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) were seen passing the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway) the previous [Monday](/wiki/Monday), Frodo realized that he should have known this from the way the Goatleaf spoke. Later, when Strider predicts that something may happen that night before they leave Bree, he mentioned that the Goatleaf may be a spy for the Nazgûl, noting the conversation he had overheard.<sup id="cite_ref-Monday_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Monday-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before the end of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), the [Men of Bree](/wiki/Men_of_Bree) kicked the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) among the [Southerners](/wiki/Southerners) out of Bree. Early in the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=New_Year&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="New Year (page does not exist)">New Year</a> however, the town was attacked in the night by the very same Ruffians. During the battle, Goatleaf fought on the side of the Ruffians along with [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny). After the battle, Goatleaf left Bree alongside the other Ruffians, hiding out in either the [Chetwood](/wiki/Chetwood) beyond [Archet](/wiki/Archet), or in the wilds north of Bree. Later, it was believed by [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) that Goatleaf and Ferny were the ones who let the Ruffians in on the night of the battle.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Goatleaf's position as a Gate-keeper was presumably later taken by another Gate-keeper.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft, Harry Goatleaf whispered with Bill Ferney and the [squint-eyed southerner](/wiki/Squint-eyed_southerner "Squint-eyed southerner") in a corner of the Prancing Pony and slipped out behind them after Frodo's song. He is described later on in the draft as "*a mean old curmudgeon*". In another draft, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) is at the gate and asks Goatleaf if he had seen any Shire-hobbits. Goatleaf revealed that he did not as he was in [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle) and his brother, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ned_Goatleaf&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ned Goatleaf (page does not exist)">Ned</a>, was at the gate in his place. After advising Gandalf to head to the inn, he shut the gate. Later in this draft, the Black Riders came to the gate and Harry revealed Gandalf's entry. A few moments later, when Frodo passed through, he was more friendly than in the published text, and the dialogue was slightly expanded.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Harry Goatleaf makes a brief appearance early in the second episode of the radio series. He is portrayed by an uncredited actor (making him the only notable character in the entire series to go uncredited).</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i></b>:

<dl><dd>Harry Goatleaf (portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Martyn_Sanderson" title="Martyn Sanderson">Martyn Sanderson</a>) is never named, but is referred to as the <b>Gate Keeper</b> in the end credits. Unlike the novel, he doesn&#39;t seem to be in league with the Black Riders. In fact, he is killed when the Black Riders knock the gate down and crush him underneath it as they storm Bree.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Harry Goatleaf is first encountered as one of the patrons in the Prancing Pony Inn, where he warns Frodo that suspicious folk have been seen in Bree, and seemingly shows concern for Frodo&#39;s safety and well-being. He is later seen in a cutscene opening the town&#39;s gates and letting wolves loose into the streets. After this, he is never seen again. He is voiced by an uncredited <a href="/wiki/Tom_Kane" title="Tom Kane">Tom Kane</a>.</dd></dl>
