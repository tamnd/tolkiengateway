The **House of Elros** was the line of descendants begun by [Elros](../E/Elros.md), the son of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) and brother of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md).

## History {#History}

### Ancestry {#Ancestry}

Elros, due to his ancestry, was counted as one of the [Half-elven](Half-elven.md), and given the choice of the life of the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) or the [Gift of Men](../G/Gift_of_Men.md). He chose to live as a mortal, but was granted an extraordinary lifespan of five hundred years. He passed his longevity on to the descendants of his House, though none enjoyed such a count of years as Elros himself.

### **Beginning of Númenor** {#Beginning_of_Númenor}

As [Tar-Minyatur](../E/Elros.md), Elros was the first [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md). After his time, that island was ruled by Kings and Queens of his House through nearly three thousand years of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). In the end, Elros' distant descendant [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) was manipulated by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) into bringing about the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md), and with it the end of the Line of Kings.<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-1">[1]</a></sup>

Though Númenor and its Kings were no more, the House of Elros did not end in the Downfall. The Kings had been descended through Elros' great-great-grandson [Meneldur](../M/Meneldur.md), but another branch of the House also survived: the [Lords of Andúnië](../../category/L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md), descended from Meneldur's elder sister [Silmariën](../S/Silmariën.md). The line of the Lords of Andúnië continued down to Elendil and his sons [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md), and so was carried from Númenor to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), surviving in the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) and of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). Even at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), some six thousand years after the death of its founder, there was still a living heir to the House of Elros: none other than [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) himself.

A family of [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) had ruled over the land of [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md) since the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). This family of [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) were akin to the Lords of Andúnië, and thus related to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and descended from the House of Elros.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) they had been given the title *Prince of Belfalas* by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and their land, now a fief of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), was called [Dor-en-Ernil](../D/Dor-en-Ernil.md). He granted this family a noble title, making them the hereditary Princes of Belfalas which was succeeded by the [Princes of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md).

#### Notes {#Notes_2}

<div class="mw-references-wrap"><ol class="references">
<li id="cite_note-12"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-12">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Tar-Telperiën became the second ruling queen even though she had a brother; apparently the law of succession was of simple primogeniture.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-13"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-13">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Tar-Atanamir the Great was also called &#34;the Unwilling&#34; since he was the first king to refuse to lay down his life or to renounce the Sceptre.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-14"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-14">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Herucalmo, the husband of  Tar-Vanimeldë, took the sceptre upon her death, calling himself Tar-Anducal.  He was not counted in the Line of Kings as the 17th ruler.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-15"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-15">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Tar-Calmacil was the first to be called (by the King&#39;s Men) by his name in Adûnaic: Ar-Belzagar.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-16"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-16">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Ar-Adûnakhôr was the first to take the sceptre with an Adûnaic title (however, his name was inscribed in the Scrolls in Quenya as Tar-Herunúmen).</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-17"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-17">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Amandil sailed into the West in the year <a href="../S/Second_Age_3316.md" title="Second Age 3316">3316</a>, to seek clemency for Númenor; he was never heard from again.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-18"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-18">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Míriel should have been the fourth Ruling Queen, but upon the death of her father her cousin Pharazôn took her to wife against her will and usurped the sceptre.  She died in the Downfall.</span>
</li>
<li id="cite_note-19"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-19">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Ar-Pharazôn seized the sceptre from Míriel.  He died in the Downfall.</span>
</li>
</ol></div>
