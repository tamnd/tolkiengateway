The **Hill of Erech** was a hill in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) situated at the mouth of the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale), far to the west of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It lay near to the southern entrance to the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead). Upon the summit stood a smooth black globe, the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech).

## History {#History}

The Hill of Erech was a hill lying on the southern fringes of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), near the [source](/wiki/Mornan "Mornan") of the River [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond).

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) placed a great [Black Stone](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech "Stone of Erech"), upon the summit of the Hill of Erech.

It was there that the [King of the Mountains](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead "King of the Dead") swore allegiance to Isildur's cause at the time of the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men"). However, when he failed to fulfil his oath, he and his people were cursed to wander and haunt the hills as the [Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") until they made good on their oath.

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers,_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers%2C_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers%2C_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="256" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/8a/Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers%2C_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers%2C_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/8a/Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers%2C_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Oathbreakers%2C_Why_Have_Ye_Come.jpg 2x" data-file-width="792" data-file-height="676"/></a><figcaption><i>Oathbreakers, Why Have Ye Come?</i> by Abe Papakhian.</figcaption></figure>

Thus, the Hill of Erech gained a sinister reputation, becoming a mysterious and eerie place, shunned by the people of the [Blackroot Vale](/wiki/Blackroot_Vale), who claimed that it was haunted by the whispering Dead.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-2">[2]</a></sup>

After waiting through the long years of the Third Age, at midnight on [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> the heir of Isildur, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), came to the Hill of Erech to hold the Dead to their oath again, promising that upon its fulfillment they would have peace.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-2">[2]</a></sup> [The next day](/wiki/9_March "9 March") they left the Hill of Erech for [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel).<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> Indeed, on [13 March](/wiki/13_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-3">[3]</a></sup> they held true to their oath and formed the Host of the Dead, aiding Aragorn in the defeat of the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). After the battle at Pelargir, Aragorn released them from their oath and they were allowed to rest.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The fate of the Hill of Erech during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) is not known.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Erech*, like so many names in Gondor, was of unknown [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) [pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "Pre-Númenórean") origin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
