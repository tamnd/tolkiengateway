The **House of Dol Amroth** was a noble house of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) whose male heirs were the [Princes of Dol Amroth](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md) descended from [Galador](../G/Galador.md) the first prince. Legend told that his father, [Imrazôr](../I/Imrazôr.md) [the Númenórean](../T/The_Númenórean.md) married a [Silvan Elf](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) named [Mithrellas](../M/Mithrellas.md), and from that time forward the house and kin were noble by blood and fair in face and mind.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background and history {#Background_and_history}

A family of [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md) from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) had ruled over the land of [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md) since the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). This family of [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) were akin to the [Lords of Andúnië](../L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md), being related to [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) himself and thus ultimately descending from the [House of Elros](House_of_Elros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) they had been given the title *Prince of Belfalas* by [High King](<High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> and their land, now a fief of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), was called [Dor-en-Ernil](../D/Dor-en-Ernil.md). He granted this family a noble title, making them the hereditary Princes of Belfalas.

One branch of their descendants removed to [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-3">[3]</a></sup> [Morwen Steelsheen](../M/Morwen_Steelsheen.md), a daughter of that family, married [Thengel](../T/Thengel.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Through Morwen, both Thengel's heir [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and his grandson [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) carried the blood of the ancient Princes of Belfalas.

One of those princes, [Adrahil](<../A/Adrahil_(Captain_of_the_Left_Wing).md>), fought under [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) against the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
