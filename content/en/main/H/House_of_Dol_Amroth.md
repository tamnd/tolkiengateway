The **House of Dol Amroth** was a noble house of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) whose male heirs were the [Princes of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth) descended from [Galador](/wiki/Galador) the first prince. Legend told that his father, [Imrazôr](/wiki/Imraz%C3%B4r) [the Númenórean](/wiki/The_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean "The Númenórean") married a [Silvan Elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") named [Mithrellas](/wiki/Mithrellas), and from that time forward the house and kin were noble by blood and fair in face and mind.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Background and history {#Background_and_history}

A family of [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) had ruled over the land of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) since the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). This family of [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) were akin to the [Lords of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB), being related to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) himself and thus ultimately descending from the [House of Elros](/wiki/House_of_Elros).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) they had been given the title *Prince of Belfalas* by [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> and their land, now a fief of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), was called [Dor-en-Ernil](/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil). He granted this family a noble title, making them the hereditary Princes of Belfalas.

One branch of their descendants removed to [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-3">[3]</a></sup> [Morwen Steelsheen](/wiki/Morwen_Steelsheen), a daughter of that family, married [Thengel](/wiki/Thengel) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Through Morwen, both Thengel's heir [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) and his grandson [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) carried the blood of the ancient Princes of Belfalas.

One of those princes, [Adrahil](</wiki/Adrahil_(Captain_of_the_Left_Wing)> "Adrahil (Captain of the Left Wing)"), fought under [King](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) against the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

Showing the descendants of Galador, the first of the [Princes of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth). All information in the tree is from *[The Peoples of Middle-Earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-Earth)*, "The Heirs of Elendil", either *The Line of Dol Amroth* or *The House of Dol Amroth*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> The figures after the names are those of birth and death where recorded (when only one figure is shown it is marked as either the date of birth, 'b.'). A dagger symbol, '†' signifies those who did not die of old age. A name preceded by '\*' indicates one of the Princes of Dol Amroth. A name in *italics* indicates a woman.
