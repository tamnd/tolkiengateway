**Harding of [the Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill "Hobbiton Hill")** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire).

## History {#History}

Harding was the son of [Holfast Gardner](/wiki/Holfast_Gardner) and a great-grandson of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), the hero of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-tree_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tree-1">[1]</a></sup> He became known as "of the Hill".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name <i><b>Harding</b></i> comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *hearding* ("hard one, strong one").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
