**Harding of [the Hill](Hobbiton_Hill.md)** was a [Hobbit](Hobbits.md) of [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Harding was the son of [Holfast Gardner](Holfast_Gardner.md) and a great-grandson of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), the hero of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-tree_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tree-1">[1]</a></sup> He became known as "of the Hill".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name <i><b>Harding</b></i> comes from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *hearding* ("hard one, strong one").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
