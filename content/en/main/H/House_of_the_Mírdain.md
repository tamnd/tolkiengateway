The **House of the Mírdain** was the guildhouse and chief dwelling of [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) and the jewelsmiths of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) - the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain). It contained the smithies and the treasures of the Mírdain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was destroyed by the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697") when he invaded [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The House of the Mírdain stood at the center of <a href="/wiki/Ost-in-Edhil" title="Ost-in-Edhil">Caras Gelebren</a>. Its <a href="/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion" title="Sack of Eregion">fall</a> is depicted in a flashback, where the players play the parts of the defenders.</dd></dl>
