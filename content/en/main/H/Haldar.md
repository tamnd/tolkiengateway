**Haldar** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) who dwelled in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion). He was the son of [Haldad](/wiki/Haldad) and brother of [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth) and had a son, [Haldan](/wiki/Haldan).

## History {#History}

His family survived an [Orc](/wiki/Orc) attack against his people. His father built a [stockade](/wiki/Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Gelion-Ascar Stockade") in defence. In the [ensuing siege](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade "Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade") their food was exhausted, and Haldad was slain during a sortie against the attacking [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs). Haldar rushed to recover his father's body, and was slain as well.

Haldar thus did not live to succeed his father as [Chieftain of the Haladin](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin). It was his sister [Haleth](/wiki/Haleth) who took up the rule of her people, but she died childless, and Haldar's son Haldan and his descendants became the rulers of the people later known as the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
