<b><i>Hither Shore</i> Band 13: <i>Tolkien&#39;s Philosophy of Language</i></b> is the thirteenth volume of the journal *[Hither Shore](</wiki/Hither_Shore_(journal)> "Hither Shore (journal)")*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Preface/Vorwort
- Tolkien Seminar 2016
    - "A Natural Product of our Humanity: Tolkien's Philosophy of Language" by Ross Smith (Madrid)
    - "Sprache und Weltverständnis" by Thomas Fornet-Ponse (Köln)
    - "Tolkien's Linguistic World-Building Examined" by Patrick Schmitz &amp; David Graziano (Alsdorf/Zweibrücken)
    - "Behind the Names of Torhthelm and Tídwald: Tolkien's Onomastic Imagination in *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth Beorhthelm's Son](/wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth_Beorhthelm%27s_Son)*" by Lukasz Neubauer (Koszalin)
    - "Nomen est Omen - Funktion und Bedeutung von Namen in Tolkiens Werk" by Helmut W. Pesch (Bergisch Gladbach)
    - "Mediation of Names in *The Lord of the Rings*" by Allan Turner (Gateshead)
    - "Ein heimlliches Laster oder der Untergrund der Sprache: Eine Untersunchung zu Tolkiens Semiotik" by Wilhelm Kuehs (Klagenfurt)
    - "Thresholds and Departures: Narrative Functions for Non-Westron Languages in Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*" by Jonathan Nauman (Vaughan Association, USA)
    - "Hermeneutical Perspectives on Tolkien's Rhetorical Craftsmanship" by Annie Birks (Angers)
    - "Angeborene, natürliche und Heimatsprache - Native Language - und was die Wissenschaft heute davon hält" by Friedhelm Schneidewind (Hemsbach)

- Additional Essays
    - "*The Lord of the Rings Stage Musical* as a Fairy-Story" by Tobias Escher (Bingen)
    - Zusammenfassungen der englischen Essays
    - Summaries of the German Essays

- Reviews/Rezensionen
- Our Authors/Unsere Autorinnen &amp; Autoren
- Index
