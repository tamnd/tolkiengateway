**Hiril** was the name of [Beren](../B/Beren.md)'s sister in a later emendation to the genealogical table of the [House of Bëor](House_of_Bëor.md). Nothing else is known about her.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hiril* means "lady" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (*[Hîr](Hîr.md)* + *[-il](../_/-il.md)*).
