**Hiril** was the name of [Beren](/wiki/Beren)'s sister in a later emendation to the genealogical table of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor). Nothing else is known about her.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hiril* means "lady" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (*[Hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr)* + *[-il](/wiki/-il)*).
