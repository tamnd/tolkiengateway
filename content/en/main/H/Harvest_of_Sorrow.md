**Harvest of Sorrow** is a bonus track on [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-themed album *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)*. The song tells about the [Children of Húrin](</wiki/Narn_i_Ch%C3%AEn_H%C3%BArin_(tale)> "Narn i Chîn Húrin (tale)").
