**Haldir** was, in the early stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the elder son of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was trapped and hung to a tree by the [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Grey Annals](/wiki/Grey_Annals) he was referenced as being slain in the year [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [488](/wiki/First_Age_488 "First Age 488") in a note that was struck out.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Haldir* is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) for "Hidden Hero", from *hall* ("hidden") + *dîr* ("man").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> A rejected meaning was "fur-hunter".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The character was originally named <i>Hal<b>m</b>ir</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In *Ælfwine’s translation of the Quenta into Old English; Old English equivalents of Elvish names*, the name of Orodreth's first son was given as <i><b>Ordhelm</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
