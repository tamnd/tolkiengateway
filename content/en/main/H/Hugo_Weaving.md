**Hugo Wallace Weaving** (born 1960) is an Australian actor who portrayed [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
