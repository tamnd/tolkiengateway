**Horn** was a man of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

Horn fought and died in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). His name is remembered in the poem *[Song of the Mounds of Mundburg](/wiki/Song_of_the_Mounds_of_Mundburg)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Horn* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name,<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-IE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-3">[3]</a></sup> which means "horn". It contains the Old English element *horn*<sup id="cite_ref-IE_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IE-3">[3]</a></sup> ("horn").

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Riders_of_Rohan" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan">The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Horn is a prominent character in the game and accompanies the player throughout all of their travels in Riddermark. He is the son of the Reeve of <a href="/wiki/Entwash_Vale" title="Entwash Vale">Entwash Vale</a> and the brother of Ingyth, the betrothed of <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred" title="Théodred">Théodred</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Horn&#39;s ultimate fate depends on a decision made by the player much earlier in the game. During the summoning of the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Company" title="Grey Company">Grey Company</a>, Halros can be persuaded to either journey south to the aid of <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> or stay and perform the very task that Aragorn instructed him to do. If Halros stays in the Shire, Horn dies in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>. However, if Halros is persuaded to go south, he shields Horn from the mortal blow at the cost of his own life. Horrified by the death around him and the sacrifice of a man he didn&#39;t even know, Horn loses his will to go on and abandons his duty. He forsakes his name and, now a broken man, leaves into paths unknown, telling the player to report him as dead.</dd></dl>
