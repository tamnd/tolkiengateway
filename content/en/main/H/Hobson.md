**Hobson** was the son of [Hob Gammidge](/wiki/Hob_Gammidge). He was the first to adopt the family's birth place as a family name, with his byname **Roper Gamgee**.

## History {#History}

Hobson was the son of Hob Gammidge, and had four children: [Andy](/wiki/Andwise_Roper "Andwise Roper"), [Ham](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee"), [May](</wiki/May_(hobbit)> "May (hobbit)") and [Halfred](/wiki/Halfred_of_Overhill "Halfred of Overhill").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Hobson owned a ropewalk in [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield). After his death, Andy took over business, and took the surname "Roper".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Hobson was fittingly named the "Son of [Hob](/wiki/Hob_Gammidge "Hob Gammidge"). His byname "Roper Gamgee" he earned from his profession and the birthplace of his ancestors, Gamwich. His two eldest sons would each use part of the byname as a surname: [Andwise Roper](/wiki/Andwise_Roper) and [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee").
