The **high feast** was a feast that [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) made in praise of [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md) to celebrate each gathering of fruits. For this feast, the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) were bidden to come to the [halls of Manwë and Varda](../I/Ilmarin.md). Just before the [Darkening of Valinor](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md), a feast was held to which also the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) came, and they sang together with the Maiar before Manwë and [Varda](../V/Varda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

Alternate names used for this event include the **great festival**,<sup id="cite_ref-sm30_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sm30-2">[2]</a></sup> **autumn feast**,<sup id="cite_ref-sm30_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sm30-2">[2]</a></sup> and **high festival**.<sup id="cite_ref-sm57_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sm57-3">[3]</a></sup>

## High feast of 1495 {#High_feast_of_1495}

During great high feast of [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1495](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1495.md), Manwë sought to heal the rifts between the houses of the Noldor.

To [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), dwelling at [Formenos](../F/Formenos.md) following his banishment, Manwë had sent the following message of invite: "*Fëanor son of Finwë, come and do not deny my bidding! In my love thou remainest and wilt be honoured in my hall.*"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Fëanor did come to the feast, but only because he had read Manwë's message as a command;<sup id="cite_ref-MR58c_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR58c-5">[5]</a></sup> he wore no raiment of festival or ornaments, and denied sight of his [Silmarils](../S/Silmaril.md) by keeping them locked back in Formenos.<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup> Finwë would not come and remained in Formenos, stating that "*While the ban lasts upon Fëanor my son, that he may not go to Túna, I hold myself unkinged, and will not meet my people, nor those that rule in my stead.*"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> With Finwë remained the [sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-MR58c_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR58c-5">[5]</a></sup> along with the other Noldor residing at Formenos.<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fëanor was reconciled, in word, with [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) before the throne of Manwë after Fingolfin held forth his hand and stated: "*As I promised, I do now. I release thee, and remember no grievance.*" Fëanor took his hand and remained silent, with Fingolfin then adding: "*Half-brother in blood, full brother in heart will I be. Thou shalt lead and I will follow. May no new grief divide us.*" to which Fëanor replied "*I hear thee, so be it.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was told that in this very hour [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) destroyed the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the corresponding event is the **Samírien**, which means "Feast of Double Mirth" in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md). [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) noted that the name was "Presumably derive from the root MIRI ‘smile’, *sa-* is referred to in [QL](../Q/Qenya_Lexicon.md) as an ‘intensive prefix’."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
