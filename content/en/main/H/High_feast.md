The **high feast** was a feast that [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) made in praise of [Eru](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar "Ilúvatar") to celebrate each gathering of fruits. For this feast, the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) were bidden to come to the [halls of Manwë and Varda](/wiki/Ilmarin "Ilmarin"). Just before the [Darkening of Valinor](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor), a feast was held to which also the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) came, and they sang together with the Maiar before Manwë and [Varda](/wiki/Varda).<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

Alternate names used for this event include the **great festival**,<sup id="cite_ref-sm30_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sm30-2">[2]</a></sup> **autumn feast**,<sup id="cite_ref-sm30_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sm30-2">[2]</a></sup> and **high festival**.<sup id="cite_ref-sm57_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sm57-3">[3]</a></sup>

## High feast of 1495 {#High_feast_of_1495}

During great high feast of [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495"), Manwë sought to heal the rifts between the houses of the Noldor.

To [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor), dwelling at [Formenos](/wiki/Formenos) following his banishment, Manwë had sent the following message of invite: "*Fëanor son of Finwë, come and do not deny my bidding! In my love thou remainest and wilt be honoured in my hall.*"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Fëanor did come to the feast, but only because he had read Manwë's message as a command;<sup id="cite_ref-MR58c_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR58c-5">[5]</a></sup> he wore no raiment of festival or ornaments, and denied sight of his [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmaril "Silmaril") by keeping them locked back in Formenos.<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup> Finwë would not come and remained in Formenos, stating that "*While the ban lasts upon Fëanor my son, that he may not go to Túna, I hold myself unkinged, and will not meet my people, nor those that rule in my stead.*"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> With Finwë remained the [sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor")<sup id="cite_ref-MR58c_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR58c-5">[5]</a></sup> along with the other Noldor residing at Formenos.<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

Fëanor was reconciled, in word, with [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) before the throne of Manwë after Fingolfin held forth his hand and stated: "*As I promised, I do now. I release thee, and remember no grievance.*" Fëanor took his hand and remained silent, with Fingolfin then adding: "*Half-brother in blood, full brother in heart will I be. Thou shalt lead and I will follow. May no new grief divide us.*" to which Fëanor replied "*I hear thee, so be it.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was told that in this very hour [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) destroyed the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees).<sup id="cite_ref-Darkening_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Darkening-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the corresponding event is the **Samírien**, which means "Feast of Double Mirth" in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted that the name was "Presumably derive from the root MIRI ‘smile’, *sa-* is referred to in [QL](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon "Qenya Lexicon") as an ‘intensive prefix’."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
