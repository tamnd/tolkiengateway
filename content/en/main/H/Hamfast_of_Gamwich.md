**Hamfast of Gamwich** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Hamfast lived in [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich). He was the father of [Wiseman](/wiki/Wiseman_Gamwich "Wiseman Gamwich"), who moved to [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield), and as such, he was the progenitor of the [Gamwich](/wiki/Gamwich_Family "Gamwich Family") (later [Gamgee](/wiki/Gamgee_Family "Gamgee Family")) family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Hamfast* comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *hámfæst*, meaning "stay-at-home". It was a translation of Westron *Ranugad*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
