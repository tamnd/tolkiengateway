**Hamfast of Gamwich** was a [Hobbit](Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Hamfast lived in [Gamwich](../G/Gamwich.md). He was the father of [Wiseman](../W/Wiseman_Gamwich.md), who moved to [Tighfield](../T/Tighfield.md), and as such, he was the progenitor of the [Gamwich](../G/Gamwich_Family~66369.md) (later [Gamgee](../G/Gamgee_Family~66365.md)) family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Hamfast* comes from [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) *hámfæst*, meaning "stay-at-home". It was a translation of Westron *Ranugad*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
