**Hallacar** was the son of [Hallatan](/wiki/Hallatan) of [Hyarastorni](/wiki/Hyarastorni),<sup id="cite_ref-Further_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Further-2">[2]</a></sup> and a descendant of [Vardamir Nólimon](/wiki/Vardamir "Vardamir").<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ancalimë was living in [Emerië](/wiki/Emeri%C3%AB), hiding on a farm bordering the lands of Hallatan to avoid suitors, when she met a shepherd named *Mámandil*. She enjoyed his company since he was a skilled singer and met him often in the pastures. Eventually he declared his love for her but Ancalimë demurred since she was the Heir of the King. Mamandil then revealed that he was actually Hallacar of the line of [Elros](/wiki/Elros) and pressed his suit. Still, Ancalimë rejected him although they were later wed.<sup id="cite_ref-Further_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Further-2">[2]</a></sup>

Accounts differ as to the particulars of their marriage. One version stated that Hallacar persisted in seeking her hand and that Ancalimë was pressured by the [Council of the Sceptre](/wiki/Council_of_the_Sceptre) to accept him to bring quiet to the realm. Another version said that when her cousin [Soronto](/wiki/Soronto) called upon her to surrender the [Heirship](/wiki/King%27s_Heir "King's Heir") (which at the time required that a female Heir must resign if unwed beyond a certain limit), she married Hallacar to spite Soronto. Still another account suggested that Ancalimë married Hallacar after the provision had been removed, to ensure that Soronto could not become King if she were childless.<sup id="cite_ref-Further_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Further-2">[2]</a></sup>

Thus, both married in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1000](/wiki/Second_Age_1000 "Second Age 1000"),<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-3">[3]</a></sup> despite it was clear was that Ancalimë desired neither love nor a son. After the birth of their son [Anárion](/wiki/Tar-An%C3%A1rion "Tar-Anárion") in [1003](/wiki/Second_Age_1003 "Second Age 1003")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> there was strife between them<sup id="cite_ref-Kings_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kings-3">[3]</a></sup> and after Hallacar secretly arranged for her serving-women to become married, which the Queen had forbidden, she hated Hallacar.<sup id="cite_ref-Further_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Further-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hallacar* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Tall Helmet", from *[halla](/wiki/Halla "Halla")* ("tall") + *[car(ma)](/wiki/Karma "Karma")* ("helmet").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Mámandil* is also a Quenya name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Sheep-friend", from *[máma](/wiki/M%C3%A1ma "Máma")* ("sheep") + *[-ndil](/wiki/-ndil)* ("friend, lover").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
