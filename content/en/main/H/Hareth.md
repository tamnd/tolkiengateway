**Hareth** was the elder daughter of [Halmir](/wiki/Halmir), [chieftain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_Haladin "Chieftain of the Haladin") of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), and sister of [Haldir](</wiki/Haldir_(Chieftain_of_the_Haladin)> "Haldir (Chieftain of the Haladin)"), [Hundar](/wiki/Hundar),<sup id="cite_ref-Haladin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Haladin-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Hiril](/wiki/Hiril).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [436](/wiki/First_Age_436 "First Age 436")<sup id="cite_ref-guide_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-4">[4]</a></sup> there was a great feast of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), when she and her brother married with two children of [Hador](/wiki/Hador) of the [House of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Marach); whereas Hareth married [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)"), [Lord of Dor-lómin](/wiki/Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min), her brother married Galdor's sister, [Glóredhel](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3redhel).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Hareth bore him two sons, [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Huor](/wiki/Huor).<sup id="cite_ref-Haladin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Haladin-2">[2]</a></sup>

Disasters such as the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), the death of childless [Brandir the Lame](/wiki/Brandir_the_Lame) by Hareth's grandson [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and the coming of her son Húrin to [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil), causing the ruin of [Obel Halad](/wiki/Obel_Halad),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> resulted to the dwindling and decline of Hareth's people.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Fortunately, her union with the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador), kept the lineage of her House alive through her grandson, [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Hareth* also appears in *[Tûr Haretha](/wiki/Haudh-en-Arwen#Names "Haudh-en-Arwen")*, the "Ladybarrow", so the name probably means "Lady" in the [Haladin tongue](/wiki/Halethian "Halethian").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) also suggests that *Hareth* means "Lady",<sup id="cite_ref-guide_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-4">[4]</a></sup> however the entry erroneously labels the name as [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}
