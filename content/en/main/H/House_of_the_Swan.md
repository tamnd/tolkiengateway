The **house of Swan** is an isolated concept in the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

[Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) referred to himself as belonging to "the house of the Swan of the sons of the [Men of the North](</wiki/Men_of_the_North_(First_Age)> "Men of the North (First Age)")".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:160</sup> It may be supposed that [Peleg](/wiki/Peleg) and [Indor](/wiki/Indor) (Tuor's father and grandfather respectively in this stage of the legendarium) also belonged to this house; or, more probably, Tuor may have invented it for himself, as he had personally chosen the [Swan](/wiki/Swans "Swans") as emblem.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:151</sup> Later, the house Tuor founds in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) is called the "[house of the Wing](/wiki/House_of_the_Wing "House of the Wing")" and its emblem was the wing of a "swan or gull".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:172</sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a [prose fragment](/wiki/Prose_Fragments_Following_the_Lost_Tales "Prose Fragments Following the Lost Tales") following the Lost Tales, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) changed the name and explored the concept more deeply: after the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears), a great part of the kindreds of Men dwelt in [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) about the waters of the lake [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim), and they were friends of both the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) and the [Dark Elves](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi"). They were called <i><b>Tunglin</b></i> ([Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) for "folk of the Harp"), as their joy was in the wild music.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the later [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the motif of the swan remained: in *[Of Tuor and his Coming to Gondolin](/wiki/Of_Tuor_and_his_Coming_to_Gondolin)* is said that the swan was the token of Tuor's foster-father [Annael](/wiki/Annael) and his folk, who were indeed "men of the North", dwelling in [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondolin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondolin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:205</sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
