**Hayward** was the surname of at least one [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"), [Hob Hayward](/wiki/Hob_Hayward).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is derived from *hay* 'fence' + *ward* 'guard'.

The English word refers to a local official with the duty of inspecting fences and keeping cattle from straying, and survives in the very common real-life surname Hayward.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
