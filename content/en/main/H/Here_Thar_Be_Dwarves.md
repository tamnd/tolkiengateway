**Here Thar Be Dwarves** is the 23rd episode overall (the seventh of the third season) of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grim_Adventures_of_Billy_%26_Mandy" title="wikipedia:The Grim Adventures of Billy &amp; Mandy">The Grim Adventures of Billy &amp; Mandy</a></i>. It was first aired on [July 23](../_/23_July.md), [2004](../_/2004.md), along with *Bully Boogie*. The episode incorporates several elements from *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, as well as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars" title="wikipedia:Star Wars">Star Wars</a></i>.

## Plot {#Plot}

Billy prepares for a picnic, but Mandy, Grim, and Gladys try to prevent him from going. Billy is kidnapped by a bear later revealed to be Harold, but his friends hardly notice. Harold is able to convince them to let Billy have his picnic.

As he searches for a picnic spot, Billy's picnic basket is nearly stolen by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogi_Bear" title="wikipedia:Yogi Bear">Yogi Bear</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boo-Boo" title="wikipedia:Boo-Boo">Boo-Boo</a>. Billy defeats them by hiding in a cave and telling the bears that they can't follow him because they are afraid of caves.

Billy heads farther into the cave and finds a sign. A dwarf appears, mistaking him for an elf, and takes him prisoner. He introduces Billy to the dwarven leader, Beardbottom, by divulging personal details of his romantic relationships. Beardbottom questions Billy and the dwarf and leads them on a tour of the dwarves' mushroom growing facilities. Billy tries one and says it tastes like his cat, which the dwarves have also noticed. He wonders why the dwarves grow mushrooms if they dislike them.

Beardbottom explains to them that the dwarves were assigned by a council member named Reagan to manage baking cookies, while the elves were assigned to harvest the mushrooms. The head elf objected to this due to his people being far too dainty to effectively and efficiently harvest mushrooms. He instead suggests that the dwarves and elves swap jobs, and Beardbottom agrees. Billy asks why the dwarves agreed to the proposal so quickly, and Beardbottom reveals that cookies are far more profitable than mushrooms and the elves must have known despite their denial.

Beardbottom asks Billy to help the dwarves by infiltrating the elf headquarters disguised as an elf, dropping their shields, and opening a way for the dwarves to attack. The dwarves and elves fight a violent battle, which is comically censored by cutting to a story of a man named Joe while a koala eats eucalyptus. The footage is accidentally cut back to the fight, and once the battle is over, the ranger returns the audience to the regular episode.

The battle ends in a tie, and Billy is left to mediate an agreement for the elves and dwarves to sell mushroom cookies for a 60/40 split of the profits. Billy breaks into song, and serenades the crowd of elves, dwarves, Grim, and Mandy with *I Love You*.

The episode ends with a mock advertisement for "Mushookies", starring a girl declaring the cookies "taste like \[her\] cat".
