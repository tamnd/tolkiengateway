**Valanya** (or **Tárion**) was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name of the sixth day of the [Eldarin](../E/Eldar.md) six-day week (and of the seventh day of the [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) seven-day week), its name referring to the [Valar](Valar.md). Its [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name was **Orbelain** (or **Rodyn**).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
