**Valanya** (or **Tárion**) was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of the sixth day of the [Eldarin](/wiki/Eldar "Eldar") six-day week (and of the seventh day of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") seven-day week), its name referring to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). Its [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name was **Orbelain** (or **Rodyn**).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
