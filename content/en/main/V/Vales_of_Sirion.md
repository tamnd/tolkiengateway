The **Vale(s) of Sirion** was the wide valley of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), that separated [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) from [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The term seems to apply especially to that part of the river's course around the [Pass of Sirion](../P/Pass_of_Sirion.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> that lay to the north of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

These waters were protected by [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> until some point prior to [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md); at that time, two messengers from [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) bore tidings to [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) that the Evil of the North had defiled the springs of Sirion, and Ulmo's power had withdrawn.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
