<i><b>verya-</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) verb for "to marry (of husband and wife), be joined to".<sup id="cite_ref-Vinyar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vinyar-1">[1]</a></sup> In earlier Quenya, the term for "to marry" was *vesta-*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> while *verya-* meant "to dare".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Root) BER ("to mate, be mated, joined in marriage")<sup id="cite_ref-Vinyar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vinyar-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *veryanen* = "I married"
