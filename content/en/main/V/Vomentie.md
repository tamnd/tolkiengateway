**vomentie** is [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) for "meeting (of two)", cognate of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[omentie](/wiki/Omentie "Omentie")*.

It is only attested with a pronominal ending inflected for genitive: [vomentienguo](/wiki/Vomentienguo "Vomentienguo") "of our meeting".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word contains the prefix *[vo-](/wiki/V%C3%B3 "Vó")*.
