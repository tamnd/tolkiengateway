**Vilma** was a [Harfoot](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Her character was invented for *[The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power")* television series by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios), where she was portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maxine_Cunliffe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maxine Cunliffe (page does not exist)">Maxine Cunliffe</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
