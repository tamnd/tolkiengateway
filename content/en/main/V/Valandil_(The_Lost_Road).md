**Valandil** was the father of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*.

## History {#History}

When [Herendil](/wiki/Herendil) informed his father that though the two of them are the blood of kings, his peers called him names such as "Terendul" the day prior, and accused him of being a "half-Noldo",<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup> [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) responded by saying that then it may be dangerous for Herendil to be his son as his own father's name, Valandil, meant *"God-friend"*.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later. Elendil tells his son that he cannot be corrupted by the servants of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) due to being Valandil his father being the "chief of the wise men of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valandil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), being a compound of *vanan* ("god") + *(-n)dil* ("friend"). Its [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) cognate was *[Oswine](/wiki/Oswin_Errol "Oswin Errol")*, which also means "God-friend".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In another stage of the [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium) separate from *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*, Valandil was the brother of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) the Tall. In this stage, Valandil had the role of [Anarion](/wiki/Anarion) and founded [Ondor](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
