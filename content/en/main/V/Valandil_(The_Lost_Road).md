**Valandil** was the father of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*.

## History {#History}

When [Herendil](../H/Herendil.md) informed his father that though the two of them are the blood of kings, his peers called him names such as "Terendul" the day prior, and accused him of being a "half-Noldo",<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup> [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) responded by saying that then it may be dangerous for Herendil to be his son as his own father's name, Valandil, meant *"God-friend"*.<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

Later. Elendil tells his son that he cannot be corrupted by the servants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) due to being Valandil his father being the "chief of the wise men of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valandil* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), being a compound of *vanan* ("god") + *(-n)dil* ("friend"). Its [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) cognate was *[Oswine](../O/Oswin_Errol.md)*, which also means "God-friend".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In another stage of the [Legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) separate from *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*, Valandil was the brother of [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) the Tall. In this stage, Valandil had the role of [Anarion](../A/Anarion.md) and founded [Ondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
