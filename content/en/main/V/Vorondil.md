**Vorondil** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [1919](../T/Third_Age_1919.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> – [2029](../T/Third_Age_2029.md),<sup id="cite_ref-App|South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 110 years) was the second of three [Stewards](../S/Stewards.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) that served King [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md).

## History {#History}

Upon the death of his father, [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md), the Stewardship of Gondor became hereditary within the [House of Húrin](../H/House_of_Húrin.md) and thus Vorondil succeeded to the position.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup>

Early in Vorondil's Stewardship Gondor was attacked and suffered a serious defeat. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2000](../T/Third_Age_2000.md), the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) issued from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) to besiege [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md). The city fell in [2002](../T/Third_Age_2002.md) and with it they took the *[Palantír](../P/Palantíri.md)* therein.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Vorondil was remembered as "Vorondil the Hunter" for his hunting of the [Kine of Araw](../K/Kine_of_Araw.md), found near the shores of the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-App|South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|South-2">[2]</a></sup> From one of the wild white oxen he fashioned a [Great Horn](../G/Great_Horn.md), bound with silver and written with characters.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It had been passed to the eldest son for generations within the House of Húrin and [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) was the last to carry this horn.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Vorondil was succeeded by his son [Mardil](../M/Mardil.md), who would become the first of the [Ruling Stewards](../R/Ruling_Steward.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vorondil* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Ever-friend" and is a compound of *voro* ("ever") and the suffix *[-(n)dil](../_/-ndil.md)* ("friend", "lover", "devoted to").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
