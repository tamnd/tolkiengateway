**Vorondil** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [1919](/wiki/Third_Age_1919 "Third Age 1919")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> – [2029](/wiki/Third_Age_2029 "Third Age 2029"),<sup id="cite_ref-App|South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 110 years) was the second of three [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that served King [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II).

## History {#History}

Upon the death of his father, [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur), the Stewardship of Gondor became hereditary within the [House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin) and thus Vorondil succeeded to the position.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup>

Early in Vorondil's Stewardship Gondor was attacked and suffered a serious defeat. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2000](/wiki/Third_Age_2000 "Third Age 2000"), the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) issued from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) to besiege [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil). The city fell in [2002](/wiki/Third_Age_2002 "Third Age 2002") and with it they took the *[Palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* therein.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Vorondil was remembered as "Vorondil the Hunter" for his hunting of the [Kine of Araw](/wiki/Kine_of_Araw), found near the shores of the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-App|South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|South-2">[2]</a></sup> From one of the wild white oxen he fashioned a [Great Horn](/wiki/Great_Horn), bound with silver and written with characters.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It had been passed to the eldest son for generations within the House of Húrin and [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) was the last to carry this horn.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Vorondil was succeeded by his son [Mardil](/wiki/Mardil), who would become the first of the [Ruling Stewards](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward").<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vorondil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Ever-friend" and is a compound of *voro* ("ever") and the suffix *[-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil")* ("friend", "lover", "devoted to").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
