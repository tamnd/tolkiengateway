**Varin** was a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) created for [Sierra Entertainment](../S/Sierra_Entertainment.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)*. He was voiced by [Steven Jay Blum](../S/Steven_Jay_Blum.md).

## History {#History}

Varin was sent out by [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) to provide "the novice" (the player) with a tutorial for the game. Much to his chagrin, Gandalf also asked [Belenthrond](../B/Belenthrond.md), an Elf from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-tut1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut1-1">[1]</a></sup> The two squabble back and forth to teach the player basic skills, while Varin outlines basic controls Belenthrond explains the finer details. Varin explains the novice about moving the camera,<sup id="cite_ref-tut1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut1-1">[1]</a></sup> commodities, fighting,<sup id="cite_ref-tut2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut2-2">[2]</a></sup> heroes and fate.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Varin has an hatred for [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md)<sup id="cite_ref-tut1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut1-1">[1]</a></sup> and loves war.<sup id="cite_ref-tut2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut2-2">[2]</a></sup>
