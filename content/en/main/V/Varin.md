**Varin** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") created for [Sierra Entertainment](/wiki/Sierra_Entertainment)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*. He was voiced by [Steven Jay Blum](/wiki/Steven_Jay_Blum).

## History {#History}

Varin was sent out by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to provide "the novice" (the player) with a tutorial for the game. Much to his chagrin, Gandalf also asked [Belenthrond](/wiki/Belenthrond), an Elf from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-tut1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut1-1">[1]</a></sup> The two squabble back and forth to teach the player basic skills, while Varin outlines basic controls Belenthrond explains the finer details. Varin explains the novice about moving the camera,<sup id="cite_ref-tut1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut1-1">[1]</a></sup> commodities, fighting,<sup id="cite_ref-tut2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut2-2">[2]</a></sup> heroes and fate.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Varin has an hatred for [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins)<sup id="cite_ref-tut1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut1-1">[1]</a></sup> and loves war.<sup id="cite_ref-tut2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tut2-2">[2]</a></sup>
