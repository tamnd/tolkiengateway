**Vairë the Weaver** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvaɪre]</span>) was a [Valië](/wiki/Valar "Valar") and the wife of [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos).

The [Valie](/wiki/Valie) was responsible for weaving the story of the World, with which the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos) are clothed, and ever widen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the death of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel) returned to life and entered the service of Vairë, recording all the deeds of the [House of Finwë](/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name *Vairë* means "Weaver",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "Ever-weaving", derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") WIR.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, Quenya *Vaire* ("Weaver") is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *weirē*, deriving from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") WEY ("wind, weave").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[5]</a></sup>

Her [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name is said to be <i><b>Gwîr</b></i> ("Weaver").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-5">[5]</a></sup> An early, [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) version of her name was *Gwairil*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A different [Vairë](</wiki/Vair%C3%AB_(wife_of_Lindo)> "Vairë (wife of Lindo)") appeared in some of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s earliest writings. In *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*, she was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), and the daughter of [Tulkastor](/wiki/Tulkastor). She and her husband [Lindo](/wiki/Lindo) tell the stories that would become the [Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion "Quenta Silmarillion") to the human mariner [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine)/[Eriol](/wiki/Eriol). Her role as storyteller may have influenced the naming of the Vala responsible for recording stories.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
