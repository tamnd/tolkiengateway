<i><b>víne</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "youth".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the form *vínë* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [WIN](/wiki/WIN)<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>
