**Vinyalondë** was a harbour founded by [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) and the [Venturers](Venturers.md) at the mouth of the [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-1">[1]</a></sup> It was one of the first settlements built by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), and survived (at least in part) into the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) as the port of **Lond Daer**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Vinyalondë, or "New Haven", was founded by [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md)'s crown prince [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) on the estuary of the river [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md), between [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [750](../S/Second_Age_750.md) and [800](../S/Second_Age_800.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was the first permanent settlement of the Númenóreans in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> From here Aldarion's [Guild of Venturers](../G/Guild_of_Venturers.md) began harvesting the local timber for the shipbuilding industry of Númenor. It was one of their primary shipyards, fed with their reckless cutting of trees.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Within a few centuries, the deforestation of the outlying regions [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md) and [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md) became cataclysmic, angering the [local native population](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md), and they began to fight back with increasing ferocity, destroying the haven several times, and frequently burning the great wood-stores in "hit-and-run" attacks.<sup id="cite_ref-6d_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6d-6">[6]</a></sup>

By the time of the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md) in the middle of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), this 'New Haven' had become very well established, and its name had shifted over the intervening one thousand years to **Lond Daer**, the "Great Haven". As such, it was one of the two beachheads of the Númenórean forces in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) that were used to support the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1700](../S/Second_Age_1700.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Its shipyards were starved of timber following the war, as the forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) had burnt almost all of what remained in Enedwaith and Minhiriath. Once its shipbuilding yards were no longer practicable, it declined in importance, and the Númenórean hunger for ever more wealth shifted focus to the establishment of newer havens to the south, [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) and [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md). Following this, the haven was now one of several major ports in Middle-earth, and was known as Lond Daer Enedh **(**also spelt Ened**)**, or "Great Middle Haven". Despite this, Númenor still maintained traffic on the [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md) as far as the city of [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6d_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6d-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vinyalondë* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "New Haven",<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-1">[1]</a></sup> clearly a compound of *[vinya](Vinya.md)* ("new") + *[londë](../L/Londë.md)* ("haven").

Its second name, *Lond Daer Enedh* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Great Middle Haven",<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup><sup>:264</sup> composed of *[lond](../L/Lond.md)* ("haven"), <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Daer&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Daer (page does not exist)">daer</a></i> ("great, large") and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Enedh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Enedh (page does not exist)">enedh</a></i> ("middle").
