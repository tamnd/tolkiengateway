**Voronwë** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). He accompanied [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) when he was fleeing from the [destruction of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-Seventy_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seventy-1">[1]</a></sup> [Wilfrid Trewin Jeremy](/wiki/Wilfrid_Trewin_Jeremy), a member of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club) was said to be his descendant.<sup id="cite_ref-Seventy_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seventy-1">[1]</a></sup>
