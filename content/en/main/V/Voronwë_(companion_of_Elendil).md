**Voronwë** was a [Man](../M/Man.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). He accompanied [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) when he was fleeing from the [destruction of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Seventy_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seventy-1">[1]</a></sup> [Wilfrid Trewin Jeremy](../W/Wilfrid_Trewin_Jeremy.md), a member of the [Notion Club](../N/Notion_Club.md) was said to be his descendant.<sup id="cite_ref-Seventy_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seventy-1">[1]</a></sup>
