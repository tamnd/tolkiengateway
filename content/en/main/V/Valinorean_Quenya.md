**Valinorean Quenya** was a dialect of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) spoken by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) who stayed in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). It has some differences with [Exilic](/wiki/Exilic_Quenya "Exilic Quenya"), or Noldorin Quenya, but they seem to be only phonetical.

## History {#History}

This dialect was spoken primarily by those [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) that remained in the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands) following the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). These Elves were the kin and followers of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), who had broken away from the followers of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and chose to return to Valinor rather than undertake the journey to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Finarfin and his folk were dismayed by the [Kinslaying](/wiki/First_Kinslaying "First Kinslaying") that had taken place at [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) and by the subsequent [Doom of Mandos](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos) that had been pronounced upon the Noldor. They repented and begged forgiveness of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), who allowed them to remain in the Blessed Realm.

Sundered from their kinsfolk, the Noldor of Valinor came to speak a language that differed in subtle ways from the [Exilic Quenya](/wiki/Exilic_Quenya) spoken by the Noldor in Middle-earth. Finarfin's people continued to speak a form of Quenya that was closer to the original state of the language. Conversely, the language of the Noldorin Exiles began to evolve and change. It also underwent standardization when it became a language of lore due to its ban from everyday use by the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") king [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).

The Quenya that appears in the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch) is Exilic Quenya. As such, there are few official sources for Valinorean Quenya or the ways in which it differed. It is likewise unknown if Valinorean Quenya underwent further changes when the Exiled Noldor returned to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) after the First Age or when various Noldor were slain and became re-embodied in Valinor.

Although Valinorean is considered a dialect of the Quenya language, it also acquired separate dialects of its own. [Vanyarin](/wiki/Vanyarin) is perhaps the most prominent dialect of Valinorean Quenya. This form of the language was spoken by the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), another clan of Elves in the Undying Lands. It contained many loanwords from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin), due to the Vanyar living in proximity to the Valar.

## Context and creation {#Context_and_creation}

The Quenya language developed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and used in his writings, such as *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, was that of the Noldor in Middle-earth (later referred to as Exilic Quenya). Tolkien indicated that the Valinorean form of Quenya differed, but he did not develop a full syntax or wordlist for this dialect.
