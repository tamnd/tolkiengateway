The **Valaróma** was the horn of the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), the Huntsman of the Valar.

## History {#History}

Upon first settling in [Aman](/wiki/Aman), Oromë would often use Valaróma in the woods of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) to train his followers against [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor). It was said that Valaróma could be heard above all other horns, for its sound was like lightning cleaving the clouds,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> echoing the mountains, and forcing [shadows](/wiki/Shadow "Shadow") to flee, even causing [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) to quail in [Utumno](/wiki/Utumno).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After Melkor and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) [killed](/wiki/Darkening_of_Valinor "Darkening of Valinor") the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), Oromë tried to blow Valaróma to disperse the [Cloud of Ungoliant](/wiki/Unlight "Unlight"), only to be met with disappointment when the sound faltered and failed him.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), when the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) heard Valaróma, they knew that once again the hunter was pursuing the servants of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valaróma* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), and contains *rom-* ("horn noise"), same as in *Oromë*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> An early name was *Rombaras*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
