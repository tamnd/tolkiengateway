The **Valaróma** was the horn of the [Vala](Valar.md) [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), the Huntsman of the Valar.

## History {#History}

Upon first settling in [Aman](../A/Aman.md), Oromë would often use Valaróma in the woods of [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) in [Valinor](Valinor.md) to train his followers against [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md). It was said that Valaróma could be heard above all other horns, for its sound was like lightning cleaving the clouds,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> echoing the mountains, and forcing [shadows](../S/Shadow.md) to flee, even causing [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) to quail in [Utumno](../U/Utumno.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After Melkor and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) [killed](../D/Darkening_of_Valinor.md) the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md), Oromë tried to blow Valaróma to disperse the [Cloud of Ungoliant](../U/Unlight.md), only to be met with disappointment when the sound faltered and failed him.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), when the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) heard Valaróma, they knew that once again the hunter was pursuing the servants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valaróma* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), and contains *rom-* ("horn noise"), same as in *Oromë*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> An early name was *Rombaras*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
