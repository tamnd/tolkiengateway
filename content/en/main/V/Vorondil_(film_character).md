**Vorondil**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a [Man of Gondor](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") and a member of [the Gondorian Military Forces](/wiki/Gondorian_Military_Forces "Gondorian Military Forces"). He equipped himself with armour, a helmet and a spear.

Vorondil is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* film.
