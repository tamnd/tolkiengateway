**Val Mayerik** (born [29 March](/wiki/29_March) [1950](/wiki/1950)) is an American artist known for illustrating [Conan the Barbarian](/wiki/Robert_E._Howard "Robert E. Howard") and Howard the Duck comics.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.
