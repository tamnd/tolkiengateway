**Val Mayerik** (born [29 March](../_/29_March.md) [1950](../_/1950.md)) is an American artist known for illustrating [Conan the Barbarian](../R/Robert_E._Howard.md) and Howard the Duck comics.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.
