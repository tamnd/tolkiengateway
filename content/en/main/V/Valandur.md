**Valandur** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [462](../T/Third_Age_462.md)<sup id="cite_ref-elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>-[652](../T/Third_Age_652.md),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> age 190 years) was the eighth [King of Arnor](../K/King_of_Arnor.md).

## History {#History}

In [552](../T/Third_Age_552.md) he had a son, Elendur.<sup id="cite_ref-elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> He succeeded his father, [Tarondor](<../T/Tarondor_(King_of_Arnor).md>), to the throne in [602](../T/Third_Age_602.md).

Little record remained of his reign of fifty years, except that it was cut short in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [652](../T/Third_Age_652.md) when he died prematurely. He was the first and only King of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) after [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) who died violently.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

He was succeeded by his son, [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>), upon his death.<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valandur* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Servant of the Vala" and is a compound of *[vala](Valar.md)* ("Vala") and *[-(n)dur](../_/-ndur.md)* ("servant").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
