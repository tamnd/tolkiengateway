**Voronwë Aranwion** was a mariner of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).

## History {#History}

Voronwë was affiliated with the [House of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin) through his father, [Aranwë](/wiki/Aranw%C3%AB), and to [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) through his mother, who was one of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) Elves of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas).

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"), following the [breaking of the Siege of Angband](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach "Dagor Bragollach"), [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon), the King of Gondolin, sent out a few of his people on a secret errand; they went down to the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) where they built ships. It accomplished little, however, as the Noldor lacked the art of ship-building, and they were only able to go so far as the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar) where they established lonely dwellings.<sup id="cite_ref-tcg_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tcg-4">[3]</a></sup>

After Turgon learned of the [ravaging of the Falas](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas "Fall of the Falas"), and of Círdan's relocation of a remnant of his people to the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar), he sent further messengers, including Voronwě.<sup id="cite_ref-tcg_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tcg-4">[3]</a></sup>

Voronwë delayed on the road, tarrying in [Nan-tathren](/wiki/Nan-tathren) and because of this he was the final one to embark on the last ship that Círdan, at the request of Turgon, had built for them. After sailing for seven years without reaching the lands in the West, his ship attempted to return to Middle-earth defeated. In a storm within sight of the coast the ship was wrecked and all save him were drowned. By the grace of Ulmo, Voronwë was saved from the wreck and washed ashore in [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), near [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar). He was sitting at its wall when he encountered [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) and heard his story as the messenger of Ulmo and led him back to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) he was ordered by Tuor to guard [Idril](/wiki/Idril). He escaped the sack.

It is said that he may have left Middle-earth with Tuor and Idril.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Voronwë* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Steadfast"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> or "steadfastness".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-7">[6]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) cognate for *Voronwë* was <i><b>Bronwë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-7">[6]</a></sup>

His epithet <i><b>Aranwion</b></i> was his patronymic, meaning simply "Son of [Aranwë](/wiki/Aranw%C3%AB)".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, his [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name *Voronwë* meant "the Faithful", but he was mostly known in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) <i><b>Bronweg</b></i> ("the Constant One").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> As in later works, he is the guide of Tuor, but also the father of [Littleheart](/wiki/Littleheart) – a figure that does not recur elsewhere.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2Gondolin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2Gondolin-10">[9]</a></sup>

According to the earlier and most accounts, he sailed with [Eärendel](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendel) on his voyages, not Tuor.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-11">[10]</a></sup>
