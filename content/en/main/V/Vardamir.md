**Vardamir Nólimon** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvardamir ˈnoːlimɔn]</span>) was the brief second [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Vardamir_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vardamir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

As the eldest of the four children of [Elros Tar-Minyatur](/wiki/Elros "Elros"),<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup> Vardamir was the heir to the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor). He was called **Nólimon**, because his chief love was for ancient lore gathered from [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men).<sup id="cite_ref-Vardamir_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vardamir-1">[1]</a></sup> Throughout his life, Vardamir had four children: three sons, [Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil "Tar-Amandil"), [Aulendil](</wiki/Aulendil_(son_of_Vardamir)> "Aulendil (son of Vardamir)"), and [Nolondil](/wiki/Nolondil); and one daughter, [Vardilmë](/wiki/Vardilm%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-earlier_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-earlier-2">[2]</a></sup>

Upon the death of Elros, the Sceptre of Númenor fell to Vardamir. However, due to the extraordinary length of Elros' life, Vardamir was 381 years old at the time in which he inherited the Scepter - old even for a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") of royal blood. Instead of ascending the throne, Vardamir immediately passed on the Sceptre to his eldest son [Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil "Tar-Amandil"), who would rule as **Tar-Amandil**. Probably because of this decision, unlike all the rulers of Númenor who took royal titles in Quenya, the prefix *tar-* ("high") was not added to his name. Despite this decision, Vardamir was still officially counted as the second King of Númenor, being deemed to have ruled nominally for a single year.<sup id="cite_ref-Vardamir_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vardamir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Vardamir's abdication of the Scepter to his son Amandil, began the custom wherein which future rulers of Númenor would pass on the Scepter to their [heir](/wiki/King%27s_Heir "King's Heir") before dying of their own free will while still being in good health and mind. This tradition lasted for many generations until the reign of [Tar-Atanamir](/wiki/Tar-Atanamir).<sup id="cite_ref-Vardamir_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vardamir-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vardamir* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it probably means "Varda-jewel". It is probably a compound of *[Varda](/wiki/Varda)* ("the name of the Valië of the stars, the spouse of Manwë", "exalted", "lofty", "sublime") and *[mírë](/wiki/M%C3%ADr%C3%AB "Mírë")* ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

His sobriquet *Nólimon* is Quenya. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Learned One". It seems to be a compound of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C3%B3l%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nólë (page does not exist)">nólë</a></i> ("lore, knowledge") and the agental suffix *[-mo](/wiki/-mo)* ("one", "anyone", "someone", "somebody").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a text written around [1959](/wiki/1959) and published in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, it is said that either the slow maturity of the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven), or the "choice of kindred" extended to the second generation, suggesting that Vardamir, like [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen), was among the Half-elven.<sup id="cite_ref-Nature_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:78</sup> In the same text, Vardamir was said to have lived to be 391, apparently being ninety-one years older than the usual [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) age of 300.<sup id="cite_ref-Nature_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:79</sup>
