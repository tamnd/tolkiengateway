**Voronwë** is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Steadfast" and may refer to:

- [Mardil Voronwë](../M/Mardil.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1960](../T/Third_Age_1960.md) - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2080](../T/Third_Age_2080.md)), the first [Ruling Steward](../R/Ruling_Steward.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).
