**Vivendi** is a French media conglomerate, owner of [Sierra Entertainment](/wiki/Sierra_Entertainment) and for a short time also of [Houghton Mifflin](/wiki/Houghton_Mifflin). The former branch concerned with interactive multimedia, **Vivendi Games**, published several [video games](/wiki/Category:Games "Category:Games") on the license of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, before the book rights went to [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts):

- [2002](/wiki/2002): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")*
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)")*
- [2004](/wiki/2004): *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)*

In 2008, Vivendi Games was merged with Activision, to create Activision Blizzard.
