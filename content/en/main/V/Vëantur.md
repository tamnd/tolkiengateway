**Vëantur** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[veˈantur]</span>) was the [Captain of the King's Ships](../C/Captain_of_the_King's_Ships.md) during the reign of [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md), the fourth [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).

## History {#History}

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md) Vëantur commanded the *[Entulessë](../E/Entulessë.md)* or "*Return*", the first [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) ship to venture back to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He sailed into the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md) and became acquainted with [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the Elven King [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), and [twelve Men](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) from [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) who came to see those "who had returned from death in the deeps of the Sea".<sup id="cite_ref-Note3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note3-1">[1]</a></sup>

Vëantur's daughter, [Almarian](../A/Almarian.md), married [Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md), who would be the fifth King of Númenor, and bore him a son, [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md). Vëantur greatly loved his grandson and welcomed him often to his house, on the southern side of the [firth of Rómenna](../F/Firth_of_Rómenna.md), where Aldarion learned how to row, to sail, and then to captain his own ship.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [725](../S/Second_Age_725.md), when Aldarion was twenty-five years old, the aging Vëantur invited him to sail to Middle-earth to meet Círdan and Gil-galad. Aldarion obtained Meneldur's permission and in the great ship *[Númerrámar](../N/Númerrámar.md)* or "West-wings", Vëantur and Aldarion proceeded to journey to the eastern lands on a voyage that lasted two years.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vëantur* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) suggests it means either "Sea-lord" or "Vigorous lord" and is a compound of either <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Vea&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vea (page does not exist)">vea</a></i> ("sea") or *vea* ("adult", "manly", "vigorous") and the suffixal form *-(n)tur* of *[tur](../T/Tur.md)* ("master", "lord", "ruler").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
