**Vëantur** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[veˈantur]</span>) was the [Captain of the King's Ships](/wiki/Captain_of_the_King%27s_Ships) during the reign of [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), the fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).

## History {#History}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600") Vëantur commanded the *[Entulessë](/wiki/Entuless%C3%AB)* or "*Return*", the first [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") ship to venture back to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He sailed into the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) and became acquainted with [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the Elven King [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), and [twelve Men](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") from [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) who came to see those "who had returned from death in the deeps of the Sea".<sup id="cite_ref-Note3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note3-1">[1]</a></sup>

Vëantur's daughter, [Almarian](/wiki/Almarian), married [Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur "Tar-Meneldur"), who would be the fifth King of Númenor, and bore him a son, [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion"). Vëantur greatly loved his grandson and welcomed him often to his house, on the southern side of the [firth of Rómenna](/wiki/Firth_of_R%C3%B3menna "Firth of Rómenna"), where Aldarion learned how to row, to sail, and then to captain his own ship.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [725](/wiki/Second_Age_725 "Second Age 725"), when Aldarion was twenty-five years old, the aging Vëantur invited him to sail to Middle-earth to meet Círdan and Gil-galad. Aldarion obtained Meneldur's permission and in the great ship *[Númerrámar](/wiki/N%C3%BAmerr%C3%A1mar)* or "West-wings", Vëantur and Aldarion proceeded to journey to the eastern lands on a voyage that lasted two years.<sup id="cite_ref-wife_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wife-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vëantur* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) suggests it means either "Sea-lord" or "Vigorous lord" and is a compound of either <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Vea&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vea (page does not exist)">vea</a></i> ("sea") or *vea* ("adult", "manly", "vigorous") and the suffixal form *-(n)tur* of *[tur](/wiki/Tur "Tur")* ("master", "lord", "ruler").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
