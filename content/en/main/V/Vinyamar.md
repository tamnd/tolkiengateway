**Vinyamar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvinjamar]</span>) was a settlement built by [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) at [Taras-ness](/wiki/Taras-ness) in [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast), on the slopes of [Mount Taras](/wiki/Mount_Taras),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> early in the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor). It was perched upon high terraces that looked towards the sea. It was never visited by the servants of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) but after its abandonment it became weathered. Turgon's great hall had its walls and roof covered with a deep growth of plants.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Vinyamar was the first recorded [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) stone construction after they had returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-3">[3]</a></sup> Vinyamar's population was primarily Noldorin, with a large influx of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") elves.

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [50](/wiki/First_Age_50 "First Age 50")<sup id="cite_ref-GA|74_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA|74-1">[1]</a></sup> Turgon and [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) took a journey along the river [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion). At the [Meres of Twilight](/wiki/Meres_of_Twilight) they slept and both received a dream from [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo), bidding them to seek places of strength.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Two years later<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Turgon discovered the hidden way and vale of [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen) wherein he determined to build [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [64](/wiki/First_Age_64 "First Age 64") Turgon led a third of his people to the valley to begin construction of his hidden city.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> By [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [116](/wiki/First_Age_116 "First Age 116") Gondolin was full-wrought and Turgon led the remainder of his folk to the city; from then on Vinyamar was abandoned and fell into decay.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Much later, in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495"), [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) came to the deserted settlement. Marvelling, he entered Turgon's old <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=High_Seat_of_Vinyamar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="High Seat of Vinyamar (page does not exist)">hall</a> and on a wall, lit by the setting sun, he found a shield, hauberk, helm, and sword. The shield bore a field of blue and a white swan's wing. Tuor took these items and wore them. In the meantime, [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) was rescued by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) from a shipwreck and was brought near the city; when the two met, they left Vinyamar together for a journey that led to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "New Home" from *[vinya](/wiki/Vinya "Vinya")* "young, new" and *[már](/wiki/M%C3%A1r "Már")* "house, home, dwelling".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
