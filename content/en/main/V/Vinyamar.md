**Vinyamar** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvinjamar]</span>) was a settlement built by [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) at [Taras-ness](../T/Taras-ness.md) in [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md), on the slopes of [Mount Taras](../M/Mount_Taras.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> early in the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md). It was perched upon high terraces that looked towards the sea. It was never visited by the servants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) but after its abandonment it became weathered. Turgon's great hall had its walls and roof covered with a deep growth of plants.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Vinyamar was the first recorded [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) stone construction after they had returned to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-3">[3]</a></sup> Vinyamar's population was primarily Noldorin, with a large influx of [Sindarin](../S/Sindar.md) elves.

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [50](../F/First_Age_50.md)<sup id="cite_ref-GA|74_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA|74-1">[1]</a></sup> Turgon and [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) took a journey along the river [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). At the [Meres of Twilight](../M/Meres_of_Twilight.md) they slept and both received a dream from [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), bidding them to seek places of strength.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Two years later<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Turgon discovered the hidden way and vale of [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md) wherein he determined to build [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [64](../F/First_Age_64.md) Turgon led a third of his people to the valley to begin construction of his hidden city.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> By [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [116](../F/First_Age_116.md) Gondolin was full-wrought and Turgon led the remainder of his folk to the city; from then on Vinyamar was abandoned and fell into decay.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Much later, in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md), [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) came to the deserted settlement. Marvelling, he entered Turgon's old <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=High_Seat_of_Vinyamar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="High Seat of Vinyamar (page does not exist)">hall</a> and on a wall, lit by the setting sun, he found a shield, hauberk, helm, and sword. The shield bore a field of blue and a white swan's wing. Tuor took these items and wore them. In the meantime, [Voronwë](Voronwë.md) was rescued by [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) from a shipwreck and was brought near the city; when the two met, they left Vinyamar together for a journey that led to Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "New Home" from *[vinya](Vinya.md)* "young, new" and *[már](../M/Már.md)* "house, home, dwelling".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
