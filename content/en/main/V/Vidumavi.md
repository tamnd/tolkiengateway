**Vidumavi**, also known as **Galadwen**,<sup id="cite_ref-PMA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMA-5">[3]</a></sup> was the daughter of [Vidugavia](/wiki/Vidugavia), the [King of Rhovanion](/wiki/King_of_Rhovanion).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-6">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1248](/wiki/Third_Age_1248 "Third Age 1248") Vidugavia, who was the most powerful of the Northern princes and called himself King of Rhovanion, helped [Minalcar](/wiki/Minalcar), the prince and [Regent of Gondor](/wiki/Regent_of_Gondor) in a war against the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-6">[4]</a></sup>

Two years later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1250](/wiki/Third_Age_1250 "Third Age 1250"), Minalcar sent his son, prince [Valacar](/wiki/Valacar), to live for a while with Vidugavia as an ambassador to learn the language, manners and policies of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen). Valacar loved the Northern lands and people and married Vidumavi, the daugther of Vidugavia, during his stay in Rhovanion.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-6">[4]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1255](/wiki/Third_Age_1255 "Third Age 1255")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[1]</a></sup> Vidumavi gave birth to a son, who was named *Vinitharya* in the language of his mother.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-6">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1260](/wiki/Third_Age_1260 "Third Age 1260") Minalcar recalled Valacar back to Gondor to take part in the councils of the realm and the command of its armies. Vidumavi was brought by her husband to Gondor with her children and a household of noble men and women from Rhovanion. They were welcomed in Gondor and Vinitharya was given the name [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") for public use in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and Vidumavi agreed to be called Galadwen, a translation of her name into [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-PMA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMA-5">[3]</a></sup>

Vidumavi learned the language and manners of Gondor well. Vidumavi was fair, noble and of high courage, which she passed on to her children.<sup id="cite_ref-PMA_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMA-5">[3]</a></sup> Although Vidumavi was short-lived compared to the longer lifespan of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain),<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-6">[4]</a></sup> she died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1332](/wiki/Third_Age_1332 "Third Age 1332") at a great age compared to the lifespan of her own people<sup id="cite_ref-PMA_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMA-5">[3]</a></sup> before Valacar became king.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) suggests that *Vidumavi* is a Latinized spelling of the [Gothic](/wiki/Gothic) name *Widumawi*, which means "wood-maiden".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup> [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith) proposes that the name is a combination of the Gothic elements <i>*widus</i> ("wood", "forest") and *mawi* ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>

*Galadwen* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name with the same meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-PMA_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMA-5">[3]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests that it is a compound of *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")* ("tree") and the suffixal form of *[gwend](/wiki/-wen "-wen")* ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup>
