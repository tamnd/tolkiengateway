The **Valarindi** were the **Children of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)** in the earliest phases of the mythology, especially in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. In the later versions the concept was abandoned and some of the Valarindi characters survived as [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar). The Quenya name is attested for the last time in *[The Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The term Valarindi has also been used to denote a fictive kinship relation between the Valar and the lesser spirits, as exampled in *[The Earliest Annals of Valinor](/wiki/The_Earliest_Annals_of_Valinor)*: *"With them came many lesser spirits, their children, or beings of their own kind but of less might; these are the Valarindi."*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## List of Valarindi {#List_of_Valarindi}

- [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Varda](/wiki/Varda): [Fionwë](/wiki/Fionw%C3%AB), [Erinti](/wiki/Ilmar%C3%AB "Ilmarë")
- [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna): [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), [Nessa](/wiki/Nessa)
- Oromë and [Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na): [Nielíqui](/wiki/Niel%C3%ADqui)
- [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) and Nessa: [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar)
- [Melko](/wiki/Melko) and the [ogress](/wiki/Ogres "Ogres") [Ulbandi](/wiki/Ulbandi): [Kalimbo](/wiki/Kalimbo)/<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Gothmog" title="Gothmog">Kosomot</a>

- [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar) was named among the sons of the Gods in both the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the *[Quenta Silmarillion](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)> "Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)")* of 1937,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> although it was later removed along with the concept.
- [Melian](/wiki/Melian) was named as "a child of [Lórien](/wiki/Irmo "Irmo")".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:note 10</sup>
