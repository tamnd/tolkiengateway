**Valimar** or **Valmar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. [N.](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin") <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvalimar]</span>, [V.](/wiki/Vanyarin "Vanyarin") <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈβalimar]</span>) was the city of many bells in the heart of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) where many of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) dwelt. It lay on the [Girdle of Arda](/wiki/Girdle_of_Arda) in the east-central part of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Plains_of_Valinor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Plains of Valinor (page does not exist)">Plains of Valinor</a>, west of [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor) grew nearby on the mound of [Ezellohar](/wiki/Ezellohar). It is said that the houses in Valimar had roofs of gold and their floors silver and their doors were of polished bronze; and that they were lifted with spells and their stones were bound with magic.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Valimar* means "dwelling of the Valar" (from *[Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar")* and *[mar](/wiki/-mbar "-mbar")* = "dwelling, abode").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
