<i><b>vanwa</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) adjective meaning "lost".<sup id="cite_ref-WAN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WAN-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](/wiki/Root) <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=WAN&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="WAN (page does not exist)">WAN</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-WAN_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WAN-1">[1]</a></sup> adjetivation from *[auta-](/wiki/Auta- "Auta-")*.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mar_Vanwa_Tyali%C3%A9va" title="Mar Vanwa Tyaliéva">Mar <b>Vanwa</b> Tyaliéva</a></i> = "The Cottage of Lost Play"
- <i>Si <b>vanwa</b> ná, Rómello <b>vanwa</b>, Valimar!</i> = "Now **lost**, **lost** to those of the East is Valimar!"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
