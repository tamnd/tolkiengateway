<i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Valier" title="Valier">Valier</a> - Queens of the <a href="/wiki/Valar" title="Valar">Valar</a></b></i> is a song by the epic metal band [Battlelore](/wiki/Battlelore). It appears in their third full-length album, [Third Age of the Sun](/wiki/Third_Age_of_the_Sun).
