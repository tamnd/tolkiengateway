<i><b>Vinge</b></i> is a noun meaning "foam" or "spray" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), derived from [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) <i><b>wingē</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>*.* Tolkien vacillated greatly on the form of this word, and so it is also found as <b><i>winga</i>, <i>vinga</i>,</b> and <i><b>winge,</b></i> and with stem form <b><i>vingi-</i>.</b><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> It is cognates with *[gwing](../G/Gwing.md)* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

Example:

- [Vingilótë](Vingilótë.md) = "Foam-flower"
