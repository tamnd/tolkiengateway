**Valandil** means "Devoted to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar)" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[-ndil](/wiki/-ndil)*, "friend, lover, devoted to") and may refer to:

- [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)") (born [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [630](/wiki/Second_Age_630 "Second Age 630")), the first [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").
- [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)") ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [249](/wiki/Third_Age_249 "Third Age 249")), youngest son of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and third [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).
- [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(The_Lost_Road)> "Valandil (The Lost Road)"), the father or brother of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) in the earlier stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

- [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Valandil (The Rings of Power)"), an original character created for the television series *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.
