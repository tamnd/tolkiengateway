<i><b>Víga-Glúms Saga</b></i> contains an annotated edition of the Old Norse text of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V%C3%ADga-Gl%C3%BAms_saga" title="wikipedia:Víga-Glúms saga">Víga-Glúms Saga</a>, together with a glossary and introduction.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

This is volume one in the Oxford English Monographs series. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was one of the General Editors for the series. In the introduction Turville-Petre says: "*It would be difficult to overestimate all that I owe to Professor Tolkien; his sympathy and encouragement have been constant and, throughout the work, I have had the benefit of his wide scholarship.*"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
