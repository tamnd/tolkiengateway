<i><b>vanua</b></i> in [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) means "gone, lost, no longer to be had, vanished, departed, dead, past and over"; past participle of the verb *[auta-](../A/Auta-.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-WJA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJA-1">[1]</a></sup>
