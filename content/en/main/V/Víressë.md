**Víressë** was the name for the month of the year (*[asta](../A/Asta.md)*) that followed the month [Súlimë](../S/Súlimë.md) and preceded the month [Lótessë](../L/Lótessë.md). It was the fourth month in the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md); in the latter calendar, it was preceded by the feast-day of [Tuilérë](../T/Tuilérë.md). After the introduction of [New Reckoning](../N/New_Reckoning.md), Víressë became the first month.<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Víressë* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Astron](../A/Astron.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [23 March](../_/23_March.md) through [21 April](../_/21_April.md)).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

This [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name is related to words such as <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Virya&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Virya (page does not exist)">virya</a></i> "fresh" and perhaps derives from an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) root having to do with freshness or youth.<sup id="cite_ref-arda_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-arda-2">[2]</a></sup> Víressë was the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name inherited from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) and traditionally used by the [Westron](../W/Westron.md)-speaking peoples. The [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) themselves however used the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name of the month, [Gwirith](../G/Gwirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As the Númenorean calendars seem to be based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Republican_Calendar" title="wikipedia:French Republican Calendar">French Republican Calendar</a>, the name and meaning of the month corresponds to the month <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germinal_(French_Republican_Calendar)" title="wikipedia:Germinal (French Republican Calendar)">Germinal</a>, meaning "seed-time".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
