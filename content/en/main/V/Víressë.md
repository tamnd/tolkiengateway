**Víressë** was the name for the month of the year (*[asta](/wiki/Asta "Asta")*) that followed the month [Súlimë](/wiki/S%C3%BAlim%C3%AB) and preceded the month [Lótessë](/wiki/L%C3%B3tess%C3%AB). It was the fourth month in the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning); in the latter calendar, it was preceded by the feast-day of [Tuilérë](/wiki/Tuil%C3%A9r%C3%AB). After the introduction of [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning), Víressë became the first month.<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Víressë* in the Kings' Reckoning (and for the most part in the Stewards' Reckoning) corresponded to [Astron](/wiki/Astron) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [23 March](/wiki/23_March) through [21 April](/wiki/21_April)).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

This [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name is related to words such as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Virya&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Virya (page does not exist)">virya</a></i> "fresh" and perhaps derives from an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) root having to do with freshness or youth.<sup id="cite_ref-arda_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-arda-2">[2]</a></sup> Víressë was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name inherited from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and traditionally used by the [Westron](/wiki/Westron)-speaking peoples. The [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) themselves however used the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of the month, [Gwirith](/wiki/Gwirith).<sup id="cite_ref-app_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-app-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

As the Númenorean calendars seem to be based on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Republican_Calendar" title="wikipedia:French Republican Calendar">French Republican Calendar</a>, the name and meaning of the month corresponds to the month <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germinal_(French_Republican_Calendar)" title="wikipedia:Germinal (French Republican Calendar)">Germinal</a>, meaning "seed-time".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
