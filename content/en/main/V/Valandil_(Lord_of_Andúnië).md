**Valandil** was the son of [Elatan](/wiki/Elatan) of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) and [Silmariën](/wiki/Silmari%C3%ABn), the eldest daughter of [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil), the fourth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When the time came for Tar-Elendil to relinquish the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor) it did not pass on to Silmariën or her sister [Isilmë](/wiki/Isilm%C3%AB); instead it went to Tar-Elendil's third child [Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur "Tar-Meneldur").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

As Silmarien or Valandil could not become Kings of Númenor, Tar-Elendil instead made Valandil the first of the [Lords of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB), who ruled the city in [Andustar](/wiki/Andustar) in western [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) and were held in the highest honor after the Kings.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-6">[5]</a></sup> As a ruler of one of the divisions of Númenor Valandil was also a member of the [Council of the Sceptre](/wiki/Council_of_the_Sceptre),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> a position that was inherited by his descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-6">[5]</a></sup>

Valandil was a close cousin of [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") and twice hosted feasts for him in Andúnië, on the one hundredth anniversary of the founding of the [Guild of Venturers](/wiki/Guild_of_Venturers) and again in midsummer of [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [870](/wiki/Second_Age_870 "Second Age 870") when the newly wedded Aldarion and [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis) visited. Valandil once attempted to honor Erendis with the name *Uinéniel*, but Erendis rejected the title.<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

In time, one of the descendants of Valandil was [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup> the first [High King of the Dúnedain](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Elendil's son was [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and from his line, many generations later, came [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valandil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "Lover of the Valar".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> It is a compound of [vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") ("Vala") and [-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil") ("lover", "friend").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

It has the same meaning as the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name <i><b><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/%C3%93swine_(disambiguation)" title="Óswine (disambiguation)">Óswine</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

<li id="cite_note-4"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-4">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">The royal law of succession at that time (actually an unquestioned inherited custom) stated that the Sceptre was to be inherited by the Ruler&#39;s eldest son.<br/>In <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Aldarion_and_Erendis" title="Aldarion and Erendis">Aldarion and Erendis</a></i> we learn that later, Tar-Meneldur&#39;s son <a href="/wiki/Tar-Aldarion" title="Tar-Aldarion">Tar-Aldarion</a> altered the law so that the eldest <i>child</i> of the King, whether man or woman, would receive the Sceptre. Had this law existed when Tar-Elendil relinquished the Sceptre, Silmariën would have been the first <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ruling_Queen" title="Ruling Queen">Ruling Queen</a> and Valandil and his descendants would have been the rulers of Númenor.</span>
