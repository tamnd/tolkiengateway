In its singular form the word *vala* is also the name in Quenya of the twenty-second letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the second letter of the sixth grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "semi-vocalic" consonants and it is the sixth letter in the labial or *p*-series of consonants, the *[Parmatéma](../P/Parmatéma.md)*. In written Quenya, since *[Ampa](../A/Ampa.md)* (letter 14) is used for **MP**, *Vala* is used for **V**. In other modes (where *Ampa* is used for **V**) *Vala* represents **W**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](../M/Mode_of_Beleriand.md) this letter represents **M**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Wí</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
