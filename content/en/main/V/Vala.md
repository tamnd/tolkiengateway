In its singular form the word *vala* is also the name in Quenya of the twenty-second letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the second letter of the sixth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "semi-vocalic" consonants and it is the sixth letter in the labial or *p*-series of consonants, the *[Parmatéma](/wiki/Parmat%C3%A9ma)*. In written Quenya, since *[Ampa](/wiki/Ampa)* (letter 14) is used for **MP**, *Vala* is used for **V**. In other modes (where *Ampa* is used for **V**) *Vala* represents **W**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand) this letter represents **M**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Wí</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
