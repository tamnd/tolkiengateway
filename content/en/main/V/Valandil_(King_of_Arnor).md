**Valandil** ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup> – [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [249](/wiki/Third_Age_249 "Third Age 249")<sup id="cite_ref-north_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-north-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the third [King of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor"), succeeding his father, [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), after he and Valandil's older brothers were killed in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2").

## History {#History}

Valandil was the youngest of the four sons of Isildur. When his mother was still pregnant, the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) was to begin, and Isildur sent her to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) for safety.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Valandil was born there,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in the same year that the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men) was formed. He was only one year old, remaining with his mother in Rivendell, when the host of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) marched forth.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>

Before he was slain, Isildur became briefly king of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). While he had been High King of both realms, he committed the rule of Gondor to his nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> who would become [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor"). With the death of his father and brothers in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), Valandil became heir to the throne of Arnor. However, since Valandil was only 13 years old, he would wait until he was 21 to become the king (in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [10](/wiki/Third_Age_10 "Third Age 10")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>

He ruled longer than any other king in the North or South kingdoms - 239 years. When he died in [249](/wiki/Third_Age_249 "Third Age 249"), he was succeeded by his son, [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eldacar (King of Arnor)").<sup id="cite_ref-north_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-north-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Valandil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. It means "Lover of the Valar".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It is a compound of [vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") ("Vala") and [-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil") ("lover", "friend").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It is likely that he was named after his ancestor [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Valandil (Lord of Andúnië)"), the first [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").
