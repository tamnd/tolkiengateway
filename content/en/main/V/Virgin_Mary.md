The **Virgin Mary** is a key figure in [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity). As a devout Roman Catholic, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) had a special devotion towards her, to the point of stating that all his "perception of beauty both in majesty and simplicity" was founded upon her.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Her inspiration in Tolkien's work is reflected by poems, translations of prayers, and character development.

    - *[Aia María](/wiki/Aia_Mar%C3%ADa)*

    - *[Litany of Loreto](/wiki/Litany_of_Loreto)*

- [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) (originally modeled after Mary, a connection that Tolkien acknowledged but would later veer from)
