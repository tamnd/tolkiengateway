The **Virgin Mary** is a key figure in [Christianity](../C/Christianity.md). As a devout Roman Catholic, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) had a special devotion towards her, to the point of stating that all his "perception of beauty both in majesty and simplicity" was founded upon her.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Her inspiration in Tolkien's work is reflected by poems, translations of prayers, and character development.
