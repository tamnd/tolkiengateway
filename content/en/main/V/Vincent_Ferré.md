**Vincent Ferré** (born [23 October](../_/23_October.md) [1974](../_/1974.md)) is a French [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) scholar, lecturer and translator. He has written numerous books on the subject of Tolkien and medievalism, contributed articles to the *[Hither Shore](<../H/Hither_Shore_(journal).md>)* and the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), and runs his own dedicated Tolkien website <a class="external text" href="http://www.pourtolkien.fr/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Pour Tolkien</a>. Professionally, he is a Professor of General and Comparative Literature at the Sorbonne Nouvelle University. He is credited on the <a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkienestate.com/en/paths/credits.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien Estate website</a> as having written several articles and supervised translations.
