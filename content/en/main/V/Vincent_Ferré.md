**Vincent Ferré** (born [23 October](/wiki/23_October) [1974](/wiki/1974)) is a French [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") scholar, lecturer and translator. He has written numerous books on the subject of Tolkien and medievalism, contributed articles to the *[Hither Shore](</wiki/Hither_Shore_(journal)> "Hither Shore (journal)")* and the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), and runs his own dedicated Tolkien website <a class="external text" href="http://www.pourtolkien.fr/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Pour Tolkien</a>. Professionally, he is a Professor of General and Comparative Literature at the Sorbonne Nouvelle University. He is credited on the <a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkienestate.com/en/paths/credits.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien Estate website</a> as having written several articles and supervised translations.
