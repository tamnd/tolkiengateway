**Valacirca** ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) "Sickle of the Valar") was one of the ancient [constellations](../C/Constellations.md) set in the heavens by [Varda](Varda.md) from the silver dews of [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md) to enlighten the awakening of the Elves. It was [seven stars](../S/Seven_Stars.md) set in the north as a challenge to [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> when [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) came to the [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md) (on the [Wood-elves](../N/Nandor.md)' raft) he noticed the Valacirca twinkling in the north above the entry of the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md) into the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [29 September](../_/29_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) looked out of the window in the hobbits' room in [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and saw the Valacirca bright above the shoulder of [Bree-hill](../B/Bree-hill.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

[Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) called it the *Burning Briar*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>. It was also referred to as the *Wain* in *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-3">[3]</a></sup> and the *Sickle* in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-5">[5]</a></sup>. The constellation is also known as *the Plough*, a name used in British English to refer to the seven brightest stars in *Ursa Major*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> known in American English as *Big Dipper*. It is unclear whether the Dwarvish constellation *[Durin's Crown](../D/Durin's_Crown.md)*, seen in the reflection of [Mirrormere](../M/Mirrormere.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> is the *Valacirca*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

This constellation is the first to appear in the mythology that [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) began to develop in the [1910s](../_/1910.md), giving it the name <i><b>Telpea Kalka</b></i> ("Silvern Sickle").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)* it is stated that "yet the Seven Stars were not set by Varda, being indeed the sparks from [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md)’s forge whose brightness in the ancient heavens urged Varda to make their rivals; yet this did she never achieve."<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:144</sup>

It is referred to as the "Silver Bear" in the [1915](../_/1915.md) poem *[Kortirion among the Trees](../T/The_Trees_of_Kortirion.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> but was later amended to "Silver Wain" in a revision submitted to Rayner Unwin in [1962](../_/1962.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, the Q(u)enya names for the constellation were *Valakirka* ("Sickle of the Gods") and <i><b>Otselen</b></i> ("Seven Stars"), with the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) cognates <i><b>Cerch iMbelain</b></i> and <i><b>Edegil</b></i> respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In the earliest map that Tolkien made for *The Hobbit* (referred to as Fimbulfambi's map by [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) in *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*) the compass rose used a tiny diagram of the Valacirca to indicate "North".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Valacirca is identified as the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Dipper" title="wikipedia:Big Dipper">Big Dipper</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
