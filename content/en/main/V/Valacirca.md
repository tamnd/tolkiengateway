**Valacirca** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") "Sickle of the Valar") was one of the ancient [constellations](/wiki/Constellations "Constellations") set in the heavens by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) from the silver dews of [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion) to enlighten the awakening of the Elves. It was [seven stars](/wiki/Seven_Stars "Seven Stars") set in the north as a challenge to [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> when [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) came to the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake) (on the [Wood-elves](/wiki/Nandor "Nandor")' raft) he noticed the Valacirca twinkling in the north above the entry of the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) into the lake.<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) looked out of the window in the hobbits' room in [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and saw the Valacirca bright above the shoulder of [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill).<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

[Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men) called it the *Burning Briar*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>. It was also referred to as the *Wain* in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*<sup id="cite_ref-Welcome_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Welcome-3">[3]</a></sup> and the *Sickle* in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*<sup id="cite_ref-Strider_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Strider-5">[5]</a></sup>. The constellation is also known as *the Plough*, a name used in British English to refer to the seven brightest stars in *Ursa Major*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> known in American English as *Big Dipper*. It is unclear whether the Dwarvish constellation *[Durin's Crown](/wiki/Durin%27s_Crown)*, seen in the reflection of [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> is the *Valacirca*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

This constellation is the first to appear in the mythology that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) began to develop in the [1910s](/wiki/1910 "1910"), giving it the name <i><b>Telpea Kalka</b></i> ("Silvern Sickle").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* it is stated that "yet the Seven Stars were not set by Varda, being indeed the sparks from [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB)’s forge whose brightness in the ancient heavens urged Varda to make their rivals; yet this did she never achieve."<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:144</sup>

It is referred to as the "Silver Bear" in the [1915](/wiki/1915) poem *[Kortirion among the Trees](/wiki/The_Trees_of_Kortirion "The Trees of Kortirion")*<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> but was later amended to "Silver Wain" in a revision submitted to Rayner Unwin in [1962](/wiki/1962).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, the Q(u)enya names for the constellation were *Valakirka* ("Sickle of the Gods") and <i><b>Otselen</b></i> ("Seven Stars"), with the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognates <i><b>Cerch iMbelain</b></i> and <i><b>Edegil</b></i> respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In the earliest map that Tolkien made for *The Hobbit* (referred to as Fimbulfambi's map by [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) in *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*) the compass rose used a tiny diagram of the Valacirca to indicate "North".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Valacirca is identified as the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Dipper" title="wikipedia:Big Dipper">Big Dipper</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
