<i><b>vakko</b></i> is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) noun meaning "jacket, coat".<sup id="cite_ref-GL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GL-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the form *vacco* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Goldogrin](/wiki/Gnomish "Gnomish") *Bacha* ("jacket, coat")<sup id="cite_ref-GL_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GL-1">[1]</a></sup>
