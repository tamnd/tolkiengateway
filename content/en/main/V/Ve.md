<i><b>ve</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "as" or "like".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Obviously it comes from earlier [PQ](../P/PQ.md) \**be* or \**bê* (see <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tambe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tambe (page does not exist)">tambe</a></i>).

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>Yéni únótimë <b>ve</b> rámar aldaron!</i> = "long years numberless **as** the wings of trees!"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
