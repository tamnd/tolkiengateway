**Valwë** was the father of [Lindo](../L/Lindo.md), the keeper of the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md) on [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Valw_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valw-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Valwë followed the [Vala](Valar.md) [Noldorin](../S/Salmar.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) back to the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md) after the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) in order to free the [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) who lived there under the thralldom of [Melko](../M/Melko.md). However, both Noldorin and Valwë were overwhelmed in the [Battle of Tasarinan](../B/Battle_of_Tasarinan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Valw_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valw-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Evidently he survived the battle, since it was mentioned afterwards by Lindo his son that he settled on [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) among other figures such as [Tulkastor](../T/Tulkastor.md) (the father of [Vairë](<Vairë_(wife_of_Lindo).md>)) and [Ingil](../I/Ingwion.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) (the son of [Inwë](../I/Ingwë.md#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium)).<sup id="cite_ref-Valw_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valw-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Valwë* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md). Its [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) equivalent is <i><b>Gwalweg</b></i>. [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) has suggested that both appear to mean "the happy (or fortunate) one".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Originally the name of the character was *Manwë*, though according to [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) that might have merely been a slip on his father's part.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
