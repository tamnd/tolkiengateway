**Valwë** was the father of [Lindo](/wiki/Lindo), the keeper of the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play) on [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Valw_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valw-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Valwë followed the [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") [Noldorin](/wiki/Salmar#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Salmar") back to the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) after the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) in order to free the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) who lived there under the thralldom of [Melko](/wiki/Melko). However, both Noldorin and Valwë were overwhelmed in the [Battle of Tasarinan](/wiki/Battle_of_Tasarinan).<sup id="cite_ref-Valw_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valw-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Evidently he survived the battle, since it was mentioned afterwards by Lindo his son that he settled on [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) among other figures such as [Tulkastor](/wiki/Tulkastor) (the father of [Vairë](</wiki/Vair%C3%AB_(wife_of_Lindo)> "Vairë (wife of Lindo)")) and [Ingil](/wiki/Ingwion#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Ingwion") (the son of [Inwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Ingwë")).<sup id="cite_ref-Valw_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Valw-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Valwë* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) equivalent is <i><b>Gwalweg</b></i>. [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) has suggested that both appear to mean "the happy (or fortunate) one".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Originally the name of the character was *Manwë*, though according to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) that might have merely been a slip on his father's part.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
