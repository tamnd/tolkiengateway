<i><b>Vinyar Tengwar</b></i>, or <i><b>VT</b></i> for short, is a linguistic journal published by the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship), dedicated to the scholarly study of the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

The name *Vinyar Tengwar* is a [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish") rendering of **"Newsletters"** as a loose compound of the neologism *vinyar* "news" (a noun plural from *[vinya](/wiki/Vinya "Vinya")* "new", compare *[vanya](/wiki/Vanya "Vanya")* "fair" and the clan of the *[Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar)*) and the plural of the word *[tengwa](/wiki/Tengwa "Tengwa")* "letter (of the alphabet)". Its accuracy is disputed, mostly due to the semantic difference between "letter of the alphabet" and "letter, mail, post".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

*Vinyar Tengwar* first appeared in [1988](/wiki/1988), at first edited by [Jorge Quiñónez](/wiki/Jorge_Qui%C3%B1%C3%B3nez) and later taken over by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter). It appeared in bimonthly intervals at first, but after July [1994](/wiki/1994), issues appeared more irregularly, roughly once a year. This coincided with the greater frequency of publication of texts written by Tolkien himself; the journal is now dedicated primarily to such texts. Several of these texts were mentioned in volumes of *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), but not published in that series owing to their specialist or recondite nature.

All 50 back issues are now collected and published in 5 volumes on print-on-demand.
