**Verna** was an obscure location in southwest [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) near the [Mouths of Sirion](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md).

[Arvernien](../A/Arvernien.md) was named after it.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Verna does not appear in narration or any maps of Beleriand; [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) appears to have invented the name in order to explain the etymology of *Arvernien*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Since Tolkien explains Arvernien as "Land beside Verna", geographically, Verna could refer to adjacent regions like the forest of [Nimbrethil](../N/Nimbrethil.md) or [Lisgardh](../L/Lisgardh.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Verna could be related to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[feren](../F/Feren.md)* which means "birch, beech tree", and if so, it could refer to the forest of Nimbrethil ("(forest of) silver birches").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Another possibility is that it refers to a river, obviously [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md), and comes from the [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=WER&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="WER (page does not exist)">WER</a></i>, "winding" (thus, "Winding River").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
