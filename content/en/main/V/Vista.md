**Vista** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvista]</span>) or **Wista** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈwista]</span>) was a part of the atmosphere that surrounded the world of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) before the cataclysm at the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

Vista was the lowest layer of the atmosphere, filled with normal breathable air: above it was [Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md), and above that [Vaiya](Vaiya.md). Vista was divided into two parts: The upper part was [Fanyamar](../F/Fanyamar.md), the home of clouds, and the lower part was [Aiwenórë](../A/Aiwenórë.md), the "land" of birds. Vista only lay upon [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and the seas; it was bounded by the [Mountains of Valinor](../P/Pelóri.md) in the West, the [Walls of the Sun](../G/Gates_of_Morning.md) in the East, and the [Walls of the World](../W/Walls_of_the_World.md) in the North and South.

The air in [Valinor](Valinor.md) is that of [Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md) which can only sustain the servants of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) or those to whom he has granted this ability. Thus the mortal creatures of Middle-earth were bound within the confines of Vista while they lived.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early schematic titled *[I Vene Kemen](../I/I_Vene_Kemen.md)*, the lowest layer of airs was marked as *Vilna* ([Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md)). <span></span>
