**Vista** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈvista]</span>) or **Wista** (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈwista]</span>) was a part of the atmosphere that surrounded the world of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) before the cataclysm at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

Vista was the lowest layer of the atmosphere, filled with normal breathable air: above it was [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen), and above that [Vaiya](/wiki/Vaiya). Vista was divided into two parts: The upper part was [Fanyamar](/wiki/Fanyamar), the home of clouds, and the lower part was [Aiwenórë](/wiki/Aiwen%C3%B3r%C3%AB), the "land" of birds. Vista only lay upon [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and the seas; it was bounded by the [Mountains of Valinor](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri "Pelóri") in the West, the [Walls of the Sun](/wiki/Gates_of_Morning "Gates of Morning") in the East, and the [Walls of the World](/wiki/Walls_of_the_World) in the North and South.

The air in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) is that of [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen) which can only sustain the servants of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) or those to whom he has granted this ability. Thus the mortal creatures of Middle-earth were bound within the confines of Vista while they lived.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early schematic titled *[I Vene Kemen](/wiki/I_Vene_Kemen)*, the lowest layer of airs was marked as *Vilna* ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya)). <span></span>
