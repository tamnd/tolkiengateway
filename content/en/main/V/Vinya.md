<i><b>vinya</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "young".<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>

A deleted entry (for a root GWIN) in the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") has the form *winya* ("new, fresh, young").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [WIN](/wiki/WIN)<sup id="cite_ref-VT47_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT47-1">[1]</a></sup>
