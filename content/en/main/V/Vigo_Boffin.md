**Vigo Boffin** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Vigo was the son of [Jago Boffin](../J/Jago_Boffin.md), and had a son [Folco](../F/Folco_Boffin.md). They were both present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
