**Valkotukka** was a minor character in [The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/The_Father_Christmas_Letters). He was a polar bear cub. Valkotukka had a brother named [Paksu](/wiki/Paksu), and he was the nephew of the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Valkotukka, along with Paksu, came for a visit one Christmas to visit their uncle and [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas). The two never left.

Valkotukka, just like his brother, was mischievous and naughty, to the extent that Father Christmas wrote of them; "they seem to be growing up just like their uncle."

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Valkotukka* (<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/valko-" title="wiktionary:valko-">valko-</a></i> + <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/tukka" title="wiktionary:tukka">tukka</a></i>) means "white-hair" in [Finnish](/wiki/Finnish).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
