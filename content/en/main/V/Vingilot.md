**Vingilot** was the ship in which [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) and [Elwing](../E/Elwing.md) sailed to [Aman](../A/Aman.md) to seek pardon and assistance from the [Valar](Valar.md). This vessel was said to be the fairest ever fashioned – it had white timbers of [birchwood](../B/Birches.md) from [Nimbrethil](../N/Nimbrethil.md), oars of gold, sails of silver,<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-1">[1]</a></sup> and its prow was swan-shaped.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) and the [Fall of the Falas](../F/Fall_of_the_Falas.md), the remnant of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) gathered about the [Havens of Sirion](../H/Havens_of_Sirion.md) where Eärendil was their lord. Eärendil sought to sail into the West to seek [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) and [Idril](../I/Idril.md) and to ask for pardon from the [Valar](Valar.md) for his people. For these purposes Vingilótë was built by Eärendil and [Círdan the Shipwright](../C/Círdan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

Eärendil sailed in Vingilótë while his wife Elwing waited by the mouths of [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md). Although he had many adventures he could not make his way to the shores of [Valinor](Valinor.md). Finally, having fearful dreams concerning Elwing, he hastened back to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). It was well that he hurried, for the sons of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md), having learned that Elwing possessed a [Silmaril](../S/Silmaril.md), made a sudden attack upon the exiles and refugees of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) and [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). Though [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md) won the battle they did not gain the Silmaril; Elwing cast herself into the sea with the jewel upon her breast.

[Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) lifted Elwing from the [sea](../B/Belegaer.md) and transformed her into a great white bird. In this guise she flew to Vingilótë and fell into a swoon, but in the morning regained her normal form. Eärendil and Elwing, with the mariners [Falathar](../F/Falathar.md), [Erellont](../E/Erellont.md), and [Aerandir](../A/Aerandir.md), turned westward and again sought Valinor. On this voyage Eärendil stood in the prow of Vingilótë with the Silmaril bound to his brow. The jewel shone ever brighter as they traveled west and by its light and power they pierced the [Shadowy Seas](../S/Shadowy_Seas.md).

Once in Valinor, the Vingilot was remade. Eärendil then flew through the skies in it and participated in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) where Eärendil, along with a host of great birds, gave battle to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s [dragons](../D/Dragon.md). Finally Eärendil slew [Ancalagon](../A/Ancalagon.md) and the host of the Valar prevailed.<sup id="cite_ref-Earendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Earendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

After this, Eärendil eternally sails the vessel with the Silmaril still upon his brow and he and his ship are called [Gil-Estel](../G/Gil-Estel.md), the Star of High Hope, and [Star of Eärendil](../S/Star_of_Eärendil.md).

## Appearance {#Appearance}

In Valinor, Vingilótë was hallowed, and became fair and marvelous;<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 2]</a></sup> it was made transparent so that the light of the Silmaril could be seen.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:Eärendil</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Vingilótë* means "Foam-flower" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), from *[winga](../W/Winga.md)* ("foam, spray") + *[lótë](../L/Lótë.md)* ("flower").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

*[Rothinzil](../R/Rothinzil.md)* is its cognate in [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

In a manuscript, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) considered the name *Eälótë*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>
