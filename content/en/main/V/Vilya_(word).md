<i><b>vilya</b></i> or <i><b>wilya</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "air, sky".<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- <i><a href="../N/Nieninquë.md" title="Nieninquë">yan i <b>wilyar</b> antar miquelis</a></i> = "to whom the air gives kisses"

## Tengwa {#Tengwa}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/V/Vilya.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/5/5c/Vilya.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Vilya* is also the name for the twenty-fourth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the fourth letter of the sixth grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "semi-vocalic" consonants and it is the sixth letter in the fourth series of consonants.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In early written Quenya this letter represented **W** when the letter was called *Wilya*. Over time this letter ceased to have a clear function. However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](../M/Mode_of_Beleriand.md) (as seen in the [West-gate](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) inscription) *Vilya* is used for a following **W** in the diphthong *iw*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name for this letter is '<i><b>Á</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), the word was *wilma* ("inner or lower air") and comes from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=WIL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="WIL (page does not exist)">WIL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[Ambarkanta](../A/Ambarkanta.md)*, there are different terms for the air of the atmosphere ([Vista](Vista.md)) and the sky ([Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md)).
