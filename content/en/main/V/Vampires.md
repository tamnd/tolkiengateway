**Vampires** were mysterious bat-like creatures in the service of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). The only vampire whose name is recorded in the annals of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) is [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s messenger, [Thuringwethil](/wiki/Thuringwethil),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but Sauron himself took the form of a vampire on at least one occasion, to flee [Huan](/wiki/Huan).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="/wiki/1989" title="1989">1989</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i> (<i><a href="/wiki/Dark_Mage_of_Rhudaur" title="Dark Mage of Rhudaur">Dark Mage of Rhudaur</a></i>):</b>

<dl><dd>Vampires are described as former <a href="/wiki/Maiar" title="Maiar">Maia</a> patrons of normal bats, but corrupted by Morgoth and transformed into demons appearing as man-sized bats with &#34;<i>the faces of hideous Men or Women</i>&#34;, having the ability to change shape.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> A related creature is also envisioned, the <i>Blood-wight</i>, which is an undead <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Mannish</a> shapechanger, draining blood from living beings.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2007" title="2007">2007</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A new creature created for the game, the Merrevail (singular, Morroval) appear to be based on Tolkien&#39;s vampires. They are fearsome bat-winged women with sharp talons. The males of the species are the Mervyl (singular, Morvul).</dd></dl>
