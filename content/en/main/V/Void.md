The **Timeless Void**, also called the **Outer Void**, the **Ancient Darkness**, and the **Outer Dark**, were terms used in various ways.

## History {#History}

Cosmologically, the Void is the uninhabited space outside [Time](/wiki/Time) and [Creation](/wiki/E%C3%A4 "Eä"), the absence of the [Flame Imperishable](/wiki/Flame_Imperishable). It is told that [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") used to wander in the Void to look in vain for the Flame Imperishable.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Timeless Halls](/wiki/Timeless_Halls) of [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) are apart from the Void; [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) is created amidst the Void but is not a part of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>

After his defeat in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) was banished into the Timeless Void through the [Door of Night](/wiki/Door_of_Night)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and thus was put outside Time and Space, outside Eä altogether;<sup id="cite_ref-MT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-3">[3]</a></sup> however, legends predict that he will return to the World and cause its [End](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath "Dagor Dagorath").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The 'Void' in that context refers to the state of Not-being, that existed before the creation of [Eä](/wiki/E%C3%A4) and, after its creation, outside it. This conception was often confused by [Men](/wiki/Men) and even [Elves](/wiki/Elves) with the conception of vast spaces within Eä, especially those conceived to lie all about the enisled [Kingdom of Arda](/wiki/Arda "Arda").<sup id="cite_ref-MT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-3">[3]</a></sup>

Following his apparent utter defeat in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s weakened spirit was left bound to the confines of the world, having stepped on the same path to powerlessness as Morgoth had in his banishment to the Void .<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

It is also possible that [Gandalf's](/wiki/Gandalf "Gandalf") spirit crossed the Void and entered the [Timeless Halls](/wiki/Timeless_Halls) after his death during [the battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Peak "Battle of the Peak") with the [Balrog of Moria](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane"), as he "passed out of thought and time."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
