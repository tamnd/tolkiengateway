The **Timeless Void**, also called the **Outer Void**, the **Ancient Darkness**, and the **Outer Dark**, were terms used in various ways.

## History {#History}

Cosmologically, the Void is the uninhabited space outside [Time](../T/Time.md) and [Creation](../E/Eä.md), the absence of the [Flame Imperishable](../F/Flame_Imperishable.md). It is told that [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) used to wander in the Void to look in vain for the Flame Imperishable.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>

The [Timeless Halls](../T/Timeless_Halls.md) of [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) are apart from the Void; [Eä](../E/Eä.md) is created amidst the Void but is not a part of it.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>

After his defeat in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) was banished into the Timeless Void through the [Door of Night](../D/Door_of_Night.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and thus was put outside Time and Space, outside Eä altogether;<sup id="cite_ref-MT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-3">[3]</a></sup> however, legends predict that he will return to the World and cause its [End](../D/Dagor_Dagorath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The 'Void' in that context refers to the state of Not-being, that existed before the creation of [Eä](../E/Eä.md) and, after its creation, outside it. This conception was often confused by [Men](../M/Men.md) and even [Elves](../E/Elves.md) with the conception of vast spaces within Eä, especially those conceived to lie all about the enisled [Kingdom of Arda](../A/Arda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-3">[3]</a></sup>

Following his apparent utter defeat in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s weakened spirit was left bound to the confines of the world, having stepped on the same path to powerlessness as Morgoth had in his banishment to the Void .<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

It is also possible that [Gandalf's](../G/Gandalf.md) spirit crossed the Void and entered the [Timeless Halls](../T/Timeless_Halls.md) after his death during [the battle](../B/Battle_of_the_Peak.md) with the [Balrog of Moria](../D/Durin's_Bane.md), as he "passed out of thought and time."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
