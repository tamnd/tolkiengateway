**Atanalcar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌataˈnalkar]</span>) was the youngest of the four children of [Elros Tar-Minyatur](/wiki/Elros "Elros"), first [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Atanalcar* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Man Glory" and is a compound of *[Atan](/wiki/Atani "Atani")* ("Man") and *[alcar](/wiki/Alcar "Alcar")* ("glory").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
