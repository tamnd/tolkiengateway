<i><b>A Philological Miscellany Presented to Eilert Ekwall</b></i> is a 1942 <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift" title="wikipedia:Festschrift">Festschrift</a> presented to mark the occasion of the sixty-fifth birthday of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eilert_Ekwall" title="wikipedia:Eilert Ekwall">Eilert Ekwall</a>, Professor of English at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University" title="wikipedia:Lund University">Lund University</a>, Sweden. It constitutes vol. XIV and vol. XV of *Studia Neophilologica*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The volume includes an introductory note signed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), Kenneth Sisam, [R.W. Chambers](/wiki/R.W._Chambers), J. Turville-Petre, [C.L. Wrenn](/wiki/Charles_Leslie_Wrenn "Charles Leslie Wrenn"), and others.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
