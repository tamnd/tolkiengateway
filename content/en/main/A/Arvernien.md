**Arvernien** was the coastland of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) west of the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion). It had the protection of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).

## History {#History}

Arvernien was originally an uninhabited land, known mainly for the forest of [Nimbrethil](/wiki/Nimbrethil),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but after the rout that became known as the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") and the subsequent destruction of the Havens of [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest), [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and [his folk](/wiki/Falathrim "Falathrim") dwelt in this region. Though at first they lived on the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar), they later founded the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-5B_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-2">[2]</a></sup> The area became a known refuge, as when [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) [fell](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin "Fall of Gondolin"), many of the [Gondolindrim](/wiki/Gondolindrim) fled southward too<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-3">[3]</a></sup> and those were followed by [refugees](/wiki/Iathrim "Iathrim") from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).

The Havens were devastated by the [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying) when the remaining [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) came to claim the [Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr) held by [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil). Following Eärendil's return from his journeys out at sea and the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) in which Morgoth was defeated, Beleriand, and Arvernien with it, was destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tolkien only ever gave an incomplete translation of the name *Arvernien*: "(the land) beside the *[Verna](/wiki/Verna)*".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Except the core element *Verna*, the name can be analyzed as prefix *[ar-](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* meaning "beside, without" (see also <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Argad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Argad (page does not exist)">argad</a></i>) and the geographical ending *[-ien](/wiki/-ien)* which translates as "land of". The meaning of *Verna* is, however, unclear.

Considering that the name was an invention of Tolkien's early legendarium, when Beleriand was still called *Broseliand*, [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggested the name might actually have been inspired by the French region of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auvergne" title="wikipedia:Auvergne">Auvergne</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <span></span>
