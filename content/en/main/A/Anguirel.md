**Anguirel** was one of a pair of swords forged by [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l), the [Dark Elf](/wiki/Dark_Elf), from an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_meteorite" title="wikipedia:Iron meteorite">iron meteorite</a>. Both swords were black and could cleave through any earth-delved iron.

Eöl gave Anguirel's mate, [Anglachel](/wiki/Anglachel), to [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) as payment to live in [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth). He kept Anguirel until it was stolen by his son [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin), who fled to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). What became of it later is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology of *Anguirel* is not as clear as that of its mate. It might mean "Iron of the Eternal Star", from *[ang](/wiki/Ang "Ang")* "iron", <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Uir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Uir (page does not exist)">uir</a></i> "eternal" and *[el](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* "star".
