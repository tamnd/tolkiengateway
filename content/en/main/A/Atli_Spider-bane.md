In [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online), **Atli Spider-bane** is a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") from [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) dedicated to fighting spiders. He travelled across [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) to fight spiders. He aids [Archet](/wiki/Archet)’s people against the spider infestation near the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Old_East_Pass&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Old East Pass (page does not exist)">Old East Pass</a> and [Cal Sprigley](/wiki/List_of_minor_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_characters_in_Bree-land#Cal_Sprigley "List of minor The Lord of the Rings Online characters in Bree-land")'s farm.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Assault_of_Archet&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Assault of Archet (page does not exist)">Assault of Archet</a>, he assists [Jon Brackenbrook](/wiki/Jon_Brackenbrook) and the player.

When the story reaches [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), Atli re-appears, as he is on the hunt for the brood of [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
