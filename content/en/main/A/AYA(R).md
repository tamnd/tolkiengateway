**AYA(R)** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "sea".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the root ÁYAR/AIR (with an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inverted_breve" title="wikipedia:Inverted breve">inverted breve below</a> on the "I") signifying "sea, only used of the inner seas of Middle-earth", derived from root [AY](/wiki/AY). The root yields the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) derivatives *ear (earen)*, *aire (airen)*, and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *oear, oer*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
