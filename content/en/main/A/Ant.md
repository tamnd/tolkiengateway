<i><b>ant</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word meaning *gift*. The word is derived from the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) <a href="ANA.md" title="ANA">ANA<sup>1</sup></a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- Cognates: [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[anna](Anna.md)*

In later [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), *ant* was revised to *ann* and *anw*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Obsolete forms: [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *ant* was originally written as *ann*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
