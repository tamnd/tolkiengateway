<i><b>ant</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning *gift*. The word is derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a href="/wiki/ANA" title="ANA">ANA<sup>1</sup></a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- Cognates: [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[anna](/wiki/Anna "Anna")*

In later [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), *ant* was revised to *ann* and *anw*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Obsolete forms: [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *ant* was originally written as *ann*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
