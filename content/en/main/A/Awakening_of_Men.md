The **Awakening of Men** was the stirring of the first [Men](../M/Men.md), in the far eastern land of [Hildórien](../H/Hildórien.md), towards the end of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), when the [Years of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md) began. They were awakened by [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md), when the first Sun rose in the West.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, the Men awoke thanks to the [Elf](../E/Elves.md) [Nuin](../N/Nuin.md), who discovered them sleeping in an enclosed valley of the East called [Murmenalda](../M/Murmenalda.md), the Vale of Sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Accidentally he stirred a sleeper, overcome with curiosity, and he woke the first pair of [Men](../M/Men.md), right before the first rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) from the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a>. Their names were [Ermon](../E/Ermon.md) and [Elmir](../E/Elmir.md). Nuin also taught them [speech](../I/Ilkorin.md), and thus was the **Father of Speech**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [Round World version of the Silmarillion](../R/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion.md) of c. [1955](../_/1955.md), the awakening of Men is proposed to be much earlier, sometime between the [Awakening of the Elves](Awakening_of_the_Elves.md) and the [Battle of the Powers](../B/Battle_of_the_Powers.md) ([Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1050](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1050.md) - [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1100](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1100.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In a draft published in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](../T/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth.md)*, the date is given as [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1075](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1075.md) (which is of course by no means final desision).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
