The **Awakening of Men** was the stirring of the first [Men](/wiki/Men), in the far eastern land of [Hildórien](/wiki/Hild%C3%B3rien), towards the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), when the [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun) began. They were awakened by [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar), when the first Sun rose in the West.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the Men awoke thanks to the [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") [Nuin](/wiki/Nuin), who discovered them sleeping in an enclosed valley of the East called [Murmenalda](/wiki/Murmenalda), the Vale of Sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Accidentally he stirred a sleeper, overcome with curiosity, and he woke the first pair of [Men](/wiki/Men), right before the first rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) from the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/West" title="West">West</a>. Their names were [Ermon](/wiki/Ermon) and [Elmir](/wiki/Elmir). Nuin also taught them [speech](/wiki/Ilkorin "Ilkorin"), and thus was the **Father of Speech**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In the [Round World version of the Silmarillion](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion) of c. [1955](/wiki/1955), the awakening of Men is proposed to be much earlier, sometime between the [Awakening of the Elves](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves) and the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers) ([Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1050](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1050 "Year of the Trees 1050") - [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1100](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1100 "Year of the Trees 1100")).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In a draft published in *[The Nature of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Nature_of_Middle-earth)*, the date is given as [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1075](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1075 "Year of the Trees 1075") (which is of course by no means final desision).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
