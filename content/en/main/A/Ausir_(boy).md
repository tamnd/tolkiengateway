**Ausir** was an [Elf](../E/Elf.md) of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. He was one of the children in the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md), and he told [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) of the fate of [Huan](../H/Huan.md) the Hound, and revealed to him that the old man whose guidance brought him to the Lonely Isle was none other than [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md) himself. He argued with [Vëannë](../V/Vëannë.md), suggesting [elvish](../Q/Qenya.md) names *[Tinwë Linto](../T/Thingol.md)* and *[Wendelin](../M/Melian.md)* instead of [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) *Tinwelint* and *Gwendeling*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
