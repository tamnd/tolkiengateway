**Amazon.com Incorporated**, also known as **Amazon.com, Inc** or simply **Amazon**, is an American multinational technology company.It is the parent company of [Amazon MGM Studios](/wiki/Amazon_MGM_Studios), of Amazon Music, of Amazon Prime Video, and of [MGM](/wiki/MGM). It was founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994.

Currently, it has the television rights of the books [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit), which acquired them from [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
