**Amazon.com Incorporated**, also known as **Amazon.com, Inc** or simply **Amazon**, is an American multinational technology company.It is the parent company of [Amazon MGM Studios](Amazon_MGM_Studios.md), of Amazon Music, of Amazon Prime Video, and of [MGM](../M/MGM.md). It was founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994.

Currently, it has the television rights of the books [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) and [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md), which acquired them from [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
