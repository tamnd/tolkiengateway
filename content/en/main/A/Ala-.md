<i><b>ala-</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) prefix meaning "not-, un-". Seen in **alahasta** "unmarried".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This prefix seems to have the power to turn a following verbal stem into a past participle even if no explicit participle ending is present. Unlike [ú](/wiki/%C3%9A "Ú")-, this prefix does not seem to have negative connotations.
