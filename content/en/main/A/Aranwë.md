**Aranwë** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[aˈranwe]</span> was an [Elf](../N/Noldor.md) of the [House of Fingolfin](../H/House_of_Fingolfin.md).

It is possible that he was a nobleman of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), because of the meaning of his name and because his son [Voronwë](../V/Voronwë.md) came from Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aranwë* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "King-person" and is a compound of *[aran](Aran.md)* ("king") and the (mainly masculine) name-suffix *[-wë](../_/-wë.md)* ("man", "person").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
