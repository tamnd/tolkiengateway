**Aranwë** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[aˈranwe]</span> was an [Elf](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") of the [House of Fingolfin](/wiki/House_of_Fingolfin).

It is possible that he was a nobleman of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), because of the meaning of his name and because his son [Voronwë](/wiki/Voronw%C3%AB) came from Gondolin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aranwë* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "King-person" and is a compound of *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* ("king") and the (mainly masculine) name-suffix *[-wë](/wiki/-w%C3%AB)* ("man", "person").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
