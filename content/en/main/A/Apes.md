**Apes** were animals living [far South](/wiki/Far_Harad "Far Harad") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Said to live in the dark forests of the South,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> they seem to have only been known in stories in the North. Probably due to their lesser intelligence, the word "ape", along with "maggot" and <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Snaga" title="Snaga">snaga</a></i>, became an insult used by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-UH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UH-1">[1]</a></sup>
