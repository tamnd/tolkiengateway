**A Long-Expected Soundscape** is an audio project created by Jordan Rannells, designed as a 3D audio dramatization of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).* It can also be used as a chapter-by-chapter accompaniment as people read the books. It can also be synchronized with the audiobooks read by [Andy Serkis](/wiki/Andy_Serkis) and [Rob Inglis](/wiki/Rob_Inglis). **A Long-Expected Soundscape** encompasses layers of sound FX, ambient nature environmental sounds, and an original score.

The track titles match the titles of the book.
