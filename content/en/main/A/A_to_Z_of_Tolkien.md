<i><b>A to Z of Tolkien</b></i> is an encyclopedia-style book written by David Day, first published in [1993](/wiki/1993).

The book has been re-packaged and published under many different titles, including:

- <i><b>The Tolkien Companion</b></i> (1993)
- <i><b>A Guide to Tolkien</b></i> (2001)
- <i><b>Tolkien: A Dictionary</b></i> (2013)
- <i><b>A Dictionary of Tolkien</b></i> (2013)

*A Dictionary of Tolkien* later became the first installment of "The World of Tolkien" set.
