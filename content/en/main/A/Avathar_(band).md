**Avathar** is a Finnish black metal band whose works are inspired by the writings of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). They name Austrian black metal band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning) as one of their main musical influences.

### History {#History}

Inspired by the works of Tolkien, Avathar were founded in [2000](/wiki/2000) as a two-man project of Witch king and Scatha.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Their work is inspired by the Austrian black metal band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning), however their work makes greater use of keyboards and vocals, and the approach to their is in creating soundtracks.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
