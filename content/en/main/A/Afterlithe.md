**Afterlithe** was the seventh month of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) Calendar, following, as its name suggests, the midsummer feastdays of [Lithe](../L/Lithe.md), and approximately corresponding to modern [24 June](../_/24_June.md) through [23 July](../_/23_July.md). This month was called [Mede](../M/Mede.md) in [Bree](../B/Bree.md).

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name was [Cermië](../C/Cermië.md), and the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name - used by the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) - was <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cerveth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cerveth (page does not exist)">Cerveth</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Afterlithe was named for its occurrence after the holiday of Lithe. (See [Forelithe](../F/Forelithe.md)) The name corresponds to the Old English name for July, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%A6fterra_li%C3%BEa" title="wiktionary:æfterra liþa">æftera Líða</a>, or "after Litha."
