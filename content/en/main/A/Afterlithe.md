**Afterlithe** was the seventh month of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") Calendar, following, as its name suggests, the midsummer feastdays of [Lithe](/wiki/Lithe), and approximately corresponding to modern [24 June](/wiki/24_June) through [23 July](/wiki/23_July). This month was called [Mede](/wiki/Mede) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name was [Cermië](/wiki/Cermi%C3%AB), and the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name - used by the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) - was <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cerveth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cerveth (page does not exist)">Cerveth</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Afterlithe was named for its occurrence after the holiday of Lithe. (See [Forelithe](/wiki/Forelithe)) The name corresponds to the Old English name for July, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%C3%A6fterra_li%C3%BEa" title="wiktionary:æfterra liþa">æftera Líða</a>, or "after Litha."
