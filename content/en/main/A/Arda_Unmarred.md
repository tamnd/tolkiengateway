**Arda Unmarred** ([Val](../V/Valarin.md). <i><b>Aþāraphelūn Amanaišal</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Q](../Q/Quenya.md). <i><b>Arda Alahasta</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or <i><b>Arda Vanya</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>) is a concept in [Elvish](../E/Elves.md) religion and philosophy. It refers to [Arda](Arda.md) (the world) as it existed in a perfect state following its creation, as opposed to **[Arda Marred](Arda_Marred.md)**, the flawed world as it currently exists. Arda Unmarred was a world not yet tainted by the evil of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). This reflects the original intention of the creator, [Eru Ilúvatar](../E/Eru_Ilúvatar.md), and his servants the [Ainur](Ainur.md).

The Elves lament the spiritual and physical corruption of Arda. They live in [hope](../E/Estel.md) that one day this damage will be undone and the world will be purged of all evil, restoring it to a paradise state.
