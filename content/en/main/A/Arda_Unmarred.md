**Arda Unmarred** ([Val](/wiki/Valarin "Valarin"). <i><b>Aþāraphelūn Amanaišal</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"). <i><b>Arda Alahasta</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or <i><b>Arda Vanya</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>) is a concept in [Elvish](/wiki/Elves "Elves") religion and philosophy. It refers to [Arda](/wiki/Arda) (the world) as it existed in a perfect state following its creation, as opposed to **[Arda Marred](/wiki/Arda_Marred)**, the flawed world as it currently exists. Arda Unmarred was a world not yet tainted by the evil of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). This reflects the original intention of the creator, [Eru Ilúvatar](/wiki/Eru_Il%C3%BAvatar), and his servants the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur).

The Elves lament the spiritual and physical corruption of Arda. They live in [hope](/wiki/Estel "Estel") that one day this damage will be undone and the world will be purged of all evil, restoring it to a paradise state.
