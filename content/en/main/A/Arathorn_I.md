**Arathorn I** was the twelfth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Dúnedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arathorn_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arathorn-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the days of his father, [Arassuil](Arassuil.md), the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) emerged again.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Arathorn succeeded Arassuil, on his death in [2784](../T/Third_Age_2784.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arassuil_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arassuil-3">[3]</a></sup>

Arathorn died prematurely<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> in [2848](../T/Third_Age_2848.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arathorn_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arathorn-2">[2]</a></sup> He was succeeded by his son [Argonui](Argonui.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Arathorn I's year of birth is only mentioned in earlier manuscript versions of what would later become Appendix A. In general, J.R.R. Tolkien did not include the year of birth of kings and rulers in Appendix A or Appendix B.

In the later manuscript C Arathorn's years of birth and death were [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2693](../T/Third_Age_2693.md) and [2848](../T/Third_Age_2848.md), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-ManuscriptC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ManuscriptC-7">[6]</a></sup> In the same manuscript it is also mentioned that [Dírhael](../D/Dírhael.md), the maternal grandfather of [Aragorn](Aragorn.md), was descended from Arathorn I "by a younger branch".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

In the earlier manuscript B, the years of Arathorn I's birth and death were [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2683](../T/Third_Age_2683.md) and [2838](../T/Third_Age_2838.md), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>
