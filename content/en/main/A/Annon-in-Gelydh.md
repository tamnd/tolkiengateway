**Annon-in-Gelydh** ([S](../S/Sindarin.md). "Gate of the Noldor") was the eastern entrance in the western hills of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) that led to a subterranean river-bed which started from [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md) and ended at the [Cirith Ninniach](../C/Cirith_Ninniach.md).

It was built by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) of [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) in the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). After Turgon's departure to [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), the Gate was forgotten, but it was rediscovered and used centuries later by [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) in his escape from the oppressed lands of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) while finding his way into [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Annon-in-Gelydh* is a combination of *[annon](Annon.md)* ("gate"), the plural of the definite article *[i](../I/I.md)*, and the plural of *[Golodh](../G/Golodh.md)* ("Noldo").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
