**Annon-in-Gelydh** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). "Gate of the Noldor") was the eastern entrance in the western hills of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) that led to a subterranean river-bed which started from [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim) and ended at the [Cirith Ninniach](/wiki/Cirith_Ninniach).

It was built by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) in the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). After Turgon's departure to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), the Gate was forgotten, but it was rediscovered and used centuries later by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) in his escape from the oppressed lands of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) while finding his way into [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Annon-in-Gelydh* is a combination of *[annon](/wiki/Annon "Annon")* ("gate"), the plural of the definite article *[i](/wiki/I "I")*, and the plural of *[Golodh](/wiki/Golodh)* ("Noldo").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
