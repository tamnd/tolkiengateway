<i><b>An Afternoon in Middle-earth</b></i> is the programme booklet of the event "An Afternoon in Middle-earth", held on [30 November](/wiki/30_November) [1969](/wiki/1969) at the Studio Theatre, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/mac_(Birmingham)" title="wikipedia:mac (Birmingham)">Midlands Art Centre</a>, Cannon Hill ([Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham)). The programme includes [a letter from J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Letter_to_Leslie_Holloway "Letter to Leslie Holloway").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Included in the booklet (which "*amounts to a fair-sized magazine*") is also an introduction by Leslie Holloway (the producer of the event), an article on [Tolkien fandom](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom) by [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) ("Daily Life in Middle-earth"), as well as "*articles by Keith Brace of the Birmingham Post, and Charlotte and Denis Plimmer, from the Daily Telegraph, among others*".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
