**Aglahad** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2827 – 2932, aged 105 years) was the nineteenth [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Aglahad was a descendant of [Galador](/wiki/Galador), the first Prince of Dol Amroth; through Galador's mother [Mithrellas](/wiki/Mithrellas), Aglahad had some [Elven blood](/wiki/Half-elven "Half-elven").<sup id="cite_ref-GalCel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GalCel-2">[2]</a></sup>

Aglahad ruled Dol Amroth around the time of [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") [Turgon](</wiki/Turgon_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Turgon (Steward of Gondor)"). During Aglahad's reign as Prince, [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) had to abandon much of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After his death, his son [Angelimir](/wiki/Angelimir) became the twentieth Prince.<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
