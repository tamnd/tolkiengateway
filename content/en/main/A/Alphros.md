**Alphros** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3017](../T/Third_Age_3017.md) - [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [95](../F/Fourth_Age_95.md)) was the twenty-fourth [Prince of Dol Amroth](../P/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Alphros was born near the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), and was only a toddler when the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) raged in Gondor. Growing up in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), he became Prince after his father [Elphir](../E/Elphir.md)'s death, and served under [King Elessar](Aragorn.md). He died in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [95](../F/Fourth_Age_95.md).<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Alphros* seems to be a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but it seems to mean "Swan Foam" and to be a combination of *[alph](Alph.md)* "swan" and *[ros](../R/Ros.md)* ("foam").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
