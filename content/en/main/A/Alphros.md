**Alphros** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017") - [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [95](/wiki/Fourth_Age_95 "Fourth Age 95")) was the twenty-fourth [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Alphros was born near the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), and was only a toddler when the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) raged in Gondor. Growing up in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), he became Prince after his father [Elphir](/wiki/Elphir)'s death, and served under [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"). He died in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [95](/wiki/Fourth_Age_95 "Fourth Age 95").<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Alphros* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but it seems to mean "Swan Foam" and to be a combination of *[alph](/wiki/Alph "Alph")* "swan" and *[ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros")* ("foam").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
