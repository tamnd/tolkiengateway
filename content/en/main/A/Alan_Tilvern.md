**Alan Tilvern** ([November 5](../_/5_November.md), [1918](../_/1918.md) - [December 17](../_/17_December.md), [2003](../_/2003.md)) was an actor. He was the voice of the "Innkeeper" ([Barliman Butterbur](../B/Barliman_Butterbur.md)) in [1978 film *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>).
