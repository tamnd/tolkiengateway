**Ailin** was the name of a great lake in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about it.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ailin* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). It means "lake, pool".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
