**Alfirin** was an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) name for the flower [Men](../M/Men.md) called [simbelmynë](../S/Simbelmynë.md) (they also used the name [uilos](../U/Uilos.md) for the same flower).<sup id="cite_ref-Note38_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note38-1">[1]</a></sup> The name comes from its habit of growing thickly on the tombs of [Men](../M/Men.md): it was found among the Kings' mounds in the [Barrowfield](../B/Barrowfield.md) of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md), and also on the [Tomb of Elendil](../T/Tomb_of_Elendil.md). Tolkien provided a description of "an immortelle, but not dry and papery: simply a beautiful bell-like flower, running through many colours, but soft and gentle."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The white variety seems to have been most common.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Alfirin* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, said to mean "not dying". While first said to consist of *[alph](Alph.md)* ("[swan](../S/Swans.md)") + *irin* (unglossed), [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) amended the compound to consist of *al-* ("not") + *fĭrin* ("mortal"; see also [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[firin](../F/Firin.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In his comments in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) points out that [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md)' description of "golden bells ... of *mallos* and *alfirin*", is not quite in harmony with other descriptions, and he suggests that this particular use of the name may refer to a different flower altogether.<sup id="cite_ref-Note38_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note38-1">[1]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup> Alternatively, the golden flowers of [Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md) might simply be a differently-coloured variety of the white *alfirin* commonly seen on Men's tombs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>
