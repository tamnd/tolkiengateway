<i><b>anto</b></i> is a noun meaning "mouth" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anto.gif"><img src="/w/images/9/9c/Anto.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Anto* is also the name in Quenya of the thirteenth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the first letter of the fourth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiced spirants" and it is the fourth letter in the dental or *t*-series of consonants, the *[Tincotéma](/wiki/Tincot%C3%A9ma)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> In the standard spelling of Quenya *Anto* represents **NT**.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> In all other modes this letter represents **DH** (or "voiced" **TH**).<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Adho</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
