**Ambar-metta** is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) term that refers to [the End](../T/The_End.md) of the World.

It is mostly known as was part of a line used by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) upon his arrival to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), declaring "*In this place will I abide, and my heirs, unto the ending of the [world](Arda.md)*". [Elessar](Aragorn.md) would later use this line at his <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Crowning_of_the_King&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Crowning of the King (page does not exist)">coronation</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ambar-metta*, meaning "World's-end", is a composition of two [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) words, *[ambar](Ambar.md)* ("world") and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Metta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Metta (page does not exist)">metta</a></i> ("end").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> The alternate form <i><b>ambarmetta</b></i> was used in one of the sentences related to the *[Alcar mi Tarmenel na Erun](Alcar_mi_Tarmenel_na_Erun.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
