**Andúril** was the name for the sword **[Narsil](/wiki/Narsil)** after its reforging in [Third Age 3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018) for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), the heir of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).

## Appearance {#Appearance}

Its blade had engraved the designs of [Seven Stars](/wiki/Seven_Stars) between the rayed [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the crescent [Moon](/wiki/Moon),<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> symbols of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), and his sons [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) and [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur). It also had many [cirth](/wiki/Cirth "Cirth"). The sword was very bright, with the light of the sun making it shine red, and in the light of the moon it shined cold.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, it was given an Elven [sheath](/wiki/And%C3%BAril%27s_sheath "Andúril's sheath") with a tracery of flowers and leaves wrought of silver and gold and the name Andúril and the lineage of the sword set in elven runes formed of gems.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Narsil was a symbol of the kingship of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), but was broken at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Its fragments remained an heirloom for the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor), and the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain).

[Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), son of the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor"), travelled to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) for the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) because of the prophetic dream of his brother [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir). The dream told him to "[seek for the Sword that was broken](/wiki/Seek_for_the_Sword_that_was_broken "Seek for the Sword that was broken")".<sup id="cite_ref-CoE_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CoE-3">[3]</a></sup>

Finally, after three thousand years, when [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) set out for the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Narsil was reforged by Elven smiths of Rivendell, and Aragorn renamed it "Andúril".

Aragorn carried the sword during his journey south as part of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), and fought with it, the **Sword That Was Broken** or the **Sword Reforged**. In his departure of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), the first of the [Gifts of Galadriel](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel) was [Andúril's sheath](/wiki/And%C3%BAril%27s_sheath), specially made for it.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-2">[2]</a></sup>

Aragorn often used the sword to help establish his credentials as the [Heir of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur) and the throne of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-King_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-King-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Andúril* means "Flame of the West" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> composed of *[andúnë](/wiki/And%C3%BAn%C3%AB "Andúnë")* ("west") and *[ril](/wiki/Ril "Ril")* ("brilliance"). "West" here refers to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Broken swords that are reforged are also seen in the *Volsungasaga* and the *Nibelungenlied*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<b><a href="/wiki/1978" title="1978">1978</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Narsil is reforged after Rivendell, though this reforging is not shown. Neither Narsil nor Andúril are named.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/1981" title="1981">1981</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Narsil is reforged after Rivendell, and Aragorn carries Andúril thereafter.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2001" title="2001">2001</a>: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Because of Aragorn&#39;s <i>warrior</i>-persona in this video game, Andúril merely serves as a melee weapon upgrade after Rivendell.<sup id="cite_ref-Veugen_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Veugen-8">[8]</a></sup>  Aragorn does not use the broken sword at any point prior. Notably, the sword&#39;s blade is constantly illuminated; it glows red during the day, and blue at night. In the game&#39;s credits, artist Jason Rosenstock is explicitly mentioned as the designer of Andúril.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2001" title="2001">2001</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Narsil remains broken and Andúril is not reforged in the Fellowship of the Ring movie. Shards of Narsil are kept on a display in <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2002" title="2002">2002</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In <i><a href="/wiki/The_Two_Towers" title="The Two Towers">The Two Towers</a></i>, when asked by <a href="/wiki/H%C3%A1ma" title="Háma">Háma</a> to disarm outside <a href="/wiki/Meduseld" title="Meduseld">Meduseld</a>, Aragorn is very reluctant, saying that normally he would give up his weapon, &#34;if I bore now any sword but Andúril&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-King_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-King-5">[5]</a></sup> This scene and dialogue is omitted from the film on the grace that the sword Aragorn surrenders there was not Andúril.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2003" title="2003">2003</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Andúril is not forged from the shards of Narsil until late in the third film. <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> had the sword reforged only after <a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>&#39;s pleading. He then took the sword to Aragorn in the camp of the <a href="/wiki/Rohirrim" title="Rohirrim">Rohirrim</a> at <a href="/wiki/Dunharrow" title="Dunharrow">Dunharrow</a>. Aragorn&#39;s acceptance of the sword, along with his decision at that time to take the <a href="/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead" title="Paths of the Dead">Paths of the Dead</a>, showed his willingness to accept his destiny of becoming king.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)">extended edition</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Mouth_of_Sauron" title="Mouth of Sauron">Mouth of Sauron</a> refers to the blade as Elvish; this could either be a reference to its reforging or a mistake on behalf of the writers.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd>
<dd>Design-wise, Andúril does not differ significantly from its predecessor, Narsil. The only notable difference is the Elvish inscription running up the length of the blade.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2003" title="2003">2003</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Andúril is represented as a permanent upgrade called &#34;Andúril&#39;s Fury&#34; for Aragorn, who is represented as a hero unit. It makes his sword glow white as well as increasing his damage greatly, with bonus damage against enemy Heroes, Wraiths, and the Nazgûl.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2007" title="2007">2007</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The reforging of Narsil is experienced by the player in the quest arc called &#34;The Blade that was Broken&#34;. The player sets out to find the last of the Silithair, shining adamants crafted by Elves of the house of <a href="/wiki/F%C3%ABanor" title="Fëanor">Fëanor</a> in days of old, in order for the Elven smiths of Imladris to reforge the sword into Andúril.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>
