**Amon Lanc**, the Naked Hill, was a hill in the southern parts of [Greenwood the Great](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Amon Lanc was the highest point of the highland at the southwestern corner of Greenwood. It gained its name from the fact that no trees ever grew upon its summit.<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup> Once the [Silvan Elves](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) had lived near the hill under [Oropher](../O/Oropher.md) in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but Oropher abandoned the dwellings upon Amon Lanc after becoming disturbed by Sauron's rising power, and he moved to the western side of the [Emyn Duir](../M/Mountains_of_Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AMON_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMON-3">[3]</a></sup>

Around a millennium after his [defeat](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md), [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) came to Amon Lanc and reoccupied Oropher's abandoned stronghold. The hill and fortress were then together called [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md), and the wood in which it stood became known as [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md).<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), southern Mirkwood became known as [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and Amon Lanc became part of it.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Lanc* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, consisting of *[amon](Amon.md)* ("hill") + *[lanc](../L/Lanc.md)* ("naked").<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The hill was first occupied by Drúin and his tribe in <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_20&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 20 (page does not exist)">20</a>, who established a stronghold within the hill named <i>Tumûn-gabil</i> (<a href="../K/Khuzdul.md" title="Khuzdul">Kh.</a> “The Great Hollow”) and mined for <a href="../S/Stones_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md#Laen" title="Stones in Middle-earth Role Playing">red laen</a> and other riches. In <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1617&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1617 (page does not exist)">1617</a>, a Noldo jewel-smith named Celedhring, who was secretly a servant of Sauron, offered to teach the Dwarves. Under his guidance, lord Dworin forged the Khazed-khezed (<a href="../K/Khuzdul.md" title="Khuzdul">Kh.</a> “The Dwarf-stone”). Envious of this jewel, other Dwarves mined so much red laen that by <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1847&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1847 (page does not exist)">1847</a> the mines were depleted and many departed. Infighting among the remaining Dwarves led to only Dworin and several kinsmen surviving by {{<a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1847&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1847 (page does not exist)">1847</a>]], who were eventually slain by Dworin. Dworin wandered his fortress with his jewel until he died in <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1896&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1896 (page does not exist)">1896</a>. After the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md" title="War of the Last Alliance">War of the Last Alliance</a>, Celedhring returned to Amon Lanc and hid himself in the Dwarf-hold.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
