**Amon Lanc**, the Naked Hill, was a hill in the southern parts of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood").<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Amon Lanc was the highest point of the highland at the southwestern corner of Greenwood. It gained its name from the fact that no trees ever grew upon its summit.<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup> Once the [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves) had lived near the hill under [Oropher](/wiki/Oropher) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but Oropher abandoned the dwellings upon Amon Lanc after becoming disturbed by Sauron's rising power, and he moved to the western side of the [Emyn Duir](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood "Mountains of Mirkwood").<sup id="cite_ref-AMON_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMON-3">[3]</a></sup>

Around a millennium after his [defeat](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr "Siege of Barad-dûr"), [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) came to Amon Lanc and reoccupied Oropher's abandoned stronghold. The hill and fortress were then together called [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), and the wood in which it stood became known as [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), southern Mirkwood became known as [East Lórien](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and Amon Lanc became part of it.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Lanc* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, consisting of *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") + *[lanc](/wiki/Lanc "Lanc")* ("naked").<sup id="cite_ref-GLADDEN_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GLADDEN-1">[1]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The hill was first occupied by Drúin and his tribe in <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_20&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 20 (page does not exist)">20</a>, who established a stronghold within the hill named <i>Tumûn-gabil</i> (<a href="/wiki/Khuzdul" title="Khuzdul">Kh.</a> “The Great Hollow”) and mined for <a href="/wiki/Stones_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Laen" title="Stones in Middle-earth Role Playing">red laen</a> and other riches. In <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1617&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1617 (page does not exist)">1617</a>, a Noldo jewel-smith named Celedhring, who was secretly a servant of Sauron, offered to teach the Dwarves. Under his guidance, lord Dworin forged the Khazed-khezed (<a href="/wiki/Khuzdul" title="Khuzdul">Kh.</a> “The Dwarf-stone”). Envious of this jewel, other Dwarves mined so much red laen that by <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1847&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1847 (page does not exist)">1847</a> the mines were depleted and many departed. Infighting among the remaining Dwarves led to only Dworin and several kinsmen surviving by {{<a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1847&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1847 (page does not exist)">1847</a>]], who were eventually slain by Dworin. Dworin wandered his fortress with his jewel until he died in <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">S.A.</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1896&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1896 (page does not exist)">1896</a>. After the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance" title="War of the Last Alliance">War of the Last Alliance</a>, Celedhring returned to Amon Lanc and hid himself in the Dwarf-hold.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
