<i><b>aika</b></i> means "fell, terrible, dire", in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

In order to conform to the orthography used in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested the spelling *aica*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*aika* descended from the [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) adjectival form *gayakā*, derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") GAYA ("awe, dread").<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- [Aikanáro](/wiki/Aikan%C3%A1ro) = "fell fire"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
