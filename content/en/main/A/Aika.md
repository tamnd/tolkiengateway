<i><b>aika</b></i> means "fell, terrible, dire", in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

In order to conform to the orthography used in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested the spelling *aica*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*aika* descended from the [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) adjectival form *gayakā*, derived from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) GAYA ("awe, dread").<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- [Aikanáro](Aikanáro.md) = "fell fire"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
