- The [Atanatári](/wiki/Atanat%C3%A1ri) ([First Age](/wiki/First_Age)), the Fathers of [Men](/wiki/Men).
- [Atanatar I](/wiki/Atanatar_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [480](/wiki/Third_Age_480 "Third Age 480") - [748](/wiki/Third_Age_748 "Third Age 748")), tenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
- [Atanatar II](/wiki/Atanatar_II) *Alcarin* ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [977](/wiki/Third_Age_977 "Third Age 977") - [1226](/wiki/Third_Age_1226 "Third Age 1226")), sixteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) during the height of its power.
