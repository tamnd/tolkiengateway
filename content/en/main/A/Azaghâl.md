**Azaghâl** was the [Lord of Belegost](../L/Lord_of_Belegost.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

### Early History {#Early_History}

While traveling on the [Dwarf-road](../D/Dwarf-road_of_Beleriand.md) in [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md), Azaghâl was waylaid by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md). [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) came to his rescue, saving his life and treasure, and as a token of gratitude, the Dwarf king gave him his [helm](../D/Dragon-helm_of_Dor-lómin.md) made by [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Union of Maedhros {#Union_of_Maedhros}

Azaghâl and his [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) joined the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md), and during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), the [Dwarves of Belegost](../D/Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) covered the retreat of the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) by surrounding the Dragon [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md). They hewed at him with their axes, for his scales were not strong enough to shield him from the blows of Dwarven axes. In his rage Glaurung struck down Azaghâl, and crawled over him. With his last breath Azaghâl drove a knife into Glaurung's belly, so wounded him that he fled the field with many of the dismayed beasts of [Angband](Angband.md) following.

The Dwarves raised up the body of their lord and bore him away; and with slow steps they walked behind singing a dirge in deep voices, as it were a funeral pomp in their country, and gave no heed to their foes; and none dared attack them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) would later recount the tale of Azaghâl to the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Azaghâl* is one of the few [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) names known. It is not determined whether this was his birthname or an epithet. Its meaning is not known, but it has been argued that it is of the same [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) as the [Adûnaic](Adûnaic.md) verb *azgara*, "to wage war" and even perhaps *[Azog](Azog.md)*. *Azaghâl* would then mean "warrior".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
