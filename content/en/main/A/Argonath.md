The **Argonath**, also known as the **Pillars of the Kings**,<sup id="cite_ref-River_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-1">[1]</a></sup> or the **Gate of Kings**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was a landmark on the northern edge of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Description {#Description}

The Argonath consisted of two enormous rock pillars, carved in the likenesses of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) facing to the north. Placed upon huge pedestals, each of the two figures held an axe in its right hand and its left hand rose in a gesture of defiance to the enemies of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). The two statues stood upon either side of the River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-River_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-1">[1]</a></sup> A narrow gap between them<sup id="cite_ref-River_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-1">[1]</a></sup> led to a long dark chasm with high sheer cliffs which led to the lake [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/ea/John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="409" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/ea/John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg/450px-John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/ea/John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg/600px-John_Howe_-_The_Argonath.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1122" data-file-height="1528"/></a><figcaption><i>The Argonath</i> by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

When the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) attacked [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) that settled south of [Greenwood the Great](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood"), [Minalcar](/wiki/Minalcar), the nephew of King [Narmacil I](/wiki/Narmacil_I) and [Regent of Gondor](/wiki/Regent_of_Gondor), led a great army that defeated the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) between [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) and the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1248](/wiki/Third_Age_1248 "Third Age 1248"). When he returned he fortified the eastern banks of the Anduin and built the pillars of the Argonath.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) the Argonath and the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) with [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen) with its Seat of Seeing and [Amon Lhaw](/wiki/Amon_Lhaw), the Hill of Hearing were still a part of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> but the area was deserted.

On [25 February](/wiki/25_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) rowed down the river Anduin between the Pillars of the Kings on their journey south. Time had blurred their eyes and crannied their brows but the two figures still exuded power and majesty.<sup id="cite_ref-River_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Argonath* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "royal stones",<sup id="cite_ref-N_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-9">[9]</a></sup> "pair of royal stones"<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> or "the group of (two) noble stones".<sup id="cite_ref-Letter_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter-11">[11]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[Ar(a)](/wiki/Ara "Ara")* (a prefix expressing royalty) and the shorter form *gon* ("stone" used for smaller objects made of stone, especially carved figures) of *[gond](/wiki/Gond "Gond")* and *[-ath](/wiki/-ath)* (a collective or group plural, because the Argonath were a pair of statues).<sup id="cite_ref-N_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-9">[9]</a></sup> A valid variant of the name in dual form was <i><b>Argonad</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Letter_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In pencilled notes for what would later become the chapter "[The Great River](/wiki/The_Great_River)", [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) first used the name *Sern Erain* and then the name *Sarn Aran*, which he translated as "King Stones".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote in a [letter](/wiki/Letter_211 "Letter 211") that the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) of Gondor resembled Egyptians in many ways, including their love of, and ability to construct, the gigantic and massive, and in their interest in ancestry and tombs.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) pointed out that the *Argonath* recall ancient Egyptian statues, like the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colossi_of_Memnon" title="wikipedia:Colossi of Memnon">two gigantic statues of Memnon</a> at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortuary_Temple_of_Amenhotep_III" title="wikipedia:Mortuary Temple of Amenhotep III">Mortuary Temple of Amenophis III</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
