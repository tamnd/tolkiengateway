**Amaranth Brandybuck** was a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Amaranth was the second child of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) and [Mirabella Took](/wiki/Mirabella_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Amaranth" is a poetic name for a flower that does not fade, derived from Greek *αμάραντος*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is also the common name for a group of 60-70 plants belonging to the family Amaranthaceae. They are used as food and in ornamental gardens; some are weeds.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
