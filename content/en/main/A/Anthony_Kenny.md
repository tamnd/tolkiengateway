**Sir Anthony John Patrick Kenny** (born [16 March](/wiki/16_March) [1931](/wiki/1931)) is an English Catholic philosopher.

Anthony Kenny is the nephew of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alexander_Jones&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alexander Jones (page does not exist)">Alexander Jones</a>, general editor of *[The Jerusalem Bible](/wiki/The_Jerusalem_Bible)* to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) contributed. Kenny's autobiography includes a [reminiscence of Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien")'s work with the translation.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
