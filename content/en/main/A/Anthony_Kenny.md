**Sir Anthony John Patrick Kenny** (born [16 March](../_/16_March.md) [1931](../_/1931.md)) is an English Catholic philosopher.

Anthony Kenny is the nephew of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alexander_Jones&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alexander Jones (page does not exist)">Alexander Jones</a>, general editor of *[The Jerusalem Bible](../T/The_Jerusalem_Bible.md)* to which [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed. Kenny's autobiography includes a [reminiscence of Tolkien](../../index/R/Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work with the translation.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
