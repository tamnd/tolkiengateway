<i><b>Arda Philology 1</b></i> is the first volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](<Arda_(annual).md>)*, and contains the proceedings of the First International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/ominya.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Minya</a> (<a href="../O/Omentielva.md" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Stockholm in August [2005](../_/2005.md).
