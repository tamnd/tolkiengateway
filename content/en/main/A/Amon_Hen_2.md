<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 2</b> is the second issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*, published in December [1972](../_/1972.md). It was the first issue to be published under the name *Amon Hen*, following *[Henneth Annûn 1](../H/Henneth_Annûn_1.md)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

<dl><dd><i>Note: All content is presumably written by John Martin unless indicated otherwise</i></dd></dl>

- Editorial note
- "*[Mallorns](<../M/Mallorn_(journal).md>)* to Come"
- Committee meeting business
    - *Mallorn* news
    - Annual General Meeting announcement by Belladonna Took ([Vera Chapman](../V/Vera_Chapman.md))
    - "Innmoot, London" (report by Vera Chapman)
    - Announcement of Archie Mercer as membership secretary
- Letters to the editor by John B. Abbott, Jon Harvey, Graham England, Grahame Lamb, Tony Fallone, Bernard Peek, Gordon Larkin, and Roger Johnson
- "BOOKS…BOOKS…BOOKS…BOOKS…BOOKS…and more BOOKS still!"
    - "Books to borrow" by Peter Herring (suggested sci-fi, fantasy, and non-fiction books)
    - *Rocannon's World* by [Ursula K. Le Guin](../U/Ursula_K._Le_Guin.md) (reviewed by Graham England)
    - *Njal's Saga*, Penguin Classics edition (reviewed by Graham England)
    - *Grendel* by John Gardiner (reviewed by Graham England)
- Note on Smials (local branches of the Tolkien Society)
- Letter from [Marion Zimmer Bradley](../M/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley.md)
- List of new members and changes of address
- Note on the sale of [Tolkien's desk](../T/Tolkien's_desk.md) by Vera Chapman
- Untitled poem by Robert Grant ("Now Bilbo of Bag End loved rest")
- "Fourteen Twenty" by [Hartley Patterson](../H/Hartley_Patterson.md) (column on Tolkien Society committee business)
