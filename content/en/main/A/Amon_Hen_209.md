<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 209</b> is the two-hundred-and-ninth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)")*, published in [January](/wiki/January) [2008](/wiki/2008).

The article "Some Family Connections with J.R.R. Tolkien" by B.S. Benedikz contains many [reminiscences](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien"), and also includes two [quotes attributed](/wiki/Index:Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Writings by J.R.R. Tolkien") to [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien").
