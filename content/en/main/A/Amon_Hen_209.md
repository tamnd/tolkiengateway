<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 209</b> is the two-hundred-and-ninth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*, published in [January](../J/January.md) [2008](../_/2008.md).

The article "Some Family Connections with J.R.R. Tolkien" by B.S. Benedikz contains many [reminiscences](../../index/R/Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and also includes two [quotes attributed](../../index/W/Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
