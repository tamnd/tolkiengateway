**Aïlior** were probably the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") of lakes and pools, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ail_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ail-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Aïlior* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), probably being related to the word *[ailin](/wiki/Ailin "Ailin")* ("lake").<sup id="cite_ref-Ail_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ail-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the *[Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon)*, there also appear the words <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ailo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ailo (page does not exist)">ailo</a></i> ("a lake, pool") and its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ail&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ail (page does not exist)">ail</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
