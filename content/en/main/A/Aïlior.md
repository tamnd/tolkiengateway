**Aïlior** were probably the [fays](../F/Fays.md) of lakes and pools, according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ail_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ail-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Aïlior* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), probably being related to the word *[ailin](Ailin.md)* ("lake").<sup id="cite_ref-Ail_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ail-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the *[Gnomish Lexicon](../G/Gnomish_Lexicon.md)*, there also appear the words <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ailo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ailo (page does not exist)">ailo</a></i> ("a lake, pool") and its [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) cognate <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ail&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ail (page does not exist)">ail</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
