**Aulendil** was the second son and third child of [Vardamir Nólimon](/wiki/Vardamir "Vardamir"), the heir of [Elros Tar-Minyatur](/wiki/Elros "Elros"), first [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). His older brother took up the [Sceptre](/wiki/Sceptre) as [Tar-Amandil](/wiki/Tar-Amandil). Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aulendil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name, meaning "Servant of [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB)" according to [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Paul Strack explains that according to ordinary nomenclature, it should mean "Friend of Aulë", because of the suffix *[-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil")* ("friend", "lover", "devoted to").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
