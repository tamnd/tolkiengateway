<i><b>A Spring Harvest</b></i> is a collection of poetry by [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith), one of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.). It contains [a prefatory note](/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_A_Spring_Harvest_Note.png "File:J.R.R. Tolkien - A Spring Harvest Note.png") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## History {#History}

The poems were edited by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Despite some claims to the contrary,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman) was not involved in editing. He later told [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) he was credited alongside Tolkien at the latter's request, since they were the only two surviving members of the "Great Four".<sup id="cite_ref-Biblio_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biblio-2">[2]</a></sup>

Several fragments of the poems were reproduced in [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth)'s *[Tolkien and the Great War](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War)*, who notes that while this collection predated the flurry of soldier's poetry - much of it published by Erskine - it had the same disenchantment and desillusion as most others.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Also, some poems were reproduced in [Mark Atherton](/wiki/Mark_Atherton)'s book *[There and Back Again: J.R.R. Tolkien and the Origins of The Hobbit](/wiki/There_and_Back_Again:_J.R.R._Tolkien_and_the_Origins_of_The_Hobbit)*. Atherton notes that [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) is currently working on a new edition of *A Spring Harvest*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
