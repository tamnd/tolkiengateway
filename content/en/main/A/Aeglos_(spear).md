**Aeglos** was the spear of King [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) which was greatly feared by the enemy. It was carried by the [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") to the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance), until Gil-galad fell in combat against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) himself, on the slopes of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom) [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3431](/wiki/Second_Age_3431 "Second Age 3431").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology and names {#Etymology_and_names}

*Aeglos* is the name of [a plant](</wiki/Aeglos_(plant)> "Aeglos (plant)") and means "snow-point"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "icicle".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The element *aeg* means "point", from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [AYAK](/wiki/AYAK) ("sharp, pointed");<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and the element *los* means "snow".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* prior to 2005, the spear is spelled *Aiglos*. Tolkien, however, commented in a manuscript that the more correct [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) spelling is in fact *Aeglos*.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Aeglos is a Holy weapon with magical abilities.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Aiglos, only playable by Warriors, gives a direct influence against Elves.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gil-galad is seen wielding the spear. <a href="/wiki/Warren_Mahy" title="Warren Mahy">Warren Mahy</a> desgined the weapon.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2017: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War" title="Middle-earth: Shadow of War">Middle-earth: Shadow of War</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Aeglos is wielded by <a href="/wiki/Celebrimbor" title="Celebrimbor">Celebrimbor</a>&#39;s wraith. The blade bears the inscription: &#34;Gil-galad wields a well made spear / The Orc will fear my point of ice / When he sees me, in fear of death / He will know my name: Aeglos&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2018: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Gil-galad wields the spear during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad" title="Battle of Dagorlad">Battle of Dagorlad</a>. After <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>&#39;s fall, Aeglos is discovered perched atop a pillar-like pinnacle in the Plateau of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Gorgoroth" title="Gorgoroth">Gorgoroth</a>, where it continues to emit a bright light. It is left as a beacon of hope to those who traverse the scorched plains of <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>: <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 2 (page does not exist)">Season 2</a></b>

<dl><dd>In episode 7, <i><a href="/wiki/Doomed_to_Die" title="Doomed to Die">Doomed to Die</a></i>, Gil-galad wields Aeglos during <a href="/wiki/Sack_of_Eregion" title="Sack of Eregion">the Sack of Eregion</a>.</dd></dl>
