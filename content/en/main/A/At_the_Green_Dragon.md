**At the Green Dragon** is the ninth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) and [Sam](/wiki/Sam) are at the [Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon) inn in [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater). [Merry](/wiki/Merry) and [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin) are singing as well. Two older hobbits discuss rumors of strange creatures being seen in the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) and fear the outbreak of a war. Sam pines after [Rosie Cotton](/wiki/Rosie_Cotton). Soon, the tavern closes and Frodo heads back to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).

## Differences {#Differences}

The lyrics of Merry and Pippin's drinking song are adapted from two songs in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*: [Ho! Ho! Ho! To the Bottle I Go](/wiki/Ho!_Ho!_Ho!_To_the_Bottle_I_Go) from the chapter [A Short Cut to Mushrooms](/wiki/A_Short_Cut_to_Mushrooms) and [The Bath Song](/wiki/The_Bath_Song) from [A Conspiracy Unmasked](/wiki/A_Conspiracy_Unmasked).

This scene could be interpreted as a very loose adaptation of a conversation in [Chapter 2](/wiki/The_Shadow_of_the_Past "The Shadow of the Past") of the *The Fellowship of the Ring*. The book does depict unrest in the Shire as dwarves and other beings flee the growing power of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).
