**Ailinel** was the second of the three children of [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) and [Almarian](/wiki/Almarian) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Her older brother [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") later became King [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion); her younger sister was [Almiel](/wiki/Almiel).<sup id="cite_ref-Early_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Early-1">[1]</a></sup> She was the eldest out of Tar-Aldarion's sisters. Ailinel married [Orchaldor](/wiki/Orchaldor), who was descended from the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) and they had a son named [Soronto](/wiki/Soronto).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ailinel* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) suggests it means "Lake-woman" or something similar and consists of the elements *[ailin](/wiki/Ailin "Ailin")* ("lake") + the feminine suffix *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
