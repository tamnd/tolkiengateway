**Ailinel** was the second of the three children of [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md) and [Almarian](Almarian.md) of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). Her older brother [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) later became King [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md); her younger sister was [Almiel](Almiel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Early_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Early-1">[1]</a></sup> She was the eldest out of Tar-Aldarion's sisters. Ailinel married [Orchaldor](../O/Orchaldor.md), who was descended from the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) and they had a son named [Soronto](../S/Soronto.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ailinel* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) suggests it means "Lake-woman" or something similar and consists of the elements *[ailin](Ailin.md)* ("lake") + the feminine suffix *[-iel](../_/-iel.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
