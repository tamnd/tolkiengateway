**Anórien** was a region and fiefdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Geography {#Geography}

When Isildur and Anárion divided the South Kingdom of Gondor among them Anárion took the region between the river [Erui](/wiki/Erui) and the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to Mount [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin) and then west to the [Firienholt](/wiki/Firienholt) (back then called the North Wood).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus Anórien originally included Minas Tirith (back then called Minas Anor) and the land between the river Erui and the river Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> It is unknown if and when the land between the eastern end of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and the river Anduin between the falls of [Rauros](/wiki/Rauros) and the river Erui ceased to be a part of Anórien and if the land between the Erui and the Anduin became a part of Lebennin, Lossarnach or another region.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) was given to the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) to become the Kingdom of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), the western borders of Anórien were the [Mering Stream](/wiki/Mering_Stream) and the Mouths of the [Entwash](/wiki/Entwash),<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup> the Anduin and the west-cliffs of the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/40/Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png/300px-Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="170" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/4/40/Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png 1.5x" data-file-width="437" data-file-height="247"/></a><figcaption>Map of Anorien</figcaption></figure>

The land contained the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest) and fertile farmlands. The [Great West Road](/wiki/Great_West_Road) traversed the fief. The [Warning beacons of Gondor](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor) were located to the south of the road.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days), the region was occupied by [Pre-Númenóreans](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> These were separated relatives of the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth) while the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) migrated to the west during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). The aboriginal [Woses](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain") fled to the forests of that region, hunted by the [Men of Darkness](/wiki/Men_of_Darkness).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

At the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the region was given to [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) as part of the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile) and was named 'Anórien' after him. Anárion built [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) on [Amon Tirith](/wiki/Hill_of_Guard "Hill of Guard") of the [Mindolluin](/wiki/Mindolluin).

Peoples from Anórien, as well as from [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) and [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) joined with [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)") against [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir) in the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

As the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) spread, cities were evacuated and many fled to [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) and Anórien.

Steward [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) fortified the isle of [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros) to defend Anórien.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Anórien was one of the few fiefs which was dominated by the [Dúnedain of Gondor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Gondor); specifically, in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and the adjacent townlands where [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) was still known and used as a daily language.<sup id="cite_ref-languages_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-languages-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Language_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Language-11">[11]</a></sup> It would appear that the western part of Anórien (from [Min-Rimmon](/wiki/Min-Rimmon) westward) was deserted or depopulated by this time, as tidings of the coronation of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) went out into "*all parts of Gondor, from Min-Rimmon even to [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) and the far coasts of the sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

When [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) released a secondary force from the [Morannon](/wiki/Morannon), they overwhelmed the defenders of [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros), and used the island to cross into Anórien. They blocked the eastward progress of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) as they rode to Gondor's aid, though after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm) and 3000 Rohirrim chased them down and pushed them back out of Anórien.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anórien* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-14">[14]</a></sup> It means "Sunlending" in the language of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> Its meaning in modern English is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it literally means "Sun-lands" and is a compound of *[Anor](/wiki/Sun "Sun")* ("sun") and the plural *[-ien](/wiki/-nd "-nd")* of the place name suffix *-ian(d)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-14">[14]</a></sup>

The name does not refer to a climate characterised by the sun, but to the '[heraldic](/wiki/Heraldry "Heraldry")' name of [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
