**Anórien** was a region and fiefdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

When Isildur and Anárion divided the South Kingdom of Gondor among them Anárion took the region between the river [Erui](../E/Erui.md) and the river [Anduin](Anduin.md) to Mount [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md) and then west to the [Firienholt](../F/Firienholt.md) (back then called the North Wood).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus Anórien originally included Minas Tirith (back then called Minas Anor) and the land between the river Erui and the river Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> It is unknown if and when the land between the eastern end of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and the river Anduin between the falls of [Rauros](../R/Rauros.md) and the river Erui ceased to be a part of Anórien and if the land between the Erui and the Anduin became a part of Lebennin, Lossarnach or another region.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) was given to the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) to become the Kingdom of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), the western borders of Anórien were the [Mering Stream](../M/Mering_Stream.md) and the Mouths of the [Entwash](../E/Entwash.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup> the Anduin and the west-cliffs of the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/40/Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png/300px-Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="170" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/4/40/Mark_Fisher_-_Anorien.png 1.5x" data-file-width="437" data-file-height="247"/></a><figcaption>Map of Anorien</figcaption></figure>

The land contained the [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md) and fertile farmlands. The [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md) traversed the fief. The [Warning beacons of Gondor](../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md) were located to the south of the road.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md), the region was occupied by [Pre-Númenóreans](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> These were separated relatives of the [House of Haleth](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) while the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) migrated to the west during the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). The aboriginal [Woses](../D/Drúedain.md) fled to the forests of that region, hunted by the [Men of Darkness](../M/Men_of_Darkness.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

At the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the region was given to [Anárion](Anárion.md) as part of the [Realms in Exile](../R/Realms_in_Exile.md) and was named 'Anórien' after him. Anárion built [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md) on [Amon Tirith](../H/Hill_of_Guard.md) of the [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md).

Peoples from Anórien, as well as from [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) and [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) joined with [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) against [Castamir](../C/Castamir.md) in the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

As the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) spread, cities were evacuated and many fled to [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) and Anórien.

Steward [Túrin II](../T/Túrin_II.md) fortified the isle of [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) to defend Anórien.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

By the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Anórien was one of the few fiefs which was dominated by the [Dúnedain of Gondor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Gondor.md); specifically, in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and the adjacent townlands where [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) was still known and used as a daily language.<sup id="cite_ref-languages_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-languages-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Language_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Language-11">[11]</a></sup> It would appear that the western part of Anórien (from [Min-Rimmon](../M/Min-Rimmon.md) westward) was deserted or depopulated by this time, as tidings of the coronation of [Aragorn](Aragorn.md) went out into "*all parts of Gondor, from Min-Rimmon even to [Pinnath Gelin](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md) and the far coasts of the sea*".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

When [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) released a secondary force from the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md), they overwhelmed the defenders of [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md), and used the island to cross into Anórien. They blocked the eastward progress of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) as they rode to Gondor's aid, though after the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md) and 3000 Rohirrim chased them down and pushed them back out of Anórien.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anórien* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-14">[14]</a></sup> It means "Sunlending" in the language of the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> Its meaning in modern English is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests that it literally means "Sun-lands" and is a compound of *[Anor](../S/Sun.md)* ("sun") and the plural *[-ien](../_/-nd.md)* of the place name suffix *-ian(d)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-14">[14]</a></sup>

The name does not refer to a climate characterised by the sun, but to the '[heraldic](../H/Heraldry.md)' name of [Anárion](Anárion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>
