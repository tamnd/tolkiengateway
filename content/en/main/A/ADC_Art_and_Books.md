**ADC Art &amp; Books** is a British dealer in books, specialising in the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and Middle-earth inspired artwork. Under the name *ADC Publications Ltd*, they also publish books and other media related to Tolkien and his legendarium.

ADC Art &amp; Books is the agent for the following Tolkien-inspired artists: [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), [Ruth Lacon](/wiki/Ruth_Lacon), [Jef Murray](/wiki/Jef_Murray), and [Peter Pracownik](/wiki/Peter_Pracownik).

## Publications {#Publications}

- [2005](/wiki/2005): *[The Art of Ruth Lacon](/wiki/The_Art_of_Ruth_Lacon)*
- [2007](/wiki/2007): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Hidden_Door&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hidden Door (page does not exist)">The Hidden Door: Songs in the Key of Enchantment</a></i> (cd)
- [2009](/wiki/2009): *[Black &amp; White Ogre Country: The Lost Tales of Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Black_%26_White_Ogre_Country "Black & White Ogre Country")*
- [2009](/wiki/2009): *[The Epic Realm of Tolkien](/wiki/The_Epic_Realm_of_Tolkien)*
- (Cancelled): *[Wheelbarrows at Dawn: Memories of Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Wheelbarrows_at_Dawn "Wheelbarrows at Dawn")*
