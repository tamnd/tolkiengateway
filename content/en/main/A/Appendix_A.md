**Appendix A** of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* contains historical background of the events in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) prior to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). Its main focus is on the [Mannish](../M/Men.md) kingdoms of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), and on the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of the line of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../D/Durin.md" title="Durin">Durin</a>. It also contains *[The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen](../T/The_Tale_of_Aragorn_and_Arwen.md)*.

## Contents {#Contents}

- The Númenórean Kings
    - Númenor <small>(A synopsis of the events of <i><a href="../T/The_Silmarillion.md" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a></i>, especially the <i><a href="Akallabêth.md" title="Akallabêth">Akallabêth</a></i>)</small>
    - The Realms in Exile <small>(A list of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md" title="Kings of Arnor">kings of Arnor</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md" title="Kings of Gondor">Gondor</a>, including the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md" title="Chieftains of the Dúnedain">Chieftains of the Dúnedain</a> and the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../R/Ruling_Steward.md" title="Ruling Steward">Ruling Stewards</a> of Gondor.)</small>
        - The Northern Line: Heirs of Isildur
        - The Southern Line: Heirs of Anarion
    - Eriador, Arnor, and the Heirs of Isildur <small>(The history of the Northern Kingdom, through the time of <a href="Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn II</a>)</small>
    - Gondor and the Heirs of Anarion <small>(The history of Gondor, including a few details of the lives of <a href="../D/Denethor.md" title="Denethor">Denethor II</a>, <a href="../B/Boromir.md" title="Boromir">Boromir</a>, and <a href="../F/Faramir.md" title="Faramir">Faramir</a>.)</small>
        - The Stewards
    - [The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen](../T/The_Tale_of_Aragorn_and_Arwen.md) <small>(Traces the love story of that couple, including their deaths.)</small>
- The House of Eorl <small>(The history of <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a>.)</small>
    - The Kings of the Mark
- Durin's Folk <small>(Focuses on the Dwarves of that line. Some of the events leading up to the <a href="../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md" title="Quest of Erebor">Quest of Erebor</a> are given. This section contains a family tree of Durin, including some details about <a href="../G/Gimli.md" title="Gimli">Gimli</a>.)</small>

## Composition {#Composition}

After finishing the book in [1948](../_/1948.md), Tolkien expanded further the Appendix A before publication around [1950](../_/1950.md), even after saying to [Milton Waldman](../M/Milton_Waldman.md) (who expressed interest in publishing the book) that he would try to cut the book's length.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
