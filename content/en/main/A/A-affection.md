**A-affection** or **A-mutation** is a phonology phenomenon in the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) language through its evolution from [Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin), regarding the final vowels of a word.

The phenomenon is summarized as *-iCa* &gt; *-eC*: the final *-a* vowel caused the preceding *i* to slide into *e*. It also caused the change of *u* into *o*.

Since all final vowels disappeared in mature Sindarin, final *-an* also disappeared, leaving as a trace of its existence the change of the preceding vowel.

The phenomenon is seen mostly in adjectives ending in *-ina* resulting in the common Sindarin adjectival ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-en&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-en (page does not exist)">-en</a></i>. Other endings which possibly show A-affection are <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eth (page does not exist)">-eth</a> (*-itta), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eb&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eb (page does not exist)">-eb</a> (*-ipa), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eg (page does not exist)">-eg</a> (*-ika).</i>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [OS](/wiki/OS) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rista&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rista (page does not exist)">rista</a> &gt; resta &gt; <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rest&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rest (page does not exist)">rest</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- OS <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Berina&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Berina (page does not exist)">berina</a> &gt; berena &gt; <a href="/wiki/Beren" title="Beren">beren</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [CE](/wiki/CE) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Taika&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Taika (page does not exist)">taika</a> &gt; taeka &gt; <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Taeg&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Taeg (page does not exist)">taeg</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- CE \**aklarikwâ<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> &gt; aklarepa &gt; [aglareb](/wiki/Aglareb "Aglareb")*
- OS \*<i>kulda &gt; kolda &gt; <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Coll&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Coll (page does not exist)">coll</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Mithrimin](/wiki/Mithrimin) perhaps did not undergo the A-affection.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Hence we have OS \**lâmina* &gt; S. <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Loven&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Loven (page does not exist)">loven</a></i> but M. *[lómin](/wiki/L%C3%B3min "Lómin")*
