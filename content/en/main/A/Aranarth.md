**Aranarth** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1938](../T/Third_Age_1938.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 3]</a></sup> - [2106](../T/Third_Age_2106.md),<sup id="cite_ref-NorthArvedui_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NorthArvedui-5">[2]</a></sup> died aged 168) was the first of the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md), and would have been the sixteenth [King of Arthedain](../K/King_of_Arthedain.md) had [Angmar](Angmar.md) not destroyed the realm.

## History {#History}

Aranarth was the son of [Arvedui](Arvedui.md), the son of King [Araphant](Araphant.md) of Arthedain,<sup id="cite_ref-NorthArvedui_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NorthArvedui-5">[2]</a></sup> and [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md), the daughter of King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Ondoher_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ondoher-6">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA1940_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1940-7">[4]</a></sup> and as such, he was descended from both [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](Anárion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[6]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md) when Aranarth was a young man, the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) of [Angmar](Angmar.md) overran Arthedain and conquered [Fornost](../F/Fornost_Erain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[7]</a></sup> Most of the remnants of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) fled over the [Lune](../L/Lhûn.md) into [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md). However, Aranarth's father held out upon the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) until he escaped to the north. After hiding in abandoned dwarf-mines in the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md) the King and his men sought the help of the [Lossoth](../L/Lossoth.md), who aided out of pity and fear.<sup id="cite_ref-Arvedui_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arvedui-11">[8]</a></sup>

Aranarth reported to [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) of the King's flight to the north. Círdan sent a ship to the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md) to seek and rescue him.<sup id="cite_ref-Arvedui_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arvedui-11">[8]</a></sup> However, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[9]</a></sup> a short time after Arvedui and his men had boarded the ship, a storm drove the ship against the ice and its hull was broken. Arvedui drowned and the two *[palantíri](../P/Palantíri.md)* the King had taken with him during the fall of Fornost were lost in the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Arvedui_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arvedui-11">[8]</a></sup> As a result, Aranarth nominally became the King of Arthedain in exile in Lindon.

In the same year, the remnants of the Dúnedain, forces from Lindon and a large expeditionary force from Gondor, led by [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md), the son of King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Eärnil.md" title="Eärnil">Eärnil</a> of Gondor, destroyed the kingdom of Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-13">[10]</a></sup> However, the Kingdom of the Dúnedain of the north was discontinued, because there were now only few Dúnedain in the north and the population of Eriador was reduced.<sup id="cite_ref-Aranarth_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aranarth-14">[11]</a></sup> Aranarth took the title of [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[12]</a></sup>

Aranarth's son [Arahael](Arahael.md) was born and raised in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), as were all the sons of chieftains after him; and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) was entrusted with the safekeeping of the heirlooms of the North-kingdom: the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md), the shards of [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md), the [Star of Elendil](../S/Star_of_Elendil.md), and the [Sceptre of Annúminas](../S/Sceptre_of_Annúminas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aranarth_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aranarth-14">[11]</a></sup>

Aranarth died in 2106, after ruling for 130 years - longer than any other chieftain.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aranarth* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but the Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Noble King".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-16">[13]</a></sup> It is suggested to be a compound of *[aran](Aran.md)* ("king", "lord", "chief", \[lit.\] high or noble person) and *arth* ("noble"),<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-16">[13]</a></sup> which may be related to the initial element of <i><a href="Arthedain.md" title="Arthedain"><b>Arth</b>edain</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[14]</a></sup>

[David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) suggested that the final element could be <i>*arth</i> ("noble"), which would be cognate to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[arta](Arta.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A late manuscript of what would later become [Appendix A](Appendix_A.md) lists Aranarth's date of birth as [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1938](../T/Third_Age_1938.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[1]</a></sup> but in the same manuscript,<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[16]</a></sup> as well as in [Appendix A](Appendix_A.md), "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion"<sup id="cite_ref-Ondoher_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ondoher-6">[3]</a></sup> and in the [Tale of Years](Appendix_B.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA1940_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1940-7">[4]</a></sup> his parents' wedding year is given as [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1940](../T/Third_Age_1940.md). It is possible that the year of birth being two years before the year of marriage of his parents, who were of royal descent, was an oversight.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Aranarth is a Dúnadan based in Fornost, famous for his tracking abilities and described as a man &#34;who leaves no footprints&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[17]</a></sup></dd></dl>
