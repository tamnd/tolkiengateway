**Aranarth** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1938](/wiki/Third_Age_1938 "Third Age 1938")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 3]</a></sup> - [2106](/wiki/Third_Age_2106 "Third Age 2106"),<sup id="cite_ref-NorthArvedui_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NorthArvedui-5">[2]</a></sup> died aged 168) was the first of the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain), and would have been the sixteenth [King of Arthedain](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain) had [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) not destroyed the realm.

## History {#History}

Aranarth was the son of [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), the son of King [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) of Arthedain,<sup id="cite_ref-NorthArvedui_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NorthArvedui-5">[2]</a></sup> and [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel), the daughter of King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor),<sup id="cite_ref-Ondoher_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ondoher-6">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA1940_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1940-7">[4]</a></sup> and as such, he was descended from both [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[6]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974") when Aranarth was a young man, the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) overran Arthedain and conquered [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost_Erain "Fornost Erain").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[7]</a></sup> Most of the remnants of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) fled over the [Lune](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn") into [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon). However, Aranarth's father held out upon the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) until he escaped to the north. After hiding in abandoned dwarf-mines in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) the King and his men sought the help of the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth), who aided out of pity and fear.<sup id="cite_ref-Arvedui_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arvedui-11">[8]</a></sup>

Aranarth reported to [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) of the King's flight to the north. Círdan sent a ship to the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel) to seek and rescue him.<sup id="cite_ref-Arvedui_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arvedui-11">[8]</a></sup> However, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975")<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[9]</a></sup> a short time after Arvedui and his men had boarded the ship, a storm drove the ship against the ice and its hull was broken. Arvedui drowned and the two *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* the King had taken with him during the fall of Fornost were lost in the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-Arvedui_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arvedui-11">[8]</a></sup> As a result, Aranarth nominally became the King of Arthedain in exile in Lindon.

In the same year, the remnants of the Dúnedain, forces from Lindon and a large expeditionary force from Gondor, led by [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur), the son of King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil" title="Eärnil">Eärnil</a> of Gondor, destroyed the kingdom of Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-13">[10]</a></sup> However, the Kingdom of the Dúnedain of the north was discontinued, because there were now only few Dúnedain in the north and the population of Eriador was reduced.<sup id="cite_ref-Aranarth_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aranarth-14">[11]</a></sup> Aranarth took the title of [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[12]</a></sup>

Aranarth's son [Arahael](/wiki/Arahael) was born and raised in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), as were all the sons of chieftains after him; and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) was entrusted with the safekeeping of the heirlooms of the North-kingdom: the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir), the shards of [Narsil](/wiki/Narsil), the [Star of Elendil](/wiki/Star_of_Elendil), and the [Sceptre of Annúminas](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas).<sup id="cite_ref-Aranarth_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aranarth-14">[11]</a></sup>

Aranarth died in 2106, after ruling for 130 years - longer than any other chieftain.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aranarth* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but the Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Noble King".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-16">[13]</a></sup> It is suggested to be a compound of *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* ("king", "lord", "chief", \[lit.\] high or noble person) and *arth* ("noble"),<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-16">[13]</a></sup> which may be related to the initial element of <i><a href="/wiki/Arthedain" title="Arthedain"><b>Arth</b>edain</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[14]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggested that the final element could be <i>*arth</i> ("noble"), which would be cognate to [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[arta](/wiki/Arta "Arta")*.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

A late manuscript of what would later become [Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A) lists Aranarth's date of birth as [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1938](/wiki/Third_Age_1938 "Third Age 1938"),<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-3">[1]</a></sup> but in the same manuscript,<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[16]</a></sup> as well as in [Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A), "Gondor and the Heirs of Anárion"<sup id="cite_ref-Ondoher_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ondoher-6">[3]</a></sup> and in the [Tale of Years](/wiki/Appendix_B "Appendix B"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA1940_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1940-7">[4]</a></sup> his parents' wedding year is given as [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1940](/wiki/Third_Age_1940 "Third Age 1940"). It is possible that the year of birth being two years before the year of marriage of his parents, who were of royal descent, was an oversight.
