**A Light from the Shadows: The Spiritual Heart of JRR Tolkien** is a [2026](../_/2026.md) religious book about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In this inspiring new book on the spiritual heart of JRR Tolkien, Fr Michael Halsall takes the reader on a journey alongside characters who vividly embody the key themes of Tolkien’s literary vision: the urgent need to re-enchant our narratives, the ongoing struggle between good and evil, the question of what it means to be virtuous, and the inescapable reality of death – and what may lie beyond it – for every human being.
</p><p>A Light from the Shadows is essential reading for every Tolkien or Lord of the Rings fan, and for anyone interested in the Catholic influence on one of the greatest fantasy writers of all time.
</p>
