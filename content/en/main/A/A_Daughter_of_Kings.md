**A Daughter of Kings** is the eighteenth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and the twenty-sixth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) opens a chest and pulls out a sword. As she practices sword forms, [Aragorn](Aragorn.md) appears, telling her that she "*has some skill with a blade*". As she puts her sword away, Aragorn asks her what she fears.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>A cage. To stay behind bars until use and old age accept them. And all chance of valor has gone beyond recall or desire.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Éowyn on her fears, to Aragorn</cite></p>
</blockquote>

Aragorn looks at her wonderingly, and tells her that he does not think that will be her fate. After lightly bowing to her, he walks away.
