**Argonui** was the thirteenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Dúnedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Argonui_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonui-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Argonui succeeded his father, [Arathorn I](Arathorn_I.md), in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2848](../T/Third_Age_2848.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arathorn_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arathorn-3">[3]</a></sup>

During his reign, [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) was born in [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) in [2890](../T/Third_Age_2890.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> as well as [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md), [Aragorn](Aragorn.md)'s mother, in [2907](../T/Third_Age_2907.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Towards the end of his rule, in [2911](../T/Third_Age_2911.md), the [Fell Winter](<../F/Fell_Winter_(Third_Age).md>) began, with the river [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) freezing over. This was a catalyst for the [White Wolves](../W/White_Wolves.md) invading [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) from the North.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the last year of his reign, great floods devastated [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md) and [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md), leaving the town of [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) ruined and deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Argonui died in [2912](../T/Third_Age_2912.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Argonui_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonui-2">[2]</a></sup> after having ruled for 64 years, and was followed by his son [Arador](Arador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Arador_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arador-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Argonui* is in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), possibly meaning "Royal Valour" according to *[Tolkiendil](<../T/Tolkiendil_(Tolkien_society).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The first element of the name is *[ar(a)](Ara.md)*- ("noble, royal, high"); however, the second element is unclear, though it might consist of the noun *[caun](../C/Caun.md)* ("prince, chief, head") + an adjectival suffix <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-ui&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-ui (page does not exist)">-ui</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Argonui's year of birth is only mentioned in earlier manuscript versions of what would later become Appendix A. In general, J.R.R. Tolkien did not include the year of birth of kings and rulers in Appendix A or Appendix B.

In the later manuscript C Argonui's years of birth and death were [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2757](../T/Third_Age_2757.md) and [2912](../T/Third_Age_2912.md), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-ManuscriptC_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ManuscriptC-12">[12]</a></sup> In the same manuscript it is also mentioned that [Dírhael](../D/Dírhael.md), the maternal grandfather of [Aragorn](Aragorn.md), was descended from Arathorn I "by a younger branch".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In the earlier manuscript B, the years of Argonui's birth and death were [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2746](../T/Third_Age_2746.md) and [2901](../T/Third_Age_2901.md), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
