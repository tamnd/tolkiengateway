**Angerthas Daeron** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). 'Daeron's Runes') was the full extension of the [Certhas Daeron](/wiki/Certhas_Daeron) which was used by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) to represent [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and other languages.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

## Structure {#Structure}

They were renamed Angerthas because the Certhas of Daeron were extended and added several more cirth. The adding of 2 whole new rows (CH-row and QU-row) was attributed to them.

The addition of these caused the following: a new certh for *h* to replace the previous certh <em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">#</em> which became *ch*. This certh was preferred for *ch* because its shape seemed to be between the shapes of the T and K cirth (<em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">8</em> T + <em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">e</em> K &gt; <em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">#</em> CH), to represent how the *ch* sound also (phonologically) exist between *t* and *c*. There was also a new certh for *nd*, which as Tolkien notes, its form wasn’t much related to the dental cirth.

Some of the new letters were used for sounds of Quenya (and other tongues) not found in Sindarin. In that stage, the Angerthas reached its fullest extent, but for most forms of writing other than carving, the Tengwar were used.

## Notes {#Notes}

Not to be confused with Sindarin <i>ch</i>!</span>

<li id="cite_note-6"><span class="mw-cite-backlink"><a href="#cite_ref-6">↑</a></span> <span class="reference-text">Maybe near that time, this new certh was made for the value of <i>h</i>, similar in shape to the former <em style="font-family: &#39;Cirth Erebor&#39;; font-style: normal;">#</em> (and to the tengwa <a href="/wiki/Hyarmen" title="Hyarmen">Hyarmen</a> <em style="font: normal 1.7em/1.5em &#39;Tengwar Annatar&#39;, &#39;Tengwar Quenya&#39;, &#39;Tengwar Sindarin&#39;, &#39;Tengwar Formal&#39;, &#39;Tengwar Parmaite&#39;, &#39;Tengwar Elfica&#39;;">9</em>).</span>

## History {#History}

By the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) were the dominant writing script, but the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain) used the runes in formal documents, as a "matter of lore".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 2]</a></sup> The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) made use of the [cirth](/wiki/Cirth "Cirth") and made a name for them in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Certar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Certar (page does not exist)">Certar</a></i>.

The [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) adapted this system to [Angerthas Moria](/wiki/Angerthas_Moria), as they were ideal for carved inscriptions, which they preferred.
