<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 298</b> is the two-hundred-and-ninety-eighth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*, published in December [2022](../_/2022.md).

## Contents {#Contents}

- Front and back cover art: Death of Elenwë by Sara Morello
- **2** - From the Editor’s Desk
- **4** - Bilbo Baggins as a Literary Embodiment of J.R.R. Tolkien by Emilian Wojnowski
- **8** - Poem: Théodred by Eren Goetz
- **10** - From Goth Persephone to our Lady of Sorrows: The Evolving Nature of Nienna by Marilyn R. Pukkila
- **17** - Oxonmoot
- **18** - Centerfold: Winter in the Shire by [Matěj Čadil](../M/Matěj_Čadil.md)
- **20** - Snow Games by Helen Armstrong
- **24** - Review of [The Fall of Númenor: and Other Tales from the Second Age of Middle-earth](../T/The_Fall_of_Númenor.md) by Amanda Drak
- **26** - Lembas Extra 2022 Númenor by [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md)
- **28** - Call for Papers: The Mighty and Frail Númenor
- **32** - Behind Glass Doors by Ian Collier
- **34** - All Brân
