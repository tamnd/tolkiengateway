**Arden Ray Smith** (January 4, 1966) is a member of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md) and holds a Ph.D. in Germanic Linguistics from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley" title="wikipedia:University of California, Berkeley">University of Californa, Berkeley</a>. He has published numerous articles relating to the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He was a columnist and editor of *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)*, for which he wrote the popular column "Transitions in Translations", in which odd elements in translations of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work were described and commented upon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
