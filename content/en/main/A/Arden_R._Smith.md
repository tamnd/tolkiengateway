**Arden Ray Smith** (January 4, 1966) is a member of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](/wiki/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship) and holds a Ph.D. in Germanic Linguistics from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley" title="wikipedia:University of California, Berkeley">University of Californa, Berkeley</a>. He has published numerous articles relating to the invented languages of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He was a columnist and editor of *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*, for which he wrote the popular column "Transitions in Translations", in which odd elements in translations of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s work were described and commented upon.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
