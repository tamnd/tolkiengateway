**Alan Howard** was an English actor. He was the voice for both [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and his [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*.
