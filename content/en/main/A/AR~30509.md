**AR** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") meaning "beyond, further than".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- Root [ARI/ARAN/ARAT](/wiki/ARI "ARI")
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ar](/wiki/Ar#Quenya "Ar")*
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[ar-](/wiki/Ar#Sindarin "Ar")*

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In [the *Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") there are two homophonous roots: AR<sup>1</sup> is a root which signifies "day" and it seems to have been redefined by Tolkien (cf. [AS](/wiki/AS)); the root AR<sup>2</sup> has no exact signification although its derivatives relate to "outsideness"; the general sense seems to be somewhat retained for the root AR as given in *Eldarin Roots and Stems*).<sup id="cite_ref-LR348_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR348-3">[3]</a></sup>
