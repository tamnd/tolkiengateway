<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 173</b> is the one-hundred-and-seventy-third issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*, published in January [2002](../_/2002.md).

The journal includes a large excerpt from a formerly unpublished [letter by J.R.R. Tolkien to Christopher Fettes](../L/Letter_to_Christopher_Fettes.md).
