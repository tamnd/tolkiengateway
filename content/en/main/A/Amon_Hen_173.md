<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 173</b> is the one-hundred-and-seventy-third issue of the [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)")*, published in January [2002](/wiki/2002).

The journal includes a large excerpt from a formerly unpublished [letter by J.R.R. Tolkien to Christopher Fettes](/wiki/Letter_to_Christopher_Fettes "Letter to Christopher Fettes").
