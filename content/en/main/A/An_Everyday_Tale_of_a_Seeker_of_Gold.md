<i><b>An Everyday Tale of a Seeker of Gold</b></i> is a Text-Adventure made by [John Wilson](/wiki/John_Wilson)<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>. Together with *[Bulbo and the Lizard King](/wiki/Bulbo_and_the_Lizard_King)* and *[Fuddo and Slam](/wiki/Fuddo_and_Slam)* it formed [*The Balrogian Trilogy*](/wiki/The_Balrogian_Trilogy "The Balrogian Trilogy").<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> It is the prequel to *[Retarded Creatures and Caverns](/wiki/Retarded_Creatures_and_Caverns)*.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup>

The game is a parody of the 1982 video game *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1982_video_game)> "The Hobbit (1982 video game)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-3">[3]</a></sup> It was originally supposed to be called *The Boggit,* but [Delta 4 Software](/wiki/Delta_4_Software) had already released [a game with that name](/wiki/The_Boggit "The Boggit"), so Wilson changed the title.
