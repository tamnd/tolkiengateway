**Aiwenórë**, "Bird-land", is the lower region of [Vista](/wiki/Vista), the breathable air, and that part of the atmosphere wherein birds may fly.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), *Aiwe* means "(small) bird", and *nórë* means "land" or "country". <span></span>
