<i><b>am</b></i> is a preposition meaning "up, upwards, upon" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It derives from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/AM-" title="AM-">AM<sup>2</sup>-</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Examples:

- <i><a href="/wiki/Amlach" title="Amlach"><b>Am</b>lach</a></i> = "Uprising Flame"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Amroth" title="Amroth"><b>Am</b>roth</a></i> = "Up-climber"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Amloth" title="Amloth"><b>am</b>loth</a></i> = "Uprising Flowers"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Amon" title="Amon"><b>am</b>on</a></i> = "Hill"

## Cognates {#Cognates}
