## History {#History}

After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), Anárion and his brother [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) established and shared the realm of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), setting thrones side by side in the Great Hall of Osgiliath. Unbeknownst to them, [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) also survived the Downfall and had reestablished himself in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). When he deemed the time to be right, Sauron led a great force that [took](/wiki/First_Fall_of_Minas_Ithil "First Fall of Minas Ithil") [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil), destroyed the [White Tree of Isildur](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor"), and forced Isildur and his family (with a seedling of the tree) to flee down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). While Isildur journeyed to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), Anárion held Osgiliath and even drove the Enemy back to the mountains. However, Sauron gathered new forces to attack so that Anárion had to maintain a defence of the city until help could arrive. With the coming of Elendil and [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) to the [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) the threat to the city was ended.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
