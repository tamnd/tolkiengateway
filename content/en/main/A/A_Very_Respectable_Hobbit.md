**A Very Respectable Hobbit** is the fourteenth track of disc two of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition.md)*, by [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md). It is the first of four 'exclusive bonus tracks'.
