**Avathar** was the land in the south-eastern part of [Aman](/wiki/Aman), south of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). It formed a dark strip of land at the feet of the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) Mountains. It seems to have been totally deserted and forgotten and in ancient times [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) made her home there.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In "The Later [Quenta Silmarillion](/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion)" section of *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") translates Avathar as "The Shadows" in "ancient" [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya);<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> this translation also appears in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, in the "[Quendi and Eldar](/wiki/Quendi_and_Eldar)", Tolkien states that Avathar has no [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) translation, meaning that it must have been adapted from its [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> (however, in the Index to that book it still says "The Shadows" in a short description<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>)

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the region where Ungoliant dwelt prior to the Darkening of Valinor was called *Arvalin*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
