**Adamanta Chubb** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") who flourished around the 29th century of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Adamanta was the wife of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"). They had twelve children: [Isengrim III](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_III "Isengrim Took III"), [Hildigard](/wiki/Hildigard_Took "Hildigard Took"), [Isumbras IV](/wiki/Isumbras_Took_IV "Isumbras Took IV"), [Hildigrim](/wiki/Hildigrim_Took "Hildigrim Took"), [Isembold](/wiki/Isembold_Took "Isembold Took"), [Hildifons](/wiki/Hildifons_Took "Hildifons Took"), [Isembard](/wiki/Isembard_Took "Isembard Took"), [Hildibrand](/wiki/Hildibrand_Took "Hildibrand Took"), [Belladonna](/wiki/Belladonna_Took "Belladonna Took"), [Donnamira](/wiki/Donnamira_Took "Donnamira Took"), [Mirabella](/wiki/Mirabella_Took "Mirabella Took"), [Isengar](/wiki/Isengar_Took "Isengar Took").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Adamanta comes from from "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/adamant" title="wikipedia:adamant">adamant</a>", a mythical unbreakable substance, now referring to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/diamond" title="wikipedia:diamond">diamonds</a>. Jewel names were common among female Hobbits.
