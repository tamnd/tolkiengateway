The **Authorities** are a group mentioned fleetingly in a discussion regarding the [Riddle-game](/wiki/Riddle-game) between [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum).

Considering that the rules of the Game were sacred, it is possible that the Authorities simply referred to the [Valar](/wiki/Valar),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or just to the [Aratar](/wiki/Aratar), who were coincidentally also called the Authorities.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
