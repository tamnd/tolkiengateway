The **Andrath** was a defile between the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) (to the west) and the [South Downs](../S/South_Downs.md) (to the east) in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). Through this narrow way just south of [Bree](../B/Bree.md) passed the [North-South Road](../N/North-South_Road.md) ([Greenway](../G/Greenway.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Ind_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ind-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When the [Black Riders](../N/Nazgûl.md) came up the Greenway in search of the [One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), the [Black Captain](../W/Witch-king.md) established a camp at Andrath. From there he sent some Riders out to patrol while he visited the Barrow-downs.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Andrath* means "Long Climb",<sup id="cite_ref-Ind_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ind-1">[1]</a></sup> apparently consisting of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) elements *[and](And.md)* + *[rath](../R/Rath.md#Sindarin)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Andrath is a prominent area within <a href="../B/Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a> and contains the prominent &#34;Halecatch Lake&#34;. The &#34;South-guard Ruins&#34; on the <a href="../G/Greenway.md" title="Greenway">Greenway</a> divide into norther and southern Andrath, the latter belonging to the in-game <a href="../C/Cardolan.md" title="Cardolan">Cardolan</a> region.</dd></dl>
