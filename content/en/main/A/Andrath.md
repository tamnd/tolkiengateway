The **Andrath** was a defile between the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) (to the west) and the [South Downs](/wiki/South_Downs) (to the east) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). Through this narrow way just south of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) passed the [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road) ([Greenway](/wiki/Greenway)).<sup id="cite_ref-Ind_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ind-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") came up the Greenway in search of the [One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), the [Black Captain](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") established a camp at Andrath. From there he sent some Riders out to patrol while he visited the Barrow-downs.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Andrath* means "Long Climb",<sup id="cite_ref-Ind_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ind-1">[1]</a></sup> apparently consisting of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) elements *[and](/wiki/And "And")* + *[rath](/wiki/Rath#Sindarin "Rath")*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Andrath is a prominent area within <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a> and contains the prominent &#34;Halecatch Lake&#34;. The &#34;South-guard Ruins&#34; on the <a href="/wiki/Greenway" title="Greenway">Greenway</a> divide into norther and southern Andrath, the latter belonging to the in-game <a href="/wiki/Cardolan" title="Cardolan">Cardolan</a> region.</dd></dl>
