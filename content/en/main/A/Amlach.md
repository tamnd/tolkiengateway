**Amlach** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [337](/wiki/First_Age_337 "First Age 337")) was the son of [Imlach](/wiki/Imlach) and a member of the [House of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the years after they crossed the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), the [Men](/wiki/Men) who would become known as the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) dwelt mainly in encampments at [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad), south of [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth). There, they were divided into two factions: the [Elf-friends](/wiki/Elf-friend "Elf-friend"), who argued that Men should ally themselves with the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) against [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), and the dissenters who wished to leave the war to the Elves.

Around [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [369](/wiki/First_Age_369 "First Age 369") a council was held to debate the issue, and "Amlach" spoke forcefully against joining the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in their war. After the council, though, it was discovered that Amlach had not even been present, and the speaker had been an emissary of Morgoth that had taken on his form. Amlach's anger at this deceit caused him to join the party of the Elf-friends, and he went north to [Himring](/wiki/Himring) to enter the service of the Elf-lord [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Amlach appears to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, though its meaning is not entirely certain. A possibility is "Uprising Flame" (from *[am](/wiki/Am "Am")* = "up, upwards" and *[lach](/wiki/Lhach "Lhach")* = "flame (leaping)").
