<i><b>ando</b></i> is a noun meaning "gate" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Ando.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/6/62/Ando.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Ando* is also the name in Quenya of the fifth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the first letter of the second grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiced stops" and it is the second letter in the dental or *t*-series of consonants, the *[Tincotéma](../T/Tincotéma.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> In the standard spelling of Quenya *Ando* represents **ND**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In all other modes this letter represents **D**.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Dó</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<br>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [AD](AD.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
