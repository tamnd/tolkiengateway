**Appendix B** is the second Appendix to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. It is entitled *The Tale of Years (Chronology of the Westlands)*. It primarily contains timelines (lists of years) from the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age"), [Third](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age"), and [Fourth](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") Ages and the events of those years. There are also a few brief narrative sections, including a short discussion of the [Wizards](/wiki/Wizards) and how [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) received the [Ring of Fire](/wiki/Narya "Narya") from [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<i><b>The Tale of Years</b></i> was said to have been put together at the [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials) (its library contained sources about [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), histories and legends about [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his descendants and other copies of [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") scribes) and with the assistance of material collected by [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck), who collected much information from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). Many of its dates are said to be conjectural but important, especially concerning the Second Age.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Appendix B is divided into several sub-sections:

<dl><dt>The Second Age</dt>
<dd>It covers the major events of the little-documented <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, concerning <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Rings_of_Power" title="Rings of Power">Rings of Power</a>.</dd>
<dt>The Third Age</dt>
<dd>It covers the years <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2" title="Third Age 2">2</a> - <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3017" title="Third Age 3017">3017</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">Third Age</a>, focusing mainly on events in <a href="/wiki/Arnor" title="Arnor">Arnor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, and <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">The Shire</a>.</dd>
<dt>The Great Years</dt>
<dd>It covers specific dates for particular events of the time period of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> (the years <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3018" title="Third Age 3018">3018</a> and <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a>); it begins with <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>&#39;s return to <a href="/wiki/Hobbiton" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a> and concludes with the destruction of the Ring. This section also gives a brief account of the battles of the Northern campaign during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> that are not mentioned in the main book.</dd>
<dt>The Chief Days from the Fall of Barad-dur to the End of the Third Age</dt>
<dd>It finishes the events of <i>The Lord of the Rings</i>, giving specific dates up through <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a>&#39;s return to <a href="/wiki/Bag_End" title="Bag End">Bag End</a>.</dd>
<dt>Later Events Concerning the Members of the Fellowship of the Ring</dt>
<dd>Chronology of the early <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Age" title="Fourth Age">Fourth Age</a> (although dates are given in the <a href="/wiki/Shire-reckoning" title="Shire-reckoning">Shire-reckoning</a>, and primarily follow what happened with the hobbits).  The last entry tells the final fate of <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, <a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a>.</dd></dl>
