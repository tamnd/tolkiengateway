**Araphant** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1789](../T/Third_Age_1789.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> - [1964](../T/Third_Age_1964.md),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 175) was the fourteenth king of [Arthedain](Arthedain.md), and came to the throne upon the death of his father, [Araval](Araval.md), in 1891.

## History {#History}

Despite his father's successes, [Angmar](Angmar.md) rebuilt its strength and continued its attacks on Arthedain which Araphant struggled to hold off with dwindling strength.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> Arthedain had been at war with Angmar for 500 years, and this was taking its toll on the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) as their resistance was starting to fail.

It was during Araphant's reign that the long silence and estrangement between the southern kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and the northern kingdom ended when Araphant took counsel with the [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md), [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md). They realised that a single force was co-ordinating the attacks on both of their kingdoms and that they should work together to combat this evil. In [1940](../T/Third_Age_1940.md), Araphant's son, [Arvedui](Arvedui.md), married the daughter of [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md), [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md), in token of the new alliance between Gondor and Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, the alliance proved fruitless because neither kingdom could spare help for the other. Arthedain was soon attacked by the forces of the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md), whilst Gondor was under attack by the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md).

When Ondoher died four years later, [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was left kingless, so [Arvedui](Arvedui.md) claimed the kingship of Gondor on behalf of his wife, but the Steward [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md) persuaded the [Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md) to select [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md), a distant relative of Ondoher, instead. However, the heirs of Arvedui did not forget this and claimed to be the heirs of both [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](Anárion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>

Araphant ruled Arthedain for seventy-three years and was succeeded by his son, Arvedui,<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> who was the last king until [Aragorn Elessar](Aragorn.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Araphant* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but the Association Tolkiendil suggests that it means "Full King" and is a compound of *[aran](Aran.md)* ("king", "lord", "chief", \[lit.\] high or noble person) and the nasal mutation *phant* of *pant* ("full") (as suggested by David Salo).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> His name could be a reference to Araphant asserting himself as the [king](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) of all [Arnor](Arnor.md).
