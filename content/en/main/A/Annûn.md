<i><b>annûn</b></i> is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "sunset", and thus used as "West".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Annûn* has a peculiar spelling because it is spelled with a circumflex (ˆ), which Tolkien used in monosyllables of Sindarin. An explanation is that *annûn* was only a variant of *[dûn](../D/Dûn.md)*.

The evolution was parallel of that of *dûn*, only that it came from [OS](../O/OS.md) \**andûne*. The [PQ](../P/PQ.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nd%C3%BBn%C3%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ndûnê (page does not exist)">ndûnê</a></i> ([root](../R/Root.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=ND%C3%9B&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDÛ (page does not exist)">NDÛ</a>) evolved a "syllabic nasal" \**ṇdûnê* which further evolved in the full vowel \**a-*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
