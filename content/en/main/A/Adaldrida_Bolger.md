**Adaldrida Brandybuck, née Bolger**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")

## History {#History}

Adaldrida was the daughter of [Gundahar Bolger](/wiki/Gundahar_Bolger) and [Dina Diggle](/wiki/Dina_Diggle). She was the younger sister of [Adalgar](/wiki/Adalgar_Bolger "Adalgar Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Adaldrida married [Marmadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Marmadoc_Brandybuck), and had four children: [Gorbadoc](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck "Gorbadoc Brandybuck"), [Orgulas](/wiki/Orgulas_Brandybuck "Orgulas Brandybuck") and two daughters.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Adaldrida* may be a Germanic name composed of the elements *adal* "noble" and a 2nd element.
