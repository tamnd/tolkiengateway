**Amon Hen** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen)**, the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the Hill of Sight, the location of the [Breaking of the Fellowship](/wiki/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship).

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- "[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(soundtrack)> "Amon Hen (soundtrack)")", a track from the [Original Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack") to *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

## Fandom {#Fandom}

- *[Amon Hen (journal)](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)>)*, a bi-monthly journal published by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society).
