**Apples** were the fruits of apple trees cultivated in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and in [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land). [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) served apple-tart to [Bifur](/wiki/Bifur) at the [Unexpected Party](/wiki/An_Unexpected_Party "An Unexpected Party").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Before [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") left the Shire he noticed how fine the weather had been, such that "the trees were laden with apples".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> When [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") led the hobbits from [Bree](/wiki/Bree), [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") chewed on one of the apples given to him by [Nob](/wiki/Nob) and [Bob](/wiki/Bob) at [the Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony"). Apples were so much a part of the diet of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) that Sam recited a proverb: "Apples for walking, and a pipe for sitting." As the party passed the house of the scornful [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny), Sam chucked an apple at him and struck Bill square on the nose.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Apple trees are not directly mentioned in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* but one of the men of Bree was [Rowlie Appledore](/wiki/Rowlie_Appledore), whose last name was an old English word for "apple tree".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) translation of the name *[Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took")* is *Cordof*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> As <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cox%27s_Orange_Pippin" title="wikipedia:Cox&#39;s Orange Pippin">pippin</a> is a kind of apple, it has been suggested that *cordof* refers to a "small red apple".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

In the movie Sam does not eat any apples and he does not throw one at Bill Ferny. However, in Scene 16, "The Nazgûl", when the hobbits start to prepare a breakfast Strider tells them, "Gentlemen, we do not stop until nightfall." Startled, [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") asks, "What about second breakfast?" Strider says nothing and walks on. Soon though he tosses an apple from behind a bush, which [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") catches. A second tossed apple smacks Pippin in the forehead.
