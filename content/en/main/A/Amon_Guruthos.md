In *[The One Ring](<../T/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game).md>)* roleplaying game, **Amon Guruthos** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) “Hill of the Fear of Death”) or the **Hill of Fear** was a hill north of the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md). It was created by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) to aid in the domination of mortal minds. As such, it primarily served as a place to practice dark rites to accomplish this. Some “fathers of the fathers of Men” worshipped the Dark Lord there, and in later times it was used by the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) and his servants to influence [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-mainsnippet_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mainsnippet-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the heyday of the [Angmar](Angmar.md), the adjacent fortress Sennas Gaer made it a stronghold of the kingdom. A passage in the cellar of the fortress, the exit of which was flanked by [Silent Watchers](../S/Silent_Watchers.md), led to a spiral path towards the hill's summit and its temple. Further inside the hill were the barrows of servants of Angmar and cultists of Morgoth. At the hill's very centre, which opened up to a well in the temple above, was a void, perceived as either a black pit or a “gap in Creation”.<sup id="cite_ref-mainsnippet_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mainsnippet-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was from the temple that the Witch-king had called forth the Barrow-wights.<sup id="cite_ref-mainsnippet_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mainsnippet-1">[1]</a></sup> Heroes have tried to vanquish the hill again and again. One such hero had forged a sword named [Estelang](../G/Gurthang.md#Portrayal_in_adaptations) to help accomplish this, but failed in this quest. This hero may have been the knight Tarandis, who served King [Araval](Araval.md), or the exiled dwarf Dvalin who was friendly with [Arthedain](Arthedain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-overview_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-overview-3">[3]</a></sup>

By [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2965](../T/Third_Age_2965.md), agents of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) had awakened the hill once more.<sup id="cite_ref-overview_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-overview-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
