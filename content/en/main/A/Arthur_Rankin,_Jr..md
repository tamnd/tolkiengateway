<br> **Arthur Rankin Davenport, Jr.** ([19 July](/wiki/19_July) [1924](/wiki/1924) - [30 January](/wiki/30_January) [2014](/wiki/2014)) was one of the founders of ["Videocraft International"](/wiki/Rankin/Bass "Rankin/Bass") in the early 1960's. Together with [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass), he directed and produced [The Hobbit (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)>) and [The Return of the King (1980 film)](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)>).
