**Avarin** is a term used to refer to the combined languages of the [Avari](Avari.md).

## History {#History}

There were at least six different languages, or dialects, in the Avarin group.<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup> The various dialects did not have a common development,<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-2">[2]</a></sup> although all descended from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup>

Three larger divisions of Avarin are recorded: **West Avarin**, **North Avarin**, and **East Avarin**. The West Avarin group was nearest to the [Eldarin](../E/Eldarin.md) languages of the west; North Avarin was very peculiar, with no initial consonant groups. Not much is known of East Avarin.<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Avarin* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word derived from the elements *[avar](Avar.md)* ("Refuser, Elf who did not journey to Aman") + *[in](../I/In.md)* ("-ian, racial-adjective, language").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The West, North, and East **Lemberin** tongues was a name within the early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) for what would later be called the West, North, and East Avarin languages.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> They were originally intended to have a resemblance in sound to Goidelic, Baltic, and Finnic languages.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Early etymology {#Early_etymology}

*Lemberin* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word derived from the elements *[lembi](../L/Lembi.md)* ("Those That Lingered") + *[in](../I/In.md)* ("-ian, racial-adjective, language").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
