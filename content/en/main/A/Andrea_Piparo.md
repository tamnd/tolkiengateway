**Andrea Piparo** is an Italian fantasy illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, he created art for the [Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit")<sup id="cite_ref-Reddit_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reddit-2">[2]</a></sup> expansions. He also worked on artwork for [War of the Ring: The Card Game](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_The_Card_Game).

Beyond his Tolkien-based work, he also did the cover art for *Against the DarkMaster*, a spiritual successor to [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
