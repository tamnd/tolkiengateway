**Adalbert Bolger** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Adalbert was the son of [Rudibert Bolger](../R/Rudibert_Bolger.md) and [Amethyst Hornblower](Amethyst_Hornblower.md). He married [Gerda Boffin](../G/Gerda_Boffin.md); their son [Filibert](../F/Filibert_Bolger.md), was born in [1342](../T/Third_Age_2942.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Adalbert* is a Germanic name composed of the elements *adal* "noble" and *beraht* "bright".

Adalbert was the first name of a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adalbert_Duke_of_Alsace" title="wikipedia:Adalbert Duke of Alsace">Duke of Alsace</a> who died in 723, and of two <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adalbert_I_of_Tuscany" title="wikipedia:Adalbert I of Tuscany">margraves of Tuscany</a> who died in 886 and 915, respectively.
