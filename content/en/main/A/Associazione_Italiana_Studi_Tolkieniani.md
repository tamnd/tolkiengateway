The **Associazione Italiana Studi Tolkieniani** (English: "Italian Association of Tolkien Studies"), or **AIST**, is a [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) based in [Italy](../I/Italy.md).

## History {#History}

The AIST was founded in [2014](../_/2014.md) as a non-profit and non-political association by a group of readers and scholars of the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), including some former members of the [Associazione Romana Studi Tolkieniani](../L/L’Associazione_romana_studi_Tolkieniani.md) and other independent scholars and artists.

## Headquarters {#Headquarters}

<figure class="mw-halign-left mw-image-border" typeof="mw:File/Frameless"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Logo_Tana_Drago.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6d/Logo_Tana_Drago.jpg/170px-Logo_Tana_Drago.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="170" height="170" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/6d/Logo_Tana_Drago.jpg/255px-Logo_Tana_Drago.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/6/6d/Logo_Tana_Drago.jpg 2x" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="300"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The AIST headquarters are based in the **Tana del Drago** (English: "Dragon's Lair"), located in <a class="external text" href="https://www.fondazionedozza.it/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Dozza Imolese</a>, one of Emilia-Romagna's most beautiful medieval villages. The Tana del Drago is a at the same time a centre for studies focused on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and the fantastic, a museum devoted to the exhibition of work of arts, books, games and handcrafted objects inspired by the Professor, and a place where his fans can reunite and play their favorite tabletop or roleplaying games set in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

The Dragon's Lair owes its name to <b><a class="external text" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FYDZbyJX1bw" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Fyrstan</a></b>, a dragon who has elected the main tower of Dozza's Castle (<a class="external text" href="https://www.fondazionedozza.it/entra-in-rocca/la-rocca-di-dozza.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Rocca di Dozza</a>), only a few hundred meters from the Tana, as its residence.

## Events {#Events}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Frameless"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/L/Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e4/Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg/170px-Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="170" height="170" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e4/Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg/255px-Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e4/Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg/340px-Logo_FantastikA_copy.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="500"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The main event in which the association is involved is <i><b>FantastiKa</b></i>, organised by the <a class="external text" href="https://www.fondazionedozza.it/fondazione-dozza/la-fondazione.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Fondazione Dozza Città d&#39;Arte</a> with the patronage of Dozza municipality. Every two years, when Fyrstan wakes up from its slumber, fantasy artists - illustrators, sculptors, artisans - from all over Italy gather in Dozza to show their works, while seminars, shows and book presenations are organised troughout the whole village of Dozza.

AIST is also present at the main italian book fairs (like <a class="external text" href="https://www.salonelibro.it/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Salone del Libro di Torino</a>) and comicons (as <a class="external text" href="https://www.luccacomicsandgames.com/it/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Lucca Comics and Games</a>) where its scholarly members often take part in workshops, seminars and round tables, while various artists collaborating with the association expose their works in dedicated exhibitions or at the AIST stand.

## Publications and collaborations {#Publications_and_collaborations}

<figure class="mw-halign-center" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/Q/Quaderni_di_Arda.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d6/Quaderni_di_Arda.jpg/600px-Quaderni_di_Arda.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="600" height="109" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/d/d6/Quaderni_di_Arda.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="851" data-file-height="154"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

AIST studies are published within the association journal <i><a class="external text" href="https://etereaedizioni.com/product-category/collane/i-quaderni-di-arda/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">I quaderni di Arda</a></i>. From 2019 the journal collects studies around a selected theme or academics conferences proceedings resulting from the collaboration with italian universities (Palermo, Parma, Milano, Trento) and international experts ([Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md), [Renée Vink](../R/Renée_Vink.md), [Mark Atherton](../M/Mark_Atherton.md), [Thomas Honegger](../T/Thomas_M._Honegger.md)). From 2025 the journal became part of the University Library System of the **University of Messina** and aligned its scholarly production with the principles of open access.

Members of the association collaborate with several publishers as consultants, translators, editors and independent scholars; the following collaborations are of note:

- the essays serie "<a class="external text" href="https://www.mariettieditore.it/collana-1089-tolkien-e-dintorni" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien e Dintorni</a>" published by <a class="external text" href="https://www.mariettieditore.it/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Marietti</a>
- the essays serie "I Rami" published by <a class="external text" href="https://etereaedizioni.com/collezioni/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Eterea Edizioni</a>
- the recent translations of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (<a class="external text" href="https://www.bompiani.it/catalogo/il-signore-degli-anelli-9788830104716" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Il Signore degli Anelli</i></a>) and *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* (<a class="external text" href="https://www.bompiani.it/catalogo/il-libro-dei-racconti-perduti-prima-parte-9788830118423" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>La Storia della Terra di Mezzo</i></a>) published by <a class="external text" href="https://www.bompiani.it/autori/j-r-r-tolkien-517" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Bompiani</a>

Some of the essays published with Marietti and Eterea have also been translated in English with the [Walking Tree Publishers](../W/Walking_Tree_Publishers.md):

- *[The Broken Scythe: Death and Immortality in the Works of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Broken_Scythe.md)* (2012)
- *[Tolkien and Philosophy](../T/Tolkien_and_Philosophy.md)* (2014)
- *[Pagan Saints in Middle-earth](../P/Pagan_Saints_in_Middle-earth.md)* (2018)
- *[Tolkien and the Classics](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Classics.md)* (2019)
