**Antonio De Luca** is an Italian fantasy illustrator.

He illustrated most of the covers and chapter images for *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)")*’s second edition and *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*, following [Martin Grip](/wiki/Martin_Grip) who had illustrated these for the former's first core rulebook and starter set.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> De Luca has also worked on artwork for *[War of the Ring: The Card Game](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_The_Card_Game)*.
