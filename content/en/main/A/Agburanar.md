In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*, **Agburanar** was a reclusive blue-black dragon who was rarely active.

His mother was cruelly killed by the [Lossoth](/wiki/Lossoth), and he thus generally avoided conflict, even preferring easy prey such as goats. He was nevertheless a fast and stealthy dragon, despite his lack of wings.

His lair was in the Caves of Ûlund, high in the Grey Mountains, chosen because of how inaccessible it was to intruders. There he sat on the wealth of Fain, a Dwarf-lord of Barin's folk who he had slain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

He was scarred by Khuzadrepa, a Dragon whose territory he once crossed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
