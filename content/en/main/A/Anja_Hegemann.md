**Anja Bertsch-Hegemann (née Hegemann)**\]\] ([11 June](../_/11_June.md), [1920](../_/1920.md) —?<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>) was a German writer, translator and poetess.

Anja Hegemann was born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich" title="wikipedia:Munich">Munich</a> and grew up in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne" title="wikipedia:Cologne">Cologne</a>. In [1945](../_/1945.md) she began to study studies of languages and since [1949](../_/1949.md) she worked as a secretary for a journalist. In [1954](../_/1954.md) she gave up her job and began to work as a freelance author when she got a literary scholarship.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

She translated books by authors like <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Lodwick" title="wikipedia:John Lodwick">John Lodwick</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Lamorisse" title="wikipedia:Albert Lamorisse">Albert Lamorisse</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anwar_El_Sadat" title="wikipedia:Anwar El Sadat">Anwar El Sadat</a> into German. In [1972](../_/1972.md) her poetic anthology *Atemzeit* was published.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

Anja Hegemann translated [J. R. R. Tolkien](../J/J._R._R._Tolkien.md)’s works *[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)* (*Herr Glück*, [1983](../_/1983.md)) and *[The Father Christmas Letters](../T/The_Father_Christmas_Letters.md)* (*Die Briefe vom Weihnachstmann*, [1975](../_/1975.md); *Briefe vom Weihnachtsmann* (revised edition), [2005](../_/2005.md)) into German.
