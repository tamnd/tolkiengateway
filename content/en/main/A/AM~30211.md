**AM** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md), with two different meanings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## AM<sup>1</sup> {#AM1}

Meaning: "mother"

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *amil* or *amme*
- [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md): *aman* (pl. *emnin*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>)
- [Exilic Noldorin](../E/Exilic_Noldorin.md) poetic: *emil*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## AM<sup>2</sup> {#AM2}

Meaning: "up" (usually in form *amba-*) or "go up"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *am-* ("up", prefix); *[amba](Amba.md)* ("up(wards)", adverb); *ambon*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup> ("upward slope, hill-side"); *ambo* ("hill")<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)): *[am](Am.md)* ("up"); *am-bend*, *amben* ("uphill"); *[amon](Amon.md)* ("hill"); *[am-rún](Amrûn.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup> ("uprising, sunrise"; hence Quenya *ambaron*, "Orient" \[cf. *[Ambaróna](Ambaróna.md)*\])

### Obsolete forms {#Obsolete_forms}

In the *Qenya Lexicon*, the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) form is AM(U) ("up(wards)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
