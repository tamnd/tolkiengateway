<i><b>Adventures in Middle-earth</b></i> was a role-playing game first released by [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](/wiki/Cubicle_7_Entertainment) in late 2016, adapting the 5th edition *[Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons)* ruleset to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), inspired by its already existing *[The One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Announce_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Announce-1">[1]</a></sup> Accordingly, many supplements are direct equivalents of their respective *The One Ring* sourcebook.

In late [2019](/wiki/2019), Cubicle 7 announced the end of its licensing agreement with [Sophisticated Games](/wiki/Sophisticated_Games), with publishing ceasing in the first half of [2020](/wiki/2020). This also meant the *[Rohan Region Guide](/wiki/Horse-lords_of_Rohan "Horse-lords of Rohan")* would only be released in PDF form.<sup id="cite_ref-TORE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORE-2">[2]</a></sup>

Several years later, [Free League Publishing](/wiki/Free_League_Publishing) would release *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*, a completely new adaptation based on 5th edition Dungeons and Dragons and taking inspiration from *The One Ring*’s second edition.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

*Adventures in Middle-earth*’s journey rules, mechanics developed by the staff of Cubicle 7, did not appear in *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying)*. Instead, an expanded version of the system, stripped of its specific Middle-earth flavour, was released as the standalone *Uncharted Journeys* book for use in any 5th edition Dungeons and Dragons game.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
