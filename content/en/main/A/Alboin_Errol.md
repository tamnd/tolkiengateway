**Alboin Errol** was one of the main characters in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s unfinished "[Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)" story of time travel. Alboin was the son of [Oswin](../O/Oswin_Errol.md) and father of [Audoin Errol](Audoin.md). Alboin and his son travel through time to the fall of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), and after that the story falls apart with many different ideas and opinions, none of which seem to be fully formed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
