**Alboin Errol** was one of the main characters in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s unfinished "[Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road "The Lost Road")" story of time travel. Alboin was the son of [Oswin](/wiki/Oswin_Errol "Oswin Errol") and father of [Audoin Errol](/wiki/Audoin "Audoin"). Alboin and his son travel through time to the fall of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), and after that the story falls apart with many different ideas and opinions, none of which seem to be fully formed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
