**Anders Blixt** (born [20 March](../_/20_March.md) [1959](../_/1959.md)) is a prolific Swedish game designer. He co-authored several supplements for the *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* game during the 1990s,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and occasionally writes about topics related to [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) at his blog (in English).

### Books {#Books}

- [1990](../_/1990.md): *[Gorgoroth](<../G/Gorgoroth_(book).md>)* (co-author)
- [1995](../_/1995.md): *[The Kin-strife](../T/The_Kin-strife.md)* (co-author)
- [1996](../_/1996.md): *[Southern Gondor: The Land](../S/Southern_Gondor%253A_The_Land.md)* (co-author)
- [1996](../_/1996.md): *[Southern Gondor: The People](../S/Southern_Gondor%253A_The_People.md)* (co-author)

- [2012](../_/2012.md): *Fenix*, nr. 1
    - "Midgård Noir" (campaigning in Umbar during the Fourth Age)
- [2012](../_/2012.md): *Fenix*, nr. 6
