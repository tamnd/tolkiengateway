**Anders Blixt** (born [20 March](/wiki/20_March) [1959](/wiki/1959)) is a prolific Swedish game designer. He co-authored several supplements for the *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* game during the 1990s,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and occasionally writes about topics related to [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") at his blog (in English).

- [1995](/wiki/1995): *[The Kin-strife](/wiki/The_Kin-strife)* (co-author)
- [1996](/wiki/1996): *[Southern Gondor: The Land](/wiki/Southern_Gondor:_The_Land)* (co-author)
- [1996](/wiki/1996): *[Southern Gondor: The People](/wiki/Southern_Gondor:_The_People)* (co-author)
