<i><b>A Night In Rivendell</b></i> is the second album by the Danish group [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble), released in April [2000](/wiki/2000). It features songs composed to the lyrics found in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and forms the second part of what was to become a complete musical interpretation of all lyrics in the book (a project given approval by both the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins)).

The album features cover art by [Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark).
