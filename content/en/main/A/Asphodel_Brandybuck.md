**Asphodel Burrows, née Brandybuck**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Asphodel was the daughter of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck) and [Mirabella Took](/wiki/Mirabella_Took). She married [Rufus Burrows](/wiki/Rufus_Burrows), and their child, [Milo Burrows](/wiki/Milo_Burrows), was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1347](/wiki/Third_Age_2947 "Third Age 2947"). They all attended [Bilbo's Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"[Asphodel](/wiki/Asphodel)" is a plant of the lily family with white, yellow or pink flowers.
