**ai** is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for *alas*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- <i><b>Ai!</b> <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Laure" title="Laure">Laurië</a> <a href="/wiki/Lanta" title="Lanta">lantar</a> <a href="/wiki/Lass%C3%AB" title="Lassë">lassi</a>...</i> = "**Ah!** like gold fall the leaves"
- <i><b>Ai</b> lintulindova Lasselanta</i> = "**Oh!** Fall with its many swallows"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
