<i><b>ampa</b></i> is a noun meaning "hook" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Ampa.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/3/3f/Ampa.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Ampa* is also the name in Quenya of the fourteenth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the second letter of the fourth grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiced spirants", and it is the fourth letter of the series of labial consonants, the *[Parmatéma](../P/Parmatéma.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> In standard Quenya spelling *Ampa* represents **MP**<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> but in all other modes it represents **V**.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ivi</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
