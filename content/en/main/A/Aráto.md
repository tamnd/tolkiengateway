<i><b>aráto</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) noun meaning "champion, eminent man"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "a noble, lord, king".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Compare with the prefix *[ara](Ara.md)-*.

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="Aegnor.md" title="Aegnor">Amb<b>aráto</b></a></i> = "High Champion"
- <i><a href="Angrod.md" title="Angrod">Ang<b>aráto</b></a></i> = "Iron Champion"
- <i><a href="../F/Finrod.md" title="Finrod">Find<b>aráto</b></a></i> = "\[Golden-\]Haired Champion"
