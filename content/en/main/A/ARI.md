**ARI** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") meaning "good, excellent, noble". The root also appears as ARA/RĀ, ARAT/ARAN.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Derived from root [AR](</wiki/AR_(root)> "AR (root)").

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[arta](/wiki/Arta "Arta")*
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")*
