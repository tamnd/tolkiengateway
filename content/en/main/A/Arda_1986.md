<i><b>Arda 1986, Volume VI</b></i> is an issue of the publication *[Arda](<Arda_(annual).md>)*. The issue is noteworthy for including a manuscript facsimile of "[The Clerkes Compleinte](../T/The_Clerkes_Compleinte.md)", a rare poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). Notes to the manuscript are provided by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and [Anders Stenström](Anders_Stenström.md).
