<i><b>Arda 1986, Volume VI</b></i> is an issue of the publication *[Arda](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)")*. The issue is noteworthy for including a manuscript facsimile of "[The Clerkes Compleinte](/wiki/The_Clerkes_Compleinte)", a rare poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Notes to the manuscript are provided by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) and [Anders Stenström](/wiki/Anders_Stenstr%C3%B6m).
