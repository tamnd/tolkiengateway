**Adalgrim Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Adalgrim was the only child of [Hildigrim Took](/wiki/Hildigrim_Took) and [Rosa Baggins](/wiki/Rosa_Baggins). He had five children, four of them daughters. His youngest children were [Paladin](/wiki/Paladin_Took_II "Paladin Took II") and [Esmeralda](/wiki/Esmeralda_Took "Esmeralda Took"), born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1333](/wiki/Third_Age_2933 "Third Age 2933") and [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1336](/wiki/Third_Age_2936 "Third Age 2936").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Adalgrim comes from the Germanic elements *adal* meaning "noble" and *grim* meaning "fierce".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
