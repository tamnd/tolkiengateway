<i><b>anna</b></i> is a noun meaning "gift" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anna.gif"><img src="/w/images/d/dd/Anna.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Anna* is also the name in Quenya of the twenty-third letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the third letter of the sixth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "semi-vocalic" consonants and it is the fifth letter in the third series of consonants. This letter is commonly used for consonantal **Y**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> except in the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand). As seen in the [West-gate](/wiki/West-gate) of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) inscription, *Anna* in this "full writing" mode is used for **O**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name for this letter is <i><b>Yé</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Annatar" title="Annatar"><b>Anna</b>tar</a></i> = "Lord of Gifts"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Andor" title="Andor"><b>An</b>dor</a></i> (*anna*+*[ndórë](/wiki/N%C3%B3re "Nóre")*) = "Land of Gift"
- <i>Eru<b>anna</b></i> = "God's Grace"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Yavanna" title="Yavanna">Yav<b>anna</b></a></i> = "Giver of Gifts (Fruits)"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ANA](/wiki/ANA).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
