<i><b>anna</b></i> is a noun meaning "gift" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anna.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/d/dd/Anna.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Anna* is also the name in Quenya of the twenty-third letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the third letter of the sixth grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "semi-vocalic" consonants and it is the fifth letter in the third series of consonants. This letter is commonly used for consonantal **Y**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> except in the [Mode of Beleriand](../M/Mode_of_Beleriand.md). As seen in the [West-gate](../W/West-gate.md) of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) inscription, *Anna* in this "full writing" mode is used for **O**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name for this letter is <i><b>Yé</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Annatar.md" title="Annatar"><b>Anna</b>tar</a></i> = "Lord of Gifts"
- <i><a href="Andor.md" title="Andor"><b>An</b>dor</a></i> (*anna*+*[ndórë](../N/Nóre.md)*) = "Land of Gift"
- <i>Eru<b>anna</b></i> = "God's Grace"
- <i><a href="../Y/Yavanna.md" title="Yavanna">Yav<b>anna</b></a></i> = "Giver of Gifts (Fruits)"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [ANA](ANA.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
