**Angrim** was a [man](/wiki/Men "Men") of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Angrim lived around the fifth century of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). He had at least one son, [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
