**AS** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") meaning "warmth".<sup id="cite_ref-Elda_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elda-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish): \*āsi
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): [*āze*, *āre*, *ārĭ-*](/wiki/%C3%81re "Áre") ("warmth, especially of the sun, sunlight"); *[Árien](/wiki/%C3%81rien)*; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Arma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arma (page does not exist)">arma</a></i> ("a ray of sunlight"); <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Asta-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Asta- (page does not exist)">asta-</a></i> ("to heat, bake"); *[ar](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* ("day")<sup id="cite_ref-Elda_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elda-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[as](/wiki/As "As")*
