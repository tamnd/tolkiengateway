**Arossiach**, the **Fords of Aros**, was the ford on the upper reaches of the River [Aros](Aros.md), where the long and perilous road through the valley of [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md) crossed eastward into the lands of [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

The River [Aros](Aros.md) had its sources in two streams that flowed southward out of the hills of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md). Where those streams met, an ancient east-west road crossed the upper reaches of the river at a ford. The ford marked the boundary between two regions. To the east was [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md), and the other lands held by the sons of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md). To the west was [Dor Dínen](../D/Dor_Dínen.md), and beyond it the perilous valley of [Nan Dungortheb](../N/Nan_Dungortheb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Fords of Aros seem to date back to the days before the beginning of the [Years of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md), when the roads north of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) were safe to travel. After the coming of [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md), and the raising of the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md), the lands west of the Arossiach became a haunted and dangerous place, and in the late [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) the Fords were only used by the bravest or most desperate travellers.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Aredhel](Aredhel.md), after leaving her brother [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) and the city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), desired to see the sons of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md). Denied entrance into [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), she took the road north of the Hidden Kingdom and crossed the Aros at the Arossiach on her journey into Himlad.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arossiach* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name meaning "Fords of Aros", from *[Aros](Aros.md)* + *[iach](../I/Iach.md)* ("ford").
