<i><b>and</b></i> means 'long' in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

It was simplified to just *ann* or *an-* in compounds

- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cuia&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cuia (page does not exist)">Cuio</a> <a href="/wiki/I" title="I">i</a>-<a href="/wiki/Perian" title="Perian">Pheriain</a> <a href="/wiki/An" title="An">anann</a></i> "Long live the [Halflings](/wiki/Halflings)"

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Andram](/wiki/Andram)
- [Androth](/wiki/Androth): 'Long delving'
- [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin): 'Great River' (lit. "Long River")
- [andaith](/wiki/Andaith "Andaith"): 'long mark'
- [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas): "Langstrand"
- [Ann-thennath](/wiki/Ann-thennath)

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[anda](/wiki/Anda "Anda")*
