<i><b>and</b></i> means 'long' in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

It was simplified to just *ann* or *an-* in compounds

- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cuia&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cuia (page does not exist)">Cuio</a> <a href="../I/I.md" title="I">i</a>-<a href="../P/Perian.md" title="Perian">Pheriain</a> <a href="An.md" title="An">anann</a></i> "Long live the [Halflings](../H/Halflings.md)"

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Andram](Andram.md)
- [Androth](Androth.md): 'Long delving'
- [Anduin](Anduin.md): 'Great River' (lit. "Long River")
- [andaith](Andaith.md): 'long mark'
- [Anfalas](Anfalas.md): "Langstrand"
- [Ann-thennath](Ann-thennath.md)

## Cognates {#Cognates}
