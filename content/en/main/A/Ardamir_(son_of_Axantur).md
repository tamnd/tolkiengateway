**Ardamir** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md): "[Arda](Arda.md)-jewel", pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈardamir]</span>, stem *Ardamír-*) was the second child and first son of [Axantur](Axantur.md), a nobleman of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md). His great-nephew [Hallacar](../H/Hallacar.md) would marry [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md), the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md). Though he left descendants, nothing of their lives was recorded.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ardamir* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. It means "Jewel of the World". Ardamire (or Ardamir) was the [mother-name](../M/Mother-name.md) given to [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md), the son of [Idril](../I/Idril.md) and [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Paul Strack suggests it means "World-jewel" and is a comppound of *[arda](Arda.md)* and *-[mir](../M/Mir.md)* ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
