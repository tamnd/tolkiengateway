**Argon** was the fourth and last child and the youngest son of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), who was the second son of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB), [King of the Noldor](/wiki/King_of_the_Noldor).

## History {#History}

The mother of Argon was [Anairë](/wiki/Anair%C3%AB) who was of the Noldor as well, and his elder brothers were [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) and [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon). Argon and his elder brothers also had only one sister, *[Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel)* (only daughter of Fingolfin and Anairë) who was elder to Argon.

His [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name was <i><b>Arakáno</b></i> ("High Commander"), named so partially for his impetuous character. Argon set foot on [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) with his father and siblings, but he was killed during the [Battle of the Lammoth](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Lammoth). Argon distinguished himself during the fighting by hewing a path through his foes and slaying the captain of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Note_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-5">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Argon* is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) form of <i><b>Arakáno</b></i>, although he never used it. It was devised posthumously for him in the records. It is recorded that this later became a popular name of Argon which was used by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar).<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Argon does not appear in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion")* at all, as he only came to light in very late writings by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"). His character was developed as Tolkien devised the genealogies. At first he fell in the fighting at [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB) and then he perished while crossing the [Helkaraxë](/wiki/Helkarax%C3%AB) before his death was set in the [Battle of the Lammoth](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Lammoth).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-5">[3]</a></sup>
