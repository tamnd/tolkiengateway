**Argon** was the fourth and last child and the youngest son of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), who was the second son of [Finwë](../F/Finwë.md), [King of the Noldor](../K/King_of_the_Noldor.md).

## History {#History}

The mother of Argon was [Anairë](Anairë.md) who was of the Noldor as well, and his elder brothers were [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) and [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md). Argon and his elder brothers also had only one sister, *[Aredhel](Aredhel.md)* (only daughter of Fingolfin and Anairë) who was elder to Argon.

His [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name was <i><b>Arakáno</b></i> ("High Commander"), named so partially for his impetuous character. Argon set foot on [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) with his father and siblings, but he was killed during the [Battle of the Lammoth](../B/Battle_of_the_Lammoth.md). Argon distinguished himself during the fighting by hewing a path through his foes and slaying the captain of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Note_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-5">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Argon* is the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) form of <i><b>Arakáno</b></i>, although he never used it. It was devised posthumously for him in the records. It is recorded that this later became a popular name of Argon which was used by the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Argon does not appear in the published *[Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* at all, as he only came to light in very late writings by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). His character was developed as Tolkien devised the genealogies. At first he fell in the fighting at [Alqualondë](Alqualondë.md) and then he perished while crossing the [Helkaraxë](../H/Helkaraxë.md) before his death was set in the [Battle of the Lammoth](../B/Battle_of_the_Lammoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Note_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note-5">[3]</a></sup>
