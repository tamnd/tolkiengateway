The **Aratar**, also known as the **Máhani** ([Val.](../V/Valarin.md) <i><b>māchanumāz</b></i>) or simply just the **Eight**, were the **High Ones of Arda**, the greatest of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md): [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md), [Varda](../V/Varda.md), [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md), [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md), [Aulë](Aulë.md), [Mandos](../M/Mandos.md), [Nienna](../N/Nienna.md) and [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md). Though [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) was held to be the [High King](../E/Elder_King.md) of [Arda](Arda.md), the Eight were held in equal reverence, and were said to possess a majesty that surpassed even the other [Valar](../V/Valar.md).

The Aratar were originally nine, and included [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). He was removed from this 'order' during and after his rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aratar* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "The High Ones",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> "The Exalted"<sup id="cite_ref-Notes_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notes-3">[3]</a></sup> or "The Supreme".<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Aratar* is the plural of *[arata](Arata.md)* ("high, lofty, noble"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> being an extended form of the stem *[ara](Ara.md)-*, which in Quenya had become specialized for the Aratar, but also used in noble names.<sup id="cite_ref-Notes_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notes-3">[3]</a></sup> It has the same root as the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) term *Rodon* (pl. *Rodyn*).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Another form is *Máhan*/*Máhani*, a loan from the [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) term *māchanāz* pl. *māchan<u>um</u>āz* "[Authorities](Authorities.md)". These terms are also the source of *[Máhanaxar](../M/Máhanaxar.md)*/*Māchananaškad*.<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The idea of some exalted Valar is present since the earlier versions of the *Valaquenta*, but the number increased with the development.
