The **Aratar**, also known as the **Máhani** ([Val.](/wiki/Valarin "Valarin") <i><b>māchanumāz</b></i>) or simply just the **Eight**, were the **High Ones of Arda**, the greatest of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar): [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), [Varda](/wiki/Varda), [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo), [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB), [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos), [Nienna](/wiki/Nienna) and [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB). Though [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) was held to be the [High King](/wiki/Elder_King "Elder King") of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), the Eight were held in equal reverence, and were said to possess a majesty that surpassed even the other [Valar](/wiki/Valar).

The Aratar were originally nine, and included [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). He was removed from this 'order' during and after his rebellion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aratar* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "The High Ones",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> "The Exalted"<sup id="cite_ref-Notes_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notes-3">[3]</a></sup> or "The Supreme".<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Aratar* is the plural of *[arata](/wiki/Arata "Arata")* ("high, lofty, noble"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> being an extended form of the stem *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")-*, which in Quenya had become specialized for the Aratar, but also used in noble names.<sup id="cite_ref-Notes_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Notes-3">[3]</a></sup> It has the same root as the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) term *Rodon* (pl. *Rodyn*).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Another form is *Máhan*/*Máhani*, a loan from the [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) term *māchanāz* pl. *māchan<u>um</u>āz* "[Authorities](/wiki/Authorities)". These terms are also the source of *[Máhanaxar](/wiki/M%C3%A1hanaxar)*/*Māchananaškad*.<sup id="cite_ref-Quendi_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quendi-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The idea of some exalted Valar is present since the earlier versions of the *Valaquenta*, but the number increased with the development.
