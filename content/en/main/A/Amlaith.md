**Amlaith** of [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [726](../T/Third_Age_726.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> - [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [946](../T/Third_Age_946.md),<sup id="cite_ref-north_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-north-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 220) was the eldest son of [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>), the tenth King of [Arnor](Arnor.md).

## History {#History}

He was destined to become King of [Arnor](Arnor.md) after his father's death in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [861](../T/Third_Age_861.md), but there were constant quarrels between [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>)'s sons. A civil war erupted when [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>) finally died. Amlaith, however, failed to overcome his two younger brothers, so the Kingdom of Arnor was split into three and Amlaith became the first King of [Arthedain](Arthedain.md). (His brothers founded the kingdoms of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

His reign in Arthedain was largely a peaceful one, although border disputes with the realms of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) - especially over [Amon Sûl](../W/Weathertop.md) and the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md) - never fully ceased. Under his rule the capital was formally removed from [Annúminas](Annúminas.md) to Fornost, due to the dwindling of the people.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

Unlike his forefathers, he did not take a name in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> the "High-elven Tongue",<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> but instead took a name in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), as did all the [Kings of Arthedain](../K/Kings_of_Arthedain.md) and [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md) that descended from him.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Amlaith ruled for eighty-five years, and was succeeded at his death in 946 by his son, [Beleg](<../B/Beleg_(King_of_Arthedain).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-north_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-north-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amlaith* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Mark Fisher suggests it might contain the elements *[am](Am.md)* ("up") and *laith* (as in the name of the river *[Limlaith](../L/Limlaith.md)* where *laith* means "light").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
