**Ardaranye** was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for "the **Kingdom of [Arda](/wiki/Arda)**" under the guardianship and headship of [Manwë Súlimo](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB "Manwë"), the Elder King of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), which is [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar#Ambar) 'the Earth' as a whole planet (including the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Aman "Aman") until its [removal](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World "Changing of the World") from the [circles of the world](/wiki/Circles_of_the_World "Circles of the World") after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319")) together with all the celestial objects nearest to it, including the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), and even some '[stars](/wiki/Stars "Stars")'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In other words, *Ardaranye* was precisely an equivalent for the whole <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_System" title="wikipedia:Solar System">Solar System</a></b>.
