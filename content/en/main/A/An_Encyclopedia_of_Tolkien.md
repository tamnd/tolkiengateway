<i><b>An Encyclopedia of Tolkien: The history and mythology that inspired Tolkien&#39;s world</b></i> is an encyclopedia-style book written by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day), published in [2019](/wiki/2019).

The same book was also published under another title (same sub-title), with a different cover: <i><b>A Dictionary of Sources of Tolkien: The history and mythology that inspired Tolkien&#39;s world</b></i>.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The fantasy world of J.R.R. Tolkien’s Middle-earth contains a rich assortment of people, cities, and creatures – as well as a deep, intertwined history that spans thousands of years. In this beautifully illustrated, leather-bound volume, best-selling author and Tolkien scholar David Day presents four decades of research and writing on the lands and inhabitants Middle-earth. Sections of this A-to-Z dictionary are devoted to discussion of the battles, history, beasts, and heroes of Tolkien’s stories, and are accompanied by black-and-white illustrations. This comprehensive volume on Tolkien’s world also includes an appendix of three primary legends that served as sources for Tolkien’s creations – the Volsunga saga, the Nibelungenlied, and Richard Wagner’s Ring Cycle—and more than 200 black-and-white illustrations.
</p>
</blockquote>
