<i><b>aha</b></i> is a noun meaning "rage" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Harma.gif"><img src="/w/images/6/64/Harma.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Aha* was also the later name in Quenya of the eleventh letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> and the third letter of the third grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "voiceless spirants". It was the third letter in the third series of consonants.

For written Quenya, the third series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](/wiki/Calmat%C3%A9ma)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> Originally the letter *Aha* was called *[Harma](/wiki/Harma)* and it represented spirant **CH** in all positions. While the letter retains this value in medial positions, over time the sound softened into breath **H** at the beginning of words and the name of the letter when so used is *Aha*.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>
