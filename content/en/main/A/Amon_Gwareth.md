**Amon Gwareth** was the hill that stood in the valley of [Tumladen](../T/Tumladen.md), within the [Encircling Mountains](../E/Encircling_Mountains.md), on which [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) built the city of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On its north side was the [Caragdûr](../C/Caragdûr.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Gwareth* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). It has meant "Hill of Watching", from *[amon](Amon.md)* ("hill") and *[gwareth](../G/Gwareth.md)* ("watching"), since its first conception.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) wrote that his father later changed the name to *Amon Gwared* but this change did not make it into the published *[Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
