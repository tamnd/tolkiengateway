**Amon Gwareth** was the hill that stood in the valley of [Tumladen](/wiki/Tumladen), within the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains), on which [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) built the city of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On its north side was the [Caragdûr](/wiki/Caragd%C3%BBr).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Gwareth* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). It has meant "Hill of Watching", from *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") and *[gwareth](/wiki/Gwareth "Gwareth")* ("watching"), since its first conception.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) wrote that his father later changed the name to *Amon Gwared* but this change did not make it into the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion "The Silmarillion")*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
