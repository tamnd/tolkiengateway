**Araman** is the northern coastland of [Aman](/wiki/Aman), north of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), that lay outside the mountain-fence of the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri). It's a wasteland of long and dreary plains without hindrance to passage, but bleak, and ever colder as the Ice of [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB) (which joined it with [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)) drew nearer. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The coastland that was visible from [Nevrast](/wiki/Nevrast) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), was known as **[Haerast](/wiki/Haerast)** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

This was the way [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") (passing through [Oiomúrë](/wiki/Oiom%C3%BAr%C3%AB)), and later the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) took into Exile. In Araman the host of the Noldor heard the [Doom of Mandos](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos), delivered by a dark figure standing upon a high rock. Upon hearing the prophecy the host divided, with the followers of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) pressing onward while those of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) turned back. Later, when Fëanor and his kin burnt their ships at [Losgar](/wiki/Losgar), [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) in Araman knew that he had been betrayed and that their only course was to march across the Helcaraxë.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Araman* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "outside Aman", consisting of *[ar](/wiki/Ar#Quenya "Ar")* + *[Aman](/wiki/Aman#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) forms *Eruman* and *Erumání* from root ERE "outside" appear. [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) *Edhofon*. Compare *Harwalin* or *[Arvalin](/wiki/Arvalin)* "close to Valinor".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
