**Araman** is the northern coastland of [Aman](Aman.md), north of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), that lay outside the mountain-fence of the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md). It's a wasteland of long and dreary plains without hindrance to passage, but bleak, and ever colder as the Ice of [Helcaraxë](../H/Helcaraxë.md) (which joined it with [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md)) drew nearer. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The coastland that was visible from [Nevrast](../N/Nevrast.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), was known as **[Haerast](../H/Haerast.md)** in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

This was the way [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) (passing through [Oiomúrë](../O/Oiomúrë.md)), and later the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) took into Exile. In Araman the host of the Noldor heard the [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md), delivered by a dark figure standing upon a high rock. Upon hearing the prophecy the host divided, with the followers of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) pressing onward while those of [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) turned back. Later, when Fëanor and his kin burnt their ships at [Losgar](../L/Losgar.md), [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) in Araman knew that he had been betrayed and that their only course was to march across the Helcaraxë.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Araman* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "outside Aman", consisting of *[ar](Ar.md#Quenya)* + *[Aman](Aman.md#Etymology)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)* [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) forms *Eruman* and *Erumání* from root ERE "outside" appear. [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) *Edhofon*. Compare *Harwalin* or *[Arvalin](Arvalin.md)* "close to Valinor".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
