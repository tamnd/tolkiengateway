**Andraste** is a character created specifically for [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online). She was the mistress of *[crebain](/wiki/Crebain "Crebain")* that attacked [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She was slain in the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) along with her crebain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
