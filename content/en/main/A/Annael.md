**Annael** was a [Sinda](/wiki/Sindar "Sindar") of the [Elves of Mithrim](/wiki/Elves_of_Mithrim) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## History {#History}

Annael was the leader of a small group of Grey-elves at Mithrim, having the [swan](/wiki/Swan "Swan") as his [token](/wiki/Heraldry "Heraldry").<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Sindar of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) joined the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros), but after the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), Annael was the only Sinda who returned.

While [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan) was hosted by the Grey-elves at [Lake Mithrim](/wiki/Lake_Mithrim) during the birth of her son [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), she asked them to foster him, as she wished to part in search of her husband [Huor](/wiki/Huor). Annael explained to her how Huor had died, and she left to die at the [Hill of the Slain](/wiki/Hill_of_the_Slain).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Thus Annael became the foster-father of Tuor during his early years.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup>

As years passed, in order to seek refuge from [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), Annael led his small group of people to the caves of [Androth](/wiki/Androth). When Tuor was sixteen years of age, Annael had to prevent him from going to fight against the enemies of [his House](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador"), [foreseeing](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight") that [Morgoth's](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") power would not be defeated until [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) [was overthrown](/wiki/War_of_Wrath "War of Wrath"), and that they had to march South to [Annon-in-Gelydh](/wiki/Annon-in-Gelydh), which was built by [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) long ago. Tuor was much intrigued by the name of Turgon and wished to meet him, but Annael told him how well hidden Turgon's [realm](/wiki/Gondolin "Gondolin") was.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Despite having parted in secret from Androth, Annael's people were located and ambushed by a large force of Orcs and Easterlings. The Elves were all scattered, and Tuor was captured. However, Annael and many of his folk escaped [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and joined [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) in the [distant south](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion "Mouths of Sirion") of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Annael* is clearly [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but it is never glossed. It may contain the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)/[Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) elements *[anna](/wiki/Anna "Anna")* ("gift") and *[el](/wiki/El "El")* ("star").
