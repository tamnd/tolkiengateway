**Annael** was a [Sinda](../S/Sindar.md) of the [Elves of Mithrim](../E/Elves_of_Mithrim.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

Annael was the leader of a small group of Grey-elves at Mithrim, having the [swan](../S/Swan.md) as his [token](../H/Heraldry.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Sindar of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) joined the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md), but after the disastrous [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), Annael was the only Sinda who returned.

While [Rían](../R/Rían.md) was hosted by the Grey-elves at [Lake Mithrim](../L/Lake_Mithrim.md) during the birth of her son [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), she asked them to foster him, as she wished to part in search of her husband [Huor](../H/Huor.md). Annael explained to her how Huor had died, and she left to die at the [Hill of the Slain](../H/Hill_of_the_Slain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Thus Annael became the foster-father of Tuor during his early years.<sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup>

As years passed, in order to seek refuge from [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) and [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), Annael led his small group of people to the caves of [Androth](Androth.md). When Tuor was sixteen years of age, Annael had to prevent him from going to fight against the enemies of [his House](../H/House_of_Hador.md), [foreseeing](../F/Foresight.md) that [Morgoth's](../M/Morgoth.md) power would not be defeated until [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) [was overthrown](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), and that they had to march South to [Annon-in-Gelydh](Annon-in-Gelydh.md), which was built by [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) long ago. Tuor was much intrigued by the name of Turgon and wished to meet him, but Annael told him how well hidden Turgon's [realm](../G/Gondolin.md) was.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Despite having parted in secret from Androth, Annael's people were located and ambushed by a large force of Orcs and Easterlings. The Elves were all scattered, and Tuor was captured. However, Annael and many of his folk escaped [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) and joined [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) in the [distant south](../M/Mouths_of_Sirion.md) of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tuor_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Quenta_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quenta-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Annael* is clearly [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), but it is never glossed. It may contain the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)/[Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) elements *[anna](Anna.md)* ("gift") and *[el](../E/El.md)* ("star").
