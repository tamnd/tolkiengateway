**Afros** and **Gruir** were two rivers in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), joining at the [bridge](/wiki/Tram_Nybol "Tram Nybol") of [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tower_of_Tavrobel&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tower of Tavrobel (page does not exist)">Tower of Tavrobel</a> was located near them.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Battle of the Heath of the Sky-roof](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Heath_of_the_Sky-roof), which took place during the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), and ended in complete disaster for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of Tol Eressëa, took place near Tavrobel.

Following the battle, the remaining Elves, and in one outline, [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), fled across the rivers Afros and Gruir in order to escape the invading [Men](/wiki/Men).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The two rivers of Tavrobel, Afros and Gruir, might have been inspired by the rivers <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Sow" title="wikipedia:River Sow">Sow</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_Trent" title="wikipedia:River Trent">Trent</a>, which meet near the village of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Haywood" title="wikipedia:Great Haywood">Great Haywood</a> (which was the inspiration for Tavrobel).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
