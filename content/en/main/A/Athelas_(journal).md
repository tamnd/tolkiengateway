<i><b>Athelas</b></i> was the Danish-language journal of Danmarks Tolkienforening (later called [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening "Imladris, Danmarks Tolkienforening")). It was published between [1993](/wiki/1993) and [1998](/wiki/1998). From the start and until [1997](/wiki/1997), its editor was [Lars-Terje Lysemose](/wiki/Lars-Terje_Lysemose). After Lysemose left, the last issues of the journal turned into a newsletter, which only survived about a year.

The journal was published four times a year (ca. 50 pages per issue). The journal can now be found at the Municipal Library of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus" title="wikipedia:Aarhus">Aarhus</a> and at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Library" title="wikipedia:Royal Danish Library">Royal Danish Library</a> (ISSN 0908-259X).

*Athelas* was also annually published in English (ISSN 0909-1627).
