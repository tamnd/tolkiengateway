<i><b>Athelas</b></i> was the Danish-language journal of Danmarks Tolkienforening (later called [Imladris](../I/Imladris,_Danmarks_Tolkienforening.md)). It was published between [1993](../_/1993.md) and [1998](../_/1998.md). From the start and until [1997](../_/1997.md), its editor was [Lars-Terje Lysemose](../L/Lars-Terje_Lysemose.md). After Lysemose left, the last issues of the journal turned into a newsletter, which only survived about a year.

The journal was published four times a year (ca. 50 pages per issue). The journal can now be found at the Municipal Library of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus" title="wikipedia:Aarhus">Aarhus</a> and at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Library" title="wikipedia:Royal Danish Library">Royal Danish Library</a> (ISSN 0908-259X).

*Athelas* was also annually published in English (ISSN 0909-1627).
