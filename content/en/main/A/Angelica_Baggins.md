**Angelica Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Angelica was the daughter of [Ponto II](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_II "Ponto Baggins II"), the grand-niece three times removed of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Though her relationship with Bilbo was more distant, she would call him "Uncle Bilbo". She was invited to [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001") and received a round convex mirror as a gift because she obviously admired her appearance.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

As many female Hobbit names refer to flowers,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angelica" title="wikipedia:Angelica">Angelica</a></i> is a genus of plants with medicinal, flavoring, fragrant and medicinal properties.
