## Contents {#Contents}

\[Titles below in translation from Polish by the editors of *Aiglos*.\]

- **Cover Art**
    - *Autumn Ent*
        - By: Joanna Cheda 'Niuchaczaus'

- **Articles and essays**
    - "Frodo’s dreams"
        - By: Marek Gumkowski
    - "Good sides of Sauron"
        - By: [Helge Kåre Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Kåre_Fauskanger.md)
    - "Who’s blowing?"
        - By: Maria Błaszkiewicz
    - "Sauron’s war with the Elves 1693–1701. A draft monograph."
        - By: [Michał Leśniewski 'M.L.'](../M/Michał_Leśniewski.md)
    - "Representations of Queen Mab in English literature from Shakespeare to Susanna Clarke"
        - By: Bartłomiej Błaszkiewicz

- **Fanfiction and poetry**
    - *Two halves*
        - By: Ula Kotwica 'Ariana'
    - *Palantír*
        - By: [Jakub Tymiński 'Ominous'](../J/Jakub_Tymiński.md)

- **Reviews and interviews**
    - An interview with [Agnieszka Sylwanowicz 'Evermind HS Took'](Agnieszka_Sylwanowicz.md)
        - By: Jakub Tymiński 'Ominous'
    - "About The Hobbit, joke and allegory once again – a polemic against Maria Błaszkiewicz’s article"
        - By: Tadeusz Andrzej Olszański 'Halbarad Dúnadan'
    - "A long-expected end" – a review of the extended DVD edition of *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md)*"
        - By: Agnieszka Sylwanowicz 'Evermind HS Took'
    - A review of the game *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)*
        - By: Krzysztof Mysiak 'Draugnimir'
    - "Tolkien in the context of his times a review of Dimitra Fimi’s book *[Tolkien, Race and Cultural History](../T/Tolkien,_Race_and_Cultural_History.md)*"
        - By: Agnieszka Sylwanowicz 'Evermind HS Took'
    - "On laundry tickets, inspirations, fanfiction and collecting points. A few words about the publication of J.R.R. Tolkien’s *[The Story of Kullervo](../T/The_Story_of_Kullervo.md)*"
        - By: Michał Leśniewski 'M.L.'
    - "*[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* for the eye, a review of Wayne G. Hammond and Christina Scull’s *[The Art of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Art_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*
        - By: Agnieszka Sylwanowicz 'Evermind HS Took'

The issue contains also a hobbit genealogical crossword puzzle by Karolina Stopa-Olszanska 'Melinir' and Tolkien news from Poland and abroad.

- **Illustrations**
    - By: Marta Aguado Garrido
    - By: Jessica Albert ‘Sieskja’
    - By: Joanna Cheda ‘Niuchaczaus’ (cover)
    - By: Gerwell
    - By: ['Kasiopea'](../C/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel.md)
    - By: Līga Kļaviņa
    - By: lomacchi
    - By: Righon
    - By: [Karolina Stopa-Olszańska 'Melinir'](../K/Karolina_Stopa-Olszańska.md)
    - By: [Karolina Węgrzyn 'Sirielle'](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Sirielle)
    - By: Katarzyna Wojdak 'Avari'
    - By: [Peter Xavier Price](../P/Peter_Xavier_Price.md)
