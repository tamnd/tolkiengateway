The **Alliance of Dwarves and Men** was an alliance formed in the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) by the [Men of the North](/wiki/Northmen "Northmen") and the [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards).

## History {#History}

The Men and Dwarves in the north had coexisted peacefully ever since the [ancestors](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador") of the Northmen settled in the area<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). When [Angband](/wiki/Angband) fell, multitudes of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) fled east reaching the lands occupied by the Dwarves and Men. The Orcs far outnumbered the Dwarves in battles but their Mannish neighbours helped them overcome the Orcs' superiority of numbers and together they were able to drive them back. Thus began a warm friendship between the two races, the Dwarves supplied them with better arms while the Men, who were fierce and skilled riders, sent scouts to look for enemy movement and there was relative peace in the region.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[1]</a></sup>

When [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) invaded [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695"), hordes of Orcs invaded the mountains again. [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad) was [taken](/wiki/Fall_of_Mount_Gundabad "Fall of Mount Gundabad") and [Ered Mithrin](/wiki/Ered_Mithrin) was raided. It was not only Orcs the Alliance fought but also other savage [Men](/wiki/Easterlings "Easterlings") that came from the east. When Sauron was finally driven back from Eriador in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1701](/wiki/Second_Age_1701 "Second Age 1701"), the Alliance ended. The Men were heavily diminished in number and remained scattered and impoverished living in caves or the edges of the forest. The Longbeards still maintained [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) but their communication to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills) were cut off for some time since the enemy now occupied their former lands.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

It was during this period when the Longbeards adopted the [speech](/wiki/Northern_Mannish "Northern Mannish") of Men, keeping [their own language](/wiki/Khuzdul "Khuzdul") to themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-relations_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-relations-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

The two peoples continued to occupy the same regions and be neighbours. The same kind of friendship and coexistence would be seen again centuries later, between the [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) and the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
