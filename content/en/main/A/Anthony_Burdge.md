**Anthony Scott Burdge** is the founder and co-chairman of the [North East Tolkien Society](/wiki/North_East_Tolkien_Society), and the webmaster of their site, <a class="external text" href="http://herenistarionnets.blogspot.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Heren Istarion Nets</a>. He has given many lectures on [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), and has contributed to the *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia:_Scholarship_and_Critical_Assessment "J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia: Scholarship and Critical Assessment")*, *[Beyond Bree](/wiki/Beyond_Bree)*, *[Parma Nölé](/wiki/Parma_N%C3%B6l%C3%A9)* and *[Amon Hen](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)")*.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2005](/wiki/2005): **[Beyond Bree Award](/wiki/Beyond_Bree "Beyond Bree")**, "In recognition of outstanding contribution to the study of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), from the Readers of Beyond Bree"
- [2006](/wiki/2006): **[Mythopoeic Society Starving Scholar Award](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society "Mythopoeic Society")**, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MythCon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MythCon (page does not exist)">MythCon</a> 37
