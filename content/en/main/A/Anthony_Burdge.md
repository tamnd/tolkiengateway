**Anthony Scott Burdge** is the founder and co-chairman of the [North East Tolkien Society](../N/North_East_Tolkien_Society.md), and the webmaster of their site, <a class="external text" href="http://herenistarionnets.blogspot.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Heren Istarion Nets</a>. He has given many lectures on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), and has contributed to the *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia%253A_Scholarship_and_Critical_Assessment.md)*, *[Beyond Bree](../B/Beyond_Bree.md)*, *[Parma Nölé](../P/Parma_Nölé.md)* and *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2005](../_/2005.md): **[Beyond Bree Award](../B/Beyond_Bree.md)**, "In recognition of outstanding contribution to the study of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), from the Readers of Beyond Bree"
- [2006](../_/2006.md): **[Mythopoeic Society Starving Scholar Award](../M/Mythopoeic_Society.md)**, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=MythCon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MythCon (page does not exist)">MythCon</a> 37
