**Arahad II** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2563](/wiki/Third_Age_2563 "Third Age 2563")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [2719](/wiki/Third_Age_2719 "Third Age 2719"),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 156) was the tenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain), following his father, [Aravorn](/wiki/Aravorn), when he died in [2654](/wiki/Third_Age_2654 "Third Age 2654").

## History {#History}

During his chieftainship, the [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") flourished under the protection of the [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"), with [Tobold Hornblower](/wiki/Tobold_Hornblower) planting the first [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed") in the [Southfarthing](/wiki/Southfarthing), and the tenth Thain, [Isengrim Took II](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_II), started digging the [Great Smials](/wiki/Great_Smials).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Arahad died in [2719](/wiki/Third_Age_2719 "Third Age 2719"), after having ruled for 65 years, and was followed by his son, [Arassuil](/wiki/Arassuil).<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology of Arahad's name is uncertain, but it is likely that he was named after his great-grandfather, [Arahad I](/wiki/Arahad_I). For a discussion on the meaning of the name, see [Arahad I - Etymology](/wiki/Arahad_I#Etymology "Arahad I").
