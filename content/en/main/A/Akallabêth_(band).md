**Akallabêth** is a Tolkien-related black metal band from Sweden. The band takes its name from the [Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth), the account of the downfall of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), and its lyrics draw on Tolkien's legendarium. Its 1996 demo *Barad Dûr* features songs such as "Orodruin", "Nirnaeth Arnoediad" and "Ered Gorgoroth".
