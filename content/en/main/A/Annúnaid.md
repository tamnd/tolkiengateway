**Annúnaid** is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for the language "[Adûni](/wiki/Westron "Westron")" ([Westron](/wiki/Westron)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word incorporates the element *[annún](/wiki/Ann%C3%BBn "Annûn")*. <span></span>
