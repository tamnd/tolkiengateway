**Ae Adar Nín: The Lord’s Prayer in Sindarin** ("Pater noster" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) is [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s Sindarin translation of most of the <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Lord%27s_Prayer_(disambiguation)" title="Lord&#39;s Prayer (disambiguation)">Lord&#39;s Prayer</a>. He left the last two lines untranslated, but his notes show that he intended to use <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%9Athaes&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Úthaes (page does not exist)">úthaes</a></i> as the word for "temptation" (cognate with [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *úsahtië*). The poem can be read in *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*, [no. 44](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_44 "Vinyar Tengwar 44") on pages 21–30, edited by [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden).

Tolkien's notes on the poem, written on the back of a postcard, are reproduced on the cover of [*Tolkien Studies*: Volume 4](/wiki/Tolkien_Studies:_Volume_4 "Tolkien Studies: Volume 4").<sup id="cite_ref-TB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-1">[1]</a></sup>
