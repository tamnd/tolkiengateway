<i><b>A Tale of Tales</b></i> is a concept studio album by [InfuseR](../I/InfuseR.md) released on 1 November 2025. Drawing inspiration from the professor's vast work and crafted in an English modern epic metal style, the album merges symphonic textures with power-metal energy to tell a sweeping narrative across ten tracks.
