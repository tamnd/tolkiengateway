**A Short Rest** is the third chapter of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="254" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e9/Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Rivendell.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1354"/></a><figcaption>The valley of Rivendell</figcaption></figure>

Following their ordeal with the three Trolls, the adventurers continued their journey eastward. As they came within (what they thought) a days journey to the feet of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) asked "Is that *The* Mountain?". [Balin](../B/Balin.md) explained that the Mountains ahead were merely one obstacle in the path towards the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md).

[Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) informed the party that hidden somewhere before them was the valley of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and that he had told his Elf-friends to expect their arrival. Gandalf led the way on a path - it seems - he knew very well. Just as the Dwarves and Bilbo were growing anxious, they came across unexpected valleys. Gandalf continued to lead them and eventually they saw a fair valley far below them. Following a slippery journey down a zigzag path they entered Rivendell, where the Elves greeted them with song and laughter from within the trees.

At last they came to the [Last Homely House](../R/Rivendell.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> where they stayed (quite happily) for at least fourteen days.

On midsummer eve,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) inspected the swords which [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and Gandalf had acquired from the [Trolls' cave](../T/Trolls'_cave~67749.md). The runes on Thorin's sword named it [Orcrist](../O/Orcrist.md), the Goblin-cleaver; the runes on Gandalf's sword read [Glamdring](../G/Glamdring.md), Foe-hammer - both were ancient blades forged in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md). Elrond then inspected [Thrór's Map](../T/Thrór's_Map.md), he found Moon letters which read: "Stand by the grey stone when the thrush knocks, and the setting sun with the last light of [Durin's Day](../D/Durin's_Day.md) will shine upon the keyhole."

On [Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md)'s morning, the adventurers departed from Rivendell for the Misty Mountains and the land beyond. <span></span>
