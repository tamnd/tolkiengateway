**Andúril - Flame of the West** is the twenty-third scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the thirtieth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>).

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

A hooded rider on a white horse slowly makes his way to the camp at [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).

[Aragorn](Aragorn.md) dreams of [Arwen](Arwen.md) regretting her decision to leave him. The <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Evenstar.md" title="Evenstar">Evenstar</a> falls from his grasp and shatters. At this he wakes to a soldier telling him [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) is awaiting him.

Aragorn enters the king's tent, where he sees Théoden conversing with the hooded rider. Théoden leaves and the rider reveals himself to be [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md). He brings Aragorn grim tidings, saying that Arwen is dying, and Aragorn is out outnumbered in the battle to come, citing the fleet of [Corsair](../C/Corsair.md) ships arriving to attack [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). Elrond calls upon Aragorn to call upon "[those who dwell in the mountain](../D/Dead_Men_of_Dunharrow.md)" to fight for him. Elrond tells him they will answer to the [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) and flourishes [Andúril](Andúril.md) — [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md) reforged. Aragorn takes it from him and draws it. "Put aside the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../R/Ranger.md" title="Ranger">Ranger</a>. Become who you were born to be," Elrond says to Aragorn.

## Differences {#Differences}

The idea of Aragorn being given Andúril at this moment in the story is a large deviation from the source material. In the book, Aragorn was given the shards of Narsil by Elrond when he was twenty years of age,<sup id="cite_ref-App_Tale_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App_Tale-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1057</sup> and Andúril was forged from the shards before the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) departed [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The final words spoken between Elrond and Aragorn in this scene are the words spoken in the book by [Gilraen](../G/Gilraen.md), the mother of Aragorn, to her son, at their final parting. She told Aragorn she was unable to face the coming darkness and would soon leave [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). After Aragorn tried to comfort her, she replied *"Ónen i-Estel Edain, ú-chebin estel anim (I gave Hope to the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md), I have kept no hope for myself)"*.<sup id="cite_ref-App_Tale_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App_Tale-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1061</sup> <span></span>
