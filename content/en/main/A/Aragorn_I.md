**Aragorn I** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2227](/wiki/Third_Age_2227 "Third Age 2227")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [2327](/wiki/Third_Age_2327 "Third Age 2327"),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 100) was the son of [Aravir](/wiki/Aravir), and fifth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain).

## History {#History}

Little is known of his life as it was during the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace), other than that he succeeded his father when Aravir died in 2319. It is likely that his short rule was uneventful, with small battles to free the lands of evil.

Indeed, after having ruled as Chieftain for only eight years, his life was cut short in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2327](/wiki/Third_Age_2327 "Third Age 2327"), when he was killed by savage [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) (which were always a peril in that land). He was succeeded by his son [Araglas](/wiki/Araglas).<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="/wiki/Aragorn#Etymology" title="Aragorn">Aragorn#Etymology</a></div></div>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r398211"/>

Aragorn's name is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), and means "Revered King", from *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")*, a prefix for royalty, and *gorn* "revered".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
