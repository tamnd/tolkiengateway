**Acocks Green** is an area of south [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) located two miles north-east of [Sarehole](/wiki/Sarehole) (now Hall Green). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) used to walk to this location to see his uncle.

The following is an extract from a letter Tolkien sent to a group of primary school children in Acocks Green:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I lived till I was 8 at Sarehole and used to walk to A[cocks] G[reen] to see my uncle. It was all &#39;country&#39; then ...
</p>

</blockquote>
