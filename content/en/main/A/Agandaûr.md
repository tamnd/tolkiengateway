**Agandaûr** is one of the main characters and antagonist of *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md)*. He is a descendant of the [Black Númenóreans](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s most cruel servant.<sup id="cite_ref-backstory_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-backstory-1">[1]</a></sup> He was both a mighty [sorcerer](../M/Magic.md) and warrior, being able to fight [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md) at the same time.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadeltower_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadeltower-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### The Lord of the Rings: War in the North {#The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North}

#### Early life {#Early_life}

Agandaûr's birth date, as well as most of his personal details, are unknown. He must have started serving Sauron somewhere in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md). Agandaûr became a great warrior and sorcerer, likely being taught by Sauron himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadeltower_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadeltower-2">[2]</a></sup>

#### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Agandaûr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e4/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e4/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/e4/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_War_in_the_North_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_fights_Elladan_and_Elrohir.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="720"/></a><figcaption>Agandaûr fights <a class="mw-redirect" href="../E/Elladan.md" title="Elladan">Elladan</a> and <a class="mw-redirect" href="../E/Elrohir.md" title="Elrohir">Elrohir</a>.</figcaption></figure>

At the same time that the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) were sent to retrieve the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md), Agandaûr was sent north to conquer [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and destroy [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Having stirred the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md), he assembled a great army amidst the ruins of [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-4">[4]</a></sup> With [Tharzog](../T/Tharzog.md), chieftain of the Orcs of [Mount Gram](../M/Mount_Gram.md), in charge of his soldiery<sup id="cite_ref-backstory_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-backstory-1">[1]</a></sup> he passed his knowledge of sorcery to the Orcs through the scrolls of his lieutenant, Wulfrun.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadeltower_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadeltower-2">[2]</a></sup>

Just shortly after the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) defeated the [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) at [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md), Agandaûr met there with the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md), Telling him he was ready to attack, as assistance for the Nine Riders in the [Hunt for the Ring](../H/Hunt_for_the_Ring.md), he was ordered by the Witch-king to return and prepare his forces to join them.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-4">[4]</a></sup>

After his return to Fornost his soldiers captured a [giant Eagle](../E/Eagles.md), named [Beleram](../B/Beleram.md), who was spying on the gathering of Orcs. On [Aragorn](Aragorn.md)'s order [Eradan](<../E/Eradan_(video_game_character).md>), a [Dúnadan](../D/Dúnedain.md), [Andriel](Andriel.md), an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) loremaster from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md), and [Farin](<../F/Farin_(video_game_character).md>), a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) from [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), infiltrated Fornost to stop the army of Orcs from joining the Nazgûl. When they found the chained Eagle, they helped him to escape.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When they met with two more infiltrators, [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md), they formed a team to cripple the Orc forces and slay Agandaûr.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After they fought a way to the citadel tower Eradan, Andriel and Farin dealt with Tharzog while Elladan and Elrohir confronted Agandaûr. Tharzog was killed but Agandaûr had the upper hand in his battle with the Half-elves. However, when the Half-elves where joined by Eradan, Andriel and Farin, he fled on the back of a [Fell beast](../F/Fell_beast.md). Even though Beleram pursued him in the air, Agandaûr escaped the Eagle's clutches by summoning a thunder storm.<sup id="cite_ref-Citadeltower_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Citadeltower-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the aftermath of his retreat from Fornost Agandaûr turns to rallying his forces for his campaign in the North and forging alliances. Therefore, he recruits the renegade Stone-giant Bargrisar to Sauron's cause, using his vile war-bands of Orcs and [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) to destroy the nests of the Great Eagles.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Further, he enlists the Spider-Queen Saenathra of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) to silence the Wizard [Radagast](../R/Radagast.md), in order to prevent him from sending messages to the Free Peoples. Both of his plots are foiled, yet Agandaûr resolves to treat with the [Dragon](../D/Dragon.md) Urgost on behalf of Sauron, offering him the neighboring Dwarven realm of Nordinbad in exchange for joining the Dark Lord's force that assaults the North.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Guardians of Middle-earth {#Guardians_of_Middle-earth}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/G/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Agandaûr_Icon.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_Icon.jpg/300px-Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_Icon.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="276" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_Icon.jpg/450px-Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_Icon.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_Icon.jpg/600px-Guardians_of_Middle-earth_-_Aganda%C3%BBr_Icon.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3000" data-file-height="2763"/></a><figcaption>Agandaûr in <i><a href="../G/Guardians_of_Middle-earth.md" title="Guardians of Middle-earth">Guardians of Middle-earth</a></i></figcaption></figure>

Agandaûr was a sadistic and bloodthirsty [Black Númenórean](../B/Black_Númenóreans.md) whose lust for power bent him to the will of the dark arts, becoming one of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s most feared Lieutenants.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> In the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md)'s absence, Agandaûr led the Dark Army, spreading fear and destruction in the name of the Dark Lord. He was a powerful sorcerer who could channel his power into bolts of lightning and ruled his underlings through fear and pain.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Agandaûr's conversation with the Dwarves of Nordinbad mentioned by Lord Gorin appears to be based on that of [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) and the emissary of Sauron mentioned in *[The Council of Elrond](../T/The_Council_of_Elrond.md)*.
