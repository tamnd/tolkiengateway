**Amon Uilos** was the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) translation of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name [Oiolossë](../O/Oiolossë.md). It was better known by the name of [Taniquetil](../T/Taniquetil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- *[amon](Amon.md)* ("steep-sided mount") + *[uilos](../U/Uilos.md)* ("always white")
