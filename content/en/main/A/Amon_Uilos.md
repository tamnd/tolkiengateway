**Amon Uilos** was the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) translation of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB). It was better known by the name of [Taniquetil](/wiki/Taniquetil).<sup id="cite_ref-SIndex_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIndex-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("steep-sided mount") + *[uilos](/wiki/Uilos "Uilos")* ("always white")
