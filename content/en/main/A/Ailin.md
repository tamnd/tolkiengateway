<i><b>ailin</b></i> means "pool, lake"<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> or "large lake"<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup> in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), *ailin* is conceived as deriving from [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *ai-lin-*, derived from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [AY](/wiki/AY).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> In a later conception, the word is said to have "originally contained [√AYA(R)](</wiki/AYA(R)> "AYA(R)") + *lin*" (cf. [LINI](/wiki/LINI)).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>
