<i><b>ailin</b></i> means "pool, lake"<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> or "large lake"<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md), *ailin* is conceived as deriving from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *ai-lin-*, derived from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [AY](AY.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> In a later conception, the word is said to have "originally contained [√AYA(R)](<AYA(R).md>) + *lin*" (cf. [LINI](../L/LINI.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>
