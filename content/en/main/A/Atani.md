**Atani** was a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for [Men](../M/Men.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and especially the [Men](../M/Men.md) of the [Three Houses](../E/Edain.md#The_Three_Houses) of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md). However it was seldomly applied to the Men east of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Atan* pl. *Atani* is glossed as "Second People" in the Silmarillion Index. (cf: *[atta](Atta.md)* "two").

The equivalents of [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) are *[Adan](Adan.md)* pl. *Edain*.

The word appears in names such as [Núnatani](../N/Núnatani.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hr%C3%B3atani&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hróatani (page does not exist)">Hróatani</a>, [Atanalcar](Atanalcar.md), [Atanamir](Atanamir.md), <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Atanatar.md" title="Atanatar">Atanatar</a> and [Atanatari](Atanatari.md).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a draft to the text *[Of Dwarves and Men](../O/Of_Dwarves_and_Men.md)*, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) considered that the word is derived from the [language](../T/Taliska.md) of the [Folk of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md), but as [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) noted, it contradicted the final version of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
