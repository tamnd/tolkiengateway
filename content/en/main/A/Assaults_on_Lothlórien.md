The **assaults on [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien)** were a series of conflicts in [March](/wiki/March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") initiated by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) against the realm during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

All attempts by Sauron’s armies failed. Besides the valour of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim), the realm was so powerful that it could have only been overcome by Sauron himself.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Prelude {#Prelude}

In the years leading to the War of the Ring, [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) had become active<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (possibly under the power of [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl), its former commander).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Escaping from [Moria](/wiki/Moria), the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) arrived at Lothlórien, [bringing](/wiki/Ring-bearer "Ring-bearer") the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring). Soon after, a band of hundred [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) (presumably [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) pursuing them) crossed the [Nimrodel](</wiki/Nimrodel_(river)> "Nimrodel (river)"). The wardens used feigned voices and led them into a trap, where a regiment or so of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) were sent out, and destroyed them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

But the coming of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") and the orc pursuers were only the heralds of greater conflicts that were to occur. [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) unleashed attacks on the Galadhrim.

## Assaults {#Assaults}

On [11 March](/wiki/11_March), forces from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) swarmed into the forest in a full-scale assault and were held back by the power of the Wood-elves.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup> This was noticed by [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), who saw smoke raising from Lórien's border, for he was wearing [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") at the [Seat of Seeing](/wiki/Seat_of_Seeing).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [15 March](/wiki/15_March), there was a [big battle outside Minas Tirith](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields"). At the same time forces from Dol Guldur [invaded Mirkwood](/wiki/Battle_under_the_trees "Battle under the trees") and once more attacked Lothlórien.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

The third and last attack was made seven days later, on [22 March](/wiki/22_March). They caused much destruction on the wood's borders, but the valiant Galadhrim routed them utterly, and the realm remained safe for the following days until the passing of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

In [28 March](/wiki/28_March),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> after the [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow) of Sauron was lifted, Celeborn led the hosts of the [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) across the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), and they stormed [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) and cleansed [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood); [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) gave its southern part into the dominion of Lothlórien, and it was called [East Lórien](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>
