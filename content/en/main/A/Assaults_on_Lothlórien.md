The **assaults on [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md)** were a series of conflicts in [March](../M/March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) initiated by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) against the realm during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).

All attempts by Sauron’s armies failed. Besides the valour of the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md), the realm was so powerful that it could have only been overcome by Sauron himself.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Prelude {#Prelude}

In the years leading to the War of the Ring, [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) had become active<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (possibly under the power of [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md), its former commander).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Escaping from [Moria](../M/Moria.md), the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) arrived at Lothlórien, [bringing](../R/Ring-bearer.md) the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md). Soon after, a band of hundred [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) (presumably [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) pursuing them) crossed the [Nimrodel](<../N/Nimrodel_(river).md>). The wardens used feigned voices and led them into a trap, where a regiment or so of the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) were sent out, and destroyed them.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

But the coming of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) and the orc pursuers were only the heralds of greater conflicts that were to occur. [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) unleashed attacks on the Galadhrim.

## Assaults {#Assaults}

On [11 March](../_/11_March.md), forces from [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) swarmed into the forest in a full-scale assault and were held back by the power of the Wood-elves.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup> This was noticed by [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), who saw smoke raising from Lórien's border, for he was wearing [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) at the [Seat of Seeing](../S/Seat_of_Seeing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [15 March](../_/15_March.md), there was a [big battle outside Minas Tirith](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). At the same time forces from Dol Guldur [invaded Mirkwood](../B/Battle_under_the_trees.md) and once more attacked Lothlórien.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

The third and last attack was made seven days later, on [22 March](../_/22_March.md). They caused much destruction on the wood's borders, but the valiant Galadhrim routed them utterly, and the realm remained safe for the following days until the passing of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

In [28 March](../_/28_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> after the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) of Sauron was lifted, Celeborn led the hosts of the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) across the [Anduin](Anduin.md), and they stormed [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) and cleansed [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md); [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) gave its southern part into the dominion of Lothlórien, and it was called [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>
