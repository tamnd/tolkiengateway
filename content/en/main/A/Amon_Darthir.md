**Amon Darthir** was a mountain in the range of the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md), the [Mountains of Shadow](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) that separated [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) to the north from [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It stood near the house of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) in the south-east of Dor-lómin, and the springs of [Nen Lalaith](../N/Nen_Lalaith.md) were among its northern slopes. The secret and difficult passes that ran beneath Amon Darthir's peak were the only way through the Ered Wethrin for many leagues in either direction.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Amon Darthir is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md):

- *[amon](Amon.md)* 'steep-sloped mountain'
- *[dartha](../D/Dartha.md)* 'to wait'
