**Amon Darthir** was a mountain in the range of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin "Ered Wethrin") that separated [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) to the north from [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It stood near the house of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) in the south-east of Dor-lómin, and the springs of [Nen Lalaith](/wiki/Nen_Lalaith) were among its northern slopes. The secret and difficult passes that ran beneath Amon Darthir's peak were the only way through the Ered Wethrin for many leagues in either direction.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Amon Darthir is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin):

- *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* 'steep-sloped mountain'
- *[dartha](/wiki/Dartha "Dartha")* 'to wait'
