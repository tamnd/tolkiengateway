**Anfalas**, or **Langstrand**, was a coastal fief<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> between the rivers [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui) and [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) south of the [Pinnath Gelin](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) in the southwest of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is not known when Anfalas became a part of the kingdom of Gondor. It is possible that it became a part of Gondor when king [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur) extended the borders of Gondor along the coasts west of the [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the reign of King [Telumehtar](/wiki/Telumehtar_Umbardacil "Telumehtar Umbardacil") the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) raided the coasts of Gondor as far as the Anfalas. As a consequence, King Telumehtar assembled his troops, conquered Umbar by storm<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1810](/wiki/Third_Age_1810 "Third Age 1810") and drove out the Corsairs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) [Golasgil](/wiki/Golasgil), the lord of Anfalas, led a long line of men of many sorts from Anfalas, hunters, herdsmen and men from little villages, which except the men of his household were scantily equipped from Anfalas to defend [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anfalas* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-PE_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-7">[7]</a></sup> which means "Long Beach".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It consists of the elements *[and](/wiki/And "And")*<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and *[falas](/wiki/Falas "Falas")* ("beach", "strand").<sup id="cite_ref-PE_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> It is also translated as <i><b>Andafalasse</b></i> in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and <i><b>Langstrand</b></i> in [Westron](/wiki/Westron) ("translated" into English).<sup id="cite_ref-N_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-13">[13]</a></sup> [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted that the shortening of *long* to *lang* is very frequent in English place-names.<sup id="cite_ref-N_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-13">[13]</a></sup>
