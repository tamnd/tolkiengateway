**Anfalas**, or **Langstrand**, was a coastal fief<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> between the rivers [Lefnui](../L/Lefnui.md) and [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md) south of the [Pinnath Gelin](../P/Pinnath_Gelin.md) in the southwest of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is not known when Anfalas became a part of the kingdom of Gondor. It is possible that it became a part of Gondor when king [Tarannon Falastur](../T/Tarannon_Falastur.md) extended the borders of Gondor along the coasts west of the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the reign of King [Telumehtar](../T/Telumehtar_Umbardacil.md) the [Corsairs of Umbar](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) raided the coasts of Gondor as far as the Anfalas. As a consequence, King Telumehtar assembled his troops, conquered Umbar by storm<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1810](../T/Third_Age_1810.md) and drove out the Corsairs.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) [Golasgil](../G/Golasgil.md), the lord of Anfalas, led a long line of men of many sorts from Anfalas, hunters, herdsmen and men from little villages, which except the men of his household were scantily equipped from Anfalas to defend [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anfalas* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name,<sup id="cite_ref-PE_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-7">[7]</a></sup> which means "Long Beach".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It consists of the elements *[and](And.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and *[falas](../F/Falas.md)* ("beach", "strand").<sup id="cite_ref-PE_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> It is also translated as <i><b>Andafalasse</b></i> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and <i><b>Langstrand</b></i> in [Westron](../W/Westron.md) ("translated" into English).<sup id="cite_ref-N_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-13">[13]</a></sup> [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the shortening of *long* to *lang* is very frequent in English place-names.<sup id="cite_ref-N_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-13">[13]</a></sup>
