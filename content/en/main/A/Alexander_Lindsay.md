**Alexander Lindsay** (born [July 2](../_/2_July.md) [1976](../_/1976.md))<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup> portrayed [Erethón](../E/Erethón.md) and an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup>

Lindsay read *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* for the first time at the age of 10, and read it many more times before production of [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) begun. He did not wear a wig as Erethón.<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup>
