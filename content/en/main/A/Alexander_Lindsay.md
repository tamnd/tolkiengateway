**Alexander Lindsay** (born [July 2](/wiki/2_July "2 July") [1976](/wiki/1976))<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup> portrayed [Erethón](/wiki/Ereth%C3%B3n) and an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup>

Lindsay read *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* for the first time at the age of 10, and read it many more times before production of [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") begun. He did not wear a wig as Erethón.<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup>
