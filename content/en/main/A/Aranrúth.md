**Aranrúth** was the sword of the [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Thingol apparently prized his weapon: when [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) asked Thingol for a sword of worth for his quest to seek [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), the King said he could have his choice of any except his own.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Aranrúth was possessed by the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor"), which was descended to [Elros](/wiki/Elros) from [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) his mother. It did not survive the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aranrúth* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "King's Ire",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> from *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* ("king") + and *[rûth](/wiki/R%C3%BBth "Rûth")* ("anger").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Nauglafring](/wiki/The_Nauglafring)*, from *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, among the items Tinwelint has wrought from the treasure of the Rodothlim ([Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond)) is a precious sword:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
