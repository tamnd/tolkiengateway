**Adrahil** was the twenty-first [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth "Princes of Dol Amroth").<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Adrahil was the son of [Angelimir](/wiki/Angelimir). He became the twenty-first Prince of Dol Amroth after his father's death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2977](/wiki/Third_Age_2977 "Third Age 2977").<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

Adrahil had three children. His oldest daughter, [Ivriniel](/wiki/Ivriniel), his second daughter, [Finduilas](</wiki/Finduilas_(wife_of_Denethor)> "Finduilas (wife of Denethor)") and his youngest child and only son, [Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2976](/wiki/Third_Age_2976 "Third Age 2976")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Adrahil's daughter Finduilas married [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), the son of [Steward](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor "Steward of Gondor") [Ecthelion II](/wiki/Ecthelion_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Two years later in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2978](/wiki/Third_Age_2978 "Third Age 2978") Finduilas' older son [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) was born and five years after that in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2983](/wiki/Third_Age_2983 "Third Age 2983") her younger son [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) was born.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Five years after the birth of her second son Faramir, Finduilas died young at the age of 38 in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2988](/wiki/Third_Age_2988 "Third Age 2988").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Adrahil died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3010](/wiki/Third_Age_3010 "Third Age 3010") and was succeeded by his only son Imrahil.<sup id="cite_ref-DolAmroth_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DolAmroth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Adrahil presumably is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> of unknown meaning.

The second element *-hil* (also seen in the name *[Imrahil](/wiki/Imrahil)*) perhaps is related to the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) ending *-kil* (cf. *[banakil](/wiki/Banakil "Banakil"), [Tarkil](/wiki/Tarkil)*) meaning "man", ultimately related to [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) "follower".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
