**ARES Games** is an Italian company established in 2011. They focus on a wide variety of board and card game products, including their licensed "War of the Ring" line of board and card games, primarily focused on games that allow players to re-enact political and military events from [The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit) and [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings).
