<i><b>Atalantë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "the downfallen", from the verb-root *[talat-](/wiki/Talat- "Talat-")*. It was the name given to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) after its [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") called it "*a happy accident*", because it invokes the name <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantis" title="wikipedia:Atlantis">Atlantis</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
