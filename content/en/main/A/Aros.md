**Aros** was a river of [East Beleriand](../E/East_Beleriand.md) that formed much of the border of the Kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).

It arose in the northern lands west of the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md) in the [Shadowy Spring](../S/Shadowy_Spring.md), and flowed south between [Dor Dínen](../D/Dor_Dínen.md) and [Himlad](../H/Himlad.md); between these two lands the river could be crossed at [Arossiach](Arossiach.md). The river's course then followed the eastern and, turning west, the southern boundaries of the [Forest of Region](../F/Forest_of_Region.md); at that point it was joined by its main tributary, [Celon](../C/Celon.md). Aros then met the [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) at [Aelin-uial](Aelin-uial.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, river Aros was flowing south of Artanor ([Doriath](../D/Doriath.md)). Near its spring it was passing the caves of the [Rodothlim](../R/Rodothlim.md) ([Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md)),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but this was soon changed to [Narog](../N/Narog.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Aros*, derived from the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=YAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YAR (page does not exist)">YAR-</a> ("blood"), is said to be a "*name of river with reddish water*". The [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) form of the word was *iaros*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
