**Aros** was a river of [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) that formed much of the border of the Kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).

It arose in the northern lands west of the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon) in the [Shadowy Spring](/wiki/Shadowy_Spring), and flowed south between [Dor Dínen](/wiki/Dor_D%C3%ADnen) and [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad); between these two lands the river could be crossed at [Arossiach](/wiki/Arossiach). The river's course then followed the eastern and, turning west, the southern boundaries of the [Forest of Region](/wiki/Forest_of_Region); at that point it was joined by its main tributary, [Celon](/wiki/Celon). Aros then met the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) at [Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, river Aros was flowing south of Artanor ([Doriath](/wiki/Doriath)). Near its spring it was passing the caves of the [Rodothlim](/wiki/Rodothlim) ([Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond)),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but this was soon changed to [Narog](/wiki/Narog).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *Aros*, derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=YAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="YAR (page does not exist)">YAR-</a> ("blood"), is said to be a "*name of river with reddish water*". The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) form of the word was *iaros*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
