**Amazon MGM Studios**, formerly known as **Amazon Studios**, is an American television and film producer and distributor that is a subsidiary of [Amazon](Amazon.com_Incorporated.md). It specializes in developing television series and distributing and producing films.

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Amazon_Studios_logo.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/20/Amazon_Studios_logo.png/200px-Amazon_Studios_logo.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="85" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/20/Amazon_Studios_logo.png/300px-Amazon_Studios_logo.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/20/Amazon_Studios_logo.png/400px-Amazon_Studios_logo.png 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="817"/></a><figcaption>Amazon Studios logo</figcaption></figure>

The company has produced the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*, while the second season is currently in production.
