**Amazon MGM Studios**, formerly known as **Amazon Studios**, is an American television and film producer and distributor that is a subsidiary of [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon.com_Incorporated "Amazon.com Incorporated"). It specializes in developing television series and distributing and producing films.

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Amazon_Studios_logo.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/20/Amazon_Studios_logo.png/200px-Amazon_Studios_logo.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="85" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/20/Amazon_Studios_logo.png/300px-Amazon_Studios_logo.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/20/Amazon_Studios_logo.png/400px-Amazon_Studios_logo.png 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="817"/></a><figcaption>Amazon Studios logo</figcaption></figure>

The company has produced the first season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*, while the second season is currently in production.
