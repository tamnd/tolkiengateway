**Annúminas** was the city of the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor) before the capital of the [North Kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") was relocated to [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain). The city lay on the south-east shore of [Lake Nenuial](/wiki/Lake_Evendim "Lake Evendim"), near the source of the [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) river. To the south and west of Annúminas rose the [Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The city was founded by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320") shortly after his arrival in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) following the destruction of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> With the establishment of Arnor the city became the capital of the realm for several centuries; and was home to the [Annúminas-stone](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas-stone), one of the three *[palantíri](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* of the North-Kingdom,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the [Sceptre of Annúminas](/wiki/Sceptre_of_Ann%C3%BAminas), originally the rod of office of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") [Lords of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB), the heirloom of and symbol of kingship in Arnor.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-4">[4]</a></sup>

Due to losses in the war against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), the number of the [Dúnedain of Arnor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor) began to dwindle, until eventually Annúminas was deserted and [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) became the capital.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup> After [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861") the ruins of Annúminas belonged to the petty Arnorian successor kingdom of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain).

The two relics which had been housed in Annúminas survived the city: The *palantír* remained for more than a millennium after the loss of its city, but was ultimately drowned with [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) in the [cold northern seas](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel "Icebay of Forochel").<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-4">[4]</a></sup> The silver Sceptre of Annúminas would have been removed to Fornost by the Kings, and eventually came to be kept by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

At the time of the coronation of [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) Annúminas had lain in ruins for more than two thousand years. Yet Aragorn soon set his mind to preparing a place to stay in the ancient city. Aragorn sometimes came North to his house in Annúminas restored and stayed for a while beside Lake Evendim, and when that happened everyone in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") was said to be glad.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Annúminas* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) from *[annún](/wiki/Ann%C3%BBn "Annûn")* ("west") and *[minas](/wiki/Minas "Minas")* ("tower"): "Tower of the West".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Early names for Annúminas were Tarkilmar,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> or Westermanton. Torfirion is an Elvish name appearing in the [The 1943 Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_1943_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings) and on the western part of the [The First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings).

A late manuscript version of what would later become Appenix A stated in an entry for king Eärendur that the kingdom of Arnor was broken up after Eärendur, that the direct line of the eldest son ruled Arthedain with their city at Fornost and that Annúminas became deserted, because of the dwindling of the people.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> An earlier manuscript version stated in an entry for king Valandil that he removed to Fornost and that Annúminas was deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>

A later manuscript version of what would later become Appendix B stated in an entry for the year 861 of the Third Age the death of Eärendur, that the North-kingdom became divided among his sons, that the direct line of his eldest son Amlaith of Fornost ruled Arthedain, and that Annuminas is deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a> — <a href="/wiki/Arnor:_The_Land" title="Arnor: The Land">Arnor: The Land</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Annúminas is described as having remained the capital of <a href="/wiki/Arnor" title="Arnor">Arnor</a> throughout its existence, as well as having been the first capital of <a href="/wiki/Arthedain" title="Arthedain">Arthedain</a>. The city was designed without walls, though it was defended by five guard towers, the <i>Beraid Tirith</i>. Even before the city&#39;s destruction by <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_1409" title="Third Age 1409">1409</a>, military matters had drawn the royal family towards the citadel of Fornost Erain, with the palace mostly serving as a retreat for scholars. The city&#39;s reputation for scholarship was such that <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a> both resided in the city for a while, when they first came to Middle-earth. By <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1643&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1643 (page does not exist)">1643</a>, Rivermen had established a small fishing village in the ruins of the city&#39;s old quarter.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg/200px-Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="132" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/8d/Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg/300px-Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/8d/Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg/400px-Ann%C3%BAminas_LOTRO.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1820" data-file-height="1200"/></a><figcaption>A view of Annúminas in <i>The Lord of the Rings Online</i></figcaption></figure>

<dl><dd>The city of Annúminas is available for exploration and adventuring in its entirety. It is partially submerged in the waters of the lake and is overrun by servants of Angmar, who invaded the city looking for instructions to operate its palantír. A group of the Dúnedain of the North, calling themselves Wardens of Annuminas, strives to preserve the ancient city of their ancestors and its rich history, taking fight against Angmar forces, Tomb-Robbers seeking to plunder ancient relics and even the forces of nature itself when necessary. The city is situated on the hill slope and as such is presented as a multi-levelled place akin to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>: travellers are constantly going either uphill or downhill to reach their destination. Inside the city are several market places, living quarters, pools, gardens and other artefacts of everyday life. At the highest point is the King&#39;s Seat of power: the chamber of Ost Elendil. To the east of the city lies the Way of Kings, a row of tombs where Kings of Arnor reside, including a ceremonial Tomb of Elendil, where some of his belongings are buried in memory of the city&#39;s founder.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>As part of their effort to re-create all of Middle-earth, <a href="/wiki/ArdaCraft" title="ArdaCraft">ArdaCraft</a> built Annúminas in the late Third Age, long after it had been abandoned. It drew inspiration from Roman, Byzantine and Babylonian ruins.</dd></dl>
