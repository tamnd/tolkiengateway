**American Tolkien Society Calendar 1988** contains black and white illustrations by fourteen different artists. It is printed on tan paper and is:

<dl><dd>A dual calendar 1988: featuring the everyday calendar most of us use, and the Shire Reckoning calendar set forth by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> in Appendix D of <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a>.</dd></dl>

- Dimensions: 11 x 8.5 inches
- Stapled on the fold
- Punched hanging hole
