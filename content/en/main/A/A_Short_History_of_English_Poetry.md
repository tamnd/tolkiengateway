<i><b>A Short History of English Poetry</b></i> is a book which includes Stanza 32 from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s Modern English translation of the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) poem "<a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a>". The text as printed here contains a number of minor variations compared to the text published in *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl_and_Sir_Orfeo)* (1975).

Tolkien entered into negiations with Heinemann and James Reeves in [1959](/wiki/1959) to publish the complete translation, but later had to withdraw when he realised that he had already offered it to [Allen and Unwin](/wiki/Allen_and_Unwin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
