<i><b>Ambarmenië</b></i> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "the way of the world", which is, the conditions in which the creatures lived within [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar#Ambar), the world. These conditions, being created by [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) himself, were fixed and could not be altered by the creatures themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The term is clearly a combination of *[Ambar](/wiki/Ambar#Ambar)* ("the World") + gerund of *[men-](/wiki/Men- "Men-")* ("to go, proceed, move").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
