**A Plea to the Rocks** is the twenty-fourth track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One:_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack)>)*. The song was sung by [Sophia Nomvete](/wiki/Sophia_Nomvete) as the [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") [Disa](/wiki/Disa).
