**Alcarondas**, the "Castle of the Sea", was the golden-and-black flagship of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n), the last King of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). It had many oars and many masts, upon which were sails of gold and sable.

In this ship, he sailed to the shores of [Aman](/wiki/Aman), leading the [Great Armament](/wiki/Great_Armament) to challenge the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). Alcarondas indeed reached the Undying Lands and anchored at shores of the Undying Lands and Ar-Pharazôn reached [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion), but this act only condemned him, his fleet and his kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><b>Alcarondas</b></i>, also written Alkarondas <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> means "Castle of the Sea" <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and is probably [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic). Note that \[k\] is normally spelled with "c" only in [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) words.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<i><b>Aglarrâma</b></i> appears in earlier text and is probably [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) meaning the same.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
