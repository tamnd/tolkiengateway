**AIWĒ** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "(small) [bird](/wiki/Birds "Birds")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[aiwe](/wiki/Aiwe "Aiwe")*
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): *[aew](/wiki/Aew "Aew")*
