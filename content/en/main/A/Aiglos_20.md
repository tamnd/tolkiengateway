<i><b>Aiglos 20</b></i> is a jubilee Polish language issue of <i><b><a href="/wiki/Aiglos_(journal)" title="Aiglos (journal)">Aiglos</a></b></i>.,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which contains 326 pages filled with articles and artwork of artists from around the world. The highlight of the jubilee gallery is a page from the <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Parma_Eldali%C3%A9va&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Parma Eldaliéva (page does not exist)">Parma Eldaliéva</a></i> - a calligraphic art book in tengwar by 'Elmenel'. The first print run contains also a present in a form of decorative metal bookmark with *Aiglos* logo.

## Contents {#Contents}

\[Titles below in translation from Polish by the editors of *Aiglos*.\]

- **Cover Art**
    - *Battle of Five Armies: Elves of Mirkwood*
        - By: Joona Kujanen

- **Articles and essays**
    - "Tolkien’s philosophy and theology of death"
        - By: Christopher Garbowski
    - "Reinterpretation of the heroic ideal in Tolkien’s writings on the basis of selected fragments of *The Lord of the Rings*"
        - By: [Łukasz Neubauer](/wiki/%C5%81ukasz_Neubauer)
    - "All things considered, they shouldn’t be called by name. Proper names’ magic in Arda"
        - By: Zofia Żółtek 'Lassemista'
    - "The Necromancer’s necromancy. Black magic, possession and zombie"
        - By: [Jakub Tymiński 'Ominous'](/wiki/Jakub_Tymi%C5%84ski "Jakub Tymiński")
    - "Éomer’s classical sources? Éomund’s son vs. young hero figure in antiquity"
        - By: Łukasz Szeląg 'leszli'

- **Fanfiction and poetry**
    - *Gaffer Gamgee’s Bedtime Story'*
        - By: Zosia Stanecka 'Nasturcja Gamgee'
    - *The last queen*
        - By: Tekla Cichocka 'Tici'

- **Reviews and interviews**
    - An interview with [Thomas Honegger](/wiki/Thomas_Honegger)
        - conducted and translated by [Łukasz Neubauer](/wiki/%C5%81ukasz_Neubauer)
    - A collective review of *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)* by the editorial staff
        - By: [Michał Leśniewski 'M.L'](/wiki/Micha%C5%82_Le%C5%9Bniewski "Michał Leśniewski")

        - By: Jakub Tymiński 'Ominous'
        - By: Magdalena Ułas 'Solaya'
        - By: [Karolina Stopa-Olszańska 'Melinir'](/wiki/Karolina_Stopa-Olsza%C5%84ska "Karolina Stopa-Olszańska")
        - By: [Agnieszka Sylwanowicz 'Evermind HS Took'](/wiki/Agnieszka_Sylwanowicz "Agnieszka Sylwanowicz")
        - By: Małgorzata Dzikowska 'Marille Undómiel'
        - By: [Karolina Węgrzyn 'Sirielle'](/wiki/User:Sirielle "User:Sirielle")
    - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Beowulf" title="Beowulf">Beowulf</a></i>
        - By: Arletta Chwojnicka 'Eilíf'
    - "Beasts, plants, creatures, and miraculous occurrences” - review of *[A Tolkien Bestiary](/wiki/A_Tolkien_Bestiary)* by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day)
        - Karolina Stopa - Olszańska 'Melinir'
    - “Bilbo, or rather his ‘biographer’, astray” - review of *[Bilbo's Journey: Discovering the Hidden Meaning in The Hobbit](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Journey:_Discovering_the_Hidden_Meaning_in_The_Hobbit)* by [Joseph Pearce](/wiki/Joseph_Pearce)
        - By: Tadeusz Andrzej Olszański ‘Halbarad Dúnadan’
    - “A new trend in blockbusters?” - review of the extended DVD edition of *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*
        - By: Agnieszka Sylwanowicz 'Evermind HS Took'
    - Report from Medieval Fantasy Symposium 2014 in Unieście, Poland
        - By: [Łukasz Neubauer](/wiki/%C5%81ukasz_Neubauer)
    - "Would J.R.R. Tolkien attend Tolk-Folk?" - report from XVI Tolk-Folk in Bielawa, Poland
        - By: Arletta Chwojnicka 'Eilíf'
    - Reports from [Oxonmoot 2014](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2014) in Oxford, UK
        - By: Karolina Węgrzyn 'Sirielle'
        - By: Ewa Żygadło 'Hithlome'

- **Polemics**
    - "What to make of this Hobbit?" a polemic against Maria Błaszkiewicz’s article
        - By: Tadeusz Andrzej Olszański ‘Halbarad Dúnadan’
    - "What to make of this Hobbit?" answer to Tadeusz Andrzej Olszański’s polemic
        - By: Maria Błaszkiewicz
    - "A few more words on Gil-galad’s paternity" – a polemic against Barbara Cecylia Liwo’s article "Gil-galad – a paternity case"
        - By: Michał Leśniewski 'M.L.'

The issue contains also a hobbit genealogical crossword puzzle by Karolina Stopa-Olszanska 'Melinir' and Tolkien news from Poland and abroad.
