**Arachon** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## History {#History}

He was the husband of [Bregil](/wiki/Bregil), daughter of [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor). They had two children together: [Brandir](</wiki/Brandir_(son_of_Arachon)> "Brandir (son of Arachon)") and [Beldis](/wiki/Beldis), who would become the mother of [Brandir the Lame](/wiki/Brandir "Brandir").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
