<i><b>amlug</b></i> is a noun meaning "[dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons")" in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety349_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety349-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ANGWA/ANGU](/wiki/ANGWA "ANGWA") and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=LOK&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="LOK (page does not exist)">LOK</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety349_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety349-1">[1]</a></sup>
