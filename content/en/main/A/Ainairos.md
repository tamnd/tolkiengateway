**Ainairos** was an [Elf](/wiki/Solosimpi "Solosimpi") of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainairos_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainairos-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ainairos was one of the [Solosimpi](/wiki/Solosimpi) that escaped the [Kinslaughter at Alqaluntë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB "Kinslaying at Alqualondë") in which his brother was killed. As the result of this, he, along with many of his people, became bitter and resentful toward the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli).

When [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) summoned the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to a council about the final path of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), Ainairos was the one to speak against the [Exiles](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") and for the concealment of Valinor, and the most that were gathered there agreed with him, desiring neither [Melko](/wiki/Melko) nor the Noldoli to return.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainairos_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainairos-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), both *Ainairos* and *Oivárin* (an earlier name for the character<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) seem to be in [Solosimpilin](/wiki/Solosimpilin), however, their meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
