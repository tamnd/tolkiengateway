**Ainairos** was an [Elf](../S/Solosimpi.md) of [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainairos_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainairos-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Ainairos was one of the [Solosimpi](../S/Solosimpi.md) that escaped the [Kinslaughter at Alqaluntë](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md) in which his brother was killed. As the result of this, he, along with many of his people, became bitter and resentful toward the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md).

When [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) summoned the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) to a council about the final path of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and the [Moon](../M/Moon.md), Ainairos was the one to speak against the [Exiles](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) and for the concealment of Valinor, and the most that were gathered there agreed with him, desiring neither [Melko](../M/Melko.md) nor the Noldoli to return.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainairos_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainairos-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to [Paul Strack](../P/Paul_Strack.md), both *Ainairos* and *Oivárin* (an earlier name for the character<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) seem to be in [Solosimpilin](../S/Solosimpilin.md), however, their meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
