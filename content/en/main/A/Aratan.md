**Aratan** was the second son of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur).

## History {#History}

In the War of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men), Aratan and his brother [Ciryon](/wiki/Ciryon) did not participate in the [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr). Isildur had sent these two brothers to man [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) so as to guard [Cirith Dúath](/wiki/Cirith_D%C3%BAath) and prevent a possible breakout of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) should he escape the siege.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), Ciryon was the first brother to fall; Aratan was mortally wounded while trying to save him. After Aratan was wounded, [Elendur](</wiki/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur)> "Elendur (son of Isildur)") advised his father to put on [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") and escape.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aratan* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Stracks suggests it means "Noble Man" and is a compound of *[(a)ra-](/wiki/Ar "Ar")* ("noble") and *[atan](/wiki/Atan "Atan")* ("Man").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early manuscripts Aratan was named "Eärnur".<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2019: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The three elder sons of Isildur appear during an extended flashback set during the <a href="/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Siege of Barad-dûr">Siege of Barad-dûr</a> in the year <a href="/wiki/Second_Age_3440" title="Second Age 3440">S.A. 3440</a>. However, because the game&#39;s license does not cover the works in which they are named, they are referred to only by their war-time aliases, meant to hide their kinship with the King. Aratan uses the name &#34;Ornaher&#34; as his alias.</dd></dl>
