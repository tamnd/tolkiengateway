**Aratan** was the second son of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md).

## History {#History}

In the War of the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md), Aratan and his brother [Ciryon](../C/Ciryon.md) did not participate in the [Siege of Barad-dûr](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md). Isildur had sent these two brothers to man [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) so as to guard [Cirith Dúath](../C/Cirith_Dúath.md) and prevent a possible breakout of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) should he escape the siege.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md), Ciryon was the first brother to fall; Aratan was mortally wounded while trying to save him. After Aratan was wounded, [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>) advised his father to put on [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) and escape.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aratan* is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Stracks suggests it means "Noble Man" and is a compound of *[(a)ra-](Ar.md)* ("noble") and *[atan](Atan.md)* ("Man").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2019: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The three elder sons of Isildur appear during an extended flashback set during the <a href="../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md" title="Siege of Barad-dûr">Siege of Barad-dûr</a> in the year <a href="../S/Second_Age_3440.md" title="Second Age 3440">S.A. 3440</a>. However, because the game&#39;s license does not cover the works in which they are named, they are referred to only by their war-time aliases, meant to hide their kinship with the King. Aratan uses the name &#34;Ornaher&#34; as his alias.</dd></dl>
