**Atyamar** was the region about the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) which the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) settled during their [Great March](../G/Great_March.md) to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aty_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aty-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When the Eldar first arrived to this land, they were so fond of it that their chieftains (the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Three_Ambassadors&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Three Ambassadors (page does not exist)">Three Ambassadors</a>) could not get them to carry on their journey at first. They were delighted by its waters and its woods, and the [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) settled for a time on the eastern banks of the [Anduin](Anduin.md). And since there were some [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) there with them, who protected them from the evils of [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) and [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), they were content for a while.<sup id="cite_ref-Aty_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aty-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

However, after many years of living there, either due to the actions of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), or because [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) withdrew his protection to get the Elves to move along, their new homes were struck by terrible weather and life grew hard for them. For the most part, this convinced the Eldar to continue on their journey westwards - crossing the Anduin (which was wild and flooded due to the great snowstorms in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md)) on the boats of the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md). But many of these Teleri were against this and they elected to stay: these became the [Nandor](../N/Nandor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Aty_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Aty-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Atyamar* means "second-home" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
