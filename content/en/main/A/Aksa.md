<i><b>Aksa</b></i> means "narrow path, ravine" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") **[AK-](/wiki/AK-)** ("narrow, confined").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
