**Alan Pollack** (born [5 September](../_/5_September.md) [1964](../_/1964.md)) is an American fantasy artist. He worked as a full-time artist for [TSR](../T/TSR.md) from [1994](../_/1994.md) to [1997](../_/1997.md), illustrating for [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) and [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md), after which he started freelancing. In that capacity, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).

Generally working in oils, he also began illustrating digitally in 2003. He has cited [Donato Giancola](../D/Donato_Giancola.md) as one of his artistic influences.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
