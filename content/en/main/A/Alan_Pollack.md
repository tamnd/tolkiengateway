**Alan Pollack** (born [5 September](/wiki/5_September) [1964](/wiki/1964)) is an American fantasy artist. He worked as a full-time artist for [TSR](/wiki/TSR) from [1994](/wiki/1994) to [1997](/wiki/1997), illustrating for [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons) and [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine), after which he started freelancing. In that capacity, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).

Generally working in oils, he also began illustrating digitally in 2003. He has cited [Donato Giancola](/wiki/Donato_Giancola) as one of his artistic influences.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
