<i><b>aiwë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "(small) bird".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Compare with *[maiwë](/wiki/Maiw%C3%AB "Maiwë")* ("seagull").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aiwe* derives from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [AIWĒ-](/wiki/AIW%C4%92-).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Aiwendil" title="Aiwendil"><b>Aiwe</b>ndil</a></i> = "Lover of birds"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Aiwen%C3%B3r%C3%AB" title="Aiwenórë"><b>Aiwe</b>nórë</a></i> = "Birdland"
