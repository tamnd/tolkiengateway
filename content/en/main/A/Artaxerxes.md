**Artaxerxes** is the wizard, and main antagonist, in *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. Artaxerxes is described as an old man in ragged trousers wearing a green hat with a blue feather.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1</sup>

## History {#History}

Originally from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persia" title="wikipedia:Persia">Persia</a>, Artaxerxes ended up in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pershore" title="wikipedia:Pershore">Pershore</a> by mistake.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:14</sup>

After Artaxerxes picks up [Rover](/wiki/Rover)'s yellow ball, Rover demands it back without saying "please". Rover bites Artaxerxes trousers when he continues to refuse to return the ball; in retaliation, Artaxerxes turns Rover into a toy dog and transports Rover to a toy shop.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1–2</sup> Although Rover visits the [Man-in-the-Moon](/wiki/Man-in-the-Moon) with the help of [Mew](/wiki/Mew), and seeks the help of the sand-sorcerer [Psamathos Psamathides](/wiki/Psamathos_Psamathides), the great whale [Uin](/wiki/Uin) takes Roverandom (as he is now known) to ask Artaxerxes to return him to being a normal dog.

Artaxerxes is living at the bottom of the Deep Blue Sea and is married to the daughter of the Mer-king and has a white Mer-dog called Rover. As a wizard, he has been appointed Pacific and Atlantic Magician and is known as **PAM**. All the woes of the mer-people are blamed on PAM, including the emergence of a Sea-serpent. Because he is so deeply unpopular, Artaxerxes leaves with his wife and Roverandom: he takes Roverandom with him as the dog continues to be polite and show faith in his magical abilities.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:55 ff</sup>

Back on land, Artaxerxes turns Roverandom back into a real dog. Wearing a purple velvet suit, Artaxerxes sets up a chocolate and cigarette shop by the beach (selling <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_(confectionery)" title="wikipedia:Rock (confectionery)">Pam&#39;s Rock</a>), whilst his wife gave swimming lessons.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:83–7</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artaxerxes" title="wikipedia:Artaxerxes">Artaxerxes</a> is the name of five Kings of Persia.

[Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond "Wayne G. Hammond") and [Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull "Christina Scull") note that the abbreviation for Pacific and Atlantic Magaician, PAM, is the same nickname that was given to former British Prime Minister <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_John_Temple,_3rd_Viscount_Palmerston" title="wikipedia:Henry John Temple, 3rd Viscount Palmerston">Lord Palmerstone</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:109</sup>
