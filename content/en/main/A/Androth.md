**Androth** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), from *[and](And.md)* = "long" and *[roth](../R/Roth.md)* = "cave") was the name of a complex of caves in the [Mountains of Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md). After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears"), [Annael](Annael.md) led some of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) and [Edain](../E/Edain.md) that survived the battle to the caves and took refuge there. [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) was fostered by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of Androth until they left for the [Havens of Sirion](../H/Havens_of_Sirion.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [488](../F/First_Age_488.md). Later he returned to lie there as an outlaw.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
