**Androth** ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[and](/wiki/And "And")* = "long" and *[roth](/wiki/Roth "Roth")* = "cave") was the name of a complex of caves in the [Mountains of Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim "Mithrim"). After the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) ("Battle of Unnumbered Tears"), [Annael](/wiki/Annael) led some of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) and [Edain](/wiki/Edain) that survived the battle to the caves and took refuge there. [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) was fostered by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of Androth until they left for the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [488](/wiki/First_Age_488 "First Age 488"). Later he returned to lie there as an outlaw.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
