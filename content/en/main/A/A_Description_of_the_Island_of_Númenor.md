**A Description of the Island of Númenor** is a chapter in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and published posthumously by his son, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md). As the title indicates, it contains a description of the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), also called [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md), comprising its geography, flora, fauna and people.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

After a short introduction detailing the sources for the account itself, the author describes the shape of the island, a star with five promontories, called as follows:

- [Forostar](../F/Forostar.md), the Northlands
- [Andustar](Andustar.md), the Westlands
- [Hyarnustar](../H/Hyarnustar.md), the Southwestlands
- [Hyarrostar](../H/Hyarrostar.md), the Southeastlands
- [Orrostar](../O/Orrostar.md), the Eastlands

The middle region of the island was the largest province and the most populated area:

- [Mittalmar](../M/Mittalmar.md), the Inlands

Here was [Armenelos](Armenelos.md), the City of Kings, as well as the sacred mountain [Meneltarma](../M/Meneltarma.md), where religious processions took place three times a year. On its southern slope was [Noirinan](../N/Noirinan.md), the Valley of the Graves. It was guarded by the eagles of [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md), one of the two species of birds mentioned in the chapter, the other being the *[Kirinki](../K/Kirinki.md)*.

Starting with the Northlands, each promontory is described in turn: Forostar, where the [Eagles of Manwë](../E/Eagles_of_Manwë.md) dwelt and where the mountain [Sorontil](../S/Sorontil.md) stood next to the sea. Andustar, which had three bays on its western side, the first being [Andúnië](Andúnië.md), which held the city with the same name, largest of the island and harbor for the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md). Here, the beauty of [Eldalondë](../E/Eldalondë.md) is described, and its greatest treasures were the majestic trees: [Oiolairë](../O/Oiolairë.md), [Lairelossë](../L/Lairelossë.md), [Nessamelda](../N/Nessamelda.md), [Vardarianna](../V/Vardarianna.md), [Taniquelassë](../T/Taniquelassë.md), [Yavannamírë](../Y/Yavannamírë.md) and [Malinornë](../M/Malinornë.md). The two rivers of the island, [Nunduinë](../N/Nunduinë.md) and [Siril](../S/Siril.md), flowed on each side of Hyarnustar, while in Hyarrostar grew [Laurinquë](../L/Laurinquë.md), a tree whose only purpose was to charm the people with its beauty. Of Orrostar little is told, only that grains could be cultivated in this cold part of the island.

A description of the people follows, mostly [Edain](../E/Edain.md) skilled in riding; the shaping of terrible weapons of war, especially bows; and in the building of ships used to sail towards [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The first of these ships to cross the sea was [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md). Many of the legendary weapons were kept here, among them [Tuor's axe](../D/Dramborleg.md) and the bow of [Bregor](../B/Bregor.md), as well as the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md), the only artifact to survive the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md).
