**A Description of the Island of Númenor** is a chapter in *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*, written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and published posthumously by his son, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien). As the title indicates, it contains a description of the island of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), also called [Elenna](/wiki/Elenna), comprising its geography, flora, fauna and people.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

After a short introduction detailing the sources for the account itself, the author describes the shape of the island, a star with five promontories, called as follows:

- [Forostar](/wiki/Forostar), the Northlands
- [Andustar](/wiki/Andustar), the Westlands
- [Hyarnustar](/wiki/Hyarnustar), the Southwestlands
- [Hyarrostar](/wiki/Hyarrostar), the Southeastlands
- [Orrostar](/wiki/Orrostar), the Eastlands

The middle region of the island was the largest province and the most populated area:

- [Mittalmar](/wiki/Mittalmar), the Inlands

Here was [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos), the City of Kings, as well as the sacred mountain [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma), where religious processions took place three times a year. On its southern slope was [Noirinan](/wiki/Noirinan), the Valley of the Graves. It was guarded by the eagles of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB), one of the two species of birds mentioned in the chapter, the other being the *[Kirinki](/wiki/Kirinki)*.

Starting with the Northlands, each promontory is described in turn: Forostar, where the [Eagles of Manwë](/wiki/Eagles_of_Manw%C3%AB) dwelt and where the mountain [Sorontil](/wiki/Sorontil) stood next to the sea. Andustar, which had three bays on its western side, the first being [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB), which held the city with the same name, largest of the island and harbor for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa "Tol Eressëa"). Here, the beauty of [Eldalondë](/wiki/Eldalond%C3%AB) is described, and its greatest treasures were the majestic trees: [Oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB), [Lairelossë](/wiki/Laireloss%C3%AB), [Nessamelda](/wiki/Nessamelda), [Vardarianna](/wiki/Vardarianna), [Taniquelassë](/wiki/Taniquelass%C3%AB), [Yavannamírë](/wiki/Yavannam%C3%ADr%C3%AB) and [Malinornë](/wiki/Malinorn%C3%AB). The two rivers of the island, [Nunduinë](/wiki/Nunduin%C3%AB) and [Siril](/wiki/Siril), flowed on each side of Hyarnustar, while in Hyarrostar grew [Laurinquë](/wiki/Laurinqu%C3%AB), a tree whose only purpose was to charm the people with its beauty. Of Orrostar little is told, only that grains could be cultivated in this cold part of the island.

A description of the people follows, mostly [Edain](/wiki/Edain) skilled in riding; the shaping of terrible weapons of war, especially bows; and in the building of ships used to sail towards [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The first of these ships to cross the sea was [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur). Many of the legendary weapons were kept here, among them [Tuor's axe](/wiki/Dramborleg "Dramborleg") and the bow of [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor), as well as the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir), the only artifact to survive the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).
