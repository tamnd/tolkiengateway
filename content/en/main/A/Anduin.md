**Anduin** was the river that crossed most of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) east of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). Passing through many lands, it had received many names - **Langflood** by the ancestors of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), the **Great River of Wilderland** in the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and simply the **Great River** in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). All these names are in recognition of the river's length.

It flowed from its source in the [Grey](/wiki/Grey_Mountains "Grey Mountains") and [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to the [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin) in the [Great Sea (Belegaer)](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). The river had perhaps a total run of 1,388 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was always the frontier of the west-lands of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Sources {#Sources}

The Anduin began as two different streams near where the Misty Mountains met the Grey. These were called the [Langwell](/wiki/Langwell) and the [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin) by the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) when they lived in the triangle of land formed by it. Their old capital [Framsburg](/wiki/Framsburg) was built at the confluence of these streams where the Anduin proper began. The [Langwell](/wiki/Langwell) had its source in the Misty Mountains, close to [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad"), and the [Greylin](/wiki/Greylin) began in the westernmost heights of the Grey Mountains.

## Course {#Course}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/32/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="309" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/32/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/32/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Argonath.jpg 2x" data-file-width="933" data-file-height="960"/></a><figcaption><i>The Argonath</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The Anduin flowed parallel to the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) in a [broad vale](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin "Vales of Anduin") which formed the western part of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), lying between the mountains and [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). After passing [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), the river and mountains parted company, and the river flowed through the [Brown Lands](/wiki/Brown_Lands) via the [North](/wiki/North_Undeep "North Undeep") and [South Undeeps](/wiki/South_Undeep "South Undeep") until it flowed past the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) and the [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath) and entered [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel) through [Sarn Gebir](/wiki/Sarn_Gebir), a series of ferocious rapids. Thence it flowed over the Falls of [Rauros](/wiki/Rauros), and past the [Mouths of Entwash](/wiki/Mouths_of_Entwash) and the marshes known as the [Wetwang](/wiki/Nindalf "Nindalf"). It then passed between the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath "Ephel Dúath") by the capital of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), before swinging past the harbour of [Harlond](/wiki/Harlond_in_Gondor "Harlond in Gondor") close to the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor), south of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). It flowed through the port of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), entering the Great Sea in the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) in a broad delta known as the Mouths of Anduin.

### Tributaries {#Tributaries}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Mark_Poole_-_Anduin.jpg"><img src="/w/images/a/a4/Mark_Poole_-_Anduin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="296" height="252" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="296" data-file-height="252"/></a><figcaption><i>Anduin River</i> by <a href="/wiki/Mark_Poole" title="Mark Poole">Mark Poole</a></figcaption></figure>

In order from north to south: the [Rhimdath](/wiki/Rhimdath) (Rushdown), the [Ninglor](/wiki/Gladden_River "Gladden River") (Gladden) which joined at the marshes known as the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields), the [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant) (Silverlode), the River [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight), the [Onodló](/wiki/Entwash "Entwash") (Entwash), the [Morgulduin](/wiki/Morgulduin), the [Erui](/wiki/Erui), the [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith) and the [Poros](/wiki/Poros). The first five had their sources in the Misty Mountains, the Morgulduin and (presumably) the Poros in the Ephel Dúath on the border of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), and the rest in the White Mountains.

### Crossing points {#Crossing_points}

The [Old Forest Road](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road) which led from the [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass) into Mirkwood crossed the river at the [Old Ford](/wiki/Old_Ford), to the south of [Beorn's Halls](/wiki/Beorn%27s_Halls). There used to be a bridge, which was fortified when [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) crossed the river to [go to war](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance "War of the Last Alliance") with [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), but it had been destroyed by the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). The only other bridge was in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), but during the late Third Age, this was broken by forces of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). The [Ford of Carrock](/wiki/Ford_of_Carrock) is mentioned during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

### Islands {#Islands}

The principal islands appear to have been [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros), on the borders of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) and [Tol Brandir](/wiki/Tol_Brandir) in Nen Hithoel. [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock), in the north was where the Eagles deposited [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company). There was also an eyot, where the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") rested during their travel between Lórien and [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen).

## Settlements {#Settlements}

During the [March of the Elves](/wiki/Great_March "Great March") in the [Time of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees"), the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) left the [Eldarin](/wiki/Eldar "Eldar") host when faced with the great heights of the Misty Mountains, and lived in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin). Some of those people later left and became the Green-elves of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), but Elves remained present even until the time of the War the Ring, strengthed by refugees from [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) (at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age)) and [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) (during the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age")).

Settlements in the Vale of Anduin during the Third Age included the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) city of [Framsburg](/wiki/Framsburg), [Rhosgobel](/wiki/Rhosgobel), [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s Halls and the [Stoor](/wiki/Stoors "Stoors") settlements near the Gladden Fields. It was in the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) in the northern reaches of Anduin that [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was slain in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2") and [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") lost; and it was there, more than two millennia later, that [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol) found the Ring and [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol) took it from him.

Once it had entered Gondor the river flowed past Osgiliath and Minas Tirith and then Pelargir, close to the sea. After the fall of Osgiliath the river effectively marks the eastern limit of Gondor's influence.

*Anduin* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Great River", literally "Long River", from *[and](/wiki/And "And")* ("long") + *[duin](/wiki/Duin "Duin")* ("large river").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) cognate was <i><b>Anduinë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The course of Anduin can be followed in the game from its headwater all the way to <a href="/wiki/Pelargir" title="Pelargir">Pelargir</a>. Along its course it passes through twelve in-game regions and is joined by many tributaries, some canonical and others original to the game. These are: <a href="/wiki/Langwell" title="Langwell">Langwell</a>, &#34;Wyrmsgráf&#34;, <a href="/wiki/Greylin" title="Greylin">Greylin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rhimdath" title="Rhimdath">Rhimdath</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gladden_River" title="Gladden River">Gladden</a>, &#34;Linduin&#34;, <a href="/wiki/Celebrant" title="Celebrant">Celebrant</a>, <a href="/wiki/Limlight" title="Limlight">Limlight</a>, <a href="/wiki/Entwash" title="Entwash">Entwash</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mering_Stream" title="Mering Stream">Mering</a>, &#34;Sírnarthan&#34;, &#34;Forant&#34;, <a href="/wiki/Morgulduin" title="Morgulduin">Morgulduin</a>, &#34;Gaelos&#34;, <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sirith" title="Sirith">Sirith</a>, <a href="/wiki/Celos" title="Celos">Celos</a> and <a href="/wiki/Poros" title="Poros">Poros</a>. The portion of the Anduin that separates <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a> from Southern <a href="/wiki/Mirkwood" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a> is not traversible due to the way the Mirkwood region was implemented: attempting to swim across results in instant death, and the only crossing possible is by using a boat. At the Fall of <a href="/wiki/Rauros" title="Rauros">Rauros</a> is the transition between the in-game landmasses of <a href="/wiki/Rhovanion" title="Rhovanion">Rhovanion</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, very noticeable because all of Gondor is covered under the <a href="/wiki/Dawnless_Day" title="Dawnless Day">Dawnless Day</a> at that time, regardless of the daylight conditions on the other side. Attempting to swim across Anduin as it flows through Gondor will cause the player&#39;s character to take signficant damage every few seconds, however with sufficiently high level this can be overcome and the river traversed.</dd></dl>
