**Andrew Butler** is a former editor of the [journal *Amon Hen*](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)"). Butler has been a member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) since [1980](/wiki/1980), and he edited *Amon Hen* from [issue 161](/wiki/Amon_Hen_161 "Amon Hen 161") in January [2000](/wiki/2000) to [issue 282](/wiki/Amon_Hen_282 "Amon Hen 282") in March [2020](/wiki/2020).

He also set up *The Baldric Press* to publish a collection of fantasy short stories by Alex Hall, illustrated by Mike Johnson, inspired by the works of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), but not featuring his characters or settings.
