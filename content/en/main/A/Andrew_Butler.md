**Andrew Butler** is a former editor of the [journal *Amon Hen*](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>). Butler has been a member of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) since [1980](../_/1980.md), and he edited *Amon Hen* from [issue 161](Amon_Hen_161.md) in January [2000](../_/2000.md) to [issue 282](Amon_Hen_282.md) in March [2020](../_/2020.md).

He also set up *The Baldric Press* to publish a collection of fantasy short stories by Alex Hall, illustrated by Mike Johnson, inspired by the works of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), but not featuring his characters or settings.
