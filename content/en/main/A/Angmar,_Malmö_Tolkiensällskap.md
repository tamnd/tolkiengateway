**Angmar, Malmö Tolkiensällskap**, is the Swedish Tolkien Society of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malm%C3%B6" title="wikipedia:Malmö">Malmö</a>, hived off from [Forodrim](/wiki/Forodrim). The association published the magazine *Certhas Angmar* (a publication which, as of 2010, seems to have ceased to exist).

Angmar is composed of seven guilds: a Language Guild, an Equipment/Decoration Guild (hard to translate; "Rustgillet"), a Gaming Guild, an Elf-guild, a Food-and-Wine Guild, a Bow Guild, and a Dance Guild.
