**Amon Obel** was a hill in the midst of the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Brethil "Brethil")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> on which was built the settlement of [Ephel Brandir](/wiki/Ephel_Brandir), which became the main settlement of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Obel* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for "Fortified Hill", composed of the words *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") and *[gobel](/wiki/Gobel "Gobel")* ("walled settlement").
