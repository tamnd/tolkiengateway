**Amdir** was a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word literally meaning "looking up". It was used to describe a concept similar to the ideas of "hope" or "optimism". *Amdir* is more closely defined in the *[Athrabeth Finrod ah Andreth](Athrabeth_Finrod_ah_Andreth.md)* as "an expectation of good, which though uncertain has some foundation in what is known". *Amdir* was often contrasted with *[Estel](../E/Estel.md)*, which is a more deeply grounded type of hope.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
