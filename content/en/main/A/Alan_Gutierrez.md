**Alan Gutierrez** (born [1958](/wiki/1958)) is an American fantasy artist.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).
