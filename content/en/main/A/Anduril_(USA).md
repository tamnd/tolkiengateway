<i><b>Anduril</b></i> was a fanzine published in Rochester, Texas, edited by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Paul_Zimmer&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Paul Zimmer (page does not exist)">Paul Zimmer</a> and [Marion](/wiki/Marion_Zimmer_Bradley "Marion Zimmer Bradley") and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=David_Bradley&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="David Bradley (page does not exist)">David Bradley</a>. Only one issue was published (Summer 1962; held at the Marquette Hunnewell collection). Anduril is said to be "the first Tolkien fanzine ever", and according to the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Marquette_Library&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Marquette Library (page does not exist)">Marquette Library</a>, the "\[p\]ublication of Anduril ceased when rumors surfaced that Tolkien did not like it".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
