**Anne C. Petty** ([21 May](../_/21_May.md) [1945](../_/1945.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> — [21 July](../_/21_July.md) [2013](../_/2013.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>), Ph.D., was a [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) scholar specializing in Mythology and Finnish folkore. She was also an author, and wrote several books and articles on Tolkien. Petty was a regular speaker at conventions like Dragon\*Con and [The One Ring Celebration](../T/The_One_Ring_Celebration.md).
