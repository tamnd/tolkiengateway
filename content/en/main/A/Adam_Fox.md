Canon **Adam Fox** ([15 July](/wiki/15_July) [1883](/wiki/1883) – [17 January](/wiki/17_January) [1977](/wiki/1977)) was a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He was the Dean of Divinity at [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)'s Magdalen College, Oxford. Between 1938 and 1942 he was Professor of Poetry. Later he became Canon of Westminster Abbey and he is buried there in the Poets' Corner.
