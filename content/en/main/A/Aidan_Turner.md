**Aidan Turner** ([June 19](/wiki/19_June "19 June"), [1983](/wiki/1983)) is an Irish actor. He portrayed [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) in *[The Hobbit (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)>)*.

When his participation was announced, some confusion arose whether the actor cast was Aidan Turner, star of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Being_Human_(TV_Series)" title="wikipedia:Being Human (TV Series)">Being Human</a></i>, or <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aiden_Turner" title="wikipedia:Aiden Turner">Aiden Turner</a>, star of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_My_Children" title="wikipedia:All My Children">All My Children</a></i>, as an article on Deadline.com that was amongst the first to announce the cast used both spellings in its report.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
