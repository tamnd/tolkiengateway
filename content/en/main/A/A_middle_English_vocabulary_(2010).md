<i><b>A middle English vocabulary. Designed for use with Sisam&#39;s Fourteenth century verse and prose</b></i> is an unauthorized reprint of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[A Middle English Vocabulary](A_Middle_English_Vocabulary.md)* ([1922](../_/1922.md)). For unknown reasons, the reprint uses the author name "J R. R. 1892-1973 Tolkien" (alongside Kenneth Sisam, author of *[Fourteenth Century Verse and Prose](../F/Fourteenth_Century_Verse_and_Prose.md)*).
