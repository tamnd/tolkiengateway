<i><b>Arda Philology 6</b></i> is the sixth volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)")*, and contains the proceedings of the Sixth International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/oenquea.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Enquea</a> (<a href="/wiki/Omentielva" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Jenins, Switzerland, in August [2015](/wiki/2015).
