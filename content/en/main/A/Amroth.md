**Amroth**, was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), and king of [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien).

He was lost in the waters of the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) when his ship, waiting for the arrival of his beloved [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel), was blown to sea by a great storm and he attempted to swim back in order to not be parted from her.<sup id="cite_ref-lorien_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lorien-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

Amroth was [Sinda](/wiki/Sinda), the son of [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr).<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697"), after the fall of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) during the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron), Amroth led a force of Elves from [Lórinand](/wiki/L%C3%B3rinand), joined by [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Durin](/wiki/Durin_III "Durin III"), against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). Sauron's forces were engaged with those of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), and would have overwhelmed them, but the intervention of Amroth and Durin enabled Elrond to extricate himself and withdraw north. Sauron then turned upon the Elves of Lórinand and the Dwarves and drove them back, but did not pursue them as his more immediate purpose was to capture [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), the capital of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), where he believed one or more of the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) were kept. Eventually, with the arrival of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) Sauron was beaten and retreated to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-4">[4]</a></sup>

Amroth became King of Lórien in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434") after Amdír was slain in the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad).<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1050](/wiki/Third_Age_1050 "Third Age 1050"), the shadow started to grow in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The establishment of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) especially caused Amroth to dwell in growing disquiet, and made his home in a high [flet](/wiki/Flet "Flet") so that a watch over it might be kept.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> Galadriel too felt this foreboding and, with Celeborn, journeyed to Lórien and stayed there a long time with Amroth. He was valiant and wise, and his people were content with him, so after many long journeys of enquiry throughout [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), Galadriel and Celeborn eventually returned west to dwell in [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris).<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409"), Elves from Lórien passed over the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) to join forces with the [Elves of Rivendell](/wiki/Elves_of_Rivendell) under [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) against [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar); along with assistance from the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon), they were successful in subduing the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) and [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) for some time.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### With Nimrodel {#With_Nimrodel}

For many years Amroth loved [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel), a [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan) Elf and for her he lived in their manner, housed in the tall trees of the great green mount of [Cerin Amroth](/wiki/Cerin_Amroth). Though she returned his love, she would not wed him. She regretted the coming of Elves from the West as she believed that they brought wars and destroyed the peace.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrog) emerged in [Moria](/wiki/Moria), drove out the Dwarves, and Moria became filled with the servants of Sauron. Nimrodel and many other Silvan folk fled away south and Amroth departed to seek for her, never to return to Lórien.<sup id="cite_ref-silvan_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silvan-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:fn. 9</sup>

Amroth eventually found her near <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Fangorn" title="Fangorn">Fangorn</a>, which she would not enter as she felt the trees had menaced her and blocked her path. There they spoke, and plighted their troth, but Nimrodel insisted that they would only be wed once Amroth had brought her to a land of peace. Amroth agreed to this, even though it meant abandoning his people in their time of need. He told her of the [Elf-haven in the south](/wiki/Edhellond "Edhellond") from where many of his own people had sailed into the West, and that there still remained a remnant who built ships and offered passage for those of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) who had grown weary of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

As they travelled they became separated, and seeking for her Amroth came to the Elf-haven. There he found few other Elves still lingering, less than a ship-load, and but one remaining seaworthy ship, which was already preparing to depart. He was welcomed, but the others were unwilling to wait for Nimrodel as their was little hope she would actually come: if she had come by [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) she would have been untroubled, and maybe even received aid; but if she came through the [mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains "White Mountains"), there were many unfriendly Men and other evil things. The year was already waning to autumn, and soon great winds would be expected which would make travel dangerous, even to Elven-ships. However, for the grief of Amroth, they relented and waited many weeks, living aboard the ship as their houses had already been stripped and made empty.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

Eventually a great storm blew down from the north, sweeping away many ships of Men and tearing the light Elven-ship from its moorings. Amroth awoke to find the ship already far from land and in despair leapt into the sea in an attempt to swim back to shore. He battled for a long time against the waves, but was never seen again in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

With the disaster in Moria, and the loss of Amroth, Lórien was in great peril and its people began to flee, risking it being left deserted to be occupied by Orcs. Galadriel and Celeborn then returned to Lórien where they were welcomed by its people and took up rule of the realm.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) entered Lórien and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) sung to them the [Lay of Nimrodel](/wiki/Lay_of_Nimrodel) which detailed the tale of Amroth and Nimrodel. Legolas further declared that in spring, when the winds were in the South, the voice of Amroth came up from the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Amroth was said to be beautiful even for one of the Eldar, and valiant and wise. His hair was 'bright' and the sun gleaming off it was like a spark of gold.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Namesakes {#Namesakes}

Several places are named in honour of Amroth. Asides from Cerin Amroth, there were also [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth), a princedom of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and Edhellond was otherwise known as "Amroth's haven".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Separate characters {#Separate_characters}

The name *Amroth* first appears as the leader of the Númenóreans who survived the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor), the character that would become [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Drafts of Lord of the Rings {#Drafts_of_Lord_of_the_Rings}

Before settling on *Amroth* for the lost king of Lórien, Tolkien considered the names <i><b>Ammalas</b></i> and <i><b>Amaldor</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn {#Concerning_Galadriel_and_Celeborn}

In the earliest versions of Amroth's origin, he was the son of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), and brother of [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan).

His birth was first given as between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [350](/wiki/Second_Age_350 "Second Age 350") and [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_400&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 400 (page does not exist)">400</a> while his parents dwelt at [Nenuial](/wiki/Nenuial). He travelled with his parents and came to dwell at [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion). Galadriel entered friendship with the [Dwarves of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Longbeards "Longbeards"), and with their assistance further came into contact with the realm of [Lórinand](/wiki/L%C3%B3rinand) which was peopled by [Nandorin](/wiki/Nandorin) Elves who had no princes or rulers and led lives free of care. At some point between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1350](/wiki/Second_Age_1350 "Second Age 1350") and [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1400&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1400 (page does not exist)">1400</a> strife between Galadriel &amp; Celeborn and the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain) resulted in the Mírdain seizing power. Galadriel, Amroth, and Celebrían then passed through [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm) to Lórinand, though Celeborn remained behind as he would not enter the mansions of the Dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695") Amroth provided his aid by leading the Elves of Lórinand in the War of the Elves and Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-4">[4]</a></sup>

Shortly after the war Galadriel, along with Celebrían, departed Lórinand, and committed it to Amroth. She reunited with Celeborn in [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris) and they remained there a long time before departing and settling in the [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) as the place that would afterwards be called Dol Amroth. There, Amroth would come at times to visit them, and their company was swelled by Elves from Lórinand. Galadriel and Celeborn remained there until Amroth was eventually lost in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981").<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-4">[4]</a></sup>

While dating of this text is not certain, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted this lineage must have been later than the writing of the [Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Rings), otherwise "had \[Amroth\] been supposed to be their son when it was written, the fact would surely have been mentioned."<sup id="cite_ref-concerning_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-concerning-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Other accounts {#Other_accounts}

In a note concerning the House of Dol Amroth, Amroth is described as the brother of Celeborn. This was struck out however, and replaced with "was a Sinda from Beleriand".<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

While working out Elf aging, Tolkien considered the following for Amroth: born in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_33&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 33 (page does not exist)">33</a>, full-grown by [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_105&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 105 (page does not exist)">105</a>, (apparent age of) 29 by the founding of Eregion in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [750](/wiki/Second_Age_750 "Second Age 750"), 35 at the sack of Eregion in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697"), 47⅙ at the end of the Second Age, and 60-61 by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") at the time of the disaster in Moria. He was thus nearing the end of his youth, but (in mortal years) was about 45.<sup id="cite_ref-ages_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ages-14">[14]</a></sup>

Tolkien also considered, but rejected, that Amroth might be the son of Celeborn, but not Galadriel. In this thought Celeborn's wife stole away, leaving him with their son Amroth. Tolkien considered that Elves, after the judgement of [Míriel](/wiki/M%C3%ADriel) were permitted to lawfully remarry if one partner deserted the other. It would have been a very seldom occurrence, but could happen in such as time as the divided feelings of the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-ages_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ages-14">[14]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:fn. 6</sup>

As son of Galadriel and Celeborn, Tolkien considered Amroth remaining in Eregion with Celeborn. With the onset of Sauron, Celeborn sent Amroth to Lórien, bearing one of the Three Rings for Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amroth* means "upclimber, high climber".<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup> It was probably of [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan_Elvish "Silvan Elvish") origin, but adapted to [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> It was actually a nickname derived from his residing in a high [talan](/wiki/Talan "Talan") or [flet](/wiki/Flet "Flet"), as living in such lofty houses was no doubt at first thought remarkable, and Amroth was probably the first to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-amroth_2-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amroth-2">[2]</a></sup>
