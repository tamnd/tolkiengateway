<i><b>A Walk Through the Shire</b></i> is a book with blank pages decorated by illustrations depicting various scenes from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), intended to be used as a note-book. It also contains an introductory text.
