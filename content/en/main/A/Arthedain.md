**Arthedain** was one of the three realms of the [Dúnedain of the North](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md) created from the fragmentation of the kingdom of [Arnor](Arnor.md). After the death of Arnor's King [Eärendur](<../E/Eärendur_(King_of_Arnor).md>), his sons divided the realm into the kingdoms of Arthedain, [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) and [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md).

## Description {#Description}

Arthedain lay primarily between the [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) and the [Brandywine](../B/Baranduin.md) in northwestern [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). However, it also claimed the land north of the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) from the [Brandywine Bridge](../B/Brandywine_Bridge.md) to the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> Notable features within Arthedain were the [Hills of Evendim](../H/Hills_of_Evendim.md), [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md), [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md), [Tower Hills](../T/Tower_Hills.md), [Far Downs](../F/Far_Downs.md), [White Downs](../W/White_Downs.md), [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), [Annúminas](Annúminas.md), and [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the tenth king of Arnor, Eärendur, died in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [861](../T/Third_Age_861.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> dissensions between his sons fractured the realm. Arnor was divided into Arthedain, Rhudaur and Cardolan. The line of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) was maintained in Arthedain, but in Rhudaur and Cardolan the line faded quickly. Arthedain retained [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md) and [its](Amon_Sûl-stone.md) *[Palantír](../P/Palantíri.md)* as well as [two](Annúminas-stone.md) [others](../E/Elostirion-stone.md), which led to contention between the three kingdoms.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Malvegil](../M/Malvegil.md) of Arthedain became king in [1272](../T/Third_Age_1272.md)<sup id="cite_ref-North_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-4">[4]</a></sup> [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) began to trouble the region.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md), the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) founded the kingdom of [Angmar](Angmar.md) north of the [Ettenmoors](../E/Ettenmoors.md), soon to become the foe of Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Malvegil, his son [Argeleb I](Argeleb_I.md) reclaimed lordship over all of Arnor since Rhudaur and Cardolan had no living descendants of Isildur. Rhudaur refused this claim and allied with Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> Argeleb I fell in battle with Rhudaur in [1356](../T/Third_Age_1356.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> His son, [Arveleg I](Arveleg_I.md), avenged his father by retaking the Weather Hills, aided by [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and Cardolan. However, a great host issued from Angmar in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md), invading Arthedain and taking Weathertop. King Arveleg I was slain, as well as the [last prince of Cardolan](../L/Last_prince_of_Cardolan.md) (later interred in the Barrow-downs).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Rhudaur was occupied by Angmar and Cardolan was ravaged.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1601](../T/Third_Age_1601.md) two [Fallohide](../F/Fallohides.md) brothers, [Marcho](../M/Marcho.md) and [Blanco](../B/Blanco.md), crossed the Brandywine with many [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) and, with the permission of King [Argeleb II](Argeleb_II.md), established [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The Hobbits and the remaining [Men](../M/Men.md) in Arthedain suffered losses in the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) of [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> the Witch-king invaded Arthedain, capturing Fornost and driving most of the few Dúnedain across the Lhûn. King [Arvedui](Arvedui.md) held out for awhile in the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md) until he and his guard were driven north.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> Although the realm of Angmar was crushed at the [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md) in [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> the realm of Arthedain also came to an end and its lands remained mostly empty (except for the Shire) and its people became [Rangers](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md); until the [reestablishment](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) of the northern kingdom under king [Elessar](Aragorn.md) at the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arthedain* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack and Wayne G. Hammond and Cristina Scull suggest that it means "Realm of the Edain" and is a compound of a form *arth* of *arthor* ("realm") and the plural *edain* of *[adan](Adan.md)* ("Man (as a race)").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
