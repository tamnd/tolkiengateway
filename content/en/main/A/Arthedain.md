**Arthedain** was one of the three realms of the [Dúnedain of the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor "Dúnedain of Arnor") created from the fragmentation of the kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor). After the death of Arnor's King [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eärendur (King of Arnor)"), his sons divided the realm into the kingdoms of Arthedain, [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur) and [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan).

## Description {#Description}

Arthedain lay primarily between the [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) and the [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") in northwestern [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). However, it also claimed the land north of the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) from the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) to the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> Notable features within Arthedain were the [Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim), [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim), [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs), [Tower Hills](/wiki/Tower_Hills), [Far Downs](/wiki/Far_Downs), [White Downs](/wiki/White_Downs), [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas), and [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the tenth king of Arnor, Eärendur, died in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> dissensions between his sons fractured the realm. Arnor was divided into Arthedain, Rhudaur and Cardolan. The line of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was maintained in Arthedain, but in Rhudaur and Cardolan the line faded quickly. Arthedain retained [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop) and [its](/wiki/Amon_S%C3%BBl-stone "Amon Sûl-stone") *[Palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* as well as [two](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas-stone "Annúminas-stone") [others](/wiki/Elostirion-stone "Elostirion-stone"), which led to contention between the three kingdoms.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Malvegil](/wiki/Malvegil) of Arthedain became king in [1272](/wiki/Third_Age_1272 "Third Age 1272")<sup id="cite_ref-North_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-4">[4]</a></sup> [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) began to trouble the region.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300"), the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) founded the kingdom of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) north of the [Ettenmoors](/wiki/Ettenmoors), soon to become the foe of Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

After Malvegil, his son [Argeleb I](/wiki/Argeleb_I) reclaimed lordship over all of Arnor since Rhudaur and Cardolan had no living descendants of Isildur. Rhudaur refused this claim and allied with Angmar.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> Argeleb I fell in battle with Rhudaur in [1356](/wiki/Third_Age_1356 "Third Age 1356").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> His son, [Arveleg I](/wiki/Arveleg_I), avenged his father by retaking the Weather Hills, aided by [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and Cardolan. However, a great host issued from Angmar in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409"), invading Arthedain and taking Weathertop. King Arveleg I was slain, as well as the [last prince of Cardolan](/wiki/Last_prince_of_Cardolan "Last prince of Cardolan") (later interred in the Barrow-downs).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Rhudaur was occupied by Angmar and Cardolan was ravaged.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601") two [Fallohide](/wiki/Fallohides "Fallohides") brothers, [Marcho](/wiki/Marcho) and [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco), crossed the Brandywine with many [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and, with the permission of King [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II), established [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The Hobbits and the remaining [Men](/wiki/Men) in Arthedain suffered losses in the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) of [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> the Witch-king invaded Arthedain, capturing Fornost and driving most of the few Dúnedain across the Lhûn. King [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) held out for awhile in the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs) until he and his guard were driven north.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-1">[1]</a></sup> Although the realm of Angmar was crushed at the [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost) in [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"),<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup> the realm of Arthedain also came to an end and its lands remained mostly empty (except for the Shire) and its people became [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"); until the [reestablishment](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom "Reunited Kingdom") of the northern kingdom under king [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arthedain* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack and Wayne G. Hammond and Cristina Scull suggest that it means "Realm of the Edain" and is a compound of a form *arth* of *arthor* ("realm") and the plural *edain* of *[adan](/wiki/Adan "Adan")* ("Man (as a race)").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
