<i><b>amon</b></i> is a noun meaning "hill" or "steep-sided mount" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md); the plural is <i><b>emyn</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Compare with <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ambo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ambo (page does not exist)">ambo</a></i> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Amon Hen](Amon_Hen.md) = "Hill of the Eye"
- [Amon Rûdh](Amon_Rûdh.md) = "Bald Hill"
- [Amon Sûl](../W/Weathertop.md) = [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md)
- [Amon Ereb](Amon_Ereb.md) = "Lonely Hill"
- [Amon Ethir](Amon_Ethir.md) = "Spyhill"
- [Amon Gwareth](Amon_Gwareth.md) = "Hill of Watch"
- [Amon Lanc](Amon_Lanc.md) = "Naked Hill"
- [Amon Lhaw](Amon_Lhaw.md) = "Hill of Hearing"
- [Amon Obel](Amon_Obel.md) = "Fortified Hill"
- [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md) = "Drear Hills"
- [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md)
