<i><b>amon</b></i> is a noun meaning "hill" or "steep-sided mount" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin); the plural is <i><b>emyn</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Compare with <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ambo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ambo (page does not exist)">ambo</a></i> in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen) = "Hill of the Eye"
- [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh) = "Bald Hill"
- [Amon Sûl](/wiki/Weathertop "Weathertop") = [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop)
- [Amon Ereb](/wiki/Amon_Ereb) = "Lonely Hill"
- [Amon Ethir](/wiki/Amon_Ethir) = "Spyhill"
- [Amon Gwareth](/wiki/Amon_Gwareth) = "Hill of Watch"
- [Amon Lanc](/wiki/Amon_Lanc) = "Naked Hill"
- [Amon Lhaw](/wiki/Amon_Lhaw) = "Hill of Hearing"
- [Amon Obel](/wiki/Amon_Obel) = "Fortified Hill"
- [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) = "Drear Hills"
- [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen)
