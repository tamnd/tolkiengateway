**Aldburg** was a fortified town in [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), situated on a green hill at the feet of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup> in the region known as the [Folde](/wiki/Folde).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup> Originally the first capital of the kingdom of Rohan, it later became the base of the [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark) and the Muster of the [East-mark](/wiki/East-mark).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Aldburg was built by [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) after Rohan had been granted to the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) by [Steward](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup> After Brego, son of Eorl, moved the capital to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), the lordship of Aldburg passed to his third son, [Eofor](/wiki/Eofor). At the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Aldburg was the home of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)'s nephew, [Éomer,](/wiki/%C3%89omer "Éomer") who was Third Marshal of the Riddermark, and son of [Éomund](/wiki/%C3%89omund), who claimed descent from Eofor.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aldburg* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) word meaning "Old Fortress".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Aldburg is the capital town of the <a href="/wiki/Eastfold" title="Eastfold">Eastfold</a>, ruled by Éomer in times of peace. After Éomer departed for war, the defence of Aldburg fell to his steward Arcil, who often  disagreed with Éomer&#39;s cousin, <a href="/wiki/%C3%89othain" title="Éothain">Éothain</a>.</dd></dl>

Aldburg serves as the in-game "capital settlement" of the West Rohan region, functioning as the hub for swift travel and player activities after [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) is evacuated early in the game and its inhabitants move to [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow).
