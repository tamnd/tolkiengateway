**Aldburg** was a fortified town in [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), situated on a green hill at the feet of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup> in the region known as the [Folde](../F/Folde.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup> Originally the first capital of the kingdom of Rohan, it later became the base of the [Third Marshal of the Riddermark](../T/Third_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark.md) and the Muster of the [East-mark](../E/East-mark.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Aldburg was built by [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) after Rohan had been granted to the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) by [Steward](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-1">[1]</a></sup> After Brego, son of Eorl, moved the capital to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md), the lordship of Aldburg passed to his third son, [Eofor](../E/Eofor.md). At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Aldburg was the home of King [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md)'s nephew, [Éomer,](../_/Éomer.md) who was Third Marshal of the Riddermark, and son of [Éomund](../_/Éomund.md), who claimed descent from Eofor.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aldburg* is an [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) word meaning "Old Fortress".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Aldburg is the capital town of the <a href="../E/Eastfold.md" title="Eastfold">Eastfold</a>, ruled by Éomer in times of peace. After Éomer departed for war, the defence of Aldburg fell to his steward Arcil, who often  disagreed with Éomer&#39;s cousin, <a href="../_/Éothain.md" title="Éothain">Éothain</a>.</dd></dl>

Aldburg serves as the in-game "capital settlement" of the West Rohan region, functioning as the hub for swift travel and player activities after [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) is evacuated early in the game and its inhabitants move to [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md).
