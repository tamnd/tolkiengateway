<i><b>Arda Philology 7</b></i> is the seventh volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](<Arda_(annual).md>)*, and contains the proceedings of the Seventh International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/oquainea.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Quainea</a> (<a href="../O/Omentielva.md" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held at California State University East Bay in Hayward, in August [2017](../_/2017.md).
