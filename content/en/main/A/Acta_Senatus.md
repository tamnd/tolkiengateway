<i><b>Acta Senatus</b></i> was a report in Latin of a Latin debate at [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) which was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It was published in [King Edward's School Chronicle](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School_Chronicle) on [10 March](/wiki/10_March)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [1911](/wiki/1911) on pages 26–27. The report is not signed but Tolkien later revealed that he was the author.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
