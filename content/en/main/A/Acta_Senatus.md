<i><b>Acta Senatus</b></i> was a report in Latin of a Latin debate at [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md) in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md) which was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). It was published in [King Edward's School Chronicle](../K/King_Edward's_School_Chronicle.md) on [10 March](../_/10_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [1911](../_/1911.md) on pages 26–27. The report is not signed but Tolkien later revealed that he was the author.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
