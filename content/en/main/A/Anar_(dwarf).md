**Anar** was one of the four Dwarves that accompanied [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) on his journeys after his birthday party.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the early writings of the chapter *[A Long-expected Party](A_Long-expected_Party.md)*, Anar was given some dialogue and a bit more development. However, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) later changed his mind, and did not disclose his name in the book nor gave him any dialogue.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Anar, or *Anarr*, is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. His name most likely means "the Other" or "Starer".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
