<i><b>angaina</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "of iron". The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) cognate is *[angren](/wiki/Angren "Angren")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Related to Quenya *[anga](/wiki/Anga "Anga")* ("iron"), *angaina* derives from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ANGĀ](/wiki/ANGA "ANGA").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
