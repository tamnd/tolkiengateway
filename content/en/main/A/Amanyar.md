**Amanyar** ("Those of Aman" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya); or **Amaneldi** in the longer form<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:373</sup>) was another name for the [Elves of the Light](/wiki/Calaquendi "Calaquendi"), those who dwelt in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They included the [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar), [Ñoldor](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor"), and those [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) who passed over [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> known as the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari).

The Vanyar dwelt with the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) in their city [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar), the Noldor in [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion) (as well as various fortresses and mines in the encircling [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) mountains), and the Falmari in [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB). After the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), those Noldor who had remained in [exile](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor") in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) returned, followed by many of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) and [Silvan Elves](/wiki/Silvan_Elves). The Vanyar and Falmari never departed from Valinor save during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).

One Elf of the Light who was not of the Amanyar was King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Historic names for the Amanyar, which eventually fell out of everyday use during the period of the [Noldorin Exile](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor "Exile of the Noldor"), were <i><b>Oäreldi</b></i> (sing. <i><b>Oärel</b></i>) or, in the [speech of the Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyarin "Vanyarin"), <i><b>Oäzeldi</b></i> (sing. <i><b>Oäzel</b></i>). Even more archaic form of these were <i><b>Aureldi</b></i> (sing. <i><b>Aurel</b></i>) or <i><b>Auzeldi</b></i> (sing. <i><b>Auzel</b></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:374</sup> Proper [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) equivalents of these ancient names were <i><b>Ódhil</b></i> or <i><b>Ódhellim</b></i> (sing. <i><b>Ódhel</b></i>), which later became applied to the Noldor only after their arrival to Beleriand, taking the form *Gódhil* or *Gódhellim* (sing. *Gódhel*) due to the blending with an old clan-name for the Noldor in Sindarin, [*Gelydh* or *Golodhrim*](/wiki/Golodh "Golodh").<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:364</sup>
