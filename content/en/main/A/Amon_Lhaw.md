**Amon Lhaw**, also called the **Hill of Hearing**, was one of the three peaks above the [Rauros-falls](/wiki/Rauros "Rauros"), where [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel) was drained.<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Amon Lhaw towered among the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) on the eastern banks of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) opposite [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen), which lay upon the western bank. Between these two peaks was the island hill of [Tol Brandir](/wiki/Tol_Brandir).<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup>

Amon Lhaw may possibly have been a little "over 1,000 feet" according to a [1959](/wiki/1959) letter that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote to a reader about the height of Amon Hen.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Amon Lhaw was first fortified "in the days of the great kings" according to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), though the precise dating is unspecified.<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup> The hill was fortified before the time of the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor"), which meant that by the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), the fortifications were at a minimum 969 years old. One likely possibility is that the hill was fortified at the same time as the [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath), which was built after [Minalcar](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_II "Rómendacil II") defeated the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1248](/wiki/Third_Age_1248 "Third Age 1248") but before the end of his reign in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1366](/wiki/Third_Age_1366 "Third Age 1366").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

At one time Amon Lhaw had been part of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=North_March&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="North March (page does not exist)">North March</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and a high seat<sup id="cite_ref-breaking_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-breaking-5">[5]</a></sup> was built there upon which [Men](/wiki/Men) kept watch. By the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) however, it had long since fallen under the influence of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), as spies often roamed there. Such spies may have been [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the year 3019, following the [Breaking of the Fellowship](/wiki/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship) at [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) took a boat and paddled across [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel). They landed upon the southern slopes of Amon Lhaw where there was a shelving shore.<sup id="cite_ref-breaking_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-breaking-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Lhaw* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), and translates as "Hill of Hearing", or "Hill of Ears",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") and *[lhaw](/wiki/Lhaw "Lhaw")* ("ears").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In his drafts, Tolkien first wrote that Amon Lhaw was fortified "in the days of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)" instead of "in the days of the great kings". He also originally gave Amon Lhaw the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name <i><b>Larmindon</b></i> ("Listen Tower")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

At the top of a draft entitled "Trotter on Amon Hen", Tolkien wrote "many experimental [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) names" such as <i><b>Llawhen</b></i>, <i><b>Amon Tirlaw</b></i>, <i><b>Lhawdir</b></i>, <i><b>Lasthen</b></i>, <i><b>Henlas</b></i>, and <i><b>Hendlas</b></i>. However, all of these names were struck through in favor of Amon Lhaw and Amon Hen.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
