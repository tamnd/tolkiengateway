**Amon Lhaw**, also called the **Hill of Hearing**, was one of the three peaks above the [Rauros-falls](../R/Rauros.md), where [Nen Hithoel](../N/Nen_Hithoel.md) was drained.<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Amon Lhaw towered among the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md) on the eastern banks of the [Anduin](Anduin.md) opposite [Amon Hen](Amon_Hen.md), which lay upon the western bank. Between these two peaks was the island hill of [Tol Brandir](../T/Tol_Brandir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup>

Amon Lhaw may possibly have been a little "over 1,000 feet" according to a [1959](../_/1959.md) letter that [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote to a reader about the height of Amon Hen.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Amon Lhaw was first fortified "in the days of the great kings" according to [Aragorn](Aragorn.md), though the precise dating is unspecified.<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup> The hill was fortified before the time of the [Stewards](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md), which meant that by the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), the fortifications were at a minimum 969 years old. One likely possibility is that the hill was fortified at the same time as the [Argonath](Argonath.md), which was built after [Minalcar](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) defeated the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) in the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1248](../T/Third_Age_1248.md) but before the end of his reign in the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1366](../T/Third_Age_1366.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

At one time Amon Lhaw had been part of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=North_March&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="North March (page does not exist)">North March</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and a high seat<sup id="cite_ref-breaking_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-breaking-5">[5]</a></sup> was built there upon which [Men](../M/Men.md) kept watch. By the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) however, it had long since fallen under the influence of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), as spies often roamed there. Such spies may have been [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-greatriver_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greatriver-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the year 3019, following the [Breaking of the Fellowship](../B/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship.md) at [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md), [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) took a boat and paddled across [Nen Hithoel](../N/Nen_Hithoel.md). They landed upon the southern slopes of Amon Lhaw where there was a shelving shore.<sup id="cite_ref-breaking_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-breaking-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Lhaw* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), and translates as "Hill of Hearing", or "Hill of Ears",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from *[amon](Amon.md)* ("hill") and *[lhaw](../L/Lhaw.md)* ("ears").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In his drafts, Tolkien first wrote that Amon Lhaw was fortified "in the days of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)" instead of "in the days of the great kings". He also originally gave Amon Lhaw the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name <i><b>Larmindon</b></i> ("Listen Tower")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

At the top of a draft entitled "Trotter on Amon Hen", Tolkien wrote "many experimental [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) names" such as <i><b>Llawhen</b></i>, <i><b>Amon Tirlaw</b></i>, <i><b>Lhawdir</b></i>, <i><b>Lasthen</b></i>, <i><b>Henlas</b></i>, and <i><b>Hendlas</b></i>. However, all of these names were struck through in favor of Amon Lhaw and Amon Hen.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
