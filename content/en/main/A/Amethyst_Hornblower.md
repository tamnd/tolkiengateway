**Amethyst Hornblower** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Amethyst married [Rudibert Bolger](../R/Rudibert_Bolger.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1260](../T/Third_Age_2860.md)) and was the mother of [Adalbert Bolger](Adalbert_Bolger.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1301](../T/Third_Age_2901.md)-[1397](../T/Third_Age_2997.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amethyst" title="wikipedia:Amethyst">Amethyst</a> is named after the gemstone.
