**Airefëa** is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for the <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Spirit_in_Christianity" title="wikipedia:Holy Spirit in Christianity">Holy Spirit</a></b>, the Third Person of the [Blessed Trinity](../E/Eru.md) in [Christianity](../C/Christianity.md). The term only appears in the *[Alcar i Ataren](Alcar_i_Ataren.md)* (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Patri" title="wikipedia:Gloria Patri">Gloria Patri</a>) prayer, translated by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1950s](../_/1950.md). It is clearly a compound of *[airë](Airë.md)* ("holy") + *[fëa](../F/Fëa.md)* ("spirit").<sup id="cite_ref-Airefea_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Airefea-1">[1]</a></sup>

The term in the text was emended from *fairë aista*. As the editors point out, the term *fëa* in the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) is applied to the [Incarnate](../I/Incarnate.md), while the term <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fair%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fairë (page does not exist)">fairë</a></i> is rather applied to (disembodied) [spirits](../S/Spirits.md) in general, but both terms meant originally "radiance".<sup id="cite_ref-Airefea_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Airefea-1">[1]</a></sup>

In addition, Tolkien explicitely identified the **[Secret Fire](../S/Secret_Fire.md)** from his [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) with the Holy Spirit.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](../Q/Qenya_Lexicon.md)*, the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) term <i><b>Sâ</b></i> is glossed as "Fire, especially in temples, etc. A mystic name identified with Holy Ghost".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
