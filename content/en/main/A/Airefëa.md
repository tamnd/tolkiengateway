**Airefëa** is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the <b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Spirit_in_Christianity" title="wikipedia:Holy Spirit in Christianity">Holy Spirit</a></b>, the Third Person of the [Blessed Trinity](/wiki/Eru "Eru") in [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity). The term only appears in the *[Alcar i Ataren](/wiki/Alcar_i_Ataren)* (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Patri" title="wikipedia:Gloria Patri">Gloria Patri</a>) prayer, translated by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in [1950s](/wiki/1950 "1950"). It is clearly a compound of *[airë](/wiki/Air%C3%AB "Airë")* ("holy") + *[fëa](/wiki/F%C3%ABa "Fëa")* ("spirit").<sup id="cite_ref-Airefea_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Airefea-1">[1]</a></sup>

The term in the text was emended from *fairë aista*. As the editors point out, the term *fëa* in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") is applied to the [Incarnate](/wiki/Incarnate), while the term <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fair%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fairë (page does not exist)">fairë</a></i> is rather applied to (disembodied) [spirits](/wiki/Spirits "Spirits") in general, but both terms meant originally "radiance".<sup id="cite_ref-Airefea_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Airefea-1">[1]</a></sup>

In addition, Tolkien explicitely identified the **[Secret Fire](/wiki/Secret_Fire)** from his [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") with the Holy Spirit.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*, the [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) term <i><b>Sâ</b></i> is glossed as "Fire, especially in temples, etc. A mystic name identified with Holy Ghost".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
