<i><b>Arda Philology 4</b></i> is the fourth volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)")*, and contains the proceedings of the Fourth International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/ocantea.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Cantea</a> (<a href="/wiki/Omentielva" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Valencia in August [2011](/wiki/2011).
