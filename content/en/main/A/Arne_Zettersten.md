**Arne Zettersten** ([1934](../_/1934.md)-[2015](../_/2015.md)) was a Swedish professor emeritus. Before retirement, Zettersten was Head of Department and Professor in English at the University of Copenhagen.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1965](../_/1965.md), Zettersten presented his doctoral thesis on the [AB language](AB_language.md), a medieval literary dialect first identified by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). And during the 1960s in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md), Zettersten became a friend and colleague of Tolkien, as they both shared an interest in a certain collection of medieval manuscripts (the "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Group" title="wikipedia:Katherine Group">Katherine Group</a>"). Their contact continued throughout the last 13 years of Tolkien's life. Arne Zettersten died 2015, aged 81.
