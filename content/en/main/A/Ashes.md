**Ashes** were tall, straight, grey trees common in northern lands, and found throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The spears of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) were made from their wood,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> as was [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)'s staff.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Wood of the regular ash tree can be foraged by journeyman foresters in <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ered_Luin" title="Ered Luin">Ered Luin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Barrow-downs" title="Barrow-downs">Barrow-downs</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Wood of the black ash tree can be foraged by master foresters in the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>, <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Wood of the wind-swept black ash tree can be foraged by <i>Gundabad</i> foresters in the <a href="/wiki/Ettenmoors" title="Ettenmoors">Ettenmoors</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>, and King&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Wood of the wind-swept black ash tree can be foraged by <i>Umbar</i> foresters in the Ettenmoors, King&#39;s Gondor, and Outer Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>
