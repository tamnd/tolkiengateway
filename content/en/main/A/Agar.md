**Agar** was the name for the town in the story of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tal-Elmar" title="Tal-Elmar">Tal-Elmar</a> while the land in the immediate vicinity was known as the [Hills of Agar](/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin "Pinnath Gelin").

## Description {#Description}

There is a small green hilltop overlooking the town. The town itself is situated on the side of the hill while around it are many fields. There is also a place called the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Moot-hill&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Moot-hill (page does not exist)">Moot-hill</a>, where the town-master gives meetings and announcements.

### Geography {#Geography}

On its location little information is given, except that it was a region of green hills, situated "three leagues away" from the shores of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"), and apparently closer to the ocean than the rival town of [Udul](/wiki/Udul). In a note near the end of the story there was a suggestion that the place was on the estuary of the [Isen](/wiki/Isen) or perhaps the [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond). There is also a hill between Agar and the sea known as the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Golden_Hill&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Golden Hill (page does not exist)">Golden Hill</a>.

## Politics {#Politics}

The town was ruled by a town-master, who is known as the [Master of Agar](/wiki/Master_of_Agar). The town-master during the time when Tal-Elmar lived in the town was named [Mogru](/wiki/Mogru).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
