<i><b>A Northern Venture: Verses by Members of the <a href="../U/University_of_Leeds.md" title="University of Leeds">Leeds University</a> English School Association</b></i> is collection of twenty-three poems by ten different authors. The booklet contains three poems by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md):

1. "[Tha Eadigan Saelidan: The Happy Mariners](../T/Tha_Eadigan_Saelidan%253A_The_Happy_Mariners.md)"
2. "[Why the Man in the Moon Came Down Too Soon](../T/The_Man_in_the_Moon_Came_Down_Too_Soon.md)"
3. "[Enigmata Saxonica Nuper Inventa Duo](../E/Enigmata_Saxonica_Nuper_Inventa_Duo.md)"

Other noteworthy authors include Tolkien's friends and colleagues [Wilfred Rowland Childe](../W/Wilfred_Rowland_Childe.md) (contributing two poems) and [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) (two poems), and his student <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=A.H._Smith&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="A.H. Smith (page does not exist)">A.H. Smith</a> (two poems).

A 2nd impression of the booklet, dated July 1923, was also issued. It is essentially identical to the 1st impression.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Review {#Review}

*"The pictures, brightly tinted, of Mr. W.R. Childe's fancy, the dialect verses of Mr. A.H. Smith, who knows his Yorkshire, and a couple of riddles in Saxon by Mr. J.R.R. Tolkien are perhaps the outstanding things in this unpretentious anthology"* (*Times Literary Supplement* of [July 12](../_/12_July.md), [1923](../_/1923.md)).
