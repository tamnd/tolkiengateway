**Aracáno** is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "High Chieftain", being the [amilessë](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin). In turn he gave it to his youngest son, which had the name [Sindarized](/wiki/Sindarized) as <i><b><a href="/wiki/Argon" title="Argon">Argon</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is the combination of *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")* ("high") + *[cáno](/wiki/C%C3%A1no "Cáno")* ("chieftain").<sup id="cite_ref-Shibboleth_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibboleth-1">[1]</a></sup>
