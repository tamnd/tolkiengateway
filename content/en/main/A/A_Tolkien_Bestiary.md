<i><b>A Tolkien Bestiary</b></i> is an A-Z guide to the flora and fauna of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), written by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day) and first published in [1979](/wiki/1979).

The book contains monochrome and full-colour illustrations by [Ian Miller](/wiki/Ian_Miller), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Michael_Foreman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Foreman (page does not exist)">Michael Foreman</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Allan_Curless&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Allan Curless (page does not exist)">Allan Curless</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lidia_Postma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lidia Postma (page does not exist)">Lidia Postma</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=John_Blanche&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Blanche (page does not exist)">John Blanche</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pauline_Martin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pauline Martin (page does not exist)">Pauline Martin</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sue_Porter&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sue Porter (page does not exist)">Sue Porter</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Linda_Garland&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Linda Garland (page does not exist)">Linda Garland</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jaroslav_Bradac&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jaroslav Bradac (page does not exist)">Jaroslav Bradac</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Victor_Ambrus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Victor Ambrus (page does not exist)">Victor Ambrus</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=John_Davis&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="John Davis (page does not exist)">John Davis</a>.

The book has been published under different titles, by different publishers, including:

- <i><b>Guide to Tolkien&#39;s World: A Bestiary</b></i> (2002, 2010)
- <i><b>Characters from Tolkien</b></i> (2001)

## Reception {#Reception}

The book has been accused of containing extrapolations and thus deviating from [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s writings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> For example, there is a reference to "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraken" title="wikipedia:Kraken">Kraken</a>" referring to the [Watcher in the Water](/wiki/Watcher_in_the_Water), but the word does not appear as a species in Tolkien's works.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

While [fans](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") have argued that the book is less reliable as a [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") reference than similar works by [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) and [J.E.A. Tyler](/wiki/J.E.A._Tyler), many are fond of its illustrations.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
