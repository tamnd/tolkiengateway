<i><b>Arda</b></i> is a bilingual (Scandinavian/English) publication of the [Arda Society](/wiki/Arda-s%C3%A4llskapet "Arda-sällskapet"), edited by [Anders Stenström](/wiki/Anders_Stenstr%C3%B6m). It is in intention annual, but has not appeared since [1994](/wiki/1994). In a special series, the proceedings of [Omentielva](/wiki/Omentielva) are published by *Arda* as *Arda Philology*.
