<i><b>athae</b></i> (also *athe*) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word given no direct translation, except for what can be determined from its etymology.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup> It is related to words having to do with help, therefore it probably means \*"helpful" or \*"beneficial".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=A%C3%9Eay%C4%81&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="AÞayā (page does not exist)">aÞayā</a></i>, from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ATH](/wiki/ATH)<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *ath(a)elas* = *[athelas](/wiki/Athelas "Athelas")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
