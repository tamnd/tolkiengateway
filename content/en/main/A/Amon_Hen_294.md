<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 294</b> is the two-hundred-and-ninety-fourth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*, published in April [2022](../_/2022.md). This is the first issue Amanda Drake joined as editor, replacing Ana Demartino.

## Contents {#Contents}

- Cover: *Nerdanel Sculpting Feanor*
    - By: Tatyafinwe
- From the Editor's Desk
    - By: Cate Palmer-Billing, Amanda Drake, Mathew Suta
- Dates for your Diary
    - By: Ian Collier
- Table of Contents
- Tolkien Society Bursary
- Priscilla Tolkien, 1929-2022
    - By: Charles E. Noad
- Memories of Priscilla
- Poem: Like the Tales of Old
    - By: Madison Ryan
- Oxonmoot 2022
- On Love by Molly Ostertag
- Centerfold: Priscilla Tolkien
    - By: Michael Gabriel
- Did Frodo Master the One Ring?
    - By: Sergei Soloviev
- Dating Tolkien #3
    - By: Mick Henry
- Amon Hen Art Challenge
    - By: Amanda Drake
- Mathom
- Belladonna Took
    - By: Kathrin Heirli
- News From Fangorn
- All Brân
- Back Cover: Hidden Protector
    - By: Amanda Brent

- Artwork: Amanda Brent, Michael Gabriel, Tatyafinwe, Veronica Martinez Medellin, Charles Noad, Molly Ostertag
