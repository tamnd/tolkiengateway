<b><i>A Elbereth Gilthoniel!</i> 1, no. 2</b> is an issue of the fanzine/newsletter *A Elbereth Gilthoniel!* published by the Fellowship of Middle Earth (a [Tolkien fan-group](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University" title="wikipedia:Monash University">Monash University</a>). Included is an article containing the first 19 lines of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s poem "[Doworst](/wiki/Visio_de_Doworst "Visio de Doworst")" (p. 3).<sup id="cite_ref-RG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-DA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Guide_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guide-4">[4]</a></sup>

The newsletter was likely released in the month of June [1978](/wiki/1978).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
