<i><b>Akallabêth and other Tolkien works</b></i> is an album of music by composer [Paul Corfield Godfrey](/wiki/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey)'s Tolkien works - *[Daeron](</wiki/Daeron,_Op._45_(Flute_and_Piano)> "Daeron, Op. 45 (Flute and Piano)"), [Akallabêth (for solo piano)](</wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth_(for_solo_piano),_Op._42> "Akallabêth (for solo piano), Op. 42")* and the *[Tolkien Songs](/wiki/Tolkien_Songs,_Op._9 "Tolkien Songs, Op. 9")*. It also contains another song cycle, *Mysteries of Time*, based upon non-Tolkien sources.

Featuring Tara Mcswiney (soprano), Andrew Henley (tenor), Adam Jondelius (baritone), Nicola Loten (flute), Niamh Ferris (viola), Immanuel Carl Maria Vogt (piano), and Connor Fogel (piano).
