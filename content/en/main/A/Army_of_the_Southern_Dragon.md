In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), the **Army of the Southern Dragon** was [Akhôrahil](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl")'s host, which operated from [Ny Chennacatt](/wiki/Ny_Chennacatt) and sought to subjugate the regions around the [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains). It was first founded by Wyatan in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), and was reestablished several times.<sup id="cite_ref-citadel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-citadel-1">[1]</a></sup> The army's sights weren't set on [Sîrayn](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn), since it was considered largely under control during the time the army was active in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-gharad_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gharad-2">[2]</a></sup>

The army's higher officers wore golden armour, and their red surcoats prominently displayed the black dragon, which was described as a Sand Drake or “Were-worm”. The same emblem was displayed on shields as well.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Formation {#Formation}

At head is the Warlord ([Ta.](/wiki/Languages_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing#Tazinain "Languages in Middle-earth Role Playing") *Alaqa*), the successor to Wyatan. Four generals, each representing a wind direction, report directly to him. Each Wind comprised a field-army with a specific responsibility. The West Wind, usually led directly by the Warlord, was focused on the conquest of [Ciryatanor](/wiki/Ciryatanor) and [Tulwang](/wiki/Tulwang). The North Wind sought to to conquer [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad) while the South Wind focused on the south of the Yellow Mountains. Finally, the East Wind's general was in charge of Ny Chennacatt's defence as its captain, and could even directly report to the Storm King or the citadel's lord if necessary.

The army's cavalry was its primary force, and consisted of elite warriors riding [horses](/wiki/Horses "Horses") and [camels](/wiki/Camels "Camels"). The infrantry was of secondary importance, and was considered much less prestigious, often performing menial labour. The Winds could also field up to 2000 [Mûmakil](/wiki/M%C3%BBmakil) as siege weapons.

The Army of the Southern Dragon could also call upon the orc legions of Ny Chennacatt and Fhûl, each numbering around ten thousand troops. While they were primarily used for labour and support roles, select companies were deployed as shock troops to disrupt enemy formations before the cavalry engaged.<sup id="cite_ref-citadel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-citadel-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Warlords {#Warlords}

#### Wyatan {#Wyatan}

Wyatan was born in [Ciryatanor](/wiki/Ciryatanor) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1899&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1899 (page does not exist)">1899</a>, as the son of a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) sea captain. His sea-sickness caused him to receive much ridicule, and he instead focused on studying. Eventually he broke with his family and traveled to the capital. There he put his tactical mind to work by organizing raids of street gangs, and in adulthood bribed his way into prestigious jobs. He had become a palace guard by the time Akhôrahil influenced his father into taking his own life. The commander of the guard drew his weapon when Akhôrahil proclaimed himself king, but was cut down by Wyatan. This earned him the trust of his king, and he ended up as the commander of Ciryatanor's armies, which ultimately became the first Army of the Southern Dragon.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

#### Utchû {#Utchû}

Utchû was a [Black Númenórean](/wiki/Black_N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) born in [Dûshera](/wiki/D%C3%BBshera) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1327&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1327 (page does not exist)">1327</a>. The son of a Prince, he lived in spoiled luxury until his younger brother surpassed him in skill and attempted to expose his laziness. Killing his brother in retaliation and being found out, Utchû had to flee.<sup id="cite_ref-Utchû_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Utchû-5">[5]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1367&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1367 (page does not exist)">1367</a>, he became the Warlord of the reestablished Army of the Southern Dragon.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1641&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1641 (page does not exist)">1641</a>, he wielded a lesser ring of power previously worn by [Tartas Izain](/wiki/Tartas_Izain).<sup id="cite_ref-Utchû_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Utchû-5">[5]</a></sup> After conquering Ciryatanor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1802&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1802 (page does not exist)">1802</a>, he became its lord. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1812&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1812 (page does not exist)">1812</a>, he renounced his liege and renames the forces with him to the Army of the Burning Sun. The Army of the Southern Dragon, now led by Sangarunya, attacked Utchû's capital in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1815&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1815 (page does not exist)">1815</a>, and he was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-7">[7]</a></sup>

#### Sangarunya {#Sangarunya}

Sangarunya was an [Umbarean](/wiki/Umbar "Umbar") of Númenórean heritage. His father was a high priest of the [Dark Workship](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"), who was slain when that cult was persecuted, after which the family fled to [Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad). When training as a warrior, he shared a unit with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Pon_Ivic&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Pon Ivic (page does not exist)">Pon Ivic</a>, with whom he came into conflict. His humiliation after losing a duel led him to joining the Army of the Southern Dragon to seek revenge. Moving to Ny Chennacatt, he quickly rose in rank.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> After Utchû's defection in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1812&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1812 (page does not exist)">1812</a>, he became the new Warlord.<sup id="cite_ref-timeline_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timeline-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After Akhôrahil's orc forces were defeated in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1201&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1201 (page does not exist)">1201</a> due to its incompetent leadership, he reestablished the Army of the Southern Dragon a century later, with [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) warriors and a few Black Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-gharad_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gharad-2">[2]</a></sup> It is noted that a division of troops in the hosts of [Nurn](/wiki/Nurn) during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) originated from the elite of the Army of the Southern Dragon.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> <span></span>
