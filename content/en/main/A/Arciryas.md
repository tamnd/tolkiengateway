**Arciryas** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈarkirʲas]</span>) was the younger brother of King [Narmacil II](/wiki/Narmacil_II). His great-grandson [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II") would become [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor) after King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) and both of his sons perished in battle in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arciryas* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it seems to mean "Noble Ship" and to be a compound of [(a)ra](/wiki/Ara "Ara") ("noble") and [cirya](/wiki/Cirya "Cirya") ("ship").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
