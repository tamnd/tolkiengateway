**Arciryas** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈarkirʲas]</span>) was the younger brother of King [Narmacil II](../N/Narmacil_II.md). His great-grandson [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md) would become [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) after King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) and both of his sons perished in battle in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arciryas* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it seems to mean "Noble Ship" and to be a compound of [(a)ra](Ara.md) ("noble") and [cirya](../C/Cirya.md) ("ship").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
