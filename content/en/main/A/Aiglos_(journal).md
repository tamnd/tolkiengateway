<b>Polish Tolkienists Almanac <i>Aiglos</i></b> is the annual journal of the *Parmadili*, [The Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](/wiki/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_%C5%9AKF "Sekcja Tolkienowska ŚKF").

## History {#History}

*Aiglos* was founded in [November](/wiki/November) [2004](/wiki/2004). The regular Polish language issues were published biannually since the first issue in Winter [2004](/wiki/2004) till the #16 issue in Summer [2011](/wiki/2011). From the Summer of [2012](/wiki/2012) *Aiglos* continues as the annual. There are two English language issues: *[Special Issue I](/wiki/Aiglos_Special_Issue_I "Aiglos Special Issue I")*, Summer [2005](/wiki/2005) and *[Special Issue II](/wiki/Aiglos_Special_Issue_II "Aiglos Special Issue II")*, Summer 2012.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Content {#Content}

*Aiglos* is a non-commercial and non-profit annual journal of the *Parmadili*, the [Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](/wiki/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_%C5%9AKF "Sekcja Tolkienowska ŚKF"). Publication consists of articles and essays in Tolkien studies, both academic and popular papers. The texts concern Tolkien himself, his work, and the world he created. *Aiglos* also publishes reviews of Polish and foreign books on Tolkien and his work and presents fanfiction, including poetry, and fan art in black and white.
