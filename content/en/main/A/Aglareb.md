<i><b>aglareb</b></i> is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "glorious, brilliant". The word contains the element *[aglar](/wiki/Aglar "Aglar")* ("glory").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
