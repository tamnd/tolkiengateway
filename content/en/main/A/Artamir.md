**Artamir** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md): "noble jewel", pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈartamir]</span>, stem *Artamír-*; died [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md)) was the elder son and heir of King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Prince Artamir was the son of the [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md), and elder brother of [Faramir](<../F/Faramir_(son_of_Ondoher).md>) and [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md). They were born during perilous years for Gondor. King Ondoher had been warned by [Forthwini](../F/Forthwini.md) of [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) that the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) were on the move. Indeed the realm was assaulted simultaneously from the East and the South.

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) Artamir and his father Ondoher were the two top leaders of the northern army of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and led their forces into [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) in order to deploy upon the [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md), expecting the enemy from the north or north-east. Unfortunately the enemy not only came quicker than expected, but also directly from the east along the outskirts of the [Ered Lithui](../E/Ered_Lithui.md). The army of Gondor was strung out when the vanguard was struck by a powerful force of mixed cavalry and chariot-borne warriors. The King, Artamir, and most of the King's Guard were annihilated in the attack.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Artamir was the eldest son and heir of Ondoher, and with his death, the line of succession should move to close male relatives of the [House of Anárion](../H/House_of_Anárion.md). However his younger brother Faramir was also slain (he secretly disguised himself to participate in the battle) as well as the King's sister-son, [Minohtar](../M/Minohtar.md) and the throne of Gondor remained vacant.

His sister Fíriel was married to [Arvedui](Arvedui.md), son and heir of the northern King of [Arthedain](Arthedain.md), and partly based on his marriage, he asked to be granted the throne. However, the Steward [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md) and the [Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md) selected general [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md), who had crushed the enemies in the [Battle of the Camp](../B/Battle_of_the_Camp.md) and saved the realm, to be the next king.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Artamir* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it means "Noble Jewel" and is a compound of the prefixal form [arta](Arta.md) of *arata* ("noble") and [mírë](../M/Mir.md) ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
