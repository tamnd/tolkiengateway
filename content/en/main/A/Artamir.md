**Artamir** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"): "noble jewel", pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈartamir]</span>, stem *Artamír-*; died [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944")) was the elder son and heir of King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Prince Artamir was the son of the [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor), and elder brother of [Faramir](</wiki/Faramir_(son_of_Ondoher)> "Faramir (son of Ondoher)") and [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel). They were born during perilous years for Gondor. King Ondoher had been warned by [Forthwini](/wiki/Forthwini) of [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) that the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) were on the move. Indeed the realm was assaulted simultaneously from the East and the South.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") Artamir and his father Ondoher were the two top leaders of the northern army of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and led their forces into [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) in order to deploy upon the [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad), expecting the enemy from the north or north-east. Unfortunately the enemy not only came quicker than expected, but also directly from the east along the outskirts of the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui). The army of Gondor was strung out when the vanguard was struck by a powerful force of mixed cavalry and chariot-borne warriors. The King, Artamir, and most of the King's Guard were annihilated in the attack.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Artamir was the eldest son and heir of Ondoher, and with his death, the line of succession should move to close male relatives of the [House of Anárion](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion). However his younger brother Faramir was also slain (he secretly disguised himself to participate in the battle) as well as the King's sister-son, [Minohtar](/wiki/Minohtar) and the throne of Gondor remained vacant.

His sister Fíriel was married to [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), son and heir of the northern King of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), and partly based on his marriage, he asked to be granted the throne. However, the Steward [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur) and the [Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor) selected general [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II"), who had crushed the enemies in the [Battle of the Camp](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp) and saved the realm, to be the next king.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Artamir* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it means "Noble Jewel" and is a compound of the prefixal form [arta](/wiki/Arta "Arta") of *arata* ("noble") and [mírë](/wiki/Mir "Mir") ("jewel").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
