**Ann Griselda Lennox** ([December 25](../_/25_December.md), [1954](../_/1954.md)), is a Scottish singer. She recorded "[Into the West](../I/Into_the_West.md)" for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, which won her several awards.

She also recorded the less known "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Use_Well_the_Days&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Use Well the Days (page does not exist)">Use Well the Days</a>". This song was not used in the film, but appears on a bonus DVD included with the "special edition" of the film's soundtrack CD.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2004](../_/2004.md) – <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.goldenglobes.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Golden Globe</a></b>, Best Original Song - Motion Picture, "[Into the West](../I/Into_the_West.md)"
- 2004 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscars.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Music, Original Song, "Into the West"
- [2005](../_/2005.md) – <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.grammy.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Grammy</a></b>, Best Song Written for a Motion Picture, Television or Other Visual Media, "Into The West"
