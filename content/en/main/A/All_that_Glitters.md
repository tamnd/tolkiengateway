<i><b>All that glitters</b></i> is an EP or mini-album by [Soundestiny](../S/Soundestiny.md) that was released in 2009.

This is a short CD of music inspired by *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* with original songs written by Darryl Grant Potter. In 2009, [Soundestiny](../S/Soundestiny.md) were busily recording a full length album of Hobbit-inspired songs and decided to take a break from the project to finish their [Winds of Change](../W/Winds_of_Change.md) album. Consequently, it was decided to release four of the already-recorded *Hobbit* songs as an EP; the full album of songs inspired by [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md) was released in 2012 as [Dragon Quest](../D/Dragon_Quest.md). The four songs on *All that Glitters* are a brief, musical glimpse into [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s magical, fantasy world that later became even more famous with the publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
