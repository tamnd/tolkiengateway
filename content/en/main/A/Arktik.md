**Arktik**, also called **Arctick** or **Arctic**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the language spoken by the folk of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Pole" title="wikipedia:North Pole">North Pole</a>: the [Red Elves](/wiki/Red_Elves), the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear), and others.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Arktik samples {#Arktik_samples}

A sample of Arktik is seen in one of the letters: <i><b>Mára mesta an ni véla tye ento, ya rato nea</b></i>, which the Polar Bear translates as "Good-bye until I see you next, and I hope it will be soon".

Another sample of Arktik was published in 2019 in the deluxe version of the [Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas), dating from [1929](/wiki/1929): <i><b>Raiqe! Telkume kiryandon nolo</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Although the *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)* are not part of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the known sample of Arktik written by Tolkien is actually [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), which he had developed by then.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Tolkien linguists](/wiki/Category:Linguists "Category:Linguists") sometimes refer to it as **Arctic Q(u)enya**.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
