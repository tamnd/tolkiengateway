**Agh** means "and" in the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i>ash nazg thrakatulûk <b>agh</b> burzum-ishi krimpatul</i> - One ring to bring them all and in the darkness bind them
