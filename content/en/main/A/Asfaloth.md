**Asfaloth** was the white [horse](/wiki/Horses "Horses") of [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel).

## History {#History}

Asfaloth was ridden by Glorfindel during the search for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") on their way to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). After he found them and the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") attacked, Glorfindel dismounted and placed [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) (the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearers "Ring-bearers")) on its back, then ordered it forward to safety at the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen). Asfaloth was a remarkably fast horse, outpacing the black steeds of the Black Riders, even so that it reached the Fords first.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Asfaloth* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Sunlit Foam", from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ast&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ast (page does not exist)">ast</a></i> ("light or heat of the sun") + *[faloth](/wiki/Faloth "Faloth")* ("large foamy wave").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In answer to a question from [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare) in 1958, Tolkien admitted that Glorfindel would not have used a bridle and bit when riding Asfaloth; instead he would only have used an ornamental headstall with a plume, jewels, and small bells. He assured her that he would change *bridle and bit* to *headstall* in future editions, a change that was made.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
