**Aerlinn** is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for 'holy song' or maybe 'sea song'.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

The ony use of this word is given in the title of the song [A Elbereth Gilthoniel](/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel): <i><b>Aerlinn</b> in edhil o imladris</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore an *aerlinn* could be an Elven song or hymn addressed to [Elbereth](/wiki/Varda "Varda"), or more probably to any of the Valar.

The exact meaning of the word is ambiguous: the word for "holy" has been attested in Sindarin as the equivalent of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word *[airë](/wiki/Air%C3%AB "Airë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> On the other hand *[aer](/wiki/Aer "Aer")* means 'sea'. In that case these songs express their longing for [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) beyond the [Sea](/wiki/Sea).
