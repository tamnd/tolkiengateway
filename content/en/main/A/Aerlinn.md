**Aerlinn** is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for 'holy song' or maybe 'sea song'.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

The ony use of this word is given in the title of the song [A Elbereth Gilthoniel](A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel.md): <i><b>Aerlinn</b> in edhil o imladris</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Therefore an *aerlinn* could be an Elven song or hymn addressed to [Elbereth](../V/Varda.md), or more probably to any of the Valar.

The exact meaning of the word is ambiguous: the word for "holy" has been attested in Sindarin as the equivalent of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word *[airë](Airë.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> On the other hand *[aer](Aer.md)* means 'sea'. In that case these songs express their longing for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) beyond the [Sea](../S/Sea.md).
