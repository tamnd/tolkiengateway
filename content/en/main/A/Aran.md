**Aran** is the word for "king" or "chief" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). Its plural form is *erain*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) it was *aran*, as well.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The same root was shared by its [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) cognate, *ârû*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> However, the seemingly unrelated [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan_language "Rohan language") *tûrac* was their word for "king."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> A [Westron](/wiki/Westron) cognate is not known.
