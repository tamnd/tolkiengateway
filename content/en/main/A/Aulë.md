**Aulë** was a [Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar") and one of the [Aratar](/wiki/Aratar), also known as *the Smith* and *Smith of the Valar*, concerned with rock, metal, nature of substances and works of craft.

## Attributes {#Attributes}

Aulë governed the substances of the [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar) and he delighted in all works and crafts all of which he was master, from small works of skin to the forging of all lands and mountains and basins of the sea. He made the rocks, the gems and all minerals.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup>

He enjoyed devising and making new things, and being praised. In these thoughts and powers he was like [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor), as [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) gave him scarce less skill and knowledge. Aulë had always been faithful to Eru and submitted all his creations to His will. He was never jealous of others' creations, but sought and gave counsel. He enjoyed the things he has created and in act of making itself and passed ever to some new work, without hoarding or possessing. For this, there was much strife between him and Melkor who envied him; Melkor always marred his work and Aulë always sought to repair them.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ai_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was the third most powerful of the [Aratar](/wiki/Aratar). He was husband to [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), with whom he [dwelt](/wiki/Mansions_of_Aul%C3%AB "Mansions of Aulë") in central [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).

Several [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) are associated with Aulë: [Mairon](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron"), before being corrupted by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") and becoming Sauron; and [Curumo](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman"), who later went to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) as an [Istar](/wiki/Istar) to combat the darkness, only to be swayed by the willpower of Sauron.

## History {#History}

In the [Song of the Ainur](/wiki/Song_of_the_Ainur) Aulë thought most of the fabric of the Earth.<sup id="cite_ref-ai_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-2">[2]</a></sup> During the creation of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), Aulë worked much with [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo);<sup id="cite_ref-ai_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ai-2">[2]</a></sup> Aulë fashioned the substances that composed [Ambar](/wiki/Ambar), and was most involved in building the continents and mountains. As he is much like Melkor, there was strife between them and he grew weary repairing the tumults and disorders caused by Melkor on his work.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> One of his [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar), [Mairon](/wiki/Mairon), would be ensnared by his enemy in those early strifes.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

When [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) was also seduced by Melkor, Aulë pleaded with [Uinen](/wiki/Uinen) to calm and restrain him, and so the Maia returned to his master Ulmo.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

He constructed [Angainor](/wiki/Angainor), the chain of [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor), the lamps [Illuin](/wiki/Illuin) and [Ormal](/wiki/Ormal) and the vessels of the Sun and Moon.

Aulë created his own race of beings, the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), because he was unwilling to wait for the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) to appear. [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) knew of this and even as Aulë was instructing them He chastened Aulë. Aulë humbly repented, offering his children to the will of Ilúvatar, whom He accepted as His adopted children. Since [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) had decided that the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were to be the first-born race, He put the Dwarves to sleep until the Elves [woke](/wiki/Awakening_of_the_Elves "Awakening of the Elves") on [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

When the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) came to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) became the students of Aulë and his friends.<sup id="cite_ref-vala_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vala-1">[1]</a></sup> [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) was his greatest pupil, and from him learned the craft to make the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup><b></b>

*Aulë* is said to derive from [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) <i><b>Aȝūlēz</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the earlier *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, the name apparently means "invention" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=GAWA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="GAWA (page does not exist)">GAWA</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul), his name is <i><b>Mahal</b></i> ("[The Maker](/wiki/The_Maker)").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Sometimes he was called <i><b>Návatar</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> probably meaning "Dwarf-father" in Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In earlier [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), his name is <i><b>Gaul</b></i>, usually called *Belegol* ("great Aulë") or *Barthan* ("World-artificer").<sup id="cite_ref-Etymologies_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etymologies-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Aule is referred to as *Craeftfrea* ("Craft-ruler").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the early *[Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon)*, the [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) version of *Aulë* is *Óli*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> He is also given the title *Talka Marda* ("Smith of the World") during the text.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2017: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Aulë is frequently invoked by the dwarves, although due to the game not having the license to the <i>Silmarillion</i>, he is referred to simply as &#34;The First Crafter&#34;.</dd></dl>
