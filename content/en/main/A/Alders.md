**Alders** were deciduous trees that grew in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), especially in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), such as those that bordered [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water") west of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and by the brooks between [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills) and [Midgewater Marshes](/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They also were mentioned in the songs of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) to have grown by the banks of [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The elves of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) mentioned alders in their song under the window of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins): "Lullaby! Lullaby! Alder and Willow!".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Moreover, alders were known to have grown in the First Age in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) by the lake [Aeluin](/wiki/Aeluin).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
