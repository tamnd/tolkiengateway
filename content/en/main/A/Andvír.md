**Andvír**, son of [Andróg](/wiki/Andr%C3%B3g), was a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men"). With his father he was an outlaw in the band called the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith), which [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") joined and later led. When [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attacked the Gaurwaith's hideout upon [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh) there were only three survivors: Túrin (captured by the Orcs), [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) (whom Túrin would accidentally kill after Beleg later rescued him), and Andvír, who joined other refugees that made their way to the Havens.

As a very old man he supplied the poet [Dírhaval](/wiki/D%C3%ADrhaval) with the story of Túrin and the Gaurwaith, which filled the gap in Túrin's history between his flight from [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and his arrival at [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Note {#Note}

Andvír was not mentioned in *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* nor in any other version of the story. He only appeared in an introductory note that was excluded from the published book.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
