**Amon Ereb** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). "Lonely Hill"), sometimes just **Ereb**, was the broad, shallow-sided hill that dominated the southern plains of [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The hill was the highest point in that region and the easternmost hill of [Andram](/wiki/Andram), but was standing alone. Within sight of the [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), it had tremendous strategic importance, because it guarded the eastern passage around the Long Wall of the Andram into the southern parts of [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) and the northern [Taur-im-Duinath](/wiki/Taur-im-Duinath).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was here that [Denethor](</wiki/Denethor_(Nando)> "Denethor (Nando)") of the [Nandor](/wiki/Nandor) met his end in the [First Battle](/wiki/First_Battle).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Much later, [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir) fortified it to guard his escape into the south after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and the Fëanorions withdrew there after [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> But only after that, in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [540](/wiki/First_Age_540 "First Age 540"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) captured the hill driving the last remaining Sons of Fëanor, [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), to retreat further south to the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-AB_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Fate {#Fate}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Didier_Willis_-_Mount_Dolmed_and_Amon_Ereb.png"><img src="/w/images/b/bc/Didier_Willis_-_Mount_Dolmed_and_Amon_Ereb.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="321" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="321"/></a><figcaption>Collage of maps showing where Amon Ereb would be (if surviving the submersion of <a href="/wiki/Beleriand" title="Beleriand">Beleriand</a>).</figcaption></figure>

A small circle appears in [Forlindon](/wiki/Forlindon) on Tolkien's early draft map for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Isengard_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isengard-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:302</sup> Christopher Tolkien cannot explain this feature<sup id="cite_ref-Isengard_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isengard-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:301</sup> and notes that in his own 1943 redrawn map, the circular area is no longer present.<sup id="cite_ref-Isengard_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isengard-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:322</sup> It is possible to create a collage of the Beleriand and the Eriador maps to show the small circular area on the Third Age map coinciding with the location of Amon Ereb.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Noad suggests that Amon Ereb may have been once thought to have survived the submersion of Beleriand, although there is no textual evidence for it, and other Tolkienists such as [Karen Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Fonstad) have reconciled the two maps in a different way.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Ereb* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) "Lonely Hill", from *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* "hill" + *[ereb](/wiki/Ereb "Ereb")* "isolated, lonely".

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

In MERP, Amon Ereb does indeed survive the destruction of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).
