**Alan Joseph Bliss** (1921-1985) was a former student of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

Bliss studied under Tolkien from [1946](/wiki/1946) to [1948](/wiki/1948). In [1966](/wiki/1966), while he was working on a piece on Finn and Hengest, Tolkien lent him his old lecture notes from the 1920s. The notes were still in disarray when Tolkien died, and in 1979, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) handed them over to Bliss for publication.
