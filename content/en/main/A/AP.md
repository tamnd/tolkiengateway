**AP** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) of an unknown signification.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> A rejected signification was "cook(?)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): \**apsā*
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) (?):<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Apsa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Apsa (page does not exist)">apsa</a></i> ("cooked food, meat")
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Aes&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aes (page does not exist)">aes</a></i> ("cooked food, meat")
    - [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md): *[ass](Ass.md)* ("cooked food, meat")
