**AP** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") of an unknown signification.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> A rejected signification was "cook(?)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): \**apsā*
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (?):<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Apsa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Apsa (page does not exist)">apsa</a></i> ("cooked food, meat")
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aes&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aes (page does not exist)">aes</a></i> ("cooked food, meat")
    - [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin): *[ass](/wiki/Ass "Ass")* ("cooked food, meat")
