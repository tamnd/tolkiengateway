**Arathorn II** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2873](/wiki/Third_Age_2873 "Third Age 2873")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> - [2933](/wiki/Third_Age_2933 "Third Age 2933"),<sup id="cite_ref-North_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-3">[3]</a></sup> died aged 60) was the fifteenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) and succeeded his father, [Arador](/wiki/Arador), when he was killed in [2930](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930"). He was the father of [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), King of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom).

## History {#History}

In the last year of his father's reign as Chieftain, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2929](/wiki/Third_Age_2929 "Third Age 2929"), Arathorn sought [Gilraen](/wiki/Gilraen) as his wife. She was only twenty-two, so her father, [Dírhael](/wiki/D%C3%ADrhael), opposed the marriage. He also warned that Arathorn would not live long. Yet [Ivorwen](/wiki/Ivorwen), Gilraen's mother, was gifted with [foresight](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight") and perceived that [hope](/wiki/Estel "Estel") would be born from their marriage for the people of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain).<sup id="cite_ref-Av_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Av-1">[1]</a></sup> Thus, Gilraen and Arathorn were married in [2929](/wiki/Third_Age_2929 "Third Age 2929").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Indeed, [the next year](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930"), [Arador](/wiki/Arador), Arathorn's father, was slain by [hill-trolls](/wiki/Hill-trolls "Hill-trolls"), and Arathorn became Chieftain. A year later, Gilraen bore him a son, whom they named [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). Young Aragorn would never know his father; in [2933](/wiki/Third_Age_2933 "Third Age 2933"), Arathorn went hunting [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") with [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), but was shot through the eye and killed. Gilraen took their son to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) to be fostered by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).

Arathorn II served as [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) for just three years. He was succeeded by his only son, who would become [Chieftain Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"), and in time King Elessar of the [Reunited Kingdom of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain).<sup id="cite_ref-Av_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Av-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Arathorn was likely named after his ancestor, [Arathorn I](/wiki/Arathorn_I). There have been two explanations for the name:

In a [letter](/wiki/Letter_347 "Letter 347") dated [1972](/wiki/1972), [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") gave the name the meaning "Eagle King" (from *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* "king" and *[thoron](/wiki/Thoron "Thoron")* "eagle").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

However, in work written in the late 50's, Tolkien gave "Steadfast King" (from *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* "king" and *[thorn](/wiki/Thorn "Thorn")* "steadfast") instead.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth" title="Guardians of Middle-earth">Guardians of Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arathorn II is a playable Warrior in <i><a href="/wiki/Guardians_of_Middle-earth" title="Guardians of Middle-earth">Guardians of Middle-earth</a></i>. He tracked down the Troll who killed his father and single-handedly slew it on a cliff above the <a href="/wiki/Ettenmoors" title="Ettenmoors">Ettenmoors</a>. Heroic deeds of the like earned Arathorn the respect of his people and of <a href="/wiki/Elladan_and_Elrohir" title="Elladan and Elrohir">Elladan and Elrohir</a>. His abilities combine ranged and melee attacks. His &#39;Staggering Shot&#39; stuns enemies with his bow. &#39;Rangers Strike&#39; stabs the enemy, slowing them and reducing movement speed, before sending Arathorn into stealth mode. With &#39;<a href="/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain" title="Dúnedain">Dúnedain</a> Blade&#39;, Arathorn swings his blade in an arc, damaging and slowing his opponent. &#39;Chieftain&#39;s Focus&#39; lands a powerful blow on his opponent and increases his attack speed. His passive ability, &#39;Heir of <a href="/wiki/Isildur" title="Isildur">Isildur</a>&#39;, means every 10th successful attack of his will deal critical damage and his movement speed is faster to other ranked characters in the base game.</dd></dl>
