**Amárië** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[aˈmari.e]</span>) was a [Vanyarin](../V/Vanyar.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) who was loved by [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md).

Finrod, son of [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md), was a [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) prince who met Amárië in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). Although in love with one another, they did not marry, and Amárië did not follow Finrod into exile to [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S-Noldor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup> According to *[The Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)*, this was because she was not permitted.<sup id="cite_ref-GA-1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1-2">[2]</a></sup> Following Finrod's death at the hands of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) during the [Quest for the Silmaril](../Q/Quest_for_the_Silmaril.md), he was brought back to life and lives with Amárië in Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-GA-2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-2-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is not given an interpretation within the legendarium. [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) suggests that this [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name is perhaps derived from *mára* "good".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Another possibility is that the name includes the element *[mar](../M/Mar.md)*, "home"<sup id="cite_ref-EOA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EOA-5">[5]</a></sup> [Ruth S. Noel](../R/Ruth_S._Noel.md) makes a similar interpretation and defines *Amárië* as "Of the Home".<sup id="cite_ref-LOTME_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTME-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to a version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) following the completion of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, [Felagund](../F/Felagund.md) had a wife named <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../M/Meril.md" title="Meril">Meril</a></b> who went with him into exile and bore the [child](../E/Elven_life_cycle.md#Early_life) [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md). She was sent away by Felagund along with their son to her [own folk](../F/Falathrim.md) at [Eglorest](../E/Eglorest.md) in the [Havens of the Falas](../H/Havens_of_the_Falas.md) during the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md), due to Felagund fearing "that all strong places were doomed to fall at last before the might of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-7">[7]</a></sup> This was later changed so that Finrod had no child - with Gil-galad becoming the son of [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md), Finrod's nephew - and Amárië remaining in Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-8">[8]</a></sup>
