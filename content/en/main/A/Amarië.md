**Amárië** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[aˈmari.e]</span>) was a [Vanyarin](/wiki/Vanyar "Vanyar") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") who was loved by [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod).

Finrod, son of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), was a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") prince who met Amárië in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Although in love with one another, they did not marry, and Amárië did not follow Finrod into exile to [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-S-Noldor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S-Noldor-1">[1]</a></sup> According to *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, this was because she was not permitted.<sup id="cite_ref-GA-1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-1-2">[2]</a></sup> Following Finrod's death at the hands of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) during the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril), he was brought back to life and lives with Amárië in Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-GA-2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GA-2-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is not given an interpretation within the legendarium. [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) suggests that this [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name is perhaps derived from *mára* "good".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Another possibility is that the name includes the element *[mar](/wiki/Mar "Mar")*, "home"<sup id="cite_ref-EOA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EOA-5">[5]</a></sup> [Ruth S. Noel](/wiki/Ruth_S._Noel) makes a similar interpretation and defines *Amárië* as "Of the Home".<sup id="cite_ref-LOTME_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOTME-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

According to a version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") following the completion of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [Felagund](/wiki/Felagund) had a wife named <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Meril" title="Meril">Meril</a></b> who went with him into exile and bore the [child](/wiki/Elven_life_cycle#Early_life "Elven life cycle") [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad). She was sent away by Felagund along with their son to her [own folk](/wiki/Falathrim "Falathrim") at [Eglorest](/wiki/Eglorest) in the [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas) during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), due to Felagund fearing "that all strong places were doomed to fall at last before the might of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-7">[7]</a></sup> This was later changed so that Finrod had no child - with Gil-galad becoming the son of [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), Finrod's nephew - and Amárië remaining in Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-8">[8]</a></sup>
