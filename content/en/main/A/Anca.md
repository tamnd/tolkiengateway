<i><b>anca</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> is a noun meaning "jaws" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anca.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/be/Anca.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Anca* is also the name in Quenya of the fifteenth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[1]</a></sup> It is the third letter of the fourth grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiced spirants" and it is the fourth letter in the third series of consonants, which for Quenya and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) was the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](../C/Calmatéma.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-3">[2]</a></sup> In the standard spelling of Quenya this letter represents **NC** or **NK**<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[1]</a></sup> while in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) this letter represents **GH**. In [Westron](../W/Westron.md), the third series is used for a variety of consonantal sounds and this letter represents **ZH**.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-3">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Izhe</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Cognate {#Cognate}

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [ÁNAK](../_/ÁNAK.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
