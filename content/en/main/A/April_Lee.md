**April Lee** is an artist. In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)* and illustrations for [The Shire](</wiki/The_Shire_(book)> "The Shire (book)") supplement.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
