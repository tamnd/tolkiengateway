<i><b>Arda&#39;s Herbarium: A Musical Guide to the Mystical Garden of Middle-Earth and Stranger Places</b></i> is an album series by Canadian musician <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ithildin_(musician)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ithildin (musician) (page does not exist)">Ithildin</a>. It aims to compose music for every plant in Tolkien's works, inspired by [Flora of Middle-Earth](/wiki/Flora_of_Middle-Earth).

## Volumes {#Volumes}

As of [2025](/wiki/2025), six volumes have been released.
