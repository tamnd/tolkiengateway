**Andrew Lesnie** ([1 January](/wiki/1_January), [1956](/wiki/1956) - [27 April](/wiki/27_April), [2015](/wiki/2015))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was an Australian cinematographer. He served as the director of photography for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and for [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). He won the Academy Award for Best Cinematography in [2002](/wiki/2002) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
