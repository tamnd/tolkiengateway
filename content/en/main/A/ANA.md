**ANA** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"), with two different meanings.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## ANA<sup>1</sup> {#ANA1}

Meaning: "to, towards" (see also <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C4%80&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NĀ (page does not exist)">NĀ<sup>1</sup></a>)

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *anta-* ("to present, give"), *annā* ("gift")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:90</sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *anta-* ("give"); *[anna](/wiki/Anna "Anna")*, *anwa* ("gift"); *ante* (fem., "giver"), *anto* (masc., "giver"); *anta* ("face")
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *anno* ("to give"); *[ant](/wiki/Ant "Ant")* ("gift")
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *anha-, aned* ("to give");,<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:93</sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *ann, anw* ("gift")

Compounds:

- <i><a href="/wiki/Yavanna" title="Yavanna">Yav<b>anna</b></a></i>
- <i>Ary<b>ante</b></i> ("Day-bringer")

## ANA<sup>2</sup> {#ANA2}

Meaning: "be, exist" (see also <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C4%80&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NĀ (page does not exist)">NĀ<sup>2</sup></a>)

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[anwa](/wiki/Anwa "Anwa")*, *[nanwa](/wiki/Nanwa "Nanwa")*
