**Anemones** were small flowers of many pale colours. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) found white and blue anemones growing in the fragrant flowered lands of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) also hints that [simbelmynë](../S/Simbelmynë.md) was also a variety of anemone, or at least similar in appearance.
