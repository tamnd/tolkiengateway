**Amnon** was a [prophet](../S/Seer.md) of old according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. His most known prophesy was "*Great is the [fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md)*", which was quoted again by [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md), King of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), at the time the event took place.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first version of the [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md) appearing in *The Book of Lost Tales*, it is a servant of [Vefántur](../V/Vefántur.md) who proclaims, among other misfortunes, the fall of Gondolin; and the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) remembered those words as the "Prophecies of Amnos" (*[Amnos](Amnos.md)* being the place where they had heard them).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) wrote a couple of notes referring to the "Prophecy of Amnon", with the words "*Great is the fall of Gondolin. When the [lily of the valley](../L/Lothengriol.md) withers then shall Turgon fade*", but it is unclear if here *Amnon* is a place or a person. As [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) suggests, the prophet Amnon could be the servant of Vefántur that proclaimed the doom of the Noldoli.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In the Bible, Amnon was the wicked eldest son of David. [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was surely aware of this character (as he was a Catholic), and it is curious to speculate on his particular choosing of this name, as it was in his earlier works.
