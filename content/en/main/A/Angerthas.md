**Angerthas** was a term for [Runic](/wiki/Runes "Runes") writing. There were numerous variants, but the two principal forms were the [Angerthas Daeron](/wiki/Angerthas_Daeron), used by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to represent [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), and the [Angerthas Moria](/wiki/Angerthas_Moria), the classical form used by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), which was further evolved to [Angerthas Erebor](/wiki/Angerthas_Erebor).<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Angerthas* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "long rune-rows".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The first element is *[an(d)](/wiki/And "And")* ("long"), and the second is likely *[certhas](/wiki/Certhas "Certhas")*.<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
