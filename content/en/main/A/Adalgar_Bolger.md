**Adalgar Bolger** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Adalgar was the eldest child of [Gundahar Bolger](../G/Gundahar_Bolger.md) and [Dina Diggle](../D/Dina_Diggle.md). He had three children: [Rudigar](../R/Rudigar_Bolger.md), [Rudibert](../R/Rudibert_Bolger.md) and [Ruby](../R/Ruby_Bolger.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Adalgar* is a Germanic name composed of the elements *adal* "noble" and *gar* "spear".

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adalgar" title="wikipedia:Adalgar">Adalgar</a> was the name of the third archbishop of Bremen and a saint who died in 909.
