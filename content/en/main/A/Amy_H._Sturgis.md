**Amy H. Sturgis** is an author, professor, and scholar specializing in science fiction, fantasy, gothic, and Native American studies.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She has taught at Belmont University, Lenoir-Rhyne University, and Signum University, including courses about the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
