**Ambarussa** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌambaˈrusːa]</span>) was [Nerdanel's](../N/Nerdanel.md) [mother-name](Amilessë.md) for her twin sons [Amrod](Amrod.md) and [Amras](Amras.md). It translates as "top-russet", referring to the color of their hair. After [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) their father insisted on giving them each separate names Nerdanel named Amrod *[Umbarto](../U/Umbarto.md)*, the fated, and Fëanor changed it to *[Ambarto](Ambarto.md)*. But the twins each still called the other *Ambarussa*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
