**Ambarussa** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˌambaˈrusːa]</span>) was [Nerdanel's](/wiki/Nerdanel "Nerdanel") [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") for her twin sons [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amras](/wiki/Amras). It translates as "top-russet", referring to the color of their hair. After [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) their father insisted on giving them each separate names Nerdanel named Amrod *[Umbarto](/wiki/Umbarto)*, the fated, and Fëanor changed it to *[Ambarto](/wiki/Ambarto)*. But the twins each still called the other *Ambarussa*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
