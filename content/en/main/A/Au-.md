<i><b>au-</b></i> means "without" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It is the adjectival form of *[ú-](/wiki/%C3%9A- "Ú-")*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup> **au** could also be used as an adjective meaning "off, away, not here, of position".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

**au-** derives from the prefix **ú-** from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C5%AA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ū (page does not exist)">Ū</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Example {#Example}

- **aunote(a)** = "not counted, uncounted"<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **auvane(a)** = "without beauty"<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup>
