<i><b>auth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "a dim shape, [spectral](../S/Spectres.md) or vague apparition".<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) ([Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) or [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md)) *aw'tha* (from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [WATH](../W/WATH.md))<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>
