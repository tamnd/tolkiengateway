<i><b>auth</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "a dim shape, [spectral](/wiki/Spectres "Spectres") or vague apparition".<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) ([Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) or [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin)) *aw'tha* (from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [WATH](/wiki/WATH))<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[gwath](/wiki/Gwath "Gwath")*
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[auþa, ausa](/wiki/Ausa "Ausa")*
