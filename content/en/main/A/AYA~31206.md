**AYA** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying reverence and holiness. AYA-N is given the meaning "treat with awe/reverence", AYA is given the meaning "revere", and AYA/AYA is given the meaning "blessed".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Roots of opposite meaning are [SKŪ, KHŪ](/wiki/SK%C5%AA "SKŪ") ("curse"), [OKO](/wiki/OKO) ("wicked, evil"), and [UK, UKLA](/wiki/UK "UK").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[ainur](/wiki/Ainur "Ainur")*; *[aire (airē)](/wiki/Air%C3%AB "Airë")*; *airëa* ("holy", applied to persons); *[aina (ayanā)](/wiki/Aina "Aina")*; *ainima* ("blessed, holy", applied to things); *ainas* ("a hallow, a fane"); *áyan, aian* ("a holy thing or object or place"); *[aia, aiya!](/wiki/Aiya "Aiya")*<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *Quenya Lexicon* appears the root AYA, signifying "honour, revere".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the roots AYAN (yielding Common Eldarin *ayan-*, "holy") and YAN (yielding Quenya *yána*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *iaun*, "holy place, fane, sanctuary").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
