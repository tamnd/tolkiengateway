**AYA** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying reverence and holiness. AYA-N is given the meaning "treat with awe/reverence", AYA is given the meaning "revere", and AYA/AYA is given the meaning "blessed".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Roots of opposite meaning are [SKŪ, KHŪ](../S/SKŪ.md) ("curse"), [OKO](../O/OKO.md) ("wicked, evil"), and [UK, UKLA](../U/UK.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[ainur](Ainur.md)*; *[aire (airē)](Airë.md)*; *airëa* ("holy", applied to persons); *[aina (ayanā)](Aina.md)*; *ainima* ("blessed, holy", applied to things); *ainas* ("a hallow, a fane"); *áyan, aian* ("a holy thing or object or place"); *[aia, aiya!](Aiya.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *Quenya Lexicon* appears the root AYA, signifying "honour, revere".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* appears the roots AYAN (yielding Common Eldarin *ayan-*, "holy") and YAN (yielding Quenya *yána*, [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *iaun*, "holy place, fane, sanctuary").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
