**Asmodee** (officially **Asmodee Group AB**) is a French/Swedish tabletop publishing company. Originally founded in 1995 in Boulogne-Billancourt, France, they have since grown massively, having aquired multiple high-visibility names in tabletop gaming since, including [Fantasy Flight Games](../F/Fantasy_Flight_Games.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Z-Man_Games&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Z-Man Games (page does not exist)">Z-Man Games</a>, Catan Studio, and many more.

They themselves were bought by Eurazeo in 2014, sold to PAI Partners in 2018 and acquired by the [Embracer Group](../E/Embracer_Group.md) in 2022. They were spun out into their own independent company again in 2025, officially incorporated in Sweden. Later that year they were made the exclusive category manager for all [Middle-earth Enterprises](../M/Middle-earth_Enterprises.md) licensed tabletop games.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
