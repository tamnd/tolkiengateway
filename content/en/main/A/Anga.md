<i><b>anga</b></i> is a noun meaning "iron" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anga.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/a/af/Anga.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Anga* is also the name in Quenya of the seventh letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the third letter of the second grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiced stops" and it is the second letter in the third series of consonants, which in Quenya and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) was the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](../C/Calmatéma.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> In the standard spelling of Quenya *Anga* represents **NG**,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> while in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) this letter represents **G**. In [Westron](../W/Westron.md), the third series is used for a variety of consonantal sounds and this letter represents **J**.<sup id="cite_ref-Letters_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letters-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Jé</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

At the end of the second week in [September](../S/September.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3001](../T/Third_Age_3001.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) rode through [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) in a cart. Halting at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) he unloaded bundles of firewords, all of them inscribed with the tengwa *Anga*. It was his initial but the hobbit-children who had followed his cart thought the letter stood for "Grand!"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [ANGA](ANGA~30450.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

- <i><a href="Angband.md" title="Angband"><b>Anga</b>mandi</a></i> = Hells of Iron"
