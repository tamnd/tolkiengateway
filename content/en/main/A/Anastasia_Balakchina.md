**Anastasia Balakchina** is an Armenian concept artist. As a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, she created art for the [Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth") and [The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit") expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
