**Adelard Took** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Adelard was the son of [Flambard Took](/wiki/Flambard_Took), and had several children; [Reginard](/wiki/Reginard_Took "Reginard Took"), [Everard](/wiki/Everard_Took "Everard Took") and two daughters. They were all present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The next morning, he joined a crowd who went to [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) to ask [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) about [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo)'s disapparance. Bilbo had left for him an umbrella as a parting gift, although Adelard left taking some others.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Adelard is a combination of the Germanic elements *adel* ("noble") and *hard* ("strong").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In older editions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the Took family tree in [Appendix C](/wiki/Appendix_C) showed that Adelard had **three** daughters. The number was changed to **two** in later editions.
