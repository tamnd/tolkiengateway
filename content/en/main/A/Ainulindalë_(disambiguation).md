**Ainulindalë** or **Music of the Ainur** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- The First [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur), which created the world, narrated by <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/R%C3%BAmil" title="Rúmil">Rúmil</a> in his [*Ainulindalë*](</wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(R%C3%BAmil%27s_work)> "Ainulindalë (Rúmil's work)").
- The [Second Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Second_Music_of_the_Ainur), which will re-create the world.

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB" title="Ainulindalë">Ainulindalë</a></b></i>, the first part of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
- *[The Music of the Ainur](/wiki/The_Music_of_the_Ainur)*, the second chapter of *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*.
- *[Ainulindalë (Lost Road)](</wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(Lost_Road)>)*, the seventh chapter of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)*.
- *[Ainulindalë (Morgoth's Ring)](</wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB_(Morgoth%27s_Ring)>)*, the first chapter of *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*.

## Popular Media {#Popular_Media}

- [Ainulindale (band)](</wiki/Ainulindale_(band)>), a French folk metal band.
    - *Ainulindale*, a song by the French band in their album *[The Lay Of Leithian](</wiki/The_Lay_Of_Leithian_(album)> "The Lay Of Leithian (album)")*.
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ainulindale_Ensemble&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ainulindale Ensemble (page does not exist)">Ainulindale Ensemble</a>, an Italian orchestral jazz group.
