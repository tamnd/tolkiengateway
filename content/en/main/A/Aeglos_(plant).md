**Aeglos** was a tall, thorny plant, similar to a gorse, but somewhat larger than that. Its sweet-smelling flowers were white, and from them it took its name: *aeglos* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "snow-thorn".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was found on the lower slopes of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> upon the surrounding moors,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> in West [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and perhaps elsewhere. <span></span>
