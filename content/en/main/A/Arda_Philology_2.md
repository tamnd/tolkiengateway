<i><b>Arda Philology 2</b></i> is the second volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)")*, and contains the proceedings of the Second International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/otatya.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Tatya</a> (<a href="/wiki/Omentielva" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Antwerp in August [2007](/wiki/2007).
