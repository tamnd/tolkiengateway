<i><b>A Hobbit&#39;s Travels</b></i> is a book with blank pages decorated by illustrations depicting various scenes (of mostly landscapes) from *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, intended to be used as a note-book. It also contains a one-page introductory text by "Sam Gamgee".

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

Printed on deluxe recycled parchment paper, this journal celebrating [J. R. R. Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) classic tales makes a lovely gift, and is just as nice to keep! With magical two-color illustrations throughout (drawings made by [Frodo Baggins's](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) devoted companion, [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), on their travels throughout [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md)), it provides ample space for recording personal thoughts, reflections on Tolkien's masterpiece, or fantasies of your own creation.
