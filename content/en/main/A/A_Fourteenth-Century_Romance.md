**A Fourteenth-Century Romance** is the title of an essay written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and published in the magazine *[Radio Times](../R/Radio_Times_4_December_1953.md)* on [4 December](../_/4_December.md) [1953](../_/1953.md). The article consists of the foreword to Tolkien's translation of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](<../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition).md>)*, which was broadcast on the BBC Third Programme in 1953.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was reprinted in abbreviated form as the second part of the Introduction to *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo.md)*.
