**A Fourteenth-Century Romance** is the title of an essay written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and published in the magazine *[Radio Times](/wiki/Radio_Times_4_December_1953 "Radio Times 4 December 1953")* on [4 December](/wiki/4_December) [1953](/wiki/1953). The article consists of the foreword to Tolkien's translation of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (edition)")*, which was broadcast on the BBC Third Programme in 1953.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was reprinted in abbreviated form as the second part of the Introduction to *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo)*.
