<i><b>Ancrene Wisse: The English Text of the Ancrene Riwle</b></i> is an edition of the 13th-century religious text <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancrene_Wisse" title="wikipedia:Ancrene Wisse">Ancrene Wisse</a></i>, edited by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). It was published in [1962](/wiki/1962) by the Oxford University Press, commissioned by the Early English Text Society.

*Ancrene Wisse* (or *Ancrene Riwle*) is early [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) for "Guide for Anchoresses", it is a set of rules written for the female religious recluses. There are several surviving manuscripts of this text, this volume is edited from one known as "MS CCCC 402",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> which especially interested Tolkien.

The book includes a prefatory note by Tolkien, and an introduction by N.R. Ker.
