<i><b>A New Glossary of the Dialect of the Huddersfield District</b></i> is a [1928](/wiki/1928) reference book of the dialect of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield" title="wikipedia:Huddersfield">Huddersfield</a>. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) contributed with a six page [Foreword](/wiki/Foreword_to_A_New_Glossary_of_the_Dialect_of_the_Huddersfield_District "Foreword to A New Glossary of the Dialect of the Huddersfield District") (pp. xiii-xviii).<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>

In his preface, Haigh thanks Tolkien: "*In the first place, I would sincerely thank Professor J.R.R. Tolkien, formerly Professor in [Leeds University](/wiki/University_of_Leeds "University of Leeds"), now Professor of [Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") at [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford"). Not only has he almost from the first shown his warm approval of the work, and befriended me with ever-ready advice and encouragement throughout, but he has also generously contributed a valuable Foreword to the Glossary.*"<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
