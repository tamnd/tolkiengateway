<i><b>A New Glossary of the Dialect of the Huddersfield District</b></i> is a [1928](../_/1928.md) reference book of the dialect of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huddersfield" title="wikipedia:Huddersfield">Huddersfield</a>. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed with a six page [Foreword](../F/Foreword_to_A_New_Glossary_of_the_Dialect_of_the_Huddersfield_District.md) (pp. xiii-xviii).<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>

In his preface, Haigh thanks Tolkien: "*In the first place, I would sincerely thank Professor J.R.R. Tolkien, formerly Professor in [Leeds University](../U/University_of_Leeds.md), now Professor of [Anglo-Saxon](../O/Old_English.md) at [Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md). Not only has he almost from the first shown his warm approval of the work, and befriended me with ever-ready advice and encouragement throughout, but he has also generously contributed a valuable Foreword to the Glossary.*"<sup id="cite_ref-TB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TB-2">[2]</a></sup>
