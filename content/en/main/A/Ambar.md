<i><b>ambar</b></i> means "doom" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") MBART ("doom, fate").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-E_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/T%C3%BArin" title="Túrin">Tur<b>ambar</b></a></i> = "Master of Doom"
- *[umbar](</wiki/Umbar_(word)> "Umbar (word)")*

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), *[amarth](/wiki/Amarth "Amarth")*

# Ambar {#Ambar}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Sage_-_Arda_in_the_Second_Age.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a4/Sage_-_Arda_in_the_Second_Age.png/300px-Sage_-_Arda_in_the_Second_Age.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="254" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/a/a4/Sage_-_Arda_in_the_Second_Age.png 1.5x" data-file-width="445" data-file-height="377"/></a><figcaption>The flat World during the <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a></figcaption></figure>

<i><b>Ambar</b></i> also refers to "The World" with the connotation of "habitation" or "home" ("<a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oikumene" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Oikoumene</a>"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>), which referred to the inhabited world, as opposed to the barbaric wild.

In this sense *Ambar* is different than *[Arda](/wiki/Arda)*, which refers to the world as a physical whole.

## Etymology {#Etymology_2}

[Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") A-[MBAR](/wiki/MBAR) ("dwelling, habitation")<sup id="cite_ref-E_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates_2}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Amar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Amar (page does not exist)">amar</a></i> ("earth"), *[bar](/wiki/Bar "Bar")* ("land")

## See also {#See_also}

- *[már](/wiki/M%C3%A1r "Már")*, "home, dwelling"
- *[Ambar-metta](/wiki/Ambar-metta)*, "the Ending of the World"

## Notes {#Notes}

The [Elves](/wiki/Elves) may have equated the concept of "doom" with the inhabited world because their *[fëar](/wiki/F%C3%ABar "Fëar")* were tied eternally with Arda, as opposed to [Men](/wiki/Men), whose souls escape beyond the world upon death (see: the [Gift of Men](/wiki/Gift_of_Men)).

Furthermore, the roots MBAR and MBAR-AT might be ultimately related.
