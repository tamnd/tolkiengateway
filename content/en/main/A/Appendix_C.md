**Appendix C** of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* contains several family trees of notable [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) Families. According to the introduction of the appendix, the names shown are just a selection:

> The names given in these Trees are only a selection from many. Most of them are either guests at Bilbo's [Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md), or their direct ancestors. .... A few other names of persons concerned in the events recounted are also given. In addition some genealogical information is provided concerning [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) the founder of the family of [Gardner](../G/Gardner_Family~66367.md), later famous and influential.<br> The figures after the names are those of birth (and death where that is recorded). All dates are given according to the [Shire-reckoning](../S/Shire-reckoning.md), calculated from the crossing of the [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) by the brothers [Marcho](../M/Marcho.md) and [Blanco](../B/Blanco.md) in the Year 1 of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) 1601).

The Family Trees of Appendix C are:

- [Baggins of Hobbiton](../B/Baggins_Family~66343.md#Inspiration)
- [Took of Great Smials](../T/Took_Family.md)
- [Brandybuck of Buckland](../B/Brandybuck_Family~66350.md#Brandybuck_Family_Tree)
- [The Longfather-Tree of Master Samwise](../G/Gamgee_family.md#The_Longfather.E2.80.93Tree_of_Master_Samwise)

In the [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) two further Family Trees (first published in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*) were included:

- [Bolger of Budgeford](../B/Bolger_Family~66348.md#Bolger_Family_Tree)
- [Boffin of the Yale](../B/Boffin_Family~66342.md#Boffin_Family_Tree)
