**Appendix C** of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* contains several family trees of notable [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") Families. According to the introduction of the appendix, the names shown are just a selection:

> The names given in these Trees are only a selection from many. Most of them are either guests at Bilbo's [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party"), or their direct ancestors. .... A few other names of persons concerned in the events recounted are also given. In addition some genealogical information is provided concerning [Samwise](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") the founder of the family of [Gardner](/wiki/Gardner_Family "Gardner Family"), later famous and influential.<br> The figures after the names are those of birth (and death where that is recorded). All dates are given according to the [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning), calculated from the crossing of the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) by the brothers [Marcho](/wiki/Marcho) and [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco) in the Year 1 of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 1601).

The Family Trees of Appendix C are:

- [Baggins of Hobbiton](/wiki/Baggins_Family#Inspiration "Baggins Family")
- [Took of Great Smials](/wiki/Took_Family "Took Family")
- [Brandybuck of Buckland](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family#Brandybuck_Family_Tree "Brandybuck Family")
- [The Longfather-Tree of Master Samwise](/wiki/Gamgee_family#The_Longfather.E2.80.93Tree_of_Master_Samwise "Gamgee family")

In the [50th Anniversary Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)") two further Family Trees (first published in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*) were included:

- [Bolger of Budgeford](/wiki/Bolger_Family#Bolger_Family_Tree "Bolger Family")
- [Boffin of the Yale](/wiki/Boffin_Family#Boffin_Family_Tree "Boffin Family")
