## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>a</b></i> is used in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) as an interjection "O, Oh".

### Examples {#Examples}

- *a Túrin Turambar turún’ ambartanen* = (O master of doom by doom mastered)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>a</b></i> is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "and"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L230_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L230-3">[3]</a></sup> as well as "<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/o#Interjection" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">O!</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-L230_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L230-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ar](/wiki/Ar "Ar")*

## Gnomish {#Gnomish}

<i><b>a</b></i> is a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) prefix, used to (1) cause an "initial consonant change; (2) form several adjectives and nouns".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
