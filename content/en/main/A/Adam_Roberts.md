**Adam Roberts**, also known by the pseudonym [A.R.R.R. Roberts](/wiki/A.R.R.R._Roberts), is an English science-fiction writer and a Professor of Nineteenth-Century Literature. He is the author of the [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) parodies *[The Soddit](/wiki/The_Soddit)* and *[The Sellamillion](/wiki/The_Sellamillion)* as well as designing the video game trilogy *[Mines of Moria](</wiki/Mines_of_Moria_(Video_game_series)> "Mines of Moria (Video game series)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
