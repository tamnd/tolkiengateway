The **Arch of Inwë** was located in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was the western entrance to the [Square of the Folkwell](/wiki/Square_of_the_Folkwell). During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(elf_of_Gondolin)> "Galdor (elf of Gondolin)") and his last men fought there against an incoming horde of [goblins](/wiki/Goblins "Goblins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The origin of its name, concerning [Inwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Ingwë"), is unknown as it is only mentioned once.
