**Arwen Undómiel**, often called **Arwen Evenstar**, was one of the [half-elven](../H/Half-elven.md), married to [Aragorn II](Aragorn.md). She was the daughter of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

She was considered to be the fairest of the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), resembling [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) who would never again appear in [Ëa](../_/Ëa.md). Her romance with [Aragorn](Aragorn.md) was reminiscent of that between the Man [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and the Elf Lúthien. Like Lúthien, she rejected her [Elven](../E/Elves.md) immortality to marry Aragorn and die with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg/300px-Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="412" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg/450px-Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/8/8c/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg 2x" data-file-width="567" data-file-height="778"/></a><figcaption><i>Evenstar</i> by <a href="Anna_Lee.md" title="Anna Lee">Anna Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

Arwen was born in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [241](../T/Third_Age_241.md) and was the younger sister of [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup> From her mother she inherited the [Elfstone](../E/Elfstone.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-6">[6]</a></sup> As one of the half-elven, she shared the right of her father to choose her fate. She lived most of her life in [Imladris](../I/Imladris.md), or in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) with her grandparents.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

Her father fostered the sons of the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md), who were the exiled [Heirs of Isildur](../H/Heir_of_Isildur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It was in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2952](../T/Third_Age_2952.md) when she returned from Lothlórien to Rivendell when she met a young foster-son of her father, [Aragorn](Aragorn.md). The young [Dúnadan](../D/Dúnadan.md) fell in love when he first saw her, though as he was still a youth, Arwen did not return his love.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup>

Elrond insisted that Arwen could not marry Aragorn until he became king of both [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](Arnor.md). Whether Arwen chose to marry him and become mortal, or remain with her kin as one of the Elves, she would be parted from either Elrond or Aragorn forever.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/66/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="246" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/66/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/6/66/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="839"/></a><figcaption>Arwen in Rivendell, art by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Abe_Papakhian.md" title="Category:Images by Abe Papakhian">Abe Papakhian</a></figcaption></figure>

It was not until they met many years later in Lórien that Arwen returned Aragorn's love, and in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2980](../T/Third_Age_2980.md) they plighted their troth on [Cerin Amroth](../C/Cerin_Amroth.md). Aragorn gave her the [Ring of Barahir](../R/Ring_of_Barahir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the years leading up to the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and all lands eastward became dangerous, and at the request of her father, Arwen returned to Imladris in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3009](../T/Third_Age_3009.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup>

Arwen was later reunited with Aragorn when he brought the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). She was present at a feast attended by [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md). Later that night, she and Aragorn spoke together.<sup id="cite_ref-Many_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Many-3">[3]</a></sup> When the Fellowship departed and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) offered them [her gifts](../G/Gifts_of_Galadriel.md), Arwen's Elfstone, given from Galadriel to Celebrían, and Celebrían to Arwen, was the gift for Aragorn. He would wear the Elfstone ever after.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-8">[8]</a></sup> This giving held the function of a wedding gift from the family of the bride to the groom, foretelling his marriage to Arwen.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Arwen wove the [standard of Elendil](../S/Standard_of_Elendil.md) for Aragorn from black cloth, decorating it with [mithril](../M/Mithril.md), gems and gold. She sent the banner with her brothers and a group of [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) to Aragorn before he took the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md), thereby inspiring him to take the difficult path.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The banner was unfurled at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md) to triumphantly announce the King's return.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

After the defeat of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), Aragorn was crowned as [King of the West](../K/King_of_the_West.md) and the new [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md); [on that day](../_/1_May.md) Arwen and her father departed for [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). On [20 May](../_/20_May.md) they reached Lothlórien where they stayed for [a week](../_/27_May.md) before Arwen resumed with her escort. On [14 June](../_/14_June.md) she met her brothers and together they went to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) where they stayed [two days](../_/16_June.md) before continuing to Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Queen {#Queen}

Arwen arrived to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) on [1 Lithe](../_/1_Lithe.md) and wed Aragorn on [Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-12">[12]</a></sup> being the third [union of Elves and Men](../U/Unions_of_Elves_and_Men.md). Arwen chose a fate different from her father's and did not sail to the [West](Aman.md). Instead, she gifted Frodo, as the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearers.md), passage in her stead, as she foresaw that his burden would not be lifted. She also gifted him a necklace with a white gem.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

As Queen of Gondor, Arwen maintained a close connection with Gondor's allies, as did her husband. In [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [15](../F/Fourth_Age_15.md) they visited the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) outside [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) and she took as a maid of honour [Elanor Gardner](../E/Elanor_Gardner.md), daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> She was mother of one son, [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md), and at least two unnamed daughters. After Aragorn's death, Arwen departed from Minas Tirith and returned to the abandoned Lothlórien. There she stayed for a time until she gave up her life in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [121](../F/Fourth_Age_121.md) at [Cerin Amroth](../C/Cerin_Amroth.md), and was buried there.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

<i><b>Arwen</b></i> means "Noble Maiden" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), from *[ara](Ara.md)-* ("noble") and *[gwenn](../G/Gwenn.md)* ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Tolkien remarked that it also means "greatly blessed" in [Welsh](../W/Welsh.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) form of her name is not entirely certain, but in his Quenya greeting, Aragorn refers to her again as "Arwen" (*Arwen vanimelda, namárië!*).<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-8">[8]</a></sup> This suggests that the form *Arwen* itself is also coincidentally a valid, or at least understandable, Quenya calque (using *[ar](Ar.md)-*, stem *Arwend-*).

## Other names {#Other_names}

Her [epessë](../E/Epessë.md), <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="../U/Undómiel.md" title="Undómiel">Undómiel</a></b></i>, means "Evenstar", from *[Undómë](../U/Undómë.md)* ("evening twilight") and *[el](../E/Elen.md)* ("star").

She is also given the names *Elrenniel* and *Elerondiel*, both meaning "Daughter of Elrond" in Sindarin and Quenya respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

Arwen was a very distant cousin of Aragorn. By their marriage, the long-sundered lines of the [Half-elven](../H/Half-elven.md) were joined.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-18">[18]</a></sup> Their union also served to unite and preserve the bloodlines of the kings of the three kindreds of the High Elves as well as the only line with [Maia](../M/Maia.md) blood through Arwen's great-great grandmother, [Melian](../M/Melian.md).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

While writing the drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* the character was named *[Finduilas](../F/Finduilas.md)*; only during the final proofreading in [1949](../_/1949.md) Tolkien changed the name to *Arwen*.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

Tolkien considered to postpone the birth of Arwen from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [241](../T/Third_Age_241.md) to [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_341&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 341 (page does not exist)">341</a> , or even in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_421&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 421 (page does not exist)">421</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> to increase the interval between the birth of the twins and the birth of Arwen where the parents were at rest, and he assigned the error in dates to a "probable scribal origin" (with the inversion of numbers 241/421).

<b>1978: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen does not appear in this film.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen is voiced by <a href="../S/Sonia_Fraser.md" title="Sonia Fraser">Sonia Fraser</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen appears in Rivendell after the <a href="../C/Council_of_Elrond.md" title="Council of Elrond">Council of Elrond</a>. She has a brief dialogue with Aragorn, and recites several lines from the <a href="../R/Riddle_of_Strider.md" title="Riddle of Strider">Riddle of Strider</a>. No voice actress is specified, but it is likely <a href="../K/Kath_Soucie.md" title="Kath Soucie">Kath Soucie</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen is played by <a href="../L/Liv_Tyler.md" title="Liv Tyler">Liv Tyler</a>. Various additional scenes pertaining to Arwen are inserted, some of which deviate from the books and some of which seem to be inspired by <i><a href="../T/The_Tale_of_Aragorn_and_Arwen.md" title="The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen">The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen</a></i>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the first film, she rescues <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a> from the <a href="../N/Nazgûl.md" title="Nazgûl">Black Riders</a> at <a href="../B/Bruinen.md" title="Bruinen">Bruinen</a>, thwarting them with a sudden flood, summoned by an incantation. In the book, it was <a href="../G/Glorfindel.md" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a> who put Frodo on horseback and sent him alone to flee the Black Riders, and Elrond and <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> arranged the flood. Also, in the book, Frodo defends himself against the Black Riders, whereas in the movie Arwen defends him.  Arwen wields the sword <a href="../H/Hadhafang.md" title="Hadhafang">Hadhafang</a>,a non-canonical sword that belonged to her father.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>There is a scene in which Aragorn has a dream about Arwen in which they kiss, a scene where Arwen has an argument with her father about leaving for <a href="../V/Valinor.md" title="Valinor">Valinor</a>, and a scene where she actually departs for Valinor and then changes her mind and returns when she sees a vision of her future son, Eldarion.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In addition, towards the end of the cinematic trilogy she apparently becomes sick, seemingly as a result of her forsaking her immortality. Elrond takes the reforged <a href="../N/Narsil.md" title="Narsil">Narsil</a>, now <a href="Andúril.md" title="Andúril">Andúril</a>, to Aragorn at Dunharrow, and tells him that her fate has become bound with <a href="../T/The_One_Ring.md" title="The One Ring">the One Ring</a>, and that she is dying. However, no explanation is ever given for these statements. It is possible that Elrond&#39;s comments of her dying is simply a metaphor of her becoming mortal, and that he is not implying she has a connection with the Ring, but that Arwen&#39;s decision to stay means that her future, like the other free peoples of Middle-earth, now rests on the destruction of the ring. Later, after the Ring is destroyed, Arwen is present at Aragorn&#39;s coronation without any signs of illness.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Arwen is provided by Slávka Halčáková. She only appears in person in the third series (season) of the adaptation, during the events of <i>The Return of the King</i>, but is alluded to earlier.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen can be found in a gazebo overlooking the path from <a href="../R/Rivendell.md" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a> into the Misty Mountains. Her roles in the game was initially small, and her only significant interaction with the player was the passing of Elessar&#39;s banner that she made for <a href="Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and bid the player to deliver to <a href="../H/Halbarad.md" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a>. After the downfall of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, her prominence increases as <a href="../E/Elrond.md" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> and his household travel to <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> for her wedding. On their way towards <a href="../L/Lothlórien.md" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a> from the lands of the <a href="../B/Beornings.md" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>, Arwen asks her father to visit the cave in which her mother <a class="mw-redirect" href="../C/Celebrian.md" title="Celebrian">Celebrian</a> was tormented by <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">orcs</a> before being rescued. She later discusses that experience with Lady <a href="../G/Galadriel.md" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen resides in Rivendell, where players can discuss with her both historical and present subjects. While sporting the appearance of <a href="../L/Liv_Tyler.md" title="Liv Tyler">Liv Tyler</a>, her role is closer to the book than that in the movie: for example, she provides Halbarad and the Grey Company with the banner she had made for Aragorn, an event omitted from the movie adaptation. She is voiced by <a href="../C/Courtenay_Taylor.md" title="Courtenay Taylor">Courtenay Taylor</a>.</dd></dl>
