**Arwen Undómiel**, often called **Arwen Evenstar**, was one of the [half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven "Half-elven"), married to [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn"). She was the daughter of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

She was considered to be the fairest of the [Children of Ilúvatar](/wiki/Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), resembling [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) who would never again appear in [Ëa](/wiki/%C3%8Ba). Her romance with [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was reminiscent of that between the Man [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and the Elf Lúthien. Like Lúthien, she rejected her [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") immortality to marry Aragorn and die with him.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg/300px-Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="412" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/8c/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg/450px-Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/8/8c/Anna_Lee_-_Evenstar.jpg 2x" data-file-width="567" data-file-height="778"/></a><figcaption><i>Evenstar</i> by <a href="/wiki/Anna_Lee" title="Anna Lee">Anna Lee</a></figcaption></figure>

Arwen was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [241](/wiki/Third_Age_241 "Third Age 241") and was the younger sister of [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup> From her mother she inherited the [Elfstone](/wiki/Elfstone).<sup id="cite_ref-CG_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-6">[6]</a></sup> As one of the half-elven, she shared the right of her father to choose her fate. She lived most of her life in [Imladris](/wiki/Imladris), or in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) with her grandparents.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

Her father fostered the sons of the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain), who were the exiled [Heirs of Isildur](/wiki/Heir_of_Isildur "Heir of Isildur").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It was in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2952](/wiki/Third_Age_2952 "Third Age 2952") when she returned from Lothlórien to Rivendell when she met a young foster-son of her father, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). The young [Dúnadan](/wiki/D%C3%BAnadan) fell in love when he first saw her, though as he was still a youth, Arwen did not return his love.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup>

Elrond insisted that Arwen could not marry Aragorn until he became king of both [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor). Whether Arwen chose to marry him and become mortal, or remain with her kin as one of the Elves, she would be parted from either Elrond or Aragorn forever.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/66/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/300px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="246" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/66/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/450px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/66/Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg/600px-Abe_Papakhian_-_Lord_of_Rivendell.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="839"/></a><figcaption>Arwen in Rivendell, art by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Abe_Papakhian" title="Category:Images by Abe Papakhian">Abe Papakhian</a></figcaption></figure>

It was not until they met many years later in Lórien that Arwen returned Aragorn's love, and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") they plighted their troth on [Cerin Amroth](/wiki/Cerin_Amroth). Aragorn gave her the [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the years leading up to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and all lands eastward became dangerous, and at the request of her father, Arwen returned to Imladris in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3009](/wiki/Third_Age_3009 "Third Age 3009").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-5">[5]</a></sup>

Arwen was later reunited with Aragorn when he brought the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). She was present at a feast attended by [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"). Later that night, she and Aragorn spoke together.<sup id="cite_ref-Many_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Many-3">[3]</a></sup> When the Fellowship departed and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) offered them [her gifts](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel"), Arwen's Elfstone, given from Galadriel to Celebrían, and Celebrían to Arwen, was the gift for Aragorn. He would wear the Elfstone ever after.<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-8">[8]</a></sup> This giving held the function of a wedding gift from the family of the bride to the groom, foretelling his marriage to Arwen.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Arwen wove the [standard of Elendil](/wiki/Standard_of_Elendil "Standard of Elendil") for Aragorn from black cloth, decorating it with [mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril"), gems and gold. She sent the banner with her brothers and a group of [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North) to Aragorn before he took the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead), thereby inspiring him to take the difficult path.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The banner was unfurled at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) to triumphantly announce the King's return.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

After the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), Aragorn was crowned as [King of the West](/wiki/King_of_the_West) and the new [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom); [on that day](/wiki/1_May "1 May") Arwen and her father departed for [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). On [20 May](/wiki/20_May) they reached Lothlórien where they stayed for [a week](/wiki/27_May "27 May") before Arwen resumed with her escort. On [14 June](/wiki/14_June) she met her brothers and together they went to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) where they stayed [two days](/wiki/16_June "16 June") before continuing to Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-12">[12]</a></sup>

### Queen {#Queen}

Arwen arrived to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on [1 Lithe](/wiki/1_Lithe) and wed Aragorn on [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-12">[12]</a></sup> being the third [union of Elves and Men](/wiki/Unions_of_Elves_and_Men "Unions of Elves and Men"). Arwen chose a fate different from her father's and did not sail to the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"). Instead, she gifted Frodo, as the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearers "Ring-bearers"), passage in her stead, as she foresaw that his burden would not be lifted. She also gifted him a necklace with a white gem.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

As Queen of Gondor, Arwen maintained a close connection with Gondor's allies, as did her husband. In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15") they visited the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) outside [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and she took as a maid of honour [Elanor Gardner](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner), daughter of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> She was mother of one son, [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion), and at least two unnamed daughters. After Aragorn's death, Arwen departed from Minas Tirith and returned to the abandoned Lothlórien. There she stayed for a time until she gave up her life in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [121](/wiki/Fourth_Age_121 "Fourth Age 121") at [Cerin Amroth](/wiki/Cerin_Amroth), and was buried there.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-4">[4]</a></sup>

<i><b>Arwen</b></i> means "Noble Maiden" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")-* ("noble") and *[gwenn](/wiki/Gwenn "Gwenn")* ("maiden").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Tolkien remarked that it also means "greatly blessed" in [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form of her name is not entirely certain, but in his Quenya greeting, Aragorn refers to her again as "Arwen" (*Arwen vanimelda, namárië!*).<sup id="cite_ref-Farewell_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Farewell-8">[8]</a></sup> This suggests that the form *Arwen* itself is also coincidentally a valid, or at least understandable, Quenya calque (using *[ar](/wiki/Ar "Ar")-*, stem *Arwend-*).

## Other names {#Other_names}

Her [epessë](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë"), <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Und%C3%B3miel" title="Undómiel">Undómiel</a></b></i>, means "Evenstar", from *[Undómë](/wiki/Und%C3%B3m%C3%AB)* ("evening twilight") and *[el](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* ("star").

She is also given the names *Elrenniel* and *Elerondiel*, both meaning "Daughter of Elrond" in Sindarin and Quenya respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

Arwen was a very distant cousin of Aragorn. By their marriage, the long-sundered lines of the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven) were joined.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-18">[18]</a></sup> Their union also served to unite and preserve the bloodlines of the kings of the three kindreds of the High Elves as well as the only line with [Maia](/wiki/Maia) blood through Arwen's great-great grandmother, [Melian](/wiki/Melian).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

While writing the drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* the character was named *[Finduilas](/wiki/Finduilas)*; only during the final proofreading in [1949](/wiki/1949) Tolkien changed the name to *Arwen*.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

Tolkien considered to postpone the birth of Arwen from [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [241](/wiki/Third_Age_241 "Third Age 241") to [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_341&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 341 (page does not exist)">341</a> , or even in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_421&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 421 (page does not exist)">421</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> to increase the interval between the birth of the twins and the birth of Arwen where the parents were at rest, and he assigned the error in dates to a "probable scribal origin" (with the inversion of numbers 241/421).

No voice actress is specified, but it is likely <a href="/wiki/Kath_Soucie" title="Kath Soucie">Kath Soucie</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2001-03: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen is played by <a href="/wiki/Liv_Tyler" title="Liv Tyler">Liv Tyler</a>. Various additional scenes pertaining to Arwen are inserted, some of which deviate from the books and some of which seem to be inspired by <i><a href="/wiki/The_Tale_of_Aragorn_and_Arwen" title="The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen">The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen</a></i>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the first film, she rescues <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Black Riders</a> at <a href="/wiki/Bruinen" title="Bruinen">Bruinen</a>, thwarting them with a sudden flood, summoned by an incantation. In the book, it was <a href="/wiki/Glorfindel" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a> who put Frodo on horseback and sent him alone to flee the Black Riders, and Elrond and <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> arranged the flood. Also, in the book, Frodo defends himself against the Black Riders, whereas in the movie Arwen defends him.  Arwen wields the sword <a href="/wiki/Hadhafang" title="Hadhafang">Hadhafang</a>,a non-canonical sword that belonged to her father.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>There is a scene in which Aragorn has a dream about Arwen in which they kiss, a scene where Arwen has an argument with her father about leaving for <a href="/wiki/Valinor" title="Valinor">Valinor</a>, and a scene where she actually departs for Valinor and then changes her mind and returns when she sees a vision of her future son, Eldarion.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In addition, towards the end of the cinematic trilogy she apparently becomes sick, seemingly as a result of her forsaking her immortality. Elrond takes the reforged <a href="/wiki/Narsil" title="Narsil">Narsil</a>, now <a href="/wiki/And%C3%BAril" title="Andúril">Andúril</a>, to Aragorn at Dunharrow, and tells him that her fate has become bound with <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring" title="The One Ring">the One Ring</a>, and that she is dying. However, no explanation is ever given for these statements. It is possible that Elrond&#39;s comments of her dying is simply a metaphor of her becoming mortal, and that he is not implying she has a connection with the Ring, but that Arwen&#39;s decision to stay means that her future, like the other free peoples of Middle-earth, now rests on the destruction of the ring. Later, after the Ring is destroyed, Arwen is present at Aragorn&#39;s coronation without any signs of illness.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Arwen is provided by Slávka Halčáková. She only appears in person in the third series (season) of the adaptation, during the events of <i>The Return of the King</i>, but is alluded to earlier.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen can be found in a gazebo overlooking the path from <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a> into the Misty Mountains. Her roles in the game was initially small, and her only significant interaction with the player was the passing of Elessar&#39;s banner that she made for <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> and bid the player to deliver to <a href="/wiki/Halbarad" title="Halbarad">Halbarad</a>. After the downfall of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>, her prominence increases as <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> and his household travel to <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> for her wedding. On their way towards <a href="/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien" title="Lothlórien">Lothlórien</a> from the lands of the <a href="/wiki/Beornings" title="Beornings">Beornings</a>, Arwen asks her father to visit the cave in which her mother <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Celebrian" title="Celebrian">Celebrian</a> was tormented by <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">orcs</a> before being rescued. She later discusses that experience with Lady <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Arwen resides in Rivendell, where players can discuss with her both historical and present subjects. While sporting the appearance of <a href="/wiki/Liv_Tyler" title="Liv Tyler">Liv Tyler</a>, her role is closer to the book than that in the movie: for example, she provides Halbarad and the Grey Company with the banner she had made for Aragorn, an event omitted from the movie adaptation. She is voiced by <a href="/wiki/Courtenay_Taylor" title="Courtenay Taylor">Courtenay Taylor</a>.</dd></dl>
