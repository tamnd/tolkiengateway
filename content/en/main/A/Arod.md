**Arod** was a light horse of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). He bore a Knight of Rohan in [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)'s [éored](../_/Éored.md) that engaged the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) outside [Fangorn](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md). His rider was lost during the skirmish, but Arod himself survived and remained with the rest of the éored on their way back. In [the Wold](../T/The_Wold.md), Éomer loaned him to [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Arod served his new rider faithfully, and for much of this time, Arod carried also Legolas's friend [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md). At the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) Legolas spoke [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) to calm the beast so that he would enter the [Dark Door](../D/Dark_Door.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Presumably he bore Legolas on his way throughout the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-3">[3]</a></sup> Legolas and Gimli rode upon Arod with the rest of their company to take [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md)'s body from the [Hallows](../H/Hallows.md) in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md), to rest in [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

According to both versions of the unpublished Epilogue, Sam told his son Merry that Legolas let Arod run free when he and Gimli entered Fangorn.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) (which represents [Rohanese](../R/Rohanese.md)), the name means simply "quick, swift".<sup id="cite_ref-guide_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-3">[3]</a></sup>

By coincidence, there is also a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *arod*, meaning "noble".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
