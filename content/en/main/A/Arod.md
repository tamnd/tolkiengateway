**Arod** was a light horse of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). He bore a Knight of Rohan in [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s [éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored "Éored") that engaged the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) outside [Fangorn](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest "Fangorn Forest"). His rider was lost during the skirmish, but Arod himself survived and remained with the rest of the éored on their way back. In [the Wold](/wiki/The_Wold "The Wold"), Éomer loaned him to [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Arod served his new rider faithfully, and for much of this time, Arod carried also Legolas's friend [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli). At the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) Legolas spoke [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) to calm the beast so that he would enter the [Dark Door](/wiki/Dark_Door).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Presumably he bore Legolas on his way throughout the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-3">[3]</a></sup> Legolas and Gimli rode upon Arod with the rest of their company to take [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)'s body from the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), to rest in [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

According to both versions of the unpublished Epilogue, Sam told his son Merry that Legolas let Arod run free when he and Gimli entered Fangorn.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) (which represents [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese)), the name means simply "quick, swift".<sup id="cite_ref-guide_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-3">[3]</a></sup>

By coincidence, there is also a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *arod*, meaning "noble".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
