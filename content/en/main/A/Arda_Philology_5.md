<i><b>Arda Philology 5</b></i> is the fifth volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)")*, and contains the proceedings of the Fifth International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/olempea.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Lempea</a> (<a href="/wiki/Omentielva" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Helsinki in August [2013](/wiki/2013).
