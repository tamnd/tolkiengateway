**Andy Serkis** is an English actor. He portrayed [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) in [*The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), as well as providing voices for various characters in *The Lord of the Rings* (film series). He was the second unit director for *The Hobbit* (film series). In [2020](/wiki/2020), he narrated *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* audiobook,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and followed this up with an audiobook narration of all three volumes of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* in [2021](/wiki/2021), and *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* in [2023](/wiki/2023).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Since 2002, he has been married to actress [Lorraine Ashbourne](/wiki/Lorraine_Ashbourne), and together they have three children.

## Gollum {#Gollum}

When he heard from his agent that <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0399193/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">John Hubbard</a> wanted to hear him as the voice of an animated character, Serkis originally felt dismayed. When told it was Gollum, it changed little: he had read *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* when he was eight, but nothing else. Not until his (later) wife Lorraine convinced him to go to the audition did he actually think of doing it.

He recorded a demo, in which he portrayed Gollum full of self-pity and guilt, and he based the gulping swallow sound "gollum" on his cat Diz coughing up a furball.

At the advice of [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh), he kept a diary, and this diary was later edited to become *[Gollum: How We Made Movie Magic](/wiki/Gollum:_How_We_Made_Movie_Magic)*. It details his casting, the shooting, the additional motion capture, and the voice dubbing pick-ups.

Serkis spent a total of five years in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand), and did extra work later in London. He dubbed most of his lines as Gollum there, and also provided an eerie voice for the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar). Together with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jim_Dunk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jim Dunk (page does not exist)">Jim Dunk</a>, he also voiced most of the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) in [Uglúk](/wiki/Ugl%C3%BAk)'s troop.
