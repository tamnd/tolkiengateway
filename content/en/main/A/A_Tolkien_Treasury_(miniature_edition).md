<i><b>A Tolkien Treasury</b></i> is a miniature book which contains numerous quotes from and about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), interspersed with color illustrations by [Tim Kirk](/wiki/Tim_Kirk) and black and white illustrations by [Michael Green](/wiki/Michael_Green).

- Note: The texts and pictures included in this edition are parts of the content first published in *[The Tolkien Scrapbook](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Scrapbook)*.
