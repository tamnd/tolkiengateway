An **ambalotsë** was a crested helm, especially one worn by an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")-lord in battle. The crest was typically of a flower or flowers.

*Ambalotsë* means "Uprising Flower" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), from *[amba](/wiki/Amba "Amba")* ("upwards") + *[lótë](/wiki/L%C3%B3t%C3%AB "Lótë")* ("flower"). This style of helm was called *[amloth](/wiki/Amloth "Amloth")* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
