<i><b>An Evening in Rivendell</b></i> is the debut album by the [Tolkien Ensemble](../T/Tolkien_Ensemble.md), released on [September 30](../_/30_September.md), [1997](../_/1997.md). It features songs composed to the lyrics found in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and forms the first part of what was to become a complete musical interpretation of all lyrics in the book (a project given approval by both the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) and [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md)).

The album features cover art by [Margrethe II of Denmark](../M/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark.md).
