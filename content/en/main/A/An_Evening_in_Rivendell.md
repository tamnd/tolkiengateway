<i><b>An Evening in Rivendell</b></i> is the debut album by the [Tolkien Ensemble](/wiki/Tolkien_Ensemble), released on [September 30](/wiki/30_September "30 September"), [1997](/wiki/1997). It features songs composed to the lyrics found in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and forms the first part of what was to become a complete musical interpretation of all lyrics in the book (a project given approval by both the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate) and [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins)).

The album features cover art by [Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark).
