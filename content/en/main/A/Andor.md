**Andor** or **Yôzâyan** in [Adûnaic](Adûnaic.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a name for the island of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md) or [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), meaning 'The Land of Gift'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and contains the elements *[anna](Anna.md)* "gift" + *[dor](../D/Dor.md)* "land".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Similarly, the native name Yôzâyan seems to contain the Adûnaic word *zâyan* "land".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
