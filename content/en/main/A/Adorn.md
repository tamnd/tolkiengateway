The **Adorn** was one of the rivers which arose in the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) flowing northwest, and joined with the River Isen about 150 miles west of the [Gap of Rohan](../G/Gap_of_Rohan.md) at the borders of [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Together with the [Isen](../I/Isen.md), the Adorn formed the western border of the Kingdom of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [triangle of land](../W/West-march.md) between the Isen, Adorn, and the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) was nominally part of the Kingdom of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but in the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) it was a contested area between the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md). Lord [Freca](../F/Freca.md) held lands at either side of the river around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Adorn* is said to be a of pre-[Númenórean](../N/Númenórean.md) origin, in a form suitable to the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) language.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
