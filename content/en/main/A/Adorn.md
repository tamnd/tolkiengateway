The **Adorn** was one of the rivers which arose in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) flowing northwest, and joined with the River Isen about 150 miles west of the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan) at the borders of [Enedwaith](/wiki/Enedwaith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Together with the [Isen](/wiki/Isen), the Adorn formed the western border of the Kingdom of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [triangle of land](/wiki/West-march "West-march") between the Isen, Adorn, and the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) was nominally part of the Kingdom of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) it was a contested area between the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) and the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings). Lord [Freca](/wiki/Freca) held lands at either side of the river around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Adorn* is said to be a of pre-[Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) origin, in a form suitable to the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) language.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> <span></span>
