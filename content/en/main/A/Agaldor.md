**Agaldor** was the chieftain of a people living in the north-west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Agaldor was a long-lived [man](/wiki/Men "Men") living among the exiled people in Middle-earth. He was the chief of a people on the edge of the [Western Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and a [twilight-walker](/wiki/Seer "Seer") who often wandered along the beaches and coastlines. One evening upon a rocky shore, He witnessed great clouds that looked similarly to the "*eagles of the Lord of the West*". These clouds filled him with [foreboding](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight"). Immediately afterwards, Agaldor left the shore to walk along the beach and behind the land-wall to some houses that have lights inside. The [Men](/wiki/Men) sitting outside waited until he was out of earshot to speak of him. Agaldor [foresaw](/wiki/Foresight "Foresight") among other things, the arrival of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) and the redemption of mankind ahead. Agaldor was later killed by the Númenóreans when they arrived as rebels of the Gods. They took the chieftainship of Agaldor's lands.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Agaldor* appears to be a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name, though it’s meaning is unclear. *Galdor* is only glossed in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* as a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name from *Gala* ("thrive").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The name *Amroth* appears to also be a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) name possibly comprised of the element *[am](/wiki/Am "Am")* ("up").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In an alternative version, Agaldor was referred to as **Amroth** as he wrestled with [Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB).
