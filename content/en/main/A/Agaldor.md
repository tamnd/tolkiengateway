**Agaldor** was the chieftain of a people living in the north-west of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Agaldor was a long-lived [man](../M/Men.md) living among the exiled people in Middle-earth. He was the chief of a people on the edge of the [Western Sea](../B/Belegaer.md) and a [twilight-walker](../S/Seer.md) who often wandered along the beaches and coastlines. One evening upon a rocky shore, He witnessed great clouds that looked similarly to the "*eagles of the Lord of the West*". These clouds filled him with [foreboding](../F/Foresight.md). Immediately afterwards, Agaldor left the shore to walk along the beach and behind the land-wall to some houses that have lights inside. The [Men](../M/Men.md) sitting outside waited until he was out of earshot to speak of him. Agaldor [foresaw](../F/Foresight.md) among other things, the arrival of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) and the redemption of mankind ahead. Agaldor was later killed by the Númenóreans when they arrived as rebels of the Gods. They took the chieftainship of Agaldor's lands.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Agaldor* appears to be a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name, though it’s meaning is unclear. *Galdor* is only glossed in *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* as a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name from *Gala* ("thrive").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The name *Amroth* appears to also be a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) name possibly comprised of the element *[am](Am.md)* ("up").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In an alternative version, Agaldor was referred to as **Amroth** as he wrestled with [Thû](../T/Thû.md).
