**Adam Reuel Tolkien** (born [13 March](/wiki/13_March) [1969](/wiki/1969)) is the son of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), brother to [Rachel Tolkien](/wiki/Rachel_Tolkien) and half brother to [Simon Tolkien](/wiki/Simon_Tolkien). He is a lighting engineer by profession, but has also translated into French several works of his grandfather: *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)* (1994), *[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)* (1995), and *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)* (1998). In the preface to *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* he is also credited as having helped his father edit his grandfather's notes. On the <a class="external text" href="https://www.tolkienestate.com/en/paths/credits.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien Estate website</a>, he is also credited: 'The original idea of the website, its structure, its organization, and its content was conceived by Adam Tolkien. He coordinated the project...' In November 2019 he delivered <a class="external text" href="https://www.bnf.fr/fr/mediatheque/carte-blanche-adam-tolkien" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a conference</a> in relation to the exhibition 'Tolkien : Voyage en Terre du Milieu' at the Bibliotheque nationale de France.
