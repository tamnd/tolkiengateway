**Arien** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈari.en]</span>) was the [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") who guided the [Sun](/wiki/Sun).

## History {#History}

Arien was originally a spirit of fire whom [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") failed to corrupt.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-1">[1]</a></sup> In the days of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees), Arien was a [Maia](/wiki/Maia) maiden who tended to the golden flowers in the gardens of [Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na). She would water the flowers of Vána with dew gathered from the golden tree [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>, which didn’t hurt her due to her original nature as a spirit of fire.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the destruction of the Two Trees of Valinor by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"), [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) managed to produce one last golden fruit before it died away. [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) crafted a vessel to hold the golden fruit. Arien was chosen by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) to guide the vessel of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) above the airs of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) to give light to the World; [Tilion](/wiki/Tilion), another Maia and one of Oromë's hunters, was chosen to steer the [Moon](/wiki/Moon). The Moon had already traversed the sky seven times before the Sun was finally ready. The first dawn of the Sun was like a great fire glowing upon the towers of the [Pelóri Mountains](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri_Mountains) of [Aman](/wiki/Aman). [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) was dismayed, and he descended into the uttermost pit of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) and his servants withdrew. He sent up dark clouds of smoke to conceal his land from the light of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Tilion](/wiki/Tilion) loved Arien and followed her through the night sky. When he occasionally caught up to her, his vessel became singed by her heat.<sup id="cite_ref-S11_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S11-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arien* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Maiden of Sun".<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-3">[3]</a></sup> It is derived from the root *as-*, seen also in *[árë](/wiki/%C3%81re "Áre")* ("sunlight"),<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-3">[3]</a></sup> and *-[ien](/wiki/Ien "Ien")* (feminine suffix).

The [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name *[Ûrî](/wiki/%C3%9Br%C3%AE)* refers to "The Lady of the Sun".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In the earliest writings, Arien's name was <i><b>Urwen</b></i> or <i><b>Urwendi</b></i>, which translated in "Maiden of the Sunship". She was one of Vána's maidens who took care of the tree Laurelin; her task was to water it.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor) were destroyed, she offered to steer the Ship of the Sun.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1Sun_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1Sun-6">[6]</a></sup>

It was told that Urwendi and her maidens, before the rising of the Sun, entered into [Fôs Almir](/wiki/F%C3%B4s%27_Almir "Fôs' Almir") (Faskalan), a bath of flame that would make one pure.<sup id="cite_ref-LT1Sun_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1Sun-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Later versions {#Later_versions}

In *[Myths Transformed](/wiki/Myths_Transformed)*, which changed the timing of the Sun to have been primordial to Arda's inception, [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) wanted to claim Arien as a wife,<sup id="cite_ref-Myths_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Myths-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:380–1</sup> but when she refused him, he attempted to ravish her.<sup id="cite_ref-Myths_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Myths-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:405</sup> However, she released her spirit and burned Melkor leaving him ashen and grey. The Sun was then left without a guide and caused uneven seasons and periods of excessive heat or cold in [Arda](/wiki/Arda).<sup id="cite_ref-Myths_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Myths-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:381</sup> In an alternative version, Melkor assaulted her and tainted the light of the Sun, which caused Arien's (*Arie* in this version) spirit to flee in anguish and wrath from the world. In other abandoned writings, she was a Maia of [Varda](/wiki/Varda).<sup id="cite_ref-Myths_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Myths-7">[7]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:380</sup>
