**Aldúya** was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of the fourth day of the [Eldarin](/wiki/Eldar "Eldar") six-day week, its name referring to the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor "Two Trees of Valinor"). It was called **Orgaladhad** in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the [Númenorean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") calendar, Aldúya was replaced by [Aldëa](/wiki/Ald%C3%ABa) in honour of the [White Tree](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Nimloth of Númenor").<sup id="cite_ref-App_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App-1">[1]</a></sup>
