**Arwen's Vision** is the seventh scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the ninth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)").

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) travels with a company of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens). She has a vision of a child clothed in white running across her path. She watches as the boy runs with his arms outstretched to his father, an aged [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), who picks him up with joy and kisses him. As the boy looks at her and she sees he is wearing the [Evenstar](/wiki/Elfstone "Elfstone"), she knows the boy to be [her child](/wiki/Eldarion "Eldarion"). Tears fill her eyes as she remembers the words of [her father](/wiki/Elrond "Elrond"): *"There is nothing for you here, only death"*.

She opens her eyes and finds the vision has disappeared. [An elf](/wiki/Figwit "Figwit") tells her they must not delay, however she makes the decision to return to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell). Arwen asks Elrond what his gift of foresight showed him regarding her future. He tells her he saw death, but Arwen tells him that he saw her son. *"That future is almost gone. Nothing is certain,"* Elrond says, looking careworn. Arwen says to him *"Some things are certain. If I leave [him](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") now, I will regret it forever."*
