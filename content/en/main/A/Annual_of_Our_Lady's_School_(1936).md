The <b><i>Annual</i> of Our Lady&#39;s School (1936)</b> is an Annual published by Our Lady's School, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abingdon-on-Thames" title="wikipedia:Abingdon-on-Thames">Abingdon</a> (near Oxford), in [1936](/wiki/1936). It includes the poems *[The Shadow Man](/wiki/Shadow-Bride "Shadow-Bride")* (p. 9) and *[Noel](/wiki/Noel)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
