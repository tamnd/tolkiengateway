**Ar-Sakalthôr** was the 22nd [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor). As had now become the custom in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), he took his royal name in the [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) tongue. His name in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) was **Tar-Falassion**. Ar-Sakalthôr ruled [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) for sixty-nine years.<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Sakalthor_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Sakalthor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Sakalthôr* is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Shore-son" and is a compound of *sakal* ("shore") and the suffix *-thôr* ("-son").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The meaning of the Adûnaic prefix *Ar-* is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "king" and is a prefixal form of *ârû* ("king") or derived from the same root.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

*Falassion* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Shore-son" and is a compound of *falassë* ("shore") and the patronymic suffix [-ion](/wiki/-ion) ("-son").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Like for all the rulers of Númenor whose name was inscribed in the Scroll of Kings, the prefix *tar-* ("high") was added to his name.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
