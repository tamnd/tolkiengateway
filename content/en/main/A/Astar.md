**Astar** (sg. *asta*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for the *months* of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) calendars.

Typically, an *asta* had 30 days, with the exception of the [two](/wiki/N%C3%A1ri%C3%AB "Nárië") [medial](/wiki/Cermie "Cermie") *astar* in the [Kings' Reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning), that had 31 days each.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*asta* derives from the [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") SAT.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
