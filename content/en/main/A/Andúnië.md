**Andúnië** was an important city and port in the realm of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

It was located on the [Bay of Andúnië](../B/Bay_of_Andúnië.md) in the [Andustar](Andustar.md) region, on the western end of [Elenna](../E/Elenna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Part of the town lay beside the shore but many dwellings were set upon the steep slopes behind.

The main road of Númenor, which ran from [Rómenna](../R/Rómenna.md) westward through [Armenelos](Armenelos.md), continued on to Andúnië.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Andúnië was initially the largest city of Númenor because the [Elves](../F/Falmari.md) of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) would often visit the haven.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [630](../S/Second_Age_630.md), [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md) created the title [Lord of Andúnië](../L/Lord_of_Andúnië.md) in honor of his first grandson, [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(Lord_of_Andúnië).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> A distaff branch of the line of [Elros](../E/Elros.md), the [Lords of Andúnië](../../category/L/Lords_of_Andúnië.md) were important nobles and members of the [Council of the Sceptre](../C/Council_of_the_Sceptre.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Since Andúnië was an Elf-haven, the Lords of Andúnië were also the leaders of the "[Faithful](../F/Faithful.md)" — those who advocated continued friendship with the Elves and obedience to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).

However, as the [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md) was falling upon Númenor, Armenelos became larger and more important than Andúnië. By the close of the Second Age, the Faithful were labeled as traitors by the [King's Men](../K/King's_Men.md) and deported to Rómenna in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> Andúnië was ultimately destroyed in the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) in [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md).

[Amandil](Amandil.md), the last of the Lords of Andúnië, was the ancestor of the Kings of [Arnor](Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Andúnië* is a variant of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word <i><b><a href="Andúnë.md" title="Andúnë">andúnë</a></b></i>, meaning "sunset".
