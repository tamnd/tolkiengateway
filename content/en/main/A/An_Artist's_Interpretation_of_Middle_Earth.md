**An Artist's Interpretation of Middle Earth** is a 24" x 36" color map for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*, 1st Edition. Beyond Tolkien's [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), a large part of the map depicts newly invented areas that were featured in modules for the role-playing game.

Peter Fenlon also wrote a gazetteer based on the geographical names used on the map. The gazetteer was only published by [ICE](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises "Iron Crown Enterprises") on the internet and is now off-line.
