**Andrew Gunn**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> is a Kiwi actor from Wellington<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> who portrayed a [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> and a [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves")<sup id="cite_ref-Facebook_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Facebook-3">[3]</a></sup> in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, a [Ranger of Ithilien](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien"),<sup id="cite_ref-Facebook_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Facebook-3">[3]</a></sup> a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") Soldier,<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-4">[4]</a></sup> [Balglin](/wiki/Balglin),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> a [Rohir](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim")<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> and an [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai")<sup id="cite_ref-Facebook_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Facebook-3">[3]</a></sup> in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and a [Wandering Company](/wiki/Wandering_Companies "Wandering Companies") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup>
