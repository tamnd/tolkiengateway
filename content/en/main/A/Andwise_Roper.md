**Andwise "Andy" Roper** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Andwise was the son of [Hobson "Roper" Gamgee](/wiki/Hobson "Hobson"), an older brother of [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee"). Whereas his brother became a gardener, Andwise inherited the family <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ropewalk" title="wikipedia:ropewalk">rope-walk</a> in [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield), and continued the family trade.<sup id="cite_ref-taming_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-taming-1">[1]</a></sup> He left his ropewalk to his son [Anson](/wiki/Anson_Roper "Anson Roper") after his death, founding the [Roper family](/wiki/Roper_family).<sup id="cite_ref-Gamgee_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gamgee-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Sam_Gamgee), seeing how the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) used ropes to cross the [Silverlode](/wiki/Silverlode) in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), said that not even his "Uncle Andy" did those tricks.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later he mentioned him and Hobson as an example that to know about ropes and knots is "in the family".<sup id="cite_ref-taming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-taming-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name comes from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *andwīs* which means "expert, skilful".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
