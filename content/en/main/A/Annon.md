<i><b>annon</b></i> is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for 'great door' or 'gate'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [AD](AD.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Annon-in-Gelydh](Annon-in-Gelydh.md)*
- *[Forannest](../F/Forannest.md)* - The northernmost gate of [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md)
- *[Morannon](../M/Morannon.md)* - "Black Gate"
- *[Sirannon](../S/Sirannon.md)* - "Gate-stream"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[ando](Ando.md)*
