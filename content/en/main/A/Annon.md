<i><b>annon</b></i> is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for 'great door' or 'gate'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [AD](/wiki/AD).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Annon-in-Gelydh](/wiki/Annon-in-Gelydh)*
- *[Forannest](/wiki/Forannest)* - The northernmost gate of [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor)
- *[Morannon](/wiki/Morannon)* - "Black Gate"
- *[Sirannon](/wiki/Sirannon)* - "Gate-stream"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ando](/wiki/Ando "Ando")*
