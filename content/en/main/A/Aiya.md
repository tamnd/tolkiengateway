<i><b>aiya</b></i> (*aiya!*) or <i><b>aia</b></i> means "hail" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). In the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), it was only addressed to "great or holy persons", such as the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), or to [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil).<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L297_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-3">[3]</a></sup> In [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s Quenya translation of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hail_Mary" title="wikipedia:Hail Mary">Ave Maria</a></i> (*[Aia María](/wiki/Aia_Mar%C3%ADa)*), the [Latin](/wiki/Latin) word *ave* was first given the translated form *aiya*, later changed to the form *aia*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [AYA](/wiki/AYA)<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Aiya_E%C3%A4rendil_elenion_ancalima!" title="Aiya Eärendil elenion ancalima!"><b>Aiya</b> Eärendil elenion ancalima!</a></i> = "**Hail**, Eärendil, brightest of stars"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Ut%C3%BAlie%27n_aur%C3%AB!" title="Utúlie&#39;n aurë!"><b>Aiya</b> eldalië ar atanatári, utúlie&#39;n aurë!</a></i> = "Behold, people of the Eldar and Fathers of Men, the day has come!"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Aia_Mar%C3%ADa" title="Aia María"><b>Aia</b> María quanta Eruano</a></i> = "**Hail** Mary, full of grace"
