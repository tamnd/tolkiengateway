**Alfrida of [the Yale](/wiki/The_Yale "The Yale")** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Alfrida was the wife of [Gundolpho Bolger](/wiki/Gundolpho_Bolger). They had three children: [Gundahar](/wiki/Gundahar_Bolger "Gundahar Bolger"), [Rudolph](/wiki/Rudolph_Bolger "Rudolph Bolger") and [Gundahad](/wiki/Gundahad_Bolger "Gundahad Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A name of germanic origin.
