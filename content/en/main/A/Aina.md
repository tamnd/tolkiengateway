<i><b>aina</b></i> (<i><b>ayanā</b></i>) means "to hallow, bless, treat as holy" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). *airi-* or *aina* means "holy, revered, numinous", also in Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>
