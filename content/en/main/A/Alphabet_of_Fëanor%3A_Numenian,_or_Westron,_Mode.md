<i><b>Alphabet of Fëanor: Numenian, or Westron, Mode</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> is a manuscript of a [Westron](/wiki/Westron) chart for [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) devised by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), and included in [Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan)'s [unpublished](/wiki/Unpublished "Unpublished") <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Report_from_Marquette&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Report from Marquette (page does not exist)">Report from Marquette</a></i>. It was also published in [*Parma Eldalamberon* 23](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_23 "Parma Eldalamberon 23").
