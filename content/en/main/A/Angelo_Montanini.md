**Angelo Montanini** (b. [21 May](/wiki/21_May) [1951](/wiki/1951)<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>) is an Italian [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-inspired artist.

## Career {#Career}

While he had been an artist before, Montanini attributes his focus on fantasy art to [Angus McBride](/wiki/Angus_McBride). He began working for Stratelibri, an Italian games publishing house in [1993](/wiki/1993), even serving as its art director until [1999](/wiki/1999). The company's founder, Giovanni Ingellis, recommended Montanini to [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises). This led to him creating over one hundred illustrations for their games. He also created calendar illustrations for the [Italian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Italian_Tolkien_Society).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

Montanini taught drawing and illustration in the European Fashion School of Milan.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
