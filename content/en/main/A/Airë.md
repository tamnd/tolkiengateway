<i><b>airë</b></i> (alt. <i><b>aira</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) adjective meaning "holy".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

It also appears as a noun: *airë-* is glossed as "holiness",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> *airë* (*airē*) is glossed as "sanctity, holiness",<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-3">[3]</a></sup> and *airë* is glossed as "sanctity".<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is said that the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md) used the word *Airë* as a title of address to the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and greater [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> An example is [Varda](../V/Varda.md), who was addressed as *Airë [Tári](../T/Tári.md)*, the "holy queen"<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup> (see also *airetári* in [Galadriel's Lament](../N/Namárië.md)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>).
