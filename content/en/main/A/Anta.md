<i><b>anta</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word with two different meanings. The word is derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a href="/wiki/ANA" title="ANA">ANA<sup>1</sup></a>.

## Verb {#Verb}

As a verb, *anta* means "to give".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety348_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety348-1">[1]</a></sup>

Other forms:

- *antanen* "I gave"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - *áne*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Noun {#Noun}

As a noun, *anta* means "face".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety348_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety348-1">[1]</a></sup>
