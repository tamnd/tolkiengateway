<b><i>Amon Hen</i> 116</b> is the one-hundred-and-fifteenth issue of the [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md)'s bimonthly journal *[Amon Hen](<Amon_Hen_(journal).md>)*, published in May [1992](../_/1992.md).

The section "Important item for auction at Sotherby's \[sic\] in July" (in Reports) includes several quotations from and a description of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s notes in proof copies of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
