**Walking-songs** were a type of short song that [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) hummed especially when walking during the night towards their home.<sup id="cite_ref-hike_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hike-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann) wrote music for Bilbo's walking-song, referred to as **Upon the Hearth the Fire is Red**. It can be found in the book *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*;<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it is also found on the accompanying CD of the latest version.

## History {#History}

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) had written the words of a walking-song, putting it to a tune that was "as old as the hills".<sup id="cite_ref-hike_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hike-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the [24](/wiki/24_September "24 September") of [September](/wiki/September) in the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") sang Bilbo's walking-song as they hiked through the [Woody End](/wiki/Woody_End):<sup id="cite_ref-hike_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hike-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><audio src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/bd/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_A_Walking_Song.mp3" width="300" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 300px;" controls=""><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_A_Walking_Song.mp3">https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_A_Walking_Song.mp3</a></audio><figcaption><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> reads A Walking Song</figcaption></figure>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Upon the hearth the fire is red,<br/>
Beneath the roof there is a bed;<br/>
But not yet weary are our feet,<br/>
Still round the corner we may meet<br/>
A sudden tree or standing stone<br/>
That none have seen but we alone.<br/>
  Tree and flower and leaf and grass,<br/>
  Let them pass! Let them pass!<br/>
  Hill and water under sky,<br/>
  Pass them by! Pass them by!<br/>
<br/>
Still round the corner there may wait<br/>
A new road or a secret gate,<br/>
And though we pass them by today,<br/>
Tomorrow we may come this way<br/>
And take the hidden paths that run<br/>
Towards the Moon or to the Sun.<br/>
  Apple, thorn, and nut and sloe,<br/>
  Let them go! Let them go!<br/>
  Sand and stone and pool and dell,<br/>
  Fare you well! Fare you well!<br/>
<br/>
Home is behind, the world ahead,<br/>
And there are many paths to tread<br/>
Through shadows to the edge of night,<br/>
Until the stars are all alight.<br/>
Then world behind and home ahead,<br/>
We&#39;ll wander back to home and bed.<br/>
  Mist and twilight, cloud and shade,<br/>
  Away shall fade! Away shall fade!<br/>
  Fire and lamp, and meat and bread,<br/>
  And then to bed! And then to bed!
</p>
</div>

As the song ended, a tired Pippin re-sang the last line but changed it to match his desire for rest: "And *now* to bed! And *now* to bed!" Frodo hushed him when he heard the sound of hoofs approaching.<sup id="cite_ref-hike_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hike-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3021](/wiki/Third_Age_3021 "Third Age 3021"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") heard an altered version of Bilbo's walking-song being softly sung by Frodo as they rode westward through [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") just before they met [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), [Gildor](/wiki/Gildor) and other [Elves](/wiki/Elves) heading for the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) to depart for the [Undying Lands](/wiki/Undying_Lands):<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Still round the corner there may wait<br/>
A new road or a secret gate<br/>
And though I oft have passed them by<br/>
A day will come at last when I<br/>
Shall take the hidden paths that run<br/>
West of the Moon and East of the Sun
</p>
</div>
