**Andúril's sheath** was the [gift](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") Galadriel bestowed to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).

Before the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") left [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) presented each of them with gifts. The first gift was given to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn). It was an Elvish<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> sheath made for his sword **[Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril)**. The sheath was "overlaid with a tracery of flowers and leaves wrought of silver and gold, and on it were set in elven-runes formed of many gems the name Andúril and the lineage of the sword." Galadriel told that "*the blade that is drawn from this sheath shall not be stained or broken even in defeat.*"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
