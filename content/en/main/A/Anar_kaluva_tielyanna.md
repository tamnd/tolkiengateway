<i><b>Anar kaluva tielyanna!</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) farewell given by [Gelmir](</wiki/Gelmir_(messenger_of_C%C3%ADrdan)> "Gelmir (messenger of Círdan)") to [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) when they encountered each other in [Annon-in-Gelydh](/wiki/Annon-in-Gelydh).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It translates as "The sun shall shine upon your path".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
