<i><b>anda</b></i> means 'long' in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [ÁNAD](/wiki/%C3%81NAD).

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[andamacil](/wiki/Andamacil "Andamacil")*

## Cognates {#Cognates}
