<i><b>A Guide to Middle-Earth 2005 Calendar</b></i> was published by Andrews McMeel Publishing and is based on the book by [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md). The calendar contains information about the characters and events combined with daily entries that provide insight into the details of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).
