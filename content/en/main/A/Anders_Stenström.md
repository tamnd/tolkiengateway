**Beregond, Anders Stenström** (born [1957](../_/1957.md)) is a Swedish [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) scholar.

Since [1967](../_/1967.md), Stenström has been an enthusiast of Tolkien, and since [1976](../_/1976.md), when he became a member of the Tolkien Society [Forodrim](../F/Forodrim.md), he carries the byname Beregond.

Beregond has researched different aspects of the works of Tolkien (among his contributions can be found suggestions on principles for [structural references](../S/Structural_references.md)), published several articles, and been a speaker at and organizer of conferences and conventions. Together with Kristina Valinger, Beregond was responsible for the creation of the Swedish subtitles for [Peter Jackson's *The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>). He was consulted for [Erik Andersson](../E/Erik_Andersson.md)'s new Swedish translation of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. He is the translator of [The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Bilbo's Last Song](../B/Bilbo's_Last_Song.md) into Swedish.

He is currently one of the organizers of [Omentielva](../O/Omentielva.md), the International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien's Invented Languages, a series of biennial conferences at changing locations. He is also editor of [*Arda*](<Arda_(annual).md>) and *Arda Philology*.

Stenström lives in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alby,_Botkyrka" title="wikipedia:Alby, Botkyrka">Alby, Botkyrka</a> within the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_urban_area" title="wikipedia:Stockholm urban area">Stockholm urban area</a> and works as a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verger#Modern_function" title="wikipedia:Verger">verger</a>.
