**Beregond, Anders Stenström** (born [1957](/wiki/1957)) is a Swedish [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") scholar.

Since [1967](/wiki/1967), Stenström has been an enthusiast of Tolkien, and since [1976](/wiki/1976), when he became a member of the Tolkien Society [Forodrim](/wiki/Forodrim), he carries the byname Beregond.

Beregond has researched different aspects of the works of Tolkien (among his contributions can be found suggestions on principles for [structural references](/wiki/Structural_references "Structural references")), published several articles, and been a speaker at and organizer of conferences and conventions. Together with Kristina Valinger, Beregond was responsible for the creation of the Swedish subtitles for [Peter Jackson's *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). He was consulted for [Erik Andersson](/wiki/Erik_Andersson)'s new Swedish translation of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. He is the translator of [The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Bilbo's Last Song](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Last_Song) into Swedish.

He is currently one of the organizers of [Omentielva](/wiki/Omentielva), the International Conference on J.R.R. Tolkien's Invented Languages, a series of biennial conferences at changing locations. He is also editor of [*Arda*](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)") and *Arda Philology*.

Stenström lives in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alby,_Botkyrka" title="wikipedia:Alby, Botkyrka">Alby, Botkyrka</a> within the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_urban_area" title="wikipedia:Stockholm urban area">Stockholm urban area</a> and works as a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verger#Modern_function" title="wikipedia:Verger">verger</a>.
