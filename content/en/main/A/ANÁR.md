**ANÁR** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "sun". It is a derivative of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NAR (page does not exist)">NAR<sup>1</sup></a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The stem is also glossed as (A)NAR.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *anār-*
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Anar](/wiki/Sun "Sun")*
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin") ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)): *[Anor](/wiki/Sun "Sun")*
