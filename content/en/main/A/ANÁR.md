**ANÁR** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "sun". It is a derivative of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NAR (page does not exist)">NAR<sup>1</sup></a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The stem is also glossed as (A)NAR.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *anār-*
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[Anar](../S/Sun.md)*
    - [Exilic Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)): *[Anor](../S/Sun.md)*
