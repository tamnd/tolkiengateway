This **ambush** was conducted by a company of [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) led by [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) against a mixed force of [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) from [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) and [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md). It occurred in late February of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), near the edge of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Uruk_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Uruk-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The [Uruks](../U/Uruk-hai.md), who had been carrying two prisoners ([Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md)) in tow, were anxiously attempting to reach Isengard as quickly as possible. However, they were joined by an [Orc of Mordor](../O/Orcs_of_Mordor.md) named [Grishnákh](../G/Grishnákh.md), as well as a company of [Orcs from the Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md). Grishnákh was seeking [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) to deliver to his master [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s [Ringwraiths](../N/Nazgûl.md), and the latter group sought revenge against the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) for their previous intrusion in [Moria](../M/Moria.md).

This led to a series of disagreements with the Uruks that delayed their progress many times. During one of these conflicts, a few of the Mountain-Orcs were killed, leading to Grishnákh disappearing and returning with a squad of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) Orcs.

By the time the three parties came to an agreement, the riders of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) had caught wind of the Orcs and Uruks (due to their failure to kill one of their scouts), and pursued them across Rohan. In a panic, most of the Mountain-Orcs fled ahead, but were debilitated by the bright sunlight and left for dead by the Isengarders as they reached the eaves of [Fangorn Forest](../F/Fangorn_Forest.md).

There, the Rohirrim launched their final attack and slaughtered all of the Orcs and Uruk-hai present. Éomer himself engaged in a duel with [Uglúk](../U/Uglúk.md), the leader of the Isengarders, and slew him.

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

During the confusion, Merry and Pippin managed to escape their captors and avoid most of the conflict by disappearing into Fangorn Forest, where they would later meet [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md). The Rohirrim piled the corpses of the slain Uruks and Orcs on the battlefield and had them burned.

The horses [Hasufel](../H/Hasufel.md) and [Arod](Arod.md), whose riders had been killed in the skirmish, were lent to the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The circumstances of the battle are simplified. The force of Orcs consists of Uruk-hai, led by Uglúk, and Orcs, seemingly loyal to Grishnakh. When the Orcs start quarreling over whether or not they would eat one of the Hobbits, Uglúk decapitates one of the Mordor-Orcs and his Uruks began eating its corpse. At this point, Grishnakh catches Merry and Pippin attempting to escape, only to be shot by an arrow. Then, the Rohirrim arrive and launch their attack from out of the darkness. However, unlike the book, Grishnakh survives the battle and pursues Merry and Pippin into the forest where he is killed by Treebeard.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</i> (video game)</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Although omitted from the console versions, the GameBoy Advance version features another version of the ambush. The battle appeared to have occurred between the Uruks of Isengard and a group of Orcs from Mordor. The former seem to have been victorious, and drove the latter into the forest, where they are presumably killed by Huorns.</dd></dl>
