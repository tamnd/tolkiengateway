**A.F. Colborn** was a B.Litt student at [St Edmund Hall, Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md#St_Edmund_Hall), under [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s supervision. Tolkien was appointed supervisor on [30 October](../_/30_October.md) [1931](../_/1931.md) and Colborn was admitted on [4 December](../_/4_December.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>
