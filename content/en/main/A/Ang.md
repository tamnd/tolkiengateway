<i><b>ang</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "iron". Its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) cognate was *[anga](/wiki/Anga "Anga")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ANGĀ](/wiki/ANGA "ANGA")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Angband" title="Angband"><b>Ang</b>band</a></i> = "Iron Prison"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Anghabar" title="Anghabar"><b>Ang</b>habar</a></i> = "Iron-delvings"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Anglachel" title="Anglachel"><b>Ang</b>lahel</a></i> = "Iron-of-the-flaming-star"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar"><b>Ang</b>mar</a></i> = "Iron Home"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Angrenost" title="Angrenost"><b>Ang</b>renost</a></i> = "Iron Fostress"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Angrist" title="Angrist"><b>Ang</b>rist</a></i> = "Iron Cleaver"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Angrod" title="Angrod"><b>Ang</b>rod</a></i> = "Iron Champion"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Anguirel" title="Anguirel"><b>Ang</b>uirel</a></i> = "Iron-of-the-fiery-star"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Gurthang" title="Gurthang">Gurth<b>ang</b></a></i> = "Iron of Death"
