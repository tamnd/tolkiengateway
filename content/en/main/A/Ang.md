<i><b>ang</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) noun meaning "iron". Its [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) cognate was *[anga](Anga.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [ANGĀ](ANGA~30450.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- <i><a href="Angband.md" title="Angband"><b>Ang</b>band</a></i> = "Iron Prison"
- <i><a href="Anghabar.md" title="Anghabar"><b>Ang</b>habar</a></i> = "Iron-delvings"
- <i><a href="Anglachel.md" title="Anglachel"><b>Ang</b>lahel</a></i> = "Iron-of-the-flaming-star"

- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="Angrenost.md" title="Angrenost"><b>Ang</b>renost</a></i> = "Iron Fostress"
- <i><a href="Angrist.md" title="Angrist"><b>Ang</b>rist</a></i> = "Iron Cleaver"
- <i><a href="Angrod.md" title="Angrod"><b>Ang</b>rod</a></i> = "Iron Champion"
- <i><a href="Anguirel.md" title="Anguirel"><b>Ang</b>uirel</a></i> = "Iron-of-the-fiery-star"
