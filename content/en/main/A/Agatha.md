**Agatha** was [Farmer Giles](../F/Farmer_Giles.md)' wife. When [Garm](../G/Garm.md) woke up the two of them with the news of the [Giant](../T/The_Giant.md)'s arrival, she first recommended drowning the dog, but when Giles indicated that he believed the dog she told him to be bold and quick.

When Giles became king of the [Little Kingdom](../L/Little_Kingdom.md), Agatha became a queen of great size and majesty. She was known to keep a tight account upon the household.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
