<i><b>Aeglos</b></i>, or <i><b>aiglos</b></i>, means "Snowthorn" or "icicle" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[aeg](/wiki/Aeg "Aeg")*, "point" and *[loss](/wiki/Loss "Loss")*, "snow") and may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Aeglos](</wiki/Aeglos_(plant)> "Aeglos (plant)"), a plant common to [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand).
- **[Aeglos](</wiki/Aeglos_(spear)> "Aeglos (spear)"), the spear of [Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad").**

## Fandom {#Fandom}

- *[Aiglos (journal)](</wiki/Aiglos_(journal)>)*, the annual journal of the *Parmadili*, the [Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](/wiki/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_%C5%9AKF "Sekcja Tolkienowska ŚKF").
