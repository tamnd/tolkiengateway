<i><b>Aeglos</b></i>, or <i><b>aiglos</b></i>, means "Snowthorn" or "icicle" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) (from *[aeg](Aeg.md)*, "point" and *[loss](../L/Loss.md)*, "snow") and may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Aeglos](<Aeglos_(plant).md>), a plant common to [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md).
- **[Aeglos](<Aeglos_(spear).md>), the spear of [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md).**

## Fandom {#Fandom}

- *[Aiglos (journal)](<Aiglos_(journal).md>)*, the annual journal of the *Parmadili*, the [Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](../S/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_ŚKF.md).
