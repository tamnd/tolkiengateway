<i><b>Aiya Eärendil elenion ancalima!</b></i> was the cry in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) while crossing through [Shelob's Lair](/wiki/Shelob%27s_Lair). It seems that it was a common cry among [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in the past. It meant "Hail [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil), brightest of Stars".<sup id="cite_ref-Letter_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After Frodo and [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Sam_Gamgee) found themselves in complete darkness, they noticed [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) had abandoned them to a trap. Then Sam reminded his master about the [Phial of Galadriel](/wiki/Phial_of_Galadriel), which Frodo had forgotten he had. He cried "<i><b>Aiya Eärendil elenion ancalima!</b></i>". The light of the phial then showed them the eyes of [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob), who knew that cry from ages old and was not afraid of it.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later on, Frodo cried a variant: "<i><b>Aiya elenion ancalima!</b></i>", while crossing the [Two Watchers](/wiki/Two_Watchers), again with help of the Phial's light.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) explained that the phrase is derived from the first line of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) poem <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_I" title="wikipedia:Christ I">Christ</a></i>: *éala éarendel engla beorhtast*.<sup id="cite_ref-Letter_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Letter-1">[1]</a></sup>
