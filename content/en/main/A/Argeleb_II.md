**Argeleb II** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1473](../T/Third_Age_1473.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [1670](../T/Third_Age_1670.md),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 197) was the tenth king of [Arthedain](Arthedain.md).

## History {#History}

He succeeded his father, [Araphor](Araphor.md), upon his death in 1589, at the age of 116.

According to the records of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), Argeleb was the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/High_King.md" title="High King">High King</a> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1601](../T/Third_Age_1601.md) when many [hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) migrated from [Bree](../B/Bree.md); the King granted them permission to cross the [Bridge of Stonebows](../B/Bridge_of_Stonebows.md) and to settle the land beyond the [Baranduin](../B/Baranduin.md) to dwell in. He demanded in return to acknowledge his lordship, keep all bridges and roads, and speed the royal messengers.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> These hobbits were joined in [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) by [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md) from [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) around [1630](../T/Third_Age_1630.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[4]</a></sup> Prior to this, the area of the Shire had been unpopulated and regarded as the king's hunting ground.

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md) the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) devastated [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) (the [King](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) [Telemnar](../T/Telemnar.md), his children and the [White Tree](../W/White_Tree_of_Gondor.md) perished) and moved north to the lands of Argeleb's rule.<sup id="cite_ref-TA_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-4">[4]</a></sup> Although the Great Plague decreased in severity as it moved north, there was still much suffering throughout [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). A remnant of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) in Cardolan survived until the Great Plague of 1636; most of them perished, especially in [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md). The [last prince of Cardolan](../L/Last_prince_of_Cardolan.md) was buried in the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md); in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md) evil spirits out of [Angmar](Angmar.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) entered into the deserted mounds and dwelt there.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After having ruled for eighty-one years, he was succeeded by his son, [Arvegil](Arvegil.md), upon his death in 1670.<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup>

See [Argeleb I - Etymology](Argeleb_I.md#Etymology).

It is possible that Argeleb II was named after his great-grandfarther, [Argeleb I](Argeleb_I.md)

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1997: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a> — <a href="Arnor%253A_The_People.md" title="Arnor: The People">Arnor: The People</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Argeleb II was a largely military ruler, mostly wearing his signature black armour. By <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1640.md" title="Third Age 1640">1640</a>, with the wars against <a href="Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> at a stalemate, he was tired of war and instead sought to patronize the arts and tend to the gardens of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../F/Fornost.md" title="Fornost">Fornost</a>, which the nobles saw as a sign of weakness. While the long lives of the royal family normally meant kings could draw support from a large extended family, the <a href="../G/Great_Plague.md" title="Great Plague">Great Plague</a> had taken the relatives he had relied on.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
