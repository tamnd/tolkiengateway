<i><b>angren</b></i> (pl. *engrin*) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "of iron". The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) cognate is *[angaina](/wiki/Angaina "Angaina")*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*angren*, related to Sindarin *[ang](/wiki/Ang "Ang")* ("iron"), derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ANGĀ](/wiki/ANGA "ANGA").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possible that the full [Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin) word is *angrina* (unattested).

The obsolete, variant or misspelt plural form *engren* appears in a typescript.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
