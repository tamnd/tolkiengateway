<i><b>Athelas</b></i>, also known as **kingsfoil** or <i><b>asëa aranion</b></i>, was a sweet-smelling herb with healing properties, such as curing wounds, poison and counteracting evil influence such as the [Black Breath](../B/Black_Breath.md). It held a special power of healing in the hands of the [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md).

## History {#History}

During the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) *athelas* was perhaps growing in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). [Huan](../H/Huan.md) had found *athelas* to heal [Beren](../B/Beren.md) of his wounds.<sup id="cite_ref-Canto10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto10-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Athelas* most notably grew on the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) and was brought to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> It grew sparsely in [the North](../E/Eriador.md) and only in places where the Men of Westernesse had camped or lived. By the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) only the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) retained the knowledge of its healing properties.

In [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) (where it was known as **kingsfoil**) the knowledge of its healing virtues had been lost by the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) (on account of their dependence on the long-absent kings),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and its leaves were esteemed only for their refreshing scent and their potential ability to cure headaches. However, the memory of its former uses was retained in [verses](../W/When_the_black_breath_blows.md) remembered by some of the older women in the Houses of Healing, and the special power it held in the hands of the [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) was once again demonstrated by [Aragorn](Aragorn.md) upon his return to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-4">[4]</a></sup>

Aragorn was very familiar with the plant, and its long leaves<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> were used by him on several occasions: healing [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) from the Morgul wound,<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> tending the wounds of Frodo and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) after the exit from [Moria](../M/Moria.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-5">[5]</a></sup> and secretly entering [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) upon his return to [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) to heal those touched by the [Black Breath](../B/Black_Breath.md), an act that enhanced his reputation and strengthened his claim to the crown.<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Properties and Effects {#Properties_and_Effects}

*Athelas*, when dried and crushed in hot water, was refreshing. It cleared and calmed<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> the minds of those who smelled it. *Athelas* also strengthened<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-5">[5]</a></sup> those smelling the scent. It had a particular scent that was either unique to the individual who smelled the herb or influenced by the recipient of the herb's effects:

- [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md)'s scent was of "dewy mornings of unshadowed sun... \[in which\] Spring is itself but a fleeting memory."<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Ioreth](../I/Ioreth.md) smelled the "roses of Imloth Melui" from her childhood.<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) smelled no scent, as if the air was clean, fresh, and had never "been breathed by any living thing and came new-made from snowy mountains high beneath a dome of stars, or from shores of silver far away washed by seas of foam."<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md)'s scent was that of "orchards, and of heather in the sunshine full of bees."<sup id="cite_ref-Healing_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healing-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Athelas* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word, consisting of *[athae](Athae.md)* + *[lass](../L/Lass.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-6">[6]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) cognate is <i><b>asëa aranion</b></i> ("asëa of the Kings").<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-6">[6]</a></sup> It has been suggested that the whole name could mean "Beneficial (leaf) of Kings".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The rejected form *asea aranaite* was used in a manuscript version of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

*Kingsfoil* has the Old French element *foil*, "leaf" as seen in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/cinquefoil" title="wikipedia:cinquefoil">cinquefoil</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

*Athelas* does not appear in the published *[Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* or in the tales of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) in general; however in the early *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)* it was used by [Huan](../H/Huan.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) to heal the wounded [Beren](../B/Beren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Canto10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Canto10-1">[1]</a></sup> This contradicts the information from *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* of it being brought to Middle-earth by [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Flight_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flight-2">[2]</a></sup> so its history was either revised by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), or *athelas* grew in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md) before it was destroyed, and then brought *back* to Middle-earth by Númenóreans in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

## In other media {#In_other_media}

Kingsfoil is also mentioned in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ursula_K._Le_Guin" title="wikipedia:Ursula K. Le Guin">Ursula K. Le Guin</a>'s <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Wizard_of_Earthsea" title="wikipedia:A Wizard of Earthsea">A Wizard of Earthsea</a></i>, as one of the herbs in the witch of Gont's hut.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

*Athelas* is also mentioned in the game <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quest_for_Glory_I" title="wikipedia:Quest for Glory I">Quest for Glory</a>.

The [font](../F/Fonts.md) *Athelas*,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> named for the plant, was released in 2008 by independent type foundry TypeTogether. The font was designed by TypeTogether's founders, Veronika Burian and Jose Scaglione.
