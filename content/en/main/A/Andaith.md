The **Andaith** or "long mark" was the long-vowel diacritical mark used in [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) of the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Tengwar/Mode_of_Beleriand "Tengwar/Mode of Beleriand"). In this mode of 'full writing', vowels were represented by separate letters rather than as [tehtar](/wiki/Tehtar "Tehtar") so a different means was needed to indicate long vowels. The andaith appeared as an 'acute accent'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
