The **Andaith** or "long mark" was the long-vowel diacritical mark used in [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) of the [Mode of Beleriand](../T/Tengwar%252FMode_of_Beleriand.md). In this mode of 'full writing', vowels were represented by separate letters rather than as [tehtar](../T/Tehtar.md) so a different means was needed to indicate long vowels. The andaith appeared as an 'acute accent'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
