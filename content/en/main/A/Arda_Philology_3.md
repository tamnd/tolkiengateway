<i><b>Arda Philology 3</b></i> is the third volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](<Arda_(annual).md>)*, and contains the proceedings of the Third International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/onelya.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Nelya</a> (<a href="../O/Omentielva.md" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Whitehaven in August [2009](../_/2009.md).
