<i><b>Arda Philology 3</b></i> is the third volume of the journal *[Arda Philology](</wiki/Arda_(annual)> "Arda (annual)")*, and contains the proceedings of the Third International Conference on Tolkien's Invented Languages, <i><a class="external text" href="https://www.omentielva.com/onelya.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Omentielva Nelya</a> (<a href="/wiki/Omentielva" title="Omentielva">Omentielva</a>)</i>, which was held in Whitehaven in August [2009](/wiki/2009).
