<i><b>aeg</b></i> is a noun meaning "point" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). Compare *[ecthel](/wiki/Ecthel "Ecthel")*, "spear point".

Examples:

- [Aegamloth](/wiki/Egalmoth "Egalmoth") = "A Spike upon a Flowered Crest"
- *[aeglos](</wiki/Aeglos_(spear)> "Aeglos (spear)")* = "icicle"

## Conflicting Definition {#Conflicting_Definition}

Earlier in [Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") development of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), *aeg* meant "fell" or "dire" and was the equivalent of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[aica](/wiki/Aica "Aica")*. Tolkien later rejected this definition, deciding that *aeg* only translated as "point". This complicated the translations of some names, as Tolkien never developed another Sindarin equivalent for *[aica](/wiki/Aica "Aica")*.

Example:

- [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor) = "Fell Fire"
