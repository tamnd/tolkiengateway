<i><b>aeg</b></i> is a noun meaning "point" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Compare *[ecthel](../E/Ecthel.md)*, "spear point".

Examples:

- [Aegamloth](../E/Egalmoth.md) = "A Spike upon a Flowered Crest"
- *[aeglos](<Aeglos_(spear).md>)* = "icicle"

## Conflicting Definition {#Conflicting_Definition}

Earlier in [Tolkien's](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) development of [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), *aeg* meant "fell" or "dire" and was the equivalent of the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[aica](Aica.md)*. Tolkien later rejected this definition, deciding that *aeg* only translated as "point". This complicated the translations of some names, as Tolkien never developed another Sindarin equivalent for *[aica](Aica.md)*.

Example:

- [Aegnor](Aegnor.md) = "Fell Fire"
