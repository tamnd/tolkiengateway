**Andrew Higgins** is a [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") scholar. His PhD thesis, entitled 'The Genesis of Tolkien's Mythology', was supervised by Dr. [Dimitra Fimi](/wiki/Dimitra_Fimi). He also teaches courses on Language Inventions and Tolkien, through Signum University/[Mythgard Institute](/wiki/Mythgard_Institute).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
