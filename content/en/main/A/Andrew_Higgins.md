**Andrew Higgins** is a [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) scholar. His PhD thesis, entitled 'The Genesis of Tolkien's Mythology', was supervised by Dr. [Dimitra Fimi](../D/Dimitra_Fimi.md). He also teaches courses on Language Inventions and Tolkien, through Signum University/[Mythgard Institute](../M/Mythgard_Institute.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
