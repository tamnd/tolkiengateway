<i><b>Anaxarta</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The etymology of the word is not obvious.

Arguably, it is a compound of the words \**anaxara* \*"most rushing" (*[an-](/wiki/An- "An-")* "most" + \**axara*, a possible derivative from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=SKAR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SKAR (page does not exist)">SKAR</a>; cf. *[ascar](/wiki/Ascar "Ascar")*) plus *[arta](/wiki/Arta "Arta")* "exalted". The word \**Anaxararta* would be simplified as *Anaxarta*.<sup id="cite_ref-Wynne_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wynne-2">[2]</a></sup>

Other possibilities link the word to [Máhanaxar](/wiki/M%C3%A1hanaxar), therefore being of [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) origin. In that case it could tentatively be an epithet for the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) who participate in the Ring of Doom.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

- *Anaxartaron* (gen.pl.) \*"of the Valar" or \*"of the Great Eagles"
