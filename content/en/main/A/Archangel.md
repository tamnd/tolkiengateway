**Archangel** is a Tolkien-related progressive metal/rock band from Milan, Italy, formed in 2006 and led by multi-instrumentalist Gabriele Manzini. Its debut album *The Akallabeth* (2009) takes its title from the [Akallabêth](Akallabêth.md), Tolkien's account of the downfall of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), and features songs such as "The Shade of Númenor", "Rings of Power" and "Lidless Eye".

## Members {#Members}

- Gabriele Manzini - piano, Hammond organ, Mellotron, synthesizers, guitars
