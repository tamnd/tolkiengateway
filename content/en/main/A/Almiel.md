**Almiel** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈalmi.el]</span>, stem *Almield-*) was the third child and second daughter of [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) and [Almarian](/wiki/Almarian). Her older brother was [Anardil](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion"), who when he became king was known as [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When Aldarion built the great ship [Palarran](/wiki/Palarran) Tar-Meneldur was angry. Though Queen Almarian stayed the king from prohibiting Aldarion from sailing, the king did forbid his wife and daughters from bringing a bough of *[oiolairë](/wiki/Oiolair%C3%AB "Oiolairë")* to the ship. Because Almarian, Ailinel, and Almiel obeyed the king's command it gave [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis) the opportunity to present the token of goodwill to the prince, which kindled love in Aldarion for Erendis.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Almiel* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Blessed Daughter" and is a compound of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alma&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alma (page does not exist)">almë</a></i> ("good (or blessed) thing", "blessing", "piece of good fortune") and *[-iel](/wiki/-iel)* (feminine suffix, "-daughter").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
