**Angrist**was a long<sup id="cite_ref-long_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-long-1">[1]</a></sup> [dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md)-knife<sup id="cite_ref-dwarfknife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwarfknife-2">[2]</a></sup> made by [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and borne by [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md). It was later taken by [Beren](../B/Beren.md),<sup id="cite_ref-took_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-took-3">[3]</a></sup> who used it to cut a [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) from [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md). However, it broke soon after.<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), Angrist was created by the [dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md)-smith [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md). It was said that it could cleave iron like green wood.<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup>

The knife was later wielded by the [Elf](../E/Elves.md)-lord [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md), one of [Fëanor's](../F/Fëanor.md) [sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md). When Curufin and his brother, [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md), were banished from [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [465](../F/First_Age_465.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> he laid his hand on the haft of the knife as he laughed, mockingly naming [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) "Sir Steward".<sup id="cite_ref-long_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-long-1">[1]</a></sup> After they left, the brothers initially intended to ride to [Himring](../H/Himring.md),<sup id="cite_ref-history_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-6">[6]</a></sup> but coincidentally, they encountered [Beren](../B/Beren.md) and [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) in the [Forest of Brethil](../F/Forest_of_Brethil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup>. The knife was worn by Curufin "sheathless by his side"<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup> when he attempted to kidnap Lúthien, but triggered the [Leap of Beren](../L/Leap_of_Beren.md)<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup> as a result, causing the knife to be taken from him along with his horse and other weapons and gear.<sup id="cite_ref-took_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-took-3">[3]</a></sup>

Later, early in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [466](../F/First_Age_466.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>, Beren snuck into [Angband](Angband.md) with Lúthien and used the knife to cut out a [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) from the [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md) of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). But as Beren tried to cut out the other two, the knife snapped and Morgoth stirred as "a shard of the blade"<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup> grazed his cheek. Beren and Lúthien left the knife behind as they fled<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-dwarfknife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-dwarfknife-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Angrist* means "Iron-cleaver" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-index_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-9">[9]</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-meaning_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meaning-10">[10]</a></sup> from *[ang](Ang.md)* ("iron")<sup id="cite_ref-peoples_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-peoples-11">[11]</a></sup> + *[crist](../C/Crist.md)* ("a cleaver, sword")<sup id="cite_ref-crist_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-crist-12">[12]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ris_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ris-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*, Beren grabbed a great knife in fear when the castle of [Tevildo](../T/Tevildo.md) shook because of the spell being broken by Tinúviel.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-15">[15]</a></sup> He kept the knife, but later drew it when he seized the [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md), labouring "as noiselessly as may be to prise out a Silmaril with his knife" until it snapped "with a loud crack"; the sound of which awakened the sleeping Melko.<sup id="cite_ref-tales_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tales-15">[15]</a></sup> Later, despite the knife snapping, Beren somehow fingered the knife (or the remnant of it) as he lay dying after fleeing [Karkaras Knife-fang](../C/Carcharoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> In his commentary, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) identified this "kitchen-knife" as the direct predecessor of Angrist.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

The knife was mentioned as "the dirk of [Nargil](../N/Nargil.md)" in [Beleg's](../B/Beleg.md) [whetting spell](../W/Whetting_spell.md) in *[The Lay of the Children of Húrin](../T/The_Lay_of_the_Children_of_Húrin.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-spell_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-spell-18">[18]</a></sup> In *[The Lay of Leithian](../T/The_Lay_of_Leithian.md)*, the knife is described as pale and made of steel.<sup id="cite_ref-pale_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pale-19">[19]</a></sup> It is also described as being bitter-cold and hard-edged.<sup id="cite_ref-hard_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hard-20">[20]</a></sup> At one point, the unnamed Angrist was a magic knife that was given to Beren by Celegorm as a passing idea.<sup id="cite_ref-given_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-given-21">[21]</a></sup> In the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* the knife of Curufin remained unnamed and it was referred to as "the knife of the treacherous Dwarves".<sup id="cite_ref-shaping_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shaping-22">[22]</a></sup>

In later versions and the version published in *The Silmarillion*,<sup id="cite_ref-silm_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-4">[4]</a></sup> this blade became Angrist, which was the blade of Curufin that was forged by [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md) of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md).<sup id="cite_ref-meaning_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meaning-10">[10]</a></sup>
