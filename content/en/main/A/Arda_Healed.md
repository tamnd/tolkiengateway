**Arda Healed** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Arda Envinyanta*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) was a new world said in prophecy to come into being after being [sung](/wiki/Second_Music_of_the_Ainur "Second Music of the Ainur") into existence after the [Last Battle](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath "Dagor Dagorath"). It would be the renewal of [Arda Marred](/wiki/Arda_Marred), the fulfilment of a perfected world that would have been were it not for the intervention of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). The [Ainulindalë](/wiki/Ainulindal%C3%AB) says that [Men](/wiki/Men) will sing with the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) in this Second Music, but that [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) has not revealed what role, if any, will be played by [Elves](/wiki/Elves).
