**Aravorn** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2497](/wiki/Third_Age_2497 "Third Age 2497")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [2654](/wiki/Third_Age_2654 "Third Age 2654"),<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 157) was the ninth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain).

## History {#History}

Aravorn succeeded his father, [Aragost](/wiki/Aragost), when he died in [2588](/wiki/Third_Age_2588 "Third Age 2588"). Aravorn's rule seems to be uneventful as the enemy centered a lot of its attacks on the newly-formed [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

Aravorn died in 2654, after having ruled as chieftain for 66 years, and was succeeded by his son, [Arahad II](/wiki/Arahad_II).<sup id="cite_ref-North_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aravorn* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but the Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Black King".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-3">[3]</a></sup> It is probably a compound of the prefix *[ar(a)-](/wiki/Ara "Ara")* ("noble", "royal", "high") and *[morn](/wiki/Morn "Morn")* ("black", "dark", "night")<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkiendil_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkiendil-3">[3]</a></sup> or *born* ("\[stead\]fast").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
