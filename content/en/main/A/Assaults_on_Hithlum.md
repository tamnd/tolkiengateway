**Morgoth's assaults on Hithlum** were a series of minor battles in the [War of the Great Jewels](/wiki/War_of_the_Great_Jewels).

## During the Siege of Angband {#During_the_Siege_of_Angband}

Knowing the vigilance of [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros), in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [155](/wiki/First_Age_155 "First Age 155") [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) endeavoured to take [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) at unawares.<sup id="cite_ref-ga115_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga115-1">[1]</a></sup> He sent an army of [Orcs](/wiki/Orc "Orc") north into the unguarded ice and snow behind [Angband](/wiki/Angband), where many of them perished, until they reached the outer end of the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains) where they then turned to the west until they came to the shores of [the sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"); from there they turned south, following the same route Fingolfin's host took after crossing the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB), until they came to the costs of the [Firth of Drengist](/wiki/Firth_of_Drengist).<sup id="cite_ref-quenta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-quenta-2">[2]</a></sup> From here, the intention was to invade into the heart of Hithlum, from the rear.<sup id="cite_ref-quenta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-quenta-2">[2]</a></sup>

This force was espied by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in time however, and a force led by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) took them into a trap among the hills at the head of firth.<sup id="cite_ref-ga115_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga115-1">[1]</a></sup> None of these Orcs returned to Morgoth, with most being driven into the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-quenta_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-quenta-2">[2]</a></sup>

This encounter was not considered to be among the great battles, as the Orcs were not in great number and only a part of the folk of Hithlum where involved.<sup id="cite_ref-quenta_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-quenta-2">[2]</a></sup> It was, however, the most dangerous of the many trials that Morgoth would continuously make against the leaguer. Following this battle, Morgoth perceived that Orcs alone, unless it were in numbers that he could not yet muster, would be no match against the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor). He thus started to consider new approaches, and came to the thought of [dragons](/wiki/Dragon "Dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-ga115_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ga115-1">[1]</a></sup>

## After Dagor Bragollach {#After_Dagor_Bragollach}

Another attack occurred seven years after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) when [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) sent an invasion of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum). It began with a bitter attack through the passes of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) and a siege of [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion). At the fortress [Galdor](</wiki/Galdor_(Lord_of_Dor-l%C3%B3min)> "Galdor (Lord of Dor-lómin)") fell, pierced by an arrow. [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) replaced his father in command, and drove the Orcs from the mountains and across the sands of [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith). However, an army also came from [Angband](/wiki/Angband) into Hithlum from the north, leading to battle upon the plains. Initially King [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) was outnumbered and hard-pressed until [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) sent a task force up the Firth of [Drengist](/wiki/Drengist) which fell upon the Orcs from the west. This broke the Orc-host, which fled to the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
