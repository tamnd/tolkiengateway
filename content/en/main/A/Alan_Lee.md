**Alan Lee** (born [20 August](../_/20_August.md) [1947](../_/1947.md)) is a renowned British artist. He has illustrated numerous [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) books and calendars. Together with [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md), Lee was a concept artist for both [*The Lord of the Rings*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

## History {#History}

Alan Lee was born in London, and attended the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ealing" title="wikipedia:Ealing">Ealing</a> School of Art.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the advice of a friend, he read *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* when he was 17, and it greatly influenced his professional work. He had at that time never heard of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) or *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He moved to Dartmoor and married Marja Kruÿt. They have one daughter together, Virginia.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Alan has suffered many deaths in his lifetime, including his father's when he was only 13 years of age.

In his paintings, Lee uses watercolour. He describes his style as a combination of <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realism_(visual_arts)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Realistic</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanticism" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Romantic</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Through the publishers, Lee has communicated with [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) in an effort to ensure he is happy with his illustrations. Lee sent through drafts and had discussions with him about what subjects he did and did not wish to see depicted by Lee. For *[Beren and Lúthien](../B/Beren_and_Lúthien.md)*, Christopher asked Lee to refrain from depicting [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), as he believed a figure as powerful as the first Dark Lord is better left described or hinted towards in the text.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Christopher once told Lee that his illustrations "didn’t change \[my\] father’s vision of Middle-earth, they just made it feel larger."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<h3><span id="Peter_Jackson.27s_Middle-earth_film_series"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Peter_Jackson&#39;s_Middle-earth_film_series">Peter Jackson&#39;s Middle-earth film series</span></h3>

On [November 13](../_/13_November.md), [1997](../_/1997.md), New Zealand filmmaker [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) sent a parcel containing <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0116344/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Forgotten Silver&#34;</a> and <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0110005/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">&#34;Heavenly Creatures&#34;</a>, and a letter explaining an upcoming movie project, then still for Miramax. Jackson was greatly inspired by Lee, and wanted him to be part of the crew. Lee watched the movies, and phoned back 3,5 hours later to say yes.<sup id="cite_ref-Lord_of_the_Drawings_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lord_of_the_Drawings-8">[8]</a></sup> Three weeks later he flew to New Zealand, where he and [John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md) joined the [Weta Workshop](../W/Weta_Workshop.md). He would live in New Zealand for six years, before returning to Dartmoor.<sup id="cite_ref-designing_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-designing-9">[9]</a></sup> His daughter Virginia also worked on the movie; one of her contributions was woodwork for the throne of [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md).<sup id="cite_ref-designing_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-designing-9">[9]</a></sup>

Lee has two cameos in *The Lord of the Rings* movies by Peter Jackson: he appears as the second from the right of the nine [Kings of Men](../N/Nazgûl.md) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, along with John Howe, and as a man of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) at the armoury of the [Hornburg](../H/Hornburg.md) in *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*, along with [Dan Hennah](../D/Dan_Hennah.md).

Lee expressed interest in working on *The Hobbit*, and stated that "It would have to be a pretty special film to match the experience of working on The Lord of the Rings".<sup id="cite_ref-Lord_of_the_Drawings_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lord_of_the_Drawings-8">[8]</a></sup> Lee was involved on work on [The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), once again along with John Howe.

Lee has one cameo too in *The Hobbit* movies by Peter Jackson: he appears as a man-musician of [Laketown](../L/Lake-town.md) in *[The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)*, along with John Howe.

Lee and Howe together drew the artwork of the cast at the end of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md)*. For *The Battle of the Five Armies*, the sketching of the cast was split by nationality; John Howe drew [Tauriel](../T/Tauriel.md) / [Evangeline Lilly](../E/Evangeline_Lilly.md), as they are both Canadian.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
