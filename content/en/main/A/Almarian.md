**Almarian** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[alˈmari.an]</span>) was the daughter of [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Queen to [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur), the fifth King of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), and mother to [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), the sixth King, and to [Ailinel](/wiki/Ailinel) and [Almiel](/wiki/Almiel). She was said to possess great beauty.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Almarian* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it contains <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Almare&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Almare (page does not exist)">almare</a></i> ("blessedness").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
