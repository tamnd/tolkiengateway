**Anardil** means "Devoted to the [Sun](/wiki/Sun)" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (from *[Anar](/wiki/Sun "Sun")*, "[Sun](/wiki/Sun)" and *-[dil](/wiki/Dil "Dil")*, "friend, lover, devoted to") and can refer to:

- [Anardil](/wiki/Anardil_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Anardil of Númenor") ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [700](/wiki/Second_Age_700 "Second Age 700") - [1098](/wiki/Second_Age_1098 "Second Age 1098")), who became the sixth [King](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor") of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) as [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion).
- [Anardil](</wiki/Anardil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Anardil (King of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [136](/wiki/Third_Age_136 "Third Age 136") - [411](/wiki/Third_Age_411 "Third Age 411")), sixth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
