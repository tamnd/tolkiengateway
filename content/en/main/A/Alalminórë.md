**Alalminórë** was the central region of the island of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ala_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ala-1">[1]</a></sup>

The region was regarded as the fairest part of the island, where many towns and villages of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were located. It was also where [Kortirion](/wiki/Kortirion), the chief city of Tol Eressëa was situated in,<sup id="cite_ref-Ala_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ala-1">[1]</a></sup> on the banks of the [Gliding Water](/wiki/Gliding_Water).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After the [March of Liberation](/wiki/March_of_Liberation) and the defeat of [Melko](/wiki/Melko), many of the exiled Elves and [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) moved to Alalminórë, led by [Ingil](/wiki/Ingil) son of [Inwë](/wiki/Inw%C3%AB). There, they built the city of Koromas, later known as *Kortirion* due to the [great tower](/wiki/Tirin_na_Gilweth "Tirin na Gilweth") that Ingil built there at the topmost part of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Ala_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ala-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was also where the [Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/Cottage_of_Lost_Play) could be found, which [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), the [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") mariner, visited on his sojourn in Tol Eressëa.<sup id="cite_ref-Ala_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ala-1">[1]</a></sup> Another place in the city that he visited was the home of [Meril-i-Turinqi](/wiki/Meril-i-Turinqi),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> the Lady of Tol Eressëa, at the foot of Ingil's tower in a *[korin](/wiki/Korin "Korin")* of [elms](/wiki/Elms "Elms").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> A famous half-[fay](/wiki/Fays "Fays") [minstrel](/wiki/Minstrels "Minstrels"), [Tinfang](/wiki/Tinfang), loved to visit Alalminórë above all the other parts of the island.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Sometime during the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_century" title="wikipedia:5th century">5th century AD</a>, following the disastrous [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth) and the dragging of Tol Eressëa across the Ocean to be anchored off the coast of the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), [Men](/wiki/Men) invaded the island, along with other evil creatures, such as [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls), which resulted in the displacement and eventual fading of the Elves on the island.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

However, not all Men were hostile to the Elves, and these became the ancestors of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_people" title="wikipedia:English people">English</a>. One of them was [Hengest](/wiki/Hengest_and_Horsa "Hengest and Horsa") son of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), who invaded Kortirion, and made it his capital, afterwards known as *Warwíc* and eventually as *Warwick* - with Alalminórë, the region surrounding Warwick, becoming known as *Warwickshire*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Etymology and names {#Etymology_and_names}

The name *Alalminórë* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), meaning the "Land of [Elms](/wiki/Elms)". It consists of the elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alalm%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alalmë (page does not exist)">alalmë</a></i> ("elm") + *[nórë](/wiki/N%C3%B3r%C3%AB "Nórë")* ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup> Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate is <i><b>Dor Lalmin</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> with earlier forms of the name being *Lalmindor* and *Dor na Lalmion*.<sup id="cite_ref-Los_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Los-12">[11]</a></sup>

It was also called <i><b>Gar Lossion</b></i> in Gnomish, meaning the "Place of Flowers", from *[gar](/wiki/Gar "Gar")* ("place, district") + *lossion*, a plural of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%B4s&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lôs (page does not exist)">lôs</a></i> ("flower").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup> The earlier forms of the name were *Losgar*,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup> *Lothrod*<sup id="cite_ref-Los_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Los-12">[11]</a></sup> and *Losior*.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### Early legendarium {#Early_legendarium}

In some of the outlines for the continuation of *The Book of Lost Tales*, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) decided to completely change the nature of the framework of the tales. In that reimagining of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), Tol Eressëa ceased to be identified with <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>/<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Isles" title="wikipedia:British Isles">British Isles</a> - instead, it became a completely different island, and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain" title="wikipedia:Great Britain">Great Britain</a> became the island of [Luthany](/wiki/Luthany), while the mariner that came there was called [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_people" title="wikipedia:English people">Englishman</a> sailing *from* England.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[16]</a></sup>

So, as opposed to the previous conception, where the Elves of the Great Lands sailed directly to Tol Eressëa following the defeat of Melko in the March of Liberation, the Elves now sailed to Luthany instead.

There, they built many cities and settlements - and, after their *eventual* return to Tol Eressëa, named many places there *after* the ones in Luthany. For example, the city of Kortirion (the New) in Tol Eressëa was named after Kortirion the Old (i.e. Warwick) in Luthany.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[17]</a></sup>

In one text from c. [1920](/wiki/1920), called *[Ælfwine of England](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine_of_England)*, **Alalminórë** (Warwickshire) is mentioned as the region surrounding the town of Kortirion (the Old) ruled by [Óswine](</wiki/%C3%93swine_(Prince_of_Gwar)> "Óswine (Prince of Gwar)"), the Prince of Gwar, who was friendly to the Elves that still lived on the island, many of whom congregated in Alalminórë.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[18]</a></sup>

### Later legendarium {#Later_legendarium}

In the later [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), there appears the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name **Alalvinórë**, glossed as the "Land of Many Elms".<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[19]</a></sup> It consists of the elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alalv%C3%ABa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alalvëa (page does not exist)">alalvëa</a></i> ("having many elms") + *[nórë](/wiki/N%C3%B3r%C3%AB "Nórë")* ("land, country").

Since this name was never used in any narrative, however, it is unclear how it would have been applied in the post-*[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* mythology.

The name itself was changed from the earlier forms *Alalminórë* and *Alalbinórë*.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[20]</a></sup>

The place (identified there with Warwickshire) was also called <i><b>Alalminor</b></i> in the poem *[The Trees of Kortirion](/wiki/The_Trees_of_Kortirion)* from c. [1962](/wiki/1962).<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[21]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Alalminórë was inspired by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwickshire" title="wikipedia:Warwickshire">Warwickshire</a>, a region of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a> in which the town of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warwick" title="wikipedia:Warwick">Warwick</a> (which served as the inspiration for the city of Kortirion) is situated in.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[22]</a></sup>
