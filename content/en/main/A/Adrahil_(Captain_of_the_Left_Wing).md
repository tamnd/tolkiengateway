**Adrahil** was a [Gondorian](../G/Gondorian.md) nobleman<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup> who was a captain in [King](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md)'s army in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md).

## History {#History}

As a descendant of the noble [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) family that settled in [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md) in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), he was an ancestor of the line of the [Princes of Dol Amroth](../P/Princes_of_Dol_Amroth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CirionNote_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CirionNote-3">[2]</a></sup>

When King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) led his army to face the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) who threatened [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), Adrahil commanded the army's left wing. The soldiers of the right wing were under the command of the King's nephew [Minohtar](../M/Minohtar.md), while the centre was commanded by the king himself and his heir [Artamir](Artamir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Ondoher's enterprise came to a devastating end: he was surprised by the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md), and his forces were overwhelmed. Adrahil's soldiers, however, were shielded from the attack and survived it. Ondoher and Artamir had both been killed, and Minohtar took charge, sending Adrahil along with both his own command of the Left Wing and those at the rear of the Right Wing who had not yet been engaged.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Adrahil withdrew south into [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) to form a defensive line from [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md) to the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md). With these forces he was to cover the approaches of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and allow Minohtar to form a rearguard and attempt to stem the advance of the Wainriders. Adrahil should at once message [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md), and inform him of the disaster of the Morannon and of the position of the retreating Northern Army.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-1">[1]</a></sup>

Adrahil's fate is uncertain; the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) eventually broke through [Minohtar](../M/Minohtar.md)'s more northerly defence, but whether they penetrated to Adrahil's position is unknown. Nothing more is said of him.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the chapter "[Cirion and Eorl](../C/Cirion_and_Eorl_and_the_Friendship_of_Gondor_and_Rohan.md)" of *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, Adrahil is referred to as *"Adrahil of Dol Amroth"*. However, since [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md) would not be named as such until after the drowning of [Amroth](Amroth.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md) this must be an anachronistic error on the part of the chronicler.<sup id="cite_ref-CirionNote_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CirionNote-3">[2]</a></sup>
