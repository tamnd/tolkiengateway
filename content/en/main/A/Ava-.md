<i><b>ava-</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) prefix indicating something forbidden. An example is **avaquétima** "not to be said, that must not be said", **avanyárima** "not to be told or related.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>
