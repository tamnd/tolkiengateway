**Anfauglith** was the charred desert to the north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), created by the fires of [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) during the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) ("Battle of Sudden Flame") in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [455](../F/First_Age_455.md). It was originally the green plain of [Ard-galen](Ard-galen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup> Anfauglith remained barren until after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), when one hill, the [Haudh-en-Ndengin](../H/Haudh-en-Ndengin.md) or "Hill of Slain", again was covered with long green grass.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was upon Anfauglith that [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) assembled his whole power in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) yet they were conquered by the hosts of the West.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anfauglith* means "By Gasping Dust" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup> from *[an](An.md)-* ("with, by") + *[faug](../F/Faug.md)* ("thirsty, gasping"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>) + *[lith](../L/Lith.md)* ("ash, sand, dust"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>).

Another name was <i><b>Dor-nu-Fauglith</b></i> translated as "Land under Choking Ash".<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup>
