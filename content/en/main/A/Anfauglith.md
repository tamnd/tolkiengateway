**Anfauglith** was the charred desert to the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), created by the fires of [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) ("Battle of Sudden Flame") in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"). It was originally the green plain of [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen).<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup> Anfauglith remained barren until after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), when one hill, the [Haudh-en-Ndengin](/wiki/Haudh-en-Ndengin) or "Hill of Slain", again was covered with long green grass.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was upon Anfauglith that [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) assembled his whole power in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) yet they were conquered by the hosts of the West.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Anfauglith* means "By Gasping Dust" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup> from *[an](/wiki/An "An")-* ("with, by") + *[faug](/wiki/Faug "Faug")* ("thirsty, gasping"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>) + *[lith](/wiki/Lith "Lith")* ("ash, sand, dust"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>).

Another name was <i><b>Dor-nu-Fauglith</b></i> translated as "Land under Choking Ash".<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-1">[1]</a></sup>
