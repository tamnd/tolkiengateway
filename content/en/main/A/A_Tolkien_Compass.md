<i><b>A Tolkien Compass: Fascinating Studies and Interpretations of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Most Popular Epic Fantasies</b></i> is a collection of essays on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings, edited by [Jared Lobdell](../J/Jared_Lobdell.md). Most contributors were, like Lobdell, part of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=University_of_Wisconsin_Tolkien_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="University of Wisconsin Tolkien Society (page does not exist)">University of Wisconsin Tolkien Society</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The first edition was published in [1975](../_/1975.md), a second printing followed in [1980](../_/1980.md) and then a revised second edition was released in [2003](../_/2003.md). The first edition also contained Tolkien's *[Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings](../G/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (pp. 153–201), but this has been omitted from the second edition due to complaints from the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md), who are the legal owners of the text.

## First Edition ([1975](../_/1975.md)) {#First_Edition_(1975)}

### From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>For those who have dismissed Tolkien&#39;s writings as simply escapist literature, the sophisticated tome and perceptive, probing insights of most of the collected articles may be an awakening to a deeper appreciation of these complex classics
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>The A.R.E. Journal</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

The intricacies of Tolkien's puns, the moral geography of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), the true character of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) and the hobbits' friends and foes, the sheer musical beauty of the names Tolkien invents - all are given careful attention. Tolkien's consuming involvement in his books merits this loving exactness.

[W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md) wrote that he was especially astounded by Tolkien's gift for inventing proper names. Auden would have avidly studied the final offering of this book. For here is a true polestar for all wondering Tolkien fans: Tolkien's own unpublished "[Guide to the Names in *The Lord of the Rings*](../G/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)." Originally, Tolkien prepared this guide for translators of his work. Here, he shares his dragon-hoard of etymological treasures. His asides to the reader on how he came to choose some of the names bring us into Tolkien's own studyroom.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Second Edition ([2003](../_/2003.md)) {#Second_Edition_(2003)}

### From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher_2}

*A Tolkien Compass* offers surprising insights into the mind of the creator of *Lord of the Rings*. Tolkien's political, religious, cosmological, and psychological principles are explored by ten writers with different backgrounds and divergent approaches. As leading authority [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md) observes in his foreword, *A Tolkien Compass* lives up to its name by providing vital guidance to both new and seasoned travelers in Middle-earth.
