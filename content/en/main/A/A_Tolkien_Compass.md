<i><b>A Tolkien Compass: Fascinating Studies and Interpretations of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Most Popular Epic Fantasies</b></i> is a collection of essays on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s writings, edited by [Jared Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell). Most contributors were, like Lobdell, part of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=University_of_Wisconsin_Tolkien_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="University of Wisconsin Tolkien Society (page does not exist)">University of Wisconsin Tolkien Society</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The first edition was published in [1975](/wiki/1975), a second printing followed in [1980](/wiki/1980) and then a revised second edition was released in [2003](/wiki/2003). The first edition also contained Tolkien's *[Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (pp. 153–201), but this has been omitted from the second edition due to complaints from the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate), who are the legal owners of the text.

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The intricacies of Tolkien's puns, the moral geography of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the true character of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) and the hobbits' friends and foes, the sheer musical beauty of the names Tolkien invents - all are given careful attention. Tolkien's consuming involvement in his books merits this loving exactness.

[W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden) wrote that he was especially astounded by Tolkien's gift for inventing proper names. Auden would have avidly studied the final offering of this book. For here is a true polestar for all wondering Tolkien fans: Tolkien's own unpublished "[Guide to the Names in *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings")." Originally, Tolkien prepared this guide for translators of his work. Here, he shares his dragon-hoard of etymological treasures. His asides to the reader on how he came to choose some of the names bring us into Tolkien's own studyroom.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Second Edition ([2003](/wiki/2003)) {#Second_Edition_(2003)}

### From the Publisher {#From_the_Publisher_2}

*A Tolkien Compass* offers surprising insights into the mind of the creator of *Lord of the Rings*. Tolkien's political, religious, cosmological, and psychological principles are explored by ten writers with different backgrounds and divergent approaches. As leading authority [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) observes in his foreword, *A Tolkien Compass* lives up to its name by providing vital guidance to both new and seasoned travelers in Middle-earth.
