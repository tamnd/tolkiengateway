<i><b>Arcastar</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[arˈkastar]</span>) is the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) rendition of the surname [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) (German *Tollkühn* "foolhardy, reckless, desperately brave, rushbold").

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) signed as *Arcastar [Mondósaresse](../M/Mondósar.md)* "Tolkien in Oxford" in a [1968](../_/1968.md) letter to the producer of the [BBC](../B/BBC.md) documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The exact meaning of *Arcastar* is unknown. [Ryszard Derdziński](../R/Ryszard_Derdziński.md) has suggested that *arcastar* could possibly mean "translator" (or "one who carries across"), based on his research about the [Tolkien family](../T/Tolkien_Family.md) name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
