<i><b>Arcastar</b></i> (pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[arˈkastar]</span>) is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) rendition of the surname [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") (German *Tollkühn* "foolhardy, reckless, desperately brave, rushbold").

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) signed as *Arcastar [Mondósaresse](/wiki/Mond%C3%B3sar "Mondósar")* "Tolkien in Oxford" in a [1968](/wiki/1968) letter to the producer of the [BBC](/wiki/BBC) documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The exact meaning of *Arcastar* is unknown. [Ryszard Derdziński](/wiki/Ryszard_Derdzi%C5%84ski) has suggested that *arcastar* could possibly mean "translator" (or "one who carries across"), based on his research about the [Tolkien family](/wiki/Tolkien_Family "Tolkien Family") name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
