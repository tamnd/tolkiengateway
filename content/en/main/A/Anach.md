The **Pass of Anach** (from *[an](/wiki/An "An")* = "long") was a long and narrow pass that ran down out of the highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), cutting south-west between the near-impassable mountains of the [Crissaegrim](/wiki/Crissaegrim) and [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth). Towards its southern end, where it opened into the land of [Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar), were the springs of the River [Mindeb](/wiki/Mindeb).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the later years of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), Dorthonion fell under [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s control, and with it the Pass of Anach. His [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) began to use it as a way down from the highlands, building a road through the valley to reach and harass the lands beyond.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Ultimately they succeeded in conquering not only [Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar), but also the regions to the east that ran along the borders of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).
