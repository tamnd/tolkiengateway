The **Pass of Anach** (from *[an](An.md)* = "long") was a long and narrow pass that ran down out of the highlands of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), cutting south-west between the near-impassable mountains of the [Crissaegrim](../C/Crissaegrim.md) and [Ered Gorgoroth](../E/Ered_Gorgoroth.md). Towards its southern end, where it opened into the land of [Dimbar](../D/Dimbar.md), were the springs of the River [Mindeb](../M/Mindeb.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the later years of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), Dorthonion fell under [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s control, and with it the Pass of Anach. His [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) began to use it as a way down from the highlands, building a road through the valley to reach and harass the lands beyond.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Ultimately they succeeded in conquering not only [Dimbar](../D/Dimbar.md), but also the regions to the east that ran along the borders of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).
