The river **Adurant** was the southernmost of the seven rivers of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand) marking its southern boundary. It ran from the southern [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) into the river [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion), marking the southern boundary of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand). Halfway along its course the river divided and later rejoined, forming the island of [Tol Galen](/wiki/Tol_Galen).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "Doublestream" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[adu](/wiki/Adu "Adu")* ("double") + *[rant](/wiki/Rant "Rant")* ("course"), referring to a parting of the river about the isle of Tol Galen.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
