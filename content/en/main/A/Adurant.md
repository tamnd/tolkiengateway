The river **Adurant** was the southernmost of the seven rivers of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md) marking its southern boundary. It ran from the southern [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) into the river [Gelion](../G/Gelion.md), marking the southern boundary of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). Halfway along its course the river divided and later rejoined, forming the island of [Tol Galen](../T/Tol_Galen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "Doublestream" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[adu](Adu.md)* ("double") + *[rant](../R/Rant.md)* ("course"), referring to a parting of the river about the isle of Tol Galen.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
