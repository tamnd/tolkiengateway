**Albert Dreary** was a drunken [Bree-lander](/wiki/Bree-landers "Bree-landers") outside the [Prancing Pony Inn](/wiki/Prancing_Pony_Inn). Albert Dreary was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson).

## History {#History}

Albert Dreary was a drunken man in [Bree](/wiki/Bree) outside the [Prancing Pony Inn](/wiki/Prancing_Pony_Inn). When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and the other four [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) entered the town, he was seen munching loudly on a carrot.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
