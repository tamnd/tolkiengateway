The word <i><b>ar</b></i> or <i><b>âr</b></i> can have various meanings:

## Root {#Root}

- [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md): [AR](<AR_(root).md>)

## [Adûnaic](Adûnaic.md) {#Adûnaic}

1. A prefix used to denominate [kingship](../K/King_of_Númenor.md), equivalent to [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[Tar-](../T/Tar.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-ara_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ara-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

It means "king" and is a prefixal form of *ârû* ("king") or derived from the same root. Obviously from Elvish.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) {#Quenya}

1. "And"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>
2. "Beside, outside, without"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=ADA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ADA (page does not exist)">ADA</a><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

## [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) {#Sindarin}

1. "Beside, without" (archaic)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
2. A shorter form of *[ara](Ara.md)*, "high, lofty, noble"<sup id="cite_ref-ara_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ara-1">[1]</a></sup>
3. **Âr** "king (used of a lord or king of a specified region)"

## [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) {#Noldorin}

Tolkien apparently used the word *ar* to translate "and" in a Noldorin phrase inscribed on a draft of Thrór's Map.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-H1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H1-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Th_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Th-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- Phrase on a draft of [Thrór's Map](../T/Thrór's_Map.md): "<i><a href="../L/Lheben.md" title="Lheben">Lheben</a> <a href="../T/Tâl.md" title="Tâl">teil</a> <a href="../B/Brand_(word).md" title="Brand (word)">brann</a> <a href="../I/I.md" title="I">i</a> <a href="Annon.md" title="Annon">annon</a> ar <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Neledh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neledh (page does not exist)">neledh</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Neledhi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neledhi (page does not exist)">neledhi</a> <a href="../G/Gar.md" title="Gar">gar</a> <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Godrebh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Godrebh (page does not exist)">godrebh</a></i>" ("Five feet **high** the gate and three by three they go through together"<sup id="cite_ref-AI_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-5">[5]</a></sup> or "Five feet **high** the door and three may walk abreast"<sup id="cite_ref-H1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H1-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Th_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Th-7">[7]</a></sup>)

## [Westron](../W/Westron.md) {#Westron}

1. The name of the twenty-first [Tengwa](../T/Tengwa.md).
