The word <i><b>ar</b></i> or <i><b>âr</b></i> can have various meanings:

## Root {#Root}

- [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"): [AR](</wiki/AR_(root)> "AR (root)")

## [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) {#Adûnaic}

1. A prefix used to denominate [kingship](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor "King of Númenor"), equivalent to [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[Tar-](/wiki/Tar "Tar")*<sup id="cite_ref-ara_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ara-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

It means "king" and is a prefixal form of *ârû* ("king") or derived from the same root. Obviously from Elvish.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) {#Quenya}

1. "And"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>
2. "Beside, outside, without"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=ADA&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ADA (page does not exist)">ADA</a><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

## [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) {#Sindarin}

1. "Beside, without" (archaic)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
2. A shorter form of *[ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara")*, "high, lofty, noble"<sup id="cite_ref-ara_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ara-1">[1]</a></sup>
3. **Âr** "king (used of a lord or king of a specified region)"

## [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) {#Noldorin}

Tolkien apparently used the word *ar* to translate "and" in a Noldorin phrase inscribed on a draft of Thrór's Map.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-H1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H1-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Th_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Th-7">[7]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- Phrase on a draft of [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map): "<i><a href="/wiki/Lheben" title="Lheben">Lheben</a> <a href="/wiki/T%C3%A2l" title="Tâl">teil</a> <a href="/wiki/Brand_(word)" title="Brand (word)">brann</a> <a href="/wiki/I" title="I">i</a> <a href="/wiki/Annon" title="Annon">annon</a> ar <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Neledh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neledh (page does not exist)">neledh</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Neledhi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neledhi (page does not exist)">neledhi</a> <a href="/wiki/Gar" title="Gar">gar</a> <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Godrebh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Godrebh (page does not exist)">godrebh</a></i>" ("Five feet **high** the gate and three by three they go through together"<sup id="cite_ref-AI_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-5">[5]</a></sup> or "Five feet **high** the door and three may walk abreast"<sup id="cite_ref-H1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H1-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Th_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Th-7">[7]</a></sup>)

## [Westron](/wiki/Westron) {#Westron}

1. The name of the twenty-first [Tengwa](/wiki/Tengwa).
