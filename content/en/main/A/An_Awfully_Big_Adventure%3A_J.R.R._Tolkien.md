<i><b>An Awfully Big Adventure: J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> is the fourth episode of *An Awfully Big Adventure*, a documentary miniseries about authors of children's literature produced by the BBC. The episode is fully dedicated to J.R.R. Tolkien and features interviews with notable people such as [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md), [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), [Alan Lee](Alan_Lee.md), [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md), [Wayne Hammond](../W/Wayne_Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md). Archival recordings of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) (in the latter's case footage from [J.R.R.T.: A Film Portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R.T.%253A_A_Film_Portrait_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)) are also featured.
