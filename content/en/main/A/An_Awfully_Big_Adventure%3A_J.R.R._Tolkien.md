<i><b>An Awfully Big Adventure: J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> is the fourth episode of *An Awfully Big Adventure*, a documentary miniseries about authors of children's literature produced by the BBC. The episode is fully dedicated to J.R.R. Tolkien and features interviews with notable people such as [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter), [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), [Alan Lee](/wiki/Alan_Lee), [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey), [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger), [Wayne Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull). Archival recordings of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) (in the latter's case footage from [J.R.R.T.: A Film Portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R.T.:_A_Film_Portrait_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)) are also featured.
