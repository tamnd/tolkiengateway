**Atarinya** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) term meaning 'my father'. It is recorded twice in [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s work, used once by [Elendur](<../E/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur).md>) to his father [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and also long before by [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) to his father [Meneldur](../M/Meneldur.md) in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup> In both cases, the opposite form would be [onya](../O/Onya.md), 'my son'. The same word-ending could be used to create affectionate forms from personal names: for example, [Meneldur](../M/Meneldur.md) also called [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) [Anardilya](Anardilya.md) ('my Anardil' - with Anardil being [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md)'s original name).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
