**Atarinya** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) term meaning 'my father'. It is recorded twice in [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s work, used once by [Elendur](</wiki/Elendur_(son_of_Isildur)> "Elendur (son of Isildur)") to his father [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and also long before by [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") to his father [Meneldur](/wiki/Meneldur) in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup> In both cases, the opposite form would be [onya](/wiki/Onya "Onya"), 'my son'. The same word-ending could be used to create affectionate forms from personal names: for example, [Meneldur](/wiki/Meneldur) also called [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") [Anardilya](/wiki/Anardilya) ('my Anardil' - with Anardil being [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion)'s original name).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
