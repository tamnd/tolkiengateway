**Arador** ([S](../S/Sindarin.md), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈarador]</span>; [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2820](../T/Third_Age_2820.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [2930](../T/Third_Age_2930.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 110) was the fourteenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftain_of_the_Dúnedain.md).

## History {#History}

Arador succeeded his father, [Argonui](Argonui.md), when he died in [2912](../T/Third_Age_2912.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In his time many [Dúnedain of the North](../D/Dúnedain_of_the_North.md) lived in woods between the river [Hoarwell](../H/Hoarwell.md) and the river [Loudwater](../L/Loudwater.md). Arador lived in a house north of the [Trollshaws](../T/Trollshaws.md) in woods near the river [Hoarwell](../H/Hoarwell.md), which was threatened by a group of [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

His rule was uneventful except for the fact that he was captured by [Hill-trolls](../H/Hill-trolls.md) in the [Coldfells](../C/Coldfells.md) north of [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and killed by them.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He was succeeded by his young son, [Arathorn II](Arathorn_II.md), in [2930](../T/Third_Age_2930.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arador* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Noble-lord" and is a compound of the prefix *[ar(a)-](Ara.md)* ("noble", "royal", "high") and the [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) form *-dor* of *taur* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Royal Lord".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
