**Arador** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈarador]</span>; [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2820](/wiki/Third_Age_2820 "Third Age 2820")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> - [2930](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> died aged 110) was the fourteenth [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftain_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain).

## History {#History}

Arador succeeded his father, [Argonui](/wiki/Argonui), when he died in [2912](/wiki/Third_Age_2912 "Third Age 2912").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In his time many [Dúnedain of the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_the_North) lived in woods between the river [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell) and the river [Loudwater](/wiki/Loudwater). Arador lived in a house north of the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws) in woods near the river [Hoarwell](/wiki/Hoarwell), which was threatened by a group of [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

His rule was uneventful except for the fact that he was captured by [Hill-trolls](/wiki/Hill-trolls) in the [Coldfells](/wiki/Coldfells) north of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and killed by them.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> He was succeeded by his young son, [Arathorn II](/wiki/Arathorn_II), in [2930](/wiki/Third_Age_2930 "Third Age 2930").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Arador* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Noble-lord" and is a compound of the prefix *[ar(a)-](/wiki/Ara "Ara")* ("noble", "royal", "high") and the [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form *-dor* of *taur* ("lord").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The Association Tolkiendil suggests it means "Royal Lord".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
