**Andrast** was a long cape in the south-west of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) at the end of the northern arm of the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) between the rivers [Isen](/wiki/Isen) and [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> The [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) ended their southward bend at the cape of Andrast.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup>

The cape of Andrast was never occupied by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor") of Gondor with permanent settlements. However, they maintained a coast guard force and beacons at the end of the cape of Andrast.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> At the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the Men of the [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas) believed that some of the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain) still lived in the mountains of Andrast secretly.<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-5">[5]</a></sup> The lands of the Drúedain were on the western side of the mountains of Andrast and extended north along the coast on to the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen),<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> and were collectively known as [Drúwaith Iaur](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur) or "Old Púkel land"<sup id="cite_ref-Note13_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note13-6">[6]</a></sup> or "Old Púkel-wilderness".<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-5">[5]</a></sup> After the [Battles of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Battles_of_the_Fords_of_Isen) many Drúedain came out of the caves where they lived in the Drúwaith Iaur and attacked remnants of Saruman's forces that had been driven away to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-Note13_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note13-6">[6]</a></sup> Drúwaith Iaur remained a wilderness at the end of the Third Age and was not inhabited by the Men of Gondor or the Men of Rohan and any of them entered it seldom.<sup id="cite_ref-FurtherNotes_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FurtherNotes-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Andrast* (earlier form *Angast*) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "Long Cape".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
