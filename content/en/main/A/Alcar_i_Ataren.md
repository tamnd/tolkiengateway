**Alcar i Ataren** is [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s unfinished translation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Patri" title="wikipedia:Gloria Patri">Gloria Patri</a> into [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) which he wrote in the 1950s. The manuscript was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 43](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_43 "Vinyar Tengwar 43"), January 2002, edited by [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne), [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith), and [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter).

## Prayer {#Prayer}

<dl><dd><i>Alcar i ataren ar i yondon ar i <a href="/wiki/Airef%C3%ABa" title="Airefëa">airefean</a>.</i></dd>
<dd>&#34;Glory [be] to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.&#34;</dd></dl>
