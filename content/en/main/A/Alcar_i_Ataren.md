**Alcar i Ataren** is [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s unfinished translation of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Patri" title="wikipedia:Gloria Patri">Gloria Patri</a> into [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) which he wrote in the 1950s. The manuscript was published in [*Vinyar Tengwar*, Number 43](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar_43.md), January 2002, edited by [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md), [Arden R. Smith](Arden_R._Smith.md), and [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md).

## Prayer {#Prayer}

<dl><dd><i>Alcar i ataren ar i yondon ar i <a href="Airefëa.md" title="Airefëa">airefean</a>.</i></dd>
