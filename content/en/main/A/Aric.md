**Aric** is one of the main characters in *[The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Return_to_Moria)*. He is a white [raven](/wiki/Ravens "Ravens") of [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") belonging to the rookery of Lord [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli)'s Company. Aric's family originally hails from [Ravenhill](/wiki/Ravenhill).

## History {#History}

During the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), Aric assisted a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") who was employed by Lord Gimli on an excavation of Moria and would later become the Lord of Moria. This Dwarf had become trapped in Moria, and Aric helped them escape to the surface.
