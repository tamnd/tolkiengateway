**Anna Grob** is a Austrian musician and singer who has made a rock album inspired by Tuor and the fall of Gondolin, called 'Circle of Light', a Faërie rock opera. On January 25, 2026 she played some of her songs at [Ozmoot 2026](../O/Ozmoot_2026.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

As of August 2026 she has posted three of the songs, 'The Messenger', 'Intuition' and 'The King'. The album is officially releasing on November 20, 2026.
