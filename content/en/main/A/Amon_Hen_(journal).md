<i><b>Amon Hen</b></i> is the bulletin of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). Published bimonthly continually since [1972](../_/1972.md), *Amon Hen* contains Tolkien-related news, reviews, articles, event details, artwork, letters, and features.

Notable contributors to *Amon Hen* have included: [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), [Ted Nasmith](../T/Ted_Nasmith.md), [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md), [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md), [Roger Lancelyn Green](../R/Roger_Lancelyn_Green.md), [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) and [Brian Sibley](../B/Brian_Sibley.md).
