<i><b>Amon Hen</b></i> is the bulletin of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). Published bimonthly continually since [1972](/wiki/1972), *Amon Hen* contains Tolkien-related news, reviews, articles, event details, artwork, letters, and features.

Notable contributors to *Amon Hen* have included: [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith), [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey), [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond), [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull), [Roger Lancelyn Green](/wiki/Roger_Lancelyn_Green), [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) and [Brian Sibley](/wiki/Brian_Sibley).
