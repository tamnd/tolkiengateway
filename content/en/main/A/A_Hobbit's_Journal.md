<i><b>A Hobbit&#39;s Journal</b></i> is a book with blank pages decorated by illustrations depicting various scenes from *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, intended to be used as a notebook. It also contains a one-page introductory text.
