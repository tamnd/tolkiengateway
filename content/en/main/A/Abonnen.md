<i><b>abonnen</b></i> (*pl. ebœnnin*) is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning:

1. *adj.* Born later, born after
2. *n.* Man, one born later than the elves, a human being

Derived from *[ab-](Ab-.md)* + *[onnen](../O/Onnen.md)* "after-born".
