**Alexander Cameron** was an occasional member of the [Notion Club](../N/Notion_Club.md).

## History {#History}

Alexander Cameron was educated at Exeter and became a modern historian, especially interested in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_history" title="wikipedia:Spanish history">Spanish</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_American_history" title="wikipedia:South American history">South American</a> history. His interests included collecting coins and stamps and playing a pianola.

Other members do not seem to remember his being invited to join the Club, and he never signed the Club's records as was the habit among the other members.<sup id="cite_ref-List_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-List-1">[1]</a></sup>
