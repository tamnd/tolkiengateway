**Andreas Möhn** (born [1964](/wiki/1964)) is a German freelance writer and editor. He first used the pseudonym "Lalaith"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and now uses the pseudonym "Codex Regius" together with his wife, Metka Klemenčič.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

He has an on-line presence concerning the study of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s [Arda](/wiki/Arda) by participating on the [Tolklang](/wiki/Tolklang) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Mailing_List&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Mailing List (page does not exist)">Tolkien Mailing Lists</a>. His website, <i><a class="external text" href="http://lalaithmesp.blogspot.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Lalaith&#39;s Middle-earth Science Pages</a></i>, is centred on more obscure aspects of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") and scientific reasoning on several information Tolkien gives out. His essays concern [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) tongues, calendaric calculations, and various geographical topics of [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

In October [2014](/wiki/2014) some of the articles were removed from the site, and were updated and provided as purchasable books. By [2016](/wiki/2016) the Science Pages were moved to another site as blog articles. The content of the books about Middle-earth largely replicates the articles from Lalaith's Middle-earth Sciences page.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
