<i><b>ArdaCraft</b></i> is a <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Minecraft</a> build server recreating the world of J.R.R. Tolkien's [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The project began on 15 November 2014 and as of August 2024 has completed around a third of Middle-earth at a 1:58 scale. The final size of the map is estimated at around 2,300 square kilometres (\~890 square miles), making it the largest re-creation of Middle-earth ever attempted, outside of works relying on <a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procedural_generation" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">procedural generation</a>.

## Conception {#Conception}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a3/ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg/300px-ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/a/a3/ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg/450px-ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/a/a3/ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg/600px-ArdaCraft_Rivendell.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2287" data-file-height="1286"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a> was completed in 2018</figcaption></figure>

The project was founded by players Fornad and dags, who believed that they could use Minecraft to re-create Middle-earth in the late Third Age in a larger and more accurate way than had ever been attempted before in any video game. Given its popularity and shallow learning curve, Minecraft was a good medium in which to work. However, the limitations of the game led ArdaCraft towards a modded approach, where it was able to use more textures and models than were available in the normal game. Eventually it adopted the <a class="external text" href="http://conquestreforged.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Conquest Reforged mod</a> in order to do this.

The project is run by volunteers and funded by donations, and is an entirely non-profit endeavour.

## Approach {#Approach}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/2/26/ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png/300px-ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="200" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/2/26/ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png/450px-ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/2/26/ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png/600px-ArdaCraft_Hobbiton.png 2x" data-file-width="3062" data-file-height="2037"/></a><figcaption>ArdaCraft&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Hobbiton" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a> drew heavily upon Tolkien&#39;s watercolour</figcaption></figure>

Despite being built in the blocky environment of Minecraft, ArdaCraft focuses on geological, botanical, and historical research in order to make its world as believable as possible. This includes researching historical farming techniques, understanding the likely climate of various areas in order to predict the kinds of plant species which should occur there, and examining analagous historical cultures for various regions - for example, Iron Age Celts for [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), the Anglo-Saxons for [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), or various ancient Mediterranean cultures (particularly the Eastern Roman Empire) for [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

In addition, a heavy emphasis is placed upon lore accuracy. Tolkien's legendarium, letters and sketches are all analysed to create as good of a representation of his world as possible, given the constraints of the game.

Other details are added to the world to help explorers feel more immersed. For instance, the project has its own soundtrack, composed by volunteers. The sun angle changes in the sky as the player gets further south, corresponding to the lines of latitude indicated by Tolkien. Real-world constellations that are below the horizon in the north are revealed in the south.

## Progress {#Progress}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Middle-earth_DEM_(ArdaCraft).png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/c5/Middle-earth_DEM_%28ArdaCraft%29.png/300px-Middle-earth_DEM_%28ArdaCraft%29.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="241" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/c/c5/Middle-earth_DEM_%28ArdaCraft%29.png/450px-Middle-earth_DEM_%28ArdaCraft%29.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/c/c5/Middle-earth_DEM_%28ArdaCraft%29.png/600px-Middle-earth_DEM_%28ArdaCraft%29.png 2x" data-file-width="1183" data-file-height="951"/></a><figcaption>ArdaCraft&#39;s Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of Middle-earth is the most detailed ever made</figcaption></figure>

As of August 2024, regions and locations which have been completed or are being worked on include [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire), The [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains), [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land), [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), the [Angle](/wiki/Angle), [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel), [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin), [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard), [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland), [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad), [Moria](/wiki/Moria), and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). Regions such as [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) and [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) are planned for the future.

They have also created the most detailed heightmap of Middle-earth ever made during the course of their work, which is in the early stages of being used for the [Digital Tolkien Project](/wiki/Digital_Tolkien_Project). It aims for accuracy with the texts above all else, with the various - often contradictory - maps of Middle-earth published by J.R.R. Tolkien and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) acting as a secondary source. As it is a three-dimensional map, the sightlines described in the text have also been taken into account to ensure complete accuracy.

ArdaCraft's founder presented the project's work to the [Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society "The Tolkien Society") at [Oxonmoot](/wiki/Oxonmoot) 2024, and the project was nominated for the [Tolkien Society Award](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society_Awards "The Tolkien Society Awards") for Best Online Content in 2025 and 2026.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
