**Alex Lewis**, born and raised in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md), England, joined [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) in [1976](../_/1976.md) and was the fifth chairman of the society during [1988](../_/1988.md)-[1992](../_/1992.md). He was also the editor for the magazine <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Nigglings&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nigglings (page does not exist)">Nigglings</a></i>.

He is also a composer known for his song cycles inspired by Tolkien’s First Age tales, The Fall of Gondolin and The Flight of the Noldor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
