The **Australian Tolkien Society** was founded by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Paul_Novitski&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Paul Novitski (page does not exist)">Paul Novitski</a>, a member of the [Tolkien Society of America](../T/Tolkien_Society_of_America.md) in [1967](../_/1967.md) while on a trip to Australia. The society produced the Fanzine *[Carandaith](../C/Carandaith.md)* and The Tolkien Bulletin (originally called the Australian Tolkien Society Newsletter).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
