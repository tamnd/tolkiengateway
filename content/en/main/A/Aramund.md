<i><b>Aramund</b></i> is an [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name said to mean "Kingly bull".<sup id="cite_ref-L_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) includes the word in his [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) wordlist, but notes that the form "*may not be pure [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), because of the final consonant cluster*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

As the Elvish element *[ar(a)](Ar.md)* is said to mean "high, noble, royal",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and an "elvish" word for "bull" is said to be *[mundo](../M/Mundo.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> it is likely that *Aramund* derives from these two elements (considering its given meaning, "Kingly bull").
