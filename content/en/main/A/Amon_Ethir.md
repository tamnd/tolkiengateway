**Amon Ethir**, the Spy-hill was raised artificially by the people of [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) in the wide plain of [Talath Dirnen](../T/Talath_Dirnen.md), a league east of the [Doors of Felagund](../D/Doors_of_Felagund.md) above the river [Narog](../N/Narog.md). Over the years, trees grew on its flanks, but from its clear summit the watchers of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) could watch the lands about with the clear sight of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), and so the hill got its name, Amon Ethir.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:205</sup>

After the [Sack of Nargothrond](../S/Sack_of_Nargothrond.md), the hill still stood, and it was here that [Niënor](../N/Niënor.md) encountered [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md). Having plunged the land into a thick fog of dragon-reek, so that only the hill remained above the mists, he cast Niënor into a deep spell of forgetfulness. After the departure of the dragon, [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) found Niënor in a daze upon the hill and led her away.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:208–210</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Ethir* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Hill of Spies"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "Spyhill",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> from *[amon](Amon.md)* ("hill") + *[ethir](../E/Ethir.md)* ("spy").
