**Amon Ethir**, the Spy-hill was raised artificially by the people of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) in the wide plain of [Talath Dirnen](/wiki/Talath_Dirnen), a league east of the [Doors of Felagund](/wiki/Doors_of_Felagund) above the river [Narog](/wiki/Narog). Over the years, trees grew on its flanks, but from its clear summit the watchers of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) could watch the lands about with the clear sight of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), and so the hill got its name, Amon Ethir.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:205</sup>

After the [Sack of Nargothrond](/wiki/Sack_of_Nargothrond), the hill still stood, and it was here that [Niënor](/wiki/Ni%C3%ABnor) encountered [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"). Having plunged the land into a thick fog of dragon-reek, so that only the hill remained above the mists, he cast Niënor into a deep spell of forgetfulness. After the departure of the dragon, [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) found Niënor in a daze upon the hill and led her away.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:208–210</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Ethir* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Hill of Spies"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "Spyhill",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> from *[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* ("hill") + *[ethir](/wiki/Ethir "Ethir")* ("spy").
