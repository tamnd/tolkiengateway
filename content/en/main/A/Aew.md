<i><b>aew</b></i> means "(small) bird" in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*aew* derives from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [AIWĒ-](/wiki/AIW%C4%92-).

## Cognates {#Cognates}
