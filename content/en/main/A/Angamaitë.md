**Angamaitë** was one of the great-grandsons of [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir) the Usurper.<sup id="cite_ref-Telemnar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Telemnar-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

He and [Sangahyando](/wiki/Sangahyando), another great-grandson of Castamir, were leaders of the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar).<sup id="cite_ref-ElendilC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ElendilC-2">[2]</a></sup> The name Angamaitë was possibly not his birth name, but a personal warrior name (or nickname). There was no need to assert his royal descent with this name, because his royal descent was clear.<sup id="cite_ref-L347_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-3">[3]</a></sup>

Angamaitë and Sangayando learned through spies that [Minardil](/wiki/Minardil), the [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor), was in the [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") port of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and that he suspected no peril since his father had crushed Harad and Umbar.<sup id="cite_ref-ElendilC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ElendilC-2">[2]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1634](/wiki/Third_Age_1634 "Third Age 1634")<sup id="cite_ref-TA1634_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1634-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-T4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-T4-5">[5]</a></sup> the Corsairs of Umbar, led by Angamaitë and Sangayando, made a raid up the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), killed king Minardil in Pelargir,<sup id="cite_ref-Telemnar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Telemnar-1">[1]</a></sup> ravaged Pelargir<sup id="cite_ref-TA1634_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA1634-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-T4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-T4-5">[5]</a></sup> and the coasts and escaped with great booty.<sup id="cite_ref-ElendilC_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ElendilC-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Angamaitë* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "Iron-handed",<sup id="cite_ref-L347_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L347-3">[3]</a></sup> from *[anga](/wiki/Anga "Anga")* ("iron") and *[maitë](/wiki/Mait%C3%AB "Maitë")* ("handed").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}
