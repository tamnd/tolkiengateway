**Araval** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1711](/wiki/Third_Age_1711 "Third Age 1711") - [1891](/wiki/Third_Age_1891 "Third Age 1891"),<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup> died aged 180) was thirteenth king of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), coming to the throne in 1813 after the death of his father, [Arveleg II](/wiki/Arveleg_II).<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When Araval came to the throne Arthedain had been at war with [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) for 400 years, and the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) had achieved very little in preventing the power of this old enemy from spreading.

Araval, in coalition with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), went to battle with the forces of Angmar in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1851](/wiki/Third_Age_1851 "Third Age 1851") and returned victorious. After this victory, Araval attempted to re-occupy [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan), but the "evil [wights](/wiki/Wights "Wights")" (likely referring to the [Barrow-wights](/wiki/Barrow-wights)) terrified anyone who attempted to dwell there.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>

Unfortunately, Araval's victory was of no avail in the long term: under [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant), Araval's son, Angmar recovered and continued its attacks on Arnor; and under [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), Araval's grandson, Angmar finally defeated Arthedain, laying Arnor to waste and scattering the remnants of the Dúnedain.

Araval ruled Arthedain for seventy-eight years, and was succeeded by his son [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) upon his death in 1891.<sup id="cite_ref-North_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Araval* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). Its meaning is not glossed. It means likely "Golden King"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> or "King of Gold".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Paul Strack explains, that the intial element of his name is [ara](/wiki/Ara "Ara") ("noble") and its final element might be the [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") form *val* of [malt](/wiki/Malt "Malt") ("gold") (as suggested by David Salo).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> His [earlier name](#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium) *Arvallen*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> possibly contains *mallen* ("golden") and has a similar meaning. It is comparable to <i><a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Argeleb_(disambiguation)" title="Argeleb (disambiguation)">Argeleb</a></i> ("Lord of Silver").

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}
