**Ainulindalë** is a Tolkien-related epic black metal band from Paris, France. The band takes its name from the [Ainulindalë](Ainulindalë.md), the creation myth of [Arda](Arda.md), and its lyrics draw on Tolkien's legendarium. Their debut EP *Les chroniques d'Arda* ("The Chronicles of Arda"), released in 2021 through Naturmacht Productions, features songs such as "Les damnés de Numénor", "Dagor-nuin-Giliath" and "Le geste de Maeglin".
