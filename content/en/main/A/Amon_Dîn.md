**Amon Dîn**, sometimes just **Dîn**, was the first and easternmost of the seven [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Lying to the east of the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest), it was the nearest of the beacon-hills to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Amon Dîn was the first Beacon-hill west of Minas Tirith and perhaps the oldest. It was originally used as a fortified outpost of Minas Tirith. It kept watch over the passage into North [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) from [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad) and any attempt by enemies to cross the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) near [Cair Andros](/wiki/Cair_Andros).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup> In the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the eastern side of Amon Dîn was covered with wide grey thickets.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Dîn* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "silent hill" (*[amon](/wiki/Amon "Amon")* "hill" + *[dîn](/wiki/D%C3%AEn "Dîn")* "silent").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT42_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-7">[7]</a></sup>, which may refer to its distinct rocky and barren appearance that stood out and isolated it from the heavily wooded hills of Drúadan Forest.
