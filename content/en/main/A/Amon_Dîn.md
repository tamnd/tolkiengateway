**Amon Dîn**, sometimes just **Dîn**, was the first and easternmost of the seven [Beacon-hills](../W/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Lying to the east of the [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md), it was the nearest of the beacon-hills to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Amon Dîn was the first Beacon-hill west of Minas Tirith and perhaps the oldest. It was originally used as a fortified outpost of Minas Tirith. It kept watch over the passage into North [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) from [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md) and any attempt by enemies to cross the [Anduin](Anduin.md) near [Cair Andros](../C/Cair_Andros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-UT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup> In the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), the eastern side of Amon Dîn was covered with wide grey thickets.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](../_/7_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Amon Dîn* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), meaning "silent hill" (*[amon](Amon.md)* "hill" + *[dîn](../D/Dîn.md)* "silent").<sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT42_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-7">[7]</a></sup>, which may refer to its distinct rocky and barren appearance that stood out and isolated it from the heavily wooded hills of Drúadan Forest.
