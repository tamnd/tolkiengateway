**Angelimir** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2866](/wiki/Third_Age_2866 "Third Age 2866") – [2977](/wiki/Third_Age_2977 "Third Age 2977"), aged 111 years) was the twentieth [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Angelimir's reign was uneventful. He was eventually succeeded by his son [Adrahil](/wiki/Adrahil) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2977](/wiki/Third_Age_2977 "Third Age 2977").<sup id="cite_ref-Dol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dol-1">[1]</a></sup>
