**AWA** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "away, forth; out"<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> or "go, move (from speaker), go away, depart", "go, depart, pass away; go away".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-2">[2]</a></sup> Its meaning is also given as referring to "movement away, viewed from the point of view of the thing, person, or place left" or "away from the speaker or the place of his thought".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *au-*; *awā*<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[auta](/wiki/Auta- "Auta-")*;<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup> *ava* ("outside, beyond");<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-4">[4]</a></sup> *öa, öar*;<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup> *aukiri* ("cut of, so that a portion is lost or no longer available")<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[gwaen](/wiki/Gwaen "Gwaen")*<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *au-, avad*; *auta-*; *[vanua](/wiki/Vanua "Vanua")*<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *ar-*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root AVA, signifying "go away, depart, leave".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
