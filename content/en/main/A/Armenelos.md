**Armenelos**, also known as the <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/City_of_the_Kings" title="City of the Kings">City of the Kings</a></b>,<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup> **Armenelos the Golden**<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> or **Arminalêth**,<sup id="cite_ref-PMV7_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV7-3">[3]</a></sup> was the capital city of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup> It was the fairest of all cities<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> on the island and in later years possibly the largest after [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) declined.<sup id="cite_ref-RBAR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RBAR-4">[4]</a></sup>

It was situated on a hill<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> in eastern central Númenor, in the district called [Arandor](/wiki/Arandor),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the Kingsland, close to the [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> The chief road of Númenor ran through the royal city, eastward it led to the haven of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna) while westward it led to Andúnië.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Armenelos contained the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=King%27s_house&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="King&#39;s house (page does not exist)">King&#39;s house</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-6">[6]</a></sup> a tower and a citadel that had been built by [Elros](/wiki/Elros),<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> as well as the [King's Court](/wiki/King%27s_Court), the place where the White Tree [Nimloth](</wiki/Nimloth_(tree)> "Nimloth (tree)") grew.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup> At the top of the tower of the King's palace was an eyrie where a pair of great eagles lived on the bounty of the King from the time of Elros until the time of [Tar-Ancalimon](/wiki/Tar-Ancalimon).<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Armenelos was founded as far back as the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), when the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) first migrated to the island. Conceivably [Arandor](/wiki/Arandor) was the first district they settled after landing in a firth that later led to the haven of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna).

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [32](/wiki/Second_Age_32 "Second Age 32"), the coronation of Elros in Armenelos marked the beginning of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") realm.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

The Elves of [Eressëa](/wiki/Eress%C3%ABa) brought many gifts to Númenor, one of which was a seedling of the White Tree [Celeborn](</wiki/Celeborn_(White_Tree)> "Celeborn (White Tree)") and it grew in the courts of the King in Armenelos and it was named [Nimloth](</wiki/Nimloth_(tree)> "Nimloth (tree)").<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [870](/wiki/Second_Age_870 "Second Age 870"), the city celebrated the wedding of the King's Heir [Aldarion](/wiki/Aldarion) and [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis).<sup id="cite_ref-Wife_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wife-6">[6]</a></sup>

Near the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the [Temple](/wiki/Temple) was built in the city by order of the [King's](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n "Ar-Pharazôn") adviser [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and the White Tree Nimloth was burned on its altar and later human sacrifice was performed there.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

The city was destroyed in the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Armenelos* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name, which means "Noble Heaven City". It seems to be a compound of the elements *[ar-](/wiki/Ar- "Ar-")* ("noble", "high"), *[menel](/wiki/Menel "Menel")* ("heavens") and the suffixal form *-os* of *[osto](/wiki/Osto "Osto")* ("city", "fortress", "strongholf", "strong place").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name is *Arminalêth*,<sup id="cite_ref-PMV7_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV7-3">[3]</a></sup> which appears to be a direct translation of the Quenya name, analyzed as *ar-* + *minul* (Adûnaic name of menel).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

At early stages of writing, the city was called by other names: **Númenos**, **Númar**<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> or **Nûmar**<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> (at first used of what became Númenor). In later developments it was known as **Antirion** (the Golden).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The city's Adûnaic name was **Ar-Minalêth** or **Arminalêth**<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> which survived in later drafts of the *Akallabêth*. Additionally in *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, Christopher corrected a mistake he made in *[Sauron Defeated](/wiki/Sauron_Defeated)* where he said the name Arminalêth was replaced by Armenelos, instead the Eldarin name Armenelos replaced the Eldarin name **Tar Kalimos**.<sup id="cite_ref-PMV7_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PMV7-3">[3]</a></sup>
