**Aloe** is a plant that appears in [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing) and [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online).

## In *Middle-earth Role Playing* {#In_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

**Aloe** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Sauthoel*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Úrenuhtar*), also known as *Heat-heal*, was a spiky plant native to the south, which was grown in sheltered gardens in [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain). It was used to treat burns, both as a leaf or as a salve.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p84_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p84-2">[2]</a></sup>

Lesser aloe ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *Sauthoel Niben*, [Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *Úrenuhtarellë*), also known as Culkas, was a related succulent found in deserts. Its long triangular leaves exuded a thick gel when cut, which treated burns just like the salve of regular aloe.<sup id="cite_ref-page96_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page96-3">[3]</a></sup>

## In *The Lord of the Rings Online* {#In_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

Aloe was found in Idagâl and Kighân (northern [Harad](/wiki/Harad)).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Various types of aloe from Idagâl are available as housing decorations.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
