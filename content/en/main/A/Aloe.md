**Aloe** is a plant that appears in [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md) and [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md).

## In *Middle-earth Role Playing* {#In_Middle-earth_Role_Playing}

**Aloe** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *Sauthoel*, [Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) *Úrenuhtar*), also known as *Heat-heal*, was a spiky plant native to the south, which was grown in sheltered gardens in [Angmar](Angmar.md) and [Arthedain](Arthedain.md). It was used to treat burns, both as a leaf or as a salve.<sup id="cite_ref-firstpage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-firstpage-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-p84_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-p84-2">[2]</a></sup>

Lesser aloe ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) *Sauthoel Niben*, [Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) *Úrenuhtarellë*), also known as Culkas, was a related succulent found in deserts. Its long triangular leaves exuded a thick gel when cut, which treated burns just like the salve of regular aloe.<sup id="cite_ref-page96_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-page96-3">[3]</a></sup>

## In *The Lord of the Rings Online* {#In_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online}

Aloe was found in Idagâl and Kighân (northern [Harad](../H/Harad.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Various types of aloe from Idagâl are available as housing decorations.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
