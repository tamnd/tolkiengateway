{{short description|Artist for Middle-earth Role Playing and Toys}}

In the 1980s and 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create illustrations for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. Later, he made designs for [Middle-earth Toys](/wiki/Middle-earth_Toys).
