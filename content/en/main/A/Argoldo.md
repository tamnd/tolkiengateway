**Argol**(**do**) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "Noble [Ñoldo](/wiki/%C3%91oldo)", being only attested as an example of phonetic changes.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> No other information is given about it.
