<i><b>andave</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) adverb meaning "long"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-2">[2]</a></sup> or "at great length".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was apparently derived from the adjective *[anda](/wiki/Anda "Anda")*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *Andave laituvalmet!* "Praise them for a long time!"<sup id="cite_ref-Cormallen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cormallen-2">[2]</a></sup>
