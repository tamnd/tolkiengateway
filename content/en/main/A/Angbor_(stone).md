The **Angbor** or **Doom-rock** was a great flat stone in the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Angbor was a stone that was located at the center of the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Moot-ring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Moot-ring (page does not exist)">Moot-ring</a>, the place where the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) held <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Folkmoot_for_Judgment&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Folkmoot for Judgment (page does not exist)">meetings</a> of judgement. During these meetings, the [Halad](/wiki/Halad) would sit on the stone itself. During the judgement of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) on the charge of assaulting the Halad, the meeting turned into a riot, largely ending the Haladin's existence as a coherent people.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
