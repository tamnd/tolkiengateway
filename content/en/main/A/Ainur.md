The **Ainur** (singular **Ainu**) were divine [spirits](../S/Spirits.md), the "Holy Ones". They were the first beings created by [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md), the "order" of the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md), made before [Eä](../E/Eä.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Origins {#Origins}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="../C/Creation_of_the_Ainur.md" title="Creation of the Ainur">Creation of the Ainur</a></b></div></div>

The Ainur were the first, and mightiest, beings created by [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) before the beginning of the World. The Ainur were the "offspring of [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md)'s thought", and each was given understanding only of that part of the mind of Ilúvatar from which they came. The exception to this was [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md), the greatest of the Ainur, who had a part of the gifts of all the others.

Although all Ainur are made of the thought of Ilúvatar, in his mind some were siblings. Thus for instance, [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) and [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) were considered brothers.

The Ainur were "kindled with the [Flame Imperishable](../F/Flame_Imperishable.md)". Ilúvatar instructed them in music, until he brought them together to make the [Music of the Ainur](../M/Music_of_the_Ainur.md), the great song that created the [Vision of Ilúvatar](../V/Vision_of_Ilúvatar.md) and ultimately the world itself.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Within Eä {#Within_Eä}

Through the Music of the Ainur, Ilúvatar created a vision of the world, and explained much of its nature and destiny to them. Therefore, the Ainur have much knowledge of the world but are not omniscient. Then [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) granted the world true being. Many of the Ainur desired to descend into it and form it in readiness for the coming of the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md) ([Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup> Fourteen of these great Ainur became the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), or Powers of [Arda](Arda.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The fifteenth, Melkor, turned aside from that path and became known as [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), the Dark Enemy.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The many lesser Ainur that accompanied the Valar into [Arda](Arda.md) are known as [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Those Ainur who entered the world at its beginning remain bound to it until its end.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup> Though Melkor was eventually thrown into the [Void](../V/Void.md) by the others, he is prophesied to return before [the End](../T/The_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-5">[5]</a></sup> Little is known of the ultimate future of the Ainur, even by themselves, but it is said that, after the death of the world, they will make a [Second Music](../S/Second_Music_of_the_Ainur.md), even greater, with the race of [Men](../M/Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

The Ainur are [immortal](../I/Immortal.md) angelic beings. They do nevertheless possess gender. Those who later descended to Arda took on common male and female forms in which to appear to the Elves and have an inherent male or female gender even when formless. Their gender is based on their being, which is independent of any physical form they may assume.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup> Unlike the [Children of Ilúvatar](../C/Children_of_Ilúvatar.md), they do not require [hröar](../H/Hröa.md). Nevertheless, they can take tangible physical form called <i><b><a href="../F/Fana.md" title="Fana">fana</a></b></i>, but this is a manifestation of their spirit. They are not embodied in the same way as [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md). As such, the Ainur cannot be truly killed. Their spirits can be weakened, however, so that their ability to manifest in the world or to affect the world can be greatly diminished. <sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

For example, [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s spirit was repeatedly weakened by successive damage when he was defeated by [Huan](../H/Huan.md) in the form of a [Werewolf](../W/Werewolf.md);<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> in the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) which destroyed his ability to take fair form;<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> his defeat in the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md) when he lost the ability to take a physical form<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and had to regain his power in the [East](../E/East.md) for about 1,000 years;<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and finally with the destruction of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) which broke his ability to affect the world at all, reducing his spirit to a state that could no longer do harm.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> None of these things killed Sauron outright. A similar fate faced [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) when he was murdered by [Gríma](../G/Gríma.md) – because of his evil deeds, his spirit was allowed to fade as Sauron's spirit faded.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In contrast, when [Gandalf the Grey](../G/Gandalf.md) was killed by [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md), not only was he brought back, but his spirit was significantly strengthened into an even more powerful manifestation who was named [Gandalf the White](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

[Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) was the most powerful of the Ainur, and his spirit, instead of fading away, was exiled from [Arda](Arda.md) altogether through the [Door of Night](../D/Door_of_Night.md) into the [Void](../V/Void.md). It is prophesied that Melkor will return at the [Dagor Dagorath](../F/Final_Battle.md) in Arda's last days for one final confrontation against the Valar and the Children of Ilúvatar. In this confrontation, it is stated that he will be destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Line of Melian {#Line_of_Melian}

One of the [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) named [Melian](../M/Melian.md), alone of all the Ainur, wedded an [Elf](../E/Elves.md), King [Elu Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> From her, a strain of the Ainur entered the bloodlines of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md), passed down through generations. [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and [Elros](../E/Elros.md) were Melian's descendants as well as the [Kings of Númenor](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md) and their heirs.

The word *Ainu* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and is related to words for "holiness" such as *[aina](Aina.md)*;<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> the female form is given as <i><b>Aini</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

It is derived from the original [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) word for *Ainur*, which was <i><b>Ayanûz</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

They are also referred to as **Great Ones** or **Holy Ones**.<sup id="cite_ref-Ainu_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ainu-1">[1]</a></sup>
