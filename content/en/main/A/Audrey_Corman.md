**Audrey Corman** is an Amercan artist who has created numerous [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-inspired illustrations, many of them for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game). While she prefers to work in oils, the long time to dry forced her to use acrylic gouache for card art.<sup id="cite_ref-MECCG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MECCG-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Career {#Career}

After her childhood in the Bronx, she entered Lehman College as an Art Major after [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine) cover artists got her interest in art. She ended up graduating with a degree in Chemistry instead, due to pressure from her parents and discouragement from the faculty when it came to fantasy and realistic paintings. While working as a research chemist she picked up drawing again, attending night classes at the Rhode Island School of Design.

Her work for [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) started after her private airbrushing instructor, whose classes she was attending, recommended her to the company while they were scouting for new talents.<sup id="cite_ref-MECCG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MECCG-1">[1]</a></sup>
