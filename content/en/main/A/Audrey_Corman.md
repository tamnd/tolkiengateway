**Audrey Corman** is an Amercan artist who has created numerous [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-inspired illustrations, many of them for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md). While she prefers to work in oils, the long time to dry forced her to use acrylic gouache for card art.<sup id="cite_ref-MECCG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MECCG-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Career {#Career}

After her childhood in the Bronx, she entered Lehman College as an Art Major after [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md) cover artists got her interest in art. She ended up graduating with a degree in Chemistry instead, due to pressure from her parents and discouragement from the faculty when it came to fantasy and realistic paintings. While working as a research chemist she picked up drawing again, attending night classes at the Rhode Island School of Design.

Her work for [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) started after her private airbrushing instructor, whose classes she was attending, recommended her to the company while they were scouting for new talents.<sup id="cite_ref-MECCG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MECCG-1">[1]</a></sup>
