**Axes** were tools for chopping wood and excellent mêlée weaponry, usually associated with [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), but not exclusively used by them.

## History

When the threat of [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth) started to loom over [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), there was unrest among the people of [Fëanor](/wiki/Fëanor). So they came to smithing weaponry. The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), renowned steelwrights, crafted sword, spears and axes.

> Reference: {{S|Noldor}}

Dwarves also made axes; their (doomed) alliance with [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) made them trade many fine axes, swords and spears with the King of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar).

> Reference: {{S|Sindar}}

Axes would remain to be the main weapon of the march wardens of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath),

> Reference: {{S|Fingolfin}}

and were also used by their allies, the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth) in [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).

> Reference: {{S|Fifth}}

## By faction

[|220px|thumb|*Dwarven Axes* by [[:Category:Images by Artigas|Artigas](/wiki/File:Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg) ]]

* **[Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)** had the axe as their chief weapon. Dwarven warriors would carry at least one standard axe, though sometimes carried backup weapons as well.

> Reference: {{H|Burst}}

The [Dwarves of Belegost](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Belegost) and their lord, [Azaghâl](/wiki/Azaghâl), bore axes.

> Reference: Axes were also used during the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs). Durin had a [legendary axe](/wiki/Durin's_Axe),<ref name="journey">{{FR|Journey}}

[Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) bore one,

> Reference: {{FR|South}}

and [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/Dáin_Ironfoot) had a red axe.

> Reference: {{UT|Quest}}

* The **Forces of Darkness** were known to use axes in combat. [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) used it as a weapon in battles. [Gothmog](/wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)), the Lord of Balrogs himself bore a black axe.

> Reference: During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) wielded great axes, and were among the most effective forces of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<ref>{{RK|Battle}}

*Among the **[Atani](/wiki/Atani) of the First Age**, the most notable axe-bearers are probably [Huor](/wiki/Huor)

> Reference: and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), whose [axe](/wiki/Dramborleg) became a royal heirloom in Númenor.<ref name="Numenor">{{UT|3a}}, p. 172, note 2

The [Men of Brethil](/wiki/Men_of_Brethil) carried little weaponry, but mostly axes.

* **[Númenóreans](/wiki/Númenóreans)** were passed the craft of weaponsmithing by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), and they managed to forge great axes. Their main purpose seems to have been not in war, but in deforesting the woods of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for ship-timber.

> Reference: {{UT|Numenor}}

However, the [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath) depicted [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/Anárion) gripping axes.

> Reference: {{FR|River}}, p. 392
