**Axes** were tools for chopping wood and excellent mêlée weaponry, usually associated with [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), but not exclusively used by them.

## History {#History}

When the threat of [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") started to loom over [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), there was unrest among the people of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor). So they came to smithing weaponry. The [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), renowned steelwrights, crafted sword, spears and axes.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Dwarves also made axes; their (doomed) alliance with [Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") made them trade many fine axes, swords and spears with the King of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Axes would remain to be the main weapon of the march wardens of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and were also used by their allies, the [House of Haleth](/wiki/House_of_Haleth) in [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup>

## By faction {#By_faction}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/3/31/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg/220px-Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="111" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/3/31/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg/330px-Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/3/31/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg/440px-Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2366" data-file-height="1196"/></a><figcaption><i>Dwarven Axes</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Artigas" title="Category:Images by Artigas">Artigas</a></figcaption></figure>

- **[Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)** had the axe as their chief weapon. Dwarven warriors would carry at least one standard axe, though sometimes carried backup weapons as well.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The [Dwarves of Belegost](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Belegost) and their lord, [Azaghâl](/wiki/Azagh%C3%A2l), bore axes.<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup> Axes were also used during the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs). Durin had a [legendary axe](/wiki/Durin%27s_Axe "Durin's Axe"),<sup id="cite_ref-journey_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-journey-6">[6]</a></sup> [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) bore one,<sup id="cite_ref-South_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-7">[7]</a></sup> and [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) had a red axe.<sup id="cite_ref-Quest_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quest-8">[8]</a></sup>

- The **Forces of Darkness** were known to use axes in combat. [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) used it as a weapon in battles. [Gothmog](</wiki/Gothmog_(balrog)> "Gothmog (balrog)"), the Lord of Balrogs himself bore a black axe.<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup> During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) wielded great axes, and were among the most effective forces of [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

- Among the **[Atani](/wiki/Atani) of the First Age**, the most notable axe-bearers are probably [Huor](/wiki/Huor)<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), whose [axe](/wiki/Dramborleg "Dramborleg") became a royal heirloom in Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-10">[10]</a></sup> The [Men of Brethil](/wiki/Men_of_Brethil) carried little weaponry, but mostly axes.

- **[Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans)** were passed the craft of weaponsmithing by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), and they managed to forge great axes. Their main purpose seems to have been not in war, but in deforesting the woods of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) for ship-timber.<sup id="cite_ref-Description_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-11">[11]</a></sup> However, the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Argonauth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Argonauth (page does not exist)">Argonauth</a> depicted [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) gripping axes.<sup id="cite_ref-River_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-12">[12]</a></sup>
