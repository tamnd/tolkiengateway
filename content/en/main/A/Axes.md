**Axes** were tools for chopping wood and excellent mêlée weaponry, usually associated with [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), but not exclusively used by them.

## History {#History}

When the threat of [Melkor](../M/Morgoth.md) started to loom over [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md), there was unrest among the people of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md). So they came to smithing weaponry. The [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), renowned steelwrights, crafted sword, spears and axes.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Dwarves also made axes; their (doomed) alliance with [Elu Thingol](../T/Thingol.md) made them trade many fine axes, swords and spears with the King of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Axes would remain to be the main weapon of the march wardens of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and were also used by their allies, the [House of Haleth](../H/House_of_Haleth.md) in [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup>

## By faction {#By_faction}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/31/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg/220px-Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="220" height="111" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/31/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg/330px-Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/3/31/Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg/440px-Artigas_-_Dwarven_Axes.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2366" data-file-height="1196"/></a><figcaption><i>Dwarven Axes</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Artigas.md" title="Category:Images by Artigas">Artigas</a></figcaption></figure>

- **[Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md)** had the axe as their chief weapon. Dwarven warriors would carry at least one standard axe, though sometimes carried backup weapons as well.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The [Dwarves of Belegost](../D/Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) and their lord, [Azaghâl](Azaghâl.md), bore axes.<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup> Axes were also used during the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md). Durin had a [legendary axe](../D/Durin's_Axe.md),<sup id="cite_ref-journey_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-journey-6">[6]</a></sup> [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) bore one,<sup id="cite_ref-South_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-7">[7]</a></sup> and [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) had a red axe.<sup id="cite_ref-Quest_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Quest-8">[8]</a></sup>

- The **Forces of Darkness** were known to use axes in combat. [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) used it as a weapon in battles. [Gothmog](<../G/Gothmog_(balrog).md>), the Lord of Balrogs himself bore a black axe.<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup> During the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) wielded great axes, and were among the most effective forces of [Minas Morgul](../M/Minas_Morgul.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

- Among the **[Atani](Atani.md) of the First Age**, the most notable axe-bearers are probably [Huor](../H/Huor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5B_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5B-4">[4]</a></sup> and [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md), whose [axe](../D/Dramborleg.md) became a royal heirloom in Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-10">[10]</a></sup> The [Men of Brethil](../M/Men_of_Brethil.md) carried little weaponry, but mostly axes.

- **[Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md)** were passed the craft of weaponsmithing by the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), and they managed to forge great axes. Their main purpose seems to have been not in war, but in deforesting the woods of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for ship-timber.<sup id="cite_ref-Description_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-11">[11]</a></sup> However, the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Argonauth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Argonauth (page does not exist)">Argonauth</a> depicted [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](Anárion.md) gripping axes.<sup id="cite_ref-River_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-12">[12]</a></sup>
