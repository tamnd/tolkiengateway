The **Alley of Roses** was a street in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

It was the northern entrance to the [Square of the Palace](../S/Square_of_the_Palace.md), and a fair place to see and to walk in. During the [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) it was burned and destroyed by a [Fire-drake](../F/Fire-drake.md) during the barricade in the Square hold by [Egalmoth](../E/Egalmoth.md) and [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
