The **Alley of Roses** was a street in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was the northern entrance to the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace), and a fair place to see and to walk in. During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin) it was burned and destroyed by a [Fire-drake](/wiki/Fire-drake) during the barricade in the Square hold by [Egalmoth](/wiki/Egalmoth) and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
