**Anson Roper** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Anson was the son of [Andwise Roper](/wiki/Andwise_Roper), and cousin of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee). He lived in [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield) and probably followed his father's trade of ropery.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name likely means "Andy's son".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
