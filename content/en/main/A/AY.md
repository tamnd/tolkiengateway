**AY** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") without any given signification, except for yielding the derivative root [ÁYAR, AIR](</wiki/AYA(R)> "AYA(R)").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *ai-lin-* ("pool, lake")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[ailin](/wiki/Ailin "Ailin")*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[oel](/wiki/Oel "Oel")*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
