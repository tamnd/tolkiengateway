**AY** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) without any given signification, except for yielding the derivative root [ÁYAR, AIR](<AYA(R).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md): *ai-lin-* ("pool, lake")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[ailin](Ailin.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *[oel](../O/Oel.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
