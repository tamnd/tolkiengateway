**Aranel** was a [Galadhrim](/wiki/Galadhrim) [elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). Aranel was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Third_Age)* by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts), where he is voiced by Rhys Lloyd.

## History {#History}

A scout from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), he was the brother of the warrior Idrial. He, along with a small company, was dispatched from Lórien to investigate the activities of the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) at the borders of their realm. He encountered a company of four — a dwarf, two men and his sister — in the [Eastemnet](/wiki/Eastemnet) gullies and aided them against [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai), as well as well as rescuing a company of royal guards from [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Upon reaching the borders of the land, Aranel took his leave of the company, but noted to Idrial that they would reunite in the future.
