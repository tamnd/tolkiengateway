**Aranel** was a [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) [elf](../E/Elves.md) of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). Aranel was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Third Age](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Third_Age.md)* by [Electronic Arts](../E/Electronic_Arts.md), where he is voiced by Rhys Lloyd.

## History {#History}

A scout from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), he was the brother of the warrior Idrial. He, along with a small company, was dispatched from Lórien to investigate the activities of the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) at the borders of their realm. He encountered a company of four — a dwarf, two men and his sister — in the [Eastemnet](../E/Eastemnet.md) gullies and aided them against [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md)'s [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md), as well as well as rescuing a company of royal guards from [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Upon reaching the borders of the land, Aranel took his leave of the company, but noted to Idrial that they would reunite in the future.
