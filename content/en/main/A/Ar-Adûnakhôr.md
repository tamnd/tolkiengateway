**Ar-Adûnakhôr** was the son of King [Tar-Ardamin](/wiki/Tar-Ardamin) and the twentieth [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Adûnakhôr was born in a time when most [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) already had stopped using the [Elven tongues](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") generations before, although it was still customary for the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor) to take their royal names in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

When Ar-Adûnakhôr succeeded his father, however, he was the first King to break with tradition and took his title in [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic).<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AppAr-Adunakhor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppAr-Adunakhor-4">[4]</a></sup> Tar-Herunúmen, a name in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) was still recorded in the [Scroll of Kings](/wiki/Scroll_of_Kings), because the kings feared that a break with this ancient custom could cause evil to befall.<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

His names were perceived as blasphemous and ill-omened<sup id="cite_ref-AppAr-Adunakhor_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppAr-Adunakhor-4">[4]</a></sup> by the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) Númenóreans, because they mean "Lord of the West", a title that the Faithful only used for one of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), especially [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup>

During his reign the Elven-tongues were no longer used, nor permitted to be taught, but were maintained in secret by the Faithful. After that elven ships from [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) only came seldom and secretly to the west shores of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Númenórean settlers in the north of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) (between the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) and the city of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir)) refused to participate in the rebellion against the Valar and were joined by many exiles of the Faithful who fled from persecution by Ar-Adûnakhôr and the later Kings of Númenor. During the reign of Ar-Adûnakhôr the term "[Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men)" originated from these settlers.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

He was succeeded to the throne by his son, [Ar-Zimrathôn](/wiki/Ar-Zimrath%C3%B4n).<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Zimrathon_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Zimrathon-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology and names {#Etymology_and_names}

*Adûnakhôr* is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name that means "Lord of the West".<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup> It can be analyzed as *[adûn](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBn "Adûn")* "West" ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *dûn*) and *khôr* "Lord" ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *heru*). It is said that as a title, it signifies [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB). Although the name was taken in defiance of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), ironically its elements are said to be of [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) origin.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> See also *[ar-](/wiki/Ar#Adûnaic "Ar")*.

*Herunúmen* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name that means "Lord of the West".<sup id="cite_ref-Ar-Adunakhor_1-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ar-Adunakhor-1">[1]</a></sup> Ostensibly a compound of [heru](/wiki/Heru "Heru") ("lord") and [númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen "Númen") ("west").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Like for all the rulers of Númenor whose name was inscribed in the Scroll of Kings, the prefix *[tar](/wiki/Tar "Tar")-* ("high") was added to his name.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
