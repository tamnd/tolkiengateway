**Almáriel** was a [girl](../M/Men.md) in [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)*.

She was a peer of [Herendil](../H/Herendil.md), who complained to his father [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) that he was barely of height to her shoulder, despite being of the same age. It is unknown if she joined Herendil's other peers in calling him names such as *Terendul* ("slender and dark") and accusing him of being a "half-Noldo."<sup id="cite_ref-P1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Almáriel* is clearly [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), having the elements *almarë* ("blessedness, blessings, good fortune, bliss"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and perhaps *[-iel](../_/-iel.md)* ("daughter").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
