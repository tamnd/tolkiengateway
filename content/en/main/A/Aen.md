**aen** is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word which probably means "should", "rather", "they" or generally a passive voice marker; its exact meaning, usage and etymology is unclear.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is not easy to analyze. If its meaning is "should", there is a possibility that it is a cognate or related to the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) expression *[nai](../N/Nai.md)*, "may it be that".

## Example {#Example}

The word *aen* is seen in the phrase of the *[King's Letter](../K/King's_Letter.md)*

- *[i](../I/I.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sennui&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sennui (page does not exist)">sennui</a> "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Panthael&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Panthael (page does not exist)">Panthael</a>" <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Esta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Esta (page does not exist)">estathar</a> aen*

This is translated as "who ought to be called 'Fullwise'"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> (referring to [Samwise](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)).

## Syntax {#Syntax}

*aen* follows the verb *estathar*, which is future tense and means "they will name". It is not known if *aen* can follow verbs of other tenses. Likewise, it is not known if *aen* is the pronoun ("**they** should name him Fullwiise"), a marker of the passive voice ("He should **be called** Fullwise"), or an optative marker which expresses a wish ("Whom instead **should** call Fullwise").

Another riddle is the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sennui&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sennui (page does not exist)">sennui</a></i>, which is also of unknown meaning, with possibilities being "should" or "instead".
