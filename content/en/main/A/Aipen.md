**aipen** in [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) means "if anybody, whoever".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

This word is one of the few old compounds<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It includes the element *[pen](/wiki/Pen "Pen")* "person". The prefix <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ai-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ai- (page does not exist)">ai-</a></i> would seem to mean something like "any-", hence literally \*"any person"
