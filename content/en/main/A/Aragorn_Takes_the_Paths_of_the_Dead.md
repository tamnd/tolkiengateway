**Aragorn Takes the Paths of the Dead** is the twenty-fourth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the thirty-first scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

As [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) readies his horse in the night to travel to [Dwimorberg](/wiki/Dwimorberg), [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) pleads with him to stay, telling him he has given the men hope. He asks her why she has come, to which she replies, "Do you not know?" He stops and tells her he cannot return her love for him.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I have wished you joy since first I saw you.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> to <a href="/wiki/%C3%89owyn" title="Éowyn">Éowyn</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

He leads his horse away and encounters [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), who tell him they are going with him.

The men of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) watch Aragorn, Legolas, and Gimli leave the camp. They wonder why Aragorn is leaving with the coming battle now imminent. [Gamling](/wiki/Gamling) tells them that it is because there is no hope. [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) enters the cluster of men and tells them all that "he leaves because he must", and that even though there is no hope of victory against the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), the men of Rohan will nevertheless meet them in battle.
