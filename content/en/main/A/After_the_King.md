<i><b>After the King: Stories In Honor of J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i>, is a Festschrift anthology published to celebrate the centenary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s birth. The book contains nineteen short stories of fantasy and science fiction, and also includes an introduction on the role of Tolkien for the genre of fantasy.

- Note: None of the short stories are set in, or directly related to, Tolkien's [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).
