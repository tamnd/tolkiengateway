**Ancalagon the Black** was the greatest of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s [Dragons](../D/Dragons.md), and the mightiest winged [fire-dragon](../U/Urulókë.md) that ever existed.

## History {#History}

During the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md), the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) waged their final war on [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). Their victory was at hand, with most of the Dark Lord's [Balrogs](../B/Balrogs.md) and other troops destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-Voyage_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Voyage-1">[1]</a></sup> But in the year [587](../F/First_Age_587.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Morgoth unleashed the winged dragons, which he had been hiding in [Angband](Angband.md). Ancalagon was among them, and their attack drove back the forces of the [Host of the Valar](../H/Host_of_the_Valar.md), as they came with thunderbolts and fire storms.<sup id="cite_ref-Voyage_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Voyage-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Ancalagon* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. It is not properly glossed, but it contains *[anc](Anc.md)* ("jaws") + [root](../R/Root.md) *[alak-](../L/LAK.md)* ("rushing").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) phase of the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) language it is glossed as "Biting-storm", from *[anc](Anc.md)* ("jaw, row of teeth") + *alagos* ("storm of wind").<sup id="cite_ref-EtymAlag_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EtymAlag-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md)'s [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) translations, Ancalagon is referred as *Anddraca* ("Enemy-dragon").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The first apparition of Ancalagon in [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) was in the *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* (1930s), which gives a version even more summarized of the War of Wrath than what is given above, although the story does not differ from later versions.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Tolkien never fully completed the *Quenta Silmarillion*, so the information about Ancalagon was never developed, and although he revised the text of the War of Wrath, they were very brief expansions, made in the *[Quenta Silmarillion](<../Q/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)* of [1937](../_/1937.md),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and in [The Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](../T/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion.md) of [1951](../_/1951.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> This last version was the main text used by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) for his edition of *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The only mention of Ancalagon in later texts is in a note about the language of the [Haladin](../H/Haladin.md), written in Tolkien's last years:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The language of the Folk of Haleth was not used, for they had perished and would not rise again. Nor would their tongue be heard again, unless the prophecy of <a href="Andreth.md" title="Andreth">Andreth</a> the <a href="../W/Wise-woman.md" title="Wise-woman">Wise-woman</a> should prove true, that <a href="../T/Túrin.md" title="Túrin">Túrin</a> in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="../L/Last_Battle.md" title="Last Battle">Last Battle</a> should return from the Dead, and before he left the <a href="../C/Circles_of_the_World.md" title="Circles of the World">Circles of the World</a> for ever should challege the Great Dragon of Morgoth, Ancalagon the Black, and deal him the death-stroke.<sup id="cite_ref-Andreth_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Andreth-11">[11]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) explains that this prophecy is in line with the different versions of the [End of the World](../D/Dagor_Dagorath.md) prophecies, as his father always used the term "Last Battle" to the apocalyptic event. However, he also notes that in the specific essay from which this quote is excerpted, the term "Last Battle" is clearly used to describe the battle at the end of the Elder Days, despite that in all previous texts it was Eärendil who destroys Ancalagon in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Andreth_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Andreth-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Christopher explains that Túrin slaying Ancalagon in Andreth's prophecy resembles <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigurd" title="wikipedia:Sigurd">Sigurd</a> killing <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mi%C3%B0gar%C3%B0sormr" title="wikipedia:Miðgarðsormr">Miðgarðsormr</a> in the *[Völuspá](../V/Völuspá.md)*, especially in the retelling his father made in the <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=New_Lay_of_the_V%C3%B6lsungs&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="New Lay of the Völsungs (page does not exist)">New Lay of the Völsungs</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## In popular culture {#In_popular_culture}

Two animals are named after Ancalagon: *Ancalagon*, a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambrian" title="wikipedia:Cambrian">Cambrian</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priapulida" title="wikipedia:Priapulida">priapulid</a> by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Conway_Morris" title="wikipedia:Simon Conway Morris">Simon Conway Morris</a> in [1977](../_/1977.md), and *Ankalagon* by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leigh_Van_Valen" title="wikipedia:Leigh Van Valen">Leigh Van Valen</a> in [1980](../_/1980.md), a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleocene" title="wikipedia:Paleocene">Paleocene</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesonychid" title="wikipedia:Mesonychid">mesonychid</a> mammal.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
