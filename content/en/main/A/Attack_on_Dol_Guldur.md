The **attack on Dol Guldur**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an expedition launched by the [White Council](/wiki/White_Council) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941") against the fortress of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) in southern [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

When Sauron re-emerged in the Third Age, he established Dol Guldur on [Amon Lanc](/wiki/Amon_Lanc) after [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1000](/wiki/Third_Age_1000 "Third Age 1000").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His corrupting [shadow](/wiki/Shadow "Shadow"), known only as the "Necromancer", came over Mirkwood, and as a result, [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) led his people away. The Council of the Wise long feared the Necromancer might indeed be Sauron; [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) himself entered Dol Guldur a couple of times, finally confirming that Sauron was its master. At first [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) opposed the idea of an attack, as he was secretly searching for the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring) in the area by then, but later, fearing that Sauron was also looking of the Ring, he finally agreed to an attack in [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

### The Battle {#The_Battle}

After leaving [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) at the outskirts of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> he joined the White Council and they marched against the great fortress.

Little is known of the actual battle. It is unclear if this was simply a duel between great powers, or if it involved the use of armies of any kind. Thanks to the devices of Saruman the Wise, Sauron was driven out from Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> However, having already made his plans, he fled to his ancient realm of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

With victory theirs, Gandalf immediately left for [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), where he would take part in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After these events, Gandalf and Elrond discussed their victories and agreed that it would be better if the Necromancer were banished from the world altogether.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

However, Dol Guldur was not destroyed; it remained standing, staffed by the [lieutenant of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king"), and later [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> It was not until the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) that the fortress was finally [destroyed](/wiki/Fall_of_Dol_Guldur "Fall of Dol Guldur").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
