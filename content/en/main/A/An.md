<i><b>an</b></i> is a preposition meaning "to, towards, for" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-lang_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lang-1">[1]</a></sup> In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) the preposition means "for" or "because".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When used as a prefix, <i><b>an-</b></i> means "by" or "with" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) *an-* or '*n-* means "of"<sup id="cite_ref-lang_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lang-1">[1]</a></sup>
