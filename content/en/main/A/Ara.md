<i><b>Ar-</b></i> or <i><b>ara-</b></i> is a prefix in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) used to confer nobility or royalty.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was a prefixed form of the stem Quenya/Sindarin *[aran](Aran.md)*, referring to a king.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Númenoreans](../N/Númenoreans.md) also had borrowed this prefix in their native [Adûnaic](Adûnaic.md) language, used with the names of the [Kings of Númenor](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md) (like [Ar-Pharazôn](Ar-Pharazôn.md)); however when they still took Quenya names, they used the prefix **[Tar](../T/Tar.md)-** (like [Tar-Elendil](../T/Tar-Elendil.md)).

When the royal line of [Arnor](Arnor.md) failed in [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) and [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md), the [King of Arthedain](../K/King_of_Arthedain.md) [Argeleb I](Argeleb_I.md) took a name with this prefix, claiming sovereignty in all former Arnor, as did his descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
