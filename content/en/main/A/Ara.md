<i><b>Ar-</b></i> or <i><b>ara-</b></i> is a prefix in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) used to confer nobility or royalty.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was a prefixed form of the stem Quenya/Sindarin *[aran](/wiki/Aran "Aran")*, referring to a king.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The [Númenoreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenoreans) also had borrowed this prefix in their native [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) language, used with the names of the [Kings of Númenor](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor) (like [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n)); however when they still took Quenya names, they used the prefix **[Tar](/wiki/Tar)-** (like [Tar-Elendil](/wiki/Tar-Elendil)).

When the royal line of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) failed in [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur) and [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan), the [King of Arthedain](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain) [Argeleb I](/wiki/Argeleb_I) took a name with this prefix, claiming sovereignty in all former Arnor, as did his descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- <i><a href="/wiki/Finarfin#Etymology" title="Finarfin"><b>Ara</b>finwë</a></i> = "Noble Finwë"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Araglas#Etymology" title="Araglas"><b>Ara</b>glas</a></i> = "Royal Joy"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Aragorn#Etymology" title="Aragorn"><b>Ara</b>gorn</a></i> = "Revered King"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Arathorn_II#Etymology" title="Arathorn II"><b>Ara</b>thorn</a></i> = "Eagle King"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Arnor#Etymology" title="Arnor"><b>Ar</b>nor</a></i> = "Land of the King"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Arwen#Etymology" title="Arwen"><b>Ar</b>wen</a></i> = "Noble Maiden"
