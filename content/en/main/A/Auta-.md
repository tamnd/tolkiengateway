<i><b>auta-</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) verb meaning "go away, leave (the point of the speaker's thought)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><b>Auta</b> i <a href="../L/Lómë.md" title="Lómë">lómë</a>!</i> = "The night **is passing**!"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> (<a class="external text" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aorist" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">aorist tense</a>)
- <i>Yéni ve lintë yuldar <b>avánier</b></i> = "The years **have passed** like swift draughts"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> (plural perfect tense)
