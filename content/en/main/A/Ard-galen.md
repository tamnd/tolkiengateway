**Ard-galen** ([S](../S/Sindarin.md). <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ardh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ardh (page does not exist)">ardh</a></i> "region" + *[calen](../C/Calen.md)* "green"), later *[Anfauglith](Anfauglith.md)*, was the wide green plain that lay north of the highlands of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) and south of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s fortress of [Angband](Angband.md) in the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md), in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the first days after the rising of the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), Ard-galen was a green plain with rich grass, reaching from [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md) and the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md) in the west to the [Ered Luin](../E/Ered_Luin.md) in the east, and rising into highlands of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) in the south.

In the aftermath of the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md), the Battle-under-Stars that was fought when the Host of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) was attacked in [Mithrim](../M/Mithrim.md), the fleeing [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) were hunted over the mountains and into the plain of Ard-galen. The Orcs were driven into the [Fen of Serech](../F/Fen_of_Serech.md) and few escaped to return to Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup> Thereafter Ard-galen would be the site of many battles.

The next clash that occurred upon Ard-galen was the [Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md), the Glorious Battle in which Morgoth suddenly sent forth a host of Orcs to try the strength of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md). As the Orcs assaulted Dorthonion the armies of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md) and [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) struck both their flanks. Again the Orcs were routed as the Elves pursued them across Ard-galen to the very gates of Angband, and none of the enemy’s soldiers survived.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md), [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) assaulted Ard-galen and pushed the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) from Ard-galen, which he took and defiled, to Dorthonion and the [Ered Wethrin](../E/Ered_Wethrin.md). This victory was short-lived, however, and Glaurung was forced back to Angband when [Fingon](../F/Fingon.md) rode against him with a company of mounted archers.

The plain of Ard-galen was laid waste by rivers of flame and poisonous gases at the beginning of the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md). Thereafter a desolate waste, it was renamed [Anfauglith](Anfauglith.md) ("Gasping Dust").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The battle of [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md) was fought in the plain, and the dead piled up in a hill in the midst of the plain, named *[Haudh-en-Ndengin](../H/Haudh-en-Ndengin.md)*, the [Hill of Slain](../H/Hill_of_Slain.md), by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), and *[Haudh-en-Nirnaeth](../H/Haudh-en-Nirnaeth.md)*, the [Hill of Tears](../H/Hill_of_Tears.md), and grass grew again upon the hill and nowhere else in [Anfauglith](Anfauglith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Like the other lands around it, [Anfauglith](Anfauglith.md) sank beneath the waves after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) at the end of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
