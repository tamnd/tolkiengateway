**Ard-galen** ([S](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin"). <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ardh&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ardh (page does not exist)">ardh</a></i> "region" + *[calen](/wiki/Calen "Calen")* "green"), later *[Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith)*, was the wide green plain that lay north of the highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) and south of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s fortress of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) in the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains), in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the first days after the rising of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), Ard-galen was a green plain with rich grass, reaching from [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum) and the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin) in the west to the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin) in the east, and rising into highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) in the south.

In the aftermath of the [Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath), the Battle-under-Stars that was fought when the Host of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) was attacked in [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim), the fleeing [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were hunted over the mountains and into the plain of Ard-galen. The Orcs were driven into the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech) and few escaped to return to Angband.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup> Thereafter Ard-galen would be the site of many battles.

The next clash that occurred upon Ard-galen was the [Dagor Aglareb](/wiki/Dagor_Aglareb), the Glorious Battle in which Morgoth suddenly sent forth a host of Orcs to try the strength of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves). As the Orcs assaulted Dorthonion the armies of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) struck both their flanks. Again the Orcs were routed as the Elves pursued them across Ard-galen to the very gates of Angband, and none of the enemy’s soldiers survived.<sup id="cite_ref-Return_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Return-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband), [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) assaulted Ard-galen and pushed the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) from Ard-galen, which he took and defiled, to Dorthonion and the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin). This victory was short-lived, however, and Glaurung was forced back to Angband when [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) rode against him with a company of mounted archers.

The plain of Ard-galen was laid waste by rivers of flame and poisonous gases at the beginning of the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). Thereafter a desolate waste, it was renamed [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) ("Gasping Dust").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The battle of [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) was fought in the plain, and the dead piled up in a hill in the midst of the plain, named *[Haudh-en-Ndengin](/wiki/Haudh-en-Ndengin)*, the [Hill of Slain](/wiki/Hill_of_Slain), by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves), and *[Haudh-en-Nirnaeth](/wiki/Haudh-en-Nirnaeth)*, the [Hill of Tears](/wiki/Hill_of_Tears), and grass grew again upon the hill and nowhere else in [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Like the other lands around it, [Anfauglith](/wiki/Anfauglith) sank beneath the waves after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) at the end of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
