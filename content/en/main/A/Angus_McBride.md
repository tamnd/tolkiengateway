**Angus McBride** ([11 May](/wiki/11_May) [1931](/wiki/1931) - [15 May](/wiki/15_May) [2007](/wiki/2007)) was a Scottish artist who was already well-established as a military and historical illustrator before working on fantasy art. He mostly worked in gouache, although some of his paintings are in oil. <sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Fellow Middle-earth artist [Angelo Montanini](/wiki/Angelo_Montanini) considers McBride to be the reason he started doing fantasy art.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Career {#Career}

After a long career illustrating for educational magazines and booklets, he eventually became a main artist for military history publisher Osprey, working on over 90 books between [1976](/wiki/1976) and the early 2000s. For fantasy gaming art, he worked exclusively for [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) in the 1980s and 1990s, who commissioned him to create covers for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and card art for the *[MECCG](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
