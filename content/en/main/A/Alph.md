<i><b>alph</b></i>, pl. <i><b>eilph</b></i> is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "[swan](/wiki/Swans "Swans")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [OS](/wiki/Old_Sindarin "Old Sindarin") *alpha*, from [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpa (page does not exist)">alpa</a></i>, itself from [PQ](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian "Primitive Quendian") <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alkw%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alkwâ (page does not exist)">alkwâ</a></i> ([root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [ÁLAK](/wiki/LAK "LAK")).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Alphros#Etymology" title="Alphros"><b>Alph</b>ros</a></i> = "Swan Foam"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Elphir#Etymology" title="Elphir"><b>Elph</b>ir</a></i> = "Swan Lord"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Swanfleet#Etymology" title="Swanfleet">Nîn-in-<b>Eilph</b></a></i> = "Water-lands of the Swans"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- *alpa* ([Telerin](/wiki/Telerin))
- *[alqua](/wiki/Alqua "Alqua")* ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya))

The form is given to be common both in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) and [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin).
