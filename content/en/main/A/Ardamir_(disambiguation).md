**Ardamir** ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈardamir]</span>) means "[Arda](/wiki/Arda)-jewel" and can refer to:

- [Ardamírë](/wiki/Ardam%C3%ADr%C3%AB), the [mother-name](/wiki/Amiless%C3%AB "Amilessë") of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil).
- [Ardamir](</wiki/Ardamir_(son_of_Axantur)> "Ardamir (son of Axantur)") (born [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [562](/wiki/Second_Age_562 "Second Age 562")), son of [Axantur](/wiki/Axantur) and member of the [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") royal family.
