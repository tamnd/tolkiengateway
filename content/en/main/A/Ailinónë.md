**Ailinónë** was the name of a "[fairy](/wiki/Fays "Fays") who dwelt in a [lily](/wiki/Lilies "Lilies") on a pool", according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about her, and she doesn't appear in any of the narratives.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ailinónë* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), possibly being a feminized form of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ailinon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ailinon (page does not exist)">ailinon</a></i> ("water lily").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
