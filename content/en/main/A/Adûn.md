**Adûn** is [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) for *west*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Derived from [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[dûn](/wiki/D%C3%BBn "Dûn")* and *[annûn](/wiki/Ann%C3%BBn "Annûn")*
