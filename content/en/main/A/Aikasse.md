## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>aikasse</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "mountain peak".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that the form *aicassë* (retaining its meaning, and perhaps also include the meaning of Qenya *aikasse*, see below) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](../N/Neo-Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- Root [AYAK](AYAK.md)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *oegas* ("mountain peak")
- Quenya *aikale* ("peak")

## Qenya {#Qenya}

<i><b>aikasse</b></i> is a [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word meaning "[pinetree](../P/Pines.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

### Cognates {#Cognates_2}

- [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) *[aigos](../P/Pines.md#Names)* ("pinetree")
