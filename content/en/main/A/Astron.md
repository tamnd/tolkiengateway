**Astron**, was the fourth month of the year according to the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), approximately corresponding to modern [23 March](../_/23_March.md) through [21 April](../_/21_April.md). This month was called [Chithing](../C/Chithing.md) in [Bree](../B/Bree.md) and the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](../H/Hobbitish.md) of the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for the Easter, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eastre" title="wiktionary:eastre">ēastre</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The actual name for April was <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/eastermona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:eastermonaþ">ēastermōnaþ</a></i>.
