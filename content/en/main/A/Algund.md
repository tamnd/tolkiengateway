**Algund** was originally one of the people of [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) and had lived for a time under the lordship of [Húrin Thalion](../H/Húrin.md). At the time of the great battle of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), Algund fled from his homeland, and travelled down the [Vales of Sirion](../V/Vales_of_Sirion.md). Eventually, he fell in with a band of outlaws, the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md), who haunted the woodlands of [Brethil](../B/Brethil.md) on the western borders of Doriath. Algund was the oldest of the Gaurwaith.<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Late in his life, Algund saw a new member join the band. This was [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md), Húrin's son, who had exiled himself from the land of his foster-father [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup> Túrin eventually came to lead the outlaws - when Túrin killed [Forweg](../F/Forweg.md), the previous captain, it was Algund who supported Túrin as "the best man among us" to replace the former leader.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later, when the outlaws captured [Beleg](../B/Beleg.md) when Túrin was absent, [Andróg](Andróg.md) wished to kill him but Algund stayed his hand, saying that they should wait for Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> According to some versions of the tale, Algund was able to recognise the newcomer from his resemblance to his father.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> No record exists of Algund's fate: most likely, he was slain just a few years after the coming of Túrin, when the outlaws were ambushed by [Orcs](../O/Orc.md) on the summit of [Amon Rûdh](Amon_Rûdh.md).
