**Algund** was originally one of the people of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) and had lived for a time under the lordship of [Húrin Thalion](/wiki/H%C3%BArin "Húrin"). At the time of the great battle of the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), Algund fled from his homeland, and travelled down the [Vales of Sirion](/wiki/Vales_of_Sirion). Eventually, he fell in with a band of outlaws, the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith), who haunted the woodlands of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) on the western borders of Doriath. Algund was the oldest of the Gaurwaith.<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Late in his life, Algund saw a new member join the band. This was [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin"), Húrin's son, who had exiled himself from the land of his foster-father [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-Narn_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narn-1">[1]</a></sup> Túrin eventually came to lead the outlaws - when Túrin killed [Forweg](/wiki/Forweg), the previous captain, it was Algund who supported Túrin as "the best man among us" to replace the former leader.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Later, when the outlaws captured [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) when Túrin was absent, [Andróg](/wiki/Andr%C3%B3g) wished to kill him but Algund stayed his hand, saying that they should wait for Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> According to some versions of the tale, Algund was able to recognise the newcomer from his resemblance to his father.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> No record exists of Algund's fate: most likely, he was slain just a few years after the coming of Túrin, when the outlaws were ambushed by [Orcs](/wiki/Orc "Orc") on the summit of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh).
