**Aulendur**, was a name used for those [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) who entered [Aulë](Aulë.md)'s service, and who in return received instruction from him. [Mahtan](../M/Mahtan.md) is the only named member. Tolkien first wrote this word as <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Aulendil.md" title="Aulendil">Aulendil</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aulendur* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) for "Servant of [Aulë](Aulë.md)", from *[-ndur](../_/-ndur.md)* ("servant").
