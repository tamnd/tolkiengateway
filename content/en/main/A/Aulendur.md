**Aulendur**, was a name used for those [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) who entered [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB)'s service, and who in return received instruction from him. [Mahtan](/wiki/Mahtan) is the only named member. Tolkien first wrote this word as <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Aulendil" title="Aulendil">Aulendil</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Aulendur* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "Servant of [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB)", from *[-ndur](/wiki/-ndur)* ("servant").
