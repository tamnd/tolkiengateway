**Butterbur** was an old family of [Bree](/wiki/Bree), that had kept the inn there, the [Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony), for years beyond reckoning. The innkeeper at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) was [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The family is mentioned by Barliman Butterbur, who tells the player that the Prancing Pony has been in possession of the Butterbur family for many generations.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Butterbur (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_Butterbur" title="wikipedia:Common Butterbur">Petasites vulgaris</a>) is a fleshy plant with a heavy flower-head on a thick stalk, and very large leaves. It contains the element 'butter' also found in English place-names and surnames.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
