**Blunt the Knives** is the fourth track of disc one of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition)*. It is based on [the book's](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit") "[Chip the glasses and crack the plates!](/wiki/Chip_the_glasses_and_crack_the_plates!)"
