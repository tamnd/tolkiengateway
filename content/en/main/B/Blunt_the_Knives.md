**Blunt the Knives** is the fourth track of disc one of *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack - Special Edition](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack_-_Special_Edition.md)*. It is based on [the book's](../T/The_Hobbit.md) "[Chip the glasses and crack the plates!](../C/Chip_the_glasses_and_crack_the_plates!.md)"
