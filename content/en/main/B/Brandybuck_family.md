The **Brandybuck family** began as the [Oldbuck](../O/Oldbuck_Family.md) clan, who named themselves such apparently after a *[Bucca of the Marish](Bucca_of_the_Marish.md)*, who became the first [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Ten Oldbuck Thains followed, until *Gorhendad Oldbuck*, the twelfth Thain,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> migrated into [Buckland](Buckland.md) over the river [Brandywine](Baranduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-4">[4]</a></sup> This familly is of [Fallohide](../F/Fallohides.md) and [Stoor](../S/Stoors.md) origins<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Gorhendad renamed himself Brandybuck, and began delving the great [smials](../S/Smials.md) of [Brandy Hall](Brandy_Hall.md). Around the hill was built the village of *[Bucklebury](Bucklebury.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-4">[4]</a></sup> Starting with Gorhendad, the head of the Brandybuck family was called the *[Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Translation_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> or the *Master of the Hall*, and was in this function the master of Buckland, a virtually independent part of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). The farmers between [Stock](../S/Stock.md) and [Rushey](../R/Rushey.md) in the [Marish](../M/Marish.md) also acknowledged the authority of the Master of Buckland.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-4">[4]</a></sup>

It was traditional for each of the Masters to have an epigraph attached to their names, such as [Gorbadoc](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md) "[Broadbelt](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md)", [Saradoc](../S/Saradoc_Brandybuck.md) "Scattergold", and the most famous, [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) "the Magnificent".<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-10">[10]</a></sup>

The Bucklanders were seen as strange and half-foreigners by most of the other [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of the old Shire. However the Bucklanders were not very different from other Hobbits, except that they liked boats, that some of them could swim and that they locked their doors at night (because of the exposure to the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name contains the elements of <i><b>Brandy</b>wine</i> and <i>Old<b>buck</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-N_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-16">[12]</a></sup> It is a translation of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) *[Brandagamba](Brandagamba.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*Brandybuck* is a rare English name which [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) had come across.<sup id="cite_ref-N_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-16">[12]</a></sup>
