**Beren** means "bold" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and may refer to:

- [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(son_of_Belemir)> "Beren (son of Belemir)") (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [374](/wiki/First_Age_374 "First Age 374")), son of [Belemir](/wiki/Belemir) and grandfather of Beren Erchamion.
- **[Beren](/wiki/Beren) ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [443](/wiki/First_Age_443 "First Age 443") - [503](/wiki/First_Age_503 "First Age 503")), husband of [Lúthien Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien") and hero of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).**
- [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2655](/wiki/Third_Age_2655 "Third Age 2655") - [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763")), the nineteenth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
- Beren, a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name for [Beorn](</wiki/Beorn_(son_of_Heden)> "Beorn (son of Heden)"), son of [Heden](/wiki/Heden), in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
