**Baran** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [289](/wiki/First_Age_289 "First Age 289") - [380](/wiki/First_Age_380 "First Age 380"), age 91) was the second head of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Baran was the oldest son of [Bëor the Old](/wiki/B%C3%ABor "Bëor") and brother to [Belen](/wiki/Belen).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup> He took over leadership of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) in [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad) when his father left to serve as vassal to [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During his rule the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin), the [people of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador"), and the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) crossed over the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the introduction to *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) stated that they were written in the 1930s.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> This work was laid aside while [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote *The Lord of the Rings*, after which he returned to his work on the elder days. In his later writings Tolkien relocated the coming of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) over the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) nearly a century earlier, which occasioned a large-scale overhaul of the chronology and genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the older *Grey Annals* Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas) and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir). In the new version four generations (*Baran*, Boron, Boromir, and Bregor) were inserted after Bëor, with Bregor the father of Bregolas and Barahir.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup>
