The **Birmingham Oratory** is a [Roman Catholic](../C/Christianity.md) church in [Birmingham](Birmingham.md), England. It is used primarily by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oratory_of_Saint_Philip_Neri" title="wikipedia:Oratory of Saint Philip Neri">Oratory of Saint Philip Neri</a>, a congregation of priests and lay-brothers. Because of its baroque design, it is often called the "Little Rome in Birmingham".

The church was constructed between 1907 and 1910 as a memorial to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Henry_Newman" title="wikipedia:John Henry Newman">John Henry Newman</a>, a prominent English cardinal who converted from Anglicanism to Catholicism, now a canonized saint.

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), a devout Catholic, attended Mass here for about seven years. [Francis Xavier Morgan](../F/Francis_Xavier_Morgan.md), Ronald and [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md)'s guardian, was a priest at the Oratory.
