The **Bolger family** was a Hobbit family of [Fallohidish](../F/Fallohides.md) origin<sup id="cite_ref-trans_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-trans-1">[1]</a></sup> associated with the village of [Budgeford](Budgeford.md), on [The Water](../T/The_Water.md) in the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In common with the [Brandybucks](Brandybuck_Family~66350.md) and the [Tooks](../T/Took_Family.md), the Bolgers had a penchant for heroic names,<sup id="cite_ref-trans_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-trans-1">[1]</a></sup> and so as well as [Fredegar](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md), we find such noble names as [Fastolph](../F/Fastolph_Bolger.md), [Gundabald](../G/Gundabald_Bolger.md) and [Odovacar](../O/Odovacar_Bolger.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name **Bolger** is a reference to the "bulged" size of the average Hobbit belly, referring to their fatness and tubbiness and presumably begun as a nickname.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Both Bolger and Bulger are real-life English surnames although not necessarily of the same etymology.

It was a translation of Westron <b><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Bolgra&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bolgra (page does not exist)">Bolgra</a></b> of similar meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
