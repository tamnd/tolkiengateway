**Robert Bryan Cogman** ([July 25](../_/25_July.md), [1979](../_/1979.md)) is an American television writer and producer.He is mostly known for his work as writer and producer in television series <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_Thrones" title="wikipedia:Game of Thrones">Game of Thrones</a></i>, based on [George R. R. Martin](../G/George_R._R._Martin.md)'s books. He was a consulting producer and co-writer of the first season only of the *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power‎](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
