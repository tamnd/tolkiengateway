The **Battle of Tasarinan** took place in the [Land of Willows](../N/Nan-tathren.md) during the great expedition from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) to release the enslaved [Gnomes](../G/Gnomes.md) in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> No full, detailed account of the battle is given, as it is only mentioned passingly in the Lost Tales, and in various outlines for their continuation.

What little is known about it is that:

- The first host from the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../W/West.md" title="West">West</a>, including the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Noldorin](../S/Salmar.md), as well as the [Eldar](../E/Eldar.md), including [Valwë](../V/Valwë.md), father of [Lindo](../L/Lindo.md) the keeper of the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md), made camp in the Land of Willows.<sup id="cite_ref-Tulk_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tulk-2">[2]</a></sup>
- There, "sleeping and dancing", they grew complacent.<sup id="cite_ref-Tas_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tas-3">[3]</a></sup>
- After which they were ambushed and defeated by the [goblins](../O/Orcs.md) sent by [Melko](../M/Melko.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tas_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tas-3">[3]</a></sup>

Yet following the battle (according to some outlines of the story), Noldorin managed to escape (as well as Valwë<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>), and some time later Melko was defeated by [Tulkas](../T/Tulkas.md) in the [Battle of the Silent Pools](Battle_of_the_Silent_Pools.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tulk_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tulk-2">[2]</a></sup>
