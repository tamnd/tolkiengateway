The Orc **Berserker** was a concept developed for [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s film *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*. The Berserkers are portrayed as a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juggernaut" title="wikipedia:Juggernaut">juggernaut</a>-type of [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) with a great doubly-bent two-handed sword without guards. They are shock troops and wear no armor apart from a helmet, and, according to the [Weta Workshop](/wiki/Weta_Workshop) designers, they fill it with blood before placing them on their heads and charging into battle, developing a blood-lust for their enemies.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

A *Berserker Uruk-hai* is also featured in [LEGO: The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Lego "Lego") in the set *The Battle of Helm's Deep*.<sup id="cite_ref-2012_sets_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2012_sets-1">[1]</a></sup>

Berserker orcs also appear in *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*, although this time they are a type of [Gundabad Orcs](/wiki/Mount_Gundabad "Mount Gundabad"). They were part of [Bolg](/wiki/Bolg)'s second Orc army, and are apparently stronger than the average Gundabad Orc, with some coming close to killing [Thorin Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin_Oakenshield). Unlike the Uruk berserkers, they wear armor, with some having articulated breastplates resembling faces.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
