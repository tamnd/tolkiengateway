**Brent McIntyre** played the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), in the scenes when the character is hooded (as a "[Black Rider](../N/Nazgûl.md)"). He was only credited in the first film though. McIntyre also portrayed the Witch-king in the "[Wraith-world](../W/Wraith-world.md)" during the [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md) scene in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.

When the Witch-king battled [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) in *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*, the role was played by [Lawrence Makoare](../L/Lawrence_Makoare.md).

At 6'10" (2.08 meters), Brent McIntyre was the tallest actor in the films.
