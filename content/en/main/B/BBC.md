The **British Broadcasting Corporation**, or **BBC**, is the world's largest broadcasting corporation. As such, the company, and especially BBC Radio, have had a major influence on the adaptation of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s works.

## BBC Radio Drama {#BBC_Radio_Drama}

The <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/soundstart/rdc.shtml" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">BBC Repertory Company</a></i>, was founded in 1940. Early stars included [Norman Shelley](/wiki/Norman_Shelley) and Carleton Hobbs, who have played <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Watson_(Sherlock_Holmes)" title="wikipedia:John Watson (Sherlock Holmes)">Watson</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes" title="wikipedia:Sherlock Holmes">Holmes</a> in over nintey radio dramas.

The "*Rep*" kept an ensemble cast of 30 to 35 actors, up until the mid-1980's. This cast included young theatre talent, such as [Anthony Daniels](/wiki/Anthony_Daniels) and [William Nighy](/wiki/Bill_Nighy "Bill Nighy"), and was sometimes expanded with popular names.

This section of the BBC is now called the *National Radio Company* or *NRC*, and has been awarded many times.

## Programs {#Programs}

- [January 14](/wiki/14_January "14 January"), [1938](/wiki/1938): An unspecified program on BBC Radio, where Tolkien gave a talk on *Anglo-Saxon Verse*.
- [December 3](/wiki/3_December "3 December"), [1954](/wiki/1954): The broadcast of the radio play of *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son](</wiki/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm%27s_Son_(radio_play)> "The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son (radio play)")*, on the BBC Third Programme.
- [1955](/wiki/1955)-[1956](/wiki/1956): The [first adaptation](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* is aired on BBC Radio.
- [March 30](/wiki/30_March "30 March"), [1968](/wiki/1968): BBC Television airs the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](/wiki/Tolkien_in_Oxford)*.
- September–November [1968](/wiki/1968): The BBC's [radio adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1968 radio series)") of *The Hobbit* is aired.
- January [1971](/wiki/1971): [An Interview with J.R.R.T.](/wiki/An_Interview_with_J.R.R.T.) (recorded in [1965](/wiki/1965)) is first aired.
- [1978](/wiki/1978): Though shot in Spain, [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi) recorded the voices of his *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)")* with several actors from the NRC.
- [October](/wiki/October), [1979](/wiki/1979): The popular children's show *[Jackanory](/wiki/Jackanory)* airs ten episodes on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.
- [1980](/wiki/1980): [Hordes of the Things](/wiki/Hordes_of_the_Things)
- [1981](/wiki/1981): The famous [second radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") of *The Lord of the Rings* is aired.
- [1987](/wiki/1987): *[The Adventures of Frodo](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Frodo)* with [Michael Hordern](/wiki/Michael_Hordern)
- [1992](/wiki/1992): The 6-episode radio series *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](</wiki/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series)> "Tales from the Perilous Realm (1992 radio series)")* is aired.
- [2012](/wiki/2012): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_in_love&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien in love (page does not exist)">Tolkien in love</a></i> is aired on BBC radio 4.
- [2012](/wiki/2012): *The Hobbit, the Musical*, a BBC Radio 4 programme about the [1967 stage adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation)> "The Hobbit (1967 stage adaptation)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*
- [2013](/wiki/2013): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Beyond_Belief&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beyond Belief (page does not exist)">Beyond Belief</a></i> discuss the theology of J.R.R. Tolkien.
- [2013](/wiki/2013): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=When_Tolkien_Stole_Wagner%27s_Ring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="When Tolkien Stole Wagner&#39;s Ring (page does not exist)">When Tolkien Stole Wagner&#39;s Ring</a></i> is aired on BBC radio 3.
- [2013](/wiki/2013): *Lewis and Tolkien: The Lost Road*
- [26 October](/wiki/26_October) [2014](/wiki/2014): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hope_in_the_Darkness&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hope in the Darkness (page does not exist)">Hope in the Darkness</a></i> Dr Simon Jones and Alister McGrath explore the life and spirituality of J.R.R. Tolkien
- [15 November](/wiki/15_November) [2014](/wiki/2014): *[War of Words: Soldier-Poets of the Somme](/wiki/War_of_Words:_Soldier-Poets_of_the_Somme)*
