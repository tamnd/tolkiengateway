The **British Broadcasting Corporation**, or **BBC**, is the world's largest broadcasting corporation. As such, the company, and especially BBC Radio, have had a major influence on the adaptation of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works.

## BBC Radio Drama {#BBC_Radio_Drama}

The <i><a class="external text" href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/soundstart/rdc.shtml" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">BBC Repertory Company</a></i>, was founded in 1940. Early stars included [Norman Shelley](../N/Norman_Shelley.md) and Carleton Hobbs, who have played <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Watson_(Sherlock_Holmes)" title="wikipedia:John Watson (Sherlock Holmes)">Watson</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes" title="wikipedia:Sherlock Holmes">Holmes</a> in over nintey radio dramas.

The "*Rep*" kept an ensemble cast of 30 to 35 actors, up until the mid-1980's. This cast included young theatre talent, such as [Anthony Daniels](../A/Anthony_Daniels.md) and [William Nighy](Bill_Nighy.md), and was sometimes expanded with popular names.

This section of the BBC is now called the *National Radio Company* or *NRC*, and has been awarded many times.

## Programs {#Programs}

- [January 14](../_/14_January.md), [1938](../_/1938.md): An unspecified program on BBC Radio, where Tolkien gave a talk on *Anglo-Saxon Verse*.
- [December 3](../_/3_December.md), [1954](../_/1954.md): The broadcast of the radio play of *[The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth, Beorhthelm's Son](<../T/The_Homecoming_of_Beorhtnoth,_Beorhthelm's_Son_(radio_play).md>)*, on the BBC Third Programme.
- [1955](../_/1955.md)-[1956](../_/1956.md): The [first adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md>) of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* is aired on BBC Radio.
- [March 30](../_/30_March.md), [1968](../_/1968.md): BBC Television airs the documentary *[Tolkien in Oxford](../T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*.
- September–November [1968](../_/1968.md): The BBC's [radio adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md>) of *The Hobbit* is aired.
- January [1971](../_/1971.md): [An Interview with J.R.R.T.](../A/An_Interview_with_J.R.R.T..md) (recorded in [1965](../_/1965.md)) is first aired.
- [1978](../_/1978.md): Though shot in Spain, [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md) recorded the voices of his *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>)* with several actors from the NRC.
- [October](../O/October.md), [1979](../_/1979.md): The popular children's show *[Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)* airs ten episodes on *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.
- [1980](../_/1980.md): [Hordes of the Things](../H/Hordes_of_the_Things.md)
- [1981](../_/1981.md): The famous [second radio series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of *The Lord of the Rings* is aired.
- [1987](../_/1987.md): *[The Adventures of Frodo](../T/The_Adventures_of_Frodo.md)* with [Michael Hordern](../M/Michael_Hordern.md)
- [1992](../_/1992.md): The 6-episode radio series *[Tales from the Perilous Realm](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>)* is aired.
- [2012](../_/2012.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_in_love&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien in love (page does not exist)">Tolkien in love</a></i> is aired on BBC radio 4.
- [2012](../_/2012.md): *The Hobbit, the Musical*, a BBC Radio 4 programme about the [1967 stage adaptation](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_stage_adaptation).md>) of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*
- [2013](../_/2013.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Beyond_Belief&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Beyond Belief (page does not exist)">Beyond Belief</a></i> discuss the theology of J.R.R. Tolkien.
- [2013](../_/2013.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=When_Tolkien_Stole_Wagner%27s_Ring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="When Tolkien Stole Wagner&#39;s Ring (page does not exist)">When Tolkien Stole Wagner&#39;s Ring</a></i> is aired on BBC radio 3.
- [2013](../_/2013.md): *Lewis and Tolkien: The Lost Road*
- [26 October](../_/26_October.md) [2014](../_/2014.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hope_in_the_Darkness&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hope in the Darkness (page does not exist)">Hope in the Darkness</a></i> Dr Simon Jones and Alister McGrath explore the life and spirituality of J.R.R. Tolkien
- [15 November](../_/15_November.md) [2014](../_/2014.md): *[War of Words: Soldier-Poets of the Somme](../W/War_of_Words%253A_Soldier-Poets_of_the_Somme.md)*
