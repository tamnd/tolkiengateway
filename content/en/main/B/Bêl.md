<i><b>Bêl</b></i> is a verb stem which means "to befriend" or "to love" in [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

## See also {#See_also}

- [Belzagar](/wiki/Ar-Belzagar "Ar-Belzagar") could be an Adûnaic translation of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word [*Calmacil*](/wiki/Tar-Calmacil "Tar-Calmacil"), which means "Light-sword".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In this case *bêl* could mean "light". It is not clear whether [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") changed the meaning of *bêl* at some point, or whether these two meanings co-exist<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.
- *[Azrubêl](/wiki/Azrub%C3%AAl)* from *Azar* ("sea") and the stem -bêl, translates to "Sea-lover"<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup>.
