**Beornings** were a people that lived east of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell)<sup id="cite_ref-Beornings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beornings-1">[1]</a></sup> between the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood)<sup id="cite_ref-FRII1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRII1-2">[2]</a></sup> in the upper [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin)<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup> on both sides of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Beornings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beornings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Beornings were [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen), and as such descended from the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) or from their close kin.<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup> Specifically, they were close akin to the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) (the ancestors of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim)),<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> who were most nearly akin to the [third people of the Edain](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies) and the decimation of the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains), many Northmen gathered around [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) who became a great chief. It is possible that they were named Beornings, because they were descendants of people whose chief was Beorn.<sup id="cite_ref-H18_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H18-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[note 1]</a></sup>

The Beornings kept open the passages of the [Ford of Carrock](/wiki/Ford_of_Carrock) and the [High Pass](/wiki/High_Pass) in return for high tolls, likely keeping the paths clear of evil creatures such as [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Wolves](/wiki/Wolves).<sup id="cite_ref-FRII1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRII1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

In the events leading up to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the Beornings helped [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), who was taking [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) to Mirkwood, to cross the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) near the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

During the War of the Ring, [Grimbeorn](/wiki/Grimbeorn), son of Beorn, was the lord of the Beornings.<sup id="cite_ref-FRII1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRII1-2">[2]</a></sup> It is possible that [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s forces had attacked them in early [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), because [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) saw the land of the Beornings aflame when he wore the [One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") on the Seat of Seeing upon [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen)<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> on 26 February [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

After the War of the Ring, the Beornings and the [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen) were given the central [Eryn Lasgalen](/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen) between the [Mountains of Mirkwood](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood) and the [Narrows of the Forest](/wiki/Narrows_of_the_Forest) by [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

The language of the Beornings was closely related to [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) and the [language of Dale](/wiki/Language_of_Dale "Language of Dale"), and more distantly related to [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) and to its descendant the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron").<sup id="cite_ref-OfMen_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OfMen-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[13]</a></sup> They also spoke the [Common Speech](/wiki/Westron "Westron") as a second language of intercourse.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[15]</a></sup>

Through many generations, the descendants of Beorn were like him [skin-changers](/wiki/Skin-changers "Skin-changers"), able to take the shape of a [bear](/wiki/Bears "Bears").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[note 2]</a></sup> Some of Beorn's descendants were grim like him and even "bad", but none of them matched Beorn in size and strength.<sup id="cite_ref-H18_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H18-6">[6]</a></sup>

The Beornings seemed to have shared a dislike for [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves),<sup id="cite_ref-FRII1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRII1-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup> perhaps related to the latter's praise of metals and the former's disinterest in it.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup>

They were known as great bakers, famous for their [honey-cakes](/wiki/Honey-cakes "Honey-cakes") (which could feed travelers similarly to the *[lembas](/wiki/Lembas "Lembas")*) although they were reluctant to share them with travelers around the time of the War of the Ring.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[18]</a></sup>

<b>2011-: <a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)"><i>The One Ring</i> (role-playing game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Beornings are one of the playable cultures. The game describes them as rough Men, sometimes outlaws, gathered under the banner of Beorn. One of their cultural Virtues is the ability to take control of a &#39;spirit animal&#39; whilst sleeping, a talent taught to some Beornings by Beorn himself.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[19]</a></sup></dd></dl>

**2013: [The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug):**

<dl><dd>Beorn tells <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> that in the past Azog hunted him and his people for sport and that, as a result, few of his kind are left. It is one of his prime motivations to help them in <a href="/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor" title="Quest of Erebor">their Quest</a>.</dd></dl>

**2014: [The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online):**

<dl><dd>The ancestors of the Beornings were taught the art of <a href="/wiki/Skin-changers" title="Skin-changers">skin-changing</a> as a gift by <a href="/wiki/Radagast" title="Radagast">Radagast</a> the Brown, &#34;master of shapes and hues&#34;. Evil men jealous of this gift captured a skin-changer and tortured him until he gave the secret away. Hearing of this, Radagast grieved and changed the gift so it could no longer be taught, only passed down to progeny. But the two new <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Skin-changer" title="Skin-changer">Skin-changer</a> tribes - &#34;Gauredain&#34;, men of the wolf, and &#34;Ungoledain&#34;, men of the spider, - persisted and remained the most bitter enemies of the Beornings, even moreso than the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Goblins" title="Goblins">goblins</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In the present day, Beornings live in and around the <a href="/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin" title="Vales of Anduin">Vales of Anduin</a>. Outside <a href="/wiki/Beorn%27s_Hall" title="Beorn&#39;s Hall">Beorninghús</a> they have two notable settlements: Limlók, a village to the north, and Vegbár, a toll-collecting station on the road to the <a href="/wiki/Misty_Mountains" title="Misty Mountains">Misty Mountains</a>. Other Beornings live in the wild in small groups or by themselves, as far north as the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Mountains" title="Grey Mountains">Grey Mountains</a>, but even those who have little to no contact with their kin to the south still acknowledge <a href="/wiki/Grimbeorn" title="Grimbeorn">Grimbeorn</a> as their chieftain. All Beornings have a special love for honey-cakes, dedicating a large amount of attention and effort to their baking.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Beornings were made playable in 2014, uniquely combining the race and the class. They can transform into the shape of the bear at will and perform skills in both man- and bear-form. The player&#39;s character is the youngest child of <a href="/wiki/Grimbeorn" title="Grimbeorn">Grimbeorn</a> who is sent by their sire to deliver a message to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Strider" title="Strider">Strider</a> from <a href="/wiki/Radagast" title="Radagast">Radagast</a> and takes on the adventuring life. Other Beornings also travel away from their home on various errands, ranging from the <a href="/wiki/Lone-lands" title="Lone-lands">Lone-lands</a> in the west to the <a href="/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls" title="Elvenking&#39;s Halls">Elvenking&#39;s Halls</a> in the east and as far south as the river <a href="/wiki/Limlight" title="Limlight">Limlight</a>.</dd></dl>
