**Beowulf Launch Party** was an online event dated of [24 May](/wiki/24_May) [2014](/wiki/2014) to celebrate the release of the book *[Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary)*. It was organized by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) and [Middle-earth Network](/wiki/Middle-earth_Network) with support from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Brasil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Brasil (page does not exist)">Tolkien Brasil</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Tolkienist&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Tolkienist (page does not exist)">The Tolkienist</a>.
