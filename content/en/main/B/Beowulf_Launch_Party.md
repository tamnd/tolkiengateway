**Beowulf Launch Party** was an online event dated of [24 May](../_/24_May.md) [2014](../_/2014.md) to celebrate the release of the book *[Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](Beowulf%253A_A_Translation_and_Commentary.md)*. It was organized by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) and [Middle-earth Network](../M/Middle-earth_Network.md) with support from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Tolkien_Brasil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tolkien Brasil (page does not exist)">Tolkien Brasil</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Tolkienist&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Tolkienist (page does not exist)">The Tolkienist</a>.
