**Barliman Butterbur** was a [Man of Bree](../M/Men_of_Bree.md) who was the owner of *[The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md)*.

A short, fat, red-faced [Man](../M/Men.md), he appeared to have had a rather bad memory.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md) in [Bree](Bree.md) was kept by the [Butterbur Family](Butterbur_Family.md) for many generations and Barliman inherited it before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He had two [hobbit](Bree-hobbits.md) employees: [Bob](Bob.md), who worked in the stables, and [Nob](../N/Nob.md), a servant.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the War, [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) wrote a [letter](../G/Gandalf's_letter.md) to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and told Barliman to send it to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md). Barliman could not find anyone for the task, and eventually forgot the letter altogether, leaving Frodo uncertain as to Gandalf's fate and causing him to start his journey much later than the letter indicated.<sup id="cite_ref-strider_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-strider-3">[3]</a></sup>

He did not remember it until autumn, when Frodo and his companions arrived to stay at the inn, and even then it was long before the connection was made. He did, however, remember to produce [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md)'s credentials.<sup id="cite_ref-strider_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-strider-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the night of the hobbits' stay, the inn was attacked by the [Black Riders](Black_Riders.md). Barliman was dismayed by the terrible events of the night, and was further upset in the morning, when he discovered that the stable-doors had been opened, and all of the [horses](../H/Horses.md) and [ponies](../P/Ponies.md) kept there had escaped. As compensation, he purchased the only [pony](Bill_the_pony~28103.md) found to be available from [Bill Ferny](Bill_Ferny.md), who demanded at least thrice its proper price. This he gave to the hobbits along with eighteen silver pennies, although the loss of the money was a harsh blow to him. Fortunately, many of the horses had only been set loose, and were found again later. [Merry](../M/Merry.md)'s ponies fled to [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md), who eventually returned them to Barliman for use at the inn.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When Gandalf finally arrived at The Prancing Pony, he was furious with Barliman's forgetfulness, but quickly forgave him when he found out the Hobbits were alive. He even went so far as to put a blessing on his beer.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Barliman was amazed to learn that Strider, a former patron of the Prancing Pony, had become king of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Barliman means "barley-man", a suitable name for an ale-brewer.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first version of the events at the Prancing Pony, written in pencil, the name of the innkeeper was **Timothy Titus**, "a very small man" whose friends called him **Tim Tip**,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> from an old incomplete oral story by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> This character bore no resemblance to Mr. Butterbur. Timothy Titus was written over in ink with the name **Barnabas Butterbur** in Tolkien's manuscript and was created as a very fat Hobbit before he was subsequently changed into the man called Barliman.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Barliman is a non-playable character that can be found at <a href="../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md" title="The Prancing Pony">the Prancing Pony</a>. The player meets with him during Volume I <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Shadows_of_Angmar.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Shadows of Angmar">Shadows of Angmar</a></i>, Book I: &#34;Stirrings in the Darkness&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b>2010: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Aragorn&#39;s_Quest.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest">The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Butterbur appears briefly at the start of the Bree mission, where he informs Aragorn that he might learn the location of the Hobbits by asking around town.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Barliman Butterbur is the inn-keeper, and can be found behind the bar in the Prancing Pony. He tells the player news about troubles in the south, and <a href="../S/Southerners.md" title="Southerners">Southerners</a> who have come lately to Bree, and information about the Prancing Pony, that it almost exists as long as Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>
