The **battle of the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros)** was a battle in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2882](/wiki/Third_Age_2882 "Third Age 2882") in which [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward") [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), aided by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) defeated the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) who had invaded [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).

## Prelude {#Prelude}

In the days of [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards "Ruling Stewards") [Túrin II](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had again regrown his power. All except the toughest of the people of Ithlien fled west over the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), because Ithilien was plagued by invasions of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). Túrin II had secret refuges, such as [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn) built for his soldiers in Ithilien. The most important danger to Gondor was in its south, because the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) had occupied [South Gondor](/wiki/South_Gondor) and there were many armed conflicts along the river [Poros](/wiki/Poros).<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Battle {#Battle}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2885](/wiki/Third_Age_2885 "Third Age 2885") emissaries of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) stirred up the Haradrim who crossed the Poros invaded Ithilien<sup id="cite_ref-TA2885_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA2885-2">[2]</a></sup> with a large army.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-1">[1]</a></sup> When King [Folcwine](/wiki/Folcwine) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) heard of the invasion he fulfilled the [Oath of Eorl](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl)<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-1">[1]</a></sup> and sent many men led by his sons Folcred and Fastred to help Túrin II.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-3">[3]</a></sup>

Túrin II defeated the Haradrim invaders with the help of the troops from Rohan in a battle at the [Crossings of Poros](/wiki/Crossings_of_Poros),<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-1">[1]</a></sup> but Folcred and Fastred fell side by side in battle in Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-Folcwine_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folcwine-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

While Túrin achieved victory at the crossing of Poros, the Rohirrim suffered the loss of their two oldest princes. Folcred and Fastred were buried together high upon the shore of the river Poros according to the customs of their [people](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") in a burial mound, which was called *[Haudh in Gwanûr](/wiki/Haudh_in_Gwan%C3%BBr)*. The mound stood there for a long time and the enemies of Gondor were afraid to pass the mound.<sup id="cite_ref-TurinII_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TurinII-1">[1]</a></sup> As a consequence of the death of his two older brothers Folcred and Fastred, [Fengel](/wiki/Fengel), the third and youngest son of King Folcwine became heir to the throne of Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Fengel_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fengel-4">[4]</a></sup>
