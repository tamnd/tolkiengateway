<i><b>bara</b></i> is an [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin") word meaning "fiery; eager". The word is said to be "frequent in masculine names".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
