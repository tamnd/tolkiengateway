**Buck Hill** was a hill overlooking the village of [Bucklebury](/wiki/Bucklebury) in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), deeply burrowed with the [smials](/wiki/Smials "Smials") that made up [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall), the ancestral home of the [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family") family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
