**BAR** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "uplift, save, rescue(?)". Its original significance was probably "raise".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *varna* ("safe, protected, secure"); *varnasse* ("security"); *varya-* ("to protect")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *berio* ("to protect")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
