**Burin** was a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) that [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) abandoned while in the process of creating *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.

## History {#History}

This character was first mentioned as Frár, who attended the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) with [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md). He was changed to Burin the son of [Balin](Balin.md), that later would join the [Fellowship](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) on their quest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When that idea was abandoned and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) was created in his stead, the name was used for the father of Balin, but that was scrapped in favour of [Fundin](../F/Fundin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Burin*, or *Burinn*, is a dwarf name from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*, meaning "son-like".<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarf_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarf-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:943</sup> Frár means "swift".<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarf_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarf-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:947</sup>
