**Burin** was a [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") that [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") abandoned while in the process of creating *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## History {#History}

This character was first mentioned as Frár, who attended the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) with [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in). He was changed to Burin the son of [Balin](/wiki/Balin), that later would join the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") on their quest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When that idea was abandoned and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) was created in his stead, the name was used for the father of Balin, but that was scrapped in favour of [Fundin](/wiki/Fundin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Burin*, or *Burinn*, is a dwarf name from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*, meaning "son-like".<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarf_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarf-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:943</sup> Frár means "swift".<sup id="cite_ref-Dwarf_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dwarf-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:947</sup>
