**Belegaer**, the **Great Sea**, the **Sundering Seas**, or the **Western Sea**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> was the sea of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) that lay west of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Prior to the [Changing of the World](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World), Belegaer was bordered by the continent [Aman](/wiki/Aman), home of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), on its western shores. [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) formed the sea's eastern border.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> Belegaer stretched to the edge of Middle-earth in the far north, where its waters met those of the [Encircling Sea](/wiki/Encircling_Sea) at the icy wastes of the [Helcaraxë](/wiki/Helcarax%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> Presumably, it stretched likewise to the uttermost south, perhaps terminating in another icy waste where it met the Encircling Seas once more.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>

Following the [Battle of the Powers](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Powers), the shape of Middle-earth was altered, and Belegaer was widened and deepened. It formed many new gulfs and bays along the coast of Middle-earth, including the bay of Balar.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup> The sea was expanded once more as a result of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) at the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), when [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and many of the western and northern lands of Middle-earth were sunk beneath its waters.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

During the events of the [Akallabêth](/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the seas were bent and the world was made round. [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) was sunk beneath the sea, Aman was removed from the world, and [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) cast back the Great Seas west of Middle-earth, forming new lands and seas. From this point on, only the chosen could find the Straight Road to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-akallabeth_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-akallabeth-8">[6]</a></sup> The full extent of Belegaer after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) is never made clear, but it reached far enough to the north to be ice-covered, and far to the south.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> The new western end of Belegaer is never described in the narrative, but [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) refugees sailing in search of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) were said to have discovered new mortal lands, or else sailed even farther and come around the world in a circle.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup>

## Islands {#Islands}

Belegaer had several islands and island chains:

- [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), the Lonely Isle close to the shore of Aman
- the Twilit Isles or [Enchanted Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles) seaward of Tol Eressëa
- [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) (Second Age only)
- the Western Isles (after the First Age)
    - [Tol Morwen](/wiki/Tol_Morwen)
    - [Tol Fuin](/wiki/Tol_Fuin)
    - [Himring](/wiki/Himring)
- [Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar "Isle of Balar"), in the Bay of Balar (First Age only)
- [Tolfalas](/wiki/Tolfalas), in the Bay of Belfalas
- [Ened](/wiki/Ened), in the Firth of Drengist

## Bays and inlets {#Bays_and_inlets}

Other features of Belegaer:

- Bay of [Eldamar](/wiki/Eldamar)
- [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar) (First Age only)
- the [Great Gulf](/wiki/Great_Gulf) (First Age only)
- [Firth of Drengist](/wiki/Firth_of_Drengist) (First Age only)
- Ice Bay of [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel) (after First Age)
- [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune) (after First Age)
- [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) (after First Age)

*Belegaer* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Great Sea", from *[beleg](</wiki/Beleg_(word)> "Beleg (word)")* ("mighty") + *[aer](/wiki/Aer "Aer")* ("sea").<sup id="cite_ref-SilApp_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SilApp-10">[8]</a></sup> The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of Belegaer, never used in primary writing, is <i><b>Alatairë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-etym_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-11">[9]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Belegaer is comparable to the real <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Ocean" title="wikipedia:Atlantic Ocean">Atlantic Ocean</a>, which was once known to English cartographers as the Great Western Ocean.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup>

This is further reinforced by Henry Resnick quoting Tolkien as saying that "*Middle-earth is Europe*",<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup> and [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) noting that "*Belegaer was comparable to the Atlantic*".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup>
