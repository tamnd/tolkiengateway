**BARÁS** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) given no signification, except from what can be determined from its derivatives. The root is said to be "only found in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *barasá* ("hot, burning")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *barasa*, *baraha* ("hot, burning")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *[bara](Bara.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *b’rás-sē* ("heat")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *brasse* ("white heat")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *brass*, *[brassen](Brassen.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
