**BARÁS** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") given no signification, except from what can be determined from its derivatives. The root is said to be "only found in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *barasá* ("hot, burning")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *barasa*, *baraha* ("hot, burning")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *[bara](/wiki/Bara "Bara")*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *b’rás-sē* ("heat")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *brasse* ("white heat")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *brass*, *[brassen](/wiki/Brassen "Brassen")*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
