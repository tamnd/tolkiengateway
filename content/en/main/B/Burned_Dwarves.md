The **burned Dwarves** were a posthumously named group of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) who were proudly honoured in memoriam after their bodies were burned with fire by their kin after the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar).<sup id="cite_ref-Burned_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burned-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the aftermath of the bloody [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar),<sup id="cite_ref-pyre_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pyre-2">[2]</a></sup> the surviving Dwarves were unable to properly dispose of their dead, having no time to build stone tombs and refusing to bury their fallen comrades in earth, this being against their traditions.<sup id="cite_ref-Burned_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burned-1">[1]</a></sup> Therefore, they had no choice but to burn the corpses upon many pyres after relieving them of their arms and armor<sup id="cite_ref-pyre_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pyre-2">[2]</a></sup> in order to prevent the desecration of their kin by [birds](/wiki/Category:Birds "Category:Birds"), [beasts](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar"), and carrion-[orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs").<sup id="cite_ref-Burned_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burned-1">[1]</a></sup> The smoke of this burning was seen as far as [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-pyre_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pyre-2">[2]</a></sup>

To this day, any Dwarf whose relative was burned at [Azanulbizar](/wiki/Azanulbizar) would proudly say that their ancestor was a burned Dwarf, which was enough to honour them.<sup id="cite_ref-Burned_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burned-1">[1]</a></sup>
