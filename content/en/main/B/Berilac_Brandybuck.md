**Berilac Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Berilac was the son of [Merimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Merimac_Brandybuck). He was a guest at [Bilbo's](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Berilac* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

A note in the [edition](</wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition)> "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight (edition)") of the poem <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a> by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) mentions the name *Bercilak de Hautdesert* as the name of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Knight" title="wikipedia:Green Knight">Green Knight</a>. This mote also mentions that it is not clear if the fourth letter in the name of this character should be read as a "c" or a "t". The similar name *Bertilak* was used by J.R.R. Tolkien as the name of the Green Knight in his [translation](/wiki/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo "Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, Pearl, and Sir Orfeo") of the poem *Sir Gawain and the Green Knight*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he had the name Bercilac.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
