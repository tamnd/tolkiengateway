**Berilac Brandybuck** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Berilac was the son of [Merimac Brandybuck](../M/Merimac_Brandybuck.md). He was a guest at [Bilbo's](Bilbo_Baggins.md) [Farewell Party](Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Berilac* is unknown. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-2">[2]</a></sup>

A note in the [edition](<../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition).md>) of the poem <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight.md" title="Sir Gawain and the Green Knight">Sir Gawain and the Green Knight</a> by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) mentions the name *Bercilak de Hautdesert* as the name of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Knight" title="wikipedia:Green Knight">Green Knight</a>. This mote also mentions that it is not clear if the fourth letter in the name of this character should be read as a "c" or a "t". The similar name *Bertilak* was used by J.R.R. Tolkien as the name of the Green Knight in his [translation](../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight,_Pearl,_and_Sir_Orfeo.md) of the poem *Sir Gawain and the Green Knight*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of the family tree of the Brandybuck family he had the name Bercilac.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
