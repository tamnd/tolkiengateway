<i><b>Bonhams Books, Maps, Manuscripts and Historical Photographs 12 June 2012</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Bonhams (London). The description for Lot 150 includes extracts of a postcard from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to Miss Kilbride (cf. "[Letter to Miss Kilbride](/wiki/Letter_to_Miss_Kilbride)"). The postcard is reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
