<i><b>Barbie</b></i> is a popular doll franchise owned by <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattel" title="wikipedia:Mattel">Mattel</a></i>. They produced officially licensed <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbie" title="wikipedia:Barbie">Barbie</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_(doll)" title="wikipedia:Ken (doll)">Ken</a></i> dolls based on [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>). A [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) doll were sold separately, and there was a two-pack of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) and [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md).
