**Beregar** was a nobleman of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), descended from the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Beregar was a descendant of [Beleth](/wiki/Beleth), daughter of [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas), of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>

He lived in the [Andustar](/wiki/Andustar) region of Númenor. Beregar married [Núneth](/wiki/N%C3%BAneth) and they had a daughter named [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis), whom Beregar brought to the feast held when the son of [Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) was proclaimed as his heir. There Erendis became enamoured of [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") and eventually became his queen. Aldarion and Erendis had a daughter of their own, and so Beregar became the grandfather of the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor), [Tar-Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Beregar* could be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "Fierce lord", from [BEREK](/wiki/BEREK) ("fierce, wild") and *[ar](/wiki/Aran "Aran")* ("noble");<sup id="cite_ref-Chapter_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chapter-3">[3]</a></sup> or it could be related to the old [Bëorian](/wiki/Taliska "Taliska") name [Bereg](/wiki/Bereg).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>
