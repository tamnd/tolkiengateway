**Beregar** was a nobleman of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md), descended from the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).

## History {#History}

Beregar was a descendant of [Beleth](Beleth.md), daughter of [Bregolas](Bregolas.md), of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>

He lived in the [Andustar](../A/Andustar.md) region of Númenor. Beregar married [Núneth](../N/Núneth.md) and they had a daughter named [Erendis](../E/Erendis.md), whom Beregar brought to the feast held when the son of [Tar-Meneldur](../T/Tar-Meneldur.md) was proclaimed as his heir. There Erendis became enamoured of [Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md) and eventually became his queen. Aldarion and Erendis had a daughter of their own, and so Beregar became the grandfather of the first [Ruling Queen of Númenor](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md), [Tar-Ancalimë](../T/Tar-Ancalimë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Beregar* could be [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), meaning "Fierce lord", from [BEREK](BEREK.md) ("fierce, wild") and *[ar](../A/Aran.md)* ("noble");<sup id="cite_ref-Chapter_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chapter-3">[3]</a></sup> or it could be related to the old [Bëorian](../T/Taliska.md) name [Bereg](Bereg.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>
