The **Bay of Belfalas**, also called the **windy Bay of Bel**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a great gulf of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") between the cape of [Andrast](/wiki/Andrast) and [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar).<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the east of the bay the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) flowed into the bay with the island of [Tolfalas](/wiki/Tolfalas) near its mouth. The Gondorian fief of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) was located north west of Tolfalas.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The shores around the Bay were mostly desolate in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), except from the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) settlement of [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) which became the haven [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

During the [Drowning of Númenor](/wiki/Drowning_of_N%C3%BAmenor), the Bay of Belfalas was much filled at the east and south, and land upheaved around it. Anduin carved a new path by many [mouths](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin") to the Bay.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Tolfalas was almost destroyed, and was left at last like a barren and lonely mountain in the water not far from the issue of the River.

The coasts of Gondor were assaulted by the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar). In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") three fleets from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) attacked and waged war on all the Gondorian coasts until [Beregond](</wiki/Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beregond (Steward of Gondor)") overcame the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the Bay of Belfalas was called <i><b><a href="/wiki/C%C3%B4f" title="Côf">Côf</a> Belfalas</b></i>, or <i><b>Côf <a href="/wiki/Gwaeren" title="Gwaeren">gwaeren</a> Bêl</b></i> ("the windy Bay of Bêl").<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Belfalas* is composed of [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) *[bel](/wiki/Bel "Bel")* + Sindarin *[falas](/wiki/Falas "Falas")*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
