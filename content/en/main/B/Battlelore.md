**Battlelore** is an epic fantasy power metal band from Lappeenranta, Finland. Their style takes elements from gothic metal, power metal, death metal, and even some industrial in the mix. All of Battlelore's lyrics specifically revolve around people and events in [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## Members {#Members}

### Current members {#Current_members}

- Kaisa Jouhki (b. 7 July 1980) - vocals

- Jussi Rautio - lead guitars
- Jyri Vahvanen - guitars
- Maria - keyboards &amp; flute
- Timo Honkanen (b. 14 December 1979) - bass
- Henri Vahvanen - drums
