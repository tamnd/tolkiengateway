The **Battle of Greenfields** or **Battle of the Green Fields**<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup> was a battle that took place at the [Greenfields](../G/Greenfields.md) region in the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). It was the first battle fought in the Shire, and the only one until the [Battle of Bywater](Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

The Shire was generally untroubled by any harm for the first 1140 years of its history. [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) was quiet until [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2740](../T/Third_Age_2740.md), when [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) invaded several parts of Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-AppB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppB-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md) fought many battles trying to hold them back, but one party managed to reach the Shire.

### The Battle {#The_Battle}

Seven years after the start of the unrest, a band of Orcs of [Mount Gram](../M/Mount_Gram.md), led by their king [Golfimbul](../G/Golfimbul.md), entered the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-2">[2]</a></sup> [Bandobras Took](Bandobras_Took.md), who was living in [Long Cleeve](../L/Long_Cleeve.md), crossed the river [Norbourn](../N/Norbourn.md) to engage them in the region of the [Greenfields](../G/Greenfields.md), between the Norbourn and the [Brandywine](Brandywine.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Index_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-4">[4]</a></sup> Large enough to ride a horse, Bandobras charged the helm of the Orc-band and knocked Golfimbul's head off with his club. The head landed in a [rabbit](../R/Rabbits.md)-hole a hundred yards away.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup> Dismayed by his death, the orcs fled.<sup id="cite_ref-Concerning_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Concerning-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

With the rout of the orcs and the death of their king, their short uprisings within Eriador ceased, and eventually their attacks on the region stopped altogether.<sup id="cite_ref-Index_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-4">[4]</a></sup> The Shire, and Eriador as a whole, would not suffer from any more invasions until the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-2">[2]</a></sup>

Bandobras became a hero of Hobbit legend for his feat of arms, and was regarded in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) as one of the greatest Hobbits in history. To commemorate Golfimbul's head landing in the rabbit-hole, the hobbits invented the game of [golf](../G/Golf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first phase of *The Hobbit*, Golfimbul was called "[Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md)" and the conflict was called "’the **Battle of the**’ followed by a cancelled, illegible word of four or five letters that ended in -ll (possibly ‘Bull-’?)".<sup id="cite_ref-bullroarer_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bullroarer-6">[6]</a></sup> This was immediately changed to the **battle of the Green Fields of Fellin**,<sup id="cite_ref-Pryftan_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pryftan-7">[7]</a></sup> before swiftly changing *Fellin* to <i><b>Fao</b></i> which in turn was changed, likely at once, to <i><b>Merria</b></i> before the word was fully written.<sup id="cite_ref-bullroarer_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bullroarer-6">[6]</a></sup> [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) notes that while these names never appear again, *Merria* bears a resemblance to the [Merrill](../M/Merrill.md), a stream in [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-bullroarer_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bullroarer-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-river_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-river-8">[8]</a></sup> While Rateliff stated that he could not decipher the meanings of the names nor figure out what languages they are from, he noted that his friend, [Taum Santoski](../T/Taum_Santoski.md), had suggested in a linguistic note that *Fellin* may be connected with the [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) word *fela* ("cave") and that *Merria* may be connected with the [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word *merka* ("wild").<sup id="cite_ref-bullroarer_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bullroarer-6">[6]</a></sup> Rateliff concluded that regardless of the names had actually meant, he is certain that they were not direct translations of "Green Field".<sup id="cite_ref-bullroarer_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bullroarer-6">[6]</a></sup>

Tolkien originally wrote that chess, in addition to golf, was also invented, because the battle was won due to a "checkmate".<sup id="cite_ref-Pryftan_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pryftan-7">[7]</a></sup> It is possible that the reference to chess was likely removed because it was a philologist's joke that would have gone over too many heads: "checkmate" comes from Persian *shah mat* ("the King is Dead").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The name "Battle of the Green Fields" first arose in the second phase. The reference to chess was removed at this stage.<sup id="cite_ref-Bladorthin_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bladorthin-10">[10]</a></sup>

In early versions of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../P/Prologue.md" title="Prologue">Prologue</a> to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Tolkien gave the date of the battle as 1137,<sup id="cite_ref-date_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-date-11">[11]</a></sup> before changing it to 1347 and then 1147.<sup id="cite_ref-date_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-date-11">[11]</a></sup> It was changed to the published form in an early version of the Appendices.<sup id="cite_ref-peoples_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-peoples-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2003: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The first Hobbit outside <a href="Bag_End.md" title="Bag End">Bag End</a> recounts the story of Bandobras, followed by the accusations that young hobbits do not know enough about their history.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the evil campaign, the Goblins lay siege (and conquer) the Shire as part of their revenge; the Battle of Greenfields is not yet forgotten.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Category:Shire_Epic_Prologue_Quests" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Shire Epic Prologue Quests</a>, a group of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../G/Goblins.md" title="Goblins">Goblins</a> led by <a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Lubach" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Lubach</a>, were convinced by <a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Éogan" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Éogan</a> to invade the Shire by claiming that Lubach was the heir of Golfimbul.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> mentions the battle to <a class="mw-redirect" href="Bilbo.md" title="Bilbo">Bilbo</a>, including the role of Bandobras Took, as part of an encouragement for Bilbo to go with the Dwarves.</dd></dl>
