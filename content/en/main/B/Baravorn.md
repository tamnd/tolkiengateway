**Baravorn** is the translation into [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) of the [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) name *[Ranugad](../R/Ranugad.md)*. [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) addressed [Samwise Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md) as such in the *[King's Letter](../K/King's_Letter.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element is *[bar](Bar.md)*, "home". The second element, <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Avorn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Avorn (page does not exist)">avorn</a></i>, is unclear. Though it is usually taken to be related to *[bor(o)n](Boron.md)*, " steadfast",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it could also be related to the root *[ABA](../A/ABA.md)*, indicating a reluctance to leave.
