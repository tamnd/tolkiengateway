**Baravorn** is the translation into [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) of the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name *[Ranugad](/wiki/Ranugad)*. [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") addressed [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") as such in the *[King's Letter](/wiki/King%27s_Letter)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element is *[bar](/wiki/Bar "Bar")*, "home". The second element, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Avorn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Avorn (page does not exist)">avorn</a></i>, is unclear. Though it is usually taken to be related to *[bor(o)n](/wiki/Boron "Boron")*, " steadfast",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it could also be related to the root *[ABA](/wiki/ABA)*, indicating a reluctance to leave.
