The inhabitants of [Buckland](Buckland.md) on the eastern borders of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), derive from the folk of the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md) who followed [Gorhendad Oldbuck](../G/Gorhendad_Oldbuck.md) across the [Brandywine](Brandywine.md), and were mainly [Stoors](../S/Stoors.md).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Bucklanders acknowledged the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md) as their leader. The [Brandybucks](Brandybuck_Family~66350.md), descendants of Gorhendad who founded Buckland, were considered the most important family of the Bucklanders; the Masters had a strong [Fallohidish](../F/Fallohides.md) strain in their blood, it is said, and this made them more adventurous than other Bucklanders.<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

While Hobbits are known to be afraid of rivers and boats, and few could swim (let alone of the Sea, a token of fear and death for them, and very few had ever saw it) the Bucklanders's boats were often seen on the [Brandywine](Brandywine.md) River that bordered their land.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the eastern border of Buckland lay the dangerous [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md), and the constant threat of the dark trees made the Bucklanders somewhat hardier than typical hobbits.
