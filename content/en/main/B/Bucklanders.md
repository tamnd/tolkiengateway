The inhabitants of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) on the eastern borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), derive from the folk of the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) who followed [Gorhendad Oldbuck](/wiki/Gorhendad_Oldbuck) across the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine), and were mainly [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors).<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Bucklanders acknowledged the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland) as their leader. The [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family"), descendants of Gorhendad who founded Buckland, were considered the most important family of the Bucklanders; the Masters had a strong [Fallohidish](/wiki/Fallohides "Fallohides") strain in their blood, it is said, and this made them more adventurous than other Bucklanders.<sup id="cite_ref-hobbits_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hobbits-2">[2]</a></sup>

While Hobbits are known to be afraid of rivers and boats, and few could swim (let alone of the Sea, a token of fear and death for them, and very few had ever saw it) the Bucklanders's boats were often seen on the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) River that bordered their land.<sup id="cite_ref-party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the eastern border of Buckland lay the dangerous [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest), and the constant threat of the dark trees made the Bucklanders somewhat hardier than typical hobbits.
