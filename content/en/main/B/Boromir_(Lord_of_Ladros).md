**Boromir** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [338](/wiki/First_Age_338 "First Age 338")-[432](/wiki/First_Age_432 "First Age 432")<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the first [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).

## History {#History}

Boromir was the older son of [Boron](/wiki/Boron), and had three children: [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor) his eldest child and only son, *[Andreth](/wiki/Andreth)* his second child and elder daughter, and *[Beril](/wiki/Beril)* his third child and younger daughter.

He became the fourth Chieftain of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [408](/wiki/First_Age_408 "First Age 408").<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup> Since his House had long been loyal in service to the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [410](/wiki/First_Age_410 "First Age 410"),<sup id="cite_ref-WJWest_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJWest-2">[2]</a></sup> he was given the region of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros) in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) by the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) Elf-lords [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor), the younger sons of the Elven-king [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")*, the name *Boromir* is discussed under the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=BOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="BOR (page does not exist)">BOR</a>. The name is said to be a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) form derived from [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Old_Noldorin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Boronm%C3%ADro&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Boronmíro (page does not exist)">Boronmíro</a></i>, being originally an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) name borne by [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). The first element is from Old Noldorin *boron* ("steadfast, trusty man, faithful vassal").<sup id="cite_ref-Ety353_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety353-4">[4]</a></sup> The ending *-[mir](/wiki/Mir "Mir")*, derived from Old Noldorin <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=M%C3%AEre&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mîre (page does not exist)">mîre</a></i> (root [MIR](/wiki/MIR), with derivatives meaning "jewel, precious thing, treasure")).<sup id="cite_ref-Ety372_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety372-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> According to the framework of the *Etymologies*, the name *Boromir* would thus likely mean "faithful jewel".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices) to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* give a slightly different etymology for the name of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), son of [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor), mentioning it as being a mixed form of both [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> See [Boromir: Etymology](/wiki/Boromir#Etymology "Boromir").
