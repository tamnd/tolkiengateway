**Bruno Bracegirdle** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Bruno was the son of [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco_Bracegirdle "Blanco Bracegirdle") and [Primrose](/wiki/Primrose_Boffin "Primrose Boffin"). He had a younger sister, the infamous [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins), and two children, [Hugo](/wiki/Hugo_Bracegirdle "Hugo Bracegirdle") and [Hilda](/wiki/Hilda_Bracegirdle "Hilda Bracegirdle").<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bruno attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party).<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup>
