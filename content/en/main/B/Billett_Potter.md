**Billet Genn Potter** is a British photographer. His photographs include Oxford landmarks and notable personalities such as [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky" title="wikipedia:Noam Chomsky">Noam Chomsky</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Popper" title="wikipedia:Karl Popper">Karl Popper</a> and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Notably, his photographs of J.R.R. Tolkien have served as covers for many books, including several editions of [J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography) and [The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien).
