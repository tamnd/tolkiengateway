**Brodda's hall**, or the **Hall of Brodda**, was the dwelling of the [Easterling](/wiki/Easterlings "Easterlings") [Brodda](/wiki/Brodda) and his wife [Aerin](/wiki/Aerin), who he forcefully wedded after the invasion of Dor-lómin in [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472") of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-CoH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CoH-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/11/Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg/300px-Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="427" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/11/Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg/450px-Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/11/Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg/600px-Olga_Kalinichenko_-_Brodda_and_Aerin.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="1457"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Brodda and Aerin&#34; by Olga Kalinichenko</figcaption></figure>

When [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) returned to [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [496](/wiki/First_Age_496 "First Age 496") he confronted Brodda in his halls, where he is also reunited with his old friend [Sador](/wiki/Sador). In his rage Túrin killed Brodda, thereby sealing Aerin's fate, who burnt herself alive in her hall. After this incident the remainder of the [House of Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador) were persecuted with even greater cruelty.<sup id="cite_ref-CoH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CoH-1">[1]</a></sup>
