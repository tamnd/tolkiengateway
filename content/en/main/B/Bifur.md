**Bifur** most likely lived in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) with [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) after the loss of [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin").<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> Bifur was one of the thirteen [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Thorin's company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") who journeyed to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") to challenge the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug). After the death of Smaug, Bifur lived at the Lonely Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Family and birth {#Family_and_birth}

While Bifur was descended from the Dwarves of [Moria](/wiki/Moria), he himself was not of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin</a>'s line.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup> He was a cousin<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-5">[5]</a></sup> of the brothers [Bofur](/wiki/Bofur) and [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur).<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-6">[6]</a></sup>

Bifur was older than [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli) and [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) (b. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864")<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>) at least "by some fifty years",<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-7">[7]</a></sup> but younger than [Balin](/wiki/Balin) (b. [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763")<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-3">[3]</a></sup>), since it was said that Balin was "the eldest left" after Thorin's capture.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Therefore, Bifur was born anytime between [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763") and c. 2814, and he would have been between c. 127 and 178 years old when he joined King [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin)'s operation to reclaim [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain").

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), Bifur was one of the four Dwarves in the fifth group to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End), wearing a yellow hood. He asked for raspberry jam and apple-tart. During the evening when Thorin called for music, Bifur (along with [Bofur](/wiki/Bofur)) played a clarinet.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the wolves' glade Bifur shared a pine tree with Bofur, [Bombur](/wiki/Bombur), and Thorin.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was telling the story of the company's adventures to [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), Bifur, Bofur, and Bombur were the last of the Dwarves to show up.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

After the barrel ride down the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running) both Bifur and Bofur came out fairly dry and not very knocked about, but they lay down and refused to do anything.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

On the doorstep on the side of the Lonely Mountain, when Thorin commented that the next day was the beginning of the last week of autumn, Bifur pointedly added, "And winter comes after autumn".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

Bifur fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), and made his home in Erebor.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

By [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") Bifur was living in Erebor, as [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) told [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name of *Bívurr* originates in the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It is of Old Frisian origin, meaning "[Beaver](/wiki/Beavers "Beavers")" or, by extension, "Hard Worker".<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the earliest manuscript fragments of *The Hobbit*, Bifur and Bofur went into the hall and came back with their walking sticks, which they *turned into* clarinets.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> In the published text the two Dwarves came back from the hall with clarinets that they *had left* with their walking sticks.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup> [John Rateliff](/wiki/John_Rateliff) (author of *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*) said that in the earliest phase Tolkien had added fairy tale touches of Dwarven magic to emphasize the uncanny, other-worldly nature of the Dwarves as opposed to Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

Originally, when Bombur needed propping up in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) after being cut from the spiders' webbing, it was *his cousins* Bifur and Bofur who provided support.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> In the published story this was changed to Bombur's cousin Bifur and brother Bofur.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-6">[6]</a></sup> Also, in the earlier version, when [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) first came out and the Dwarves in the valley had to be hauled up to the [Back Door](/wiki/Back_Door), Bifur cried out to save Bombur and Bofur, who were both his *brothers*.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> In the published text Bifur cried out for his two *cousins*.<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-5">[5]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's unfinished [1960](/wiki/1960) rewrite of *The Hobbit*, Bifur, Bofur and Bombur were Thorin's attendants.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) assumes this made them either courtiers or honor-guard.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

In the earliest partial manuscript of *The Hobbit* more of the dialogue later assigned to a few of the Dwarves is more evenly distributed. In the opinion of [John Rateliff](/wiki/John_Rateliff) (author of *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*) this streamlining strengthened the story through simplification, but at the cost of relegating some of the Dwarves to obscurity since they barely speak at all. Bifur is one of these "silenced" members of the company.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>
