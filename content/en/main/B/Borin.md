**Borin** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2450](../T/Third_Age_2450.md) – [2711](../T/Third_Age_2711.md), aged 261 years) was a [dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Durin's folk](../D/Durin's_folk.md).

## History {#History}

Borin was the younger son of [King](../K/Kings_of_Durin's_Folk.md) [Náin II](../N/Náin_II.md). During his youth, the [House of Durin](../H/House_of_Durin.md) lived in the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md)-plagued [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md) that were ruled by his brother [Dáin I](../D/Dáin_I.md).

When the King and his son [Frór](../F/Frór.md) were slain by a [Cold-drake](../C/Cold-drake.md), Durin's folk divided. Borin followed the new King [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) back to [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), while others followed [Grór](../G/Grór.md) to the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md). Borin lived with the [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md) under [King](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md) Thrór for the rest of his life.

Through his son, [Farin](../F/Farin.md), Borin was the ancestor of four of the thirteen Dwarves of [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) ([Balin](Balin.md), [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md), [Óin](../_/Óin.md), and [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md)) and [Gimli Elf-friend](../G/Gimli.md) of the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](../J/Jim_Allan.md) has suggested that *Borin* might be derived from [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) *bora* ("to bore holes"), thus meaning "Borer".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
