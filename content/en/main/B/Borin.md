**Borin** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2450](/wiki/Third_Age_2450 "Third Age 2450") – [2711](/wiki/Third_Age_2711 "Third Age 2711"), aged 261 years) was a [dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk).

## History {#History}

Borin was the younger son of [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Durin%27s_Folk "Kings of Durin's Folk") [Náin II](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_II). During his youth, the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin) lived in the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons")-plagued [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) that were ruled by his brother [Dáin I](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_I).

When the King and his son [Frór](/wiki/Fr%C3%B3r) were slain by a [Cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drake), Durin's folk divided. Borin followed the new King [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) back to [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), while others followed [Grór](/wiki/Gr%C3%B3r) to the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills). Borin lived with the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) under [King](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain "King under the Mountain") Thrór for the rest of his life.

Through his son, [Farin](/wiki/Farin), Borin was the ancestor of four of the thirteen Dwarves of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) ([Balin](/wiki/Balin), [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin), [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in), and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in)) and [Gimli Elf-friend](/wiki/Gimli "Gimli") of the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) has suggested that *Borin* might be derived from [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) *bora* ("to bore holes"), thus meaning "Borer".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
