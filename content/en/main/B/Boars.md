**Boars** were aggressive tusked beasts common to woodland regions. The most famous of all the boars of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) was the great [Boar of Everholt](Boar_of_Everholt.md), that lived beneath the trees of the [Firien Wood](../F/Firienholt.md). This boar was hunted by King [Folca](../F/Folca.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), and though he eventually killed his quarry, the King was mortally wounded in the encounter.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

[Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) names for "boar" are *úro* and *karkapolka* (*karka* "tusk" + *polka* "[pig](../P/Pigs.md)").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boars are found in many places of Middle-earth, especially in the <a href="Bree-land.md" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>. They are typically not aggressive, but will fight if the player threatens them.</dd></dl>
