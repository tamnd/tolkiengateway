**Baranor** was the father of [Eradan](</wiki/Eradan_(video_game_character)> "Eradan (video game character)") and the brother of [Dunhir](/wiki/Dunhir). Baranor was invented by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts) for *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)*. Although he doesn't make an actual appearance in the game, he is mentioned in <a class="external text" href="http://www.warinthenorth.com/the-game/heroes/backstory#dunedainAnchor" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Eradan&#39;s History</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Backstory_Eradan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Backstory_Eradan-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Baranor is a descendant of a noble family from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), who were loyal to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil). When the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) destroyed Númenor, they went into exile to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). They lived in [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), where they served the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor) and, after the fall of Arnor, the [Chieftains of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain).

Baranor lived in the [Angle](/wiki/Angle), where he served as a [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North"), and had one son, Eradan. In the time of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) he was lost while patrolling the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). Afterwards his son was fostered by his brother, Dunhir.<sup id="cite_ref-Backstory_Eradan_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Backstory_Eradan-1">[1]</a></sup>
