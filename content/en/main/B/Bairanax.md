In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game "Middle-earth Collectible Card Game")*, **Bairanax** the “Bear-hunter” was a [Cold-drake](/wiki/Cold-drake) in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains). He has brownish-red scales and measures fifty-five feet in length.

When he escaped the doom of Angband as a young dragon, a North Bear almost killed him. This instilled a deep hatred of bears into the dragon. Likewise, [Northman](/wiki/Northmen "Northmen") Bear-cults view him as their ancient enemy. Beyond Bears, Bairanax has a rivalry with the dragon <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lomaw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lomaw (page does not exist)">Lomaw</a>.

The lair of Bairanax was situated in the caves at Ovir Hollow, surrounded by a flower-filled moat, somewhere in the southern Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-creatures_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-creatures-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
