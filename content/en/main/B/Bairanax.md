In *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and the *[Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*, **Bairanax** the “Bear-hunter” was a [Cold-drake](../C/Cold-drake.md) in the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md). He has brownish-red scales and measures fifty-five feet in length.

When he escaped the doom of Angband as a young dragon, a North Bear almost killed him. This instilled a deep hatred of bears into the dragon. Likewise, [Northman](../N/Northmen.md) Bear-cults view him as their ancient enemy. Beyond Bears, Bairanax has a rivalry with the dragon <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lomaw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lomaw (page does not exist)">Lomaw</a>.
