**Badger-folk** were creatures of legend, mentioned in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") verse.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-2">[2]</a></sup> Nothing is known of their origin or history.

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

According to the poem, the Badger-folk lived in secret houses connected by tunnels under a hill in, or nearby, the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest). They appear to have been sentient and capable of speaking. Although [Badger-brock](/wiki/Badger-brock) once forcefully brought down [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) to their burrows, the Badger-folk do not appear as truly evil creatures, but perhaps rather cunning or tricky.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Only mentioned briefly, the Badger-folk of the <a href="/wiki/Withywindle" title="Withywindle">Withywindle</a> appear badgerish but live after the manner of <a href="/wiki/Hobbits" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a>. They are one of the <a href="/wiki/Fairies" title="Fairies">Creatures of Faerië</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
