**Badger-folk** were creatures of legend, mentioned in [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) verse.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-2">[2]</a></sup> Nothing is known of their origin or history.

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

According to the poem, the Badger-folk lived in secret houses connected by tunnels under a hill in, or nearby, the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md). They appear to have been sentient and capable of speaking. Although [Badger-brock](Badger-brock.md) once forcefully brought down [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) to their burrows, the Badger-folk do not appear as truly evil creatures, but perhaps rather cunning or tricky.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Only mentioned briefly, the Badger-folk of the <a href="../W/Withywindle.md" title="Withywindle">Withywindle</a> appear badgerish but live after the manner of <a href="../H/Hobbits.md" title="Hobbits">Hobbits</a>. They are one of the <a href="../F/Fairies.md" title="Fairies">Creatures of Faerië</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
