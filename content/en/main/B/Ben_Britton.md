**Ben Britton** (born [January 6](/wiki/6_January "6 January") [1978](/wiki/1978)) is a Kiwi English teacher<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> who was Production Engineer for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and Systems Coder for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

Britton was an extra in multiple scenes. He portrayed a [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*<sup id="cite_ref-Britton_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Britton-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Fereveldir](/wiki/Fereveldir),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> a [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim)<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> and an [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai")<sup id="cite_ref-Q&amp;A_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q&amp;A-4">[4]</a></sup> in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup>
