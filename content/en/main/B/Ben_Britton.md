**Ben Britton** (born [January 6](../_/6_January.md) [1978](../_/1978.md)) is a Kiwi English teacher<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> who was Production Engineer for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and Systems Coder for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.

Britton was an extra in multiple scenes. He portrayed a [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Britton_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Britton-2">[2]</a></sup> and [Fereveldir](../F/Fereveldir.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> a [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> and an [Uruk](../U/Uruk-hai.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Q&amp;A_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Q&amp;A-4">[4]</a></sup> in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup>
