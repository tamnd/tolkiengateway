The issue reprints The Tengwar Numerals article from <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quettar_13&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quettar 13 (page does not exist)"><i>Quettar</i> 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
