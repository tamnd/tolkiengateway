<b><i>Beyond Bree</i> December 1984</b> is an issue of *[Beyond Bree](Beyond_Bree.md)*, edited by [Nancy Martsch](../N/Nancy_Martsch.md). The issue reprints The Tengwar Numerals article from <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Quettar_13&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quettar 13 (page does not exist)"><i>Quettar</i> 13</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "The Tengwar Numerals" by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)
- "The Cirth Numerals" &amp; "The Names of the Numbers" by [Nancy Martsch](../N/Nancy_Martsch.md)
- "Dragon" ill. by Giovanna Fregni
- Oxonmoot report by [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md)
- *The Hobbit* illustrated by Michael Hague &amp; the 1985 Tolkien Calendar, reviews by [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md)
- "Donaldson's Double Trilogy" by Robert Acker
- "Iron Tower" review by Michael McCausland
- Responses to Tolkien Memorial Forest proposal
- "How I First Learned About Tolkien" (readers' responses)
- Letters, News<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
