**Blodren** was character from earlier versions of the legendarium whose role as betrayer of [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) was replaced by [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md).

## History {#History}

Blodren son of [Ban](Ban~49919.md) was an [Easterling](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) who was captured by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) and tormented because he was one of the faithful that had withstood [Uldor the Accursed](../U/Uldor.md). Subsequently, Blodren entered the service of Morgoth and was released; he was sent in search of Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-Blodren_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blodren-1">[1]</a></sup>

Afterwards, he entered the hidden [company](../G/Gaurwaith.md) in [Dimbar](../D/Dimbar.md), and served Túrin faithfully for two years. After seeing his chance, he betrayed the refuge of [Beleg](Beleg.md) and Túrin to the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), fulfilling his errand.<sup id="cite_ref-Blodren_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blodren-1">[1]</a></sup>

Thus their hideout was surrounded and taken. Túrin was captured alive and carried towards [Angband](../A/Angband.md); but Beleg was left for dead among the slain.<sup id="cite_ref-Blodren_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blodren-1">[1]</a></sup>

However, in the same battle Blodren himself was slain by a chance arrow in the dark.<sup id="cite_ref-Blodren_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blodren-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Blodren* is a [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) name with an unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-eldamo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eldamo-2">[2]</a></sup> The character's former name, <i><b>Blodrin</b></i>, is the plural form of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) adjective *blodren* ("arrogant"), which is derived from the word *blaud* ("pride") and possibly the suffix *-ren*<sup id="cite_ref-suffix1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-suffix1-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-suffix2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-suffix2-4">[4]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-pride_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pride-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Blodrin, a [Gnome](../G/Gnome.md) of the [House of Fëanor](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md), and son of Ban, was a member of Túrin's outlaw company in the earlier versions of the legendarium.<sup id="cite_ref-q12_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-q12-6">[6]</a></sup>

He had lived long with the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), and was of evil heart. He joined Túrin's outlaws for his love of plunder;<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> but after Túrin's encounter with Beleg and subsequent reformation, Blodrin became dissatisfied with the new life where the outlaws would only target Orcs, a "life in which wounds were more plentiful than booty". Blodrin betrayed Túrin's hiding place to the Orcs, but was killed by chance by an arrow of his new evil allies.<sup id="cite_ref-q12_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-q12-6">[6]</a></sup>
