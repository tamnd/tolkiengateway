The **Brockhouse family** was a populous family of the [Bree-hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits). Some members of the family lived in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"): [Sapphira Brockhouse](/wiki/Sapphira_Brockhouse) was the wife of [Uffo Boffin](/wiki/Uffo_Boffin),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) greeted the Brockhouses among other hobbit families in his famous farewell speech on [September 22](/wiki/22_September "22 September"), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3001](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Brock* is an old (up to the end of the 19th century) country word for "badger" and, in that Hobbit name, refers to its building complicated and well-ordered underground dwellings.

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted that English place-names and surnames contain this old word, such as *Brockbanks*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the fifth version of the first chapter of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* the Brockhouse family did not have any members living in the Shire; instead those that attended Bilbo's party were from [Combe-under-Bree](/wiki/Combe "Combe") and were [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) that Bilbo had befriended on his travels.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
