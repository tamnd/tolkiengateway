**Beregond** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) 2700<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> – 2811,<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 111 years) was the twentieth Ruling Steward of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

Before Beregond became a Ruling Steward, three great fleets<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> of the [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> that had been long prepared came from [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md) and [Harad](../H/Harad.md) and attacked the coasts of Gondor in great numbers<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup>. The invaders landed in many places,<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> including the mouth of the river [Lefnui](../L/Lefnui.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> as far north as the mouth of the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> before winter in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup>. At the same time [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) was invaded from the east<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> by Eastern invaders<sup id="cite_ref-Frealaf_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frealaf-6">[6]</a></sup> and from the west<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> by the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) led by [Wulf](../W/Wulf.md) and by Corsairs that had landed at the mouths of the rivers Lefnui and Isen<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup>. Because of the attacks on its coasts Gondor could not send an army to help Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> Before the beginning of spring in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md) Beregond had defeated the invaders in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> After his victory he immediatly sent an army to help Rohan,<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> which came to Rohan by the roads east and west of the mountains and the Dunlendings were driven from Rohan and from [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md) before the end of the year<sup id="cite_ref-Frealaf_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frealaf-6">[6]</a></sup>. Beregond was the greatest captain in Gondor since [Boromir](<Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>), the son of Steward [Denethor I](../D/Denethor_I.md) of Gondor. Because Rohan had been weakened by the invasions and would need more time to recover, Beren welcomed Saruman and gave the keys of [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md) to him in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2759](../T/Third_Age_2759.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup>

Beregond became the twentieth Ruling Steward at the death of his father, Steward [Beren](<Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2763](../T/Third_Age_2763.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During Beregond's rule, the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md) was fought ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2793](../T/Third_Age_2793.md) - [2799](../T/Third_Age_2799.md)). Only rumours of the fighting came south until Orcs fleeing from the [Battle of Nanduhirion](Battle_of_Nanduhirion.md) crossed [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) and established themselves in the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md). Fighting occurred in many dales before the danger was ended.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Beregond ruled as Steward for fortyeight years,<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> and was succeeded by his son, [Belecthor II](Belecthor_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Beregond* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) suggest that it means "Valiant Stone" and that the name is a combination of the *beren* ("bold") and *[gond](../G/Gond.md)* ("stone").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md) suggests that it could mean "fierce stone" and that it is a combination of *bereg* and *gond*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

Beregond was probably named after a renowned heroe in the royal lines of old as recorded in legend.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
