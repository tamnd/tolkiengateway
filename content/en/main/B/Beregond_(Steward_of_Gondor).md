**Beregond** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) 2700<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> – 2811,<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 111 years) was the twentieth Ruling Steward of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## History {#History}

Before Beregond became a Ruling Steward, three great fleets<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> of the [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar")<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> that had been long prepared came from [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Harad](/wiki/Harad) and attacked the coasts of Gondor in great numbers<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup>. The invaders landed in many places,<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> including the mouth of the river [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui),<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> as far north as the mouth of the river [Isen](/wiki/Isen)<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> before winter in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758")<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup>. At the same time [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) was invaded from the east<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> by Eastern invaders<sup id="cite_ref-Frealaf_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frealaf-6">[6]</a></sup> and from the west<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> by the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) led by [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) and by Corsairs that had landed at the mouths of the rivers Lefnui and Isen<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup>. Because of the attacks on its coasts Gondor could not send an army to help Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Helm_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helm-5">[5]</a></sup> Before the beginning of spring in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759") Beregond had defeated the invaders in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> After his victory he immediatly sent an army to help Rohan,<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup> which came to Rohan by the roads east and west of the mountains and the Dunlendings were driven from Rohan and from [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) before the end of the year<sup id="cite_ref-Frealaf_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Frealaf-6">[6]</a></sup>. Beregond was the greatest captain in Gondor since [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Boromir (Steward of Gondor)"), the son of Steward [Denethor I](/wiki/Denethor_I) of Gondor. Because Rohan had been weakened by the invasions and would need more time to recover, Beren welcomed Saruman and gave the keys of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) to him in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759").<sup id="cite_ref-Beren_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beren-4">[4]</a></sup>

Beregond became the twentieth Ruling Steward at the death of his father, Steward [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

During Beregond's rule, the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs) was fought ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2793](/wiki/Third_Age_2793 "Third Age 2793") - [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")). Only rumours of the fighting came south until Orcs fleeing from the [Battle of Nanduhirion](/wiki/Battle_of_Nanduhirion) crossed [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) and established themselves in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains). Fighting occurred in many dales before the danger was ended.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Beregond ruled as Steward for fortyeight years,<sup id="cite_ref-South_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-2">[2]</a></sup> and was succeeded by his son, [Belecthor II](/wiki/Belecthor_II).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Beregond* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) suggest that it means "Valiant Stone" and that the name is a combination of the *beren* ("bold") and *[gond](/wiki/Gond "Gond")* ("stone").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that it could mean "fierce stone" and that it is a combination of *bereg* and *gond*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

Beregond was probably named after a renowned heroe in the royal lines of old as recorded in legend.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
