**Bath-songs** were a type of short song that [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) sung when bathing.<sup id="cite_ref-bath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bath-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) had taught [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) the words to his favorite bath-song during one of their walks in the lanes of the [Water-valley](/wiki/Water-valley).<sup id="cite_ref-bath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bath-1">[1]</a></sup>

This bath-song was sung by [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) in [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) while he, Frodo, and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) were bathing after their long journey from [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-bath_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bath-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><audio src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/3/33/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Bath-song.mp3" width="300" class="mw-file-element" style="max-width: 100%; width: 300px;" controls=""><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Bath-song.mp3">https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Bath-song.mp3</a></audio><figcaption><a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> reads A Bath-song</figcaption></figure>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Sing hey! for the bath at close of day <br/>
that washes the weary mud away! <br/>
A loon is he that will not sing: <br/>
O! Water Hot is a noble thing! <br/>
<br/>
O! Sweet is the sound of falling rain, <br/>
and the brook that leaps from hill to plain; <br/>
but better than rain or rippling streams <br/>
is Water Hot that smokes and steams. <br/>
<br/>
O! Water cold we may pour at need <br/>
down a thirsty throat and be glad indeed; <br/>
but better is Beer if drink we lack, <br/>
and Water Hot poured down the back. <br/>
<br/>
O! Water is fair that leaps on high <br/>
in a fountain white beneath the sky; <br/>
but never did fountain sound so sweet <br/>
as splashing Hot Water with my feet!
</p>
</div>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i> (<a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a>)</b>

<dl><dd><b></b></dd>
<dd>In the scene &#34;<a href="/wiki/At_the_Green_Dragon" title="At the Green Dragon">At the Green Dragon</a>&#34;, <a href="/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>, <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> sing a song closely resembling <i><a href="/wiki/Ho!_Ho!_Ho!_To_the_Bottle_I_Go" title="Ho! Ho! Ho! To the Bottle I Go">Ho! Ho! Ho! To the Bottle I Go</a></i>, with slightly altered lyrics that incorporate a small part of <i>Sing Hey! For the Bath at Close of Day</i>:</dd></dl>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Hey Ho to the bottle I go<br/>
To heal my heart and drown my woe<br/>
Rain may fall and wind may blow but there&#39;ll still be<br/>
Many miles to go<br/>
<br/>
Sweet is the sound of the pouring rain,<br/>
And the stream that falls from hill to plain.<br/>
Better than rain or rippling brook,<br/>
Is a mug of beer inside this <a href="/wiki/Took_Family" title="Took Family">Took</a>.
</p>
</div>
