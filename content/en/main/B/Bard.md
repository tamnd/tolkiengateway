**Bard**, titled "**the Bowman**", was a descendant of [Girion](../G/Girion.md), the [Lord of Dale](../L/Lord_of_Dale.md), who slew the dragon [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) and refounded [Dale](../D/Dale.md). He ruled his [new kingdom](../K/Kingdom_of_Dale.md) as **Bard I**.

## History {#History}

Bard, the heir of [Girion](../G/Girion.md), the last [Lord of Dale](../L/Lord_of_Dale.md) whose wife and children had fled from the ruin of Dale down the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md) was known for his worth and courage and lived in Lake-town when Smaug attacked the town<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When Bard recognized that the dragon was coming, he ran to the [Master of Lake-town](../M/Master_of_Lake-town.md) and shouted to cut the bridges and to get arms. Bard cheered on the archers and held out with a great yew bow in the burning town as the captain of a company of archers. He was an able archer and had inherited a [black arrow](Black_arrow.md) from his ancestors, which had never failed him and which he had always recovered.<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Black_Arrow.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Black_Arrow.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Black_Arrow.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="410" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/d/d9/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Black_Arrow.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="329" data-file-height="450"/></a><figcaption><i>The Black Arrow</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

When Bard had shot all his arrows, except the black arrow, which he had saved for his last shot, the old [thrush](../T/Thrushes.md) (who had overheard [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md)' description of [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md))<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> revealed an unarmoured spot on the Dragon's armpit to him and Bard killed the dragon by shooting him in the unarmoured spot with the black arrow. Because of his miraculous shot, he was given the epithet "the Bowman" and "the Dragon-shooter".<sup id="cite_ref-Fire_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fire-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="217" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg/450px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/e/eb/Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg/600px-Ted_Nasmith_-_The_Arkenstone.jpg 2x" data-file-width="650" data-file-height="470"/></a><figcaption><i>The Arkenstone</i> by <a href="../T/Ted_Nasmith.md" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

After the disaster, he led the [Lake-men](../L/Lake-men.md) to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) demanding a part of the treasure from the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gather_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gather-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Wood-elves](../E/Elves_of_Mirkwood.md) and [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) came to demand their own shares. Seeing [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md)'s reluctance to help, Bilbo Baggins delivered the [Arkenstone](../A/Arkenstone.md) to them to bargain with.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Afterwards, when the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Wargs](../W/Wargs.md) came, Bard led his Men in the [Battle of Five Armies](Battle_of_Five_Armies.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

The victors divided the treasure and Bard took Bilbo's fourteenth share of the gold and silver in return for the Arkenstone, whereupon he shared his reward with the [Master of Lake-town](../M/Master_of_Lake-town.md) to rebuild the town, and gave Thranduil the emeralds of Girion.<sup id="cite_ref-Gather_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gather-4">[4]</a></sup> However, the Master stole the money and ran off into the wild where he died of hunger.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Three years later, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2944](../T/Third_Age_2944.md) after the rebuilding of the city, Bard became the first [King](../K/King_of_Dale.md) of [restored Dale](../K/Kingdom_of_Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> He was succeeded by his son, [Bain](Bain.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2977](../T/Third_Age_2977.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is not known, if Bard was a name in the [language of Dale](../L/Language_of_Dale.md) and the [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md). This language is only represented by a few names, especially the names of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), which came from that region. The names of those Dwarves are [Old Norse](../O/Old_Norse.md) names.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> In other Germanic names (such as [Isembard](../I/Isembard_Took.md)), *bard* refers to *beard*. This could be either the facial hair, or more likely *"Battle-Axe"* (*beard* is also a term for a part of an axe).

Jim Allan suggests that Bard is a Germanic element, which means "Battle-axe".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Bjoern Fromen suggests that Bard could be a modernized (i.e. anglicised/westronized) form of Old Norse *Barthi* (recte *bartha*) ("battle-axe").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

Robert Ireland and [Ruth S. Noel](../R/Ruth_S._Noel.md), who perhaps overlooked the connection to Old Norse, provide as translations the [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) words, *bárd* ("guardian")<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> and *bard* ("poet").<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In his original concept of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) intended Bilbo Baggins to be the slayer of Smaug the Dragon, stabbing him in his sleep in his lair in the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md). Tolkien then changed his mind and in a major shift decided that Smaug would die at [Esgaroth](../L/Lake-town.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> This necessitated the creation of a hero, Bard, who was revealed to be the descendant of Girion. Having created this dragon-slayer, Tolkien was going to let him die in the wreck of Lake-town.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> However, realizing new possibilities for the story, especially having a rightful claimant to part of the dragon's hoard other than the Dwarves, Tolkien kept Bard alive.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

<b>1968: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series).md" title="The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1968 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bard is played by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Peter_Williams&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Williams (page does not exist)">Peter Williams</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1977: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bard&#39;s voice is provided by <a href="../J/John_Stephenson.md" title="John Stephenson">John Stephenson</a>. Thorin and co. meet him upon their arrival in Lake-town. Although Bard is chosen as king after slaying the dragon, there is no mention of him being of the line of Girion. He is also made king prior to the Battle of Five Armies.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1979_radio_series).md" title="The Hobbit (1979 radio series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bard’s voice is provided by <a href="../E/Erik_Bauersfeld.md" title="Erik Bauersfeld">Erik Bauersfeld</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>1980: <a href="../D/Der_Hobbit_(1980_German_radio_series).md" title="Der Hobbit (1980 German radio series)"><i>Der Hobbit</i> (1980 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bard the Bowman is played by Rolf Schult.</dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bard Bowman is a Warrior/Scout Man, who can be used to influence the Men of Northern Rhovanion.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>No actor is credited for the part of Bard, but it appears to be <a href="../A/André_Sogliuzzo.md" title="André Sogliuzzo">André Sogliuzzo</a>. He is portrayed as the <a href="../C/Captain_of_the_guard.md" title="Captain of the guard">captain of the guard</a>, a stout black haired man with a full beard. Bilbo helps him retrieve the black arrow and stop a gang of Orcs and men from taking over Lake-town.</dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../L/Luke_Evans.md" title="Luke Evans">Luke Evans</a> portrays Bard.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-19">[19]</a></sup> He is first introduced coming across <a href="Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> and <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin</a>&#39;s <a href="../T/Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">Company</a> along the <a href="../R/River_Running.md" title="River Running">River Running</a> when waiting to retrieve the empty wine barrels returned by the <a href="../W/Woodland_Realm.md" title="Woodland Realm">Woodland Realm</a>. The Dwarves agree to pay him to smuggle them into <a href="../L/Lake-town.md" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> and get the weapons and supplies they need for reaching the <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a>. He takes them in to his home until they plan to leave. When he learns of Thorin&#39;s true identity, he becomes worried about the Dwarves&#39; quest, believing the <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Dragon</a> will destroy everything in its path if awakened. In addition to his son, <a href="Bain.md" title="Bain">Bain</a>, Bard also has two daughters: <a href="../S/Sigrid.md" title="Sigrid">Sigrid</a> and <a href="../T/Tilda.md" title="Tilda">Tilda</a>. He is also revealed to be a widower.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Known by the <a href="../M/Master_of_Lake-town.md" title="Master of Lake-town">Master of Lake-town</a> (and his right hand man <a class="mw-redirect" href="../A/Alfrid.md" title="Alfrid">Alfrid</a>) to be the descendant of <a href="../G/Girion.md" title="Girion">Girion</a>, the last <a href="../L/Lord_of_Dale.md" title="Lord of Dale">lord of Dale</a>, and fearing he may be trying to undermine their authority, Bard is under constant surveillance by various spies. He is ultimately arrested at the Master&#39;s behest on some vague charge and imprisoned at the end of the film.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bard escapes from his prison cell during <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>&#39;s destruction of Lake-town and attempts to take down the <a href="../F/Fire-drakes.md" title="Fire-drakes">fire-drake</a>. Bain manages to find him and get him the last <a href="Black_arrow.md" title="Black arrow">black arrow</a>, which allows him to finally take down the <a href="../D/Dragons.md" title="Dragons">Dragon</a>. He becomes a hero among the town&#39;s survivors and leads them in taking refuge in the remains of <a href="../D/Dale.md" title="Dale">Dale</a>. When <a href="../T/Thranduil.md" title="Thranduil">Thranduil</a> arrives with an army of <a href="../E/Elves.md" title="Elves">Elves</a> to attack the mountain, Bard attempts to peacefully come to terms with Thorin. He asks him for a portion of the treasure which Thorin had promised to the people of Lake-town, but Thorin refuses to give them anything while Thranduil&#39;s army remains. After this, Bard reluctantly joins Thranduil in his attack. However, the arrival of an <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orc</a> army leads all involved to refocus their attention and battle the Orcs instead.</dd></dl>
