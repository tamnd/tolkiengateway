**Barad Eithel** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") "Tower of the Well"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the mountain fortress of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin) and his son [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon), thus the seat of the [High Kings of the Noldor](/wiki/High_Kings_of_the_Noldor) (until the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad)).

It was set in the eastern foothills of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), at [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion), the source of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was here that the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad "Nirnaeth Arnoediad") took place, and where Fingon was slain, giving [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) the chance to take control over the chief stronghold of both [Mithrim](/wiki/Mithrim) and [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Barad Eithel* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) meaning "Tower of the Well" referring to the Wells of Sirion; from *[barad](/wiki/Barad "Barad")* "tower" and *[eithel](/wiki/Eithel "Eithel")* "well".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
