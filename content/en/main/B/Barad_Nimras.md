**Barad Nimras** was a tower on a headland westward of [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> built by [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [65](/wiki/First_Age_65 "First Age 65").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Its purpose was to watch the western seas, and warn of any naval attack by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s forces. As Morgoth never mounted any attack from the ocean, the tower was never in fact needed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, in the year after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Morgoth did launch [a land-based attack](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas "Fall of the Falas") on [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) and [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar), and Barad Nimras was torn down at that time by the very foes it was meant to guard against.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Barad Nimras* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Tower of the White Horn",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> from *[barad](/wiki/Barad "Barad")* ("tower"), *[nimp](/wiki/Nimp "Nimp")* ("white") + *[ras](/wiki/Ras "Ras")* ("horn"). It may be a reference to the tower itself, or perhaps to the narrow cape on which it stood.
