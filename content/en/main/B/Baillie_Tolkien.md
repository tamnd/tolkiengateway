**Baillie Jean Tolkien, née Klass** (born [10 December](../_/10_December.md) [1941](../_/1941.md)) is the second wife of [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s third son, with whom she had two children, [Adam](../A/Adam_Tolkien.md) and [Rachel](../R/Rachel_Tolkien.md).

Baillie is Canadian, and is the daughter of Winnipeg surgeon <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alan_Klass&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alan Klass (page does not exist)">Alan Klass</a>, and Helen Klass (née Jacob). She has a BA in English from the University of Manitoba and an MA from Oxford.

Before marrying Christopher, Klass was J.R.R. Tolkien's secretary. For the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* ([Ballantine Books](Ballantine_Books.md), [1965](../_/1965.md)) she compiled an index to Songs and Verses in the book, complementing the [existing index](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Index.md) of names.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

She edited J.R.R. Tolkien's *[The Father Christmas Letters](../T/The_Father_Christmas_Letters.md)* for posthumous publication.

Christopher dedicated *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*, *[The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)* and *[The Fall of Gondolin](../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin.md)* to her. <span></span>
