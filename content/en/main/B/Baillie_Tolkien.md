**Baillie Jean Tolkien, née Klass** (born [10 December](/wiki/10_December) [1941](/wiki/1941)) is the second wife of [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s third son, with whom she had two children, [Adam](/wiki/Adam_Tolkien "Adam Tolkien") and [Rachel](/wiki/Rachel_Tolkien "Rachel Tolkien").

Baillie is Canadian, and is the daughter of Winnipeg surgeon <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alan_Klass&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alan Klass (page does not exist)">Alan Klass</a>, and Helen Klass (née Jacob). She has a BA in English from the University of Manitoba and an MA from Oxford.

Before marrying Christopher, Klass was J.R.R. Tolkien's secretary. For the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* ([Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books), [1965](/wiki/1965)) she compiled an index to Songs and Verses in the book, complementing the [existing index](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Index "The Lord of the Rings Index") of names.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

She edited J.R.R. Tolkien's *[The Father Christmas Letters](/wiki/The_Father_Christmas_Letters)* for posthumous publication.

Christopher dedicated *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* and *[The Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin)* to her. <span></span>
