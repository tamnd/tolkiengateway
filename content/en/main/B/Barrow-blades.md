The **Barrow-blades**<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup> were weapons that [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) brought out of the mound in which [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and his friends had been imprisoned. Each Barrow-blade had a long, leaf-shaped blade, damasked with serpent-forms in red and gold. They had black sheaths of an unknown metal, light and strong, adorned with fiery stones. Despite their great age they were sharp and unrusted by time.<sup id="cite_ref-Barrow_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrow-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Barrow-blades were created by the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) during their war against the [Witch-king of Angmar](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king"). They were made for fighting forces sent from [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).<sup id="cite_ref-Barrow_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrow-4">[4]</a></sup> After the fall of Cardolan, at least four of these Barrow-blades ended up in the tomb<sup id="cite_ref-Barrow_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrow-4">[4]</a></sup> of which it was said that it was the grave of the last prince of that realm who fell in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [28 September](/wiki/28_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1091</sup> Frodo, [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") became lost in the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) and were captured by one of the [wights](/wiki/Barrow-wights "Barrow-wights") and imprisoned in the tomb. Frodo was able to call [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) to their aid, and he opened the tomb and drove the wight away. Tom gave each of them a Barrow-blade from the tomb and told them their history.<sup id="cite_ref-Barrow_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Barrow-4">[4]</a></sup>

On [13 October](/wiki/13_October),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1092</sup> at the [Ford of Bruinen](/wiki/Ford_of_Bruinen) the Witch-king raised up his hand and Frodo's Barrow-blade broke and fell out of his shaking hand.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The pieces were recovered and passed into the possession of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins). Having forgotten to ask the elven-smiths if they could repair the blade, he gifted Frodo with [Sting](/wiki/Sting) as a replacement.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

On [15 January](/wiki/15_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1092</sup> Sam felled an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") with a thrust with his Barrow-blade in the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul).<sup id="cite_ref-Bridge_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bridge-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [26 February](/wiki/26_February),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Pippin and Merry had drawn their swords to defend themselves against a group of Orcs in the woods near [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen) and Merry cut off several of the arms and hands of the Orcs with his Barrow-blade before they were captured by the Orcs.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The Orcs cast the weapons away in fear, and they were found and recovered by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> He would return the weapons to Merry and Pippin upon their reunion at [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

On [6 March](/wiki/6_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1093</sup> in the [Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg) Merry laid his sword in the lap of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) offering service to him. The king gladly made Merry an esquire of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) of the household of [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

When Pippin came before [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on [9 March](/wiki/9_March),<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> he offered his sword to the Steward in token of service. Denethor smiled at first but was impressed by the Barrow-blade, recognizing it as coming from his own [kindred in the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor "Dúnedain of Arnor").<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

On [13 March](/wiki/13_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1093</sup> the the monstrous spider [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob) stung Frodo and Sam picked up Frodo's sword [Sting](/wiki/Sting), which had fallen to the ground, to fight against Shelob. Sam believed Frodo to be dead who had been incapacitated by the poison on Shelob's sting and left his Barrow-blade beside the unconscious Frodo. A patrol of Orcs found the unconscious Frodo and took him and Sam's Barrow-blade with them to the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol). The Orcs were under orders to send a full description of every weapon or ring of any trespasser they had found to [Lugbúrz](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr "Barad-dûr").<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> Sam's Barrow-blade was probably taken by [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat) to Barad-dûr after Shagrat had fled from the Tower of Cirith Ungol with a heavy bundle.

On [15 March](/wiki/15_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1094</sup> during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields), Merry stabbed the Witch-king from behind and pierced his sinew in the back of the knee with his Barrow-blade, which enabled [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) to thrust her sword into his face.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Barrow-blade that Merry stabbed into the Witch-king had a power beyond mere metal, for it broke the spell that linked the will of the Ringwraith to his unseen sinews. However, after Merry's blow the blade of the sword writhed and withered, vanishing like a dry branch in a fire. Thus while the Barrow-blade was created centuries before to battle against the forces of Angmar, it contributed to the demise of the Witch-king.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

<p>So passed the sword of the Barrow-downs, work of Westernesse. But glad would he have been to know its fate who wrought it slowly long ago in the North-kingdom when the Dúnedain were young, and chief among their foes was the dread realm of Angmar and its sorcerer king. No other blade, not though mightier hands had wielded it, would have dealt that foe a wound so bitter, cleaving the undead flesh, breaking the spell that knit his unseen sinews to his will.

On [25 March](/wiki/25_March),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_6-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-6">[6]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1094</sup> the [Mouth of Sauron](/wiki/Mouth_of_Sauron) showed Sam's sword to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) during a parley in front of the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) and Gandalf seized it from him before he rejected the terms offered by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). During the following [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon), Pippin stabbed the great troll-chief of a company of [Hill-trolls](/wiki/Hill-trolls) from [Gorgoroth](/wiki/Gorgoroth) with his barrow-blade causing the troll to lose a lot of blood and to crash down.<sup id="cite_ref-Pippin_Troll_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pippin_Troll-2">[2]</a></sup>

On [8 April](/wiki/8_April),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-17">[17]</a></sup> Gandalf returned Sam's Barrow-blade to Sam when they rested in the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen) after the Battle of the Morannon.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

On [2 November](/wiki/2_November),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-17">[17]</a></sup> Pippin drew his Barrow-blade and threatened to use it, referring to it as "this troll's bane", and Sam also drew his sword to support him when they were confronted by [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) at [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

On [3 November](/wiki/3_November),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_17-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-17">[17]</a></sup> during the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) in [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) Pippin probably used his Barrow-blade when he and Merry charged the Ruffians.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Swords that dwindle can be seen in the *Volsungasaga* and the *Nibelungenlied*. In *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")*, the sword melts "like ice" after decapitating Grendel and his mother.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>
