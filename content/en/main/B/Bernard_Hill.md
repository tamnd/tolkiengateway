British actor **Bernard Hill** ([17 December](/wiki/17_December) [1944](/wiki/1944) - [5 May](/wiki/5_May) [2024](/wiki/2024)) played [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

## Biography {#Biography}

Hill was born in Manchester, where he attended the Manchester Polytechnic School of Drama. He made his screen debut in 1973, and gained some fame as unemployed <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scouser" title="wikipedia:Scouser">Scouser</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yosser_Hughes" title="wikipedia:Yosser Hughes">Yosser Hughes</a> in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Stuff" title="wikipedia:The Black Stuff">The Black Stuff</a></i> and its sequel, *Boys from the Black Stuff*. Before that, he had made minor appearances in [BBC](/wiki/BBC) series, including <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I,_Claudius_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:I, Claudius (TV series)">I, Claudius</a></i> (which boasts many great actors, including [Brian Blessed](/wiki/Brian_Blessed), [Christopher Guard](/wiki/Christopher_Guard), [Norman Shelley](/wiki/Norman_Shelley), [John Hurt](/wiki/John_Hurt), [John Rhys-Davies](/wiki/John_Rhys-Davies) and [Derek Jacobi](/wiki/Derek_Jacobi) in the title role).

A steady career with few peaks followed. He had a minor role in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gandhi_(film)" title="wikipedia:Gandhi (film)">Gandhi</a></i> and a remarkable bigger one as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Smith" title="wikipedia:Edward Smith">Captain Smith</a> in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanic_(1997_film)" title="wikipedia:Titanic (1997 film)">Titanic</a></i> in 1997.

Hill's appearance in both the *Titanic* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* make him the only actor to have appeared in more than one of the three films awarded 11 Oscars (the third being *Ben Hur*) and one of only three actors to have starred in more than one film grossing more than $1 billion USD (another one being [Orlando Bloom](/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) who also starred in *The Return of the King*, as well as *Pirates of the Caribbean*). Both movies (and *Gandhi*) have won Best Picture.

The irony that he died in both led to some jokes about having to kill Bernard Hill in order to clear a billion.

His birthday coincides with the premiere of *The Return of the King*.
