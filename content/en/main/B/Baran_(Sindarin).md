<i><b>baran</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word glossed as "brown, swart, dark brown",<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> "golden brown",<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and "brown, yellow-brown".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
