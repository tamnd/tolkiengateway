<i><b>Between Faith and Fiction: Tolkien and the Powers of His World</b></i> collects the proceedings of the Arda symposium at the Second Northern Tolkien Festival in Oslo, August 1997.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "All in one, one in all" ([Marjorie Burns](/wiki/Marjorie_Burns))
- "The fall and man's mortality : an investigation of some theological themes in J.R.R. Tolkien's 'Athrabeth Finrod ah Andreth'" ([Nils Ivar Agøy](/wiki/Nils_Ivar_Ag%C3%B8y))
- "Whose myth is it?" ([Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger))
- "On entering the same river twice : mythology and theology in the Silmarillion corpus" (Kaj André Apeland)
- "Sub-creator and Creator : Tolkien and the design of the one" (Daniel Timmons)
- "'Old human', or 'The voice in our hearts': J.R.R. Tolkien on the origin of language" (Maria Kuteeva)

## From the Preface {#From_the_Preface}

"The Second Northern Tolkien Festival took place at the University of Oslo ... August 8. -10., 1997, hosted by the Tolkien Society of Norway. It included a scholarly symposium arranged in co-operation with ... the Arda Society over the general theme Ainur: Divine and Semi-Divine Powers in Tolkien's World ... six of the papers are now collected in the present publication ... followed by abstracts in Norwegian" <span></span>
