**Bilbo Slider Puzzle** is a 3x3 Slider Puzzle with a picture of [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") from [The Hobbit (1977 film)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)>).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> It was online but <a class="external text" href="http://www.geocities.com/Area51/Corridor/4689/slider.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the site</a> where it was located and playable is now outdated or moved to another web address.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It was made by [Eric Harshbarger](/wiki/Eric_Harshbarger) and [Benjamin Milder](/wiki/Benjamin_Milder).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
