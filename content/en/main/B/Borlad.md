**Borlad** was one of the sons of [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r).

## History {#History}

Borlad, like his father and brothers, entered [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) late in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), and entered the service of [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He fought in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), and was faithful to the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), taking a part in the slaying of [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang)'s sons before he was himself slain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Early versions of the name were directly tied to the root BOR-, signifying loyalty. The meaning of the second element of the final name has not been explained. The earlier name *Borlas* contained <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Glass&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Glass (page does not exist)">glass</a></i>, "joy".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), he was called *Borlas*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> (a name later used for [Borlas of Pen-arduin](/wiki/Borlas "Borlas")). In the appendix of *[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)* it states that Borlad (then called Borlas) was born in the year 143.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
