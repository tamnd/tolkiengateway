**Borlad** was one of the sons of [Bór](Bór.md).

## History {#History}

Borlad, like his father and brothers, entered [Beleriand](Beleriand.md) late in the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), and entered the service of [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He fought in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), and was faithful to the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md), taking a part in the slaying of [Ulfang](../U/Ulfang.md)'s sons before he was himself slain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Early versions of the name were directly tied to the root BOR-, signifying loyalty. The meaning of the second element of the final name has not been explained. The earlier name *Borlas* contained <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Glass&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Glass (page does not exist)">glass</a></i>, "joy".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), he was called *Borlas*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> (a name later used for [Borlas of Pen-arduin](Borlas.md)). In the appendix of *[The Lost Road](../T/The_Lost_Road.md)* it states that Borlad (then called Borlas) was born in the year 143.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
