The **Battle** or **War of Palisor** was a battle according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) did not complete this tale and it is only given in different outlines.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
