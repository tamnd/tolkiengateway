The northernmost of the three bays that ran along the western coasts of the [Andustar](/wiki/Andustar) in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). The greatest of the three, its shores were no more than a narrow shelf of land from which steep cliffs rose up. Beneath these cliffs at its eastern end was the famous haven of [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There are suggestions that there may have been a second haven on this bay, too, named [Almaida](/wiki/Almaida).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
