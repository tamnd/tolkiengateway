The **Battle of Rôs** was a great battle between [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> [Gongs](../G/Gongs.md) and a few evil [Men](../M/Men.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> No full, detailed account of the battle is given, as it is only mentioned passingly in the *Lost Tales*.

It took place near the promontory of [Rôs](../R/Rôs.md) in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md) during the [Faring Forth](../F/Faring_Forth.md), and ended in disaster for the Elves (both for those already living in the Great Lands, and the Elves of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) who came to their rescue), who afterwards went into hiding and eventually ceased to be visible to most Men.<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-2">[1]</a></sup>
