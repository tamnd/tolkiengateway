The **Battle of Rôs** was a great battle between [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> [Gongs](/wiki/Gongs) and a few evil [Men](/wiki/Men), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-2">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> No full, detailed account of the battle is given, as it is only mentioned passingly in the *Lost Tales*.

It took place near the promontory of [Rôs](/wiki/R%C3%B4s) in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands) during the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), and ended in disaster for the Elves (both for those already living in the Great Lands, and the Elves of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) who came to their rescue), who afterwards went into hiding and eventually ceased to be visible to most Men.<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-2">[1]</a></sup>
