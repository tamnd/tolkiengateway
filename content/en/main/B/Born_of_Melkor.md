**Born of Melkor** is a black metal band from York, England. The solo project of the musician Morgulbane, it takes its name from [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor), the first Dark Lord of Tolkien's legendarium. Its 2024 EP *Flight Of the Noldor* takes its title from the flight of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), the exile of the Elves recounted in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.
