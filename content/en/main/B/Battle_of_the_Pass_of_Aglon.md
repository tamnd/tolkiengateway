The **battle of the Pass of Aglon** was one of the many defeats suffered by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) following the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).

## History {#History}

After being held for four centuries, the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) was broken in the year [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"). [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) released great fires and the dragon [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) against the armies in what is known as the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). The [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) suffered several assaults in their territories in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand). [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) fought in the Hill of [Himring](/wiki/Himring), which did not fall, but the armies of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) split and many went south through the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon) and the [March of Maedhros](/wiki/March_of_Maedhros). In Aglon, [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm) and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) had many forces, mostly horsed archers, but they were overthrown.<sup id="cite_ref-GADB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GADB-1">[1]</a></sup>

The armies of Morgoth took the Pass despite high losses, and Celegorm and Curufin fled southwards to [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), increasing the armies of King [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod). Maedhros achieved great military deeds in those times, even retaking the Pass of Aglon for a time.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
