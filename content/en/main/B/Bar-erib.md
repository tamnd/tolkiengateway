**Bar-erib** (also spelled as **Bar Erib**<sup id="cite_ref-ListOfNames_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ListOfNames-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>) was a stronghold of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) some leagues south of [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh).

## History {#History}

The stronghold of Bar-erib was one of the many guarded camps and fortresses that were established around the [Echad i Sedryn](/wiki/House_of_Ransom "House of Ransom"), during the brief existence of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol), the realm ruled by the [Two Captains](/wiki/Two_Captains). Bar-erib was located within *"the once fertile land between [Narog](/wiki/Narog) and the [Meres of Sirion](/wiki/Fens_of_Sirion "Fens of Sirion")."* It was said that from Bar-erib, there was a view to the north where the summit of Amon Rûdh could be seen and commands and messages could be received to and from both locations through signals.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Bar-erib* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Dwelling of the Lonely Ones",<sup id="cite_ref-ListOfNames_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ListOfNames-2">[2]</a></sup> from *[bâr](/wiki/Bar "Bar")* ("home, house, dwelling") + *[erib](/wiki/Ereb "Ereb")* ("single, alone, lonely") (pl.).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
