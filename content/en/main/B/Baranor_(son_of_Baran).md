**Baranor** (b. [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [317](../F/First_Age_317.md)) was a [man](../M/Men.md) of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).

## History {#History}

Baranor was the second son of [Baran](Baran.md) and grandson of [Bëor](Bëor.md). He had one son, [Bereg](Bereg.md), who was born in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [340](../F/First_Age_340.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[The Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)*, created in the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](Bregolas.md) and [Barahir](Barahir.md). After publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* a new genealogy for the House of Bëor was made in the 1950s and at that time four generations (Baran, Boron, Boromir, and Bregor) were inserted after Bëor and before Bregolas and Barahir. **Baranor** was also added as the brother of Boron.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>
