The **Black Gate** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): **Morannon**) was the main entrance into the land of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

## Appearance {#Appearance}

The Black Gate blocked the pass of [Cirith Gorgor](../C/Cirith_Gorgor.md), the gap between the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md) and the [Ered Lithui](../E/Ered_Lithui.md) in the northwestern corner of Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> The Black Gate was a single gate of iron, which consisted of two vast iron doors under a frowning arch,<sup id="cite_ref-Opens_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Opens-2">[2]</a></sup> in a rampart of stone with a battlement that stretched between the high cliffs on either side of the mouth of the pass. On two sheer, black-boned and bare hills that were thrust forward from the mouth of the pass stood two strong and tall towers, the [Towers of the Teeth](../T/Towers_of_the_Teeth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-3">[3]</a></sup>

Immediately north of the Black Gate was the [Desolation of the Morannon](../D/Desolation_of_the_Morannon.md) and the [Slag-hills](../S/Slag-hills.md). Further north of it was the [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> North of the Black Gate a road running north from [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) met another road that went north in the direction of the [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md) and a third road that ran east along the Ered Lithui.<sup id="cite_ref-Note15_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note15-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Roads_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roads-5">[5]</a></sup> South of the Black Gate was the valley of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../U/Udûn.md" title="Udûn">Udûn</a> that led to the plain of [Gorgoroth](../G/Gorgoroth.md) in Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Black Gate was built by Sauron<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-3">[3]</a></sup> after he chose Mordor as a land to make into a stronghold in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1000](../S/Second_Age_1000.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md) the [Battle of Dagorlad](Battle_of_Dagorlad.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> took place at the plain [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md) in front of the Black Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

After Sauron's fall, the Men of Gondor built the Towers of the Teeth to prevent his return to Mordor<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-3">[3]</a></sup> and the Black Gate was manned by the forces of Gondor.

After the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) devastated Gondor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md),<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> the watch on the borders of Mordor ceased and the fortresses that guarded the passes were unmanned in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md) because of a lack of troops.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> It is possible that the Towers of the Teeth and the other fortresses that guarded the passes into Mordor were only abandoned temporarily and were manned again by troops from Gondor when sufficient troops became available again later.

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) when king [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of Gondor and both his sons fell in a battle against the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) north of the Morannon<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-13">[13]</a></sup> the Morannon and the Towers of the Teeth were still manned by troops from Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Note15_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note15-4">[4]</a></sup>

As the strength of Gondor failed the Towers of the Teeth and probably also the Black Gate were abandoned by the Men of Gondor and stood empty for long years.<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that the Black Gate was occupied by the forces of Sauron after the Witch-king came to Mordor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), on [24 March](../_/24_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> the Army of the West, numbering under 6,000 men, arrived at the Black Gate with the intention of drawing the [Eye of Sauron](../E/Eye_of_Sauron.md) away from [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md), to allow [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) the Ringbearer to cast [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) into the [Crack of Doom](../C/Cracks_of_Doom.md) within it.<sup id="cite_ref-Opens_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Opens-2">[2]</a></sup> On [25 March](../_/25_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> the Army of the West challenged Sauron. As a reaction, the forces of Sauron came out of the Black Gate, an army of Easterlings that had hidden in the shadows of the Ered Lithui beyond the eastern Tower of the Teeth approached and orcs poured down from the hills on either side of the Black Gate and attacked the Army of the West. When the [One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md) fell into the Cracks of Doom the volcano Mount Doom erupted,<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> and the Towers of the Teeth, the rampart and the Black Gate collapsed in an earthquake. The forces of Sauron fled, most of the Men of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) and [Harad](../H/Harad.md) fled eastward, some of them surrendered and the rest were destroyed.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The Black Gate was also referred to as the Morannon,<sup id="cite_ref-Closed_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Closed-3">[3]</a></sup> the Gate of Mordor,<sup id="cite_ref-Roads_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roads-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> the Gates of Mordor<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> or Sauron's gate.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

*Morannon* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-annon_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annon-22">[22]</a></sup> name which means "Black Gate".<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[môr](../M/Môr.md)* ("black", "dark", "darkness")<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> and *[annon](../A/Annon.md)* ("(great) gate", "door").<sup id="cite_ref-annon_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-annon-22">[22]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The <i><a href="../T/Teeth_of_Mordor_(book).md" title="Teeth of Mordor (book)">Teeth of Mordor</a></i> module in the <i>Fortresses of Middle-earth</i> series contains drawings of the Black Gate with the Towers of the Teeth and a floor plan and description of the Black Gate. It is described that <a href="../I/Isildur.md" title="Isildur">Isildur</a> began a grand plan to seal off Mordor and that the construction began before the end of <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a> and was completed during the reign of <a href="../R/Rómendacil_I.md" title="Rómendacil I">Rómendacil I</a> (<a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_492.md" title="Third Age 492">492</a> - <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_541.md" title="Third Age 541">541</a>). It is mentioned that by <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_1640.md" title="Third Age 1640">1640</a> the last citadel guarding Mordor was abandoned. It is said that in the same year of the closing of the Dúnadan fortresses guarding Mordor Sauron sent all <a class="mw-redirect" href="../R/Ringwraiths.md" title="Ringwraiths">Ringwraiths</a> except the <a href="../W/Witch-king.md" title="Witch-king">Witch-king</a> and <a href="../K/Khamûl.md" title="Khamûl">Khamûl</a> to lake <a class="mw-redirect" href="../N/Núrnen.md" title="Núrnen">Núrnen</a> with the mission to ready Mordor for his return. It is described that they sent scouts north who found that the Gondorian fortresses were abandoned and that the Ringwraith Dwar of Waw occupied the Black Gate with Orcs, Trolls and mannish servants. It is further said that he repaired and changed the towers and the Black Gate in secret and that Gondor concerned with internal matters scarcely noted the activity. It is described that the Black Gate was destroyed in the cataclysm of Sauron&#39;s fall.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2001.md" title="2001">2001</a>-<a href="../_/2003.md" title="2003">03</a>: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Black Gate is present in the film series. in <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">the second film</a> it appears as a double gate, operated by Trolls, where <a href="../F/Frodo_Baggins.md" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> and <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a> attempt to go through the Gate until convinced by <a href="../G/Gollum.md" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> not to do so. In <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">the third film</a> the Black Gate is a focal point of the <a href="Battle_of_the_Morannon.md" title="Battle of the Morannon">Battle of the Morannon</a>, and after the destruction of <a href="../T/The_One_Ring.md" title="The One Ring">the One Ring</a> the Black Gate and the <a href="../T/Towers_of_the_Teeth.md" title="Towers of the Teeth">Towers of the Teeth</a> are seen to collapse.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="../_/2017.md" title="2017">2017</a>: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Black Gate appears in the game as the location of a number of quests in the lead up to the defeat of <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>.</dd></dl>
