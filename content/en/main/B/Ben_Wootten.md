**Benjamin Wootten**<sup id="cite_ref-Sword_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sword-2">[2]</a></sup> (born [28 March](/wiki/28_March) [1969](/wiki/1969)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essex" title="wikipedia:Essex">Essex</a>, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> is a Kiwi graphic designer based in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellington" title="wikipedia:Wellington">Wellington</a><sup id="cite_ref-ArtStation_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ArtStation-1">[1]</a></sup> who worked at [Wētā Workshop](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop). He was a designer and sculptor for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

He created art for *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)* and portrayed [Brand](/wiki/Brand) for *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)*. Later, as a freelancer, he created an art print of [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) for Wētā's store and helped design the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*. As a *[Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering)* artist, he illustrated several pieces of alternate art for *[The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-MTG_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MTG-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After completely a Zoology degree, Wootten became disillusioned with the prospect of becoming a tertiary educator of the subject and chose instead to take a design course in Christchurch, where he met <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jamie_Beswarick&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jamie Beswarick (page does not exist)">Jamie Beswarick</a>. Beswarick got him interested him in sculpting and they moved to Wellington, where they would join [Wētā Workshop](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-6">[6]</a></sup>

While working at [Wētā Workshop](/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop), he and Beswarick served as a designer and sculptor for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*. Based on the designs by [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe), for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Wootten created a sketch for a [troll](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls").<sup id="cite_ref-Fellowship_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fellowship-8">[8]</a></sup> He designed [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)'s [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") sword and [Glamdring](/wiki/Glamdring), both of which were then made by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Lyon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Lyon (page does not exist)">Peter Lyon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Wootten was supportive of the more minimal armour worn by Aragorn during [the Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon "Battle of the Morannon") and believed that the initial design of [the Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king") during [the Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields "Battle of the Pelennor Fields") lead people to believe he was [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) because it was too far removed from the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl)'s "Black Rider" design from *The Fellowship of the Ring*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

He created illustrations for [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> When Decipher, with Wētā Workshop, began producing *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)* cards depicting aspects omitted from [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") as part of the "Book Cards" label, Wootten portrayed [Brand](/wiki/Brand)<sup id="cite_ref-Decipher_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Decipher-13">[13]</a></sup> in two cards.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> Wootten greatly enjoyed playing the role and had firm ideas of how the character should be brought to life.<sup id="cite_ref-Decipher_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Decipher-13">[13]</a></sup>

He later left Wētā and became a freelancer.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Interview_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-6">[6]</a></sup> He created illustrations for [Ares Games](/wiki/Ares_Games)' *[The Battle of Five Armies: The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Battle_of_Five_Armies_(2014_board_game)> "The Battle of Five Armies (2014 board game)")* board game.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> Wootten still did freelance work for Wētā and created the "Disturber of the Peace" art print for their store.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Interview_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-6">[6]</a></sup> He worked as an artist and art director for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*, designing swords for the [Elves of Lindon](/wiki/Elves_of_Lindon).<sup id="cite_ref-RoP_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RoP-19">[19]</a></sup> For [Wizards of the Coast](/wiki/Wizards_of_the_Coast), Wootten illustrated alternate art for the *[Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering)* expansion *[The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-MTG_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MTG-5">[5]</a></sup>
