The **Broadbeams** were one of the seven houses of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). They were originally paired with the [Firebeards](../F/Firebeards.md). The ancestor of the Broadbeams was among the oldest of the Seven Fathers of the Dwarves (together with the ancestors of the [Firebeards](../F/Firebeards.md) and [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Broadbeams (with the Firebeards) awoke in the [Blue Mountains](Blue_Mountains.md) and lived there throughout the history of their people. These two houses built the great Dwarven cities of [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](Belegost.md) in the Blue Mountains, and dwelt in them before their ruining in the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). It is not clear whether they shared the two cities or whether each house dwelt in its own.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In an [earlier version of the legendarium](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md) the two cities are clearly inhabited by separate houses; however, Belegost is said to be the home of the [Longbeards](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md).<sup id="cite_ref-#_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-#-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), around the year [40](../S/Second_Age_40.md),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[5]</a></sup> the Firebeards and Broadbeams who lived in Nogrod and Belegost left the destruction behind and came to [Khazad-dûm](../K/Khazad-dûm.md), increasing its wealth and power.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

However, there always remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The House corresponding to the Broadbeams is called <b>Linnar&#39;s Folk</b>, said to reside mainly in the Iron Hills during the late Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The native <a href="../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md" title="Dwarves of the Blue Mountains">Dwarves of the Blue Mountains</a> were first introduced in the form of the villainous &#34;<a href="../D/Dourhands.md" title="Dourhands">Dourhand</a>&#34; clan, but a later update introduced the true House corresponding to the Broadbeams: the <b>Landorrim</b>, &#34;<i>long allies of Durin&#39;s Folk</i>&#34;. Their banners depict a tree with a think trunk and branches.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The <a href="../S/Seven_Rings.md" title="Seven Rings">Ring</a> of this House was either &#34;Úrya&#34; or &#34;Vanya&#34;, both of which were devoured by the great <a href="../D/Dragons.md" title="Dragons">Worm</a>, &#34;Fingar the Greedy&#34;, a survivor of the <a href="../W/War_of_Wrath.md" title="War of Wrath">War of Wrath</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>At the start of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md" title="War of the Dwarves and Orcs">War of the Dwarves and Orcs</a>, the Landorrim and their King, &#34;Agni the Wild&#34;, swiftly answered <a href="../T/Thráin.md" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>&#39;s summons and marched from the Blue Mountains. Agni fell in the <a href="Battle_of_Azanulbizar.md" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a> and was succeeded by his son &#34;Onúr&#34;.</dd></dl>
