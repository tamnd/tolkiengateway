The **Broadbeams** were one of the seven houses of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). They were originally paired with the [Firebeards](/wiki/Firebeards). The ancestor of the Broadbeams was among the oldest of the Seven Fathers of the Dwarves (together with the ancestors of the [Firebeards](/wiki/Firebeards) and [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Broadbeams (with the Firebeards) awoke in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) and lived there throughout the history of their people. These two houses built the great Dwarven cities of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost) in the Blue Mountains, and dwelt in them before their ruining in the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). It is not clear whether they shared the two cities or whether each house dwelt in its own.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In an [earlier version of the legendarium](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two "The Book of Lost Tales Part Two") the two cities are clearly inhabited by separate houses; however, Belegost is said to be the home of the [Longbeards](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk "Durin's Folk").<sup id="cite_ref-#_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-#-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), around the year [40](/wiki/Second_Age_40 "Second Age 40"),<sup id="cite_ref-SA_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-5">[5]</a></sup> the Firebeards and Broadbeams who lived in Nogrod and Belegost left the destruction behind and came to [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm), increasing its wealth and power.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

However, there always remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The House corresponding to the Broadbeams is called <b>Linnar&#39;s Folk</b>, said to reside mainly in the Iron Hills during the late Third Age.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The native <a href="/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains" title="Dwarves of the Blue Mountains">Dwarves of the Blue Mountains</a> were first introduced in the form of the villainous &#34;<a href="/wiki/Dourhands" title="Dourhands">Dourhand</a>&#34; clan, but a later update introduced the true House corresponding to the Broadbeams: the <b>Landorrim</b>, &#34;<i>long allies of Durin&#39;s Folk</i>&#34;. Their banners depict a tree with a think trunk and branches.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The <a href="/wiki/Seven_Rings" title="Seven Rings">Ring</a> of this House was either &#34;Úrya&#34; or &#34;Vanya&#34;, both of which were devoured by the great <a href="/wiki/Dragons" title="Dragons">Worm</a>, &#34;Fingar the Greedy&#34;, a survivor of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_Wrath" title="War of Wrath">War of Wrath</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>At the start of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs" title="War of the Dwarves and Orcs">War of the Dwarves and Orcs</a>, the Landorrim and their King, &#34;Agni the Wild&#34;, swiftly answered <a href="/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>&#39;s summons and marched from the Blue Mountains. Agni fell in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a> and was succeeded by his son &#34;Onúr&#34;.</dd></dl>
