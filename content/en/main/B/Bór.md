**Bór the Faithful** was, along [Ulfang](/wiki/Ulfang), one of the two greatest chieftains of the [Easterlings](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)") that moved into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) during the late [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## History {#History}

Bór's people came into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach). He and his sons [Borlad](/wiki/Borlad), [Borlach](/wiki/Borlach), and [Borthand](/wiki/Borthand) swore allegiance to [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was one of the few leaders of the Eastern Tribes of Men to remain faithful to the Eldar during the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad) (Battle of Unnumbered Tears).

This loyalty came at the cost of his life. The Elves, grateful for his loyalty, posthumously awarded him the title "Faithful".<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bór* was probably not the character's true name, as it seems to be [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Faithful": from *[bôr](/wiki/B%C3%B4r "Bôr")* ("steadfast; trusty man, faithful vassal").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

An earlier name for the character was *Bor* (without the accent).<sup id="cite_ref-Pin_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pin-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<h3><span id="Bor.27s_year_of_birth"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Bor&#39;s_year_of_birth">Bor&#39;s year of birth</span></h3>

In the appendix of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)* in <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Genealogies&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Genealogies (page does not exist)">The Genealogies</a></i> (dating from the early [1930s](/wiki/1930 "1930")), it is stated that Bor (as the character was called at that point in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium")) was born in the year 120.<sup id="cite_ref-Pin_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pin-4">[3]</a></sup>

When comparing the above date with its associated text *[The Earliest Annals of Beleriand](/wiki/The_Earliest_Annals_of_Beleriand)*, Bor was born 43 years before the [Swarthy Men](/wiki/Swarthy_Men) entered Beleriand in the year 163.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

Also, the dates found in *The Earliest Annals of Beleriand* were by and large moved 300 years into the future in *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)* (c. early [1950s](/wiki/1950 "1950")), such that, for example, an event happening in the year 195 in *The Earliest Annals* now happened in the year [495](/wiki/First_Age_495 "First Age 495").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
