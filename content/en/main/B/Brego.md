**Brego** was the second [King of Rohan](/wiki/King_of_Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Brego became King at the death of his father [Eorl the Young](/wiki/Eorl_the_Young) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2545](/wiki/Third_Age_2545 "Third Age 2545").<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup> During his rule the migration of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) to [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) continued, and Brego defended the borders of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) against their enemies (the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) and [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings)) and drove off the [Wold](/wiki/Wold) the remaining [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth).

Brego built the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Golden_Hall) of [Meduseld](/wiki/Meduseld) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2569](/wiki/Third_Age_2569 "Third Age 2569")),<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup> and made [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) the capital of Rohan.

His eldest son [Baldor](/wiki/Baldor) in his pride went into the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) never to be found again, and Brego died of grief the next year.<sup id="cite_ref-ta_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-2">[2]</a></sup> He was followed by his second son, [Aldor](/wiki/Aldor).<sup id="cite_ref-Mark_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mark-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Brego* means "chief, leader, king, lord" in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), which Tolkien used to represent [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohanese) in his works.

Outside *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, the plural form of the word, *Bregan* ("Rulers") also appeared in earlier writings as one of the Old English words for the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) by [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
