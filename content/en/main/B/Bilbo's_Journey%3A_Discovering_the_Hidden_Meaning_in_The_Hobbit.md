<i><b>Bilbo&#39;s Journey: Discovering the Hidden Meaning in The Hobbit</b></i> is a book which looks at *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* from a theological perspective.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In Bilbo&#39;s Journey go beyond the dragons, dwarves, and elves, and discover the surprisingly deep meaning of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s classic novel The Hobbit. Bilbo&#39;s quest to find and slay the dragon Smaug is a riveting tale of daring and heroism, but as renowned Tolkien scholar Joseph Pearce shows, it is not simply Bilbo&#39;s journey, it is our journey too. It is the Christian journey of self-sacrifice out of love for others, and abandonment to providence and grace.In Bilbo&#39;s Journey: Discovering the Hidden Meaning of The Hobbit you will relive the excitement of Tolkien&#39;s classic tale, while discovering the profound Christian meaning that makes The Hobbit a truly timeless adventure.
</p>
</blockquote>
