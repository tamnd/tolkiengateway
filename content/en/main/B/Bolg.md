**Bolg of the North** was a vengeful Orc chieftain, son of [Azog](/wiki/Azog), who led a great army in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was crushed in the ensuing battle by [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn).

## History {#History}

Bolg was the son of Azog,<sup id="cite_ref-H17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H17-1">[1]</a></sup> succeeding his father as a prominent leader of the northern [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) after Azog was killed by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) at the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar) in [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799").<sup id="cite_ref-App|Durin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-App|Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> This started Bolg's hatred towards the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and after the [Great Goblin](/wiki/Great_Goblin) was killed during an encounter with [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), the goblins' hatred for them grew even more.<sup id="cite_ref-H17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H17-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bolg gathered an army of [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) to their capital at [Mount Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad "Gundabad"). They marched eastward through the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains) with a host of [Wargs](/wiki/Wargs) and a cloud of [bats](/wiki/Bats "Bats") overhead.<sup id="cite_ref-H17_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H17-1">[1]</a></sup>

Once they reached the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), Bolg led the Orcs and Wargs into [battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies "Battle of the Five Armies") with the Dwarves, [Wood-elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood"), and [Lake-men](/wiki/Lake-men). Surrounding him was his [bodyguard](/wiki/Bodyguard_of_Bolg "Bodyguard of Bolg") with steel scimitars.<sup id="cite_ref-H17_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H17-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) attempted to pierce their ranks he became surrounded, and would later die from the wounds he sustained. The [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) arrived and after them came [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn) in the shape of a bear. Beorn killed Bolg and the Orcs and Wargs were soon defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

There have been several attempts to give a meaning of the name *Bolg*. One is a regular [Orkish](/wiki/Orkish) treatment of *[Boldog](/wiki/Boldog)*, another a connection to [Westron](/wiki/Westron) *[bolg](/wiki/Bolger_Family#Etymology "Bolger Family")*.

In *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)*, a word from a little known and little expanded language, *[Mágol](/wiki/M%C3%A1gol)*, is given, *bolg*, which means "strong". [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") at one time considered making Mágol an Orkish language. An <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primitive_Irish" title="wikipedia:Primitive Irish">Ivernian</a> word "bolg", of unknown meaning, is also cited by [Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff "John D. Rateliff").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bolg was originally going to be played by <a href="/wiki/Conan_Stevens" title="Conan Stevens">Conan Stevens</a> in this film via practical costume.<sup id="cite_ref-TORNBolg_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TORNBolg-5">[5]</a></sup> He makes a brief appearance during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a>, where he is seen fighting against <a href="/wiki/Dwalin" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a> during the flashback to the battle outside <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a>, until he is incapacitated and knocked down. He is, however, never actually identified as being Bolg in the film.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bolg is played by <a href="/wiki/Lawrence_Makoare" title="Lawrence Makoare">Lawrence Makoare</a> via motion capture in this film. Azog - who survives the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs" title="War of the Dwarves and Orcs">War of the Dwarves and Orcs</a> in these films - is summoned to Dol Guldur to become the commander of <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> armies. While there, <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a> dispatches his son Bolg-who delivere the summons earlier in the film-to take his place in hunting Thorin and Company. With his pack of Orcs, Bolg tracks Thorin and Company through <a href="/wiki/Mirkwood" title="Mirkwood">Mirkwood</a>, attacks the Wood-elves as the Company escapes in barrels, and eventually follows the <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Dwarves</a> to <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>.</dd>
<dd>At Lake-town, Bolg and a team of stealth-Orcs raid Bard&#39;s home in search of the Dwarves, but find only four. When they attack, they are in turn attacked by two <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a>: <a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tauriel" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a>. He goes after Legolas, and the two begin a duel (in which the Elf uses <a href="/wiki/Orcrist" title="Orcrist">Orcrist</a>, the weapon of <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a>). However, the two adversaries prove to be evenly-matched, so Bolg decides to make his retreat. While Legolas is distracted by other Orcs, Bolg withdraws and rides a Warg down a dock. He is then pursued by Legolas, now on a horse.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Once again performed via motion capture, Bolg is played by <a href="/wiki/John_Tui" title="John Tui">John Tui</a> this time. He is first seen in this film when he meets his father who is leading Sauron&#39;s forces to capture <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a>. When he realises <a href="/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood" title="Elves of Mirkwood">Elves</a> will come to Erebor, Azog sends Bolg to lead a separate Orc army detachment raised in <a href="/wiki/Gundabad" title="Gundabad">Gundabad</a>. Arriving with an advance force, he aids his father against Thorin <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Oakenshield" title="Oakenshield">Oakenshield</a>&#39;s commando team on <a href="/wiki/Ravenhill" title="Ravenhill">Ravenhill</a>, nearly kills the Elf Tauriel, and slays the Dwarf <a href="/wiki/K%C3%ADli" title="Kíli">Kíli</a> when he comes to her defence. Soon afterwards, however, he is confronted and killed by Legolas, who drives one of his battle-knives into his head. Bolg&#39;s body subsequently falls off the mountain and is crushed by a boulder falling on top of it.</dd></dl>
