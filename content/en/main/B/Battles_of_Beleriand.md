The **Wars of Beleriand**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> also known as the **War of the [Great Jewels](/wiki/Great_Jewels)**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or the **War of the Jewels**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and referred to by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) as the **Goblin-wars**,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> were a series of great conflicts throughout the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days) pertaining of the vain attempts of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) to reclaim the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils) from the Dark Lord [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).

From his mighty fortress of [Angband](/wiki/Angband), Morgoth used this base from which to launch his attacks on the peoples of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). While the Noldor had some victories, the [Doom of Mandos](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos) always hung over their efforts.

## Major Battles {#Major_Battles}

The following are the major battles listed chronologically:

1. The **[First Battle](/wiki/First_Battle)** was fought before the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) arrived, and was fought by the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), [Laiquendi](/wiki/Laiquendi), and [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains).
2. The **[Dagor-nuin-Giliath](/wiki/Dagor-nuin-Giliath)**, or *Battle-under-Stars*, fought by the Noldor following [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and his [Seven Sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor").
    - Following this battle the [Battle of the Lammoth](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Lammoth) was fought by the host of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin).
3. The **[Dagor Aglareb](/wiki/Dagor_Aglareb)**, or *Glorious Battle*, which led to the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband).
    - Various minor battles were fought during the Siege which are not counted among the majors, such as the first [Assault on Hithlum](/wiki/Assaults_on_Hithlum "Assaults on Hithlum"), the [Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade), and an attempt to [breach the Pass of Aglon](/wiki/First_Age_402 "First Age 402")
4. The **[Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach)**, or *Battle of Sudden Flame*, in which [Ard-galen](/wiki/Ard-galen) burned and many Noldor, including [King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), were slain.
    - [Battle of the Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon), where the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) had to retreat towards [Himring](/wiki/Himring).
    - [Massacre at Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Massacre_at_Tarn_Aeluin), in which the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) was destroyed.
5. The **[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad)** or *Battle of Unnumbered Tears*, in which the [Union of Maedhros](/wiki/Union_of_Maedhros) was utterly broken by Morgoth.
    - [Fall of the Falas](/wiki/Fall_of_the_Falas), in which the havens of [Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar) and [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) were sacked and fell, took place after the Nirnaeth.
    - [Sack of Bar-en-Danwedh](/wiki/Sack_of_Bar-en-Danwedh), in which the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith) were destroyed and [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) lost.
    - [Battle of Tumhalad](/wiki/Battle_of_Tumhalad), followed by the [Sack of Nargothrond](/wiki/Sack_of_Nargothrond), where the city was destroyed by [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) and his [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs).
    - [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), in which Morgoth's hosts besieged and destroyed the Noldor and their citadel of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), killing King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).
6. The **[War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath)**, which defeated Morgoth, ended the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), and destroyed most of Beleriand.

    - The [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB) (the only conflict not happening in Beleriand)
    - The [Second Kinslaying](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying)
    - The [Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying)
- A brief conflict involving the Dwarves coveting the [Nauglamir](/wiki/Nauglamir):
    - The [battle in the Thousand Caves](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves")
    - The [Battle of Sarn Athrad](/wiki/Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad)
