Against the will of the <a href="../V/Valar.md" title="Valar">Valar</a> <a href="../F/Fëanor.md" title="Fëanor">Fëanor</a> forsook the <a class="mw-redirect" href="Blessed_Realm.md" title="Blessed Realm">Blessed Realm</a> and went in exile to Middle-earth, leading with him a great part of his people; for in his pride he purposed to recover the Jewels from Morgoth by force. Thereafter followed the hopeless war of the <a href="../E/Eldar.md" title="Eldar">Eldar</a> and the <a href="../E/Edain.md" title="Edain">Edain</a> against <a href="../T/Thangorodrim.md" title="Thangorodrim">Thangorodrim</a>, in which they were at last utterly defeated.

The **Wars of Beleriand**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> also known as the **War of the [Great Jewels](../G/Great_Jewels.md)**<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or the **War of the Jewels**<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and referred to by [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) as the **Goblin-wars**,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> were a series of great conflicts throughout the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md) pertaining of the vain attempts of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) to reclaim the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md) from the Dark Lord [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).

From his mighty fortress of [Angband](../A/Angband.md), Morgoth used this base from which to launch his attacks on the peoples of [Beleriand](Beleriand.md). While the Noldor had some victories, the [Doom of Mandos](../D/Doom_of_Mandos.md) always hung over their efforts.

## Major Battles {#Major_Battles}

The following are the major battles listed chronologically:

1. The **[First Battle](../F/First_Battle.md)** was fought before the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) arrived, and was fought by the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), [Laiquendi](../L/Laiquendi.md), and [Dwarves of the Blue Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Blue_Mountains.md).
2. The **[Dagor-nuin-Giliath](../D/Dagor-nuin-Giliath.md)**, or *Battle-under-Stars*, fought by the Noldor following [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) and his [Seven Sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md).
    - Following this battle the [Battle of the Lammoth](Battle_of_the_Lammoth.md) was fought by the host of [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md).
3. The **[Dagor Aglareb](../D/Dagor_Aglareb.md)**, or *Glorious Battle*, which led to the [Siege of Angband](../S/Siege_of_Angband.md).
    - Various minor battles were fought during the Siege which are not counted among the majors, such as the first [Assault on Hithlum](../A/Assaults_on_Hithlum.md), the [Battle of the Gelion-Ascar Stockade](Battle_of_the_Gelion-Ascar_Stockade~66779.md), and an attempt to [breach the Pass of Aglon](../F/First_Age_402.md)
4. The **[Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md)**, or *Battle of Sudden Flame*, in which [Ard-galen](../A/Ard-galen.md) burned and many Noldor, including [King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), were slain.
    - [Battle of the Pass of Aglon](Battle_of_the_Pass_of_Aglon.md), where the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) had to retreat towards [Himring](../H/Himring.md).
    - [Massacre at Tarn Aeluin](../M/Massacre_at_Tarn_Aeluin.md), in which the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) was destroyed.
5. The **[Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md)** or *Battle of Unnumbered Tears*, in which the [Union of Maedhros](../U/Union_of_Maedhros.md) was utterly broken by Morgoth.
    - [Fall of the Falas](../F/Fall_of_the_Falas.md), in which the havens of [Brithombar](Brithombar.md) and [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md) were sacked and fell, took place after the Nirnaeth.
    - [Sack of Bar-en-Danwedh](../S/Sack_of_Bar-en-Danwedh.md), in which the [Gaurwaith](../G/Gaurwaith.md) were destroyed and [Dor-Cúarthol](../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md) lost.
    - [Battle of Tumhalad](Battle_of_Tumhalad.md), followed by the [Sack of Nargothrond](../S/Sack_of_Nargothrond.md), where the city was destroyed by [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) and his [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md).
    - [Fall of Gondolin](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md), in which Morgoth's hosts besieged and destroyed the Noldor and their citadel of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md), killing King [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md).
6. The **[War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md)**, which defeated Morgoth, ended the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), and destroyed most of Beleriand.

## See also {#See_also}

There were other parallel conflicts where [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) was not involved, but were directly or indirectly connected to the main War, and the [Silmarilli](../S/Silmarilli.md):

- The <b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../K/Kinslaying.md" title="Kinslaying">Kinslayings</a></b>, three conflicts caused by the [House of Fëanor](../H/House_of_Fëanor.md) against other Elves to claim the Silmarilli:
    - The [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md) (the only conflict not happening in Beleriand)
    - The [Second Kinslaying](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md)
    - The [Third Kinslaying](../T/Third_Kinslaying.md)
- A brief conflict involving the Dwarves coveting the [Nauglamir](../N/Nauglamir.md):
    - The [battle in the Thousand Caves](Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves.md)
    - The [Battle of Sarn Athrad](Battle_of_Sarn_Athrad.md)
