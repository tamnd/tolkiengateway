**Bain** was the [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale), the second of the restored line.

## History {#History}

Bain was the son of [Bard the Bowman](/wiki/Bard "Bard"), and succeeded his father in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2977](/wiki/Third_Age_2977 "Third Age 2977"). He ruled in the lands south of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) for thirty years, during which [Balin](/wiki/Balin) and [a group](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk "Balin's folk") of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) left [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") to retake [Moria](/wiki/Moria).

He was succeeded by his son [Brand](/wiki/Brand) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3007](/wiki/Third_Age_3007 "Third Age 3007"), whose realm was said to extend far to the south and east of [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

As the [Language of Dale](/wiki/Language_of_Dale) is rendered with [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse), the name perhaps represents *Beinn* meaning "mover".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayals in adaptations {#Portrayals_in_adaptations}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/d/d1/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg/100px-The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="100" height="154" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/d/d1/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg/150px-The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/d/d1/The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg/200px-The_Hobbit_-_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_-_Bain.jpg 2x" data-file-width="280" data-file-height="432"/></a><figcaption>Bain in <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a></figcaption></figure>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>While in <a href="/wiki/Canon" title="Canon">canon</a> there is no mention about Bain&#39;s birth year, and there is nothing to indicate that he was alive during the events of <i>The Hobbit</i>, Bain appears in the <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>Hobbit</i> films</a> at age of about 13 and has two sisters, older <a href="/wiki/Sigrid" title="Sigrid">Sigrid</a> and younger <a href="/wiki/Tilda" title="Tilda">Tilda</a>. He is played by <a href="/wiki/John_Bell" title="John Bell">John Bell</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bell_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bell-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Bain assists his father <a href="/wiki/Bard" title="Bard">Bard</a> in smuggling <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thorin_and_Company" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> into their house. When the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> see a windlance outside their window, they recall the <a href="/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor" title="Sack of Erebor">Sack of Erebor</a>, and Bain relates the story of how <a href="/wiki/Girion" title="Girion">Girion</a> managed to knock one of <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>&#39;s scales loose in the attack, though the Dwarves don&#39;t believe him. Just before being arrested, Bard instructs Bain to hide the last <a href="/wiki/Black_arrow" title="Black arrow">black arrow</a> he has in his possession. When <a href="/wiki/Bolg" title="Bolg">Bolg</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> pack attacks <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>, Bain assists <a href="/wiki/Legolas" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tauriel" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a>, <a href="/wiki/F%C3%ADli" title="Fíli">Fíli</a>, <a href="/wiki/K%C3%ADli" title="Kíli">Kíli</a>, <a href="/wiki/%C3%93in" title="Óin">Óin</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Bofur" title="Bofur">Bofur</a> in fighting off the Orcs, and protects his sisters the best he can.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bain, Sigrid, and Tilda follow Tauriel and the Dwarves to evacuate Lake-town via boat while Smaug is destroying it. When he sees Bard making a last stand against the <a href="/wiki/Dragons" title="Dragons">Dragon</a>, Bain goes off to find the boat in which he hid the black arrow, and manages to deliver it to his father atop the bell tower. Bard constructs a large makeshift bow from the tower to fire the arrow at Smaug, using his son&#39;s shoulder to balance and aim the arrow. Bard urges him to stay calm as he fires the shot that ultimately kills the <a href="/wiki/Fire-drakes" title="Fire-drakes">fire-drake</a>. Later, as the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a> is getting underway, he and his sisters go into hiding in the ruins of <a href="/wiki/Dale" title="Dale">Dale</a> until the battle is over.</dd></dl>
