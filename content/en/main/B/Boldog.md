**Boldog** was an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)-name<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that was given to [Maiar](../M/Maiar.md) that took on the physical form of a primitive [Orc](../O/Orc.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Boldog was a title taken by many of the servants of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) that appeared multiple times during the [Battles of Beleriand](Battles_of_Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-ORC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ORC-3">[3]</a></sup> While some bearers of the Boldog title may have been no more than *"delusions and phantoms"*, many others that bore the name took on shapes that mocked and degraded [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> These *"Orc-formed Maiar"* were said to be *"only less formidable than the [Balrogs](Balrogs.md)"*.<sup id="cite_ref-ORC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ORC-3">[3]</a></sup>

Boldogs were apparently, similar to [Melian](../M/Melian.md), capable of embodied procreation. Doing so, however, would make them more and more earthbound, unable to return to spirit-state. Due to this diminishment, when they were slain they would be dwindle in force and be 'damned' and reduced to impotence.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Boldog* is [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) for "Torment-slayer", from *baul* ("torment") and *daug* ("warrior").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg/300px-Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="300" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9b/Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="380" data-file-height="380"/></a><figcaption><i>Thingol fights Boldog</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Tom_Loback.md" title="Category:Images by Tom Loback">Tom Loback</a></figcaption></figure>

According to the early version of the [Legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) within the *[Lay of Leithian](../L/Lay_of_Leithian.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> **Boldog** was the name of an Orc-captain that was the leader of a raid into the [north-marches](../D/Dimbar.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) and tasked by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) to capture [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md) and bring her to [Angband](../A/Angband.md), for reasons undisclosed. However, the [Iathrim](../I/Iathrim.md) defeated the invading army<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> with Boldog himself being slain by [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Some time later, when [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md), [Beren](Beren.md), [Edrahil](../E/Edrahil.md) and their companions were brought before [Thû](../T/Thû.md) clad within Orc-form, they said that they were a part of the army under the command of Boldog, and had urgent news to deliver to [Thangorodrim](../T/Thangorodrim.md) - but Thû saw through their disguise, revealing to them that Boldog was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
