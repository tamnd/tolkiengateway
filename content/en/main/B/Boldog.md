**Boldog** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")-name<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> that was given to [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) that took on the physical form of a primitive [Orc](/wiki/Orc).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Boldog was a title taken by many of the servants of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) that appeared multiple times during the [Battles of Beleriand](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-ORC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ORC-3">[3]</a></sup> While some bearers of the Boldog title may have been no more than *"delusions and phantoms"*, many others that bore the name took on shapes that mocked and degraded [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> These *"Orc-formed Maiar"* were said to be *"only less formidable than the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs)"*.<sup id="cite_ref-ORC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ORC-3">[3]</a></sup>

Boldogs were apparently, similar to [Melian](/wiki/Melian), capable of embodied procreation. Doing so, however, would make them more and more earthbound, unable to return to spirit-state. Due to this diminishment, when they were slain they would be dwindle in force and be 'damned' and reduced to impotence.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Boldog* is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) for "Torment-slayer", from *baul* ("torment") and *daug* ("warrior").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/9b/Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg/300px-Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="300" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/9/9b/Tom_Loback_-_Thingol_fights_Boldog.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="380" data-file-height="380"/></a><figcaption><i>Thingol fights Boldog</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Tom_Loback" title="Category:Images by Tom Loback">Tom Loback</a></figcaption></figure>

According to the early version of the [Legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium) within the *[Lay of Leithian](/wiki/Lay_of_Leithian)*,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> **Boldog** was the name of an Orc-captain that was the leader of a raid into the [north-marches](/wiki/Dimbar "Dimbar") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) and tasked by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) to capture [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) and bring her to [Angband](/wiki/Angband), for reasons undisclosed. However, the [Iathrim](/wiki/Iathrim) defeated the invading army<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> with Boldog himself being slain by [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Some time later, when [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod), [Beren](/wiki/Beren), [Edrahil](/wiki/Edrahil) and their companions were brought before [Thû](/wiki/Th%C3%BB) clad within Orc-form, they said that they were a part of the army under the command of Boldog, and had urgent news to deliver to [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim) - but Thû saw through their disguise, revealing to them that Boldog was slain.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
