**Bindbole Wood** was a small wood in the southern part of the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name presumably contains *bind* and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bole#Etymology_1" title="wiktionary:bole">bole</a></i> "trunk of a tree".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Misspelling {#Misspelling}

The name appears in the map of the Shire, but the letter *o* in the 1954 edition was not very clear; thus the name has been rendered **Bindbale** in many later maps (e.g. by [Barbara Strachey](Barbara_Strachey.md) and [Karen Wynn Fonstad](../K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md)), and elsewhere, like in [Robert Foster](../R/Robert_Foster.md)'s *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](../T/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth.md)* (s.v. *Bindbale*).

Even Tolkien himself referred to *Bindbale* in a manuscript note when he was preparing the document later known as *[Nomenclature](../G/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (Hammond and Scull suggest that perhaps writing this, Tolkien referred to the printed map and fell victim too of the misprinted *o*); as a result, in dutch it was translated (after Tolkien's suggestion) as *Pakkebaal Bos* which includes <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/baal#Dutch" title="wiktionary:baal">baal</a></i> "b**a**le".<sup id="cite_ref-rc_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rc_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) would reminisce that there is an actual English place named "Bindbole Wood", being one of the real place-names borrowed while drawing *[A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md)* map with his father. However this must be a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/false_memory" title="wikipedia:false memory">false memory</a> as there is no trace of such a place, not even Christopher himself was able to find any information afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Bindbole Wood begins almost immediately north of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/The_Hill.md" title="The Hill">the Hill</a> and stretches all the way east to <a class="mw-redirect" href="Brockenborings.md" title="Brockenborings">Brockenborings</a>. <a href="../O/Overhill.md" title="Overhill">Overhill</a> sits entirely within the Bindbole Wood, on the single road that connects Brockenborings to <a href="../H/Hobbiton.md" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a>.</dd></dl>
