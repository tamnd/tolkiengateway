**Bindbole Wood** was a small wood in the southern part of the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name presumably contains *bind* and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bole#Etymology_1" title="wiktionary:bole">bole</a></i> "trunk of a tree".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Misspelling {#Misspelling}

The name appears in the map of the Shire, but the letter *o* in the 1954 edition was not very clear; thus the name has been rendered **Bindbale** in many later maps (e.g. by [Barbara Strachey](/wiki/Barbara_Strachey) and [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad)), and elsewhere, like in [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster)'s *[The Complete Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Complete_Guide_to_Middle-earth)* (s.v. *Bindbale*).

Even Tolkien himself referred to *Bindbale* in a manuscript note when he was preparing the document later known as *[Nomenclature](/wiki/Guide_to_the_Names_in_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "Guide to the Names in The Lord of the Rings")* (Hammond and Scull suggest that perhaps writing this, Tolkien referred to the printed map and fell victim too of the misprinted *o*); as a result, in dutch it was translated (after Tolkien's suggestion) as *Pakkebaal Bos* which includes <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/baal#Dutch" title="wiktionary:baal">baal</a></i> "b**a**le".<sup id="cite_ref-rc_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-rc_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) would reminisce that there is an actual English place named "Bindbole Wood", being one of the real place-names borrowed while drawing *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)* map with his father. However this must be a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/false_memory" title="wikipedia:false memory">false memory</a> as there is no trace of such a place, not even Christopher himself was able to find any information afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Bindbole Wood begins almost immediately north of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Hill" title="The Hill">the Hill</a> and stretches all the way east to <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Brockenborings" title="Brockenborings">Brockenborings</a>. <a href="/wiki/Overhill" title="Overhill">Overhill</a> sits entirely within the Bindbole Wood, on the single road that connects Brockenborings to <a href="/wiki/Hobbiton" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a>.</dd></dl>
