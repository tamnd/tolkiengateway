**Borgil** was a bright red star near the constellation of [Menelvagor](/wiki/Menelvagor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Borgil* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, composed of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Born&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Born (page does not exist)">born</a></i> "hot, red" and *[gil](/wiki/Gil "Gil")* "star".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[Tolkien fans](/wiki/Tolkien_fandom "Tolkien fandom") have suggested that Borgil might either refer to the star <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betelgeuse" title="wikipedia:Betelgeuse">Betelgeuse</a> or the star <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldebaran" title="wikipedia:Aldebaran">Aldebaran</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Basing her approach both on literary assumptions and astronomical evidences, Prof. [Kristine Larsen](/wiki/Kristine_Larsen) has argued that Borgil can be identified as Aldebaran.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
