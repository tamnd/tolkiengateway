The **Back Door** was the secret entrance on the western side of [the Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"). It opened into the passage that led to bottommost cellar of the halls of the dwarves.

Tolkien&#39;s schematic drawing of the Lonely Mountain with the secret entrance, labeled the &#34;Back Door&#34;, in the center.</figcaption></figure>

The Back Door’s dimensions were described on [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map) as: "Five feet high the door and three may walk abreast." When [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) brought out the map at the unexpected party in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' home he also produced the small and curious silver key with a long barrel and intricate wards, which would open the door.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) was given permission to examine Thrór's Map and discovered the [moon-letters](/wiki/Moon-letters "Moon-letters") which revealed the method of opening the door. Elrond read them out as: "Stand by the grey stone when the thrush knocks and the setting sun with the last light of [Durin's Day](/wiki/Durin%27s_Day) will shine upon the key-hole."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Presumably, some other way existed to open it, at least from the inside, since Thrór and Thráin seem to have used it in order to escape during Smaug's attack, but that way is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

When Bilbo and the dwarves reached the Lonely Mountain they eventually found rough steps that led upwards to a little steep-walled bay above a cliff. In the back was a flat wall that rose as smooth and upright as masons' work. Although without any sign of a post or lintel or threshold, nor any sign of bar or bolt or key-hole, they knew that they had found the door. Days passed and then, on Durin's Day, a thrush came to a grey stone in the bay and knocked a snail against the rock. Right at sunset a last gleam of light struck the smooth rock-face and with a crack a flake of rock fell from the wall and revealed a keyhole. After [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) turned the key in the hole the dwarves pushed the door inward and the way into the mountain was opened.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
