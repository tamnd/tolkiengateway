The **black serpent** was an emblem of a "*black serpent upon scarlet*" that was depicted on the standard of a chieftain of the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the battle, the horsemen of the main force of the Haradrim gathered around the black serpent standard as their chieftain spotted the banner of King [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), noticing few riders around it. The chieftain displayed the black serpent and led his men against the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim). However, Théoden gathered his men and charged as well, throwing down the chieftain with his spear before spurring to the standard. With his sword, Théoden hewed down the staff and the bearer of the standard, causing the black serpent to founder as the Haradrim fled.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

After being mortally wounded, Théoden claimed to [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") before his death on the field that he was satisfied, for he had felled the black serpent.<sup id="cite_ref-Battle_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Battle-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early manuscript entitled with the name *Fall of Théoden in the Battle of Osgiliath*, the black serpent is never described, being referred to only as "*the standard of the [Haradoth](/wiki/Haradrim "Haradrim")*", later changed to [Haradhoth](/wiki/Haradrim "Haradrim"). Théoden also never hewed the standard nor did he fight any chieftain.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

<b><a href="/wiki/2016" title="2016">2016</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The &#34;Black Serpent&#34; is the title of &#34;Kâlach Hûl&#34;, the King of the <a href="/wiki/Haradrim" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a>, and the emblem born by his army. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> the player reaches the encampment of Kâlach Hûl and kill his two sons, Princes Raghtâ Sai and Methân Sai, shortly before <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden" title="Théoden">Théoden</a> kills Kâlach Hûl himself.</dd></dl>

In the months after Sauron's defeat, the name of the "Great Southern Empire" he ruled is given as "Ordâkh". Uprisings in [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) and [Near Harad](/wiki/Near_Harad) cause the "Ordâkhai" to lose much territory, but they continue to be a threat under a new Hûl.

**[2022](/wiki/2022)**: <b><i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Rise_to_War" title="The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War">The Lord of the Rings: Rise to War</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <b>Black Serpent</b> is the name of one of the evil faction commanders in the <i>Lord of the Rings: Rise to War</i> strategy game based on <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i> The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>. He is specifically skilled in battling other evil faction commanders due to his High Alert ability. He is based on the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/M%C3%BBmakil" title="Mûmakil">Mûmakil</a> rider and chieftain of the Haradrim forces in the Battle of Pelennor Fields in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a>. The Black Serpent counters Théoden&#39;s charge at the battle by sounding the bone-chilling and signature Haradrim war horn while mounted from the most painted Mûmakil.</dd></dl>
