<i><b>Brothers and Friends: The Diaries of Major Warren Hamilton Lewis</b></i> presents a selection of [Warren Lewis](/wiki/Warren_Lewis)'s diaries, edited by [Clyde S. Kilby](/wiki/Clyde_S._Kilby) and Marjorie Lamp Mead.

Several of the entries mention recollections of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) (see for example page 195).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
