<i><b>Bored of the Rings</b></i> is a short novel by [Henry N. Beard](/wiki/Henry_N._Beard) and [Douglas C. Kenney](/wiki/Douglas_C._Kenney) that parodies *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## Publication {#Publication}

<i><b>Bored of the Rings</b></i> was first published in 1969 by Signet for the Harvard Lampoon.

## Parody {#Parody}

The Signet first edition cover parodies the *The Lord of the Rings* paperback cover by artist Barbara Remington <a class="external autonumber" href="http://ringlord.com/people/walrus/lotr/wilderness-poster.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">[1]</a>. It was drawn by Michael K. Frith.

### Characters, races, and places {#Characters,_races,_and_places}
