"**Bregalad's song**" is a lament that [Bregalad](Bregalad.md) the [Ent](../E/Ents.md) sings for [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) on [30 February](../_/30_February.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> while he keeps them company as the other Ents continue to hold their [Entmoot](../E/Entmoot.md). The first line names three trees that Bregalad was especially fond of that are now gone, cut down by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) from [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). Bregalad softly sings in many tongues and the two hobbits fall asleep to the sound of his voice.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>O <a href="../O/Orofarnë.md" title="Orofarnë">Orofarnë</a>, <a href="../L/Lassemista.md" title="Lassemista">Lassemista</a>, <a href="../C/Carnimírië.md" title="Carnimírië">Carnimírië</a>! <br/>
O <a href="../R/Rowans.md" title="Rowans">rowan</a> fair, upon your hair how white the blossom lay! <br/>
O rowan mine, I saw you shine upon a summer&#39;s day, <br/>
Your rind so bright, your leaves so light, your voice so cool and soft: <br/>
Upon your head how golden-red the crown you bore aloft! <br/>
O rowan dead, upon your head your hair is dry and grey; <br/>
Your crown is spilled, your voice is stilled for ever and a day. <br/>
O Orofarnë, Lassemista, Carnimírië!
</p>
</div>
