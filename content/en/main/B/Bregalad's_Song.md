"**Bregalad's song**" is a lament that [Bregalad](/wiki/Bregalad) the [Ent](/wiki/Ents "Ents") sings for [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") on [30 February](/wiki/30_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> while he keeps them company as the other Ents continue to hold their [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot). The first line names three trees that Bregalad was especially fond of that are now gone, cut down by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) from [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). Bregalad softly sings in many tongues and the two hobbits fall asleep to the sound of his voice.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Text {#Text}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>O <a href="/wiki/Orofarn%C3%AB" title="Orofarnë">Orofarnë</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lassemista" title="Lassemista">Lassemista</a>, <a href="/wiki/Carnim%C3%ADri%C3%AB" title="Carnimírië">Carnimírië</a>! <br/>
O <a href="/wiki/Rowans" title="Rowans">rowan</a> fair, upon your hair how white the blossom lay! <br/>
O rowan mine, I saw you shine upon a summer&#39;s day, <br/>
Your rind so bright, your leaves so light, your voice so cool and soft: <br/>
Upon your head how golden-red the crown you bore aloft! <br/>
O rowan dead, upon your head your hair is dry and grey; <br/>
Your crown is spilled, your voice is stilled for ever and a day. <br/>
O Orofarnë, Lassemista, Carnimírië!
</p>
</div>
