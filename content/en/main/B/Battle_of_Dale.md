The **Battle of Dale** and the subsequent siege were decisive battles in the northern theatre of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). Even though it cost the lives of Kings [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) and [Brand of Dale](/wiki/Brand "Brand"), it was a decisive victory for the [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples).

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

The [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) and the [Bardings](/wiki/Bardings) refused to acknowledge the overlordship and alliance of Sauron<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and a host of his allies threatened the borders of King [Brand](/wiki/Brand).<sup id="cite_ref-B3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B3-3">[3]</a></sup>

### The Battle {#The_Battle}

[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had a large contingent of [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) to attack the [Kingdom of Dale](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Dale). King Brand held them at [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen) but around [14 March](/wiki/14_March) they managed to cross and push him back to [Dale](/wiki/Dale).

The combined forces of the Men and the Dwarves, under King [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), marched out to meet the Easterlings in battle at the feet of [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor). The armies fought bravely before the [Gate of Erebor](/wiki/Gate_of_Erebor), which was not taken. After three days of heavy close-quarters fighting, on [17 March](/wiki/17_March), Brand fell and Dáin was killed<sup id="cite_ref-B3_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B3-3">[3]</a></sup> as he stood defending the body of his ally.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Easterlings were victorious and many Dwarves and Men were forced to retreat towards the Lonely Mountain. The defenders of the Mountain, led by crown princes [Bard](/wiki/Bard_II "Bard II") and [Thorin Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm) were able to withstand the siege.<sup id="cite_ref-B3_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B3-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/15/Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg/300px-Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="193" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/15/Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg/450px-Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/15/Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg/600px-Jan_Pospisil_-_Dale_units_vs._Rhun.jpg 2x" data-file-width="670" data-file-height="430"/></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Jan_Posp%C3%AD%C5%A1il" title="Jan Pospíšil">Jan Pospíšil</a> - <i>Dale units vs. Rhun</i></figcaption></figure>

However, the forces of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) defeated the main power of Sauron in the Morannon theatre on [25 March](/wiki/25_March), causing the northern army to lose hope. Seeing the morale of their foes being sapped by news of victory in the south, Bard and Thorin managed to lift the siege on [27 March](/wiki/27_March) and drove the Easterlings out of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) and back to the [East](/wiki/East).<sup id="cite_ref-B3_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B3-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

The battle was incredibly important in the course of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring): if Sauron's Easterling armies had beaten the Dwarves and Men of Dale, they would have been able to join up with Sauron's forces from [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) in their attacks on the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) and [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), tipping the scales in favor of Mordor. This would have enabled Mordor's armies to flank the forces of Gondor and Rohan from the North and rear. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) himself commented that had the Battle of Dale been lost in this way, the forces of the West would have been crushed regardless of the victory at the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II) became [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale) and [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_III_Stonehelm) became [King under the Mountain](/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain) after the battle. They sent their ambassadors to the coronation of King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn_II_Elessar "Aragorn II Elessar"). [Dale](/wiki/Dale) and the [Kingdom under the Mountain](/wiki/Kingdom_under_the_Mountain) remained in perpetual friendship with [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and they were under the crown and protection of the [King of the West](/wiki/King_of_the_West).<sup id="cite_ref-B3_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-B3-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Extended Edition, while the army of Rohan is mustering at Edoras, Gimli comments to Legolas that he wishes they had a legion of Dwarves to help.  Legolas responds that the Dwarves of Lonely Mountain and Elves of Mirkwood cannot march south to war against Mordor, for he fears that war is already marching upon their own lands.  In the DVD commentary, Peter Jackson explained that this brief exchange was a nod to the Battle of Dale, as well as Sauron&#39;s attacks on the Woodland Elves and Lothlorien in the northern theatre of the war.  However, Jackson explained that while he wished to show these other battles and that the War of the Ring was a truly global conflict, they simply didn&#39;t have the resources or time to construct entirely new sets and film more expansive battle scenes.  Reluctantly Jackson could not show these events, but wanted to somehow acknowledge why the Elves and Dwarves aren&#39;t coming to aid the besieged Minas Tirith.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2006" title="2006">2006</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Siege of Erebor is the penultimate level of the good campaign. The forces are Mordor are led by the <a href="/wiki/Mouth_of_Sauron" title="Mouth of Sauron">Mouth of Sauron</a>, and originate from <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a> rather than the <a href="/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn" title="Rhûn">East</a>. Groups of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Corsairs" title="Corsairs">Corsairs</a> and <a href="/wiki/Haradrim" title="Haradrim">Haradrim</a> first attack Dale, before the main host attacks. The first of three waves is led by a horsed <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ringwraith" title="Ringwraith">Ringwraith</a>, and the third (made up almost entirely of trolls and siege weapons) by a Ringwraith on a <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Fell_beast" title="Fell beast">Fell beast</a>. After the third wave is broken, the <a href="/wiki/Mouth_of_Sauron" title="Mouth of Sauron">Mouth of Sauron</a> enters Erebor via a hidden entrance, and has to be killed. Almost simultaneously, <a href="/wiki/Glorfindel" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in" title="Glóin">Glóin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thranduil" title="Thranduil">Thranduil</a> arrive with reinforcements from Mirkwood. At the level&#39;s conclusion, they are credited with defeating the enemy.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dale and Erebor were first featured in a series of group &#34;instances&#34; that depict their invasion by the &#34;Jangovar&#34; Easterlings. The first battle in the series features a race to ring the warning bells of Dale. The next has the players defending the crossing of the River Running so that the folk of Dale can evacuate safely to Erebor. Here King Brand is slain by archers, in a departure from his fate in the book. In the third such instance, players must defend <a href="/wiki/Ravenhill" title="Ravenhill">Ravenhill</a> from a smoke-spewing Jangovar machine wrought in <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>&#39;s likeness and powered by a fire-spirit. The final encounter takes place at the end of the siege and pits the players in an arena-style battle against two mighty <a href="/wiki/Olog-hai" title="Olog-hai">Olog-hai</a> champions in order to break the attackers&#39; morale once and for all.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Player characters can also visit Erebor and Dale after the War, and assist the two new Kings and their people with recovering. Despite being defeated, some stubborn Jangovar still linger in Dale&#39;s territory. One quest points out the inconsistency of Brand&#39;s death as well as the unlikelyhood of the Jangovar machine by having such occurrences recounted by an infamous Dwarf rumour-monger and spinner of tales.</dd></dl>
