The **Book of the Foxrook** is a manuscript by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), likely composed around [10 June](../_/10_June.md) [1909](../_/1909.md) (this date was added to the title-page).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

It consists of a 16-page notebook containing "*the key to a secret code consisting of a rune-like phonetic alphabet and a sizable number of ideographic symbols*".<sup id="cite_ref-Seven_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seven-2">[2]</a></sup> The invented script is called *[Privata Kodo Skauta](../P/Privata_Kodo_Skauta.md)* ([Modern English](../M/Modern_English.md): "Private Scout Code").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The book also contains comments on [Esperanto](../E/Esperanto.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Seven_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seven-2">[2]</a></sup>

The existence of this manuscript was apparently first mentioned by [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md) in his *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md)* (p. 37).<sup id="cite_ref-Seven_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seven-2">[2]</a></sup> A page from the Book of the Foxrook was reproduced in the exhibition catalogue *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Life and Legend](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Life_and_Legend.md)*. An analysis and transcription of the same page appeared in the article "Tolkien and Esperanto" (cf. pp. 29–34), published in [*SEVEN*, Volume 17](../S/SEVEN_17.md).

The Book of the Foxrook is kept at the [Bodleian Library](Bodleian_Library.md) (Family papers 1/29(3), fol. 1r).<sup id="cite_ref-Seven_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seven-2">[2]</a></sup>
