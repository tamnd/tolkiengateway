<i><b>band</b></i> or <i><b>bann</b></i> is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)/[Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "prison, Hell".<sup id="cite_ref-VT45_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin)/[Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *banda* ("prison, Hell"), from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a href="/wiki/BAD" title="BAD">BAD<sup>[2]</sup></a>.<sup id="cite_ref-VT45_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *vanda* ("prison, Hell")
