**Brian Cox** is a Scottish actor and voice actor. In [2009](/wiki/2009), he narrated [The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn) [audiobook](/wiki/Audiobooks "Audiobooks"), yet it was never released by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins).<sup id="cite_ref-Bartch_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bartch-1">[1]</a></sup> He portrayed [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm_Hammerhand) in the 2024 anime film *[The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_War_of_the_Rohirrim)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
