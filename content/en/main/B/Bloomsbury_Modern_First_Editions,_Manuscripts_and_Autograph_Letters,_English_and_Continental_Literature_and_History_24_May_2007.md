<i><b>Bloomsbury Auctions: Modern First Editions, Manuscripts and Autograph Letters, English and Continental Literature &amp; History 24 May 2007</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Bloomsbury Auctions. Lots 247-250 (pp. 42–3) are early editions of Tolkien's books. Other related lots are:

- 493: A two page [1967 letter to Elsie Honeybourne](</wiki/Letter_to_Elsie_Honeybourne_(18_September_1967)> "Letter to Elsie Honeybourne (18 September 1967)"). The first page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.
- 494: A two page [1967 letter to Elsie Honeybourne](</wiki/Letter_to_Elsie_Honeybourne_(21_December_1967)> "Letter to Elsie Honeybourne (21 December 1967)"). The second page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.
- 495: A one page [1969 letter to Mr. Burrows](/wiki/Letter_to_Mr_Burrows "Letter to Mr Burrows"). The letter is reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
