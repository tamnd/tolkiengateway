**Bane** is an archaic word referring to something that causes harm or ruin to a person or people. [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) used this term often in his [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Below are some of the titles including the word *bane*:

- [Bain](Bain.md), a [King of Dale](../K/King_of_Dale.md), whose name is translated by some as "bane".
- [Dagnir](../D/Dagnir.md), a man of [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), whose name translates as "bane".
- **[Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md), the Balrog of [Moria](../M/Moria.md).**
- [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md)'s Bane, [Túrin Turambar](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)'s Bane, [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).**
- Mansbane, a name given to [Felaróf](../F/Felaróf.md) by [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Master's bane, a name given to [Snowmane](../S/Snowmane.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Troll's bane, a name given by [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) to his sword.
