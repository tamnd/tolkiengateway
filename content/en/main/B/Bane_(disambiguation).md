**Bane** is an archaic word referring to something that causes harm or ruin to a person or people. [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") used this term often in his [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Below are some of the titles including the word *bane*:

- [Bain](/wiki/Bain), a [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale), whose name is translated by some as "bane".
- [Dagnir](/wiki/Dagnir), a man of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), whose name translates as "bane".
- **[Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane), the Balrog of [Moria](/wiki/Moria).**
- [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung)'s Bane, [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **[Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s Bane, [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").**
- Mansbane, a name given to [Felaróf](/wiki/Felar%C3%B3f) by [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- Master's bane, a name given to [Snowmane](/wiki/Snowmane).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
- Troll's bane, a name given by [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) to his sword.
