**Berúthiel** was a [Queen of Gondor](../Q/Queen_of_Gondor.md), the wife of King [Tarannon Falastur](../T/Tarannon_Falastur.md), who ruled from [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [830](../T/Third_Age_830.md) until [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [913](../T/Third_Age_913.md). She was noted as being "nefarious, solitary, and loveless".<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup> It is therefore of no surprise that she and Tarannon did not have any children.<sup id="cite_ref-TarannonFalastur_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TarannonFalastur-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Berúthiel was a [Black Númenórean](Black_Númenóreans.md), who was possibly from an [inland city](../H/Harad.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup> that was located to the south of [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

Tarannon's reason for marrying Berúthiel is not known. It is possible that the marriage was in connection with his extensions of the realm of Gondor along the coasts south of the mouths of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) and his victories as the [Captain of the Hosts](../C/Captain_of_the_Hosts.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tarannon_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tarannon-5">[4]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that it was a diplomatic marriage to form an alliance with or to maintain peaceful relations with the realm that Berúthiel came from.

Berúthiel loathed the sounds and the smells of the [sea](Belegaer.md), [Fish](../F/Fish.md), and the [Gulls](../G/Gulls.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup> She also hated "the house that Tarannon had built below [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md)" on arches in the water of the [mouths of Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md). As a consequence, she lived in the [King's House](../K/King's_House.md) in [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) instead. Berúthiel hated all colours and elaborate adornments and wore only black and silver clothing. She lived in bare chambers in the house in Osgiliath, but decorated its gardens "with tormented sculptures beneath [cypresses](../C/Cypresses.md) and [yews](../Y/Yews.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup>

Berúthiel loathed cats, but they became attracted to her for precisely that reason. They followed her around, and eventually she took advantage of their company by enslaving and torturing them for her amusement.<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup> Of the [cats](../C/Cats_of_Queen_Berúthiel.md) that she kept<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup> as her slaves: there were ten of them: nine black and one white.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup> Berúthiel trained the cats to go on evil errands throughout the night to spy on her enemies or to terrify them,<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup> in order to discover the dark secrets of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), so that she knew the things that men wished the most to keep hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup> She sent the white cat to spy on the black ones to torment them. Berúthiel was able to converse with them or read their memories. Her cats were infamous among the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md). All were afraid of them, did not dare to touch them, and cursed whenever they saw them.<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eventually, Tarannon exiled Berúthiel from Gondor<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 2]</a></sup> and her name was erased from the [Book of the Kings](Book_of_the_Kings.md). He had her set adrift at sea before a north wind on a ship alone that "was last seen flying past Umbar under a sickle moon, with a cat at the masthead and another as a figure-head on the prow"<sup id="cite_ref-Istari_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Istari-1">[1]</a></sup> and Berúthiel went back to live in the inland city<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup>.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

No wars with Umbar or Harad are mentioned during the reign of King Tarannon.<sup id="cite_ref-Tarannon_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tarannon-5">[4]</a></sup> It is possible that the setting of Berúthiel on a ship that was last seen flying past Umbar offended the realm that Berúthiel was from. The conquest of Umbar took place during the reign of King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../E/Eärnil.md" title="Eärnil">Eärnil</a> twenty-three years after the death of King Tarannon.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

Despite the erasure of her name from the Book of the Kings, Berúthiel and her cats were so notorious that they were held in the memory of [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) for centuries; [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) alluded to them more than 2,000 years after her exile.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[6]</a></sup>

The name *Berúthiel* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains it might mean "Angry Queen" and might be a combination of *[ber(eth)](Bereth.md)* ("queen", "spouse"; "supreme", "sublime") + *[rúth](../R/Rúth.md)* ("wrath", "anger") + *[-iel](../_/-iel.md)* ("daughter"; "feminine suffix").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup> Since the Black Númenóreans did not use the [Elven tongues](../E/Elvish.md), this title was probably given to her by the Gondorians and is not her real name.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier version of what would later become the chapter [A Journey in the Dark](../A/A_Journey_in_the_Dark.md), it was the narrator rather than Aragorn II who mentioned the cats of Queen Berúthiel. This first version of the sentence referred simply to cats in general with no reference to Berúthiel. The second version of the sentence referred to "the cat of **Benish Armon**", before the reference was changed to "the cats of Queen **Tamar**", with a slight revision from *Tamar* ("Smith")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup> to **Margoliantë Berúthiel**, before dropping *Margoliantë* and keeping *Berúthiel*.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[9]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) did not indicate when the manuscript with the story of queen Berúthiel was written, but [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) wrote in a letter to [W.H. Auden](../W/W.H._Auden.md) on [7 June](../_/7_June.md) [1955](../_/1955.md),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[10]</a></sup> in a letter to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Giffard,_3rd_Earl_of_Halsbury" title="wikipedia:Tony Giffard, 3rd Earl of Halsbury">Lord Halsbury</a> on [10 November](../_/10_November.md) [1955](../_/1955.md)<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[11]</a></sup> and in a letter to Mr. Thompson on [14 January](../_/14_January.md) [1956](../_/1956.md)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup> that he had not yet written the story about the cats of Queen Berúthiel.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In an interview from [1966](../_/1966.md), Tolkien likened Berúthiel to the giantess <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ska%C3%B0i" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Skaði</a> of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norse_mythology" title="wikipedia:Norse mythology">Norse mythology</a>, since they both shared a dislike for "seaside life". Skadi was from <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%9Erymheimr" title="wikipedia:Þrymheimr">Thrymheim</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B6tunheimr" title="wikipedia:Jötunheimr">Jötunheim</a> and ended up being married to the sea-god <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nj%C3%B6r%C3%B0r" title="wikipedia:Njörðr">Njord</a>. After her marriage, Skadi got fed up with the seaside life and went back to live in Jotunheim.<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>
