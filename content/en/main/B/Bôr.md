<i><b>bôr</b></i> (plural <i><b>bŷr</b></i>) is a noun meaning "steadfast, trustworthy, a faithful vassal" in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Compare with <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Vor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vor (page does not exist)">vor</a></i> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).

Examples:

- [Borlach](Borlach.md) = "Faithful Flame"
- [Boromir](Boromir.md) = "Faithful Jewel"
