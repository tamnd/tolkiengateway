<i><b>bôr</b></i> (plural <i><b>bŷr</b></i>) is a noun meaning "steadfast, trustworthy, a faithful vassal" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). Compare with <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Vor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Vor (page does not exist)">vor</a></i> in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).

Examples:

- [Borlach](/wiki/Borlach) = "Faithful Flame"
- [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) = "Faithful Jewel"
