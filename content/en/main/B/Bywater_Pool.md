The **Bywater Pool** was a pond located in the eastern corner of the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), just north of the town of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater). The main river of the Shire, [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water"), flowed into its western end and exited its eastern end. A stream from the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) (which may have been the [Norbourn](/wiki/Norbourn)) entered Bywater Pool on its northern side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Before the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Bywater Pool had had a pleasant row of old [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") holes in the north bank, with little gardens that had run down to the water’s edge. Along the Pool-side where the [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) Road ran close to the south bank there had grown an avenue of trees. On [2 November](/wiki/2_November), [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), when the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) arrived at Bywater, they found the holes in the north bank deserted, their gardens filled with weeds, and the avenue of trees gone – replaced by a line of ugly new houses.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
