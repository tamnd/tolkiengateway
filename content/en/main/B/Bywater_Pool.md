The **Bywater Pool** was a pond located in the eastern corner of the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), just north of the town of [Bywater](Bywater.md). The main river of the Shire, [the Water](../T/The_Water.md), flowed into its western end and exited its eastern end. A stream from the [Northfarthing](../N/Northfarthing.md) (which may have been the [Norbourn](../N/Norbourn.md)) entered Bywater Pool on its northern side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Before the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Bywater Pool had had a pleasant row of old [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) holes in the north bank, with little gardens that had run down to the water’s edge. Along the Pool-side where the [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) Road ran close to the south bank there had grown an avenue of trees. On [2 November](../_/2_November.md), [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), when the [Travellers](../T/Travellers.md) arrived at Bywater, they found the holes in the north bank deserted, their gardens filled with weeds, and the avenue of trees gone – replaced by a line of ugly new houses.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
