**Bridgefields** was one of the most easterly regions of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), lying within the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing). Bridgefields was a stretch of about ten miles that lay along the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) immediately before it reached the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) and left the Shire. In the western parts of Bridgefields were the townships of [Whitfurrows](/wiki/Whitfurrows) and, a little to the north, [Budgeford](/wiki/Budgeford) on the Water.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Budgeford was best known as being the home of the [Bolger family](/wiki/Bolger_Family "Bolger Family"), one of whom - [Fredegar](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger") - played a part in the departure of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") on his journey to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
