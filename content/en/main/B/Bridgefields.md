**Bridgefields** was one of the most easterly regions of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), lying within the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md). Bridgefields was a stretch of about ten miles that lay along the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) immediately before it reached the [Brandywine Bridge](Brandywine_Bridge.md) and left the Shire. In the western parts of Bridgefields were the townships of [Whitfurrows](../W/Whitfurrows.md) and, a little to the north, [Budgeford](Budgeford.md) on the Water.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Budgeford was best known as being the home of the [Bolger family](Bolger_Family~66348.md), one of whom - [Fredegar](../F/Fredegar_Bolger.md) - played a part in the departure of [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) on his journey to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
