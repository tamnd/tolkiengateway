**Blanco Bracegirdle** was a [hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Blanco married [Primrose Boffin](/wiki/Primrose_Bracegirdle "Primrose Bracegirdle"), and they had two children. [Bruno](/wiki/Bruno_Bracegirdle "Bruno Bracegirdle") was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1313](/wiki/Third_Age_2913 "Third Age 2913") and [Lobelia](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins "Lobelia Sackville-Baggins") in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1318](/wiki/Third_Age_2918 "Third Age 2918").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
