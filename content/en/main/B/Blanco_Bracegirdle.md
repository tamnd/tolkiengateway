**Blanco Bracegirdle** was a [hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Blanco married [Primrose Boffin](../P/Primrose_Bracegirdle.md), and they had two children. [Bruno](Bruno_Bracegirdle.md) was born in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1313](../T/Third_Age_2913.md) and [Lobelia](../L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1318](../T/Third_Age_2918.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
