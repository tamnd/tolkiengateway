**Bill** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) stated he used **Bill** to translate [Westron](../W/Westron.md) **Bil**, which is a short for (untranslated and further unknown) names as *Arambil*, *Bildad* and *Bilcuzal*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possibly related to [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) *bêl-*, "love", as seen in *[Azrubêl](../A/Azrubêl.md)*, a name of [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Bill Butcher](Bill_Butcher.md), a character from the poem "[Perry-the-Winkle](../P/Perry-the-Winkle.md)".
- [Bill Ferny](Bill_Ferny.md), a man of [Bree](Bree.md) who spied on [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md).
    - [Bill the Pony](Bill_the_Pony.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s loyal pony named after Bill Ferny.
- [Bill Huggins](../W/William.md), sometimes referred to as "William", one the three [Trolls](../T/Trolls.md) that [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) encountered.
