**Bill** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) stated he used **Bill** to translate [Westron](/wiki/Westron) **Bil**, which is a short for (untranslated and further unknown) names as *Arambil*, *Bildad* and *Bilcuzal*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is possibly related to [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) *bêl-*, "love", as seen in *[Azrubêl](/wiki/Azrub%C3%AAl)*, a name of [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Bill Butcher](/wiki/Bill_Butcher), a character from the poem "[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)".
- [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny), a man of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) who spied on [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins").
    - [Bill the Pony](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee")'s loyal pony named after Bill Ferny.
- [Bill Huggins](/wiki/William "William"), sometimes referred to as "William", one the three [Trolls](/wiki/Trolls) that [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) encountered.
