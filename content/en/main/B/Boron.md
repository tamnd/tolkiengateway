**Boron** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [315](/wiki/First_Age_315 "First Age 315")-[408](/wiki/First_Age_408 "First Age 408")<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the third head of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Boron was the son of [Baran](/wiki/Baran) and grandson of [Bëor the Old](/wiki/B%C3%ABor "Bëor"), and inherited the title of family head after his father's death in [380](/wiki/First_Age_380 "First Age 380"). He had two sons, [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros)> "Boromir (Lord of Ladros)") and [Belegor](/wiki/Belegor), the eldest of which inherited his title and also became the first [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Boron* is a word in [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Old_Noldorin) and [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) for "Steadfast, trusty man, faithful vassal".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, created in the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas) and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir). After publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* a new genealogy for the House of Bëor was made in the 1950s and at that time four generations (Baran, **Boron**, Boromir, and Bregor) were inserted after Bëor, with Bregor the father of Bregolas and Barahir.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>
