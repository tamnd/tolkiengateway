**Boron** ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [315](../F/First_Age_315.md)-[408](../F/First_Age_408.md)<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the third head of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).

## History {#History}

Boron was the son of [Baran](Baran.md) and grandson of [Bëor the Old](Bëor.md), and inherited the title of family head after his father's death in [380](../F/First_Age_380.md). He had two sons, [Boromir](<Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros).md>) and [Belegor](Belegor.md), the eldest of which inherited his title and also became the first [Lord of Ladros](../L/Lord_of_Ladros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Boron* is a word in [Old Noldorin](../O/Old_Noldorin.md) and [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) for "Steadfast, trusty man, faithful vassal".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[The Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)*, created in the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](Bregolas.md) and [Barahir](Barahir.md). After publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* a new genealogy for the House of Bëor was made in the 1950s and at that time four generations (Baran, **Boron**, Boromir, and Bregor) were inserted after Bëor, with Bregor the father of Bregolas and Barahir.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>
