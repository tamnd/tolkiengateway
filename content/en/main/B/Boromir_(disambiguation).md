**Boromir** may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Boromir son of Bor](/wiki/Borlach#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Borlach"), early name later replaced by [Borlach](/wiki/Borlach).
- [Boromir of Ladros](</wiki/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros)> "Boromir (Lord of Ladros)") ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [338](/wiki/First_Age_338 "First Age 338") - [432](/wiki/First_Age_432 "First Age 432")), Head of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) and first [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).
- [Boromir (Steward of Gondor)](</wiki/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor)>) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2410](/wiki/Third_Age_2410 "Third Age 2410") - [2489](/wiki/Third_Age_2489 "Third Age 2489")), the eleventh [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
- **[Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2978](/wiki/Third_Age_2978 "Third Age 2978") - [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), elder son of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") and a member of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring).**
    - [Boromir's belt](/wiki/Boromir%27s_belt), a gift from [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) when the Fellowship stayed in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).
    - [Song of Boromir](/wiki/Song_of_Boromir), lament by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) following Boromir's death.

## Books {#Books}

- *[The Departure of Boromir](/wiki/The_Departure_of_Boromir)*, the first chapter of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

- *[The Departure of Boromir (scene)](</wiki/The_Departure_of_Boromir_(scene)>)*, the penultimate scene of the [2001](/wiki/2001) film, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- *[The Departure of Boromir](/wiki/The_Departure_of_Boromir)*, the 34th track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*.
