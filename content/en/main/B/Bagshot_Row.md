**Bagshot Row** was the row of [Hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-holes) that were delved into [Hobbiton Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill) beneath [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). The [Gamgee Family](/wiki/Gamgee_Family) lived at Number 3, Bagshot Row.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Their neighbour was [Daddy Twofoot](/wiki/Daddy_Twofoot).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> The dwellings had gardens and earthwalls made of the earth removed during the excavation of Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-nom_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nom-3">[2]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and the occupation of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) had it torn up, and it had become a yawning sand and gravel pit. After the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) the destruction was replaced by a new row called, simply, [New Row](/wiki/New_Row) (in the town of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) it became a joke to refer to it as Sharkey's End).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It was so named because the earth removed in excavating Bag End was *shot* over the edge of the sudden fall in the hillside onto the ground.<sup id="cite_ref-nom_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nom-3">[2]</a></sup>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bagshot Row is much longer than described in the book; numbers run up to ten.</dd></dl>

- 1 Bagshot Row: [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End)
- 2 Bagshot Row: [Daddy Twofoot](/wiki/Daddy_Twofoot)
- 3 Bagshot Row: The Gamgees
- 4 Bagshot Row: [Milo Burrows](/wiki/Milo_Burrows)
- 5 Bagshot Row: [The Proudfeet](/wiki/Proudfoot_Family "Proudfoot Family")
- 6 Bagshot Row: Halfast Hornblower
- 8 Bagshot Row: [Hugo Bracegirdle](/wiki/Hugo_Bracegirdle)
- 10 Bagshot Row: [Fredregar *(sic)* Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger")

<dl><dd>Numbers 7 and 9, inhabited by <a href="/wiki/Folco_Boffin" title="Folco Boffin">Folco Boffin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Robin_Smallburrow" title="Robin Smallburrow">Robin Smallburrow</a>, are inexplicably located on the &#34;Water Road&#34; and the &#34;Hill Road&#34;.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In a deviation from the book, <a href="/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a> and <a href="/wiki/Rose_Cotton" title="Rose Cotton">Rosie</a> still live at Number 3.</dd></dl>
