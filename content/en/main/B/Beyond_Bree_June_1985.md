<b><i>Beyond Bree</i> June 1985</b> is an issue of *[Beyond Bree](/wiki/Beyond_Bree)*, edited by [Nancy Martsch](/wiki/Nancy_Martsch).

It includes a correction and explanation of the genealogical tree '[Kinship of the Half-elven](/wiki/Kinship_of_the_Half-elven)' that was published in the [May 1985 issue](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_May_1985 "Beyond Bree May 1985").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Also included is a transcription of the descriptive text by Tolkien noted below the chart.
