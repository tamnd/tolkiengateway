**Brian Donald Hewens Miller** ([14 October](/wiki/14_October) [1924](/wiki/1924) - [26 February](/wiki/26_February) [2001](/wiki/2001)) was an English scholar and philologist.

Miller was a Lecturer in English Philology at Oriel College and Brasenose College in Oxford in [1953](/wiki/1953)-[57](/wiki/1957 "1957").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During his time in Oxford, Miller also became one of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s B.Litt. (*Bachelor of Literature* or *Bachelor of Letters*) students (starting on [January 20](/wiki/20_January "20 January"), [1952](/wiki/1952)). During four years he worked on his thesis on the [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) fabliau (comic bourgeois tale) *Dame Sirith*, examined by Tolkien and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=G.V_Smithers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="G.V Smithers (page does not exist)">G.V Smithers</a> on [March 2](/wiki/2_March "2 March"), [1956](/wiki/1956).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Miller and Tolkien seem to have continued staying in touch. In [1971](/wiki/1971), he sent Tolkien a copy of C.T. Onions' *Modern English Syntax* (the revised edition by Miller, published in the same year). Tolkien replied to Miller on [June 21](/wiki/21_June "21 June"), thanking for the gift (see letter *[Letter to Brian Miller](/wiki/Letter_to_Brian_Miller)*).
