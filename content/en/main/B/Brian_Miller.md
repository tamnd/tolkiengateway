**Brian Donald Hewens Miller** ([14 October](../_/14_October.md) [1924](../_/1924.md) - [26 February](../_/26_February.md) [2001](../_/2001.md)) was an English scholar and philologist.

Miller was a Lecturer in English Philology at Oriel College and Brasenose College in Oxford in [1953](../_/1953.md)-[57](../_/1957.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During his time in Oxford, Miller also became one of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s B.Litt. (*Bachelor of Literature* or *Bachelor of Letters*) students (starting on [January 20](../_/20_January.md), [1952](../_/1952.md)). During four years he worked on his thesis on the [Middle English](../M/Middle_English.md) fabliau (comic bourgeois tale) *Dame Sirith*, examined by Tolkien and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=G.V_Smithers&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="G.V Smithers (page does not exist)">G.V Smithers</a> on [March 2](../_/2_March.md), [1956](../_/1956.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Miller and Tolkien seem to have continued staying in touch. In [1971](../_/1971.md), he sent Tolkien a copy of C.T. Onions' *Modern English Syntax* (the revised edition by Miller, published in the same year). Tolkien replied to Miller on [June 21](../_/21_June.md), thanking for the gift (see letter *[Letter to Brian Miller](../L/Letter_to_Brian_Miller.md)*).
