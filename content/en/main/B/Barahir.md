**Barahir** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400") – [460](/wiki/First_Age_460 "First Age 460")) was a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), heir to the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) and most famous as the father of [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren").

## History {#History}

Barahir was the fifth and youngest child of [Bregor](/wiki/Bregor). He had three elder sisters: [Bregil](/wiki/Bregil), [Hirwen](/wiki/Hirwen), and [Gilwen](/wiki/Gilwen), and an elder brother [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas). Barahir married [Emeldir *the Man-Hearted*](/wiki/Emeldir "Emeldir"), who was also of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) through the line of [Belen](/wiki/Belen) (youngest son of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor)). They had two children, the renowned [Beren](/wiki/Beren) the *One-handed* and a daughter named [Hiril](</wiki/Hiril_(daughter_of_Barahir)> "Hiril (daughter of Barahir)")<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455"), Barahir fought in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) at the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion). Near the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech), he hastened to [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod")'s rescue with a small retinue of his bravest men. They formed a wall of spears around the Elven King, and cut their way out of the battle at great loss. Finrod was saved, and swore an oath in gratitude, promising help to Barahir and his kin should they need it. To reinforce this oath, he gave Barahir [his ring](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir "Ring of Barahir"), which would become an heirloom in his house.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-3">[3]</a></sup>

After [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) conquered the lands, Barahir contested his dominion. Gradually all of the people of that region fled until Barahir had but twelve companions left: his son Beren, his nephews [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund) and [Belegund](/wiki/Belegund), and nine close and loyal kinsmen, [Radhruin](/wiki/Radhruin), [Dairuin](/wiki/Dairuin), [Dagnir](/wiki/Dagnir), [Ragnor](/wiki/Ragnor), [Gildor](</wiki/Gildor_(outlaw)> "Gildor (outlaw)"), [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim), [Arthad](/wiki/Arthad), [Urthel](/wiki/Urthel), and [Hathaldir](/wiki/Hathaldir). As Dorthonion fell under dark enchantment and was called <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin" title="Taur-nu-Fuin">Taur-nu-Fuin</a> the desperate band retreated to the rocky moors above the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [460](/wiki/First_Age_460 "First Age 460") they were betrayed to [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) by [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim), who was tricked by Sauron into giving up their location. Barahir and the others were [slaughtered](/wiki/Massacre_of_Tarn_Aeluin "Massacre of Tarn Aeluin") near [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin). Barahir's son Beren alone escaped, as he was away on an errand when the outlaws' hideout was discovered. Returning to the camp Beren buried his father and pursued the Orcs who slew him. The captain of the Orcs had Barahir's hand with the [ring of Felagund](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir "Ring of Barahir") on it when Beren found them; Beren killed the captain and retrieved the hand.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Barahir* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word and consists of *[bara](/wiki/Bara "Bara")* ("fiery; eager") + *[hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* ("master, lord").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In such early manuscripts as *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)* and *[The Lay of Leithian](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian)*, the father of Beren was a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnomes "Gnomes") named **Egnor bo-Rimion**, who was called [Rog the Fleet](/wiki/Rog_the_Fleet) by the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of [Melko](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Egnor was a forester and huntsmen who "hunted in the darker places of [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB)." He was well-liked by the [Hounds](/wiki/Hounds) of [Hisilómë](/wiki/Hisil%C3%B3m%C3%AB) and friend of [Huan](/wiki/Huan)'s.

In the *Grey Annals* (written in the 1930s<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>) Barahir was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [402](/wiki/First_Age_402 "First Age 402"), two years after his brother Bregolas, and they were both the sons of Bëor the Old.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> After *The Lord of the Rings* was written [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) overhauled the genealogy of the House of Bëor.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Four generations were inserted after Bëor with Bregor made the father of Bregolas and Barahir (whose birth was now set in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400")).<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup>
