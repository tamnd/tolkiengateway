The **Battle of the Gwathló** was the final battle of the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md), fought between the forces of Sauron and the combined forces of [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) and [Minastir](../T/Tar-Minastir.md). It ended in a serious defeat for the Dark Lord forcing him to retreat to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

## Background {#Background}

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1695](../S/Second_Age_1695.md) [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), the [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), requested aid from [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) invaded [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md). [Minastir](../T/Tar-Minastir.md), the [heir](../K/King's_Heir.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Delay_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Delay-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> of Queen [Tar-Telperiën](../T/Tar-Telperiën.md), sent a fleet commanded by the admiral [Ciryatur](../C/Ciryatur.md), but it was delayed until [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1700](../S/Second_Age_1700.md), by which time the beleaguered [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and Númenóreans (that were already in Lindon before the invasion) were barely holding the line of the River [Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md). The arrival of the Númenórean fleet immediately changed the balance of forces such that Sauron's host was driven back with heavy casualties.<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

After being defeated at Lhûn, Sauron retreated with his host to [Sarn Ford](../S/Sarn_Ford.md), the crossing on the [Baranduin](Baranduin.md), and suffered another defeat. He further retreated southwards to [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md) where more of his forces joined him after coming up from the south-east. Unbeknownst to Sauron, Ciryatur also had more forces at his disposal and sent part of his fleet at the [small Númenórean harbour](../L/Lond_Daer.md) at the mouth of the [Gwathló](../G/Gwathló.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

This new contingent fell upon Sauron's rear from the south-west while Gil-galad's Elves and the other Númenóreans attacked from the north. Sauron's army was completely routed and he barely escaped himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Sauron and his small remaining forces fled across the [Isen](../I/Isen.md) but were caught in eastern [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md). Only a small bodyguard was left when Sauron escaped to [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md). Gil-galad moved north to lift the [siege of Imladris](../F/First_Siege_of_Imladris.md) and [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) was cleared of the enemy. Eriador had peace for many years but the humiliation inflicted on Sauron made him determined to seek vengeance upon Númenor. [Imladris](../R/Rivendell.md) became the new Elvish stronghold in eastern Eriador as [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) was never rebuilt. Elrond became Gil-galad's vice-regent and received the ring [Vilya](../V/Vilya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>
