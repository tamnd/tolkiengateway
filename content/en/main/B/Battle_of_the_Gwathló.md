The **Battle of the Gwathló** was the final battle of the [War of the Elves and Sauron](/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron), fought between the forces of Sauron and the combined forces of [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir "Tar-Minastir"). It ended in a serious defeat for the Dark Lord forcing him to retreat to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

## Background {#Background}

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1695](/wiki/Second_Age_1695 "Second Age 1695") [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), the [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), requested aid from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) invaded [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador). [Minastir](/wiki/Tar-Minastir "Tar-Minastir"), the [heir](/wiki/King%27s_Heir "King's Heir")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Delay_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Delay-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup> of Queen [Tar-Telperiën](/wiki/Tar-Telperi%C3%ABn), sent a fleet commanded by the admiral [Ciryatur](/wiki/Ciryatur), but it was delayed until [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1700](/wiki/Second_Age_1700 "Second Age 1700"), by which time the beleaguered [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and Númenóreans (that were already in Lindon before the invasion) were barely holding the line of the River [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn). The arrival of the Númenórean fleet immediately changed the balance of forces such that Sauron's host was driven back with heavy casualties.<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Prelude {#Prelude}

After being defeated at Lhûn, Sauron retreated with his host to [Sarn Ford](/wiki/Sarn_Ford), the crossing on the [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin), and suffered another defeat. He further retreated southwards to [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) where more of his forces joined him after coming up from the south-east. Unbeknownst to Sauron, Ciryatur also had more forces at his disposal and sent part of his fleet at the [small Númenórean harbour](/wiki/Lond_Daer "Lond Daer") at the mouth of the [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3).<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Battle {#Battle}

This new contingent fell upon Sauron's rear from the south-west while Gil-galad's Elves and the other Númenóreans attacked from the north. Sauron's army was completely routed and he barely escaped himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Aftermath {#Aftermath}

Sauron and his small remaining forces fled across the [Isen](/wiki/Isen) but were caught in eastern [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon). Only a small bodyguard was left when Sauron escaped to [Dagorlad](/wiki/Dagorlad). Gil-galad moved north to lift the [siege of Imladris](/wiki/First_Siege_of_Imladris "First Siege of Imladris") and [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) was cleared of the enemy. Eriador had peace for many years but the humiliation inflicted on Sauron made him determined to seek vengeance upon Númenor. [Imladris](/wiki/Rivendell "Rivendell") became the new Elvish stronghold in eastern Eriador as [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) was never rebuilt. Elrond became Gil-galad's vice-regent and received the ring [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya).<sup id="cite_ref-Galad_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Galad-2">[2]</a></sup>
