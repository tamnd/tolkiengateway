**BAN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") with two different significations. BAN<sup>[1]</sup> signifies "fair, beautiful", originally having "referred simply to 'beauty' — but with implication that it was due to *lack of fault*, or *blemish*".<sup id="cite_ref-PE149_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE149-1">[1]</a></sup> BAN<sup>[2]</sup> signifies "meet, come up against".<sup id="cite_ref-PE150_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE150-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") experimented with the idea that BAN was related to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=MAN&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="MAN (page does not exist)">MAN</a> ("good").<sup id="cite_ref-PE149_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE149-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives of BAN<sup>[1]</sup> {#Derivatives_of_BAN[1]}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na)*; *[vanya](/wiki/Vanyar#etymology "Vanyar")*; *[linda](/wiki/Linda "Linda")*; *vanima* ("beautiful"); *[úvano, úvanimo](/wiki/%C3%9Avanimo "Úvanimo")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE149_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE149-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *bân*, *bain* ("blessed, fair, favourable, good, without evil/bad element"); *gwain* ("blond")<sup id="cite_ref-PE149_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE149-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives of BAN<sup>[2]</sup> {#Derivatives_of_BAN[2]}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[govannen](/wiki/Govannen "Govannen")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE150_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE150-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root VANA-, yielding [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *vane* ("fair, lovely").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the root BAN-, yielding Quenya *vanya* ("beautiful"), *vanima* ("fair"), *úvanimo* ("monster"), etc.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
