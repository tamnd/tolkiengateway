<i><b>bereth</b></i> is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "queen, spouse" or "supreme, sublime".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

From [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *barathî*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

The early [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) explains it comes from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") [BARATH](/wiki/BARAT "BARAT").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Varda" title="Varda">El<b>bereth</b></a></i> = "Queen of Stars"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Brethil_(Sindarin_word)" title="Brethil (Sindarin word)"><b>breth</b>il</a></i> = "princess"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel" title="Berúthiel"><b>Ber</b>úthiel</a></i>

## Notes {#Notes}

It is unrelated to root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=BER%C3%89TH&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="BERÉTH (page does not exist)">BERÉTH</a> ("beech, birch"). Also, it doesn't contain the feminine ending <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eth (page does not exist)">-eth</a></i>.

Although [Beleth](/wiki/Beleth) is referred to as "Bereth" in *Unfinished Tales*, this was noted to be a mistake.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
