**Barach** was one of the [Folk of Haleth](../F/Folk_of_Haleth.md) who dwelt in the Forest of [Brethil](Brethil.md) during the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). He and his family had the friendship of a [Drúadan](../D/Drúedain.md) named [Aghan](../A/Aghan.md), and at a time when [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) were prowling the woodlands, [Aghan](../A/Aghan.md) watched the homestead of his friend Barach through each night.

The story of [The Faithful Stone](../T/The_Faithful_Stone.md) describes the events that unfolded when [Aghan](../A/Aghan.md) was forced to leave Barach and his family unprotected for a time. In his place, the [Drûg](../D/Drúedain.md) left a carved 'watch-stone', and unknown to Barach he passed some of his own power into it, so that it could truly guard the house.

While [Aghan](../A/Aghan.md) was away, Barach was indeed attacked by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), who attempted to burn down his home. The fire was stamped out by a Drûg - apparently the stone come alive - and the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) were driven away. When [Aghan](../A/Aghan.md) returned the following day, he found the scorched and broken carving, and Barach saw that his friend, too, had been injured - a result of passing part of his power into the watch-stone.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
