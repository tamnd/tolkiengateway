**Barach** was one of the [Folk of Haleth](/wiki/Folk_of_Haleth) who dwelt in the Forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). He and his family had the friendship of a [Drúadan](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain") named [Aghan](/wiki/Aghan), and at a time when [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were prowling the woodlands, [Aghan](/wiki/Aghan) watched the homestead of his friend Barach through each night.

The story of [The Faithful Stone](/wiki/The_Faithful_Stone) describes the events that unfolded when [Aghan](/wiki/Aghan) was forced to leave Barach and his family unprotected for a time. In his place, the [Drûg](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain") left a carved 'watch-stone', and unknown to Barach he passed some of his own power into it, so that it could truly guard the house.

While [Aghan](/wiki/Aghan) was away, Barach was indeed attacked by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), who attempted to burn down his home. The fire was stamped out by a Drûg - apparently the stone come alive - and the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were driven away. When [Aghan](/wiki/Aghan) returned the following day, he found the scorched and broken carving, and Barach saw that his friend, too, had been injured - a result of passing part of his power into the watch-stone.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
