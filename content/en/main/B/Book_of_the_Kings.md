The **Book of the Kings** was a record of the histories of all the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) and their families.

After [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel) was exiled from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), her husband, King [Tarannon Falastur](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur), had her name erased from the Book of the Kings. Nevertheless, she and her cats were still remembered by the [Men](/wiki/Men) of Gondor centuries later.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
