**Brego** was a [horse](../H/Horse.md) of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) that [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) personally rode. Brego was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), where he was portrayed by a horse named <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Uraeus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Uraeus (page does not exist)">Uraeus</a>. [Viggo Mortensen](../V/Viggo_Mortensen.md) purchased the horse after the production of the films.

## History {#History}

During the evacuation of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md), it is proposed by the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) that they leave Brego behind, due to how skittish and uncontrollable he is. However, when [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) offers his aid to the Rohirrim, he is able to calm the horse through some words in the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) language. While he did so, [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) informs him that Brego belonged to her now-deceased cousin [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md). Aragorn advises the grooms to set Brego free, as the horse had "seen enough of war".

Later, when [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) was presumed dead and lost, it is Brego who found Aragorn’s unconscious body,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> taking him to the Hornburg while still somehow finding the time to show him [Saruman's](../S/Saruman.md) fast approaching army of [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) from [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).

After the [Battle of the Hornburg](Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md) at Helm's Deep, Brego bore Aragorn once more as a rider on the road to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md). With Aragorn, they returned to [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).

Later, he accompanied Aragorn to [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md), and even up to the [Dark Door](../D/Dark_Door.md) itself. However, due to the [darkness](../O/Oathbreakers.md) of that [path](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md), Brego choose to flee with the other horses, leaving Aragorn, [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), and [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) to brave the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) themselves. Brego later reunited with Aragorn, who rode him to the [Black Gate](Black_Gate.md) to face [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and his forces. Brego's later fate is untold.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The horse *Brego* was apparently named after the king *[Brego](Brego.md)*.
