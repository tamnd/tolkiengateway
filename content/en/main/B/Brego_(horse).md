**Brego** was a [horse](/wiki/Horse "Horse") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) that [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) personally rode. Brego was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where he was portrayed by a horse named <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Uraeus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Uraeus (page does not exist)">Uraeus</a>. [Viggo Mortensen](/wiki/Viggo_Mortensen) purchased the horse after the production of the films.

## History {#History}

During the evacuation of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), it is proposed by the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) that they leave Brego behind, due to how skittish and uncontrollable he is. However, when [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) offers his aid to the Rohirrim, he is able to calm the horse through some words in the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) language. While he did so, [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) informs him that Brego belonged to her now-deceased cousin [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred). Aragorn advises the grooms to set Brego free, as the horse had "seen enough of war".

Later, when [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was presumed dead and lost, it is Brego who found Aragorn’s unconscious body,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> taking him to the Hornburg while still somehow finding the time to show him [Saruman's](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") fast approaching army of [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) from [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).

After the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg) at Helm's Deep, Brego bore Aragorn once more as a rider on the road to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard). With Aragorn, they returned to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras).

Later, he accompanied Aragorn to [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow), and even up to the [Dark Door](/wiki/Dark_Door) itself. However, due to the [darkness](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") of that [path](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead "Paths of the Dead"), Brego choose to flee with the other horses, leaving Aragorn, [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) to brave the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) themselves. Brego later reunited with Aragorn, who rode him to the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate) to face [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and his forces. Brego's later fate is untold.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The horse *Brego* was apparently named after the king *[Brego](/wiki/Brego)*.
