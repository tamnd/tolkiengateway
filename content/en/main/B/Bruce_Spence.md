New Zealand actor **Bruce Spence** (born 1945) played [the Mouth of Sauron](/wiki/The_Mouth_of_Sauron "The Mouth of Sauron") in [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King). To enhance the sinister aspect of the character, Spence's real mouth was digitally enlarged.
