**Brandyfoot** was a family of [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) that travelled in a [wandering](/wiki/Wandering_Days "Wandering Days") caravan that was led by [Sadoc Burrows](/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows) sometime during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

The family and its members were invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
