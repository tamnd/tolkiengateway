**Brandyfoot** was a family of [Harfoots](../H/Harfoots.md) that travelled in a [wandering](../W/Wandering_Days.md) caravan that was led by [Sadoc Burrows](../S/Sadoc_Burrows.md) sometime during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

The family and its members were invented by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
