**Dagorlad**, the Battle Plain, was named so for the number of battles took place on or near there:

- **The [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad) ([S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434")), the first documented battle in the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance).**
- An unnamed [battle](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_Dagorlad "Second Battle of Dagorlad") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1899](/wiki/Third_Age_1899 "Third Age 1899")) between [Calimehtar](</wiki/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calimehtar (King of Gondor)") and the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders).
- The [Disaster of the Morannon](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Morannon) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944")), between [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders).
- The [Battle of the Camp](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Camp) (also [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944")), between [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders).
- The [Battle of the Morannon](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon) ([March 25](/wiki/25_March "25 March"), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")), between the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Host_of_the_West" title="Host of the West">Host of the West</a> and the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).
