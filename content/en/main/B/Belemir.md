**Belemir** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [339](/wiki/First_Age_339 "First Age 339")) was a [man](/wiki/Men "Men") of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Belemir was the son of [Beldir](/wiki/Beldir) and husband to [Adanel](/wiki/Adanel) of the [House of Marach](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador"). He fathered [Beren](</wiki/Beren_(son_of_Belemir)> "Beren (son of Belemir)"), grandfather of [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren"), hero of the First Age.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

There was great love between he and his younger kinswoman [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth), and she dwelt long in his house. While there, Andreth learned much of the lore of the folk of [Marach](/wiki/Marach) from Adanel.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [409](/wiki/First_Age_409 "First Age 409"), [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) was a guest in his house and [held a long debate](/wiki/Athrabeth_Finrod_ah_Andreth "Athrabeth Finrod ah Andreth") with Andreth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

There were two versions of the genealogical table for the House of Hador. In the first version of the table there was a note that Beren, son of Belemir and Adanel, was their fifth child. In the second version there was no reference to these other children. The section of the second version shown above shows only the first Beren although the other children may have existed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In a struck out note, it was stated of Adanel that she "wedded Belemir of the House of Bëor, and he joined the people of Aradan".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
