**Betsy Butterbur** was a [woman](/wiki/Bree-landers "Bree-landers") of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) who worked in the [Prancing Pony Inn](/wiki/Prancing_Pony_Inn). Betsy Butterbur was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), where she was portrayed by [Katie Jackson](/wiki/Katie_Jackson).

## History {#History}

Betsy Butterbur was a relative of "[Butterbur Snr](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur "Barliman Butterbur")", and worked as a barmaid at the Prancing Pony Inn. She waited on both [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) when the two met in the inn.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
