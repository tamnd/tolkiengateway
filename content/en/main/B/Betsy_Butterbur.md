**Betsy Butterbur** was a [woman](Bree-landers.md) of [Bree](Bree.md) who worked in the [Prancing Pony Inn](../P/Prancing_Pony_Inn.md). Betsy Butterbur was invented by [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md) for [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>), where she was portrayed by [Katie Jackson](../K/Katie_Jackson.md).

## History {#History}

Betsy Butterbur was a relative of "[Butterbur Snr](Barliman_Butterbur.md)", and worked as a barmaid at the Prancing Pony Inn. She waited on both [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) when the two met in the inn.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
