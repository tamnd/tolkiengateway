**Black Speech** was a Tolkien-related atmospheric doom/black metal band from Adelaide, Australia. The band took its name from the [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech), the language devised by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) for the inhabitants of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor). It released a single EP, *Black Speech*, in 2014 before disbanding.

## Members {#Members}

- B - bass
- G - drums
- P - guitars
- K - piano
