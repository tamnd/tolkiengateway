**Black Speech** was a Tolkien-related atmospheric doom/black metal band from Adelaide, Australia. The band took its name from the [Black Speech](Black_Speech.md), the language devised by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) for the inhabitants of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md). It released a single EP, *Black Speech*, in 2014 before disbanding.

## Members {#Members}

- B - bass
- G - drums
- P - guitars
- K - piano
