## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Borlas* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and means perhaps "enduring joy", from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=BOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="BOR (page does not exist)">Bór</a></i> + *[glass](/wiki/GAL%C3%81S "GALÁS")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), *Borlas* was the name (later changed to *[Borlad](/wiki/Borlad)*) of a son of [Bór](/wiki/B%C3%B3r).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Since the events of *The New Shadow* is described taking place during the reign of Eldarion and *after the death of [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn")*, this dating contradicts to the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, where in [Appendix B](/wiki/Appendix_B) the date of Aragorn's death was ultimately changed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) from [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [100](/wiki/Fourth_Age_100 "Fourth Age 100") to [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120").
