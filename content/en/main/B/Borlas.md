## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Borlas* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and means perhaps "enduring joy", from <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=BOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="BOR (page does not exist)">Bór</a></i> + *[glass](../G/GALÁS.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), *Borlas* was the name (later changed to *[Borlad](Borlad.md)*) of a son of [Bór](Bór.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Since the events of *The New Shadow* is described taking place during the reign of Eldarion and *after the death of [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md)*, this dating contradicts to the second edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, where in [Appendix B](../A/Appendix_B.md) the date of Aragorn's death was ultimately changed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) from [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [100](../F/Fourth_Age_100.md) to [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md).
