**Badger-brock** was an individual of the [Badger-folk](/wiki/Badger-folk), mentioned only in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") verse. He had a "*snowy forehead*" and "*dark blinking eyes*", and lived with his wife and many sons in a secret house near the [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle). On one occasion, Badger-brock caught and pulled down [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) to the burrows of the Badger-folk under earth. But Tom displayed his power using chant, which caused all the Badger-folk to become frightened, and they showed him out again.<sup id="cite_ref-AB_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AB-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Brock* is an [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) and [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English) word for [badger](/wiki/Badgers "Badgers").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
