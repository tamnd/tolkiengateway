The **Barrowfield** was a field outside the wall and dike of [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md) where the [Rulers of Rohan](../K/Kings_of_Rohan.md) were buried. By the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) there were two rows of tombs: one for the First Line ([Eorl](../E/Eorl.md) to [Helm Hammerhand](../H/Helm.md)) and one for the Second Line ([Fréaláf](../F/Fréaláf.md) to [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md)). The main road leading to the city passed between these two rows.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Simbelmynë](../S/Simbelmynë.md) grew on the western side of the mounds, and more thickly on Helm's.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is not known if the Kings of the Third Line (starting with [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md)) were still buried there during the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "a field containing a grave-mound".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
