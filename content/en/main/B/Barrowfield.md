The **Barrowfield** was a field outside the wall and dike of [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras) where the [Rulers of Rohan](/wiki/Kings_of_Rohan "Kings of Rohan") were buried. By the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) there were two rows of tombs: one for the First Line ([Eorl](/wiki/Eorl) to [Helm Hammerhand](/wiki/Helm "Helm")) and one for the Second Line ([Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f) to [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden)). The main road leading to the city passed between these two rows.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Simbelmynë](/wiki/Simbelmyn%C3%AB) grew on the western side of the mounds, and more thickly on Helm's.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is not known if the Kings of the Third Line (starting with [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)) were still buried there during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "a field containing a grave-mound".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
