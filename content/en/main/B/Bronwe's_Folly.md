**Bronwe's Folly** is an [Arnorian](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") ruin in [Archet](/wiki/Archet)-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was originally a fortress of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) but in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") it became infested by wolves.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the Bree-land Introduction [Celandine Brandybuck](/wiki/Celandine_Brandybuck) asks the player to pick some *[athelas](/wiki/Athelas "Athelas")* flowers at the ruins to heal [Amdir](</wiki/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar)> "Amdir (Shadows of Angmar)")'s [Morgul](/wiki/Morgul-knife "Morgul-knife")-wound.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
