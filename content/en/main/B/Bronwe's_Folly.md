**Bronwe's Folly** is an [Arnorian](../A/Arnor.md) ruin in [Archet](../A/Archet.md)-Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was originally a fortress of [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) but in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) it became infested by wolves.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the Bree-land Introduction [Celandine Brandybuck](../C/Celandine_Brandybuck.md) asks the player to pick some *[athelas](../A/Athelas.md)* flowers at the ruins to heal [Amdir](<../A/Amdir_(Shadows_of_Angmar).md>)'s [Morgul](../M/Morgul-knife.md)-wound.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
