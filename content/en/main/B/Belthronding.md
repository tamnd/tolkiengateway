**Belthronding** was a mighty [bow](Bows.md), made of black yew-wood, that was carried by the most mighty archer of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), [Beleg](Beleg.md) Strongbow of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md). To it he owed his own [epithet](../E/Epessë.md), *Cúthalion* ("Strongbow").

With it, he long defended the borders of King [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md), and when he left [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) in search of his friend [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md), he took it with him. When he found him, together, from the halls of [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) in [Amon Rûdh](../A/Amon_Rûdh.md), they founded the realm of [Dor-Cúarthol](../D/Dor-Cúarthol.md) - the Land of Bow and Helm - with him being the 'Bow'.

When the pair were betrayed to the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md), Beleg went to free his friend, but in a tragic turn of fate Túrin mistook him for a foe, and slew him. Overcome with grief, Túrin dug a grave for Beleg's body, and he buried Belthronding beside its late owner.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Belthronding* is [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md), and it is never glossed, being a compound of *bel* ("strength") + *thrôn* ("stiff, hard") and *ding* (an onomatopoeic sound).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
