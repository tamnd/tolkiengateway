**Belthronding** was a mighty [bow](/wiki/Bows "Bows"), made of black yew-wood, that was carried by the most mighty archer of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) Strongbow of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath). To it he owed his own [epithet](/wiki/Epess%C3%AB "Epessë"), *Cúthalion* ("Strongbow").

With it, he long defended the borders of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol), and when he left [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) in search of his friend [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), he took it with him. When he found him, together, from the halls of [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) in [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh), they founded the realm of [Dor-Cúarthol](/wiki/Dor-C%C3%BAarthol) - the Land of Bow and Helm - with him being the 'Bow'.

When the pair were betrayed to the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), Beleg went to free his friend, but in a tragic turn of fate Túrin mistook him for a foe, and slew him. Overcome with grief, Túrin dug a grave for Beleg's body, and he buried Belthronding beside its late owner.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Belthronding* is [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin), and it is never glossed, being a compound of *bel* ("strength") + *thrôn* ("stiff, hard") and *ding* (an onomatopoeic sound).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
