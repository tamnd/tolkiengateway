**Bear McCreary** (born [1979](/wiki/1979)) is an American musician and composer. He composed the soundtrack for the television series [The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> He writes extensively on his blog about the process of scoring the series.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

<br>
