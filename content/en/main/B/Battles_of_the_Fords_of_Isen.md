The **Battles of the Fords of the Isen** were several conflicts that occurred on the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md), the main entry to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) from the west.

- There were two battles between [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) and Rohan during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md):
    - The **[First Battle of the Fords of Isen](../F/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md)** was on [25 February](../_/25_February.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md). Its main objective seems to have been to kill Prince [Théodred](../T/Théodred.md) (in which Saruman succeeded).
    - The **[Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](../S/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen.md)** was on [2 March](../_/2_March.md) where Saruman finally won passage over the river, beating a heavily outnumbered army of [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) under [Grimbold](../G/Grimbold.md) and [Elfhelm](../E/Elfhelm.md).
- A battle also happened in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2758](../T/Third_Age_2758.md) between [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md) under [Wulf](../W/Wulf.md) and Rohirrim under [Helm](../H/Helm.md). Wulf was successful in invading Rohan.
