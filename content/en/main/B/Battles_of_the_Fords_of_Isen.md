The **Battles of the Fords of the Isen** were several conflicts that occurred on the [Fords of Isen](/wiki/Fords_of_Isen), the main entry to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) from the west.

- There were two battles between [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and Rohan during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring):
    - The **[First Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/First_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen)** was on [25 February](/wiki/25_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"). Its main objective seems to have been to kill Prince [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred) (in which Saruman succeeded).
    - The **[Second Battle of the Fords of Isen](/wiki/Second_Battle_of_the_Fords_of_Isen)** was on [2 March](/wiki/2_March) where Saruman finally won passage over the river, beating a heavily outnumbered army of [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) under [Grimbold](/wiki/Grimbold) and [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm).
- A battle also happened in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2758](/wiki/Third_Age_2758 "Third Age 2758") between [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings) under [Wulf](/wiki/Wulf) and Rohirrim under [Helm](/wiki/Helm). Wulf was successful in invading Rohan.
