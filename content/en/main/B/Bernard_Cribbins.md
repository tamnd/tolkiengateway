**Bernard Cribbins** was a well-known British comedy actor, of "<a class="external text" href="http://www.carryonline.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Carry On</a>" fame. His most recent well-known role was playing the recurring character *Wilfred Mott* in the popular science-fiction show *Doctor Who*. He played the part of [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo.md) of [Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)'s telling of *The Hobbit* in [1979](../_/1979.md).
