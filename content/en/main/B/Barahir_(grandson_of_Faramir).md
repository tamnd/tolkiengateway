**Barahir** was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorian) of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

## History {#History}

Barahir was the grandson of the [Steward](/wiki/Steward) [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), [Prince of Ithilien](/wiki/Prince_of_Ithilien), and Lady [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-2">[1]</a></sup> He was likely named after [a famed hero](/wiki/Barahir "Barahir") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the father of [Beren](/wiki/Beren), and/or [one of his ancestors](</wiki/Barahir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Barahir (Steward of Gondor)") who had served as [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

Barahir flourished during the second century of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) and after the death of [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") [Aragorn II Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn_II_Elessar) he authored *[The Tale of Aragorn and Arwen](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Aragorn_and_Arwen)*.<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-2">[1]</a></sup>

His work survived in abbreviated form in the [Thain's Book](/wiki/Thain%27s_Book).<sup id="cite_ref-prologue_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prologue-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Barahir* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word and consists of *[bara](/wiki/Bara "Bara")* ("fiery; eager") + *[hîr](/wiki/H%C3%AEr "Hîr")* ("master, lord").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>
