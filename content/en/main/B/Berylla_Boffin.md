**Berylla Baggins, née Boffin**, was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Berylla married [Balbo Baggins](Balbo_Baggins.md). Their children were [Mungo](../M/Mungo_Baggins.md), [Pansy](../P/Pansy_Baggins.md), [Ponto](../P/Ponto_Baggins_I.md), [Largo](../L/Largo_Baggins.md), and [Lily](../L/Lily_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Berylla's brother was [Basso](Basso_Boffin.md), who is said to have "gone to [sea](Belegaer.md)" in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1195](../T/Third_Age_2795.md) at the age of 26.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Berylla is named after <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/beryl" title="wikipedia:beryl">beryl</a>, a type of mineral which includes emerald and aquamarine.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
