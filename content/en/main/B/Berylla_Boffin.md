**Berylla Baggins, née Boffin**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Berylla married [Balbo Baggins](/wiki/Balbo_Baggins). Their children were [Mungo](/wiki/Mungo_Baggins "Mungo Baggins"), [Pansy](/wiki/Pansy_Baggins "Pansy Baggins"), [Ponto](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I"), [Largo](/wiki/Largo_Baggins "Largo Baggins"), and [Lily](/wiki/Lily_Baggins "Lily Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Berylla's brother was [Basso](/wiki/Basso_Boffin "Basso Boffin"), who is said to have "gone to [sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer")" in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1195](/wiki/Third_Age_2795 "Third Age 2795") at the age of 26.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Berylla is named after <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/beryl" title="wikipedia:beryl">beryl</a>, a type of mineral which includes emerald and aquamarine.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
