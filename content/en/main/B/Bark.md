<i><b>Bark</b></i>, pl. <i><b>baruk</b></i>, is a [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) word which means "axe".

It is another example of "broken plurals" in Khuzdul, similar to "khuzd" and "khazâd".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](/wiki/Root) \*B-R-K

It is possible that *baruk* is also a genitive case, meaning "axes of &lt;something&gt;" rather than just "axes". The phrase *baruk Khazâd* can be compared to a "construct pair" in Semitic languages, such as [Hebrew](/wiki/Hebrew) and Arabic.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
