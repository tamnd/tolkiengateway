**Banks** was a common [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") family name, found in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and [Bree](/wiki/Bree). Known members of the family are [Eglantine Banks](/wiki/Eglantine_Banks), the mother of [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Willie Banks](/wiki/Willie_Banks), a hobbit from Bree, who was killed in the battles against [the strangers](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") who attacked Bree early in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is not known if the members of these families between the two regions are related.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is topographical in the sense "steep slope or hill-side".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
