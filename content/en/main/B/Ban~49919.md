**Ban** was an [Easterling](</wiki/Easterlings_(First_Age)> "Easterlings (First Age)"), father of [Blodren](/wiki/Blodren), a member of the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith).<sup id="cite_ref-Ban_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ban-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Ban is mentioned in the earlier stages of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), where he was an [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

His name in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) was *Bana*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
