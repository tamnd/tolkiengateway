**Belba Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Belba was the eldest daughter, and second-oldest child, of [Mungo Baggins](/wiki/Mungo_Baggins) and [Laura Grubb](/wiki/Laura_Grubb). This made her the aunt of [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins"). She married [Rudigar Bolger](/wiki/Rudigar_Bolger), and they had one child together, [Herugar](/wiki/Herugar_Bolger "Herugar Bolger"), the grandfather of Fredegar.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
