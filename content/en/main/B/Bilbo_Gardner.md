**Bilbo Gardner** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Bilbo was the tenth child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton). He was named after [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> his father's old hero.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bilbo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Bilba*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
