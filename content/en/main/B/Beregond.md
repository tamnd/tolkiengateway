**Beregond**, son of [Baranor](<Baranor_(Man_of_Gondor)~29833.md>), was a soldier of Gondor and later Captain of the [White Company](../W/White_Company.md).

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Beregond's fathers were from the vales of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md), and prior to that they lived in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> Beregond had two sons, [Bergil](Bergil.md), who was ten years of age at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md),<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> and a younger son named [Borlas](Borlas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-New_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-New-2">[2]</a></sup>

Beregond was a member of the [Third Company](../T/Third_Company_of_the_Citadel.md) of the [Citadel](../G/Guard_of_the_Citadel.md). He admired Faramir and saw him as a brave warrior and a great leader. Beregond did not travel outside of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) often but understood the threat that [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) posed to the city.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

On the morning of [March 9th](../_/9_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), Beregond was assigned to help [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) who had just become a Guard of the Citadel. As Beregond had never met a [hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) before, he was eager to meet Pippin and he was astounded that Pippin looked much like a child yet had faced more dangers than most Men of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

#### Battle of Pelennor Fields {#Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Maureval_-_The_judgement_on_Beregond.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/Maureval_-_The_judgement_on_Beregond.jpg/300px-Maureval_-_The_judgement_on_Beregond.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="360" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/d/d9/Maureval_-_The_judgement_on_Beregond.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="400" data-file-height="480"/></a><figcaption>Maureval - <i>The judgement on Beregond</i></figcaption></figure>

During the [Battle of Pelennor Fields](Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md), Beregond was on watch as the sentinel at the gate of the Citadel. Pippin rushed to tell him that Denethor was attempting to burn Faramir alive. Beregond knew his duty of protecting Faramir was more important than to remain at his post so he rushed to his Captain at the [Silent Street](../S/Silent_Street.md). The guard to the street would not let him pass and Beregond was forced to kill him so he could enter. He regretted his decision but he was forced to act quickly. Two more servants of Denethor's guard were killed as they attempted to light the funeral pyre. He stood upon the steps with his sword to ward off the rest until [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) arrived. After Faramir was rescued, Denethor would still not give up, and drew a knife to attempt to kill his son, but Beregond stepped forward to block Denethor. Denethor then gave up and set himself on fire and died.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-3">[3]</a></sup>

After helping to carry Faramir to the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md), Gandalf gave the key to the [Closed Door](../F/Fen_Hollen.md) to the injured Faramir. Beregond stood guard over Faramir at the Houses of Healing and was present when he awoke.<sup id="cite_ref-houses_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-houses-4">[4]</a></sup>

#### Battle of the Morannon {#Battle_of_the_Morannon}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/78/Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg/250px-Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="152" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/78/Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg/375px-Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/7/78/Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg/500px-Mithril_Miniatures_-_Beregond_fighting_Troll_at_the_Black_Gate.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="777"/></a><figcaption><i>Beregond fighting Troll at the Black Gate</i> - <a href="../M/Mithril_Miniatures.md" title="Mithril Miniatures">Mithril Miniature</a></figcaption></figure>

Pippin and Beregond served under the command of [Imrahil](../I/Imrahil.md) at the [Battle of the Morannon](Battle_of_the_Morannon.md). When a Troll-chief charged their ranks, Beregond was stunned and nearly killed, but Pippin managed to save his friend by killing the Troll, which fell on top of him.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

Upon [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)'s coronation on [May 1st](../_/1_May.md), Beregond was brought forward to receive judgement for leaving his post and for killing Denethor's guards on hallowed ground. The penalty would have been death, but the King showed mercy as the deeds were done out of love for Faramir, and Beregond was merely dismissed from the Guard of the Citadel. For his bravery in battle, Aragorn appointed him the Captain of the Guard of Faramir, which was also called the White Company. He later went to live in [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md) in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) to serve his lord, Faramir, the new [Prince of Ithilien](../P/Prince_of_Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Beregond* may stem from *[beren](Beren.md#Etymology)* meaning ("bold") and *[gond](../G/Gond.md)* ("stone").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> He was likely named after [the steward bearing the same name](<Beregond_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>).

## Genealogy {#Genealogy}

Beregond was descended from people of the vales of the White Mountains and Ithilien. What exactly his relation to [Iorlas](../I/Iorlas.md), described only as an uncle of Bergil,<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> was, is unknown.

<b>1956: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1955 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../D/Derek_Prentice.md" title="Derek Prentice">Derek Prentice</a> provided the voice of Beregond in episode 4 of the second half of the series, &#34;The Siege of Gondor&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-RT1726_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RT1726-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1979: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Beregond - pronounced Buh-ray-gond - appears, though no actor is specified for the part.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="../C/Christopher_Scott.md" title="Christopher Scott">Christopher Scott</a> played the part of Beregond. His scenes from the book are entirely cut and he only has a short dialogue with Denethor to inform him of the state of the battle, taking over the part of an unarmed messenger from the book.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="../D/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series).md" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Beregond is played by Klaus Spürkel.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">Peter Jackson&#39;s The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>According to actor <a href="../I/Ian_Hughes.md" title="Ian Hughes">Ian Hughes</a>, he was cast as Beregond, but the character&#39;s name was changed to <a href="../I/Irolas.md" title="Irolas">Irolas</a> post-production.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../P/Pán_prsteňov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series).md" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Beregond is provided by Marek Majeský.</dd></dl>

**2012: [Guardians of Middle-earth](../G/Guardians_of_Middle-earth.md):**

<dl><dd>Beregond is a playable warrior.</dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Beregond is first met in the seventh circle of <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> shortly before the battle. The player assists Beregond and his brother with their duties around Merethrond and the guest houses. For his actions in killing two guards while saving <a href="../F/Faramir.md" title="Faramir">Faramir</a>&#39;s life, <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">King Elessar</a> banishes Beregond from residing in <a href="../M/Minas_Tirith.md" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, although he and his wife Gilithel are still permitted to attend Aragorn and <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>&#39;s wedding. After King Elessar charges Steward <a href="../F/Faramir.md" title="Faramir">Faramir</a> with cleansing the evil of <a href="../M/Minas_Morgul.md" title="Minas Morgul">Minas Morgul</a> and the <a href="../M/Morgul_Vale.md" title="Morgul Vale">Morgul Vale</a>, Beregond joins the White Company and can be found in a camp outside the Dead City.</dd></dl>
