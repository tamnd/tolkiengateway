**Baranor** was a man of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

## History {#History}

Baranor came from a line of [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) who then moved to the vales of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md), most likely in [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md).

He had a son, [Beregond](Beregond.md), a hero of the [siege of Minas Tirith](../S/Siege_of_Minas_Tirith.md), and seems to have also dwelt in that city.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Baranor was likely named after [the first name Man of the same name](<Baranor_(son_of_Baran).md>). It could mean various things:

- *[Bara](Bara.md)* + *[naur](../N/Naur.md)*, "Fierce Fire"
- *[Baran](Baran.md)* + *[Or](../O/Or.md)*, "Ruddy mountain"
- *[Bar](Bar.md)* + *[Anor](../S/Sun.md)*, "House of the Sun"
