**BAD** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") with two different etymologies (BAD<sup>[1]</sup> and BAD<sup>[2]</sup>). Both appear without any any signification, besides from what can be determined from their derivatives.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT45_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives of BAD<sup>[1]</sup> {#Derivatives_of_BAD[1]}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *bad-* ("judge")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *bauð*, *bād-* ("judgement"); *badhor*, *baðron* ("judge")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

There were no [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) derivatives of BAD<sup>[1]</sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives of BAD<sup>[2]</sup> {#Derivatives_of_BAD[2]}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *banda* ("prison, Hell")<sup id="cite_ref-VT45_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *vanda* ("prison, Hell")<sup id="cite_ref-VT45_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): [*band*, *bann*](/wiki/Band "Band") ("prison, Hell")<sup id="cite_ref-VT45_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT45-2">[2]</a></sup>
