**Bohle** is a heavy metal/hard rock band from Santiago, Chile. The band was formed in 2010 under the name Carlos Molina and adopted its current name in 2017. Its songs include ones named after [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).

## Members {#Members}

- Paulo Valverde - bass, backing vocals
- Jonathan Senesi - drums, backing vocals
- Carlos Molina - lead vocals, guitars, bass

## Discography {#Discography}

- Sangre fría (Full-length, 2010; as Carlos Molina)
- Abismos del tiempo (Full-length, 2014; as Carlos Molina)
- Fuego y sangre (Full-length, 2017)
- Rockabilly Biking (Single, 2020)
- Kingdom of Rats (Full-length, 2026)
