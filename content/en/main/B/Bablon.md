**Bablon** was a city of [Men](../M/Men.md) in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. It was identified with the city of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon" title="wikipedia:Babylon">Babylon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bab_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bab-1">[1]</a></sup>

The city was mentioned in the tale of *[the Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)* by [Littleheart](../L/Littleheart.md), where he compared its [fall](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) as even being worse than Bablon's.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bablon* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), but its meaning is unclear. However, it was probably a phonological imitation of *Babylon*.<sup id="cite_ref-Bab_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bab-1">[1]</a></sup>
