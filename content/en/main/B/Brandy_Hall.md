**Brandy Hall** was in the center of [Buckland](Buckland.md) on the east bank of the River [Brandywine](Baranduin.md) near the [Bucklebury Ferry](Bucklebury_Ferry.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was a [smial](../S/Smials.md) deeply-delved into [Buck Hill](Buck_Hill.md) and was the ancestral home of the [Brandybuck Family](Brandybuck_Family~66350.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Gorhendad Oldbuck](../G/Gorhendad_Oldbuck.md) began the first excavations of Brandy Hall after [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2340](../T/Third_Age_2340.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Brandybucks kept making additions to Brandy Hall until it occupied the whole of the hill, had three large front doors, many side-doors, and about a hundred windows. When the Brandybucks and numerous dependents outgrew Brandy Hall they burrowed and built all around Buck Hill.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [2980](../T/Third_Age_2980.md) [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)' parents, [Drogo Baggins](../D/Drogo_Baggins.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and [Primula Brandybuck](../P/Primula_Brandybuck.md),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> died in a boating accident. Frodo then lived in Brandy Hall until [2989](../T/Third_Age_2989.md) when his ninety-nine year old cousin, [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md), invited him to come live at [Bag End](Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

By the end of the first century of the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) Brandy Hall contained one of the largest collection of historical books and records, in particular many works dealing with [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and the history of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md). Some of the works composed or begun by [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and kept in the Brandy Hall library were *[Herblore of the Shire](../H/Herblore_of_the_Shire.md)*, the *[Reckoning of Years](../R/Reckoning_of_Years.md)*, and the short treatise *[Old Words and Names in the Shire](../O/Old_Words_and_Names_in_the_Shire.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, the map *[A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md)* showed the Buck Hill to be beyond the main Buckland road (that is: the road ran between Brandywine and the Hill) which was inconsistent to the narrative;<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Tolkien himself had written concerning the map: "it is unfortunate that Brandy Hall clearly on the river-bank is placed so that the main road runs in front of it instead of behind".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The location was corrected on the map of the second edition ([1966](../_/1966.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
