**Brandy Hall** was in the center of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) on the east bank of the River [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") near the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was a [smial](/wiki/Smials "Smials") deeply-delved into [Buck Hill](/wiki/Buck_Hill) and was the ancestral home of the [Brandybuck Family](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Gorhendad Oldbuck](/wiki/Gorhendad_Oldbuck) began the first excavations of Brandy Hall after [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2340](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Brandybucks kept making additions to Brandy Hall until it occupied the whole of the hill, had three large front doors, many side-doors, and about a hundred windows. When the Brandybucks and numerous dependents outgrew Brandy Hall they burrowed and built all around Buck Hill.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [2980](/wiki/Third_Age_2980 "Third Age 2980") [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins)' parents, [Drogo Baggins](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and [Primula Brandybuck](/wiki/Primula_Brandybuck),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> died in a boating accident. Frodo then lived in Brandy Hall until [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989") when his ninety-nine year old cousin, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), invited him to come live at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

By the end of the first century of the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) Brandy Hall contained one of the largest collection of historical books and records, in particular many works dealing with [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and the history of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan). Some of the works composed or begun by [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and kept in the Brandy Hall library were *[Herblore of the Shire](/wiki/Herblore_of_the_Shire)*, the *[Reckoning of Years](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Years)*, and the short treatise *[Old Words and Names in the Shire](/wiki/Old_Words_and_Names_in_the_Shire)*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first edition of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, the map *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)* showed the Buck Hill to be beyond the main Buckland road (that is: the road ran between Brandywine and the Hill) which was inconsistent to the narrative;<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Tolkien himself had written concerning the map: "it is unfortunate that Brandy Hall clearly on the river-bank is placed so that the main road runs in front of it instead of behind".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The location was corrected on the map of the second edition ([1966](/wiki/1966)).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
