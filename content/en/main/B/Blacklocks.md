The **Blacklocks** were one of the seven houses of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) that dwelt in [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn). They were originally paired with the [Stonefoots](/wiki/Stonefoots).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The locations of the four [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") clans, including the Blacklocks, who lived in the [East](/wiki/East) are unknown. The distance between their mansions in the East and the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), specifically [Gundabad](/wiki/Gundabad), was said to be as great or greater than that of Gundabad's distance from the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) in the West.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

It is possible that the Blacklocks were among the "*Houses of other Fathers*" who sent troops to help [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) in the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Late in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), when war and terror grew in the [East](/wiki/East) itself, considerable numbers of Blacklocks and dwarves of the Eastern clans left their ancient homelands. They sought refuge in Middle-earth's western lands, where some of them may have met [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The unnamed Dwarven tribes were given invented names in an early supplement.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> While these were published before the publication of Tolkien&#39;s late note on the name of the Dwarven houses of the East, it has been suggested that the Blacklocks could correspond to <b>Drúin&#39;s Tribe</b> (as portrayed in <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">MERP</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The House corresponding to the Blacklocks is called <b>Var&#39;s Folk</b>, said to wander the lands of the East and South. Their beards are &#34;<i>black as coal and thick as wire, often left tangled and unkempt</i>&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Dwalin" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> mentioned Thorin, the heir of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin</a> having a meeting with his <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">kin</a>, with &#34;envoys from the Seven Kingdoms&#34;, the same number as the Houses of the Dwarves, and also <a href="/wiki/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills" title="Dwarves of the Iron Hills">Dwarves of the Iron Hills</a>. It&#39;s unspecified on why Thorin had a meeting with all houses of the Dwarves, however it&#39;s likely for the purpose of declaring war on <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> and to reclaim his homeland.</dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/7/72/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="169" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/7/72/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/7/72/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Blacklocks.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="1080"/></a><figcaption>The &#34;Temámir&#34; in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i></figcaption></figure>

<dl><dd>The House corresponding to the Blacklocks is called the <b>Temámir</b>. Dark-skinned with raven-black hair, they are considered the wisest of the Seven Houses; their banners display a quill and written scrolls. The Temámir are described as being more prone to voyaging than other dwarves, even over sea. Their <a href="/wiki/Seven_Rings" title="Seven Rings">Ring</a> was either &#34;Taurya&#34;, which was reclaimed by Sauron, or &#34;Tulcya&#34;, which was consumed by the <a href="/wiki/Dragons" title="Dragons">dragon</a> &#34;Thostír the Rank&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Temámir originate from the south, and have strongholds in <a href="/wiki/Near_Harad" title="Near Harad">Near Harad</a> and <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbar</a>. At the beginning of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs" title="War of the Dwarves and Orcs">War of the Dwarves and Orcs</a>, they were led by &#34;King Brítu the Redeful&#34;. By the time of the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar" title="Battle of Azanulbizar">Battle of Azanulbizar</a>, all that remained of the royal line was young princess &#34;Jegín&#34;, who became King and ruled through the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> and its aftermath. After Azanulbizar, the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Longbeards" title="Longbeards">Longbeards</a> gave to the Temámir some of their halls in the <a href="/wiki/White_Mountains" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> that they could no longer maintain. During the War of the Ring, these dwarves shut their gates and did not participate. Once the danger had passed, they emerged to take part in rebuilding <a href="/wiki/Gondor" title="Gondor">Gondor</a>, for a price.</dd></dl>
