The **Braids** was a wide floodplain in the [Thistledell](../T/Thistledell.md) that merged two rivers flowing out of the [Rhovanion Hills](../R/Rhovanion_Hills.md) into a single river flowing eastwards into [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) and southwards towards the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md). The Braids was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the Braids was a floodplain that a certain [Harfoot community](../H/Harfoots.md) passed through when migrating from one seasonal haven to the next, particularly from the [Norfield Glen](../N/Norfield_Glen.md) to the [Grove](../G/Grove.md).

During one such migration, the Brandyfoot family, [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md), and [The Stranger](../T/The_Stranger.md) passed through the Braids while following in their haste after the main caravan of the Harfoot community which was far ahead of them.

Later, a group of three [Mystics](../M/Mystics.md) possibly passed through the Braids when they were stalking the Harfoot migration trail, seeking to find the Stranger.
