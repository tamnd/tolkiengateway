**Bored of the Rings** is a satirical book by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Harvard_Lampoon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harvard Lampoon (page does not exist)">Harvard Lampoon</a>. Since its publication, the title has been used for several other satires, mostly unrelated to the book.

- *[Bored of the Rings (book)](</wiki/Bored_of_the_Rings_(book)>)*, the original [1969](/wiki/1969) book.
- *[Bored of the Rings (video game)](</wiki/Bored_of_the_Rings_(video_game)>)*, a [1985](/wiki/1985) parody adventure game.
- *[Bored of the Rings (comic)](</wiki/Bored_of_the_Rings_(comic)>)*, a three-issue storyline of MAD magazine, which satirizes *The Lord of the Rings*.
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bored_of_the_Rings:_The_Trilogy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bored of the Rings: The Trilogy (page does not exist)">Bored of the Rings: The Trilogy</a></i>, a [2005](/wiki/2005) spoof film.
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bored_of_the_Rings_(theatre)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bored of the Rings (theatre) (page does not exist)">Bored of the Rings (theatre)</a></i>, a [2006](/wiki/2006) cabaret show.
- *Bored of the Rings*, [an episode of *The Sarah Silverman Program*](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_works_in_popular_media#The_Sarah_Silverman_Program "Tolkien's works in popular media")*.*
