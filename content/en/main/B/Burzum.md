<i><b>burzum</b></i> is a word in [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech) which translates to "darkness". It is found within the [Ring-inscription](/wiki/Ring-inscription). It is formed from the stem *[búrz](/wiki/B%C3%BArz "Búrz")* ("dark") and the particularizing suffix or 'article' *um*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><span></span>
