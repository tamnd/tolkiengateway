**Bob** was a [Bree-lander](/wiki/Bree-landers "Bree-landers") (either a [Man](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") or a [Hobbit](/wiki/Bree-hobbits "Bree-hobbits"))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who worked in [The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony).

## History {#History}

Bob worked alongside [Nob](/wiki/Nob) for [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur), primarily as an ostler in [The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony) at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Bob was the one who located the last [pony](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony "Bill the Pony") left in Bree when all others has bolted following the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl)'s attack. Later when Merry's [ponies](/wiki/Ponies "Ponies") found their way back to Bree (thanks to [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil)) it was Bob who cared for them; he put them to work but treated them well.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) entered Bree, Bob stopped working evenings, so that he could be home before nightfall.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
