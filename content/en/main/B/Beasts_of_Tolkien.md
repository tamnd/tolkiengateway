<i><b>Beasts of Tolkien: A Colouring Book</b></i> is an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coloring_book#Adult_coloring_books" title="wikipedia:Coloring book">adult colouring book</a>, illustrated by Mauro Mazzara and Andrea Piparo. The illustrations are based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings.<sup id="cite_ref-AMUK_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMUK-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This intricately drawn adult colouring book dives into the depths of J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s imagination, exploring the creatures that fly, crawl and stalk their way through his magnificent works. Bestiary of Tolkien features <a href="../H/Horses.md" title="Horses">horses</a> of <a href="../A/Arda.md" title="Arda">Arda</a>, winged <a href="../F/Fire-drakes.md" title="Fire-drakes">Fire-drakes</a> including <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> the Golden, and of course the evil <a href="../G/Gollum.md" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> who finally meets his fate at <a href="../M/Mount_Doom.md" title="Mount Doom">Mount Doom</a> after the deadly quest of <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>. Put your own imagination to good use and step away from the day-today with this enchanting colouring book which brings Tolkien&#39;s beasts to life. This book is unofficial and not authorised by the <a href="../T/Tolkien_Estate.md" title="Tolkien Estate">Tolkien Estate</a> or <a href="../H/HarperCollins.md" title="HarperCollins">HarperCollins</a> Publishers.<sup id="cite_ref-AMUK_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AMUK-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
