The **Bay of Eldamar** was a wide bay in the coasts of [Aman](/wiki/Aman) on the western shores of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer"). It was here that the island of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) would finally come to be anchored.

## History {#History}

For many years the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) dwelled on Tol Eressëa. Eventually, [Ossë](/wiki/Oss%C3%AB) guided them across the windless surface of the Bay to the shores of Aman, where they would come to dwell in the haven of [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Bay of Eldamar was visited long afterwards by the mortal [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) the Mariner during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). He anchored his ship [Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot) in the Bay while he entered the Blessed Realm on his embassy to the [Powers](/wiki/Valar "Valar") who dwelled there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Bay of Eldamar is referenced in Tolkien's *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. In the story, written down in [1927](/wiki/1927), the great whale [Uin](/wiki/Uin) takes the enchanted dog [Roverandom](/wiki/Rover "Rover") (formerly known as Rover) on adventures through the seas. Uin takes Roverandom through the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas) to the "Bay of Fairyland" beyond the [Magic Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles "Enchanted Isles") where Rover saw the [Mountains of Elvenhome](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri "Pelóri") and the light of Faery. Roverandom thought he could see the white glint of [a city of Elves on a green hill far away in the distance](/wiki/Tirion "Tirion").<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-3">[3]</a></sup>
