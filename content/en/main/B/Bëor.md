**Bëor the Old**, originally named **Balan**,<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup> was the leader of the first [Men](/wiki/Men) to cross the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) into [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the early [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).

## History {#History}

The man once born as Balan (perhaps in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador)) was one of the people who sought to reach the [Gods](/wiki/Valar "Valar") in the [West](/wiki/Uttermost_West "Uttermost West"). With his sons [Belen](/wiki/Belen) and [Baran](/wiki/Baran) and his people, they met [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) and [Avari](/wiki/Avari) before eventually crossing the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [310](/wiki/First_Age_310 "First Age 310").

One night as they slept in [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), the Noldorin King [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"), eldest son of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin), came upon Balan and his people and enchanted them with his music as they woke up. Finrod quickly befriended the Men and advised them to settle in [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad) in the realm of the Noldorin Elf-lords [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amras](/wiki/Amras), the sons of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor).<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [311](/wiki/First_Age_311 "First Age 311"), Balan left with Finrod for his kingdom of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), where he served as his vassal until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:218, 227</sup> Bëor's eldest son, [Baran](/wiki/Baran), ruled the [people of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor") in his stead. The friendship of Bëor and Baran with the Eldar led to the creation of the [First House](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor "House of Bëor") of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain), which produced some of the greatest heroes of the First Age.

Balan was called *Bëor* by the [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond), which meant "vassal" in [Taliska](/wiki/Taliska), the language of the people of Bëor. When Bëor finally died at the age of 93 willingly, a very old age for men at that time, he was given the epithet *the Old*.<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/ba/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg/110px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="110" height="110" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/ba/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg/165px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/ba/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg/220px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_B%C3%ABor_heraldic_device.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1380" data-file-height="1380"/></a><figcaption>Bëor&#39;s heraldic device by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a></figcaption></figure>

In the introduction to *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) stated that they were written in the 1930s.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This work was laid aside while J.R.R. Tolkien wrote *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, after which he returned to his work on the elder days. In Tolkien's later writings the coming of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) over the Blue Mountains was relocated nearly a century earlier, which occasioned a large-scale overhaul of the chronology and genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:229</sup>

In the older *Grey Annals*, Bëor was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") 370,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> met Felagund in [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and died in [450](/wiki/First_Age_450 "First Age 450").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In the new version Bëor was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [262](/wiki/First_Age_262 "First Age 262"), encountered Felagund in [310](/wiki/First_Age_310 "First Age 310"), and died at age 93 in [355](/wiki/First_Age_355 "First Age 355").<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:229</sup>
