**Bëor the Old**, originally named **Balan**,<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup> was the leader of the first [Men](../M/Men.md) to cross the [Blue Mountains](Blue_Mountains.md) into [Beleriand](Beleriand.md) in the early [First Age](../F/First_Age.md).

## History {#History}

The man once born as Balan (perhaps in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md)) was one of the people who sought to reach the [Gods](../V/Valar.md) in the [West](../U/Uttermost_West.md). With his sons [Belen](Belen.md) and [Baran](Baran.md) and his people, they met [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and [Avari](../A/Avari.md) before eventually crossing the [Blue Mountains](Blue_Mountains.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [310](../F/First_Age_310.md).

One night as they slept in [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md), the Noldorin King [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md), eldest son of [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md), came upon Balan and his people and enchanted them with his music as they woke up. Finrod quickly befriended the Men and advised them to settle in [Estolad](../E/Estolad.md) in the realm of the Noldorin Elf-lords [Amrod](../A/Amrod.md) and [Amras](../A/Amras.md), the sons of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [311](../F/First_Age_311.md), Balan left with Finrod for his kingdom of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md), where he served as his vassal until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:218, 227</sup> Bëor's eldest son, [Baran](Baran.md), ruled the [people of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) in his stead. The friendship of Bëor and Baran with the Eldar led to the creation of the [First House](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md), which produced some of the greatest heroes of the First Age.

Balan was called *Bëor* by the [Elves of Nargothrond](../E/Elves_of_Nargothrond.md), which meant "vassal" in [Taliska](../T/Taliska.md), the language of the people of Bëor. When Bëor finally died at the age of 93 willingly, a very old age for men at that time, he was given the epithet *the Old*.<sup id="cite_ref-West_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-West-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the introduction to *[The Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)*, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) stated that they were written in the 1930s.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This work was laid aside while J.R.R. Tolkien wrote *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, after which he returned to his work on the elder days. In Tolkien's later writings the coming of the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) over the Blue Mountains was relocated nearly a century earlier, which occasioned a large-scale overhaul of the chronology and genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:229</sup>

In the older *Grey Annals*, Bëor was born in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) 370,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> met Felagund in [400](../F/First_Age_400.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and died in [450](../F/First_Age_450.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In the new version Bëor was born in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [262](../F/First_Age_262.md), encountered Felagund in [310](../F/First_Age_310.md), and died at age 93 in [355](../F/First_Age_355.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:229</sup>
