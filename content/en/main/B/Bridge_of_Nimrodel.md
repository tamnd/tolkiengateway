The **Bridge of Nimrodel** once spanned the river [Nimrodel](</wiki/Nimrodel_(river)> "Nimrodel (river)") near its meeting with the [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant). It was part of the path from [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") into [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). By the time the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) came to the river on [15 January](/wiki/15_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the bridge had long broken down.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-2">[2]</a></sup> In the absence of a bridge, [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) led the party down the deep-cloven bank to wade across the soothing waters.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
