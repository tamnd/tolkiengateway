The **Bridge of Nimrodel** once spanned the river [Nimrodel](<../N/Nimrodel_(river).md>) near its meeting with the [Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md). It was part of the path from [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) into [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). By the time the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) came to the river on [15 January](../_/15_January.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the bridge had long broken down.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-2">[2]</a></sup> In the absence of a bridge, [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) led the party down the deep-cloven bank to wade across the soothing waters.<sup id="cite_ref-Lorien_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lorien-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
