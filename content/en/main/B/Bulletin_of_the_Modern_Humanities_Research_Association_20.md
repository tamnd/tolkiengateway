<b><i>Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association</i> 20</b> is an issue (number 20, October [1923](/wiki/1923)) of the *Bulletin of the Modern Humanities Research Association*. It includes an obituary by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) for [Henry Bradley](/wiki/Henry_Bradley), entitled <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Henry_Bradley,_3_Dec.,_1845-23_May,_1923&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Henry Bradley, 3 Dec., 1845-23 May, 1923 (page does not exist)">Henry Bradley, 3 Dec., 1845-23 May, 1923</a></i> (pp. 4–5).

A copy seen was bound with other issues into a single volume. Each number may have been issued in card covers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
