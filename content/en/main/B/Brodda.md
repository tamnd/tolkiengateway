**Brodda** was an [Easterling](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) who was made a lord in [Dor-lómin](../D/Dor-lómin.md) by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) following the [Battle of Unnumbered Tears](Battle_of_Unnumbered_Tears.md). He took possession of [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md)'s lands and was eventually slain by [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).

## History {#History}

During the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), after Morgoth captured the land of [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), Brodda was made a lord. He took to wife (against her will) [Aerin](../A/Aerin.md) of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md), a kinswoman of Húrin, and took Húrin's lands and possessions.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> When in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [495](../F/First_Age_495.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Túrin, son of Húrin, returned to Dor-lómin, he learned from Aerin that his mother, [Morwen](../M/Morwen.md), had left [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md). In his rage he killed Brodda, thereby also sealing Aerin's fate, who burnt herself alive in her [hall](Brodda's_hall.md), and the remainder of the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md) now was persecuted even more cruelly.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Brodda already appears in the earliest version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. The concept of the Easterlings invading [Hisilómë](../H/Hisilómë.md) was not present, but he is the lord of those lands inhabited by [Men](../M/Men.md), as well as a horrible husband for [Airin](../A/Airin.md). When [Mavwin](../M/Mavwin.md) decided to leave Hisilómë and asked to Airin that Brodda took care of her properties, he accepted without problem. But once Mavwin and [Nienóri](../N/Nienóri.md) left, he added all her goods to his own, and did not take care her house or lands, so he was considered a bad lord and thief by his own people. When <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Turambar.md" title="Turambar">Turambar</a> heard about this, became enraged and went to [Brodda's hall](Brodda's_hall.md). Airin was not there, but he was welcomed to the dinner party that was taking place, although he rejected the food they offered to him.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Then Turambar stepping out into the midst of them before the high place where Brodda sat said: &#34;Behold, I am Turambar son of the forest&#34;, and men laughed thereat, but Turambar&#39;s eyes were full of wrath. Then said Brodda in doubt: &#34;What wilt thou of me, O son of the wild forest?&#34; But Turambar said: &#34;Lord Brodda, I am come to repay thy stewardship of others&#39; goods,&#34; and silence fell in that place; but Brodda laughed, saying again: &#34;But who art thou?&#34; And thereupon Turambar leapt upon the high place and ere Brodda might foresee the act he drew <a class="mw-redirect" href="../G/Gurtholfin.md" title="Gurtholfin">Gurtholfin</a> and seizing Brodda by the locks all but smote his head from off his body, crying aloud: &#34;So dieth the rich man who addeth the widow&#39;s little to his much. Lo, men die not all in the wild woods, and am I not in truth the son of <a class="mw-redirect" href="../_/Úrin.md" title="Úrin">Úrin</a>, who having sought back unto his folk findeth an empty hall despoiled.&#34;
</p>
</blockquote>

Seeing Turambar's crime, people in the hall revolted. Some did not appreciated Brodda and did not even unsheath their swords, but others attacked Turambar, of which [Orlin](../O/Orlin.md) was killed. Airin appeared and stopped everyone. She was shocked because of the crime commited, but spoke a favorable judgement against Turambar, recognizing how unfair Brodda had been towards Mavwin. Everyone approved, ignoring how horrible was Airin's life beside that man.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
