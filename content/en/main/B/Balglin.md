**Balglin**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") warrior who fought in [the Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg").

Balglin is an original creation for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* film. He is portrayed by [Andy Gunn](/wiki/Andy_Gunn).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Interview_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Balglin was an [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") archer of [the Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age"). He was a strong warrior who asked for <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/no_quarter" title="wikipedia:no quarter">no quarter</a> and received none in return.

When the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim), under leadership from [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan"), retreated to [the Hornburg](/wiki/Hornburg "Hornburg") in [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), [Saruman the White](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman") sent an army of over ten thousand [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) to assail them. When Lord [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and Lady [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and Lord [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) of Lothlórien heard of this, they decided to honour the ages-old allegiance between [Men](/wiki/Men) and [Elves](/wiki/Elves). They sent a join force of Elves, led by [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir), to assist the Rohirrim in their battle against the Uruks. Balglin was part of this force sent to the Hornburg.

Balglin was stationed on the front line of the Deeping Wall of the Hornburg. When the Uruks began marching toward the fortress, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) yelled "Release arrows!" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and Balglin fired. After the forces of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) breached the Deeping Wall with the [fire of Orthanc](/wiki/Fire_of_Orthanc "Fire of Orthanc"), a group of Uruks marched forward with pikes. Gimli jumped into the group and killed a few Uruk-hai before being knocked to the ground. Again, Aragorn yelled "Hado i philinn!" and the group of archers behind him, including Balglin,<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-4">[4]</a></sup> fired arrows into the Uruks. Aragorn yelled "Charge"! in Sindarin and the Elves ran forward, knocking the pikes out of the way and slaying Uruks.

Ultimately, Haldir and the rest of the Elven reinforcements were slain during the Battle. Their efforts were not in vain, however, as the few Rohirrim left alive were able to hold out until [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrived with [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer)'s [Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored) and forced the Uruks to retreat into the forest, where they were killed by [Huorns](/wiki/Huorns).
