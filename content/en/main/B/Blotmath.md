**Blotmath** (pronounced Blodmath or Blommath) was the eleventh month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), approximately corresponding to modern [22 October](/wiki/22_October) through [20 November](/wiki/20_November). It was called [Blooting](/wiki/Blooting) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree).

Occurring after the harvest, Blotmath was the first month of the Hobbit year in older times. However after the Hobbits adopted the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning), Blotmath became the eleventh month. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernisation](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for November, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/blotmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:blotmonaþ">blotmonaþ</a></i>, meaning "month of sacrifice".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
