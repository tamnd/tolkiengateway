**Blotmath** (pronounced Blodmath or Blommath) was the eleventh month of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), approximately corresponding to modern [22 October](../_/22_October.md) through [20 November](../_/20_November.md). It was called [Blooting](Blooting.md) in [Bree](Bree.md).

Occurring after the harvest, Blotmath was the first month of the Hobbit year in older times. However after the Hobbits adopted the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md), Blotmath became the eleventh month. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernisation](../H/Hobbitish.md) of the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) name for November, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/blotmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:blotmonaþ">blotmonaþ</a></i>, meaning "month of sacrifice".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
