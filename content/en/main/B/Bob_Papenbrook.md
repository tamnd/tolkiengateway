**Robert DeWayne Papenbrook** ([1955](../_/1955.md) - [2006](../_/2006.md)) was an American voice actor. He voiced [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and unnamed [Uruks](../U/Uruk-hai.md) in the video game *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)*.
