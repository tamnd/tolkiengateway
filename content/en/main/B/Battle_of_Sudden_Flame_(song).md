**Battle of Sudden Flame** is the twelfth track of the [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-themed album [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth) by [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian). The song tells of the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), in which [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) breaks the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband) using his [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) and [Dragons](/wiki/Dragons).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
