**Briffo Boffin** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md).

## History {#History}

Briffo was the third child of [Buffo Boffin](Buffo_Boffin.md) and [Ivy Goodenough](../I/Ivy_Goodenough.md). In [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1210](../T/Third_Age_2810.md), he moved to [Bree](Bree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
