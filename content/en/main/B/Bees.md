In [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), **bees** were buzzing domestic insects kept for their ability to make honey. Particularly famous were those of [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), which reached an enormous size.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The fine [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") weather of summer and autumn in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") was such that "honey was dripping in the combs".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), the word for "honey bee" is *nier* (*nies*-), and "a bee" is *nion* or *nier*. The [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) word for "a bee" is *nios* or *nio* (*niwin* is "a female bee", and *niosturwin* means "queen bee").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ground Bees, producers of honey, are relatives of the yellow jackets and true bees. Due to their large numbers, the Ground Bees pose a threat to characters.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="/wiki/Adar_(episode)" title="Adar (episode)">third episode</a> of the first season, it is revealed during <a href="/wiki/Sadoc_Burrows" title="Sadoc Burrows">Sadoc Burrows&#39;s</a> speech of remembrance that one Harfoot, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Blovo_Bolgerbuck&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Blovo Bolgerbuck (page does not exist)">Blovo Bolgerbuck</a>, was lost during a previous migration due to bees. The other Harfoots loved him, but considered him to be an ijit.</dd></dl>
