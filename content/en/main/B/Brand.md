**Brand** was a [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).

## History {#History}

Brand was the son of King [Bain](/wiki/Bain) and grandson of [Bard the Bowman](/wiki/Bard "Bard")<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup> who had killed the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He became the king of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) and by [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") he had extended the realm of Dale far south and east of [Esgaroth](/wiki/Esgaroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond) the dwarf [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in) reported that a year earlier (in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017")) a messenger from [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) had come to speak to [Dáin](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot"). He also said that messengers had spoken to King Brand and that he was afraid, for war appeared to be gathering on his eastern borders.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

King Brand remained steadfast though. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") when a host of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s allies crossed the River [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen) he retreated into [Dale](/wiki/Dale). There the [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) and the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) fought for three days. Brand was slain at the side of [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") during the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale), at the end of which the victorious [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) drove the allies (including his son, Bard) into [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor) where they were besieged.

However, when news of Sauron's defeat came his northern army was dismayed with his son (and Dáin's son, [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin_III "Thorin III")) breaking the siege. King Brand was succeeded by his son, [Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) possibly derived the name *Brand* from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/brand#Old_English" title="wiktionary:brand">brand</a></i> ("fire-brand, torch; sword").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Jim Allan](/wiki/Jim_Allan) suggests that it is derived from the Germanic element *brand*, which means "brand".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Though Brand does not appear in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, <a href="/wiki/Decipher" title="Decipher">Decipher</a> produced two cards depicting him in <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i> as part of the &#34;Book Cards&#34; label, which denotes cards produced by <a href="/wiki/W%C4%93t%C4%81_Workshop" title="Wētā Workshop">Wētā Workshop</a> specifically for the TCG. <a href="/wiki/Ben_Wootten" title="Ben Wootten">Ben Wootten</a> portrayed him<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> in the &#34;Brand - King of Dale&#34; card from the &#34;Expanded Middle-earth&#34; set which depicts him with a bow, quiver and bird<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and in the &#34;Last Stand&#34; card from &#34;Treachery &amp; Deceit&#34; set, depicting him evading <a href="/wiki/Uruk-hai" title="Uruk-hai">Uruk-hai</a> during <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dale" title="Battle of Dale">the Battle of Dale</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2012" title="2012">2012</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Brand makes two minor appearances during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dale" title="Battle of Dale">Battle of Dale</a> and before the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Siege_of_Erebor" title="Siege of Erebor">Siege of Erebor</a> in two adventure &#34;instances&#34; designed for groups of six or twelve players. Unlike in the books, Brand is depicted being slain by arrows in the crossing of the <a href="/wiki/River_Running" title="River Running">River Running</a> while guarding his people&#39;s retreat to the Mountain. This inconsistency is referenced in a later quest in which a notorious dwarf tale-spinner describes events unfolding this way, and the player has the option to agree with the veracity of his tale or to disagree in favour of the canonical version of Brand&#39;s death.</dd></dl>
