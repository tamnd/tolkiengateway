**William Lloyd "Bill" Welden** is a linguist and member of the [Elvish Linguistic Fellowship](../E/Elvish_Linguistic_Fellowship.md). In the early days of the Fellowship, his house was the meeting place for the "ELFConners". The first true language convention, *Colloquium on the Languages of Middle-earth*, was also held at his house, and he served as an organizer of all *[Omentielva](../O/Omentielva.md)* meetings. Welden has contributed to *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)*, *[Quettar](../Q/Quettar.md)* and *[Parma Eldalamberon](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon.md)*, and worked as a translator on [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).
