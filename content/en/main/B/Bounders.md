The **Bounders** were responsible for policing the borders of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and ensuring that outsiders behaved themselves. This was known as 'beating the bounds'. The Bounders was the larger of the two branches of the [Watch](/wiki/Watch) (the other being the twelve [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs)).<sup id="cite_ref-shire_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shire-1">[1]</a></sup>

The number of Bounders at any given time was varied according to need. In the run up to the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) they were greatly increased<sup id="cite_ref-shire_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-shire-1">[1]</a></sup> and they were busy as strange persons and creatures approached the borders.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Unbeknownst to the Hobbits of the Shire this was the first sign that the troubles of the south would soon reach even their borders. [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) and spies of [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) began to enter [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word means "persons watching the boundaries".

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") noted that the word was rare, probably because in the late 19th century slang it meant an offensively pushing and in-bred man.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>
