**Belfalas** was a great fief<sup id="cite_ref-Fief_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fief-1">[1]</a></sup> in the south of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The fief of Belfalas was located between the mouth of the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3) and the mouth of the river [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain).<sup id="cite_ref-CirionN39_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CirionN39-3">[3]</a></sup> West of the Ringló was the fief of [Anfalas](/wiki/Anfalas). East of the river Gilrain was the fief of [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin). In the south the coast of Belfalas lay the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas). The fief of Lamedon was north of Belfalas at the foot of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains). The castle of [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) was located in the west of Belfalas on a high promontory that formed the southern end of the small bay of [Cobas Haven](/wiki/Cobas_Haven) into which the Ringló flowed.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-2">[2]</a></sup> The Elven haven of [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond) was about 50 miles north of Dol Amroth south of the confluence of the river [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) with the river Ringló.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) overwhelmed the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Atani](/wiki/Atani) towards the end of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), a group of [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) fled in three small ships from the western havens of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) and established the haven and a small settlement of [Edhellond](/wiki/Edhellond)<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-5">[5]</a></sup> at the south of the confluence of the river [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) with the river Ringló north of the hill that was later named [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth) in the Third Age. Later the settlement was increased by Silvan Elves that sailed down the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) to search for the sea.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Before [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [2221](/wiki/Second_Age_2221 "Second Age 2221"), after which the Númenóreans became divided into the party of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) and the party of the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men) on [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor), the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) established settlements in Belfalas.<sup id="cite_ref-Beards_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beards-9">[9]</a></sup> A family of [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) related to [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) emigrated from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) before the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) and settled in Belfalas. After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), the new [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") of the [Realms in Exile](/wiki/Realms_in_Exile) of Arnor and Gondor, granted them the title "Prince"<sup id="cite_ref-CirionN39_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CirionN39-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and their land became a fief<sup id="cite_ref-Fief_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fief-1">[1]</a></sup> of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and was called [Dor-en-Ernil](/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil) ("Land of the Prince").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981") [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), the [King of Lórien](/wiki/King_of_L%C3%B3rien), left his [realm](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") with his beloved [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel) to sail to [Aman](/wiki/Aman) from Edhellond, because Nimrodel wanted to flee to a land of peace from the [terror](/wiki/Balrog "Balrog") that came out of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and that had driven out the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). Amroth became separated from Nimrodel who got lost in the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and waited for her on the last ship that was anchoring at Edhellond. When the ship was blown prematurely out to sea, he jumped overboard in a futile attempt to reach the shore to search for her and drowned in the bay. The last of the Elves of Edhellond left Middle-earth on that ship.<sup id="cite_ref-Amroth_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Amroth-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the drowning of Amroth, the hill on which the stronghold of the Princes of Belfalas was located was called Dol Amroth (the "Hill of Amroth") and [Galador](/wiki/Galador), the son of [Imrazôr](/wiki/Imraz%C3%B4r), who according to legend had married the Elven-lady [Mithrellas](/wiki/Mithrellas), a companion of Nimrodel, became known as the first [Prince of Dol Amroth](/wiki/Prince_of_Dol_Amroth).<sup id="cite_ref-CirionN39_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CirionN39-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the nearby city of [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir) was attacked by the [Corsairs of Umbar](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar) and the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) and Men of [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) under [Angbor](/wiki/Angbor) tried to keep them off, until the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) with the [wraiths](/wiki/Wraiths "Wraiths") of the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) arrived and ended the conflict.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Population {#Population}

The people of Belfalas were mostly descendents of Númenórean settlers. They frequently used [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) names out of tradition, because their Númenórean ancestors had settled in Befalas before the division of the Númenóreans into the Faithful and the King's Men and the use of Adûnaic was at that time not connected with a rebellion against the Valar.<sup id="cite_ref-Beards_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beards-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Belfalas* consists of *[bel](/wiki/Bel "Bel")*, (a [Pre-Númenórean](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) element of unknown meaning) and the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *[falas](/wiki/Falas "Falas")* ("shore").<sup id="cite_ref-VT_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-13">[13]</a></sup>
