The **Bridge of Tharbad** was where the [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road) crossed the [Gwathló](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3) River within the town of [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad).

## History {#History}

After their founding, both of the kingdoms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) shared an interest in [Enedwaith](/wiki/Enedwaith), although it belonged to neither. Since the great North-South Road was the chief route of communication between the two kingdoms together they built and maintained the Bridge of Tharbad. Long causeways carried the road to the bridge and a considerable garrison of soldiers, mariners and engineers were kept nearby. The Bridge was guarded by a fort spanning both sides of the river.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

However, after the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) came in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636") the region fell quickly into decay. By the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), when [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) made his great journey from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) the causeways were crumbling remains, Tharbad was reduced to dwindling mounds, and the ruins of the bridge had formed a dangerous ford.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Boromir lost his horse at the ruined bridge.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
