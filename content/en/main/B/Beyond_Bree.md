<i><b>Beyond Bree</b></i> is a monthly publication of the [Tolkien Special Interest Group of Mensa](/wiki/American_Mensa_Tolkien_Special_Interest_Group "American Mensa Tolkien Special Interest Group"). It is a journal of record that is held in the collections of [Marquette University](/wiki/Marquette_University) in Milwaukee, Wisconsin; the [Wade Collection of Wheaton College](/wiki/Marion_E._Wade_Center "Marion E. Wade Center"), Wheaton, Illinois; and the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library) of Oxford University, England. It is cited, for example, in *[The Annotated Hobbit](/wiki/The_Annotated_Hobbit)*, *[J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_Encyclopedia)*, *[Tolkien the Medievalist](/wiki/Tolkien_the_Medievalist)*, and *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## From their website {#From_their_website}

A typical issue is 12 pages long, contains short articles on J.R.R. Tolkien and his works; reviews of books, games, films, events by, about, or inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien and his works;reviews of books, games, films, events by, about, or inspired by J.R.R. Tolkien and his works, and general fantasy which might be of interest to the Tolkien fan. Also readers' views; fan publications, news, art, occasional poetry and puzzles. We seldom publish fiction. Since we are too widely scattered to meet in person, Beyond Bree carries news of conventions and gatherings where the Tolkien fan might find others of similar interests. And, if readers have questions about Tolkien or his works, we try to answer them.

## Bree Moots {#Bree_Moots}

[Bree Moot](/wiki/Bree_Moot) is a small informal, irregularly scheduled event, sponsored by <i><b>Beyond Bree</b></i>. They are sometimes held in combination with another conference.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Beyond Bree Awards {#Beyond_Bree_Awards}

An irregular award presented by <i><b>Beyond Bree</b></i> in recognition of outstanding contributions to the study of J.R.R. Tolkien.

- I ([1999](/wiki/1999)) presented to <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Charles_B._Elston&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Charles B. Elston (page does not exist)">Charles B. Elston</a> and Dr [Richard E. Blackwelder](/wiki/Richard_E._Blackwelder).
- II ([2002](/wiki/2002)) presented to [Richard &amp; Perri West](/wiki/Richard_C._West "Richard C. West") and [Sumner Gary](/wiki/Sumner_Gary_Hunnewell "Sumner Gary Hunnewell") &amp; Sylvia Hunnewell.
- III ([2004](/wiki/2004)) presented to [René van Rossenberg](/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_van_Rossenberg) and [Mike Foster](/wiki/Mike_Foster).
- IV ([2005](/wiki/2005)) presented to [David](/wiki/David_Salo "David Salo") &amp; Dorothea Salo, [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) &amp; [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull), and [Anthony Burdge](/wiki/Anthony_Burdge) &amp; <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jessica_Burke&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jessica Burke (page does not exist)">Jessica Burke</a>.
- V ([2012](/wiki/2012)) awarded to [Mark T. Hooker](/wiki/Mark_T._Hooker) at [The Return of the Ring](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Ring) in Loughborough, England.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - Unlike previous award votes, this time the Readers voted for a single clear winner, Mark Hooker. Honorary mentions went to [Dinah Hazell](/wiki/The_Plants_of_Middle-earth "The Plants of Middle-earth") and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Phil_Goss&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Phil Goss (page does not exist)">Phil Goss</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
    - The nominees were: <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jeremy_Edmonds&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jeremy Edmonds (page does not exist)">Jeremy Edmonds</a>, Phil Goss, Dinah Hazell, Mark T. Hooker, and [Chris Seeman](/wiki/Chris_Seeman).
