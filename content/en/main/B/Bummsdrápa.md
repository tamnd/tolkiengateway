<i><b>Bummsdrápa</b></i> is an unpublished three paged satirical poem "in pen and ink"<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup> that was written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in the [1920s](/wiki/1920 "1920") or the early [1930s](/wiki/1930 "1930")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>. The [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds) library obtained the manuscripts of *Bummsdrápa* on [6 February](/wiki/6_February), [1952](/wiki/1952).<sup id="cite_ref-poem_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-poem-1">[1]</a></sup> The poem refers to the Kolbítar, a group founded by Tolkien to talk about Icelandic mythology. [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), [Richard Wagner](/wiki/Richard_Wagner), and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Talbut_Onions" title="wikipedia:Charles Talbut Onions">C.T. Onions</a> were members of this group. The group preceded the creation of the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings).<sup id="cite_ref-guide_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-guide-3">[3]</a></sup>
