<i><b>Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem with a Discussion of the Stories of Offa and Finn</b></i> is a 1921 book by [R.W. Chambers](../R/R.W._Chambers.md) about *[Beowulf](<Beowulf_(poem).md>)*.

By [28 July](../_/28_July.md) [1922](../_/1922.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) had agreed to review this book for G.N. Clark, editor of the *English Historical Review*. He made [several pages of notes](../../index/U/Unpublished_material.md), but would not complete the review (or perhaps it was never published).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [24 May](../_/24_May.md) [1932](../_/1932.md), Chambers sent Tolkien an inscribed copy of the second edition of *Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem* (published the same month).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
