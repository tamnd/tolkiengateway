<i><b>Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem with a Discussion of the Stories of Offa and Finn</b></i> is a 1921 book by [R.W. Chambers](/wiki/R.W._Chambers) about *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")*.

By [28 July](/wiki/28_July) [1922](/wiki/1922), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) had agreed to review this book for G.N. Clark, editor of the *English Historical Review*. He made [several pages of notes](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material"), but would not complete the review (or perhaps it was never published).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [24 May](/wiki/24_May) [1932](/wiki/1932), Chambers sent Tolkien an inscribed copy of the second edition of *Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem* (published the same month).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
