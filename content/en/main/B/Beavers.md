**Beavers** were large rodents, famous for their habit of building dams and lodges. They lived in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) as well as in the southern regions of the [island of Númenor](/wiki/Elenna "Elenna").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> *"Beaver the builder"* was mentioned among other [animals](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar") in the [Long List of the Ents](/wiki/Long_List_of_the_Ents).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Dwarven](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") name *[Bífur](/wiki/Bifur "Bifur")* meant "Beaver" or, by extension, "Hard Worker".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
