**Beavers** were large rodents, famous for their habit of building dams and lodges. They lived in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) as well as in the southern regions of the [island of Númenor](../E/Elenna.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> *"Beaver the builder"* was mentioned among other [animals](../K/Kelvar.md) in the [Long List of the Ents](../L/Long_List_of_the_Ents.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The [Dwarven](../D/Dwarves.md) name *[Bífur](Bifur.md)* meant "Beaver" or, by extension, "Hard Worker".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
