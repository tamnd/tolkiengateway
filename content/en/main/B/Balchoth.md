The **Balchoth** were a numerous<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> fierce people<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> of [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> under the shadow of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> who had migrated from east of the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> to the wide lands of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md) between the forest of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) and the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Balchoth were no doubt related to the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md). They had crude weapons and few horses for riding, because they mostly used horses to draw their large number of wagons.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Around the time when [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) became [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2489](../T/Third_Age_2489.md) the Balchoth migrated from the East and drifted from beyond the Sea of Rhûn to the west. They killed the remnants of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) that had still lived east of Mirkwood or drove them away up the River Running or into the forest of Mirkwood.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

Cirion was constantly concerned about the threat of an invasion from the east. He stationed a few men in the previously abandoned old [Gondorian](../G/Gondorian.md) forts along the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), especially on the western shore of the [Undeeps](../U/Undeeps.md) in order to keep a watch there, and he sent scouts to the land between the forest of Mirkwood and the plain of [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md). It was then that news of what happened to the Northmen reached [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), but Cirion could not help them.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Balchoth stayed in the lands between Mirkwood and River Running and often made raids through the forest of Mirkwood. As a consequence, the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md) south of the [Gladden River](../G/Gladden_River.md) were largely deserted. The numbers of the Balchoth constantly increased with reinforcements from the [East](../E/East.md) (in contrast, the Gondorian population of [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md), which was located west of the river Anduin, had decreased).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> It is possible that [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) influenced the Balchoth and the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) to invade Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Balchoth mustered a large force all along the southern edge of Mirkwood. Cirion found out after the end of the winter in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2509](../T/Third_Age_2509.md) and in March [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md) riders were sent north to the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) to ask for help against the invaders. [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md), the [Lord of the Éothéod](../L/Lord_of_the_Éothéod.md), gathered an army <sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> and left that April.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Meanwhile the Balchoth<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> had come from the north-east through the [Brown Lands](Brown_Lands.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> and had built many boats and rafts on the eastern shore of Anduin, crossed the river and drove away the Gondorian defenders.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> The northern army of Gondor<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> led by Cirion<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup> marched up from the south,<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> but the Balchoth defeated them in the [Wold](../W/Wold.md), cut off from the south<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> and drove north over the river [Limlight](../L/Limlight.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup> There the Gondorian army was surprised by an attack from a host of Orcs from the Misty Mountains and was forced in the direction of Anduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup>

Suddenly Eorl arrived from the north with a large host of cavalry of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) and attacked the enemy from the rear.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> In the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> the land between the river [Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md) and the river Limlight, the enemies were driven south over the Limlight; after that the enemies in the Wold were also routed<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-3">[3]</a></sup> and the cavalry of the Éotheod pursued and hunted down the fleeing Balchoth over the fields of Calenardhon.<sup id="cite_ref-Cirion_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cirion-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Balchoth* was the name of those people given by the people of Gondor and consists of the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) element *[balc](Balc.md)* ("horrible") and the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) element *[hoth](../H/Hoth.md)* ("horde"). It was actually a generic term for disliked peoples, including the [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
