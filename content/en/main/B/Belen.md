**Belen** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [292](/wiki/First_Age_292 "First Age 292")) was the younger son of [Bëor the Old](/wiki/B%C3%ABor "Bëor").

## History {#History}

Belen was born three years after [Baran](/wiki/Baran), who inherited the title of family head from his father. It was to Belen, his second son, that Bëor the Old chiefly transmitted his wisdom and lore.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Belen fathered [Beldir](/wiki/Beldir), and was an ancestor of [Beren Erchamion](/wiki/Beren "Beren").<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the introduction to *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) stated that they were written in the 1930s.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This work was laid aside while [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) wrote *The Lord of the Rings*, after which he returned to his work on the elder days. In his later writings Tolkien relocated the coming of the [Edain](/wiki/Edain) over the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) nearly a century earlier, which occasioned a large-scale overhaul of the chronology and genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In the older *Grey Annals* Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas) and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir). In the new version four generations were inserted between Bëor and the brothers Bregolas and Barahir. Along with this change *Belen* was added as the ancestor of Beren.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup>
