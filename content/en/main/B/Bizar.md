**bizar** in [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) means "dale, valley"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "a small stream (running down from a spring)"<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup> in which case it would be form *bizâr*.

[Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) B-Z-R<sup id="cite_ref-RC_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) explored various possibilities regarding the exact interpretation of the elements occurring in the name *[Azanulbizar](../A/Azanulbizar.md)*; it was to somehow mean ["Dimrill Dale"](../D/Dimrill_Dale.md).

In one interpretation, *Azanulbizar* means "The rills of the shadows", whereas the word "dale" is understood (the full name being *[duban](../D/Duban.md) Azanulbizar*, but *duban* "dale, valley" is left out so that the place was simply called *Azanulbizar*).

In another, the whole phrase "Dimrill Dale" fits into the word *Azanulbizar* since the *bizar* part that means "dale", and "rill(s)" or "streams" corresponds to the middle element *[ûl](../_/Ûl.md)*. <span></span>
