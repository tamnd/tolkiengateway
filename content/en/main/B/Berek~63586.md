**Berek** was a [horse](/wiki/Horse "Horse") from [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos) in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) that [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) personally rode. Berek was invented by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.

## History {#History}

Berek was Isildur's horse and was ridden by him during the [Battle](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)> "Udûn (episode)") of [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad).

After the disappearance of Isildur after the eruption of [Orodruin](/wiki/Mount_Doom "Mount Doom"), Berek refused to allow [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) to handle him and was let loose, dashing off across the fields in search of Isildur.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Berek* contains the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) root *[BERÉK](/wiki/BER%C3%89K)*.
