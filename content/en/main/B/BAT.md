**BAT** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "tread".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *bata* ("beaten track, pathway")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *bád* ("beaten track, pathway");<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *batho* ("trample")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
