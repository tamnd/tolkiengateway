**Beeches** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") *[neldor](/wiki/Neldor "Neldor")*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) were broad and tall [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") that grew throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and especially in its northern regions. The most famous beech-forest of all was [Neldoreth](/wiki/Forest_of_Neldoreth "Forest of Neldoreth") in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Hírilorn](/wiki/H%C3%ADrilorn), the three-trunked tree in which [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) was imprisoned, was perhaps the greatest beech that had ever grown.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Beeches also grew in the [Trollshaws](/wiki/Trollshaws), where [trolls](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") used them as firewood<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and in the valley of [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> as well as in [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> where they were the favorite trees of the [Wood-elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> In the attack on [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) by the [Ents](/wiki/Ents), the Ent which caught fire and burned to death was called [Beechbone](/wiki/Beechbone).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1994: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Treasures_of_Middle-earth_(2nd_edition)" title="Treasures of Middle-earth (2nd edition)">Treasures of Middle-earth</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>A type of beech tree, <i><b>Chap-beech</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S</a>. <i>Hlosneldor</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q</a>. <i>Lussaferen</i>), was described in the book as a deciduous pale-white tree. Its thin leaves sounded like hissing in the wind. These trees grew along the path to <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>. Chap-beechnuts had a stinging husk, but the <a href="/wiki/Beornings" title="Beornings">Beornings</a> and <a href="/wiki/Radagast" title="Radagast">Radagast</a> knew that they taste good once the husk was removed.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>
