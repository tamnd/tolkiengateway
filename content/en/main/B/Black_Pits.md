The **Black Pits** were dungeons of torment located beneath the tower of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr).<sup id="cite_ref-index_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-index-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Between the years [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3009](/wiki/Third_Age_3009 "Third Age 3009") and [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3017](/wiki/Third_Age_3017 "Third Age 3017") of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age),<sup id="cite_ref-appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) was held captive in the Black Pits, only being taken out to be interrogated.<sup id="cite_ref-past_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-past-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that he was interrogated by the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Questioner&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Questioner (page does not exist)">Questioner</a><sup id="cite_ref-question_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-question-4">[4]</a></sup>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Gollum was released from the Black Pits only after he revealed the information that "[Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins")" from "[Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")" held the [One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-past_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-past-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [14 March](/wiki/14_March) of the year [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), the Captain of the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol), [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat), cursed "that filthy rebel [Gorbag](/wiki/Gorbag)" to the Black Pits after a [skirmish](/wiki/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol "Battle of Cirith Ungol") broke out between his [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and Gorbag's company from [Minas Morgul](/wiki/Minas_Morgul).<sup id="cite_ref-asdf_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-asdf-6">[6]</a></sup>

When Shagrat's underling, [Snaga](</wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor)> "Snaga (orc of Mordor)"), informed him that he was not willing to report the happenings in the Tower to [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) at [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr), Shagrat told him that they both would "be for the Black Pits" if such news would not get through to Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-asdf_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-asdf-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b><a href="/wiki/2020" title="2020">2020</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Stout-axe introduction storyline, players begin as a prisoner in the Black Pits trying to break out of <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> After the <a href="/wiki/Downfall_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr" title="Downfall of Barad-dûr">Downfall of Barad-dûr</a>, the Black Pits<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and the abyss Mordath<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> beneath them were all that remained of the tower.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2023" title="2023">2023</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Gollum" title="The Lord of the Rings: Gollum">The Lord of the Rings: Gollum</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>During the opening chapters of the game, <a href="/wiki/Gollum" title="Gollum">Gollum</a> is thrown into the Black Pits after being tortured by Sauron. There are multiple locations within the Black Pits that are featured within each chapter.</dd></dl>
