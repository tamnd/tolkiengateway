The **battle under the trees** was a conflict fought on [15 March](../_/15_March.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), as part of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup> It was fought simultaneously with the second assault on [Lórien](../L/Lórien.md), a battle also fought against the forces of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md).

## Prelude {#Prelude}

[Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) had recently become active<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>(possibly under the power of [Khamûl](../K/Khamûl.md), its former commander)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> under the service of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). On [11 March](../_/11_March.md), Dol Guldur sent a force of Orcs against [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup> Though acting as the first of [three assaults](../A/Assaults_on_Lothlórien.md), it ended in failure.

## The Battle {#The_Battle}

On the 15 March, the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md) took place in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Not soon after its end, Orcs were sent from Dol Guldur to Lórien and the [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md). Another long battle then took place under the trees of Mirkwood when Thranduil's realm was invaded, and a great ruin of fire. In the end Thranduil took the victory and the Elves of Mirkwood won.<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the same time, the Orcs sent to Lothlórien for a second time, once again failed the assault.<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

A third assault on Lórien was made on [22 March](../_/22_March.md) and ended comparably to the others. On [28 March](../_/28_March.md), [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) took Dol Guldur and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) laid it bare, presumably by way of [Nenya](../N/Nenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [6 April](../_/6_April.md), Celeborn and King [Thranduil](../T/Thranduil.md) met in the midst of the forest and renamed it [Eryn Lasgalen](../E/Eryn_Lasgalen.md), "wood of green leaves". Celeborn took the land south of [The Narrows](../T/The_Narrows.md) for himself and named it [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md), while Thranduil took all wood north of the [Mountains of Mirkwood](../M/Mountains_of_Mirkwood.md). They gave the middle of the wood to the [Beornings](Beornings.md) and [Woodmen](../W/Woodmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>
