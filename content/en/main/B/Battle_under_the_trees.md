The **battle under the trees** was a conflict fought on [15 March](/wiki/15_March), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), as part of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup> It was fought simultaneously with the second assault on [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien), a battle also fought against the forces of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur).

## Prelude {#Prelude}

[Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) had recently become active<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>(possibly under the power of [Khamûl](/wiki/Kham%C3%BBl), its former commander)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> under the service of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). On [11 March](/wiki/11_March), Dol Guldur sent a force of Orcs against [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup> Though acting as the first of [three assaults](/wiki/Assaults_on_Lothl%C3%B3rien "Assaults on Lothlórien"), it ended in failure.

## The Battle {#The_Battle}

On the 15 March, the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields) took place in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Not soon after its end, Orcs were sent from Dol Guldur to Lórien and the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm). Another long battle then took place under the trees of Mirkwood when Thranduil's realm was invaded, and a great ruin of fire. In the end Thranduil took the victory and the Elves of Mirkwood won.<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the same time, the Orcs sent to Lothlórien for a second time, once again failed the assault.<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

A third assault on Lórien was made on [22 March](/wiki/22_March) and ended comparably to the others. On [28 March](/wiki/28_March), [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) took Dol Guldur and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) laid it bare, presumably by way of [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [6 April](/wiki/6_April), Celeborn and King [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil) met in the midst of the forest and renamed it [Eryn Lasgalen](/wiki/Eryn_Lasgalen), "wood of green leaves". Celeborn took the land south of [The Narrows](/wiki/The_Narrows) for himself and named it [East Lórien](/wiki/East_L%C3%B3rien), while Thranduil took all wood north of the [Mountains of Mirkwood](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood). They gave the middle of the wood to the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) and [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen).<sup id="cite_ref-battle_under_trees_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-battle_under_trees-1">[1]</a></sup>
