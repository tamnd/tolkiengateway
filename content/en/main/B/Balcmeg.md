**Balcmeg** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") slain by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Balcmeg was considered to be *"a great fighter among the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Orclim&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Orclim (page does not exist)">Orclim</a>"*.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>

During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), Balcmeg was among those Orcs which assailed the [Gondothlim](/wiki/Gondothlim) alongside the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) at the coming of the [Drakes](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons"). Balcmeg was ultimately slain by Tuor, who hewed him asunder with his axe, [Dramborleg](/wiki/Dramborleg), in the process.<sup id="cite_ref-LT2Gondolin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT2Gondolin-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Balcmeg* is a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name meaning "heart of evil",<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> being comprised of the element *[Balc](/wiki/Balc)* ("evil, cruel").<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup> Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) form was *Malkamekte*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Balcmeg never appeared within any of Tolkien's writing after "[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")" in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
