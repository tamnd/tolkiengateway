**Bungo Baggins** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2846](../T/Third_Age_2846.md) - [2926](../T/Third_Age_2926.md), died aged 80<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a well-to-do [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md), and the father of [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md).

## History {#History}

Bungo was the eldest child of [Mungo Baggins](../M/Mungo_Baggins.md) and [Laura Grubb](../L/Laura_Grubb.md). He married [Belladonna Took](Belladonna_Took.md), daughter of the [Old Took](../G/Gerontius_Took.md), towards [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1290](../T/Third_Age_2890.md). Bungo and Belladonna had only one child together, the famous [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>

Father and son were so much alike, in looks and behaviour, that Bilbo seemed like a "second edition" of his "solid and comfortable" father.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

Apart from fathering Bilbo, Bungo's other achievements were that he built and excavated the rather luxurious [Bag End](Bag_End.md) in the [Hill](../H/Hobbiton_Hill.md) in [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) and that he laid down several bottles of [Old Winyards](../O/Old_Winyards.md) wine that would be enjoyed by [Rorimac Brandybuck](../R/Rorimac_Brandybuck.md) after [Bilbo's Farewell Party](Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup> and by the small party on [21 September](../_/21_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md) that [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) gave before leaving Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Bungo had an aptitude for either remembering or coining proverbs. Bilbo quoted his father's sayings "third time pays for all" before making his first descent into [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-4">[4]</a></sup> "Every worm has his weak spot" while talking to the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-4">[4]</a></sup> and "while there's life there's hope" when leading the Dwarves down to Smaug's lair.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bungo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) [Westron](../W/Westron.md). It represented an original name *Bunga*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
