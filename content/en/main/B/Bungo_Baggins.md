**Bungo Baggins** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2846](/wiki/Third_Age_2846 "Third Age 2846") - [2926](/wiki/Third_Age_2926 "Third Age 2926"), died aged 80<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a well-to-do [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"), and the father of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).

## History {#History}

Bungo was the eldest child of [Mungo Baggins](/wiki/Mungo_Baggins) and [Laura Grubb](/wiki/Laura_Grubb). He married [Belladonna Took](/wiki/Belladonna_Took), daughter of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"), towards [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1290](/wiki/Third_Age_2890 "Third Age 2890"). Bungo and Belladonna had only one child together, the famous [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-1">[1]</a></sup>

Father and son were so much alike, in looks and behaviour, that Bilbo seemed like a "second edition" of his "solid and comfortable" father.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup>

Apart from fathering Bilbo, Bungo's other achievements were that he built and excavated the rather luxurious [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) in the [Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill "Hobbiton Hill") in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) and that he laid down several bottles of [Old Winyards](/wiki/Old_Winyards) wine that would be enjoyed by [Rorimac Brandybuck](/wiki/Rorimac_Brandybuck) after [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party)<sup id="cite_ref-Party_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-2">[2]</a></sup> and by the small party on [21 September](/wiki/21_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") that [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) gave before leaving Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Bungo had an aptitude for either remembering or coining proverbs. Bilbo quoted his father's sayings "third time pays for all" before making his first descent into [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor),<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-4">[4]</a></sup> "Every worm has his weak spot" while talking to the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug),<sup id="cite_ref-Inside_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Inside-4">[4]</a></sup> and "while there's life there's hope" when leading the Dwarves down to Smaug's lair.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bungo* was meaningless in contemporary [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) [Westron](/wiki/Westron). It represented an original name *Bunga*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
