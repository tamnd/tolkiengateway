**Bucca of the Marish** was the first [Thain](/wiki/Thain) of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).

## History {#History}

Around [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [379](/wiki/Third_Age_1979 "Third Age 1979"), some four years after the fall of the [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor"), the Hobbits of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") elected from their midst one Bucca of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish) as the first Thain. He was to rule the land in the [King](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain "King of Arthedain")'s stead. Little else is known about him, other than that he brought peace and prosperity to the Hobbits.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

His family became the [Oldbucks](/wiki/Oldbucks) and eventually the [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybuck_Family "Brandybuck Family").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word **Bucca** is [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) for "buck", "male deer" or "he-goat" which is a translation of the original [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name <b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gamba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gamba (page does not exist)">Gamba</a></b>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
