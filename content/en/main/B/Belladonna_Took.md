**Belladonna Baggins** (née **Took**) ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2852](/wiki/Third_Age_2852 "Third Age 2852") - [2934](/wiki/Third_Age_2934 "Third Age 2934"), died aged 82) was the mother of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).

## History {#History}

Belladonna was one of the three remarkable daughters of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took").<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup> She had eight older brothers (and one younger), and was the eldest of the three daughters.<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup> Belladonna married [Bungo Baggins](/wiki/Bungo_Baggins), who built a spacious hole for her (partly with her money).<sup id="cite_ref-Party_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-1">[1]</a></sup> It became the residence of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family), and in [2890](/wiki/Third_Age_2890 "Third Age 2890"), her son and only child [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") was born. She died in [2934](/wiki/Third_Age_2934 "Third Age 2934"),<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup> eight years after her husband.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atropa_belladonna" title="wikipedia:Atropa belladonna">belladonna</a></i> is the name of a poisonous plant,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> fitting in with the Hobbit-habit of naming girls after flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The word is understood as an Italian name, a rarity among Hobbits, and translates as "beautiful lady" although it derives from Latin *bladone* "nightshade".

The name participates in a circular wordplay with the names of her sisters, [Mira**bella**](/wiki/Mirabella_Took "Mirabella Took") and [**Donna**mira](/wiki/Donnamira_Took "Donnamira Took"), also Italian names.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In her first appearance in the fiction, Belladonna - which has stayed unchanged from the earliest survived sketch of the story<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> - is described as "one of three remarkable daughters of the Old Took". [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter), in his attempt to draw an analogy between Bilbo and Tolkien, noted that Tolkien's mother, [Mabel Suffield](/wiki/Mabel_Suffield), was one of three remarkable daughters of [John Suffield](/wiki/John_Suffield), who lived to be nearly a hundred.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
