**Barbara Remington** ([23 June](/wiki/23_June) [1929](/wiki/1929) - [23 January](/wiki/23_January) [2020](/wiki/2020)) was an American artist. She painted the cover art for [Ballantine Books](/wiki/Ballantine_Books)' first paperback editions of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, for which [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) expressed dislike.

In [1965](/wiki/1965) Ballantine published a poster with the map of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands), bearing the signature "BRem". The poster is bordered with images adapted by her covers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [1 November](/wiki/1_November) [1967](/wiki/1967), she sent a letter to Tolkien, explaining that the publisher was trying to get the books out as fast as possible because of competition, which did not give her time to read the books.<sup id="cite_ref-CG_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CG-2">[2]</a></sup>
