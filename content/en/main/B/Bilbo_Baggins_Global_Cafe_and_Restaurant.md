**Bilbo Baggins Global Wine Cafe and Restaurant** was a restaurant situated in Alexandria, Virginia which was themed around none other than [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md). It was opened in [1980](../_/1980.md) by Michael Armellino and closed permanently in [2020](../_/2020.md).<sup id="cite_ref-living_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-living-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-connection_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-connection-2">[2]</a></sup>
