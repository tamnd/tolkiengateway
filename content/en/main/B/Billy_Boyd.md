Scottish actor **Billy Boyd** (born [1968](/wiki/1968)) played [Peregrin "Pippin" Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). An accomplished singer, he wrote and sung the dirge "[The Edge of Night](/wiki/The_Edge_of_Night)" for *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*, which is featured during [Faramir's](/wiki/Faramir "Faramir") hopeless charge to [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). He also wrote and sung "[The Last Goodbye](/wiki/The_Last_Goodbye)" for *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*.

In 2003, when *The Return of the King* was released, Boyd also starred in <a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0311113/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Master and Commander: The Far Side of the World</a>. Both films ended up being nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture.

Boyd and fellow *Lord of the Rings* actor [Dominic Monaghan](/wiki/Dominic_Monaghan) started the *[The Friendship Onion](/wiki/The_Friendship_Onion)* podcast in 2021, which includes stories from their time making the films.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
