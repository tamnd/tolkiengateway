**Brad Williams** is an American artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create illustrations for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)* and card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.
