**Boromir's belt** was [gifted](/wiki/Gifts_of_Galadriel "Gifts of Galadriel") to **[Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)** by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). It appeared as it were of linked golden leaves.

## History {#History}

Before the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") left [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), each of its members was presented with a gift by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). Boromir was given a belt of gold which he carried in the journey until the [Breaking of the Fellowship](/wiki/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) laid him in his burial boat, the golden belt gleamed about his waist.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later on, [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) one night saw the boat by [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and Boromir lay there dead, and the belt was the only thing he did not recognize. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") said to Faramir that the belt was given to him in Lórien, and thus positively identified the dead man as Boromir.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Boromir" title="Boromir">Boromir</a> is the only member of the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Fellowship" title="Fellowship">Fellowship</a> who does not receive a gift from <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a>. He does wear a belt throughout the film, but it is made of leather, not gold. It is possible <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a> wished to convey the mistrust that Galadriel and the rest of the group felt toward him.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>
