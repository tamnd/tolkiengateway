The **Battle of the Peak** was [Gandalf's](/wiki/Gandalf "Gandalf") name for his climactic battle with the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") known as [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane), on the peak of the mountain called [Silvertine](/wiki/Celebdil "Celebdil").

Gandalf pursued the Balrog from the deepests dungeons of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) to [Durin's Tower](/wiki/Durin%27s_Tower), climbing through the whole [Endless Stair](/wiki/Endless_Stair).<sup id="cite_ref-White_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-1">[1]</a></sup> On the peak they fought for three days and two nights from [23](/wiki/23_January "23 January") to [25 January](/wiki/25_January) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> During the battle, the Tower was destroyed and the entrance to the Stair was blocked. In the end, the Balrog was cast down and it broke the mountain-side as it fell. Gandalf himself died following this ordeal, but was later sent back to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) with even greater powers as Gandalf the White.<sup id="cite_ref-White_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-White-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
