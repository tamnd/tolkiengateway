**Breredon** (Briar Hill) was a little village in [Buckland](Buckland.md) near [Haysend](../H/Haysend.md) on rising ground behind the hythe, or small landing, on the north bank of the [Withywindle](../W/Withywindle.md) in the narrow tongue between the south end of the [High Hay](../H/High_Hay.md) and the [Baranduin](Baranduin.md) River.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that *Breredon* is consisting of the elements *brere* ("<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brier" title="wikipedia:Brier">briar</a>") + *-don* ("down, hill").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This etymology would represent a possible [Old Hobbitish](../O/Old_English.md) form \**Braerdun* "hill of briers".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
