**Breredon** (Briar Hill) was a little village in [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) near [Haysend](/wiki/Haysend) on rising ground behind the hythe, or small landing, on the north bank of the [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle) in the narrow tongue between the south end of the [High Hay](/wiki/High_Hay) and the [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) River.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It has been suggested that *Breredon* is consisting of the elements *brere* ("<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brier" title="wikipedia:Brier">briar</a>") + *-don* ("down, hill").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

This etymology would represent a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") form \**Braerdun* "hill of briers".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
