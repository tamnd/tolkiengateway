**Ban(a)** is a [Westron](../W/Westron.md) word meaning *half*.

The element is seen in compounds. It usually is followed by an *a* but it is not known if it belongs to the full form of the word [\*](../A/Asterisk.md)*bana*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word seems to come from and [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) element, like some root \*BAN.

Cf. [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ban%C3%A2th&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Banâth (page does not exist)">banâth</a></i> "wife" that would mean "the half of the couple"
