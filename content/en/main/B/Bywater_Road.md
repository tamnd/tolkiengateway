**Bywater Road** is a road in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), branching from the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) and leading to [Little Delving](../L/Little_Delving.md).

## Course {#Course}

A little to the west of the [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md), a branch of the East Road sloped upward between high banks<sup id="cite_ref-rk_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rk-1">[1]</a></sup> leading to the village of [Bywater](Bywater.md) from which the Road took its name. From there, it carried on to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md), where a traveller would turn right and cross a bridge across [the Water](../T/The_Water.md) for about thirty miles, to reach [Hobbiton Hill](../H/Hobbiton_Hill.md) and [Bag End](Bag_End.md). The road itself continued into the west, in the direction of [Little Delving](../L/Little_Delving.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [3 November](../_/3_November.md) [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1419](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) used this feature to trap the band of [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) who came to crush the rebellion of the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) against [Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md)'s rule. Wagons were used to close the trap on either end, which gave the Hobbits the edge in the resulting [Battle of Bywater](Battle_of_Bywater.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rk_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rk-1">[1]</a></sup>
