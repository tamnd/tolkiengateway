**Bywater Road** is a road in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), branching from the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) and leading to [Little Delving](/wiki/Little_Delving).

## Course {#Course}

A little to the west of the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone), a branch of the East Road sloped upward between high banks<sup id="cite_ref-rk_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rk-1">[1]</a></sup> leading to the village of [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) from which the Road took its name. From there, it carried on to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), where a traveller would turn right and cross a bridge across [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water") for about thirty miles, to reach [Hobbiton Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill) and [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). The road itself continued into the west, in the direction of [Little Delving](/wiki/Little_Delving).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On [3 November](/wiki/3_November) [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1419](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") used this feature to trap the band of [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) who came to crush the rebellion of the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) against [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s rule. Wagons were used to close the trap on either end, which gave the Hobbits the edge in the resulting [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-rk_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rk-1">[1]</a></sup>
