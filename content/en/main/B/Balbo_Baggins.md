**Balbo Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), and the first known member of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family).

## History {#History}

Balbo was born when the Shire was recovering from both the [Days of Dearth](/wiki/Days_of_Dearth) where [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) came to the aid of the Hobbits.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Balbo was the original patriarch of the [Baggins Family](/wiki/Baggins_Family), being the first recorded member of the respected family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). He married [Berylla Boffin](/wiki/Berylla_Boffin) and their children were [Mungo](/wiki/Mungo_Baggins "Mungo Baggins"), [Pansy](/wiki/Pansy_Baggins "Pansy Baggins"), [Ponto](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I"), [Largo](/wiki/Largo_Baggins "Largo Baggins") and [Lily](/wiki/Lily_Baggins "Lily Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Balbo was the great-grandfather of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), the great-great-grandfather of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), and the great-great-great-grandfather of both [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
