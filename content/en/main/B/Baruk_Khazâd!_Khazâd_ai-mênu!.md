**Baruk Khazâd! Khazâd ai-mênu!** was a [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) battlecry.

## History {#History}

Though the battle-cry was much older, the first recorded use was in the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><i>But a <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">small dark figure</a> that none had observed sprang out of the shadows and gave a hoarse shout: </i>Baruk Khazâd! Khazâd ai-mênu!<i> An axe swung and swept back. Two Orcs fell headless. The rest fled.</i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

Later, Gimli gave some variations on the battle-cry: *Khazâd! Khazâd!* and *Khazâd ai-mênu!*.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The cry is translated "Axes of the Dwarves! The Dwarves are upon you!".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Appearances in adaptations {#Appearances_in_adaptations}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Hobbit_(2003)_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/f0/The_Hobbit_%282003%29_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg/250px-The_Hobbit_%282003%29_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="188" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/f0/The_Hobbit_%282003%29_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg/375px-The_Hobbit_%282003%29_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/f0/The_Hobbit_%282003%29_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg/500px-The_Hobbit_%282003%29_Balfor_Baruk-Khaz%C3%A2d.jpg 2x" data-file-width="640" data-file-height="480"/></a><figcaption>Balfor in the video game <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)">The Hobbit</a></i></figcaption></figure>

<b>1968: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1968_radio_series)" title="The Hobbit (1968 radio series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1968 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> shouts <i>Baruk Khazâd! Khazâd ai-mênu!</i> at the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> simply yells <i>Khazâd! Khazâd!</i> at the battle of the Hornburg.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Baruk Khazâd! To Battle!</i> is a war-cry used by Gimli prior to the <a href="/wiki/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship" title="Breaking of the Fellowship">skirmish at Amon Hen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite the movie&#39;s use of <a href="/wiki/Khuzdul" title="Khuzdul">Khuzdul</a> lines,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> no Dwarvish battle-cry is heard at the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a>, or anywhere else. <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a> expansion <i>Battle of Helm&#39;s Deep</i> purports that <a href="/wiki/Gimli" title="Gimli">Gimli</a> yells it, though this is not actually present in the film.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>During the &#34;Hornburg Courtyard&#34; stage, Gimli shouts <i>Khazâd! Khazâd ai-mênu!</i> while fighting off an oncoming force of Uruk-hai.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Dwarf <a href="/wiki/Balfor" title="Balfor">Balfor</a> shouts <i>Baruk-Khazâd</i> upon his release from goblin captivity.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dwarven soldiers will yell, among other phrases, &#34;Baruk Khazâd&#34; and &#34;Khazâd ai-mênu&#34;, when ordered to fight.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Players that play with a Dwarf will shout <i>Baruk Khazâd!</i> when doing certain skills, while Stout-axe characters shout both <i>Baruk Khazad!</i> and <i>Khazad ai-menu!</i>.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Farin shouts <i>Baruk khazâd</i> and <i>khazâd ai-mênu</i> during combat. Other dwarves from Nordinbad also use the war cry.</dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies_(extended_edition)" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies (extended edition)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Dáin uses both parts of the phrase during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>. He yells &#34;Baruk Khazâd&#34; as he orders the ballistae to shoot, and later uses the long version as he fights Orcs.</dd></dl>
