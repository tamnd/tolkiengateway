The **Bracegirdle family** were an important and populous family of [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits), associated with the town or village of [Hardbottle](/wiki/Hardbottle). The most famous of them was [Lobelia Bracegirdle](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins "Lobelia Sackville-Baggins"), who married [Otho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Otho_Sackville-Baggins).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Hobbitish name referred to their tendency to be fat and so to strain their belts, meaning "Tight-belt", or "Belt-tightener / strainer / stretcher". Bracegirdle is a real-life English surname. [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") had noted that it followed the type of Romance compounds with the verbal element first (instead of \*"Girdlebracer").<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>
