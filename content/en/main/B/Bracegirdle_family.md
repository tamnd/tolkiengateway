The **Bracegirdle family** were an important and populous family of [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md), associated with the town or village of [Hardbottle](../H/Hardbottle.md). The most famous of them was [Lobelia Bracegirdle](../L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md), who married [Otho Sackville-Baggins](../O/Otho_Sackville-Baggins.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The Hobbitish name referred to their tendency to be fat and so to strain their belts, meaning "Tight-belt", or "Belt-tightener / strainer / stretcher". Bracegirdle is a real-life English surname. [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) had noted that it followed the type of Romance compounds with the verbal element first (instead of \*"Girdlebracer").<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-1">[1]</a></sup>
