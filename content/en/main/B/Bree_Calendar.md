The **Bree Calendar** is the calendar used by the [Bree-folk](/wiki/Bree-folk) during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

Some of the month names were different from those of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), which evolved independently. Those names were however also used in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

When the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) were still a [wandering](/wiki/Wandering_Days "Wandering Days") people, their calendaric unit was not a 'week' , but a 'month', governed more or less by the [Moon](/wiki/Moon). In their old calendar, the new year began after harvest. This can be seen in the name of the month [Winterfilth](/wiki/Winterfilth) meaning "filling (of the year) before winter". However, through contact with alien peoples (perhaps the [Dúnedain of Arnor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor)) they adopted the notion of weeks.

The Bree calendar is obviously a [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbit "Hobbit") localization of the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) using [Mannish](/wiki/Mannish) month-names and several minor alterations to fit their customs. It is not known when the Calendar was introduced, but the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bree_Reckoning&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bree Reckoning (page does not exist)">Bree Reckoning</a> begun at [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300") (when Hobbits arrived in [Bree](/wiki/Bree)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The Bree Calendar followed that of the Shire in a couple of reforms, such as the shortening of the two medial months and the introduction of the [Lithedays](/wiki/Lithe "Lithe"). The Bree Calendar also followed [Thain](/wiki/Thain) [Isengrim II](/wiki/Isengrim_Took_II "Isengrim Took II")'s reform, which put the [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day) outside the reckoning of weekdays, so that every year started and ended at the same day of the week. <span></span>
