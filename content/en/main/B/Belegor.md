**Belegor** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [340](/wiki/First_Age_340 "First Age 340")) was a man of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Belegor was the second son of [Boron](/wiki/Boron), and the younger brother of [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros)> "Boromir (Lord of Ladros)"). He could trace his ancestry directly to [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor), who was his great-grandfather.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
