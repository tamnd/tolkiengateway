**Benjamin Fransham** is a Kiwi actor who portrayed [Thandronen](/wiki/Thandronen) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") Soldier in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* [season 1](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1 "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 1") episode *[A Shadow of the Past](/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past)*.

He was a stunt performer in [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")<sup id="cite_ref-AAANZ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAANZ-3">[3]</a></sup> and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* [season 1](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1 "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 1") episode *[A Shadow of the Past](/wiki/A_Shadow_of_the_Past)* and a motion-capture performer in [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-AAANZ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAANZ-3">[3]</a></sup>

Fransham also portrayed Charles in director [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s film <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavenly_Creatures" title="wikipedia:Heavenly Creatures">Heavenly Creatures</a>.
