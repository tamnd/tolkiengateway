**Benjamin Fransham** is a Kiwi actor who portrayed [Thandronen](../T/Thandronen.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and an [Elf](../E/Elves.md) Soldier in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* [season 1](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1.md) episode *[A Shadow of the Past](../A/A_Shadow_of_the_Past.md)*.

He was a stunt performer in [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)<sup id="cite_ref-AAANZ_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAANZ-3">[3]</a></sup> and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* [season 1](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1.md) episode *[A Shadow of the Past](../A/A_Shadow_of_the_Past.md)* and a motion-capture performer in [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-AAANZ_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AAANZ-3">[3]</a></sup>

Fransham also portrayed Charles in director [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md)'s film <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavenly_Creatures" title="wikipedia:Heavenly Creatures">Heavenly Creatures</a>.
