**BEL**, or **MBEL**, is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "large".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

It seems like [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") experimented with the form MELEK, signifying "great, mighty, powerful", when trying to devise an etymology for the name *[Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth")*. However, in what appears to be the later revision, *Melkor* was made a derivative of BEL, MBEL.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *melek* ("mighty"); *melehta* ("mighty"); *melehte* ("might, power (inherent)")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[beleg](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg")*; *belaith* ("mighty")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root BEL-, signifying "strong".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
