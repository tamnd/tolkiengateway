The **Bonfire Glade** was a reminder of the strange 'war' that took place between the malevolent trees of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) of [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), sometime between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2340](/wiki/Third_Age_2340 "Third Age 2340") and [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018").

The trees attacked the [Hedge](/wiki/High_Hay "High Hay") that protected the [Bucklanders](/wiki/Bucklanders), and in retaliation, the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) invaded the forest and burnt hundreds of trees in a huge bonfire. The Bonfire Glade was the place of that great burning, and even after many years, the trees left it an open and empty place.

[Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") led [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") to the glade and out the other side on a path leading into the heart of the Old Forest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
