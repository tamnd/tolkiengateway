The **Bonfire Glade** was a reminder of the strange 'war' that took place between the malevolent trees of the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) of [Buckland](Buckland.md), sometime between [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2340](../T/Third_Age_2340.md) and [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md).

The trees attacked the [Hedge](../H/High_Hay.md) that protected the [Bucklanders](Bucklanders.md), and in retaliation, the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) invaded the forest and burnt hundreds of trees in a huge bonfire. The Bonfire Glade was the place of that great burning, and even after many years, the trees left it an open and empty place.

[Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) led [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) to the glade and out the other side on a path leading into the heart of the Old Forest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
