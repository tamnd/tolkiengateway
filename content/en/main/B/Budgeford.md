**Budgeford** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") settlement in the [eastern part](/wiki/Eastfarthing "Eastfarthing") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), so close to the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) that it fell in the district known as [Bridgefields](/wiki/Bridgefields). It lay on the tributary of the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) known simply as [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water"), north of [Whitfurrows](/wiki/Whitfurrows). The town of Budgeford was the seat of the [Bolgers](/wiki/Bolger_Family "Bolger Family"), an important family among the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The 'ford' in its name apparently derives from a ford across this stream. On the [Shire Map](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire "A Part of the Shire") the name is written **Budge Ford**, perhaps denoting especially the ford.

*Budge-* was an obscure [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) element of no clear meaning in [Westron](/wiki/Westron), believed to be connected to *bulge* and [Bolgers](/wiki/Bolgers).<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> Based on this, [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) has suggested that the name represents a possible [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") form \**Bolgraford* "Ford of the Bolgers".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
