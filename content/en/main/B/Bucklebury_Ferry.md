**Bucklebury Ferry** was a raft-ferry at the main crossing point of the River [Brandywine](/wiki/Baranduin "Baranduin") from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), about ten miles south of the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge).<sup id="cite_ref-consp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-consp-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From the [Causeway](/wiki/Causeway) on the west bank, two tall white posts marked the entrance of the lane;<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> the straight lane ran about a hundred yards, edged with large white-washed stones, leading to a gangway and broad wooden landing-stage. Near the bank were some bollards, and two high posts with lanterns.<sup id="cite_ref-consp_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-consp-1">[1]</a></sup>

On the east side the bank was steep and a winding lane climbed up from the landing. The ferry-boat itself was large and flat.<sup id="cite_ref-consp_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-consp-1">[1]</a></sup>

The lane was well-kept and every night someone lit the lanterns on the lamp-posts.<sup id="cite_ref-consp_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-consp-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Ferry was said to be *twenty* miles south of the Bridge by [Merry](/wiki/Merry)<sup id="cite_ref-consp_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-consp-1">[1]</a></sup> but this was corrected to *ten* in the [50th Anniversary Edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition)> "The Lord of the Rings (50th Anniversary Edition)").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
