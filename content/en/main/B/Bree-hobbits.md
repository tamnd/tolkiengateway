**Bree-hobbits** were the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) native to [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and the [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land) around; their interaction with [Men](/wiki/Men) (who also lived in the land), made them more open to the outside world than their parochial [cousins](/wiki/Shire-hobbits "Shire-hobbits") in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Bree itself was a very ancient township of [Men](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree"). The first [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) (mostly [Fallohides](/wiki/Fallohides) and [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots)) came there from the east in about [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300") fleeing [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-hob_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hob-2">[2]</a></sup> This made Bree the only place in the [world](/wiki/Arda "Arda") where Hobbits and Men lived together side by side. Most of the Hobbits of the Bree-land lived in [Staddle](/wiki/Staddle), a village on the southeastern slopes of the [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill), but some also dwelt in Bree itself, living in [Hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-holes) burrowed in the hill above the houses of Men.

It was where and when the Hobbits (who by that time were speaking a language probably related to that of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>) adopted the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) tongue; they also learned literacy with the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar), in the fashion of the [Dúnedain of Arnor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-hob_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hob-2">[2]</a></sup> The Hobbits of Bree claimed to have originated most Hobbit customs, and certainly it was thought that they were the ones who first discovered the [smoking](/wiki/Smoking "Smoking") properties of [pipe-weed](/wiki/Pipe-weed "Pipe-weed").<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601") two Fallohides, [Marcho](/wiki/Marcho) and [Blanco](/wiki/Blanco) with permission from King [Argeleb II](/wiki/Argeleb_II) of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) led a group of colonists from Bree westward across [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) and founded [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-hob_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hob-2">[2]</a></sup> The Bree-hobbits, therefore, tended to look down somewhat on their cousins in the Shire, referring to them as 'Colonists'<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[4]</a></sup> and 'Outsiders'.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup> On the other hand, the [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) considered Bree-hobbits as [Outsiders](/wiki/Outsiders) and thought of them as dull and rustic.

At the end of the Third Age, the Bree-hobbits still occasionally travelled west to the Shire, but they rarely ventured further than [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) or the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), those parts of the Shire that were closest to their home.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup> However ancient close relationships between Bree-land and the Shire were not completely forgotten. [Brandybucks](/wiki/Brandybuck_family "Brandybuck family") had Bree-blood in them<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup>, and [Briffo Boffin](/wiki/Briffo_Boffin) moved from the Shire to Bree-land in [T.A. 2810.](/wiki/Third_Age_2810 "Third Age 2810")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) two hobbits, [Bob](/wiki/Bob) and [Nob](/wiki/Nob) worked at the *[Prancing Pony](/wiki/Prancing_Pony)*. When the [Travelers](/wiki/Travelers) entered the common room of the inn, they associated with native hobbits and travelers.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Families {#Families}

The more prominent Hobbit families living in the Bree-land at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) were [Banks](/wiki/Banks_Family "Banks Family"), [Brockhouse](/wiki/Brockhouse_Family "Brockhouse Family"), [Longholes](/wiki/Longholes_Family "Longholes Family"), [Mugwort](/wiki/Mugwort_Family "Mugwort Family"), [Sandheaver](/wiki/Sandheaver_Family "Sandheaver Family"), [Tunnelly](/wiki/Tunnelly_Family "Tunnelly Family") and [Underhill](/wiki/Underhill_Family "Underhill Family").

There were also hobbits by the name of Banks living in the Shire, and [Underhill](</wiki/Underhill_(Hobbiton)> "Underhill (Hobbiton)") was a hamlet of Hobbiton, but we can do no more than speculate on the links between these families and regions.
