**Bree-hobbits** were the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) native to [Bree](Bree.md) and the [Bree-land](Bree-land.md) around; their interaction with [Men](../M/Men.md) (who also lived in the land), made them more open to the outside world than their parochial [cousins](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Bree itself was a very ancient township of [Men](../M/Men_of_Bree.md). The first [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) (mostly [Fallohides](../F/Fallohides.md) and [Harfoots](../H/Harfoots.md)) came there from the east in about [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md) fleeing [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-hob_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hob-2">[2]</a></sup> This made Bree the only place in the [world](../A/Arda.md) where Hobbits and Men lived together side by side. Most of the Hobbits of the Bree-land lived in [Staddle](../S/Staddle.md), a village on the southeastern slopes of the [Bree-hill](Bree-hill.md), but some also dwelt in Bree itself, living in [Hobbit-holes](../H/Hobbit-holes.md) burrowed in the hill above the houses of Men.

It was where and when the Hobbits (who by that time were speaking a language probably related to that of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>) adopted the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) tongue; they also learned literacy with the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md), in the fashion of the [Dúnedain of Arnor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-hob_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hob-2">[2]</a></sup> The Hobbits of Bree claimed to have originated most Hobbit customs, and certainly it was thought that they were the ones who first discovered the [smoking](../S/Smoking.md) properties of [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1601](../T/Third_Age_1601.md) two Fallohides, [Marcho](../M/Marcho.md) and [Blanco](Blanco.md) with permission from King [Argeleb II](../A/Argeleb_II.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) led a group of colonists from Bree westward across [Baranduin](Baranduin.md) and founded [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-TA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-hob_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hob-2">[2]</a></sup> The Bree-hobbits, therefore, tended to look down somewhat on their cousins in the Shire, referring to them as 'Colonists'<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">[4]</a></sup> and 'Outsiders'.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup> On the other hand, the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) considered Bree-hobbits as [Outsiders](../O/Outsiders.md) and thought of them as dull and rustic.

At the end of the Third Age, the Bree-hobbits still occasionally travelled west to the Shire, but they rarely ventured further than [Buckland](Buckland.md) or the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md), those parts of the Shire that were closest to their home.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup> However ancient close relationships between Bree-land and the Shire were not completely forgotten. [Brandybucks](Brandybuck_family.md) had Bree-blood in them<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup>, and [Briffo Boffin](Briffo_Boffin.md) moved from the Shire to Bree-land in [T.A. 2810.](../T/Third_Age_2810.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

During the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) two hobbits, [Bob](Bob.md) and [Nob](../N/Nob.md) worked at the *[Prancing Pony](../P/Prancing_Pony.md)*. When the [Travelers](../T/Travelers.md) entered the common room of the inn, they associated with native hobbits and travelers.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Families {#Families}

The more prominent Hobbit families living in the Bree-land at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) were [Banks](Banks_Family~66347.md), [Brockhouse](Brockhouse_Family~66352.md), [Longholes](../L/Longholes_Family.md), [Mugwort](../M/Mugwort_Family.md), [Sandheaver](../S/Sandheaver_Family.md), [Tunnelly](../T/Tunnelly_Family.md) and [Underhill](../U/Underhill_Family.md).

There were also hobbits by the name of Banks living in the Shire, and [Underhill](<../U/Underhill_(Hobbiton).md>) was a hamlet of Hobbiton, but we can do no more than speculate on the links between these families and regions.
