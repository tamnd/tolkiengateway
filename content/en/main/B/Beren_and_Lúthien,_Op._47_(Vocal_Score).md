Published by Zarathustra Music in 2019, the complete Vocal Score of [Paul Corfield Godfrey](../P/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey.md)'s *[Beren and Lúthien, Op. 47 (Opera)](<Beren_and_Lúthien,_Op._47_(Opera).md>)* updated to reflect amendments made during the production of Volante Opera Production's Demo Recording of the work.

This is a 119 page A4 sized book with full gloss covers.

This is a complete piano reduction of the score with introduction by the composer, synopsis and translations of the Elvish.
