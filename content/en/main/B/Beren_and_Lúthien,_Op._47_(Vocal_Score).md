Published by Zarathustra Music in 2019, the complete Vocal Score of [Paul Corfield Godfrey](/wiki/Paul_Corfield_Godfrey)'s *[Beren and Lúthien, Op. 47 (Opera)](</wiki/Beren_and_L%C3%BAthien,_Op._47_(Opera)>)* updated to reflect amendments made during the production of Volante Opera Production's Demo Recording of the work.

This is a 119 page A4 sized book with full gloss covers.

This is a complete piano reduction of the score with introduction by the composer, synopsis and translations of the Elvish.
