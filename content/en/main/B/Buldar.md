**Buldar** was a [Man](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) of [Agar](../A/Agar.md) who was the father of [Hazad Longbeard](../H/Hazad_Longbeard.md) and the grandfather of [Tal-Elmar](<../T/Tal-Elmar_(character).md>) during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md).

## History {#History}

During his days of youth, Buldar fought in the army of the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=North_King&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="North King (page does not exist)">North King</a> in the valley of [Ishmalog](../I/Ishmalog.md) against the "<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Fell_Folk_of_the_East&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fell Folk of the East (page does not exist)">Fell Folk of the East</a>" and came back home to Agar with "a wound, a sword and a woman". The latter, named [Elmar](../E/Elmar.md), he wedded mostly against her wish and had many sons, of which the youngest was Hazad. Elmar warned Buldar, however,that though ehe was forced to live with him she would have her vengeance: <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Tal-Elmar~25132.md" title="Tal-Elmar">one</a> of her bloodline would arise in Agar and bring about the downfall of Buldar's folk.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a penciled note added to the manuscripts, J.R.R. Tolkien proposed that Buldar should be left out of the story and Elmar to be Hazad's wife. However, in the published version, this change did not occur.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The rejected version of Buldar’s name is Tal-Bulda.
