**Bree-land** was a small inhabited region around [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill),<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> about forty miles east of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which contained a few settlements:

- **[Bree](/wiki/Bree)** was under and on the western slopes of Bree-hill. It was the chief village of the area with the famous *[Prancing Pony Inn](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony")* and was populated by Men and Hobbits. The [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) ran through Bree.

- **[Staddle](/wiki/Staddle)** on the south-eastern slopes of Bree-hill was populated by Men and Hobbits. Pipe-weed grew well on the south-slopes of the hill and was smoked by Bree-hobbits. Most of the Hobbits of Bree-land lived in Staddle in hobbit-holes but there were also houses for Men in Staddle. It was the only of the villages (other than Bree itself) visible from the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) and was located north of the East Road.

- **[Combe](/wiki/Combe)** was located north of the East Road in a deep valley a little further eastward from Staddle.

- **[Archet](/wiki/Archet)** was located north of the East Road north of Combe among the trees probably on the southern edge of the [Chetwood](/wiki/Chetwood).

## History {#History}

The origins of its [inhabitants](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") are obscure, but apparently they were [Pre-Númenóreans](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), related to the [Dunlendings](/wiki/Dunlendings), who had moved far to the North during the [Dark Years](/wiki/Dark_Years), and the Bree-landers managed to survive through the violent history of Middle-earth until they were absorbed into [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor). It was also the only place in Middle-earth where Men lived with Hobbits on a daily basis: the population of the Bree-land had become a mix of Men and Hobbits, who had migrated from [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300"). The ratio varied between each of the four townships of the Bree-land.

In any case, they were still thriving in Bree long after Arnor had collapsed and the memory of [fallen kings](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor") faded into the grass. The now independent land enjoyed the (unknowing) protection of the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North). After the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Bree-land became part of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) offered to construct new names for place names and to find places for them on the map of Bree in a [letter](/wiki/Letter_276 "Letter 276") that he wrote to [Dick Plotz](/wiki/Dick_Plotz) on 12 September 1965.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that there were more settlements in Bree-land than the four, which were mentioned in *The Lord of the Rings*.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Arnor:_The_Land" title="Arnor: The Land">Arnor: The Land</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Bree-land was settled by <a href="/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Pre-Númenóreans">Pre-Númenóreans</a>, who also built the initial burial mounds. They remained there for two hundred years, until they left to fight in the <a href="/wiki/War_of_Wrath" title="War of Wrath">War of Wrath</a>. Other Men moved in immediately, but they were overwhelmed by the creatures of Morgoth. In the <a href="/wiki/Second_Age" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, the region was settled by the Mebion Bron, a wandering people related to the <a href="/wiki/Dunlendings" title="Dunlendings">Dunlendings</a>, who fled the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron" title="War of the Elves and Sauron">War of the Elves and Sauron</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bree-land is a major region and one of the starting locations available to new characters. It encompasses not only the four villages and their immediate surroundings, but all the terrain from <a href="/wiki/Buckland" title="Buckland">Buckland</a> in the far west to the <a href="/wiki/Weather_Hills" title="Weather Hills">Weather Hills</a> to the far east. The <a href="/wiki/Old_Forest" title="Old Forest">Old Forest</a>, <a href="/wiki/Barrow-downs" title="Barrow-downs">Barrow-downs</a>, <a href="/wiki/Midgewater_Marshes" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a> and <a href="/wiki/Chetwood" title="Chetwood">Chetwood</a> are all included within its boundaries.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, Bree-land is threatened by both <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a> and <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. <a href="/wiki/Saruman" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Ruffians" title="Ruffians">ruffians</a> and <a href="/wiki/Half-orcs" title="Half-orcs">half-orcs</a> came up from the south and camp at <a href="/wiki/Andrath" title="Andrath">Andrath</a> and the &#34;Bree-fields&#34;, while Angmar&#39;s Orcs encroach from the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>. Angmar also temporarily secured the allegiance of the &#34;Blackwold&#34; bandit gang, which sets off the events of the Bree-land introduction quests.</dd></dl>
