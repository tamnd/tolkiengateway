**Bree-land** was a small inhabited region around [Bree-hill](Bree-hill.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> about forty miles east of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which contained a few settlements:

- **[Bree](Bree.md)** was under and on the western slopes of Bree-hill. It was the chief village of the area with the famous *[Prancing Pony Inn](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md)* and was populated by Men and Hobbits. The [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) ran through Bree.

- **[Staddle](../S/Staddle.md)** on the south-eastern slopes of Bree-hill was populated by Men and Hobbits. Pipe-weed grew well on the south-slopes of the hill and was smoked by Bree-hobbits. Most of the Hobbits of Bree-land lived in Staddle in hobbit-holes but there were also houses for Men in Staddle. It was the only of the villages (other than Bree itself) visible from the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) and was located north of the East Road.

- **[Combe](../C/Combe.md)** was located north of the East Road in a deep valley a little further eastward from Staddle.

- **[Archet](../A/Archet.md)** was located north of the East Road north of Combe among the trees probably on the southern edge of the [Chetwood](../C/Chetwood.md).

## History {#History}

The origins of its [inhabitants](../M/Men_of_Bree.md) are obscure, but apparently they were [Pre-Númenóreans](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md), related to the [Dunlendings](../D/Dunlendings.md), who had moved far to the North during the [Dark Years](../D/Dark_Years.md), and the Bree-landers managed to survive through the violent history of Middle-earth until they were absorbed into [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). It was also the only place in Middle-earth where Men lived with Hobbits on a daily basis: the population of the Bree-land had become a mix of Men and Hobbits, who had migrated from [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md). The ratio varied between each of the four townships of the Bree-land.

In any case, they were still thriving in Bree long after Arnor had collapsed and the memory of [fallen kings](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) faded into the grass. The now independent land enjoyed the (unknowing) protection of the [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md). After the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Bree-land became part of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) offered to construct new names for place names and to find places for them on the map of Bree in a [letter](../L/Letter_276.md) that he wrote to [Dick Plotz](../D/Dick_Plotz.md) on 12 September 1965.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> As a consequence, it is possible that there were more settlements in Bree-land than the four, which were mentioned in *The Lord of the Rings*.

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1997: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="../A/Arnor%253A_The_Land.md" title="Arnor: The Land">Arnor: The Land</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Bree-land was settled by <a href="../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md" title="Pre-Númenóreans">Pre-Númenóreans</a>, who also built the initial burial mounds. They remained there for two hundred years, until they left to fight in the <a href="../W/War_of_Wrath.md" title="War of Wrath">War of Wrath</a>. Other Men moved in immediately, but they were overwhelmed by the creatures of Morgoth. In the <a href="../S/Second_Age.md" title="Second Age">Second Age</a>, the region was settled by the Mebion Bron, a wandering people related to the <a href="../D/Dunlendings.md" title="Dunlendings">Dunlendings</a>, who fled the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md" title="War of the Elves and Sauron">War of the Elves and Sauron</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bree-land is a major region and one of the starting locations available to new characters. It encompasses not only the four villages and their immediate surroundings, but all the terrain from <a href="Buckland.md" title="Buckland">Buckland</a> in the far west to the <a href="../W/Weather_Hills.md" title="Weather Hills">Weather Hills</a> to the far east. The <a href="../O/Old_Forest.md" title="Old Forest">Old Forest</a>, <a href="Barrow-downs.md" title="Barrow-downs">Barrow-downs</a>, <a href="../M/Midgewater_Marshes.md" title="Midgewater Marshes">Midgewater Marshes</a> and <a href="../C/Chetwood.md" title="Chetwood">Chetwood</a> are all included within its boundaries.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>During the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, Bree-land is threatened by both <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a> and <a href="../A/Angmar.md" title="Angmar">Angmar</a>. <a href="../S/Saruman.md" title="Saruman">Saruman</a>&#39;s <a href="../R/Ruffians.md" title="Ruffians">ruffians</a> and <a href="../H/Half-orcs.md" title="Half-orcs">half-orcs</a> came up from the south and camp at <a href="../A/Andrath.md" title="Andrath">Andrath</a> and the &#34;Bree-fields&#34;, while Angmar&#39;s Orcs encroach from the <a href="../N/North_Downs.md" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>. Angmar also temporarily secured the allegiance of the &#34;Blackwold&#34; bandit gang, which sets off the events of the Bree-land introduction quests.</dd></dl>
