**Bosco Boffin** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Bosco was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1167](/wiki/Third_Age_2767 "Third Age 2767"), the son of [Buffo Boffin](/wiki/Buffo_Boffin) and [Ivy Goodenough](/wiki/Ivy_Goodenough). He had one son, [Otto](/wiki/Otto_Boffin "Otto Boffin"), who was born in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1212](/wiki/Third_Age_2812 "Third Age 2812"). Bosco died in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1258](/wiki/Third_Age_2858 "Third Age 2858").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
