The **Bunce family** was a family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits). [Mimosa Bunce](/wiki/Mimosa_Bunce) is the only recorded member of the family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

However, according to [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s poem *[Perry-the-Winkle](/wiki/Perry-the-Winkle)*, there was an "old [Mrs. Bunce](/wiki/Mrs._Bunce)" living in [Michel Delving](/wiki/Michel_Delving); this character might have been based on an actual Hobbit, or just a character in the Hobbit folklore poetry.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
