**BERÉK** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") without any given signification, except for what can be determined from its derivatives.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *berékā-* ("wild")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *verka* ("wild")
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *[bregol](/wiki/Bregol "Bregol")* ("violent, sudden"); *breged* ("violence, suddenness"); *breitho* ("break out suddenly")
