**Brocelïande** is an American band based in California. They have released an album inspired by the works of Tolkien, titled [The Starlit Jewel: Songs of J. R. R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Starlit_Jewel:_Songs_of_J._R._R._Tolkien).

They have also released four other studio albums, which have no connection with Tolkien.

## Members {#Members}

- Margaret Davis: Vocals, Celtic Harp, Flute, Recorders
- Kristoph Klover: Vocals, Guitars, Octave Mandolin, Oboe
- Kris Yenney: Vocals, Cello, Nyckelharpa, Hardanger cello d’Amore, Percussion
