**Beowulf** can refer to:

## The original work {#The_original_work}

- <b><a href="/wiki/Beowulf_(poem)" title="Beowulf (poem)"><i>Beowulf</i> (poem)</a></b>, an 8th-century epic long poem in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), which Tolkien much studied.

## Books by J.R.R. Tolkien {#Books_by_J.R.R._Tolkien}

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Beowulf:_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics" title="Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics">Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics</a></b></i>, lecture delivered in [1936](/wiki/1936), booklet published in [1937](/wiki/1937).
    - Reprinted in *[The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays)* (1983).

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Critics" title="Beowulf and the Critics">Beowulf and the Critics</a></b></i>, edited by [Michael D.C. Drout](/wiki/Michael_D.C._Drout), published in [2002](/wiki/2002).

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary" title="Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary">Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary</a></b></i>, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), published in [2014](/wiki/2014).

#### Contributed by Tolkien {#Contributed_by_Tolkien}

- *[Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment)*, a 1940 edition includes *[Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of 'Beowulf'](/wiki/Prefatory_Remarks_on_Prose_Translation_of_%27Beowulf%27)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

#### In other languages {#In_other_languages}

- *[Om Beowulfsagan](/wiki/Om_Beowulfsagan)*, ("On the Tale of Beowulf") a 1975 Swedish collection of essays by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

## Books by others {#Books_by_others}

- *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](/wiki/Beowulf:_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem)*, a 1921 book by [R.W. Chambers](/wiki/R.W._Chambers).
- [*Beowulf* (Crossley-Holland translation)](</wiki/Beowulf_(Crossley-Holland_translation)> "Beowulf (Crossley-Holland translation)"), a 1968 translation of *Beowulf* by Kevin Crossley-Holland
- *[Beowulf and the Dragon](/wiki/Beowulf_and_the_Dragon)*, illustrated by [Anke Eißmann](/wiki/Anke_Ei%C3%9Fmann) and a Foreword by [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey).
- *[Beowulf: Translation and Commentary](</wiki/Beowulf:_Translation_and_Commentary_(Tom_Shippey)> "Beowulf: Translation and Commentary (Tom Shippey)")*, a 2023 translation of *Beowulf* by [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey).

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- "[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](/wiki/Beowulf:_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics)", a chapter in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays)*.
- "[On Translating Beowulf](/wiki/On_Translating_Beowulf)", a chapter in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays)*.
