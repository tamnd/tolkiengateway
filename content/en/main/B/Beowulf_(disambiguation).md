**Beowulf** can refer to:

## The original work {#The_original_work}

- <b><a href="Beowulf_(poem).md" title="Beowulf (poem)"><i>Beowulf</i> (poem)</a></b>, an 8th-century epic long poem in [Old English](../O/Old_English.md), which Tolkien much studied.

## Books by J.R.R. Tolkien {#Books_by_J.R.R._Tolkien}

- <i><b><a href="Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md" title="Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics">Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics</a></b></i>, lecture delivered in [1936](../_/1936.md), booklet published in [1937](../_/1937.md).
    - Reprinted in *[The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays.md)* (1983).

- <i><b><a href="Beowulf_and_the_Critics.md" title="Beowulf and the Critics">Beowulf and the Critics</a></b></i>, edited by [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md), published in [2002](../_/2002.md).

- <i><b><a href="Beowulf%253A_A_Translation_and_Commentary.md" title="Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary">Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary</a></b></i>, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), published in [2014](../_/2014.md).

#### Contributed by Tolkien {#Contributed_by_Tolkien}

- *[Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment](Beowulf_and_the_Finnsburg_Fragment.md)*, a 1940 edition includes *[Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of 'Beowulf'](../P/Prefatory_Remarks_on_Prose_Translation_of_'Beowulf'.md)* by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

#### In other languages {#In_other_languages}

- *[Om Beowulfsagan](../O/Om_Beowulfsagan.md)*, ("On the Tale of Beowulf") a 1975 Swedish collection of essays by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

## Books by others {#Books_by_others}

- *[Beowulf: An Introduction to the Study of the Poem](Beowulf%253A_An_Introduction_to_the_Study_of_the_Poem.md)*, a 1921 book by [R.W. Chambers](../R/R.W._Chambers.md).
- [*Beowulf* (Crossley-Holland translation)](<Beowulf_(Crossley-Holland_translation).md>), a 1968 translation of *Beowulf* by Kevin Crossley-Holland
- *[Beowulf and the Dragon](Beowulf_and_the_Dragon.md)*, illustrated by [Anke Eißmann](../A/Anke_Eißmann.md) and a Foreword by [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md).
- *[Beowulf: Translation and Commentary](<Beowulf%253A_Translation_and_Commentary_(Tom_Shippey).md>)*, a 2023 translation of *Beowulf* by [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md).

## Chapters {#Chapters}

- "[Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics](Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md)", a chapter in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays.md)*.
- "[On Translating Beowulf](../O/On_Translating_Beowulf.md)", a chapter in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays.md)*.
