**Brithombar** was the western of the two [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas), at the mouth of the River [Brithon](/wiki/Brithon), from which it took its name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was occupied by the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim) since the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) until it was invaded and destroyed<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [473](/wiki/First_Age_473 "First Age 473") along with its twin, [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

*Brithombar* comes from the name of the river *[Brithon](/wiki/Brithon)*, which means "pebbly", and *[-mbar](/wiki/-mbar)* ("dwelling, house").
