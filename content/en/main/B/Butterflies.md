**Butterflies** were insects with large wings, often colorful.

## History {#History}

A legend says that all kinds of butterflies appeared in the [Land of Willows](../N/Nan-tathren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Butterflies are noted for their wings bearing colorful markings, and the "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/purple_emperor" title="wikipedia:purple emperor">purple emperor</a>" type loves the top of [oak](../O/Oak.md)-woods. In [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) however, [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md) found hundreds of totally black butterflies, without any markings, at the treetops and he imagined them as 'black emperors'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>

Some of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)'s fireworks fluttered like butterflies.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

*[Wilwarin](../W/Wilwarin.md)* is also a name the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) gave to a <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Constellation&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Constellation (page does not exist)">constellation</a> of stars.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md)/[Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), the word for "butterfly" is *wilwarin* (pl. *wilwarindi*). Cognates of the same meaning are [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) *vilverin*, [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *gwilwileth*, and [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) *gwilwering*.<sup id="cite_ref-ety_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ety-6">[4]</a></sup> In earlier [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md) (the precursor of Noldorin) "butterfly" is *gwilbrin* (or *-vrin*).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[5]</a></sup>

The words *wilwarindëa* and *wilwarindië* (older [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) form was *wilwarindeën*<sup id="cite_ref-matc_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-matc-8">[6]</a></sup>) are the respective singular and plural forms meaning "like a *wilwarin* or butterfly".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[7]</a></sup> In Qenya there is also the word *wilwarindon* ("like a butterfly").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[8]</a></sup>

The words come from the same root (*wil-*, "fly, float in air") as Q. *[vilya](<../V/Vilya_(word).md>)*/S. *[gwilith](../G/Gwilith.md)* "air", and the name for the Ring of Air, *[Vilya](../V/Vilya.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-ety_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ety-6">[4]</a></sup>
