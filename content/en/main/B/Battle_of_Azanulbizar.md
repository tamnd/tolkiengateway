The **Battle of Azanulbizar** was the final climatic battle in the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md). It was fought beneath the [East-gate of Moria](../G/Great_Gates.md) in the valley of Azanulbizar, called *[Nanduhirion](../N/Nanduhirion.md)* in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md). Therefore the event is also known as the **Battle of Nanduhirion** and sometimes the **Battle of the Mines of Moria**.

## Prelude {#Prelude}

The War of the Dwarves and Orcs began when [Azog](../A/Azog.md) the [Orc](../O/Orcs.md)-chieftain of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) captured and mutilated [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md), King of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md). Azog branded his own name in runes onto Thrór's severed head, then let Thrór's companion [Nár](../N/Nár.md) escape so that all [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) would know that an Orc now ruled Moria. Full of righteous fury, Thrór's son [Thráin II](../T/Thráin.md) summoned a great army of Dwarves, including those not of Durin's Folk ([Firebeards](../F/Firebeards.md) and [Broadbeams](Broadbeams.md) from the [Blue Mountains](Blue_Mountains.md), and others from the far East of Middle-earth). For six years they systematically sacked the Orc strongholds of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), until only Moria was left. There the Orcs that had survived the destruction had gathered to Azog.

## Battle {#Battle}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/Jacek_Kopalski_-_Náin_at_Azanulbizar.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/57/Jacek_Kopalski_-_N%C3%A1in_at_Azanulbizar.jpg/300px-Jacek_Kopalski_-_N%C3%A1in_at_Azanulbizar.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="457" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/5/57/Jacek_Kopalski_-_N%C3%A1in_at_Azanulbizar.jpg/450px-Jacek_Kopalski_-_N%C3%A1in_at_Azanulbizar.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/5/57/Jacek_Kopalski_-_N%C3%A1in_at_Azanulbizar.jpg 2x" data-file-width="576" data-file-height="878"/></a><figcaption><i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../N/Náin.md" title="Náin">Náin</a> at <a class="mw-redirect" href="../A/Azanulbizar.md" title="Azanulbizar">Azanulbizar</a></i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Jacek_Kopalski.md" title="Category:Images by Jacek Kopalski">Jacek Kopalski</a></figcaption></figure>

The battle began on a dark winter day, and no sun was said to have shined through the clouds. The Dwarves had marched into the Dimrill Dale where they found the East-Gate and sent up a great noise. They discovered that on the western slopes above thousands of Orcs had gathered, while more still came pouring out of the gate. The Dwarves there stood outnumbered and on the lower end of a sloping hill.

The first Vanguard led by King Thráin, assaulted the slopes only to be driven back with casualties. In a woods near the Mirrormere, the dwarves noted that [Frerin](../F/Frerin.md), youngest son of Thráin was slain along with [Fundin](../F/Fundin.md), father of [Balin](Balin.md); and many others. Thraín himself was wounded, as was his eldest son [Thorin II Oakenshield](../T/Thorin.md), whose shield was broken during the battle, forcing him to resort to using an oak branch that he cut off a tree to defend himself.

Elsewhere, the battle swayed back and forth until [Náin](<../N/Náin_(son_of_Grór).md>) from the [Iron Hills](../I/Iron_Hills.md) arrived with a contingent of fresh troops. Náin and his Dwarves cut through the Orc lines with their [mattocks](../M/Mattocks.md) shouting, "AZOG! AZOG! AZOG!" until they had reached the steps of the gate, at which Naín called Azog to come out and fight. When Azog emerged from the inner gate with his guards, Náin was exhausted and half blind with rage. He tried to swing as hard as he could, but Azog darted aside and Náin missed, splintering his mattock on the stone. The orc kicked him in the leg when he dodged the Dwarf's blow, making him stumble, at which point Azog attempted to hew his neck with a swing, succeeding only in breaking Náin's neck because of the strong mail he was wearing. Náin died instantly.

Even as Azog gloated over his duel, he looked out over the valley which the east gate overlooks, and came to the realization that his entire force was routed. Those that could were fleeing southwards, and all his guard was dead. With that he fled back to the gate. Náin's son, [Dáin](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md), leaped up the steps after him with his red axe, and there before the gate he decapitated the Orc chieftain, thus ending the battle. The slaying of Azog was considered a great feat, as Dáin was only 32 years of age (very young for a Dwarf). Dáin would later become [King under the Mountain](../K/King_under_the_Mountain.md) as [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppDurin_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppDurin-1">[1]</a></sup>

Azog's head was then impaled on a pike, and bag of coins he had thrown at Nàr years before was stuffed in his mouth.

## Aftermath {#Aftermath}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Steamey_-_Vengeance.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Steamey_-_Vengeance.jpg/300px-Steamey_-_Vengeance.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="555" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/ac/Steamey_-_Vengeance.jpg/450px-Steamey_-_Vengeance.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/a/ac/Steamey_-_Vengeance.jpg 2x" data-file-width="460" data-file-height="851"/></a><figcaption><i>Vengeance</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Steamey.md" title="Category:Images by Steamey">Steamey</a></figcaption></figure>

The Dwarves were victorious, but half of their forces were dead or mortally wounded. The Orcs suffered even worse casualties, with ten thousand dead. After the battle, Thráin wanted to enter and reclaim Moria, the ancestral home of Durin's folk. However, due to their losses, the other Houses were not willing to participate, and since Dáin had seen [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md) beyond the East-gate, Thráin refrained from entering.<sup id="cite_ref-AppDurin_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppDurin-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Dwarves stripped their dead so the Orcs could not plunder them, and cut down all the trees in the valley, which was to remain bare ever after. They made many pyres on which to burn their dead. They could not bury them all in tombs of stone, as was their custom, because it would take too long. From then on those that died in Dimrill Dale were remembered proudly as [burned Dwarves](Burned_Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Burned_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burned-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Houses parted ways, returning to their homes to the North, East, and West. Thráin, with what was left of the Longbeard contingent, went back to [Dunland](../D/Dunland.md) and wandered in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), eventually settling in the Southern Blue Mountains. There Durin's folk repopulated slowly, waiting for the day when they could take back the halls of [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and Khazad-dum.<sup id="cite_ref-AppDurin_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppDurin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Referred to as the <b>Battle of <a href="../M/Moria.md" title="Moria">Moria</a></b> in this <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)">film trilogy</a> (and portrayed more as an isolated battle rather than the final conflict for an entire war), the battle is presented in a flashback sequence as <a href="Balin.md" title="Balin">Balin</a> recounts <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin</a>&#39;s past to <a href="Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a>. The narrative of the battle has been heavily condensed. In the film version, rather than recounting Thrór&#39;s death at the hands of <a href="../A/Azog.md" title="Azog">Azog</a> and the resulting war of many years, Balin simply says that after <a href="../S/Smaug.md" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> drove the <a href="../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md" title="Dwarves of Erebor">Dwarves</a> from <a href="../L/Lonely_Mountain.md" title="Lonely Mountain">Erebor</a>, Thrór attempted to lead all of his people back to their ancestral realm in Moria, only to find that the <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> had gotten there first. <a href="../A/Azog.md" title="Azog">Azog</a> does decapitate <a href="../T/Thrór.md" title="Thrór">Thrór</a>, but the deed takes place during the battle itself, and the head is thrown at Thorin&#39;s feet rather than <a href="../N/Nár.md" title="Nár">Nár</a>&#39;s. Balin similarly condenses <a href="../T/Thráin.md" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>&#39;s story, stating that he went mad with grief and wandered away, and his people never knew if he was dead or captured. <a href="../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a> is completely absent from the story, and it is Thorin who rallies the dwarves by facing Azog in single combat and hacking off his left arm at the elbow. The wounded Azog is dragged back into Moria by his subordinates, and the Dwarves assume he has died of his injuries, but he survives to appear in a present-time sub-plot in which he is hunting Thorin and Company for revenge. No mention is made of the burning of the Dwarves&#39; dead, but Balin, <a href="../D/Dwalin.md" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a>, <a href="Bifur.md" title="Bifur">Bifur</a>, and Thorin are seen in the aftermath of the battle walking amidst heaps of their slain kin, and Balin recalls the battle as a pyrrhic victory, and the number of dead as beyond the count of grief, echoing closely the wording of the account from <a href="../A/Appendix_A.md" title="Appendix A">Appendix A</a> of <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a></i>.</dd></dl>

<b>2021: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Azanulbizar during and immediately after the battle is featured as a flashback-style location. The story of the region is framed as reading the written recollections of the veteran &#34;Hermáth Stormhammer&#34;, a close friend of <a href="../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a>, to relive the battle from his point of view.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>King Thráin instructs the kings of the other six Houses to secure the borders of the Dale, including the slopes of <a href="../C/Celebdil.md" title="Celebdil">Zirakzigil</a> and <a href="../F/Fanuidhol.md" title="Fanuidhol">Bundushathûr</a>, to deny Azog&#39;s forces the high ground and slay any Orcs that attempt to flee. The <a href="../D/Dwarves_of_the_Iron_Hills.md" title="Dwarves of the Iron Hills">Dwarves of the Iron Hills</a> are present from the beginning, but held in reserve until, as in the books, they enter the battle to assist Thráin&#39;s vanguard.</dd></dl>
