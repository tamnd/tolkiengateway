<b><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch" title="wikipedia:Birch">Birches</a></b> were smooth-barked forest trees that grew in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>, [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>, [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, and [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "birch" was <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Hwinn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hwinn (page does not exist)">hwinn</a></i>, while in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) was *[hwindë](/wiki/Hwind%C3%AB "Hwindë")*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

A "silver birch" in Sindarin was known as *brethil*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> This consequently gave its name to the Forest of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil).
