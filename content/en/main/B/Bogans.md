<i><b>Bogans</b></i> is a 26-minute fan film, written by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Tait&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Tait (page does not exist)">Peter Tait</a>. It was released on DVD in [2003](/wiki/2003).

## Plot {#Plot}

Three "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/bogan" title="wikipedia:bogan">Bogans</a>", Greg, Ray and Luke, travel across [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) to meet [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson). Their goal is to be cast as extras in *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")*.

They first travel to [Wellington](/wiki/Wellington), where Peter Jackson was shooting the film series. After arriving, they decide to purchase *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* books because none of them actually know anything about it. When they open the first page, they discover that [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbit "Hobbit") live in holes, which gives them the idea to dig a hole to sleep in. They decided to do this in the yard of Peter Jackson, but later realize that they have the wrong Peter.

In a strange turn of fate, two of the actors actually ended up in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*: [Ian Hughes](/wiki/Ian_Hughes) played [Irolas](/wiki/Irolas), and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Tait&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Peter Tait (page does not exist)">Peter Tait</a> was [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat).
