**Banazîr** is a [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name meaning "simple minded". It was sometimes shortened to **Ban**.

It was the first name of [Ban Galpsi](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It comes from the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) element *[ban(a)](</wiki/Ban(a)> "Ban(a)")* meaning "half" and *[zîr](/wiki/Z%C3%AEr "Zîr")* meaning "wise"

## Translations {#Translations}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[Perhael](/wiki/Perhael)*

The name was translated as "Samwise", and Ban as Sam. <span></span>
