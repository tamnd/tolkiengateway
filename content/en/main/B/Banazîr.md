**Banazîr** is a [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) name meaning "simple minded". It was sometimes shortened to **Ban**.

It was the first name of [Ban Galpsi](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It comes from the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) element *[ban(a)](<Ban(a).md>)* meaning "half" and *[zîr](../Z/Zîr.md)* meaning "wise"

## Translations {#Translations}

- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[Perhael](../P/Perhael.md)*

The name was translated as "Samwise", and Ban as Sam. <span></span>
