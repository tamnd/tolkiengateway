**Brandir** was a man of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Brandir was the elder child of [Arachon](/wiki/Arachon) and [Bregil](/wiki/Bregil). His younger sister [Beldis](/wiki/Beldis) married [Handir](/wiki/Handir) of [Brethil](/wiki/Brethil) and their child, known as [Brandir the Lame](/wiki/Brandir "Brandir"), was named after his uncle.<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Brandir* is said to be a compound of *[brand](</wiki/Brand_(word)#Exilic_Noldorin> "Brand (word)")* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=D%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dîr (page does not exist)">dîr</a></i>, meaning "Noble Man".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
