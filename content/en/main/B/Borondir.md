**Borondir Udalraph** (died [15 April](../_/15_April.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md)) was a [Gondorian](../G/Gondor.md) soldier during their war against the [Balchoth](Balchoth.md). He was one of six riders sent to seek the aid of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md), and was the only one to reach them alive. He brought the Northmen to the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md), but died protecting his lord [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Borondir was a great rider who possessed great courage and great endurance, whose family claimed to be descended from a captain of the Northmen who served under the old [kings of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the end of the winter of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2509](../T/Third_Age_2509.md), a large force of [Balchoth](Balchoth.md) was mustering along the southern edge of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md). When Cirion, the [Steward](../R/Ruling_Stewards.md) of Gondor, learned of this danger and called for volunteers to ride to the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), a group of Northmen who lived near the upper [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md), to ask them for help. Borondir came forward and was chosen by Cirion as one of six riders. On [10 March](../_/10_March.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../T/Third_Age_2510.md), Borondir was senty by Cirion in the first of three pairs of riders to ride to the Éothéod. Each pair was sent out with a day's interval between them, and each rider was given a message learned by heart and a small stone incised with the seal of the Stewards.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

Borondir's companion was killed in an ambush near [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md), but Borondir escaped with luck and the speed of his horse. He was pursued as far north as the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md). When he reached the Éothéod on [25 March](../_/25_March.md), he had gone two days without food, and was so spent that he could barely speak Cirion's message to their leader [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md). He was the only messenger that arrived at Éothéod. Nothing was ever heard again of the other four riders.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eorl gathered an army of approximately seven thousand fully-armed riders and some hundreds of horsed archers, and set forth on [6 April](../_/6_April.md). Borondir rode by Eorl's right hand to serve as guide. When a gleaming white mist flowed from the [elven](../E/Elves.md) realm of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) over the river Anduin drove back the glooms of [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) and hid their approach, it seemed to Borondir that [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), the lady of Lothlórien was assisting them on their way past Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Éothéod arrived at the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md) in time to rescue the Gondorian army, which was backed against the [Limlight](../L/Limlight.md) river in a hopeless situation. Borondir was the first to cross the river, and cleft a path to aid Cirion.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup> The arrival of the Éothéod swung the battle, and the Balchoth were crushed,<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but Borondir died defending Cirion.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the battle, Borondir was laid in a tomb in the [Hallows](../H/Hallows.md) of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). He was long remembered in the song of *[Rochon Methestel](../R/Rochon_Methestel.md)*, the "Rider of Last Hope".<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Borondir* is probably a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. Its meaning and components are not glossed, but it possibly means "Steadfast-man" and is a compound of *boron* ("steadfast", "trusty man", "faithful vassal") and *dîr* ("man").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Borondir's *Sindarin*<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> epithet **Udalraph** means "the Stirrupless".<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup>
