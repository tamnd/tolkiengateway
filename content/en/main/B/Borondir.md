**Borondir Udalraph** (died [15 April](/wiki/15_April), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510")) was a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") soldier during their war against the [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth). He was one of six riders sent to seek the aid of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen), and was the only one to reach them alive. He brought the Northmen to the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant), but died protecting his lord [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion).<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Borondir was a great rider who possessed great courage and great endurance, whose family claimed to be descended from a captain of the Northmen who served under the old [kings of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

At the end of the winter of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2509](/wiki/Third_Age_2509 "Third Age 2509"), a large force of [Balchoth](/wiki/Balchoth) was mustering along the southern edge of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood). When Cirion, the [Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards "Ruling Stewards") of Gondor, learned of this danger and called for volunteers to ride to the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), a group of Northmen who lived near the upper [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin), to ask them for help. Borondir came forward and was chosen by Cirion as one of six riders. On [10 March](/wiki/10_March) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), Borondir was senty by Cirion in the first of three pairs of riders to ride to the Éothéod. Each pair was sent out with a day's interval between them, and each rider was given a message learned by heart and a small stone incised with the seal of the Stewards.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

Borondir's companion was killed in an ambush near [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur), but Borondir escaped with luck and the speed of his horse. He was pursued as far north as the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields). When he reached the Éothéod on [25 March](/wiki/25_March), he had gone two days without food, and was so spent that he could barely speak Cirion's message to their leader [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl). He was the only messenger that arrived at Éothéod. Nothing was ever heard again of the other four riders.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eorl gathered an army of approximately seven thousand fully-armed riders and some hundreds of horsed archers, and set forth on [6 April](/wiki/6_April). Borondir rode by Eorl's right hand to serve as guide. When a gleaming white mist flowed from the [elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") realm of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) over the river Anduin drove back the glooms of [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) and hid their approach, it seemed to Borondir that [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), the lady of Lothlórien was assisting them on their way past Dol Guldur.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Éothéod arrived at the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant) in time to rescue the Gondorian army, which was backed against the [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight) river in a hopeless situation. Borondir was the first to cross the river, and cleft a path to aid Cirion.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup> The arrival of the Éothéod swung the battle, and the Balchoth were crushed,<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> but Borondir died defending Cirion.<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the battle, Borondir was laid in a tomb in the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). He was long remembered in the song of *[Rochon Methestel](/wiki/Rochon_Methestel)*, the "Rider of Last Hope".<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Borondir* is probably a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. Its meaning and components are not glossed, but it possibly means "Steadfast-man" and is a compound of *boron* ("steadfast", "trusty man", "faithful vassal") and *dîr* ("man").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Borondir's *Sindarin*<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> epithet **Udalraph** means "the Stirrupless".<sup id="cite_ref-UTNote_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTNote-2">[2]</a></sup>
