**Beldir** (born [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [316](../F/First_Age_316.md)) was a [man](../M/Men.md) of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).

## History {#History}

Beldir was the son of [Belen](Belen.md). He had one son himself, [Belemir](Belemir.md), who was born in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [339](../F/First_Age_339.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>
