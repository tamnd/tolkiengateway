**Beldir** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [316](/wiki/First_Age_316 "First Age 316")) was a [man](/wiki/Men "Men") of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).

## History {#History}

Beldir was the son of [Belen](/wiki/Belen). He had one son himself, [Belemir](/wiki/Belemir), who was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [339](/wiki/First_Age_339 "First Age 339").<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}
