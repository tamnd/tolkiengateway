**The Barrow Downs**, or <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.barrowdowns.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">www.barrowdowns.com</a></b>, is a [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) related website with games, FAQ, mad-libs, quizzes, personality tests, and other activities. It also has two offshoots, a forum site and a fanfiction site.
