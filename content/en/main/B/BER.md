**BER** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "valiant".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *bérya-*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *verya-* ("to dare"); <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Verya&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Verya (page does not exist)">verya</a></i> (adj., "bold");<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *verie* ("boldness")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *berina* ("bold, brave"); *bértha-* ("to be bold")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *beren* ("bold"); *bertho* ("dare")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin): *ber* ("valiant man, warrior"); *bereth* ("valor")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Danian](/wiki/Nandorin "Nandorin"): *[beorn](</wiki/Beorn_(Nandorin)> "Beorn (Nandorin)")* ("man")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
