**BER** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "valiant".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *bérya-*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *verya-* ("to dare"); <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Verya&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Verya (page does not exist)">verya</a></i> (adj., "bold");<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *verie* ("boldness")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *berina* ("bold, brave"); *bértha-* ("to be bold")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *beren* ("bold"); *bertho* ("dare")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md): *ber* ("valiant man, warrior"); *bereth* ("valor")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Danian](../N/Nandorin.md): *[beorn](<Beorn_(Nandorin).md>)* ("man")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
