The **Blackroot Vale** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) "Morthond Vale")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a valley of rich grassland through which the River [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md) flowed.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-2">[2]</a></sup> The river rose in a [small valley](../M/Mornan.md) beneath the sheer mountainsides of the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md)' southern extent,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and in the shadowed rocks above its spring was the hidden gateway leading out from the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The people of the vale were known in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) for their archery skills, and their lord [Duinhir](../D/Duinhir.md) took a small company of his bowmen to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> With them went [Duinhir](../D/Duinhir.md)'s sons, [Derufin](../D/Derufin.md) and [Duilin](<../D/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir).md>), both of whom were lost in the defence of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [8 March](../_/8_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md), and the [Grey Company](../G/Grey_Company.md) exited the Paths of the Dead at the source of the Morthond.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> They rode to the [Stone of Erech](../S/Stone_of_Erech.md) where Aragorn, as the heir of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md), called upon the [Oathbreakers](../O/Oathbreakers.md) to follow him and fulfill their pledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Blackroot Vale was an area in the region of Western Gondor. It was accessed by traveling from &#34;Kingstead&#34; in Western <a href="../R/Rohan.md" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> through the <a href="../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md" title="Paths of the Dead">Paths of the Dead</a>. <a href="../T/Tarlang&#39;s_Neck.md" title="Tarlang&#39;s Neck">Tarlang&#39;s Neck</a> connected the Vale to <a href="../L/Lamedon.md" title="Lamedon">Lamedon</a> in the southeast. The Havens of <a href="Belfalas.md" title="Belfalas">Belfalas</a> lay to the south, connected by a road that runs through &#34;Tarlang&#39;s Crown&#34; on the west side of &#34;Tarlang&#39;s Peaks&#34;. The capital was &#34;Morlad&#34;, built upon a hill on the western shore of the river <a href="../M/Morthond.md" title="Morthond">Morthond</a>, south of the entrance to the <a href="../D/Dwimorberg.md" title="Dwimorberg">Dwimorberg</a>. Further south along the river were the villages of &#34;Alagrant&#34; and &#34;Sardol&#34;. East of Morlad, on the other side of the <a href="../H/Hill_of_Erech.md" title="Hill of Erech">Hill of Erech</a>, were the mining village of &#34;Tingobel&#34; and the town of &#34;Lancrath&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Vale faced several threats in March of <a href="../T/Third_Age.md" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="../T/Third_Age_3019.md" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a>, during the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>. <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> of the <a href="../W/White_Mountains.md" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> captured Tingobel, <a href="../U/Umbar.md" title="Umbar">Umbarrim</a> <a href="../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md" title="Corsairs of Umbar">Corsairs</a> and sorcerors held Tarlang&#39;s Crown, and some of the cursed <a href="../O/Oathbreakers.md" title="Oathbreakers">Oathbreakers</a> had rejected <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s offer and reaffirmed their allegiance to <a href="../S/Sauron.md" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>. These twice-traitorous shades called themselves the Ruthless Dead, and the fear they spread was driving the local fauna to madness and death. &#34;Malthellam&#34; was Steward of the Vale in Lord <a href="../D/Duinhir.md" title="Duinhir">Duinhir</a>&#39;s absence. He required the cooperation of Duinhir&#39;s wife, the Lady Rosfin, but Rosfin blamed him for the loss of Tingobel and was initially reluctant to work with him. The emblem of the Blackroot Vale was a black circle on a white field.</dd></dl>
