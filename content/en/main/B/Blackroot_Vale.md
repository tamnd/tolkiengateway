The **Blackroot Vale** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") "Morthond Vale")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a valley of rich grassland through which the River [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond) flowed.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-2">[2]</a></sup> The river rose in a [small valley](/wiki/Mornan "Mornan") beneath the sheer mountainsides of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains)' southern extent,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and in the shadowed rocks above its spring was the hidden gateway leading out from the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead).<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The people of the vale were known in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) for their archery skills, and their lord [Duinhir](/wiki/Duinhir) took a small company of his bowmen to the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> With them went [Duinhir](/wiki/Duinhir)'s sons, [Derufin](/wiki/Derufin) and [Duilin](</wiki/Duilin_(son_of_Duinhir)> "Duilin (son of Duinhir)"), both of whom were lost in the defence of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

On [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), and the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company) exited the Paths of the Dead at the source of the Morthond.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> They rode to the [Stone of Erech](/wiki/Stone_of_Erech) where Aragorn, as the heir of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur), called upon the [Oathbreakers](/wiki/Oathbreakers) to follow him and fulfill their pledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Passing_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Passing-2">[2]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Blackroot Vale was an area in the region of Western Gondor. It was accessed by traveling from &#34;Kingstead&#34; in Western <a href="/wiki/Rohan" title="Rohan">Rohan</a> through the <a href="/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead" title="Paths of the Dead">Paths of the Dead</a>. <a href="/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck" title="Tarlang&#39;s Neck">Tarlang&#39;s Neck</a> connected the Vale to <a href="/wiki/Lamedon" title="Lamedon">Lamedon</a> in the southeast. The Havens of <a href="/wiki/Belfalas" title="Belfalas">Belfalas</a> lay to the south, connected by a road that runs through &#34;Tarlang&#39;s Crown&#34; on the west side of &#34;Tarlang&#39;s Peaks&#34;. The capital was &#34;Morlad&#34;, built upon a hill on the western shore of the river <a href="/wiki/Morthond" title="Morthond">Morthond</a>, south of the entrance to the <a href="/wiki/Dwimorberg" title="Dwimorberg">Dwimorberg</a>. Further south along the river were the villages of &#34;Alagrant&#34; and &#34;Sardol&#34;. East of Morlad, on the other side of the <a href="/wiki/Hill_of_Erech" title="Hill of Erech">Hill of Erech</a>, were the mining village of &#34;Tingobel&#34; and the town of &#34;Lancrath&#34;.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>The Vale faced several threats in March of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a>, during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>. <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> of the <a href="/wiki/White_Mountains" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> captured Tingobel, <a href="/wiki/Umbar" title="Umbar">Umbarrim</a> <a href="/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar" title="Corsairs of Umbar">Corsairs</a> and sorcerors held Tarlang&#39;s Crown, and some of the cursed <a href="/wiki/Oathbreakers" title="Oathbreakers">Oathbreakers</a> had rejected <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>&#39;s offer and reaffirmed their allegiance to <a href="/wiki/Sauron" title="Sauron">Sauron</a>. These twice-traitorous shades called themselves the Ruthless Dead, and the fear they spread was driving the local fauna to madness and death. &#34;Malthellam&#34; was Steward of the Vale in Lord <a href="/wiki/Duinhir" title="Duinhir">Duinhir</a>&#39;s absence. He required the cooperation of Duinhir&#39;s wife, the Lady Rosfin, but Rosfin blamed him for the loss of Tingobel and was initially reluctant to work with him. The emblem of the Blackroot Vale was a black circle on a white field.</dd></dl>
