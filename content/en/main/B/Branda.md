## Teleri {#Teleri}

<i><b>Branda</b></i> is a [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) adjective meaning "lofty, noble" or "fine".

### Etymology {#Etymology}

The form in [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Elvish) (or perhaps [Common Telerin](/wiki/Common_Telerin)) was *b'randâ*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This was apparently representing an even more primitive adjective \**barándâ* (stem [BARÁD](/wiki/BARAT "BARAT")).

## Westron {#Westron}

In [Westron](/wiki/Westron)**,** *branda* means "border, march".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

It may possibly originate from the [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) \**barrad*, which has the root [BAR](/wiki/BAR), though this is unlikely.

## See also {#See_also}

- *[Branda-nîn](/wiki/Branda-n%C3%AEn)*
- *[Brandagamba](/wiki/Brandagamba)* (translated as [Brandybuck](/wiki/Brandybuck)) is derived from this word and the ending *-gamba*. While a more literal translation of the name would be "Marchbuck," Tolkien instead translated it as "Brandybuck" in order to retain the visible connection with the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) River.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
