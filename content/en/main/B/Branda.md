## Teleri {#Teleri}

<i><b>Branda</b></i> is a [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) adjective meaning "lofty, noble" or "fine".

### Etymology {#Etymology}

The form in [Primitive Elvish](../P/Primitive_Elvish.md) (or perhaps [Common Telerin](../C/Common_Telerin.md)) was *b'randâ*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This was apparently representing an even more primitive adjective \**barándâ* (stem [BARÁD](BARAT.md)).

## Westron {#Westron}

In [Westron](../W/Westron.md)**,** *branda* means "border, march".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

It may possibly originate from the [Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) \**barrad*, which has the root [BAR](BAR~31359.md), though this is unlikely.

## See also {#See_also}

- *[Branda-nîn](Branda-nîn.md)*
- *[Brandagamba](Brandagamba.md)* (translated as [Brandybuck](Brandybuck.md)) is derived from this word and the ending *-gamba*. While a more literal translation of the name would be "Marchbuck," Tolkien instead translated it as "Brandybuck" in order to retain the visible connection with the [Brandywine](Brandywine.md) River.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
