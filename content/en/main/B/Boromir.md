**Boromir** was the eldest son of [Denethor II](/wiki/Denethor "Denethor") and [Finduilas](</wiki/Finduilas_(wife_of_Denethor)> "Finduilas (wife of Denethor)") and the older brother of [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir).<sup id="cite_ref-stew_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-6">[6]</a></sup>

Boromir was beloved by his father and was like him in face and pride, but in little else. Boromir's temperament was similar to King [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) of old; he took no wife and was more interested in arms and warfare. He cared little for lore, save for the tales of old battles.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-6">[6]</a></sup> Boromir was a man of great strength and valour. He had a fair and noble face, dark-haired and grey-eyed, proud and stern of glance.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup>

He was one of the nine members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring),<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup> until his death at the hands of the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai) which led to the [Breaking of the Fellowship](/wiki/Breaking_of_the_Fellowship)<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-7">[7]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/f/fc/Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg/300px-Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="368" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/f/fc/Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg/450px-Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/f/fc/Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg/600px-Catherine_Chmiel_-_The_Gift_1.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="980"/></a><figcaption>Boromir and baby Faramir, art by <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Catherine_Chmiel" title="Catherine Chmiel">Catherine Chmiel</a></figcaption></figure>

Boromir was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2978](/wiki/Third_Age_2978 "Third Age 2978"), five years before his brother Faramir.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Between the brothers there was great love, and had been since childhood, when Boromir was the helper and protector of Faramir. He was only ten years old when their mother Finduilas died,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and after her death their father became more grim and silent than before.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-6">[6]</a></sup>

Boromir grew to be a valorous warrior, interested in arms, warfare and tales of old battles and would serve as a commander to the military of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-stew_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stew-6">[6]</a></sup>

On the evening before Sauron's [attack on Osgiliath](/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath "Sauron's attack on Osgiliath"), Faramir repeatedly had a dream. A similar dream came to Boromir once. Both Boromir and Faramir could understand little of these words; therefore, they spoke to their father who was wise in the lore of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). But even he could only say that [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), which is also known as Imladris, was a dale in the far north where the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven) Lord [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) lived.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:320–1</sup>

Another day, when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) [attacked](/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath "Sauron's attack on Osgiliath") [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) at the dawn of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Boromir was a commander. His company drove back the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and held the last bridge; eventually they destroyed it behind them, to prevent the Orcs from crossing the river.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup>

Regarding the brothers' earlier dream, Faramir was eager to seek Imladris. However, the way was full of doubt and danger, so out of protection for his brother Boromir took the journey upon himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:321</sup>

Boromir's journey took 110 days.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:320</sup> He passed through the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan) and travelled north through the lands west of the Misty Mountains. He faced many hardships, for that land had fallen into decay and the [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road) no longer existed except for crumbling remains of the old causeways.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Near [Tharbad](/wiki/Tharbad) he crossed the [Greyflood](/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3 "Gwathló") using a dangerous ford formed by a ruined bridge and he lost his [horse](/wiki/Horses "Horses")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>, which returned home to Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-Riders_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riders-12">[12]</a></sup> The rest of the way he travelled on foot.

When he reached [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), he attended the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond). Representatives from races and lands of the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) spoke about the current situations, and Boromir spoke about the [Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor)'s vigilance and the struggle to keep the forces of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) from crossing the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). However, he said, he had not come seeking military aid but counsel. When he told of the dream that had led him to Rivendell the answers to its riddles were revealed: [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) brought out the [Shards of Narsil](/wiki/Shards_of_Narsil) and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) the [Halfling](/wiki/Halflings "Halflings") held up [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") that was [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s Bane. There he attempted to persuade the Council to let him take the One Ring to Gondor so that it could be used in the defence of the realm. Elrond explained, however, that the Ring could not be used by anyone other than [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). For even though one might use it for the purpose of doing good, it would twist all deeds and intentions to evil in the end.<sup id="cite_ref-Council_4-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Fellowship of the Ring {#Fellowship_of_the_Ring}

It was Boromir's intention to return to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), which is why he joined the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring). When the Company of the Ring reached [Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras), Boromir advised that each of them should carry a faggot of wood.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> This saved the Company from freezing to death when they got caught in a blizzard on Caradhras. The next morning Boromir and Aragorn ploughed their way through the snow and carried the four [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) back down the mountain to safety.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

As the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Pass) was now blocked, Boromir proposed that they should travel southward on the west side of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and then either pass through the Gap of Rohan or cross the [Isen](/wiki/Isen) and go through the southern lands of [Langstrand](/wiki/Anfalas "Anfalas") and [Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin). But [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) opposed this saying that, because of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s treachery those lands were no longer safe. He proposed that the Company should instead go through the mines of [Moria](/wiki/Moria), but Boromir said that he would not go that way, not unless the whole company voted against him.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-15">[15]</a></sup>

Boromir finally agreed, but upon reaching the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) he was angry that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) did not know the password and he threw a stone into the Dark Water. This alerted the [Watcher in the Water](/wiki/Watcher_in_the_Water). When Gandalf stepped into the mines the Watcher attacked Frodo, but all the Company were able to escape into the mines.<sup id="cite_ref-Journey_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Journey-15">[15]</a></sup>

Boromir fought valiantly when the Company was attacked in the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul), and he blocked the western door and hewed at the arm of a [Cave-troll](/wiki/Cave-trolls "Cave-trolls"). After escaping from the Chamber, the company reached the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) they were again attacked by Orcs, and with them was a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs"). Boromir sounded his [Great Horn](/wiki/Great_Horn) which prompted the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to pause, uneasy. But when they heard no answer, nor saw reinforcements, they led a second onslaught. As Gandalf fought with the Balrog, Boromir and Aragorn ran towards him, but just as they reached the crossing Gandalf shattered the bridge and the Balrog fell into the abyss, dragging Gandalf in as well.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/6c/Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg/250px-Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="167" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/6c/Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg/375px-Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/6/6c/Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg/500px-Catherine_Chmiel_-_Galadriel_and_Boromir.jpg 2x" data-file-width="900" data-file-height="602"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel and Boromir</i> by <a href="/wiki/Catherine_Karina_Chmiel" title="Catherine Karina Chmiel">Catherine Chmiel</a></figcaption></figure>

Aragorn then led the Company towards [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). Boromir advised against this, saying that the woods had a perilous reputation in Gondor and that few escaped unscathed. Aragorn replied that only those who were evil or brought evil with them had reason to fear Lothlórien. After [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) had spoken to each member of the Company in their thoughts, Boromir was keen to know about the conversation between the Lady and the Ring-bearer, but what he thought that the Lady had offered him he would not tell. When the Company were set to depart from Lothlórien, Galadriel gave each of them a gift. Boromir received a [golden Belt](/wiki/Boromir%27s_belt "Boromir's belt").<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> As the Company set out down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), Boromir shared a boat with [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). As they travelled down the river, Boromir became increasingly consumed with thoughts of the Ring. He muttered to himself and bit his nails and sometimes paddled closer to Frodo's boat. His behaviour made Merry and Pippin uneasy, and Pippin noticed a strange glint in Boromir's eye as he looked at Frodo. As the Company approached the rapids of [Sarn Gebir](/wiki/Sarn_Gebir) it was Boromir who alerted them and they were able to turn back. Boromir then advised the company to leave the river and strike a westward and southward road to Gondor. But Frodo did not intend to go to Gondor, hence the Company continued on their way down the river.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

When the Company reached [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel), it was time for the Company to decide their next course. When Frodo wandered off into the forest to think, Boromir followed him after a while. He tried to persuade Frodo to bring [The Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") to Minas Tirith. As Boromir spoke, the lure of the Ring grew stronger in him. He envisioned himself as a mighty king who would overthrow Sauron, rescue his people beyond hope, and lead Gondor to victory and glory. When Frodo refused to accompany him to Minas Tirith, Boromir became enraged and stressed and tried to take the Ring from Frodo by force. Frodo put the Ring on and vanished. When [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo) had gone, Boromir was overwhelmed with the realization of what he had done. He wept and called for Frodo to return, but it was too late.<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-7">[7]</a></sup>

When Boromir returned to the Company he would only say that he had argued with Frodo and that the hobbit had disappeared. Merry and Pippin ran off looking for their friend, and Aragorn told Boromir to follow them and guard them.<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-7">[7]</a></sup> Boromir did as he was asked. When he found the Hobbits they were surrounded by dozens of Orcs. Boromir slew many of them and the rest fled. He began to lead the Hobbits back to the campsite, but they were attacked again by at least 100 Orcs. Boromir sounded the Great Horn and fought valiantly to prevent the Orcs from seizing Merry and Pippin. He was pierced arrow after arrow, but he refused to die so easily. Many arrows were shot before he fell at last, and the Hobbits were taken prisoner.<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-5">[5]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/d/dc/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg/300px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="202" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/d/dc/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg/450px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/d/dc/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg/600px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Death_of_Boromir.jpg 2x" data-file-width="700" data-file-height="472"/></a><figcaption><i>The Death of Boromir</i> by Peter Xavier Price</figcaption></figure>

Boromir was still alive when Aragorn found him. At least twenty Orcs lay slain around him. Boromir still held his sword, though the blade had been broken off at the hilt, and the Great Horn had been cloven in two. Before he died, Boromir told Aragorn that he had tried to take the Ring from Frodo. He asked Aragorn to forgive him, that he had fallen to the lure of the Ring, but Aragorn assured him that he was forgiven and that he had redeemed himself before he died. Then Aragorn, Gimli, and Legolas laid Boromir's body in one of the boats. His shattered horn and sword they laid across his lap and the weapons of his enemies were laid at his feet. Aragorn and Legolas sang the [Song of Boromir](/wiki/Song_of_Boromir) while they set his funeral boat adrift on the Anduin. The river then carried Boromir's body over the [Rauros-falls](/wiki/Rauros "Rauros").<sup id="cite_ref-Departure_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Departure-5">[5]</a></sup>

Three nights later, Faramir was sitting by the banks of the Anduin in Osgiliath when he saw or envisioned a boat float past him on the river. Faramir had heard the Great Horn sounding in the distance when Boromir was in need, and now he saw his brother's body laid out in the boat, but the Great Horn was missing. The two halves of the horn were later washed ashore and they were returned to Denethor. It was said that the boat bearing Boromir's body was borne down the Anduin and out into the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

Boromir was tall<sup id="cite_ref-Description_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-3">[3]</a></sup> (at least 6 ft. 4.)<sup id="cite_ref-Descriptions_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Descriptions-1">[1]</a></sup> and had long dark hair.<sup id="cite_ref-Long_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Long-2">[2]</a></sup> He had a fair<sup id="cite_ref-Description_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-3">[3]</a></sup> and beardless face<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup> with grey eyes.<sup id="cite_ref-Description_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Description-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Jean_Fouquet-Mort_de_Roland.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/5/51/Jean_Fouquet-Mort_de_Roland.jpg/300px-Jean_Fouquet-Mort_de_Roland.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="244" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/5/51/Jean_Fouquet-Mort_de_Roland.jpg/450px-Jean_Fouquet-Mort_de_Roland.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/5/51/Jean_Fouquet-Mort_de_Roland.jpg 2x" data-file-width="590" data-file-height="480"/></a><figcaption>Roland is found by his brother Baudoin next to his olifant, during the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Roncevaux" title="wikipedia:Battle of Roncevaux">Battle of Roncevaux</a>.</figcaption></figure>

Many similarities have been suggested between <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roland" title="wikipedia:Roland">Roland</a>, the paladin of Charlemagne, and Boromir: both bore a white horn, were military leaders who defended their lord's realm, shared the flaw of pride and both died tragically away from home in the wilderness while defending their companions against an overwhelming enemy force and blew their horn before their death.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> Like Roland in battle with the Saracens blew his horn to call for Charlemagne, Boromir called for Aragorn. In both cases, help came too late.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

<b>1955: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1955_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1955 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1955 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Derek_Prentice" title="Derek Prentice">Derek Prentice</a> voiced Boromir.</dd></dl>

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Michael_Graham_Cox" title="Michael Graham Cox">Michael Graham Cox</a> provided the voice of Boromir. His appearance was inspired by the popular image of a Viking: he wore a fur tunic and a horned helmet. He did not have a shield.</dd></dl>

<b>1979: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1979 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Erik_Bauersfeld" title="Erik Bauersfeld">Erik Bauersfeld</a> provided the voice of Boromir.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Cox reprised his role of Boromir. In the meantime, he learned to pronounce Elvish words correctly: he came from &#34;My Nasty Rith&#34; in Bakshi&#39;s film, but he uses the correct pronunciation here.</dd></dl>

<b>1992: <a href="/wiki/Der_Herr_der_Ringe_(1992_German_radio_series)" title="Der Herr der Ringe (1992 German radio series)"><i>Der Herr der Ringe</i> (1992 German radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir is played by Christian Redl.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir is played by <a href="/wiki/Sean_Bean" title="Sean Bean">Sean Bean</a>. Differing from the book, Boromir dies (killed by the <a href="/wiki/Uruk-hai" title="Uruk-hai">Uruk-hai</a> leader <a href="/wiki/Lurtz" title="Lurtz">Lurtz</a>) at the end of the film instead of the beginning of <i>The Two Towers</i>. He also fights without his shield in his last battle.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/James_Horan" title="James Horan">James Horan</a> provides the voice of Boromir, clad in blue and carrying a great shield. His death is not shown; he is not seen again after the first camp at the foot of <a href="/wiki/Amon_Hen" title="Amon Hen">Amon Hen</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers">The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a> includes a scene not taken from the book in which Boromir and his brother Faramir see each other for the last time, which displays their father <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a>&#39;s attitude toward his two sons. At the Council of Elrond in Rivendell, Boromir says that his prophetic dream came to first his brother, Faramir, and then to him, on the night before an attack on <a href="/wiki/Osgiliath" title="Osgiliath">Osgiliath</a>. This reference to Osgiliath may be the inspiration for the flashback scene in The Two Towers.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir is seen in a brief flashback being pierced by arrows, as Pippin explains to Denethor the circumstances of his death. In the <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)">Extended Edition</a>, he is seen again in a vision of Denethor, walking proudly towards his father until he fades away.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Boromir is provided by Jozef Vajda. The same actor also portrayed Boromir&#39;s brother Faramir later in the radio series.</dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir is the Hero unit of the Gondor faction and also appears in the storyline campaign. In a notable deviation from the original story, the player may save Boromir on Amon Hen, in which case he will accompany the Three Hunters into Rohan, participate in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg" title="Battle of the Hornburg">Battle of the Hornburg</a> and late journey with Gandalf and Pippin back to Gondor. He will also fight during the Siege of Gondor and participate in the last stand of the Men of the West in the Battle of Morannon.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Boromir is now the Hero of the Men of the West faction.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The players first meet Boromir in Rivendell and later again during the Fellowship&#39;s stay in Lothlorien. He can also be played during his stand against the Uruk-hai at Parth Galen.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/Lego" title="Lego">LEGO The Lord of the Rings</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A mini figure of Boromir is included in the <a href="/wiki/Lego#Sets" title="Lego">set</a> The Mines of Moria, he wears a sword and shield.<sup id="cite_ref-2012_sets_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2012_sets-26">[26]</a></sup> In the <i><a href="/wiki/Lego_The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Video_Game" title="Lego The Lord of the Rings: The Video Game">Lego The Lord of the Rings: The Video Game</a></i> in 2012, Boromir&#39;s death and funeral scenes are softened by various comedic elements such as being shot by an arrow, a broom, and a banana. In the cutscene that plays after the player achieves 100%, Boromir&#39;s spirit also appears at the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Havens" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a> with the other members of the <a href="/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="Fellowship of the Ring">Fellowship of the Ring</a> to see Frodo and Gandalf off on their voyage.</dd></dl>
