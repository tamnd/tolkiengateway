The **Battle of Eglarest** was a battle fought early during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).

## The Battle {#The_Battle}

At the head of the battle was [Ingwion](/wiki/Ingwion), son of [Ingwë](/wiki/Ingw%C3%AB), leading the [Vanyarin](/wiki/Vanyar "Vanyar") host. At [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest) Ingwion and his host made a landing and found a swarm of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) around it. Thereafter he and his host drove away the Orcs from the shores of the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing more is said of the battle in detail.
