Bargrisar was a stone-[Giant](/wiki/Giant) in [*The Lord of the Rings: War in the North*](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North "The Lord of the Rings: War in the North") that led a league of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) to destroy the nests of [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) in the North.

## History {#History}

Bargrisar was a notoriously vicious and violent Stone Giant who was shunned by other Stone Giants due to his antisocial nature. This shunning aggravated Bargrisar's combative tendencies and created a reputation that eventually drew the attention of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). The Dark Lord sent one of his servants, [Agandaûr](/wiki/Aganda%C3%BBr), to successfully persuade Bargrisar to swear allegiance to Sauron.

After forming an alliance with Sauron, Bargrisar and Agandaûr planned to kill the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) and plunder the North. They raised an army and led an attack on the Eagles, slaughtering them and destroying their eggs. As they was doing so, Gandalf sent a trio of warriors - [Eradan](/wiki/Eradan), [Andriel](/wiki/Andriel), and [Farin](/wiki/Farin) - to investigate the hiding of Bargrisar's Trolls.

The trio, with the help of the eagle [Beleram](/wiki/Beleram), destroyed most of Bargrisar's army before he was ready to destroy the remaining [Men](/wiki/Men) of the North. Eventually they found and fought Bargrisar, successfully killing him after he feigned death and Beleram swooped down and plucked his eyes out.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
