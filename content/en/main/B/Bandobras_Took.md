**Bandobras "the Bullroarer" Took** was the son of [Thain](../T/Thain.md) [Isumbras III](../I/Isumbras_Took_III.md) of the [Took](../T/Took_Family.md) line.<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup> He was distinguished by his great height (for a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md)), being four foot five and thus able to ride a horse.<sup id="cite_ref-Hobbits_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Bandobras was famous for averting a [goblin](../O/Orcs.md) invasion of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) at the [Battle of Greenfields](Battle_of_Greenfields.md) of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2747](../T/Third_Age_2747.md),<sup id="cite_ref-ta_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ta-3">[3]</a></sup> and personally slaying the goblins' king, [Golfimbul](../G/Golfimbul.md). Bandobras took off the head of the goblin king with a wood club, causing it to fly through the air for 100 yards and fall down a rabbit hole; it is said that this is how the game of [golf](../G/Golf.md) was invented.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-4">[4]</a></sup>

Nothing is known about Bandobras' family, other than he had "many descendants", some of whom were the [North Tooks](../N/North-took_Family.md) of [Long Cleeve](../L/Long_Cleeve.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Took_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Took-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Bandobras is a combination of the elements *bando* ("band", "stripe", "banner") and *bras* ("arm").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the alliteration between Bandobras and Bullroarer was significant and that translators should attempt to keep it.

Tolkien believed while writing *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* that "bullroarer" was an instrument of primitive peoples that made a roaring sound, as named by anthropologists, though he could not find it in any dictionaries.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In editions prior to the [50th Anniversary Edition](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(50th_Anniversary_Edition).md>) of *The Lord of the Rings*, the Prologue gave Bandobras' father as [Isengrim II](../I/Isengrim_Took_II.md). This was based on an earlier version of the Took family tree,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> but was never corrected.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Inspirations {#Inspirations}

It is possible [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was inspired by one George von Hohenzollern, who, like the Bullroarer to Bilbo, held the image of the bold ancestor to Tolkien:

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>He [the Bullroarer] charged the ranks of the goblins of Mount Gram in the Battle of the Green Fields, and knocked their king Golfimbul&#39;s head clean off with a wooden club.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>&#34;<a href="../A/An_Unexpected_Party.md" title="An Unexpected Party">An Unexpected Party</a>&#34;<sup id="cite_ref-Party_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-4">[4]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>She [Tolkien&#39;s aunt Grace] alleged that the family name [&#34;Tolkien&#34;] had originally been &#34;von Hohenzollern&#34;, for they had emanated from the Hohenzollern district of the Holy Roman Empire. A certain George von Hohenzollern had, she said, fought on the side of Archduke Ferdinand of Austria at the Siege of Vienna in 1529. He had shown great daring in leading an unofficial raid against the Turks and capturing the Sultan’s standard. This (said Aunt Grace) was why he was given the nickname Tollkühn, &#34;foolhardy&#34;; and the name stuck.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md" title="Humphrey Carpenter">Humphrey Carpenter</a><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1966: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit.md" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bandobras is briefly mentioned as Bilbo&#39;s &#34;great-great-grandhobbit&#34;. In Bag End Bilbo has a drawing of Bandobras,sword upraised,standing triumphantly over the corpse of a wolf.</dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In trying to convince <a href="Bilbo_Baggins.md" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo</a> of his <a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/Tooks.md" title="Tooks">Tookishness</a>, <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> talks about the story of Bandobras Took and his victory at the <a href="Battle_of_Greenfields.md" title="Battle of Greenfields">Battle of Greenfields</a>. He also says that Bandobras invented the game of golf in his killing of <a href="../G/Golfimbul.md" title="Golfimbul">Golfimbul</a> but qualifies this by suggesting that it is an embellishment. The portrait was based on <a href="../D/Dan_Hennah.md" title="Dan Hennah">Dan Hennah</a>, the production designer for the films.</dd></dl>

### Games {#Games}

<b>2003: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md" title="The Hobbit (2003 video game)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (2003 video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The first Hobbit outside <a href="Bag_End.md" title="Bag End">Bag End</a> recounts the story of Bandobras, followed by the accusations that young hobbits do not know enough about their history.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The public test server is named Bullroarer after Bandobras Took.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In <a class="mw-redirect" href="Brockenborings.md" title="Brockenborings">Brockenborings</a> there is a wooden statue depicting Bandobras just after he defeated Golfimbul. Every Spring <a href="../T/The_Shire.md" title="The Shire">the Shire</a> celebrates Bullroarer Took Day when the Hobbits drink tankards of Bullroarer&#39;s Brew and also place tankards of the drink under his statue in his honor. Bullroarer&#39;s Brew was Bandobras&#39; favorite drink and it was named after him. It is a beer brewed out of <a href="../C/Clovers.md" title="Clovers">clovers</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Besides inventing golf, Bandobras also invented fence walking. Every morning Bullroarer would drink his favorite beer and then walk across a fence. It is said that he did this to improve his balance. Hobbits would play fence walking as part of the Bullroarer Took Day celebration.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="../L/Lego_The_Hobbit%253A_The_Video_Game.md" title="Lego The Hobbit: The Video Game">Lego The Hobbit: The Video Game</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Bandobras is an obtainable playable character.</dd></dl>
