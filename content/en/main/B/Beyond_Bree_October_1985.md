<b><i>Beyond Bree</i> October 1985</b> is an issue of *[Beyond Bree](/wiki/Beyond_Bree)*, edited by [Nancy Martsch](/wiki/Nancy_Martsch). In his article, [Taum Santoski](/wiki/Taum_Santoski) provides a transcription of the [elvish](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish") notes reproduced in *[Les aventures de Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Les_aventures_de_Tom_Bombadil)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- "The Living Flower in its Casket" by Katherine Schroeder (ill.)
- "Homecoming, SR 1419" poem by Lee Garig
- "Tolkien-san, Yoku Irasshaimashitaf ... Some Observations on Similarities Between Japanese and the Elvish Languages of J.R.R. Tolkien" by [Patrick Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne "Patrick H. Wynne")
- "The Nature of Eru" comments by Michael Fath &amp; Steve Deyo
- "A Manuscript by J.R.R. Tolkien in the French edition of *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*" by [Taum Santoski](/wiki/Taum_Santoski)
- "The Stone of Erech" by Geoff Davies
- Publications, Letters, News<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
