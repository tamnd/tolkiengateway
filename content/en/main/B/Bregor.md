**Bregor** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [359](/wiki/First_Age_359 "First Age 359") - [448](/wiki/First_Age_448 "First Age 448")) was the second [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).

## History {#History}

Born into the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor), Bregor was the eldest child and only son of [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros)> "Boromir (Lord of Ladros)"); his sisters were [Andreth](/wiki/Andreth) and [Beril](/wiki/Beril).

In total, Bregor fathered five children who were three daughters: [Bregil](/wiki/Bregil), [Hirwen](/wiki/Hirwen) and [Gilwen](/wiki/Gilwen), and two sons: [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas) and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir).

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [402](/wiki/First_Age_402 "First Age 402") there was fighting on the north-marches, more bitter than there had been since the routing of [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) where [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) attempted to pierce the [Pass of Aglon](/wiki/Pass_of_Aglon). [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor) were aided by the sons of [Finarfin](/wiki/Finarfin) and the House of Bëor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [410](/wiki/First_Age_410 "First Age 410"), when Bregor was 51 years old, the region of [Ladros](/wiki/Ladros) in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) was given to his House, and his father became its first Lord.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Bregor became the head of his House and Lord of Ladros after the passing of his father in [432](/wiki/First_Age_432 "First Age 432"). He ruled his people peacefully for sixteen years until his death, when Bregor was succeeded by his eldest son, Bregolas.

Through his many descendants, Bregor was a direct ancestor of the great heroes of men, [Beren](/wiki/Beren), [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin), and [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-3">[3]</a></sup> His weapon was a famed [bow](/wiki/Bow_of_Bregor "Bow of Bregor") which became an heirloom of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, created in the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas) and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir). After publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* a new genealogy for the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) was made in the 1950s and at that time four generations (Baran, Boron, Boromir, and *Bregor*) were inserted after Bëor, with Bregor the father of Bregolas and Barahir.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-3">[3]</a></sup>
