**Bregor** ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [359](../F/First_Age_359.md) - [448](../F/First_Age_448.md)) was the second [Lord of Ladros](../L/Lord_of_Ladros.md).

## History {#History}

Born into the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md), Bregor was the eldest child and only son of [Boromir](<Boromir_(Lord_of_Ladros).md>); his sisters were [Andreth](../A/Andreth.md) and [Beril](Beril.md).

In total, Bregor fathered five children who were three daughters: [Bregil](Bregil.md), [Hirwen](../H/Hirwen.md) and [Gilwen](../G/Gilwen.md), and two sons: [Bregolas](Bregolas.md) and [Barahir](Barahir.md).

In [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [402](../F/First_Age_402.md) there was fighting on the north-marches, more bitter than there had been since the routing of [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) where [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) attempted to pierce the [Pass of Aglon](../P/Pass_of_Aglon.md). [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md) and [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md) were aided by the sons of [Finarfin](../F/Finarfin.md) and the House of Bëor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [410](../F/First_Age_410.md), when Bregor was 51 years old, the region of [Ladros](../L/Ladros.md) in [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md) was given to his House, and his father became its first Lord.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Bregor became the head of his House and Lord of Ladros after the passing of his father in [432](../F/First_Age_432.md). He ruled his people peacefully for sixteen years until his death, when Bregor was succeeded by his eldest son, Bregolas.

Through his many descendants, Bregor was a direct ancestor of the great heroes of men, [Beren](Beren.md), [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md), [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md), and [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-3">[3]</a></sup> His weapon was a famed [bow](Bow_of_Bregor.md) which became an heirloom of the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[The Grey Annals](../T/The_Grey_Annals.md)*, created in the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Bëor was followed by his sons [Bregolas](Bregolas.md) and [Barahir](Barahir.md). After publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* a new genealogy for the [House of Bëor](../H/House_of_Bëor.md) was made in the 1950s and at that time four generations (Baran, Boron, Boromir, and *Bregor*) were inserted after Bëor, with Bregor the father of Bregolas and Barahir.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-3">[3]</a></sup>
