**Beorn** was a son of a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") named [Heden](/wiki/Heden), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Beo_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beo-2">[2]</a></sup>

Beorn was noted for the murder of his brother [Eoh](/wiki/Eoh), the father of [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol),<sup id="cite_ref-Beo_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beo-2">[2]</a></sup> who died either "in the siege" of his town or "in a great battle".<sup id="cite_ref-Has_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Has-3">[3]</a></sup>

Many years later, Beorn himself was killed by [Hengest](/wiki/Hengest) and [Horsa](/wiki/Horsa), Eoh's grandsons, in an act of vengeance.<sup id="cite_ref-Has_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Has-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Beorn* is in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English). It means "warrior", but its original meaning was "bear".<sup id="cite_ref-Beo_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beo-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Another variation of his name was ([Gothic](/wiki/Gothic)) <i><b>Bernus</b></i>, which was probably intended as a form of *baírnus*, a Gothic equivalent of the name, with [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) changing the spelling from *aí* to *e*.

In [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) he was called <i><b>Veran</b></i> or <i><b>Verain</b></i>, with the name <i><b>Beren</b></i> being its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate.<sup id="cite_ref-Ber_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ber-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

The name *Veran* itself was changed from *Verus*, an earlier name.<sup id="cite_ref-Var_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Var-5">[4]</a></sup>

Another (Old English) name for the character was <i><b>Hasen</b></i> of Isenóra ("iron shore").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>
