<i><b>bâd</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) noun meaning "beaten track" or "pathway".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](/wiki/Didier_Willis) has suggested that *bâd* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish").<sup id="cite_ref-DW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-2">[2]</a></sup>
