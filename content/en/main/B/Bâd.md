<i><b>bâd</b></i> is a [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) noun meaning "beaten track" or "pathway".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Didier Willis](../D/Didier_Willis.md) has suggested that *bâd* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Sindarin](../N/Neo-Elvish.md).<sup id="cite_ref-DW_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DW-2">[2]</a></sup>
