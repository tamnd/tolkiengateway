**Beldis** was the mother of [Brandir the Lame](/wiki/Brandir "Brandir") and the wife of [Handir](/wiki/Handir).

## History {#History}

Beldis was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [411](/wiki/First_Age_411 "First Age 411"), the second child of [Arachon](/wiki/Arachon) and [Bregil](/wiki/Bregil) of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor). She married [Handir](/wiki/Handir), the lord of the [Haladin](/wiki/Haladin) and they had a son called [Brandir the Lame](/wiki/Brandir "Brandir") (whom Beldis named after her brother [Brandir](</wiki/Brandir_(son_of_Arachon)> "Brandir (son of Arachon)")) who was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [465](/wiki/First_Age_465 "First Age 465"). Beldis' son Brandir the Lame later became the last chieftain of the Haladin.<sup id="cite_ref-Beor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Comment {#Comment}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) comments that the introduction of Beldis in the genealogies was a later addition by [his father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") to make [Brandir](/wiki/Brandir) a second cousing of [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) from both the houses of Bëor and of [Hador](/wiki/House_of_Hador "House of Hador").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, no explanation is given on how Beldis could marry a man thirty years younger than her or give birth to Brandir being 54 years old. Even though both cases are not completely impossible, this was probably a miscalculation on J.R.R. Tolkien's part during this later addition. Even if he should have included another generation between her and Brandir, it is clear he intented Beldis to be Brandir's mother, as he was named after his uncle.<sup id="cite_ref-Beor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beor-1">[1]</a></sup>
