**Bard II** was the son of [Brand](/wiki/Brand) and heir to the [Kingdom of Dale](/wiki/Kingdom_of_Dale).

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) his father was slain in the [Battle of Dale](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale), and Bard with many [Men of Dale](/wiki/Men_of_Dale) and [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor) were forced back to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") by [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings). There he was besieged with his ally [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm") for several days.

When news came from the south of the destruction of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), Bard and Thorin emerged from the Lonely Mountain and broke the siege. Later he sent an ambassador to the crowning of King [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and thereafter his kingdom enjoyed the protection of the [King of the West](/wiki/King_of_the_West).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
