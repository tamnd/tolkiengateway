**Bree** was a village in [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land), just to the east of the crossing of the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) and the [Greenway](/wiki/Greenway).<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography and description {#Geography_and_description}

Bree was the chief village of Bree-land, a small inhabited region<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> about forty miles east of the [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>east of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) and the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs), west of [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop), south of [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) and north of the [South Downs](/wiki/South_Downs).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Men](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") and [Hobbits](/wiki/Bree-hobbits "Bree-hobbits") lived together in Bree. It is claimed in the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch) that the arrangement between Men and Hobbits in Bree was that each minded their own affairs in their own ways, but that each regarded the other as necessary parts of the population. The friendly relationship between Men and Hobbits existed only in Bree-land.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/b0/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg/200px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="274" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/b0/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg/300px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/b0/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg/400px-J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Plan_of_Bree.jpeg 2x" data-file-width="499" data-file-height="684"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Plan of Bree&#34; by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a></figcaption></figure>

The village was built under and on the western slopes of [Bree-hill](/wiki/Bree-hill). It was surrounded by a deep ditch with a thick hedge on its inner side that ran in more than half of a circle from the hill and back to it. The great East Road entered the village through the West-gate of the hedge on the western side of the village, curved to the right to run around the foot of the hill, exited the village in its southern corner<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> through the South-gate of the hedge and then curved to the left back to its easward line.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Knife_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Knife-5">[5]</a></sup> Both gates<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> were closed and guarded by gatekeepers after nightfall who had small lodges on the inside of the gates.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), the [West-gate of Bree](/wiki/West-gate_of_Bree) was guarded by [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf).<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-7">[7]</a></sup>

The village consisted of about a hundred stone houses, most of them on the slope of the hill above the road with windows on their western side, in which the Men lived. The hill itself was burrowed by [Hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-holes), in which the Hobbits lived,<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Sign_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Sign-8">[8]</a></sup> especially on the higher slopes above the houses of the Men. An inn called [The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony) was located on the eastern side of the road where it curved to the south to go around the hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

According to their tales, the [Men of Bree](/wiki/Men_of_Bree) had settled in Bree in the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days). They were [Pre-Númenóreans](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) that had migrated from the valleys of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) to the southern valleys of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) and then north as far as the Barrow-downs.<sup id="cite_ref-Men_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-9">[9]</a></sup> Although few of them survived the turmoils of the Elder Days, they continued to settle in Bree when the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) returned to Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320"),<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Bree and the Bree-land became a part of the kingdom of Arnor,<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and the Men of Bree abandoned their old language and adopted [Westron](/wiki/Westron) as their native language.<sup id="cite_ref-Men_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Men-9">[9]</a></sup> When the Kingdom of Arnor was divided in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861"),<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Bree probably became a part of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), one of the three successor states of Arnor, because Arthedain included the land north of the East Road as far as the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills),<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> and because most of the houses of Bree were north of the East Road on the slopes of Bree-hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Sign_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sign-1">[1]</a></sup> Similarly, Staddle, Combe and Archet, the other villages of Bree-land, were also located north of the East Road.<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Knife_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Knife-5">[5]</a></sup> However, the possession of Weathertop and the land westwards towards Bree was a matter of debate between Arthedain and the other successor kingdoms of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur). This was because Cardolan and Rhudaur desired to have the [palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri") on Weathertop, as Arthedain already possessed the other two palantíri of the North.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1300](/wiki/Third_Age_1300 "Third Age 1300"), many [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) migrated westward from eastern Eriador, and many of these settled in Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1601](/wiki/Third_Age_1601 "Third Age 1601"), many Hobbits left Bree and went west beyond the river [Baranduin](/wiki/Baranduin) and founded a colony within Arthedain, the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

After the [Fall of Fornost](/wiki/Fall_of_Fornost) and Arnor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1945](/wiki/Third_Age_1945 "Third Age 1945"),<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> the north-south road was only used seldomly and was referred to as the Greenway by the Bree-folk because it had become overgrown by grass. Bree continued to exist with the unknown protection of the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North). Even at the end of the Third Age, Bree remained relatively prosperous. Bree was the most westerly settlement of Men from the point-of-view of the Shire by the time of the War of the Ring, and it had a sizable Hobbit population. Thanks to its location near a major road crossing, one could encounter travelers of Men, Dwarves or Hobbits in a bustling Bree inn. The Rangers of the North also were known to stop at Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

On [15 March](/wiki/15_March)<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) met seemingly by accident at Bree. They were both thinking about the same problem: the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) at the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain). The meeting led to the undertaking of the [Quest of Erebor](/wiki/Quest_of_Erebor), which resulted in the death of Smaug and the finding of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

From about [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2953](/wiki/Third_Age_2953 "Third Age 2953") on, the wizard [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) began to have agents in Bree.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

On [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee), [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) arrived in Bree and met [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") at the largest inn in Bree, the Prancing Pony, owned by [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur).<sup id="cite_ref-FR|Sign_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FR|Sign-8">[8]</a></sup> After Meriadoc had been attacked by a [Ringwraith](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl"), Strider feared an attack by servants of the Nazgûl and convinced the Hobbits to spend the night in the parlour of the inn instead of their rooms. [Nob](/wiki/Nob), an employee of Barliman, stuffed the beds in their rooms to make it appear as if someone was sleeping in them.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup> In the morning of [30 September](/wiki/30_September), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> they discovered that the windows to their rooms had been forced open and that their stuffed beds had been slashed. They then left Bree on the same day.<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup>

When [Frodo and his companions](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") returned to Bree on [28 October](/wiki/28_October), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup> Barliman Butterbur told them that Bree had been raided early in the year by [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") from the south during the War of the Ring. Barliman Butterbur believed that they had been let into Bree by Harry Goatleaf and Bill Ferny, who had been on the side of the ruffians and who had fled Bree with them after they had lost the fight against the inhabitants of Bree. In the resulting [set-to](/wiki/Set-to_in_Bree "Set-to in Bree"), three Men and two Hobbits from Bree were killed. The defeated ruffians fled to the woods beyond Archet and the wilds, where they lived as robbers, making travelling on the road unsafe. As a result, the inhabitants of Bree-land locked up their dwellings early, kept watchers all around the hedge and guarded the gates during the night. He also informed them that the Bree-landers had not understood how much the Rangers of the North had done for their safety, because after they all left to fight in the War of the Ring, wolves had howled around the fences in the winter and there were dark, dreadful shapes in the woods.<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-7">[7]</a></sup> After staying in Bree for two nights, Frodo and his companions left Bree on [30 October](/wiki/30_October), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup>

After the War of the Ring at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), the North-kingdom of Arnor was reestablished by [Aragorn II](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").<sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> It is probable that Bree became a part of Arnor again and became more prosperous through increased traffic on the Greenway and the East Road. Because Gandalf announced that the king would soon be turning his mind to Bree, he predicted that the Greenway would be opened again, and that there would be much traffic, as well as people and fields where there was wilderness before. Furthermore, he believed there would be room enough for people between the [Isen](/wiki/Isen) and the [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood), and along the shorelands south of the Baranduin.<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bree* is an English place name that is based on a [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) word for "hill".<sup id="cite_ref-N_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-29">[29]</a></sup>

[Wayne Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) suggest that the name *Bree* is derived from the [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh) word *bre*, which means "hill".<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup> [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey) also points out that *Bree* means "hill" in Welsh.<sup id="cite_ref-Road_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-31">[31]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) chose the name *Bree* to represent the survival of a trace of the former language of the Men of Bree and modelled it on a British (i.e. Celtic) word, which meant "hill".<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup> He referred to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brill,_Buckinghamshire" title="wikipedia:Brill, Buckinghamshire">Brill</a> in his [Nomenclature of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Nomenclature_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings) when he explained that the name [Chetwood](/wiki/Chetwood) was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tautological_place_names" title="wikipedia:List of tautological place names">tautological</a> compound of a Celtic word for "wood" and the english word "wood" and stated that the name *Brill* was derived from the Celtic word *bree* and the English word "hill".<sup id="cite_ref-N_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-N-29">[29]</a></sup>

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) confirmed that the name *Bree* is based on *Brill* in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckinghamshire" title="wikipedia:Buckinghamshire">Buckinghamshire</a>, which his father knew well.<sup id="cite_ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33">[33]</a></sup> Tom Shippey also suggests that J.R.R. Tolkien was inspired by the town of *Brill*, which is located on a hill ten miles from [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), when he chose the names *Bree* and Bree-hill.<sup id="cite_ref-Road_31-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Road-31">[31]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

J.R.R. Tolkien drew a plan of Bree during the writing of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), which corresponds to the description of Bree in the final published version.<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup>

An earlier manuscript about of the Heirs of Elendil, which later became the Annals of the Kings and Rulers and an earlier manuscript version of The Tale of Years mention that Bree was in Cardolan.<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[35]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[36]</a></sup> It is possible that the statement that Bree was in Cardolan was deliberately not retained in the published text, because a statement was added in a later manuscript about the Heirs of Elendil that Cardolan was south of the Great Road before the existing statement that Cardolan was east of the Baranduin.<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37">[37]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/8/84/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="225" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/8/84/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/8/84/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Bree_map.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="768"/></a><figcaption>Map of Bree in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a>.</figcaption></figure>

With the absence of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) and the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) in most adaptations, the distance between Bree and the Shire seems shorter than it really was.

<b>1978: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1978 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>After leaving the <a href="/wiki/Old_Forest" title="Old Forest">Old Forest</a>, the Hobbits appear in the Prancing Pony without delay or introduction. The rooms were raided by <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Ringwraiths</a> rather than servants of them.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Here too, Bombadil and the Barrow-downs were omitted. After taking a short walk through Bree, the hobbits enter the Pony. Merry goes out for a walk, and is found by <a href="/wiki/Bob" title="Bob">Bob</a> the ostler. Once again, it is the Ringwraiths rather than servants of them who raid the Pony.</dd></dl>

<b>1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Arnor:_The_Land" title="Arnor: The Land">Arnor: The Land</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>The armies of <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> razed Bree in <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_1409" title="Third Age 1409">1409</a>, though its inhabitants had found refuge among the elves of <a href="/wiki/Siragal%C3%AB" title="Siragalë">Siragalë</a> thanks to the intervention of Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38">[38]</a></sup> Near the end of the <a href="/wiki/Angmar_conflict" title="Angmar conflict">Angmar conflict</a>, Bree was razed once again, and its inhabitants also fled to the Shire. Rebuilding went slower because of the fall of Arthedain.<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39">[39]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Frodo and his companions arrive at Bree almost immediately after the scene in which they leave the Shire. All are present in Strider&#39;s room when the Ringwraiths crash the gate, trample the gatekeeper, and raid the Prancing Pony.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The role of Bree is greatly expanded, due to the interactiveness of the medium. It is the place where the Hobbits meet Strider, and the player shifts perspective. Strider searches the streets of Bree for Merry and materials to make decoys for (once again) the Ringwraiths to slash, while the streets are crowded with robbers and wolves.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bree is one of the major cities in the north, located in Bree-land, probably the most important city in the game. It faces threats from several fronts: <a href="/wiki/Sharkey" title="Sharkey">Sharkey</a>&#39;s brigands have already began making their way into <a href="/wiki/Bree-land" title="Bree-land">Bree-land</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Barrow-downs" title="Barrow-downs">Barrow-downs</a> are stirring following the passage of the <a href="/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl" title="Nazgûl">Nazgûl</a> and a small band of <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> has made their way from the <a href="/wiki/North_Downs" title="North Downs">North Downs</a>. The citizens of Bree know more of the bigger world than <a href="/wiki/The_Shire" title="The Shire">Shire</a> dwellers, but are still oblivious to the dangers they could face. The town&#39;s mayor and the watchers don&#39;t trust the <a href="/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North" title="Rangers of the North">Rangers of the North</a>, insisting they can manage their own affairs. Later, during the winter of <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_3019" title="Third Age 3019">3019</a>, Bree is overrun by Sharkey&#39;s Brigands and <a href="/wiki/Half-orcs" title="Half-orcs">Half-orcs</a> and has to be liberated by Free Peoples - an event references as &#34;Thievery and Mischief&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur" title="Barliman Butterbur">Barliman Butterbur</a> later.</dd></dl>

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Bree-WITN1.png"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/ee/Bree-WITN1.png/250px-Bree-WITN1.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="87" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/ee/Bree-WITN1.png/375px-Bree-WITN1.png 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/ee/Bree-WITN1.png/500px-Bree-WITN1.png 2x" data-file-width="890" data-file-height="310"/></a><figcaption>Bree in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a>.</figcaption></figure>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Eradan_(video_game_character)" title="Eradan (video game character)">Eradan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Andriel" title="Andriel">Andriel</a> and <a href="/wiki/Farin_(video_game_character)" title="Farin (video game character)">Farin</a> visit Bree in the beginning of the game, just a few days before <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo</a> reached the town. They meet with <a href="/wiki/Aragorn" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a> in the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Prancing_Pony" title="Prancing Pony">Prancing Pony</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-40">[40]</a></sup></dd>
<dd>Bree is protected by a dike and hedge and can be entered through two of its gates.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[41]</a></sup> The most important structures in Bree are the Prancing Pony, <a href="/wiki/Elmund_Brushwood" title="Elmund Brushwood">Elmund Brushwood</a>&#39;s smithy and <a href="/wiki/Adalgar_Oldbank" title="Adalgar Oldbank">Adalgar Oldbank</a>&#39;s shop. Several characters appear in Bree: <a href="/wiki/Otto_Aster" title="Otto Aster">Otto Aster</a>, <a href="/wiki/Harley_Bramble" title="Harley Bramble">Harley Bramble</a>, Elmund Brushwood, <a href="/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur" title="Barliman Butterbur">Barliman Butterbur</a>, <a href="/wiki/Grof" title="Grof">Grof</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tedder_Hedgerow" title="Tedder Hedgerow">Tedder Hedgerow</a>, Adalgar Oldbank and a <a href="/wiki/Southerners" title="Southerners">Southerner</a>, as well as several unnamed <a href="/wiki/Bree-landers" title="Bree-landers">Bree-landers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_40-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-40">[40]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The opening scene takes place in Bree - once again featuring a cameo from <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a> eating a carrot (see <a href="/wiki/Albert_Dreary" title="Albert Dreary">Albert Dreary</a>) - where <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> meet in the Prancing Pony. Gandalf tells Thorin of his meeting with <a href="/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in" title="Thráin">Thráin</a>, his worries about <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">the dragon</a>, and his wish to see Thorin retake the <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a>. Gandalf convinces Thorin that they need <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">a burglar</a> to retrieve the <a href="/wiki/Arkenstone" title="Arkenstone">Arkenstone</a> in order to summons the &#34;Seven Armies&#34; of the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a>.</dd></dl>
