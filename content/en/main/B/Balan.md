<i><b>balan</b></i> (pl. *belain*) means "[Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar"), divine power, divinity" in [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

**Balan** was also the original name of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- Root [BAL](/wiki/BAL)<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup> In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, *balrog* is glossed as [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) "Demon of Might", indicating the contracted form *bal-*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
