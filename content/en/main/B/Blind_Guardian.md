**Blind Guardian** is a power metal band formed in the mid-1980s in Krefeld, Germany. The band takes inspiration from fantasy authors as well as traditional legends and epics. The 1998 album *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)*, most notably, is a concept album based on Tolkien's *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*.

Beyond projects that came to fruition, the band was at some point under negotiations for soundtrack work for [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). They had also planned to release *The Lord of the Rings* orchestral music around [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-high_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-high-1">[1]</a></sup>
