<i><b>Bloomsbury Auctions: Manuscripts &amp; Autograph Letters, Continental &amp; English Literature and History, and Modern First Editions, 15th June 2006</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Bloomsbury Auctions. The description for Lot 715 (p. 137) includes extracts from a three page [letter to Michael Blashka](/wiki/Letter_to_Michael_Blashka "Letter to Michael Blashka") (not dated, but c.1960s) - sent to [Joy Hill](/wiki/Joy_Hill) for typing along with a single page note to Joy about the letter and addressee. The first page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
