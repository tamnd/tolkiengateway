The **Brithonin** were a group of people that invaded [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) during the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth), they were likely identified with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtic_Britons" title="wikipedia:Celtic Britons">Britons</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
