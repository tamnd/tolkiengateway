The **Brithonin** were a group of people that invaded [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) during the [Faring Forth](../F/Faring_Forth.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md), they were likely identified with the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celtic_Britons" title="wikipedia:Celtic Britons">Britons</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
