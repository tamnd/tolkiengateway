A **bow of the Galadhrim** was the [gift](../G/Gifts_of_Galadriel.md) [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) bestowed to [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md).

## History {#History}

Before the [Company of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) left [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) presented each of them with gifts: "To [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) she gave a bow such as the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md) used. The bow was longer and stouter than the bows of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md), and it was strung with a string of elf-hair". The gift was accompanied by a quiver of arrows.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The bow proved to be a very useful gift indeed, for it was with this bow that Legolas shot down the descending [Fell beast](../F/Fell_beasts.md) from the sky, near the rapids of [Sarn Gebir](../S/Sarn_Gebir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Legolas laid it down with his other weapons when [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) and the [Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md) entered [Edoras](../E/Edoras.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) recovered [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md) afterward<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and Legolas fought with a bow in [the Battle of the Hornburg](Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> presumably the recovered bow of Galadriel.
