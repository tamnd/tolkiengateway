The **Buckland Gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-VI7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VI7-1">[1]</a></sup> **Hay Gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-VI8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VI8-2">[2]</a></sup> or the **North- gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> were names for a gate at the northern end of the hedge<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> known as the [High Hay](/wiki/High_Hay).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Leaving [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") across the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge), a traveler would come to the northern end of the High Hay, where it ran down to the bank of the River [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) just on the eastern side of the Brandywine Bridge south of the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road). In the Hedge at this point was a guarded gate that led to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), the Eastmarch of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>
