The **Buckland Gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-VI7_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VI7-1">[1]</a></sup> **Hay Gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-VI8_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VI8-2">[2]</a></sup> or the **North- gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> were names for a gate at the northern end of the hedge<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> known as the [High Hay](../H/High_Hay.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Leaving [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) across the [Brandywine Bridge](Brandywine_Bridge.md), a traveler would come to the northern end of the High Hay, where it ran down to the bank of the River [Brandywine](Brandywine.md) just on the eastern side of the Brandywine Bridge south of the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md). In the Hedge at this point was a guarded gate that led to [Buckland](Buckland.md), the Eastmarch of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-Conspiracy_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conspiracy-3">[3]</a></sup>
