**Bill** was [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)'s [pony](/wiki/Ponies "Ponies"), named for its original owner [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny).

## History {#History}

Bill was owned by [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) in [Bree](/wiki/Bree), mistreated, half-starved, and probably without a name. He was the only animal in Bree after Bill Ferny or his allies had opened the stables of *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony)*, loosing all the mounts.

The next day, Bill Ferny sold the pony to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) for twelve [silver pennies](/wiki/Silver_pennies "Silver pennies"), which was more than three times its worth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This sale proved fortunate for Bill, as [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") dearly loved him and nursed him back to health.

Bill the Pony travelled with [Strider](</wiki/Strider_(Aragorn)> "Strider (Aragorn)") and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) through the leagues of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> carrying Frodo at one point on their journey to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He then accompanied the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), carrying their rations. Outside the [West-gate of Moria](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin "Doors of Durin"), Bill was released into the wild for his safety and encouraged to make his way back to Rivendell, or "wherever \[he wished\] to go."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On the homeward journey of the hobbits, Sam was reunited with Bill the Pony at the *Prancing Pony*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When the hobbits reached the gate at the bridge across the [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine), Bill Ferny threw the keys at [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and was kicked by Bill the Pony as he ran off into the night.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Sam rode Bill when he accompanied [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") on his trip to the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
