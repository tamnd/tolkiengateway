**Bill** was [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)'s [pony](../P/Ponies.md), named for its original owner [Bill Ferny](Bill_Ferny.md).

## History {#History}

Bill was owned by [Bill Ferny](Bill_Ferny.md) in [Bree](Bree.md), mistreated, half-starved, and probably without a name. He was the only animal in Bree after Bill Ferny or his allies had opened the stables of *[The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md)*, loosing all the mounts.

The next day, Bill Ferny sold the pony to [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) for twelve [silver pennies](../S/Silver_pennies.md), which was more than three times its worth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This sale proved fortunate for Bill, as [Sam Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) dearly loved him and nursed him back to health.

Bill the Pony travelled with [Strider](<../S/Strider_(Aragorn).md>) and the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) through the leagues of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> carrying Frodo at one point on their journey to [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He then accompanied the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), carrying their rations. Outside the [West-gate of Moria](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md), Bill was released into the wild for his safety and encouraged to make his way back to Rivendell, or "wherever \[he wished\] to go."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

On the homeward journey of the hobbits, Sam was reunited with Bill the Pony at the *Prancing Pony*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> When the hobbits reached the gate at the bridge across the [Brandywine](Brandywine.md), Bill Ferny threw the keys at [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and was kicked by Bill the Pony as he ran off into the night.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

Sam rode Bill when he accompanied [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) on his trip to the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
