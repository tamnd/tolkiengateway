**Brian Blessed** ([October 9](../_/9_October.md), [1937](../_/1937.md)) is a British actor. Blessed, notorious for his large red beard, played the similarly red-bearded [Farmer Giles](../F/Farmer_Giles.md) in the [*Tales from the Perilous Realm* radio series](<../T/Tales_from_the_Perilous_Realm_(1992_radio_series).md>).

Blessed broke through as Police Constable Smith in long-running police series <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-Cars" title="wikipedia:Z-Cars">Z-Cars</a></i>, and after that, most of his roles were influenced by his imposing appearance and voice. He appeared in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I,_Claudius_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:I, Claudius (TV series)">I, Claudius</a></i>, served as a narrator at *[Jackanory](../J/Jackanory.md)*, played <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_IV_of_England_(Blackadder)" title="wikipedia:Richard IV of England (Blackadder)">King Richard IV</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/blackadder" title="wikipedia:blackadder">Blackadder</a>, and many other iconic performances. It was initially rumoured that Blessed would be offered a role in [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but this later proved to be unfounded.
