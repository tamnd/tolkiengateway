**Benedict Timothy Carlton Cumberbatch**, [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire"), is a British actor who portrayed the dragon [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) and the [Necromancer/Sauron](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") using motion capture as well as his voice .<sup id="cite_ref-THCumberbatch_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-THCumberbatch-1">[1]</a></sup> He starred with [Martin Freeman](/wiki/Martin_Freeman) in the BBC TV series <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Sherlock (TV series)">Sherlock</a>.

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2013](/wiki/2013) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.bafta.org/los-angeles/britannia-awards" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Britannia Award</a></b>, British Artist of the Year, *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*
- [2014](/wiki/2014) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.behindthevoiceactors.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Behind the Voice Actors Awards</a></b>, Best Male Lead Vocal Performance in a Feature Film, *The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug*
- 2014 - **Behind the Voice Actors Awards**, Breakthrough Voice Actor of the Year.
