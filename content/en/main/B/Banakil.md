**Banakil** is the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name for the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), meaning literally 'halfling'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*[ban(a)](<Ban(a).md>)* means "half", and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=-kil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-kil (page does not exist)">-kil</a></i> is an ending meaning "person"
