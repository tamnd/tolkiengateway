<i><b>Bulbo and the Lizard King</b></i> is a Text-Adventure, made as a parody of Tolkien's works. The player is *"Bulbo"* who must defeat the evil lizard king "*Stratos*".<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It is part of "[the Balrogian Trilogy](/wiki/The_Balrogian_Trilogy "The Balrogian Trilogy")"<sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-2">[2]</a></sup> together with *[An Everyday Tale of a Seeker of Gold](/wiki/An_Everyday_Tale_of_a_Seeker_of_Gold)* and *[Fuddo and Slam](/wiki/Fuddo_and_Slam)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It is also a prequel of *[Retarded Creatures and Caverns](/wiki/Retarded_Creatures_and_Caverns)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> The game was designed by [John Wilson](/wiki/John_Wilson).<sup id="cite_ref-Spectrum_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spectrum-2">[2]</a></sup>
