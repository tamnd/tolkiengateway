<i><b>brannon</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> word meaning "lord".<sup id="cite_ref-LR351_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR351-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [BARÁD](BARAT.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LR351_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR351-2">[1]</a></sup>

*brannon* could be analyzed as *[brand](<Brand_(word).md#Exilic_Noldorin>)* ("high, noble") and the masculine ending *[-on](../_/-on.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In Welsh, <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/brenin" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">brenin</a></i> means "king".

## See also {#See_also}

- *[brennil](Brennil.md)* ("lady")
- *[hîr](../H/Hîr.md)* ("master, lord")
