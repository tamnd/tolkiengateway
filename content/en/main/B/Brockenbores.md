**Brockenbores**, or **Brockenborings**, was a network of tunnels in the northern part of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire")'s [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), in the hilly region of [Scary](/wiki/Scary).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> This was one of the more outlying parts of the Shire, which was perhaps the reason that a band of [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") rebels led by [Fredegar Bolger](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger), hid here for a time when '[Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)' took control of the Shire during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name of this place is given as 'Brockenbores' in the text of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, but as "Brockenborings" on *[A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire)*.

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests a speculative derivation from [Old Hobbitish](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") \**Broccesboran*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name(s) translate approximately as "badger-burrowings".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Brockenborings is not mere tunnels but a proper large town, with an inn, a market, multiple hobbit holes and farmfields to the north and east. It is located at the eastern edge of a wood and at the souther edge of the <a href="/wiki/Greenfields" title="Greenfields">Greenfields</a>.</dd></dl>
