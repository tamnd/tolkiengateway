**Beyond Bree 1991 J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar** was produced by the [American Mensa Tolkien Special Interest Group](../A/American_Mensa_Tolkien_Special_Interest_Group.md) and edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](../C/Carl_F._Hostetter.md). The calendar contains black and white illustrations by an international list of nine artists.
