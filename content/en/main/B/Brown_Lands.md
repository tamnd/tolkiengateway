The **Brown Lands** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) <i><b>Berennyr</b></i>) was a devastated region east of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) between [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) to the north and the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RCCorrigenda_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RCCorrigenda-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The area was the home of the [Entwives](../E/Entwives.md) who made their gardens there; but during the [War of the Last Alliance](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md) at the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), they were driven away when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) ruined the land against the advance of the [Allies](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md) down the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Ents](../E/Ents.md) vainly had crossed the Anduin in search of the [Entwives](../E/Entwives.md). [Treebeard](../T/Treebeard.md) appeared convinced that the Entwives were not all destroyed but were lost.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Even by the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), when the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) drifted by in boats upon the Great River, they saw only long, formless slopes with a withered look, without trees or even grass. What had caused such desolation not even [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) could tell.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> A one or two days long boatride downstream the Brown Lands rose into high open land.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) describes the "Brown Lands" as a translation of [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) <i><b>Berennyr</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RCCorrigenda_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RCCorrigenda-2">[2]</a></sup> from the plural forms of *[baran](<Baran_(Sindarin).md>)* ("brown", "yellow-brown")<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> and *[dôr](../D/Dôr.md)* ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
