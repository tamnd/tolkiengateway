The **Brown Lands** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") <i><b>Berennyr</b></i>) was a devastated region east of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) between [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) to the north and the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RCCorrigenda_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RCCorrigenda-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The area was the home of the [Entwives](/wiki/Entwives) who made their gardens there; but during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), they were driven away when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) ruined the land against the advance of the [Allies](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men "Last Alliance of Elves and Men") down the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The [Ents](/wiki/Ents) vainly had crossed the Anduin in search of the [Entwives](/wiki/Entwives). [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) appeared convinced that the Entwives were not all destroyed but were lost.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Even by the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), when the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) drifted by in boats upon the Great River, they saw only long, formless slopes with a withered look, without trees or even grass. What had caused such desolation not even [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) could tell.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> A one or two days long boatride downstream the Brown Lands rose into high open land.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) describes the "Brown Lands" as a translation of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) <i><b>Berennyr</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RCCorrigenda_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RCCorrigenda-2">[2]</a></sup> from the plural forms of *[baran](</wiki/Baran_(Sindarin)> "Baran (Sindarin)")* ("brown", "yellow-brown")<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> and *[dôr](/wiki/D%C3%B4r "Dôr")* ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
