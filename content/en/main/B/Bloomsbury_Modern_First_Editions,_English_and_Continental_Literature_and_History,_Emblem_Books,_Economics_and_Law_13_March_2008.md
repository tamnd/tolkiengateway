<i><b>Bloomsbury Auctions: Modern First Editions, English &amp; Continental Literature &amp; History, Emblem Books, Economics &amp; Law 13 March 2008</b></i> is an auction catalogue from Bloomsbury Auctions (London). The description for Lot 156 includes quotes from a letter from [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to Ingrid Pridgeon (cf. "[Letter to Ingrid Pridgeon](/wiki/Letter_to_Ingrid_Pridgeon)"). The second page of the letter is reproduced in a photograph.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
