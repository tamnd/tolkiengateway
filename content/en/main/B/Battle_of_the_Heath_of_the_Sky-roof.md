The **Battle of the Heath of the Sky-roof** took place during the [Faring Forth](../F/Faring_Forth.md), the great expedition by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) for the rescue of their [lost kin](../I/Ilkorindi.md) in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Sky_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sky-1">[1]</a></sup>

No full, detailed account of the battle is given, as it is only mentioned passingly in the *Lost Tales*.

## History {#History}

It took place at the [Heath of the Sky-roof](../H/Heath_of_the_Sky-roof.md), not far from [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md), and was fought between two different groups of [Men](../M/Men.md), following their invasion of Tol Eressëa. It ended in defeat for the Men friendly to the Elves, and the remaining Elves fled over the rivers [Afros](../A/Afros.md) and [Gruir](../G/Gruir.md), hoping to escape the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-Sky_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sky-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to one outline, the Mannish mariner [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) witnessed the battle and ultimately fled with the Elves, and wrote the final words of the *[Golden Book](../G/Golden_Book.md)* in the ruins of Tavrobel.<sup id="cite_ref-High_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-High-2">[2]</a></sup>

Following the battle, the last remnants of the Elves of Tol Eressëa and the Great Lands went into hiding, and eventually faded and ceased to be visible to most Men.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The battle was also called the <i><b>Battle of the High Heath</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-High_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-High-2">[2]</a></sup> as well as the <i><b>Battle of the Heaven Roof</b></i>, and the <i><b>Battle of Dor-na-Dhaideloth</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
