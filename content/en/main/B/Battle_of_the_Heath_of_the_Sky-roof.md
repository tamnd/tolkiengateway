The **Battle of the Heath of the Sky-roof** took place during the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), the great expedition by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) for the rescue of their [lost kin](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi") in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Sky_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sky-1">[1]</a></sup>

No full, detailed account of the battle is given, as it is only mentioned passingly in the *Lost Tales*.

## History {#History}

It took place at the [Heath of the Sky-roof](/wiki/Heath_of_the_Sky-roof), not far from [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel), and was fought between two different groups of [Men](/wiki/Men), following their invasion of Tol Eressëa. It ended in defeat for the Men friendly to the Elves, and the remaining Elves fled over the rivers [Afros](/wiki/Afros) and [Gruir](/wiki/Gruir), hoping to escape the invaders.<sup id="cite_ref-Sky_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sky-1">[1]</a></sup>

According to one outline, the Mannish mariner [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol) witnessed the battle and ultimately fled with the Elves, and wrote the final words of the *[Golden Book](/wiki/Golden_Book)* in the ruins of Tavrobel.<sup id="cite_ref-High_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-High-2">[2]</a></sup>

Following the battle, the last remnants of the Elves of Tol Eressëa and the Great Lands went into hiding, and eventually faded and ceased to be visible to most Men.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The battle was also called the <i><b>Battle of the High Heath</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-High_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-High-2">[2]</a></sup> as well as the <i><b>Battle of the Heaven Roof</b></i>, and the <i><b>Battle of Dor-na-Dhaideloth</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
