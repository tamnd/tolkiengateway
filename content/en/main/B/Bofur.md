**Bofur** most likely lived in the [Blue Mountains](Blue_Mountains.md) with [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) after the loss of [Thráin II](../T/Thráin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> Bofur was one of the thirteen [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Thorin's company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) who journeyed to [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) to challenge the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md). After the death of Smaug, Bofur lived at the Lonely Mountain in great wealth.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Family and birth {#Family_and_birth}

Bofur's parentage is unknown; the only familial connections mentioned are that [Bombur](Bombur.md) is his brother and [Bifur](Bifur.md) is his cousin.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-4">[4]</a></sup> He was not of Durin's line although he was descended from the Dwarves of [Moria](../M/Moria.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> His birth year is also unknown.

However, in [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md)' account of the quest to Erebor he stated that the brothers [Fíli](../F/Fíli.md) and [Kíli](../K/Kíli.md) were the youngest of the thirteen "by some fifty years",<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-5">[5]</a></sup> and that when Thorin was missing [Balin](Balin.md) was "the eldest left".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Since Kíli was born in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2864](../T/Third_Age_2864.md) and Balin was born in [2763](../T/Third_Age_2763.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> this would put Bofur's birth between [2763](../T/Third_Age_2763.md) and c. 2814, and he would have been between c. 127 and 178 years old during the quest to Erebor.

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), Bofur was one of the four Dwarves in the fifth group to arrive at [Bag End](Bag_End.md), wearing a yellow hood along with his cousin Bifur. He asked for mince pies and cheese as also stating what Bifur and Bombur wanted also. During the evening when Thorin called for music, Bofur (along with [Bifur](Bifur.md)) played a clarinet.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-7">[7]</a></sup>

After escaping the Goblins, the company was chased by wolves into a glade, Bofur shared a pine tree with Bifur, [Bombur](Bombur.md), and Thorin, and later was rescued by the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> When [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) was telling the story of the company's adventures to [Beorn](Beorn.md), Bofur, Bifur, and Bombur were the last of the Dwarves to show up. The following morning, Bilbo realizes Bofur had fallen over him and then told him about breakfast left for Bilbo from the skin-changer<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Later on in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) when Bombur needed support to escape the spiders, Bifur and Bofur helped him. Then after the barrel ride down the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md) both Bofur and Bifur came out fairly dry and not very knocked about, but they lay down and refused to do anything.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Later Bofur and Bombur would stay down to take care of the ponies, nearly killed by Smaug, though saved.

Bofur fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](Battle_of_Five_Armies.md). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md), Thorin's cousin and now king under the mountain after Thorin's death, and made his home in Erebor along with Bifur and Bombur and the other survivors of the Battle.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

At [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md) told [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) that Bofur was still alive and living in Erebor, along with Bifur and Bombur.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that Bofur fought in the [Battle of Dale](Battle_of_Dale.md) against [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s forces the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md). His death date is unknown though it would have been before [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) as he was the last dwarf of the company to pass away in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [91](../F/Fourth_Age_91.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Bǫvurr* appears in the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. Its meaning is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one of the earliest manuscript fragments of *The Hobbit*, Bofur and Bifur went into the hall and came back with their walking sticks, which they *turned into* clarinets.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> In the published text the two Dwarves came back from the hall with clarinets that they *had left* with their walking sticks.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-7">[7]</a></sup> [John Rateliff](../J/John_Rateliff.md) (author of *[The History of The Hobbit](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)*) said that in the earliest phase Tolkien had added fairy tale touches of Dwarven magic to emphasize the uncanny, other-worldly nature of the Dwarves as opposed to Bilbo.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

Originally, when Bombur needed propping up in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) after being cut from the spiders' webbing, it was *his cousins* Bofur and Bifur who provided support.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> In the published story this was changed to Bombur's cousin Bifur and brother Bofur.<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-4">[4]</a></sup> Also, in the earlier version, when [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) first came out and the Dwarves in the valley had to be hauled up to the [Back Door](Back_Door.md), Bifur cried out to save Bombur and Bofur, who were both his *brothers*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> In the published text Bifur cried out for his two *cousins*.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

In Tolkien's unfinished [1960](../_/1960.md) rewrite of *The Hobbit*, Bifur, Bofur and Bombur were Thorin's attendants.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup> [John D. Rateliff](../J/John_D._Rateliff.md) assumes this made them either courtiers or honor-guard.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

In the earliest partial manuscript of *The Hobbit* more of the dialog later assigned to a few of the Dwarves is more evenly distributed. In the opinion of John Rateliff this streamlining strengthened the story through simplification, but at the cost of relegating some of the Dwarves to obscurity since they barely speak at all. Bofur is one of these "silenced" members of the company.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup>

### Films {#Films}

<b>1967: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1967_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1967 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1967 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bofur is omitted. <a href="../T/Thorin.md" title="Thorin">Thorin Oakenshield</a> only travels with an unnamed guard and the princess of Dale.<sup id="cite_ref-Deitch_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Deitch-22">[22]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1977: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>No actor is specified for the role of Bofur. If he does speak, it is only ever in unison with the rest of the Dwarves.</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bofur was portrayed by <a href="../J/James_Nesbitt.md" title="James Nesbitt">James Nesbitt</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-23">[23]</a></sup> A description of Bofur in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Endearing and likeable, Bofur has a disastrous tendency to state the obvious and blurt out things without thinking. With a love of music and a fine singing voice, Bofur delights in good food and good company and is unfailingly optimistic. Though not especially brave, he will do his best to help those in need, especially those he counts as friends. Along with his brother Bombur and his cousin Bifur, Bofur joined the Quest for the Lonely Mountain partly to seek his fortune – and partly because he was told the beer was free.

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../W/Warner_Bros..md" title="Warner Bros.">Warner Bros.</a><sup id="cite_ref-HobbitApp_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HobbitApp-24">[24]</a></sup></cite></p>

<dl><dd>In <i><a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a></i>, due to excessive partying the night before, Bofur ends up oversleeping and being left behind in <a href="../L/Lake-town.md" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> when the other Dwarves finally head up to the Lonely Mountain. With <a href="../_/Óin.md" title="Óin">Óin</a> and <a href="../F/Fíli.md" title="Fíli">Fíli</a> also having stayed behind to tend to the sickly <a href="../K/Kíli.md" title="Kíli">Kíli</a> (due to having been struck by a poisoned arrow), he joins them in doing so. He is ultimately assigned with the task of finding some <a href="../A/Athelas.md" title="Athelas">Kingsfoil</a> to help heal Kíli&#39;s wound, which he does.</dd></dl>
