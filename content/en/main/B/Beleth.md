**Beleth** (born [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [417](/wiki/First_Age_417 "First Age 417")) was the eldest child and only daughter of [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas).

## History {#History}

Beleth was two years older than her elder brother [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund) and four years older than her younger brother [Belegund](/wiki/Belegund). After the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach), most women and children of the house of Bëor were forced to flee Dorthonion. No tales relate the fate of Beleth; her brothers fell with their uncle [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) when their [band of outlaws](/wiki/Barahir%27s_outlaws "Barahir's outlaws") was betrayed.<sup id="cite_ref-Ruin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ruin-1">[1]</a></sup> Through her father Bregolas, Beleth was the cousin of the renowned *[Beren](/wiki/Beren) the One-handed*.

Beleth's remote descendant in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) was [Beregar](/wiki/Beregar), father of [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup> Hence, it is assumed that Beleth was married and had descendants from her unknown children.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of the House of Bëor, Beleth was the daughter of [Boron](/wiki/Boron) and thus aunt to Bregor. In the last revision to the genealogy Beleth was moved down three generations.<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeor-2">[2]</a></sup>

Beleth is also known as "Bereth" in a note to "*Aldarion and Erendis*" in *Unfinished Tales*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> however [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) noted that this was a mistake on his part years later.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
