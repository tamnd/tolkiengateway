**Bereg** was a [Man](/wiki/Men "Men") of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) who fought in the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg). Bereg was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), where he was portrayed by [Ray Trickitt](/wiki/Ray_Trickitt).

## History {#History}

When the Elf Army arrived at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep), it was Bereg who ordered the gate to be opened for them. It is unknown if he survived the battle.
