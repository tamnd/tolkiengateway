**Brian Sibley** (born [14 July](../_/14_July.md) [1949](../_/1949.md)) is an English author, broadcaster and screenwriter. He is author of over 100 hours of radio drama and has written and presented hundreds of radio documentaries, features and weekly programmes.

Born in London, he grew up and was educated in Chislehurst, Kent.

In 1981, he co-wrote BBC Radio 4's [adaptation](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>) of [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* with [Michael Bakewell](../M/Michael_Bakewell.md). Sibley subsequently wrote *[The Lord of the Rings: The Making of the Movie Trilogy](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Making_of_the_Movie_Trilogy.md)* and other books accompanying [*The Lord of the Rings* trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), including a biography of [Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md). He reprised this role a decade later by writing three companion books for [*The Hobbit* trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

He is also the author of a series of map books starting with *[The Map of Tolkien's Middle-earth](../T/The_Map_of_Tolkien's_Middle-earth.md)*, as well as the editor of book *[The Fall of Númenor](<../T/The_Fall_of_Númenor_(book).md>)*.
