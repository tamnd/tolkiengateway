<i><b>Beyond the Western Seas</b></i> is the first album by the folk band *[The Lonely Mountain Band](/wiki/The_Lonely_Mountain_Band)*. The album was released on [September 22](/wiki/22_September "22 September"), [2010](/wiki/2010). The cover art of the CD was done by [Ted Nasmith](/wiki/Ted_Nasmith).
