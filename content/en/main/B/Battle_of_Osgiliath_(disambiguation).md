The **Battle of Osgiliath** can refer to:

- [Anárion's defence of Osgiliath](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion%27s_defence_of_Osgiliath) in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), from [3429](/wiki/Second_Age_3429 "Second Age 3429") to [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434").
- [Burning of Osgiliath](/wiki/Burning_of_Osgiliath) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1437](/wiki/Third_Age_1437 "Third Age 1437"), during the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife).
- [Ruin of Osgiliath](/wiki/Ruin_of_Osgiliath) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2475](/wiki/Third_Age_2475 "Third Age 2475"), featuring the first appearance of the [Uruk-hai](/wiki/Uruk-hai).
- [Sauron's attack on Osgiliath](/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath) on [20 June](/wiki/20_June) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), which gave him control of the eastern part of the city.
- [Faramir's defence of Osgiliath](/wiki/Faramir%27s_defence_of_Osgiliath) on [12 March](/wiki/12_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") as the Morgul-host headed for the [Battle of the Pelennor](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor).
