The **Battle of Osgiliath** can refer to:

- [Anárion's defence of Osgiliath](../A/Anárion's_defence_of_Osgiliath.md) in the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), from [3429](../S/Second_Age_3429.md) to [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md).
- [Burning of Osgiliath](Burning_of_Osgiliath.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1437](../T/Third_Age_1437.md), during the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md).
- [Ruin of Osgiliath](../R/Ruin_of_Osgiliath.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2475](../T/Third_Age_2475.md), featuring the first appearance of the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md).
- [Sauron's attack on Osgiliath](../S/Sauron's_attack_on_Osgiliath.md) on [20 June](../_/20_June.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), which gave him control of the eastern part of the city.
- [Faramir's defence of Osgiliath](../F/Faramir's_defence_of_Osgiliath.md) on [12 March](../_/12_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) as the Morgul-host headed for the [Battle of the Pelennor](Battle_of_the_Pelennor.md).
