**Bill Stickers** was a villain — a figure in a "long-running family epic" told by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to his children. The stories "revolved around the tireless efforts of [Major Road Ahead](../M/Major_Road_Ahead.md) to prosecute the villain".<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bill Stickers was an "irrepressible villain" and "a huge hulk of a man who always got away with everything".<sup id="cite_ref-Olney_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Olney-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tolkien was inspired by "signs hanging on various Oxford gates",<sup id="cite_ref-Bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio-1">[1]</a></sup> saying "Bill Stickers Will Be Prosecuted".<sup id="cite_ref-Olney_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Olney-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> Basing his discussion on a newspaper article revealing that [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) was a member a secret gang during the 1920s,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> [David Bratman](../D/David_Bratman.md) has speculated that Tolkien in addition might have been inspired by the gang member Margaret Pollard's (a friend of Lewis's) gang pseudonym, "Bill Stickers".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

The stories about Bill Stickers and Major Road Ahead were only told orally (possibly around [1926](../_/1926.md) and [1930](../_/1930.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>), and have thus not been recorded,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> unlike, for example, *[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)* and *[Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)*.
