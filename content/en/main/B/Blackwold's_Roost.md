**Blackwold's Roost** was an [Arnorian](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") ruin in [Archet](/wiki/Archet)-Dale in *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After Arnor was divided the fortress was held by [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan). After the fall of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) the fortress fell into disrepair. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018") a band of [Blackwolds](/wiki/Blackwolds) made their camp in the ruin<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and started to attack [Archet](/wiki/Archet) under leading of [Otto the Brigand](/wiki/List_of_minor_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_characters_in_Bree-land#Otto_the_Brigand "List of minor The Lord of the Rings Online characters in Bree-land").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After Otto became a prisoner in [Archet](/wiki/Archet) an <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Angmarim&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Angmarim (page does not exist)">Angmarim</a> named [Éogan](/wiki/%C3%89ogan) succeed him. He assaulted Archet to capture two [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) named [Mundo Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Mundo_Sackville-Baggins) and [Celandine Brandybuck](/wiki/Celandine_Brandybuck) but was defeated by [Captain Brackenbrook](/wiki/Captain_Brackenbrook)'s men.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Jon Brackenbrook](/wiki/Jon_Brackenbrook) then drove the [Blackwolds](/wiki/Blackwolds) back into Blackwold's Roost.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
