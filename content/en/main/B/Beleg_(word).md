<i><b>beleg</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "mighty",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or "great, large".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It is applied as a proper name to the character [**Beleg** Cúthalion](/wiki/Beleg "Beleg") and [**Beleg** (King of Arthedain)](</wiki/Beleg_(King_of_Arthedain)> "Beleg (King of Arthedain)").

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="/wiki/Belegaer" title="Belegaer"><b>Beleg</b>aer</a></i> = "Great Sea"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Belegost" title="Belegost"><b>Beleg</b>ost</a></i> = "Great Fortress"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Beleg%C3%BBr" title="Belegûr"><b>Beleg</b>ur</a></i> = "He who arises in Might"
- <i><a href="/wiki/C%C3%BBl_Veleg" title="Cûl Veleg">Cûl <b>Veleg</b></a></i> = "Bigload"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Laer_C%C3%BA_Beleg" title="Laer Cú Beleg">Laer Cú <b>Beleg</b></a></i> = "Song of the Great Bow"
