**Badgers** were known in the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) for being excellent hole-diggers.<sup id="cite_ref-FRII4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRII4-1">[1]</a></sup> [Men](/wiki/Men) used traps to catch these animals.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

A mysterious kind of apparently sentient badgers, only told of in legends, were called the [Badger-folk](/wiki/Badger-folk).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Indeed, [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) told to [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and his companions some absurd stories about the queer ways of badgers.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Badgers (which are given game statistics) are carnivorous animals of the fauna of Rhovanion. They are timid but can be dangerous if cornered.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Badgers (which are given game statistics) can be find in the Shire and throughout Eriador.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>
