**Bears** were large and ferocious animals, notorious for their love of honey.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

There were black and brown bears in considerable number on the island of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Like all the animals on the isle, they were friendly to [Men](/wiki/Men). Though they did not like to be disturbed, living far from humans within mountainous or rocky areas, with the great black bears being mostly found within the [Forostar](/wiki/Forostar). Yet they often came to visit houses and were given honey. While sometimes on rare occurrences, a [Númenorean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenoreans "Númenoreans") may be killed by bears or a *"bad bear"* would occasionally raid domesticated beehives, neither race considered these reasons to make war on the other. The strangest occurrence between the relations of Men and bears were the *"bear-dances"*, which were curious dances put on by the bears, often black bears, for the delight and entertainment of Men, by whose instruction these bear-dances may have been improved upon. The most famous of the bear-dances was the [Great Bear-dance](/wiki/Great_Bear-dance) of [Tompollë](/wiki/Tompoll%C3%AB), performed annually in the [Forostar](/wiki/Forostar) during autumn.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Great and ancient bears had lived within the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) for some time. Though by the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), their number had diminished. [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) told [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") that [Giants](/wiki/Giants) had taken up their homes in that mountain range. According to Gandalf, some believe that [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), a skin changer, was a black bear that was descended from them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word for "bear" was *graw*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Early [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) had two words for *bear*: *brôg* and *megli*, with the variation *meglivorn* ("Black bear").<sup id="cite_ref-morok_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morok-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word is given as <i><b>morco</b></i>, sharing a [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") with *brôg*.<sup id="cite_ref-morok_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morok-5">[5]</a></sup> In [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) is given as *morokō*<sup id="cite_ref-morok_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morok-5">[5]</a></sup> and *mor(o)kē* ("she-bear").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) also had its own terms, specifically *urug* in "common" gender singular ("bear", as a species/creature), the masculine singular *urgû* ("male bear") and the feminine singular *urgî* ("female bear", "she-bear").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest outlines of *[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")*, [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) was clad in the skins of bears.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-8">[8]</a></sup> The sinews of bears were also used to make bowstring<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and harp strings.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

### *Mr. Bliss* {#Mr._Bliss}

*[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)* includes three mischievous bears <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Archie&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Archie (page does not exist)">Archie</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Teddy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Teddy (page does not exist)">Teddy</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bruno&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bruno (page does not exist)">Bruno</a>, who more resemble teddybears in Tolkien's drawings.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### *Letters from Father Christmas* {#Letters_from_Father_Christmas}

Bears in [Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas) include <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Polar_Bears&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Polar Bears (page does not exist)">Polar Bears</a>, most notably the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear) and his nephews [Paksu](/wiki/Paksu) and [Valkotukka](/wiki/Valkotukka) and the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cave_Bears&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cave Bears (page does not exist)">Cave Bears</a>, including [Mr Cave Bear](/wiki/Mr_Cave_Bear) and his sons and nephews.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
