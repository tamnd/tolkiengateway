**Bears** were large and ferocious animals, notorious for their love of honey.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

There were black and brown bears in considerable number on the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). Like all the animals on the isle, they were friendly to [Men](../M/Men.md). Though they did not like to be disturbed, living far from humans within mountainous or rocky areas, with the great black bears being mostly found within the [Forostar](../F/Forostar.md). Yet they often came to visit houses and were given honey. While sometimes on rare occurrences, a [Númenorean](../N/Númenoreans.md) may be killed by bears or a *"bad bear"* would occasionally raid domesticated beehives, neither race considered these reasons to make war on the other. The strangest occurrence between the relations of Men and bears were the *"bear-dances"*, which were curious dances put on by the bears, often black bears, for the delight and entertainment of Men, by whose instruction these bear-dances may have been improved upon. The most famous of the bear-dances was the [Great Bear-dance](../G/Great_Bear-dance.md) of [Tompollë](../T/Tompollë.md), performed annually in the [Forostar](../F/Forostar.md) during autumn.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Great and ancient bears had lived within the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) for some time. Though by the late [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), their number had diminished. [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) told [Bilbo](Bilbo_Baggins.md) that [Giants](../G/Giants.md) had taken up their homes in that mountain range. According to Gandalf, some believe that [Beorn](Beorn.md), a skin changer, was a black bear that was descended from them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "bear" was *graw*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Early [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) had two words for *bear*: *brôg* and *megli*, with the variation *meglivorn* ("Black bear").<sup id="cite_ref-morok_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morok-5">[5]</a></sup>

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word is given as <i><b>morco</b></i>, sharing a [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) with *brôg*.<sup id="cite_ref-morok_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morok-5">[5]</a></sup> In [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) is given as *morokō*<sup id="cite_ref-morok_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-morok-5">[5]</a></sup> and *mor(o)kē* ("she-bear").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Adûnaic](../A/Adûnaic.md) also had its own terms, specifically *urug* in "common" gender singular ("bear", as a species/creature), the masculine singular *urgû* ("male bear") and the feminine singular *urgî* ("female bear", "she-bear").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest outlines of *[The Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)*, [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) was clad in the skins of bears.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-8">[8]</a></sup> The sinews of bears were also used to make bowstring<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> and harp strings.<sup id="cite_ref-Fall_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fall-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other writings {#Other_writings}

### *Mr. Bliss* {#Mr._Bliss}

*[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)* includes three mischievous bears <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Archie&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Archie (page does not exist)">Archie</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Teddy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Teddy (page does not exist)">Teddy</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Bruno&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bruno (page does not exist)">Bruno</a>, who more resemble teddybears in Tolkien's drawings.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### *Letters from Father Christmas* {#Letters_from_Father_Christmas}

Bears in [Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md) include <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Polar_Bears&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Polar Bears (page does not exist)">Polar Bears</a>, most notably the [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) and his nephews [Paksu](../P/Paksu.md) and [Valkotukka](../V/Valkotukka.md) and the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Cave_Bears&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cave Bears (page does not exist)">Cave Bears</a>, including [Mr Cave Bear](../M/Mr_Cave_Bear.md) and his sons and nephews.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
