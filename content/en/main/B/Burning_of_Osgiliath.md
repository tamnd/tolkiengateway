The **Burning of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath)** was a siege that occurred in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1437](/wiki/Third_Age_1437 "Third Age 1437").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During the [Kin-strife](/wiki/Kin-strife), [Castamir](/wiki/Castamir), a descendant of the kings, led a rebellion against [Eldacar](</wiki/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Eldacar (King of Gondor)"), the son of King [Valacar](/wiki/Valacar). Eldacar had the support of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) in the service of Gondor and of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) of the northern part of the realm, but they were overmatched by the forces of Castamir, drawn from the people of the coasts and of the great havens of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar). Although he fought valiantly, Eldacar was pushed back into capital and there besieged until Castamir's greater army and hunger drove him out.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the siege and subsequent burning of the city the Tower of the [Stone of Osgiliath](/wiki/Stone_of_Osgiliath) was destroyed and the *palantír* was lost in the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). When the city was taken, Castamir showed his cruel side, putting [Ornendil](/wiki/Ornendil), the son of Eldacar, to death and causing slaughter and destruction far above the needs of war.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
