The **Burning of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md)** was a siege that occurred in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1437](../T/Third_Age_1437.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> During the [Kin-strife](../K/Kin-strife~3650.md), [Castamir](../C/Castamir.md), a descendant of the kings, led a rebellion against [Eldacar](<../E/Eldacar_(King_of_Gondor).md>), the son of King [Valacar](../V/Valacar.md). Eldacar had the support of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md) in the service of Gondor and of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) of the northern part of the realm, but they were overmatched by the forces of Castamir, drawn from the people of the coasts and of the great havens of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md) and [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md). Although he fought valiantly, Eldacar was pushed back into capital and there besieged until Castamir's greater army and hunger drove him out.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the siege and subsequent burning of the city the Tower of the [Stone of Osgiliath](../S/Stone_of_Osgiliath.md) was destroyed and the *palantír* was lost in the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). When the city was taken, Castamir showed his cruel side, putting [Ornendil](../O/Ornendil.md), the son of Eldacar, to death and causing slaughter and destruction far above the needs of war.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
