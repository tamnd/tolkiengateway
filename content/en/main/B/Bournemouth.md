**Bournemouth** is a large town on the south coast of England. It was a place that [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) frequently visited for his holidays; staying in the same room at the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hotel_Miramar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hotel Miramar (page does not exist)">Hotel Miramar</a>, with a second room to write in. In the late 1960s Tolkien retired to nearby [Poole](../W/Woodridings.md) with his [wife](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) to escape the [attention](../T/Tolkien_fandom.md) of [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md). After his wife's death, Tolkien moved back to Oxford and lived at <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=21_Merton_Street&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="21 Merton Street (page does not exist)">21 Merton Street</a>. In late August [1973](../_/1973.md), he visited Bournemouth to see some friends; it was here that he died on [September 2](../_/2_September.md), [1973](../_/1973.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
