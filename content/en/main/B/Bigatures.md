**Big-atures** or **Bigatures** are terms referring to miniature models created by [Weta Workshop](../W/Weta_Workshop.md) for [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>). Because of their size, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Christy_Hennah&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Christy Hennah (page does not exist)">Christy Hennah</a> named them "big-atures" instead of "miniatures".

During pre-production of the films, instead of creating structures fully digitally, [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) decided to create small-scale models of structures which depicted them down to their last detail. The models were then filmed and enhanced digitally.

## Examples {#Examples}

Some of the Big-atures Weta created include:

- **[Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md)**

Minas Tirith was built in a 1/72 scale and some of its parts in a 1/14 scale for closer scenes. According to the Extended DVD edition of [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), it required almost a thousand houses to be completed. Its total height was around 7 meters.

- **[Morannon](../M/Morannon.md)**

- **[Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md)**

[John Howe](../J/John_Howe.md) revisited the original drawings based on [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s writings, and the model was built on a rocky pinnacle instead directly on a mountain.
