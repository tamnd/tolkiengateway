<i><b>Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment</b></i> (later impressions have *Finnesburg*) is a Modern English edition of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) poems *[Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)")* and the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnesburg_Fragment" title="wikipedia:Finnesburg Fragment">Finnesburg Fragment</a></i>. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) contributed with the section *[Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of 'Beowulf'](/wiki/Prefatory_Remarks_on_Prose_Translation_of_%27Beowulf%27)* (pp. vii-xli).

The first edition of the translation was published in [1911](/wiki/1911). Later printings counted the 1940 edition as the second impression. Wrenn and Tolkien's contributions were not included until [1940](/wiki/1940).

Tolkien's essay was reprinted in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](/wiki/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays)* as "[On Translating Beowulf](/wiki/Prefatory_Remarks_on_Prose_Translation_of_%27Beowulf%27 "Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of 'Beowulf'")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
