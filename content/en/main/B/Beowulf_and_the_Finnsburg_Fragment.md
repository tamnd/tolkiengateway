<i><b>Beowulf and the Finnsburg Fragment</b></i> (later impressions have *Finnesburg*) is a Modern English edition of the [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) poems *[Beowulf](<Beowulf_(poem).md>)* and the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnesburg_Fragment" title="wikipedia:Finnesburg Fragment">Finnesburg Fragment</a></i>. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) contributed with the section *[Prefatory Remarks on Prose Translation of 'Beowulf'](../P/Prefatory_Remarks_on_Prose_Translation_of_'Beowulf'.md)* (pp. vii-xli).

The first edition of the translation was published in [1911](../_/1911.md). Later printings counted the 1940 edition as the second impression. Wrenn and Tolkien's contributions were not included until [1940](../_/1940.md).

Tolkien's essay was reprinted in *[The Monsters and the Critics and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics_and_Other_Essays.md)* as "[On Translating Beowulf](../P/Prefatory_Remarks_on_Prose_Translation_of_'Beowulf'.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
