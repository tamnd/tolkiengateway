**Big Folk** and **Big People** were names for the race of [Men](/wiki/Men), given them by the [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), who they in turn called the [Little Folk](/wiki/Little_Folk). The term was especially used for the **[Men of Bree](/wiki/Men_of_Bree)** (q.v.), as in the [Bree-land](/wiki/Bree-land) the two races lived side by side.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
