**William Francis "Bill" Nighy** (born [1949](../_/1949.md)) is an English actor.

## History {#History}

Born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caterham" title="wikipedia:Caterham">Caterham</a>, Surrey in [1949](../_/1949.md), Nighy was the third child of mechanic Alfred Martin Nighy and nurse Catherine Josephine Whittaker. He was raised a catholic (his mother was Irish) and attended Guildford School of Drama. Toward the end of the seventies, he made his West End debut in *Illuminatus!*. He continued with roles in radio drama, stage and television, and though the role of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) in [BBC](BBC.md)'s [1981](../_/1981.md) radio production of *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series).md>)* was among his larger roles, he did not get widespread attention in the world of drama until the 1991 serial *The Men's Room*. His fame as an on-screen actor came from *Love Actually*, and he has been cast in several big productions since.
