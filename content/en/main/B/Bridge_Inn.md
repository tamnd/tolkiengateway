The **Bridge Inn** was an inn on the West side of [Brandywine Bridge](Brandywine_Bridge.md) in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). It was torn down by the [Ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), and replaced with a guard house. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) were arrested here by the [Shirriffs](../S/Shirriffs.md) when they returned home from their adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
