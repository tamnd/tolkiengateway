The **Bridge Inn** was an inn on the West side of [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was torn down by the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), and replaced with a guard house. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") were arrested here by the [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs) when they returned home from their adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
