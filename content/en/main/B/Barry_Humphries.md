**John Barry Humphries** ([17 February](/wiki/17_February), [1934](/wiki/1934)-[22 April](/wiki/22_April), [2023](/wiki/2023)) was an Australian actor and entertainer. He portrayed the [Goblin King](/wiki/Great_Goblin "Great Goblin") in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
