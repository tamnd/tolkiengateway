<i><b>buri</b></i> is a word meaning "son of" in the [Drúadan language](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_language).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Ghân-buri-Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n-buri-Gh%C3%A2n) (Ghân son of Ghân; *[Ghân](/wiki/Gh%C3%A2n)* + *buri*)
