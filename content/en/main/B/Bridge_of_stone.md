The "**bridge of stone**" was built by the Dwarves of [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost) in return for knowledge taught to them by [Melian](/wiki/Melian) and for pearls given to them by King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol). The great bridge was constructed of stone and was the only means of entering the gates of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
