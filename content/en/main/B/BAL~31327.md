**BAL** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "powerful, mighty, have power".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin)/[Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *bálā*, *bal-ī*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> ("Power, God")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Vala](/wiki/Valar "Valar")*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *Bala*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *Bala*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin"): *[Balan](/wiki/Balan)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
