**BAL** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "powerful, mighty, have power".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md)/[Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *bálā*, *bal-ī*<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> ("Power, God")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[Vala](../V/Valar.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md): *Bala*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Old Noldorin](../K/Kornoldorin.md): *Bala*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Exilic Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *[Balan](Balan.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[2]</a></sup>
