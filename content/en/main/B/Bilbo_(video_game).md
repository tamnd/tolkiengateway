**Bilbo** is Swedish video game made by [Nils Eng](/wiki/Nils_Eng). In this game it isn't [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") but [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") who takes [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") to [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> But because of criticism on his webpage that Eng didn't follow the books the names of Bilbo were changed to Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> This new version was released in [1997](/wiki/1997). The title however was not changed.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
