**Bilbo** is Swedish video game made by [Nils Eng](../N/Nils_Eng.md). In this game it isn't [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) but [Bilbo](Bilbo_Baggins.md) who takes [the Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) to [Mount Doom](../M/Mount_Doom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> But because of criticism on his webpage that Eng didn't follow the books the names of Bilbo were changed to Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> This new version was released in [1997](../_/1997.md). The title however was not changed.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
