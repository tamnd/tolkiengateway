**Bret McKenzie** is a New Zealand actor and musician, best known as half of the entertainment duo <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_of_the_Conchords" title="wikipedia:Flight of the Conchords">Flight of the Conchords</a></i>. He is the son of actor [Peter McKenzie](/wiki/Peter_McKenzie).

McKenzie gathered a huge web following for his tiny role as a background character in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), later dubbed "[Figwit](/wiki/Figwit)". He portrayed [Lindir](/wiki/Lindir) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). He also wrote and performed the song "<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Frodo,_Don%27t_Wear_the_Ring&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Frodo, Don&#39;t Wear the Ring (page does not exist)">Frodo, Don&#39;t Wear the Ring</a>" with *Flight of the Conchords*.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2001](/wiki/2001): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*: [Figwit](/wiki/Figwit)
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*: Figwit (labelled as *Aegnor, Elven escort*)
- [2012](/wiki/2012): *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)*: [Lindir](/wiki/Lindir)
