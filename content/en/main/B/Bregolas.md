**Bregolas** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [393](/wiki/First_Age_393 "First Age 393")-[455](/wiki/First_Age_455 "First Age 455")<sup id="cite_ref-WJwest_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJwest-1">[1]</a></sup>) was the fifth chieftain of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) and third [Lord of Ladros](/wiki/Lord_of_Ladros).

Bregolas was slain, along with a great part of the warriors of his folk, in the assault upon the defences of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) during the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> His brother [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), Barahir's son [Beren](/wiki/Beren), and Bregolas' own sons [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund) and [Belegund](/wiki/Belegund) were not at this time slain, and [continued resisting Morgoth](/wiki/XII_B%C3%ABorings "XII Bëorings").

Bregolas was further the grandfather of both [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) and [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, *Bregolas* is said to be an [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Exilic_Noldorin) word for "fierceness", from the root BERÉK-.<sup id="cite_ref-Etym_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etym-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *[The Grey Annals](/wiki/The_Grey_Annals)*, created in the 1930s,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Bëor was directly followed by Bregolas and [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir), who were his sons (and Bregolas was born in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [400](/wiki/First_Age_400 "First Age 400")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>). After publication of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* a new genealogy for the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) was made in the 1950s and at that time four generations were inserted after Bëor, with Bregor becoming the father of Bregolas and Barahir.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
