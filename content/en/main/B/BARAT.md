**BARAT**, **BARATH**, **BARAD**, **BARAS** are [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [roots](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "great height combined with strength, size, majesty". The root also appears as **BAR-AT/AD**, signifying "lofty, high".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- BARAT
    - [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[barad](Barad.md)*; *[brand, brann](<Brand_(word).md#Sindarin>)*

- BARATH
    - Sindarin: *[bereth](Bereth.md)*

- BARAD
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[Varda](../V/Varda.md)*; *[varanda](../V/Varanda.md)*

- BARAS
    - Sindarin: *brasta-* ("tower up, loom"); 'brass *("great cliff");* avras *("a precipice")*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appear the roots BARÁD- ("lofty, sublime"), BARAT- (from which was derived Noldorin *barad* "tower, fortress"), and BARATH- (from which was derived *Barathī* "spouse of [Manwe](../M/Manwë.md), Queen of Stars").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
