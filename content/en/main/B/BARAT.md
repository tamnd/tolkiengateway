**BARAT**, **BARATH**, **BARAD**, **BARAS** are [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "great height combined with strength, size, majesty". The root also appears as **BAR-AT/AD**, signifying "lofty, high".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- BARAT
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[barad](/wiki/Barad "Barad")*; *[brand, brann](</wiki/Brand_(word)#Sindarin> "Brand (word)")*

- BARATH
    - Sindarin: *[bereth](/wiki/Bereth "Bereth")*

- BARAD
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Varda](/wiki/Varda)*; *[varanda](/wiki/Varanda "Varanda")*

- BARAS
    - Sindarin: *brasta-* ("tower up, loom"); 'brass *("great cliff");* avras *("a precipice")*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appear the roots BARÁD- ("lofty, sublime"), BARAT- (from which was derived Noldorin *barad* "tower, fortress"), and BARATH- (from which was derived *Barathī* "spouse of [Manwe](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB "Manwë"), Queen of Stars").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
