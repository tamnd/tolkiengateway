**Barrow Wight** is a Tolkien-related heavy/black metal band from Ottawa, Canada, formed in 2006. The band takes its name from the [Barrow-wights](Barrow-wight.md) of Tolkien's legendarium, and its releases draw heavily on Middle-earth. Its debut full-length *Kings in Saurons Service* (2016) includes songs such as "Osgiliath", "Grond" and "The Palantir".

## Members {#Members}

- Antero Wight - bass, vocals
- Akiva - guitars
- Elf Ripper - drums

## Discography {#Discography}

- Rock into Mordor (Demo, 2010)
- In League with Sauron (Demo, 2012)
- Power from the East (EP, 2014)
- Unfinished Tales (Compilation, 2015)
- No Sleep Till Gondor (Single, 2015)
- Kings in Saurons Service (Full-length, 2016)
- Morgül Blade (Single, 2019)
