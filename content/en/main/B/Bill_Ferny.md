**Bill Ferny** was a man of [Bree](/wiki/Bree), known to be an associate of the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) and later, a ruffian of the [Chief's Men](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians").

## Description {#Description}

Descriptions of Ferny's appearance are limited to his being a "swarthy Bree-lander"<sup id="cite_ref-FRI9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRI9-1">[1]</a></sup> or a "sneery swarthy fellow."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Bill Ferny lived in the last house of Bree, and was an opportunistic backstabber who was little liked by respectable folk of the town. He also served as a spy for the [Black Riders](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl"), and relayed information about [Shire-hobbits](/wiki/Shire-hobbits) to them.

On [September 29th](/wiki/September_29 "September 29") of [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), Ferny and his [associate](/wiki/Squint-eyed_southerner "Squint-eyed southerner") were at [The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony) when the two of them saw [Mr. Underhill](/wiki/Mr._Underhill) vanish into thin air. They reported the information to the Black Riders.<sup id="cite_ref-UT10_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT10-3">[3]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/1/1e/Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg/100px-Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="100" height="131" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/1/1e/Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg/150px-Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/1/1e/Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg/200px-Cor_Blok_-_Bill_Ferney_hit_by_an_apple.jpg 2x" data-file-width="700" data-file-height="915"/></a><figcaption>&#34;Bill Ferney hit by an apple&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Cor_Blok" title="Cor Blok">Cor Blok</a></figcaption></figure>

After reporting to the Black Riders, Ferny and his allies went to the inn's stables and set all mounts loose. There was only one pony left in all of Bree: [Bill's mistreated one](/wiki/Bill_the_Pony "Bill the Pony"). He sold him for twelve [silver pennies](/wiki/Silver_pennies "Silver pennies") to [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and the [Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers"). As a parting gift, [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) threw an [apple](/wiki/Apples "Apples") at his head.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ferny's associate was spotted inside his house when the company set out to [Weathertop](/wiki/Weathertop).<sup id="cite_ref-FRI11_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRI11-5">[5]</a></sup>

After the [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians) among the [Southerners](/wiki/Southerners) were kicked out of Bree, it was believed by [Barliman Butterbur](/wiki/Barliman_Butterbur) that Bill Ferny and [Harry Goatleaf](/wiki/Harry_Goatleaf) opened the gates to let them in since the two of them sided with Ruffians in the battle that followed.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> After fleeing Bree with the Ruffians, he threw in his lot with the [Men](/wiki/Sharkey%27s_Men "Sharkey's Men") serving [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins) in the [Shire](/wiki/Shire). He guarded the outer gate at the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge) as the Chief's Big Man and his command over the local [Shirriffs](/wiki/Shirriffs) was hard. When the four [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) returned to the Shire, he attempted to threaten them, but was threatened by [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) in turn, and as such he fled into the night after giving him the gate key, though not until after his old pony had kicked him in his back. He was never heard of again.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Bill* is a name Tolkien used to represent the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name **Bil**, which was a short for names as *Arambil*, *Bildad* and *Bilcuzal*, all of unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> *Ferny* is a reference to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fern" title="wikipedia:Fern">plant</a>, as many surnames of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) were botanical in nature.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an earlier draft of [The Scouring of the Shire](/wiki/The_Scouring_of_the_Shire), it is Frodo who threatens Ferny rather than Merry. Later, Sam compares [Ted Sandyman](/wiki/Ted_Sandyman) to Ferny.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

At one points he attempts to forge an alliance between Sharkey&#39;s Men and a band of <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a> from <a href="/wiki/Angmar" title="Angmar">Angmar</a> that have been cut off, but that arrangement falls through due to intervention of the Dunedain Rangers.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2013" title="2013">2013</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a>:</i></b><i></i>

<dl><dd>A character credited as &#34;Bill Ferny Sr.&#34; appears in the prologue in Bree. Along with him associate, another character credited as &#34;Squint&#34; (loosely based on the squint-eyed southerner), Ferny, Sr. has seemingly tracked <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> to the Prancing Pony (presumably to collect on the bounty put out on Thorin&#39;s head by <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a>). The two Men are preparing to confront Thorin when <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> goes over and sits and starts talking with the <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarf</a>. When it becomes apparent the Wizard is not going anywhere, the two Men get up and leave the inn.</dd></dl>

<b><a href="/wiki/2021" title="2021">2021</a>: <i><a href="/wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)" title="The One Ring (role-playing game)">The One Ring</a> — <a href="/wiki/Core_Rules_(The_One_Ring)" title="Core Rules (The One Ring)">Core Rules</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In <a href="/wiki/Third_Age" title="Third Age">T.A.</a> <a href="/wiki/Third_Age_2965" title="Third Age 2965">2965</a>, a foul-tempered horse trader named John Ferny lived in Bree. While his father and grandfather traded fine horses, John was known for his deceptive business practices and for trading with foreigners.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>
