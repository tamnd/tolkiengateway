**Bronwyn** was a [Southlander](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) of [Tirharad](../T/Tirharad.md) in the [Southlands](../M/Mordor.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Bronwyn was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) and she is portrayed by [Nazanin Boniadi](../N/Nazanin_Boniadi.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Bronwyn was born and grew up in the village of [Hordern](../H/Hordern.md), and at some point, she moved to [Tirharad](../T/Tirharad.md), though her family remained behind. At some point later, she became the mother of [Theo](../T/Theo.md) and she worked as an apothecary at Tirharad. During this time, she fell in love with the Silvan Elf [Arondir](../A/Arondir.md), though they kept their affection secret because of the negative sentiment of her race towards the Elves.
