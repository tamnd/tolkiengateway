**Bronwyn** was a [Southlander](/wiki/Pre-N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Pre-Númenóreans") of [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad) in the [Southlands](/wiki/Mordor "Mordor") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Bronwyn was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) and she is portrayed by [Nazanin Boniadi](/wiki/Nazanin_Boniadi).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Bronwyn was born and grew up in the village of [Hordern](/wiki/Hordern), and at some point, she moved to [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad), though her family remained behind. At some point later, she became the mother of [Theo](/wiki/Theo) and she worked as an apothecary at Tirharad. During this time, she fell in love with the Silvan Elf [Arondir](/wiki/Arondir), though they kept their affection secret because of the negative sentiment of her race towards the Elves.
