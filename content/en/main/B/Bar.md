<i><b>bar</b></i> (pl. \*<i><b>bair</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) means "land, house" or "home, dwelling" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[PQ](/wiki/PQ) \**mbar-* from [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [MBAR](/wiki/MBAR) "dwelling, habitation"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Dimbar](/wiki/Dimbar)*: "sad dwelling"
- *[Brithombar](/wiki/Brithombar)*: "dwellings of [Brithon](/wiki/Brithon)"

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), *[már](/wiki/M%C3%A1r "Már")*
