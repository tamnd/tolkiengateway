The **Brook of Glass** was a stream near [Tavrobel](../T/Tavrobel.md) in [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in one of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Brook_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brook-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was close to the [Heath of the Sky-roof](../H/Heath_of_the_Sky-roof.md), a site of a [great battle](Battle_of_the_Heath_of_the_Sky-roof.md) during the [Faring Forth](../F/Faring_Forth.md), when the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of Tol Eressëa attempted to rescue their [kin](../I/Ilkorindi.md) living in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brook_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brook-1">[1]</a></sup> In the aftermath of the battle, [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md) the mariner stole away from the war-torn heath, and fled down the valley of the river - however, the waters of the stream were "fouled with the war of men and grime of strife".<sup id="cite_ref-Brook_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brook-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

According to [John Garth](../J/John_Garth.md), the Brook of Glass might have been inspired by the Sher Brook near <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannock_Chase" title="wikipedia:Cannock Chase">Cannock Chase</a>, not far from the village of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Haywood" title="wikipedia:Great Haywood">Great Haywood</a>, which served as the main inspiration behind the town of Tavrobel.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
