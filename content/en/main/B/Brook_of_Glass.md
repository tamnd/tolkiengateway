The **Brook of Glass** was a stream near [Tavrobel](/wiki/Tavrobel) in [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in one of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Brook_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brook-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was close to the [Heath of the Sky-roof](/wiki/Heath_of_the_Sky-roof), a site of a [great battle](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Heath_of_the_Sky-roof "Battle of the Heath of the Sky-roof") during the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), when the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of Tol Eressëa attempted to rescue their [kin](/wiki/Ilkorindi "Ilkorindi") living in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands).<sup id="cite_ref-Brook_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brook-1">[1]</a></sup> In the aftermath of the battle, [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol) the mariner stole away from the war-torn heath, and fled down the valley of the river - however, the waters of the stream were "fouled with the war of men and grime of strife".<sup id="cite_ref-Brook_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brook-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

According to [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth), the Brook of Glass might have been inspired by the Sher Brook near <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannock_Chase" title="wikipedia:Cannock Chase">Cannock Chase</a>, not far from the village of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Haywood" title="wikipedia:Great Haywood">Great Haywood</a>, which served as the main inspiration behind the town of Tavrobel.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
