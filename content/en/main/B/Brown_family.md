The **Brown family** was a Hobbit family from [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Only one of its members is known, [Lily Brown](../L/Lily_Cotton.md), who married [Farmer Cotton](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md). One of their children was [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md), wife of the mayor [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
