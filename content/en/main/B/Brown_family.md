The **Brown family** was a Hobbit family from [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Only one of its members is known, [Lily Brown](/wiki/Lily_Cotton "Lily Cotton"), who married [Farmer Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior"). One of their children was [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton), wife of the mayor [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
