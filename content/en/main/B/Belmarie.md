**Belmarie** was the name of a country in the poem *[Errantry](/wiki/Errantry)* by [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The name was not actually [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) but an invention in the Elvish style.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It is unknown if Belmarie referred to an actual region in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), or if it was just an invention by the poet.
