The **Black Breath** or **Black [Shadow](../S/Shadow.md)** was a weapon used by the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md). The Ringwraiths' breath was poisonous and could overpower those affected by it.

This evil blight caused their victims to slip into a dark cold sleep and never recover. Those stricken by the malady murmured in their sleep until a grey shadow fell on their faces as they fell into the darkness.

## History {#History}

After the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) led [Sauron's attack on Osgiliath](../S/Sauron's_attack_on_Osgiliath.md), news of a "Black Shadow" reached [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) felt a sense of dread.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the evening of [29 September](../_/29_September.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) left [the Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md) and went for a stroll in [Bree](Bree.md). He saw and followed (or was drawn by) a dark shadow, but then fell unconscious. [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) stated that Merry had been overcome by the Black Breath.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Siege of Gondor](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md) many were wounded and those stricken by the Black Shadow were brought to the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md), but the healers could not find a remedy. In the last retreat before the city of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) was besieged, [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) fell under the shadow while fighting to hold his outpost, and after being struck with an arrow was brought to the Houses of Healing on [13 March](../_/13_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It appears that both Merry and [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) were exposed to the Black Breath when they fought the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> on [15 March](../_/15_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Later on that day after the battle Aragorn came to the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md) and recognized that Faramir was suffering from the Black Breath. Aragorn used *[athelas](../A/Athelas.md)* to cure Faramir, Éowyn, Merry and others who had been hurt by the Black Breath. This led to the rapidly spread news that the King had returned.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
