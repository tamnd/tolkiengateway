The **Black Breath** or **Black [Shadow](/wiki/Shadow)** was a weapon used by the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl). The Ringwraiths' breath was poisonous and could overpower those affected by it.

This evil blight caused their victims to slip into a dark cold sleep and never recover. Those stricken by the malady murmured in their sleep until a grey shadow fell on their faces as they fell into the darkness.

## History {#History}

After the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) led [Sauron's attack on Osgiliath](/wiki/Sauron%27s_attack_on_Osgiliath), news of a "Black Shadow" reached [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) felt a sense of dread.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the evening of [29 September](/wiki/29_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) left [the Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony "The Prancing Pony") and went for a stroll in [Bree](/wiki/Bree). He saw and followed (or was drawn by) a dark shadow, but then fell unconscious. [Strider](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") stated that Merry had been overcome by the Black Breath.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor) many were wounded and those stricken by the Black Shadow were brought to the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing), but the healers could not find a remedy. In the last retreat before the city of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) was besieged, [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) fell under the shadow while fighting to hold his outpost, and after being struck with an arrow was brought to the Houses of Healing on [13 March](/wiki/13_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> It appears that both Merry and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) were exposed to the Black Breath when they fought the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> on [15 March](/wiki/15_March) [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Later on that day after the battle Aragorn came to the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing) and recognized that Faramir was suffering from the Black Breath. Aragorn used *[athelas](/wiki/Athelas "Athelas")* to cure Faramir, Éowyn, Merry and others who had been hurt by the Black Breath. This led to the rapidly spread news that the King had returned.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
