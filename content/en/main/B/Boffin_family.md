The **Boffin family** was a prominent family of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), associated with the region of [the Yale](/wiki/The_Yale "The Yale") in the [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup> Important members include [Berylla Boffin](/wiki/Berylla_Baggins "Berylla Baggins"), who was an ancestor of both [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), and [Folco Boffin](/wiki/Folco_Boffin), who helped Frodo in his move from [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Family tree of the Boffins of Yale {#Family_tree_of_the_Boffins_of_Yale}

Showing the prominent members of the Boffin clan.<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup> The figures after the names are those of birth (and death where that is recorded). A dashed line indicates marriage, or when extended vertically, indicates a line of descent with one or more generations not shown. Names in *italics* signify those who attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) on [22 September](/wiki/22_September) [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001"). Names in parentheses represent significant hobbits related to the Boffins.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name **Boffin** is a rendition into English of [Westron](/wiki/Westron) **Bophîn**, of unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[3]</a></sup> An early idea by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") was to translate it "Loffin", as Bophîn (at that time) had a connection to "Laugh".

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

In the illustrated short story *[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*, the local police officer is a Sergeant Boffin who - at the behest of [Gaffer Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee) - seeks to lock up Mr Bliss as a thief for failing to pay for his car. Sergeant Boffin also has a son named [Sam](/wiki/Sam).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[4]</a></sup>
