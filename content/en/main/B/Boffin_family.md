The **Boffin family** was a prominent family of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), associated with the region of [the Yale](../T/The_Yale.md) in the [Eastfarthing](../E/Eastfarthing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Boffin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boffin-1">[1]</a></sup> Important members include [Berylla Boffin](Berylla_Baggins.md), who was an ancestor of both [Bilbo](Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), and [Folco Boffin](../F/Folco_Boffin.md), who helped Frodo in his move from [Bag End](Bag_End.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Family tree of the Boffins of Yale {#Family_tree_of_the_Boffins_of_Yale}

A dashed line indicates marriage, or when extended vertically, indicates a line of descent with one or more generations not shown. Names in *italics* signify those who attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) on [22 September](../_/22_September.md) [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1401](../T/Third_Age_3001.md). Names in parentheses represent significant hobbits related to the Boffins.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name **Boffin** is a rendition into English of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) **Bophîn**, of unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[3]</a></sup> An early idea by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was to translate it "Loffin", as Bophîn (at that time) had a connection to "Laugh".

## In other stories {#In_other_stories}

In the illustrated short story *[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)*, the local police officer is a Sergeant Boffin who - at the behest of [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md) - seeks to lock up Mr Bliss as a thief for failing to pay for his car. Sergeant Boffin also has a son named [Sam](../S/Sam.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[4]</a></sup>
