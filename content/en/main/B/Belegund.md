**Belegund** ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [422](../F/First_Age_422.md)-[460](../F/First_Age_460.md)) was a descendant of [Bëor](Bëor.md), and one of [Barahir](Barahir.md)'s [twelve followers](Barahir's_outlaws.md).

## History {#History}

Belegund was the younger son and third child of [Bregolas](Bregolas.md). His brother was [Baragund](Baragund.md) who was elder to him, and his sister was [Beleth](Beleth.md) who was the eldest among his siblings. Belegund had only one daughter, [Rían](../R/Rían.md), with an unnamed wife.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Belegund and his brother Baragund partook in the [Dagor Bragollach](../D/Dagor_Bragollach.md) under the command of their uncle [Barahir](Barahir.md) who was their father's brother. They survived, but were beaten back.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> With Barahir and ten others, they formed a small band and lived as outlaws in [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), near [Tarn Aeluin](../T/Tarn_Aeluin.md).

They lived in hiding for some time, until [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s agents captured [Gorlim](../G/Gorlim.md) and coerced him into giving the location of their hideaway. [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) were sent to raid it, and succeeded. Belegund and the outlaws were killed. Only [Beren](Beren.md) survived, as he had been away on an errand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Belegund was the grandfather of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md) by Rían his daughter.
