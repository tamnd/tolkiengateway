**Belegund** ([F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [422](/wiki/First_Age_422 "First Age 422")-[460](/wiki/First_Age_460 "First Age 460")) was a descendant of [Bëor](/wiki/B%C3%ABor), and one of [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir)'s [twelve followers](/wiki/Barahir%27s_outlaws "Barahir's outlaws").

## History {#History}

Belegund was the younger son and third child of [Bregolas](/wiki/Bregolas). His brother was [Baragund](/wiki/Baragund) who was elder to him, and his sister was [Beleth](/wiki/Beleth) who was the eldest among his siblings. Belegund had only one daughter, [Rían](/wiki/R%C3%ADan), with an unnamed wife.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Belegund and his brother Baragund partook in the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach) under the command of their uncle [Barahir](/wiki/Barahir) who was their father's brother. They survived, but were beaten back.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> With Barahir and ten others, they formed a small band and lived as outlaws in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), near [Tarn Aeluin](/wiki/Tarn_Aeluin).

They lived in hiding for some time, until [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s agents captured [Gorlim](/wiki/Gorlim) and coerced him into giving the location of their hideaway. [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) were sent to raid it, and succeeded. Belegund and the outlaws were killed. Only [Beren](/wiki/Beren) survived, as he had been away on an errand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Belegund was the grandfather of [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) by Rían his daughter.
