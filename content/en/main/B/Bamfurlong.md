**Bamfurlong** was a farm in the boggy region of the [Marish](/wiki/Marish) in the eastern [Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It was the home of [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who (like the other [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) in this inhospitable area) lived above ground rather than in a traditional [Hobbit-hole](/wiki/Hobbit-holes "Hobbit-holes"). Farmer Maggot's farm was well-known in the area for his mushrooms, which he protected fiercely against trespassers with his three fearsome [dogs](/wiki/Dogs "Dogs").

The center of the farm consisted of a large farm house made of brick with thatched roof and associated farm-buildings. They were all enclosed within a high wall with a wooden gate to the outside <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maggot%27s_Lane&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maggot&#39;s Lane (page does not exist)">Maggot&#39;s Lane</a>. The lane led out of the farm and, after one to two miles, crossed a deep dike and climbed onto the high bank of the causeway between [Rushey](/wiki/Rushey) and [Stock](/wiki/Stock); five miles north was the [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry).

## History {#History}

When [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) was young and lived at [Brandy Hall](/wiki/Brandy_Hall), he occasionally sneaked onto the farm to poach some mushrooms. On the last visit, Maggot had caught Frodo and gave him a beating for his thievery. Frodo avoided the place and him ever after.

On [25 September](/wiki/25_September) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), Frodo, [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") came to Bamfurlong in the afternoon. There they met Farmer Maggot and learned that earlier a [Black Rider](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl "Nazgûl") had visited the farm and enquired about Baggins. The travelling hobbits gratefully accepted a hearty supper before the farmer drove them to [Bucklebury Ferry](/wiki/Bucklebury_Ferry) in his wagon.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Bamfurlong* is probably a combination of "bean" and "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/furlong" title="wiktionary:furlong">furlong</a>" (a division of a common field). It is the name for a land that is usually used to plant beans.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) suggests that the name *Bamfurlong* represents [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) *Beanfurlang*, "a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/furlong" title="wikipedia:furlong">furlong</a> of beans" or simply "bean-field".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamfurlong" title="wikipedia:Bamfurlong">Bamfurlong</a></i> is a real-life English place-name.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the first edition of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Farmer Maggot's land did not have a name. The name Bamfurlong first appeared as the name of Farmer Maggot's land in [1962](/wiki/1962) in the poem *[Bombadil Goes Boating](/wiki/Bombadil_Goes_Boating)* and was inserted in the [1967](/wiki/1967) second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In the first edition of *The Lord of the Rings*, Bamfurlong was the name of a place from which one of the special runners came with a "secret message" in the chapter "[The Scouring of the Shire](/wiki/The_Scouring_of_the_Shire)". In the second edition of *The Lord of the Rings*, this place was called [Whitfurrows](/wiki/Whitfurrows) instead of Bamfurlong.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
