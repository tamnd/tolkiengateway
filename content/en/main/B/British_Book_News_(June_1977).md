<b><i>British Book News</i> (June 1977)</b> is an issue of the magazine *British Book News*. It includes the article "Remembering Tolkien and Lewis" (pp. 429–30) by Anthony Curtis, which contains extracts from an [interview with J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and some [reminiscences of Tolkien](/wiki/Index:Reminiscences_of_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The article reproduces a [photograph of Tolkien](/wiki/File:Jrrt_1972_pipe.jpg "File:Jrrt 1972 pipe.jpg") (p. 429) and "'Dwarves Marching' ... \[an\] unpublished drawing for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*" (p. 430). <span></span>
