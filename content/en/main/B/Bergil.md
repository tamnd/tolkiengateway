**Bergil** was a boy of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md).

## History {#History}

Bergil was born around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3009](../T/Third_Age_3009.md) and was the eldest son of [Beregond](Beregond.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md). He had at least one younger brother, [Borlas](Borlas.md).

A boy at the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), he accompanied [Pippin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) when he first arrived in the city of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). He led Pippin to the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md) to view the men from the various provinces of Gondor coming to [Pelennor](../P/Pelennor.md) to reinforce the city against the impending [siege](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bergil served the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md) during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He found *athelas* for [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), which was used to heal [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md), and [Meriadoc](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Bergil* is clearly [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), but it is never glossed. It could be a combination of [root](../R/Root.md) [BER](BER.md) ("valiant") + *[gil](../G/Gil.md)* ("star").
