**Bergil** was a boy of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith).

## History {#History}

Bergil was born around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3009](/wiki/Third_Age_3009 "Third Age 3009") and was the eldest son of [Beregond](/wiki/Beregond) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). He had at least one younger brother, [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas).

A boy at the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), he accompanied [Pippin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") when he first arrived in the city of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). He led Pippin to the [Great Gate of Minas Tirith](/wiki/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith) to view the men from the various provinces of Gondor coming to [Pelennor](/wiki/Pelennor) to reinforce the city against the impending [siege](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor "Siege of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Bergil served the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing) during the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He found *athelas* for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), which was used to heal [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), and [Meriadoc](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Bergil* is clearly [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), but it is never glossed. It could be a combination of [root](/wiki/Root "Root") [BER](/wiki/BER) ("valiant") + *[gil](/wiki/Gil "Gil")* ("star").

<b><a href="/wiki/1956" title="1956">1956</a>: <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1956_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1956 radio series)">The Lord of the Rings (1956 radio series)</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The role of Bergil was played by a young <a href="/wiki/David_Hemmings" title="David Hemmings">David Hemmings</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-RT1726_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RT1726-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/P%C3%A1n_prste%C5%88ov_(2001-2003_Slovak_radio_series)" title="Pán prsteňov (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)"><i>Pán prsteňov</i> (2001-2003 Slovak radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The voice of Bergil is provided by Viktor Petrov.</dd></dl>

<b>2015: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bergil is first found is pre-battle <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a>, where he and his friends take the player on a series of adventures throughout the city. After the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Battle_of_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of Pelennor Fields">Battle of Pelennor Fields</a>, he and the player are help <a href="/wiki/Ioreth" title="Ioreth">Ioreth</a> in finding <i><a href="/wiki/Athelas" title="Athelas">athelas</a></i>.</dd></dl>
