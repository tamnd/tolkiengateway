**Bardings** were the [people](../M/Men_of_Dale.md) of the kingdom of [Dale](../D/Dale.md) during the later [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-meet_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meet-1">[1]</a></sup> They took their name from [Bard](Bard.md) the Bowman, the first [King of Dale](../K/King_of_Dale.md), descendant of Lord [Girion](../G/Girion.md).

## History {#History}

The people of the Bardings belonged to that wide-ranging branch of [Men](../M/Men.md) known as the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md), and were thus kin to both the [Beornings](Beornings.md) and the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Bard](Bard.md)'s revenge on the [dragon](../D/Dragon.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), he became [King of Dale](../K/King_of_Dale.md) in his own right. He was succeeded as its ruler by his son [Bain](Bain.md), and so his line extended down to the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and beyond.

The Bardings occupied the area between the [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md) and [Erebor](../E/Erebor.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and maintained the friendship with the [Dwarves of Erebor](../D/Dwarves_of_Erebor.md), like their ancestors. As time passed, so the lands under the power of the Bardings grew, and in the time of [Bard](Bard.md)'s grandson [Brand](Brand.md) their territories were said to extend far to the south and east of the [Long Lake](../L/Long_Lake.md).<sup id="cite_ref-meet_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meet-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the War of the Ring, the [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) [attacked](Battle_of_Dale.md) the town of Dale and forced the Men and the Dwarves into [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md); King Brand died alongside [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) at the [Front Gate](../F/Front_Gate.md) of Erebor. After seven days of siege, news came of the defeat of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). The new kings broke the siege and chased the Easterlings out of Dale. After the siege, the two peoples rebuilt Dale. The new King [Bard II](Bard_II.md) also sent an emissary to the coronation of King Elessar.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
