**Bardings** were the [people](/wiki/Men_of_Dale "Men of Dale") of the kingdom of [Dale](/wiki/Dale) during the later [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-meet_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meet-1">[1]</a></sup> They took their name from [Bard](/wiki/Bard) the Bowman, the first [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale), descendant of Lord [Girion](/wiki/Girion).

## History {#History}

The people of the Bardings belonged to that wide-ranging branch of [Men](/wiki/Men) known as the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen), and were thus kin to both the [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) and the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Bard](/wiki/Bard)'s revenge on the [dragon](/wiki/Dragon "Dragon") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), he became [King of Dale](/wiki/King_of_Dale) in his own right. He was succeeded as its ruler by his son [Bain](/wiki/Bain), and so his line extended down to the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and beyond.

The Bardings occupied the area between the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake) and [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and maintained the friendship with the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor), like their ancestors. As time passed, so the lands under the power of the Bardings grew, and in the time of [Bard](/wiki/Bard)'s grandson [Brand](/wiki/Brand) their territories were said to extend far to the south and east of the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake).<sup id="cite_ref-meet_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-meet-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the War of the Ring, the [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) [attacked](/wiki/Battle_of_Dale "Battle of Dale") the town of Dale and forced the Men and the Dwarves into [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"); King Brand died alongside [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot) at the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of Erebor. After seven days of siege, news came of the defeat of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). The new kings broke the siege and chased the Easterlings out of Dale. After the siege, the two peoples rebuilt Dale. The new King [Bard II](/wiki/Bard_II) also sent an emissary to the coronation of King Elessar.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
