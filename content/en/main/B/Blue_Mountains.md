The **Blue Mountains** ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") <i><b>Ered Luin</b></i>), also known as <i><b>Ered Lindon</b></i> or <i><b>Mountains of Lune</b></i>, was the mountain range at the far west of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and far east of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

In the early days of [Arda](/wiki/Arda) after the fall of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps), the Blue Mountains were formed as the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) widened the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and thrust [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) eastward to protect [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Blue Mountains lined up with the [Grey Mountains of the southlands](</wiki/Grey_Mountains_(ancient)> "Grey Mountains (ancient)"), forming the western wall of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). These two ranges lay parallel to the [Red Mountains](/wiki/Orocarni "Orocarni") in the northeast and [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains) in the southeast that formed the eastern wall. At their northern end, a narrow gap separated the Blue Mountains from the [Iron Mountains](/wiki/Iron_Mountains), which stretched across the entire north of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Sometime during the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) two [Dwarven Fathers](/wiki/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves "Fathers of the Dwarves") awoke under [Mount Dolmed](/wiki/Dolmed "Dolmed") and founded the two westernmost houses of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) (the [Firebeards](/wiki/Firebeards) and [Broadbeams](/wiki/Broadbeams)). They subsequently built two great city-states, [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) and [Belegost](/wiki/Belegost).

In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the Blue Mountains were an unbroken line separating Eriador from Beleriand. Seven rivers flowed from its western side, and the land these rivers flowed through was known as [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand). Later, when the [Green-elves](/wiki/Green-elves) settled there, the land was called [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), and the mountains sometimes referred to as the *Ered Lindon*. Wandering Elves would also hunt in the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Blue Mountains were ruined during the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) at the end of the First Age, and in the south central end of the range the sea broke through. The [River Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn "Lhûn") now flowed through the mountains to the [Gulf of Lune](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lune). On the western side a small section of Lindon remained, and here the retreating [Elves](/wiki/Elves) built the kingdom of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon), ruled by [Ereinion Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad "Gil-galad"), last [High King of the Noldor](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Dwarven cities of Nogrod and Belegost were also ruined when the mountains were broken, causing most of the Dwarves to migrate east to [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), leaving a remnant behind. However, there remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

By the Third Age, Dwarves dwelt in the east side of the Blue Mountains, especially in the parts south of the [Gulf of Lhun](/wiki/Gulf_of_Lhun).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974"), fleeing the fall of [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain), [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) briefly hid in old dwarven mines in the far north of the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/4/42/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg/250px-Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="206" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/4/42/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg/375px-Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/4/42/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg/500px-Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1000" data-file-height="824"/></a><figcaption>&#34;The <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> Under the Blue Mountains: <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>&#39;s Foresight&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Miriam_Ellis" title="Miriam Ellis">Miriam Ellis</a></figcaption></figure>

It also became the new home for many of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) who were exiled from their halls in [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") by [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") who had [driven them out](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor "Sack of Erebor"). After the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs), King [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin") established his throne in the Southern range beyond [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2802](/wiki/Third_Age_2802 "Third Age 2802"))<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and later his son [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) ruled after he went missing. During Thorin's reign, many of the wandering Longbeards joined him in [his halls](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls "Thorin's Halls") and they became prosperous in a fashion. However, Erebor was retaken from Smaug by Thorin and company in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), and it can be assumed most if not all of Durin's folk relocated there.

However the dwarf-mines remained active even by the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

*Ered Luin* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Blue Mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It consists of *[ered](/wiki/Ered "Ered")* ("mountains")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and *[luin](/wiki/Luin "Luin")* ("blue").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Other names were **Ered Lindon** ("Mountains of Lindon")<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and the [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name **Mountains of Lune**.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

The *[Etymologies](/wiki/Etymologies)* show [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) alternative names, *Lhúnorodrim* and *Lhúndirien* ("Blue Towers"), and also the Middle [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *Lúnoronti*,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> which is first used in a passage of the [further development of The Fall of Númenor](</wiki/The_Fall_of_N%C3%BAmenor_(chapter)#The_further_development_of_The_Fall_of_Númenor> "The Fall of Númenor (chapter)").<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

In [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, the Blue Mountains are named *Hǽwengebeorg*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

In the Second Age the Men of Middle-earth called Ered Luin the **Western Mountains**.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>The Blue Mountains appear as the game&#39;s westernmost region, Ered Luin. Ered Luin is the beginner&#39;s region for four of the game&#39;s races: Elves, <a href="/wiki/Calaquendi" title="Calaquendi">High Elves</a>, Dwarves, and &#34;<a href="/wiki/Stout-axe" title="Stout-axe">Stout-axe</a>&#34; dwarves. The region&#39;s storyline is based around a conflict in the early stages of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> between the allied Elves and Longbeards and a rogue group of Dwarves called the <a href="/wiki/Dourhands" title="Dourhands">Dourhands</a> and their allies, the &#34;Blue Crag&#34; <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Goblins</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In addition to the dwarven capital at <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Thorin%27s_Halls" title="Thorin&#39;s Halls">Thorin&#39;s Hall</a>, Ered Luin also contains the city of &#34;<a href="/wiki/Gondamon" title="Gondamon">Gondamon</a>&#34; in the &#34;Low Lands&#34; of the region and the outpost of &#34;Noglond&#34; between the two cities. The Elven territory along the shore of the Lhûn is known as &#34;Falathlorn&#34;, and contains the settlements &#34;<a href="/wiki/Duillond" title="Duillond">Duillond</a>&#34; and &#34;<a href="/wiki/Celondim" title="Celondim">Celondim</a>&#34;. The dwarves&#39; port city of &#34;Kheledûl&#34; remains under Dourhand control, as does &#34;Sarnúr&#34;, a recently-unearthed ancient dwarven city. The fortress of &#34;Orodost&#34; and the blockaded pass to the <a href="/wiki/Grey_Havens" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a>, &#34;Rath Teraig&#34;, are occupied by goblins.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Although the Blue Mountains do not appear in the game, they are mentioned by the Dwarf <a href="/wiki/Grof" title="Grof">Grof</a>.
