The **Blue Mountains** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md) <i><b>Ered Luin</b></i>), also known as <i><b>Ered Lindon</b></i> or <i><b>Mountains of Lune</b></i>, was the mountain range at the far west of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and far east of [Beleriand](Beleriand.md).

## History {#History}

### Early history {#Early_history}

In the early days of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) after the fall of the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md), the Blue Mountains were formed as the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) widened the [Great Sea](Belegaer.md) and thrust [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) eastward to protect [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Blue Mountains lined up with the [Grey Mountains of the southlands](<../G/Grey_Mountains_(ancient).md>), forming the western wall of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). These two ranges lay parallel to the [Red Mountains](../O/Orocarni.md) in the northeast and [Yellow Mountains](../Y/Yellow_Mountains.md) in the southeast that formed the eastern wall. At their northern end, a narrow gap separated the Blue Mountains from the [Iron Mountains](../I/Iron_Mountains.md), which stretched across the entire north of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Sometime during the [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) two [Dwarven Fathers](../F/Fathers_of_the_Dwarves.md) awoke under [Mount Dolmed](../D/Dolmed.md) and founded the two westernmost houses of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) (the [Firebeards](../F/Firebeards.md) and [Broadbeams](Broadbeams.md)). They subsequently built two great city-states, [Nogrod](../N/Nogrod.md) and [Belegost](Belegost.md).

In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), the Blue Mountains were an unbroken line separating Eriador from Beleriand. Seven rivers flowed from its western side, and the land these rivers flowed through was known as [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md). Later, when the [Green-elves](../G/Green-elves.md) settled there, the land was called [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), and the mountains sometimes referred to as the *Ered Lindon*. Wandering Elves would also hunt in the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Blue Mountains were ruined during the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md) at the end of the First Age, and in the south central end of the range the sea broke through. The [River Lhûn](../L/Lhûn.md) now flowed through the mountains to the [Gulf of Lune](../G/Gulf_of_Lune.md). On the western side a small section of Lindon remained, and here the retreating [Elves](../E/Elves.md) built the kingdom of [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md), ruled by [Ereinion Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), last [High King of the Noldor](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Dwarven cities of Nogrod and Belegost were also ruined when the mountains were broken, causing most of the Dwarves to migrate east to [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md), leaving a remnant behind. However, there remained some Dwarves on the eastern side of the Blue Mountains in days afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

By the Third Age, Dwarves dwelt in the east side of the Blue Mountains, especially in the parts south of the [Gulf of Lhun](../G/Gulf_of_Lhun.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md), fleeing the fall of [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md), [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) briefly hid in old dwarven mines in the far north of the Blue Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/M/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf&#39;s_Foresight.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/42/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg/250px-Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="250" height="206" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/42/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg/375px-Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/4/42/Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg/500px-Miriam_Ellis_-_The_Dwarves_Under_the_Blue_Mountains_-_Gandalf%27s_Foresight.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1000" data-file-height="824"/></a><figcaption>&#34;The <a href="../D/Dwarves.md" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> Under the Blue Mountains: <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>&#39;s Foresight&#34; by <a href="../M/Miriam_Ellis.md" title="Miriam Ellis">Miriam Ellis</a></figcaption></figure>

It also became the new home for many of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) who were exiled from their halls in [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) by [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) who had [driven them out](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md). After the [War of the Dwarves and Orcs](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Orcs.md), King [Thráin II](../T/Thráin.md) established his throne in the Southern range beyond [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2802](../T/Third_Age_2802.md))<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and later his son [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) ruled after he went missing. During Thorin's reign, many of the wandering Longbeards joined him in [his halls](../T/Thorin's_Halls.md) and they became prosperous in a fashion. However, Erebor was retaken from Smaug by Thorin and company in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2941](../T/Third_Age_2941.md), and it can be assumed most if not all of Durin's folk relocated there.

However the dwarf-mines remained active even by the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-7">[7]</a></sup>

*Ered Luin* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which means "Blue Mountains".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It consists of *[ered](../E/Ered.md)* ("mountains")<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and *[luin](../L/Luin.md)* ("blue").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

Other names were **Ered Lindon** ("Mountains of Lindon")<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> and the [Westron](../W/Westron.md) name **Mountains of Lune**.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

The *[Etymologies](../E/Etymologies.md)* show [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) alternative names, *Lhúnorodrim* and *Lhúndirien* ("Blue Towers"), and also the Middle [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *Lúnoronti*,<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> which is first used in a passage of the [further development of The Fall of Númenor](<../T/The_Fall_of_Númenor_(chapter).md#The_further_development_of_The_Fall_of_Númenor>).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

In [Ælfwine](../_/Ælfwine.md)'s [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) translations, the Blue Mountains are named *Hǽwengebeorg*.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

In the Second Age the Men of Middle-earth called Ered Luin the **Western Mountains**.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Blue Mountains are featured as a location of a fight between Dwarves and <a href="../D/Drogoth.md" title="Drogoth">Drogoth</a>.</dd></dl>

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg/300px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="225" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg/450px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/d/d9/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg/600px-The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online_-_Ered_Luin_map.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="768"/></a><figcaption>Map of Ered Luin from <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>.</figcaption></figure>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Blue Mountains appear as the game&#39;s westernmost region, Ered Luin. Ered Luin is the beginner&#39;s region for four of the game&#39;s races: Elves, <a href="../C/Calaquendi.md" title="Calaquendi">High Elves</a>, Dwarves, and &#34;<a href="../S/Stout-axe.md" title="Stout-axe">Stout-axe</a>&#34; dwarves. The region&#39;s storyline is based around a conflict in the early stages of the <a href="../W/War_of_the_Ring.md" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a> between the allied Elves and Longbeards and a rogue group of Dwarves called the <a href="../D/Dourhands.md" title="Dourhands">Dourhands</a> and their allies, the &#34;Blue Crag&#34; <a href="../O/Orcs.md" title="Orcs">Goblins</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In addition to the dwarven capital at <a class="mw-redirect" href="../T/Thorin&#39;s_Halls.md" title="Thorin&#39;s Halls">Thorin&#39;s Hall</a>, Ered Luin also contains the city of &#34;<a href="../G/Gondamon.md" title="Gondamon">Gondamon</a>&#34; in the &#34;Low Lands&#34; of the region and the outpost of &#34;Noglond&#34; between the two cities. The Elven territory along the shore of the Lhûn is known as &#34;Falathlorn&#34;, and contains the settlements &#34;<a href="../D/Duillond.md" title="Duillond">Duillond</a>&#34; and &#34;<a href="../C/Celondim.md" title="Celondim">Celondim</a>&#34;. The dwarves&#39; port city of &#34;Kheledûl&#34; remains under Dourhand control, as does &#34;Sarnúr&#34;, a recently-unearthed ancient dwarven city. The fortress of &#34;Orodost&#34; and the blockaded pass to the <a href="../G/Grey_Havens.md" title="Grey Havens">Grey Havens</a>, &#34;Rath Teraig&#34;, are occupied by goblins.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Although the Blue Mountains do not appear in the game, they are mentioned by the Dwarf <a href="../G/Grof.md" title="Grof">Grof</a>. He tells the player that the Blue Mountains are prosperous, but that they lately find more iron than gold.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup></dd></dl>
