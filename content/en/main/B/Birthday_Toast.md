"**Frodo's custom**" was an event held each year at a small gathering in honour of both [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' and [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins)' birthdays, held each year on the [22nd of September](/wiki/22_September "22 September") at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) (both Hobbits shared the same birthday). This custom began after Bilbo left Bag End after the celebration of his 111th birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Tolkien Society tradition {#Tolkien_Society_tradition}

The **Birthday Toast** is a tradition upheld by [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) in honour of the creator of Bilbo and Frodo - [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and is held on the author's birthday, [3rd January](/wiki/3_January "3 January"), each year. The 'toast' itself is: "The Professor!".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
