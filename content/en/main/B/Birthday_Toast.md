"**Frodo's custom**" was an event held each year at a small gathering in honour of both [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md)' and [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)' birthdays, held each year on the [22nd of September](../_/22_September.md) at [Bag End](Bag_End.md) (both Hobbits shared the same birthday). This custom began after Bilbo left Bag End after the celebration of his 111th birthday.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Tolkien Society tradition {#Tolkien_Society_tradition}

The **Birthday Toast** is a tradition upheld by [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) in honour of the creator of Bilbo and Frodo - [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and is held on the author's birthday, [3rd January](../_/3_January.md), each year. The 'toast' itself is: "The Professor!".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
