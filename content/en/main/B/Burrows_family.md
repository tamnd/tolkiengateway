The **Burrows family** was an important [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") family of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

The correct plural of the name is "Burrowses".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

There was a family connection between the Burrowses and the [Bagginses](/wiki/Baggins_family "Baggins family"): Bilbo's distant cousin [Peony Baggins](/wiki/Peony_Baggins) was married to [Milo Burrows](/wiki/Milo_Burrows), and together they had four children.<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-2">[2]</a></sup>

The company [Messrs Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes](/wiki/Messrs_Grubb,_Grubb_and_Burrowes) were responsible in auctioneering Bilbo's belongins.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

Several members were present at [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s famous [Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party").<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Folco Burrowes](/wiki/Folco_Burrowes) was Frodo's lawyer.
