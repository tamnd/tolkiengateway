The **Burrows family** was an important [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) family of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

The correct plural of the name is "Burrowses".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

There was a family connection between the Burrowses and the [Bagginses](Baggins_family.md): Bilbo's distant cousin [Peony Baggins](../P/Peony_Baggins.md) was married to [Milo Burrows](../M/Milo_Burrows.md), and together they had four children.<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-2">[2]</a></sup>

The company [Messrs Grubb, Grubb and Burrowes](../M/Messrs_Grubb,_Grubb_and_Burrowes.md) were responsible in auctioneering Bilbo's belongins.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>

Several members were present at [Bilbo Baggins](Bilbo_Baggins.md)'s famous [Farewell Party](Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Baggins_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baggins-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Folco Burrowes](../F/Folco_Burrowes.md) was Frodo's lawyer.
