**Benrodir** was the "prince of [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien "Anórien")" whose name appears in the early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. He is mentioned in a sketch alongside [Nosdiligand](/wiki/Northiligand "Northiligand") and [Inram](/wiki/Inram) as one of the leaders of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that came to the aid of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about him, as he appears nowhere else in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)* the name <i><b>Benrodir</b></i> is in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin). [Roman Rausch](/wiki/Roman_Rausch) suggests that the second element of the name could be *rodir* ("nobleman"), composed of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Raud&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Raud (page does not exist)">raud</a></i> ("noble, eminent; lofty, high, tall; excellent") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=D%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dîr (page does not exist)">dîr</a></i> ("man").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
