**Benrodir** was the "prince of [Anárion](../A/Anórien.md)" whose name appears in the early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. He is mentioned in a sketch alongside [Nosdiligand](../N/Northiligand.md) and [Inram](../I/Inram.md) as one of the leaders of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) that came to the aid of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about him, as he appears nowhere else in the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)* the name <i><b>Benrodir</b></i> is in [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md). [Roman Rausch](../R/Roman_Rausch.md) suggests that the second element of the name could be *rodir* ("nobleman"), composed of <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Raud&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Raud (page does not exist)">raud</a></i> ("noble, eminent; lofty, high, tall; excellent") and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=D%C3%AEr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dîr (page does not exist)">dîr</a></i> ("man").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
