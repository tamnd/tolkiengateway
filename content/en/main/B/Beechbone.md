**Beechbone** was a notably tall and handsome [Ent](/wiki/Ents "Ents").

## History {#History}

On [3 March](/wiki/3_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> he was caught in a burning spray sent up by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) from beneath the [Ring of Isengard](/wiki/Ring_of_Isengard), but his fiery end brought his fellow Ents to the peak of their destructive anger.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
