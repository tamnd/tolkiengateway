**Benjamin Walker Scodelario-Davis**, better known as **Benjamin Walker**, is an American actor and stand-up comedian. He portrayed [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_Noldor "High King of the Noldor") [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) in the *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series by [Amazon](/wiki/Amazon_Studios "Amazon Studios").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During the 2024 San Diego Comic Con, Benjamin Walker sang a song in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
