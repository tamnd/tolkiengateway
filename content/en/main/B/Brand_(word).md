## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>brand</b></i> or <i><b>brann</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "towering; 'tall and massive'". The form *brand* is also said to mean "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/steeple" title="wiktionary:steeple">steeple</a>". The words *brand*, *brann* also appear to signify "<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/sublime#English" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">sublime</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Exilic Noldorin {#Exilic_Noldorin}

<i><b>brand</b></i> or <i><b>brann</b></i> is an [Exilic Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin") word meaning "lofty, noble, fine".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") apparently used the word *brann* to translate "high" in a Noldorin <a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Examples">phrase inscribed on a draft of Thrór&#39;s Map</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AI_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-H1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H1-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Th_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Th-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

From [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *b’randā*, root [BARÁD](/wiki/BARAT#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "BARAT").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
