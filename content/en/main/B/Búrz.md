<i><b>búrz</b></i> is a [Black Speech](Black_Speech.md) word which translates to "dark".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="Barad-dûr.md#Etymology" title="Barad-dûr">Lug<b>búrz</b></a></i> = "dark fortress"
- <i><a href="Burzum.md" title="Burzum"><b>burz</b>um</a></i> = "darkness"
