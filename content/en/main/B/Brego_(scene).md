**Brego** is the twenty-fourth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)"). This scene was not featured in the theatrical release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

Men of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) try to calm a panicked horse in the stables. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sees this and is told that the horse is half mad. Nonetheless, he slowly approaches it and calms it down.

[Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn) tells him that the horse's name is [Brego](</wiki/Brego_(horse)> "Brego (horse)") and that he belonged to [Théodred](/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred). She tells him that she is surprised to find the *magic of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves)* in a <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Ranger" title="Ranger">Ranger</a>. In response, he tells her that he was raised in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), and that Brego has seen enough of war and should be set free.
