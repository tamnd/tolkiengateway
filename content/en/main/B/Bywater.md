**Bywater** was a village in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), north of the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It housed the [Green Dragon](/wiki/Green_Dragon) inn and its residents included the [Cotton Family](/wiki/Cotton_Family) and [Old Noakes](/wiki/Old_Noakes).

The village was by the [Bywater Pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool) with most of its holes and houses on the south bank by the road, the northernmost of which was The Green Dragon. On the north bank of the Pool there was a row of [Smials](/wiki/Smials) with water-side gardens. From the middle of the village, [South Lane](/wiki/South_Lane) led to Cotton’s farm. South from the East Road ran the road north to [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), lined by an avenue of trees. The village had its own post office.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), the Shire was taken over by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) and his [Ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians). A year later many houses in Bywater stood empty and some had even been burnt. At the return of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and his companions, Bywater became the centre of the rebellion, with the [Travellers](/wiki/Travellers) rousing the [Shire-folk](/wiki/Shire-folk) on [2 September](/wiki/2_September), which ultimately ended with the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) and the liberation of the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name refers to it as being beside the [pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool "Bywater Pool") occurring in the course of [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water").<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001-03: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The villages of Hobbiton and Bywater were merged in the film so that The Green Dragon was put on the other side of the water from <a href="/wiki/Bag_End" title="Bag End">Bag End</a> facing <a href="/wiki/Old_Mill" title="Old Mill">Sandyman&#39;s Mill</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Bywater is the second town encountered by the player. The <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Cotton_Family" title="Cotton Family">Cottons</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sackville-Baggins_Family" title="Sackville-Baggins Family">Sackville-Bagginses</a> and <a href="/wiki/Angelica_Baggins" title="Angelica Baggins">Angelica Baggins</a> live there. It is home to both the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Green_Dragon" title="Green Dragon">Green Dragon</a> and the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ivy_Bush" title="Ivy Bush">Ivy Bush</a>.</dd></dl>
