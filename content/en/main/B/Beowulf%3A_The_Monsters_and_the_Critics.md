<i><b>Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics</b></i> is the title of a lecture delivered by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [1936](../_/1936.md), and later appeared as an essay in various publications. [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md) has described it as "possibl\[y\] ... the single most influential essay in the history of literary studies in the twentieth century".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## The lecture {#The_lecture}

The lecture "Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics" was the Sir Israel Gollancz Memorial Lecture for 1936. Tolkien delivered it to the British Academy on [25 November](../_/25_November.md) [1936](../_/1936.md) and again two weeks later to the Manchester Mediaeval Society.

## Publication history {#Publication_history}

*Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics* has been published as and in several different publications in the past.

<dl><dt>First edition (1937)</dt></dl>

In [1937](../_/1937.md), the first edition of *Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics* was issued in the *Proceedings of the British Academy* (vol. 22, 1936, pr. 1937, pp. \[245\]-295).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also published separatey by Humphrey Milford (London) for the British Academy (\[[1 July](../_/1_July.md)\]. 56 pp. 500 copies printed. ISBN: N/A). Altogether six impressions (2.-5. impressions 1958-1971 by the Oxford University Press; 6th impression 1977, by the British Academy) were printed.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<dl><dt>Booklet (1969)</dt></dl>

Around [1969](../_/1969.md), booklets of *Beowulf: The Monsters and the Critics* (undated and unknown publisher and place of publication), appeared.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<dl><dt><i>The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays</i> (1983)</dt></dl>

In [1983](../_/1983.md), the essay was included in the collection *[The Monsters and the Critics, and Other Essays](../T/The_Monsters_and_the_Critics,_and_Other_Essays.md)*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md).

<dl><dt><i>Beowulf and the Critics</i> (2002)</dt></dl>

In [2002](../_/2002.md), two draft versions of the essay, much longer, were published in *[Beowulf and the Critics](Beowulf_and_the_Critics.md)*, edited by [Michael D.C. Drout](../M/Michael_D.C._Drout.md). The book was revised in [2011](../_/2011.md).
