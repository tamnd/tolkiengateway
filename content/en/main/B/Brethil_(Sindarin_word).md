**brethil** is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "beech-tree",<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> "silver birch".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **Examples:**
    - *[Brethil](/wiki/Brethil)*
    - *[Nimbrethil](/wiki/Nimbrethil)*
    - *[Fimbrethil](/wiki/Fimbrethil)*

The 'ordinary' Sindarin word for birch was [chwind](/wiki/Chwind "Chwind") or [whinn](/wiki/Chwind "Chwind").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*brethil* is derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=BER%C3%89TH&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="BERÉTH (page does not exist)">BERÉTH</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> and is probably related to [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) *bridh(n)ir* ("queen, princess").<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Notes {#Notes}

The word should not be related to *brethil* "daughter of the Queen; princess"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup> which obviously derives from *[bereth](/wiki/Bereth "Bereth")* (root [BARAT](/wiki/BARAT)).
