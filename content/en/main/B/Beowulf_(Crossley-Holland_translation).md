<i><b>Beowulf</b></i> is a 1968 book publishing the [*Beowulf* poem](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)") translated by Kevin Crossley-Holland. Bruce Mitchell, in his Introduction, discusses several academic works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Mitchell also provides a short quotation from an [undated letter to Mitchell](/wiki/Letter_to_Bruce_Mitchell "Letter to Bruce Mitchell") from Tolkien discussing *Beowulf*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In his "Introducer's Note", Mitchell gives thanks to "Professor J.R.R. Tolkien, for permission to quote from a cherished private letter", and expresses his "deep obligations" to "Professors J.R.R. Tolkien, [C.L. Wrenn](/wiki/Charles_Leslie_Wrenn "Charles Leslie Wrenn"), and A. Campbell for the lectures I have heard them deliver and the stimulus I have derived from them".
