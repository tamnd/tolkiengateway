**Bowman "Nick" Cotton** was the fourth child of Farmer [Tom Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

With his father and his brothers, Nick fought in the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater) against [Sharkey](/wiki/Saruman "Saruman")'s [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
