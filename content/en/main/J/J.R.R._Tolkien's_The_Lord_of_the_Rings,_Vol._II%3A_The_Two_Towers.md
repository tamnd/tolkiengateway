<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s The Lord of the Rings, Vol. II: The Two Towers</b></i> is a PC game by [Interplay Productions](/wiki/Interplay_Productions).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-IMDB_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDB-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Moby_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moby-5">[5]</a></sup> A version for the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amiga" title="wikipedia:Amiga">Amiga</a> was developed as well by [Silicon &amp; Synapse](/wiki/Silicon_%26_Synapse) but was eventually cancelled.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Production {#Production}

*The Lord of the Rings, Vol. II: The Two Towers* followed the first part, *[The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(1990_computer_game)> "The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I (1990 computer game)")*<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> and chronicles the events of [the second tome](/wiki/The_Two_Towers "The Two Towers").<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup> A third game was originally also planned, covering *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*, but it never came to be.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>

The game was designed by [Mark Whittlesey](/wiki/Mark_Whittlesey) and [Scott Bennie](/wiki/Scott_Bennie),<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-IMDB_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-IMDB-4">[4]</a></sup> who chose to retain most of the story and also mixed in elements from [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s other works.<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

The gameplay was copied from the first game,<sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup> but made considerably easier. Unlike its predecessor, there is no fixed character: several characters are playable,<sup id="cite_ref-Moby_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Moby-5">[5]</a></sup> each with a selection of weapons.

The game also featured an <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automap" title="wikipedia:Automap">automapping</a> system, and the scale was reduced to improve player orientation.
