Over the couse of the years, many books and films about Tolkien have been called **[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)**, either with or without a subtitle.

- [1984](/wiki/1984): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Myth, Morality and Religion](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Myth,_Morality_and_Religion)* by [Richard L. Purtill](/wiki/Richard_L._Purtill).
- [1988](/wiki/1988): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=J.R.R._Tolkien:_Hobbit_Chronicler&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="J.R.R. Tolkien: Hobbit Chronicler (page does not exist)">J.R.R. Tolkien: Hobbit Chronicler</a></i> by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Russell_Shorto&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Russell Shorto (page does not exist)">Russell Shorto</a>.

Tolkien: The Art of the Myth-maker</a></i> by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Carol_D._Stevens&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carol D. Stevens (page does not exist)">Carol D. - [1997](/wiki/1997): *[J.R.R. Tolkien (Writers and Their Work)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Writers_and_Their_Work)>)* by [Charles Moseley](/wiki/Charles_Moseley), in the *Writers &amp; Their Work* series.
- [2001](/wiki/2001): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Author of the Century](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Author_of_the_Century)* by [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey).

- [2002](/wiki/2002): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Beginner's Guide](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Beginner%27s_Guide)* by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Andrew_Blake&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Andrew Blake (page does not exist)">Andrew Blake</a>.
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[J.R.R. Tolkien (Library of Great Authors)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Library_of_Great_Authors)>)* by [Stanley P. Baldwin](/wiki/Stanley_P._Baldwin), in the *Library of Great Authors* series.
- [2003](/wiki/2003): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography (Greenwood Biographies)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography_(Greenwood_Biographies)>)* by [Leslie Ellen Jones](/wiki/Leslie_Ellen_Jones), in the *Greenwood Biographies* series.
- [2011](/wiki/2011): *[J.R.R. Tolkien (Holiday House)](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien_(Holiday_House)>)*, a children's book by Alexandra and John Wallner.
- [2012](/wiki/2012): *[JRR Tolkien: The True Lord of the Rings](/wiki/JRR_Tolkien:_The_True_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, a graphic novel.
- [2013](/wiki/2013): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: the Forest and the City](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_the_Forest_and_the_City)*, a collection of papers.
- [2017](/wiki/2017): *[J. R. R. Tolkien (Routledge)](</wiki/J._R._R._Tolkien_(Routledge)>)*, a collection of scholarly works, edited by [Stuart D. Lee](/wiki/Stuart_D._Lee).
- [2022](/wiki/2022): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: The Art of the Manuscript](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_The_Art_of_the_Manuscript)*, the catalog of the exhibition of the same name.
