**Jemima Catlin** (born [27 October](/wiki/27_October) [1986](/wiki/1986)) is a British artist who illustrated the [2013](/wiki/2013) edition of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* and the subsequent *[Tolkien Calendar 2014](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2014)*.

## Early life {#Early_life}

Catlin first encountered *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* when she was 12, as her grandfather was a Tolkien fan and collector. She studied a Diploma in Art and Design at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weymouth_College" title="wikipedia:Weymouth College">Weymouth College</a> and continued studying Visual Communication at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_University_Bournemouth" title="wikipedia:Arts University Bournemouth">Arts University at Bournemouth</a> before graduating with a degree in Illustration in June 2010.<sup id="cite_ref-Foes_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foes-2">[2]</a></sup> She lives in Dorchester, England with her husband.<sup id="cite_ref-Library_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Library-3">[3]</a></sup>

## *The Hobbit* {#The_Hobbit}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:The_Hobbit_2013.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/6/63/The_Hobbit_2013.jpg/200px-The_Hobbit_2013.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="263" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/6/63/The_Hobbit_2013.jpg/300px-The_Hobbit_2013.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/6/63/The_Hobbit_2013.jpg 2x" data-file-width="377" data-file-height="495"/></a><figcaption>Jemima Catlin&#39;s illustrated version of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">The Hobbit</a></i></figcaption></figure>

As part of her final project in her degree, Catlin chose to illustrate *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. After sending the images to [David Brawn](/wiki/David_Brawn) at [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins), he invited her to their offices in London; although [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) did not feel that Catlin's style suited *Roverandom*, Christopher and his wife [Baillie](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien "Baillie Tolkien") were sufficiently enamoured of her work to commission her to illustrate *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* a year later.<sup id="cite_ref-Library_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Library-3">[3]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>This edition needed to appeal to children, but The Hobbit is loved by all ages so I tried to illustrate it in a way that both audiences would enjoy it. My main influence were Tolkien’s own drawings, I really loved his illustrations and wanted to stay true to how he wanted the characters/landscapes to look. I adore Alan Lee’s work, his style is so detailed and photo-realistic, but so different from my own that I didn’t find it influenced me. I love the films and have seen The Lord of The Rings trilogy numerous times. My depiction of The Hobbit was just how I saw it in my imagination and it happened to be quite different to Peter Jackson’s vision. Tolkien had done lots of illustrations for The Hobbit and these were my inspiration – I really wanted to bring The Hobbit back to its original roots.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Jemima Catlin on comparison with <a href="/wiki/Alan_Lee" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The completed version of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* took over two years to illustrate, included 150 illustrations and was released on [12 September](/wiki/12_September) [2013](/wiki/2013) (the same day as *[Tolkien Calendar 2014](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2014)*).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Two weeks later Catlin exhibited her artwork at [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society)'s [Oxonmoot 2013](/wiki/Oxonmoot_2013) event. She describes herself as "proud" of her achievement, whilst still expressing a desire to illustrate either *Roverandom* or *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Legendarium_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Legendarium-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Artistic style {#Artistic_style}

Catlin's artistic style has been compared to [Tove Jansson](/wiki/Tove_Jansson) (illustrator of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moomin" title="wikipedia:Moomin">The Moomins</a></i> and a Swedish edition of *The Hobbit*), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._H._Shepard" title="wikipedia:E. H. Shepard">E.H. Shepard</a> (<i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winnie-the-Pooh" title="wikipedia:Winnie-the-Pooh">Winnie-the-Pooh</a></i>) as well as the anime style of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_Ghibli" title="wikipedia:Studio Ghibli">Studio Ghibli</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Foes_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foes-2">[2]</a></sup> Catlin herself states that she was only influenced by Tolkien's own drawings whilst deliberately avoiding the films.<sup id="cite_ref-Library_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Library-3">[3]</a></sup>
