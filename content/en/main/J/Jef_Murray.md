**Jef Murray** ([17 March](/wiki/17_March) [1960](/wiki/1960) - [3 August](/wiki/3_August) [2015](/wiki/2015)), employed by the Pitts Theology Library of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University" title="wikipedia:Emory University">Emory University</a> in Atlanta, has painted and sketched numerous [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")-related images for publications such as the [*Amon Hen* journal](</wiki/Amon_Hen_(journal)> "Amon Hen (journal)"), *[Parma Nölé](/wiki/Parma_N%C3%B6l%C3%A9)*, and the [*Mallorn* journal](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)"). He has also had Tolkien-related work featured in the *St. Austin Review*, a journal of Catholic culture.

In 2009, Murray made the illustrations for [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien)'s *[Black &amp; White Ogre Country](/wiki/Black_%26_White_Ogre_Country)*.
