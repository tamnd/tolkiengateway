**Jef Murray** ([17 March](../_/17_March.md) [1960](../_/1960.md) - [3 August](../_/3_August.md) [2015](../_/2015.md)), employed by the Pitts Theology Library of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University" title="wikipedia:Emory University">Emory University</a> in Atlanta, has painted and sketched numerous [Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md)-related images for publications such as the [*Amon Hen* journal](<../A/Amon_Hen_(journal).md>), *[Parma Nölé](../P/Parma_Nölé.md)*, and the [*Mallorn* journal](<../M/Mallorn_(journal).md>). He has also had Tolkien-related work featured in the *St. Austin Review*, a journal of Catholic culture.

In 2009, Murray made the illustrations for [Hilary Tolkien](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md)'s *[Black &amp; White Ogre Country](../B/Black_&_White_Ogre_Country.md)*.
