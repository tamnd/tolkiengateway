**Joseph Daniel Turner Mawle**, known as **Joseph Mawle**, is an English actor. He portrayed [Adar](<../A/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power).md>) in the first season only of the *[The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* series by [Amazon](../A/Amazon_Studios.md). His role was recast for the second season of the series, with [Samuel Hazeldine](../S/Samuel_Hazeldine.md) taking over his role.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

His character was first announced as "Oren".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
