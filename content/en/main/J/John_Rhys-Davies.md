British actor **John Rhys-Davies** (born 1944) played [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and provided the voice for [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). He had originally auditioned for the role of [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
