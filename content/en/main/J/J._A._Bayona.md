**Juan Antonio García Bayona** ([May 9](/wiki/9_May "9 May"), [1975](/wiki/1975)) is a Spanish director. He directed the first two episodes of he first season of the *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power‎](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
