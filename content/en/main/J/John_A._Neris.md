**John A. Neris** ([28 November](/wiki/28_November), [1932](/wiki/1932) - [January 16](/wiki/16_January "16 January"), [1995](/wiki/1995)) was a U.S. actor who provided the rotoscope on-set performance for [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)")*.
