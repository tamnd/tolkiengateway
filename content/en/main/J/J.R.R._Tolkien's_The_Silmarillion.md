<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s</b></i> **The Silmarillion** is a [1999](/wiki/1999) booklet about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, in the series *Babel handbooks on fantasy and SF writers* (no. 10).

A revised version of the text appeared in [The Silmarillion: *Thirty Years On*](/wiki/The_Silmarillion:_Thirty_Years_On "The Silmarillion: Thirty Years On") (2010).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
