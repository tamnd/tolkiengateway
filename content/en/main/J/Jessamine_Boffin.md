**Jessamine Bolger, née Boffin**, was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Jessamine was the daughter of [Hugo Boffin](/wiki/Hugo_Boffin) and [Donnamira Took](/wiki/Donnamira_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Herugar Bolger](/wiki/Herugar_Bolger), and had one child, [Odovacar](/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger "Odovacar Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cestrum" title="wikipedia:Cestrum">Jessamine</a> is a fragrant plant. The name is exotic but comes to English through French. <span></span>
