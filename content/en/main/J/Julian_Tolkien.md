**Julian Tolkien**, son of [Hilary Tolkien](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien), is the nephew of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He and his wife Glynis have three sons: [Timothy](/wiki/Tim_Tolkien "Tim Tolkien"), Nicholas and Stephen.

Julian Tolkien has recalled that his uncle John Ronald was a "*witty raconteur*" and that he "*saw the fun in things and had strong Christian principles*".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
