**Joshua Burnell** is a UK based musician who has released several albums since 2013, including his album *Lend An Ear* which has two song that are Tolkien's poetry put to music; [Bilbo's Last Song](</wiki/Bilbo%27s_Last_Song_(at_the_Grey_Havens)> "Bilbo's Last Song (at the Grey Havens)") which is a cover of [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann)'s arrangement, and [Far Over The Misty Mountains Cold](/wiki/Far_over_the_misty_mountains_cold "Far over the misty mountains cold") which he put to his own tune. The album *Lend An Ear* was originally published in 2013, and in 2026 he re-recorded the album due to being unhappy with his first album's results. For the 2026 album *Lend An Ear \[Revisited\]* he made his re-record cover of Bilbo's Last Song more of his own.

He also posted on YouTube under the name *Runcible Spoon*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> fourteen songs from [the Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit "The Hobbit").
