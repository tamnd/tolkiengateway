**John Ellison** ([21 February](/wiki/21_February) [1932](/wiki/1932) - [26 June](/wiki/26_June) [2016](/wiki/2016)) was an author, editor and member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). With [Patricia Reynolds](/wiki/Patricia_Reynolds) he edited six editions of the Society's annual journal *[Mallorn](</wiki/Mallorn_(journal)> "Mallorn (journal)")* and, over the years, has been a regular contributor to many of the Society's pamphlets and publications.
