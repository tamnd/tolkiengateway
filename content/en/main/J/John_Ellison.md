**John Ellison** ([21 February](../_/21_February.md) [1932](../_/1932.md) - [26 June](../_/26_June.md) [2016](../_/2016.md)) was an author, editor and member of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). With [Patricia Reynolds](../P/Patricia_Reynolds.md) he edited six editions of the Society's annual journal *[Mallorn](<../M/Mallorn_(journal).md>)* and, over the years, has been a regular contributor to many of the Society's pamphlets and publications.
