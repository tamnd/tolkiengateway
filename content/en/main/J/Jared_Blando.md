**Jared Blando** is an American artist who illustrated the map used in *[The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Fate_of_the_Fellowship)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

He was commissioned to illustrate the map for the unreleased *[The One Ring – The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_One_Ring_%E2%80%93_The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*, which he later sold independently.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
