**Jules Bass** ([September 16](/wiki/16_September "16 September"), [1935](/wiki/1935) - [October 25](/wiki/25_October "25 October") [2022](/wiki/2022)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was an American director, producer and composer. Along with fellow director [Arthur Rankin Jr.](/wiki/Arthur_Rankin_Jr.), they founded the [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) production company. He and Rankin were the directors and producers of the animated adaptation of *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)")* in 1977 and *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")* in 1980.
