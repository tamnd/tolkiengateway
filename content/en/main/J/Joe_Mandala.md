**Joe Mandala** (born [1972](/wiki/1972)) is an American role playing gaming author and Tolkien essayist.

Having started with Middle-earth gaming sometime in the mid-1980s, Mandala was involved by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) in the early 1990s to do secondary editing and vetting for [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> He worked on a 250 page *Races and Cultures of Middle-earth* supplement, which went unpublished due to ICE losing its license.<sup id="cite_ref-projects_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-projects-1">[1]</a></sup> He regularly wrote articles for *[The Guild Companion](/wiki/The_Guild_Companion)*, serving as its Middle-earth editor.<sup id="cite_ref-projects_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-projects-1">[1]</a></sup>

After having positively reviewed the core book of the *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*, he ended up writing *[Maps of Middle-earth: Cities and Strongholds](/wiki/Maps_of_Middle-earth:_Cities_and_Strongholds)* for the game.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-projects_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-projects-1">[1]</a></sup>
