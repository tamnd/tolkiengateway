**John Huston** ([5 August](/wiki/5_August) [1906](/wiki/1906) – [28 August](/wiki/28_August) [1987](/wiki/1987)) was an American actor, director and screenwriter who voiced the role of [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) in the [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) animated productions of <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)" title="The Hobbit (1977 film)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (1977 film)</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>.</i>
