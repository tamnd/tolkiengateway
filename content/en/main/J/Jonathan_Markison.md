<a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_(honorific)#Religion" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Dom</a> **Jonathan Markison** was a member of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club). He was educated at New College,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and was a monk whose polymathy extended to knowledge of Germanic origins.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
