**John Bach** (born [June 5](/wiki/5_June "5 June"), [1946](/wiki/1946)) is a Welsh-born New Zealand actor. He played [Madril](/wiki/Madril) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King")*.

His surname is pronounced "Baitch".
