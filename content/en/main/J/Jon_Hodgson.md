**Jon Hodgson** is a Scottish artist. In the 2010s, he was commissioned by [Cubicle 7 Entertainment](/wiki/Cubicle_7_Entertainment) to create artwork for the *[The One Ring](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)") role-playing game*, in which capacity he also worked as a creative director until [2018](/wiki/2018).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also contributed artwork for the [War of the Ring: The Card Game](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring:_The_Card_Game), first released in [2022](/wiki/2022).

In 2021, he designed and illustrated a role-playing game based on [Beowulf](</wiki/Beowulf_(poem)> "Beowulf (poem)"), using a modified *[Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons)* ruleset, which Hodgson described as the inevitable result of his work for *The One Ring*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
