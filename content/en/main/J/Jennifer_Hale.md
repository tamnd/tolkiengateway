**Jennifer Hale** ([January 30](/wiki/30_January "30 January"), [1972](/wiki/1972)) is a Canadian-born American voice actress. She has provided voices for several animated productions of [Disney](/wiki/Disney) and Marvel, and various roles in *Star Wars* video games.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2017](/wiki/2017): *[Middle-earth: Shadow of War](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_War)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2014](/wiki/2014): *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel)
- [2011](/wiki/2011): *[The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North)* - Idonna Bellflower
- [2003](/wiki/2003): [*The Hobbit* (2003 video game)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)") - Lianna, additional voices
- [2001](/wiki/2001): [*The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring* (video game)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)") - [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), [Lobelia](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins "Lobelia Sackville-Baggins"), [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton)
