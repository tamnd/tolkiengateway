**J.B. Casacop** is a Filipino illustrator. As a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, he created art for the [Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth") expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also contributed artwork to various expansions of [The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
