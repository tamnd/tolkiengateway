Brother **Joseph Power** ([2 February](/wiki/2_February) [1925](/wiki/1925) - [19 October](/wiki/19_October) [2011](/wiki/2011)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was a professor of English Language and Literature at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsh_University" title="wikipedia:Walsh University">Walsh University</a>. He received his B.A. from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London" title="wikipedia:University of London">University of London</a>, and later gained an exhibition to St. Catherine's College, Oxford (reading for an Honors Degree in English Language and Literature).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During this time he visited [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s house for evening instruction in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English), but instead joined the Tolkiens in a celebration of the republication of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* (likely the second edition of [1951](/wiki/1951)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In 1965, Joseph Power moved to the United States.
