<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien: Life and Legend</b></i> is the catalogue of the [1992](/wiki/1992) centenary exhibition held by the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library), Oxford, to commemorate the centenary of the birth of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

"J.R.R. Tolkien: Life and Legend" was the first large exhibition focused on Tolkien. In this exhibition 250 items were listed in the catalogue, with almost all coming from the Bodleian archives — a few were donated by Tolkien's publisher [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins), and a small number from Corpus Christi College and Exeter College from Tolkien's time there.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

This catalogue lists, describes, and gives background on the items displayed during the exhibition. It reproduces a large selection of artwork, photographs, maps, manuscript pages, annotations, letters, and other momentos in both colour and black &amp; white.

A few examples are: the cover of *[Ye Chequers Clubbe Binge](/wiki/Ye_Chequers_Clubbe_Binge)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> of *[Exeter College Smoker](</wiki/Exeter_College_Smoker_(1913)> "Exeter College Smoker (1913)")*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> a page from [Book of the Foxrook](/wiki/Book_of_the_Foxrook).<sup id="cite_ref-Seven_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Seven-4">[4]</a></sup>
