**Jonathan Harding** (born [May 2](/wiki/2_May "2 May"), [1978](/wiki/1978) in Timaru<sup id="cite_ref-Con_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Con-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup>) is a New Zealander who was a Production Assistant for [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"),<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> Production Runner for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*, Key Production Runner for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> Second Assistant Accountant for [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)") and Construction Accountant for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)*.

Harding had acting roles in [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), playing [Erestor](/wiki/Erestor) and [Dínendal](/wiki/D%C3%ADnendal) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">[4]</a></sup> and an [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") Archer at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Harding lives in Wellington<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">[4]</a></sup> as of [April 8](/wiki/8_April "8 April"), [2004](/wiki/2004).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> He considers music to be his true passion and played in a a jazz/funk trio, as well as a band which played at the Wrap Party for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup>
