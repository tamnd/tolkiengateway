**Jonathan Harding** (born [May 2](../_/2_May.md), [1978](../_/1978.md) in Timaru<sup id="cite_ref-Con_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Con-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup>) is a New Zealander who was a Production Assistant for [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>),<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> Production Runner for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*, Key Production Runner for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> Second Assistant Accountant for [*The Hobbit* film trilogy](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>) and Construction Accountant for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)*.

Harding had acting roles in [*The Lord of the Rings* film trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>), playing [Erestor](../E/Erestor.md) and [Dínendal](../D/Dínendal.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">[4]</a></sup> and an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) Archer at [Helm's Deep](../H/Helm's_Deep.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup>

Harding lives in Wellington<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">[4]</a></sup> as of [April 8](../_/8_April.md), [2004](../_/2004.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup> He considers music to be his true passion and played in a a jazz/funk trio, as well as a band which played at the Wrap Party for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-2">[2]</a></sup>
