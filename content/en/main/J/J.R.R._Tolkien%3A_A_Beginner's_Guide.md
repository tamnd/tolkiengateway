<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien: A Beginner&#39;s Guide</b></i> is an elementary school-level introduction to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md), written by Andrew Blake.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Examining the novels and other writings, Andrew Blake shows how J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s mastery of language, his establishment of a fully-fledged alternative mythology, and his brave way of looking at difficult questions have helped to inaugurate a new way of writing which re-established &#34;fantasy&#34; as a late 20th century genre.
</p>
</blockquote>
