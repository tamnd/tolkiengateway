**Jay Johnstone** is an artist who specializes in illustrations for [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/legendarium). His style is inspired from religious and medieval [iconography](/wiki/Wikipedia:icon).
