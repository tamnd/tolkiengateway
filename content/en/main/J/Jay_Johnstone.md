**Jay Johnstone** is an artist who specializes in illustrations for [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). His style is inspired from religious and medieval <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/icon" title="wikipedia:icon">iconography</a>.
