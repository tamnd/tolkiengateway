**J.R.R. Tolkien's Riders of Rohan** is an out of print video game, which was commercially distributed and licensed by the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> The game was written and designed by [Hank Howie](/wiki/Hank_Howie), [Phil Redmond](/wiki/Phil_Redmond), et al.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup> The story centered around the war between the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) and [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman). In addition to controlling the movement of the good forces, the player was able to, among other things, shoot [orcs](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") with bow and fight enemy leaders with sword.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The main characters are [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) and [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
