<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Letters to Rhona Beare</b></i> is a reproduction of two letters by [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to scholar [Rhona Beare](../R/Rhona_Beare.md) in their original form. They were printed in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* with some editorial changes and omissions.

- [Letter to Rhona Beare (14 October 1958)](<../L/Letter_to_Rhona_Beare_(14_October_1958).md>) (corresponds to [Letter 211](../L/Letter_211.md))
- [Letter to Rhona Beare (8 June 1961)](<../L/Letter_to_Rhona_Beare_(8_June_1961).md>) (corresponds to [Letter 230](../L/Letter_230.md))

The booklet was distributed to members of the New England Tolkien Society. 95 copies were printed, each numbered by hand in ink.<sup id="cite_ref-db_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-db-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Changes in the *Letters* {#Changes_in_the_Letters}

- Compared to the letter of [14 October](../_/14_October.md) [1958](../_/1958.md), Letter 211 omitted some marginal comments, replaced a paragraph with one from a draft (on the same point), and provided a prefatory note summarizing Dr. Beare's questions to which the letter is a response.

- Compared to the letter of [8 June](../_/8_June.md) [1961](../_/1961.md), Letter 230 omitted its first two paragraphs and final paragraph. The second paragraph is a translation of the [Elvish hymn to Elbereth](../A/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel.md) in *The Lord of the Rings*, which differs in detail from the literal translation given in *[The Road Goes Ever On](<../T/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-db_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-db-1">[1]</a></sup>
