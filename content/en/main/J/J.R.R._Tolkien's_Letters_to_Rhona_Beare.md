<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s Letters to Rhona Beare</b></i> is a reproduction of two letters by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to scholar [Rhona Beare](/wiki/Rhona_Beare) in their original form. They were printed in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* with some editorial changes and omissions.

- [Letter to Rhona Beare (14 October 1958)](</wiki/Letter_to_Rhona_Beare_(14_October_1958)>) (corresponds to [Letter 211](/wiki/Letter_211))
- [Letter to Rhona Beare (8 June 1961)](</wiki/Letter_to_Rhona_Beare_(8_June_1961)>) (corresponds to [Letter 230](/wiki/Letter_230))

The booklet was distributed to members of the New England Tolkien Society. 95 copies were printed, each numbered by hand in ink.<sup id="cite_ref-db_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-db-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Changes in the *Letters* {#Changes_in_the_Letters}

- Compared to the letter of [14 October](/wiki/14_October) [1958](/wiki/1958), Letter 211 omitted some marginal comments, replaced a paragraph with one from a draft (on the same point), and provided a prefatory note summarizing Dr. Beare's questions to which the letter is a response.

- Compared to the letter of [8 June](/wiki/8_June) [1961](/wiki/1961), Letter 230 omitted its first two paragraphs and final paragraph. The second paragraph is a translation of the [Elvish hymn to Elbereth](/wiki/A_Elbereth_Gilthoniel "A Elbereth Gilthoniel") in *The Lord of the Rings*, which differs in detail from the literal translation given in *[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-db_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-db-1">[1]</a></sup>
