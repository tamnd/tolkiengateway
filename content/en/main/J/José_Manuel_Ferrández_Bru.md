**José Manuel Ferrández Bru** (born [1970](../_/1970.md)) is a Spanish computer science engineer, professor and writer. He has published several articles about [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md), notably about Tolkien's relation to Spain in general and to [Francis Xavier Morgan](../F/Francis_Xavier_Morgan.md) in particular. Ferrández Bru was a founding member and the first chairman of the [Spanish Tolkien Society](../S/Sociedad_Tolkien_Española.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
