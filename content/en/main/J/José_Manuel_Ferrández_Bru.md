**José Manuel Ferrández Bru** (born [1970](/wiki/1970)) is a Spanish computer science engineer, professor and writer. He has published several articles about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), notably about Tolkien's relation to Spain in general and to [Francis Xavier Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Xavier_Morgan) in particular. Ferrández Bru was a founding member and the first chairman of the [Spanish Tolkien Society](/wiki/Sociedad_Tolkien_Espa%C3%B1ola "Sociedad Tolkien Española").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
