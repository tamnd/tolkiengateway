**J.R.R. Tolkien - Master of Middle Earth** was published by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Miles_Kimball&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Miles Kimball (page does not exist)">Miles Kimball</a> of Oshkosh, WI. The calendar is unique in that it spans the period from August 1990 to August 1991 instead of a standard calendar or academic year.

- Dimensions: 10 x 20 inches (opened for hanging)
- Metal tab with punched hanging hole
- White comb-bound
