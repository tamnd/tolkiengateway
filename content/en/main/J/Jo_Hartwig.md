**Jo Hartwig** is a German artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*. He also illustrated the cover for the German localization of *[The Hobbit Adventure Boardgame](/wiki/The_Hobbit_Adventure_Boardgame)*, and designed and illustrated *[Ringgeister](/wiki/Ringgeister)*.
