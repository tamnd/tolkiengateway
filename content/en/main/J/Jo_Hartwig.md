**Jo Hartwig** is a German artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*. He also illustrated the cover for the German localization of *[The Hobbit Adventure Boardgame](../T/The_Hobbit_Adventure_Boardgame.md)*, and designed and illustrated *[Ringgeister](../R/Ringgeister.md)*.
