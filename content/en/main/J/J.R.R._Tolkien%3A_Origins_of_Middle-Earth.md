**J.R.R. Tolkien: Origins of Middle-earth** is a half-hour documentary, directed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Michael_Pellerin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Pellerin (page does not exist)">Michael Pellerin</a>. It was included on *[The Journey Continues](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_Special_Extended_DVD_Edition#The_Appendices:_Part_Three:_The_Journey_Continues_(disc_3)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers Special Extended DVD Edition")*, the third disc of [the extended edition of *The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers (extended edition)").
