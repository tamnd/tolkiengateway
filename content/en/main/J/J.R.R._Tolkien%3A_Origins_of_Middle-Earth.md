**J.R.R. Tolkien: Origins of Middle-earth** is a half-hour documentary, directed by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Michael_Pellerin&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Michael Pellerin (page does not exist)">Michael Pellerin</a>.
