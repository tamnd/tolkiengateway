## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>He imagined realms of wizards, orcs and hobbits and elevated fantasy from pulp to respected literature. J.R.R. Tolkien, the iconic author who captivated generations with his rich and complex mythology of Middle Earth, is the subject of his own tale in a special edition of Bluewater Productions&#39; latest biography comic title &#34;Orbit.&#34;
</p>
</blockquote>
