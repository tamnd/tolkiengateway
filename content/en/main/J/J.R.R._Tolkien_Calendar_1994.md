The <i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1994</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [HarperCollins](../H/HarperCollins.md) on [12 July](../_/12_July.md) [1993](../_/1993.md). It features art by [Michael Kaluta](../M/Michael_Kaluta.md) based on *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.
