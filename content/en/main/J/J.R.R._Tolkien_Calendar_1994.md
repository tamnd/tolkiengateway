The <i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1994</b></i> was the official Tolkien calendar published by [HarperCollins](/wiki/HarperCollins) on [12 July](/wiki/12_July) [1993](/wiki/1993). It features art by [Michael Kaluta](/wiki/Michael_Kaluta) based on *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
