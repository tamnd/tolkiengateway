**Jan Pospíšil** is a Czech artist. He has illustrated for both editions of [The One Ring (role-playing game)](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), after being hired by [Jon Hodgson](/wiki/Jon_Hodgson).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also created artwork for [Hobbit Tales from the Green Dragon Inn](/wiki/Hobbit_Tales_from_the_Green_Dragon_Inn).

Beyond officially licensed work, he worked on fan projects such as [The Last Days (of the Third Age of Middle-earth)](</wiki/The_Last_Days_(of_the_Third_Age_of_Middle-earth)>).
