**Jonathan Gow** was a professor in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md). After [Wilfrid Trewin Jeremy](../W/Wilfrid_Trewin_Jeremy.md) had become a lecturer, he gave a series of lectures with the title of *Public-house School* (as he himself dubbed it), about an Oxford-based literary discussion group.<sup id="cite_ref-Gow_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gow-1">[1]</a></sup>

But professor Gow had puffed and boggled at the title. Therefore Jeremy had offered to change it to *Lewis and Carolus, or the Oxford Looking-glass*, or *Jack and the Beanstalk* but that did not smooth matters.<sup id="cite_ref-Gow_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gow-1">[1]</a></sup>
