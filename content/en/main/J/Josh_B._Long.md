**Josh B. Long** completed his M.A. thesis on *[Smith of Wootton Major](../S/Smith_of_Wootton_Major.md)* at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Polytechnic_University,_Pomona" title="wikipedia:California State Polytechnic University, Pomona">California State Polytechnic University, Pomona</a> in 2006.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He has worked as a research assistant on *[The Company They Keep](../T/The_Company_They_Keep.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
