**Janet Brennan Croft** (born [1961](../_/1961.md)) is an American author, editor, and Associate University Librarian for Content Discovery, University of Northern Iowa. She is the editor of *[Mythlore: A Journal of J.R.R. Tolkien, C.S. Lewis, Charles Williams, and Mythopoeic Literature](../M/Mythlore.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
