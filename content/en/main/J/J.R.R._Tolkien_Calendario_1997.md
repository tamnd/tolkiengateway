The **J.R.R. Tolkien Calendario 1997** was the second calendar published by the [Italian Tolkien Society](../I/Italian_Tolkien_Society.md). It features illustrations from twelve different artists.

- Dimensions: 13 3/8 x 15.75 inches
- Wire bound
- Heavy wire hanging loop

## Illustrations {#Illustrations}

- Cover by [Luca Michelucci](../L/Luca_Michelucci.md)
- [January](January.md) - Il Cavaliere Nero fiuta la presenza dell'Anello by Gloria Della Rossa
- [February](../F/February.md) - Baccador by Maura Boldi
- [March](../M/March.md) - Gandalf contro il Balrog by Dalmathio Frau
- [April](../A/April.md) - I Cavalieri Neri, al Guado del Bruinen by Ambra Colombani
- [May](../M/May.md) - Gandalf incontra Ombromanto by Giovanni Giurco
- [June](June.md) - Frodo, Sam e Gollum in viaggio per Mordor by Andrea Rivola
- [July](July.md) - Dawn on Mindolluin by Anton Spazzapan
- [August](../A/August.md) - Re Elessar by Domenico Franchi
- [September](../S/September.md) - Mithlond, i Porti Grigi by Alessandro Maccari
- [October](../O/October.md) - Il discorso di Feanor ai noldoli by Luca Canova
- [November](../N/November.md) - Una riunione inaspettata by Victor Sneep
- [December](../D/December.md) - Smaug by Michele Mariani
