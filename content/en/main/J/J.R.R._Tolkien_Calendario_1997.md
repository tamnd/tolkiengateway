The **J.R.R. Tolkien Calendario 1997** was the second calendar published by the [Italian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Italian_Tolkien_Society). It features illustrations from twelve different artists.

- Dimensions: 13 3/8 x 15.75 inches
- Wire bound
- Heavy wire hanging loop

## Illustrations {#Illustrations}

- Cover by [Luca Michelucci](/wiki/Luca_Michelucci)
- [January](/wiki/January) - Il Cavaliere Nero fiuta la presenza dell'Anello by Gloria Della Rossa
- [February](/wiki/February) - Baccador by Maura Boldi
- [March](/wiki/March) - Gandalf contro il Balrog by Dalmathio Frau
- [April](/wiki/April) - I Cavalieri Neri, al Guado del Bruinen by Ambra Colombani
- [May](/wiki/May) - Gandalf incontra Ombromanto by Giovanni Giurco
- [June](/wiki/June) - Frodo, Sam e Gollum in viaggio per Mordor by Andrea Rivola
- [July](/wiki/July) - Dawn on Mindolluin by Anton Spazzapan
- [August](/wiki/August) - Re Elessar by Domenico Franchi
- [September](/wiki/September) - Mithlond, i Porti Grigi by Alessandro Maccari
- [October](/wiki/October) - Il discorso di Feanor ai noldoli by Luca Canova
- [November](/wiki/November) - Una riunione inaspettata by Victor Sneep
- [December](/wiki/December) - Smaug by Michele Mariani
