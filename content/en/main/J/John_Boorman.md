**Sir John Boorman** [CBE](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire "Order of the British Empire") (b. [18 January](/wiki/18_January) [1933](/wiki/1933)) is an English film director who had planned [a film adaptation](/wiki/John_Boorman%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "John Boorman's The Lord of the Rings") of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, however the plan fell through, although he used some elements from his proposed film on the film *Excalibur*, which he directed.
