<b>Emily Jane Neave (<i>née</i> Suffield)</b> ([June](/wiki/June) [1872](/wiki/1872) - [22 February](/wiki/22_February) [1963](/wiki/1963)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Tolkien)'s sister, and thus one of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s aunts.

Jane Neave worked as a schoolteacher, and the young J.R.R. Tolkien on occasion stayed with his aunt and her husband Edvin Neave while [Mabel Tolkien](/wiki/Mabel_Tolkien) was hospitalized.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Jane Neave's farm was called Bag End by the locals in Dormston, Worcestershire.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Because of a suggestion from Jane Neave, J.R.R. Tolkien published the book *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* in [1962](/wiki/1962).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
