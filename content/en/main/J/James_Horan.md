American actor **James Horan** ([December 14](/wiki/14_December "14 December"), [1954](/wiki/1954)) voiced various characters in computer games based on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works.

## Voices {#Voices}

- [2006](/wiki/2006) - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* - [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)
- [2004](/wiki/2004) - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth)* - Boromir
- [2003](/wiki/2003) - *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)")* - [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug), additional voices
- [2002](/wiki/2002) - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)")* - Boromir, [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), and [Farmer Maggot](/wiki/Farmer_Maggot).
