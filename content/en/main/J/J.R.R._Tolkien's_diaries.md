**J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries** are [unpublished](/wiki/Category:Unpublished_material "Category:Unpublished material") diary entries written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). The manuscripts are part of the Tolkien Papers at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library), a collection which is only open to researchers working under the auspices of the [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter) was given access to the writings and quoted several passages in his work *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography)*, noting that Tolkien used the diaries "*chiefly as a record of sorrow and distress, and when ... his gloom dissipated he ceased to keep up the diary entries*".<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup> Permission to research the diaries was also granted to [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) for their preparation of *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)* (a work which also includes several quotes from the diaries).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- **[New Year's Day](/wiki/1_January "1 January") [1910](/wiki/1910):** "*Depressed and as much in dark as ever, \[...\] God help me. Feel weak and weary.*" \[Entry in the first of Tolkien's preserved diaries\]<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **April [1910](/wiki/1910):** \[After seeing Peter Pan at a Birmingham theatre\]: "*Indescribable but shall never forget it as long as I live. Wish E. had been with me.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **January [1913](/wiki/1913):** Tolkien starts keeping a diary (entitled "JRRT and [EMB](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien") in account together, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_maiorem_Dei_gloriam" title="wikipedia:Ad maiorem Dei gloriam">AMDG</a>") of how many hours he works and, in red ink, his performance of religious duties.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **[New Year's Day](/wiki/1_January "1 January") [1919](/wiki/1919):** Tolkien starts a new diary, mainly written with [the Alphabet of Rúmil](/wiki/Sarati "Sarati").<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- \[Unknown date, referring to his school years\]: "*Did a lot of private lang.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **January [1922](/wiki/1922):** "*Eric Valentine Gordon has come* \[to [Leeds University](/wiki/Leeds_University)\] *and got firmly established and is my devoted friend and pal.*"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- **[1924](/wiki/1924):** \[Concerning the birth of [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien"): "*Now I would not go without what God has sent.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **[1926](/wiki/1926):** \[Carpenter notes that Tolkien wrote "*his diary from 1926 to 1933*" in the alphabet 'Quenyatic'/'Feanorian'\]<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **1926:** \[As part of a summary of events of 1925, Tolkien writes:\] "*The Tale of '[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)', written to amuse [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien") (and myself as it grew) got done*."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

- **?1920s-1930s:** \[On [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)\]: "*Friendship with Lewis compensates for much, and besides giving constant pleasure and comfort has done me much good from the contact with a man at once honest, brave, intellectual - a scholar, a poet, and a philosopher - and a lover, at least after a long pilgrimage, of Our Lord.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **?1930s:** \[On [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien):\] "*a nervy, irritable, cross-grained, self-tormenting, cheeky person. Yet there is something intensely lovable about him, to me at any rate, from the very similarity between us*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **[1933](/wiki/1933):** \[On the occasion of driving his family to visit relatives in Birmingham:\] "*I pass over the pangs to me of passing through Hall Green - become a huge tram-ridden meaningless suburb, where I actually lost my way - and eventually down what is left of beloved lanes of childhood, and past the very gate of our cottage, now in the midst of a sea of new red-brick. The old mill still stands, and Mrs Hunt's still sticks out into the road as it turns uphill; but the crossing beyond the now fenced-in pool, where the bluebell lane ran down into the mill lane, is now a dangerous crossing alive with motors and red lights. The White Ogre's house (which the children were excited to see) is become a petrol station, and most of Short Avenue and the elms between it and the crossing have gone. How I envy those whose precious early scenery has not been exposed to such violent and peculiarly hideous change.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **August [1955](/wiki/1955)**: \[Notes on his visit to Italy with [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien"):\] : "*Venice seemed incredibly, elvishly lovely*";<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup> "*contrary to legend and my belief, Italians ... dislike exaggeration, superlatives, and adjectives of excessive praise. But they seem to answer to colour and poetic expression, if justified.*"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

- **Late [1963](/wiki/1963) or early [1964](/wiki/1964):** \[Carpenter notes that soon after [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis)'s death, Tolkien "*began to keep a diary, which was something he had not done for many years. In part it was an excuse for using another alphabet that he had invented; he called it his '[New English Alphabet](/wiki/New_English_Alphabet)'.*\]: "*Life is grey and grim. I can get nothing done, between stateness and boredom (confined to quarters), and anxiety and distraction. What am I going to do? Be sucked down into residence in a hotel or old-people's home or club, without books or contacts or talk with men? God help me!*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **[1968](/wiki/1968)-[1971](/wiki/1971):** \[Carpenter notes that Tolkien kept a diary "*for a brief time during \[the\] Bournemouth years*".\]<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>
