**J.R.R. Tolkien's diaries** are [unpublished](../../category/U/Unpublished_material.md) diary entries written by [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md). The manuscripts are part of the Tolkien Papers at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md), a collection which is only open to researchers working under the auspices of the [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md) was given access to the writings and quoted several passages in his work *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Biography.md)*, noting that Tolkien used the diaries "*chiefly as a record of sorrow and distress, and when ... his gloom dissipated he ceased to keep up the diary entries*".<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup> Permission to research the diaries was also granted to [Wayne G. Hammond](../W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../C/Christina_Scull.md) for their preparation of *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](../T/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide.md)* (a work which also includes several quotes from the diaries).<sup id="cite_ref-Interview_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Interview-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Contents {#Contents}

- **[New Year's Day](../_/1_January.md) [1910](../_/1910.md):** "*Depressed and as much in dark as ever, \[...\] God help me. Feel weak and weary.*" \[Entry in the first of Tolkien's preserved diaries\]<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **April [1910](../_/1910.md):** \[After seeing Peter Pan at a Birmingham theatre\]: "*Indescribable but shall never forget it as long as I live. Wish E. had been with me.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **January [1913](../_/1913.md):** Tolkien starts keeping a diary (entitled "JRRT and [EMB](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) in account together, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_maiorem_Dei_gloriam" title="wikipedia:Ad maiorem Dei gloriam">AMDG</a>") of how many hours he works and, in red ink, his performance of religious duties.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- **[New Year's Day](../_/1_January.md) [1919](../_/1919.md):** Tolkien starts a new diary, mainly written with [the Alphabet of Rúmil](../S/Sarati.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- \[Unknown date, referring to his school years\]: "*Did a lot of private lang.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **January [1922](../_/1922.md):** "*Eric Valentine Gordon has come* \[to [Leeds University](../L/Leeds_University.md)\] *and got firmly established and is my devoted friend and pal.*"<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- **[1924](../_/1924.md):** \[Concerning the birth of [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md): "*Now I would not go without what God has sent.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **[1926](../_/1926.md):** \[Carpenter notes that Tolkien wrote "*his diary from 1926 to 1933*" in the alphabet 'Quenyatic'/'Feanorian'\]<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **1926:** \[As part of a summary of events of 1925, Tolkien writes:\] "*The Tale of '[Roverandom](../R/Roverandom.md)', written to amuse [John](John_Tolkien.md) (and myself as it grew) got done*."<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

- **?1920s-1930s:** \[On [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)\]: "*Friendship with Lewis compensates for much, and besides giving constant pleasure and comfort has done me much good from the contact with a man at once honest, brave, intellectual - a scholar, a poet, and a philosopher - and a lover, at least after a long pilgrimage, of Our Lord.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **?1930s:** \[On [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md):\] "*a nervy, irritable, cross-grained, self-tormenting, cheeky person. Yet there is something intensely lovable about him, to me at any rate, from the very similarity between us*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **[1933](../_/1933.md):** \[On the occasion of driving his family to visit relatives in Birmingham:\] "*I pass over the pangs to me of passing through Hall Green - become a huge tram-ridden meaningless suburb, where I actually lost my way - and eventually down what is left of beloved lanes of childhood, and past the very gate of our cottage, now in the midst of a sea of new red-brick. The old mill still stands, and Mrs Hunt's still sticks out into the road as it turns uphill; but the crossing beyond the now fenced-in pool, where the bluebell lane ran down into the mill lane, is now a dangerous crossing alive with motors and red lights. The White Ogre's house (which the children were excited to see) is become a petrol station, and most of Short Avenue and the elms between it and the crossing have gone. How I envy those whose precious early scenery has not been exposed to such violent and peculiarly hideous change.*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **August [1955](../_/1955.md)**: \[Notes on his visit to Italy with [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md):\] : "*Venice seemed incredibly, elvishly lovely*";<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup> "*contrary to legend and my belief, Italians ... dislike exaggeration, superlatives, and adjectives of excessive praise. But they seem to answer to colour and poetic expression, if justified.*"<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

- **Late [1963](../_/1963.md) or early [1964](../_/1964.md):** \[Carpenter notes that soon after [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md)'s death, Tolkien "*began to keep a diary, which was something he had not done for many years. In part it was an excuse for using another alphabet that he had invented; he called it his '[New English Alphabet](../N/New_English_Alphabet.md)'.*\]: "*Life is grey and grim. I can get nothing done, between stateness and boredom (confined to quarters), and anxiety and distraction. What am I going to do? Be sucked down into residence in a hotel or old-people's home or club, without books or contacts or talk with men? God help me!*"<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>

- **[1968](../_/1968.md)-[1971](../_/1971.md):** \[Carpenter notes that Tolkien kept a diary "*for a brief time during \[the\] Bournemouth years*".\]<sup id="cite_ref-Biography_2-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Biography-2">[2]</a></sup>
