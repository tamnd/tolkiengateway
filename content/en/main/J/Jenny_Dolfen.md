**Jenny Dolfen** (born [1975](/wiki/1975)) is a German artist and teacher.

## History {#History}

Dolfen was introduced to Tolkien's works via her mother, who was an avid fan.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Her artwork gained notoriety in the early 2000s because it depicted events from [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion), which was not very common at the time.<sup id="cite_ref-tor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tor-2">[2]</a></sup>

She is a regular guest at Tolkien-related events, having spoken at artist panels at [The Return of the Ring](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_Ring), [Tolkien Thing](/wiki/Tolkien_Thing) 2013, and [Tolkien 2019](/wiki/Tolkien_2019).

In 2018, she was diagnosed with an unknown eye disease<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> that has since made her visually disabled. She "works around" her condition by using magnifying glasses and digital canvases.<sup id="cite_ref-tor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tor-2">[2]</a></sup> When asked about her disability's impact on her art, she said: "*\[T\]here have been times over the past four years when I felt that I couldn’t keep creating, but I’ve always found a way to get back again—I think creating is my default mode, and no matter how hard creating may be, not creating always ends up being harder!*"<sup id="cite_ref-tor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tor-2">[2]</a></sup>
