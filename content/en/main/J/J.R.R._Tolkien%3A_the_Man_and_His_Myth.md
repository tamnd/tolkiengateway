<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien: the Man and His Myth</b></i> is a four-volume box set published in [1978](/wiki/1978) containing *[A Guide to Middle-earth](/wiki/A_Guide_to_Middle-earth)* (first edition) by [Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster), *[Master of Middle-earth](/wiki/Master_of_Middle-earth)* by [Paul H. Kocher](/wiki/Paul_H._Kocher), *[Tolkien: A Look Behind The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/Tolkien:_A_Look_Behind_The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* by [Lin Carter](/wiki/Lin_Carter), and [J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography) by [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
