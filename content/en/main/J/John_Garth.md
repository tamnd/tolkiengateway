**John Garth** (born [9 June](../_/9_June.md) [1966](../_/1966.md)), winner of the 2004 [Mythopoeic Society](../M/Mythopoeic_Society.md) Scholarship Award, studied English at [Oxford University](../O/Oxford_University.md) and has since worked as a newspaper journalist in London. A long-standing taste for the works of Tolkien, combined with an interest in the [First World War](../F/First_World_War.md), fueled the five years of research that have gone into *[Tolkien and the Great War](../T/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War.md)* and he has drawn extensively on previously unpublished personal papers as well as Tolkien's service record and other unique military documents. Since 2005, he is a member of the Review Panel of *[Vinyar Tengwar](../V/Vinyar_Tengwar.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
