**John Garth** (born [9 June](/wiki/9_June) [1966](/wiki/1966)), winner of the 2004 [Mythopoeic Society](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society) Scholarship Award, studied English at [Oxford University](/wiki/Oxford_University) and has since worked as a newspaper journalist in London. A long-standing taste for the works of Tolkien, combined with an interest in the [First World War](/wiki/First_World_War), fueled the five years of research that have gone into *[Tolkien and the Great War](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War)* and he has drawn extensively on previously unpublished personal papers as well as Tolkien's service record and other unique military documents. Since 2005, he is a member of the Review Panel of *[Vinyar Tengwar](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
