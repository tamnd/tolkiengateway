**James Branch Cabell** ([14 April](/wiki/14_April) [1879](/wiki/1879) – [5 May](/wiki/5_May) [1958](/wiki/1958)) was an American author of science-fiction and belles lettres.

[Arne Zettersten](/wiki/Arne_Zettersten) remembers sending [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) a newspaper article which suggested that Tolkien had been influenced by Cabell:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Tolkien wrote back to me and denied forcefully that this was true. The next time we met he took up the matter again and maintained that he knew Cabell well, but that he had read only one of his books and that it was &#39;quite boring&#39;.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

[John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff) has proposed that the book, which Tolkien is referring to in Zettersten's reminiscence, is Cabell's <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurgen,_A_Comedy_of_Justice" title="wikipedia:Jurgen, A Comedy of Justice">Jurgen</a></i> ([1919](/wiki/1919)).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The anthology *[The Young Magicians](/wiki/The_Young_Magicians)* ([1969](/wiki/1969)) edited by [Lin Carter](/wiki/Lin_Carter), to which Tolkien contributed with two poems, also includes Cabell's "The Way of Ecben".

In *[Tales Before Tolkien](/wiki/Tales_Before_Tolkien)* ([2003](/wiki/2003)), editor [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson) includes Cabell's short story "The Thin Queen of Elfhame". Anderson writes that "Cabell represents a very different type of fantasy from Tolkien", the former often including irony in his writings.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
