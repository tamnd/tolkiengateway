**José María Miranda Boto** is Professor of Labour Law at the University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain. He is the author of "El Derecho en Tolkien" (Cinca, [2017](../_/2017.md)) and "[Law, Government, and Society in J.R.R. Tolkien's Works](../L/Law,_Government,_and_Society_in_J.R.R._Tolkien's_Works.md)" ([Walking Tree](../W/Walking_Tree.md), [2022](../_/2022.md)), for which he received the [Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Inklings Studies](../M/Mythopoeic_Society.md) in [2024](../_/2024.md). He has edited "Tolkien. Un clásico de nuestros días" (Legendaria, [2024](../_/2024.md)).

Since [2023](../_/2023.md), he has taught at the University of Vienna the course "Law and Literature: Law, Government, and Society in [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s Works". He has given lectures on Tolkien at universities in [Spain](../S/Spain.md), [Germany](../G/Germany.md), [France](../F/France.md) and [Italy](../I/Italy.md), as well as at fan associations in Colombia, Switzerland and the [UK](../U/United_Kingdom.md). He was a speaker at the Celsius 232 festival in Avilés. In [2023](../_/2023.md) he directed the summer course of the University of Santiago de Compostela "Tolkien. Un clásico dos nosos días", followed in [2026](../_/2026.md) by "Tolkien. Un clásico dos nosos días (II). 49 anos do Silmarillion".
