**José María Miranda Boto** is Professor of Labour Law at the University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain. He is the author of "El Derecho en Tolkien" (Cinca, [2017](/wiki/2017)) and "[Law, Government, and Society in J.R.R. Tolkien's Works](/wiki/Law,_Government,_and_Society_in_J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Works)" ([Walking Tree](/wiki/Walking_Tree), [2022](/wiki/2022)), for which he received the [Mythopoeic Scholarship Award in Inklings Studies](/wiki/Mythopoeic_Society "Mythopoeic Society") in [2024](/wiki/2024). He has edited "Tolkien. Un clásico de nuestros días" (Legendaria, [2024](/wiki/2024)).

Since [2023](/wiki/2023), he has taught at the University of Vienna the course "Law and Literature: Law, Government, and Society in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s Works". He has given lectures on Tolkien at universities in [Spain](/wiki/Spain), [Germany](/wiki/Germany), [France](/wiki/France) and [Italy](/wiki/Italy), as well as at fan associations in Colombia, Switzerland and the [UK](/wiki/United_Kingdom "United Kingdom"). He was a speaker at the Celsius 232 festival in Avilés. In [2023](/wiki/2023) he directed the summer course of the University of Santiago de Compostela "Tolkien. Un clásico dos nosos días", followed in [2026](/wiki/2026) by "Tolkien. Un clásico dos nosos días (II). 49 anos do Silmarillion".
