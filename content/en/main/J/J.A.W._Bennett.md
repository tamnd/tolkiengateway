**Jack Arthur Walter Bennett** ([28 February](../_/28_February.md) [1911](../_/1911.md)– [29 January](../_/29_January.md) [1981](../_/1981.md)) was a New Zealand-born literary scholar.

He is best known as a scholar of Middle English literature, and as a fellow [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He was editor of the journal *Medium Ævum* from 1957 to 1981, having earlier assisted his predecessor, Charles Talbut Onions, and was a colleague of [C. S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md) at Magdalen College, Oxford. In 1964 he succeeded Lewis as Professor of Medieval and Renaissance English at Cambridge University.
