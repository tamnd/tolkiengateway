**Jack Arthur Walter Bennett** ([28 February](/wiki/28_February) [1911](/wiki/1911)– [29 January](/wiki/29_January) [1981](/wiki/1981)) was a New Zealand-born literary scholar.

He is best known as a scholar of Middle English literature, and as a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He was editor of the journal *Medium Ævum* from 1957 to 1981, having earlier assisted his predecessor, Charles Talbut Onions, and was a colleague of [C. S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis "C.S. Lewis") at Magdalen College, Oxford. In 1964 he succeeded Lewis as Professor of Medieval and Renaissance English at Cambridge University.
