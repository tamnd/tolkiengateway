**James Dunning** is an American Tolkien artist. Dunning's artwork has been featured in the [Mark T. Hooker](/wiki/Mark_T._Hooker) publications *[A Tolkienian Mathomium](/wiki/A_Tolkienian_Mathomium)*, *[The Hobbitonian Anthology](/wiki/The_Hobbitonian_Anthology)*, *[Tolkien and Welsh](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Welsh)*, and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Tolkienn%C3%A6um&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Tolkiennæum (page does not exist)">The Tolkiennæum</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> as well as the *2011 Beyond Bree Tolkien Calendar*. In 2011, Dunning published a book, *The Bright Lady and the Astral Wind*, narrating an encounter with a Galadriel in contemporary [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-TolkienLibrary_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TolkienLibrary-2">[2]</a></sup>
