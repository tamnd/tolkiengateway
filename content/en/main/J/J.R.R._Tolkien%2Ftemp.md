Professor Dr. **John Ronald Reuel Tolkien** <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBE" title="wikipedia:CBE">CBE</a> ([1892](../_/1892.md) - [1973](../_/1973.md)) is the subject of [Tolkien Gateway](../../project/A/About.md). His life, his works, his family: everything about the man whom fans have named affectionately **the Professor** can be found here.

Besides creating [Arda](../A/Arda.md), the [Little Kingdom](../L/Little_Kingdom.md), and many other wonderful worlds, Tolkien's reading of [Beowulf](../B/Beowulf%253A_The_Monsters_and_the_Critics.md) was groundbreaking, and stands as *the* interpretation of the epic poem to this day.<sup id="cite_ref-FaHPre_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FaHPre-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Family History {#Family_History}

#### Tolkien {#Tolkien}

Since the [Tolkien family](../T/Tolkien_Family.md)'s move from Saxony several generations earlier,<sup id="cite_ref-L165_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L165-3">[3]</a></sup> much had changed. The little fortune the family had - they were piano builders - was gone when [John Benjamin Tolkien](John_Benjamin_Tolkien.md) went bankrupt in 1877.<sup id="cite_ref-LonGaz_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LonGaz-4">[4]</a></sup> His son [Arthur](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) worked at a [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md) office of Lloyds Bank, but there was little chance of promotion.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio910_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio910-5">[5]</a></sup> A golden opportunity came when gold and diamonds were discovered in [South Africa](../S/South_Africa.md); bank clerks were in high demand to battle the growing fraud and corruption.<sup id="cite_ref-Birzer_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Birzer-6">[6]</a></sup> Arthur, who had been seeing [Mabel Suffield](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md) for some time, moved to Bloemfontein. Mabel came several years later, in 1891.<sup id="cite_ref-Bio910_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bio910-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Names {#Names}

#### John {#John}

The name *John* was a custom in the [Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) family. It was usually given to the eldest son of the eldest son. Tolkien's father, [Arthur](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md), was the first son of [John Benjamin](John_Benjamin_Tolkien.md)'s second marriage; the first son of his first marriage, John, died with only three daughters. As of that moment, the name *John* went to Arthur's descendants.<sup id="cite_ref-L309_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L309-7">[7]</a></sup> It passed to Tolkien's first son, [John](John_Tolkien.md), who became a priest and left no children,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> so the name *John* has effectively lost its hereditary signififance.

The name, though Romish in nature, had at first no religious connotations. However, because his birthday ([January 3](../_/3_January.md)) was the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octave_(liturgical)" title="wikipedia:Octave (liturgical)">Octave</a> of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_the_Evangelist" title="wikipedia:John the Evangelist">John the Evangelist</a>, Tolkien chose him as his patron.<sup id="cite_ref-L309_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L309-7">[7]</a></sup>

#### Ronald {#Ronald}

Arthur Tolkien's first choice for his eldest son's second child was Benjamin, after his father. Tolkien's mother, [Mabel](../M/Mabel_Suffield.md), who was confident she would receive a daughter, chose *Rosalind*. This would become *Ronald* when the child turned out to be a boy.<sup id="cite_ref-L309_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L309-7">[7]</a></sup> Tolkien often used this name as his first name; most of his letters to [Edith](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md) were signed *Ronald*, or simply *R.*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

#### Reuel {#Reuel}

Despite its <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raguel_(name)" title="wikipedia:Raguel (name)">obvious biblical connotations</a>, the name *Reuel* does not come from any of the people mentioned in the Bible; instead, it was the surname of a friend of John Benjamin.<sup id="cite_ref-L309_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L309-7">[7]</a></sup> He gave it to his son Arthur, and both his sons, John and [Hilary](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) had it. Even most their male children and grandchildren have it. Though this family friend was never identified, Tolkien recalled the name being of Norman origin.<sup id="cite_ref-L309_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L309-7">[7]</a></sup> In this light, it might be an early anglification of the surname *Rouel*, which means "(maker of) small wheels/circular objects".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> The "biblical" *Reuel* means "Friend of God".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
