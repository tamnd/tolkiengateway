**Jed Brophy** (b. [October 29](/wiki/29_October "29 October"), [1963](/wiki/1963)) is a New Zealand actor. Often cast by [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) in his works, he is mainly known for portraying [Nori](/wiki/Nori) in [*The Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"). He also played many roles in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), though he was only credited for the second film. His children, [Sadwyn](/wiki/Sadwyn_Brophy "Sadwyn Brophy") and Riley, also appeared in the films; the former as [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion) and the latter as a Hobbit child.

In June [2007](/wiki/2007), he and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](/wiki/2008).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
