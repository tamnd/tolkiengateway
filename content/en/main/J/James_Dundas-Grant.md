**James Dundas-Grant** ([1896](../_/1896.md)–[1985](../_/1985.md)) was a fellow [Inkling](../I/Inklings.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md). He was the commander of the Oxford University naval division, the cadets that followed classes at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).

Dundas-Grant was the son of British ear, nose, and throat surgeon <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Dundas-Grant" title="wikipedia:James Dundas-Grant">Sir James Dundas-Grant</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In his memoir, "[From an 'Outsider'](../F/From_an_%2522Outsider%2522.md)" (1979), Dundas-Grant shared reminiscences of [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md).
