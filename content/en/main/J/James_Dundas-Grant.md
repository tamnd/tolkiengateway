**James Dundas-Grant** ([1896](/wiki/1896)–[1985](/wiki/1985)) was a fellow [Inkling](/wiki/Inklings "Inklings") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). He was the commander of the Oxford University naval division, the cadets that followed classes at the [University of Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

Dundas-Grant was the son of British ear, nose, and throat surgeon <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Dundas-Grant" title="wikipedia:James Dundas-Grant">Sir James Dundas-Grant</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In his memoir, "[From an 'Outsider'](/wiki/From_an_%22Outsider%22 "From an \"Outsider\"")" (1979), Dundas-Grant shared reminiscences of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).
