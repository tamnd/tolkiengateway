<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien: The Shores of Middle-earth</b></i> is a 1981 book which investigates the sources of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
