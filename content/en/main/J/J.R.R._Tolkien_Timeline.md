This article intends to list the main events of the life of [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in a chronological order.

## [1892](../_/1892.md) {#1892}

- [3 January](../_/3_January.md): J.R.R. Tolkien is born in [Bloemfontein](../B/Bloemfontein.md), [Orange Free State](../O/Orange_Free_State.md) (modern South Africa).
- [31 January](../_/31_January.md): Tolkien is baptized at the Anglican cathedral in Bloemfontein.

## [1894](../_/1894.md) {#1894}

- [17 February](../_/17_February.md): Tolkien's younger brother [Hilary](../H/Hilary_Tolkien.md) is born.

## [1895](../_/1895.md) {#1895}

- [April](../A/April.md): Tolkien's mother [Mabel](../M/Mabel_Tolkien.md) moves with her sons from South Africa to [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), England.

## [1896](../_/1896.md) {#1896}

- [15 February](../_/15_February.md): Tolkien's father [Arthur Tolkien](../A/Arthur_Tolkien.md) dies of rheumatic fever.
- Summer: Tolkien's mother moves with her sons to [Sarehole](../S/Sarehole.md).

## [1900](../_/1900.md) {#1900}

- June: Tolkien's mother is received into the Catholic Church.
- September: Tolkien begins attending [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md).

## [1902](../_/1902.md) {#1902}

- Tolkien begins attending St. Philip's School.
- Father [Francis Morgan](../F/Francis_Morgan.md) of the [Birmingham Oratory](../B/Birmingham_Oratory.md) becomes a family friend of the Tolkiens.

## [1903](../_/1903.md) {#1903}

- [January](January.md) : Tolkien returns to King Edward's School, having won a Foundation Scholarship.

## [1904](../_/1904.md) {#1904}

- [April](../A/April.md): Mabel Tolkien is hospitalized with diabetes. Tolkien is sent to live with his aunt [Jane Neave](Jane_Neave.md).
- [June](June.md): Mabel Tolkien partially recovers and goes, with her sons, to Rednal in Worcestershire to convalesce.
- [14 November](../_/14_November.md): Tolkien's mother dies after a relapse.

## [1905](../_/1905.md) {#1905}

- Tolkien and his brother go to live with their aunt Beatrice Suffield.
- Autumn: Tolkien meets his friend [Christopher Wiseman](../C/Christopher_Wiseman.md) at King Edward's.

## [1908](../_/1908.md) {#1908}

- Beginning of the year: Tolkien and Hilary move to the home of Louis Faulkner at [37 Duchess Road](../_/37_Duchess_Road.md) in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md), where Tolkien meets [Edith Bratt](../E/Edith_Bratt.md).

## [1909](../_/1909.md) {#1909}

- Summer: Tolkien and Edith Bratt fall in love.
- [December](../D/December.md): Father [Francis Morgan](../F/Francis_Morgan.md) learns of Tolkien and Edith's relationship and demands that it cease. Tolkien fails to win a University scholarship at [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).

## [1910](../_/1910.md) {#1910}

- [January](January.md): Tolkien and his brother move into the home of Thomas Macsherry at 4 Highfield Road.
- [21 January](../_/21_January.md): Tolkien and Edith have a clandestine meeting, which Father Francis learns of. He forbids Tolkien from any contact with Edith until he turns 21.
- [December](../D/December.md): Tolkien wins a scholarship to Exeter College, Oxford.

## [1911](../_/1911.md) {#1911}

- Summer: Tolkien and several friends form the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md)
- [October](../O/October.md): Tolkien begins studying at Exeter College, [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).

## [1915](../_/1915.md) {#1915}

- Tolkien graduates Oxford University with first-class honours.
- [15 July](../_/15_July.md): Tolkien is commissioned as second lieutenant in the Lancashire Fusiliers

## [1916](../_/1916.md) {#1916}

- [22 March](../_/22_March.md): Tolkien marries [Edith Bratt.](../E/Edith_Tolkien.md)

## [1917](../_/1917.md) {#1917}

- [16 November](../_/16_November.md): Birth of Tolkien's oldest son [John](John_Tolkien.md).

## [1920](../_/1920.md) {#1920}

- Autumn: Tolkien becomes a reader in English language at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md).

- [22 October](../_/22_October.md): Birth of Tolkien's second son [Michael](../M/Michael_Tolkien.md).
- [November](../N/November.md): Tolkien leaves the army retaining his rank of lieutenant.

## [1924](../_/1924.md) {#1924}

- [21 November](../_/21_November.md): Birth of Tolkien's third son [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md).

## [1925](../_/1925.md) {#1925}

- Return to Oxford as [Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon](../R/Rawlinson_and_Bosworth_Professor_of_Anglo-Saxon.md) and then Merton Professor of English Language and Literature

## [1929](../_/1929.md) {#1929}

- [18 June](../_/18_June.md): Birth of Tolkien's daughter [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md).

## [1937](../_/1937.md) {#1937}

- [21 September](../_/21_September.md): Publication of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*.

## [1954](../_/1954.md) {#1954}

- [29 July](../_/29_July.md): Publication of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
- [11 November](../_/11_November.md): Publication of *[The Two Towers](../T/The_Two_Towers.md)*.

## [1955](../_/1955.md) {#1955}

- [20 October](../_/20_October.md): Publication of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

## [1959](../_/1959.md) {#1959}

- Tolkien retires from Oxford.

## [1971](../_/1971.md) {#1971}

- [29 November](../_/29_November.md): Tolkien's wife Edith dies at the age of 82.

## [1972](../_/1972.md) {#1972}

- Tolkien is made a Commander of the [Order of the British Empire](../O/Order_of_the_British_Empire.md)
- Tolkien receives an honorary Doctorate of Letters from Oxford University.

## [1973](../_/1973.md) {#1973}

- [2 September](../_/2_September.md): J.R.R. Tolkien dies at the age of 81.
