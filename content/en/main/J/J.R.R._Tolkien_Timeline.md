This article intends to list the main events of the life of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in a chronological order.

## [1892](/wiki/1892) {#1892}

- [3 January](/wiki/3_January): J.R.R. Tolkien is born in [Bloemfontein](/wiki/Bloemfontein), [Orange Free State](/wiki/Orange_Free_State) (modern South Africa).
- [31 January](/wiki/31_January): Tolkien is baptized at the Anglican cathedral in Bloemfontein.

## [1894](/wiki/1894) {#1894}

- [17 February](/wiki/17_February): Tolkien's younger brother [Hilary](/wiki/Hilary_Tolkien "Hilary Tolkien") is born.

## [1895](/wiki/1895) {#1895}

- [April](/wiki/April): Tolkien's mother [Mabel](/wiki/Mabel_Tolkien "Mabel Tolkien") moves with her sons from South Africa to [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham), England.

## [1896](/wiki/1896) {#1896}

- [15 February](/wiki/15_February): Tolkien's father [Arthur Tolkien](/wiki/Arthur_Tolkien) dies of rheumatic fever.
- Summer: Tolkien's mother moves with her sons to [Sarehole](/wiki/Sarehole).

## [1900](/wiki/1900) {#1900}

- June: Tolkien's mother is received into the Catholic Church.
- September: Tolkien begins attending [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School).

## [1902](/wiki/1902) {#1902}

- Tolkien begins attending St. Philip's School.
- Father [Francis Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Morgan) of the [Birmingham Oratory](/wiki/Birmingham_Oratory) becomes a family friend of the Tolkiens.

## [1903](/wiki/1903) {#1903}

- [January](/wiki/January) : Tolkien returns to King Edward's School, having won a Foundation Scholarship.

## [1904](/wiki/1904) {#1904}

- [April](/wiki/April): Mabel Tolkien is hospitalized with diabetes. Tolkien is sent to live with his aunt [Jane Neave](/wiki/Jane_Neave).
- [June](/wiki/June): Mabel Tolkien partially recovers and goes, with her sons, to Rednal in Worcestershire to convalesce.
- [14 November](/wiki/14_November): Tolkien's mother dies after a relapse.

## [1905](/wiki/1905) {#1905}

- Tolkien and his brother go to live with their aunt Beatrice Suffield.
- Autumn: Tolkien meets his friend [Christopher Wiseman](/wiki/Christopher_Wiseman) at King Edward's.

## [1908](/wiki/1908) {#1908}

- Beginning of the year: Tolkien and Hilary move to the home of Louis Faulkner at [37 Duchess Road](/wiki/37_Duchess_Road) in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham), where Tolkien meets [Edith Bratt](/wiki/Edith_Bratt).

## [1909](/wiki/1909) {#1909}

- Summer: Tolkien and Edith Bratt fall in love.
- [December](/wiki/December): Father [Francis Morgan](/wiki/Francis_Morgan) learns of Tolkien and Edith's relationship and demands that it cease. Tolkien fails to win a University scholarship at [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).

## [1910](/wiki/1910) {#1910}

- [January](/wiki/January): Tolkien and his brother move into the home of Thomas Macsherry at 4 Highfield Road.
- [21 January](/wiki/21_January): Tolkien and Edith have a clandestine meeting, which Father Francis learns of. He forbids Tolkien from any contact with Edith until he turns 21.
- [December](/wiki/December): Tolkien wins a scholarship to Exeter College, Oxford.

## [1911](/wiki/1911) {#1911}

- Summer: Tolkien and several friends form the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.)
- [October](/wiki/October): Tolkien begins studying at Exeter College, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford).

## [1915](/wiki/1915) {#1915}

- Tolkien graduates Oxford University with first-class honours.
- [15 July](/wiki/15_July): Tolkien is commissioned as second lieutenant in the Lancashire Fusiliers

## [1916](/wiki/1916) {#1916}

- [22 March](/wiki/22_March): Tolkien marries [Edith Bratt.](/wiki/Edith_Tolkien "Edith Tolkien")

## [1917](/wiki/1917) {#1917}

- [16 November](/wiki/16_November): Birth of Tolkien's oldest son [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien").

## [1920](/wiki/1920) {#1920}

- Autumn: Tolkien becomes a reader in English language at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds).

- [22 October](/wiki/22_October): Birth of Tolkien's second son [Michael](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien").
- [November](/wiki/November): Tolkien leaves the army retaining his rank of lieutenant.

## [1924](/wiki/1924) {#1924}

- [21 November](/wiki/21_November): Birth of Tolkien's third son [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien").

## [1925](/wiki/1925) {#1925}

- Return to Oxford as [Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon](/wiki/Rawlinson_and_Bosworth_Professor_of_Anglo-Saxon) and then Merton Professor of English Language and Literature

## [1929](/wiki/1929) {#1929}

- [18 June](/wiki/18_June): Birth of Tolkien's daughter [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien").

## [1937](/wiki/1937) {#1937}

- [21 September](/wiki/21_September): Publication of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*.

## [1954](/wiki/1954) {#1954}

- [29 July](/wiki/29_July): Publication of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- [11 November](/wiki/11_November): Publication of *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*.

## [1955](/wiki/1955) {#1955}

- [20 October](/wiki/20_October): Publication of *[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*.

## [1959](/wiki/1959) {#1959}

- Tolkien retires from Oxford.

## [1971](/wiki/1971) {#1971}

- [29 November](/wiki/29_November): Tolkien's wife Edith dies at the age of 82.

## [1972](/wiki/1972) {#1972}

- Tolkien is made a Commander of the [Order of the British Empire](/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire)
- Tolkien receives an honorary Doctorate of Letters from Oxford University.

## [1973](/wiki/1973) {#1973}

- [2 September](/wiki/2_September): J.R.R. Tolkien dies at the age of 81.
