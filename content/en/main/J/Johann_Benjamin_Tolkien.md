**Johann** (or **John**) **Benjamin Tolkien** ([June](/wiki/June) [1752](/wiki/1752) – [27 January](/wiki/27_January) [1819](/wiki/1819)) was [George Tolkien](/wiki/George_Tolkien)'s father, and J.R.R. Tolkien's great-great-grandfather. He was born in Danzig in the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth and emigrated to England after 1770. His wife, and George's mother, was [Mary Wall](/wiki/Mary_Wall) ([1746](/wiki/1746) – [16 March](/wiki/16_March) [1837](/wiki/1837)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

John Benjamin Tolkien and his wife Mary are buried at Bunhill Fields in London, in the same grave as the composer <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shrubsole" title="wikipedia:William Shrubsole">William Shrubsole</a>.
