**Johann** (or **John**) **Benjamin Tolkien** ([June](June.md) [1752](../_/1752.md) – [27 January](../_/27_January.md) [1819](../_/1819.md)) was [George Tolkien](../G/George_Tolkien.md)'s father, and J.R.R. Tolkien's great-great-grandfather. He was born in Danzig in the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth and emigrated to England after 1770. His wife, and George's mother, was [Mary Wall](../M/Mary_Wall.md) ([1746](../_/1746.md) – [16 March](../_/16_March.md) [1837](../_/1837.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

John Benjamin Tolkien and his wife Mary are buried at Bunhill Fields in London, in the same grave as the composer <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shrubsole" title="wikipedia:William Shrubsole">William Shrubsole</a>.
