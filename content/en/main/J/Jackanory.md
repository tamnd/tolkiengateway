**Jackanory** is a children's television program on the [BBC](/wiki/BBC), in which stories are read to children. Over the years, many actors provided the part of narrator, including [Brian Blessed](/wiki/Brian_Blessed), [David Buck](/wiki/David_Buck), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Judi_Dench&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Judi Dench (page does not exist)">Judi Dench</a>, [Marian Diamond](/wiki/Marian_Diamond), [Michael Hordern](/wiki/Michael_Hordern), [Martin Jarvis](/wiki/Martin_Jarvis), [Ian McKellen](/wiki/Ian_McKellen) and [Prunella Scales](/wiki/Prunella_Scales). It was re-released on [3 October](/wiki/3_October) [2013](/wiki/2013); the boxset was illustrated by [Jemima Catlin](/wiki/Jemima_Catlin).

## The Hobbit {#The_Hobbit}

### Description {#Description}

The 3,000th programme begins a special presentation in ten parts, with four storytellers, of *The Hobbit*. In the search for dragon-guarded gold, [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) and [13 dwarves](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") encounter [trolls](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls"), [goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs"), [elves](/wiki/Elves "Elves") and giant [spiders](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders") - and many other adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Cast {#Cast}

- Narrator - Jan Francis
- [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) - Bernard Cribbins
- [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) - Maurice Denham
- [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) (many other parts) - David Wood<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Episodes {#Episodes}

The ten episodes were aired on BBC1, on weekdays from 4:25 to 4:40 PM

1. [October 1](/wiki/1_October "1 October"): *An Unexpected Party*
2. [October 2](/wiki/2_October "2 October"): *Over Hill and Under Hill*
3. [October 3](/wiki/3_October "3 October"): *Riddles in the Dark*
4. [October 4](/wiki/4_October "4 October"): *Out of the Frying-Pan into the Fire*
5. [October 5](/wiki/5_October "5 October"): *Flies and Spiders*
6. [October 8](/wiki/8_October "8 October"): *Barrels Out of Bond*
7. [October 9](/wiki/9_October "9 October"): *Inside Information*
8. [October 10](/wiki/10_October "10 October"): *Fire and Water*
9. [October 11](/wiki/11_October "11 October"): *The Clouds Burst*
10. [October 12](/wiki/12_October "12 October"): *The Last Stage*

According to David Wood, [Tolkien Estate](/wiki/Tolkien_Estate) don't allow the release on VHS.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
