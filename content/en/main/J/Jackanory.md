**Jackanory** is a children's television program on the [BBC](../B/BBC.md), in which stories are read to children. Over the years, many actors provided the part of narrator, including [Brian Blessed](../B/Brian_Blessed.md), [David Buck](../D/David_Buck.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Judi_Dench&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Judi Dench (page does not exist)">Judi Dench</a>, [Marian Diamond](../M/Marian_Diamond.md), [Michael Hordern](../M/Michael_Hordern.md), [Martin Jarvis](../M/Martin_Jarvis.md), [Ian McKellen](../I/Ian_McKellen.md) and [Prunella Scales](../P/Prunella_Scales.md). It was re-released on [3 October](../_/3_October.md) [2013](../_/2013.md); the boxset was illustrated by [Jemima Catlin](Jemima_Catlin.md).

## The Hobbit {#The_Hobbit}

### Description {#Description}

The 3,000th programme begins a special presentation in ten parts, with four storytellers, of *The Hobbit*. In the search for dragon-guarded gold, [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [13 dwarves](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) encounter [trolls](../T/Trolls.md), [goblins](../O/Orcs.md), [elves](../E/Elves.md) and giant [spiders](../S/Spiders.md) - and many other adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Cast {#Cast}

- Narrator - Jan Francis
- [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) - Bernard Cribbins
- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) - Maurice Denham
- [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) (many other parts) - David Wood<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Episodes {#Episodes}

The ten episodes were aired on BBC1, on weekdays from 4:25 to 4:40 PM

1. [October 1](../_/1_October.md): *An Unexpected Party*
2. [October 2](../_/2_October.md): *Over Hill and Under Hill*
3. [October 3](../_/3_October.md): *Riddles in the Dark*
4. [October 4](../_/4_October.md): *Out of the Frying-Pan into the Fire*
5. [October 5](../_/5_October.md): *Flies and Spiders*
6. [October 8](../_/8_October.md): *Barrels Out of Bond*
7. [October 9](../_/9_October.md): *Inside Information*
8. [October 10](../_/10_October.md): *Fire and Water*
9. [October 11](../_/11_October.md): *The Clouds Burst*
10. [October 12](../_/12_October.md): *The Last Stage*

According to David Wood, [Tolkien Estate](../T/Tolkien_Estate.md) don't allow the release on VHS.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
