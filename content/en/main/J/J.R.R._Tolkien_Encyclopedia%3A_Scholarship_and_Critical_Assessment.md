The <i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien Encyclopedia: Scholarship and Critical Assessment</b></i> is a massive reference book published in [2006](/wiki/2006), edited by [Michael D.C. Drout](/wiki/Michael_D.C._Drout), co-edited by [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson), [Marjorie Burns](/wiki/Marjorie_Burns), [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger), and [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey).

The book contains 550 articles contributed by a team of 127 Tolkien scholars, covering topics of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s fiction, his academic works, his intellectual and spiritual influences, and his biography.

A paperback edition was released on [20 December](/wiki/20_December) [2013](/wiki/2013).
