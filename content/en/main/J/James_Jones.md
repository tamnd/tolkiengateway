**James Jones** (born in [1927](/wiki/1927)) was a member of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club).

## History {#History}

Jones was a retired schoolmaster, journalist and a playwright. He lived in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) and divided his time between producing plays and his hobby of private printing. Though a very silent man, he assisted the reporter of the Club, [Nicholas Guildford](/wiki/Nicholas_Guildford), with his retentive memory.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
