<i><b>The Middle-Earth Trilogy</b></i>, also known as the *Colossal Trilogy*, is a trilogy of games by [Level 9 Computing](/wiki/Level_9_Computing).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> Its consist the following games:

- [Colossal Adventure](/wiki/Colossal_Adventure)
- [Adventure Quest](/wiki/Adventure_Quest)
- [Dungeon Adventure](/wiki/Dungeon_Adventure)

Later all names were changed and it was published as *The Jewels of Darkness* by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecomsoft" title="wikipedia:Telecomsoft">Rainbird Software</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
