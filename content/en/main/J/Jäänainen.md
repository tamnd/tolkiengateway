**Jäänainen** was an evil [Maia](../M/Maia.md) invented by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md)*. In the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), she was allied with [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), and she fled far north after the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md). Biding her time, she performed smaller evils, igniting feuds or luring men to their death by assuming the form of a beautiful woman. In the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), she dwelt near the Bay of Cracking Ice.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

While her petty evils pleased the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md), like many of the evil spirits of the [Northern Waste](../N/Northern_Waste.md), she was not in any way subservient to him or [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Elves were immune to her charms, but Men and Dwarves were vulnerable.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
