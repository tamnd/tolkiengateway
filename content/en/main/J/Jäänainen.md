**Jäänainen** was an evil [Maia](/wiki/Maia) invented by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) for *[Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing)*. In the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), she was allied with [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), and she fled far north after the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath). Biding her time, she performed smaller evils, igniting feuds or luring men to their death by assuming the form of a beautiful woman. In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), she dwelt near the Bay of Cracking Ice.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

While her petty evils pleased the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king), like many of the evil spirits of the [Northern Waste](/wiki/Northern_Waste), she was not in any way subservient to him or [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Elves were immune to her charms, but Men and Dwarves were vulnerable.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
