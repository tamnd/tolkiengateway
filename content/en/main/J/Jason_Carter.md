**Jason Brian Carter** ([September 23](/wiki/23_September "23 September"), [1960](/wiki/1960)) voiced [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) in *[EA's The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/EA%27s_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* and its expansion, *[The Rise of the Witch-king](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king")*.

He auditioned for the role of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). Carter was stated as trying out for the role but was not what the casting crew was looking for.
