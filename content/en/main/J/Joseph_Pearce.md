**Joseph Pearce** (born [12 February](../_/12_February.md) [1961](../_/1961.md)) is currently co-editor of the <a class="external text" href="http://staustinreview.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">St. Austin Review</a> journal and other magazines.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He is also former Writer-in-Residence and Professor of Literature at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_More_College_of_Liberal_Arts" title="wikipedia:Thomas More College of Liberal Arts">Thomas More College of Liberal Arts</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
