**James D. Allan** (born [1945](../_/1945.md)), more commonly known as **Jim Allan**, is a prolific writer and author especially on the subject of [Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) linguistics. He is primarily known for his [1978](../_/1978.md) book, *[An Introduction to Elvish](../A/An_Introduction_to_Elvish.md)*.
