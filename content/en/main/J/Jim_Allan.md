**James D. Allan** (born [1945](/wiki/1945)), more commonly known as **Jim Allan**, is a prolific writer and author especially on the subject of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") linguistics. He is primarily known for his [1978](/wiki/1978) book, *[An Introduction to Elvish](/wiki/An_Introduction_to_Elvish)*.
