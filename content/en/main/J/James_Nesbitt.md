**James Nesbitt** (born [15 January](/wiki/15_January) [1965](/wiki/1965)) is a Northern Irish actor, best known for playing Adam Williams in the comedy-drama <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_Feet" title="wikipedia:Cold Feet">Cold Feet</a></i> and the starring role in the detective-drama <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murphy%27s_Law_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Murphy&#39;s Law (TV series)">Murphy&#39;s Law</a></i>. He portrayed [Bofur](/wiki/Bofur) in [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").<sup id="cite_ref-ComingSoon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ComingSoon-1">[1]</a></sup>

His daughters are [Mary](/wiki/Mary_Nesbitt "Mary Nesbitt") and [Peggy](/wiki/Peggy_Nesbitt "Peggy Nesbitt") Nesbitt, who play [Bard](/wiki/Bard)'s daughters [Tilda](/wiki/Tilda) and [Sigrid](/wiki/Sigrid) in *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
