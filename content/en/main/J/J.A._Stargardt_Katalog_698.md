<i><b>J.A. Stargardt Katalog 698 5. und 6. Juni 2012</b></i> is an auction catalogue from the German autograph and manuscript dealer J.A. Stargardt.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The catalogue includes the following Tolkien-related lots:

- lot 274 (p. 153): letters from Tolkien to [Hans-Jörg Modlmayr](/wiki/Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr):
    - [Letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr (1 March 1967)](</wiki/Letter_to_Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr_(1_March_1967)>)
    - [Letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr (16 March 1967)](</wiki/Letter_to_Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr_(16_March_1967)>)
    - [Letter to Hans-Jörg Modlmayr (7 December 1967)](</wiki/Letter_to_Hans-J%C3%B6rg_Modlmayr_(7_December_1967)>)
- lot 275 (p. 154): signed and inscribed ("J.R.R. Tolkien. / To Hans Jörg &amp; Hildegard Modlmayr: with gratitude / and / best wishes. March 15th / 1967") copy of *[Tree and Leaf](/wiki/Tree_and_Leaf)* (Allen &amp; Unwin [1964](/wiki/1964)).
