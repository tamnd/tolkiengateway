**John Estill Vickery** ([November 4](/wiki/4_November "4 November"), [1950](/wiki/1950)) is an American actor. Starting on stage in Shakespeare and Broadway, he appeared on television in guest appearances on many shows. He is probably best known for playing <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neroon" title="wikipedia:Neroon">Neroon</a> on <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon_5" title="wikipedia:Babylon 5"><i>Babylon 5</i></a>*.*

## Roles {#Roles}

- [1979](/wiki/1979): [NPR's *The Lord of the Rings*](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1979_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1979 radio series)"): [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"), [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), [The Mouth of Sauron](/wiki/The_Mouth_of_Sauron)
