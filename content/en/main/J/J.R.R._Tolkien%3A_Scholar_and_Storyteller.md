<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien, Scholar and Storyteller: Essays in Memoriam</b></i> is an anthology that was published as a tribute to [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) - both an academic and as an author. 1,560 copies were printed.

It includes Tolkien's [Valedictory Address to the University of Oxford](../V/Valedictory_Address_to_the_University_of_Oxford.md) (pp. 16–32). Also includes:

- *Professor J.R.R. Tolkien: Creator of Hobbits and inventor of a new mythology* - Tolkien's obituary notice, as printed in The Times, 3 September 1973.
- *The Man and the Scholar* - A reminiscence by [S.T.R.O. d'Ardenne](../S/S.T.R.O._d'Ardenne.md).
- *Handlist of the Published Writings of J.R.R. Tolkien* - Compiled by [Humphrey Carpenter](../H/Humphrey_Carpenter.md).
