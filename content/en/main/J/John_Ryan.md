**John Sprott Ryan** ([1929](../_/1929.md) – [3 July](../_/3_July.md) [2022](../_/2022.md))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was a New Zealand born Professor of Folklore and Heritage at the University of New England, USA.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He first joined the university in 1969 as an Associate Professor.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> He had studied under [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in his Masters work at the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In 1992 he was awarded an Honorary Doctorate by the [American Tolkien Society](../A/American_Tolkien_Society.md).

He lectured and had been published in many countries and was a visiting Professor in Folklore at the University of Sheffield in both 1995 and 1999. He also lead part of a Summer School for Tolkien Studies at the University of Otago in 2007 for Pacific Rim countries.
