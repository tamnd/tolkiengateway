<i><b>The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I</b></i> is a video game developed by [Interplay Productions](/wiki/Interplay_Productions), written by [Scott Bennie](/wiki/Scott_Bennie) and [Mark Whittlesey](/wiki/Mark_Whittlesey), whilst the game music was composed by [Charles Deenen](/wiki/Charles_Deenen).<sup id="cite_ref-Giantbomb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Giantbomb-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The game designers were influenced by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises)'s [MERP](/wiki/MERP)<sup id="cite_ref-Polygon_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Polygon-5">[5]</a></sup> and used material from [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi)'s [1978](/wiki/1978) [movie](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)") for cut-scenes.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> The game features include a large open-world, thirty-seven playable characters in the PC game, and a storyline based on [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring). A sequel, *[The Lord of the Rings, Vol. II: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._II:_The_Two_Towers)*, was released in [1993](/wiki/1993). Interplay also released a [version for the SNES](</wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(SNES)> "J.R.R. Tolkien's The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I (SNES)"), which greatly differed from the PC game.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The CD-rom version of the game opens with some cut scenes from [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)")*, while the floppy disk version of the game uses simple animated cut-scenes to tell the story. The player starts as [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) and must first travel to [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland) before crossing the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest) and the [Barrow-downs](/wiki/Barrow-downs) to get to [Bree](/wiki/Bree). In the Barrow-downs the player must escape the [Barrow-wights](/wiki/Barrow-wights) with the help of [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil). Whilst at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) you must collect the cloaks of the Nazgûl that were defeated at the Ford of Bruinen, as well as the pieces of [Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril), both of which can be found in the surrounding lands.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the player escapes the darkness of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) they must search the gifts from [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). When Frodo Baggins is captured in [Dol Guldur](/wiki/Dol_Guldur) the remaining members of the Fellowship must then free Frodo with help from [Radagast the Brown](/wiki/Radagast "Radagast") and the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) before the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) arrives and brings the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer) to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and the fate of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) is lost.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

### PC version {#PC_version}

The player starts as [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and can immediately recruit [Sam Gamgee](/wiki/Sam_Gamgee) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took). Through the course of the game, the other six members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) can be recruited. The player can switch between characters to decide who leads the Fellowship, equip other party members with a range of weapons and armour, distribute skills among the group, cast spells, and perform various skill-based actions.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> Whilst progressing through the main story, the player is able to complete several side adventures and revisit previous locations, during which new characters, quests and objects can be discovered.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Polygon_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Polygon-5">[5]</a></sup> The game also included a day/night system. At night, some enemies are stronger and there is a greater chance of encountering the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl) than during day-time.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

### SNES version {#SNES_version}

The player starts as [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and can obtain the rest of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) (except [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)) as they progress through the main story.

## Characters {#Characters}

There are thirty-seven playable characters in total in the PC version of the game, some of them only available temporarily. Some characters, such as [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan), [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir), and [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel), can be recruited and taken through to the end-game, if desired.

## Development {#Development}

Interplay started developing a fantasy RPG, which was not set in Middle-earth, in the late 1980s. After Interplay obtained the license for creating a video game based on The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit, the game was scrapped and redeveloped as an adaptation of [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Polygon_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Polygon-5">[5]</a></sup>
