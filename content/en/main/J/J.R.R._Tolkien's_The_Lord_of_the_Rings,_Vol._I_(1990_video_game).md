<i><b>The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I</b></i> is a video game developed by [Interplay Productions](../I/Interplay_Productions.md), written by [Scott Bennie](../S/Scott_Bennie.md) and [Mark Whittlesey](../M/Mark_Whittlesey.md), whilst the game music was composed by [Charles Deenen](../C/Charles_Deenen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Giantbomb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Giantbomb-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The game designers were influenced by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md)'s [MERP](../M/MERP.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Polygon_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Polygon-5">[5]</a></sup> and used material from [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md)'s [1978](../_/1978.md) [movie](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>) for cut-scenes.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> The game features include a large open-world, thirty-seven playable characters in the PC game, and a storyline based on [The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md). A sequel, *[The Lord of the Rings, Vol. II: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._II%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*, was released in [1993](../_/1993.md). Interplay also released a [version for the SNES](<J.R.R._Tolkien's_The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(SNES).md>), which greatly differed from the PC game.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The CD-rom version of the game opens with some cut scenes from [Ralph Bakshi](../R/Ralph_Bakshi.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>)*, while the floppy disk version of the game uses simple animated cut-scenes to tell the story. The player starts as [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) at [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) and must first travel to [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md) before crossing the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md) and the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) to get to [Bree](../B/Bree.md). In the Barrow-downs the player must escape the [Barrow-wights](../B/Barrow-wights.md) with the help of [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md). Whilst at [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) you must collect the cloaks of the Nazgûl that were defeated at the Ford of Bruinen, as well as the pieces of [Andúril](../A/Andúril.md), both of which can be found in the surrounding lands.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the player escapes the darkness of [Moria](../M/Moria.md) they must search the gifts from [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md). When Frodo Baggins is captured in [Dol Guldur](../D/Dol_Guldur.md) the remaining members of the Fellowship must then free Frodo with help from [Radagast the Brown](../R/Radagast.md) and the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) before the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) arrives and brings the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md) to [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) and the fate of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) is lost.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

### PC version {#PC_version}

The player starts as [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and can immediately recruit [Sam Gamgee](../S/Sam_Gamgee.md) and [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md). Through the course of the game, the other six members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) can be recruited. The player can switch between characters to decide who leads the Fellowship, equip other party members with a range of weapons and armour, distribute skills among the group, cast spells, and perform various skill-based actions.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> Whilst progressing through the main story, the player is able to complete several side adventures and revisit previous locations, during which new characters, quests and objects can be discovered.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Polygon_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Polygon-5">[5]</a></sup> The game also included a day/night system. At night, some enemies are stronger and there is a greater chance of encountering the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md) than during day-time.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

### SNES version {#SNES_version}

The player starts as [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and can obtain the rest of the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) (except [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md)) as they progress through the main story.

## Characters {#Characters}

There are thirty-seven playable characters in total in the PC version of the game, some of them only available temporarily. Some characters, such as [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md), [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md), and [Glorfindel](../G/Glorfindel.md), can be recruited and taken through to the end-game, if desired.

## Development {#Development}

Interplay started developing a fantasy RPG, which was not set in Middle-earth, in the late 1980s. After Interplay obtained the license for creating a video game based on The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit, the game was scrapped and redeveloped as an adaptation of [The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Polygon_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Polygon-5">[5]</a></sup>
