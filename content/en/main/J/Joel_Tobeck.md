**Joel Tobeck** is a Kiwi actor who portrayed [Guritz](../G/Guritz.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*. His scenes were filmed for the pickups for the film<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in 2003.<sup id="cite_ref-Morphin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Morphin-2">[2]</a></sup>

The prosthetics for Guritz took five hours to apply and Tobeck became good friends with Make-up artist [Gino Acevedo](../G/Gino_Acevedo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Morphin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Morphin-2">[2]</a></sup>

It was initially reported that Tobeck would portray [Sméagol](../G/Gollum.md), prior to his transformation,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> though it was reported that he was no longer being considered. It was decided [Andy Serkis](../A/Andy_Serkis.md) would play him both before and after his transformation.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
