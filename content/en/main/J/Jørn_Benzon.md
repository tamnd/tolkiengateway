**Jørn Benzon** (born [July 12](/wiki/12_July "12 July") [1973](/wiki/1973) in Svendborg, Denmark<sup id="cite_ref-Who_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Who-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a Danish actor who played [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_L%C3%B3rien)> "Rúmil (elf of Lórien)") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> An [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") Archer at [the Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg "Battle of the Hornburg") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)* and an [Elf](/wiki/Elves_of_Rivendell "Elves of Rivendell") accompanying [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) to [the Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens "Grey Havens") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-4">[4]</a></sup> His brother, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Jarl_Benzon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Jarl Benzon (page does not exist)">Jarl Benzon</a>, played [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel).<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-4">[4]</a></sup>
