**Jane Chance Nitzsche** is Andrew W. Mellon Distinguished Professor Emerita of English, <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University" title="wikipedia:Rice University">Rice University</a> (since 13 September 2013). Her research interests center on modern medievalism ([J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in particular), mythology, literature of the Middle Ages, and medieval women writers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
