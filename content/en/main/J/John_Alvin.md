**John Alvin** ([November 24](../_/24_November.md), [1948](../_/1948.md) - [February 6](../_/6_February.md), [2008](../_/2008.md)) was a professional artist. In his long career, he has made many posters and promotional art for films such as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars" title="wikipedia:Star Wars">Star Wars</a></i>, various [Disney](../D/Disney.md) productions, as well as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner" title="wikipedia:Blade Runner">Blade Runner</a></i> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looney_Toons" title="wikipedia:Looney Toons">Looney Toons</a>. He was hired by [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) to create promotional art for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>).
