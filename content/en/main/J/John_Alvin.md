**John Alvin** ([November 24](/wiki/24_November "24 November"), [1948](/wiki/1948) - [February 6](/wiki/6_February "6 February"), [2008](/wiki/2008)) was a professional artist. In his long career, he has made many posters and promotional art for films such as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars" title="wikipedia:Star Wars">Star Wars</a></i>, various [Disney](/wiki/Disney) productions, as well as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner" title="wikipedia:Blade Runner">Blade Runner</a></i> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Looney_Toons" title="wikipedia:Looney Toons">Looney Toons</a>. He was hired by [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) to create promotional art for [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
