**John Barrington Wain** ([14 March](/wiki/14_March), [1925](/wiki/1925) – [24 May](/wiki/24_May), [1994](/wiki/1994)) was an English poet, novelist, and critic, associated with the literary group The Movement. He was a student of [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), and briefly associated with the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings), but did not join them.
