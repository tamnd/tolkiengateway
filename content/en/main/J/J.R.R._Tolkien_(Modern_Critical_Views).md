<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien (Modern Critical Views)</b></i> or <i><b>Modern Critical Views: J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> is a book published in [2000](../_/2000.md), edited by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Bloom" title="wikipedia:Harold Bloom">Harold Bloom</a>. It is a compilation of several essays and book chapters relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), all of them already published.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p> Explores the author&#39;s literary work through critical essays that examine such themes as the Middle-Earth concept, mythology, and the relationship between literature and language.
</p>
</blockquote>

## Second Edition {#Second_Edition}

A new edition of the book was published in [2008](../_/2008.md), titled <i><b>Bloom&#39;s Modern Critical Views: J.R.R. Tolkien (New Edition)</b></i>. Its contents differ from the first edition, having a whole new selection of previously published material.
