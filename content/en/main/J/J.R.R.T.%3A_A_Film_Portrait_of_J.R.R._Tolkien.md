<i><b>J.R.R.T.: A Portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> is a [1992](../_/1992.md) documentary, narrated by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench" title="wikipedia:Judi Dench">Judi Dench</a>, and produced Landseer Productions to celebrate the centenary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s birth. It features archive footage and audio recordings of J.R.R. Tolkien, and interviews with three of his children [Priscilla](../P/Priscilla_Tolkien.md), [John](John_Tolkien.md), and [Christopher](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md). It also includes interviews with [Baillie Tolkien](../B/Baillie_Tolkien.md), [Robert Murray](../R/Robert_Murray.md), [Queen Margrethe II of Denmark](../M/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark.md), [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md), [Tom Shippey](../T/Tom_Shippey.md), and [Verlyn Flieger](../V/Verlyn_Flieger.md).

## Release {#Release}

The original [1992](../_/1992.md) release of the documentary had a runtime of around an hour. In [1996](../_/1996.md), a videocassete version of the documentary was released under the title *"J.R.R.T.: A Film Portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien"* and had a runtime of almost 110 minutes. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
