<i><b>J.R.R.T.: A Portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien</b></i> is a [1992](/wiki/1992) documentary, narrated by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench" title="wikipedia:Judi Dench">Judi Dench</a>, and produced Landseer Productions to celebrate the centenary of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s birth. It features archive footage and audio recordings of J.R.R. Tolkien, and interviews with three of his children [Priscilla](/wiki/Priscilla_Tolkien "Priscilla Tolkien"), [John](/wiki/John_Tolkien "John Tolkien"), and [Christopher](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien"). It also includes interviews with [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien), [Robert Murray](/wiki/Robert_Murray), [Queen Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark "Margrethe II of Denmark"), [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin), [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey), and [Verlyn Flieger](/wiki/Verlyn_Flieger).

## Release {#Release}

The original [1992](/wiki/1992) release of the documentary had a runtime of around an hour. In [1996](/wiki/1996), a videocassete version of the documentary was released under the title *"J.R.R.T.: A Film Portrait of J.R.R. Tolkien"* and had a runtime of almost 110 minutes. <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
