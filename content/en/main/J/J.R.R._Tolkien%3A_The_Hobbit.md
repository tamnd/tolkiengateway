<i><b>J.R.R. Tolkien: The Hobbit: Drawings, Watercolors, and Manuscripts</b></i> is an exhibition catalogue which reproduces a variety of illustrations by [J.R.R. Tolkien](J.R.R._Tolkien.md) for *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* - both finished pieces and sketches. Less well known items include the original draft of the dustwrapper artwork and a manuscript page showing a "Map of the Upper River and Mirkwood".

Also includes a letter to G.E. Selby and two essays: 'J.R.R. Tolkien: Pictures Fit for an Exhibition' by Curtis L. Carter and 'The Expectant Landscape: J.R.R. Tolkien's Illustrations for The Hobbit' by Richard Schindler.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
