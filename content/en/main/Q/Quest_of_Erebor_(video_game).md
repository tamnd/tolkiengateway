**Quest of Erebor** is a Text-Adventure by [Sam Ruby](../S/Sam_Ruby.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In the game, the player needs to help [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) and [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) in their adventures east of the [Great River](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> It is part six of [Eamon](<../E/Eamon_(video_game).md>)'s <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(Eamon_series)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings (Eamon series) (page does not exist)">The Lord of the Rings</a></i> series.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>
