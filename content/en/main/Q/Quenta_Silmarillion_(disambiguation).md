**Quenta Silmarillion** can refer to:

- *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, the book edited by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md), published in [1977](../_/1977.md).
    - <i><b><a href="Quenta_Silmarillion.md" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></b></i>: **The History of the Silmarils**, third and main part of this book.

- *[The Earliest 'Silmarillion'](../T/The_Earliest_'Silmarillion'.md)* or *Sketch of the Mythology*, the second chapter of *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](../T/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth.md)*.
- *[The Quenta](../T/The_Quenta.md)* or *Quenta Noldorinwa*, the third chapter of *The Shaping of Middle-earth*.
- *[Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)](<Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road).md>)*, the ninth chapter of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](../T/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings.md)*.
- [The Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](../T/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion.md), the third part of *[Morgoth's Ring](../M/Morgoth's_Ring.md)*.
- The Later *Quenta Silmarillion*, the second part of *[The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md)*.
