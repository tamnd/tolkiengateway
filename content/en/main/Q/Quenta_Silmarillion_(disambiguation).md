**Quenta Silmarillion** can refer to:

- *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the book edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), published in [1977](/wiki/1977).
    - <i><b><a href="/wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion" title="Quenta Silmarillion">Quenta Silmarillion</a></b></i>: **The History of the Silmarils**, third and main part of this book.

- *[The Earliest 'Silmarillion'](/wiki/The_Earliest_%27Silmarillion%27)* or *Sketch of the Mythology*, the second chapter of *[The Shaping of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Shaping_of_Middle-earth)*.
- *[The Quenta](/wiki/The_Quenta)* or *Quenta Noldorinwa*, the third chapter of *The Shaping of Middle-earth*.
- *[Quenta Silmarillion (Lost Road)](</wiki/Quenta_Silmarillion_(Lost_Road)>)*, the ninth chapter of *[The Lost Road and Other Writings](/wiki/The_Lost_Road_and_Other_Writings)*.
- [The Later *Quenta Silmarillion*](/wiki/The_Later_Quenta_Silmarillion "The Later Quenta Silmarillion"), the third part of *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*.
- The Later *Quenta Silmarillion*, the second part of *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*.
