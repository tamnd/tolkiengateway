**Quinton Hoover** ([16 March](../_/16_March.md) [1964](../_/1964.md) – [20 April](../_/20_April.md) [2013](../_/2013.md)) was an American fantasy artist most known for his work for [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md). In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to make card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

While he worked in various media, for card art he favoured ink, color pencil and watercolor.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
