**Quinton Hoover** ([16 March](/wiki/16_March) [1964](/wiki/1964) – [20 April](/wiki/20_April) [2013](/wiki/2013)) was an American fantasy artist most known for his work for [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering). In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to make card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.

While he worked in various media, for card art he favoured ink, color pencil and watercolor.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
