<i><b>quár</b></i>, or <i><b>quáre</b></i>, is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "fist", as in a hand clenched around a tool or implement.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

In his Appendix to *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) notes the form <i><b>quárë</b></i> ("'fist', often used to mean 'hand'").<sup id="cite_ref-EQS_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EQS-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a late manuscript, the form *quár* is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *[kwāră](/wiki/Kw%C4%81ra "Kwāra")*, deriving from root [KWAR](/wiki/KWAR).<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

In an earlier manuscript, the form *quáre* is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *[kwārē](/wiki/Kw%C4%81ra "Kwāra")*, deriving from root [KWAR](/wiki/KWAR).<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *[pār](/wiki/P%C4%81r "Pār")*

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") seems to have rejected the form *quáre*, which was emended to *quár*.<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the forms *qár* ("hand"; ?pl. *qari*), and *qáre* ("fist"), deriving from root [KWAR](/wiki/KWAR#Other_versions) ("clutching hand, fist").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
