**Quickbeam**, or in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) **Bregalad**, was one of the younger [Ents](/wiki/Ents) of [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) belonging to the people of [Skinbark](/wiki/Skinbark). His name was given because of his uncharacteristic hastiness.

## History {#History}

The [rowan](/wiki/Rowans "Rowans")-trees in his keeping (among them [Carnimírië](/wiki/Carnim%C3%ADri%C3%AB), [Lassemista](/wiki/Lassemista), and [Orofarnë](/wiki/Orofarn%C3%AB)) suffered greatly at the hands of [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), so he was the first ent to make his mind up at the [Entmoot](/wiki/Entmoot). He sheltered, in his [home](/wiki/Ent-houses "Ent-houses"), the two [hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") while the other ents made up their minds at the Entmoot.<sup id="cite_ref-tt482_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tt482-1">[1]</a></sup> He played a great part in the Ents' resulting [attack](/wiki/Battle_of_Isengard "Battle of Isengard") on [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) and stopped Saruman when he tried to escape.<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-2">[2]</a></sup> He was among the Ents guarding Orthanc after the Battle of Isengard.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Quickbeam was considered very hasty or at least "nearest thing among us to a hasty Ent" according to Treebeard. Part of this hastiness was because he had seen first-hand the actions of Saruman as his home was ravaged by Orcs. Despite this, he was joyous, singing as he walked and laughing easily.<sup id="cite_ref-tt482_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tt482-1">[1]</a></sup> He was described by Pippin as gentle but with a fierce hatred towards Saruman for his actions towards his people.<sup id="cite_ref-Flotsam_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flotsam-2">[2]</a></sup>

Quickbeam appeared to be one of the younger ents. His hair was smooth, his lips ruddy and his hair green. He was tall but could bend and sway in the wind like a slender tree. His voice was resonant and higher and clearer than Treebeard's.<sup id="cite_ref-tt482_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tt482-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Quickbeam* was given to him because he was hasty for an Ent.<sup id="cite_ref-tt482_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tt482-1">[1]</a></sup> His Sindarin name, *Bregalad*, means "quick (lively) tree", derived from \**[breg](/wiki/BER%C3%89K "BERÉK")-* having to do with "violence, suddenness" and *[galadh](/wiki/Galadh "Galadh")*, "tree".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Quickbeam (Old English *Cwicbeám*) and Quicken are actual English names of the [rowan](/wiki/Rowans "Rowans") or mountain [ash](/wiki/Ashes "Ashes"), *Sorbus aucuparia.* It was not coincidental that "rowan" is mentioned in Quickbeam's song.<sup id="cite_ref-nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nomen-5">[5]</a></sup>
