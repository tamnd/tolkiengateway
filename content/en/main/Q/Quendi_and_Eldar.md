**Quendi and Eldar** is a long essay by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) dating from c. [1959](/wiki/1959)-[60](/wiki/1960 "1960"), which was published in *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*. *Quendi and Eldar* provides the etymological development of certain words the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) used to name themselves and other peoples, or to refer to individuals in some way.

A portion of the "Appendix D" was omitted from *The War of the Jewels* by Christopher to save space. This was published, with some additional material, in *[Vinyar Tengwar 39](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_39)* in [1998](/wiki/1998), edited by [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter).

## Content {#Content}

- A. The principal linguistic elements concerned
- B. Meanings and use of the various terms applied to the Elves and their varieties in Quenya, Telerin, and Sindarin
- C. The Clan-names, with notes on other names for divisions of the Eldar
    - Appendix A. Elvish names for Men
    - Appendix B. Elvish names for the Dwarves
    - Appendix C. Elvish names for the Orcs
    - Appendix D. \**Kwen*, *Quenya*, and the Elvish (especially Ñoldorin) words for 'Language' (incomplete)
    - Note on the 'Language of the Valar'
- Author's Notes to Quendi and Eldar
- Editorial Notes
- Appendix: [The legend of the Awaking of the Quendi (Cuivienyarna)](/wiki/Cuivienyarna "Cuivienyarna")
