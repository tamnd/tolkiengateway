**Quennar i Onótimo** was a [Loremaster](/wiki/Lambengolmor "Lambengolmor") of [Aman](/wiki/Aman).

## History {#History}

He wrote extensively about the reckoning of time among the [Valar](/wiki/Valar). His great work the *[Yénonótië](/wiki/Y%C3%A9non%C3%B3ti%C3%AB)* was cited by [Pengolodh](/wiki/Pengolodh) in the *[Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman "The Annals of Aman")*, particularly his writing explaining the length of the [Valian year](/wiki/Valian_year) and its relation to the waxing and waning of the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees). Quennar also calculated the duration of Valian years compared to the [solar years](/wiki/Coranar "Coranar").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Quennar was known to have resided in [Tirion](/wiki/Tirion) at the time when both [Vanyar](/wiki/Vanyar) and [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) resided there, at which time he devised the word *[Menel](/wiki/Menel)* ("heavens, firmament").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Quennar* consists of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for 'person', 'elf': <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quen (page does not exist)">quen</a></i>, and a derivative of the root NAR<sup>2</sup> 'tell',<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> yielding the meaning \*"Elf-speaker".

*i* is the article 'the'. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), "The Feanorian Alphabet, Part 1 &amp; Quenya Verb Structure", in [*Parma Eldalamberon* XXII](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_22 "Parma Eldalamberon 22") (edited by [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson) and [Arden R. Smith](/wiki/Arden_R._Smith))<sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:147</sup>

*Onótimo* apparently means "Reckoner", originating from the verb <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Onot-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Onot- (page does not exist)">onot-</a></i> 'count up' and the masculine agental suffix *[-mo](/wiki/-mo)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Thus his full title appears to be "Quennar the Reckoner".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally, "Quennar i Onótimo" was to have been the author of the *Annals of Aman* as a whole, having learned and borrowed much writing from [Rúmil](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_Tirion)> "Rúmil (elf of Tirion)"). Eventually, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") attributed the *Annals* to Rúmil himself, while adding "quoth Pengolodh" to the end of the section ascribed to Quennar. This probably means that Pengolodh quoted Quennar's work in his enlargement of the *Annals*, which presumably took place after the [Exile of the Noldor](/wiki/Exile_of_the_Noldor). Since Quennar's writing discusses the [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun), which did not exist while the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) still lived in Aman, it is most likely the case that in the final version of events as envisioned by Tolkien, Quennar wrote the *[Yénonótië](/wiki/Y%C3%A9non%C3%B3ti%C3%AB)* after the Exile and was thus a Noldo who came to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).
