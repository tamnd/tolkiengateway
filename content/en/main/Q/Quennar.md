**Quennar i Onótimo** was a [Loremaster](../L/Lambengolmor.md) of [Aman](../A/Aman.md).

## History {#History}

He wrote extensively about the reckoning of time among the [Valar](../V/Valar.md). His great work the *[Yénonótië](../Y/Yénonótië.md)* was cited by [Pengolodh](../P/Pengolodh.md) in the *[Annals of Aman](../T/The_Annals_of_Aman.md)*, particularly his writing explaining the length of the [Valian year](../V/Valian_year.md) and its relation to the waxing and waning of the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md). Quennar also calculated the duration of Valian years compared to the [solar years](../C/Coranar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Quennar was known to have resided in [Tirion](../T/Tirion.md) at the time when both [Vanyar](../V/Vanyar.md) and [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) resided there, at which time he devised the word *[Menel](../M/Menel.md)* ("heavens, firmament").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Quennar* consists of the [Quenya](Quenya.md) word for 'person', 'elf': <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Quen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quen (page does not exist)">quen</a></i>, and a derivative of the root NAR<sup>2</sup> 'tell',<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> yielding the meaning \*"Elf-speaker".

*i* is the article 'the'. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), "The Feanorian Alphabet, Part 1 &amp; Quenya Verb Structure", in [*Parma Eldalamberon* XXII](../P/Parma_Eldalamberon_22.md) (edited by [Christopher Gilson](../C/Christopher_Gilson.md) and [Arden R. Smith](../A/Arden_R._Smith.md))<sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:147</sup>

*Onótimo* apparently means "Reckoner", originating from the verb <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Onot-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Onot- (page does not exist)">onot-</a></i> 'count up' and the masculine agental suffix *[-mo](../_/-mo.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Thus his full title appears to be "Quennar the Reckoner".

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Originally, "Quennar i Onótimo" was to have been the author of the *Annals of Aman* as a whole, having learned and borrowed much writing from [Rúmil](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Tirion).md>). Eventually, [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) attributed the *Annals* to Rúmil himself, while adding "quoth Pengolodh" to the end of the section ascribed to Quennar. This probably means that Pengolodh quoted Quennar's work in his enlargement of the *Annals*, which presumably took place after the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md). Since Quennar's writing discusses the [Years of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md), which did not exist while the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) still lived in Aman, it is most likely the case that in the final version of events as envisioned by Tolkien, Quennar wrote the *[Yénonótië](../Y/Yénonótië.md)* after the Exile and was thus a Noldo who came to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).
