<b><i>Quettar</i>: Issue 6</b> was a publication of *[Quettar](Quettar.md)*, the bulletin of [the Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) linguistic and Tengwar group, edited by [Susan Rule](../S/Susan_Rule.md) and including the Tengwar Supplement Issue 3, edited by Steve Pillinger.

## Contents {#Contents}

Presenting plans and ideas to Tolkien Society members and in Tengwar Supplement Issue 3, there is a Dutch Tengwar Mode, made by Eltjo Poort. [Ronald Kyrmse](../R/Ronald_Kyrmse.md) presents his discovery about the numbers of Dwarves runes.
