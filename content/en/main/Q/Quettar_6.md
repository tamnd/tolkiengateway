<b><i>Quettar</i>: Issue 6</b> was a publication of *[Quettar](/wiki/Quettar)*, the bulletin of [the Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society "The Tolkien Society") linguistic and Tengwar group, edited by [Susan Rule](/wiki/Susan_Rule) and including the Tengwar Supplement Issue 3, edited by Steve Pillinger.

## Contents {#Contents}

Presenting plans and ideas to Tolkien Society members and in Tengwar Supplement Issue 3, there is a Dutch Tengwar Mode, made by Eltjo Poort. [Ronald Kyrmse](/wiki/Ronald_Kyrmse) presents his discovery about the numbers of Dwarves runes.
