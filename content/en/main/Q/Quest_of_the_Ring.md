The **Quest of Mount Doom**,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> also known as the **Quest of the Ring-bearer**<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and the **Quest of the Ring**,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> was the quest taken by the members of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) to destroy [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) to rid the evil from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) during the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). It forms the center of the story of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

## History {#History}

Though its necessity was obvious to many beforehand, the Quest was initiated during the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond). [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) appointed eight members to accompany the [ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearers "Ring-bearers") [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) on his difficult journey: [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"). Merry and Pippin were not chosen, but went on their own consent.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> After spending the autumn in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), the Company set off on the arduous journey that lay ahead.

They set off for [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), and attempted to cross the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) at the [Redhorn Pass](/wiki/Redhorn_Pass).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Stopped by snow, the Fellowship sought a different path: the long dark of [Moria](/wiki/Moria).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> There, [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) fell in battle with [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

The remaining eight members of the Fellowship, now led by Aragorn, entered [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), where they spent a month in the care of [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> They departed by swift Elven boats, and headed South over the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

And so they came to [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel) and the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil), where Boromir was slain, and the Fellowship was broken.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Frodo and Sam crossed the river to the east and, after much toil, escaped the hills.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> With the help of [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum), they crossed the [Dead Marshes](/wiki/Dead_Marshes), and came in sight of the [Black Gate](/wiki/Black_Gate).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> Unable to enter [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), they went to the pass of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol), but were divided by the treachery of Gollum. Frodo was stung by [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob) and taken prisoner.<sup id="cite_ref-TTChoices_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTChoices-15">[15]</a></sup> At the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol), he was stripped of his effects, but Sam managed to free him.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> The Ring had, at this stage, been taken by Sam, for safe-keeping.<sup id="cite_ref-TTChoices_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTChoices-15">[15]</a></sup>

Frodo and Sam continued their way, via the [Isenmouthe](/wiki/Carach_Angren "Carach Angren"), to [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom). Gollum appeared one last time to seize the [Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), but fell with it to his death into the fiery chasm of Mount Doom.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>
