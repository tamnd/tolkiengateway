<i><b>quessë</b></i> is a noun meaning "feather" in [Quenya](Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/Q/Quesse.gif.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/2/23/Quesse.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Quessë* is also the name in Quenya of the fourth letter of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the fourth letter of the first grade or [Tyellë](../T/Tyellë.md), which contains "voiceless stops" and it is the first letter in the fourth series of consonants. For written Quenya or [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), the fourth series is used for the labialized *k* or *kw*-series, the *[Quessetéma](Quessetéma.md)*, and *Quessë* represents **KW** (which may be transcribed in English as **QU**). In [Westron](../W/Westron.md) the fourth series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](../C/Calmatéma.md)*, and this letter **K** or "hard" **C'**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Cá</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
