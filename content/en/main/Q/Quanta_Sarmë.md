**Quanta Sarmë** was a spelling of [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) devised by [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) which used full letters for <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=%C3%93m%C3%ABar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ómëar (page does not exist)">vowels</a>, rather than [tehtar](../T/Tehtar.md).

Unlike [Rúmil of Tirion](<../R/Rúmil_(elf_of_Tirion).md>), Fëanor considered vowels as independent sounds and not just "colours" of the consonants; it was his preferred (and perhaps intended) way of writing, however its use is mentioned to be connected only with the [Lambengolmor](../L/Lambengolmor.md), and for special purposes only.

No texts of this original system are known, and its usage and characters are a mystery, but it is said that it was used in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) for other languages, like [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), where the tehta-mode was considered inconvenient.

It is possible (and indeed very likely) that the [Mode of Beleriand](../M/Mode_of_Beleriand.md) was one of those systems originating from the *Quanta*; the letter <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Oss%C3%AB_(tengwa)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ossë (tengwa) (page does not exist)">ossë</a></i> is maybe the only remnant of that (otherwise extinct) system.
