**Quendi** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈkʷendi]</span>, singular **Quendë**, pron. <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈkʷende]</span>, though the plural form was more common), was the name given by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to their own kind while they still dwelt at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen). It is commonly said to mean "speakers; those who speak with voices", being from a time when the Elves had not yet encountered other beings capable of speech.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-qe-d_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qe-d-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Quendë* is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form of the word, in [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) the word had been *kwendē̆*. There it derives from the root *KWENED-*, an elaboration of *KWEN-*, a root for “person”.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Although this root has no direct derivatives that are connected to language, it is probable related to the root *KWE-* "vocal speech".<sup id="cite_ref-qe-d_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-qe-d-2">[2]</a></sup>

After the [Sundering of the Elves](/wiki/Sundering_of_the_Elves), the Quenya word had been replaced by *[Elda](/wiki/Elda)* in common usage but it remained in use as a technical term distinguishing the Elves from the [Ainur](/wiki/Ainur) and in the context of lore.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) the word fell out of use after the sound change *kw &gt; p* due to collisions with the roots *PEN-* "to lack" and *PED-* "slope, slant down".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> In Quenya it became
