**Quest for Mordor** is a game made by someone who calls himself *Morgoth*.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UTM_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTM-2">[2]</a></sup> The player finds an [Elven Ring](/wiki/Rings_of_Power "Rings of Power") at an island near [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), and has to use it against [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth).<sup id="cite_ref-UTM_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UTM-2">[2]</a></sup> It was designed with [Klik &amp; Play](/wiki/Klik_%26_Play).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
