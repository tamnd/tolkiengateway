**Queen** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *tári*, [N.](/wiki/Noldorin "Noldorin") *rhien*) can refer to:

## Arda {#Arda}

- [Queen of Gondor](/wiki/Queen_of_Gondor), a title of the Queens of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)
    - Queen [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel), a famous Queen of Gondor
- [Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor), a title of the spouse of any [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).
    - [Ruling Queen of Númenor](/wiki/Ruling_Queen_of_N%C3%BAmenor), one of the three queens that ruled Númenor in the capacity of a King.

- Queen of the Valar (Q. *Valatári*), one of the seven [Valier](/wiki/Valier).
    - [Queen of the Stars or Star-queen, Q. *Elentári*, S. *Elbereth*](/wiki/Varda#Names_and_epithets "Varda"), titles of [Varda](/wiki/Varda).

## Real-world {#Real-world}

- [Margrethe II of Denmark](/wiki/Margrethe_II_of_Denmark), the queen regnant of Denmark and illustrator of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
