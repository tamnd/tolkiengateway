- [Queen of Númenor](Queen_of_Númenor.md), a title of the spouse of any [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).
    - [Ruling Queen of Númenor](../R/Ruling_Queen_of_Númenor.md), one of the three queens that ruled Númenor in the capacity of a King.

- Queen of the Valar (Q. *Valatári*), one of the seven [Valier](../V/Valier.md).
