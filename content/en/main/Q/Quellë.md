**Quellë** was the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the season of "fading", or late autumn, also called [Lasse-lanta](/wiki/Lasse-lanta) or "leaf-fall". In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), this season was called [Firith](/wiki/Firith); or [Narbeleth](/wiki/Narbeleth), which meant "sun-waning". In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell) it was the fourth of the six "seasons", and corresponded more or less to the 54 days between [7 October](/wiki/7_October) and [30 November](/wiki/30_November) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
