**Quellë** was the [Quenya](Quenya.md) name for the season of "fading", or late autumn, also called [Lasse-lanta](../L/Lasse-lanta.md) or "leaf-fall". In [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), this season was called [Firith](../F/Firith.md); or [Narbeleth](../N/Narbeleth.md), which meant "sun-waning". In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](../R/Reckoning_of_Rivendell.md) it was the fourth of the six "seasons", and corresponded more or less to the 54 days between [7 October](../_/7_October.md) and [30 November](../_/30_November.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
