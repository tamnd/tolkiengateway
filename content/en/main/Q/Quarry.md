**Quarry** was a location in the northern [Eastfarthing](/wiki/Eastfarthing), to the south of the [hills of Scary](/wiki/Hills_of_Scary "Hills of Scary") and between [Scary](/wiki/Scary) and [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Quarry could have been simply a stone-quarry<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but possibly it could also have been a small [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbit) settlement or a village<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> as well.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/quarry#Etymology_1" title="wiktionary:quarry">quarry</a> is derived from [Latin](/wiki/Latin) *quarreria* through French.

[David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) noted that the usage of a Latin-French word as a place-name is peculiar, in contrast to all other placenames which have [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) and/or [Celtic](/wiki/Celtic) etymologies; he suggests that the name perhaps represents a late [Westron](/wiki/Westron) word, and not an original [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) place-name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<b>2007:<i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Quarry is a stone quarry in the Shire, in the town Scary, currently overtaken by spiders. There is also a cave which is filled with them.</dd></dl>
