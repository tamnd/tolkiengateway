<i><b>Quest for the Ring</b></i> was a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Broadcasting_Company" title="wikipedia:Fox Broadcasting Company">Fox</a> TV special, aired on [November 29](/wiki/29_November "29 November"), [2001](/wiki/2001) in anticipation of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The 21-minute documentary chronicles the production of the films, as well as introducing its viewers to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). It features interviews with cast members, as well as footage from *The Fellowship of the Ring* and *[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers "The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers")*. Several clips that did not make it into the movie nor the extended edition, such as the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) entering [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), are shown as well. As a bonus at the end, it included a sneak preview of the scene *[Balin's Tomb](</wiki/Balin%27s_Tomb_(scene)> "Balin's Tomb (scene)")*, up until the appearance of the cave troll.
