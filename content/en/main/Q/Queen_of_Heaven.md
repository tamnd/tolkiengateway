**Queen of Heaven** (probably a provisional title) is a trilingual prayer to [Varda](/wiki/Varda) composed by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), currently [unpublished](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material"). It was presented by [Christopher Gilson](/wiki/Christopher_Gilson) at the [Omentielva Nertea](/wiki/Omentielva "Omentielva").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It consists on a prayer to Elbereth translated into [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Latin](/wiki/Latin), which resembles [traditional prayers](/wiki/Christianity "Christianity") to the [Virgin Mary](/wiki/Virgin_Mary).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
