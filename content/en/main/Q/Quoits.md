**Quoits** is a game which involves throwing rings over a set distance, attempting to land over or close to a spike. [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) was skilled at this game, which indicates that it was popular among [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
