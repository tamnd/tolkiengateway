<br> The **[Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) Numbers** were as follows:

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Min%C3%AB" title="Minë">minë</a></b></i> = one
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Atta" title="Atta">atta</a></b></i> = two
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Neld%C3%AB" title="Neldë">neldë</a></b></i> = three
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Canta" title="Canta">canta</a></b></i> = four
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Lemp%C3%AB" title="Lempë">lempë</a></b></i> = five
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Enqu%C3%AB" title="Enquë">enquë</a></b></i> = six
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Otso" title="Otso">otso</a></b></i> = seven
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Tolto" title="Tolto">tolto</a></b></i> = eight
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Nert%C3%AB" title="Nertë">nertë</a></b></i> = nine
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Cainen" title="Cainen">cainen</a></b></i> = ten
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Minqu%C3%AB" title="Minquë">minquë</a></b></i> = eleven
- <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Yunque" title="Yunque">yunquë</a></b></i> = twelve
- <i><b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quainel&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quainel (page does not exist)">quainel</a>/nelquea/yunquenta</b></i> = thirteen
- <i><b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quaican&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quaican (page does not exist)">quaican</a></b></i> = fourteen
- <i><b><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Lepenque" title="Lepenque">lepenque</a>/<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quailepen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quailepen (page does not exist)">quailepen</a></b></i> = fifteen
- <i><b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Enenque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Enenque (page does not exist)">enenque</a>/<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quainque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quainque (page does not exist)">quainque</a></b></i> = sixteen
- <i><b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Otoque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Otoque (page does not exist)">otoque</a></b></i> = seventeen
- <i><b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Toloque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Toloque (page does not exist)">toloque</a></b></i> = eighteen
- <i><b><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Neterque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Neterque (page does not exist)">neterque</a></b></i> = nineteen
- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Haran" title="Haran">haran</a></b></i> = one hundred
