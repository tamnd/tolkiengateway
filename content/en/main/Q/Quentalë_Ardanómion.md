**Quentalë Ardanómion** is a work that [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine) states having used as a source for the text *Concerning the Dwarves* beside *[Lammas](/wiki/Lammas)* and *[Dorgannas Iaur](/wiki/Dorgannas_Iaur)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The work is not mentioned elsewhere, nor is its writer known.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quental%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quentalë (page does not exist)">quentalë</a></i> means 'history'.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *Ardanómion* is not quite clear apart the beginning of the word, *[Arda](/wiki/Arda)*. [Måns Björkman](/wiki/M%C3%A5ns_Bj%C3%B6rkman) considers *-nómion* possibly a misreading of the manuscript, because *-nornion* &lt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Norno&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Norno (page does not exist)">norno</a></i> 'dwarf' would make sense in a work used as a source for a text on dwarves.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
