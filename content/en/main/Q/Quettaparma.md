The **Quettaparma**, or **Λεξικό της Quenya, της γλώσσας των υψηλών ξωτικων του J.R.R.Tolkien** (Greek: "A Lexicon of [Quenya](Quenya.md), language of the High Elves of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)"), is a Lexicon that lists all [Quenya](Quenya.md) words and translates them to Greek language, and the opposite.

The title is [neo-Quenya](../N/Neo-Quenya.md) for "word-book", a word invented to mean "dictionary".

The book, which features roughly 180 pages from Elvish to Greek and another 180 from Greek to Elvish, has also several drawings by *Xristina Prousanidou* and a prologue written by the author explaining the importance of this book to all greek Tolkien fans.
