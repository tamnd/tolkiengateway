The **King of Númenor** was the title of twenty-two rulers of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) (there were also three [Ruling Queens](/wiki/Ruling_Queens_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Ruling Queens of Númenor")). Beginning with [Elros Tar-Minyatur](/wiki/Elros "Elros") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [32](/wiki/Second_Age_32 "Second Age 32"), the line of kings lasted for 3,287 years, until [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) brought about the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

All the Kings of Númenor used either the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) prefix *[Tar](/wiki/Tar)* or the [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) prefix *[Ar](/wiki/Ar)* before their royal name. Both prefixes meant "High".

## Other names {#Other_names}

The title was rendered as <i><b>Ârû &#39;nAdûnâi</b></i> ("King of the Anadunians") in [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The term **King of the Númenóreans** is attested once in the list of Kings.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It was later applied by [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) to King [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in his deathbed,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> probably in a poetic sense, as Aragorn had restored the glory of the Númenórean lineage and was dying as the old royal customs of Númenor.
