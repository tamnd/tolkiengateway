**Kalimac Brandagamba** was the actual [Westron](/wiki/Westron) form of the Hobbit name anglicised by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") as '[Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck)', usually shortened to **Kali**.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In drafts of the [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices), Tolkien gives the Westron name **Khilimanzar/Cilimanzar** with the short form **Khilik/Cilic**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
