**Kingdom O' Magic** is a video game and parody of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Baidu_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baidu-4">[4]</a></sup> The game includes [Ringwraiths](/wiki/Ringwraiths) and [magic rings](/wiki/Rings_of_Power "Rings of Power"). It was written by [Fergus McNeill](/wiki/Fergus_McNeill).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-El_Anillo_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-El_Anillo-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Baidu_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Baidu-4">[4]</a></sup>
