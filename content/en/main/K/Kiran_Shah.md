**Kiran Shah** ([28 September](/wiki/28_September) [1956](/wiki/1956)<sup id="cite_ref-Guinness_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guinness-1">[1]</a></sup>) is a Kenyan actor and stunt double. He worked on *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")* and *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* film trilogies as a scale double, filling in for characters such as [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Thráin](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in). Due to a hormone deficency, Shah is 4′ 1.7″ (1.26 m) tall, a stature that made him well suited to portraying shorter characters in the films.<sup id="cite_ref-Guinness_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guinness-1">[1]</a></sup>

Shah portrayed the [Goblin Scribe](/wiki/Goblin_Scribe) and a burning child in [Dale](/wiki/Dale) in *[The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey)* and [Master Stadle](/wiki/Master_Stadle) in *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*.
