**King of the World** is a title claimed by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) after returning to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was later used by [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) in late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), in the peak of his power.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other forms {#Other_forms}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form was <i><b>Tarumbar</b></i>, with the rejected form *Aran Endór* ("King of Middle-earth").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) also claimed the equivalent titles **King of Kings** and **Lord of the World**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
