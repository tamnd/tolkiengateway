**Kine of Araw** was a species of [oxen](/wiki/Cows "Cows") that lived in the fields of [Rhûn](/wiki/Rh%C3%BBn) near the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn). These oxen were hardier and wilder than any other in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

The Kine were famous as the quarry of [Vorondil](/wiki/Vorondil) the Hunter, ancestor of the [Ruling Stewards of Gondor](/wiki/Ruling_Steward "Ruling Steward"). It was he who cut a horn from one of these beasts and from it fashioned a hunting-horn; the [Great Horn](/wiki/Great_Horn), which came to be carried by the eldest son of the Ruling Steward from [Vorondil](/wiki/Vorondil)'s time onward. The last heir to bear the Great Horn was [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir), who saw it broken in two in his battle with [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) beneath [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen).<sup id="cite_ref-V1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-V1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Legends claimed that these oxen descended from the cattle of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), the Huntsman of the Valar, and so they were named the Kine of Araw<sup id="cite_ref-V1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-V1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, [Araw](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB#Etymology "Oromë") being the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) form of Oromë's name.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In an unpublished manuscript held at the [Bodleian Library](/wiki/Bodleian_Library), the Wild Kine were likened by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") to <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurochs" title="wikipedia:Aurochs">aurochs</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
