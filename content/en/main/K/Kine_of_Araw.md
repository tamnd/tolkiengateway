**Kine of Araw** was a species of [oxen](../C/Cows.md) that lived in the fields of [Rhûn](../R/Rhûn.md) near the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md). These oxen were hardier and wilder than any other in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

The Kine were famous as the quarry of [Vorondil](../V/Vorondil.md) the Hunter, ancestor of the [Ruling Stewards of Gondor](../R/Ruling_Steward.md). It was he who cut a horn from one of these beasts and from it fashioned a hunting-horn; the [Great Horn](../G/Great_Horn.md), which came to be carried by the eldest son of the Ruling Steward from [Vorondil](../V/Vorondil.md)'s time onward. The last heir to bear the Great Horn was [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md), who saw it broken in two in his battle with [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) beneath [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-V1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-V1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Legends claimed that these oxen descended from the cattle of [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), the Huntsman of the Valar, and so they were named the Kine of Araw<sup id="cite_ref-V1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-V1-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>, [Araw](../O/Oromë.md#Etymology) being the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) form of Oromë's name.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In an unpublished manuscript held at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md), the Wild Kine were likened by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aurochs" title="wikipedia:Aurochs">aurochs</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
