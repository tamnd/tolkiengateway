**Kurúki** was an evil magician in a preliminary sketch of the *[Tale of Turambar](../T/Turambar_and_the_Foalókë.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.

## History {#History}

[Vainóni](../N/Nienóri.md) and her mother [Tirannë](../M/Mavwin.md) met with Kurúki and were given a baneful drink. This caused them to forget their names and wander distraught within the woods. Eventually, upon the death of the [Foalókë](../G/Glaurung.md), the dragon lifted the veil that Kurúki had placed on Vainóni, causing her to flee through the woods and throw herself over a waterfall.<sup id="cite_ref-Kurúki_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kurúki-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The meaning of the name *Kurúki* is unclear, but it seems to contain the [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md) word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kuru&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kuru (page does not exist)">kuru</a></i> ("magic, wizardry").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later versions of *[the tale of the Children of Húrin](<../N/Narn_i_Chîn_Húrin_(tale).md>)*, the character of Kurúki was discarded, and [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) took on the magician's former role of erasing [Nienóri's](../N/Nienóri.md) memory in addition to his own role of breaking the spell of forgetfulness. In addition, Nienor is chased into Brethil by [Orc-hunters](../O/Orc-hunters.md) in the final version. Interestingly, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes that it is probable that the outline fragment that Kurúki appears in may be a unique glimpse into a draft of the *[Tale of Turambar](../T/Turambar_and_the_Foalókë.md)* that predates even the erased text which many of the stories found within *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)* were written over.<sup id="cite_ref-Kurúki_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kurúki-2">[2]</a></sup>
