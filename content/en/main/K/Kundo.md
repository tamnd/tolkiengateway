<i><b>kundo</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "guardian".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has suggested that Tolkien's manuscript note "*cundo* 'lord'"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> is a related gloss, also in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). The latter form conforms to the orthography used in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* (where Tolkien represented the \[k\]-sound with the letter "c").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- *[Karma-kundo](Karma-kundo.md)* "Helm-guardian"
