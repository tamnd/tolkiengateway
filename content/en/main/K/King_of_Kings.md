**King of Kings** was a title claimed by two of [Arda](/wiki/Arda)'s major tyrants: [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), the great subversive power-hungry [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar"), and [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n), the power-hungry last King of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).

## History {#History}

During the [Black Years](/wiki/Dark_Years "Dark Years"), when his empire encompassed large parts of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), Sauron proclaimed himself "King of Kings",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L131_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L131-2">[2]</a></sup> demanding god-like deference from his great following among [Men](/wiki/Men);<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-3">[3]</a></sup> and attacked the cities of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans), which were located close to the coasts of Middle-earth and announced that he would drive the Númenóreans into the sea. This incensed Ar-Pharazôn who decided to force Sauron to become his vassal by force.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup>

Ar-Pharazôn sailed with a great armada to Middle-earth, set up a camp with his mighty army and sent envoys to Sauron demanding him to come and to swear fealty to him. When Sauron realized that he could not prevail be force, he decided to use manipulation and came and swore featly to Ar-Pharazôn. Ar-Pharazôn took Sauron with him back to Númenor as a hostage, but Sauron managed to gain the trust of Ar-Pharazôn and to become the closest counsellor of the king to whom he referred as the "King of Kings". Ultimately Sauron succeeded in convincing Ar-Pharazôn who feared death that breaking the [Ban of the Valar](/wiki/Ban_of_the_Valar) and living in [Aman](/wiki/Aman) would grant him immortality. When a Númenórean armada saild to Aman and an army landed the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) asked [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) to intervene and the island of Númenor sank below the waves, the armada fell into an abyss and their army was buried under collapsing hills in Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup>
