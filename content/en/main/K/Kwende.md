<i><b>kwende</b></i> pl. <i><b>kwendî</b></i> is the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) word for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The word was perhaps primarily used in the plural.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Formed from the [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") [KWE](/wiki/KWE)NE (see <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kwene&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kwene (page does not exist)">kwene</a></i>) with <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ostime&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ostime (page does not exist)">ostime</a></i> of *n &gt; nd*.

Since KWENE is probably expanded from KWE, the most primitive stem having to do with articulate speech, the term *kwendî* identifies the Elves as "speakers".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

Note that the final short *-e* was not lost in the later languages, as it usually happens with other words.

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[Quendi](/wiki/Quendi)*
- [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *[Pendi](/wiki/Pendi)*
- *[Kindi](/wiki/Kindi)*
- *[Cuind](/wiki/Cuind)*
- *[Hwenti](/wiki/Hwenti)*
- *[Windan](/wiki/Windan)*
- *[Kinn-lai](/wiki/Kinn-lai)*
- *[Penni](/wiki/Penni)*

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[quendë](/wiki/Quend%C3%AB "Quendë")* "Elf" is derived from a stem KWEN(ED) simply defined as "Elf", and the primitive form is given as *kwenedê*. <span></span>
