<i><b>kwende</b></i> pl. <i><b>kwendî</b></i> is the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) word for the [Elves](../E/Elves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The word was perhaps primarily used in the plural.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Formed from the [Root](../S/Sundocarmë.md) [KWE](KWE.md)NE (see <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kwene&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kwene (page does not exist)">kwene</a></i>) with <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ostime&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ostime (page does not exist)">ostime</a></i> of *n &gt; nd*.

Since KWENE is probably expanded from KWE, the most primitive stem having to do with articulate speech, the term *kwendî* identifies the Elves as "speakers".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

Note that the final short *-e* was not lost in the later languages, as it usually happens with other words.

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[Quendi](../Q/Quendi.md)*
- [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) *[Pendi](../P/Pendi.md)*
- *[Kindi](Kindi.md)*
- *[Cuind](../C/Cuind.md)*
- *[Hwenti](../H/Hwenti.md)*
- *[Windan](../W/Windan.md)*
- *[Kinn-lai](Kinn-lai.md)*
- *[Penni](../P/Penni.md)*

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) *[quendë](../Q/Quendë.md)* "Elf" is derived from a stem KWEN(ED) simply defined as "Elf", and the primitive form is given as *kwenedê*. <span></span>
