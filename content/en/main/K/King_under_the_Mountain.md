**King under the Mountain** was the title taken by [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I), founder of the Dwarf-kingdom of [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), and maintained by those Kings of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) who dwelt there. The line was broken twice, once by [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) (he and four generations of his descendants ruled from the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), not Lonely Mountain), and once by the [Dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) (who claimed the title for himself). In both cases, the line was restored to a rightful heir.

## The Line of Kings under the Mountain {#The_Line_of_Kings_under_the_Mountain}

1. [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I)
2. [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) – abandoned the Kingdom for the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains)
3. [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r) – restored the Kingdom but lost it to [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug)
4. [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) - claimed title and kingdom after the [Sack of Erebor](/wiki/Sack_of_Erebor)
5. [Thorin II Oakenshield](/wiki/Thorin "Thorin") – regained the Kingdom
6. [Dáin II Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot") - reigned after Thorin's death in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies)
7. [Thorin III Stonehelm](/wiki/Thorin_Stonehelm "Thorin Stonehelm")

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The term "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain" title="wikipedia:King under the Mountain">King under the Mountain</a>" is a philological term for a certain motif in various mythologies and folklore around the world. It refers to a kingly figure who remains hidden in a mountain or cave until a messianic return at the appointed time.

According to the Wikipedia article, the motif is also present at several points in [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"): [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n) with his [Great Armament](/wiki/Great_Armament) was buried in the [Caves of the Forgotten](/wiki/Caves_of_the_Forgotten) beneath the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) until the [End of the World](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath "Dagor Dagorath"); the [King of the Dead](/wiki/King_of_the_Dead) and the [Men of the Mountains](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") in [Dunharrow](/wiki/Dunharrow), who returned to fulfill their oath; and of course the King under the Mountain himself, with Thorin's return to Erebor. Other figures can include [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) (said to return from the dead at the [Dagor Dagorath](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath)); and the [King of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") (with [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) as the returned King).
