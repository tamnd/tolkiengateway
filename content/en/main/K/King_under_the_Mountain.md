**King under the Mountain** was the title taken by [Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md), founder of the Dwarf-kingdom of [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md), and maintained by those Kings of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) who dwelt there. The line was broken twice, once by [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md) (he and four generations of his descendants ruled from the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md), not Lonely Mountain), and once by the [Dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) (who claimed the title for himself). In both cases, the line was restored to a rightful heir.

## The Line of Kings under the Mountain {#The_Line_of_Kings_under_the_Mountain}

1. [Thráin I](../T/Thráin_I.md)
2. [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md) – abandoned the Kingdom for the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md)
3. [Thrór](../T/Thrór.md) – restored the Kingdom but lost it to [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md)
4. [Smaug](../S/Smaug.md) - claimed title and kingdom after the [Sack of Erebor](../S/Sack_of_Erebor.md)
5. [Thorin II Oakenshield](../T/Thorin.md) – regained the Kingdom
6. [Dáin II Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md) - reigned after Thorin's death in the [Battle of Five Armies](../B/Battle_of_Five_Armies.md)
7. [Thorin III Stonehelm](../T/Thorin_Stonehelm.md)

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The term "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_under_the_Mountain" title="wikipedia:King under the Mountain">King under the Mountain</a>" is a philological term for a certain motif in various mythologies and folklore around the world. It refers to a kingly figure who remains hidden in a mountain or cave until a messianic return at the appointed time.

According to the Wikipedia article, the motif is also present at several points in [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md): [Ar-Pharazôn](../A/Ar-Pharazôn.md) with his [Great Armament](../G/Great_Armament.md) was buried in the [Caves of the Forgotten](../C/Caves_of_the_Forgotten.md) beneath the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md) until the [End of the World](../D/Dagor_Dagorath.md); the [King of the Dead](King_of_the_Dead.md) and the [Men of the Mountains](../O/Oathbreakers.md) in [Dunharrow](../D/Dunharrow.md), who returned to fulfill their oath; and of course the King under the Mountain himself, with Thorin's return to Erebor. Other figures can include [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md) and [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) (said to return from the dead at the [Dagor Dagorath](../D/Dagor_Dagorath.md)); and the [King of Gondor](Kings_of_Gondor.md) (with [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) as the returned King).
