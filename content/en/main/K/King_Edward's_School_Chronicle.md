The <i><b>King Edward&#39;s School Chronicle</b></i> is a journal of [King Edward's School](King_Edward's_School.md) which has been active since the 1840s.

A certain number of issues published in the 1910s contained works by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), when he was a pupil there. All past issues have been digitized and made available online by the School.

## November 1910 {#November_1910}

Issue: vol. 25, no. 183.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d269" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains Tolkien's "Debating Society Report" (pp. 68–71).

## December 1910 {#December_1910}

Issue: vol. 25, no. 184.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d270" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains Tolkien's "Debating Society Report" (pp. 94–95).

## February 1911 {#February_1911}

Issue: vol. 26, no. 185.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d272" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains Tolkien's "Debating Society Report" (pp. 5–9).

## March 1911 {#March_1911}

Issue: vol. 26, no. 186.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d273" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains Tolkien's poem "[The Battle of the Eastern Field](../T/The_Battle_of_the_Eastern_Field.md)" (pp. 22–26) and "[Acta Senatus](../A/Acta_Senatus.md)" (a debate report in Latin; pp. 26–27).

## June 1911 {#June_1911}

Issue: vol. 26, no. 187.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d274" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains Tolkien's "Editorial" (pp. 33–34) and the "Debating Society Report" (pp. 42–45). The issue was also edited by Tolkien himself.

## July 1911 {#July_1911}

Issue: vol. 26, no. 188.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d275" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains Tolkien's "Editorial" (pp. 53–54). The issue was also edited by Tolkien himself.

## December 1913 {#December_1913}

Issue: vol. 28, no. 202.  <a class="external text" href="https://www.kingedwardvi-kes-archives.org/PDFViewer/web/viewer.html?file=%2fFilename.ashx%3ftableName%3dta_publications%26columnName%3dfilename%26recordId%3d291" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Link to the issue</a>

Contains "Oxford Letter" (pp. 80–81), a letter from Tolkien reporting the activities of former pupils of King Edward's School now at [Oxford University](../O/Oxford_University.md). Though signed as "Oxon", Tolkien's authorship was revealed in his papers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
