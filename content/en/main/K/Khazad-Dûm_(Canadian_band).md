*Not to be confused with [Khazad-dûm](</wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm_(British_band)> "Khazad-dûm (British band)"), a funeral doom/death metal band from Reading.*

**Khazad-Dûm** is a death metal/deathcore band from Edmonton, Canada.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The band takes its name from [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm), the ancient Dwarven realm also known as Moria, and its singles are named after [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) and [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob).

## Members {#Members}

- Levon Vokins - drums
- Eric Mackenzie Siemens

## Discography {#Discography}

- Smaug the Dreadful (Single, 2024)
- Shelob's Lair (Single, 2024)
