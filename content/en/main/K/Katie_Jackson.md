**Katie Jackson** (born [1996](../_/1996.md)) is a New Zealand actress, the daughter of [Peter Jackson](../P/Peter_Jackson.md) and [Fran Walsh](../F/Fran_Walsh.md), and the sister of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Billy_Jackson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Billy Jackson (page does not exist)">Billy Jackson</a> and has cameos in her parent's productions.

She portrayed a Cute [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) Child listening to Biblo's stories during the Party scene, a Cute [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md) Refugee Child hidden in the [Glittering Caves](../G/Glittering_Caves.md) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), and a [Gondorian](../G/Gondor.md) Child during the [Siege of Gondor](../S/Siege_of_Gondor.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>)* trilogy.

A decade later she returned for *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)* trilogy, portraying a Cute Young Hobbit in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), [Betsy Butterbur](../B/Betsy_Butterbur.md) in *[The Prancing Pony](../T/The_Prancing_Pony.md)*, and a Woman in [Lake-town](../L/Lake-town.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
