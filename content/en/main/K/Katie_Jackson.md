**Katie Jackson** (born [1996](/wiki/1996)) is a New Zealand actress, the daughter of [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson) and [Fran Walsh](/wiki/Fran_Walsh), and the sister of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Billy_Jackson&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Billy Jackson (page does not exist)">Billy Jackson</a> and has cameos in her parent's productions.

She portrayed a Cute [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") Child listening to Biblo's stories during the Party scene, a Cute [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) Refugee Child hidden in the [Glittering Caves](/wiki/Glittering_Caves) during the [Battle of the Hornburg](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Hornburg), and a [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondor "Gondor") Child during the [Siege of Gondor](/wiki/Siege_of_Gondor) in *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")* trilogy.

A decade later she returned for *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")* trilogy, portraying a Cute Young Hobbit in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), [Betsy Butterbur](/wiki/Betsy_Butterbur) in *[The Prancing Pony](/wiki/The_Prancing_Pony)*, and a Woman in [Lake-town](/wiki/Lake-town).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
