**Kornoldorin** ("[Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) of [Kôr](Kôr.md)"), or **Korolambë** ("Tongue of [Kôr](Kôr.md)"), was the language of the [Noldoli](../N/Noldoli.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) according to early versions of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md). The Noldoli used it beside [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), but unlike Qenya, it changed fast as the Noldoli devised new words and structures by themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Old Noldorin shares several attested words with Qenya (like <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Malina&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Malina (page does not exist)">malina</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="../M/Míre.md" title="Míre">míre</a>, <a href="../P/Parma.md" title="Parma">parma</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Randa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Randa (page does not exist)">randa</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rauta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rauta (page does not exist)">rauta</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rimba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimba (page does not exist)">rimba</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rimbe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimbe (page does not exist)">rimbe</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ringe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ringe (page does not exist)">ringe</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Runda&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Runda (page does not exist)">runda</a>, <a href="../S/Sír.md" title="Sír">sīre</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Yaiwe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yaiwe (page does not exist)">yaiwe</a></i>) and [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) (<i><a href="../B/Branda.md" title="Branda">branda</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Bana&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bana (page does not exist)">Bana</a>, <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Belda&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Belda (page does not exist)">belda</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The language also presented several minor changes of vowels and consonants over Telerin; compare T. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpa (page does not exist)">alpa</a></i> "swan" vs ON. <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Alpha&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpha (page does not exist)">alpha</a></i> (which brought Noldorin *[alph](../A/Alph.md)*)

In the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), the language evolved as [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) through contact with [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the stage of the later [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) rearranged several details: the language described as Old Noldorin is taken by the Noldorin dialect of [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), and the language called Noldorin was renamed "[Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)" and took the place of the vernacular Ilkorin in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).

Old Noldorin belongs to a chronological and phonological stage which Sindarin would normally also have passed through, specifically a stage when final vowels still existed, and consonants did not undergo [lenition](../L/Lenition.md) yet; however this stage is not named or described by Tolkien other than some example etymological reconstructions.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Fans name this phase "[Old Sindarin](../O/Old_Sindarin.md)".
