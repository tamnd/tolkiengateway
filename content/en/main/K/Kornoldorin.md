**Kornoldorin** ("[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) of [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r)"), or **Korolambë** ("Tongue of [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r)"), was the language of the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) according to early versions of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). The Noldoli used it beside [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), but unlike Qenya, it changed fast as the Noldoli devised new words and structures by themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Old Noldorin shares several attested words with Qenya (like <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Malina&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Malina (page does not exist)">malina</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/M%C3%ADre" title="Míre">míre</a>, <a href="/wiki/Parma" title="Parma">parma</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Randa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Randa (page does not exist)">randa</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rauta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rauta (page does not exist)">rauta</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rimba&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimba (page does not exist)">rimba</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rimbe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimbe (page does not exist)">rimbe</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ringe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ringe (page does not exist)">ringe</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Runda&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Runda (page does not exist)">runda</a>, <a href="/wiki/S%C3%ADr" title="Sír">sīre</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Yaiwe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Yaiwe (page does not exist)">yaiwe</a></i>) and [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) (<i><a href="/wiki/Branda" title="Branda">branda</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Bana&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Bana (page does not exist)">Bana</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Belda&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Belda (page does not exist)">belda</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The language also presented several minor changes of vowels and consonants over Telerin; compare T. <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpa (page does not exist)">alpa</a></i> "swan" vs ON. <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alpha&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alpha (page does not exist)">alpha</a></i> (which brought Noldorin *[alph](/wiki/Alph "Alph")*)

In the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), the language evolved as [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) through contact with [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the stage of the later [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") rearranged several details: the language described as Old Noldorin is taken by the Noldorin dialect of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), and the language called Noldorin was renamed "[Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)" and took the place of the vernacular Ilkorin in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).

Old Noldorin belongs to a chronological and phonological stage which Sindarin would normally also have passed through, specifically a stage when final vowels still existed, and consonants did not undergo [lenition](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") yet; however this stage is not named or described by Tolkien other than some example etymological reconstructions.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Fans name this phase "[Old Sindarin](/wiki/Old_Sindarin)".
