The **King’s Tree** was a great tree that [Smith Smithson](../S/Smith_Smithson.md) saw only once in the land of [Faery](../F/Faery.md). Smith had gotten lost one time in a grey mist. When the fog rolled away he was in a wide plain. Far away he espied a hill of shadow, yet out of the shadow rose the King's Tree. High it towered with a light like the sun at noon. It was laden with leaves, flowers, and fruits uncountable and was the only one of its kind.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
