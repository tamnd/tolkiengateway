**KELU** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "flow out swiftly".<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[Celos](/wiki/Celos)*<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *kelussë* ("freshet, water falling out swiftly from a rocky spring")<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup>
