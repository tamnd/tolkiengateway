**KEN** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "see, perceive, note".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kenda-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kenda- (page does not exist)">kenda-</a></i> ("intensive watch, sc. *not'* guard' but observe for some time (to gain information etc.). Often used for 'reading'")<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
