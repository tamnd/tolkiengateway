**King Sheave** is a character in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "[The Lost Road](/wiki/The_Lost_Road)." He is mentioned in a scene where [Ælfwine](/wiki/%C3%86lfwine), a mariner, tells the story of King Sheave, who was found as a boy in an empty ship coming from the west. He became a king in the North and ruled for many years before sailing away at the end of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
