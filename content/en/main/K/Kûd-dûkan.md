The **Kûd-dûkan** was a creature of [Rohanese](/wiki/Rohirrim "Rohirrim") folklore which referred to [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "hole-dweller" and can be analyzed as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=K%C3%BBd&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kûd (page does not exist)">kûd</a></i> "hole" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=D%C3%BBkan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dûkan (page does not exist)">dûkan</a></i> "dweller" (from a verbal stem \*<i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=D%C3%BBk&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dûk (page does not exist)">dûk</a></i> "to dwell" and a suffix *-an*).<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is translated into [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) as *[Holbytla](/wiki/Holbytla)*, "hole-builder".<sup id="cite_ref-AppF_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppF-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
