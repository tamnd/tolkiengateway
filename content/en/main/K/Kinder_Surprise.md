**Kinder Surprise** is a chocolate egg with a toy inside. Prior to the release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*, they included mini-figures based on the movie.

## History {#History}

Though Kinder Surprise is available in all countries except the United States,<sup id="cite_ref-WP_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-1">[1]</a></sup> this promotion was only for sale in Germany.<sup id="cite_ref-Gillam_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gillam-2">[2]</a></sup> Sale started on [May 11th](/wiki/11_May "11 May"), [2001](/wiki/2001), and was one of the first officially licensed *Movie Trilogy* merchandise. Inside the chocolate egg were ten minifigures, handpainted on plastic.

- [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn)
- [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen)
- [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) (with [Great Horn](/wiki/Great_Horn))
- [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") (with [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"))
- [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) (with [Mirror of Galadriel](/wiki/Mirror_of_Galadriel))
- [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf)
- [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli)
- [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas)
- [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") &amp; [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took")
- [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)

With each statuette came a code with which a lottery on the *Kinder* website could be entered. A special collector's case was also available.<sup id="cite_ref-Gillam_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gillam-2">[2]</a></sup>
