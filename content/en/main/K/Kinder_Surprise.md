**Kinder Surprise** is a chocolate egg with a toy inside. Prior to the release of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, they included mini-figures based on the movie.

## History {#History}

Though Kinder Surprise is available in all countries except the United States,<sup id="cite_ref-WP_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WP-1">[1]</a></sup> this promotion was only for sale in Germany.<sup id="cite_ref-Gillam_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gillam-2">[2]</a></sup> Sale started on [May 11th](../_/11_May.md), [2001](../_/2001.md), and was one of the first officially licensed *Movie Trilogy* merchandise. Inside the chocolate egg were ten minifigures, handpainted on plastic.

- [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md)
- [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md)
- [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) (with [Great Horn](../G/Great_Horn.md))
- [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) (with [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md))
- [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) (with [Mirror of Galadriel](../M/Mirror_of_Galadriel.md))
- [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)
- [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md)
- [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md)
- [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) &amp; [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md)
- [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md)

With each statuette came a code with which a lottery on the *Kinder* website could be entered. A special collector's case was also available.<sup id="cite_ref-Gillam_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gillam-2">[2]</a></sup>
