<i><b>Khuzd</b></i>, pl. <i><b>Khazâd</b></i>, was the [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) word for the "[Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves)".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word comes from the [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") Kh-Z-D; it is also visible in the words *[Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul)* and perhaps *[Nulukkhizdîn](/wiki/Nulukkizd%C3%AEn "Nulukkizdîn")*.

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

The plural form *Khazâd* is the basis of [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[casar](/wiki/Casar "Casar")* and [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[hadhod](/wiki/Hadhod "Hadhod")*.

The [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) word *[hazad](/wiki/Hazad "Hazad")*, meaning "seven", is presumably also related.
