**KARAK** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "sharp fang, spike, tooth".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

A rejected version read: "KARAK- tooth, spike, peak. Q *karka*".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup> {#Derivatives[1]}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[karakse](Karakse.md)*; <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Karka&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Karka (page does not exist)">karka</a></i> ("tooth"); <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Karkane&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Karkane (page does not exist)">karkane</a></i> ("row of teeth")

- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Carag&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carag (page does not exist)">carag</a></i> ("spike, tooth of rock"); <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Carch&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Carch (page does not exist)">carch</a></i> ("tooth, fang")
