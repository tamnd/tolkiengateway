**Kingfishers** were bright blue birds.

## History {#History}

Kingfishers lived on the waterside, feeding on [fish](../F/Fish.md). One notable kingfisher lived, according to [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) folklore, in the [Old Forest](../O/Old_Forest.md). It jestfully harassed [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md), who in return compared it to a weathercock, and commented that, despite its lordy appearance, it lives in a dirty home.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> Upon flying away it lost a bright blue feather which Tom took replacing the old battered [swan](../S/Swans.md)-feather in his hat.<sup id="cite_ref-Boat_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boat-1">[1]</a></sup> Tom still bore a noticeable large blue feather on his hat when he met [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and his companions.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

[Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was also familiar with the Kingfisher; when [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) saw what he thought was a black squirrel or kingfisher, he asked [Anborn](../A/Anborn.md) whether there were any black kingfishers in [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Pool_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pool-4">[3]</a></sup> a forest known for its black animals.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup> Anborn saw the mysterious creature had four limbs, and quickly dismissed both ideas. It turned out to be [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md), hiding in the [Forbidden Pool](../F/Forbidden_Pool.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Pool_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pool-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Kingfishers* were originally called *the King's fisher*, as they, like [swans](../S/Swans.md), were royal animals<sup id="cite_ref-L240_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L240-6">[5]</a></sup> although the historical association is unknown.

## Other names {#Other_names}

Several [Eldarin](../E/Eldarin.md) words for the bird are known as well: in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), it was called <i><b>halatir</b></i> (or *halatir(no)*), from the [roots](../S/Sundocarmë.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=SKAL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="SKAL (page does not exist)">SKAL</a> and [TIR](../T/TIR~30982.md), literally meaning "fishwatcher".<sup id="cite_ref-Etym_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etym-7">[6]</a></sup> [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) had a similar word, <i><b>heledirn</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Etym_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Etym-7">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The Kingfisher, and especially the rivalry between the kingfisher and the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Old_Swan&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Old Swan (page does not exist)">Old Swan</a> in *[Bombadil Goes Boating](../B/Bombadil_Goes_Boating.md)*, was inspired by its use in European royal houses. Originally, the swan was the animal owned by kings, but later, it became the kingfisher. In [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), both yearned for the return of the [rightful King](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-L240_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L240-6">[5]</a></sup>
