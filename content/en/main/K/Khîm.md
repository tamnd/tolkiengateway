**Khîm** was the son of [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm) and brother of [Ibun](/wiki/Ibun), who were the last of the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves) to survive in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

One day, when out travelling with his father and brother, the three were set upon by [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin")'s [outlaw band](/wiki/Gaurwaith "Gaurwaith"). With Ibun, Khîm fled into the mist, but a stray arrow fired by one of the outlaws struck him as he fled. Though he escaped back to Mîm's halls beneath [Amon Rûdh](/wiki/Amon_R%C3%BBdh)—no doubt with his brother's help—his wound was mortal, and he died before his father returned.<sup id="cite_ref-Mim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mim-1">[1]</a></sup>

Túrin and his companions came to dwell in Mîm's house, which became known as the [House of Ransom](/wiki/House_of_Ransom), in part because of Khîm's premature death. The archer who shot him, an outlaw named [Andróg](/wiki/Andr%C3%B3g), was cursed by Mîm, and forced to break his bow.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
