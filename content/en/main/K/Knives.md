**Knives** or **daggers** were short bladed tools and weapons used by many different peoples of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## By race {#By_race}

### Elves {#Elves}

[Elves](../E/Elves.md) made knives, carried them and used in hunting and in war. An Elf-wrought knife was a birthday-gift [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) gave [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> [Sting](../S/Sting.md) was made by Elvish smiths,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> probably in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) carried a knife<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4">[4]</a></sup> called [Angrist](../A/Angrist.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> when riding from [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) to [Himring](../H/Himring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) used a knife to rip the belly of [Carcharoth](../C/Carcharoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) carried a long white knife<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> with a silver haft.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> He used it for "knife-work" during the [battle of the Hornburg](../B/Battle_of_the_Hornburg.md), killing several enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

### Dwarves {#Dwarves}

[Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) also forged knives and carried them in battle and during journeys. Angrist was made by the dwarf-smith [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> [Azaghâl](../A/Azaghâl.md), [Lord of Belegost](../L/Lord_of_Belegost.md) used a knife to wound the [dragon](../D/Dragons.md) [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md) during [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md) had a long knife with [poisoned](../P/Poisoned_weapons.md) runes on its blade.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Some dwarves in [Thorin's company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) carried small knives<sup id="cite_ref-:4_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-13">[13]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> and used them to cut [spiders](../S/Spiders.md)' threads<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> and fight against the spiders.<sup id="cite_ref-:4_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-13">[13]</a></sup>

### Men {#Men}

[Men](../M/Men.md) used knives since the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md). [Beren](../B/Beren.md) used Angrist to cut one of the [Silmarils](../S/Silmarils.md) from the [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md) of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:5_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-16">[16]</a></sup> Húrin gave Túrin an Elf-wrought knife was a birthday-gift;<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup> Túrin later gifted that knife to [Sador](../S/Sador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> [Andróg](../A/Andróg.md) used a knife to threaten Mîm the Dwarf.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> During the fighting in the [hall](../B/Brodda's_hall.md) of [Brodda](../B/Brodda.md) thralls that rebelled against [Easterlings](<../E/Easterlings_(First_Age).md>) used meat-knives, while Easterlings themselves were armed with daggers and swords.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

In later ages [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md)<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[20]</a></sup> and [Northern Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:6_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-21">[21]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-22">[22]</a></sup> forged knives and daggers. [Men](../P/Pre-Númenóreans.md) of [Agar](../A/Agar.md) also used knives and a group of knife-men served their Master [Mogru](../M/Mogru.md).<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup> [Beorn](../B/Beorn.md) had eating knives<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">[24]</a></sup> - they were among few things made of metal in [his hall](../B/Beorn's_Hall.md).<sup id="cite_ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25">[25]</a></sup> Men of Isengard had eating knives as well.<sup id="cite_ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26">[26]</a></sup>

[Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) also used eating knives.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">[27]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28">[28]</a></sup> Sometimes they carried knives in their pockets.<sup id="cite_ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29">[29]</a></sup> [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) had a knife that he used for dressing rabbits.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">[30]</a></sup> [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md) equipped [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) with a knife in addition to his [Barrow-blade](../D/Daggers_of_Westernesse.md).<sup id="cite_ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31">[31]</a></sup> Long knives were also used by Hobbits as weapons during the [Scouring of the Shire](../S/Scouring_of_the_Shire.md).<sup id="cite_ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32">[32]</a></sup> Since Hobbits were of smaller stature than Elves or other Men, they could use knives made by Elves or other Men as swords.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-33">[33]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-:6_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-21">[21]</a></sup>

Sometimes knives were used by evil Men or for wicked deeds. [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md) carried a knife with which in his final despair he tried to kill [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34">[34]</a></sup> Knives were among the weapons used by [ruffians](../R/Ruffians.md) of [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md),<sup id="cite_ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35">[35]</a></sup> one of their leaders had a long knife.<sup id="cite_ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36">[36]</a></sup> Saruman himself tried to stab [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) with a knife, which was stopped by Frodo's [mithril-coat](../M/Mithril-coat.md).<sup id="cite_ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37">[37]</a></sup> Several moments later Saruman was killed by his henchman [Grima Wormtongue](../G/Gríma.md), who used a hidden knife to cut Saruman's throat.<sup id="cite_ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38">[38]</a></sup>

### Orcs {#Orcs}

Orcs used knives since the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) as well. They threw knives at the captured Túrin.<sup id="cite_ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39">[39]</a></sup> Later [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](../O/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains.md) forged daggers among other tools and weapons.<sup id="cite_ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40">[40]</a></sup> Some of the orcs in the orc-band that captured Merry and Pippin were armed with knives.<sup id="cite_ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41">[41]</a></sup> One of them had a black knife with a long blade, jagged like a saw-edge.<sup id="cite_ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42">[42]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-43" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-43">[43]</a></sup> [Grishnákh](../G/Grishnákh.md) had an orc-knife<sup id="cite_ref-44" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-44">[44]</a></sup> which is described as a long sharp knife (from [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md)'s point of view)<sup id="cite_ref-45" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-45">[45]</a></sup> and as a short serrated blade with a carved handle, shaped like a hideous head.<sup id="cite_ref-46" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-46">[46]</a></sup> Knives were also used by orcs during the in-fighting in the [tower of Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md).<sup id="cite_ref-47" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-47">[47]</a></sup> During that fight [Gorbag](../G/Gorbag.md) knifed [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md),<sup id="cite_ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48">[48]</a></sup> while Shagrat used his long red knife<sup id="cite_ref-49" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-49">[49]</a></sup> to slain Gorbag.<sup id="cite_ref-50" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-50">[50]</a></sup>

### Trolls {#Trolls}

[William, Tom, and Bert](../W/William,_Tom,_and_Bert.md) planned to use their knives to mince the dwarves before boiling them.<sup id="cite_ref-51" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-51">[51]</a></sup>

## Famous knives {#Famous_knives}

- **[Angrist](../A/Angrist.md)** was a knife made by Dwarven smith [Telchar](../T/Telchar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">[5]</a></sup> and wielded by [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md) and later by [Beren](../B/Beren.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4">[4]</a></sup> It could cleave iron iron as wood.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-4">[4]</a></sup> Beren used this knife to cut a [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md) from the [Iron Crown](../I/Iron_Crown.md) of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:5_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-16">[16]</a></sup>
- [**Barrow-blades**](../D/Daggers_of_Westernesse.md) were long daggers<sup id="cite_ref-:9_52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-52">[52]</a></sup> or knives<sup id="cite_ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53">[53]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54">[54]</a></sup> made by [Northern Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:6_21-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-21">[21]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-:7_22-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:7-22">[22]</a></sup> They were found in a barrow at [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) by [Tom Bombadil](../T/Tom_Bombadil.md) and given to [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:9_52-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:9-52">[52]</a></sup> The hobbits could use them as swords.<sup id="cite_ref-:6_21-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:6-21">[21]</a></sup> Merry used his Barrow-blade to deal a bitter wound to the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) during the [battle of the Pelennor fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55">[55]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-56" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-56">[56]</a></sup>
- **[Morgul-knives](../M/Morgul-knives.md)** were [magic](../M/Magic.md) blades used by the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md). A person wounded by such a blade turned into a [wraith](../W/Wraiths.md).<sup id="cite_ref-57" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-57">[57]</a></sup> The Witch-king used a long and thin Morgul-knife to wound Frodo at [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md).<sup id="cite_ref-58" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-58">[58]</a></sup>,<sup id="cite_ref-59" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-59">[59]</a></sup>
- **[Sting](../S/Sting.md)** was an Elvish knife<sup id="cite_ref-:8_33-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-33">[33]</a></sup><sup>,</sup><sup id="cite_ref-60" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-60">[60]</a></sup> or dagger<sup id="cite_ref-61" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-61">[61]</a></sup>, probably made in [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">[3]</a></sup> It was found by [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) in the [Troll's lair](../T/Trolls'_lair.md) and taken by [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), who used it as a short sword during his adventures.<sup id="cite_ref-:8_33-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:8-33">[33]</a></sup> Later Bilbo gifted it to Frodo.<sup id="cite_ref-62" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-62">[62]</a></sup> It was used by Sam to wound [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md).<sup id="cite_ref-63" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-63">[63]</a></sup>
