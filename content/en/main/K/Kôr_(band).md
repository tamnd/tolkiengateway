**Kôr** is a black metal band from Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Germany, formed in 2021.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The band takes its name from [Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r), the hill of the Elven city in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. Its 2021 album *The Horns of Ylmir* takes its title from [Tolkien's poem of the same name](/wiki/The_Horns_of_Ylmir "The Horns of Ylmir"), Ylmir being an early name of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).

- Bavragor - drums
- St. - vocals, guitars, bass
