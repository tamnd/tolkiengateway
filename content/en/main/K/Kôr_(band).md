**Kôr** is a black metal band from Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Germany, formed in 2021.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The band takes its name from [Kôr](Kôr.md), the hill of the Elven city in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*. Its 2021 album *The Horns of Ylmir* takes its title from [Tolkien's poem of the same name](../T/The_Horns_of_Ylmir.md), Ylmir being an early name of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md).

- St. - vocals, guitars, bass
