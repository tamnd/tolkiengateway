**KHUG** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "bark, bay". From it derives the word *[huan](/wiki/Huan "Huan")* ("hound") in both [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
