**KHUG** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "bark, bay". From it derives the word *[huan](../H/Huan.md)* ("hound") in both [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
