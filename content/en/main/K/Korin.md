A **korin** was a kind of enclosure according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It consisted in a circular enclosure, which could be formed by a stone wall, thorns or trees, and within there was a green pasture.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The term was [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), and despite its similarity with *[Kôr](/wiki/K%C3%B4r)*, it came from a different etymological [root](/wiki/Root "Root"). Its [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate was <i><b>gorin</b></i> or <i><b>gwarin</b></i> ("circle of trees").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
