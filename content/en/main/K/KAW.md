**KAW** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "shelter".<sup id="cite_ref-PEE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PEE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *koä* ("building used for a dwelling or other purposes")<sup id="cite_ref-PEE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PEE-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root KAVA, from which was derived [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *kava* ("cellar, cave, vault").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
