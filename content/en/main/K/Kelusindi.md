**Kelusindi** was the name of a river in [Sirnúmen](/wiki/Sirn%C3%BAmen), a place in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) where the [Noldoli](/wiki/Noldoli) were banished, according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Kel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kel-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its source was known as [Kapalinda](/wiki/Kapalinda).<sup id="cite_ref-Kel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kel-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Kelusindi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), being a combination of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kelu(m%C3%AB)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kelu(më) (page does not exist)">kelu(më)</a></i> ("stream; fountain, spring") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sindi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sindi (page does not exist)">sindi</a></i> ("river").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
