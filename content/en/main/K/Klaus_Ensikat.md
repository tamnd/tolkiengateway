**Klaus Ensikat** (born [January 16](../_/16_January.md), [1937](../_/1937.md)) is a German illustrator, graphic artist and caricaturist.

After his apprenticeship as a window dresser, Ensikat studied at the College of Applied Arts in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obersch%C3%B6neweide" title="wikipedia:Oberschöneweide">Berlin-Oberschöneweide</a> ([1954](../_/1954.md)- [1958](../_/1958.md)).

Until [1960](../_/1960.md) he worked as a self-employed graphic artist, during the middle-sixties he worked as a teacher at an art college. From [1995](../_/1995.md) to [2002](../_/2002.md) he worked as a teacher for drawing at the college of higher education in Hamburg.

Klaus Ensikat's filigree illustrations received many prizes; in 1995 Ensikat was awarded the *Children's Literature Award* and in [1996](../_/1996.md) he received the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Christian_Andersen_Award" title="wikipedia:Hans Christian Andersen Award">Hans Christian Andersen Award</a> of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Board_on_Books_for_Young_People_(IBBY)" title="wikipedia:International Board on Books for Young People (IBBY)">International Board on Books for Young People (IBBY)</a> for his lifework.

Klaus Ensikat illustrated the second German [The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)-edition of [1971](../_/1971.md) based on the first translation made by [Walter Scherf](../W/Walter_Scherf.md).
