**KUL** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "golden-red".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *kullo* ("red gold"); *kulda*, *kulina* ("flame-coloured, golden-red"); *kuluina* ("orange"); *[kuluma](Kuluma.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *kuldā*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *coll* ("red")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* also appears the root **GUL**, signifying "glow, shine gold or red", with the descendant forms [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *guldā* and [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) *goll* ("red scarlet"). [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) notes, however, that this "original entry was struck out" and replaced by KUL.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
