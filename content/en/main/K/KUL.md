**KUL** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "golden-red".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *kullo* ("red gold"); *kulda*, *kulina* ("flame-coloured, golden-red"); *kuluina* ("orange"); *[kuluma](/wiki/Kuluma "Kuluma")*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *kuldā*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *coll* ("red")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* also appears the root **GUL**, signifying "glow, shine gold or red", with the descendant forms [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *guldā* and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *goll* ("red scarlet"). [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) notes, however, that this "original entry was struck out" and replaced by KUL.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
