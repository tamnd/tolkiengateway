The **King of Khazad-dûm** (*[S.](../S/Sindarin.md)* <i><b>Aran Moria</b></i>) was the [King of Durin's Folk](King_of_Durin's_Folk~7047.md) who ruled from his seat at [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md), the greatest [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md)-city of the [Longbeards](../L/Longbeards.md).

Founded in the [Years of the Trees](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) within the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md) by [Durin the Deathless](../D/Durin_the_Deathless.md), the kingdom lasted for thousands of years before its people were driven into exile by a [Balrog](../B/Balrog.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md). However, according to prophecy, [Durin VII](../D/Durin_VII.md) reclaimed his ancestral throne sometime after [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [171](../F/Fourth_Age_171.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

## List of Kings {#List_of_Kings}

While sixty or more kings might have ruled Khazad-dûm through the ages, only nine have been recorded:

- <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../D/Durin.md" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> - the founder and first King of Khazad-dûm in the [Elder Days](../E/Elder_Days.md).
- [Durin II](../D/Durin_II.md)
- [Durin III](../D/Durin_III.md) - who was a friend of the [Elves of Eregion](../E/Elves_of_Eregion.md) and was given the [first](../R/Ring_of_Thrór.md) of the [Seven Rings](../S/Seven_Rings.md) by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md). He later sealed the [Doors of Durin](../D/Doors_of_Durin.md) when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) destroyed [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1697](../S/Second_Age_1697.md).
- [Durin IV](../D/Durin_IV.md)
- [Durin V](../D/Durin_V.md)
- [Durin VI](../D/Durin_VI.md) - the king whose people delved too deep into the earth in their search for *[mithril](../M/Mithril.md)* and awoke a [Balrog](../D/Durin's_Bane.md). He was killed shortly after its release in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1980](../T/Third_Age_1980.md).
- [Náin I](../N/Náin_I.md) - who was king for less than a year before he too was killed by the Balrog. Durin's folk then fled their ancestral home and Khazad-dûm became known as [Moria](../M/Moria.md), the "Black Pit".
    - [Balin](../B/Balin.md) led a [colony](../B/Balin's_folk.md) that attempted to retake Moria in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2989](../T/Third_Age_2989.md). Balin was a descendant of Durin, but not of the senior male line of descent, while the King of Durin's Folk was [Dáin Ironfoot](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md). As such, Balin ruled only as **[Lord of Moria](../L/Lord_of_Moria.md)** for five years.
- [Durin VII](../D/Durin_VII.md) - the king who returned to and refounded Khazad-dûm sometime after [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [171](../F/Fourth_Age_171.md).
