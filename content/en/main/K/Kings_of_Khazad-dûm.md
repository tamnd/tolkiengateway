The **King of Khazad-dûm** (*[S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin")* <i><b>Aran Moria</b></i>) was the [King of Durin's Folk](/wiki/King_of_Durin%27s_Folk) who ruled from his seat at [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), the greatest [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")-city of the [Longbeards](/wiki/Longbeards).

Founded in the [Years of the Trees](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees) within the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age) by [Durin the Deathless](/wiki/Durin_the_Deathless), the kingdom lasted for thousands of years before its people were driven into exile by a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrog) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980"). However, according to prophecy, [Durin VII](/wiki/Durin_VII) reclaimed his ancestral throne sometime after [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>

## List of Kings {#List_of_Kings}

While sixty or more kings might have ruled Khazad-dûm through the ages, only nine have been recorded:

- <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a> - the founder and first King of Khazad-dûm in the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days).
- [Durin II](/wiki/Durin_II)
- [Durin III](/wiki/Durin_III) - who was a friend of the [Elves of Eregion](/wiki/Elves_of_Eregion) and was given the [first](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r "Ring of Thrór") of the [Seven Rings](/wiki/Seven_Rings) by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor). He later sealed the [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) destroyed [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1697](/wiki/Second_Age_1697 "Second Age 1697").
- [Durin IV](/wiki/Durin_IV)
- [Durin V](/wiki/Durin_V)
- [Durin VI](/wiki/Durin_VI) - the king whose people delved too deep into the earth in their search for *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* and awoke a [Balrog](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane "Durin's Bane"). He was killed shortly after its release in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1980](/wiki/Third_Age_1980 "Third Age 1980").
- [Náin I](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_I) - who was king for less than a year before he too was killed by the Balrog. Durin's folk then fled their ancestral home and Khazad-dûm became known as [Moria](/wiki/Moria), the "Black Pit".
    - [Balin](/wiki/Balin) led a [colony](/wiki/Balin%27s_folk "Balin's folk") that attempted to retake Moria in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2989](/wiki/Third_Age_2989 "Third Age 2989"). Balin was a descendant of Durin, but not of the senior male line of descent, while the King of Durin's Folk was [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot). As such, Balin ruled only as **[Lord of Moria](/wiki/Lord_of_Moria)** for five years.
- [Durin VII](/wiki/Durin_VII) - the king who returned to and refounded Khazad-dûm sometime after [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171").
