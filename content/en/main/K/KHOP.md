KHOP is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [Root](/wiki/Root) signifying harbour. <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - hópa *n.* "haven, harbour, small landlocked bay"
    - hopasse *n.* "harbourage"
- [Noldorin Quenya](/wiki/Noldorin_Quenya) <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - hûb *n.* "haven, harbour, small landlocked bay"
    - hobas *n.* "harbourage"
