<i><b>Khazad Dúm</b></i> is the fourth track of the album [Dol Guldur](</wiki/Dol_Guldur_(album)> "Dol Guldur (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is taken from [Moria](/wiki/Moria), grandest and most famous of the mansions of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves). The lyrics directly quote the [riddle of Strider](/wiki/Riddle_of_Strider "Riddle of Strider") and "[The world was young, the mountains green,](/wiki/The_world_was_young,_the_mountains_green,)".

<p>From ashes and fire be broken <br/>
A light from the shadows shall spring <br/>
Renewed shall be blade that was broken <br/>
The crownless shall again be king <br/>
<br/>
The world is grey, the mountains old <br/>
The forge&#39;s fire is ashen and cold <br/>
No harp is wrung, no hammer falls <br/>
The darkness dwells in Durin&#39;s halls <br/>
<br/>
The shadow lies upon his tomb <br/>
But still the sunken stars appeal <br/>
In the dark and windless Mirrormere <br/>
There lies his crown in water deep <br/>
<br/>
&#39;Til Durin wakes again from sleep <br/>
A deadly sword, a healing hand <br/>
A trumpet - voice, a burning hand <br/>
A lord of Wisdom... <br/>
<br/>
Fire and shadow - both defied <br/>
In Khazad-Dum his wisdom died <br/>
In joy thou hast lived <br/>
<br/>
If thou hearest the cry <br/>
of the Gull on the shore <br/>
Thy soul shall then rest <br/>
in the forest no more...
