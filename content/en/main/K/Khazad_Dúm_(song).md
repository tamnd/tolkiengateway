<i><b>Khazad Dúm</b></i> is the fourth track of the album [Dol Guldur](<../D/Dol_Guldur_(album).md>) by the band [Summoning](../S/Summoning.md). The song name is taken from [Moria](../M/Moria.md), grandest and most famous of the mansions of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md). The lyrics directly quote the [riddle of Strider](../R/Riddle_of_Strider.md) and "[The world was young, the mountains green,](../T/The_world_was_young,_the_mountains_green,.md)".
