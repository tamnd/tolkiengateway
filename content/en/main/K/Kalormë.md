**Kalormë** was a great mountain in [Oronto](/wiki/Oronto) (the farthest East of the world). The peak seems to have been the second tallest mountain in [Arda](/wiki/Arda), corresponding to the [Oiolossë](/wiki/Oioloss%C3%AB) far in the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman").<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*, the name <i><b>kalorme(e)</b></i> is glossed as "hill-crest over which the Sun rises".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later works, Kalormë is not mentioned. However the [Land of the Sun](/wiki/Land_of_the_Sun) was marked by a great, curve-shaped mountain range called the [Walls of the Sun](/wiki/Walls_of_the_Sun), which corresponded symmetrically to the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) of Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It is notable that the name Kalormë is similar to <a class="external text" href="http://narnia.wikia.com/wiki/Calormen" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Calormen</a> of *[The Chronicles of Narnia](/wiki/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia)* by [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis), although it is unknown if [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") thought of this name.
