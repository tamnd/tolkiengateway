**Kalormë** was a great mountain in [Oronto](../O/Oronto.md) (the farthest East of the world). The peak seems to have been the second tallest mountain in [Arda](../A/Arda.md), corresponding to the [Oiolossë](../O/Oiolossë.md) far in the [West](../A/Aman.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](../Q/Qenya_Lexicon.md)*, the name <i><b>kalorme(e)</b></i> is glossed as "hill-crest over which the Sun rises".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In later works, Kalormë is not mentioned. However the [Land of the Sun](../L/Land_of_the_Sun.md) was marked by a great, curve-shaped mountain range called the [Walls of the Sun](../W/Walls_of_the_Sun.md), which corresponded symmetrically to the [Pelóri](../P/Pelóri.md) of Aman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It is notable that the name Kalormë is similar to <a class="external text" href="http://narnia.wikia.com/wiki/Calormen" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Calormen</a> of *[The Chronicles of Narnia](../T/The_Chronicles_of_Narnia.md)* by [C.S. Lewis](../C/C.S._Lewis.md), although it is unknown if [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) thought of this name.
