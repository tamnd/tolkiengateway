The **King of Dale** was the hereditary ruler of [Dale](</wiki/Dale_(Kingdom)> "Dale (Kingdom)"), after its foundation by [Bard](/wiki/Bard) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2944](/wiki/Third_Age_2944 "Third Age 2944"), who himself was a descendant of [Girion](/wiki/Girion), the last [Lord of Dale](/wiki/Lord_of_Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Bain](/wiki/Bain) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2977](/wiki/Third_Age_2977 "Third Age 2977") - [3007](/wiki/Third_Age_3007 "Third Age 3007"))<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Brand](/wiki/Brand) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3007](/wiki/Third_Age_3007 "Third Age 3007") - [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"))<sup id="cite_ref-TA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-2">[2]</a></sup>
