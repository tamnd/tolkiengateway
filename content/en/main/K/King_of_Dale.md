The **King of Dale** was the hereditary ruler of [Dale](<../D/Dale_(Kingdom).md>), after its foundation by [Bard](../B/Bard.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2944](../T/Third_Age_2944.md), who himself was a descendant of [Girion](../G/Girion.md), the last [Lord of Dale](../L/Lord_of_Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-great_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-great-1">[1]</a></sup>
