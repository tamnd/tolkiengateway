<i><b>káno</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) noun for "commander", usually referring to a lesser rank, as one who would serve the highest chief.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It could also mean "crier" or "herald".<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](../S/Sundocarme.md): [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) [KAN](KAN.md)<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <a href="../A/Aracáno.md" title="Aracáno"><i>Ara<b>káno</b></i></a> = "High Chieftain"
- <a href="../F/Fingon.md" title="Fingon"><i>Finde<b>káno</b></i></a> = "Hair Commander"
- <a class="mw-redirect" href="Kanafinwë.md" title="Kanafinwë"><i><b>Kana</b>finwë</i></a> = "Strong-voiced Finwë"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Terc%C3%A1no&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tercáno (page does not exist)">ter<b>cáno</b></a></i> = "herald"
- <a href="../T/Turgon.md" title="Turgon"><i>Turu<b>káno</b></i></a> = "Victory Commander"
