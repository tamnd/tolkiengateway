An ancient title for the rulers of the land of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), of which [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) was the last when he abandoned it leaving no heir.

When [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) came to rule Lórien, they renounced the title of "King" and "Queen" and remained known as [Lord](/wiki/Lord_of_L%C3%B3rien "Lord of Lórien") and [Lady of Lórien](/wiki/Lady_of_L%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Holders {#Holders}

- [Amdír](/wiki/Amd%C3%ADr) (c. [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [587](/wiki/First_Age_587 "First Age 587") - [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434"))
- [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth) (c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3434](/wiki/Second_Age_3434 "Second Age 3434") -[T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"))
