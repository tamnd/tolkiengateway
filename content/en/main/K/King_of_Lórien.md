An ancient title for the rulers of the land of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), of which [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) was the last when he abandoned it leaving no heir.

When [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) came to rule Lórien, they renounced the title of "King" and "Queen" and remained known as [Lord](../L/Lord_of_Lórien.md) and [Lady of Lórien](../L/Lady_of_Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Holders {#Holders}

- [Amdír](../A/Amdír.md) (c. [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [587](../F/First_Age_587.md) - [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md))
- [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) (c. [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3434](../S/Second_Age_3434.md) -[T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md))
