**KHAN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [Root](/wiki/Root) signifying "understand, comprehend". <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - hanya *v.* "to understand, know about, be skilled in dealing with"
    - hande *n.* "knowledge, understanding"
    - handa *adj.* "understanding, intelligent"
    - handele *n. "*intellect"
    - handasse *n.* "intelligence"
- [Exilic Quenya](/wiki/Exilic_Quenya) <sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - henio *v.* "to understand"
    - hann, hand *adj.* "intelligent"
    - hannas *n.* "understanding, intelligence"
