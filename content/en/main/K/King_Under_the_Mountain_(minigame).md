**King Under the Mountain** was the first game in a series of minigames that were made to promote [Turbine](/wiki/Turbine)'s *[Mines of Moria](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Mines_of_Moria "The Lord of the Rings Online: Mines of Moria")*. The minigame was published in [2008](/wiki/2008) on the Mines of Moria's official site, people could play the game by creating a account for free.<sup id="cite_ref-Reminiscence_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reminiscence-2">[2]</a></sup> Players of *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)* had the possibility to link this account to their LOTRO account, which enabled them to get special in-game rewards after they completed a deed.<sup id="cite_ref-Reminiscence_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reminiscence-2">[2]</a></sup> It is not possible to play the minigames anymore, since Mines of Moria's official site has been taken offline.

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

The game has both a singleplayer and a multiplayer, in with maximal 6 people can participate.<sup id="cite_ref-Reminiscence_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reminiscence-2">[2]</a></sup> The player has to conquer all regions of [Moria](/wiki/Moria) in 15 turns. A faction is defeated when he has lost all his regions. If not all factions have been defeated at the game's end, then the player with the most regions wins.<sup id="cite_ref-Reminiscence_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reminiscence-2">[2]</a></sup> After each turn reinforcements are randomly placed in different regions.<sup id="cite_ref-Lotrolife_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lotrolife-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Deeds {#Deeds}

In King Under the Mountain there are three deeds that need to be completed:

- **Durin's Crown**: Win three battles after each other.<sup id="cite_ref-Lotrolife_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lotrolife-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **Warmonger**: Conquer hundred regions.<sup id="cite_ref-Lotrolife_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lotrolife-1">[1]</a></sup>
- **High roller**: Roll thirty or higher in twenty battles.<sup id="cite_ref-Lotrolife_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lotrolife-1">[1]</a></sup>
