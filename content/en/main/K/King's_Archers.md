The **King's Archers** were the feared archers of the armies of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) during the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age).

## History {#History}

At the time when the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) carved out dominions and colonies within [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), they bore [bows](/wiki/Bows "Bows") crafted from hollow steel and their black-feathered arrows were famously one ell long (that is, 1.143 m, or nearly four feet).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is likely that the King's Archers perished during the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor).

<b>2022: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a>:</i></b><i></i>

<dl><dd><b>9 September: <i><a href="/wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)" title="Udûn (episode)">Udûn</a></i>:</b>
<dl><dd>While the army itself is not named, the concept may have served as a possible inspiration for the <b>Queen&#39;s Guard</b>, which appear at <a href="/wiki/Tirharad" title="Tirharad">Tirharad</a> overlooking the battle as the personal guard of <a href="/wiki/M%C3%ADriel" title="Míriel">Queen Regent Míriel</a>. Some of the <a href="/wiki/Men" title="Men">Men</a> of the Queen&#39;s Guard possess bows, though it is hard to tell what color they are or the length of the arrows.</dd></dl></dd></dl>
