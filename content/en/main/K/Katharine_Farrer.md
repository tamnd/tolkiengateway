**Katharine Dorothy Farrer** (nee **Newton**) is an English mystery novelist and a neighbour and frequent correspondent of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

Farrer and her husband <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Farrer" title="wikipedia:Austin Farrer">Austin Farrer</a> were neighbours with the Tolkiens from [1947](/wiki/1947) to [1950](/wiki/1950) when they lived at [3 Manor Road](/wiki/3_Manor_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In [1948](/wiki/1948), Tolkien sent her [a letter](/wiki/Letter_115 "Letter 115") which included manuscripts with both the flat and [Round World version of the Silmarillion](/wiki/Round_World_version_of_the_Silmarillion). She responded back saying "I like the Flat Earth versions best. The hope of Heaven is the only thing which makes modern astronomy tolerable: otherwise there must be an East and a West and Walls: aims and choices and not an endless circle of wandering." Christopher Tolkien believed that this was influential in his father's choice to abandon the round world concept.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Several letters written to her by Tolkien were published in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup>
