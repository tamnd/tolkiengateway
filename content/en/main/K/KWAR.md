**KWAR** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "squeeze, clench".<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *[kwāră, kwārē](/wiki/Kw%C4%81ra "Kwāra")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - Quenya: *[quár, quáre](/wiki/Qu%C3%A1r "Quár")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *[pār](/wiki/P%C4%81r "Pār")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[paur](/wiki/Paur#Sindarin "Paur")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the root KWAR, signifying "clutching hand, fist". Among its derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- Quenya: [*qár*, *qáre*](/wiki/Qu%C3%A1r#Other_verions "Quár")
- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): *pōre*<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *[paur](/wiki/Paur#Noldorin "Paur")*
