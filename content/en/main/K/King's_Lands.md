**King's Lands** was an area adjacent to [Edoras](/wiki/Edoras), including [Harrowdale](/wiki/Harrowdale). With Edoras it was the ward of the [First Marshal of the Riddermark](/wiki/First_Marshal_of_the_Riddermark).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2013: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The area called &#34;Kingstead&#34; encompasses <a href="/wiki/Harrowdale" title="Harrowdale">Harrowdale</a> and <a href="/wiki/Dunharrow" title="Dunharrow">Dunharrow</a>. It includes the smaller hamlets of <a href="/wiki/Upbourn" title="Upbourn">Upbourn</a> and <a href="/wiki/Underharrow" title="Underharrow">Underharrow</a> to the south of <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a> as well as two larger towns north of <a href="/wiki/Snowbourn" title="Snowbourn">Snowbourn</a>: &#34;Middlemead&#34; and <a href="/wiki/Entwade" title="Entwade">Entwade</a>, the latter guarding the ford of the same name.</dd></dl>
