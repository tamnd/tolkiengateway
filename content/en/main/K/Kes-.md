<i><b>kes-</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "to search (for something), to examine (something) in order to find (something)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It has been suggested that the regularized spelling would be *ces-* according to the Quenya of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [KEÞ](/wiki/KE%C3%9E)

## Cognates {#Cognates}

- Quenya *[kesta](/wiki/Kesta "Kesta")*
