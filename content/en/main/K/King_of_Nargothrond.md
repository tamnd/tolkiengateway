**King of Nargothrond** was the ruler of the city and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kingdom_of_Nargothrond&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kingdom of Nargothrond (page does not exist)">its kingdom</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> The King sat in the throne of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) and wore a [silver crown](/wiki/Crown_of_Nargothrond "Crown of Nargothrond").<sup id="cite_ref-Oath_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oath-2">[2]</a></sup>

The title begun by [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") of the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin), who founded the citadel and realm of Nargothrond. Finrod was overlord of all the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) (except the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim), who were [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) ruled by [Círdan the Shipwright](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan "Círdan")), and his vassals were [Angrod](/wiki/Angrod) and [Aegnor](/wiki/Aegnor), who ruled in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), and [Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth), who was the Warden of [Minas Tirith](</wiki/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand)> "Minas Tirith (Beleriand)").<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> He later gave his silver crown to Orodreth, abdicating his Kingship in order to leave and fulfill his oath to [Beren](/wiki/Beren) of the [House of Beor](/wiki/House_of_Beor).<sup id="cite_ref-Oath_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oath-2">[2]</a></sup>
