**King of Nargothrond** was the ruler of the city and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kingdom_of_Nargothrond&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kingdom of Nargothrond (page does not exist)">its kingdom</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> The King sat in the throne of [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) and wore a [silver crown](../C/Crown_of_Nargothrond.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Oath_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oath-2">[2]</a></sup>

The title begun by [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) of the [House of Finarfin](../H/House_of_Finarfin.md), who founded the citadel and realm of Nargothrond. Finrod was overlord of all the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) of [West Beleriand](../W/West_Beleriand.md) (except the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md), who were [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md) ruled by [Círdan the Shipwright](../C/Círdan.md)), and his vassals were [Angrod](../A/Angrod.md) and [Aegnor](../A/Aegnor.md), who ruled in [Dorthonion](../D/Dorthonion.md), and [Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md), who was the Warden of [Minas Tirith](<../M/Minas_Tirith_(Beleriand).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> He later gave his silver crown to Orodreth, abdicating his Kingship in order to leave and fulfill his oath to [Beren](../B/Beren.md) of the [House of Beor](../H/House_of_Beor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Oath_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oath-2">[2]</a></sup>
