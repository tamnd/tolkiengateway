**Kilmessë**, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "self-name", was a [name](/wiki/Ess%C3%AB "Essë") that some of the exiled [Elves](/wiki/Elves) gave to themselves as a disguise, or to refer to deeds or to their personal history.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In early ages, an Elf's "coming of age" when reaching physical maturity (quantolië), was a matter of ceremony, at which their essekilmë or personal ‘chosen-name’ was announced.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an account in "[Laws and Customs Among the Eldar](/wiki/Laws_and_Customs_Among_the_Eldar)" in *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*, the kilmessë is a name chosen by an Elf-child once she or he is capable of *lámatyáve*, that is once they gain fluency in their mother-tongue and find pleasure in the sounds and forms of words. It is a private name, used only by family members and close friends, and reflects the child's personality or talents. It is announced in a ceremony called *Essecilmë* (name-choosing).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
