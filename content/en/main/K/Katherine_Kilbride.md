**Katherine 'Kitty' Kilbride** ([1900](/wiki/1900)-[1966](/wiki/1966)) was a student of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and [E.V. Gordon](/wiki/E.V._Gordon) at the [University of Leeds](/wiki/University_of_Leeds). Kilbride would become a good friend of Tolkien.

Kilbride was “an invalid all her life”, according to her nephew, “and was much cheered by \[Tolkien’s\] chatty letters and cards ... books were given to her as they were published”.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

She was among the recipients to whom Tolkien sent inscribed editions of the newly published *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, on or close after [21 September](/wiki/21_September) [1937](/wiki/1937).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
