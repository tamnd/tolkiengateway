**Katherine 'Kitty' Kilbride** ([1900](../_/1900.md)-[1966](../_/1966.md)) was a student of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and [E.V. Gordon](../E/E.V._Gordon.md) at the [University of Leeds](../U/University_of_Leeds.md). Kilbride would become a good friend of Tolkien.

Kilbride was “an invalid all her life”, according to her nephew, “and was much cheered by \[Tolkien’s\] chatty letters and cards ... books were given to her as they were published”.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

She was among the recipients to whom Tolkien sent inscribed editions of the newly published *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, on or close after [21 September](../_/21_September.md) [1937](../_/1937.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
