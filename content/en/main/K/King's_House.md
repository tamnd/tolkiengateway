The **King's House** was a building where the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) lived.

## History {#History}

While the south kingdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) was co-ruled by [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), the house of Isildur was in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) and the house of Anárion was in [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor), but their thrones were side by side in the [Great Hall of Osgiliath](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Osgiliath) in the city of [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), the capital of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After the death of Anárion in the [siege](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr "Siege of Barad-dûr") of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3440](/wiki/Second_Age_3440 "Second Age 3440"), the co-ruling of the kingdom of Gondor was abandoned and Isildur committed the rule of Gondor to Anárion's son, [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil), when he left Gondor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> to travel to the north kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) to assume the high kingship over Arnor and Gondor, because his father [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) had died in combat with [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> During the earlier history of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the King's House was located in [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath).<sup id="cite_ref-Tarondor_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tarondor-5">[5]</a></sup> After King [Ostoher](/wiki/Ostoher) rebuilt Minas Anor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [420](/wiki/Third_Age_420 "Third Age 420")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the Kings of Gondor lived in Minas Anor in the summer instead of in Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Queen [Berúthiel](/wiki/Ber%C3%BAthiel), the wife of King [Tarannon](/wiki/Tarannon_Falastur "Tarannon Falastur"), was said to prefer living in the King's House in Osgiliath to the house that her husband had built by the [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin) south of [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir). While living in the King's House, she kept her chambers bare and had the gardens filled with tormented sculptures.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636") a large number of people, especially in Osgiliath, including King [Telemnar](/wiki/Telemnar) and all his children, died in the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) that had come from the East. Four years later, in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1640](/wiki/Third_Age_1640 "Third Age 1640")<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> his nephew, King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Tarondor" title="Tarondor">Tarondor</a>, permanently moved the residence of the King to the King's House in Minas Anor, because Osgiliath had become partly uninhabited and began to fall into ruin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tarondor_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tarondor-5">[5]</a></sup> The King's House was located on the west side of the [Citadel](/wiki/Citadel_of_Gondor "Citadel of Gondor") of Minas Anor behind the [White Tower](/wiki/Tower_of_Ecthelion "Tower of Ecthelion") and the [Hall of the Kings](/wiki/Tower_Hall "Tower Hall"), which stood at the centre of the Citadel.<sup id="cite_ref-SD_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-10">[10]</a></sup> it is possible that the King's House used to be the former summer residence of the kings in Minas Arnor.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Although the location of the King's House in Minas Tirith is not mentioned in the narrative of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, it is shown on a small plan of the Citadel that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) drew on a page with a rough draft for the passage about Merethrond, the [Great Hall of Feasts](/wiki/Great_Hall_of_Feasts), in the chapter [Many Partings](/wiki/Many_Partings).<sup id="cite_ref-SD_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-10">[10]</a></sup>
