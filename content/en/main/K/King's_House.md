The **King's House** was a building where the [Kings of Gondor](Kings_of_Gondor.md) lived.

## History {#History}

While the south kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) was co-ruled by [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md), the house of Isildur was in [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) and the house of Anárion was in [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md), but their thrones were side by side in the [Great Hall of Osgiliath](../G/Great_Hall_of_Osgiliath.md) in the city of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), the capital of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After the death of Anárion in the [siege](../S/Siege_of_Barad-dûr.md) of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3440](../S/Second_Age_3440.md), the co-ruling of the kingdom of Gondor was abandoned and Isildur committed the rule of Gondor to Anárion's son, [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md), when he left Gondor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> to travel to the north kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) to assume the high kingship over Arnor and Gondor, because his father [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) had died in combat with [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3441](../S/Second_Age_3441.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> During the earlier history of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the King's House was located in [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tarondor_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tarondor-5">[5]</a></sup> After King [Ostoher](../O/Ostoher.md) rebuilt Minas Anor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [420](../T/Third_Age_420.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the Kings of Gondor lived in Minas Anor in the summer instead of in Osgiliath.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

Queen [Berúthiel](../B/Berúthiel.md), the wife of King [Tarannon](../T/Tarannon_Falastur.md), was said to prefer living in the King's House in Osgiliath to the house that her husband had built by the [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) south of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md). While living in the King's House, she kept her chambers bare and had the gardens filled with tormented sculptures.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md) a large number of people, especially in Osgiliath, including King [Telemnar](../T/Telemnar.md) and all his children, died in the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) that had come from the East. Four years later, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1640](../T/Third_Age_1640.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> his nephew, King <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../T/Tarondor.md" title="Tarondor">Tarondor</a>, permanently moved the residence of the King to the King's House in Minas Anor, because Osgiliath had become partly uninhabited and began to fall into ruin.<sup id="cite_ref-Tarondor_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tarondor-5">[5]</a></sup> The King's House was located on the west side of the [Citadel](../C/Citadel_of_Gondor.md) of Minas Anor behind the [White Tower](../T/Tower_of_Ecthelion.md) and the [Hall of the Kings](../T/Tower_Hall.md), which stood at the centre of the Citadel.<sup id="cite_ref-SD_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-10">[10]</a></sup> it is possible that the King's House used to be the former summer residence of the kings in Minas Arnor.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Although the location of the King's House in Minas Tirith is not mentioned in the narrative of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, it is shown on a small plan of the Citadel that [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) drew on a page with a rough draft for the passage about Merethrond, the [Great Hall of Feasts](../G/Great_Hall_of_Feasts.md), in the chapter [Many Partings](../M/Many_Partings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SD_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SD-10">[10]</a></sup>
