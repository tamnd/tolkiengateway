**KYELEP** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "[silver](/wiki/Silver "Silver")".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *[telep-, telpe](/wiki/Telepi "Telepi")*<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[telpe, tyelpe, tyelep-](/wiki/Telpe "Telpe")* ("silver")<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *celeb*<sup id="cite_ref-UT6e_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT6e-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[celeb](/wiki/Celeb "Celeb")* ("silver"); *[celebren](/wiki/Celebren "Celebren")* ("of silver")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

The original entry in the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* had "**KYELEP-** and **TELEP-** silver". [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") rewrote the section, changing to "**KYÉLEP-** (and **TÉLEP-?**) silver".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
