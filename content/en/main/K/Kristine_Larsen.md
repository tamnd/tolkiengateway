**Kristine Larsen** is an American astronomer who teaches at Central Connecticut State University. She regularly presents and writes about the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), often with an emphasis on the astronomy and cosmology of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). Her articles have appeared in numerous journals including *[Mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn)*, *[Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen)* and the *[Journal of Tolkien Research](/wiki/Journal_of_Tolkien_Research)*.
