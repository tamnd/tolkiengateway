The **King of Doriath** was the title of two Elf-lords of the [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar), who ruled the kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) for many centuries, from its foundation long before the beginning of the [Years of the Sun](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun), until its destruction at the hands of the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) during the [Second Kinslaying](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

**[Elu Thingol](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol"):** The founder of Doriath with his queen, [Melian](/wiki/Melian). He was lord there for millennia, and in that time his realm was protected from foes by the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian) (after the return of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)). Thingol was slain by the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [503](/wiki/First_Age_503 "First Age 503"), and Melian departed from the realm. Thingol was succeeded by their grandson, the son of their daughter [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien). As ruler, Thingol was further acknowledged as the high-king of the Sindar in Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

**[Dior Eluchíl](/wiki/Dior "Dior"):** After Thingol's death he came to [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) and ruled the kingdom of his grandfather. Because Dior's treasuries held a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmarils "Silmarils"), the Sons of Fëanor assailed the citadel of Menegroth to recover it. There Dior was slain, and the kingdom ended in [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506").
