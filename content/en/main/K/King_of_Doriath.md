The **King of Doriath** was the title of two Elf-lords of the [Sindar](../S/Sindar.md), who ruled the kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) for many centuries, from its foundation long before the beginning of the [Years of the Sun](../Y/Years_of_the_Sun.md), until its destruction at the hands of the [Sons of Fëanor](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md) during the [Second Kinslaying](../S/Second_Kinslaying.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

**[Elu Thingol](../T/Thingol.md):** The founder of Doriath with his queen, [Melian](../M/Melian.md). He was lord there for millennia, and in that time his realm was protected from foes by the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md) (after the return of [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)). Thingol was slain by the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [503](../F/First_Age_503.md), and Melian departed from the realm. Thingol was succeeded by their grandson, the son of their daughter [Lúthien](../L/Lúthien.md). As ruler, Thingol was further acknowledged as the high-king of the Sindar in Beleriand.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

**[Dior Eluchíl](../D/Dior.md):** After Thingol's death he came to [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md) and ruled the kingdom of his grandfather. Because Dior's treasuries held a [Silmaril](../S/Silmarils.md), the Sons of Fëanor assailed the citadel of Menegroth to recover it. There Dior was slain, and the kingdom ended in [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [506](../F/First_Age_506.md).
