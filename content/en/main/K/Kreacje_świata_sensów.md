**Kreacje świata sensów: szkice o współczesnej powieści angielskiej** (*Creations of the world of senses: Essays on contemporary British novels*) is a Polish-language book. It contains four articles, which treat William Golding, John Ballard, T.H. White and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), respectively. The article treating Tolkien, "Konwencje gatunkowe i rodzajowe w trylogii J.R.R. Tolkiena" ("Genre and form conventions in J.R.R. Tolkien's trilogy"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> discusses issues of time and space in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and other topics.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
