**Kip Rasmussen** is an illustrator of the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).

One of his aims as an artist is to promote *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* to fans of Tolkien's more familiar works.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> His work is featured in the *[Tolkien Calendar 2023](/wiki/Tolkien_Calendar_2023)*.
