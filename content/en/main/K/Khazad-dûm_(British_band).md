**Khazad-dûm** is a funeral doom/death metal band from Reading, England, formed in 2014.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The band takes its name from [Khazad-dûm](Khazad-dûm.md), the ancient Dwarven realm also known as Moria.

## Members {#Members}

- Daniel Scrivener - all instruments, clean vocals
- Matthew Surry - lead vocals
