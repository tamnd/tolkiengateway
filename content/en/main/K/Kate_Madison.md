**Kate Madison** (born [1978](/wiki/1978)) is a British filmmaker and actress. She was the director of and an actor in the fan film *[Born of Hope](/wiki/Born_of_Hope)*. In the film, she portrayed Elgarain, a [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") and close friend of [Arathorn](/wiki/Arathorn_II "Arathorn II"). Notably, Elgarain is an original character created specifically for the film and does not appear in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
