<i><b>Klett-Cotta: Das erste Jahrzehnt 1977-1987: Ein Almanach</b></i> (German: *Klett-Cotta: The first decade 1977-1987: An almanac*) is a book by the German publisher <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klett-Cotta_Verlag" title="wikipedia:Klett-Cotta Verlag">Klett-Cotta Verlag</a> that commemorates its first decade of existence. It is notable for containing *[Mîms Klage](/wiki/The_Complaint_of_M%C3%AEm_the_Dwarf "The Complaint of Mîm the Dwarf")*,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> a german translation by [Hans J. Schütz](/wiki/Hans_J._Sch%C3%BCtz) of a poem by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
