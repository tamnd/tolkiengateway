<i><b>karma</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word for "helm".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> In the orthography used in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, *karma* would probably be written *carma*, since Tolkien usually represented the \[k\]-sound with the letter "c".

It often appears as *-car* (or *-kar*) in compounds.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>
