**Kheledûl** is a Dwarven port at the [Lhûn](/wiki/Lh%C3%BBn) held by the [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) in the game *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*.

## History {#History}

Kheledûl was originally founded by [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) when he built [Thorin's Hall](/wiki/Thorin%27s_Hall), as a port to trade with Zigilgund upstream in [Forochel](/wiki/Forochel), or maybe with the Elves of [Duillond](/wiki/Duillond), [Celondim](/wiki/Celondim) and [Mithlond](/wiki/Mithlond), and far away in the south to the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When he found the city, he built a spire: the founding Spire of Kheledûl.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

But at the time of the War of the Ring, the docks are overtaken by [Skorgrím](/wiki/Skorgr%C3%ADm)'s [Dourhands](/wiki/Dourhands) and it is now an evil place to come. They use it as a base to further the operations of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Kheledûl* is comprised of the [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) word *kheled*, which means "glass", such as in *Kheled-zâram*, [Mirrormere](/wiki/Mirrormere),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and *ûl*, which has no particular meaning.
