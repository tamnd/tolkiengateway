**Kalimban** was the name of a region in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), later known as <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany" title="wikipedia:Germany">Germany</a>, according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Kal_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kal-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its inhabitants were known as <i><b>Kalimbardi</b></i> (i.e. <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans" title="wikipedia:Germans">Germans</a>).<sup id="cite_ref-Kal_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kal-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Kalimban* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), glossed as "Barbary, Germany", being an elaboration on <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Kalimbo_(Qenya_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kalimbo (Qenya word) (page does not exist)">kalimbo</a></i> ("barbarian").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

*Kalimbardi* was probably composed of *kalimbo* + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Mardo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mardo (page does not exist)">mardo</a></i> ("dweller").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The unflattering associations of Germany and the German people with barbarism were written by [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) during the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I" title="wikipedia:World War I">World War I</a>, in which it wasn't uncommon for the English people and military to characterize their enemy as being savage and inhuman.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
