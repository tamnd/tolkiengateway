**Karsh** is an original character in the *[Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_Anthology "The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth Anthology")* video game series, developed by [Electronic Arts](/wiki/Electronic_Arts). He is a playable hero belonging to the [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) faction, where he is voiced by [Jason Connery](/wiki/Jason_Connery).

## History {#History}

Karsh was born under the name **Carthean** in the kingdom of [Cardolan](/wiki/Cardolan). In life, he was a celebrated general and fought against the [Witch-king of Angmar](/wiki/Witch-king "Witch-king").

At some point, he was made into a wraith by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Morgomir&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgomir (page does not exist)">Morgomir</a>, and became known thereafter as Karsh. He serves the Iron Crown of [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar).
