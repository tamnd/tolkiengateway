The term **Kinslaying** refers to the instances where [Elves](/wiki/Elves) fought and killed other Elves. This happened only three times in history, and all of them were done by the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) during their quest to recover the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils):

- The **First Kinslaying**, also known as the [Kinslaying at Alqualondë](/wiki/Kinslaying_at_Alqualond%C3%AB), in which [Telerin Elves](/wiki/Teleri "Teleri") were killed over the usage of their ships.
- The **[Second Kinslaying](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying)**, which contributed to the fall of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), was fought over [Dior](/wiki/Dior)'s [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril).
- The **[Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying)** occurred at the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) and was fought over [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing)'s (Dior's daughter) [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril). The Third Kinslaying was considered the worst of the three.
