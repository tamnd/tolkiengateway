**Karolina Stopa-Olszańska 'Melinir'** is a member of the [Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](/wiki/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_%C5%9AKF "Sekcja Tolkienowska ŚKF"), and an illustrator whose work has been published in several Polish [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) [calendars](/wiki/Category:Calendars "Category:Calendars").
