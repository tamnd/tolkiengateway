**Karolina Stopa-Olszańska 'Melinir'** is a member of the [Tolkien Section of the Silesian Fantasy Club](../S/Sekcja_Tolkienowska_ŚKF.md), and an illustrator whose work has been published in several Polish [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) [calendars](../../category/C/Calendars.md).
