**Karl-Heinz Urban** is a New Zealand-born actor. He played [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").
