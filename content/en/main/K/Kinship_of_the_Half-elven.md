**Kinship of the Half-elven** refers to a manuscript by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), enclosed by him in a [1964 letter to Eileen Elgar](</wiki/Letter_to_Eileen_Elgar_(5_March_1964)> "Letter to Eileen Elgar (5 March 1964)") (together with [Concerning ... 'The Hoard'](/wiki/Concerning_..._%27The_Hoard%27)).

The manuscript consists of a family tree illustrating the 'Kinship of the [Half-elven](/wiki/Half-elven)' (so entitled), together with an explanatory note.

The genealogical chart was reproduced in *[Sotheby's English Literature and English History 6-7 December 1984](/wiki/Sotheby%27s_English_Literature_and_English_History_6-7_December_1984)* (the letter and manuscript were sold on 6 December 1984 at Sotheby's, London), and in *[Beyond Bree](/wiki/Beyond_Bree)* [May 1985](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_May_1985 "Beyond Bree May 1985") (with further discussion and explanation of the family tree published in the [June 1985 issue](/wiki/Beyond_Bree_June_1985 "Beyond Bree June 1985")).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The letter and manuscript then appeared at auction on [16 July](/wiki/16_July), [2022](/wiki/2022) and sold for $150,000 to an unknown buyer.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Extract {#Extract}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>The <i><a href="/wiki/Half-elven" title="Half-elven">Half-elven</a></i> (Pereldar) were thus all closely akin, being descended on the mortal side from the <a href="/wiki/House_of_Hador" title="House of Hador">House of <i>Hador</i></a>, and the <a href="/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor" title="House of Bëor">House of <i>Bëor</i></a>: the two chief houses of the Elf-friends (<a href="/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain" title="Dúnedain">Dúnedain</a>) from whom most of the <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Numenoreans</a> came; and on the elvish side from the <a href="/wiki/House_of_Finw%C3%AB" title="House of Finwë"><i>House of Finwe</i></a> (the greatest of the Kings of the <a href="/wiki/Calaquendi" title="Calaquendi">High-elves</a> who went to the <a href="/wiki/Aman" title="Aman">Blessed Realm</a>) and from the <a href="/wiki/House_of_Elw%C3%AB" title="House of Elwë">House of <i>Elwë</i></a> (<i>Elu-<a href="/wiki/Thingol" title="Thingol">thingol</a></i>) who remained in the west of <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>. But since <i><a href="/wiki/Melian" title="Melian">Melian</a></i>, wife of Thingol and mother of <a href="/wiki/L%C3%BAthien" title="Lúthien">Lúthien</a> came of the people of the <a href="/wiki/Valar" title="Valar">Valar</a> a strain of the &#39;race divine&#39; was also possessed by them.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>J.R.R. Tolkien, [Explanatory note]<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>
