**Kiryandil** was the father of [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md), according to a draft version of the *[Appendices](../A/Appendices.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Man_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Man-2">[1]</a></sup>

In an early <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Manuscript_A_(The_Southern_Line)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Manuscript A (The Southern Line) (page does not exist)">manuscript</a> of what would become the *[Appendix A](../A/Appendix_A.md)* of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, Eärnil II was presented as the son of a man called Kiryandil, who was in turn the son of [Siriondil](<../S/Siriondil_(son_of_Calimmacil).md>), son of [Kalimmakil](../C/Calimmacil.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 2]</a></sup> son of king [Narmakil II](../N/Narmacil_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Man_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Man-2">[1]</a></sup>

However, soon after, the character of Kiryandil disappeared completely, and Eärnil instead became the son of Siriondil, son of Kalimmakil, son of Narmakil II.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>

Ultimately, however, in the published version of the *Appendix A*, the genealogy of Eärnil changed once again - there he is described as the son of Siriondil, son of Calimmacil, son of [Arciryas](../A/Arciryas.md), a newly created character envisioned as the brother of king Narmacil II.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[3]</a></sup>
