**Kiryandil** was the father of [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II), according to a draft version of the *[Appendices](/wiki/Appendices)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Man_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Man-2">[1]</a></sup>

In an early <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Manuscript_A_(The_Southern_Line)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Manuscript A (The Southern Line) (page does not exist)">manuscript</a> of what would become the *[Appendix A](/wiki/Appendix_A)* of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, Eärnil II was presented as the son of a man called Kiryandil, who was in turn the son of [Siriondil](</wiki/Siriondil_(son_of_Calimmacil)> "Siriondil (son of Calimmacil)"), son of [Kalimmakil](/wiki/Calimmacil "Calimmacil"),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 2]</a></sup> son of king [Narmakil II](/wiki/Narmacil_II "Narmacil II").<sup id="cite_ref-Man_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Man-2">[1]</a></sup>

However, soon after, the character of Kiryandil disappeared completely, and Eärnil instead became the son of Siriondil, son of Kalimmakil, son of Narmakil II.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[2]</a></sup>

Ultimately, however, in the published version of the *Appendix A*, the genealogy of Eärnil changed once again - there he is described as the son of Siriondil, son of Calimmacil, son of [Arciryas](/wiki/Arciryas), a newly created character envisioned as the brother of king Narmacil II.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[3]</a></sup>
