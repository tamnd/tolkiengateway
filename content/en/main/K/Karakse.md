<i><b>karakse</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word meaning "jagged hedge of spikes".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

It has been suggested that a regularized spelling of *karakse* according to the style of Quenya used in *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* would yield the form *caraxë*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
