In [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md), **Koronandë** was a coastal republic located in the [Mûmakan](../M/Mûmakan.md) region. Founded around [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_100&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 100 (page does not exist)">100</a>, it was unique in the region for its republican government and its mixed population of Kirani Men and Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-koronande_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koronande-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-history_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-2">[2]</a></sup>

Koronandë was the primary commercial power of the Mûmakan. To protect its merchant interests, the republic maintained a powerful navy. It was the only maritime force in the region capable of contesting the navy of neighbouring [Tantûrak](../T/Tantûrak.md).<sup id="cite_ref-koronande_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koronande-1">[1]</a></sup>

The republic was governed by a senate of twelve members, each of its six districts electing two representatives. Political posts were generally held by Kirani, while Elves preferred to advise rather than bother with complicated politics. <sup id="cite_ref-koronande_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koronande-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the middle of the Second Age, Koronandë's status as the primary commercial hub of the Mûmakan was challenged by the rapid expansion of the neighbouring colony of Tantûrak. As the imperialist policies of [Tar-Ciryatan](../T/Tar-Ciryatan.md) intensified, maritime traffic shifted from Korlan to its port of Sarûl, and the appearance of warships in the Bay of [Ûsakan](../_/Ûsakan.md) sparked fears that the republic would soon be conquered.

In [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1977&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1977 (page does not exist)">1977</a>, this anxiety facilitated a coup in which the senate was dissolved. A governor and merchant, [Jí Indûr](../F/Fourth_of_the_Nazgûl.md), centralized the government to address the Númenórean threat more effectively, though supported by an advisory council of oligarchs. By [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1978&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1978 (page does not exist)">1978</a>, the republic was formally reorganized into a kingdom, with the "King of Korlan" ruling over a centralized state. This radical transition plunged the region into twenty-three years of civil rebellion. The monarchy ultimately collapsed shortly after [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a>. During a public rally intended to mobilize the populace against the threat of the Magician in Tantûrak, Korlan's governor Loran Klien successfully appealed to the crowd for a return to traditional republican rule. The resulting riots drove king into exile and restored the Republic.

Tantûrak declared war on Koronandë in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1365&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1365 (page does not exist)">1365</a>, and during the seven years of conflict, the republic came close to losing. It was only through the intervention of the Mûmakani emperor Jí Amaav III, in truth their now-immortal former king, that a treaty was brokered in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1372&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1372 (page does not exist)">1372</a>. Koronandë was saved, but came under the influence of the empire that had saved it.<sup id="cite_ref-Nazgul_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nazgul-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The capital of the republic was Korlan, a major centre of commerce located at the mouth of the Koros River.

While the Kirani dominated the cities, the Elves inhabited the more rural, forested areas of the country, often building their homes high in the trees.<sup id="cite_ref-koronande_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koronande-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Tauronde {#Tauronde}

The most significant Elven settlement was Tauronde, a city presiding over a district of the same name located north and west of Korlan. Unlike the rest of the republic, Tauronde was almost entirely Elven in population, and both its governor and senate representatives were thus Elves.

The city was built into a cluster of Lurak trees, which grew to heights exceeding 200 feet.<sup id="cite_ref-koronande_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koronande-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
