The **Kinslaying at Alqualondë**, also known as the **First Kinslaying**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was an important event in the history of the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) in which the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) killed [Telerin](/wiki/Teleri "Teleri") Elves in order to gain their ships.

## History {#History}

When [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) decided to leave [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) he needed ships to travel to Middle-earth, but the Noldor possessed no ships, and Fëanor feared that any delay in their departure would cause the Noldor to reconsider. The Noldor, led by Fëanor and [his sons](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor "Sons of Fëanor"), tried to persuade the [Falmari](/wiki/Falmari) of [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB), to give him their [Swan-ships](/wiki/Swan-ships). However, King [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) and his people would not help them, since it was against the will of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), and in fact attempted to persuade their friends to reconsider their decision to leave. Displeased with the Teleri's answer, the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) started taking the ships by force. This angered the Teleri, and they threatened the Noldor with rocks and arrows, and they threw many of Fëanor's Noldor out of the ships into the harbour. They also attempted to block the harbour.

Then the Noldor drew swords, and the Teleri their [bows](/wiki/Bows "Bows"), and there was a bitter fight that seemed evenly matched, until the second Host of the Noldor, led by [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon), arrived together with some of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin)'s people. Misunderstanding the situation, they assumed the Teleri had attacked the Noldor under orders of the Valar, and they joined the fight. In the end, many Teleri were slain and the ships were taken. The Noldor that continued [their flight](/wiki/Flight_of_the_Noldor "Flight of the Noldor") towards [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) were therefore [cursed](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos "Doom of Mandos") by [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

In [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), [King](/wiki/King_of_Doriath "King of Doriath") [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) hosted the Noldor, but learning about the slaying of his kinsmen, banned [their language](/wiki/Exilic_Quenya "Exilic Quenya") and refused to participate in the [War of the Jewels](/wiki/Battles_of_Beleriand "Battles of Beleriand"). He also forbade them to enter [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) save the [House of Finarfin](/wiki/House_of_Finarfin) whose dynasts were his closest kin through [Eärwen](/wiki/E%C3%A4rwen) the daughter of his brother Olwë.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

During the sixth [solar century](/wiki/Years_of_the_Sun "Years of the Sun") of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), the final ruin of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) where [Dior Eluchíl](/wiki/Dior "Dior") was killed by the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) was referred to as the **[Second Kinslaying](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying)** in the chronicles of the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ToY_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ToY-6">[6]</a></sup> and the attack by the Sons of Fëanor on the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion) where [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) was attacked was called the **[Third Kinslaying](/wiki/Third_Kinslaying)**, the cruellest of all three.<sup id="cite_ref-ToY_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ToY-6">[6]</a></sup>

When [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) sought the help of Valinor, the Teleri agreed to bring ships to Middle-earth, but their role was limited to this, refusing to fight on the side of the Noldor. After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath) ended, it was stated by [Eönwë](/wiki/E%C3%B6nw%C3%AB) herald of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) that because of these evil deeds the remaining sons of Fëanor, [Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) and [Maglor](/wiki/Maglor), had lost all right to the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
