<i><b>Kelvaquenta</b></i> (composed of the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) words *[kelvar](/wiki/Kelvar "Kelvar")* + *[quenta](/wiki/Quenta "Quenta")*) was the journal of the Linguistic Fellowship of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Cleveland_Tolkien_Society&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Cleveland Tolkien Society (page does not exist)">Cleveland Tolkien Society</a>. The journal was a continuation of the *Cleveland Tolkien Society Newsletter* (numbering uninterrupted). Vol. 1, no. 3 (Oct. 1977) - Vol. 1, no. 5 (Spring 1978) are kept in the [J.R.R. Tolkien Collection at the Marquette Archives](/wiki/Marquette_University "Marquette University").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
