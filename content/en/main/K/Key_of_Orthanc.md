The **Key of Orthanc** (often referred to in the plural as the "keys") granted entry to the otherwise impenetrable tower of [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc). The keys were large, intricate in shape, black in color, and secured together on a steel ring.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The tower of Orthanc was built between [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320"), the year in which [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) were established, and [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3430](/wiki/Second_Age_3430 "Second Age 3430"), when the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](/wiki/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men) was first formed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Presumably as the tower was completed its doors and the keys that unlocked them were fashioned.

In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon), which was never densely populated, gradually lost most of its people. Eventually the tower of Orthanc became deserted and its keys were removed to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) and kept by the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"),<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-4">[4]</a></sup> the lands of [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon) became the kingdom of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), although Gondor retained control of Isengard and Orthanc. During this time Isengard was manned by a small number of Gondorians led by a hereditary Captain. However the keys of Orthanc were kept by the Stewards of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Over time, while retaining the keys of Orthanc, the Stewards forsook the tower as their thoughts looked eastwards. Eventually Isengard came under the control of the Dunlendings who led numerous raids on the [Westfold](/wiki/Westfold). The Dunlendings were eventually starved out in the great famine after the [Long Winter](/wiki/Long_Winter). [King Fréaláf](/wiki/Fr%C3%A9al%C3%A1f "Fréaláf") and [Steward Beren](</wiki/Beren_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Beren (Steward of Gondor)") welcomed [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman)'s offer to take command of Isengard and in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2759](/wiki/Third_Age_2759 "Third Age 2759")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> the wizard was given the keys of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-4">[4]</a></sup>

A week before [22 August](/wiki/22_August), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> when [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") and company arrived at Orthanc, Saruman had departed Isengard with [Gríma Wormtongue](/wiki/Gr%C3%ADma "Gríma"). However, before he left, [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) made him lock the tower and hand over the keys of Orthanc.<sup id="cite_ref-Partings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Partings-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the fall of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), one of [King Elessar's](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") first tasks in the re-ordering of his realm was the restoration of Orthanc. He ordered that the *[palantír](/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri "Palantíri")* recovered from Saruman should be returned to the tower. Having access with the key of Orthanc it was then that the tower was searched and many secrets were revealed.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
