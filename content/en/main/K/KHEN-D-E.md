**KHEN-D-E** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "eye".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[hen](/wiki/Hen "Hen")*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *hên* ("eye")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup> (cf. [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[hen](/wiki/Hen "Hen")*)
