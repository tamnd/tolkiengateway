**Kieron Elliott** is a Scottish actor. He voicematched [Billy Boyd](/wiki/Billy_Boyd)'s role of [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") in *[The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn's Quest](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
