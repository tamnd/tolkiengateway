**KAN** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) root signifying "cry, call aloud".<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- ?[Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian):<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> *kānō*<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[káno](/wiki/K%C3%A1no "Káno")* "crier", "herald"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *[cāno](/wiki/C%C4%81no "Cāno")* "herald"; *[can-](/wiki/Can- "Can-")* "cry aloud, call" or "to summons or name a person"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[can-](/wiki/Can- "Can-")* "cry out, shout, call"; *[caun](/wiki/Caun "Caun")* "outcry, clamour"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
