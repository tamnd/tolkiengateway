**KAN** is a [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) root signifying "cry, call aloud".<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- ?[Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md):<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup> *kānō*<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[káno](Káno.md)* "crier", "herald"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md): *[cāno](../C/Cāno.md)* "herald"; *[can-](../C/Can-.md)* "cry aloud, call" or "to summons or name a person"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[can-](../C/Can-.md)* "cry out, shout, call"; *[caun](../C/Caun.md)* "outcry, clamour"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
