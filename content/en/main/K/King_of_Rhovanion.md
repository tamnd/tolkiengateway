**King of Rhovanion** was a title claimed by [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) rulers of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion).

[Vidugavia](/wiki/Vidugavia), a prince of the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen), in the 13th century of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) claimed such a title, and his realm, the "Kingdom of Rhovanion", was between [Greenwood](/wiki/Mirkwood "Mirkwood") and the [River Running](/wiki/River_Running). However, even if he was the most powerful of the many Northern princes, he never truly unified all the Northmen under his claimed kingship.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Vidugavia had a line of successors, and it seems they may have continued to style themselves as "Kings of Rhovanion" after Vidugavia's time. The realm of the "Kings of Rhovanion" lay beyond Mirkwood before the invasions of the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1851](/wiki/Third_Age_1851 "Third Age 1851")).<sup id="cite_ref-eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eorl-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 1]</a></sup> In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1856](/wiki/Third_Age_1856 "Third Age 1856"), after the disastrous [Battle of the Plains](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Plains), remnants of this people were gathered by [Marhwini](/wiki/Marhwini) son of Marhari (who fell in the rearguard action after the Battle of the Plains). Passing north between Mirkwood and Anduin they settled in the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin). where they were joined by many fugitives who came through the Forest. This was the beginning of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), though nothing was known of it in Gondor for many years. The [Lords of the Éothéod](/wiki/Lord_of_the_%C3%89oth%C3%A9od "Lord of the Éothéod") therefore claimed descent from those kings of Rhovanion.<sup id="cite_ref-eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

Around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975"), "proud princes of Rhovanion"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 2]</a></sup> with their horses of the Vales of Anduin joined the [Host of the West](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") under Prince [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur).<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-1">[1]</a></sup>
