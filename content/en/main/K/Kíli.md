**Kíli** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864") – [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941")<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> 77 years old) was the son of [Dís](/wiki/D%C3%ADs), the sister of King [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin), and brother of [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli).

Kíli was one of the thirteen [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) who took part in the quest for [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), which was [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)'s great adventure. The brothers were the youngest of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) by some 50 years,<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-2">[2]</a></sup> with Kíli being five years younger than his brother.<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> The two brothers were inseparable throughout the adventure.

The brothers, along with Thorin himself, did not survive the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).

## History {#History}

Before the quest to Erebor, Fíli and Kíli probably lived in the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) where their uncle resided after the disappearance of [Thráin II](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in "Thráin").<sup id="cite_ref-Line_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Line-1">[1]</a></sup> As Thorin's sister-sons, they accompanied their uncle when he set out to challenge the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

When Bilbo first met Kíli and Fíli (in the third group of Dwarves to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End)) they wore blue hoods. After supper, when the Dwarves made music, Kíli and Fíli played small fiddles that they had brought in bags.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Early on the night when the company encountered the [trolls](/wiki/Trolls "Trolls") one of the ponies became frightened and bolted into the river; Kíli and Fíli nearly drowned getting the beast out of the water.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Up in the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) during the thunder-battle the two brothers, being the youngest Dwarves with the keenest eyes, were sent ahead to look for shelter. They found a small dry cave which was perfect for the company (until the [Goblins](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") arrived). After [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) had rescued the company and was counting the members he found Kíli and Fíli, as usual, paired together.<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the company rushed up into trees to escape the [wargs](/wiki/Wargs "Wargs") Kíli and Fíli scrambled to the top of a tall larch.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The next day, when the company arrived at [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn)'s hall, Kíli and Fíli were again together as the fourth pair of Dwarves to appear during Gandalf's story.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

When Bilbo spied the boat on the far side of the [Enchanted River](/wiki/Enchanted_River), Fíli managed to snag it with a hook on a rope but could not pull it across. Kíli came to his aid first, followed by [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in) and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in), before the boat came loose (after its painter snapped).<sup id="cite_ref-Flies_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Flies-7">[7]</a></sup>

At the opening of the barrels after the escape from the halls of the [Elvenking](/wiki/Thranduil "Thranduil"), Kíli and Fíli came out smiling since they were young and had but few bruises. The two brothers helped Thorin and Bilbo locate and free the other Dwarves. Next, they accompanied Thorin and Bilbo to the bridge into [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town") while the others recovered.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

When the company reached the Lonely Mountain, Thorin sent out a scouting party to view the Front Gate (or where it had once stood). Kíli and Fíli went along with [Balin](/wiki/Balin) and Bilbo on this expedition. On another scouting trip Bilbo was with Kíli and Fíli when the three discovered the rough steps that led up the mountain to the little bay that contained the secret door.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

Of the thirteen Dwarves, Kíli and Fíli looked uncomfortable when Bilbo asked for volunteers to accompany him on the first trip into the mountain (only Balin went with the hobbit for part of the way). Once the dragon woke up though Kíli and Fíli, along with Bilbo and Balin, were the first to go in the tunnel (sent in by Thorin).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

While searching the treasure when the full company descended into Erebor, Kíli and Fíli were in such exuberant spirits that they took down magic golden harps and played music on them.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Kíli was slain with his brother in the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies), while defending their mortally wounded uncle.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> After Thorin's death, the brothers would have been the only remaining descendants of [Thrór](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r)'s line. Their deaths ended the dynasty, and the title of King under the Mountain passed to [Dáin II](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot "Dáin Ironfoot"). It was their heroic last stand that made their mother the only [Dwarf-woman](/wiki/Dwarf-women "Dwarf-women") to be mentioned in the genealogies.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Kíli* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It can mean "Wedge" or "One who uses a wedge"; it is of Frisian or Low German origin rather than Old Norse.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

<b>2012-14: <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Kíli is played by <a href="/wiki/Aidan_Turner" title="Aidan Turner">Aidan Turner</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PJCasting_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PJCasting-16">[16]</a></sup> A description of Kíli in <i>The Hobbit</i> films was released by the studio:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Younger brother to Fíli, Kíli is a loyal nephew to Thorin Oakenshield. Carefree and somewhat reckless, Kíli has led a charmed and untroubled life to this point. Handsome and physically able, Kíli possesses the invincible courage of youth. He is a skilled fighter and expert archer, having been trained with weapons from an early age. As one of the youngest in The Company of Dwarves. Kíli is determined to make his mark and prove his worth.

<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="/wiki/Warner_Bros." title="Warner Bros.">Warner Bros.</a><sup id="cite_ref-HobbitApp_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HobbitApp-17">[17]</a></sup></cite></p>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

<dl><dd>Because <a href="/wiki/Ori" title="Ori">Ori</a> was made the youngest of the 13 Dwarves in the <i>Hobbit</i> films, Kíli is presumably the second youngest. He also comes to have an infatuation with <a href="/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood" title="Elves of Mirkwood">Mirkwood Elf</a> <a href="/wiki/Tauriel" title="Tauriel">Tauriel</a> (a character made up exclusively for the films). When struck with a poisoned arrow by <a href="/wiki/Bolg" title="Bolg">Bolg</a> during the Company&#39;s escape from the Wood-elves&#39; custody via wine barrels, it is she who ultimately heals him with <a href="/wiki/Athelas" title="Athelas">Athelas</a>. Due to said injury, Thorin did not allow him to journey with the others up to the Lonely Mounatin from <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a>. <a href="/wiki/%C3%93in" title="Óin">Óin</a> and Fíli then both opted to also stay behind in order to look after him. As he and the other Dwarves make their way to the <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a> after <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a>&#39;s demise, Kíli shares a tender moment with Tauriel, and gives her the talisman that his mother had given to him. In the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies" title="Battle of Five Armies">Battle of Five Armies</a>, he assists <a href="/wiki/Thorin" title="Thorin">Thorin</a> (along with <a href="/wiki/F%C3%ADli" title="Fíli">Fíli</a> and <a href="/wiki/Dwalin" title="Dwalin">Dwalin</a>) in attempting to kill the <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orc</a> army leader <a href="/wiki/Azog" title="Azog">Azog</a> on <a href="/wiki/Ravenhill" title="Ravenhill">Ravenhill</a>. However, after witnessing Azog kill his brother, he goes into a rage, attempting to charge the Pale Orc, only to instead come across Tauriel getting severely beaten by Bolg, and proceeds to attack him. Kíli is stabbed to death by Bolg, and a heartbroken Tauriel returns the talisman to his lifeless hand. Tauriel stayed there, mourning over his death before Thranduil came upon the pair. It was then that Tauriel declared her love for the Dwarf, before leaving his side.</dd></dl>
