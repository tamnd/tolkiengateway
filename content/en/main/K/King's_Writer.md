**King's Writer** was a position held by the royal scribes of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom). This entailed making the official records of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), working directly with original sources.

[Findegil](/wiki/Findegil), a King's Writer of renown presumably under the kingship of [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), is known to have made at least one copy of the [Red Book of Westmarch](/wiki/Red_Book_of_Westmarch) in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith). Indeed, in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [171](/wiki/Fourth_Age_171 "Fourth Age 171"), Findegil finished a copy to create the [Thain's Book](/wiki/Thain%27s_Book).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
