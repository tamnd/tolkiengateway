**King's Writer** was a position held by the royal scribes of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md). This entailed making the official records of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), working directly with original sources.

[Findegil](../F/Findegil.md), a King's Writer of renown presumably under the kingship of [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), is known to have made at least one copy of the [Red Book of Westmarch](../R/Red_Book_of_Westmarch.md) in [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md). Indeed, in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [171](../F/Fourth_Age_171.md), Findegil finished a copy to create the [Thain's Book](../T/Thain's_Book.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
