In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Khâradûnê** or **Hyarnustar**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> or just **Hyarn**<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[note 2]</a></sup>, was a coastal region on the southwestern coast of Middle-earth between the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) and the western flank of the Ered Laranor<sup id="cite_ref-Hyarn_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hyarn-4">[2]</a></sup> south of [Tulwang](/wiki/Tulwang)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[3]</a></sup> and north of the [Elorna](/wiki/Dominions_of_the_Seven "Dominions of the Seven")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[4]</a></sup>. Its northern border was formed by the river Aronduin ([S.](/wiki/Sindarin "Sindarin") "Woods-river"). Rich river vales were located along the northern boundary, but most of the inland region was semi-arid scrubland or grassland.<sup id="cite_ref-Hyarn_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hyarn-4">[2]</a></sup>

While it was originally founded by the Adena, it was later colonized by [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans). Wealthy mixed-blood descendants of the Númenórean settlers lived near the coast and in the river vales along the northern boundary, while relatively pure-blooded Adena lived throughout the inland. Many Adena deal with the numerous timber products of the region.<sup id="cite_ref-Hyarn_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hyarn-4">[2]</a></sup>

Ciryamir, father of [Akhôrahil](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl"), landed in Khâradûne in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1904&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1904 (page does not exist)">1904</a> after being given a license to create a colony. He sailed up the Aronduin and founded [Ciryatanor](/wiki/Ciryatanor) in the east.<sup id="cite_ref-nazgul_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nazgul-7">[5]</a></sup>

The [Army of the Southern Dragon](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon) began marching against Khâradûne and Ciryatanor in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1660&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1660 (page does not exist)">1660</a>, though the army was beaten back to Tulwang in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1675&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1675 (page does not exist)">1675</a>. The [Sun Lord of Far Harad](/wiki/Far_Harad "Far Harad") was defeated by Khâradûne's forces in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2643&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2643 (page does not exist)">2643</a> after a failed attack on the land.<sup id="cite_ref-timelinecitadel_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-timelinecitadel-8">[6]</a></sup>
