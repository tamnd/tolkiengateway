<i><b>kuluma</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "an orange".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the form *culuma* (retaining the same meaning) could be valid [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
