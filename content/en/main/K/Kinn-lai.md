**Kinn-Lai** was a term used by some [Avari](../A/Avari.md) to describe themselves. It is unknown if the name "Kinn-Lai" was applied to a particular group of Avari specifically or to all Avari as a whole.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is an [Avarin](../A/Avarin.md) word meaning "the People". It derives from [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *[kwendī](Kwende.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Author_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Author-1">[1]</a></sup> The suffix *-lai* may be related to [Quenyan](../Q/Quenya.md) <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Li%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lië (page does not exist)">lië</a></i> "people", coming from the [root](../R/Root.md) LI (whence also Quenyan *[-li](../_/-li.md)*) by A-infixion, attested in the primitive language.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><i>Kinn-lai</i>, an articulation of &#34;Quendi&#34; (the most ancient name of the Elves), is also the name of a tribe of Avari Elves of the jungles of the <a href="../M/Mûmakan.md" title="Mûmakan">Mûmakan</a> in the Utter South of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2024: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The branch of Avari corresponding to the Kinn-lai are found in the land of <a href="../N/Near_Harad.md" title="Near Harad">Near Harad</a>, or &#34;Shagâna&#34; as it is called by the locals, and call themselves Avabár-kinta (People Without Home), or simply <b>Kintai</b>. They seek not to travel west either in life or after death, believing that the way there is forever closed to them, and have developed an elaborate system  of beliefs and rituals regarding their fate after death, whether through fading or through violence in battle. The Wise who journey through those lands eventually reveal to some of the Kinta that some of what they believe is not entirely true.</dd></dl>
