**Kenneth Campbell "Ken" Stott** is a Scottish actor. He portrayed [Balin](../B/Balin.md) in *[The Hobbit (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)*. He is best known as DCI Red Metcalfe in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messiah_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:Messiah (TV series)">Messiah</a></i>.
