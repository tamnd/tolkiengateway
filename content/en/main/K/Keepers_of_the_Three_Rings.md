The **Keepers of the Three Rings** were those who held the [Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) after their making, through more than 4,000 years of the [Second](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") and [Third](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") Ages. Originally the three Keepers were [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel), and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan). Gil-galad gave his Ring to [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and Círdan gave his to [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the end of the Third Age, when the [Rings](/wiki/Rings_of_Power "Rings of Power") finally lost their power and were taken into [the West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"), the Keepers were Elrond, Galadriel and Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
