The **Keepers of the Three Rings** were those who held the [Three Rings](../T/Three_Rings.md) of the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) after their making, through more than 4,000 years of the [Second](../S/Second_Age.md) and [Third](../T/Third_Age.md) Ages. Originally the three Keepers were [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md), and [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md). Gil-galad gave his Ring to [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) and Círdan gave his to [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> At the end of the Third Age, when the [Rings](../R/Rings_of_Power.md) finally lost their power and were taken into [the West](../A/Aman.md), the Keepers were Elrond, Galadriel and Gandalf.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
