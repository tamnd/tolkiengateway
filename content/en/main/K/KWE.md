**KWE** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) etymological base that expresses concepts of articulate speech and (subsequently) an eloquent being, a person.

## KWENE {#KWENE}

KWENE is an extension of KWE,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the basis for the concept of "person", beings capable of articulate speech, hence "persons" as opposed to animals lacking any form of language. and specifically an Elf, since the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were the first speakers who defined themselves.

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kwene&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kwene (page does not exist)">kwene</a></i> &gt; <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kw%C3%AAn&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kwên (page does not exist)">kwên</a></i>, *[kwende](/wiki/Kwende "Kwende")*
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Quendë](/wiki/Quend%C3%AB) ([Calaquendi](/wiki/Calaquendi), [Moriquendi](/wiki/Moriquendi), [Quendya](/wiki/Vanyarin "Vanyarin") &gt; [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Quen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Quen (page does not exist)">quen</a> (<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Aiquen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Aiquen (page does not exist)">aiquen</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Roquen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Roquen (page does not exist)">roquen</a>)*

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ben (page does not exist)">ben</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Arben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Arben (page does not exist)">arben</a>, <a href="/wiki/Calben" title="Calben">calben</a>, <a href="/wiki/Morben" title="Morben">morben</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Orodben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Orodben (page does not exist)">orodben</a>, <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rochben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rochben (page does not exist)">rochben</a></i>
