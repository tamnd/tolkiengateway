**KWE** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) etymological base that expresses concepts of articulate speech and (subsequently) an eloquent being, a person.

## KWENE {#KWENE}

KWENE is an extension of KWE,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> the basis for the concept of "person", beings capable of articulate speech, hence "persons" as opposed to animals lacking any form of language. and specifically an Elf, since the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) were the first speakers who defined themselves.
