The **King's Company**, also known as the **King's Riders**, was the first [Éored](/wiki/%C3%89ored) of the [Riders of Rohan](/wiki/Riders_of_Rohan), who rode to battle beside the King himself. At the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), they were led by [Déorwine](/wiki/D%C3%A9orwine), who with six others of the Company fell with the [king](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden "Théoden") in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, when [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) came to [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to lead the funeral procession of King Théoden back to [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan), he brought the King's Company with him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001-03: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The King&#39;s Company appear to be the inspiration for the Royal Guard of <a href="/wiki/Edoras" title="Edoras">Edoras</a>. They, unlike other soldiers in Rohan, have a consistent uniform with a long leaf-mail hauberk, a rich green cloak and a helm with a horse-hair crest. <a href="/wiki/Gamling" title="Gamling">Gamling</a>, <a href="/wiki/H%C3%A1ma" title="Háma">Háma</a> and <a href="/wiki/Grimbold" title="Grimbold">Grimbold</a> all wear this same uniform to emphasize their rank. The Royal Guard notably bear the body of <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9odred" title="Théodred">Théodred</a> from the Golden Hall during his funeral.</dd></dl>
