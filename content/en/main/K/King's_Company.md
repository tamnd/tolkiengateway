The **King's Company**, also known as the **King's Riders**, was the first [Éored](../_/Éored.md) of the [Riders of Rohan](../R/Riders_of_Rohan.md), who rode to battle beside the King himself. At the end of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), they were led by [Déorwine](../D/Déorwine.md), who with six others of the Company fell with the [king](../T/Théoden.md) in the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, when [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) came to [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to lead the funeral procession of King Théoden back to [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md), he brought the King's Company with him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The King&#39;s Company appear to be the inspiration for the Royal Guard of <a href="../E/Edoras.md" title="Edoras">Edoras</a>. They, unlike other soldiers in Rohan, have a consistent uniform with a long leaf-mail hauberk, a rich green cloak and a helm with a horse-hair crest. <a href="../G/Gamling.md" title="Gamling">Gamling</a>, <a href="../H/Háma.md" title="Háma">Háma</a> and <a href="../G/Grimbold.md" title="Grimbold">Grimbold</a> all wear this same uniform to emphasize their rank. The Royal Guard notably bear the body of <a href="../T/Théodred.md" title="Théodred">Théodred</a> from the Golden Hall during his funeral.</dd></dl>
