**KHEL** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "freeze". [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has noted that this root and its derivatives were struck out by his father.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *helle* ("frost")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *hell* ("frost")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
