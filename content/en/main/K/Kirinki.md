**Kirinki** was one of the many species of birds that were native to [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). These tiny birds were not known to be found anywhere within [Aman](/wiki/Aman) and [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The kirinki were said to be no bigger than wrens, with bright scarlet feathers, and piping voices so high that they could hardly even be heard by [Men](/wiki/Men).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Kirinki* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word possibly meaning "little cutter". It is perhaps derived from <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=KIR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="KIR (page does not exist)">KIR</a></i> ("cut, cleave, pass swiftly through; shave; skim (surface), slip along, glide quickly"), <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-inc%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-incë (page does not exist)">-incë</a></i> ("diminutive ending").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
