**Rómendacil I** ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [310](/wiki/Third_Age_310 "Third Age 310")<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [541](/wiki/Third_Age_541 "Third Age 541"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 231 years) was born **Tarostar**, the son of [Ostoher](/wiki/Ostoher), the [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup> He in turn would become the eighth King of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> ruling for 49 years, from Third Age 492<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> until his untimely death.

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [490](/wiki/Third_Age_490 "Third Age 490") the first invasion of [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> fell upon [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>. Tarostar defeated the invaders in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [500](/wiki/Third_Age_500 "Third Age 500")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and drove them from Gondor<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>. After his victory against the Easterlings Tarostar assumed the name Rómendacil, "East-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the first assault of the Easterlings, Rómendacil ordered that the "Tradition of Isildur" be set down in a sealed scroll, to be delivered by the [Steward of Gondor](/wiki/Steward_of_Gondor) to the King's heir before his crowning.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Rómendacil's triumph did not end the Easterling threat. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [541](/wiki/Third_Age_541 "Third Age 541") Rómendacil was killed in a battle<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> against new hordes of Easterlings<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>. His son [Turambar](</wiki/Turambar_(King_of_Gondor)> "Turambar (King of Gondor)") avenged him and conquered much territory eastwards.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarostar* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it might mean "King of the Lands" and be a compound of [tar](/wiki/Tar "Tar") ("high, king") and the plural form of <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Sta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sta (page does not exist)">sta</a> ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

*Rómendacil* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). It means "East-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a compound of [rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men "Rómen") ("east") and [-(n)dacil](/wiki/-ndacil "-ndacil") ("victor").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
