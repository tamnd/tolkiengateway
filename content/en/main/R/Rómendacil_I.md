**Rómendacil I** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [310](../T/Third_Age_310.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-1">[1]</a></sup> – [541](../T/Third_Age_541.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 231 years) was born **Tarostar**, the son of [Ostoher](../O/Ostoher.md), the [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup> He in turn would become the eighth King of Gondor,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> ruling for 49 years, from Third Age 492<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> until his untimely death.

## History {#History}

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [490](../T/Third_Age_490.md) the first invasion of [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> fell upon [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>. Tarostar defeated the invaders in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [500](../T/Third_Age_500.md)<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> and drove them from Gondor<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>. After his victory against the Easterlings Tarostar assumed the name Rómendacil, "East-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>

After the first assault of the Easterlings, Rómendacil ordered that the "Tradition of Isildur" be set down in a sealed scroll, to be delivered by the [Steward of Gondor](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md) to the King's heir before his crowning.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Rómendacil's triumph did not end the Easterling threat. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [541](../T/Third_Age_541.md) Rómendacil was killed in a battle<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> against new hordes of Easterlings<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>. His son [Turambar](<../T/Turambar_(King_of_Gondor).md>) avenged him and conquered much territory eastwards.<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Tarostar* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Its meaning is not glossed, but as Paul Strack explains, it might mean "King of the Lands" and be a compound of [tar](../T/Tar.md) ("high, king") and the plural form of <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Sta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Sta (page does not exist)">sta</a> ("land").<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

*Rómendacil* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). It means "East-victor".<sup id="cite_ref-Gondor_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gondor-3">[3]</a></sup> It is a compound of [rómen](Rómen.md) ("east") and [-(n)dacil](../_/-ndacil.md) ("victor").<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>
