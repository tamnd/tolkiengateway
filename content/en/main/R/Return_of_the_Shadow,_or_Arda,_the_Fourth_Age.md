**Return of the Shadow, or Arda, the Fourth Age** (also known as *Return of the Shadow*, or abbreviated as *RotS*) is a MUD set in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), two thousand years after the destruction of [the Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").<sup id="cite_ref-Background_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Background-1">[1]</a></sup> In this mud [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and other evil creatures have returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and the shadow has returned to [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood).<sup id="cite_ref-Background_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Background-1">[1]</a></sup> Player versus Player-content is an important part of this mud.

## Races {#Races}

This mud has seven playable races, which are divided into four good races and three evil races.<sup id="cite_ref-Creation_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Creation-2">[2]</a></sup>

### [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples) {#Free_peoples}

- [Human](/wiki/Men "Men")
- [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves")
- [Wood Elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves")
- [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits")

### Evil Races {#Evil_Races}

- [Uruk-hai Orc](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai")
- [Common Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")
- Uruk-Lhuth

## Classes {#Classes}

Players can either make their own custom class or choose from one of the following ten classes:<sup id="cite_ref-Creation_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Creation-2">[2]</a></sup>

- Adventurer
- Barbarian
- Conjurer
- Healer
- Mage
- Mystic
- Ranger
- Swashbuckler
- Warrior
- [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards")
