## Doriathrin {#Doriathrin}

<i><b>roth</b></i> (pl. <i><b>rodhin</b></i>) is a [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) word meaning "cave".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is a descendant form of ?[Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *rōda* &gt; *rōdh* &gt; *rōth*, deriving from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=ROD&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ROD (page does not exist)">ROD</a> ("cave").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

### See also {#See_also}

- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *rhond*, *rhonn* ("cave")
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rondo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rondo (page does not exist)">rondo</a></i> ("cave")
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[groth](/wiki/Groth "Groth")*

### Other versions {#Other_versions}

The word is given in the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* to explain the name *[Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup> In a later source [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") gives the Sindarin word *[groth](/wiki/Groth "Groth")* instead.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Adûnaic {#Adûnaic}

<i><b>roth</b></i> is an [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) word meaning "foam". Its original sense was "cutting, track" (as in the compounds <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Obroth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Obroth (page does not exist)">ob<b>roth</b></a></i> "fore-cutting" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nadroth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nadroth (page does not exist)">nad<b>roth</b></a></i> "hind-track").<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
