**Return to Edoras** is the fourth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* and the fifth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King* (extended edition)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King (extended edition)"). It was extended in the latter edition.

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

[Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and the company from [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc) return to the [Golden Hall](/wiki/Golden_Hall) to take rest and honour those who died for victory at the [Battle of Helm's Deep](/wiki/Battle_of_Helm%27s_Deep).

During the feasting, [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden) speaks with [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), telling her that he is happy for Éowyn's love for [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), and that Aragorn was largely responsible for victory at [Helm's Deep](/wiki/Helm%27s_Deep). [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) take part in a drinking game with [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) watching and serving drinks. Gimli becomes quite clearly intoxicated very quickly and passes out, while Legolas merely feels *"a slight tingle"* in his fingers. [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") dance to music on a table to the delight of a large crowd. Aragorn discusses [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") with Gandalf, saying that he moves closer to [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) every day. Gandalf questions how they can know that for sure, and, in reply, Aragorn asks Gandalf, *"what does you heart tell you?"*
