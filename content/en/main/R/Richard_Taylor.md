**Sir Richard Taylor** is the leader of the crew at [Weta Workshop](../W/Weta_Workshop.md), that created the special effects, weapons, wardrobe, miniatures and prosthetics, for the [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).

- [2002](../_/2002.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.oscar.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Academy Award</a></b>, Best Effects, Visual Effects, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*
- 2002 - **Academy Award**, Best Makeup, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.bafta.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">BAFTA</a></b>, Best Achievement in Special Visual Effects, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - **BAFTA**, Best Makeup/Hair, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.lvfcs.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Sierra Award</a></b>, Best Visual Effects, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - **Sierra Award**, Best Costume Design, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.cinemaclips.com/PFCS.htm" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">PFCS Award</a></b>, Best Visual Effects, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - **PFCS Award**, Best Costume Design, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- 2002 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://pressacademy.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Golden Satellite Award</a></b>, Best Visual Effects, *Peter Jackson's The Fellowship of the Ring*
- [2003](../_/2003.md) - **BAFTA**, Best Costume Design, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*
- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.saturnawards.org/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Saturn Award</a></b>, Best Costumes, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- 2003 - **Sierra Award**, Best Costume Design, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.vesawards.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">VES Award</a></b>, Best Models and Miniatures in a Motion Picture, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- 2003 - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.imdb.com/Sections/Awards/Hollywood_Makeup_Artist_and_Hair_Stylist_Guild_Awards/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">HMAHSG Award</a></b>, Best Special Makeup Effects - Feature, *Peter Jackson's The Two Towers*
- [2004](../_/2004.md) - **Academy Award**, Best Makeup, *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*

- 2004 - **PFCS Award**, Best Costume Design, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - **VES Award**, Outstanding Models and Miniatures in a Motion Picture, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- 2004 - **HMAHSG Award**, Best Special Makeup Effects - Feature, *Peter Jackson's The Return of the King*
- [2014](../_/2014.md) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://cineuphoria09.blogspot.gr/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">CinEuphoria Award</a></b>, Best Costume Design - International Competition, *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](../T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Desolation_of_Smaug.md)*
