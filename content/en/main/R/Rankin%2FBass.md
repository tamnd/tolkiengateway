**Rankin/Bass** was a film production company founded by [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass) and [Arthur Rankin, Jr.](/wiki/Arthur_Rankin,_Jr.) In 1977 Rankin/Bass produced an animated version of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)")*. It was followed in 1980 by an animated version of *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")*, the final book of Tolkien's Lord of the Rings trilogy. Rankin/Bass could not adapt the first two books of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, as the animation rights for the two books were held by [Saul Zaentz](/wiki/Saul_Zaentz), producer of [Ralph Bakshi](/wiki/Ralph_Bakshi)'s cartoon adaptation of the first half of the trilogy, entitled [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)>).

The company was dissolved in 2001 and currently, the distribution rights of both *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)")* and *[The Return of the King](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)")* belong to [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.)
