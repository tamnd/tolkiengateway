**Rankin/Bass** was a film production company founded by [Jules Bass](../J/Jules_Bass.md) and [Arthur Rankin, Jr.](../A/Arthur_Rankin,_Jr..md) In 1977 Rankin/Bass produced an animated version of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>)*. It was followed in 1980 by an animated version of *[The Return of the King](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>)*, the final book of Tolkien's Lord of the Rings trilogy. Rankin/Bass could not adapt the first two books of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, as the animation rights for the two books were held by [Saul Zaentz](../S/Saul_Zaentz.md), producer of [Ralph Bakshi](Ralph_Bakshi.md)'s cartoon adaptation of the first half of the trilogy, entitled [The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>).

The company was dissolved in 2001 and currently, the distribution rights of both *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>)* and *[The Return of the King](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>)* belong to [Warner Bros.](../W/Warner_Bros..md)
