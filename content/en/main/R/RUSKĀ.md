**RUSKĀ** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") given no signification, except from what can be determined from its derivatives.<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin"): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ruska&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ruska (page does not exist)">ruska</a></i> ("brown")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> &gt; [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[rhosc](/wiki/Rhosc "Rhosc")* ("brown")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rusco&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rusco (page does not exist)">rusco</a></i> ("fox")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other etymologies {#Other_etymologies}

The related [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) words *[russa](/wiki/Russa "Russa")* "red-haired" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Russe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Russe (page does not exist)">russe</a></i> "(a head or pelt of) red hair" show derivation from another, more basic root \*RUS, of which RUSKĀ is obviously an extended form.

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Tolkien translates the Quenya word *russa* as "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russet_(color)" title="wikipedia:Russet (color)">russet</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The English word *russet* derives from [Latin](/wiki/Latin) <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/russus" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">russus</a></i> "reddish".

There is a possibility that the similarities of both form and sense between the Elvish root and English/Latin words is intentional.
