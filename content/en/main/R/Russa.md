<i><b>russa</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) adjective which means "red-haired" or "russet".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It obviously derives from a [root](Root.md) \*RUS-. See [RUSKĀ](RUSKĀ.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

The word *russa* represents the color called "russet" in English, which derives from the Latin word <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/russus" title="wiktionary:russus">russus</a></i> "red". Although we can't know if the similarities between the Quenya word and *russet/russus* were intentional by Tolkien, it is possible that they are.
