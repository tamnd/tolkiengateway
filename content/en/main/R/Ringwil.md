The river **Ringwil** was a short and foaming river of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) that flowed east through the hilly [High Faroth](/wiki/Taur-en-Faroth "Taur-en-Faroth") north of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) until it joined the [Narog](/wiki/Narog).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Where it met the Narog a secret door was built, which was used by [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) to escape from Nargothrond when Celegorm and [Curufin](/wiki/Curufin) held her prisoner.
