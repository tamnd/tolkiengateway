**Ronald Eduard Kyrmse** is a Brazilian [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) scholar and founder of the first Brazilian Tolkien Society in [1989](/wiki/1989), called [Heren Hyarmen](/wiki/Heren_Hyarmen) (changed in [2003](/wiki/2003) to Heren Hyarmeno). He translated most of Tolkien's books that have been published in Brazilian Portuguese. He is a member of [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Society) and used to belong to Quendili, the Society's Elvish study group.
