**Ronald Eduard Kyrmse** is a Brazilian [Tolkien](../T/Tolkien.md) scholar and founder of the first Brazilian Tolkien Society in [1989](../_/1989.md), called [Heren Hyarmen](../H/Heren_Hyarmen.md) (changed in [2003](../_/2003.md) to Heren Hyarmeno). He translated most of Tolkien's books that have been published in Brazilian Portuguese. He is a member of [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Society.md) and used to belong to Quendili, the Society's Elvish study group.
