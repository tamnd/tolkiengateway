<i><b>rost</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) adjective meaning **'rainy',** found in the name [Dimrost](../D/Dimrost.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It likely is derived from the noun *'ross',* meaning 'rain, spray'<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
