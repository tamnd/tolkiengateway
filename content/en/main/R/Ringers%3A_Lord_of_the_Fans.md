<i><b>Ringers: Lord of the Fans</b></i> is a [2005](/wiki/2005) film about the culture and fans behind *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s works.

## Reception {#Reception}

The film received mixed reviews, generally disliked by fans of J.R.R. Tolkien<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> and praised by fans of [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> It is currently rated 6.2/10 on IMDB.
