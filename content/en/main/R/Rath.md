## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>rath</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "street (in a city)".<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup>

Apparently *rath* can also mean "climb" (noun), as seen in *[Andrath](/wiki/Andrath)* ("Long Climb").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> This definition also appears in [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo)'s [Neo-Sindarin](/wiki/Neo-Elvish "Neo-Elvish") wordlist.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

[Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that the noun *rath* derives from the [stem](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") *rath-* ("climb").<sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *[Andrath](/wiki/Andrath)*
- *[Rath Celerdain](/wiki/Lampwrights%27_Street "Lampwrights' Street")*
- *[Rath Dínen](/wiki/Rath_D%C3%ADnen)*

## Noldorin {#Noldorin}

<i><b>rath</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "course, river-bed".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology_2}

The word derives from [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *rattha*, from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") RAT ("walk").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples_2}

- *[Rath Loriel](/wiki/Rathl%C3%B3riel#Etymology "Rathlóriel")*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
