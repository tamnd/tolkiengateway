**RUN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") given various significations.

## RUN<sup>1</sup> {#RUN1}

- Meaning: "flat of hand or sole of foot"<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

- Derivatives:
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *runya* ("slot, footprint"); *tallune* ("sole of foot")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *rhoein*, *rhein* ("slot, spoor, track, footprint")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup>

## RUN<sup>2</sup> {#RUN2}

- Meaning: "rub, grind, smooth, polish"<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

- Derivatives: [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *runda* ("smooth, polished")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

- See also: [Lebethron#Etymology](/wiki/Lebethron#Etymology "Lebethron")

## RUN<sup>3</sup> {#RUN3}

- Meaning: "'red, glowing', most often applied to things like embers"<sup id="cite_ref-PM_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-3">[3]</a></sup>

- Derivatives: [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *runya* ("'fiery' red"), [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *ruin*<sup id="cite_ref-PM_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-3">[3]</a></sup>
