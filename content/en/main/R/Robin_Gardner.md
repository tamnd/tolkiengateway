**Robin Gardner** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Robin Gardner was the twelfth child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

A <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robin" title="wikipedia:Robin">Robin</a></i> is a type of bird. He was possibly named after [Robin Smallburrow](/wiki/Robin_Smallburrow).
