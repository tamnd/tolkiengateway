**Ringil** was the name of the sword of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), said to have "glittered like ice". With it he wounded [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) seven times in [single combat](/wiki/Fall_of_Fingolfin "Fall of Fingolfin") after the [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ringil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name, an elaboration of *ringë* ("cold").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
