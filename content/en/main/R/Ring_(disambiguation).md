A **ring** is (usually) a jewel worn on one's finger. It can refer to:

## [Arda](/wiki/Arda) {#Arda}

### Jewellery {#Jewellery}

- **[One Ring](/wiki/One_Ring)**, forged by [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).
    - The [Ring-inscription](/wiki/Ring-inscription), the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) inscription around the One Ring.
    - The [Lord of the Ring](/wiki/Lord_of_the_Ring), a title of Sauron.
    - The [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), a great war which resulted in the destruction of the One Ring and Sauron.
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), nine members who were tasked with destroying the One Ring.
    - The [Ring-finder](/wiki/Ring-finder) and the [Ring-bearers](/wiki/Ring-bearers).
- The **[Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power)**, forged by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor).
    - [The Three](/wiki/Three_Rings "Three Rings") for the [Elves](/wiki/Elves):
        - [Narya](/wiki/Narya), the Ring of Fire, worn by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad), [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) and finally [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf).
        - [Nenya](/wiki/Nenya), the Ring of Water, worn by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel).
        - [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya), the Ring of Air, worn by [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) and [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond).
    - [The Nine](/wiki/Nine_Rings "Nine Rings") for [Men](/wiki/Men), given to what would become the [Nazgûl](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl).
    - [The Seven](/wiki/Seven_Rings "Seven Rings") for [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), of which only one is known:
        - [Ring of Thrór](/wiki/Ring_of_Thr%C3%B3r).

- The **[lesser rings](/wiki/Lesser_rings "Lesser rings")**, forged by the [Elves](/wiki/Elves).
- The Ring of Saruman, a lesser ring forged by [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) in his attempt to forge a ring of power.
- The [Ring of Barahir](/wiki/Ring_of_Barahir), an heirloom of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain).

### Locations {#Locations}

- [Máhanaxar](/wiki/M%C3%A1hanaxar), the *Ring of Doom* where the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) met.
- The [Ring of Isengard](/wiki/Ring_of_Isengard), the walls around [Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc).

## [Real-world](/wiki/Category:Real-world "Category:Real-world") {#Real-world}

### Books {#Books}

- *[Morgoth's Ring](/wiki/Morgoth%27s_Ring)*, the tenth book in *[The History of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_History_of_Middle-earth)* series.
    - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Morgoth%27s_Ring_(concept)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgoth&#39;s Ring (concept) (page does not exist)">Morgoth&#39;s Ring (concept)</a>.
- *[The Ring goes South](/wiki/The_Ring_goes_South)*, fifteenth chapter of [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).
- *[The Ring of Words](/wiki/The_Ring_of_Words)*, a book about [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s time at the [Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary).
- *[Tolkien's Ring](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Ring)*, a book by [David Day](/wiki/David_Day).

### Music {#Music}

- *[The Ring Goes South (soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Ring_Goes_South_(soundtrack)>)*, eleventh track on [Howard Shore](/wiki/Howard_Shore)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack)*
- *[Der Ring des Nibelungen](/wiki/Der_Ring_des_Nibelungen)*, the music drama composed by [Richard Wagner](/wiki/Richard_Wagner) (also known as the *Ring Cycle*), which often has been compared with *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*
