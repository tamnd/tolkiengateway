A **ring** is (usually) a jewel worn on one's finger. It can refer to:

## [Arda](../A/Arda.md) {#Arda}

### Jewellery {#Jewellery}

- **[One Ring](../O/One_Ring.md)**, forged by [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).
    - The [Ring-inscription](Ring-inscription.md), the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md) inscription around the One Ring.
    - The [Lord of the Ring](../L/Lord_of_the_Ring.md), a title of Sauron.
    - The [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), a great war which resulted in the destruction of the One Ring and Sauron.
    - The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md), nine members who were tasked with destroying the One Ring.
    - The [Ring-finder](Ring-finder.md) and the [Ring-bearers](Ring-bearers.md).
- The **[Rings of Power](Rings_of_Power.md)**, forged by [Celebrimbor](../C/Celebrimbor.md).
    - [The Three](../T/Three_Rings.md) for the [Elves](../E/Elves.md):
        - [Narya](../N/Narya.md), the Ring of Fire, worn by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md), [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) and finally [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).
        - [Nenya](../N/Nenya.md), the Ring of Water, worn by [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md).
        - [Vilya](../V/Vilya.md), the Ring of Air, worn by [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) and [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md).
    - [The Nine](../N/Nine_Rings.md) for [Men](../M/Men.md), given to what would become the [Nazgûl](../N/Nazgûl.md).
    - [The Seven](../S/Seven_Rings.md) for [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md), of which only one is known:
        - [Ring of Thrór](Ring_of_Thrór.md).

- The **[lesser rings](../L/Lesser_rings.md)**, forged by the [Elves](../E/Elves.md).
- The Ring of Saruman, a lesser ring forged by [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) in his attempt to forge a ring of power.
- The [Ring of Barahir](Ring_of_Barahir.md), an heirloom of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md).

### Locations {#Locations}

- [Máhanaxar](../M/Máhanaxar.md), the *Ring of Doom* where the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) met.
- The [Ring of Isengard](Ring_of_Isengard.md), the walls around [Orthanc](../O/Orthanc.md).

### Books {#Books}

- *[Morgoth's Ring](../M/Morgoth's_Ring.md)*, the tenth book in *[The History of Middle-earth](../T/The_History_of_Middle-earth.md)* series.
    - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Morgoth%27s_Ring_(concept)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Morgoth&#39;s Ring (concept) (page does not exist)">Morgoth&#39;s Ring (concept)</a>.

- *[Tolkien's Ring](../T/Tolkien's_Ring.md)*, a book by [David Day](../D/David_Day.md).

### Music {#Music}

- *[The Ring Goes South (soundtrack)](<../T/The_Ring_Goes_South_(soundtrack).md>)*, eleventh track on [Howard Shore](../H/Howard_Shore.md)'s *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_-_Original_Motion_Picture_Soundtrack.md)*
