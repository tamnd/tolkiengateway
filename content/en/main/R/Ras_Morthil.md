**Ras Morthil** must be another name for [Andrast](/wiki/Andrast), the great headland at the end of the northern arm of the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ras Morthil* likely consists of the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *[ras](/wiki/Ras "Ras")* ("cape, shore") and the word *Morthil*. *Morthil* likely contains the elements *[mor](/wiki/Mor "Mor")* ("dark, black") and the nasally mutated *[til](/wiki/Til "Til")* ("point, spike, (sharp) horn, tine, ending").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
