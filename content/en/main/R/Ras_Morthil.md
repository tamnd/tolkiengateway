**Ras Morthil** must be another name for [Andrast](../A/Andrast.md), the great headland at the end of the northern arm of the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ras Morthil* likely consists of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *[ras](Ras.md)* ("cape, shore") and the word *Morthil*. *Morthil* likely contains the elements *[mor](../M/Mor.md)* ("dark, black") and the nasally mutated *[til](../T/Til.md)* ("point, spike, (sharp) horn, tine, ending").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[note 1]</a></sup>
