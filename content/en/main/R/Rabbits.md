**Rabbits** were long-eared, furry animals, also called '<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/coney" title="wiktionary:coney">coneys</a>'.

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2747](/wiki/Third_Age_2747 "Third Age 2747"), the game of [Golf](/wiki/Golf) was invented in the [Battle of the Green Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Green_Fields) when [Bandobras Took](/wiki/Bandobras_Took) cut the head off the [Goblin](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") King [Golfimbul](/wiki/Golfimbul) and his head flew through the air and went down a rabbit hole.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> After rescuing [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) the [Eagles](/wiki/Eagles) brought them rabbits to eat.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") in northern [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) asked [Gollum](/wiki/Gollum) to bring him some food. Gollum brought him two small rabbits which Sam proceeded to cook, to Gollum's intense displeasure.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In a linguistic manuscript dating from the 1930s, appears the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) gloss <i><b>lopo</b></i> ("rabbit").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> In earlier [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), it was *lapatte*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
