The **Rangers of Ithilien** were a company of [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) soldiers, selected from the descendants of the people who had dwelt in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) before it fell from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)'s control.<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-1">[1]</a></sup> At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) they crossed the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) dressed in camouflaging green and brown, with green gauntlets and green hoods and masks, armed with great bows, which were almost as high as themselves with great quivers with long arrows with green feathers or spears with broad bright heads and swords to harass the forces of the Enemy in Gondor's old domain<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Attack_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Attack-3">[3]</a></sup> and used several secret retreats in the region.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

It is likely that the Rangers of Ithilien were established at the same time that [Henneth Annûn](../H/Henneth_Annûn.md) was built, in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2901](../T/Third_Age_2901.md) when most of the remaining inhabitants of the region departed due to [Uruk](../U/Uruk-hai.md) attacks from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> during the reign of [Steward](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md) [Túrin II](../T/Túrin_II.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Steward Túrin II had secret refugees, such as Henneth Annûn, built in Ithilien for his soldiers.<sup id="cite_ref-Stewards_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stewards-4">[4]</a></sup> The Rangers' main task seems to have been to harry [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)'s forces whenever they entered Ithilien.

At the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) the leader of these Rangers was [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), son of the [Ruling Steward](Ruling_Steward.md) [Denethor](../D/Denethor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-1">[1]</a></sup> Other Rangers at that time were [Damrod](../D/Damrod.md), [Mablung](<../M/Mablung_(ranger_of_Ithilien).md>),<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-1">[1]</a></sup> and [Anborn](../A/Anborn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

On [7 March](../_/7_March.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) were captured by Faramir and other Rangers<sup id="cite_ref-Herbs_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herbs-1">[1]</a></sup> and witnessed a harassing attack by the Rangers of Ithilien upon a regiment of [Southrons](../H/Haradrim.md) marching north.<sup id="cite_ref-Attack_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Attack-3">[3]</a></sup> After the battle Faramir learned of the [One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) carried by Frodo<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and the two hobbits were taken to Henneth Annûn by Faramir and the Rangers.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> There the Rangers captured [Gollum](../G/Gollum.md) at the [Forbidden Pool](../F/Forbidden_Pool.md),<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> but on [8 March](../_/8_March.md)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> he let the three go to seek entry into Mordor.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

Faramir and the Rangers of Ithilien left Henneth Annûn on [9 March](../_/9_March.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> When the [Army of the West](../A/Army_of_the_West.md) marched through Ithilien on its way to the [Morannon](../M/Morannon.md), a powerful force of [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) and [Easterlings](../E/Easterlings.md) tried to ambush its leading companies, but the Rangers of Ithilien, led by Mablung (since Faramir was recovering in the [Houses of Healing](../H/Houses_of_Healing.md)) trapped the ambushers and destroyed them.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Legacy {#Legacy}

After the defeat of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), it is possible that the Rangers of Ithilien may have become the [White Company](../W/White_Company.md), the guard of Faramir, who became the [Prince of Ithilien](../P/Prince_of_Ithilien.md) at the command of King [Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>
