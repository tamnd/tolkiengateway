**Rover**, later known as **Roverandom**, is a black-and-white dog who is the protagonist of the story *[Roverandom](/wiki/Roverandom)*. Rover is turned into a small toy dog by the wizard [Artaxerxes](/wiki/Artaxerxes) for biting the wizard's trousers and the story follows Rover's adventures on his journey to become a normal dog again.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:1 ff</sup> The story was written to console [Michael Tolkien](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien) after leaving his toy dog on a beach in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filey" title="wikipedia:Filey">Filey</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-bio_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bio-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:146</sup>

## Other Rovers {#Other_Rovers}

**Rover**, the [moon](/wiki/Moon "Moon")-dog, is the [Man-in-the-Moon](/wiki/Man-in-the-Moon)'s flying dog. As a result of the moon-dog being bearing the same name, Rover is named "Roverandom".<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:26</sup>

**Rover**, the mer-dog, is owned by the Mer-king's daughter (Mrs [Artaxerxes](/wiki/Artaxerxes)) and goes on adventures around the Deep Blue Sea with Rover.<sup id="cite_ref-Roverandom_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roverandom-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:63</sup>
