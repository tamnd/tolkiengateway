<i><b>rhî</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "crown".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Noldorin *rhî* is a descendant form of [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Kornoldorin "Kornoldorin") *ríge*, deriving from root [RIG](/wiki/RIG#Other_versions).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *[rhîs](/wiki/Rh%C3%AEs "Rhîs")* ("queen")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
