<i><b>ras</b></i> (plural **rais**) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning 'horn'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Caradhras](/wiki/Caradhras)* - "Red-horn"
- *[Nimras](/wiki/Barad_Nimras "Barad Nimras")* - "White Horn"
- *[Ered Nimrais](/wiki/Ered_Nimrais)* - "Mountains White", i.e. the White Mountains
