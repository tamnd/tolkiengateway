**Ralph Johnson** is a Kiwi actor who portrayed [Hugin](/wiki/Hugin)<sup id="cite_ref-GCM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GCM-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*,<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> an Orc, [Uruk](/wiki/Uruk-hai "Uruk-hai") and [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> and a Rohirrim in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup> He was a stand-in for [John Noble](/wiki/John_Noble) as [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) in *The Return of the King*.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-3">[3]</a></sup>

Johnson also portrayed 'Laugher' in *[The Lord of the Rings](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)")* director [Peter Jackson](/wiki/Peter_Jackson)'s film <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Kong_(2005_film)" title="wikipedia:King Kong (2005 film)">King Kong</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-GCM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GCM-1">[1]</a></sup>
