**Rufus Burrows** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Rufus married [Asphodel Brandybuck](/wiki/Asphodel_Brandybuck), and had one child, [Milo](/wiki/Milo_Burrows "Milo Burrows"). They attended [Bilbo's Birthday Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party "Bilbo's Farewell Party") along with Milo's children.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus" title="wikipedia:Rufus">Rufus</a> is a Latin name meaning "red-haired".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
