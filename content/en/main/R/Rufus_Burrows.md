**Rufus Burrows** was a Hobbit of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## History {#History}

Rufus married [Asphodel Brandybuck](../A/Asphodel_Brandybuck.md), and had one child, [Milo](../M/Milo_Burrows.md). They attended [Bilbo's Birthday Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md) along with Milo's children.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus" title="wikipedia:Rufus">Rufus</a> is a Latin name meaning "red-haired".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
