The haven of **Rómenna** was the main seaport of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) situated on its east coast, through which much of the traffic with the old lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) passed. Many of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) were forced to relocate to Rómenna late in Númenor's history.

## Overview {#Overview}

Rómenna was located in the region of [Arandor](/wiki/Arandor) about 50 miles east of the capital city of [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos). The port stood on the south side of the [Firth of Rómenna](/wiki/Firth_of_R%C3%B3menna) which opened onto the [Bay of Rómenna](/wiki/Bay_of_R%C3%B3menna) and then onto the Sea.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The main road of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) ran from Rómenna through Armenelos past [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma) and on to [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) on the west coast.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Rómenna was the greatest port in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). There were large shipyards in Rómenna and the most highly skilled shipwrights worked there. The mariner [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur) had a house with its own quay in Rómenna and [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") frequently came there and kept his ships in the harbour.

[Tar-Meneldur](/wiki/Tar-Meneldur) closed the shipyards in Rómenna during a dispute with his son [Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion "Tar-Aldarion") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [829](/wiki/Second_Age_829 "Second Age 829"). The shipyards were reopened in [843](/wiki/Second_Age_843 "Second Age 843") and Aldarion repaired and expanded the harbour works and built sea-walls. Aldarion's wife [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis) died in the water at Rómenna in [985](/wiki/Second_Age_985 "Second Age 985").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

During the reign of [Ar-Gimilzôr](/wiki/Ar-Gimilz%C3%B4r) ([3102](/wiki/Second_Age_3102 "Second Age 3102")-[3175](/wiki/Second_Age_3175 "Second Age 3175")), many of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful) of Númenor were forced to relocate from [Andúnië](/wiki/And%C3%BAni%C3%AB) to Rómenna so the [King's Men](/wiki/King%27s_Men) could keep watch on them. More were drawn there by Amandil, the leader of the Faithful during the reign of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup> When Númenor was destroyed in [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the Faithful led by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) boarded ships at Rómenna and escaped to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Rómenna* means "eastwards" in Quenya from *[rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men "Rómen")* meaning "east" and the suffix *[-nna](/wiki/-nna)* indicating movement towards.
