**Roswith Krege-Mayer (née Mayer)** (born [1937](../_/1937.md))<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-1">[1]</a></sup> is a German editor and translator.

Roswith Krege-Mayer began to study ancient languages but left the university without a graduation. She worked for academic publishing companies and wrote entries for encyclopedias. In the early 1970s, she moved with her husband [Wolfgang Krege](../W/Wolfgang_Krege.md) to <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart" title="wikipedia:Stuttgart">Stuttgart</a>, where she worked for the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egmont_Ehapa" title="wikipedia:Egmont Ehapa">Ehapa publishing house</a> since [1972](../_/1972.md). As a leading editor for the editorial department for children's and youth literature, Roswith Krege-Mayer administrated the German translations and publications of comic book series like <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asterix" title="wikipedia:Asterix">Asterix</a></i>, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Mouse" title="wikipedia:Mickey Mouse">Mickey Mouse</a></i> (*Micky Maus*), <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Duck_pocket_books" title="wikipedia:Donald Duck pocket books">Donald Duck pocket books</a></i> (*Das Lustige Taschenbuch*), <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iznogoud" title="wikipedia:Iznogoud">Iznogoud</a></i> (*Isnogud*), <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucky_Luke" title="wikipedia:Lucky Luke">Lucky Luke</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Achille_Talon" title="wikipedia:Achille Talon">Achille Talon</a></i> (*Albert Enzian*).<sup id="cite_ref-CF_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CF-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [1991](../_/1991.md) a revised anniversary edition of the first German translation of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)’s *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* was published by [Klett-Cotta](../K/Klett-Cotta.md). It contains for the first time the first part of [Appendix E](../A/Appendix_E.md) (*Pronunciation of Words and Names*), translated by [Helmut W. Pesch](../H/Helmut_W._Pesch.md). The translated text was improved by Roswith Krege-Mayer.<sup id="cite_ref-BIB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BIB-2">[2]</a></sup> Susanne Stopfel emphasizes in her essay *Traitors and Translators: Three German Versions of The Lord of the Rings*, that Krege-Mayer had corrected some of the mistranslations made by the first translator [Margaret Carroux](../M/Margaret_Carroux.md) but had added many others. Stopfel supposes, that Krege-Mayer was unfamiliar with Tolkien’s work and so particularly picked out apparent mistranslations at random, to correct them.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Roswith Krege-Mayer’s revised translation of *The Lord of the Rings* is out of print since [1998](../_/1998.md).<sup id="cite_ref-BIB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BIB-2">[2]</a></sup>
