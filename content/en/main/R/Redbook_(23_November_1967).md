<b><i>Redbook</i> (23 November 1967)</b> is an issue of the magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redbook" title="wikipedia:Redbook">Redbook</a></i>. The issue includes [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s "[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)", being the first publication of the short story in the United States.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
