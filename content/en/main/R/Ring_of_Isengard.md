The **Ring of Isengard** were the rocky walls that surrounded and protected the [Tower of Orthanc](/wiki/Orthanc "Orthanc"). They grew out of the wall of a valley at the southern end of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), and were worked by the early [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) so that they formed a complete defensive circle around the tower. They were torn down by the Ents to make way for the [Treegarth of Orthanc](/wiki/Treegarth_of_Orthanc) at the end of the Third Age.
