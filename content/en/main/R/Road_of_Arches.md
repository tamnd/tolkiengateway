The **Road of Arches** was a street in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It connected the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace) to the north-west [Place of the Well](/wiki/Place_of_the_Well).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) name was <i><b>Kúnë Mallë</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
