**RIP** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "rush, fly, fling".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rimpa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rimpa (page does not exist)">rimpa</a></i> ("rushing, flying")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rhib-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rhib- (page does not exist)">rhib-</a></i>, <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rhimp-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rhimp- (page does not exist)">rhimp-</a></i>, *rhimmo* ("to flow like a \[?torrent\]")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
