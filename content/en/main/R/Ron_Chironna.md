**Ron Chironna** is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).
