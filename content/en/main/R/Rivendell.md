**Rivendell**, or **Imladris**, was an [Elven](../E/Elves.md) outpost in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) on the [eastern edge of Eriador](../E/Eastern_Eriador.md). Due to its location, it was called the **Last Homely House** from the point of view of a traveller going to the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) and [Wilderland](../W/Wilderland.md); and also the **First Homely House** from the point of view of someone coming from these lands to the civilised lands of Eriador to the west.

It was established by [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1697](../S/Second_Age_1697.md) as a refuge from [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) after the [Fall of Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup> It remained Elrond's seat throughout the remainder of the Second Age and until the end of the Third Age, when he took the [White Ship](../W/White_Ship.md) for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). Rivendell had a strong alliance with the [Kings of Arnor](../K/Kings_of_Arnor.md) and after the fall of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md), it became a refuge for the [Rangers of the North](Rangers_of_the_North.md) and the [Heir of Isildur](../H/Heir_of_Isildur.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

Rivendell was located at the edge of a narrow gorge of the [Bruinen River](../B/Bruinen.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> but well hidden in the moorlands and foothills of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-4">[4]</a></sup>

Pine-trees grew on the top of the valley, and there was a "steep zig-zag path" to the valley bottom. The vegetation in the valley bottom was mostly oak and beech.<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Second Age {#Second_Age}

#### Foundation {#Foundation}

During the [War of the Elves and Sauron](../W/War_of_the_Elves_and_Sauron.md), and as [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) was moving against Eriador, Elrond led an army of [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) to the region. They arrived late but were joined by a detachment out of [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md) led by [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md). Despite this, Sauron's armies overwhelmed the Elves, and Elrond was unable to reach Eregion. Forced northward toward the headwaters of [Bruinen](../B/Bruinen.md), Elrond established a stronghold.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup>

This became a refuge for those seeking shelter from Sauron's onslaught in Eriador, and Elrond's and Celeborn's Elves were soon joined by many fleeing the [Sack of Eregion](../S/Sack_of_Eregion.md). More came as Sauron ravaged Eriador on his march toward [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md). By [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [1700](../S/Second_Age_1700.md), Sauron had overrun all of Eriador and Imladris was [besieged](../F/First_Siege_of_Imladris.md). The siege ended when Gil-galad's forces, strengthened by the armament sent by [Tar-Minastir](../T/Tar-Minastir.md), routed Sauron's armies and drove him out of Eriador.

A Council held at that time made Elrond Gil-galad's vice-regent in Eriador, and Imladris his seat. Gil-galad bestowed [Vilya](../V/Vilya.md) upon Elrond in secret. Aided by the power of the [Elven ring](../T/Three_Rings.md), Elrond made Imladris one of the chief seats of Elvish strength in the west in the latter part of the Second Age. Imladris remained an Elvish stronghold in Eriador to the end of the Second Age and into the Third.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

#### Last Alliance {#Last_Alliance}

At the end of the Second Age, [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and Gil-galad formed the [Last Alliance of Elves and Men](../L/Last_Alliance_of_Elves_and_Men.md) to challenge Sauron, and their host halted for a while at Imladris in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3431](../S/Second_Age_3431.md) and they stayed there for 3 years, gathered their forces before crossing the Misty Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SV_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SV-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)'s wife and his youngest son, [Valandil](<../V/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor).md>), were in Imladris at that time, and Isildur was journeying back to them after Sauron's defeat when he was ambushed at the [Gladden Fields](../G/Gladden_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> After receiving the shards of [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md), Valandil took up his role as King of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and left Imladris for [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SV_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SV-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Third Age {#Third_Age}

After the fall of Gil-galad, Elrond remained in Imladris. During the Third Age, it was a refuge and sanctuary; many Elves gathered there, and it was the chief dwelling of the [High Elves](../H/High_Elves.md) in Eriador, except the [Elves of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md), but also wise and powerful people of all the races. Most of the Elves who lived in Rivendell were High Elves.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> The [Heirs of Isildur](../H/Heir_of_Isildur.md) were also harboured there due to their kinship with Elrond, as descendants of his brother [Elros](../E/Elros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SV_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SV-6">[6]</a></sup>

Danger came to Eriador around [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1300](../T/Third_Age_1300.md) when [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) was established in the north-east beyond the [Ettenmoors](../E/Ettenmoors.md). It was late in the reign of [Arveleg I](../A/Arveleg_I.md) that [Rivendell was besieged by Angmar](../S/Second_Siege_of_Imladris~68857.md). After an incursion by Angmar into Eriador in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md), the Elves of Rivendell joined [those of Lindon](../E/Elves_of_Lindon.md) and the [Galadhrim](../G/Galadhrim.md), whom Elrond brought over the Mountains, in subduing the power of the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) for many years.<sup id="cite_ref-eriador_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-eriador-9">[9]</a></sup>

After the end of the North-kingdom in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md) [Aranarth](../A/Aranarth.md), the first [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md), had [his son](../A/Arahael.md) fostered in Rivendell, as were all subsequent sons of the chieftains. The heirlooms and treasures of the house of Isildur, including the shards of [Narsil](../N/Narsil.md), were also kept there after [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md)'s death.<sup id="cite_ref-SV_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SV-6">[6]</a></sup> Among these were also some of the few remaining documents containing the history of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

There was traffic across the Misty Mountains in the Third Age between Imladris and [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md), for Elrond's wife was [Celebrían](../C/Celebrían.md), daughter of Galadriel and Celeborn.

In the [Quest of Erebor](../Q/Quest_of_Erebor.md), [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) stopped off at Rivendell with the Dwarves on the way to the [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Rest_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rest-4">[4]</a></sup> and also on the way back to [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) with [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Bilbo retired there after his 111th birthday, writing his memoir *[There and Back Again](../T/There_and_Back_Again.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-12">[12]</a></sup>

Years later, [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and his [companions](../T/Travellers.md) journeyed to Rivendell, where they met Bilbo. Several other Elves, [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) had also arrived at Rivendell on separate errands;<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-12">[12]</a></sup> at the [Council of Elrond](../C/Council_of_Elrond.md) they learned that all of their errands were related to the fate of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md), and they had to decide what to do about it. In the end, the Hobbits influenced the decision.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

Following the destruction of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md) at the end of the Third Age, Elrond's ring lost its power, and Elrond, with many of his household, left Rivendell to sail for [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) from the [Grey Havens](../G/Grey_Havens.md); this marked the start of the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

### Later history {#Later_history}

Although Elrond departed from Rivendell, [Elladan and Elrohir](../E/Elladan_and_Elrohir.md) remained for a while with some of the remaining [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-14">[14]</a></sup> It is believed that [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) also relocated to Rivendell after the departure of [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and having also grown weary of [East Lórien](../E/East_Lórien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Prologue_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Prologue-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

It is not known when Rivendell was finally abandoned, but shortly before [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) died in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md), he said to [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) that "none now walk" in the garden of Elrond, indicating that the last of the [Noldor](../N/Noldor.md) had departed to [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rivendell* ("<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/cloven" title="wiktionary:cloven">cloven</a>-<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/dell" title="wiktionary:dell">dell</a>") is the [Common Speech](../W/Westron.md) translation of the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name <i><b>Imladris</b></i> ("deep dale of the cleft").<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup> The name *Imladris* is also glossed as "flat-floored valley of the Cleft"<sup id="cite_ref-letter_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-letter-17">[17]</a></sup> but also simply as "Canyon of the Cleft".<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup>

The original Common Speech name of *Rivendell* was <i><b>Karningul</b></i> (itself a translation of *Imladris*).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name <i><b>Arcimbelë</b></i> is used only once in a manuscript, and its meaning is unclear except for the element *[imbë](../I/Imbë.md)* ("deep vale").<sup id="cite_ref-Verb_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Verb-20">[20]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:125</sup> A more literal translation is given: *Latimberista*, from *latimbë* ("glen") + *rista* ("cut"). However, on the same page, Tolkien stated that this name was not used in practice, and was replaced the Quenyarized form of *Imladris*: <i><b>Imbeláris</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Verb_20-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Verb-20">[20]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:127</sup>

One of the names of the House of Elrond in Imladris was **Mar Vanwa Tyaliéva** ("House of Past (or Departed) Mirth") in Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">[21]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:80</sup> This name was retained from the earlier [Cottage of the Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_the_Lost_Play.md) found in the [Book of Lost Tales](../B/Book_of_Lost_Tales.md).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

A possible inspiration (so suggested by [David Salo](../D/David_Salo.md)<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">[22]</a></sup>) could have been <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauterbrunnen" title="wikipedia:Lauterbrunnen">Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland</a>, which Tolkien visited in [1911](../_/1911.md):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I am... delighted that you have made the acquaintance of Switzerland, and of the very part that I once knew best and which had the deepest effect on me. The hobbit’s journey from Rivendell to the other side of the Misty Mountains, including the glissade down the slithering stones into the pine woods, is based on my adventures in 1911 (he was 19 and travelled to Lauterbrunnen)... Our wanderings mainly on foot in a party of 12 are not now clear in sequence, but leave many vivid pictures as clear as yesterday.&#34; (He talks of similar conditions of the travel of Bilbo camping out, walking mountain paths, carrying packs).
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../L/Letter_306.md" title="Letter 306">Letter 306</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

<b>2001-03: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rivendell appears in every instalment, although its role in the second and third film is rather minor.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game).md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rivendell is the fifth level and the first where no fighting is required. After Frodo is taken to Rivendell by <a href="../G/Glorfindel.md" title="Glorfindel">Glorfindel</a>, he is first woken by <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>; after that, the <a href="../C/Council_of_Elrond.md" title="Council of Elrond">Council of Elrond</a> starts. After the council, there is an opportunity to speak to the NPCs - <a href="../A/Aragorn.md" title="Aragorn">Aragorn</a>, <a href="../A/Arwen.md" title="Arwen">Arwen</a>, <a href="../B/Boromir.md" title="Boromir">Boromir</a>, <a href="../E/Elrond.md" title="Elrond">Elrond</a>, <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a>, <a href="../G/Gimli.md" title="Gimli">Gimli</a>, <a href="../L/Legolas.md" title="Legolas">Legolas</a>, <a href="../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md" title="Meriadoc Brandybuck">Merry</a>, <a href="../P/Peregrin_Took.md" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a> and <a href="../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md" title="Samwise Gamgee">Sam</a>. The gameplay continues by entering a door. A short cutscene follows in which Bilbo gives <a href="../S/Sting.md" title="Sting">Sting</a> and the <i><a href="../M/Mithril.md" title="Mithril">mithril</a></i> shirt.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">[23]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rivendell is one of the battlegrounds in the game and a site of a skirmish between Elves and Goblins.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rivendell is one of the major cities in the game, located between Trollshaws and the Misty Mountains. Rivendell is also one of the places that players can choose as Elven character&#39;s homeland. The Last Homely House refers to a large dwelling of Elrond, not to the entire refuge of Rivendell. It is located on the eastern side of Rivendell. One can find Elrond&#39;s library, the <a href="../H/Hall_of_Fire.md" title="Hall of Fire">Hall of Fire</a>, the scholar&#39;s guild and various guest rooms.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_in_the_North.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rivendell is one of the major cities in the game and the only place characters are obliged to visit more than once (other cities can be re-visited, but this is optional).</dd></dl>

<b>2012-14: <a href="../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rivendell appears in the <a href="../T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">first film</a> after <a href="../G/Gandalf.md" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> leads <a href="../T/Thorin_and_Company.md" title="Thorin and Company">Thorin and Company</a> away from <a href="../Y/Yazneg.md" title="Yazneg">Yazneg</a> and the <a href="../W/Wargs.md" title="Wargs">Wargs</a>. Much of Rivendell is familiar from <a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a> but new areas have been included, such as the meeting place of the <a href="../W/White_Council.md" title="White Council">White Council</a> and a crystal table used for investigating the <a href="../M/Moon-letters.md" title="Moon-letters">Moon-letters</a> in <a href="../T/Thrór&#39;s_Map.md" title="Thrór&#39;s Map">Thrór&#39;s Map</a>.</dd></dl>
