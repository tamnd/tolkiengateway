**Rose "Rosie" Gamgee** (née **Cotton**) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2984](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984")<sup id="cite_ref-GamFam_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamFam-1">[1]</a></sup> - [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day) [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61")<sup id="cite_ref-Later_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-2">[2]</a></sup>) was the daughter of [Tolman Cotton Senior](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior) and [Lily Brown](/wiki/Lily_Brown). She married [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) at the end of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), and bore him a record thirteen children.

## Life {#Life}

### Youth {#Youth}

Rose Cotton was born in the year [1384](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984") by [Shire Reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning), the second child and first daughter of [Tolman Cotton](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior") and [Lily Brown](/wiki/Lily_Brown).<sup id="cite_ref-GamFam_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamFam-1">[1]</a></sup> The Cotton family lived in a farm on the [South Lane](/wiki/South_Lane), which was fairly close to [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater).<sup id="cite_ref-HandS612_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HandS612-3">[3]</a></sup> Not much of her youth is known, except that she and her brothers - [Young Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior"), [Jolly](/wiki/Wilcome_Cotton_II "Wilcome Cotton II"), [Nick](/wiki/Bowman_Cotton "Bowman Cotton") and [Nibs](/wiki/Carl_Cotton "Carl Cotton") - frequently swam in the [Bywater Pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool) with the children of the Gamgee family, to which the Cottons were close.<sup id="cite_ref-MtDoom_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MtDoom-4">[4]</a></sup>

### War of the Ring {#War_of_the_Ring}

Rosie was still fond of Sam when both grew to adulthood, and when he announced he'd be going with [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) to [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow), she did not like it.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-5">[5]</a></sup> Even though everyone had given up hope of seeing Sam - or any of the other [Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") - back, she waited patiently. Since the Spring of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), she had expected Sam to return.

At the time, [Ruffian](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians") rule over the Shire had lasted some three months, having started earlier that year.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Old Tom Cotton had been itching to get his hands on them, but he refrained from doing so as he had his wife and Rosie to look after. Rosie was relieved to find Sam on the doorstep on [November 2](/wiki/2_November "2 November").

Rosie and her mother tended to Sam, Frodo and several others that night, as they prepared to [drive out the Ruffians](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater "Battle of Bywater"). With shining eyes and a smile on her face she heard Frodo tell of Sam's adventure and fame abroad.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-7">[7]</a></sup> Sam and Frodo lived with the Cottons until [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row) and [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End) were restored, the following Spring. Sam later joined Frodo in Bag End, but was torn in two - he wanted to stay loyal to Frodo, as well as marry Rosie.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-5">[5]</a></sup>

Frodo solved this problem by telling Sam that he and Rosie would both be welcome to live with him at Bag End after the wedding. Sam and Rosie married on [May 1](/wiki/1_May "1 May"), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3020](/wiki/Third_Age_3020 "Third Age 3020"),<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-8">[8]</a></sup> and moved into Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-Grey_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grey-5">[5]</a></sup> There, they lived happily, and begot [Elanor](/wiki/Elanor_Gardner "Elanor Gardner"), their first daughter, who was born on the [anniversary of the Downfall of Sauron](/wiki/25_March "25 March").<sup id="cite_ref-Chief_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chief-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Fourth Age {#Fourth_Age}

The Fourth Age started with one child, but many more would follow. Rose bore a record thirteen children, beating [Adamanta Chubb](/wiki/Adamanta_Chubb), the wife of the [Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"), by one.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Gardner "Frodo Gardner") was born in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [2](/wiki/Fourth_Age_2 "Fourth Age 2"), [Rose-lass](/wiki/Rose_Gardner "Rose Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [4](/wiki/Fourth_Age_4 "Fourth Age 4"), [Merry](/wiki/Merry_Gardner "Merry Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [6](/wiki/Fourth_Age_6 "Fourth Age 6"), [Pippin](/wiki/Pippin_Gardner "Pippin Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [8](/wiki/Fourth_Age_8 "Fourth Age 8"), [Goldilocks](/wiki/Goldilocks_Gardner "Goldilocks Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [10](/wiki/Fourth_Age_10 "Fourth Age 10"), [Hamfast](/wiki/Hamfast_Gardner "Hamfast Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [11](/wiki/Fourth_Age_11 "Fourth Age 11"), [Daisy](/wiki/Daisy_Gardner "Daisy Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [12](/wiki/Fourth_Age_12 "Fourth Age 12"), [Primrose](/wiki/Primrose_Gardner "Primrose Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [14](/wiki/Fourth_Age_14 "Fourth Age 14"), [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Gardner "Bilbo Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15"), [Ruby](/wiki/Ruby_Gardner "Ruby Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [17](/wiki/Fourth_Age_17 "Fourth Age 17"), [Robin](/wiki/Robin_Gardner "Robin Gardner") in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [19](/wiki/Fourth_Age_19 "Fourth Age 19"), and their last child, [Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Gardner "Tolman Gardner"), in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [21](/wiki/Fourth_Age_21 "Fourth Age 21").<sup id="cite_ref-GamFam_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamFam-1">[1]</a></sup>

That year, [21](/wiki/Fourth_Age_21 "Fourth Age 21"), marked the only major undertaking of Mistress Rose. She and Sam travelled to [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), and stayed with [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") for well over a year. In the meantime, Rose's brother [Tom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Junior "Tolman Cotton Junior") took over Sam's tasks as [Mayor](/wiki/Mayor_of_Michel_Delving "Mayor of Michel Delving"). After their return, Rose would continue to live by her husband's side, until her passing at the age of 98 at [Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day) of [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [61](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61").<sup id="cite_ref-Later_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Rose* is a translation of an unknown [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name. She is, like many female Hobbits, named after a flower,<sup id="cite_ref-OnTrans_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OnTrans-10">[10]</a></sup> the [rose](/wiki/Roses "Roses"). The surname **Cotton** comes from a Westron *Hlothran*, which roughly translates as "Cottager".<sup id="cite_ref-OnTrans_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OnTrans-10">[10]</a></sup>

In the *[King's Letter](/wiki/King%27s_Letter)*, which was not included in *The Return of the King* as published, [Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") translated the name Rose as <i><b><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Meril" title="Meril">Meril</a></b></i> in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-TheEp_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TheEp-11">[11]</a></sup> In his linguistical analysis of the *King's Letter*, [Carl F. Hostetter](/wiki/Carl_F._Hostetter) noted the resemblance to [Meril-i-Turinqi](/wiki/Meril-i-Turinqi), the queen of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) in the earliest stages of the *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Hostetter could not connect it to any entry in *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, which mirrors [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)'s observations on Meril-i-Turinqi, for which no root could be found in the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*<sup id="cite_ref-BoLTApp_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BoLTApp-13">[13]</a></sup> - however, a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) translation was translated as "Queen of Flowers".<sup id="cite_ref-BoLTApp_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BoLTApp-13">[13]</a></sup> As the [second element](/wiki/Tur "Tur") represents the "queen" part, *Meril* appears to be one of the oldest words for "Flower" in the *[legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium")*.

<b>1980: <a href="/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)" title="The Return of the King (1980 film)"><i>The Return of the King</i> (1980 film)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>During a ring-induced dream at the <a href="/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol" title="Tower of Cirith Ungol">Tower of Cirith Ungol</a>, Sam thinks of going back home. An older man, he sits and watches several children playing about. A blonde woman, obviously his wife, carries a baby to him. She is not identified as Rosie on screen, so the identification is conjectural.</dd></dl>

<b>1981: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (1981 radio series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rose was voiced by <a href="/wiki/Kathryn_Hurlbutt" title="Kathryn Hurlbutt">Kathryn Hurlbutt</a>. At the end, she has one child, <a href="/wiki/Elanor_Gardner" title="Elanor Gardner">Elanor</a>, portrayed by <a href="/wiki/Harry_Holm" title="Harry Holm">Harry Holm</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1990: <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_The_Lord_of_the_Rings,_Vol._I_(1990_video_game)" title="J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I (1990 video game)"><i>J.R.R. Tolkien&#39;s The Lord of the Rings, Vol. I</i> (PC video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rose is found at Cotton farm (placed in <a href="/wiki/Buckland" title="Buckland">Buckland</a>), where she gives Sam a token of her affection.</dd></dl>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>: </b>

<dl><dd>Rosie, played by <a href="/wiki/Sarah_McLeod" title="Sarah McLeod">Sarah McLeod</a>, dances at the <a href="/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party" title="Bilbo&#39;s Farewell Party">Farewell Party</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> She is portrayed as a popular girl. In the <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)">extended edition</a>, she is the barmaid of the <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Green_Dragon" title="Green Dragon">Green Dragon</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rosie is, once again, the barmaid of the <i>Green Dragon</i>. She shows more concern and affection for Frodo than for Sam, whom she considers childish. Rose lives with her father at Number 3, <a href="/wiki/South_Lane" title="South Lane">South Lane</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bywater" title="Bywater">Bywater</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup> No voice actress is specified for this role, but it is a thinly veiled <a href="/wiki/Jennifer_Hale" title="Jennifer Hale">Jennifer Hale</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rosie was, again, played by Sarah McLeod. Still the barmaid of the <i>Green Dragon</i>, Sam makes his move on her, and they marry.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> Despite Frodo being gone, they live at Number 3, Bagshot Row. Sam and Rosie have two children: <a href="/wiki/Elanor_Gardner" title="Elanor Gardner">Elanor</a> (played by <a href="/wiki/Sean_Astin" title="Sean Astin">Sean Astin</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Alexandra_Astin" title="Alexandra Astin">daughter Alexandra</a>), and <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Gardner" title="Frodo Gardner">Frodo</a> (played by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Maisy_McLeod-Riviera&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Maisy McLeod-Riviera (page does not exist)">Maisy McLeod-Riviera</a>, Sarah McLeod&#39;s daughter).<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rosie can be found at her family farmstead, but currently plays no role in the storyline. During the Farmers Faire festival she can be seen dancing at the nearby Methel-stage.</dd></dl>

<b>2010: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Aragorn%27s_Quest" title="The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest">The Lord of the Rings: Aragorn&#39;s Quest</a>:</i></b><i></i>

<dl><dd>Rosie features prominently throughout the game. She is voiced by Deborah Baker Jr.</dd></dl>

<b>2025: <i><a href="/wiki/Tales_of_the_Shire:_A_%22The_Lord_of_the_Rings%22_Game" title="Tales of the Shire: A &#34;The Lord of the Rings&#34; Game">Tales of the Shire: A &#34;The Lord of the Rings&#34; Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rosie Cotton is an interactive character. She can be invited to meals and, after reaching a milestone, can trade with the player.</dd></dl>
