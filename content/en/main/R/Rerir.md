**Mount Rerir** was a mountain on the western side of the [Ered Luin](/wiki/Ered_Luin), in [Thargelion](/wiki/Thargelion) (a region of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand)) to the north of [Lake Helevorn](/wiki/Helevorn "Helevorn"), and it was one of the sources of [Gelion](/wiki/Gelion).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The land around the mountain belonged to [Caranthir](/wiki/Caranthir), who built a fortress on its western slopes.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The fortress was lost during [Dagor Bragollach](/wiki/Dagor_Bragollach).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Elven forces probably withdrew from the area after the battle, and most definitely after [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

After the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath), Rerir was apparently heavily damaged, and from the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) onward it was but only a shadow of its former size.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rerir* is a name in an unknown language, and of an unknown meaning.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) proposes the name is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Perhaps [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) re-used a name appearing in Norse mythology: in *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)*, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rerir" title="wikipedia:Rerir">Rerir</a></i> is the name of the grandson of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odin" title="wikipedia:Odin">Ódin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
