**Ravens** were large black birds,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> associated with ill news of death,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and were known for their harsh voices.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The intelligent ravens of [Ravenhill](/wiki/Ravenhill) near [Erebor](/wiki/Erebor), could live to a great age and some could even speak [Westron](/wiki/Westron) and were friendly with the [Dwarves of Erebor](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Erebor). [Carc](/wiki/Carc) had been one of these, and his son [Roäc](/wiki/Ro%C3%A4c) was the leader of the ravens of Erebor around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), when [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) reclaimed the mountain from [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) became one of the [Guards of the Citadel](/wiki/Guards_of_the_Citadel), his new garments included a "*high-crowned helm with small raven-wings on either side*".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It has been suggested that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s use of ravens is reminiscent of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odin" title="wikipedia:Odin">Odin</a>'s two messenger-ravens <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huginn_and_Muninn" title="wikipedia:Huginn and Muninn">Huginn and Muninn</a> of the Norse mythology, appearing in the *[Poetic Edda](/wiki/Poetic_Edda)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> *See also: [Roäc: Inspiration](/wiki/Ro%C3%A4c#Inspiration "Roäc")*

In *[The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn)*, the lord of the ravens is <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rerir" title="wikipedia:Rerir">Rerir</a>, grandson of Ódin.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Ravens are further associated with the coming of death.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Raven_(MERP).jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Raven_%28MERP%29.jpg/150px-Raven_%28MERP%29.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="150" height="128" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/ba/Raven_%28MERP%29.jpg/225px-Raven_%28MERP%29.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/b/ba/Raven_%28MERP%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="255"/></a><figcaption>Raven portrayed in <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">MERP</a></i></figcaption></figure>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Ravens, level 4 birds, are enchanted and intelligent, friends of the Free Peoples. They are reputedly the messengers of the Vala <a href="/wiki/Mandos" title="Mandos">Mandos</a>, and are able to speak many Mannish and Elvish tongues.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2002-5: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game">The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Ravens of the North&#34; are &#34;<i>creatures of especially powerful magic</i>&#34;, being able to understand Westron and having extraordinarily long lives.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Many ravens are seen returning to the <a href="/wiki/Lonely_Mountain" title="Lonely Mountain">Lonely Mountain</a> after <a href="/wiki/Smaug" title="Smaug">Smaug</a> is slain. One is later seen setting out for the <a href="/wiki/Iron_Hills" title="Iron Hills">Iron Hills</a>, and then returning just as <a href="/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot" title="Dáin Ironfoot">Dáin Ironfoot</a>&#39;s army arrives. It is possible this raven could be serving the role of <a href="/wiki/Ro%C3%A4c" title="Roäc">Roäc</a>, but it is never confirmed. Despite the fact that real-life ravens can mimic human speech, they do not speak at all, as with all other birds in <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson</a>&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a> films.</dd></dl>

<b>2023: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Return_to_Moria" title="The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria">The Lord of the Rings: Return to Moria</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Aric" title="Aric">Aric</a> is a raven of Erebor who appears periodically in the game to push the story forward, and to give quests to the player.</dd></dl>
