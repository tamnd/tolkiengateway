**Rose Gardner** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Rose was the second daughter and third child of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) and [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> named after her mother at the suggestion of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Roses](/wiki/Roses) were common flowers in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), and flower names were common in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the name *Rose* is *Meril*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
