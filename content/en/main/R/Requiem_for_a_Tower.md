<i><b>Requiem for a Tower</b></i> is the name for the up-beat song present in a trailer for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Two_Towers)*. It is often associated with [Peter Jackson's](/wiki/Peter_Jackson "Peter Jackson") [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"), but the track was neither composed for the films nor featured in them. It was the main theme for the [2000](/wiki/2000) film <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requiem_for_a_Dream" title="wikipedia:Requiem for a Dream">Requiem for a Dream</a></i>. The piece of music is called <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lux_Aeterna_(Requiem_for_a_Dream)" title="wikipedia:Lux Aeterna (Requiem for a Dream)">Lux Aeterna</a></i> and was composed by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Mansell" title="wikipedia:Clint Mansell">Clint Mansell</a>.

For the trailer the song was re-orchestrated by <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Ant_Farm&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Ant Farm (page does not exist)">The Ant Farm</a></i> with three composers (Simone Benyacar, Dan Nielsen and Veigar Mairgersson), forty musicians and twenty choral voices.

A variation of the song would later be used in *[The Hunt for Gollum](/wiki/The_Hunt_for_Gollum)*, accompanying a fight sequence.
