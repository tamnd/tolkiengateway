**Robin Wood** ([1953](../_/1953.md)-[19 April](../_/19_April.md) [2021](../_/2021.md)) was an American artist who was known for illustrating covers for [Dungeons and Dragons](../D/Dungeons_and_Dragons.md) and [Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.
