**Robin Wood** ([1953](/wiki/1953)-[19 April](/wiki/19_April) [2021](/wiki/2021)) was an American artist who was known for illustrating covers for [Dungeons and Dragons](/wiki/Dungeons_and_Dragons) and [Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.
