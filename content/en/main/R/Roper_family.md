The **Roper Family** was a young family of [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md), an offshoot of the [Gamgee Family](../G/Gamgee_Family~66365.md).

## History {#History}

[Andwise Gamgee](../A/Andwise_Roper.md) adopted the surname Roper in honour of the [family trade](Ropers.md), which was started two generations earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Ropers stayed in their ancestral home of [Tighfield](../T/Tighfield.md), while their relatives carried the Gamgee name to [other](../O/Overhill.md) [places](../H/Hobbiton.md) and followed other trades.
