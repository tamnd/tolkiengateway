The **Roper Family** was a young family of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits), an offshoot of the [Gamgee Family](/wiki/Gamgee_Family).

## History {#History}

[Andwise Gamgee](/wiki/Andwise_Roper "Andwise Roper") adopted the surname Roper in honour of the [family trade](/wiki/Ropers "Ropers"), which was started two generations earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Ropers stayed in their ancestral home of [Tighfield](/wiki/Tighfield), while their relatives carried the Gamgee name to [other](/wiki/Overhill "Overhill") [places](/wiki/Hobbiton "Hobbiton") and followed other trades.
