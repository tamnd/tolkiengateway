In an early conception of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md), **Ringil** was one of the two pillars that held one of the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) created to light [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## History {#History}

Ringil was a great pillar, raised by [Melko](../M/Morgoth.md) to house the *northern* of the [Two Lamps](../T/Two_Lamps.md) fashioned by [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md) to illuminate [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). The pillar was extremely tall, rising to the stars, and shone like pale blue crystal. Melkor claimed that Ringil and its southern twin [Helkar](../H/Helkar.md) were made of a strong, imperishable substance that he had devised; in reality he lied for they were made of ice (a material unknown to the other [Valar](../V/Valar.md)). When Aulë's lamps were placed upon them (silver in the north and gold in the south) the ice melted and caused great floods to pour into the seas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
