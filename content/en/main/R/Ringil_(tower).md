In an early conception of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), **Ringil** was one of the two pillars that held one of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) created to light [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## History {#History}

Ringil was a great pillar, raised by [Melko](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth") to house the *northern* of the [Two Lamps](/wiki/Two_Lamps) fashioned by [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) to illuminate [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth). The pillar was extremely tall, rising to the stars, and shone like pale blue crystal. Melkor claimed that Ringil and its southern twin [Helkar](/wiki/Helkar) were made of a strong, imperishable substance that he had devised; in reality he lied for they were made of ice (a material unknown to the other [Valar](/wiki/Valar)). When Aulë's lamps were placed upon them (silver in the north and gold in the south) the ice melted and caused great floods to pour into the seas.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
