**Ring-bearers** was a title used for those three [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) ([Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)) who had at one time borne [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring"), and willingly aided in its destruction. Because of their status as Ring-bearers, they were granted the passage of the [Straight Road](/wiki/Straight_Road), and each eventually went to the [Blessed Realm](/wiki/Aman "Aman") to live out their lives.

Though only these three Hobbits are strictly counted as Ring-bearers, in fact the Ring was borne by other hands besides; and over its history of about 4.8 millennia only a very few individuals had ever touched it.

## List of persons who touched the Ring {#List_of_persons_who_touched_the_Ring}

Those who actually wore the Ring, as opposed to picking it up or holding it, are shown in bold.

- **[Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)** - He forged the One Ring for himself around [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600") and bore it for approximately 1840 years until his defeat in the [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3441](/wiki/Second_Age_3441 "Second Age 3441").
- **[Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)** - Against the advice of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) and [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan), he took the Ring for himself. He did not bear it long - two years later, Isildur was ambushed by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and the One Ring was lost in the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) on [4 October](/wiki/4_October), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2](/wiki/Third_Age_2 "Third Age 2"), where it lay hidden for most of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).
- [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol) - He discovered the Ring while fishing the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) in about [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2463](/wiki/Third_Age_2463 "Third Age 2463"). He held it for only a short time before he was murdered by his friend [Sméagol](/wiki/Sm%C3%A9agol), who stole the Ring for himself.
- **[Gollum](/wiki/Gollum)** - He took the Ring from [Déagol](/wiki/D%C3%A9agol), and soon hid with it in the roots of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains). He bore it in the dark for almost five hundred years. He was also the last to touch it before its destruction.
- **[Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)** - He discovered the Ring 'by accident' while imprisoned beneath the Misty Mountains in July [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941"), and claimed it from Gollum by defeating him in a [Riddle-game](/wiki/Riddle-game). He carried it eastward to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), and then back westward to the Shire, where he kept it. He bore the Ring for almost exactly sixty years.
- **[Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins)** - He was given the Ring by Bilbo on the advice of Gandalf. He was the Ring-bearer, who carried the One Ring from the Shire to Orodruin, to the very brink of the Cracks of Doom. Once there, the Ring's power overmastered him, and he named it to himself, but Gollum bit it from his finger and fell into the Fire. Frodo bore the Ring for seventeen years until its destruction.
- [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) - He held it for a matter of moments on the morning of [13 April](/wiki/13_April), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), before throwing it into Frodo's fire. This was the moment that the Ring's fiery inscription appeared, confirming that it was the One Ring.
- **[Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil)** - He wore it on his finger for a moment on [27 September](/wiki/27_September), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3018](/wiki/Third_Age_3018 "Third Age 3018"), but was unaffected by it. The reason that Tom was immune to the effects of the Ring remains an abiding mystery.
- **[Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee)** - He bore the Ring for about two days in March [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), while Frodo his master was in the clutches of Orcs in the [Tower of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Tower_of_Cirith_Ungol).

## A possible Ring-bearer? {#A_possible_Ring-bearer?}

While Frodo was recovering in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell), one morning he found the Ring passed through a chain for him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is not known what person or persons did this while he was sleeping. It could be [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), or any [High Elf](/wiki/Calaquendi "Calaquendi") of Rivendell, but their identity, the process, and whether they touched it, are unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) translation of "Ring-bearers" is given as <i><b><a href="/wiki/Corma" title="Corma">Cormacolindor</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
