- **[Rían](Rían.md) ([F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [450](../F/First_Age_450.md) - [F.A.](../F/First_Age.md) [472](../F/First_Age_472.md)), wife of [Huor](../H/Huor.md) and mother of [Tuor](../T/Tuor.md)**
