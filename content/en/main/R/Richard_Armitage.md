**Richard Armitage** portrayed [Thorin](../T/Thorin.md) in the [2012](../_/2012.md)-[14](../_/2014.md) [*The Hobbit* film series](<../T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-Announcement_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Announcement-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Richard was born in [August 22](../_/22_August.md), [1971](../_/1971.md) at Leicester, England. He attended Huncote Primary School in Huncote Leicester. Later he attended Brockintron Collage in Enderby where he studied music, playing the cello in school and in local orchestras as well as learning to play the flute. Then he went to Pattison College in Coventry where he learned to dance and act.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
