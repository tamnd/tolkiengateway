The **Ravines of Taeglin** were the ravines into which the River [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin) descended as it skirted the eaves of the [Forest of Brethil](/wiki/Forest_of_Brethil).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They were used by [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin "Túrin") to climb beneath the belly of [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung) and slay him.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
