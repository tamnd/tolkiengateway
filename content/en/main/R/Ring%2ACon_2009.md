<b>Ring*Con 2009</b> is an upcoming convention in Germany, scheduled to take place from [October 2](/wiki/2_October "2 October") to [October 4](/wiki/4_October "4 October"), in the Maritim Hotel in Bonn.

## Guests {#Guests}

In addition to several stars from <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_(film_series)" title="wikipedia:Harry Potter (film series)">Harry Potter</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twilight_(2008_film)" title="wikipedia:Twilight (2008 film)">Twilight</a></i>, the following stars are confirmed to attend:

- [Bruce Hopkins](/wiki/Bruce_Hopkins)
- [Craig Parker](/wiki/Craig_Parker)
- [Lori Dungey](/wiki/Lori_Dungey)
- [Thomas Robins](/wiki/Thomas_Robins)
- [Robert Pollock](/wiki/Robert_Pollock)
- [Kate Madison](/wiki/Kate_Madison) (*[Born of Hope](/wiki/Born_of_Hope)*)
- Christopher Dane (*[Born of Hope](/wiki/Born_of_Hope)*)
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Alexandra_Velten&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Alexandra Velten (page does not exist)">Alexandra Velten</a>
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Friedhelm_Schneidewind&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Friedhelm Schneidewind (page does not exist)">Friedhelm Schneidewind</a>
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Oliver_Bidlo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Oliver Bidlo (page does not exist)">Oliver Bidlo</a>
- [Marcel Bülles](/wiki/Marcel_B%C3%BClles)
