The **Red Elves**, or **Red Gnomes**, lived at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Pole" title="wikipedia:North Pole">North Pole</a> with [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) in the *[Letters from Father Christmas](../L/Letters_from_Father_Christmas.md)*. They were first mentioned in a letter written on [30 November](../_/30_November.md), [1932](../_/1932.md). They were known to live in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway" title="wikipedia:Norway">Norway</a>.

## History {#History}

The Red Elves were sent for by Father Christmas in 1932, and helped him and the [North Polar Bear](../N/North_Polar_Bear.md) capture many [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md). They appeared again the [next year](../_/1933.md) around [21 November](../_/21_November.md) to help deal with another goblin attack. In the battle that followed, many Red Elves got hurt. In [1934](../_/1934.md), the Red Elves were invited to a special <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxing_Day" title="wikipedia:Boxing Day">Boxing Day</a> party. About a week before the next Christmas, the Red Elves enjoyed their time shoveling out Father Christmas' house. That year, they received magic sparkler spears to scare goblins away. During [1936](../_/1936.md), many Red Elves moved to [Cliff House](../C/Cliff_House.md).
