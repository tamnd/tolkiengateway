The **Red Elves**, or **Red Gnomes**, lived at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Pole" title="wikipedia:North Pole">North Pole</a> with [Father Christmas](/wiki/Father_Christmas) in the *[Letters from Father Christmas](/wiki/Letters_from_Father_Christmas)*. They were first mentioned in a letter written on [30 November](/wiki/30_November), [1932](/wiki/1932). They were known to live in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway" title="wikipedia:Norway">Norway</a>.

## History {#History}

The Red Elves were sent for by Father Christmas in 1932, and helped him and the [North Polar Bear](/wiki/North_Polar_Bear) capture many [Goblins](/wiki/Goblins). They appeared again the [next year](/wiki/1933 "1933") around [21 November](/wiki/21_November) to help deal with another goblin attack. In the battle that followed, many Red Elves got hurt. In [1934](/wiki/1934), the Red Elves were invited to a special <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxing_Day" title="wikipedia:Boxing Day">Boxing Day</a> party. About a week before the next Christmas, the Red Elves enjoyed their time shoveling out Father Christmas' house. That year, they received magic sparkler spears to scare goblins away. During [1936](/wiki/1936), many Red Elves moved to [Cliff House](/wiki/Cliff_House).
