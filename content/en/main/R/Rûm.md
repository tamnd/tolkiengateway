**Rûm** was the name of a great and ancient city of [Men](../M/Men.md) in the [Great Lands](../G/Great_Lands.md), according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) in *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Rum_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rum-1">[1]</a></sup> It was identified with the city of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome" title="wikipedia:Rome">Rome</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> being mentioned by [Littleheart](../L/Littleheart.md) in the tale of *[the Fall of Gondolin](<../T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)*, where he described the [sack](../F/Fall_of_Gondolin.md) of [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) as being even worse than many that befell Rûm, the greatest city of Men.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

Its inhabitants were known as the <i><b>Rúmhoth</b></i>, who were one of the peoples who <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_conquest_of_Britain" title="wikipedia:Roman conquest of Britain">invaded</a> [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md) and expelled the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) from it, not even believing they existed.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In one of the *[Early Runic Documents](../E/Early_Runic_Documents.md)*, contemporary with *The Book of Lost Tales*, there is also a mention of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_people" title="wikipedia:Roman people">Romans</a> as being the "slayers and faders of the fairies".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Rûm* is in [Gnomish](../G/Gnomish.md), clearly an [adaptation](../S/Sindarization.md) of Latin "Roma", the name used by both Elves and Men.

The Elves alone called it <i><b>Magbar</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Rum_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rum-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Rúmhoth*, the name of its inhabitants, is composed of *Rûm* + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Hoth_(Gnomish_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Hoth (Gnomish word) (page does not exist)">hoth</a></i> ("folk, people").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
