**Ryszard I. Derdziński** (born [1974](/wiki/1974)) is a Polish artist, linguist and translator. He is the editor of the magazines Simbelmynë and Gwaihir, co-editor of <a class="external text" href="https://elendilion.pl" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Elendilion.pl</a> and other Tolkien-based websites. He specializes in Tolkien linguistics and researching of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s biography and philosophy. In online forums he usually has the alias **Galadhorn**.
