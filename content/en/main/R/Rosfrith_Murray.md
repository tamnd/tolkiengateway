**Rosfrith Murray** ([1884](/wiki/1884)-[1973](/wiki/1973)) was the fourth daughter of Sir <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Murray_(lexicographer)" title="wikipedia:James Murray (lexicographer)">James A.H. Murray</a>, founder of the *[Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*. Murray spent much of her life working for her father on the Dictionary as an assistant. In November [1918](/wiki/1918), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) joined the staff as an assistant lexicographer. During this time he met Rosfrith Murray and the two would develop a close friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-1">[1]</a></sup>
