**Rosfrith Murray** ([1884](../_/1884.md)-[1973](../_/1973.md)) was the fourth daughter of Sir <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Murray_(lexicographer)" title="wikipedia:James Murray (lexicographer)">James A.H. Murray</a>, founder of the *[Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md)*. Murray spent much of her life working for her father on the Dictionary as an assistant. In November [1918](../_/1918.md), [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) joined the staff as an assistant lexicographer. During this time he met Rosfrith Murray and the two would develop a close friendship.<sup id="cite_ref-TL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TL-1">[1]</a></sup>
