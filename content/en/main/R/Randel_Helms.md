**Randel McGraw Helms** is an American Bible scholar and Professor of English. Helms studied at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside" title="wikipedia:University of California, Riverside">University of California</a> with a B.A. in 1964, and with a Ph.D. in 1968 at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington" title="wikipedia:University of Washington">University of Washington</a>. From 1968, he then taught at the University of Washington as an assistant professor of English. This was before becoming professor at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University" title="wikipedia:Arizona State University">Arizona State University</a> in the English Department.
