**Rob Alexander** (b. [15 June](../_/15_June.md) [1966](../_/1966.md)) is a Canadian fantasy card artist. He illustrated for *[Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)* and *[Dragon Magazine](../D/Dragon_Magazine.md)*. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create artwork for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

From [2010](../_/2010.md) to [2011](../_/2011.md), Alexander was a concept artist for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md)*, developing the architecture, characters and general aesthetic for its [Rohan region](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online%253A_Riders_of_Rohan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
