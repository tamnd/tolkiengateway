**Rob Alexander** (b. [15 June](/wiki/15_June) [1966](/wiki/1966)) is a Canadian fantasy card artist. He illustrated for *[Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering)* and *[Dragon Magazine](/wiki/Dragon_Magazine)*. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create artwork for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

From [2010](/wiki/2010) to [2011](/wiki/2011), Alexander was a concept artist for *[The Lord of the Rings Online](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online)*, developing the architecture, characters and general aesthetic for its [Rohan region](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Riders_of_Rohan "The Lord of the Rings Online: Riders of Rohan").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
