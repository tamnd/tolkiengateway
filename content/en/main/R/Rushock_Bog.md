**Rushock Bog** was a swamp on [The Water](/wiki/The_Water) in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing), south of [Needlehole](/wiki/Needlehole) and to the north-west of [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

According to Tolkien, the name is derived from the plant *rush*.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup> [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo), but also Hammond and Scull, suggested an etymology from [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) \**Ryscuc*/*rixuc* meaning "rushy place, rush-bed".<sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> However Tolkien alternatively suggested a possible compound *rush* + *hassock* "coarse grass".<sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

There is a village <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rushock" title="wikipedia:Rushock">Rushock</a> in Worcestershire and a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rushock,_Herefordshire" title="wikipedia:Rushock, Herefordshire">Rushock</a> in Herefordshire.<sup id="cite_ref-rc_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rc-2">[2]</a></sup>
