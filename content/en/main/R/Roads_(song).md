**Roads** is a song performed by [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough), based on "[The Road Goes Ever On](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(song)> "The Road Goes Ever On (song)")". The music was written by [Maury Laws](/wiki/Maury_Laws). It is the fourth track of the [1977](/wiki/1977) [film adaptation](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*. The song was reprised in the [1980](/wiki/1980) follow-up, [*The Return of the King*](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)"), where it was given a new lyric by [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass), and its melody also used for another song in the film called "[It's So Easy Not to Try](/wiki/It%27s_So_Easy_Not_to_Try)" (for which Bass also wrote the lyric).
