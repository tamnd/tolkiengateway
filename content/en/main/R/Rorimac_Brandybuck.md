**Rorimac Brandybuck**, also known as **Old Rory** and **Goldfather**, was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) and [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md).

## History {#History}

Rorimac was the oldest son of [Gorbadoc Brandybuck](../G/Gorbadoc_Brandybuck.md). He married [Menegilda Goold](../M/Menegilda_Goold.md) and had two sons, [Saradoc](../S/Saradoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Merimac](../M/Merimac_Brandybuck.md). He became [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md) after his father's death in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1363](../T/Third_Age_2963.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the deaths of his sister, [Primula Brandybuck](../P/Primula_Brandybuck.md) and her husband [Drogo Baggins](../D/Drogo_Baggins.md) in [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1380](../T/Third_Age_2980.md), Rorimac took their son, [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) under his guardianship in [Brandy Hall](../B/Brandy_Hall.md) for nine years. [Bilbo Baggins](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), a kinsman of Drogo, often enjoyed Old Rory's hospitality, and eventually he proposed that Frodo could come with him to [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md).<sup id="cite_ref-party_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-2">[2]</a></sup>

Rorimac was present at [Bilbo's Farewell Party](../B/Bilbo's_Farewell_Party.md). After Bilbo's [disappearance](../U/Unseen.md), Rory was one of the first, if not the only, to suspect "Mad Baggins" was off on an adventure again, and told so to his [daughter-in-law](../E/Esmeralda_Took.md) to quiet her shock; he expressed satisfaction that there was more food to feast with and promptly asked his nephew Frodo for more wine. As part of his departure, Bilbo left him a dozen of bottles of [Old Winyards](../O/Old_Winyards.md), and as soon as he tasted the first one, Rory voted Bilbo a capital fellow.<sup id="cite_ref-party_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-party-2">[2]</a></sup>

Rory died six years later, passing on the title of Master of Buckland to his eldest son [Saradoc](../S/Saradoc_Brandybuck.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Brandybuck_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brandybuck-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) noted that the names of the Bucklanders had a vaguely [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) style.<sup id="cite_ref-Translation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Translation-3">[3]</a></sup>

*Rory* is a northern Irish form of the [Celtic](../C/Celtic.md) name *Rhuadri* or *Rhuaidri*, which means "red" or "ruddy". An ancient Irish hero wasd called Rory O'More. Rory or as a full name *madadh ruadh* ("red dog") is the common name for the fox in many Higland tales where the fox is portrayed as shrewd. This is a fitting name for Rory Brandybuck, because we are told about him that "neither age nor an enormous dinner had clouded his wits".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
