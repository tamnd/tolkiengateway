<i><b>rhosc</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word meaning "brown".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-1">[1]</a></sup> The word (though possibly with the spelling *rhusc*) is also glossed as meaning "russet".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}
