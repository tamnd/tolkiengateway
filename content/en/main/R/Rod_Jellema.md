**Rod Jellema** ([1927](/wiki/1927) - [2018](/wiki/2018)) was an American poet, teacher, and translator. In 1956 Jellema began teaching introductory literature and composition courses at the University of Maryland, where he now is Professor Emeritus of English.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

During spring 1966, Jellema corresponded with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) concerning the controversy surrounding [W.H. Auden](/wiki/W.H._Auden)'s intended book on Tolkien (see letter [Letter to Rod Jellema](/wiki/Letter_to_Rod_Jellema)).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
