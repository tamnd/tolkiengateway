Lieutenant **Robert Quilter Gilson** ([25 October](../_/25_October.md) [1893](../_/1893.md) - [1 July](../_/1_July.md) [1916](../_/1916.md)) was a childhood friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). Gilson, Tolkien and [Geoffrey Bache Smith](../G/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith.md) attended [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md) in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md) together, and served in the [First World War](../W/World_War_I.md). He was killed by a shell on the first day of the [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Actor [Patrick Gibson](../P/Patrick_Gibson.md) plays him in biographical drama film about J.R.R. Tolkien, called [Tolkien](<../T/Tolkien_(film).md>).
