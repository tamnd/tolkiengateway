Lieutenant **Robert Quilter Gilson** ([25 October](/wiki/25_October) [1893](/wiki/1893) - [1 July](/wiki/1_July) [1916](/wiki/1916)) was a childhood friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Gilson, Tolkien and [Geoffrey Bache Smith](/wiki/Geoffrey_Bache_Smith) attended [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School) in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) together, and served in the [First World War](/wiki/World_War_I "World War I"). He was killed by a shell on the first day of the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Actor [Patrick Gibson](/wiki/Patrick_Gibson) plays him in biographical drama film about J.R.R. Tolkien, called [Tolkien](</wiki/Tolkien_(film)> "Tolkien (film)").
