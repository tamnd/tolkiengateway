**Ragash** was a [Moria](/wiki/Moria) [Orc scout](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who was a part of [Azog's army](/wiki/Azog "Azog"). Ragash was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for the [*Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), where he was physically portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Allan_Smith&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Allan Smith (page does not exist)">Allan Smith</a> and voiced by Martin Kwok.

## History {#History}

As [Azog](/wiki/Azog)'s army is making its way to the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) through the tunnels burrowed by the [Were-worms](/wiki/Were-worms), Ragash alerts Azog that the army will be ready to attack the Mountain by dawn. It is unknown if Ragash survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He also had a brother named <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Margush&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Margush (page does not exist)">Margush</a>.
