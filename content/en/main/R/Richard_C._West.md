**Richard C. West** (August [1944](../_/1944.md) - [29 November](../_/29_November.md) [2020](../_/2020.md))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an awarded [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) scholar and the Serials and Technical Services Librarian at the <a class="external text" href="http://www.engr.wisc.edu/services/kfw/staff/west_richard.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">University of Wisconsin-Madison</a>. When he was still a student, he also started a [Tolkien Society](../T/Tolkien_Societies~67099.md) there , the [University of Wisconsin Tolkien Society](../U/University_of_Wisconsin_Tolkien_and_Fantasy_Society.md), in September [1966](../_/1966.md). He has also contributed to its yearly journal, *[Orcrist](<../O/Orcrist_(journal).md>)*.
