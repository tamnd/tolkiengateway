**Richard C. West** (August [1944](/wiki/1944) - [29 November](/wiki/29_November) [2020](/wiki/2020))<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was an awarded [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") scholar and the Serials and Technical Services Librarian at the <a class="external text" href="http://www.engr.wisc.edu/services/kfw/staff/west_richard.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">University of Wisconsin-Madison</a>. When he was still a student, he also started a [Tolkien Society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") there , the [University of Wisconsin Tolkien Society](/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin_Tolkien_and_Fantasy_Society "University of Wisconsin Tolkien and Fantasy Society"), in September [1966](/wiki/1966). He has also contributed to its yearly journal, *[Orcrist](</wiki/Orcrist_(journal)> "Orcrist (journal)")*.
