**Rowans** were [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") of the genus Sorbus, also called 'mountain ash'. Found especially on the slopes of [Mountains](/wiki/Mountains) and hills, these trees, with their unmistakable red berries, once grew in the western [Fangorn Forest](/wiki/Fangorn_Forest) in the regions close to [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard).

[Carnimírië](/wiki/Carnim%C3%ADri%C3%AB), [Orofarnë](/wiki/Orofarn%C3%AB), and [Lassemista](/wiki/Lassemista) were individual rowan-trees of Fangorn.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
