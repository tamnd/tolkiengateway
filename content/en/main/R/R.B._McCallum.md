**Ronald Buchanan McCallum** ([28 August](../_/28_August.md) [1898](../_/1898.md) – [18 May](../_/18_May.md) [1973](../_/1973.md)) was an occasional member of the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md). McCallum was a lecturer and tutor in Modern History and Politics at Oxford.
