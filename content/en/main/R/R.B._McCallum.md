**Ronald Buchanan McCallum** ([28 August](/wiki/28_August) [1898](/wiki/1898) – [18 May](/wiki/18_May) [1973](/wiki/1973)) was an occasional member of the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings) with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). McCallum was a lecturer and tutor in Modern History and Politics at Oxford.
