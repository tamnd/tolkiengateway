**Dr. Robert Emlyn "Honest Humphrey" Havard** was an [Oxford](/wiki/University_of_Oxford "University of Oxford")-schooled physician. He was one of the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings), as well as a neighbour of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in his later life.

## History {#History}

Havard was the son of an Anglican clergyman, and read Chemistry at [Keble College](/wiki/University_of_Oxford#Keble_College "University of Oxford"),<sup id="cite_ref-I257_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-I257-2">[2]</a></sup> followed by Medicine at [Queen's College](/wiki/University_of_Oxford#Queen's_College "University of Oxford") after a conversion to Catholicism ruled him out of attending the former.<sup id="cite_ref-CaG_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CaG-3">[3]</a></sup> After his studies, he worked in [Leeds](/wiki/Leeds), where he married. He returned to Oxford in [1934](/wiki/1934).<sup id="cite_ref-I257_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-I257-2">[2]</a></sup> He took over a medical practice with surgeries in Headington and St. Giles;<sup id="cite_ref-I257_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-I257-2">[2]</a></sup> the latter was situated near the [Eagle and Child](/wiki/Eagle_and_Child).<sup id="cite_ref-Duriez_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Duriez-4">[4]</a></sup>

One of his patients was [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis). It was Lewis who introduced Havard to the Inklings after a boat trip, shortly before the outbreak of the Second World War. [Warren Lewis](/wiki/Warren_Lewis) had bought a small boat, the *Bosphorus*, and the Lewis brothers planned a trip along the Thames with [Hugo Dyson](/wiki/Hugo_Dyson). The international political tension had Warren - a Major in the Royal Army Service Corps - transferred to Le Havre, and because the others did not want to cancel the trip, C.S. Lewis decided to bring his good friend Dr. Havard along as navigator. They had a pleasant time, as the trip went past the towns Godstow, Newbridge, Radcot, Lechlade and Inglesham - and naturally, the Inns. It was slightly overshadowed by the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invasion_of_Poland" title="wikipedia:Invasion of Poland">invasion of Poland</a>, but after another dinner with Lewis and Dyson, Havard was introduced to the Inklings.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> So, Havard also became the Tolkien family's doctor, having been asked by the Professor shortly after his introduction to the Inklings.<sup id="cite_ref-JRRTE_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-JRRTE-6">[6]</a></sup> He became an appreciated family friend - as he was with the Lewis family, and often attended Thursday pub visits. He often drove the Inklings to country pubs.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

In [1943](/wiki/1943), Havard was called up for service in the Royal Navy, were he served as a medical officer. On his rare leaves, he came back to Oxford, now sporting a full red beard. This earned him the nickname **The Red Admiral**, to go along with his already existing nicknames **Useless Quack** and **Honest Humphrey**,<sup id="cite_ref-I177_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-I177-1">[1]</a></sup> the latter courtesy of Hugo Dyson when he could not remember the doctor's name.<sup id="cite_ref-Duriez_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Duriez-4">[4]</a></sup> Tolkien managed to get Havard stationed at Oxford permanently, and he was put to work on a malaria research project.<sup id="cite_ref-I177_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-I177-1">[1]</a></sup> Back in Oxford, Havard was among the first to read the outlines of what would become *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and still a respected (though not very productive) member of the Inklings.

Tolkien became a close neighbour of Havard when he moved to [76 Sandfield Road](/wiki/76_Sandfield_Road): Havard, now a widower with five children,<sup id="cite_ref-I257_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-I257-2">[2]</a></sup> lived at number 28.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> They regularly attended church together, and with the Inklings fallen apart, Havard was the last of Tolkien's male companies.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

In [1968](/wiki/1968), Havard retired from the medical profession. He moved to the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Wight" title="wikipedia:Isle of Wight">Isle of Wight</a> and lost most contact with Tolkien, though he did visit him at [Woodridings](/wiki/Woodridings) at least twice.<sup id="cite_ref-CaG_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CaG-3">[3]</a></sup> He died in 1985.<sup id="cite_ref-JRRTE_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-JRRTE-6">[6]</a></sup>
