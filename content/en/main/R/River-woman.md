The **River-woman**, said to be the mother of [Goldberry](/wiki/Goldberry), was a possibly fictitious character mentioned only in a verse as being associated with the [Withywindle](/wiki/Withywindle) of the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest).

In a poem, [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) was pulled into the river by the swimming Goldberry. She swam "back to her mother's house in the deepest hollow". Later, Tom returned to the river and asked the River-woman for Goldberry's hand; she complied unquestionably, so the two were married.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007-: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The <b>River-maidens</b> are spirits that watch over the waters of <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, one is said to be present whenever a river is flowing. <a href="/wiki/Goldberry" title="Goldberry">Goldberry</a> is the River-maiden of <a href="/wiki/Withywindle" title="Withywindle">Withywindle</a>, and others can be found watching over <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Midgewater" title="Midgewater">Midgewater</a>, <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Mitheithel" title="Mitheithel">Mitheithel</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gladden_River" title="Gladden River">Gladden</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gilrain" title="Gilrain">Gilrain</a>, <a href="/wiki/Serni" title="Serni">Serni</a>, <a href="/wiki/Celos" title="Celos">Celos</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sirith" title="Sirith">Sirith</a> and <a href="/wiki/Erui" title="Erui">Erui</a>. Some show themselves openly, others are secretive and only reveal themselves to the Wise and the descendants of the <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Númenóreans</a> who had known of River-maidens in the years of old. But some were driven mad by the bloodshed spilled over their waters, or even slain by the servants of the Enemy who then take on their fair appearance.</dd></dl>
