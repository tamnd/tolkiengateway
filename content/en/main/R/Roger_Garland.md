**Roger Garland** was an English artist, teacher and [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") illustrator.

## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG"><img src="/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG/300px-Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="324" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/b/b2/Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG/450px-Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/b/b2/Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG/600px-Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG 2x" data-file-width="700" data-file-height="755"/></a><figcaption><i>The Lord of the Nazgûl</i> by <b>Roger Garland</b></figcaption></figure>

Roger Garland attended <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_University_Plimouth" title="wikipedia:Arts University Plimouth">Plymouth Art School</a> and studied graphic design at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wolverhampton" title="wikipedia:University of Wolverhampton">University of Wolverhampton</a>, where he met his wife <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Linda_Garland&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Linda Garland (page does not exist)">Linda Garland</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-gallery_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gallery-1">[1]</a></sup> Both graduated in [1970](/wiki/1970).<sup id="cite_ref-gallery_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gallery-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Their son Seth Garland (born [1977](/wiki/1977)) is also an artist.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After graduating, Garland taught art at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poltair_School" title="wikipedia:Poltair School">Poltair School</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornwall" title="wikipedia:Cornwall">Cornwall</a> for a decade, while also building a career as an illustrator.<sup id="cite_ref-gallery_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gallery-1">[1]</a></sup> His first collaboration with [George Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/George_Allen_%26_Unwin) was in [1981](/wiki/1981),<sup id="cite_ref-gallery_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gallery-1">[1]</a></sup> when he was hired to paint a cover for the paperback release of [Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales). In 1989, he co-founded Lakeside Gallery with his wife, which permanently displays their artwork, including Roger's Tolkien-related paintings, to this day.<sup id="cite_ref-gallery_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gallery-1">[1]</a></sup> Some of these paintings were part of the italian exhibition [*Tolkien: Uomo, Professore, Autore*](/wiki/TOLKIEN_-_Uomo,_Professore,_Autore "TOLKIEN - Uomo, Professore, Autore"), that toured between 2023 and 2026. In his opinion, *[The Lord of the Nazgûl](/wiki/File:Roger_Garland_-_The_Lord_of_the_Nazgul.JPG "File:Roger Garland - The Lord of the Nazgul.JPG")* is his best painting, because it "*captures the darker side of these works*".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> He was also an honorary member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) since [1994](/wiki/1994).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
