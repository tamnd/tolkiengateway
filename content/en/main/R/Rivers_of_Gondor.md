The **Rivers of Gondor** can be classified into two overlapping groups.

## Rivers of Gondor {#Rivers_of_Gondor}

The *Five Rivers of Gondor* are named in order of their mouths from east to west:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

1. [Erui](../E/Erui.md), flowing from [Lossarnach](../L/Lossarnach.md) and into the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md);
2. [Sirith](../S/Sirith.md), arising from the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and joining the Anduin at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md);
3. [Serni](../S/Serni.md), meeting the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md) just below [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md);
4. [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md), being joined by [Ringló](Ringló.md) and [Ciril](../C/Ciril.md) it flows into the [Cobas Haven](../C/Cobas_Haven.md);
5. [Lefnui](../L/Lefnui.md), the fifth river which forms the boundary between [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Drúwaith Iaur](../D/Drúwaith_Iaur.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s earlier notes on the creation of [the map](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) - included in both *[The Treason of Isengard](../T/The_Treason_of_Isengard.md)* and *[The War of the Ring](../T/The_War_of_the_Ring.md)* - [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) shows that the original extensive list of the Rivers of Gondor also included the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), the [Duin Morghul](../M/Morgulduin.md), the [Poros](../P/Poros.md) and the [Calenhir](../C/Calenhir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In the earlier drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* there were several references to both the "Land of the Seven Streams" and "Seven Streams" (with one in the published [Song of Boromir](../S/Song_of_Boromir.md): "*I saw him ride over seven streams, over waters wide and grey*");<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> although Christopher Tolkien regards this as a "puzzle", a note from Tolkien on the map names the seven rivers (the five above with two more added):<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<ol start="6">
<li><a href="../I/Isen.md" title="Isen">Isen</a>, flowing westward to <a href="../B/Belegaer.md" title="Belegaer">Belegaer</a> from <a href="../I/Isengard.md" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>;</li>
<li><a href="../G/Gwathló.md" title="Gwathló">Gwathló</a>, representing the boundary of <a href="../A/Arnor.md" title="Arnor">Arnor</a>, passing through the port of <a href="../T/Tharbad.md" title="Tharbad">Tharbad</a> and joining the sea at <a href="../L/Lond_Daer.md" title="Lond Daer">Lond Daer</a>.</li>
</ol>

However, the poem "[The Last Ship](../T/The_Last_Ship.md)" in *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](../T/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil.md)* makes reference to the "Seven Rivers"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> which are identified as:<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

1. [Lefnui](../L/Lefnui.md), the first river going from west to east, forms the western boundary of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md);
2. [Morthond](../M/Morthond.md), starting near the southern entrance of the [Paths of the Dead](../P/Paths_of_the_Dead.md) it passes [Erech](../E/Erech.md) to reach the sea above [Dol Amroth](../D/Dol_Amroth.md)
3. [Kiril](../C/Ciril.md), a tributary of the Morthond that flows from the White Mountains past the town of [Calembel](../C/Calembel.md);
4. [Ringló](Ringló.md), another tributary of the Morthond that passes through the ford of [Ethring](../E/Ethring.md);
5. [Gilrain](../G/Gilrain.md), joining the river Serni at [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md);
6. [Serni](../S/Serni.md) - originally spelt "Sernui" - and joins with the Gilrain at Linhir;
7. [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), the Great River that flows from [Rhovanion](Rhovanion.md), over [Rauros](Rauros.md), through [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) and out into the sea after [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md).

## Five Rivers of Lebennin {#Five_Rivers_of_Lebennin}

[Lebennin](../L/Lebennin.md) is the region south of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) between the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md). Its name means "Five Rivers", and the *Five Rivers of Lebennin* are the following:<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

1. [Erui](../E/Erui.md), forming the traditional boundary between Lebennin and [Anórien](../A/Anórien.md);
2. [Celos](../C/Celos.md), a small tributary of [Sirith](../S/Sirith.md);
3. [Sirith](../S/Sirith.md), arising from the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and joining the Anduin at [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md);
4. [Gilrain](../G/Gilrain.md), which started as two unnamed tributaries in the White Mountains and flowed south meeting the Serni at [Linhir](../L/Linhir.md);
5. [Serni](../S/Serni.md), meeting the Bay of [Belfalas](../B/Belfalas.md) just below Linhir and above [Ethir Anduin](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md).
