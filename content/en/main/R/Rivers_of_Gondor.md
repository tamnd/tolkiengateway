The **Rivers of Gondor** can be classified into two overlapping groups.

## Rivers of Gondor {#Rivers_of_Gondor}

The *Five Rivers of Gondor* are named in order of their mouths from east to west:<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

1. [Erui](/wiki/Erui), flowing from [Lossarnach](/wiki/Lossarnach) and into the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin);
2. [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith), arising from the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and joining the Anduin at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir);
3. [Serni](/wiki/Serni), meeting the [Bay of Belfalas](/wiki/Bay_of_Belfalas) just below [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir);
4. [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond), being joined by [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3) and [Ciril](/wiki/Ciril) it flows into the [Cobas Haven](/wiki/Cobas_Haven);
5. [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui), the fifth river which forms the boundary between [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Drúwaith Iaur](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s earlier notes on the creation of [the map](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth "General Map of Middle-earth") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) - included in both *[The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard)* and *[The War of the Ring](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Ring)* - [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) shows that the original extensive list of the Rivers of Gondor also included the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), the [Duin Morghul](/wiki/Morgulduin "Morgulduin"), the [Poros](/wiki/Poros) and the [Calenhir](/wiki/Calenhir).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> In the earlier drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* there were several references to both the "Land of the Seven Streams" and "Seven Streams" (with one in the published [Song of Boromir](/wiki/Song_of_Boromir): "*I saw him ride over seven streams, over waters wide and grey*");<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> although Christopher Tolkien regards this as a "puzzle", a note from Tolkien on the map names the seven rivers (the five above with two more added):<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

<ol start="6">
<li><a href="/wiki/Isen" title="Isen">Isen</a>, flowing westward to <a href="/wiki/Belegaer" title="Belegaer">Belegaer</a> from <a href="/wiki/Isengard" title="Isengard">Isengard</a>;</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Gwathl%C3%B3" title="Gwathló">Gwathló</a>, representing the boundary of <a href="/wiki/Arnor" title="Arnor">Arnor</a>, passing through the port of <a href="/wiki/Tharbad" title="Tharbad">Tharbad</a> and joining the sea at <a href="/wiki/Lond_Daer" title="Lond Daer">Lond Daer</a>.</li>
</ol>

However, the poem "[The Last Ship](/wiki/The_Last_Ship)" in *[The Adventures of Tom Bombadil](/wiki/The_Adventures_of_Tom_Bombadil)* makes reference to the "Seven Rivers"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> which are identified as:<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

1. [Lefnui](/wiki/Lefnui), the first river going from west to east, forms the western boundary of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor);
2. [Morthond](/wiki/Morthond), starting near the southern entrance of the [Paths of the Dead](/wiki/Paths_of_the_Dead) it passes [Erech](/wiki/Erech) to reach the sea above [Dol Amroth](/wiki/Dol_Amroth)
3. [Kiril](/wiki/Ciril "Ciril"), a tributary of the Morthond that flows from the White Mountains past the town of [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel);
4. [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3), another tributary of the Morthond that passes through the ford of [Ethring](/wiki/Ethring);
5. [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain), joining the river Serni at [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir);
6. [Serni](/wiki/Serni) - originally spelt "Sernui" - and joins with the Gilrain at Linhir;
7. [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), the Great River that flows from [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion), over [Rauros](/wiki/Rauros), through [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath) and out into the sea after [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).

## Five Rivers of Lebennin {#Five_Rivers_of_Lebennin}

[Lebennin](/wiki/Lebennin) is the region south of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) between the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin). Its name means "Five Rivers", and the *Five Rivers of Lebennin* are the following:<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

1. [Erui](/wiki/Erui), forming the traditional boundary between Lebennin and [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien);
2. [Celos](/wiki/Celos), a small tributary of [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith);
3. [Sirith](/wiki/Sirith), arising from the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) and joining the Anduin at [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir);
4. [Gilrain](/wiki/Gilrain), which started as two unnamed tributaries in the White Mountains and flowed south meeting the Serni at [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir);
5. [Serni](/wiki/Serni), meeting the Bay of [Belfalas](/wiki/Belfalas) just below Linhir and above [Ethir Anduin](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin).
