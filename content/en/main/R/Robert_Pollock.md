**Robert Pollock** (b. [September 16](../_/16_September.md), [1951](../_/1951.md)) is a New Zealand actor who played an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)*. He also had an uncredited role as a featured Orc in *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)*.

It must be noted, that <a class="external text" href="https://aucklandactors.co.nz/actors/robert-pollock/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Auckland Actors</a> claims Pollock is 64 years old in December of 2024, therefore having been born in 1959 or 1960. However, they also claim that his roles in [*The Lord of the Rings* film series](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) were [Lugdush](../L/Lugdush.md) in 2000 and a "Hero Orc" in 2003. This seems dubious since none of the films were released in 2000, nor is the role of Lugdush known to exist in the films.

In June [2007](../_/2007.md), he and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](../N/New_Line_Cinema.md) over merchandising royalties.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The action was settled out of court in October [2008](../_/2008.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
