Dr. **Richard Sturch** is an Anglican priest, the Secretary of the <a class="external text" href="http://www.charleswilliamssociety.org.uk/index.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Charles Williams Society</a>, and also active in [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md). He wrote several essays and books about [Christianity](../C/Christianity.md) and the [Inklings](../I/Inklings.md).
