Dr. **Richard Sturch** is an Anglican priest, the Secretary of the <a class="external text" href="http://www.charleswilliamssociety.org.uk/index.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Charles Williams Society</a>, and also active in [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society). He wrote several essays and books about [Christianity](/wiki/Christianity) and the [Inklings](/wiki/Inklings).
