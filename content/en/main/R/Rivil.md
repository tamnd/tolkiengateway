The **Rivil** was a lesser tributary of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion). Its source was [Rivil's Well](/wiki/Rivil%27s_Well) in the highlands of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), from which it flowed westward to meet the Sirion at the [Fen of Serech](/wiki/Fen_of_Serech).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [472](/wiki/First_Age_472 "First Age 472"), before the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), the army that [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) sent to draw out [Fingon](/wiki/Fingon) was staged above the [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion), from the inflowing of Rivil to the walls of [Eithel Sirion](/wiki/Eithel_Sirion) to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the latter stages of the Nirnaeth Arnoediad, King [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) retreated down the [Pass of Sirion](/wiki/Pass_of_Sirion). His escape was successful because [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin) and [Huor](/wiki/Huor) formed a rearguard that used the Rivil as a defence. The host of [Angband](/wiki/Angband) bridged the stream with the corpses of their dead. Huor was slain beside the Rivil and Húrin was captured.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
