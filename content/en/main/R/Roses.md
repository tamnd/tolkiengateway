**Roses** are flowering plants with large, showy blossoms and (often) prickly stems that were found in many places throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

In [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) there was a roadway called the [Alley of Roses](/wiki/Alley_of_Roses), one of the most beautiful places in the city.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") it was common to bestow flower names upon girls<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and many maidens bore the name "Rose" – [Rosa Baggins](/wiki/Rosa_Baggins),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> [Rose](</wiki/Rose_(daughter_of_Holman)> "Rose (daughter of Holman)") (the daughter of [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded)), [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton), [Rose Gardner](/wiki/Rose_Gardner), and [Primrose Gardner](/wiki/Primrose_Gardner).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Given the stay-at-home nature of [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> their familiarity with roses certainly indicates the cultivation of these flowers in their homeland.

Roses could be found far to the south of the Shire. In [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien), [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) and [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) encountered [Eglantine](/wiki/Eglantine), a type of rose<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> (one Hobbit, [Eglantine Banks](/wiki/Eglantine_Banks),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> was named after this flower, indicating that this variety of rose also grew in the Shire). When [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) steeped *[athelas](/wiki/Athelas "Athelas")* in hot water in the [Houses of Healing](/wiki/Houses_of_Healing), [Ioreth](/wiki/Ioreth) claimed that the fragrance reminded her of the roses in [Imloth Melui](/wiki/Imloth_Melui), a valley in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1997: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a>—<a href="/wiki/Hands_of_the_Healer" title="Hands of the Healer">Hands of the Healer</a></i></b>

<dl><dd>Two specific types of roses are described in the book:</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Bramblerose</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Aegmeril</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Aicamerillë</i>) was a rose bush with large pink flowers that grew along rivers in temperate regions. It formed thick brambles, and had delicious large purple fruits with orange interiors with mild healing properties.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><i><b>Foxrose</b></i> (<a href="/wiki/Sindarin" title="Sindarin">S.</a> <i>Rosmeril</i>, <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">Q.</a> <i>Russamerillë</i>), also known as <i>Redthorn</i>, was a rose with dusky red flowers and reddish-green leaves that grew in temperate wetlands. The foxrose symbolized love, and its petals had healing properties.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup></dd></dl>
