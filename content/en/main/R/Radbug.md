**Radbug** was an [Orc](../O/Orcs.md) under the command of [Shagrat](../S/Shagrat.md) at the Tower of [Cirith Ungol](../C/Cirith_Ungol.md). During the [Battle of Cirith Ungol](../B/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol.md) Radbug had his eyes squeezed out by Shagrat, killing him. It is believed that Radbug had refused to obey him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Shagrat threatened the Orc [Snaga](<../S/Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor).md>) with a similar fate if he didn't follow his orders.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
