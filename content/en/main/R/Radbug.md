**Radbug** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") under the command of [Shagrat](/wiki/Shagrat) at the Tower of [Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Cirith_Ungol). During the [Battle of Cirith Ungol](/wiki/Battle_of_Cirith_Ungol) Radbug had his eyes squeezed out by Shagrat, killing him. It is believed that Radbug had refused to obey him.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Shagrat threatened the Orc [Snaga](</wiki/Snaga_(orc_of_Mordor)> "Snaga (orc of Mordor)") with a similar fate if he didn't follow his orders.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
