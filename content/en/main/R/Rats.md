**Rats** were creatures often found in filthy and unfriendly places of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-RKVI8_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKVI8-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A specific breed of rats seems to have been the "*brown water-rat*", mentioned in [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") poetry.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

"Rat" was also commonly used as a humiliating nickname.<sup id="cite_ref-RKVI8_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKVI8-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RKVI1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RKVI1-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), the word for "rat" is *nyarro*, and the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) cognate of the same meaning is *nâr*.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rats can infect people with the plague through their fleas.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1995-8: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Morgul-rats&#34; is a Hazard Creature, able to inflict 12 strikes. The breed is described as carrying &#34;<i>the stink of black sorcery in their bite</i>&#34;. &#34;Rats!&#34; is a Hazard Event, forcing a player to discard a minor item of one of the characters, which also become wounded.</dd></dl>

<b>2000-: <i>Lord of the Rings (board game)</i>:</b>

<dl><dd>&#34;Morgul Rats&#34; is a card (with artwork by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a>) supplied with the <i>Friends &amp; Foes</i> Expansion (2002) for the <i>Lord of the Rings</i> boardgame.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>As part of the <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> set, one hut was called the &#34;Ratcatcher&#39;s hut&#34;, after a doodle by <a href="/wiki/Alan_Lee" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a> was interpreted as a rat. It was outfitted with several taxidermied rats, but was not featured prominently in the movie.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007-: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rats are always aggressive. Breeds include Field-rats (found in the Barrow-downs), Harbour-rats (found around Falathlorn in Ered Luin), and Plague-rats.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup></dd></dl>
