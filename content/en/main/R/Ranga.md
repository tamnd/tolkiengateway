The **Ranga**, or **Full Pace**, was a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor") linear measure. The *Ranga* was said to be a full stride from the rear heel to the front toe, by a full-grown Númenórean man in quick march. It was slightly longer than a yard, measuring 38 inches (3'2", or 96.5 centimetres). 5000 *Rangar* made up a *[Lár](/wiki/L%C3%A1r)*.

In the later days, when the stature of the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) decreased, two *Rangar* was called "man-high" (suggesting that the average adult male [Dúnadan](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain "Dúnedain") was 6'4" or thereabouts). One *Ranga* was also the average height of a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") (hence the name [*half*ling](/wiki/Halflings "Halflings") by the Dúnedain).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
