The **Ranga**, or **Full Pace**, was a [Númenórean](../N/Númenor.md) linear measure. The *Ranga* was said to be a full stride from the rear heel to the front toe, by a full-grown Númenórean man in quick march. It was slightly longer than a yard, measuring 38 inches (3'2", or 96.5 centimetres). 5000 *Rangar* made up a *[Lár](../L/Lár.md)*.

In the later days, when the stature of the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) decreased, two *Rangar* was called "man-high" (suggesting that the average adult male [Dúnadan](../D/Dúnedain.md) was 6'4" or thereabouts). One *Ranga* was also the average height of a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) (hence the name [*half*ling](../H/Halflings.md) by the Dúnedain).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
