**Rothinzil** is the [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic) name for [Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot), the ship in which [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) sailed to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), and in which he later sailed the pathways of the sky. By extension, it was also used by the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) to refer to the brilliant [Star of Eärendil](/wiki/Star_of_E%C3%A4rendil) in the sky.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Rothinzil* is an exact translation of the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) *[Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot)* ("Foam-flower"),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> being the combination of *[roth](/wiki/Roth "Roth")* ("foam") + *[inzil](/wiki/Inzil "Inzil")* ("flower").

In some notes on [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) phonetics, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) seems to have considered the name as Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
