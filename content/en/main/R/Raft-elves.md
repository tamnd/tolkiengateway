The **raft-elves** were a group of presumably [Mirkwood Elves](/wiki/Elves_of_Mirkwood "Elves of Mirkwood") which dwelled within a collection of [huts](/wiki/Huts_of_the_raft-elves "Huts of the raft-elves").<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The raft-elves brought goods on the [Forest River](/wiki/Forest_River) between the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) and [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town"). These goods were stored in barrels which were tied together and then poled or rowed up the river. Sometimes they also used flat boats, on which the goods were loaded.<sup id="cite_ref-H9_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H9-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Raft-elves are a separate tribe of Nando and Sindar Elves, residing in the village of Celebannon upon the Forest River.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>1989: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit_(comic_book)" title="The Hobbit (comic book)">The Hobbit</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/David_T._Wenzel" title="David T. Wenzel">David T. Wenzel</a> portrayed the raft-elves as sturdy elves wearing garments in various tones of green.</dd></dl>

<b>2007- : <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Raft-elves live in the elven-settlement known as &#34;Loeglond&#34;, which continued to ferry goods from the <a href="/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls" title="Elvenking&#39;s Halls">Elvenking&#39;s Halls</a> of Felegoth, to the new <a href="/wiki/Lake-town" title="Lake-town">Lake-town</a> throughout and after the events of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>.</dd></dl>
