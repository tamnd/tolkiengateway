**Ralph Damiani** is a Brazilian artist known for his many illustrations of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) and the Harry Potter universe. Ralph has worked together with a number of well-known Tolkien YouTubers such as [Council of the Rings](/wiki/Council_of_the_Rings), [Nerd of the Rings](/wiki/Nerd_of_the_Rings), <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lore_of_the_Rings&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lore of the Rings (page does not exist)">Lore of the Rings</a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Steven_Gibb_(Youtube_Channel)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Steven Gibb (Youtube Channel) (page does not exist)">Steven Gibb</a>.

In 2022 he was nominated for [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) Awards 2022 Best Artwork for his 'Across Arda' artworks.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
