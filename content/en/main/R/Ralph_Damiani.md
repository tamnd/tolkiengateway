**Ralph Damiani** is a Brazilian artist known for his many illustrations of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) and the Harry Potter universe. Ralph has worked together with a number of well-known Tolkien YouTubers such as [Council of the Rings](../C/Council_of_the_Rings.md), [Nerd of the Rings](../N/Nerd_of_the_Rings.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Lore_of_the_Rings&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lore of the Rings (page does not exist)">Lore of the Rings</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Steven_Gibb_(Youtube_Channel)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Steven Gibb (Youtube Channel) (page does not exist)">Steven Gibb</a>.

In 2022 he was nominated for [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) Awards 2022 Best Artwork for his 'Across Arda' artworks.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
