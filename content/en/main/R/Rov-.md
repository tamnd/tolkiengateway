<i><b>rov-</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) prefix meaning wing.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- **roval** = "wing"
- **[Landroval](/wiki/Landroval)** = "Broad wing"
- **raw** = "wing"
