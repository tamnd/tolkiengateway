**Renée Vink**, a translator and author of historical mysteries (in Dutch), is one of the founding members of [Unquendor](/wiki/Unquendor). She has released a book about [Richard Wagner](/wiki/Richard_Wagner) and [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and translated the verse from *[The Legend of Sigurd &amp; Gudrún](/wiki/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudr%C3%BAn "The Legend of Sigurd and Gudrún")* to Dutch, as well as [The Fall of Arthur](/wiki/The_Fall_of_Arthur) and Tolkien's [Beowulf](/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary "Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary"). In [1988](/wiki/1988) she was awarded Unquendor's Golden Pin of Merit.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Aside from translating books she is also a conversational translator with an emphasis on Swedish.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
