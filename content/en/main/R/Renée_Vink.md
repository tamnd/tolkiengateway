**Renée Vink**, a translator and author of historical mysteries (in Dutch), is one of the founding members of [Unquendor](../U/Unquendor.md). She has released a book about [Richard Wagner](Richard_Wagner.md) and [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and translated the verse from *[The Legend of Sigurd &amp; Gudrún](../T/The_Legend_of_Sigurd_and_Gudrún.md)* to Dutch, as well as [The Fall of Arthur](../T/The_Fall_of_Arthur.md) and Tolkien's [Beowulf](../B/Beowulf%253A_A_Translation_and_Commentary.md). In [1988](../_/1988.md) she was awarded Unquendor's Golden Pin of Merit.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Aside from translating books she is also a conversational translator with an emphasis on Swedish.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
