The **Reunited Kingdom** was the restored kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the twin kingdoms founded at the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion). In the beginning, Elendil was [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") of both realms, but the two were divided after the deaths of his sons. Over 3,000 years later [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) was crowned as <i><b>Elessar</b></i> and reunited the kingdoms, ruling as [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain") of the newly-founded Reunited Kingdom.

## Geography {#Geography}

During Aragorn's reign, the Reunited Kingdom was comprised of the old realms of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), excluding only [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) (the [grant](/wiki/Oath_of_Eorl "Oath of Eorl") of which he renewed); and the [Shire](/wiki/Shire), the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest), and the [Treegarth of Orthanc](/wiki/Treegarth_of_Orthanc) (which became protectorates).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Reunited Kingdom included all the lands of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor); all of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), except the [regions](/wiki/Lindon "Lindon") beyond the [Lune](/wiki/Lune), and the lands east of [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood) and [Loudwater](/wiki/Loudwater), in which lay [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), the realm extended:<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>

- North to the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant) and the southern eaves of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>
- West to the [Greyflood](/wiki/Greyflood)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>
- East to the inland [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>
- South to the [River Harnen](/wiki/River_Harnen), and also along the coast to the peninsula and haven of [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Politics {#Politics}

The [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") governed the realm with the frame of ancient law, of which he was administrator (and interpreter) but not the maker.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-3">[3]</a></sup>

In all debatable matters of importance domestic, or external, however, even [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor_II "Denethor II") had a [Council](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor "Council of Gondor"), and at least listened to what the Lords of the Fiefs and the Captains of the Forces had to say.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) re-established the [Great Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor "Council of Gondor"), and in that [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir), who remained by inheritance the [Steward](/wiki/Steward) (or representative of the King during his absence abroad, or sickness, or between his death and the accession of his heir) was his chief counsellor.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-3">[3]</a></sup>

After his accession, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) established a council in [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), because in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [13](/wiki/Fourth_Age_13 "Fourth Age 13"), he chose three [Counsellors of the North-kingdom](/wiki/Counsellor_of_the_North-kingdom "Counsellor of the North-kingdom") from the people of the [Shire](/wiki/Shire) and [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-8">[8]</a></sup> These Counsellors were those appointed to the positions of the [Thain](/wiki/Thain) and [Mayor of the Shire](/wiki/Mayor_of_the_Shire), and the [Master of Buckland](/wiki/Master_of_Buckland).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-8">[8]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Background {#Background}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="/wiki/Realms_in_Exile" title="Realms in Exile">Realms in Exile</a></b></div></div>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r398211">.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote{position:relative;margin:0.5rem 0;padding:0.75rem 1rem;box-shadow:var(--tg-border-color-base)0px 0px 0px 1px inset;border-radius:var(--tg-border-radius-medium);background:var(--tg-color-surface-2);font-size:var(--tg-font-size-small);line-height:var(--tg-line-height-xx-small);display:flex;gap:0.5rem;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon{display:flex;align-items:center}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element{display:block}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote-icon .mw-file-element[src$=".svg"]{filter:var(--filter-invert);opacity:0.5}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--red{background:var(--background-color-destructive-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--yellow{background:var(--background-color-warning-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}.mw-parser-output .tg-hatnote.tg-hatnote--green{background:var(--background-color-success-subtle);color:var(--color-emphasized)}</style>

At the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons established two great kingdoms in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth): [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) in the north and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) in the south. These [Kingdoms of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Kingdoms_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain) were united under a single [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain"), [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) himself, who ruled the [North-kingdom](/wiki/Arnor "Arnor") while his sons reigned jointly over the South-kingdom of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the first years of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), this union failed. After the loss of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) at the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), his nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil) took up the [kingship of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor"), and that country remained independent from the [North-kingdom](/wiki/North-kingdom) through most of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the north, the realm of Arnor fell into troubled times. It broke into three separate kingdoms, and in time these too were lost, so that the Dúnedain of the North-kingdom of Elendil were reduced to a wandering people led by a [Chieftain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain"). Nonetheless, they were able to maintain Isildur's line in unbroken descent.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

There was only one attempt to reunite the kingdoms while they were both in existence. This attempt occurred in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") when [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), [King of Arthedain](/wiki/King_of_Arthedain), claimed the [High Kingship](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") of the [Two Kingdoms](/wiki/Two_Kingdoms).<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup> His claim was rejected by Steward [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur) and the [Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor), who elected to maintain their independence.<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>

Soon enough, the line of [Kings](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") failed in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) altogether; [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur) was lost without heirs. Its rule was taken up by the [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards) of the [House of Húrin](/wiki/House_of_H%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Reunification {#Reunification}

#### Reign of Elessar {#Reign_of_Elessar}

More than a thousand years later, after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), Arvedui's direct descendant, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), came forward to make the same claim. This time, the people of Gondor accepted a [High King](/wiki/High_King_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "High King of the Dúnedain"), and the Two Kingdoms were restored and reunited at last.

[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) restored [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and repeopled it, but retained [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) as the chief city.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup> He rebuilt [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) and when he went north, would rule from there.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup> He also had the ruins of [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) rebuilt and made it a great city where [Men](/wiki/Men) dwelt once again.<sup id="cite_ref-Bound_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bound-10">[10]</a></sup> [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) was an exception to this, and though it lay within the Reunited Kingdom, Aragorn made a law that Men should not enter it, a law that he observed himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup>

During his reign, he, alongside [King](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan") [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer), led military campaigns beyond the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn) and on the far deserts of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/South" title="South">South</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup> The threat of the [Corsairs](/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar "Corsairs of Umbar") was finally completely subdued during his reign.<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup> He also made peace with the [Haradrim](/wiki/Haradrim) after his coronation.<sup id="cite_ref-RK-StewardKing_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-StewardKing-11">[11]</a></sup>

#### Later History {#Later_History}

After Aragorn's death in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120"),<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-12">[12]</a></sup> his son [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion) took up the High Kingship.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-12">[12]</a></sup> It is said that Eldarion's reign lasted for about 100 years after the death of Aragorn.<sup id="cite_ref-L338_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L338-13">[13]</a></sup> Of [Eldarion](/wiki/Eldarion), it was foretold that he should rule a great realm, and that it should endure for a hundred generations of [Men](/wiki/Men) after him, that is until a new age brought in again new things; and from him should come the kings of many realms in long days after. But if this foretelling spoke truly, none now can say, for [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) are no more; and even the chronicles of the [House of Telcontar](/wiki/House_of_Telcontar) and all their deeds and glory are lost.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an unfinished story set some 220 years after the fall of Sauron, the Reunited Kingdom encountered a renewal of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)-worship known as the [Dark Tree](/wiki/Dark_Tree).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> However, J.R.R. Tolkien did not finish this story, because he "discovered that the King's Peace would contain no tales worth recounting".<sup id="cite_ref-L338_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L338-13">[13]</a></sup>
