The **Reunited Kingdom** was the restored kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the twin kingdoms founded at the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and his sons [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md). In the beginning, Elendil was [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) of both realms, but the two were divided after the deaths of his sons. Over 3,000 years later [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) was crowned as <i><b>Elessar</b></i> and reunited the kingdoms, ruling as [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md) of the newly-founded Reunited Kingdom.

## Geography {#Geography}

During Aragorn's reign, the Reunited Kingdom was comprised of the old realms of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), excluding only [Rohan](Rohan.md) (the [grant](../O/Oath_of_Eorl.md) of which he renewed); and the [Shire](../S/Shire.md), the [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md), and the [Treegarth of Orthanc](../T/Treegarth_of_Orthanc.md) (which became protectorates).<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup>

The Reunited Kingdom included all the lands of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md); all of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), except the [regions](../L/Lindon.md) beyond the [Lune](../L/Lune.md), and the lands east of [Greyflood](../G/Greyflood.md) and [Loudwater](../L/Loudwater.md), in which lay [Rivendell](Rivendell.md) and [Eregion](../E/Eregion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup>

In [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the realm extended:<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>

- North to the [Field of Celebrant](../F/Field_of_Celebrant.md) and the southern eaves of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>
- West to the [Greyflood](../G/Greyflood.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>
- East to the inland [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>
- South to the [River Harnen](River_Harnen.md), and also along the coast to the peninsula and haven of [Umbar](../U/Umbar.md)<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Politics {#Politics}

The [Númenórean](../N/Númenórean.md) [High King](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) governed the realm with the frame of ancient law, of which he was administrator (and interpreter) but not the maker.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-3">[3]</a></sup>

In all debatable matters of importance domestic, or external, however, even [Denethor](../D/Denethor_II.md) had a [Council](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md), and at least listened to what the Lords of the Fiefs and the Captains of the Forces had to say.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-3">[3]</a></sup> [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) re-established the [Great Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md), and in that [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md), who remained by inheritance the [Steward](../S/Steward.md) (or representative of the King during his absence abroad, or sickness, or between his death and the accession of his heir) was his chief counsellor.<sup id="cite_ref-politics_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-politics-3">[3]</a></sup>

After his accession, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) established a council in [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), because in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [13](../F/Fourth_Age_13.md), he chose three [Counsellors of the North-kingdom](../C/Counsellor_of_the_North-kingdom.md) from the people of the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) and [Buckland](../B/Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-8">[8]</a></sup> These Counsellors were those appointed to the positions of the [Thain](../T/Thain.md) and [Mayor of the Shire](../M/Mayor_of_the_Shire.md), and the [Master of Buckland](../M/Master_of_Buckland.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppBLater_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppBLater-8">[8]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Background {#Background}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="Realms_in_Exile.md" title="Realms in Exile">Realms in Exile</a></b></div></div>

At the end of the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and his sons established two great kingdoms in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md): [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) in the north and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) in the south. These [Kingdoms of the Dúnedain](../K/Kingdoms_of_the_Dúnedain.md) were united under a single [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md), [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) himself, who ruled the [North-kingdom](../A/Arnor.md) while his sons reigned jointly over the South-kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the first years of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md), this union failed. After the loss of [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) at the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md), his nephew [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md) took up the [kingship of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), and that country remained independent from the [North-kingdom](../N/North-kingdom.md) through most of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the north, the realm of Arnor fell into troubled times. It broke into three separate kingdoms, and in time these too were lost, so that the Dúnedain of the North-kingdom of Elendil were reduced to a wandering people led by a [Chieftain](../C/Chieftains_of_the_Dúnedain.md). Nonetheless, they were able to maintain Isildur's line in unbroken descent.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

There was only one attempt to reunite the kingdoms while they were both in existence. This attempt occurred in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) when [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), [King of Arthedain](../K/King_of_Arthedain.md), claimed the [High Kingship](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) of the [Two Kingdoms](../T/Two_Kingdoms.md).<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup> His claim was rejected by Steward [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md) and the [Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md), who elected to maintain their independence.<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup>

Soon enough, the line of [Kings](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) failed in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) altogether; [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md) was lost without heirs. Its rule was taken up by the [Stewards](../S/Stewards.md) of the [House of Húrin](../H/House_of_Húrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-9">[9]</a></sup>

### Reunification {#Reunification}

#### Reign of Elessar {#Reign_of_Elessar}

More than a thousand years later, after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), Arvedui's direct descendant, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), came forward to make the same claim. This time, the people of Gondor accepted a [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Dúnedain.md), and the Two Kingdoms were restored and reunited at last.

[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) restored [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and repeopled it, but retained [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) as the chief city.<sup id="cite_ref-Elendil_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Elendil-2">[2]</a></sup> He rebuilt [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md) and when he went north, would rule from there.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup> He also had the ruins of [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) rebuilt and made it a great city where [Men](../M/Men.md) dwelt once again.<sup id="cite_ref-Bound_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bound-10">[10]</a></sup> [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) was an exception to this, and though it lay within the Reunited Kingdom, Aragorn made a law that Men should not enter it, a law that he observed himself.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-6">[6]</a></sup>

During his reign, he, alongside [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md), led military campaigns beyond the [Sea of Rhûn](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md) and on the far deserts of the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../S/South.md" title="South">South</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_A_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix_A-5">[5]</a></sup> The threat of the [Corsairs](../C/Corsairs_of_Umbar.md) was finally completely subdued during his reign.<sup id="cite_ref-AppGondor_7-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppGondor-7">[7]</a></sup> He also made peace with the [Haradrim](../H/Haradrim.md) after his coronation.<sup id="cite_ref-RK-StewardKing_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RK-StewardKing-11">[11]</a></sup>

#### Later History {#Later_History}

After Aragorn's death in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [120](../F/Fourth_Age_120.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-12">[12]</a></sup> his son [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md) took up the High Kingship.<sup id="cite_ref-Tale_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tale-12">[12]</a></sup> It is said that Eldarion's reign lasted for about 100 years after the death of Aragorn.<sup id="cite_ref-L338_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L338-13">[13]</a></sup> Of [Eldarion](../E/Eldarion.md), it was foretold that he should rule a great realm, and that it should endure for a hundred generations of [Men](../M/Men.md) after him, that is until a new age brought in again new things; and from him should come the kings of many realms in long days after. But if this foretelling spoke truly, none now can say, for [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) are no more; and even the chronicles of the [House of Telcontar](../H/House_of_Telcontar.md) and all their deeds and glory are lost.<sup id="cite_ref-Third_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Third-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an unfinished story set some 220 years after the fall of Sauron, the Reunited Kingdom encountered a renewal of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md)-worship known as the [Dark Tree](../D/Dark_Tree.md).<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> However, J.R.R. Tolkien did not finish this story, because he "discovered that the King's Peace would contain no tales worth recounting".<sup id="cite_ref-L338_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L338-13">[13]</a></sup>
