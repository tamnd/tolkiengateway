**Ras Mewrim** was a headland on the coastline south-west of [Eglarest](../E/Eglarest.md), in the land of the [Falas](../F/Falas.md) in [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> The name only appears in the later additions to the [Second 'Silmarillion' Map](../T/The_Second_'Silmarillion'_Map.md).

**Bar-in-Mŷl**, the "Home of the [Gulls](../G/Gulls.md)", was another name for the headland in the essay "[Quendi and Eldar](../Q/Quendi_and_Eldar.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The name was first typed **Bar-in-Gwael**, but Tolkien later changed it to *Bar-in-Mŷl* and added the translation.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name *Bar-in-Mŷl* consists of *[bar](../B/Bar.md)* ("home") and *[mŷl](../M/Mŷl.md)* ("gulls").
