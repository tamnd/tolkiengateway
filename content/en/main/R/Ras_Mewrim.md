**Ras Mewrim** was a headland on the coastline south-west of [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest), in the land of the [Falas](/wiki/Falas) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand).<sup id="cite_ref-WJBeleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJBeleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> The name only appears in the later additions to the [Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map "The Second 'Silmarillion' Map").

**Bar-in-Mŷl**, the "Home of the [Gulls](/wiki/Gulls)", was another name for the headland in the essay "[Quendi and Eldar](/wiki/Quendi_and_Eldar)".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The name was first typed **Bar-in-Gwael**, but Tolkien later changed it to *Bar-in-Mŷl* and added the translation.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Bar-in-Mŷl* consists of *[bar](/wiki/Bar "Bar")* ("home") and *[mŷl](/wiki/M%C5%B7l "Mŷl")* ("gulls").
