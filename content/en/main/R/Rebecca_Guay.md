**Rebecca Guay** (born [9 April](/wiki/9_April) [1970](/wiki/1970)) is an American artist famed for her comic book work and [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) illustrations. In the 1990s, she was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> Later, she illustrated two pieces of alternate art for Magic's *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)* expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
