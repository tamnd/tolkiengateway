**Remmirath**, also known as the **Netted Stars**,<sup id="cite_ref-TiC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TiC-1">[1]</a></sup> was a constellation in the skies over [Arda](/wiki/Arda).

## History {#History}

Though not mentioned specifically, Remmirath's origin is likely the same to that of other constellations: it was created by [Varda](/wiki/Varda) to signal the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), it could be clearly seen in the Eastern sky during the autumn.<sup id="cite_ref-TiC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TiC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Remmirath* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), being quite accurately translated with its [Westron](/wiki/Westron) name the "Netted Stars". The word is composed of three elements: *rem* ("mesh") + *[mir](/wiki/Mir "Mir")* ("jewel") + the collective plural suffix *[-ath](/wiki/-ath)*. It has been translated as "group of gems in a net" and "group of jewels caught in a net".<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest stage of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), the Pleiades were called <i><b>Itseloktë</b></i> ("Cluster of Flies") in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and <i><b>Sithaloth</b></i> or *Sithaloctha* ("Fly-cluster") in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Remmirath is identified as the open star cluster <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleiades" title="wikipedia:Pleiades">Pleiades</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
