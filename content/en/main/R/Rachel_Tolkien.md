**Rachel Clare Reuel Tolkien** (born [13 February](/wiki/13_February) [1971](/wiki/1971)) is the daughter of [Baillie Tolkien](/wiki/Baillie_Tolkien) and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien), sibling to [Adam Tolkien](/wiki/Adam_Tolkien). This makes [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) her grandfather.

Rachel Tolkien attended the world premiere of *[The Lord of the Rings (musical)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(musical)>)* in Toronto in 2006. She said that the "set is incredible, the costumes are beautiful," and that "\[e\]verything to me that is the most important, and the most moving in the book, they've gotten on the stage".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Her last public appearance coincided with the French Tolkien exhibition in Paris, <a class="external text" href="https://www.lamontagne.fr/aubusson-23200/loisirs/lambassade-de-grande-bretagne-avant-la-bnf_13653111/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">visiting the Embassy of the United Kingdom</a> with her mother Baillie where one of the Aubusson tapestries was exhibited.
