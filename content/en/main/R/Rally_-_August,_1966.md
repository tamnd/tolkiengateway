**Rally - August, 1966** is an issue of the magazine <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rally&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rally (page does not exist)">Rally</a></i>. The issue includes "Words That Sound like Castles", an essay on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) by [Jared Lobdell](/wiki/Jared_Lobdell).

"Words That Sound Like Castles" is also the title Jared Lobdell used for a better known and completely different article one year later, in 1967, when he wrote a review of the second edition of *The Lord of the Rings* for "National Review".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

- Publication: August [1966](/wiki/1966)
- Issue: Vol. 1, No. 3
