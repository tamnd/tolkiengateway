**RAN** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "wander, stray, meander, go on uncertain course".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> There is also an alternative form of the root, **RĂNĂ**, said to signify "wander".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- RAN
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *ranya* ("erratic wandering"); *[Rána](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Sindarin: *rein*, *rain* ("erratic wandering"); *[randír](/wiki/Randir "Randir")*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

- RĂNĂ
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[randir](/wiki/Randir "Randir")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) root RAN ("wander, stray"). Among its derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- Primitive Quendian: *[Ranā](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")* ("Moon")
    - Quenya: *[Rana](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")*
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[Rhân](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")*

- Quenya: *ranya-* ("to stray"); *ráne* ("straying, wandering"); *ránen* ("errant")
- Noldorin: *rhenio* ("to stray"); *rhaun* ("errant"); *[rhandir](/wiki/Rhandir "Rhandir")*

- Primitive Quendian: *(a)raniā* ("free, \[?uncontrolling\]")<sup id="cite_ref-Corr_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corr-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
    - Noldorin: *[rhain, rhein](/wiki/Rhain "Rhain")*<sup id="cite_ref-Corr_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corr-4">[4]</a></sup>
    - Quenya: *[(a)ranya](</wiki/(a)ranya>)*<sup id="cite_ref-Corr_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corr-4">[4]</a></sup>
