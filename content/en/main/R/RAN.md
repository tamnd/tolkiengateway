**RAN** is a [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "wander, stray, meander, go on uncertain course".<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup> There is also an alternative form of the root, **RĂNĂ**, said to signify "wander".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- RAN
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *ranya* ("erratic wandering"); *[Rána](../M/Moon.md#Other_names)*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - Sindarin: *rein*, *rain* ("erratic wandering"); *[randír](Randir.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-VT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-1">[1]</a></sup>

- RĂNĂ
    - [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) *[randir](Randir.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-PE_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* appears the [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) root RAN ("wander, stray"). Among its derivatives are:<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

- Primitive Quendian: *[Ranā](../M/Moon.md#Other_names)* ("Moon")
    - Quenya: *[Rana](../M/Moon.md#Other_names)*
    - [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md): *[Rhân](../M/Moon.md#Other_names)*

- Quenya: *ranya-* ("to stray"); *ráne* ("straying, wandering"); *ránen* ("errant")
- Noldorin: *rhenio* ("to stray"); *rhaun* ("errant"); *[rhandir](Rhandir.md)*

- Primitive Quendian: *(a)raniā* ("free, \[?uncontrolling\]")<sup id="cite_ref-Corr_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corr-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[note 1]</a></sup>
    - Noldorin: *[rhain, rhein](Rhain.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-Corr_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corr-4">[4]</a></sup>
    - Quenya: *[(a)ranya](<../_/(a)ranya.md>)*<sup id="cite_ref-Corr_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corr-4">[4]</a></sup>
