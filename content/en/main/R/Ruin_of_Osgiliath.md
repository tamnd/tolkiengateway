The **ruin of Osgiliath** in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2475](../T/Third_Age_2475.md)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the first large-scale attack against [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) after the end of the [Watchful Peace](../W/Watchful_Peace.md). The race of Uruks, described as black [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) of great strength, first appeared in this battle and were able to sweep across [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) and take [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md). [Boromir](<../B/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor).md>) son of [Denethor I](../D/Denethor_I.md) was able to defeat this force and clear them from Ithilien, but the city was finally ruined, its great stone-bridge was broken, and no one lived in the city thereafter. During this brief war Boromir received a [Morgul-wound](../M/Morgul-wound.md) that shrunk him with pain and caused his premature death but twelve years after his father.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
