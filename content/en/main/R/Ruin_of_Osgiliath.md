The **ruin of Osgiliath** in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2475](/wiki/Third_Age_2475 "Third Age 2475")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the first large-scale attack against [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) after the end of the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace). The race of Uruks, described as black [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) of great strength, first appeared in this battle and were able to sweep across [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) and take [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath). [Boromir](</wiki/Boromir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Boromir (Steward of Gondor)") son of [Denethor I](/wiki/Denethor_I) was able to defeat this force and clear them from Ithilien, but the city was finally ruined, its great stone-bridge was broken, and no one lived in the city thereafter. During this brief war Boromir received a [Morgul-wound](/wiki/Morgul-wound) that shrunk him with pain and caused his premature death but twelve years after his father.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
