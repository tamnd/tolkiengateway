**Raymond Harold Reinhardt** (born [1930](/wiki/1930)) is an American actor. Born in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bronx" title="wikipedia:The Bronx">The Bronx</a>, Reinhardt learned acting by picking up many of the local accents. After starting acting in the Army, he entered Piscator Dramatic Workshop in New York and London's Academy of Music and Dramatic Art.

- [1979](/wiki/1979): [*The Hobbit* (1979 radio series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1979_radio_series)> "The Hobbit (1979 radio series)") - [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)
