The **Redhorn Gate**,<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> also known as the **Redhorn Pass**,<sup id="cite_ref-Celebrian_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Celebrian-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Stoors_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stoors-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> was a high and difficult pass over the central [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) that led from the wilds of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) to [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) beyond. On the knees of the mountains a narrow path wound its way on the southern slope of [Redhorn](/wiki/Caradhras "Caradhras") with a sheer wall of cliffs on the left and a deep ravine on the right.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> On the eastern side of the pass the [Dimrill Stair](/wiki/Dimrill_Stair),<sup id="cite_ref-Dimrill_Stair_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dimrill_Stair-8">[8]</a></sup> a deep-cloven path, descended steeply beside the Dimrill stream,<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> which fell in a ladder of short waterfalls,<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> down to the [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale) on the eastern side of the Misty Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Dimrill_Stair_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Dimrill_Stair-8">[8]</a></sup> An ancient broad, well planned road led from [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) into the hills towards the pass.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> No other passes led over the Misty Mountains south of the pass until they ended at the [Gap of Rohan](/wiki/Gap_of_Rohan).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

At the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) during the [War of the Last Alliance](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance) the main forces of the [elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") king [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) with Isildur and a part of the forces of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) crossed the Misty Mountains from Eriador into Rhovanion over the Redhorn Pass and the [Pass of Imladris](/wiki/High_Pass "High Pass").<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1150](/wiki/Third_Age_1150 "Third Age 1150") the pass was traversed by the [Stoors](/wiki/Stoors) migrating from the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin) into [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), from where they headed to the [Angle](/wiki/Angle) and [Dunland](/wiki/Dunland).<sup id="cite_ref-Stoors_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Stoors-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2509](/wiki/Third_Age_2509 "Third Age 2509") [Celebrían](/wiki/Celebr%C3%ADan), the wife of [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), was captured by [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) on the Redhorn Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-Celebrian_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Celebrian-3">[3]</a></sup>

On [11 January](/wiki/11_January) - [12](/wiki/12_January "12 January"), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) attempted to cross the Misty Mountains by the Redhorn Gate as they journeyed on the [Quest of Mount Doom](/wiki/Quest_of_Mount_Doom). They came into a fierce blizzard and could not go any further because of the deep snow. Thus were they defeated by the ill will of the mountain and were forced to cross under the Misty Mountains through the Mines of Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>

On [13 September](/wiki/13_September) [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn), [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and elves from [Lórien](/wiki/L%C3%B3rien) parted from the remaining members of the Fellowship of the Ring in Eregion to cross over the Redhorn Gate to return to their own realm.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2009: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Redhorn Gate is featured in the region of <a href="/wiki/Eregion" title="Eregion">Eregion</a>. The snowstorms are very strong in this area, and an avalanche prevents further travel to the lands of the east.</dd></dl>
