<i><b>rómen</b></i> is a noun meaning "uprising", "sunrise"<sup id="cite_ref-SApp_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SApp-1">[1]</a></sup> and "east" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Romen.gif"><img src="/w/images/3/34/Romen.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Rómen* is also the name in Quenya of the twenty-fifth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[2]</a></sup> In all modes this letter represents "full" trilled **R**, unlike *[Óre](/wiki/%C3%93re)* which is used for "weak" untrilled **R**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *Rómen* was one of the most widely known and used of all the Tengwar since it indicated "east" even in languages that used a very different term.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Aro</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<br>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [RO](/wiki/RO)+[MEN](/wiki/MEN).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the West-lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) the four cardinal directions were *[Númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen), [Hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen), Rómen,* and *[Formen](/wiki/Formen)*, and they were named in this order, beginning with (and facing) west. *Hyarmen* meant a left-hand region and *Formen* meant a right-hand region.<sup id="cite_ref-Names2_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names2-6">[6]</a></sup>
