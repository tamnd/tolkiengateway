The **River Running** or **Celduin** was a long [river](/wiki/Category:Rivers "Category:Rivers") that flowed from the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), through the [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake) and down through [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) to the inland [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn).

## Description {#Description}

The River Running was 600 miles long [river](/wiki/Category:Rivers "Category:Rivers")<sup id="cite_ref-Rhun_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rhun-1">[1]</a></sup> that poured out of the [Front Gate](/wiki/Front_Gate) of the [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain), descended over two falls and swirled around [Dale](/wiki/Dale). It turned west beyond [Ravenhill](/wiki/Ravenhill) and then east and south to [Long Lake](/wiki/Long_Lake)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> and thence through the eastern outskirts of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood),<sup id="cite_ref-Wilderland_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wilderland-3">[3]</a></sup> then south east through apparently uninhabited regions of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion) to its confluence with the [Carnen](/wiki/Carnen) and finally in a long south-eastward loop to the great inland [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn),<sup id="cite_ref-Rhun_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rhun-1">[1]</a></sup> past the land of [Dorwinion](/wiki/Dorwinion).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The Celduin had three tributaries: the [Forest River](/wiki/Forest_River), which flowed into the Long Lake from the west; a small unnamed river that flowed from the [Mountains of Mirkwood](/wiki/Mountains_of_Mirkwood) to join the Celduin just north of the [Old Forest Road](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road);<sup id="cite_ref-Wilderland_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wilderland-3">[3]</a></sup> and the Carnen, which entered the Celduin from the northeast about halfway between Mirkwood and the Sea of Rhûn.<sup id="cite_ref-Rhun_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rhun-1">[1]</a></sup> Its course then ended in the [Sea of Rhûn](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[note 1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the thirteenth century of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) the Celduin had formed the northeast border of the lands of [Vidugavia](/wiki/Vidugavia).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup> During the [Great Plague](/wiki/Great_Plague) of [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1636](/wiki/Third_Age_1636 "Third Age 1636")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> more than half of the people of Rhovanion died and when the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) invaded from the east (beginning in [1851](/wiki/Third_Age_1851 "Third Age 1851")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup>) the remaining [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen) in the region fled across the Celduin and merged with the folk of [Dale](/wiki/Dale).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup> After the threat of the Wainriders ended some of the Northmen drifted back along the banks of the Celduin but when [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion) became the Steward of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) he learned that new [Easterlings](/wiki/Easterlings) were again driving the remnant of the Northmen up the Celduin.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

Between the time when [Thráin I](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I) founded the dwarf-kingdom in the Lonely Mountain (in [1999](/wiki/Third_Age_1999 "Third Age 1999")) and the arrival of [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug) (in [2770](/wiki/Third_Age_2770 "Third Age 2770"))<sup id="cite_ref-TA_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-8">[7]</a></sup> the Northmen living between the Celduin and Carnen grew strong and repelled all eastern enemies.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> Even after the arrival of the dragon at the Lonely Mountain the Celduin was still a highway of trade for [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil)'s kingdom, the town of [Esgaroth](/wiki/Lake-town "Lake-town"), and places to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the map that would become [Thrór's Map](/wiki/Thr%C3%B3r%27s_Map) in *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, the River Running flowed nearly due east from the ruins of Dale. On this map [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") scratched out the eastward flow and put in the bend to the Long Lake, stressing the change with an arrow.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[12]</a></sup>
