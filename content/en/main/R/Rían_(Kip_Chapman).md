**Rían** is an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon). Rían was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios) and he is portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Kip_Chapman&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Kip Chapman (page does not exist)">Kip Chapman</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Throughout the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), Rían accompanied [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) in her hunt for [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron).

The latest mission was one in which they had to find a certain [fortress](/wiki/D%C3%BArnost "Dúrnost") in the [Forodwaith](/wiki/Northern_Waste "Northern Waste"). Upon finding and beginning to investigate there, it was Rían who first noticed the stalking [Hristorco](/wiki/Snow-troll "Snow-troll"), shouting a warning to rest of the company.

After the Hristorco was killed, Rían joined in with [Thondir](/wiki/Thondir) and the rest of the company in demanding that Galadriel returns to [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon).

Upon his return to Lindon, Rían, like the rest of the company, was honoured in a great ceremony by the High-king [Gil-Galad](/wiki/Gil-Galad). As such, he was granted permission to return to [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor).
