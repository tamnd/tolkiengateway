**Robert Giddings** ([29 June](/wiki/29_June) [1935](/wiki/1935) - [8 March](/wiki/8_March) [2012](/wiki/2012)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) was Professor of English at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bournemouth_University" title="wikipedia:Bournemouth University">Bournemouth University</a>, UK. Author and co-author of many works, Giddings also wrote about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Bibliography, selected {#Bibliography,_selected}

### Books {#Books}

- [1981](/wiki/1981): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: The Shores of Middle-earth](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_The_Shores_of_Middle-earth)* (co-author)
- [1983](/wiki/1983): *[J.R.R. Tolkien: This Far Land](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_This_Far_Land)* (editor)
