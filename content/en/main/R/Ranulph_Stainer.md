**Ranulph Stainer** (born in [1936](../_/1936.md)) was a member of the [Notion Club](../N/Notion_Club.md).

## History {#History}

Ranulph Stainer was educated at University College and professionally became an expert in banking and economics. Privately, however, he was devoted to the history and practice of music, and has composed several major and minor works, including one moderately successful opera: <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Midas&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Midas (page does not exist)">Midas</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

When [Lowdham](../A/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham.md) and [Jeremy](../W/Wilfrid_Trewin_Jeremy.md) stormed out of [Ramer](../M/Michael_George_Ramer.md)'s rooms during a Great Storm, in which they saw clouds in the shapes of the [Eagles](../E/Eagles.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Stainer expressed doubts concerning the legitimacy of their actions, claiming that the entire event was staged.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
