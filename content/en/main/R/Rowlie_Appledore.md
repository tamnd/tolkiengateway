**Rowlie Appledore** was a [man](/wiki/Men_of_Bree "Men of Bree") of [Bree](/wiki/Bree) and member of the [Appledore Family](/wiki/Appledore_Family).

## History {#History}

Rowlie was killed in early January, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"), fighting [Bill Ferny](/wiki/Bill_Ferny) and [Sharkey](/wiki/Sharkey)'s [ruffians](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rowlie* is diminutive of Rowland, from *hrod* "fame" + land.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> *Appledore* is an old word for "apple tree".

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North" title="The Lord of the Rings: War in the North">The Lord of the Rings: War in the North</a></i> </b>

<dl><dd>Rowlie Appledore appears as a local in <a href="/wiki/Bree" title="Bree">Bree</a>, as a non-player character at the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Prancing_Pony_Inn" title="Prancing Pony Inn">Prancing Pony Inn</a>. The player character can converse with Rowlie and help him in his efforts to confess his feelings to an old love of his, a local woman named Idona. If the player character revisits Bree during the later parts of the game&#39;s storyline, the player learns that Rowlie and several other townsmen were killed by the ruffians in January (as in the source material). <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>
