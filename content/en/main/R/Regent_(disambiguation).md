**Regent** ([Q.](../Q/Quenya.md) *karma-kundo*) can refer to:

## Arda {#Arda}

- [Regent of Gondor](Regent_of_Gondor.md), title of a person granted authority by the [king](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) to rule [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) on his behalf.
    - [Minalcar](../M/Minalcar.md), the only known Regent of Gondor
- <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Regent_of_N%C3%BAmenor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Regent of Númenor (page does not exist)">Regent of Númenor</a>, title of Regents ruling on behalf of any [King of Númenor](../K/King_of_Númenor.md).
    - [Hallatan of Hyarnastorni](../H/Hallatan.md), a [Númenórean](../N/Númenóreans.md) lord who ruled as regent on behalf of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md).
