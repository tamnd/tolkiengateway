**Regent** ([Q.](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya") *karma-kundo*) can refer to:

## Arda {#Arda}

- [Regent of Gondor](/wiki/Regent_of_Gondor), title of a person granted authority by the [king](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor") to rule [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) on his behalf.
    - [Minalcar](/wiki/Minalcar), the only known Regent of Gondor
- <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Regent_of_N%C3%BAmenor&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Regent of Númenor (page does not exist)">Regent of Númenor</a>, title of Regents ruling on behalf of any [King of Númenor](/wiki/King_of_N%C3%BAmenor).
    - [Hallatan of Hyarnastorni](/wiki/Hallatan "Hallatan"), a [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans "Númenóreans") lord who ruled as regent on behalf of [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion).
