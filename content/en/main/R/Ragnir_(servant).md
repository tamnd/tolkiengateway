**Ragnir**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup> was a blind servant of [Morwen](/wiki/Morwen) in [Húrin's](/wiki/H%C3%BArin "Húrin") house in [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).

## History {#History}

When Morwen informed her young son [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) that it was time for him to leave [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min) he objected, and part of his objection was that his mother would be left with only old women and two male servants, [Sador](/wiki/Sador) who was lame and Ragnir who was blind. Nevertheless, Túrin did leave in fulfillment of his mother's command.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>
