**Richard Ovenden**, [OBE](../O/Order_of_the_British_Empire.md) <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Society_of_Antiquaries_of_London" title="wikipedia:Fellow of the Society of Antiquaries of London">FSA</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Arts" title="wikipedia:Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts">FRSA</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Historical_Society" title="wikipedia:Royal Historical Society">FRHistS</a> (born [25 March](../_/25_March.md) [1964](../_/1964.md)), is a British librarian and author who is currently the 25th <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodley%27s_Librarian" title="wikipedia:Bodley&#39;s Librarian">Bodley&#39;s librarian</a> at the [Bodleian Library](../B/Bodleian_Library.md),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> having been appointed in [2014](../_/2014.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He is also the current head of the [University of Oxford](../U/University_of_Oxford.md)'s <a class="external text" href="https://www.glam.ox.ac.uk/home" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Gardens, Libraries and Museums</a> group.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
