**The Room of Logs** was a room found in the [Cottage of Lost Play](../C/Cottage_of_Lost_Play.md) on the isle of [Tol Eressëa](../T/Tol_Eressëa.md). It was described as having a hearth with a magic fire called the Tale-fire, which burned red and never went out. It was said to help storytellers give their tales.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the traveler [Eriol](../E/Eriol.md)'s stay in the Cottage of Lost Play, the caretakers of the house, [Lindo](../L/Lindo.md) and his wife [Vairë](<../V/Vairë_(wife_of_Lindo).md>) took Eriol into the Room of Logs to tell him about the land of Tol Eressëa. There sat Lindo in his chair by the fire, and Vairë at his feet. Alongside them joined many children to listen to the tales of the land as they often did at each thrice sounding of [Tombo](../G/Gong_of_the_Children.md), the Gong of the Children.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The room was described as having a low ceiling, with many soft rugs and cushions and a deep chair with carven arms and feet. A great hearth was there which burned with the red flamed Tale-fire, which was magic and never went out.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other Names {#Other_Names}

The Room of Logs was also called the Room of the Log Fire.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
