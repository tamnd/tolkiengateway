**Richard O'Callaghan**, born **Richard Rooke**, is a British actor. He provided the voice of [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) in [*The Lord of the Rings* 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") that was broadcast on [BBC 4](/wiki/BBC "BBC").
