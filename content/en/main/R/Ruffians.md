**Ruffians**, also known in the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) as the **[Chief](../C/Chief_Shirriff.md)'s Men** or **[Sharkey](../S/Sharkey.md)'s Men**, were a group of [Men](../M/Men.md) and [Half-orcs](../H/Half-orcs.md) from the lands south of the Shire.

## History {#History}

In the late autumn or early winter of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [a lot](../S/Southerners.md) of Men, mostly ruffians, came to the Shire with great wagons from the south. The ruffians among them were working for [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md), and it was because of him and his interest in procuring [pipe-weed](../P/Pipe-weed.md) for himself that they were traveling north. Some of them left to carry pipe-weed and other goods from the Shire back south to [Isengard](../I/Isengard.md), while others remained and helped [Lotho](../L/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins.md) take over the Shire. Soon even more Men came. They felled [trees,](../T/Trees.md) built sheds and houses for themselves across the Shire and ordered the hobbits around, taking what they wanted and suppressing any dissent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Around the same time, some Men came to [Bree](../B/Bree.md) up the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) from the south. While some of them were refugees, many were bad men.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Before the end of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), the [Bree-landers](../B/Bree-landers.md) expelled the newcomers from Bree, but early in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), after a heavy snowfall, these Men attacked Bree with the help of [Harry Goatleaf](../H/Harry_Goatleaf.md) and [Bill Ferny](../B/Bill_Ferny.md), who might have let them in. In a resulting [set-to](../S/Set-to_in_Bree.md), five Bree-landers were killed, but the ruffians were ultimately defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Some of them retreated to the woods beyond [Archet](../A/Archet.md), where they lived as robbers,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> while some others, like Bill Ferny, joined Sharkey's Men in the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Meanwhile, in the Shire, soon after the New Year of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), Lotho declared himself the [Chief Shirriff](../C/Chief_Shirriff.md).<sup id="cite_ref-:0_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-6">[6]</a></sup> However, [Paladin Took](../P/Paladin_Took_II.md), the [Thain](../T/Thain.md) of the Shire, refused to acknowledge Lotho's authority and didn't let the ruffians enter [Tookland](../T/Tookland.md). This lead to skirmishes, in which three ruffians, as well as one or two hobbits, were shot. After that, the ruffians became more ruthless.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> On [September 22](../_/22_September.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md), when Sharkey himself came to the Shire and usurped Lotho, the actions of the ruffians became even harsher.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-6">[6]</a></sup> By that time, the Shire was occupied by about 300 ruffians,<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> armed with whips, [knives](../K/Knives.md), clubs and some [bows](../B/Bows.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

One of the leaders of the ruffians was killed<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> and about a score of them<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> were captured<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> by the [Shire-hobbits](../S/Shire-hobbits.md) on [November 2](../_/2_November.md), during the rebellion in Bywater. As a result of the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md) on the next day, [November 3](../_/3_November.md), nearly seventy more ruffians were killed and buried in the [Battle Pit](../B/Battle_Pit.md), and a dozen more imprisoned.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> After that, the southern gangs of ruffians offered little resistance and fled from the Shire, and those ruffians who were captured were shown to the borders.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ruffian* is an [English](../M/Modern_English.md) word that derives from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_French" title="wikipedia:Middle French">Middle French</a> word *rufian*, which itself derives from the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language" title="wikipedia:Italian language">Italian</a> word *ruffiano* (“pimp”). The word *Ruffian* is defined as a "*scoundrel, rascal or unprincipled, deceitful, brutal and unreliable person.*"<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup>
