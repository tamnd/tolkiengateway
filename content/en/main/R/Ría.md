<i><b>ría</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) and [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) word meaning "wreath, garland".<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *rīgā*, deriving from the [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [stem](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [RIG](/wiki/RIG) ("twine, wreathe").<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>
