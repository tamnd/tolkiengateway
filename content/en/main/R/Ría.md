<i><b>ría</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md) word meaning "wreath, garland".<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) *rīgā*, deriving from the [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md) [stem](../S/Sundocarme.md) [RIG](RIG.md) ("twine, wreathe").<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>
