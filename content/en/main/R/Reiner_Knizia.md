**Reiner Knizia** (born [16 November](../_/16_November.md) [1957](../_/1957.md)) is board game designer who was born in Germany but now lives in England. Among the many prolific board games during his career are various board games based on Tolkien's works and its adaptations.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Selected list of games {#Selected_list_of_games}

- [2000](../_/2000.md): [Lord of the Rings](<../L/Lord_of_the_Rings_(board_game).md>), and expansions
- [2003](../_/2003.md): [J.R.R. Tolkien's Lord of the Rings](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien's_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)
- [2005](../_/2005.md): [Lord of the Rings: The Confrontation](../L/Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Confrontation.md)
- [2010](../_/2010.md): <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit_(board_game)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit (board game) (page does not exist)">The Hobbit</a>
- [2012](../_/2012.md): <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_-_The_Card_Game&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - The Card Game (page does not exist)">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey - The Card Game</a>
- [2013](../_/2013.md): <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey_(board_game)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey (board game) (page does not exist)">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a>
- [2013](../_/2013.md): <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug_(board_game)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug (board game) (page does not exist)">The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug</a>
