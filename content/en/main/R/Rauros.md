<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r395759"/>

The **Rauros-falls**, also known as the **Falls of Rauros** or simply **Rauros**, were the great falls of River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) beneath [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel), where the river fell from [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) to the wetland of [Nindalf](/wiki/Nindalf).

## History {#History}

The [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor) had built the [North Stair](/wiki/North_Stair), a portage-way that bypassed the Falls.

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/9/90/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg/200px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="155" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/9/90/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/9/90/Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg/400px-Ted_Nasmith_-_At_the_Falls.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1242"/></a><figcaption>&#34;At the Falls&#34; by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

At the breaking of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") paddled a boat from the west bank of the river to the east just above the falls and had to use all their strength to avoid being swept over the falls by the current. Shortly afterwards [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir)'s body was placed in another boat by [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) who sent it over the Falls. Aragorn declared in the [Song of Boromir](/wiki/Song_of_Boromir) that ever after the [Tower of Guard](/wiki/Tower_of_Guard) would gaze "*to Rauros, golden Rauros falls until the end of days*".

But it apparently survived the fall; at any rate, soon afterwards it was seen by his brother [Faramir](/wiki/Faramir) upon the lower reaches of the Anduin in what felt to him like a dream yet from which there was no waking, and the two halves of Boromir's cloven horn were separately retrieved (neither by Faramir).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In his [unfinished index](/wiki/Unfinished_index "Unfinished index"), [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") glossed the name *Rauros* as "roaring spray".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A pencilled annotation in a manuscript also gives the translation "Rush-rain" or "Roar-rain".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In another manuscript, the name *Rauros(se)* is said to be composed of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Raw&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Raw (page does not exist)">raw</a></i> and *[ros](/wiki/Ros "Ros")*, and meaning "roaring rain". It is noted that the repetition of *r* in a name is usually retained in those with "phonetic or onomatopoeic significance".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
