**Ring-finder** was a title that [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) used of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) during a conversation with [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins). He called Frodo "the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer)" and added that Frodo is "the heir of Bilbo, the Ring-finder".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
