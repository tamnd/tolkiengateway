**Royd Allan Reuel Tolkien** (born [16 July](/wiki/16_July) [1969](/wiki/1969)) is a great-grandson of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), the grandchild of Tolkien's second son, [Michael](/wiki/Michael_Tolkien "Michael Tolkien"). His birthname is Royd Baker, but he changed his name to Royd Tolkien when he became adult .<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>

Royd Tolkien played a [Ranger](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the [burier](/wiki/Rangers_of_Ithilien "Rangers of Ithilien") of the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) in the Extended Edition of *[The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug)*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He has stated that he doesn't know whether or not he ever met his great-grandfather but his sister, Amanda is was a year older, does have a photo with him. He owns a signed copy of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, with the inscription "To Royd, JRR Tolkien".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
