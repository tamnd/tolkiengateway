**Rath Dínen** was the street through the [Hallows](/wiki/Hallows) of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), where the great of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), Kings and Stewards alike, were laid after death. It was accessed through the gate of [Fen Hollen](/wiki/Fen_Hollen) in the rear wall of the sixth level of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-1">[1]</a></sup>

The Street was lined with images of those who lay there. The [Stewards](/wiki/Stewards_of_Gondor "Stewards of Gondor") had a House apart from the tombs of the Kings, the [House of the Stewards](/wiki/House_of_the_Stewards), where [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) perished in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

When the seedling of the [White Tree](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor "White Tree of Gondor") was found on the slopes of Mindolluin by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), the [Dead Tree](/wiki/Dead_Tree) that had stood in the courts of Minas Tirith was laid in Rath Dínen.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

After [Meriadoc Brandybuck](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck) and [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took) died of old age in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) they were laid to rest in Rath Dínen.<sup id="cite_ref-Later_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [120](/wiki/Fourth_Age_120 "Fourth Age 120")<sup id="cite_ref-Later_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Later-4">[4]</a></sup> when [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") felt the oncoming of death he went to Rath Dínen and laid himself down in the House of the Kings. It was there that he bade farewell to [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rath Dínen* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name. It means "Silent Street".<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-1">[1]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[rath](/wiki/Rath "Rath")* ("street (in a city)") and *[tínen](/wiki/T%C3%AEn "Tîn")* ("silent").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Named &#34;the Silent Street&#34;, Rath Dínen is inaccessible in the pre-battle version of Minas Tirith. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">battle of the Pelennor Fields</a> <a href="/wiki/Beregond" title="Beregond">Beregond</a> throws the <a href="/wiki/Fen_Hollen" title="Fen Hollen">Fen Hollen</a> open but is followed by the members of &#34;the Withered Tree&#34; conspiracy seeking to overthrow the rule of the Stewards. The players have to battle the Withered Tree within the Silent Street in a group instance before witnessing the end of <a href="/wiki/Denethor" title="Denethor">Denethor</a>. In the after-battle version of Minas Tirith, the Silent Street is opened for the mourning.</dd></dl>
