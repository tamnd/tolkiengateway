**Ruamórë** was the dwelling of [Ungweliantë](/wiki/Ungweliant%C3%AB),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Par_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Par-2">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ruamórë* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). It is glossed as "Still Night" by *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)*, consisting of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rua&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rua (page does not exist)">rua</a></i> ("steady, still, tranquil") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=M%C3%B3r%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Mórë (page does not exist)">mórë</a></i> ("night, darkness; black, dark").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

However, in a note to an entry in the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)*, the first element of the name comes from either <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=R%C3%BA_(Qenya_word)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rú (Qenya word) (page does not exist)">rú</a></i> (1. "dwelling, village"; 2. "steadfastness") or <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ruella&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ruella (page does not exist)">ruella</a></i> ("hamlet").<sup id="cite_ref-Par_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Par-2">[1]</a></sup>
