**Rupert Dolbear** (born in [1929](/wiki/1929)) was a member of the [Notion Club](/wiki/Notion_Club).

## History {#History}

Rupert Dolbear was a Research Chemist who gained an education at Wadham College, and among his interests were philosophy, psychoanalysis, and gardening. He was a close friend of [Michael George Ramer](/wiki/Michael_George_Ramer) and was known to the Club as *Ruthless Rufus*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He had a habit of sleeping loudly during long readings or discussions, but he would rouse up in the middle of a debate, showing that he had an odd ability of both sleeping and listening (he said that it was a time-saving habit that long membership of the Club had forced him to acquire).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

There was a chemist's shop in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) called *Dolbear &amp; Goodall* and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) recollects that his [father](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") found this particularly engaging and that it may be that Tolkien found in Dolbear the chemist a comic appropriateness to [Robert Havard](/wiki/Robert_Havard) or to Havard as he was going to present him, since in one of the manuscripts he wrote *Havard* beneath *Dolbear*, indicating that Dolbear, with his red hair and beard and his nickname in the Club, can be seen as a sort of a parody of Havard.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
