The **Rings of Power** can refer to:

- The **[Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power)**, twenty [Magic](/wiki/Magic) rings that were created by the [Gwaith-i-Mírdain](/wiki/Gwaith-i-M%C3%ADrdain) and [Annatar](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron").

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/Of_the_Rings_of_Power_and_the_Third_Age" title="Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age">Of the Rings of Power and the Third Age</a></b></i>, the fifth part of *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, edited by [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien).

- <i><b><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></b></i>, the television adaptation set in the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), produced by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).
