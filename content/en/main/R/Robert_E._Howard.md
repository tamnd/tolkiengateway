**Robert E. Howard** (22 January 1906 – 11 June 1936) was an American author, famous for his stories about <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conan_the_Barbarian" title="wikipedia:Conan the Barbarian">Conan the Barbarian</a> and for creating the genre <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sword_and_sorcery" title="wikipedia:Sword and sorcery">sword and sorcery</a>.

In July [1964](/wiki/1964), [L. Sprague de Camp](/wiki/L._Sprague_de_Camp) had sent [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) a copy of his anthology *[Swords &amp; Sorcery](/wiki/Swords_%26_Sorcery)*, which included Howard's short story "<a class="external text" href="https://gutenberg.net.au/ebooks06/0600971h.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Shadows in the Moonlight</a>". Tolkien sent [a letter to de Camp in August 1964](/wiki/Letter_to_L._Sprague_de_Camp "Letter to L. Sprague de Camp"), expressing mixed opinions about the stories in the anthology.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In February [1967](/wiki/1967), when de Camp and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_E._Nourse" title="wikipedia:Alan E. Nourse">Alan E. Nourse</a> visited Tolkien for an interview, he "indicated that he 'rather liked' Howard's Conan stories".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [1969](/wiki/1969), Howard's "<a class="external text" href="https://gutenberg.net.au/ebooks06/0600731h.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Valley of the Worm</a>" appeared alongside two of Tolkien's poems in the anthology *[Young Magicians](/wiki/Young_Magicians)* (edited by [Lin Carter](/wiki/Lin_Carter)).
