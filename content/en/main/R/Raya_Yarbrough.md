**Raya Yarbrough** is an American singer and songwriter<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who voiced [Goldberry](/wiki/Goldberry) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 2 (page does not exist)">Season 2</a> episode *[Eldest](</wiki/Eldest_(episode)> "Eldest (episode)")*.<sup id="cite_ref-ROP_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ROP-2">[2]</a></sup> She is a frequent musical collaborator and wife of the *The Rings of Power* composer [Bear McCreary](/wiki/Bear_McCreary).<sup id="cite_ref-ROP_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ROP-2">[2]</a></sup>

She performed multiple variations of [Bronwyn](/wiki/Bronwyn)'s theme, in the [Season 1](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_1 "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 1") episodes *[Udûn](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)> "Udûn (episode)")*<sup id="cite_ref-E203_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E203-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-E106_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E106-4">[4]</a></sup> and *[The Eye](/wiki/The_Eye)*<sup id="cite_ref-E107_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E107-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-E203_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E203-3">[3]</a></sup> and the Season 2 episode *[The Eagle and the Sceptre](/wiki/The_Eagle_and_the_Sceptre)*, the latter named on the episode soundtrack as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Funeral_Pyre_and_Reconciliation&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Funeral Pyre and Reconciliation (page does not exist)">Funeral Pyre and Reconciliation</a></i> and on the season soundtrack as <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Pyre&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Pyre (page does not exist)">The Pyre</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-ROP_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ROP-2">[2]</a></sup> In the Season 1 episode *[The Great Wave](/wiki/The_Great_Wave)*<sup id="cite_ref-E104_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E104-6">[6]</a></sup> and the Season 2 episode *[Doomed to Die](/wiki/Doomed_to_Die)*<sup id="cite_ref-E207_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-E207-7">[7]</a></sup> she sung in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) themes relating to [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). In the final chorus of <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Old_Tom_Bombadil&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Old Tom Bombadil (page does not exist)">Old Tom Bombadil</a></i> for *Eldest*, Yarbrough sings the harmony line.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
