**Rohald** was the name of [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md)'s horse in the 1960 version of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)* that [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) never completed.

In the published story the wizard rode up to the [Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) on the first day on a white horse (bringing [Bilbo's](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) pocket-handkerchiefs, pipe, and tobacco to the hobbit),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but this steed was never named. There was no mention of any special abilities for this horse and it was left in [Rivendell](Rivendell.md) when the Company ascended the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md); for the climb [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) had lent Gandalf a pony, a "jolly sturdy little white fellow" since "his horse was not suitable for the mountain-paths".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the 1960 rewrite the white horse was named Rohald.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> With Rohald, Gandalf used no stirrups and seldom used the reins, and the horse answered his commands spoken in a strange tongue. Rohald was the leader of the ponies; at the broken bridge he crossed the river and neighed to encourage the Dwarves' ponies to follow,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and on the night of the Trolls he gathered the wandering ponies and looked after them.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The 1960 version ended as the Company approached Rivendell so whether or not Rohald was to accompany Gandalf after visiting the [Last Homely House](../L/Last_Homely_House.md) is unknown. However, a final note to the unfinished chapter 3 stated that "Róhald belonged to Rivendell, &amp; had been lent by [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) to Gandalf".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> \[Only in the note was the 'o' in Rohald accented.\]
