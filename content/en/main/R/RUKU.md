**RUKU** (or **RUK**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>) is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "*the dreadful shapes ... taken by the servants of [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth")*"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> that haunted the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and "*the terror that they inspired*".<sup id="cite_ref-AC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives<sup id="cite_ref-AC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-3">[3]</a></sup> {#Derivatives[3]}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *ruk-*, *rauk-*, *uruk-*, *urk(u)*, *runk-*, *rukut/s*, *gruk-*, *guruk-*, *[ñguruk](/wiki/%C3%91guruk "Ñguruk")*
    - [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *rauku*, *raukō*
        - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[rauko](/wiki/Rauko "Rauko")*
        - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[raug](/wiki/Raug "Raug")*
    - [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *urku*, *uruku*
        - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *urko*
        - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *urug*
    - [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *urkō*, *urkā*
        - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[Orch](/wiki/Orch)*
    - [Adûnaic](/wiki/Ad%C3%BBnaic): *urku*, *urkhu*
    - [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech): *[uruk](/wiki/Uruk "Uruk")*
    - [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul): *[Rukhs](/wiki/Rukhs)*

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root **RUK**, signifying "[demon](/wiki/Demons "Demons")".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Also in the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root **ÓROK**, from which derive words signifying "[goblin](/wiki/Orcs#Orcs_and_Goblins "Orcs")":<sup id="cite_ref-LR379_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR379-5">[5]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *órku*
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *orko* (pl. *orqi*)
    - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *orch* (pl *yrch*, archaic *yrchy*<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>)
    - [Danian](/wiki/Nandorin "Nandorin"): *urc* (pl. *yrc*)
    - [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin): *urch* (pl. *urchin*)
