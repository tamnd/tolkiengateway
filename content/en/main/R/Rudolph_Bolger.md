**Rudolph Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Rudolph was the second son of [Gundolpho Bolger](/wiki/Gundolpho_Bolger) and [Alfrida of the Yale](/wiki/Alfrida_of_the_Yale). He married [Cora Goodbody](/wiki/Cora_Goodbody) and had one son, [Fastolph](/wiki/Fastolph_Bolger "Fastolph Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rudolph* contains the elements *hrod* "fame" and *wulf* "wolf".
