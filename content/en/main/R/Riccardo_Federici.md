Riccardo Federici was born in Rome, Italy in 1976. After completing art school, he enrolled in the Faculty of Archicture at the La Sapienza University while also studying Psychology. He has made anatomic illustrations, children's comics and advertising illustrations. In 2008 he had a breakthrough as a comic artist.
