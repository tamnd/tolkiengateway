**Ron Spencer** (born [30 March](../_/30_March.md) [1963](../_/1963.md)) is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

Spencer was one of the first artists working for *[Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md)*<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>, and he returned in that capacity to illustrate a single piece of alternate art for *[The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md)* expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
