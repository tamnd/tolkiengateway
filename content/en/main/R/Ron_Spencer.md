**Ron Spencer** (born [30 March](/wiki/30_March) [1963](/wiki/1963)) is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game)*.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>

Spencer was one of the first artists working for *[Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering)*<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>, and he returned in that capacity to illustrate a single piece of alternate art for *[The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit")* expansion.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
