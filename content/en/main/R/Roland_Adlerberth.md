**Roland Adlerberth** ([21 September](../_/21_September.md) [1923](../_/1923.md) - [31 July](../_/31_July.md) [1993](../_/1993.md)) was a Swedish author and translator. He translated several of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s posthumously-published works into Swedish.

## Bibliography, selected {#Bibliography,_selected}

### Translations<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> {#Translations[1]}

- [1979](../_/1979.md): *Silmarillion* (*[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*)
- [1982](../_/1982.md): *Sagor från Midgård* (*[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*)
- [1983](../_/1983.md): *Herr Salig* (*[Mr. Bliss](../M/Mr._Bliss.md)*)
- [1986](../_/1986.md): *De förlorade sagornas bok 1* (*[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One.md)*)
- [1988](../_/1988.md): *De förlorade sagornas bok 2* (*[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md)*)
