**Roland Adlerberth** ([21 September](/wiki/21_September) [1923](/wiki/1923) - [31 July](/wiki/31_July) [1993](/wiki/1993)) was a Swedish author and translator. He translated several of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s posthumously-published works into Swedish.

## Bibliography, selected {#Bibliography,_selected}

### Translations<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> {#Translations[1]}

- [1979](/wiki/1979): *Silmarillion* (*[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*)
- [1982](/wiki/1982): *Sagor från Midgård* (*[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*)
- [1983](/wiki/1983): *Herr Salig* (*[Mr. Bliss](/wiki/Mr._Bliss)*)
- [1986](/wiki/1986): *De förlorade sagornas bok 1* (*[The Book of Lost Tales Part One](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_One)*)
- [1988](/wiki/1988): *De förlorade sagornas bok 2* (*[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)*)
