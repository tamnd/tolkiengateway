**Rush** was a Canadian prog-rock band, which in its longest-standing lineup consisted of drummer and principal lyricist <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Peart" title="wikipedia:Neil Peart">Neil Peart</a>, bassist and singer <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geddy_Lee" title="wikipedia:Geddy Lee">Geddy Lee</a>, and guitarist <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Lifeson" title="wikipedia:Alex Lifeson">Alex Lifeson</a>. The trio released two songs based on Tolkien's writings.

## Rivendell {#Rivendell}

"Rivendell" is the sixth and last track on Rush's February [1975](/wiki/1975) album, <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_by_Night_(album)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Fly by Night</i></a>. A ballad, it tells of the beauties and peace afforded the weary traveller while staying in [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).

The lyrics were penned by Peart, and the music was composed by Lee, who sang and (according to conjecture) played the acoustic guitar part as well.

<p>Elfin songs and endless nights<br/>

<br/>

A place you can escape the world<br/>
Where the dark Lord cannot go<br/>

</p>

## The Necromancer {#The_Necromancer}

"The Necromancer" is the fourth track on the band's third studio album, <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caress_of_Steel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Caress of Steel</i></a>, which was released in September of [1975](/wiki/1975). The epic 12-minute track, with three distinct sections, features lyrics by Peart and music by Lee and Lifeson.

<p>Brooding in the tower<br/>
Watching over his lands<br/>
Holding ev&#39;ry creature<br/>
Helplessly they stand<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
</p>
