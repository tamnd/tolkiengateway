**Rush** was a Canadian prog-rock band, which in its longest-standing lineup consisted of drummer and principal lyricist <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Peart" title="wikipedia:Neil Peart">Neil Peart</a>, bassist and singer <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geddy_Lee" title="wikipedia:Geddy Lee">Geddy Lee</a>, and guitarist <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Lifeson" title="wikipedia:Alex Lifeson">Alex Lifeson</a>. The trio released two songs based on Tolkien's writings.

## Rivendell {#Rivendell}

"Rivendell" is the sixth and last track on Rush's February [1975](../_/1975.md) album, <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_by_Night_(album)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Fly by Night</i></a>. A ballad, it tells of the beauties and peace afforded the weary traveller while staying in [Rivendell](Rivendell.md).

The lyrics were penned by Peart, and the music was composed by Lee, who sang and (according to conjecture) played the acoustic guitar part as well.

## The Necromancer {#The_Necromancer}

"The Necromancer" is the fourth track on the band's third studio album, <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caress_of_Steel" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><i>Caress of Steel</i></a>, which was released in September of [1975](../_/1975.md). The epic 12-minute track, with three distinct sections, features lyrics by Peart and music by Lee and Lifeson.
