<i><b>Rochon Methestel</b></i> was the name of a song that was made about [Borondir Udalraph](/wiki/Borondir "Borondir"), who was sent out to seek the aid of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) by his Steward [Cirion](/wiki/Cirion). He came back with the troops of [Eorl](/wiki/Eorl), but did not survive the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant).<sup id="cite_ref-Rochon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rochon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rochon Methestel* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Rider of the Last Hope",<sup id="cite_ref-Rochon_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rochon-1">[1]</a></sup> from the elements <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rochben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rochben (page does not exist)">rochben</a></i> ("rider") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Meth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Meth (page does not exist)">meth</a></i> ("last") + *[estel](/wiki/Estel "Estel")* ("hope, trust").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
