<i><b>Rochon Methestel</b></i> was the name of a song that was made about [Borondir Udalraph](../B/Borondir.md), who was sent out to seek the aid of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) by his Steward [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md). He came back with the troops of [Eorl](../E/Eorl.md), but did not survive the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Rochon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rochon-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rochon Methestel* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Rider of the Last Hope",<sup id="cite_ref-Rochon_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rochon-1">[1]</a></sup> from the elements <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rochben&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rochben (page does not exist)">rochben</a></i> ("rider") + <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Meth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Meth (page does not exist)">meth</a></i> ("last") + *[estel](../E/Estel.md)* ("hope, trust").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
