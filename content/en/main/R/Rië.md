<i><b>rië</b></i> or (<i><b>ríe</b></i>) is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "crown, garland".<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the manuscript "Eldarin Roots and Stem", Quenya *rië* derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [RIG](/wiki/RIG).<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup> In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), Quenya *ríe* is a descendant form of [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) *rīgē*, deriving from root RIG.<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-3">[3]</a></sup>
