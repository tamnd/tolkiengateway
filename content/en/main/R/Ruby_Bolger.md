**Ruby Baggins, née Bolger**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

The daughter of [Adalgar Bolger](/wiki/Adalgar_Bolger), Ruby married [Fosco Baggins](/wiki/Fosco_Baggins).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> They had three children: [Dora](/wiki/Dora_Baggins "Dora Baggins"), [Drogo](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins "Drogo Baggins") and [Dudo](/wiki/Dudo_Baggins "Dudo Baggins").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Perhaps it's not unintentional that a Hobbit woman with a jewel-name married into the Baggins family, recently made rich by Bilbo's travels. Both Ruby's marriage and name are consistent with the Bolgers' being of a higher class.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name of the ruby derives from Latin <i><a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/rubeus" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">rubeus</a></i> meaning "red".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
