**Rosamunda Bolger, née Took**, was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Rosamunda was the daughter of [Sigismond Took](/wiki/Sigismond_Took).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> She married [Odovacar](/wiki/Odovacar_Bolger "Odovacar Bolger") of the [Bolger Family](/wiki/Bolger_Family), and had two children: [Fredegar](/wiki/Fredegar_Bolger "Fredegar Bolger") and [Estella](/wiki/Estella_Brandybuck "Estella Brandybuck"). The couple and their children attended [Bilbo's Farewell Party](/wiki/Bilbo%27s_Farewell_Party) in [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1401](/wiki/Third_Age_3001 "Third Age 3001").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In the middle ages, the name was believed to mean *rosa munda*, Latin for "pure rose", but it derived from *hros* "horse" and *mund* "hand, protection".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
