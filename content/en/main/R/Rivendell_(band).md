**Rivendell** is an epic black metal band with folk influences from Hallwang, Austria.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The solo project of Gerold Laimer (Falagar), the band was formed in 1998 under the name Fangorn and adopted its current name the same year.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It takes its name from [Rivendell](Rivendell.md), the hidden valley of [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md). Its 2000 debut *The Ancient Glory* features songs named after [Nimrodel](../N/Nimrodel.md), [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).
