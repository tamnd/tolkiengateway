**Rómendacil**, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "East-victor", can refer to two [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") kings:

- [Rómendacil I](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [310](/wiki/Third_Age_310 "Third Age 310") - [541](/wiki/Third_Age_541 "Third Age 541")), born [Tarostar](/wiki/Tarostar), the eighth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
- [Rómendacil II](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1126](/wiki/Third_Age_1126 "Third Age 1126") - [1366](/wiki/Third_Age_1366 "Third Age 1366")), born [Minalcar](/wiki/Minalcar), the nineteenth [King](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).
