**Roads Go Ever, Ever On** is a song written by [Maury Laws](../M/Maury_Laws.md) (music) and [Jules Bass](../J/Jules_Bass.md) (lyric) and performed by [Glenn Yarbrough](../G/Glenn_Yarbrough.md) for the [1980](../_/1980.md) animated movie [*The Return of the King*](<../T/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is a reprise of the song "[Roads](<Roads_(song).md>)" from the [1977](../_/1977.md) [Rankin/Bass](Rankin%252FBass.md) version of [*The Hobbit*](<../T/The_Hobbit_(1977_film).md>) with a completely new lyric; its final stanza is taken from the song "[It's So Easy Not to Try](../I/It's_So_Easy_Not_to_Try.md)," also from *The Return of the King*. The song plays at the end of the story, as [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md), [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md), among others, sail to the lands beyond the sea, bidding farewell to [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md), [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md), and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md).

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Roads go ever, ever on<br/>
To the﻿ lands beyond the sea. <br/>
On a white ship will I sail,<br/>
Watching shadows part for me.<br/>
<br/>
Leaving Havens grey with rain,<br/>
Now that years have slipped away.<br/>
Leaving friends with gentle pain<br/>
As they start another day.<br/>
 <br/>
Roads I traveled I must leave,<br/>
For I&#39;ve turned the final bend.<br/>
Weep not empty tears, but grieve<br/>
As the road comes to an end.<br/>
<br/>
It&#39;s so easy not to try; <br/>
Let the world go drifting by.<br/>
If you never say hello,<br/>
You won&#39;t have to say goodbye.<br/>
<br/>
If you never say hello,<br/>
You won&#39;t have to say goodbye!
</p>
</div>
