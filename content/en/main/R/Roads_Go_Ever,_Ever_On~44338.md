**Roads Go Ever, Ever On** is a song written by [Maury Laws](/wiki/Maury_Laws) (music) and [Jules Bass](/wiki/Jules_Bass) (lyric) and performed by [Glenn Yarbrough](/wiki/Glenn_Yarbrough) for the [1980](/wiki/1980) animated movie [*The Return of the King*](</wiki/The_Return_of_the_King_(1980_film)> "The Return of the King (1980 film)").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It is a reprise of the song "[Roads](</wiki/Roads_(song)> "Roads (song)")" from the [1977](/wiki/1977) [Rankin/Bass](/wiki/Rankin/Bass) version of [*The Hobbit*](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(1977_film)> "The Hobbit (1977 film)") with a completely new lyric; its final stanza is taken from the song "[It's So Easy Not to Try](/wiki/It%27s_So_Easy_Not_to_Try)," also from *The Return of the King*. The song plays at the end of the story, as [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins"), [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond), among others, sail to the lands beyond the sea, bidding farewell to [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck"), and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took").

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Roads go ever, ever on<br/>
To the﻿ lands beyond the sea. <br/>
On a white ship will I sail,<br/>
Watching shadows part for me.<br/>
<br/>
Leaving Havens grey with rain,<br/>
Now that years have slipped away.<br/>
Leaving friends with gentle pain<br/>
As they start another day.<br/>
 <br/>
Roads I traveled I must leave,<br/>
For I&#39;ve turned the final bend.<br/>
Weep not empty tears, but grieve<br/>
As the road comes to an end.<br/>
<br/>
It&#39;s so easy not to try; <br/>
Let the world go drifting by.<br/>
If you never say hello,<br/>
You won&#39;t have to say goodbye.<br/>
<br/>
If you never say hello,<br/>
You won&#39;t have to say goodbye!
</p>
</div>
