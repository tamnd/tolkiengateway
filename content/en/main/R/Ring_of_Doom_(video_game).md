<i><b>Ring of Doom</b></i> is a text-adventure developed by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Technology" title="wikipedia:Applied Technology">Microworld</a> and written by Chris Peters in the programming language <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BASIC" title="wikipedia:BASIC">BASIC</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup> It was released (possibly untranslated) by [Bergsla](/wiki/Bergsla) in Sweden as <i><b>Ödesringen</b></i> (Swedish for *Ring of Doom*).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Gameplay {#Gameplay}

Ödesringen is set in [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor), and is based on the journey of [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"). The player has to cross [Moria](/wiki/Moria) and [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) and throw [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") into the fires of [Mount Doom](/wiki/Mount_Doom).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-2">[2]</a></sup>
