**Rory Michael Kinnear** is an English actor and playwright. He will portray [Tom Bombadil](/wiki/Tom_Bombadil) in the second season of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* series by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

Kinnear had yet to read *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* before getting the part, but after sharing news of the offer with his partner, she excitedly exclaimed that Bombadil was her favourite character in the books.<sup id="cite_ref-VF_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VF-1">[1]</a></sup>
