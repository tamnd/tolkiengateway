**Ropers** were those whose occupation was to create rope. While all races undoubtedly had rope makers, in [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) used *[hithlain](../H/Hithlain.md)* to make a superior rope that was grey and silky to the touch.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md), some ancestors of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) took up the occupation of rope making and even used the word "Roper" as a surname:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

1. [Hob Gammidge](../H/Hob_Gammidge.md) acquired the epithet of "the Roper".
2. [Hobson](../H/Hobson.md) was known by the nickname of "Roper Gamgee".
3. [Andwise Roper](../A/Andwise_Roper.md), following in the footsteps of his grandfather and father, established "Roper" as his last name.
4. [Anson Roper](../A/Anson_Roper.md) continued the use of his father's last name.
