**Ropers** were those whose occupation was to create rope. While all races undoubtedly had rope makers, in [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) used *[hithlain](/wiki/Hithlain "Hithlain")* to make a superior rope that was grey and silky to the touch.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"), some ancestors of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) took up the occupation of rope making and even used the word "Roper" as a surname:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

1. [Hob Gammidge](/wiki/Hob_Gammidge) acquired the epithet of "the Roper".
2. [Hobson](/wiki/Hobson) was known by the nickname of "Roper Gamgee".
3. [Andwise Roper](/wiki/Andwise_Roper), following in the footsteps of his grandfather and father, established "Roper" as his last name.
4. [Anson Roper](/wiki/Anson_Roper) continued the use of his father's last name.
