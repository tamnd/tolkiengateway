**Rían** was, according to "[The Heirs of Elendil](/wiki/The_Heirs_of_Elendil)", the daughter of the Steward [Barahir](</wiki/Barahir_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Barahir (Steward of Gondor)") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor). After her brother [Dior](</wiki/Dior_(Steward_of_Gondor)> "Dior (Steward of Gondor)") died childless, her son [Denethor I](/wiki/Denethor_I) became the tenth [Ruling Steward](/wiki/Ruling_Steward).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In "[The Tale of Years of the Third Age](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Years_of_the_Third_Age)" Denethor I is told to be Dior's son.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Rían was probably named after the daughter of [Belegund](/wiki/Belegund) of the [House of Bëor](/wiki/House_of_B%C3%ABor) of the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).
