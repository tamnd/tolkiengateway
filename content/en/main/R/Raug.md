<i><b>raug</b></i> or <i><b>rog</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning '[demon](/wiki/Demons "Demons")'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Compare the name of [Rog](/wiki/Rog), a [Gnome](/wiki/Gnome)-lord in Gondolin according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.
