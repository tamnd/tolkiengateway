**Ron Walotsky** ([21 August](../_/21_August.md) [1943](../_/1943.md) – [29 July](../_/29_July.md) [2002](../_/2002.md)) was an American science fiction and fantasy artist. After graduating from the School of Visual Arts in New York in 1966, he quickly found work in various fields ranging from magazine illustrations to album cover art. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create card art for the *[Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md)*.

He mostly worked with acrylics on illustration board.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
