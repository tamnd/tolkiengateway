**Retarded Creatures and Caverns** is a parody of [the Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Lord of the Rings") written by [John Wilson](/wiki/John_Wilson)<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>. It the sequel of [An Everyday Tale of a Seeker of Gold](/wiki/An_Everyday_Tale_of_a_Seeker_of_Gold).<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup> It was not part of [the Balrogian Trilogy](/wiki/The_Balrogian_Trilogy "The Balrogian Trilogy"), but was included in the collections *Best of the Indies Vol. 1*, *Balrog's Big Four,* and *Numero Dos*.

## Story {#Story}

*Bulbo'*s old friend *Algernon*, also called *Stroller*, embark on a quest to investigate a castle called *Toidi* and slay the dragon that dwells within it.<sup id="cite_ref-Tolkien_Games_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tolkien_Games-1">[1]</a></sup>
