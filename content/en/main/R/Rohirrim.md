The **Rohirrim**, or the **Horse-lords**, were a horse people, settling in the land of [Rohan](Rohan.md), named after them. The name Rohirrim was mostly used by outsiders: the names that they used for themselves were **[Eorlingas](../E/Eorlingas.md)**, after their king [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) who had first brought them to Rohan, **Riders of Rohan** and **Riders of the Mark**. It is possible that the term **King's Riders** referred to a mounted bodyguard of the King.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Indeed it is said by our lore-masters that they have from of old this affinity with us that they are come from those same Three Houses of Men as were the Númenoreans in their beginning; not from Hador the Goldenhaired, the Elf-friend, maybe, yet from such of his sons and people as went not over Sea into the West, refusing the call.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../F/Faramir.md" title="Faramir">Faramir</a></cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

In reality, the ancestors of the Rohirrim were the **[Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md)**, a tribe of the [Northmen](../N/Northmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup> Those were [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md), related to the [Edain](../E/Edain.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md), who did not go to [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), like the other Edain, who were later rewarded with the island of [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) by the [Valar](../V/Valar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-atani_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-atani-3">[3]</a></sup>

The Gondorians described them as *Middle Men*, that being inferior to the [High Men](../N/Númenóreans.md) in both culture and descent, but superior to the *[Men of Darkness](../M/Men_of_Darkness.md)* who had worshipped and served [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md).

According to some more *simplified* views in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), the Rohirrim were perhaps directly descended from the [House of Hador](../H/House_of_Hador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> This belief was accepted by the Rohirrim themselves and strengthened their friendship with Gondor, and it comforted the Númenorean pride of the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md), who surrendered a large part of their kingdom to them.<sup id="cite_ref-atani_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-atani-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> As such, The [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) believed that the Rohirrim were distantly related to them (having descended from the [Atanatári](../A/Atanatári.md) of the [First Age](../F/First_Age.md)).

Long allies of Gondor, the Éothéod were living in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> but removed to [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md) which was granted them in perpetuity by the [Ruling Steward](../S/Stewards_of_Gondor.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) in reward for the assistance that they offered Gondor at a time of great need, the [Battle of the Field of Celebrant](../B/Battle_of_the_Field_of_Celebrant.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl-2">[2]</a></sup>

Later Calenardhon was renamed [Rohan](Rohan.md) (*Horse-land*) by the [Steward](Ruling_Steward.md) [Hallas](../H/Hallas.md) after their many horses. By the Rohirrim themselves, Rohan was usually called **[The Mark](../T/The_Mark.md)**.

For the subsequent history of the Rohirrim as allies of Gondor see [Rohan#History](Rohan.md#History)

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/J/John_Howe_-_Rohirrim.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/b/b4/John_Howe_-_Rohirrim.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="197" height="402" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="197" data-file-height="402"/></a><figcaption><i>Rohirrim</i> by <a href="../J/John_Howe.md" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

The Rohirrim were tall, blond, and mostly had blue eyes. They prized their horses more than anything, and their entire culture was based around these. They had few cities, but lived in many villages on the plains of Rohan.

<dl><dd><i>They are proud and wilful, but they are true-hearted, generous in thought and deed; bold but not cruel; wise but unlearned, writing no books but singing many songs, after the manner of the children of Men before the Dark Years. </i> — <a href="../T/The_Two_Towers.md" title="The Two Towers">The Two Towers</a></dd></dl>

## Culture {#Culture}

The Rohirrim had had contacts with [Elves](../E/Elves.md) in their ancient history and knew of [Eru](../I/Ilúvatar.md), but like the Dúnedain they did not worship him in any temples. They seem to have valued the [Vala](../V/Valar.md) [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md) the Hunter highly, whom they called [Béma](../O/Oromë.md#History).

They measured time with a calendar that came from the [Men of the Vales of Anduin](../M/Men_of_the_Vales_of_Anduin.md) and had apparent similarities to the [Calendars of the Shire](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) and [Bree](../B/Bree_Calendar.md), and to that of the [Dale](../D/Dale.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> These similarities were noted by [Meriadoc Brandybuck](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) in his work *[Reckoning of Years](Reckoning_of_Years.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

They were ruled by a line of kings descended from Eorl the Young, who had first brought them to Rohan, and in times of war, all able men rode to meet the Muster of Rohan.

### [Horses](../H/Horses.md) {#Horses}

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/P/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg/300px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="414" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/a/a9/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg/450px-Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/a/a9/Peter_Xavier_Price_-_The_Ride_of_the_Rohirrim.jpg 2x" data-file-width="507" data-file-height="700"/></a><figcaption><i>The Ride of the Rohirrim</i> by <a href="../P/Peter_Xavier_Price.md" title="Peter Xavier Price">Peter Xavier Price</a></figcaption></figure>

The Rohirrim are famous as skilled horsemen, masters and breeders. Among the horses of the Rohirrim are the famed [Mearas](../M/Mearas.md), the noblest and fastest horses who have ever roamed [Arda](../A/Arda.md); [Shadowfax](../S/Shadowfax.md) was the greatest of all Mearas. There were very few Mearas left in Middle-earth at this point, but there were enough that a breeding population was present. The armies of Rohan are almost exclusively cavalry, divided into irregular units termed [éoreds](../_/Éoreds.md). Rohan's armies were more of a very well trained militia called upon in times of war, with the actual standing army relatively small. The professional career-soldiers of Rohan may have been limited to the royal bodyguard at Edoras.

It was because of this close affiliation with horses, both in war and peace, that they received their now famous name. *Rohirrim* (or more properly *Rochirrim*) is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Horse-lords," and *Rohan* (or *Rochand*) meant "Land of the Horse-lords." These names were devised by Hallas, son of Cirion the Steward.

During the early days of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), rumours were spread that the Rohirrim supplied Sauron's armies with horses. These rumours were obviously false: the Rohirrim valued their horses more than anything and would never send them away, even as tribute. Still, these rumours had some effect in that they obscured the fact it was Saruman who had fallen, rather than Rohan. The basis of the rumour was that Sauron's [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) on raids into Rohan stole their horses for use in Mordor's army, but this was outright theft that angered the Rohirrim against Sauron.

### Language {#Language}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="Rohanese.md" title="Rohanese">Rohanese</a></b></div></div>

The Rohirrim spoke a language descended from that of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md), related to the [Northern Mannish](../N/Northern_Mannish.md) of [Rhovanion](Rhovanion.md). In comparison to [Westron](../W/Westron.md), it was mostly archaic and was not easily understood by the speakers of Westron.

The lords of the Rohirrim used [Westron](../W/Westron.md) freely, in the gracious, noble and antique manner of the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

The Rohirrim wrote with a [Mannish](../M/Mannish.md) version of the [cirth](../C/Cirth.md), although they did not have a literature, preferring oral tradition.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rohirrim* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl306_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl306-11">[11]</a></sup> name meaning "Horse-lords"<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> or "the host of the Horse-lords"<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>. It consists of the elements *[roch](../H/Horses.md#Names)* ("horse"), *[hîr](../H/Hîr.md)* ("lord", "master") and the class plural suffix *[rĩm](Rim.md)* ("host", "great number").<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> The name *Rohirrim* was first given to them by [Hallas](../H/Hallas.md), the son and successor of [Steward](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md) [Cirion](../C/Cirion.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) and was used in Gondor and often also by the Rohirrim themselves.<sup id="cite_ref-Eorl306_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eorl306-11">[11]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

The names that the Rohirrim used for themselves were **[Eorlingas](../E/Eorlingas.md)**, after their king [Eorl the Young](../E/Eorl_the_Young.md) who had first brought them to Rohan, **Riders of Rohan**<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> and **Riders of the Mark**<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[19]</a></sup>.
