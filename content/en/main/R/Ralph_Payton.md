**Ralph Stuart "the Baby" Payton** ([1894](/wiki/1894) - [22 July](/wiki/22_July) [1916](/wiki/1916)) was a friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and a member of the [T.C.B.S.](/wiki/T.C.B.S.), along with his older brother [Wilfrid](/wiki/Wilfrid_Payton "Wilfrid Payton"). A few years minor of the core members, Payton (and [Sidney Barrowclough](/wiki/Sidney_Barrowclough)) continued the T.C.B.S. after the "Great Four" left [King Edward's School](/wiki/King_Edward%27s_School).

Payton was born in 1894 in France and like most of the Barrovians, went to the [University of Cambridge](/wiki/Cambridge "Cambridge"). He did not stay long; convinced by the military lectures, he joined the 1st Birmingham Batallion, the same unit as [Tea Cake Barnsley](/wiki/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley "Thomas Kenneth Barnsley"), in late [1914](/wiki/1914). Payton fought in the [Battle of the Somme](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Somme), and were engaged south-east of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovillers-la-Boisselle" title="wikipedia:Ovillers-la-Boisselle">La Boiselle</a>. On [22 July](/wiki/22_July) [1916](/wiki/1916), he was killed in action.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> His body was never identified, and his name is inscribed on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiepval_Memorial" title="wikipedia:Thiepval Memorial">Thiepval Memorial</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilbert_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilbert-2">[2]</a></sup>
