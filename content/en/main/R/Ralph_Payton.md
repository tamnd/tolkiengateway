**Ralph Stuart "the Baby" Payton** ([1894](../_/1894.md) - [22 July](../_/22_July.md) [1916](../_/1916.md)) was a friend of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and a member of the [T.C.B.S.](../T/T.C.B.S..md), along with his older brother [Wilfrid](../W/Wilfrid_Payton.md). A few years minor of the core members, Payton (and [Sidney Barrowclough](../S/Sidney_Barrowclough.md)) continued the T.C.B.S. after the "Great Four" left [King Edward's School](../K/King_Edward's_School.md).

Payton was born in 1894 in France and like most of the Barrovians, went to the [University of Cambridge](../C/Cambridge.md). He did not stay long; convinced by the military lectures, he joined the 1st Birmingham Batallion, the same unit as [Tea Cake Barnsley](../T/Thomas_Kenneth_Barnsley.md), in late [1914](../_/1914.md). Payton fought in the [Battle of the Somme](../B/Battle_of_the_Somme.md), and were engaged south-east of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovillers-la-Boisselle" title="wikipedia:Ovillers-la-Boisselle">La Boiselle</a>. On [22 July](../_/22_July.md) [1916](../_/1916.md), he was killed in action.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> His body was never identified, and his name is inscribed on the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiepval_Memorial" title="wikipedia:Thiepval Memorial">Thiepval Memorial</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Gilbert_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gilbert-2">[2]</a></sup>
