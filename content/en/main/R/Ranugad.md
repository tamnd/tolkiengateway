<i><b>Ranugad</b></i> is a [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) name meaning "Stay-at-home" ([Hamfast](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md)).

It is often abbreviated to <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Ran&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ran (page does not exist)">Ran</a></i>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element is obviously related to <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=R%C3%A2n&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rân (page does not exist)">rân</a></i> and means "home". The second element *-gad* means "stay".
