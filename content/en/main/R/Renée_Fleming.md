**Renée Fleming** (born [February 14](../F/February_14.md), [1959](../_/1959.md)) is an American soprano opera singer.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

She has performed vocals for a number of movies, notably [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md). She carried out vocals on four tracks of the soundtrack *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings.md)*: "[The Grace of Undómiel](../T/The_Grace_of_Undómiel.md)", "[Mount Doom](<../M/Mount_Doom_(soundtrack).md>)", "[The Eagles](../T/The_Eagles.md)", and "[The Fellowship Reunited](../T/The_Fellowship_Reunited.md)".
