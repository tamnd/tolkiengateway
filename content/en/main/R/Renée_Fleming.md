**Renée Fleming** (born [February 14](/wiki/February_14), [1959](/wiki/1959)) is an American soprano opera singer.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

She has performed vocals for a number of movies, notably [The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King). She carried out vocals on four tracks of the soundtrack *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King - The Complete Recordings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King_-_The_Complete_Recordings)*: "[The Grace of Undómiel](/wiki/The_Grace_of_Und%C3%B3miel)", "[Mount Doom](</wiki/Mount_Doom_(soundtrack)> "Mount Doom (soundtrack)")", "[The Eagles](/wiki/The_Eagles)", and "[The Fellowship Reunited](/wiki/The_Fellowship_Reunited)".
