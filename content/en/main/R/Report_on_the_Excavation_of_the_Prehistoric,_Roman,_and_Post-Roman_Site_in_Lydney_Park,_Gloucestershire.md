<i><b>Report on the Excavation of the Prehistoric, Roman, and Post-Roman Site in Lydney Park, Gloucestershire</b></i> was published in July [1932](/wiki/1932). [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) contributed with an essay, "[The Name 'Nodens'](/wiki/The_Name_%27Nodens%27)", included as Appendix I (pp. 132–7). The book was printed in 1,150 copies.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
