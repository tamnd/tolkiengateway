**Radio Times 4 December 1953** is an issue (Vol. 121, No. 1569) of *Radio Times*, published on December 4, 1953. It includes the article "[A Fourteenth-Century Romance](../A/A_Fourteenth-Century_Romance.md)" (p. 9) by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), written in connection with the performance of Tolkien's translation of *[Sir Gawain and the Green Knight](<../S/Sir_Gawain_and_the_Green_Knight_(edition).md>)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

As regional editions of *Radio Times* were issued in different parts of the country to reflect local programming, Tolkien’s essay appears only in the non-regional or London edition of the issue.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
