**Robin Smallburrow** was a [Shirriff](/wiki/Shirriff) from [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton).

## History {#History}

Born and raised in [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton), Robin was of old a friend of the [Gamgees](/wiki/Gamgee_Family "Gamgee Family"). He loved being outdoors, and became a [Shirriff](/wiki/Shirriff) so he could meet new people (and, equally important, inns).<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup>

Originally one of only twelve,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> during [Lotho Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lotho_Sackville-Baggins)' reign, Robin found himself in over his head. He was reassigned to the [First Eastfarthing Troop](/wiki/First_Eastfarthing_Troop) in [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton), tasked with enforcing [The Rules](/wiki/The_Rules).

Robin was one of the Shirriffs sent to arrest [four Hobbits](/wiki/Travellers "Travellers") who had illegally entered the Shire at the [Brandywine Bridge](/wiki/Brandywine_Bridge). It was more of an escort than an arrest, as Robin and his troops were on foot and the four Hobbits had ponies. At the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone), the Shirriffs gave up and let their prisoners go on without them.<sup id="cite_ref-Scouring_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Scouring-1">[1]</a></sup> It's not stated if he was among the Sheriffs who joined in the revolt.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name "Smallburrow" may imply Robin came from a poor family, as only the rich lived in [smials](/wiki/Smials "Smials"). His name bears some resemblance to the mythical <a class="external text" href="http://www.boldoutlaw.com/puckrobin/puckbals.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Robin Goodfellow</a>.

<b>1995: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game" title="Middle-earth Collectible Card Game">Middle-earth Collectible Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A &#34;Robin Smallburrow&#34; card, illustrated by <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Montanini" title="Angelo Montanini">Angelo Montanini</a>, was produced as part of <a href="/wiki/1995" title="1995">1995</a>&#39;s <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth:_The_Wizards" title="Middle-earth: The Wizards">Middle-earth: The Wizards</a></i> expansion.</dd></dl>

<b>2002: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(video_game)" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (video game)"><i>The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</i> (video game)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>Robin Smallburrow can be found at the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Green_Dragon" title="Green Dragon">Green Dragon</a>, voiced by an uncredited <a href="/wiki/James_Arnold_Taylor" title="James Arnold Taylor">James Arnold Taylor</a>. <a href="/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins" title="Lobelia Sackville-Baggins">Lobelia Sackville-Baggins</a> orders <a href="/wiki/Frodo_Baggins" title="Frodo Baggins">Frodo Baggins</a> to ring the town bell to warn Robin that wolves are in the Shire, which Robin will dismiss as being a rumour. Later at night, he is attacked by a wolf on the streets of <a href="/wiki/Bywater" title="Bywater">Bywater</a>. Frodo can either let the wolf kill him, or save him to increase his Purity meter.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game">The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Despite not featuring in <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> film series</a>, Decipher produced a card depicting the character. He was played by <a href="/wiki/Timothy_Bartlett" title="Timothy Bartlett">Timothy Bartlett</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Shirriff Robin Smallburrow is found at <i><a href="/wiki/The_Ivy_Bush" title="The Ivy Bush">The Ivy Bush</a></i> tavern in <a href="/wiki/Hobbiton" title="Hobbiton">Hobbiton</a>. He helps the player to deal with a problem in <a href="/wiki/Overhill" title="Overhill">Overhill</a> and is also involved in several seasonal festivals.</dd></dl>
