<br> **Ralph Bakshi** ([October 29](../_/29_October.md), [1938](../_/1938.md)) is an American animator and filmmaker. He directed [*The Lord of the Rings* animated film](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film).md>) in [1978](../_/1978.md). Despite the fact that the movie was a financial success, [United Artists](../U/United_Artists.md) did not let him finish the story.

## Quotes {#Quotes}

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>I never would have started it. I was doing it to save it for Tolkien, because I loved the Rings very much. I had just finished Wizards. Wizards was my homage to Tolkien in the American idiom. I had read Tolkien, understood Tolkien, and wanted to do a sort of fantasy for American kids, and that was Wizards and I love it very much. Of course it&#39;s American as opposed to Tolkien. But when I heard that United Artists was going to collapse the three books into one picture and there was a screenplay written by (John) Boorman, I thought it best that I go down and tell them they&#39;re out of their minds. 
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Ralph Bakshi on why he did it, <a class="external text" href="http://movies.ign.com/articles/518/518805p1.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">IGN interview</a>.</cite></p>
</blockquote><div class="tg-section-divider"></div></div>

Film has to describe and show. With the brilliance of his words and his scenes, you imagine whatever you want. I&#39;m sure various people imagine different things.
</p>
