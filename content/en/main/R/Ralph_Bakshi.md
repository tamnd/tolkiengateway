<br> **Ralph Bakshi** ([October 29](/wiki/29_October "29 October"), [1938](/wiki/1938)) is an American animator and filmmaker. He directed [*The Lord of the Rings* animated film](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)") in [1978](/wiki/1978). Despite the fact that the movie was a financial success, [United Artists](/wiki/United_Artists) did not let him finish the story.
