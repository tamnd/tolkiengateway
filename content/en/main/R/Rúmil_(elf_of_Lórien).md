**Rúmil** was a [Silvan Elf](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien).

## History {#History}

Rúmil was the brother of [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir) and [Orophin](/wiki/Orophin). He was one of three wardens met by the [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") as they entered the [Golden Wood](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien").<sup id="cite_ref-lorien_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lorien-1">[1]</a></sup>

That night, after a strong company of [Orcs](/wiki/Orc "Orc") entered Lothlórien, in pursuit of the Fellowship, the brothers led the Orcs into the woods with feigned voices in preparation for an ambush by their folk. The following day, Rúmil and Haldir led the Fellowship onward and made a rope bridge to cross the [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant). Rúmil remained behind after the others crossed, returning to [Nimrodel](</wiki/Nimrodel_(river)> "Nimrodel (river)") to keep watch.<sup id="cite_ref-lorien_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lorien-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Rúmil" was a "learned" [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)-name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:54</sup> As the name was identical in form to Quenya name of the [learned sage of Valinor](</wiki/R%C3%BAmil_(elf_of_Tirion)> "Rúmil (elf of Tirion)"), and unknown to the Silvan or [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar) Elves, [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) decided it must have been given by [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) herself, but he questioned why such a name would be given to "one of the plain guards and soldiers" of the kingdom.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:51</sup>
