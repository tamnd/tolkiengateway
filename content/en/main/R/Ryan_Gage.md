**Ryan Gage** is an English actor who portrayed [Alfrid Lickspittle](/wiki/Alfrid "Alfrid") in [*The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug*](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Desolation_of_Smaug "The Hobbit: The Desolation of Smaug") and [*The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armie*s](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies "The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> He was originally cast as [Drogo Baggins](/wiki/Drogo_Baggins), father of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), but his role was later changed.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was born in [Coventry](/wiki/Coventry) and went on to attend the BRIT School and later trained at Drama Centre London.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Awards {#Awards}

- [2014](/wiki/2014) - <b><a class="external text" href="http://www.caprihollywood.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Capri Hollywood Award</a></b>, Breakout Actor Award, *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*
- 2014 - **Capri Film Festival, Italy**, Breakout Actor Award, *[The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies)*
