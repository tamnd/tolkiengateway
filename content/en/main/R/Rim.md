<i><b>rim</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word meaning "host",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> or "a people of one kind or origin".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <i><b>rim(b)</b></i> is glossed as "great number".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The word often appears in compound names as the suffix <i><b>-rim</b></i>, denoting "a group".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
