**Rhîw** is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name for winter, and is called [Hrívë](/wiki/Hr%C3%ADv%C3%AB) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya). In the [Reckoning of Rivendell](/wiki/Reckoning_of_Rivendell) it was fifth of the six "seasons", and corresponded more or less to the 72 days between [1 December](/wiki/1_December) and [10 February](/wiki/10_February) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
