**Razanur Tûk** was the actual [Westron](/wiki/Westron) form of the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) name anglicised by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") into "[Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth)*, Tolkien states that *Razanur* was the name of a legendary traveller and comes from *raza* "stranger", and *razan* "foreign".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
