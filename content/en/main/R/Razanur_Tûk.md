**Razanur Tûk** was the actual [Westron](../W/Westron.md) form of the [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) name anglicised by [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) into "[Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

In *[The Peoples of Middle-earth](../T/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth.md)*, Tolkien states that *Razanur* was the name of a legendary traveller and comes from *raza* "stranger", and *razan* "foreign".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
