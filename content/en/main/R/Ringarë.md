**Ringarë** was the last month of the year according to the [Kings'](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning "Kings' Reckoning") and [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), and the ninth month according to the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning). Ringarë in the Kings' and Stewards' Reckoning corresponded to [Foreyule](/wiki/Foreyule) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [21 November](/wiki/21_November) through [20 December](/wiki/20_December)). The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name was [Girithron](/wiki/Girithron).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word obviously contains the word *ringa* "cold". [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo) notes that the ending might be also seen in the abstract nouns *almare* "blessedness" and *fanyare* "the skies". Therefore the meaning of *Ringare* is equivalent to "\*coldness".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
