<i><b>Runes of Power</b></i> is the sixth track of the album [Let Mortal Heroes Sing Your Fame](/wiki/Let_Mortal_Heroes_Sing_Your_Fame) by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is taken from the [Song of Durin](/wiki/The_world_was_young,_the_mountains_green, "The world was young, the mountains green,")<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the lyrics are taken from the poem [The wind was on the withered heath](/wiki/The_wind_was_on_the_withered_heath).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Lyrics {#Lyrics}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>The wind came down from mountain could<br/>
And like a tide it roared and rolled<br/>
The branches groaned, the forest moaned<br/>
And leaves were laid upon the mould<br/>
<br/>
The wind went on from West to east<br/>
All movement in the forest ceased<br/>
But shrill an harsh across the marsh<br/>
Its whistling voices were released
</p>
