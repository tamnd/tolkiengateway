**Rudigar Bolger** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

The son of [Adalgar Bolger](/wiki/Adalgar_Bolger) and husband of [Belba Baggins](/wiki/Belba_Baggins), Rudigar became head of the [Bolger Family](/wiki/Bolger_Family) as the eldest heir. He had one son, [Herugar](/wiki/Herugar_Bolger "Herugar Bolger").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

J.R.R. Tolkien noted that it was a custom in the Bolger family to use high-sounding names and that he turned them into old names of Frankish and Gothic origin.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The name *Rudigar* contains *hrod* "fame" and *gar* "spear".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is the Middle High German form of Old English <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hrothgar" title="wikipedia:Hrothgar">Hrothgar</a></i> and modern *Roger*. It is cognate with the modern german name <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%BCdiger" title="wikipedia:Rüdiger">Rüdiger</a></i>.
