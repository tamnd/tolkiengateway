<i><b>Ringgeister</b></i> (“Ringwraiths”), or in full *Die abenteuerliche Reise der Hobbits ins Reich der Ringgeister*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>, is a 1992 German board game loosely based on the events in [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
