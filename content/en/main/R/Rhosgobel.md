**Rhosgobel** was the old home of the wizard [Radagast](/wiki/Radagast).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Location {#Location}

Rhosgobel was located near the borders of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood)<sup id="cite_ref-Council_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Council-2">[2]</a></sup> near the southern end of Mirkwood,<sup id="cite_ref-Queer_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Queer-3">[3]</a></sup> probably on its western side south of the [Gladden Fields](/wiki/Gladden_Fields) and opposite of the [East Bight](/wiki/East_Bight) of the forest,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> because scouts sent by [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) used the pass at the sources of the [Gladden River](/wiki/Gladden_River), went down into [Wilderland](/wiki/Wilderland) and over the Gladden Fields to reach Rhosgobel and returned over the high pass that was called the [Redhorn Gate](/wiki/Redhorn_Gate) to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell).<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

At some point in time after his arrival in Middle-earth around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1000](/wiki/Third_Age_1000 "Third Age 1000")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the wizard Radagast made his home at Rhosgobel.

According to the wizard [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) Radagast lived at Rhosgobel<sup id="cite_ref-Queer_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Queer-3">[3]</a></sup> in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2941](/wiki/Third_Age_2941 "Third Age 2941").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

After the [Council of Elrond](/wiki/Council_of_Elrond), some scouts from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) crossed over the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) using the pass at the sources of the Gladden River to come down into Wilderland and over the Gladden Fields to reach Rhosgobel, but they found that Radagast was not there.<sup id="cite_ref-South_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-South-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rhosgobel* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name,<sup id="cite_ref-Fauskanger_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fauskanger-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-9">[9]</a></sup> which means "russet village or 'town' (enclosure)".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> [Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger), [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull) suggest that it is a compound of *[rhosc](/wiki/Rhosc "Rhosc")* ("russet", "brown")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> and *[gobel](/wiki/Gobel "Gobel")* ("walled house" or "village", "town").<sup id="cite_ref-Fauskanger_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fauskanger-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Eldamo_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldamo-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> The name of the house was perhaps a reference to its resident, the Brown Wizard.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In a very late note on the names of the Istari Rhosgobel was said to have been in the forest borders between the [Carrock](/wiki/Carrock) and the [Old Forest Road](/wiki/Old_Forest_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>A layout, and a detailed description, is given of Rhosgobel. It is built around a large furry oak tree.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Card_Game" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game">The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rhosgobel is depicted as a u-shaped wooden house nestled in the woods surrounding a small blue pool of water or pond.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rhosgobel appears in a single scene, when it is attacked by <a href="/wiki/Spiders" title="Spiders">spiders</a>. Radagast later discovers that these spiders came from <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a>.</dd></dl>

<b>2014: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rhosgobel appears in a single scene, when <a href="/wiki/Radagast" title="Radagast">Radagast</a> takes <a href="/wiki/Gandalf" title="Gandalf">Gandalf</a> from <a href="/wiki/Dol_Guldur" title="Dol Guldur">Dol Guldur</a> to Rhosgobel after alerting the <a href="/wiki/White_Council" title="White Council">White Council</a> to his presence there.</dd></dl>

<b>2019: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Rhosgobel, located in the <a href="/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin" title="Vales of Anduin">Vales of Anduin</a> region, is a large set of ruins located in the region of &#34;Laerlad&#34;, once occupied by the <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Wood-elves" title="Wood-elves">Wood-elves</a>, but abandoned long ago. The cottage of <a href="/wiki/Radagast" title="Radagast">Radagast</a> is located inside the ruins, which, after briefly being overrun by the &#34;Ungoladan&#34; tribe in the aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, was reclaimed by Radagast, who restored it as a refuge and sanctuary for the animals of Middle-earth.</dd></dl>
