The **Rammas Echor** was the great wall that surrounded the city of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and the [Pelennor Fields](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md), its farmlands and pastures.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The *Rammas Echor* was ten leagues (thirty miles<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>) or more in length.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> It ran from the northeastern feet of the [Mindolluin](../M/Mindolluin.md) mountain about ten miles northwest of Minas Tirith in approximately the shape of three quarters of a circle back to the southeastern feet of the mountain about more than three miles southwest of Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> Since Minas Tirith was close to the southwestern end of the wall,<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> its furthest point was four leagues (twelve miles) from the [Great Gate](../G/Great_Gate_of_Minas_Tirith.md) of the city<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> where the [Causeway Forts](../C/Causeway_Forts.md) were located<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> and its nearest point was a bit more than one league (three miles) Minas Tirith where the [Harlond](<../H/Harlond_(Gondor).md>), the city's harbour on the river [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) was located.<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> There were three main gates in the wall, [one](../F/Forannest.md) in its northwest where the [North-way](../N/North-way.md) led to the [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md), one flanked by the Causeway Forts in its northeast where the [Causeway](<../C/Causeway_(Gondor).md>) ran to the city of [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) at the Anduin and one in its southwest where the [South Road](../S/South_Road.md) ran to the city of [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-3">[3]</a></sup> It is possible that a road from the Great Gate of Minas Tirith passed through a gate in the south-eastern part of the wall to reach the Harlond<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> because many roads crossed the green fields of the Pelennor Fields.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The *Rammas Echor* was built with a great amount of labour after the province of [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) fell under the shadow of the Enemy<sup id="cite_ref-MT_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MT-1">[1]</a></sup> during the rule of [Steward](../S/Steward_of_Gondor.md) <a class="mw-disambig" href="../D/Denethor_(disambiguation).md" title="Denethor (disambiguation)">Denethor</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup> but it is not specified if it was during the rule of Steward [Denethor I](../D/Denethor_I.md) or Steward [Denethor II](../D/Denethor.md).

It is possible that it was built during the lordship of Denethor I to protect the Pelennor Fields. [Uruks](../U/Uruks.md) from [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) appeared during the last years of his rule and attacked Ithilien and conquered Osgiliath in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2475](../T/Third_Age_2475.md) so that the city was finally ruined and abandoned by its population, and its great stone-bridge over the river Anduin was broken. The borders of Gondor were under constant threat from his rule onwards.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

It is also possible<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> that it was built during the rule of Denethor II. This is less likely, as it already seemed to be partly ruinous and was under repair<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> on [9 March](../_/9_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup> only 35 years after Denethor II became Steward of Gondor in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2984](../T/Third_Age_2984.md).<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Additionally, Minas Tirith was falling more and more into decay and had only half of its original population in that year,<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> which indicates a shortage of a labour force for the great labour that would be required to construct a thirty-mile long wall. Finally, Gondor had conquered back Osgiliath in the days of the youth of Denethor II to hold it as an outpost and to rebuild the bridge so that its troops could cross over the river,<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup> which would have been a risk for the Pelennor Fields if they had not been protected against raids by a wall at that time. [Ghân-buri-ghân](../G/Ghân-buri-ghân~28687.md), the chieftain of the [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md) of [Drúadan Forest](../D/Drúadan_Forest.md), referred to the Rammas Echor as the "Stone-folk's new walls",<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-14">[14]</a></sup> but it is not clear what age he considered "new" to mean and how far back the memory of his people reached.

Early in the morning on [11 March](../_/11_March.md) [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup> Steward Denethor II at a meeting of his [Council](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md) decided that the Rammas Echor and Osgiliath should be manned and defended against the enemy. His younger son [Faramir](../F/Faramir.md) volunteered to command the forces that defended Osgiliath and the Rammas Echor, knowing that they could cause the enemy to loose ten times their own losses during the attempt to cross the river Anduin, but that the long retreat of his forces from far out there would be dangerous if the enemy managed to cross the river.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-15">[15]</a></sup>

On [12 March](../_/12_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup> the attacking Morgul-host that was led by the Witch-king and outnumbered the defenders ten times managed to cross the Anduin on a large number of floats and barges that they had secretly built on the eastern side of Osgiliath so that Faramir retreated and rallied his men to the Causeway Forts.<sup id="cite_ref-Siege_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Siege-15">[15]</a></sup>

In the early hours of [13 March](../_/13_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup> the forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) blasted breaches in the Rammas Echor in many locations and wrecked the Causeway Forts. The defenders had to retreat over the Pelennor Fields to the Great Gate of Minas Tirith with Faramir leading the rearguard. When cavarly from [Harad](../H/Harad.md) overtook the retreating men and caused them to rout, the entire remaining cavalry of Minas Tirith charged from the city, attacked the enemy forces and escorted the retreating men including Faramir, who had been shot with a dart during a fight with a mounted champion of Harad, back to the city. Only two thirds of the defenders including Faramir made it back to Minas Tirith.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[16]</a></sup>

In the evening of [14 March](../_/14_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup> the riders of the [Rohirrim](Rohirrim.md) led by their [King](../K/King_of_Rohan.md) [Théoden](../T/Théoden.md) and guided by the Drúedain of Drúadan Forest reached the [Grey Wood](../G/Grey_Wood.md). Ghân-buri-ghân, the chieftain of the Drúedain, informed them that his scouts had seen no enemies between the Grey Wood and the Rammas Eachor and had seen that [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) had made breaches in the Rammas Echor with explosives and that many Orcs were busy on the walls, but that they did not pay attention to their surroundings, because they seemed to think that the forces of Sauron watched all the approaching roads. Théoden's nephew [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) was happy to hear those reports, because he had feared that the enemy could have defended the Rammas Echor for a long time if it had been intact.<sup id="cite_ref-Ride_14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ride-14">[14]</a></sup>

In the early hours of [15 March](../_/15_March.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Great_10-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Great-10">[10]</a></sup> the riders of the Rohirrim arrived at the northwestern gate of the Rammas Echor and found that the gate was ruined, that there was a great breach in the wall east of the gate and that only a few Orcs were left on the wall, which were busy destroying it and did not pay attention to the risk of enemies approaching from the north. The Rohirrim were able to surprise the Orcs and quickly killed them or drove them away and to use the ruined gate and the breach in the wall to ride into the Pelennor Fields.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[17]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Rammas Echor* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name. It means "great wall of the outer circle".<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[18]</a></sup> It is a compound of *[rammas](Rammas.md)* ("great wall") and *[echor](../E/Echor.md)* ("outer circle").<sup id="cite_ref-UI_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-6">[6]</a></sup>
