**RIG** is a [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "twine, wreathe"<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup> or "wreath, garland, crown".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> A detailed signification is given as "twine, especially of flower-garlands, or those of gems etc. made in their likeness".<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *-rīna* ("garlanded, crowned"); *[rië](/wiki/Ri%C3%AB "Rië")* ("garland"); *riende*<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *rīgā* ("wreath, garland")<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[ría](/wiki/R%C3%ADa "Ría")*<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *[ría](/wiki/R%C3%ADa "Ría")*<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *rî*<sup id="cite_ref-Shibb_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shibb-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions {#Other_versions}

In the *[Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies")* appears the root RIG, with the descendant forms [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) *[ríe](/wiki/Ri%C3%AB "Rië")* ("crown") and [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *[rhî](/wiki/Rh%C3%AE "Rhî")* ("crown") and *[rhîs](/wiki/Rh%C3%AEs "Rhîs")* ("queen").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-VT_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT-5">[5]</a></sup>
