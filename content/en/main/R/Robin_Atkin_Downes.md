**Robin Atkin Downes** is a British actor.

He auditioned for the role of [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>). Atkin Downes was stated as trying out for the role but was not what the casting crew was looking for. He did voice several characters in [video games](../../category/G/Games.md), but none of them were Aragorn.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2006](../_/2006.md) - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II%253A_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king.md)* - Additional voices
- 2006 - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II.md)* - Additional voices
- [2003](../_/2003.md) - *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_War_of_the_Ring.md)* - Elven Lightbearer, Additional voices
- 2003 - *[The Hobbit](<../T/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game).md>)* - Additional voices
