**Robin Atkin Downes** is a British actor.

He auditioned for the role of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)"). Atkin Downes was stated as trying out for the role but was not what the casting crew was looking for. He did voice several characters in [video games](/wiki/Category:Games "Category:Games"), but none of them were Aragorn.

## Roles {#Roles}

- [2006](/wiki/2006) - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II: The Rise of the Witch-king](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II:_The_Rise_of_the_Witch-king)* - Additional voices
- 2006 - *[The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II)* - Additional voices
- [2003](/wiki/2003) - *[The Lord of the Rings: War of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_of_the_Ring)* - Elven Lightbearer, Additional voices
- 2003 - *[The Hobbit](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(2003_video_game)> "The Hobbit (2003 video game)")* - Additional voices
