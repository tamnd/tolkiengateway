The third month of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), lying between [Solmath](/wiki/Solmath) and [Astron](/wiki/Astron), and approximately corresponding to modern [21 February](/wiki/21_February) through [22 March](/wiki/22_March).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name represents a [modernization](/wiki/Hobbitish "Hobbitish") of the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) name for March, <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/hre%C3%BEmona%C3%BE" title="wiktionary:hreþmonaþ">Hreþmōnaþ</a></i>, meaning "rough-month".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Alternatively, the name could mean "Storm-month", according to Dr. Eleanor Parker's reading of [J.R.R. Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") commentary on the word "Hreðmanna" in [Beowulf: A Translation and Commentary](/wiki/Beowulf:_A_Translation_and_Commentary).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
