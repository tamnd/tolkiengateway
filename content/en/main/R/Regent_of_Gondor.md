**Regent** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"). *[Karma](/wiki/Karma)-[kundo](/wiki/Kundo "Kundo")*, "Guardian of the Helm") was a title applied to those granted executive authority in place of a legitimate reigning king.

## [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor") custom {#Noldorin_custom}

[Orodreth](/wiki/Orodreth) acted in this capacity after [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod) departed [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) before ascending to the throne upon the death of the king.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In contrast, [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin) would refuse a similar office over [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) in favor of marching with [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) to the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Whether Turgon appointed another lord as regent is unknown.

## [Númenorean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor") precedent {#Númenorean_precedent}

Before the time of [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion), no [King](/wiki/Kings_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Kings of Númenor") had ever left the [island](/wiki/Elenna "Elenna") during his reign. When Tar-Aldarion resolved to travel to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth), the [Council of the Sceptre](/wiki/Council_of_the_Sceptre) sought a regency to rule in his place. The king's father, [Meneldur](/wiki/Meneldur), refused the regency when offered, and Meneldur's cousin, [Hallatan](/wiki/Hallatan) of [Hyarastorni](/wiki/Hyarastorni), became regent instead. [Ancalimë](/wiki/Ancalim%C3%AB) served as regent on more than one occasion while her father voyaged.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) {#Gondor}

In all the history of the [Kings of Gondor](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor), only one Regent of this kind was ever appointed, [Minalcar](/wiki/Minalcar).

Minalcar was the grandson of [Atanatar II Alcarin](/wiki/Atanatar_II "Atanatar II") and son of [Calmacil](</wiki/Calmacil_(King_of_Gondor)> "Calmacil (King of Gondor)"). In the closing years of his grandfather's rule, he had become a forceful presence in the royal councils as neither of the King's sons, [Narmacil](/wiki/Narmacil_I "Narmacil I") nor Calmacil, showed great interest in state affairs. Just a few short years after his uncle Narmacil succeeded the King, he conferred the office of Karma-kundo on Minalcar; from that point he effectively ruled [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) for no less than sixty-four years, though officially doing so in the King's name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Minalcar would eventually ascend to the throne of Gondor himself under his better-known name [Rómendacil II](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_II).

After the death of [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) and his sons, [Steward](/wiki/Steward) [Pelendur](/wiki/Pelendur) ruled Gondor briefly between [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1944](/wiki/Third_Age_1944 "Third Age 1944") and [1945](/wiki/Third_Age_1945 "Third Age 1945") until [Earnil I](/wiki/Earnil_I) was appointed as Ondoher's successor.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The [Ruling Stewards](/wiki/Ruling_Stewards) were effectively "Regents" who ruled Gondor indefinitely in the King's absence.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Karma-kundo* is translated "Helm-guardian".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the orthography used in *The Lord of the Rings*, this name would be written *Carma-cundo*, since Tolkien usually represented the \[k\]-sound with the letter "c". <span></span>
