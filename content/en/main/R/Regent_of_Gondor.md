**Regent** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md). *[Karma](../K/Karma.md)-[kundo](../K/Kundo.md)*, "Guardian of the Helm") was a title applied to those granted executive authority in place of a legitimate reigning king.

## [Noldorin](../N/Noldor.md) custom {#Noldorin_custom}

[Orodreth](../O/Orodreth.md) acted in this capacity after [Finrod](../F/Finrod.md) departed [Nargothrond](../N/Nargothrond.md) before ascending to the throne upon the death of the king.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> In contrast, [Maeglin](../M/Maeglin.md) would refuse a similar office over [Gondolin](../G/Gondolin.md) in favor of marching with [Turgon](../T/Turgon.md) to the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](../N/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Whether Turgon appointed another lord as regent is unknown.

## [Númenorean](../N/Númenor.md) precedent {#Númenorean_precedent}

Before the time of [Tar-Aldarion](../T/Tar-Aldarion.md), no [King](../K/Kings_of_Númenor.md) had ever left the [island](../E/Elenna.md) during his reign. When Tar-Aldarion resolved to travel to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), the [Council of the Sceptre](../C/Council_of_the_Sceptre.md) sought a regency to rule in his place. The king's father, [Meneldur](../M/Meneldur.md), refused the regency when offered, and Meneldur's cousin, [Hallatan](../H/Hallatan.md) of [Hyarastorni](../H/Hyarastorni.md), became regent instead. [Ancalimë](../A/Ancalimë.md) served as regent on more than one occasion while her father voyaged.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) {#Gondor}

In all the history of the [Kings of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md), only one Regent of this kind was ever appointed, [Minalcar](../M/Minalcar.md).

Minalcar was the grandson of [Atanatar II Alcarin](../A/Atanatar_II.md) and son of [Calmacil](<../C/Calmacil_(King_of_Gondor).md>). In the closing years of his grandfather's rule, he had become a forceful presence in the royal councils as neither of the King's sons, [Narmacil](../N/Narmacil_I.md) nor Calmacil, showed great interest in state affairs. Just a few short years after his uncle Narmacil succeeded the King, he conferred the office of Karma-kundo on Minalcar; from that point he effectively ruled [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) for no less than sixty-four years, though officially doing so in the King's name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Minalcar would eventually ascend to the throne of Gondor himself under his better-known name [Rómendacil II](Rómendacil_II.md).

After the death of [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) and his sons, [Steward](../S/Steward.md) [Pelendur](../P/Pelendur.md) ruled Gondor briefly between [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1944](../T/Third_Age_1944.md) and [1945](../T/Third_Age_1945.md) until [Earnil I](../E/Earnil_I.md) was appointed as Ondoher's successor.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> The [Ruling Stewards](Ruling_Stewards.md) were effectively "Regents" who ruled Gondor indefinitely in the King's absence.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Karma-kundo* is translated "Helm-guardian".<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the orthography used in *The Lord of the Rings*, this name would be written *Carma-cundo*, since Tolkien usually represented the \[k\]-sound with the letter "c". <span></span>
