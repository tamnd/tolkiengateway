<i><b>Realms of the Three Rings</b></i> is an expansion for the second edition of the [*The One Ring* Roleplaying Game](</wiki/The_One_Ring_(role-playing_game)> "The One Ring (role-playing game)"). A version for [The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying), titled <i><b>Keepers of the Elven-rings</b></i>, was also released.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>In the twilight of the Third Age, three realms of the West-elves still endure in Middle-earth. By the power of the Three Rings, they exist as a testament to a time that was. But not only the Elves remember. As Sauron gathers his strength to bring war to Middle-earth once again, he plans a devious vengeance against those who humbled him long ago.
<i>Realms of the Three Rings</i>™ is a supplement for <i>The One Ring</i>™, the official tabletop roleplaying game based on the works of J.R.R. Tolkien.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Chapter 1: <i>Where The West-elves Still Linger</i></b> describes the realms of Lórien, Rivendell and Lindon and those who dwell there, including the Elf-lords who are their guardians.</li>
<li><b>Chapter 2: <i>The Days Darken</i></b> provides the Loremaster with details about Sauron’s schemes against the Immortal Folk, including possible events, Adversaries, and Loremaster characters.</li>
<li><b>Chapter 3: <i>Landmarks</i></b> outlines twelve sites of interest, including locations mentioned in The Lord of the Rings™ and others created specifically for the game.</li>
<li><b>The Appendix</b> contains rules to play High Elves of Rivendell and Elves of Lórien, and introduces guidelines to create Elf-lords, powerful characters especially suited for solo play.</li></ul>
</blockquote>

## Background {#Background}

While writing the book, Quaglia had to carefully avoid taking material from *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, for which they had no rights, while also making sure that nothing conflicted with it, a process which he described as complicated.<sup id="cite_ref-interview_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-interview-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Reception {#Reception}

In a review for [Other Minds 28](/wiki/Other_Minds_28), Thomas Morwinsky awarded the book a perfect score, finding that it upheld the high production quality of the series, while increasing both the quantity and quality of world-building. In particular, he praised Quaglia's expertise concerning even minute details of the source material, as well as his ability to hint at lore for which there was no license.<sup id="cite_ref-otherminds_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-otherminds-2">[2]</a></sup>

## In other languages {#In_other_languages}

For the Polish localization of *The One Ring*, a unique blue collector's edition was released by publisher Black Monk.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
