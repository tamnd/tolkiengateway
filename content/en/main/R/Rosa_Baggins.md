**Rosa Baggins** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## History {#History}

Rosa was the daughter of [Ponto Baggins](/wiki/Ponto_Baggins_I "Ponto Baggins I") and [Mimosa Bunce](/wiki/Mimosa_Bunce). She married [Hildigrim Took](/wiki/Hildigrim_Took), son of [the Old Took](/wiki/Gerontius_Took "Gerontius Took"). They had only one child, [Adalgrim](/wiki/Adalgrim_Took "Adalgrim Took"), born in [1280](/wiki/Third_Age_2880 "Third Age 2880").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

<a class="external text" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/rosa#Latin" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Rosa</a> is the Latin name for the <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Rose_(disambiguation)" title="Rose (disambiguation)">rose</a>.
