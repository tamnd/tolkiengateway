<i><b>rant</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "course".<sup id="cite_ref-silm-ap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-ap-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) (an earlier conception of Sindarin) word *rant* has the additional glosses "river-bed, lode, vein".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), Noldorin *rant* derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=RAT&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RAT (page does not exist)">RAT</a> ("walk").<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *[Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant)*, "silver-course"<sup id="cite_ref-lotr-index_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotr-index-3">[3]</a></sup> or, more usually, "Silverlode";<sup id="cite_ref-lotr-loth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotr-loth-4">[4]</a></sup> a river which flows out of [Dimrill Dale](/wiki/Dimrill_Dale) and through [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien)
- *[Adurant](/wiki/Adurant)*, "double-stream";<sup id="cite_ref-silm-index_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-index-5">[5]</a></sup> one of the seven rivers of [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand).
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gondrant&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gondrant (page does not exist)">Gondrant</a></i>, "stone-trail"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### See Also {#See_Also}

- *[athrad](/wiki/Athrad "Athrad")*, "ford"
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Imrath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Imrath (page does not exist)">imrath</a></i>, "a long narrow valley with a road or watercourse running through it lengthwise"
- <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Othrad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Othrad (page does not exist)">othrad</a></i>, "street"
- *[randir](/wiki/Randir "Randir")*, "pilgrim, wanderer"
- *[ranga](/wiki/Ranga "Ranga")*, a full pace
- *[rath](/wiki/Rath "Rath")*, "street" or synonymous with *rant*
- Noldorin *[iôr](/wiki/I%C3%B4r "Iôr")*, "course"

## Ilkorin {#Ilkorin}

<i><b>rant</b></i> is an [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin) word meaning "flow, course of river".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>
