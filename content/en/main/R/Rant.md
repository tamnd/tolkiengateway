<i><b>rant</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) noun meaning "course".<sup id="cite_ref-silm-ap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-ap-1">[1]</a></sup> The [Noldorin](../N/Noldorin.md) (an earlier conception of Sindarin) word *rant* has the additional glosses "river-bed, lode, vein".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md), Noldorin *rant* derives from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=RAT&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RAT (page does not exist)">RAT</a> ("walk").<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *[Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md)*, "silver-course"<sup id="cite_ref-lotr-index_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotr-index-3">[3]</a></sup> or, more usually, "Silverlode";<sup id="cite_ref-lotr-loth_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotr-loth-4">[4]</a></sup> a river which flows out of [Dimrill Dale](../D/Dimrill_Dale.md) and through [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md)
- *[Adurant](../A/Adurant.md)*, "double-stream";<sup id="cite_ref-silm-index_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silm-index-5">[5]</a></sup> one of the seven rivers of [Ossiriand](../O/Ossiriand.md).
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Gondrant&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gondrant (page does not exist)">Gondrant</a></i>, "stone-trail"<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

### See Also {#See_Also}

- *[athrad](../A/Athrad.md)*, "ford"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Imrath&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Imrath (page does not exist)">imrath</a></i>, "a long narrow valley with a road or watercourse running through it lengthwise"
- <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Othrad&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Othrad (page does not exist)">othrad</a></i>, "street"
- *[randir](Randir.md)*, "pilgrim, wanderer"
- *[ranga](Ranga.md)*, a full pace
- *[rath](Rath.md)*, "street" or synonymous with *rant*
- Noldorin *[iôr](../I/Iôr.md)*, "course"

## Ilkorin {#Ilkorin}

<i><b>rant</b></i> is an [Ilkorin](../I/Ilkorin.md) word meaning "flow, course of river".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-2">[2]</a></sup>
