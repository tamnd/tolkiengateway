<b>Ring*Con 2002</b> was the first [Ring\*Con](/wiki/Ring*Con) convention, held from [November 22](/wiki/22_November "22 November") to [November 24](/wiki/24_November "24 November") in Bonn, Germany.

## Guests {#Guests}

- [Mark Ferguson](/wiki/Mark_Ferguson)
- [Brad Dourif](/wiki/Brad_Dourif)
- [Sarah McLeod](/wiki/Sarah_McLeod)
- [Craig Parker](/wiki/Craig_Parker)
- [Cameron Rhodes](/wiki/Cameron_Rhodes)
- [Helmut W. Pesch](/wiki/Helmut_W._Pesch)
