The **Realms in Exile** were the two kingdoms established by [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor). Often called the **Kingdoms of the Dúnedain**, it was the collective name for **[Arnor](/wiki/Arnor)** and **[Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)**, the North-Kingdom and South-kingdom of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain) in the [Westlands](/wiki/Westlands) of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

## History {#History}

### Foundation {#Foundation}

After the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3319](/wiki/Second_Age_3319 "Second Age 3319"), the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea) cast up a handful of survivors on the shores of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup> These were [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his sons [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion), the last leaders of the [Faithful](/wiki/Faithful), who had fled from the port of [Rómenna](/wiki/R%C3%B3menna) and were aboard ship when [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) was swallowed by the [Sea](/wiki/Sea). The raging waters that followed the [Downfall](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor "Downfall of Númenor") had driven their ships eastward until they came to the ruined shores of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).<sup id="cite_ref-numenor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-numenor-4">[4]</a></sup> There they established in the North-west the Númenorean realms in exile, [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) and [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320").<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) and his people landed in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) and from there they spread out across [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup> Their great cities were at [Annúminas](/wiki/Ann%C3%BAminas) on [Nenuial](/wiki/Nenuial), and [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost) on the [North Downs](/wiki/North_Downs),<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup> and they thus established a realm that came to be known as [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor), "Land of the King", because [High King](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)") [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) dwelt there by [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim).<sup id="cite_ref-numenor_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-numenor-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Elendil](/wiki/Elendil)'s sons [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion) were driven southwards by the [Sea](/wiki/Sea), and found their way at last up through the [Mouths of Anduin](/wiki/Mouths_of_Anduin) into the [Great River](/wiki/Great_River) in a region where a great [Númenórean](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean) port, [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir), had stood for centuries. They founded their own southern realm in this region, named [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), "Land of Stone", for the great feats of stonework they performed. Their main city was named [Osgiliath](/wiki/Osgiliath), built on the river on which they had entered their new land, but each of the brothers also had a fortress of their own: [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil) for [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and [Minas Anor](/wiki/Minas_Anor) for [Anárion](/wiki/An%C3%A1rion).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Division {#Division}

The Númenórean Realms formed the [Last Alliance](/wiki/Last_Alliance) with the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) against [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron). During that [War](/wiki/War_of_the_Last_Alliance "War of the Last Alliance"), [Anarion](/wiki/Anarion) and [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) were killed. [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) succeeded to the [High Kingship](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)"), but his loss at the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields) caused a division between the two realms. At that time, [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur)'s nephew [Meneldil](/wiki/Meneldil) became [King of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor) in his own right, and the two kingdoms would remain separate until the time of [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn_Elessar), more than three thousand years later.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Attempts of reunification {#Attempts_of_reunification}

It was not until the time of [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) when they took up counsel together after long silence and estrangment. Then, it was determined that [one single power and will](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") was directing attacks against the Realms in Exile. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1940](/wiki/Third_Age_1940 "Third Age 1940"), [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui), the son of King [Araphant](/wiki/Araphant) of Arthedain, wed [Fíriel](/wiki/F%C3%ADriel), the daughter of King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) of Gondor, thus reuniting the [House of Isildur](/wiki/House_of_Isildur) and the [House of Anárion](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion). However, they could not profit from the new-formed alliance as [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) and the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) reappeared simultaneously.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) would prove to be last in a direct line of kings when he was slain in battle with the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders), alongside his sons. Hoping to save [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) from [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar), [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) staked his claim to the [kingship of Gondor](/wiki/King_of_Gondor "King of Gondor"), by right of his descent from [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) and by that of his wife, mentioning also the ancient [Law of Succession in Númenor](/wiki/Law_of_Succession_in_N%C3%BAmenor). The [Council of Gondor](/wiki/Council_of_Gondor) refused (to the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians) the [remnant](/wiki/Arthedain "Arthedain") of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) seemed an insignificant despite its great heritage) and gave the crown to [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II") instead, the commander of the [royal house](/wiki/House_of_An%C3%A1rion "House of Anárion") who defeated the [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders); he maintained good relations with [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain), promising them aid. [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) did not press his claim for he had neither the power nor the will to oppose the choice of the [Dúnedain of Gondor](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Gondor).<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Arnor](/wiki/Arnor)'s strength was fast dwindling when in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1973](/wiki/Third_Age_1973 "Third Age 1973") now-King [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) sent a message to [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II") that they were in great straits, and that [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) was preparing it final assault. [Eärnil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II "Eärnil II"), accordingly, mustered a great army under the command of his son [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur). The [Gondorian](/wiki/Gondorians "Gondorians") force put to sea, but would not arrive before [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) destroyed the [North-kingdom](/wiki/North-kingdom).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Even so, the descendants of [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) never forgot his claim,<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-6">[6]</a></sup> and when the line of [Kings](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor") failed in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), it was rumored that the Northern line still wandered in secrecy.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

After the ultimate defeat of the [Dark Lord](/wiki/Dark_Lord) in the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Isildur's Heir](/wiki/Isildur%27s_Heir) [Aragorn II Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn_II_Elessar) came forward and reclaimed the [High Kingship](</wiki/High_King_(D%C3%BAnedain)> "High King (Dúnedain)"), once again reuniting the Númenórean Realms after more than three thousand years.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>
