The **Realms in Exile** were the two kingdoms established by [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and his sons after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md). Often called the **Kingdoms of the Dúnedain**, it was the collective name for **[Arnor](../A/Arnor.md)** and **[Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)**, the North-Kingdom and South-kingdom of the [Dúnedain](../D/Dúnedain.md) in the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

## History {#History}

### Foundation {#Foundation}

After the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3319](../S/Second_Age_3319.md), the [Great Sea](../G/Great_Sea.md) cast up a handful of survivors on the shores of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup> These were [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and his sons [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md), the last leaders of the [Faithful](../F/Faithful.md), who had fled from the port of [Rómenna](Rómenna.md) and were aboard ship when [Númenor](../N/Númenor.md) was swallowed by the [Sea](../S/Sea.md). The raging waters that followed the [Downfall](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) had driven their ships eastward until they came to the ruined shores of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-numenor_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-numenor-4">[4]</a></sup> There they established in the North-west the Númenorean realms in exile, [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) and [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3320](../S/Second_Age_3320.md).<sup id="cite_ref-SA_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-3">[3]</a></sup>

[Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) and his people landed in [Lindon](../L/Lindon.md) and from there they spread out across [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup> Their great cities were at [Annúminas](../A/Annúminas.md) on [Nenuial](../N/Nenuial.md), and [Fornost](../F/Fornost.md) on the [North Downs](../N/North_Downs.md),<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup> and they thus established a realm that came to be known as [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md), "Land of the King", because [High King](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>) [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) dwelt there by [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-numenor_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-numenor-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Elendil](../E/Elendil.md)'s sons [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md) were driven southwards by the [Sea](../S/Sea.md), and found their way at last up through the [Mouths of Anduin](../M/Mouths_of_Anduin.md) into the [Great River](../G/Great_River.md) in a region where a great [Númenórean](../N/Númenórean.md) port, [Pelargir](../P/Pelargir.md), had stood for centuries. They founded their own southern realm in this region, named [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), "Land of Stone", for the great feats of stonework they performed. Their main city was named [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md), built on the river on which they had entered their new land, but each of the brothers also had a fortress of their own: [Minas Ithil](../M/Minas_Ithil.md) for [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and [Minas Anor](../M/Minas_Anor.md) for [Anárion](../A/Anárion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Division {#Division}

The Númenórean Realms formed the [Last Alliance](../L/Last_Alliance.md) with the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) against [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md). During that [War](../W/War_of_the_Last_Alliance.md), [Anarion](../A/Anarion.md) and [Elendil](../E/Elendil.md) were killed. [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) succeeded to the [High Kingship](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>), but his loss at the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](../D/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields.md) caused a division between the two realms. At that time, [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md)'s nephew [Meneldil](../M/Meneldil.md) became [King of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md) in his own right, and the two kingdoms would remain separate until the time of [Aragorn Elessar](../A/Aragorn_Elessar.md), more than three thousand years later.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

### Attempts of reunification {#Attempts_of_reunification}

It was not until the time of [Araphant](../A/Araphant.md) of [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) and [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) when they took up counsel together after long silence and estrangment. Then, it was determined that [one single power and will](../S/Sauron.md) was directing attacks against the Realms in Exile. In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1940](../T/Third_Age_1940.md), [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md), the son of King [Araphant](../A/Araphant.md) of Arthedain, wed [Fíriel](../F/Fíriel.md), the daughter of King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) of Gondor, thus reuniting the [House of Isildur](../H/House_of_Isildur.md) and the [House of Anárion](../H/House_of_Anárion.md). However, they could not profit from the new-formed alliance as [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) and the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) reappeared simultaneously.<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) would prove to be last in a direct line of kings when he was slain in battle with the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md), alongside his sons. Hoping to save [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) from [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md), [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) staked his claim to the [kingship of Gondor](../K/King_of_Gondor.md), by right of his descent from [Isildur](../I/Isildur.md) and by that of his wife, mentioning also the ancient [Law of Succession in Númenor](../L/Law_of_Succession_in_Númenor.md). The [Council of Gondor](../C/Council_of_Gondor.md) refused (to the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md) the [remnant](../A/Arthedain.md) of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) seemed an insignificant despite its great heritage) and gave the crown to [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md) instead, the commander of the [royal house](../H/House_of_Anárion.md) who defeated the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md); he maintained good relations with [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md), promising them aid. [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) did not press his claim for he had neither the power nor the will to oppose the choice of the [Dúnedain of Gondor](../D/Dúnedain_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-6">[6]</a></sup>

[Arnor](../A/Arnor.md)'s strength was fast dwindling when in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1973](../T/Third_Age_1973.md) now-King [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) sent a message to [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md) that they were in great straits, and that [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) was preparing it final assault. [Eärnil](../E/Eärnil_II.md), accordingly, mustered a great army under the command of his son [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md). The [Gondorian](../G/Gondorians.md) force put to sea, but would not arrive before [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) destroyed the [North-kingdom](../N/North-kingdom.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Even so, the descendants of [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) never forgot his claim,<sup id="cite_ref-gondor_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gondor-6">[6]</a></sup> and when the line of [Kings](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md) failed in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), it was rumored that the Northern line still wandered in secrecy.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Final reunification {#Final_reunification}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/9/9c/CdxIconArticle.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content"><i>Main article:</i> <b><a href="Reunited_Kingdom.md" title="Reunited Kingdom">Reunited Kingdom</a></b></div></div>

After the ultimate defeat of the [Dark Lord](../D/Dark_Lord.md) in the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Isildur's Heir](../I/Isildur's_Heir.md) [Aragorn II Elessar](../A/Aragorn_II_Elessar.md) came forward and reclaimed the [High Kingship](<../H/High_King_(Dúnedain).md>), once again reuniting the Númenórean Realms after more than three thousand years.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>
