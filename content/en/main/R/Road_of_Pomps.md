The **Road of Pomps** was a street of [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It went southwards from the [Square of the Palace](/wiki/Square_of_the_Palace), and one the buildings along it was [Gar Ainion](/wiki/Gar_Ainion). During the [Fall of Gondolin](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin), [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) doubted if leading the survivors directly to his house taking the [Way of Running Waters](/wiki/Way_of_Running_Waters), when they were attacked and forced to take the Road. There [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) and many of the [Golden Flower](/wiki/House_of_the_Golden_Flower "House of the Golden Flower") fought manfully protecting the rear.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
