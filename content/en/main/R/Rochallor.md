**Rochallor** was the great horse of [High King](../H/High_King_of_the_Noldor.md) [Fingolfin](../F/Fingolfin.md), on which he rode to the gates of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) and challenged [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md) to single combat.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Rochallor stayed with Fingolfin until his death. When assailed by the wolves of [Angband](../A/Angband.md) the great steed escaped by outrunning them due to great swiftness. Coming to [Hithlum](../H/Hithlum.md), his heart broke and he died.<sup id="cite_ref-Annals_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Annals-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The first element in the name *Rochallor* is *[roch](../H/Horses.md#Names)*, the [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word for "horse".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The second element is of unknown meaning.
