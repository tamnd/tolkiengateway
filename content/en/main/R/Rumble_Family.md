The **Rumble Family** was a [Hobbit](../H/Hobbits.md) family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) mentions that "rumble" is not a meaningful word in the [Hobbitish](../H/Hobbitish.md) of that era.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
