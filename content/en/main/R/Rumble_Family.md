The **Rumble Family** was a [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") family.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") mentions that "rumble" is not a meaningful word in the [Hobbitish](/wiki/Hobbitish) of that era.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
