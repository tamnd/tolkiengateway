**RAK** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "stretch out, reach".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) <i>*ranku</i>
    - [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) *ranko* (plural *ranqi*; "arm")
    - [Old Noldorin](/wiki/Old_Noldorin) *ranko* (plural *rankui*)
        - [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *rhanc* (plural *rhenc*, archaic plural *rhengy*; "arm")
- Primitive Quendian <i>*rakmē</i> ("fathom")
    - Qenya *rangwe*
    - Old Noldorin *ragme*
        - Noldorin *rhaew*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
