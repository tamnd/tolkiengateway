The **Red Arrow** was an arrow used by [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) as a symbol to summon its allies from [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in times of war.<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

The Red Arrow had black feathers and barbs made of steel. Its point was painted red.<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Red Arrow had not been used to summon aid from Rohan during the life of [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), the King of Rohan, until it was presented to him on March 9, 3019 by [Hirgon](/wiki/Hirgon), a mounted messenger that had been sent by Steward [Denethor](/wiki/Denethor) of Gondor to summon the aid of the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim).<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup>

After Hirgon had spent the night and witnessed the muster of Rohan on March 10, 3019<sup id="cite_ref-Muster_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Muster-1">[1]</a></sup> he rode back to Gondor with the Red Arrow and his companion who had accompanied him on his errand. They were probably killed before they reached the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor), the outer wall that protected the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields), the townlands and fields that lay before [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith), the capital of Gondor, because on [14 March](/wiki/14_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> scouts of the Rohirrim found two Gondorian dead messengers and two dead horses between the [Grey Wood](/wiki/Grey_Wood) and the Rammas Echor. One of the dead messengers whose head was hewn off still held the Red Arrow in his hand. They found signs that indicated that the two message riders of Gondor were riding back westwards when they were killed. In the opinion of [Elfhelm](/wiki/Elfhelm), the leader of the first *éored* of the muster of the [East-mark](/wiki/East-mark) of Rohan, the two message riders of Gondor reached the Rammas [two days earlier](/wiki/12_March "12 March") (supposing they obtained fresh horses from the posts as message riders usually did), but that they could not pass through it to reach Minas Tirith, because it had either already been taken by or was already under attack by the forces of Sauron and that the two riders had turned back westwards.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It has been noted that [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s concept of the use of a red arrow by Gondor to summon the Rohirrim to war is very reminiscent of a similar use in *[The House of the Wolfings](/wiki/The_House_of_the_Wolfings)*, a novel by [William Morris](/wiki/William_Morris),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> which Tolkien owned and from which he took inspiration.<sup id="cite_ref-CGMorris_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGMorris-5">[5]</a></sup> Morris's use of the device is based on Germanic folk tradition, recorded for instance in the twelfth-century *History of the Danes* by the writer known as Saxo Grammaticus, Book Five of which says that "on all occasion of any sudden and urgent war, an arrow of wood, looking like iron, used to be passed on everywhere from man to man as a messenger.”
