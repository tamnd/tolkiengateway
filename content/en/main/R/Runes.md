The **Runic alphabet** was a system of writing based on angular shapes that could easily be carved into wood or stone. It originated and was used mainly in Northern Europe, by the [Anglo-Saxons](../O/Old_English.md) and the [Norse tribes](../O/Old_Norse.md). One theory says that Runes evolved from Etruscan writing, however other quasi-runic scripts in other parts of the world (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rov%C3%A1s%C3%ADr%C3%A1s" title="wikipedia:Rovásírás">Rovásírás</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orkhon_script" title="wikipedia:Orkhon script">Orkhon Script</a>) also exist.

## Tolkien and Runes {#Tolkien_and_Runes}

[Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) decided that his [peoples](Races.md) of [Arda](../A/Arda.md) would also use a similar script, and "Runes" are mentioned in his narratives.

In *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, Tolkien used [Old English](../O/Old_English.md) runes, or <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futhorc" title="wikipedia:Futhorc">Futhorc</a>, to display the writing of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) on the [Thrór's Map](../T/Thrór's_Map.md); his Dwarves however were based mainly on Norse culture and [their language](../K/Khuzdul.md) is influenced of Semitic languages.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The texts of course are modern English, and display the use of [Westron](../W/Westron.md) of [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

Many readers were intrigued by the usage of runes on the dust-jacket of the book and the Map, and sent inquiries to Tolkien, asking if they were real and if they could be read.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The foreword of [*The Hobbit* (50th anniversary edition and later)](<../T/The_Hobbit_(50th_Anniversary_edition).md>) explains the usage of the Old English runes and help the reader read the Map (although the translations are also revealed in the end of Chapter 3, *[A Short Rest](../A/A_Short_Rest.md)*)

Eventually Tolkien elaborated on a totally original runic writing system while writing *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. Although most of the letters between historical runes and Tolkien's "cirth" are identical, they do not share the same values, since the Cirth follow the phonetic principles of the [Tengwar](../T/Tengwar.md).

In the history of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), a runic script called [Cirth](../C/Cirth.md) was invented by [Daeron](../D/Daeron.md) of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) to represent [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) words. Runes came to be used widely by races other than the [Elves](../E/Elves.md), and especially by the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md).
