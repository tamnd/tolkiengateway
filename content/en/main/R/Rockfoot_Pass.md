The **Rockfoot Pass** was a pass close to a forest near that lay on the migration trail to the [Grove](../G/Grove.md) in [Rhovanion](Rhovanion.md). The Rockfoot Pass was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md).

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the Rockfoot Pass was a location that a certain [Harfoot community](../H/Harfoots.md) passed by when migrating from one seasonal haven to the next, particularly from the [Norfield Glen](../N/Norfield_Glen.md) to the [Grove](../G/Grove.md).

During one such migration, the normal migration trail through the Rockfoot Pass became impassable, causing the main caravan of the Harfoot community to attempt to take an alternative route through a [wolf-infested](../W/Wolves.md) eerie forest. The Brandyfoot family, [Poppy Proudfellow](../P/Poppy_Proudfellow.md), and [The Stranger](../T/The_Stranger.md) follow in haste behind them. During this time, [Dilly Brandyfoot](../D/Dilly_Brandyfoot.md) stated her dislike of the eerie forest and Poppy Proudfellow pondered on what madness drove the main caravan there. To this, [Elanor Brandyfoot](../E/Elanor_Brandyfoot.md) suggested that the Rockfoot Pass must have been blocked in. The discussion was then halted by [Marigold Brandyfoot](../M/Marigold_Brandyfoot.md), who reasoned that pulling the carts would not get any easier that night. The Stranger them mimicked her words, saying "*Easier in the dark*".
