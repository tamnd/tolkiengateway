The **Rockfoot Pass** was a pass close to a forest near that lay on the migration trail to the [Grove](/wiki/Grove) in [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion). The Rockfoot Pass was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the Rockfoot Pass was a location that a certain [Harfoot community](/wiki/Harfoots "Harfoots") passed by when migrating from one seasonal haven to the next, particularly from the [Norfield Glen](/wiki/Norfield_Glen) to the [Grove](/wiki/Grove).

During one such migration, the normal migration trail through the Rockfoot Pass became impassable, causing the main caravan of the Harfoot community to attempt to take an alternative route through a [wolf-infested](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") eerie forest. The Brandyfoot family, [Poppy Proudfellow](/wiki/Poppy_Proudfellow), and [The Stranger](/wiki/The_Stranger) follow in haste behind them. During this time, [Dilly Brandyfoot](/wiki/Dilly_Brandyfoot) stated her dislike of the eerie forest and Poppy Proudfellow pondered on what madness drove the main caravan there. To this, [Elanor Brandyfoot](/wiki/Elanor_Brandyfoot) suggested that the Rockfoot Pass must have been blocked in. The discussion was then halted by [Marigold Brandyfoot](/wiki/Marigold_Brandyfoot), who reasoned that pulling the carts would not get any easier that night. The Stranger them mimicked her words, saying "*Easier in the dark*".
