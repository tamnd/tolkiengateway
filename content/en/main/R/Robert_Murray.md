Father **Robert Patrick Ruthven Murray** (b. [8 June](/wiki/8_June) [1925](/wiki/1925) - [24 April](/wiki/24_April) [2018](/wiki/2018)) was an English [Roman Catholic](/wiki/Catholicism "Catholicism") theologian and grandson of Sir <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Murray_(lexicographer)" title="wikipedia:James Murray (lexicographer)">James Murray</a> (the founder of the *[Oxford English Dictionary](/wiki/Oxford_English_Dictionary)*).

A personal friend of the Tolkien family (a friendship which contributed to his conversion to Roman Catholicism) since 1944, Murray was entrusted by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to proofread drafts and manuscripts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. Tolkien and Murray corresponded frequently, especially on matters of religion, and several letters to Murray were published in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien)* (letters [142](/wiki/Letter_142 "Letter 142"), [156](/wiki/Letter_156 "Letter 156"), [209](/wiki/Letter_209 "Letter 209")).

He became a Jesuit in 1949 and was ordained in 1959. On [1 August](/wiki/1_August) [1959](/wiki/1959), Tolkien served Murray's first Mass, and on [6 September](/wiki/6_September) [1973](/wiki/1973), four days after Tolkien's death, he assisted in a requiem Mass for Tolkien in the Church of St Anthony of Padua in Headington, Oxford.

He was on the Faculty of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heythrop_College" title="wikipedia:Heythrop College">Heythrop College</a> in Oxfordshire and London since 1963.

During the [Tolkien Centenary Conference](/wiki/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference) in [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) ([1992](/wiki/1992)), Father Murray delivered the sermon at the Thanksgiving service.
