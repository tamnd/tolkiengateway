Father **Robert Patrick Ruthven Murray** (b. [8 June](../_/8_June.md) [1925](../_/1925.md) - [24 April](../_/24_April.md) [2018](../_/2018.md)) was an English [Roman Catholic](../C/Catholicism.md) theologian and grandson of Sir <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Murray_(lexicographer)" title="wikipedia:James Murray (lexicographer)">James Murray</a> (the founder of the *[Oxford English Dictionary](../O/Oxford_English_Dictionary.md)*).

A personal friend of the Tolkien family (a friendship which contributed to his conversion to Roman Catholicism) since 1944, Murray was entrusted by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) to proofread drafts and manuscripts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. Tolkien and Murray corresponded frequently, especially on matters of religion, and several letters to Murray were published in *[The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien](../T/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)* (letters [142](../L/Letter_142.md), [156](../L/Letter_156.md), [209](../L/Letter_209.md)).

He became a Jesuit in 1949 and was ordained in 1959. On [1 August](../_/1_August.md) [1959](../_/1959.md), Tolkien served Murray's first Mass, and on [6 September](../_/6_September.md) [1973](../_/1973.md), four days after Tolkien's death, he assisted in a requiem Mass for Tolkien in the Church of St Anthony of Padua in Headington, Oxford.

He was on the Faculty of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heythrop_College" title="wikipedia:Heythrop College">Heythrop College</a> in Oxfordshire and London since 1963.

During the [Tolkien Centenary Conference](../T/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference.md) in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md) ([1992](../_/1992.md)), Father Murray delivered the sermon at the Thanksgiving service.
