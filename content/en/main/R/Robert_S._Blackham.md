**Robert S. Blackham** was a member of [The Tolkien Society](../T/The_Tolkien_Society.md) and the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Birmingham_Tolkien_Group&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Birmingham Tolkien Group (page does not exist)">Birmingham Tolkien Group</a>. He gave tours on [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) in [Birmingham](../B/Birmingham.md) and [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md), and has written several books about it.
