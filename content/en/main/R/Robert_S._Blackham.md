**Robert S. Blackham** was a member of [The Tolkien Society](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Society) and the <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Birmingham_Tolkien_Group&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Birmingham Tolkien Group (page does not exist)">Birmingham Tolkien Group</a>. He gave tours on [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham) and [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), and has written several books about it.
