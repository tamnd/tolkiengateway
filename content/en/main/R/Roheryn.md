**Roheryn** was the [horse](/wiki/Horses "Horses") of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), brought to him out of the north by the [Rangers](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North "Rangers of the North") during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and said to have been a gift from the lady [Arwen Evenstar](/wiki/Arwen "Arwen").<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Roheryn* is a [Númenórean Sindarin](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3rean_Sindarin) name, softened from the pure [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *Rocheryn*, meaning "Steed of the Lady".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The first element in *Ro(c)heryn* is *[roch](/wiki/Horses#Names "Horses")* ("horse"), the second *heryn* ("lady").<sup id="cite_ref-Appendix_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Appendix-2">[2]</a></sup>
