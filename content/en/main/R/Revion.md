**Revion** was a [Silvan elf](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) garrisoned at the [Watchtower of Ostirith](../W/Watchtower_of_Ostirith.md) during the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md). Revion was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power.md)* by [Amazon Studios](../A/Amazon_Studios.md) and is portrayed by <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Simon_Merrells&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Simon Merrells (page does not exist)">Simon Merrells</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
