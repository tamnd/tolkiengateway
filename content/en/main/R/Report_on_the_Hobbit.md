**Report on the Hobbit** is [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin)'s reader's report on *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, written on [October 30](/wiki/30_October "30 October"), [1936](/wiki/1936), when he was age ten. The manuscript is kept in the J.R.R. Tolkien Collection at [Marquette University Libraries](/wiki/Marquette_University "Marquette University") (Series 5, Box 2, Folder 7), and was published in the *[The Annotated Hobbit](/wiki/The_Annotated_Hobbit:_Revised_and_Expanded_Edition "The Annotated Hobbit: Revised and Expanded Edition")*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Transcription {#Transcription}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Review of &#34;The Hobbit&#34;<br/>
30 October 1936<br/>
<br/>
Bilbo Baggins was a Hobbit who lived in his Hobbit hole and neverwent for adventures, at last Gandalf the wizard and his Dwarves persuaded him to go. He had a very exiting (sic) time fighting goblins and wargs. At last they get to the lonely mountain; Smaug, the dragon who guards it is killed and after a terrific battle with the goblins he returned home — rich!<br/>
<br/>
This book, with the help of maps, does not need any illustrations it is good and should appeal to all children between the ages of 5 and 9.<br/>
Rayner Unwin
</p>
</blockquote>
