**ROK** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") related to [horses](/wiki/Horses "Horses").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Primitive Elvish](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian "Primitive Quendian") *rokkō*<sup id="cite_ref-L211_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L211-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L297_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-3">[3]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[rocco/rokko](/wiki/Horses#Names "Horses")*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)/[Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[roch](/wiki/Horses#Names "Horses")*<sup id="cite_ref-LR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L211_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L211-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-L297_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-L297-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Tolkien experimented with the concept that ROK was a word related to fear and subsequently to Orcs. The origin of the root ROK as 'fear' was the horseman emissary of Melkor who abducted Elves from [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen). However, later Orcs and fear were related to a different root, [ÓROK](/wiki/%C3%93ROK) and [RUKU](/wiki/RUKU).<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup> <span></span>
