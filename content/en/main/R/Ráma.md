<i><b>ráma</b></i> (pl. **rámar**, **rámali**) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "wing" and poetically "branch" of a tree.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[PQ](/wiki/PQ) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=R%C3%A2m%C3%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Râmâ (page does not exist)">râmâ</a></i> from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=RAM&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="RAM (page does not exist)">RAM</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

In [Latin](/wiki/Latin), <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/ramus#Latin" title="wiktionary:ramus">rāmus</a></i> means "branch"<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> which mirrors the poetic usage of *rámar* as "wings (of trees)" &gt; "branches" in *Namárië*<sup id="cite_ref-Namarie_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Namarie-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
