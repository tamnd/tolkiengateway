**Rose** is a common maid-name in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") and may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Roses](/wiki/Roses)**, fair-scented flowers found throughout [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).
    - [Eglantine](/wiki/Eglantine), a type of rose found in [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien).
    - <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rock-roses&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rock-roses (page does not exist)">Rock-roses</a>, not a type of rose.
- [Rosa Baggins](/wiki/Rosa_Baggins) (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1256](/wiki/Third_Age_2856 "Third Age 2856")), great-grandmother of [Peregrin Took](/wiki/Peregrin_Took).
- [Rose](</wiki/Rose_(daughter_of_Holman)> "Rose (daughter of Holman)") (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1262](/wiki/Third_Age_2862 "Third Age 2862")), daughter, and youngest child, of [Holman the greenhanded](/wiki/Holman_the_greenhanded).
- **[Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton) ([S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1384](/wiki/Third_Age_2984 "Third Age 2984") - [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1482](/wiki/Fourth_Age_61 "Fourth Age 61")), daughter of [Tom Cottom](/wiki/Tolman_Cotton_Senior "Tolman Cotton Senior") and the wife of [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee).**
    - [Rose Gardner](/wiki/Rose_Gardner) (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1425](/wiki/Fourth_Age_4 "Fourth Age 4")), third child of Rose Cotton and Samwise Gamgee.
    - [Primrose Gardner](/wiki/Primrose_Gardner) (born [S.R.](/wiki/Shire_Reckoning "Shire Reckoning") [1435](/wiki/Fourth_Age_14 "Fourth Age 14")), ninth child of Rose Cotton and Samwise Gamgee.
