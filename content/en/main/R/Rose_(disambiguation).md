**Rose** is a common maid-name in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) and may refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- **[Roses](Roses.md)**, fair-scented flowers found throughout [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).
    - [Eglantine](../E/Eglantine.md), a type of rose found in [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md).
    - <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rock-roses&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rock-roses (page does not exist)">Rock-roses</a>, not a type of rose.
- [Rosa Baggins](Rosa_Baggins.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1256](../T/Third_Age_2856.md)), great-grandmother of [Peregrin Took](../P/Peregrin_Took.md).
- [Rose](<Rose_(daughter_of_Holman).md>) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1262](../T/Third_Age_2862.md)), daughter, and youngest child, of [Holman the greenhanded](../H/Holman_the_greenhanded.md).
- **[Rose Cotton](Rose_Cotton.md) ([S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1384](../T/Third_Age_2984.md) - [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1482](../F/Fourth_Age_61.md)), daughter of [Tom Cottom](../T/Tolman_Cotton_Senior.md) and the wife of [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md).**
    - [Rose Gardner](Rose_Gardner.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1425](../F/Fourth_Age_4.md)), third child of Rose Cotton and Samwise Gamgee.
    - [Primrose Gardner](../P/Primrose_Gardner.md) (born [S.R.](../S/Shire_Reckoning.md) [1435](../F/Fourth_Age_14.md)), ninth child of Rose Cotton and Samwise Gamgee.
