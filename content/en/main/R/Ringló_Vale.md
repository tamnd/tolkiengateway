The **Ringló Vale** was a valley in eastern [Lamedon](/wiki/Lamedon) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) traversed by the river [Ringló](/wiki/Ringl%C3%B3).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Description {#Description}

The valley was on the southern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains), in the east of the province of Lamedon in Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> The river Ringló had its source in a high snowfield, which fed an icy pool that spread into a shallow lake at the snowmelting season in the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-3">[3]</a></sup>

The road from the [Hill of Erech](/wiki/Hill_of_Erech) to [Linhir](/wiki/Linhir) crossed the Ringló at [Ethring](/wiki/Ethring) about 50 miles downstream near the south-western end of the valley.<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) on [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [3019](/wiki/TA "TA")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> [Dervorin](/wiki/Dervorin), the son of the lord of Ringló Vale arrived in [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on foot with a company of three hundred men to aid in the city's defence. In addition, a few hillmen without a captain from Lamedon arrived at the city.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> On [10 March](/wiki/10_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas), the [Grey Company](/wiki/Grey_Company), and the [Army of the Dead](/wiki/Oathbreakers "Oathbreakers") crossed the Ringló on their way from [Erech](/wiki/Erech) to [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Ringló* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Chillflood".<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-3">[3]</a></sup> It its composed of the elements *ring* ("cold", "chill")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> and *ló* ("flood").<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

The name of the river referred to its source, a high snowfield, which fed an icy pool that spread into a shallow lake at the snowmelting season.<sup id="cite_ref-NM_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NM-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rivers_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rivers-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

<dl><dd>Ringló Vale was an area in the region of Central Gondor, located east of <a href="/wiki/Lamedon" title="Lamedon">Lamedon</a> in Western Gondor and north of <a href="/wiki/Dor-en-Ernil" title="Dor-en-Ernil">Dor-en-Ernil</a>. Its emblem was a golden trout on a blue field. During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ring" title="War of the Ring">War of the Ring</a>, the elderly &#34;Lord Borhador&#34; ruled from the Crossing-Hall in <a href="/wiki/Ethring" title="Ethring">Ethring</a>, the only settlement. He was assisted by his other son &#34;Arvaethor&#34; in <a href="/wiki/Dervorin" title="Dervorin">Dervorin</a>&#39;s absence. The Vale was threatened by <a href="/wiki/Spiders" title="Spiders">spiders</a>, <a href="/wiki/White_Mountains" title="White Mountains">White Mountain</a> <a href="/wiki/Orcs" title="Orcs">Orcs</a>, <a href="/wiki/Corsairs_of_Umbar" title="Corsairs of Umbar">Corsair</a> scouts, and the twice-traitorous <a href="/wiki/Oathbreakers" title="Oathbreakers">Oathbreakers</a> known as the Ruthless Dead. Southwest of Ethring was the <a href="/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor" title="Warning beacons of Gondor">beacon</a> of &#34;Eilendoth&#34;.</dd></dl>
