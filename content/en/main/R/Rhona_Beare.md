Dr **Rhona Beare** ([1935](/wiki/1935)-[2018](/wiki/2018)), an English author and scholar, had a correspondence with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) during her studies at Exeter University ([Letter 211](/wiki/Letter_211), [Letter 230](/wiki/Letter_230), [Letter 245](/wiki/Letter_245), two of them published in full in *[J.R.R. Tolkien's Letters to Rhona Beare](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien%27s_Letters_to_Rhona_Beare)*). Eventually, she moved to Australia and became a Lecturer in Classics at Newcastle University, New South Wales, but returned to England in 2000.

While in Australia, Beare wrote some articles on Tolkien and [Charles Williams](/wiki/Charles_Williams) for *[Mythlore](/wiki/Mythlore)*. She also translated Tolkien's *[Songs for the Philologists](/wiki/Songs_for_the_Philologists)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
