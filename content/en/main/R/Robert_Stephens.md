**Sir Robert Stephens** ([14 July](/wiki/14_July) [1931](/wiki/1931) - [12 November](/wiki/12_November) [1995](/wiki/1995)) voiced the part of [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) in [The Lord of the Rings 1981 radio series](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1981_radio_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (1981 radio series)") broadcasted on the BBC 4.
