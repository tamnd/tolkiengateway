<i><b>Risk: The Lord of the Rings Trilogy Edition</b></i> is a modification of <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_(game)" title="wikipedia:Risk (game)">Risk</a></i>, published in [2003](../_/2003.md) by Hasbro. It serves as merchandise to [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) and follows the earlier *[Risk: The Lord of the Rings: The Middle-earth Conquest Game](Risk%253A_The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Middle-earth_Conquest_Game.md)*.

The detailed gameboard reflects a complete map of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) including the [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md), and [Haradwaith](../H/Haradwaith.md) territories, areas that had not appeared in *The Middle-earth Conquest Game*.

## From the publisher {#From_the_publisher}

In this expanded edition of RISK The Lord of the Rings Trilogy edition you will find:

- The detailed gameboard reflects a complete map of Middle-Earth including the Gondor, Mordor, and Haradwaith territories.
- Includes materials from the entire Lord of the Rings film trilogy.
- Additional Territory cards.
- Additional Battalions.
- Additional Adventure Cards.
- Alternate ways to play including special rules for alliance team play.
