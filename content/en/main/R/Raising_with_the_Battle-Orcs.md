<i><b>Raising with the Battle-Orcs</b></i> is the sixth track of the album [Lugburz](</wiki/Lugburz_(album)> "Lugburz (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name references the [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs), the footsoldiers and slaves to the [Dark Lords](/wiki/Dark_Lord "Dark Lord") of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).
