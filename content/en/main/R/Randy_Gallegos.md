**Randy Gallegos** (born [31 October](/wiki/31_October) [1974](/wiki/1974)) is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](/wiki/Iron_Crown_Enterprises) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](/wiki/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> Later on, as a [Magic: The Gathering](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering) artist, he created art for the *[Tales of Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Tales_of_Middle-earth "The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth")* and *[The Hobbit](/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit "Magic: The Gathering - The Hobbit")* expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

While he was initially an acrylic artist, he started working primarily in oils in 1998.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
