**Randy Gallegos** (born [31 October](../_/31_October.md) [1974](../_/1974.md)) is an American fantasy artist. In the 1990s, he was commissioned by [Iron Crown Enterprises](../I/Iron_Crown_Enterprises.md) to create art for the [Middle-earth Collectible Card Game](../M/Middle-earth_Collectible_Card_Game.md).<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup> Later on, as a [Magic: The Gathering](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering.md) artist, he created art for the *[Tales of Middle-earth](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Tales_of_Middle-earth.md)* and *[The Hobbit](../M/Magic%253A_The_Gathering_-_The_Hobbit.md)* expansions.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

While he was initially an acrylic artist, he started working primarily in oils in 1998.<sup id="cite_ref-CCGA_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CCGA-1">[1]</a></sup>
