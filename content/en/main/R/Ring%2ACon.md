<b>Ring*Con</b> is an annual [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) convention held in Germany.

After the box-office success of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, [Marcel Bülles](../M/Marcel_Bülles.md) and Stefan Servos, webmaster of <a class="external text" href="http://www.herr-der-ringe-film.de/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Herr-der-Ringe-film.de</a>, decided to organize a convention to bring together fans of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. The first Ring\*con, in 2002, had 1,500 guests.
