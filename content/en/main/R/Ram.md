<i><b>ram</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) noun and means "wall".<sup id="cite_ref-silmap_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmap-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Andram](../A/Andram.md)*, "Long Wall"<sup id="cite_ref-silmob_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmob-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *[Ramdal](Ramdal.md)*, "Wall's End"<sup id="cite_ref-silmob_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-silmob-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *[Rammas Echor](Rammas_Echor.md)*, "great wall of the outer ring";<sup id="cite_ref-lotrindex_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lotrindex-3">[3]</a></sup> the wall which encircled and enclosed the [Pelennor Fields](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## See also {#See_also}

- *[gobel](../G/Gobel.md)*, "walled house or village, town"
- *[ramba](Ramba.md)*, "wall" ([Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md))
