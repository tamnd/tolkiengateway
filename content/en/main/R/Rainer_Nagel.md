Dr. **Rainer Nagel** (born [20 February](../_/20_February.md) [1963](../_/1963.md)) works as a teacher in English and History at Humboldt School Wiesbaden and as a research assistant at the department of English Linguistics at the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz" title="wikipedia:University of Mainz">University of Mainz</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
