The **Royal Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup> also called the **Great Road**,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> was a major road in the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) that connected [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) in the northern kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) with [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) and [Osgiliath](../O/Osgiliath.md) in the southern kingdom of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Course {#Course}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Sage_-_Great_Road.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/13/Sage_-_Great_Road.png/300px-Sage_-_Great_Road.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="193" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/13/Sage_-_Great_Road.png/450px-Sage_-_Great_Road.png 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/1/13/Sage_-_Great_Road.png/600px-Sage_-_Great_Road.png 2x" data-file-width="667" data-file-height="428"/></a><figcaption>Course of the Great Road (highlighted with red) with its major stops.</figcaption></figure>

The Royal Road originally ran from Fornost in Arnor in the far north, southward for hundreds of leagues through [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) passing by [Bree](../B/Bree.md) (where it crossed the [East-West Road](../E/East_Road.md)), on through [Andrath](../A/Andrath.md) between the [Barrow-downs](../B/Barrow-downs.md) and the [South Downs](../S/South_Downs.md) and then southeastwards through [Cardolan](../C/Cardolan.md) on long causeways across the fens of [Minhiriath](../M/Minhiriath.md) to the bridge<sup id="cite_ref-Glanduin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glanduin-3">[3]</a></sup> over the river [Greyflood](../G/Greyflood.md) at the city of [Tharbad](../T/Tharbad.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup>

The road continued southeastwards on a long causeway across the fens on the plains of [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Glanduin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glanduin-3">[3]</a></sup> and then on the higher land in the north-east and centre of Enedwaith<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup> until it approached the southern end of the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md) where it ran eastwards to the [Fords of Isen](../F/Fords_of_Isen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup> The part of the Royal Road from Fornost to the Fords of Isen was called the North-South Road or the North Road.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Greenway_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Greenway-7">[7]</a></sup> It is possible that this part was also referred to as the Old South Road.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 1]</a></sup>

After the Fords of Isen, the road ran through [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md)/[Rohan](Rohan.md) eastwards for a few miles, then turned southeastwards approaching the [White Mountains](../W/White_Mountains.md) and then ran roughly parallel to the White Mountains on their northern side through the [Westfold](../W/Westfold.md), the [Eastfold](../E/Eastfold.md) and [Anorien](../A/Anorien.md) to the eastern end of the White Mountains where it entered the [Rammas Echor](Rammas_Echor.md) through its [north gate](../F/Forannest.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-4">[4]</a></sup> It is possible that the part of the Royal Road road from the Fords of Isen to Minas Tirith was called the [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> or the West Road.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[note 2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The Royal Road had been constructed by the [Númenóreans](../N/Númenóreans.md) by the year [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2](../T/Third_Age_2.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[13]</a></sup>

After the spreading of the [Great Plague](../G/Great_Plague.md) to Enedwaith and Mihiriath in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1636](../T/Third_Age_1636.md), Enedwaith began to return to a state of wild fen-lands and the causeways on which the road ran to the Greyflood started to crumble.<sup id="cite_ref-Glanduin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Glanduin-3">[3]</a></sup>

At the end of the Kings in Arnor and Gondor ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md)-[2050](../T/Third_Age_2050.md)) and the beginning of the decline of Gondor, the border of Gondor was retreated from the river Greyflood to the river [Isen](../I/Isen.md). The Royal Road in this land ceased to be maintained, and the Bridge of Tharbad became so ruinous that the Greyflood had to be crossed by a dangerous ford<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup> formed by the ruins of the bridge.<sup id="cite_ref-Isen_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Isen-1">[1]</a></sup>

After the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../F/Fell_Winter.md" title="Fell Winter">Fell Winter</a> of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2911](../T/Third_Age_2911.md),<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[14]</a></sup> in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2912](../T/Third_Age_2912.md) Enedwaith and Minhiriath were devastated by great floods and Tharbad was ruined and deserted.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">[15]</a></sup> It is probable that this event caused traffic on the road to decline further.

After it became less used and overgrown by grass the part of the Royal Road from Fornost to the Fords of Isen was called the [Greenway](../G/Greenway.md) by the Bree-folk.<sup id="cite_ref-Greenway_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Greenway-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Only parts of the North-South Road are taken in consideration in the narrative of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. The route is partially seen on the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md), which was published with earlier editions of *The Lord of the Rings* (and later in [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md)'s *[A Map of Middle-earth](../A/A_Map_of_Middle-earth.md)*). In those earlier maps, the portion of the road from [Enedwaith](../E/Enedwaith.md) to [Rohan](Rohan.md) is not seen, suggesting that the road was abandoned or ruined after contact and traffic was diminished.

The name **Great Road** in the *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)* refers to the road that links Arnor and Gondor, which is drawn without gaps in [the map accompanying the book](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md).<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">[16]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) interpolated the course between Enedwaith and Rohan to restore it on the new map.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">[17]</a></sup> Confusingly, the name **Great Road** in the [Appendices](../A/Appendices.md) of *The Lord of the Rings* refers to the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">[18]</a></sup>
