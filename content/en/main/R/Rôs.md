**Rôs** was the name of a promontory in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in some of the outlines for the continuation of *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was the place of the great [Battle of Rôs](/wiki/Battle_of_R%C3%B4s) during the [Faring Forth](/wiki/Faring_Forth), in which the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were defeated.<sup id="cite_ref-Ros_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ros-1">[1]</a></sup> While the precise location of the promontory is unknown, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) suggests that it might be identified with <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brittany" title="wikipedia:Brittany">Brittany</a>, a peninsula and region in modern day <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France" title="wikipedia:France">France</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

There is evidence that the [Gnomes](/wiki/Gnomes) living there possessed their own unique dialect.<sup id="cite_ref-Roos_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roos-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Rôs* is in [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish). The *[Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon)* glosses it as "the Sea; the all-embracing, the surrounding", with its [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) cognate being <i><b>Rása</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> However, it seems that this meaning of the name is another, different one from that mentioned in the main section of the article above.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Another possible origin of the name *Rôs*, might lie in the village of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roos" title="wikipedia:Roos">Roos</a> in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorkshire" title="wikipedia:Yorkshire">Yorkshire</a>, near which [Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien) was stationed in [1917](/wiki/1917)-[1918](/wiki/1918).<sup id="cite_ref-Roos_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roos-3">[3]</a></sup>

The name <i><b>Roos</b></i> probably means either "moor" or "promontory" - with its [Welsh](/wiki/Welsh), <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breton_language" title="wikipedia:Breton language">Breton</a> and <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_language" title="wikipedia:Irish language">Irish</a> cognates being *rhôs* ("moor, heath, plain"), *ros* ("hillock") and *ros* ("promontory"), respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-Roos_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roos-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In other, later outlines, Rôs was either the name of the [capital](/wiki/Kortirion#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Kortirion") of the Elves of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), or perhaps a name for Tol Eressëa itself.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
