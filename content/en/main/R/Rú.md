**Rú** is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name for the [Drúedain](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain). The Drúedain called their own kind [Drughu](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAedain "Drúedain"), a name which the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) rendered into their own languages and intonations. In [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) it became [Drû](/wiki/Dr%C3%BB), from which the name Drúedain has been derived. In Quenya, Drughu became Rú. When they were recognised by the Elves as belonging to the race of [Men](/wiki/Men), and became their allies in the war against [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth), they acquired the suffix [atani](/wiki/Atani "Atani"), so Quenya [Rúatani](/wiki/R%C3%BAatani) is exactly equivalent to Sindarin Drúedain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
