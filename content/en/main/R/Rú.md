**Rú** is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) name for the [Drúedain](../D/Drúedain.md). The Drúedain called their own kind [Drughu](../D/Drúedain.md), a name which the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) rendered into their own languages and intonations. In [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) it became [Drû](../D/Drû.md), from which the name Drúedain has been derived. In Quenya, Drughu became Rú. When they were recognised by the Elves as belonging to the race of [Men](../M/Men.md), and became their allies in the war against [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md), they acquired the suffix [atani](../A/Atani.md), so Quenya [Rúatani](Rúatani.md) is exactly equivalent to Sindarin Drúedain.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
