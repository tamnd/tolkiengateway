**Náin** can refer to three [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves):

- [Náin I](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_I) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1832](/wiki/Third_Age_1832 "Third Age 1832") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981")), the last [King of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Kings_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm "Kings of Khazad-dûm"), killed by [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane).
- [Náin II](/wiki/N%C3%A1in_II) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2338](/wiki/Third_Age_2338 "Third Age 2338") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2585](/wiki/Third_Age_2585 "Third Age 2585")), who ruled [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) from the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains).
- [Náin (son of Grór)](</wiki/N%C3%A1in_(son_of_Gr%C3%B3r)>) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2665](/wiki/Third_Age_2665 "Third Age 2665") - [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2799](/wiki/Third_Age_2799 "Third Age 2799")), who fought, and died, at the [Battle of Azanulbizar](/wiki/Battle_of_Azanulbizar).
