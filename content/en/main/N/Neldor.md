**Neldor** was a [beech-tree](../B/Beeches.md) that grew within the fenced arbour of [Pen-arduin](../P/Pen-arduin.md), the dwelling place of [Borlas](../B/Borlas.md) that was located within the [Emyn Arnen](../E/Emyn_Arnen.md) of [South Ithilien](../S/South_Ithilien.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Neldor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Neldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Neldor was a tree with cool bark and a shapely trunk that grew at the central the arbour which lay slightly below Pen-arduin near the steep east bank of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Neldor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Neldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Sometime during the year [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [103](../F/Fourth_Age_103.md), Borlas was trimming a whistle of green willow with a sharp nail-knife at the arbour of Pen-arduin in the late afternoon. During which, Saelon came upon him and they began having a discussion about the nature of evil. Soon afterwards, Borlas talked rhetorically to Neldor, before deciding to go inside his house.<sup id="cite_ref-Neldor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Neldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Neldor* is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word, meaning "beech". This was the early name of the great beech *[Hírilorn](../H/Hírilorn.md)* because that tree had three great trunks and thus the name was formed from *[neldë](Neldë.md)* ("three") and *[orn](../O/Orn.md)* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
