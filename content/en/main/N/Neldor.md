**Neldor** was a [beech-tree](/wiki/Beeches "Beeches") that grew within the fenced arbour of [Pen-arduin](/wiki/Pen-arduin), the dwelling place of [Borlas](/wiki/Borlas) that was located within the [Emyn Arnen](/wiki/Emyn_Arnen) of [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien).<sup id="cite_ref-Neldor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Neldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Neldor was a tree with cool bark and a shapely trunk that grew at the central the arbour which lay slightly below Pen-arduin near the steep east bank of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin).<sup id="cite_ref-Neldor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Neldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

Sometime during the year [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [103](/wiki/Fourth_Age_103 "Fourth Age 103"), Borlas was trimming a whistle of green willow with a sharp nail-knife at the arbour of Pen-arduin in the late afternoon. During which, Saelon came upon him and they began having a discussion about the nature of evil. Soon afterwards, Borlas talked rhetorically to Neldor, before deciding to go inside his house.<sup id="cite_ref-Neldor_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Neldor-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Neldor* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, meaning "beech". This was the early name of the great beech *[Hírilorn](/wiki/H%C3%ADrilorn)* because that tree had three great trunks and thus the name was formed from *[neldë](/wiki/Neld%C3%AB "Neldë")* ("three") and *[orn](/wiki/Orn "Orn")* ("tree").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
