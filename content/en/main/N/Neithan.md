<i><b>neithan</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word meaning "one who is deprived".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> When [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) dwelt among the [Gaurwaith](/wiki/Gaurwaith), he used it along with the alias "The Wronged".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*neithan* is derived from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") [NEK](/wiki/NEK) ("deprive"). Its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) cognate is *nehtanō*.<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-3">[3]</a></sup>
