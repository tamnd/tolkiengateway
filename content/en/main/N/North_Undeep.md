The **North Undeep** was one of two westward bends of the river [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) located between [The Wold](/wiki/The_Wold) and the [Brown Lands](/wiki/Brown_Lands) (the other being the [South Undeep](/wiki/South_Undeep)). The upper part of the North Undeep also bordered the [Field of Celebrant](/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant) and was where the river [Limlight](/wiki/Limlight) joined the Great River.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both of the Undeeps had many shallows and wide shoals that afforded an easy crossing for armies,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> which occurred a number of times during the history of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The North Undeep is located north of the river <a href="/wiki/Limlight" title="Limlight">Limlight</a> and thus belongs to the in-game &#34;Great River&#34; region, outside of Rohan proper. It does not connect the <a href="/wiki/Brown_Lands" title="Brown Lands">Brown Lands</a> to the <a href="/wiki/Field_of_Celebrant" title="Field of Celebrant">Field of Celebrant</a> directly, but rather to the <i>Rushgore</i> marsh that lies in between them. The Rohirrim have constructed and manned a fortified camp of <i>Etheburg</i> on the eastern bank of Anduin in the Brown Lands to watch over the North Undeep.</dd></dl>
