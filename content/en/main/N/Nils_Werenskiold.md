**Nils Werenskiold** ([8 October](/wiki/8_October) [1920](/wiki/1920) – [9 March](/wiki/9_March) [1991](/wiki/1991)) was an Norwegian author and translator. He was the first to translate [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* into Norwegian (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokm%C3%A5l" title="wikipedia:Bokmål">norsk riksmål</a>). The translation has been critized for being inaccurate, especially since it contains several changes made to the text.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Translations, selected {#Translations,_selected}

- *Krigen om ringen* (*The Lord of the Rings*; literally "The War of the Ring")
    - [1973](/wiki/1973): *De sorte rytterne* (*[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*; literally "The Black Riders")
    - [1974](/wiki/1974): *De to tårnene* (*[The Two Towers](/wiki/The_Two_Towers)*)
    - [1975](/wiki/1975): *Kongen kommer tilbake* (*[The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Return_of_the_King)*)
