**Nahar** was the legendary horse ridden by [Oromë](../O/Oromë.md), the [Huntsman of the Valar](../H/Huntsman_of_the_Valar.md). Nahar's coat was said to be white under the [Sun](../S/Sun.md), but to shimmer in silver in the night,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and his feet were shod in gold.<sup id="cite_ref-coming_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-coming-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1085](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1085.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Oromë was riding and hunting east near the [Orocarni](../O/Orocarni.md) when Nahar gave a great neighing and stood still. Wondering, Oromë too went still and heard afar the singing of many voices. Thus Nahar alerted his master to the existence of the awakened [Elves](../E/Elves.md). Later, in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1105](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1105.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> when the hosts of the [Eldalië](../E/Eldalië.md) left [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md), Oromë rode Nahar at their head.<sup id="cite_ref-coming_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-coming-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Melkor](../M/Melkor.md) and [Ungoliant](../U/Ungoliant.md) had attacked the [Two Trees](../T/Two_Trees.md) in [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md) in [Y.T.](../Y/Years_of_the_Trees.md) [1495](../Y/Year_of_the_Trees_1495.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> a terrible darkness fell upon the land. [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) perceived a Darkness beyond dark moving northward and Oromë began a pursuit. The sparks struck from the hooves of Nahar were the first light that returned to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
