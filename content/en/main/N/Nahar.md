**Nahar** was the legendary horse ridden by [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), the [Huntsman of the Valar](/wiki/Huntsman_of_the_Valar). Nahar's coat was said to be white under the [Sun](/wiki/Sun), but to shimmer in silver in the night,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and his feet were shod in gold.<sup id="cite_ref-coming_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-coming-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1085](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1085 "Year of the Trees 1085")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Oromë was riding and hunting east near the [Orocarni](/wiki/Orocarni) when Nahar gave a great neighing and stood still. Wondering, Oromë too went still and heard afar the singing of many voices. Thus Nahar alerted his master to the existence of the awakened [Elves](/wiki/Elves). Later, in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1105](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1105 "Year of the Trees 1105"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> when the hosts of the [Eldalië](/wiki/Eldali%C3%AB) left [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen), Oromë rode Nahar at their head.<sup id="cite_ref-coming_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-coming-2">[2]</a></sup>

After [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor) and [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) had attacked the [Two Trees](/wiki/Two_Trees) in [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) in [Y.T.](/wiki/Years_of_the_Trees "Years of the Trees") [1495](/wiki/Year_of_the_Trees_1495 "Year of the Trees 1495")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> a terrible darkness fell upon the land. [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) perceived a Darkness beyond dark moving northward and Oromë began a pursuit. The sparks struck from the hooves of Nahar were the first light that returned to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
