<i><b>Nightshade Forests</b></i> is the second track of the album [Dol Guldur](</wiki/Dol_Guldur_(album)> "Dol Guldur (album)") by the band [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning). The song name is taken from <a class="mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin_(disambiguation)" title="Taur-nu-Fuin (disambiguation)">Taur-nu-Fuin</a>, the name given to the forest of [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion) after it fell into darkness in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), and the dark forest of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) in the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age). Like the song [Lugburz](</wiki/Lugburz_(song)> "Lugburz (song)") from the album [Minas Morgul](</wiki/Minas_Morgul_(album)> "Minas Morgul (album)"), the lyrics directly reference the [incantation](/wiki/Cold_be_hand_and_heart_and_bone, "Cold be hand and heart and bone,") chanted by a [Barrow-wight](/wiki/Barrow-wights "Barrow-wights") who had trapped [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins"), [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee"), [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") and [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") in the [Fog on the Barrow-downs](/wiki/Fog_on_the_Barrow-downs) chapter of [The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring).
