<i><b>númen</b></i> is a noun meaning "west" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Numen.gif"><img src="/w/images/b/be/Numen.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Númen* is also the name in Quenya of the seventeenth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the first letter of the fifth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "nasal" consonants and it is the fifth letter in the dental or *t*-series of consonants, the *[Tincotéma](/wiki/Tincot%C3%A9ma)*. In most modes this letter represents **N**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> However, in the [Mode of Beleriand](/wiki/Mode_of_Beleriand), this letter represents **NN**, a common occurrence in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *Númen* was one of the most widely known and used of all the Tengwar since it indicated "west" even in languages that used a very different term.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Nó</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NDU&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDU (page does not exist)">NDU</a> plus [MEN](/wiki/MEN), indicating direction.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Also [PQ](/wiki/PQ) *nūme-n* "going down", sunset, West.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In the West-lands of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) the four cardinal directions were *Númen, [Hyarmen](/wiki/Hyarmen), [Rómen](/wiki/R%C3%B3men),* and *[Formen](/wiki/Formen)*, and they were named in this order, beginning with (and facing) west. *Hyarmen* meant a left-hand region and *Formen* meant a right-hand region.<sup id="cite_ref-Names2_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names2-7">[7]</a></sup>
