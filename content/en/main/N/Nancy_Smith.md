**Nancy Smith** compiled [the index](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Index "The Lord of the Rings Index") to *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. She was married to a former roommate of [Christopher Tolkien's](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien "Christopher Tolkien").

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) already had been working on [a detailed index with linguistic information](/wiki/Unfinished_index "Unfinished index") but expressed a desire of having a complete alphabetical "handlist" that would help him in several matters; [Rayner Unwin](/wiki/Rayner_Unwin) replied that if such a list could be compiled without much thinking, but as a "donkey work", they could arrange it. Smith was working as an indexer in [Allen &amp; Unwin](/wiki/Allen_%26_Unwin), and was pregnant when she started the compilation in late [January](/wiki/January) [1958](/wiki/1958), and completed it in [May](/wiki/May) (in a rush, because of her condition). Smith was instructed to ignore the [Appendices](/wiki/Appendices).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
