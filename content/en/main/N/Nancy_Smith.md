**Nancy Smith** compiled [the index](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Index.md) to *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. She was married to a former roommate of [Christopher Tolkien's](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md).

[J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) already had been working on [a detailed index with linguistic information](../U/Unfinished_index.md) but expressed a desire of having a complete alphabetical "handlist" that would help him in several matters; [Rayner Unwin](../R/Rayner_Unwin.md) replied that if such a list could be compiled without much thinking, but as a "donkey work", they could arrange it. Smith was working as an indexer in [Allen &amp; Unwin](../A/Allen_&_Unwin.md), and was pregnant when she started the compilation in late [January](../J/January.md) [1958](../_/1958.md), and completed it in [May](../M/May.md) (in a rush, because of her condition). Smith was instructed to ignore the [Appendices](../A/Appendices.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
