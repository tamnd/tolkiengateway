**"Norman Keeps"** was a [Man](/wiki/Man) living in the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/20th_century" title="wikipedia:20th century">20th century</a> <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England" title="wikipedia:England">England</a>.

## History {#History}

*Norman Keeps* was not his actual name, but rather a nickname that [Lowdham](/wiki/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham "Alwin Arundel Lowdham") and [Frankley](/wiki/Philip_Frankley "Philip Frankley") gave him out of the <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keeps" title="wikipedia:Keeps">Keeps</a> he mentioned. He was their barber, and a nice and moderately intelligent men, but to him, everything that the "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Ages" title="wikipedia:Dark Ages">Dark Ages</a>" stood for were "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keeps" title="wikipedia:Keeps">Norman Keeps</a>", "<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_James" title="wikipedia:King James">Them Jameses</a>", "The Squires" and "The People".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*Norman Keeps* was an actual person, who expounded to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) the view of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_history" title="wikipedia:English history">English history</a> as recounted by the eponymous character in the story. He was a barber too.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
