The **North Polar Bear** (also, the **Great Polar Bear**<sup id="cite_ref-l29b_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29b-2">[1]</a></sup>) was a polar bear who lived with [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md) at the North Pole. He is especially noted for being "helpful" and for causing a lot of accidents. The Polar Bear was, according to Father Christmas, magical,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> and he sometimes wrote comments on Father Christmas' letters and even letters of his own.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

The North Polar Bear started out as very foolish, unrepentant, and temperamental. When he broke the North Pole and the house while climbing the North Pole. Father Christmas was angry with him and so the Polar Bear refused to help with the presents.<sup id="cite_ref-l25_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-5">[4]</a></sup> He was most unrepentant when he turned on the Northern Lights, causing a huge bang and lots of inconvenience,<sup id="cite_ref-l26_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l26-6">[5]</a></sup> and when he caused lists to be blown about and mixed up, he laughed.<sup id="cite_ref-l29b_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29b-2">[1]</a></sup> The Polar Bear often stuck his nose in places it shouldn't be (including Father Christmas' cabinets)<sup id="cite_ref-l27_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l27-7">[6]</a></sup> He could be hurt easily - he ran away in shame after he fell down some steps.<sup id="cite_ref-l28_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l28-8">[7]</a></sup> The Polar Bear was very lazy, leading to accidents, including an explosion of crackers.<sup id="cite_ref-l31b_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l31b-9">[8]</a></sup>

But, in later letters, the North Polar Bear was nicer and more considerate. He was noted as being "a nice old thing" by [Father Christmas](../F/Father_Christmas.md). One year, he noticed strange smells and so, not wanting to trouble Father Christmas, he slept in the kitchen, which had steps going down to the cellars. This led to his saving Christmas, because [Goblins](../G/Goblins.md) attacked - "the worst attack in centuries" - and the Polar Bear killed many, preventing them from killing Father Christmas and the other helpers. Father Christmas called him splendid, and the Polar Bear's comment was "Say no more; I enjoyed it immensely!".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

The North Polar Bear sometimes wrote a letter as well as Father Christmas. In these, he attributed his mistakes in spelling to the fact that [Arktik](../A/Arktik.md) is the language spoken at the North Pole, instead of English.<sup id="cite_ref-l29a_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29a-11">[10]</a></sup> His handwriting was thick because he had large paws,<sup id="cite_ref-l25_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-5">[4]</a></sup> but he learned to write and paint with his mouth.<sup id="cite_ref-l28_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l28-8">[7]</a></sup> Father Christmas stated that his drawing skills were bad which he vehemently denied.<sup id="cite_ref-l28_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l28-8">[7]</a></sup>

His real name was **Karhu** (Finnish for 'bear').<sup id="cite_ref-l29a_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29a-11">[10]</a></sup> He had two nephews, [Paksu](../P/Paksu.md) and [Valkotukka](../V/Valkotukka.md) (respectively Finnish for 'fat' and 'white hair'),<sup id="cite_ref-l31b_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l31b-9">[8]</a></sup> and the Great Bear ([Big Dipper](../C/Constellations.md)) was his cousin.<sup id="cite_ref-l27_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l27-7">[6]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In November of [1925](../_/1925.md) Father Christmas's hood flew off and was blown by the wind to the top of the roof. Despite Father Christmas telling him not to, he went on to the roof to try to get it back and fell through the roof, making a hole in the living room and breaking his leg. Father Christmas had to move to a new house causing him much stress.<sup id="cite_ref-l25_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l25-5">[4]</a></sup>

The next year the North Polar bear set off a firework that shook the stars and broke the moon. The [Man in the Moon](../M/Man_in_the_Moon.md) fell into Father Christmas' garden, ate some chocolate and went back to the moon. He then turned on two years of Northern Lights in one go.<sup id="cite_ref-l26_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l26-6">[5]</a></sup> Because of this there were no Northern Light until [1928](../_/1928.md).<sup id="cite_ref-l27_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l27-7">[6]</a></sup>

The North Polar Bear was no less trouble making in 1927 when he put his tongue against the North Pole and pulled the skin of his tongue off. Later when snowballing the [Snow Man](../S/Snow_Man.md) he accidentally rolled him off the cliff breaking him.<sup id="cite_ref-l27_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l27-7">[6]</a></sup>

In 1928 a bar of stairs was left on the stairs and the North Polar Bear fell down the stairs spilling Christmas decorations.<sup id="cite_ref-l28_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l28-8">[7]</a></sup> He forgot to send that year's letter until [December 26](../D/December_26.md).<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>

In [1929](../_/1929.md) the North Polar Bear spent the summer improving his spelling so he could help Father Christmas with the Christmas letters. At the beginning of winter a bonfire was thrown to celebrate and he lot off 20,000 silver sparklers. He then spent time in Norway with a woodcutter named Olaf. While cutting Christmas trees in Norway he chopped his right paw,<sup id="cite_ref-l29b_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29b-2">[1]</a></sup> but by November he reported that he felt well again.<sup id="cite_ref-l29a_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29a-11">[10]</a></sup> While helping Father Christmas write down the names of the children that needed presents (of which there were many), he complained that it was "stuffy" and opened a window. The wind blew in and he was buried under papers and lists.<sup id="cite_ref-l29a_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l29a-11">[10]</a></sup>

The next year he got whooping cough, made worse by being buried under the snow during a snowstorm. Despite this he did his best to be helpful, mending the sleigh and looking after the reindeer (which is how he got stuck in the snowstorm). He quarreled with the Snow Man and pushed him into the roof of his snow house. He decided to put ice instead of presents in the parcels of naughty children and they melted all got the good children's presents wet too.<sup id="cite_ref-l30_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l30-13">[12]</a></sup>

In [1931](../_/1931.md) he ate gifts containing food because "to make sure they were still good" (He found stones in the currants). He was sent down to the cellar to grab fireworks and being lazy brought two snow-boys (Young snowmen)<sup id="cite_ref-l30_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l30-13">[12]</a></sup> down with to help him. A candle was knocked into a box of fireworks and sparklers and when Father Christmas rushed down to see what the commotion was, found him on fire. His nephews Paksu and Valkotukka were visiting and like their uncle were very accident prone and mischievous.<sup id="cite_ref-l31b_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-l31b-9">[8]</a></sup>
