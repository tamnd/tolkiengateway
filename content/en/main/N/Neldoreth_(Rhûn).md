**Neldoreth** was the name of the forest bordering the north-eastern shores of the [Sea of Rhûnaer](/wiki/Sea_of_Rh%C3%BBn "Sea of Rhûn"), only named in [the First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings"). This forest went unnamed in later maps.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
