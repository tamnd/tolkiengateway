**Neldoreth** was the name of the forest bordering the north-eastern shores of the [Sea of Rhûnaer](../S/Sea_of_Rhûn.md), only named in [the First Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md). This forest went unnamed in later maps.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
