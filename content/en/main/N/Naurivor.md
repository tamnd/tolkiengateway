**Naurivor** is a plant that was created for *[the Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North "The Lord of the Rings: War in the North")* video game. It is an alchemical ingredient that can be used by [Andriel](/wiki/Andriel), a master of Herblore, to make potions and other materials.
