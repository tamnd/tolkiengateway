The **North Moors** lay along the northern border of the [Shire](../S/Shire.md) in the [Northfarthing](Northfarthing.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Geography {#Geography}

The North Moors was located roughly fifty leagues (150 miles) north of the [Marshes in the south](../M/Marshes_in_the_south.md).<sup id="cite_ref-FRHobbits_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FRHobbits-1">[1]</a></sup> No elm trees grew in the North Moors,<sup id="cite_ref-Tree_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tree-2">[2]</a></sup> and it was the one region within the bounds of the Shire where heavy snowfalls were common.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Around the spring of [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Samwise Gamgee](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) heard a rumor that his cousin, [Halfast Gamgee](../H/Halfast_Gamgee.md), reported seeing [Tree-men](../T/Tree-men.md) or [giants](../G/Giants.md) away beyond the North Moors.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Halfast might have witnessed some kind of [troll](../T/Trolls.md)<sup id="cite_ref-unpublished_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-unpublished-5">[5]</a></sup> or one of some [Ents](../E/Ents.md) that [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) had asked to keep a watch on the Shire.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
