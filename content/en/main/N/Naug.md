<i><b>naug</b></i> (plural *noeg*) is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word, meaning "dwarf".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Properly speaking, this is an adjective, while the correct independent word for "Dwarf" as a race is *[Nogoth](/wiki/Nogoth)*,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> but the word *naug* can appear as an element in names referring to the Dwarves.

## Examples {#Examples}

- *[Naugrim](/wiki/Naugrim)*
- *[Nauglamír](/wiki/Nauglam%C3%ADr)*
- *[Nibin-noeg](/wiki/Nibin-noeg)*
