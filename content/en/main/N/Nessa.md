**Nessa** was the wife of [Tulkas](/wiki/Tulkas) and sister of [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB). In "due order," Nessa was the last named [Valië](/wiki/Valier "Valier").<sup id="cite_ref-SIIb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIIb-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Nessa was noted for her speed and was said to be "swift as an arrow" and she was able to outrun the [deer](/wiki/Deer "Deer") who followed her in the wild. She was also renowned for her dancing ability, as she danced on the ever-green lawns of [Valimar](/wiki/Valimar).<sup id="cite_ref-SIIb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIIb-1">[1]</a></sup> She wed Tulkas upon the Isle of [Almaren](/wiki/Almaren)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in the [Year of the Valar](/wiki/Days_before_days "Days before days") 3400.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nessa* (<span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈnesːa]</span>) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "young"; in the same text, Nessa has also the name **[Indis](/wiki/Indis)** "bride".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:14</sup>

The [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) names of Nessa were <i><b>Neth</b></i> (a cognate of the Quenya/[Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) word) or <i><b>Dineth</b></i>, a Noldorin word meaning "bride", related to the Quenya word *indis*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

In a later text, [Pengolodh](/wiki/Pengolodh) notes that Nessa's name is "not [elvish](/wiki/Elvish "Elvish")" and probably represents a [Valarin](/wiki/Valarin) title, much like the name of her spouse, adopted in Quenya, but there is no record.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, Nessa was one of the [Valarindi](/wiki/Valarindi), daughter of [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna). Her son with Tulkas was [Telimektar](/wiki/Telimektar).

Nessa was seen at the [house of Tulkas](/wiki/House_of_Tulkas "House of Tulkas") bringing goblets of wine and drinks to his champions.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:75</sup>

Nessa loved to retire to the green swards which her brother, [Oromë](/wiki/Orom%C3%AB), had cultivated from the richness of his forest.<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:75</sup> When creating the forests of Oromë, [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) had planted forest glades with spells so that the glades would always remain green and smooth. Nessa, along with her maidens, would dance there as long as [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) was in bloom, and she was said to be an even better dancer than [Vána](/wiki/V%C3%A1na).<sup id="cite_ref-Coming_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Coming-8">[8]</a></sup>

### The Annals of Aman {#The_Annals_of_Aman}

In revisions to [The Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman) "Nessa the Young" was removed from the ranks of the Valar and, along with [Estë](/wiki/Est%C3%AB), made to be among the highest of the [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar).<sup id="cite_ref-aams1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-aams1-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§3</sup>

In further emendations, Tolkien split the character between "Nessa the Evermaid", and "Lëa the Young" ("Lëa-vinya") who was the bride of Tulkas.<sup id="cite_ref-aams1_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-aams1-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§3</sup> Lëa was the fairest of the maidens of [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna).<sup id="cite_ref-aams1_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-aams1-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:§18</sup>

### Early etymology {#Early_etymology}

Her other names in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) were <i><b>Helinyetille</b></i> meaning "Eyes of Heartsease", from *helin* ("violet, pansy"), and <i><b>Melesta</b></i>, from *mele* ("to love").<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
