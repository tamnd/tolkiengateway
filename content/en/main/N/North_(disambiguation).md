## Other writings {#Other_writings}

In the Foreword of *[The End of Bovadium](../T/The_End_of_Bovadium.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-CGL_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CGL-1">[1]</a></sup>, [Doctor Sarevelk](../D/Doctor_Sarevelk.md) labeled the Vastians who dwelt north of the *Via Maxima*<sup id="cite_ref-FII_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FII-2">[2]</a></sup> in *[Bovadium](../B/Bovadium.md)* as **B-people** since they were the speakers of [Language B](../M/Modern_English.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Foreword_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foreword-3">[3]</a></sup> In Fragment II of the text, the B-people<sup id="cite_ref-Foreword_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foreword-3">[3]</a></sup> are referred to as **Northerners**. During the takeover of the city by the *Motores*, the Northerners became jealous after finding out that the [Southerners](../S/Southerners.md) were still able to enjoy a peace free from the *Motores*. Because the Northerners thought that the Southerners should share their misery, they proposed to the Minister that a new road should be built "through the southern meadows" to free up the *Via Maxima* from the Traffic Problem. The Minister choose not to do anything proposed by the Northerners or the Southerners and would have proceeded with his own plan had he not gotten trapped in the <a class="mw-disambig" href="../C/Capital_City.md" title="Capital City">Capital City</a>. The debate of the Northerners and the Southerners never reached a resolution because they were all "slain by the poisonous fumes" of the *Motores*.<sup id="cite_ref-FII_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FII-2">[2]</a></sup>

In a [related poem](../D/Domine_defende_nos_contra_hos_Motores_bos!.md), the Northerners are referred to as the **North-folk** and **North-men**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
