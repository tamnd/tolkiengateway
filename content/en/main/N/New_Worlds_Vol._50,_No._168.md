<b><i>New Worlds</i> Vol. 50, No. 168</b> is an issue of the British science fiction magazine <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Worlds_(magazine)" title="wikipedia:New Worlds (magazine)">New Worlds</a></i>.

The article "The Realms of Tolkien" by [Daphne Castell](/wiki/Daphne_Castell) includes extracts from [an interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") with [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), mainly discussing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* (see pp. 143–154).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
