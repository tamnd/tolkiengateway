**Nan** can refer to:

- [nan](</wiki/Nan_(Sindarin_noun)> "Nan (Sindarin noun)"), a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "valley".

- [Nan](</wiki/Nan_(Giant)> "Nan (Giant)"), a [Giant](/wiki/Giant) mentioned in the [Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel).
- [Nan Smithson](/wiki/Nan_Smithson), daughter of [Smith](/wiki/Smith_Smithson "Smith Smithson") and [Nell Smithson](/wiki/Nell_Smithson).
