**Nurtalë Valinóreva**, [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for the "Hiding of Valinor", was the time and action taken by the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) to render [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) unassailable by [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth). It was prompted by Morgoth's attack upon [Tilion](/wiki/Tilion), the [Maia](/wiki/Maia) charged with guidance of the [Moon](/wiki/Moon). [Shadowy spirits](/wiki/Evil_spirits "Evil spirits") had been sent against Tilion and there had been a battle in [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen) that Tilion had won. But although Morgoth had failed in this assault the Valar feared the damage he might yet contrive against Valinor.

To permanently thwart any attack they caused the [Pelóri](/wiki/Pel%C3%B3ri) to rise up to new and dreadful heights, with outer walls as smooth as glass. In the [Calacirya](/wiki/Calacirya), the only pass permitted through the mountains, strong towers were set. In the ocean the [Enchanted Isles](/wiki/Enchanted_Isles) were strewn in the [Shadowy Seas](/wiki/Shadowy_Seas) north, east, and south of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Additionally [Varda](/wiki/Varda), lifting her hands palms eastward in a gesture of rejection, summoned the vast mists and shadows that made it impossible for any living thing to find again the way westward to the shores of Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In these waters were dark rocks, and in the twilight any mariner would grow weary and loath the sea. If a sailor reached one of the isles he would sleep until the [world was changed](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World "Changing of the World").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

These defences held fast until [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) with a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) overcame all obstacles on his voyage to Valinor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
