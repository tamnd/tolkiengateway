**Narzug** was an [Orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs") who was a part of [Azog's](/wiki/Azog "Azog") pack. Narzug was invented by [Warner Bros.](/wiki/Warner_Bros.) for [*Hobbit* film series](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)"), where he was portrayed by <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ben_Mitchell&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ben Mitchell (page does not exist)">Ben Mitchell</a>.

## History {#History}

When the pack had caught up with [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) and his [Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") during their escape from the [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm), Narzug was ultimately taken prisoner by [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Tauriel](/wiki/Tauriel), and they took him with them back to the [Elvenking's Halls](/wiki/Elvenking%27s_Halls). [Thranduil](/wiki/Thranduil), promising to spare his life, interrogated about why the pack had been pursuing the company of [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves), and Narzug claimed that his master was in the service of the "[One](/wiki/Sauron "Sauron")"; this caused some alarm to the Elven-king, triggering him to cut off Narzug's head. Legolas complained to his father that he promised otherwise to the captive, with Thranduil dismissing it, saying that he did a favor to the Orc.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
