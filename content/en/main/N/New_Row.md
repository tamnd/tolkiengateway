The **New Row** was a row of [hobbit-holes](../H/Hobbit-holes.md) cut into the southern face of [Hobbiton Hill](../H/Hobbiton_Hill.md) after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md). They were made to replace [Bagshot Row](../B/Bagshot_Row.md), which had been destroyed by [Sharkey's Men](../R/Ruffians.md). It became a jest in [Bywater](../B/Bywater.md) to refer to New Row as "Sharkey's End", since [Saruman](../S/Saruman.md) (Sharkey) died there.

After the restoration the [Gaffer Gamgee](../G/Gaffer_Gamgee.md) returned to Number 3, and his son [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) lived with him until Sam married [Rose Cotton](../R/Rose_Cotton.md) and relocated to Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
