The **New Row** was a row of [hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-holes "Hobbit-holes") cut into the southern face of [Hobbiton Hill](/wiki/Hobbiton_Hill) after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring). They were made to replace [Bagshot Row](/wiki/Bagshot_Row), which had been destroyed by [Sharkey's Men](/wiki/Ruffians "Ruffians"). It became a jest in [Bywater](/wiki/Bywater) to refer to New Row as "Sharkey's End", since [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman) (Sharkey) died there.

After the restoration the [Gaffer Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee) returned to Number 3, and his son [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") lived with him until Sam married [Rose Cotton](/wiki/Rose_Cotton) and relocated to Bag End.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
