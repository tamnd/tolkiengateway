**Náin II** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [2338](/wiki/Third_Age_2338 "Third Age 2338") - [2585](/wiki/Third_Age_2585 "Third Age 2585"), died age 247) was the King of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) for 97 years, succeeding his father [Óin](</wiki/%C3%93in_(King_of_Durin%27s_Folk)> "Óin (King of Durin's Folk)") upon his death in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2488](/wiki/Third_Age_2488 "Third Age 2488"), being the heir of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Durin" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Náin became King of the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of Durin shortly after the end of the [Watchful Peace](/wiki/Watchful_Peace), when [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) returned to [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) from out of the East. The majority of Durin's Folk at this time dwelt in the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains), which had been their home since [Thorin I](/wiki/Thorin_I) had moved there from [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") in order to mine their riches. During his time, [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) increased in the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), and the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) removed from their northern lands adjacent to the Grey Mountains to settle in [Calenardhon](/wiki/Calenardhon). Towards the end of Náin's reign, [dragons](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") from the [wastes to the north](</wiki/Forodwaith_(lands)> "Forodwaith (lands)") began to come south and make [war on the Dwarves](/wiki/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons "War of the Dwarves and Dragons").

Nain was the ancestor of seven of the thirteen Dwarves of [Thorin and Company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company) ([Thorin](/wiki/Thorin), [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli), [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli), [Balin](/wiki/Balin), [Dwalin](/wiki/Dwalin), [Óin](/wiki/%C3%93in), and [Glóin](/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in)) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli) [Elf-friend](/wiki/Elf-friend) of the [Company of the Ring](/wiki/Company_of_the_Ring).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Náinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It means "Corpselike".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
