**Náin II** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [2338](../T/Third_Age_2338.md) - [2585](../T/Third_Age_2585.md), died age 247) was the King of [Durin's Folk](../D/Durin's_Folk~15256.md) for 97 years, succeeding his father [Óin](<../_/Óin_(King_of_Durin's_Folk).md>) upon his death in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2488](../T/Third_Age_2488.md), being the heir of <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../D/Durin.md" title="Durin">Durin the Deathless</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-AppA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AppA-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Náin became King of the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of Durin shortly after the end of the [Watchful Peace](../W/Watchful_Peace.md), when [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) returned to [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) from out of the East. The majority of Durin's Folk at this time dwelt in the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md), which had been their home since [Thorin I](../T/Thorin_I.md) had moved there from [Erebor](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) in order to mine their riches. During his time, [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) increased in the [Misty Mountains](../M/Misty_Mountains.md), and the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) removed from their northern lands adjacent to the Grey Mountains to settle in [Calenardhon](../C/Calenardhon.md). Towards the end of Náin's reign, [dragons](../D/Dragons.md) from the [wastes to the north](<../F/Forodwaith_(lands).md>) began to come south and make [war on the Dwarves](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons.md).

Nain was the ancestor of seven of the thirteen Dwarves of [Thorin and Company](../T/Thorin_and_Company.md) ([Thorin](../T/Thorin.md), [Fíli](../F/Fíli.md), [Kíli](../K/Kíli.md), [Balin](../B/Balin.md), [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md), [Óin](../_/Óin.md), and [Glóin](../G/Glóin.md)) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md) [Elf-friend](../E/Elf-friend.md) of the [Company of the Ring](../C/Company_of_the_Ring.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Náinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. It means "Corpselike".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
