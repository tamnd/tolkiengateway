**Nereb** (reversal of the name *Beren*) was the [orc](/wiki/Orcs "Orcs")-name that [Beren](/wiki/Beren) took upon himself when in that disguise during the [Quest for the Silmaril](/wiki/Quest_for_the_Silmaril). His companion, [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod"), called himself [Dungalef](/wiki/Dungalef).<sup id="cite_ref-LB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LB-1">[1]</a></sup>
