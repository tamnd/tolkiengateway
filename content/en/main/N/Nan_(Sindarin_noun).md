## Sindarin {#Sindarin}

<i><b>nan</b></i> or (<i><b>nand-</b></i>, <i><b>nan(d)</b></i>), is a noun meaning "valley, vale" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *[Nan Curunír](/wiki/Nan_Curun%C3%ADr#Etymology)*
- *[Nan Dungortheb](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb#Etymology)*
- *[Nanduhirion](/wiki/Nanduhirion)*

## Noldorin/Doriathrin {#Noldorin/Doriathrin}

<i><b>Nan</b></i> is a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) (or perhaps [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin)) word glossed as "land at foot of hills with many streams",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Noldorin <i><b>nand</b></i>, <i><b>nann</b></i> is glossed as "wide grassland", and Doriathrin <i><b>nand</b></i> is glossed as "field, valley".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>
