**Narsil** was the sword of King [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain). It was later known as **[Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril)**.

## History {#History}

The sword was forged "in the deeps of time", probably during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age), by [Telchar](/wiki/Telchar),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who was likely the famed Dwarven-smith of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) of the same name who made swords for the armories of King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Nothing is said of Narsil during the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) until it eventually came into the hands of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil) towards the close of the Second Age.

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/e0/John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg/200px-John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="133" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/e0/John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg/300px-John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/e0/John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg/400px-John_Howe_-_The_Breaking_of_Narsil.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="799"/></a><figcaption>&#34;The Breaking of Narsil&#34; by <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a></figcaption></figure>

Elendil carried Narsil in the [Battle of Dagorlad](/wiki/Battle_of_Dagorlad) where it shone with the light of the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon), and then in the [Siege of Barad-dûr](/wiki/Siege_of_Barad-d%C3%BBr); but [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) killed him and the sword broke into two pieces under him as he fell, and its light was extinguished. Elendil's son [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) took up the sword and used its shard to cut [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") from the hand of Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup> Isildur took the shards home with him. Shortly before Isildur was killed in the second year of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) in the [Disaster of the Gladden Fields](/wiki/Disaster_of_the_Gladden_Fields), the shards were rescued by [Ohtar](/wiki/Ohtar), esquire of Isildur.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> He took them to [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> where Isildur's youngest son [Valandil](</wiki/Valandil_(King_of_Arnor)> "Valandil (King of Arnor)") was fostered, but [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) foretold that it wouldn't be reforged until the One Ring was found again and Sauron returned.<sup id="cite_ref-rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rings-5">[5]</a></sup>

The **Shards of Narsil** were one of the heirlooms of the [Kings of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor), and after the Northern Kingdom was destroyed they remained an heirloom of the [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> Elrond delivered the shards of Narsil to [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) when he was twenty years old.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> It was not reforged until the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) at the end of the Third Age in Rivendell as **[Andúril](/wiki/And%C3%BAril)**, the "Flame of the West", for Aragorn, who was by that time the [Chieftain of the Dúnedain](/wiki/Chieftains_of_the_D%C3%BAnedain "Chieftains of the Dúnedain") and heir of Isildur.

He carried the sword during his journey south as part of the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring), and fought with it in many instances. He referred to it as the "Sword That Was Broken" or "The Sword Reforged".

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Narsil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name meaning "red and white flame".<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> The name is said to consist of the [stems](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") NAR ("fire"; cf. *[nár](</wiki/N%C3%A1r_(Quenya)> "Nár (Quenya)")* "fire") + THIL ("white light"). It was a symbolic name, pointing to the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the [Moon](/wiki/Moon), the "*chief heavenly lights, as enemies of darkness*".<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup>

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In Jackson&#39;s films, Narsil is depicted as a straight, double-edged longsword. It possesses a straight crossguard with axe-like decorations on the tips. The grip is wrapped in black and the pommel is elongated, with a hollow bottom.</dd>
<dd>Narsil was not broken in the fall of Elendil, but by Sauron stepping on the blade as Isildur tried to pull it up. Furthermore, the blade was shattered into many parts, instead of a clean cut into two parts.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Unlike in the book, Aragorn does not bear the shards with him, but they are kept on a display in <a href="/wiki/Rivendell" title="Rivendell">Rivendell</a>. Boromir drops the hilt on the floor after examining it and Aragorn is seen reverently replacing it back into the display. The sword is not reforged into Andúril until the third film.</dd></dl>

<b>2003: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King">The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a href="/wiki/Arwen" title="Arwen">Arwen</a> persuades her father, <a href="/wiki/Elrond" title="Elrond">Elrond</a> to have <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elven</a> smiths reforge it from the shards and bring to Aragorn.</dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The reforging of Narsil is experienced by the player in the quest arc called &#34;The Blade that was Broken&#34;. The player sets out to find the last of the Silithair, shining adamants crafted by Elves of the <a href="/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor" title="House of Fëanor">house of Fëanor</a> in days of old, in order for the Elf-smiths of Imladris to reforge the sword.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2012: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit:_An_Unexpected_Journey" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the Extended Edition, <a href="/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins" title="Bilbo Baggins">Bilbo Baggins</a> approaches and examines the shards in curiosity while in Rivendell, before he notices the mural of Isildur&#39;s battle with Sauron (the latter of whom is wearing the One Ring). Although the Shards are not mentioned in <a href="/wiki/The_Hobbit" title="The Hobbit">the book</a>, it establishes continuity with <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson&#39;s</a> <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd>In episode 4, <a href="/wiki/The_Great_Wave" title="The Great Wave"><i>The Great Wave</i></a>, Narsil is briefly glimpsed when <a href="/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel" title="Tar-Míriel">Míriel</a> shows <a href="/wiki/Galadriel" title="Galadriel">Galadriel</a> the <a href="/wiki/Palant%C3%ADri" title="Palantíri">Palantir</a>. <b>2024: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power">The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power</a></i>: <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_-_Season_2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power - Season 2 (page does not exist)">Season 2</a></b>

<dl><dd>In episode 8, <i><a href="/wiki/Shadow_and_Flame_(episode)" title="Shadow and Flame (episode)">Shadow and Flame</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Elendil" title="Elendil">Elendil</a> plans to leave <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor" title="Númenor">Númenor</a> with <a href="/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel" title="Tar-Míriel">Tar-Míriel</a> to escape <a href="/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n" title="Ar-Pharazôn">Ar-Pharazôn</a> and meet with <a href="/wiki/An%C3%A1rion" title="Anárion">Anárion</a> and other <a href="/wiki/Faithful" title="Faithful">faithful</a>. Tar-Míriel decides to stay but encourages Elendil to leave. She gestures towards a sheathed sword and tells him its name is Narsil. Elendil recognises this as the <a href="/wiki/Quenya" title="Quenya">quenya</a> word meaning &#34;the white flame&#34;. Tar-Míriel tells him &#34;Reclaim your Lordship and with this sword, your destiny&#34; and Elendil unsheaths it.</dd></dl>
