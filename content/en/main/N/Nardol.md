**Nardol** was the third of seven [Beacon-hills](/wiki/Warning_beacons_of_Gondor "Warning beacons of Gondor") of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), which ran west from [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) on the northern side of the [White Mountains](/wiki/White_Mountains) that looked down on the plains of [Anórien](/wiki/An%C3%B3rien).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> It stood on the broad end of a ridge of the White Mountains, west of the [Drúadan Forest](/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest).

## Description {#Description}

Nardol was the place of a guard station well-stored with fuel to support a large signal fire, which could be seen on a clear night as far way as the [Halifirien](/wiki/Halifirien) (some 120 miles to the west). The guard of Nardol also protected the quarries.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-RC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-3">[3]</a></sup>. A line of hills ran from Nardol to [Amon Dîn](/wiki/Amon_D%C3%AEn).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On the night of [7 March](/wiki/7_March) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took"), riding eastward on [Shadowfax](/wiki/Shadowfax), saw the beacons being lit to call for aid from Rohan.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nardol* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), meaning "fire-hilltop" (*[naur](/wiki/Naur "Naur")* "fire" + *[dol](/wiki/Dol "Dol")* "hill"), because its beacon-fire was so bright.<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-7">[7]</a></sup>
