The **North Borneo Frodo Society** was the [Tolkien society](/wiki/Tolkien_Societies "Tolkien Societies") in Malaysia, founded by Mr. McKee in 1971. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) himself was a member of this society, and his membership card was reproduced in *[The Tolkien Family Album](/wiki/The_Tolkien_Family_Album)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
