Canon<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup> **Norman Sandiford Power** ([31 October](/wiki/31_October) [1916](/wiki/1916) - May [1993](/wiki/1993)) received his B.A. at Worcester College, [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford), in 1938. He went on to be associated with the parish of Ladywood in [Birmingham](/wiki/Birmingham).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[1]</a></sup>

Power attended [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s invitational speech at the Lovelace Society in [1938](/wiki/1938).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup>

By the end of the life of Tolkien, they renewed their contact (at least one letter is recorded, see [Letter to Norman Power](/wiki/Letter_to_Norman_Power)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>How strange that I had no further contact with Tolkien until the nineteen-seventies when I had already been Vicar of Ladywood for over twenty years. Tolkien began to write to me about his old homes, and then, with amazing kindness, about my own scribbling.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Norman Power, <i><a href="/wiki/Tolkien_Centenary_Conference_1992" title="Tolkien Centenary Conference 1992">Tolkien Centenary Conference 1992</a></i></cite></p>
</blockquote>
