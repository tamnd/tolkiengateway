The land of **Nevrast** lay on the coast of the [Great Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) in the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age). The name meant *Hither Shore* in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as opposed to the *Distant Shore* of [Aman](/wiki/Aman), and the name was originally applied to all the shores of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Some people considered the land of Nevrast to be part of Beleriand, while others considered it part of [Hithlum](/wiki/Hithlum).<sup id="cite_ref-hithlum_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hithlum-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nevrast was sheltered by mountains that separated it from Hithlum proper, so its weather was more like the lands of [West Beleriand](/wiki/West_Beleriand) than the lands to its northeast. It received ample rains from the sea which drained into the great mere of [Linaewen](/wiki/Linaewen) in the midst of the land, for Nevrast was enclosed by mountains with no outward flowing rivers.<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Nevrast was the first part of Beleriand the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) settled, but it had been previously inhabited by [Sindar](/wiki/Sindar). Nevrast's population was soon very mixed, far more so than any other region of Beleriand.

Nevrast was the centre of an [Elven](/wiki/Elves "Elves") kingdom of [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon) for about a century until ca. [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [116](/wiki/First_Age_116 "First Age 116"), when the people began their trek to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Turgon's capital (and it seems the only city of Nevrast) was [Vinyamar](/wiki/Vinyamar).<sup id="cite_ref-Bel_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bel-2">[2]</a></sup> The land was not permanently inhabited after that, and indeed was utterly abandoned until [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) came there, guided by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

An early, rejected form of the name *Nevrast* was *Nivrost* ("West Vale" or "West-dales").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
