**Noldor (Dead Winter Reigns)** is a song in the [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)-related [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian) album [Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth). The song details the [Doom of](/wiki/Doom_of_Mandos "Doom of Mandos") [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos).
