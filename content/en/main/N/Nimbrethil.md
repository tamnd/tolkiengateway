**Nimbrethil** was a region of birchwoods that lay in the land of [Arvernien](../A/Arvernien.md), on the northern shore of the [Bay of Balar](../B/Bay_of_Balar.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Wood from the forests of Nimbrethil was used by [Eärendil](../E/Eärendil.md) to construct his ship [Vingilot](../V/Vingilot.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The woods were recalled by [Bilbo](../B/Bilbo_Baggins.md) at [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) in his *[Eärendillinwë](../E/Eärendillinwë.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nimbrethil* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md), composed of the words *[nim](Nim.md)* and *[brethil](<../B/Brethil_(Sindarin_word).md>)*. *Nimbrethil* is translated to "silver birches", and can also mean "white princess".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
