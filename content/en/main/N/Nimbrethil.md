**Nimbrethil** was a region of birchwoods that lay in the land of [Arvernien](/wiki/Arvernien), on the northern shore of the [Bay of Balar](/wiki/Bay_of_Balar).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Wood from the forests of Nimbrethil was used by [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) to construct his ship [Vingilot](/wiki/Vingilot).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

The woods were recalled by [Bilbo](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins "Bilbo Baggins") at [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) in his *[Eärendillinwë](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendillinw%C3%AB)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nimbrethil* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), composed of the words *[nim](/wiki/Nim "Nim")* and *[brethil](</wiki/Brethil_(Sindarin_word)> "Brethil (Sindarin word)")*. *Nimbrethil* is translated to "silver birches", and can also mean "white princess".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <span></span>
