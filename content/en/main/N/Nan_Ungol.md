**Nan Ungol** is a valley on the northeastern facing side of the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) in the southwestern corner of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).

It first appeared on a merchandise map of Mordor for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)* that was drawn by [Daniel Reeve](/wiki/Daniel_Reeve)<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-1">[1]</a></sup> in 2003<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-2">[2]</a></sup>.

In *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](/wiki/Middle-earth:_Shadow_of_Mordor)* it is mentioned that while some of [Shelob](/wiki/Shelob)'s children, traveling south, have

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>taken Nan Ungol (Sindarin for &#34;Valley of the Spiders&#34;) as their primary home, they&#39;ve since spread across Mordor taking residence in its caves and other dark places, and are actively reporting back to their queen.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

There, it is said that "Their nests and lairs are littered with the crushed bones of their prey" and none who enter this valley come back alive.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nan Ungol* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Valley of the Spider", because the map shows a spiderweb between two mountains and a spider close to the label "Nan Ungol". It seems to contain the elements <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Nan" title="Nan">nan</a></i> ("vale", "valley")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and *[ungol](/wiki/Spiders#Names "Spiders")* ("spider")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>.
