**Nan Ungol** is a valley on the northeastern facing side of the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md) in the southwestern corner of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).

It first appeared on a merchandise map of Mordor for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King.md)* that was drawn by [Daniel Reeve](../D/Daniel_Reeve.md)<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-1">[1]</a></sup> in 2003<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-2">[2]</a></sup>.

In *[Middle-earth: Shadow of Mordor](../M/Middle-earth%253A_Shadow_of_Mordor.md)* it is mentioned that while some of [Shelob](../S/Shelob.md)'s children, traveling south, have

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>taken Nan Ungol (Sindarin for &#34;Valley of the Spiders&#34;) as their primary home, they&#39;ve since spread across Mordor taking residence in its caves and other dark places, and are actively reporting back to their queen.
</p>
</blockquote>

There, it is said that "Their nests and lairs are littered with the crushed bones of their prey" and none who enter this valley come back alive.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nan Ungol* seems to be a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) name, which means "Valley of the Spider", because the map shows a spiderweb between two mountains and a spider close to the label "Nan Ungol". It seems to contain the elements <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="Nan.md" title="Nan">nan</a></i> ("vale", "valley")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and *[ungol](../S/Spiders.md#Names)* ("spider")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>.
