**Nísimaldar** was the country around the haven of [Eldalondë](/wiki/Eldalond%C3%AB) on [Númenor's](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor "Númenor") western coast.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was the only place in Númenor where the [mallorn](/wiki/Mallorn "Mallorn") trees brought from [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa) grew, and it was from the stock of these that the [Golden Wood](/wiki/Golden_Wood) of [Lórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien "Lothlórien") came. Many other trees grew in this land, all of them gifts from the Eldar.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means '[Fragrant Trees](/wiki/Fragrant_Trees)', and it was so called because of the great variety of rare and beautiful [trees](/wiki/Trees "Trees") that grew there.<sup id="cite_ref-Numenor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Numenor-2">[2]</a></sup>
