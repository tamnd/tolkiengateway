**Naomi Mary Margaret Mitchison** ([November 1](../_/1_November.md), [1897](../_/1897.md) – [January 11](../_/11_January.md), [1999](../_/1999.md)) was a Scottish novelist and poet.

## History {#History}

Mitchison was educated in Oxford, and contributed verse to *[Oxford Poetry](../O/Oxford_Poetry.md)* for [1915](../_/1915.md) and [1916](../_/1916.md) (in the former, in company with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s "[Goblin Feet](../G/Goblin_Feet.md)"). Eventually, she became a prolific writer of books and articles, including *Graeme and the Dragon* ([1954](../_/1954.md), illustrated by [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md)) and <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=To_the_Chapel_Perilous&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="To the Chapel Perilous (page does not exist)">To the Chapel Perilous</a></i> ([1955](../_/1955.md), which Tolkien disliked).<sup id="cite_ref-RG_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-1">[1]</a></sup>

For many years, Mitchison was a fan of Tolkien's works, and they had a long-time correspondance. She became a proofreader of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* when [George Allen &amp; Unwin](../G/George_Allen_and_Unwin.md) sent her advance proofs of the first two volumes. Of *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*, Mitchison wrote "It is timeless and will go on".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Upon the release of the volume, she wrote the article "One Ring to Bind Them" in <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Statesman" title="wikipedia:New Statesman">New Statesman and Nation</a></i> ([18 September](../_/18_September.md) [1954](../_/1954.md)) reviewing the book. Although she criticised some vague aspects, and scientific uncertainties of the Secondary World, she praised its geographic, cultural, historical and linguistic depth.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

However, later in life she became increasingly critical of Tolkien's works, critizing the "grandiose style" of *[The Return of the King](../T/The_Return_of_the_King.md)* and the lack of a feminine perspective in *The Lord of the Rings* and finding *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)* simplistic.<sup id="cite_ref-RG_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RG-1">[1]</a></sup>
