**Nargroth** is a place in [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) that appears in several adaptations.

## Chronology and Creation {#Chronology_and_Creation}

The name was originally created by cartographer and calligrapher [Daniel Reeve](/wiki/Daniel_Reeve) while working on licensed map assets for the film trilogy and related merchandise.

Its first appearance is in the [Maps of Middle-earth](/wiki/Maps_of_Middle-earth) map set for [Decipher](/wiki/Decipher)'s *[The Lord of the Rings Roleplaying Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Roleplaying_Game)*, where it appears as a minor label below what seems to be a hill on the western side of the mountain spur that branches off southwestwards from the [Ered Lithui](/wiki/Ered_Lithui) east of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) on the map of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor) without further exposition.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Reeve subsequently used the Decipher map as a template when tasked with designing the expanded merchandise map for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.<sup id="cite_ref-reeve-site_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-site-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-reeve-mail_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-reeve-mail-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Fan works {#Fan_works}

The fansite [Thelandofshadow.com](/wiki/Thelandofshadow.com) took on several of the names introduced by Reeve, and expanded on them. Nargroth was described as follows:<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>A watch tower and gateway to the south of Mordor. Nargroth is strategically placed at the very tail end of the Mithram Spur and forms the eastern watch at the base of Gorgoroth. Across a wide gap sits the fortress of Morigost at the base of the Maegond Spur. None could enter the Plain of Gorgoroth without being spied upon from these two vantage points, which formed the southern guard into the heart of Mordor. Nargroth was a high peak with a watch post and there was also a great Orc encampment at its base.
</p>
</blockquote>

<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395759">.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container{margin:1rem 0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider{margin:0}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-section-divider>span{line-height:0;font-size:2rem;margin-top:1rem}.mw-parser-output .tg-quote-container>.tg-quote{padding-left:0;border-left:none}</style>

The information from The Land of Shadow was incorporated into the fan made *Mordor Gazetteer* of the Middle-earth Role Playing (MERP) fan modules group.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nargroth* seems to be a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, which means "Fire Cave". It seems to contain the element *[naur](/wiki/Naur "Naur")* ("fire")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> that also occurs as the first element in the name [Nardol](/wiki/Nardol) and the element *[groth](/wiki/Groth "Groth")* ("large excavation", "delving", "underground dwelling")<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>.
