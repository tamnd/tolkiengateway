**Nenning** was a river of western [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) that rose in an isolated hilly region south of the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), and flowed southwards to meet the sea at [Eglarest](/wiki/Eglarest), one of the two great [Havens of the Falas](/wiki/Havens_of_the_Falas).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was considered to be the border between [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)'s lands to the west and the realm of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) to the east.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the year after the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) sent columns of [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) down the rivers [Brithon](/wiki/Brithon) and Nenning to attack the Havens. They were successful, capturing the havens and enslaving most of [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan)'s folk.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

An earlier name for this river, in [Ilkorin](/wiki/Ilkorin), was **Eglor** which translated to "Elf-river".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:ELED-</sup> It was the basis for the name [Eglorest](/wiki/Eglorest).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:RIS-</sup>
