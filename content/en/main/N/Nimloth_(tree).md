**Nimloth** was the White Tree which grew in the [King's Court](/wiki/King%27s_Court) in [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos) of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). It was brought as a gift by the [Eldar](/wiki/Eldar) from [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), in the form of a seedling from [Celeborn](</wiki/Celeborn_(White_Tree)> "Celeborn (White Tree)") as a symbol of friendship between the two races. Its blossoms appeared as the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) set, and their perfume filled the night in [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor)'s royal city.

Soon after [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3262](/wiki/Second_Age_3262 "Second Age 3262"), it was cut down at the instigation of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) and its wood was used to light the first flames in the fire of the [Temple](/wiki/Temple) of the new religion which worshipped [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth").

However, the night before [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) stole a fruit from the tree and planted it in secret. The fruit grew into a seedling that Isildur carried to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) with him during the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor). That seedling was planted in [Minas Ithil](/wiki/Minas_Ithil), and was destroyed before the end of the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age). Its seedling in turn grew into the first [White Tree of Gondor](/wiki/White_Tree_of_Gondor).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nimloth* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "White Blossom",<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> from *[nim](/wiki/Nim "Nim")* ("white") + *[loth](/wiki/Loth "Loth")* ("flower").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
