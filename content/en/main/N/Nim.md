## Etymology {#Etymology}

*nim* was historically derived from [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin) *[nimbi](/wiki/Nimbi "Nimbi")*.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
