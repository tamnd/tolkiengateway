<i><b>nóre</b></i> (or *nōre*) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word used for "'land' associated with a particular people".<sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

Originally it meant "people", but it was blended and confused with the quite distinct root *ndor* "dry land" (cf. [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[dôr](/wiki/D%C3%B4r "Dôr")*)<sup id="cite_ref-elem_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elem-3">[3]</a></sup> and the suffix forms *-nórë*, *-ndórë* or *-dórë* are also seen shortened to *-or*.

In the early manuscript the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), Quenya *nóre* is glossed as "country, land, race".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

- [ONO](/wiki/ONO) blended with <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NDOR&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NDOR (page does not exist)">DORO</a><sup id="cite_ref-WJ_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJ-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE17_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) = *Númen(n)órë* ("people of the West") and *Númendor* ("land of the West")<sup id="cite_ref-elem_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elem-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor) = *Valinórë* (strictly "the people of the Valar") and *Valandor* ("the land of the Valar")<sup id="cite_ref-elem_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elem-3">[3]</a></sup>

- On the other hand, *Endórë, [Endor](/wiki/Endor)* = ENED + NDOR<sup id="cite_ref-elem_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-elem-3">[3]</a></sup>
