**Nimloth** means "White Blossoms" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) (from *[nim](/wiki/Nim "Nim")-*, "pale, white" and *[loth](/wiki/Loth "Loth")*, "flowers, blossoms") and can refer to:

- [Nimloth of Doriath](/wiki/Nimloth_of_Doriath) (died [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506")), the wife of [Dior Eluchíl](/wiki/Dior "Dior") and mother of [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing).
- [Nimloth of Númenor](/wiki/Nimloth_of_N%C3%BAmenor) ([Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age)), the White Tree of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor) which grew in [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos).
