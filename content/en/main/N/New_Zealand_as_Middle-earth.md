**New Zealand as Middle-earth** is a series of interactive documentaries, appearing on the [extended editions of *The Fellowship of the Ring*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition).md>), [*The Two Towers*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>) and [*The Return of the King*](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Return_of_the_King_(extended_edition).md>). It shows footage of several filming locations.

## [The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) {#The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring}

### [Hobbiton](../H/Hobbiton.md) {#Hobbiton}

- **Matamata**. A field outside Matamata, a small town in the North Island, was a minor filming location for external [Bag End](../B/Bag_End.md) and [Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) scenes and possibly for Bilbo's party

- **Keeling Farm, Manukau**
- **Otaki Totara Forest**
- **Wellington**

### [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md) {#Weathertop}

- **Port Wikato**. In that hilly location nothing was built. Peter Jackson's crew left the hill untouched and used it for external background scenes and allso for the hollow scene, where Aragorn and the Hobbits camp for the night.<b></b>
- **Te Anau**
- **Wellington**. For the fight with the [Nazgûl](Nazgûl.md) scene, filmed in studio.

### [Ford of Bruinen](../F/Ford_of_Bruinen.md) {#Ford_of_Bruinen}

- **Arrowtown**
- **Tarras, Wanaka**
- **Queenstown**

### [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) {#Rivendell}

- **Kaitoke, Regional Park**

### [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) {#Lothlórien}

- **Paradise, Glenorchy**. There was filmed the scene where The Fellowship enters Lothlórien.
- **Fernside, Featherston**. In this area was shot the departure from Lothlórien scene, due to its colorful trees and beautiful river.

### River [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) {#River_Anduin}

- **Kawarau river**

where the Argonath scene was shot

- **Queenstown**
- **Poet's corner, Upper Hutt**
- **Kaitoke, regional Park**

### [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md) {#Amon_Hen}

- **Mavora Lakes**. This is the place where the scene with the fellowship leaving the boats was shot
- **Paradise, Glenorchy**

Where the fight against the [Uruk-hai](../U/Uruk-hai.md) and the death of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) were shot

- **Lake Wakatipu, Queenstown**, Where an Amon Hen ruin was built and used for some scenes
