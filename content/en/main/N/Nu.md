**nu** is the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for "under" or "beneath".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><b>nu</b> luini yassen tintilar i eleni</i> = "**beneath** the blue vaults of Varda wherein the stars tremble"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> ([Quenya](/wiki/Quenya))

- <i><a href="/wiki/Taur-nu-Fuin_(Dorthonion)" title="Taur-nu-Fuin (Dorthonion)">Taur-<b>nu</b>-Fuin</a></i> = "Forest-**under**-nightshade"<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> ([Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin))
