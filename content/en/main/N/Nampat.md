**Nampat** is the twenty-third track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One%253A_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack).md>)*. This track occurs in [episode five](<../U/Udûn_(episode).md>) as a war-chant which was used by the "children" of [Adar](<../A/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power).md>) during their march to the [Watchtower of Ostirith](../W/Watchtower_of_Ostirith.md) and then again during their march to [Tirharad](../T/Tirharad.md).
