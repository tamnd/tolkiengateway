**Nampat** is the twenty-third track of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power (Season One: Amazon Original Series Soundtrack)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power_(Season_One:_Amazon_Original_Series_Soundtrack)>)*. This track occurs in [episode five](</wiki/Ud%C3%BBn_(episode)> "Udûn (episode)") as a war-chant which was used by the "children" of [Adar](</wiki/Adar_(The_Rings_of_Power)> "Adar (The Rings of Power)") during their march to the [Watchtower of Ostirith](/wiki/Watchtower_of_Ostirith) and then again during their march to [Tirharad](/wiki/Tirharad).
