The "**nameless things**" were gnawing entities that were said by [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) to dwell so far beneath [Arda](/wiki/Arda) that not even the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) had ever chanced to come upon them.<sup id="cite_ref-tWR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tWR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Gandalf claimed that these nameless things under Arda were older than [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), suggesting that they were alive before the physical arrival of some [Maiar](/wiki/Maiar) to Arda.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[note 1]</a></sup>

Such entities were said to be "more slimy than fish" and preferred to dwell in darkness. They were known to have inhabited the caves deep within the hearts of mountains as far back as the [Elder Days](/wiki/Elder_Days). When the [Orcs of the Misty Mountains](/wiki/Orcs_of_the_Misty_Mountains) dug around [Goblin-town](/wiki/Goblin-town), delving new tunnels and expanding and joining those that already existed, their original owners withdrew to the deepest depths and lurked beyond their knowledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Riddles_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Riddles-3">[2]</a></sup>

According to Gandalf, [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane) had learned to navigate the nameless things' tunnels during its long stay in [Moria](/wiki/Moria). After the two fell from the [Bridge of Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Bridge_of_Khazad-d%C3%BBm) into an underground lake, the [Balrog](/wiki/Balrogs "Balrogs") fled through these tunnels. Gandalf pursued it closely, believing that its knowledge of the tunnels was his only hope of escape. Their chase led them to the foot of the [Endless Stair](/wiki/Endless_Stair), which they climbed to eventually emerge on the peak of [Zirakzigil](/wiki/Celebdil "Celebdil").<sup id="cite_ref-tWR_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tWR-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, [Nan Dumgorthin](/wiki/Nan_Dungortheb "Nan Dungortheb"), the "Land of the Dark Idols" (*dum* ‘secret, not to be spoken’, *dumgort*, *dungort* ‘an (evil) idol’), was a dark forested land that was located to the east of [Artanor](/wiki/Doriath "Doriath") where a collection of "evil tribes of renegade men" made sacrifices to [Gods](/wiki/Gods) whose idols were hidden upon a wooded mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup> In the *[Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, there are shrines that are hidden in secret places for the worship of **nameless gods** older than both [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) and the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Todd Jensen suggests that the nameless things may have been inspired by the dragon Nidhog of Norse mythology, who gnawed at the roots of Yggdrasil the World Tree.<sup id="cite_ref-RC_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-6">[5]</a></sup>

### Games {#Games}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Nameless is a creature type categorized as &#34;Ancient Evil&#34;. They have a variety of shapes, but most of them are humanoid, only with no eyes or no head at all. They can be found in the <a href="/wiki/Dr%C3%BAadan_Forest" title="Drúadan Forest">Drúadan Forest</a>, having come out of the <a href="/wiki/White_Mountains" title="White Mountains">White Mountains</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Dawnless_Day" title="Dawnless Day">Dawnless Day</a>; and beneath the <a href="/wiki/Iron_Hills" title="Iron Hills">Iron Hills</a>, where a horde of <a href="/wiki/Morgoth" title="Morgoth">Morgoth</a>&#39;s monsters were sealed away by the Dwarves long ago. The largest concentration of Nameless, however, are within the Foundations of Stone, the deepest level of Moria. The <a href="/wiki/Watcher_in_the_Water" title="Watcher in the Water">Watcher in the Water</a> is also classified as Nameless, as is &#34;Helchgam&#34;, another tentacled creature, found in the sewers of <a href="/wiki/Carn_D%C3%BBm" title="Carn Dûm">Carn Dûm</a>.</dd></dl>
