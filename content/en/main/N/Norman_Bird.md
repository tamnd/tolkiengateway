**Norman Bird** ([30 October](/wiki/30_October) [1924](/wiki/1924) - [22 April](/wiki/22_April) [2005](/wiki/2005)) was a British character actor. Active from the 1950s, he made guest appearances in well-known series such as <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Saint_(TV_series)" title="wikipedia:The Saint (TV series)">The Saint</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-Cars" title="wikipedia:Z-Cars">Z Cars</a></i>, playing mostly civil servants and policemen. In [1978](/wiki/1978), Bird provided the voice of [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins) in [*The Lord of the Rings* (1978 film)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(1978_film)> "The Lord of the Rings (1978 film)"). His last known role was as a taxi driver in the [C.S. Lewis](/wiki/C.S._Lewis) biopic *Shadowlands*.
