**Númendil** was the seventeenth [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië") and the cousin of king [Tar-Palantir](/wiki/Tar-Palantir).<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup> It can be assumed that Númendil was a member of the Royal Council and an important Lord of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor). Though the name of his father is not known, his grandfather was [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(Lord_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB)> "Eärendur (Lord of Andúnië)"), the fifteenth [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").<sup id="cite_ref-Tar-Palantir_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tar-Palantir-1">[1]</a></sup>

Númendil was the father of [Amandil](/wiki/Amandil), who succeeded him as [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").<sup id="cite_ref-Akallabeth_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akallabeth-3">[3]</a></sup> Amandil would later become the father of [Elendil](/wiki/Elendil), the founder of the kingdoms of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) and [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Númendil* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it means "Friend of the West" and is a compound of *[númen](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen "Númen")* ("west") and the suffix *[-(n)dil](/wiki/-ndil "-ndil")* ("-friend", "-lover", "devotion", "disinterested love").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

[Elentir](/wiki/Elentir), intended to be another son of Númendil, was a character developed but never fully adapted into the formal history of Númenor. In one version of the story of [Ar-Pharazôn](/wiki/Ar-Pharaz%C3%B4n), his future wife [Míriel](/wiki/Tar-M%C3%ADriel "Tar-Míriel"), the daughter of the current king, was betrothed to Elentir. However, she fell desperately in love with Pharazôn and chose to become his queen when he took up the [Sceptre of Númenor](/wiki/Sceptre_of_N%C3%BAmenor). This story is very different than the tale in the published *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, where Ar-Pharazôn takes Míriel very much against her will in order to claim the Sceptre. In either case, Elentir disappeared before Númendil died, leaving Amandil as the [Lord of Andúnië](/wiki/Lords_of_And%C3%BAni%C3%AB "Lords of Andúnië").<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
