**Nether Darkness** refers to the far south of [Arda](/wiki/Arda). In the [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the [Númenóreans](/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans) reached the Nether Darkness by sea.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was probably uninhabited and, as the name suggests, apparently unlit by the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
