**Nether Darkness** refers to the far south of [Arda](../A/Arda.md). In the [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md), the [Númenóreans](Númenóreans.md) reached the Nether Darkness by sea.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> It was probably uninhabited and, as the name suggests, apparently unlit by the [Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
