**NHN Corporation** (Korean: 엔에이치엔 주식회사) is a South Korean internet company. It was founded in 1999 in Seongnam, South Korea. NHN has the license to publish [The Lord of the Rings Online](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md) in South Korea.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
