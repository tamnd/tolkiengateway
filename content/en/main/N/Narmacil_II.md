**Narmacil II** ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1684](../T/Third_Age_1684.md)<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup> - [1856](../T/Third_Age_1856.md),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> aged 172 years) was the twenty-ninth [King of Gondor](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Narmacil was the son of King [Telumehtar Umbardacil](../T/Telumehtar_Umbardacil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Telumehtar_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Telumehtar-4">[4]</a></sup> and the brother of [Arciryas](../A/Arciryas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Narmacil II became King of Gondor when his father Telumehtar died in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1850](../T/Third_Age_1850.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Telumehtar_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Telumehtar-4">[4]</a></sup> A year later in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1851](../T/Third_Age_1851.md), incited by emissaries from [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) the [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md), a people or a confederacy of many peoples that came from the East, had been stirred up by emissaries from [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md) and began to attack Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> The [Northmen](Northmen.md), a confederation of peoples that lived in the wide plains between [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) and the [River Running](../R/River_Running.md),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> bore the main impact of the first attack of the Wainriders.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1856](../T/Third_Age_1856.md)<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup> Narmacil II came with a great army to the plains south of Mirkwood and assembled as many of the dispersed remnants of the Northmen as was possible<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup> and fought with the aid of horsemen of the Northmen under [Marhari](../M/Marhari.md), a descendant of [Vidugavia](../V/Vidugavia.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Note5_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note5-10">[10]</a></sup> against the Wainriders in the [Battle of the Plains](../B/Battle_of_the_Plains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup> In that battle the army of Gondor and the Northmen were defeated and Narmacil II was killed.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup> The remnant of the army of Gondor<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup> with a reardguard of the horsemen of the Northmen<sup id="cite_ref-Note5_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note5-10">[10]</a></sup> retreated over the [Dagorlad](../D/Dagorlad.md) to [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md), but Marhari was killed during the retreat.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup> However, the army of Gondor had inflicted such losses on the Wainriders that they did not enough forces left to continue with their invasion of Gondor without further reinforcements from the East and instead completed their conquest of [Rhovanion](../R/Rhovanion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Note5_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Note5-10">[10]</a></sup>

Gondor withdrew its borders for that time to the Anduin and the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-NarmacilII_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NarmacilII-11">[11]</a></sup> and abandoned all its eastern lands except Ithilien.<sup id="cite_ref-Northmen_8-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Northmen-8">[8]</a></sup> A few of the Northmen fled over the River Running and joined the folk of [Dale](../D/Dale.md) with whom they were related, some Northmen searched refuge in Gondor and other Northmen were assembled by [Marhwini](../M/Marhwini.md), the son of Marhari and migrated north between the Anduin and Mirkwood and settled in the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md). Many Northmen fled through Mirkwood and joined Marhari's group. The rest of the Northmen were enslaved by the Wainriders who had occupied all their former lands.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> It is believed that the [Ringwraiths](../R/Ringwraiths.md) reentered [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md) at this time.<sup id="cite_ref-NarmacilII_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NarmacilII-11">[11]</a></sup>

Narmacil was succeeded by his son [Calimehtar](<../C/Calimehtar_(King_of_Gondor).md>).<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The earliest version of what would later become Appendix A mentioned that there was a battle with the Ringwraiths in his time, that the Ringwraiths seized Mordor and that Osgiliath ended to be the seat of the Kings of Gondor.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup> In the next version the Ringwraiths had seized Mordor long ago, attacked Ithilien and the Witch-king killed Narmakil. This was changed to that the Ringwraiths reentered Mordor, that Men out of the East riding in great wains attacked Gondor and that Narmakil was killed in battle.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup> In the latest version the ceasing of the vigilance is added as the reason for the Ringwraiths reentering Mordor. It is added that Narmakil II died in a battle northeast of the Morannon with Men out of the East, but those men still remain unnamed.<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>
