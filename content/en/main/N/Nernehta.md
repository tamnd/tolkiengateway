<i><b>Nernehta</b></i> is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name of the battle formation known in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) as the <i><b><a href="/wiki/D%C3%ADrnaith" title="Dírnaith">Dírnaith</a></b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

It is derived from the Quenya words *[Nér](/wiki/N%C3%A9r)* "man" and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nehte&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nehte (page does not exist)">nehte</a>/<a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nehta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nehta (page does not exist)">nehta</a></i> which is applied to any formation or projection tapering to a point.<sup id="cite_ref-Gladden_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gladden-1">[1]</a></sup>
