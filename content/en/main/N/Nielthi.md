**Nielthi** was the "Handmaid of [Gwendelin](/wiki/Melian "Melian")"<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> who was briefly mentioned in an earlier stage of [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien")'s legendarium, in the tale of [The Nauglafring](/wiki/The_Nauglafring).<sup id="cite_ref-lt_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lt-2">[2]</a></sup>

While [Tinwelint](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") was out celebrating the anniversary of the [Hunting of the Wolf](/wiki/Hunting_of_the_Wolf), Gwendelin sat foreboding. When Nielthi, an [Elfmaid](/wiki/Elf "Elf"), asked her why she was sorrowful at such an occasion, Gwendelin responded that she could feel evil seeking their land, and that her days in Artanor were "speeding to their end". Nielthi attempted to reassure the queen by stating that they should not fear because the [great magic woven about them](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian "Girdle of Melian") would surely protect them. In response, Gwendelin claimed that, to her, there seemed to be "a rat that" gnawed "the threads and all the web" had "come unwoven".<sup id="cite_ref-lt_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lt-2">[2]</a></sup>

At that moment, "there was a cry about the doors" and many [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [Indrafangs](/wiki/Indrafangs) burst in, as they were [sacking Artanor](/wiki/Battle_in_the_Thousand_Caves "Battle in the Thousand Caves"). The fate of Nielthi is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-lt_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lt-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nielthi* is presumed to be a [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) name on [Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The name might be a combination of *niel* ("granddaughter")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and *-thi* ("noun suffix")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>.
