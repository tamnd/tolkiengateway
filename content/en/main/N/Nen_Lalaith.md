**Nen Lalaith** was a babbling stream that rose under the mountain of [Amon Darthir](/wiki/Amon_Darthir) in the [Mountains of Shadow](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin "Ered Wethrin"), and flowed north-westwards out onto the shaded plains of [Dor-lómin](/wiki/Dor-l%C3%B3min).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> There, it trickled past the house of [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin), and was said to have given its name to his ill-fated young daughter, [Lalaith](/wiki/Lalaith).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nen Lalaith* means "Water of Laughter" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), from *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("river, water") and *[lalaith](</wiki/Lalaith_(word)> "Lalaith (word)")* ("laughter").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
