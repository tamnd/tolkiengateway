**NEK** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme"), with two different meanings.

## NEK<sup>1</sup> {#NEK1}

Meaning: "deprive",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:167</sup> "divide, part, separate"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *nehta-* "deprive"; *nehtanō* "one deprived, exile whose rights and goods have been confiscated"; *[-enca](/wiki/-enca)* and *nec-* "without, -less"
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[neithan](/wiki/Neithan "Neithan")* (cognate of *nehtanō*)

## NEK<sup>2</sup> {#NEK2}

Meaning: "narrow"<sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup>

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *nek-tē*
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *nehte* "angle", *nerca, nexa* "sharp, angular", *nekel* "a thorn"
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *neith, [naith](/wiki/Naith "Naith")* "angle",<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:55</sup> "a spearhead, gore, wedge, narrow promontory",<sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup> *neg(e)n* "sharp, angular"

Compounds:

- [Dírnaith](/wiki/D%C3%ADrnaith), [Nernehta](/wiki/Nernehta) "man-spearhead"<sup id="cite_ref-UT_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-3">[3]</a></sup>
