**Needlehole** was a town in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing) near the border of the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). [The Water](/wiki/The_Water) flowed through the town on its way south to [Rushock Bog](/wiki/Rushock_Bog).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Needlehole is also the name of a village in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloucestershire" title="wikipedia:Gloucestershire">Gloucestershire</a>. Apart from the apparent meaning of the name, it might also refer to [hobbit-holes](/wiki/Hobbit-holes "Hobbit-holes").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
