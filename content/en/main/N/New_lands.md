**New lands** and seas were created by [Ilúvatar](../I/Ilúvatar.md) when [the world](../A/Arda.md) was remade after the [Downfall of Númenor](../D/Downfall_of_Númenor.md) and [Aman](../A/Aman.md) was removed. They were formed after Eru cast back the [Great Sea](../G/Great_Sea.md) west, and the [Empty Lands](../E/Empty_Lands.md) east of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).

Long after that [cataclysm](../C/Changing_of_the_World.md), [Men](../M/Men.md) from [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) sailed west to the [Great Sea](../G/Great_Sea.md), seeking the summit of the [Meneltarma](../M/Meneltarma.md), which they believed had risen above the waves and from which a person could still descry the Undying Lands. However, the [Isle of Meneltarma](../H/Hallow_of_Eru.md) was never found. Instead mariners only discovered new lands which were like the old lands in that they were subject to death. These new lands did not form a barrier to travel; sailors found that they could circumnavigate the world and thus could report: *īdō kātha batīna lōkhī*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> ("All roads are now bent").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Considering that Middle-earth "corresponds" to Europe<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md) to Atlantic<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> it can be suggested that the "new lands" correspond to the Americas.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[David Day](../D/David_Day.md) in *[Tolkien: The Illustrated Encyclopedia](../T/Tolkien%253A_The_Illustrated_Encyclopedia.md)* displays some chronological evolution of [Arda](../A/Arda.md), and a continent resembling the Americas forming west of Middle-earth after the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

Later in his life, Tolkien wrote about the possibility that the [Valar](../V/Valar.md) and the [Calaquendi](../C/Calaquendi.md) left the world spiritually, while the landmass of Aman remained, and became the Americas.<sup id="cite_ref-Nature_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-6">[6]</a></sup>
