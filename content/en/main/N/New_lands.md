**New lands** and seas were created by [Ilúvatar](/wiki/Il%C3%BAvatar) when [the world](/wiki/Arda "Arda") was remade after the [Downfall of Númenor](/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor) and [Aman](/wiki/Aman) was removed. They were formed after Eru cast back the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea) west, and the [Empty Lands](/wiki/Empty_Lands) east of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth).

Long after that [cataclysm](/wiki/Changing_of_the_World "Changing of the World"), [Men](/wiki/Men) from [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) sailed west to the [Great Sea](/wiki/Great_Sea), seeking the summit of the [Meneltarma](/wiki/Meneltarma), which they believed had risen above the waves and from which a person could still descry the Undying Lands. However, the [Isle of Meneltarma](/wiki/Hallow_of_Eru "Hallow of Eru") was never found. Instead mariners only discovered new lands which were like the old lands in that they were subject to death. These new lands did not form a barrier to travel; sailors found that they could circumnavigate the world and thus could report: *īdō kātha batīna lōkhī*<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> ("All roads are now bent").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

Considering that Middle-earth "corresponds" to Europe<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> and [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer) to Atlantic<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> it can be suggested that the "new lands" correspond to the Americas.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

[David Day](/wiki/David_Day) in *[Tolkien: The Illustrated Encyclopedia](/wiki/Tolkien:_The_Illustrated_Encyclopedia)* displays some chronological evolution of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), and a continent resembling the Americas forming west of Middle-earth after the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

Later in his life, Tolkien wrote about the possibility that the [Valar](/wiki/Valar) and the [Calaquendi](/wiki/Calaquendi) left the world spiritually, while the landmass of Aman remained, and became the Americas.<sup id="cite_ref-Nature_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nature-6">[6]</a></sup>
