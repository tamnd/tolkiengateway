The **Nelyar** ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"), pron <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈneʎar]</span>) or Enellië<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the original name of the third Clan of the elves, [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), before the [Great March](/wiki/Great_March). The Clan-name, meaning 'Thirds' (q. cardinal *nelya* 'third') was derived from the name of the father of the Clan, [Enel](/wiki/Enel) 'Three'. Enel was the third [Elf-father](/wiki/Elf-fathers "Elf-fathers"), who awoke at [Cuiviénen](/wiki/Cuivi%C3%A9nen).

They were the largest clan, being the 74/144 of the population. Of those, the 46/74 followed [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB) to the West and became the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), while 28/74 [stayed back](/wiki/Avari "Avari") with [Morwë](/wiki/Morw%C3%AB) and/or [Nurwë](/wiki/Nurw%C3%AB).

The other original clans were [Minyar](/wiki/Minyar) 'Firsts', and [Tatyar](/wiki/Tatyar) 'Seconds' according to the Elf-fathers [Imin](/wiki/Imin) 'One' and [Tata](/wiki/Tata) 'Two'.

Eventually some of the Avari of the Nelyar reached [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand). Unlike the Tatyar, those Avari were friendly to the Eldar, willing to learn from them and had a feeling of kinship. Later in [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador) and the [Vales of Anduin](/wiki/Vales_of_Anduin), [Nelyarin](/wiki/Penni "Penni") Avari often merged with the Eldar of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
