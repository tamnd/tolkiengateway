The **Nelyar** ([Q](../Q/Quenya.md), pron <span title="Pronunciation in IPA" class="IPA">[ˈneʎar]</span>) or Enellië<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> was the original name of the third Clan of the elves, [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), before the [Great March](../G/Great_March.md). The Clan-name, meaning 'Thirds' (q. cardinal *nelya* 'third') was derived from the name of the father of the Clan, [Enel](../E/Enel.md) 'Three'. Enel was the third [Elf-father](../E/Elf-fathers.md), who awoke at [Cuiviénen](../C/Cuiviénen.md).

They were the largest clan, being the 74/144 of the population. Of those, the 46/74 followed [Elwë](../E/Elwë.md) to the West and became the [Teleri](../T/Teleri.md), while 28/74 [stayed back](../A/Avari.md) with [Morwë](../M/Morwë.md) and/or [Nurwë](Nurwë.md).

The other original clans were [Minyar](../M/Minyar.md) 'Firsts', and [Tatyar](../T/Tatyar.md) 'Seconds' according to the Elf-fathers [Imin](../I/Imin.md) 'One' and [Tata](../T/Tata.md) 'Two'.

Eventually some of the Avari of the Nelyar reached [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md). Unlike the Tatyar, those Avari were friendly to the Eldar, willing to learn from them and had a feeling of kinship. Later in [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) and the [Vales of Anduin](../V/Vales_of_Anduin.md), [Nelyarin](../P/Penni.md) Avari often merged with the Eldar of Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
