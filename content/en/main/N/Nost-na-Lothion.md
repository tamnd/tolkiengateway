**Nost-na-Lothion**, or the **Birth of Flowers**, was a festival held in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin) according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.

It was celebrated during the early spring, with a "revelry of children". It was apparently held to celebrate the passing of winter, when the ice of the [Encircling Mountains](/wiki/Encircling_Mountains) melted and [Tumladin](/wiki/Tumladin) took the water and burst into flowers.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
