**Narn i Hîn Húrin** ([Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "**Tale of the Children of Húrin**"), also called "**The Narn**", is a chapter in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, a posthumously published work of [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) edited and published by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md). It is a long story of all that happened to [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md) and his children [Túrin](../T/Túrin.md) and [Nienor](Nienor.md), after Húrin was cursed by [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md). In 2007, Christopher Tolkien published *[The Children of Húrin](../T/The_Children_of_Húrin.md)*, wherein Tolkien completed the story and completed a single narrative of this tale with minimal editorial presence.

## Story {#Story}

The story elaborates on what is told of these characters in the published *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, starting with the childhood of Túrin, continuing through the captivity of his father in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](Nirnaeth_Arnoediad.md), Túrin's exile in [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md), to Túrin's time in [Nargothrond](Nargothrond.md) and ending with his suicide after having slain [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md).

The story has some inconsistencies when compared with *The Silmarillion*, and at points there are gaps and multiple versions. This is because [Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) never finished the story during his lifetime, and his son [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) had to choose from all the work to create a consistent narrative for *The Silmarillion*.

The story of the *Narn* continues in the *Later Narn*, which is published in *[Unfinished Tales](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md)*, and in the "Wanderings of Húrin", a text which was found to be too different in style from the rest of *The Silmarillion*, but which continues the *Narn* after Túrin's death. It ends with Húrin's eventual release and the bad deeds which follow him. This story was finally published in *[The War of the Jewels](../T/The_War_of_the_Jewels.md)*.

## Publishing notes {#Publishing_notes}

Note that the title of the *Narn* is wrongly given as **Narn i Hîn Húrin** in the published *Silmarillion* and *Unfinished Tales*. This was an editorial decision by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) which he later regretted, done only to prevent people from pronouncing *[Chîn](../H/Hîn.md)* like the English "chin".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
