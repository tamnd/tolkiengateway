**Nienna** was a Queen of the [Valar](/wiki/Valar), the sister of [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) and [Irmo](/wiki/Irmo), acquainted with grief and sorrow but also pity and courage.

## Attributes {#Attributes}

The sister of the [Fëanturi](/wiki/F%C3%ABanturi), she is ranked as one of the eight [Aratar](/wiki/Aratar), the most powerful of the Valar. Her element is grief and she is ever mourning for the wounds of the world by evil. Those who listen to her learn wisdom and endurance in grief.<sup id="cite_ref-SIIb_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIIb-1">[1]</a></sup>

Her [halls](/wiki/Halls_of_Nienna "Halls of Nienna") are in the distant west, close to the [Halls of Mandos](/wiki/Halls_of_Mandos), but on the western borders of the World, and her windows look outward from the [Walls of the World](/wiki/Walls_of_the_World). She dwells alone and rarely travels to the joyful city of [Valmar](/wiki/Valmar). She goes more often to the halls of her brother [Mandos](/wiki/Mandos) to comfort and counsel those in the [Halls of Waiting](/wiki/Halls_of_Waiting) who cry to her.<sup id="cite_ref-SIIb_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIIb-1">[1]</a></sup>

All that is known about her appearance is that she wears a grey hood.<sup id="cite_ref-S9_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Her part in the [Music of the Ainur](/wiki/Music_of_the_Ainur) was one of deep sadness, and from this grief entered the world in its beginning.<sup id="cite_ref-SIIb_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SIIb-1">[1]</a></sup> It was said that her ruth was so great by the discord of Melkor during the Music that she could not endure until it's end.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The [Maia](/wiki/Maiar "Maiar") [Olórin](/wiki/Gandalf#Names "Gandalf"), who was later to travel to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) as [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf), learned much from her.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Nienna played a part in the making of the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor); she wept on the mound of [Ezellohar](/wiki/Ezellohar), watering it with her tears.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> After the destruction of the Trees by [Melkor](/wiki/Morgoth "Morgoth"), she once again wept on their wounded remains, cleansing the filth of [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant), and helping to bring forth the last fruit and flower that were to become the [Sun](/wiki/Sun) and the [Moon](/wiki/Moon).<sup id="cite_ref-S9_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S9-2">[2]</a></sup>

The pity of Nienna is most clearly seen in her support for Melkor when he sued for the pardon of the Valar. Though she spent her time in the world mourning for the destruction he wrought in Arda, when he sued for release after his three ages of Captivity, Nienna spoke on his part.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nienna* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name which contains the word *nie* ("tear").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, some early names of Nienna were:

- <i><b>Nyenna</b></i> <sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Fui</b></i> ([Q](/wiki/Quenya "Quenya"): "Night"), though this is more correctly the name of [her dwelling](/wiki/Halls_of_Nienna#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Halls of Nienna").
- <i><b>Heskil</b></i> ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya): "Winter One")<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-9">[9]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Núri</b></i> ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya): "Sighing One")<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-9">[9]</a></sup>
- <i><b>Qalmë-Tári</b></i> ([Qenya](/wiki/Qenya): "Mistress of Death")<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-9">[9]</a></sup>

The importance of Nienna increased along the development of the Legendarium, and even in *[The Annals of Aman](/wiki/The_Annals_of_Aman)*, she is referred to as the sister of [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) and [Melkor](/wiki/Melkor).<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
