**Nightingales** were small brown birds of [thrush](/wiki/Thrushes "Thrushes") kind, famous for their clear singing and for the fact that they would sing at night as well as during the day (hence their name).

The [Maia](/wiki/Maia) [Melian](/wiki/Melian) was said to always have nightingales about her, and she taught them their songs. It was the song of these birds that enchanted [Elwë](/wiki/Elw%C3%AB), lord of the [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri), when he was wandering alone in [Nan Elmoth](/wiki/Nan_Elmoth) and came upon Melian.<sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

"Nightingale" in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) is <i><b>dúlin</b></i> (*[dû](/wiki/D%C3%BB "Dû")* + *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")*) or *merilin*. The Sindarin poetic word for "nightingale" was *[tinúviel](/wiki/Tin%C3%BAviel "Tinúviel")*, the name [Beren](/wiki/Beren) gave to [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) when he first saw her.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), nightingale is *lómelindë* (*[lómë](/wiki/L%C3%B3m%C3%AB "Lómë")* + *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")*)<sup id="cite_ref-Thingol_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thingol-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> *tindómerel*,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> or *morilinde*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> These words contain words for "night" "dusk" and "dark".

They were also called the "birds of [Melian](/wiki/Melian)", circling above her head while she sat in [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth).<sup id="cite_ref-Birdsofmelian_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Birdsofmelian-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
