**Naimi** was the niece of [Vairë](</wiki/Vair%C3%AB_(wife_of_Lindo)> "Vairë (wife of Lindo)"), an [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Tol Eressëa](/wiki/Tol_Eress%C3%ABa), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. She married [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol), the [Mannish](/wiki/Men "Men") mariner, after he was made young again by *[limpë](/wiki/Limp%C3%AB "Limpë")*, and had a son with him called [Heorrenda](/wiki/Heorrenda).<sup id="cite_ref-Nai_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nai-1">[1]</a></sup>

She is only mentioned in one outline for the continuation of the *Lost Tales*, and nothing else is known about her.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Naimi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-2">[2]</a></sup>

She was also called <i><b>Éadgifu</b></i> in [Old English](/wiki/Old_English),<sup id="cite_ref-Nai_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nai-1">[1]</a></sup> consisting of the elements *éad* ("blessedness") + *gifu* ("gift").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In one note to *[The Cottage of Lost Play](/wiki/The_Cottage_of_Lost_Play)*, there appears a character called **Nelmir**. In that text, she is depicted as the wife of Eriol and the mother of [Heorrenda](/wiki/Heorrenda) *and* [Hendwine](/wiki/Hendwine), a character who doesn't appear in any of the narratives of *The Book of Lost Tales*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

Nothing else is known about her and her relation to [Lindo](/wiki/Lindo) and Vairë, and it is unclear if *Nelmir* was supposed to be just another name for *Naimi* or an altogether separate character.

The name *Nelmir* seems to be in Qenya, but, as with *Naimi*, its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-Eld_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eld-2">[2]</a></sup>
