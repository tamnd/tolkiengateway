**1984-1997: [Middle-earth Role Playing](../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md):**

<dl><dd>Helmets of this design are usually referred to as being “<a href="../K/Karma.md" title="Karma">Karma</a> helms” or “of the Númenórean karma design”.</dd></dl>

<dl><dd><a href="../A/Anárion.md" title="Anárion">Anárion</a>&#39;s Crown-helm, <a href="../I/Isildur.md" title="Isildur">Isildur</a>&#39;s Crown-helm (which was the initial <a href="../C/Crown_of_Gondor.md" title="Crown of Gondor">Crown of Gondor</a>), and the Crown-helm of <a href="../E/Elros.md" title="Elros">Elros</a> are also noted to be of the karma design, described as resembling a stylized fish with a white-scaled ridge, though the materials of the actual helm differed. Anárion and Isildur had helms of <a href="../M/Metals_in_Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md#Ithilnaur" title="Metals in Middle-earth Role Playing">black ithilnaur</a>, inlaid with ivory and gold (Anárion) or silver (Isildur). The helm of Elros, however, was of blue ithilnaur inlaid with silver and adamant.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> The <a href="../M/Mouth_of_Sauron.md" title="Mouth of Sauron">Mouth of Sauron</a>&#39;s “Helm of the Dark”, a black helmet trimmed with dark gems and glittering scales, was designed after the karma helmets of kings.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd>Corsair Vargaelas&#39; helmet was described as being of the karma design, and so was warlord <a href="../A/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon.md#Sangarunya" title="Army of the Southern Dragon">Sangarunya</a>&#39;s silver-gold helmet.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<dl><dd><a href="../T/Tar-Ciryatan.md" title="Tar-Ciryatan">Tar-Ciryatan</a>&#39;s Sea-helm, later worn by his son <a href="../W/Witch-king.md#Tabletop_Games" title="Witch-king">Mûrazôr</a>, was a variation that ended in a crown-shaped crest. This variation was associated with Uinendil (<a href="../S/Sindarin.md" title="Sindarin">S.</a> “Followers of <a href="../U/Uinen.md" title="Uinen">Uinen</a>”) captains of Númenor.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup></dd></dl>
