**Nils-Lennart Johannesson** ([1948](/wiki/1948) - [2019](/wiki/2019)) was Professor of English Linguistics at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University" title="wikipedia:Stockholm University">Stockholm University</a>, teaching courses on English syntax, the history of the English language, and [Old](/wiki/Old_English "Old English") and [Middle English](/wiki/Middle_English).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was a member of the board of directors of [Arda-sällskapet](/wiki/Arda-s%C3%A4llskapet) (the Arda Society), and published several articles about [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien)'s works.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
