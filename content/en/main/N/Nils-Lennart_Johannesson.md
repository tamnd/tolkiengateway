**Nils-Lennart Johannesson** ([1948](../_/1948.md) - [2019](../_/2019.md)) was Professor of English Linguistics at <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University" title="wikipedia:Stockholm University">Stockholm University</a>, teaching courses on English syntax, the history of the English language, and [Old](../O/Old_English.md) and [Middle English](../M/Middle_English.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> He was a member of the board of directors of [Arda-sällskapet](../A/Arda-sällskapet.md) (the Arda Society), and published several articles about [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s works.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
