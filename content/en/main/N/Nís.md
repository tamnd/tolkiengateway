<i><b>nís</b></i> (pl. <i><b>nissi</b></i>) means "woman" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), referring to an adult female – [elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves"), [mortal](/wiki/Men "Men"), or of other [speaking race](/wiki/Races "Races").<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

PQ *ndīse* "bride" (from [root](/wiki/Root "Root") NDIS)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
