<i><b>nís</b></i> (pl. <i><b>nissi</b></i>) means "woman" in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), referring to an adult female – [elf](../E/Elves.md), [mortal](../M/Men.md), or of other [speaking race](../R/Races.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

PQ *ndīse* "bride" (from [root](../R/Root.md) NDIS)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
