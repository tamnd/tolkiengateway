<i><b>noldo</b></i> (earlier pronounced <i><b>ngoldo</b></i> or <i><b>ŋoldo</b></i>) is a noun meaning "One of the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor)" in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) (the Noldor were the second tribe of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) of the [Great Journey](/wiki/Great_Journey)).<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Noldo.gif"><img src="/w/images/e/e1/Noldo.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Noldo* is also the name in Quenya of the nineteenth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-1">[1]</a></sup> It is the third letter of the fifth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "nasal" consonants and it is the fifth letter in the third series of consonants. For written Quenya or [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the third series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](/wiki/Calmat%C3%A9ma)* and this letter represents initial **NG** or **Ŋ**. In [Westron](/wiki/Westron) the third series is used for a variety of consonantal sounds and this letter represents **NY**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Nyé</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [ÑGOL](/wiki/%C3%91GOL).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
