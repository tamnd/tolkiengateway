**Narya**,<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rings_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-5">[5]</a></sup> the **Red Ring**,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-5">[5]</a></sup> the **Ring of Fire**,<sup id="cite_ref-TA_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rings_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-5">[5]</a></sup> or the **Third Ring**<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-4">[4]</a></sup> was one of the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), specifically one of the "[Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) for the Elven Kings under the sky".

## History {#History}

Created by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) after *Annatar* had left [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion), it was free of Annatar's ([Sauron](/wiki/Sauron)'s) influence due to the fact that the Elves hid their three rings from him upon discerning his intent, but it was still bound to [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").

After [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) arrived in [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1000](/wiki/Third_Age_1000 "Third Age 1000")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> at the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) in [Lindon](/wiki/Lindon) Círdan gave *Narya* to Gandalf to aid him in his labours<sup id="cite_ref-TA_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-6">[6]</a></sup>.

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Narya* is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), deriving from the elements *[nár](/wiki/N%C3%A1r "Nár")* ("fire as an element")<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> + *[-ya](/wiki/-ya)* (adjective suffix).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early manuscript version of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* Narya was known as [Kemen](/wiki/Kemen), the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for Earth. <sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In the text *[Concerning Galadriel and Celeborn](/wiki/Concerning_Galadriel_and_Celeborn)* that was published in the book *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* Celebrimbor entrusted Narya to Gil-Galad after he discovered the existence of the One Ring after Sauron had departed from Eregion around S.A. 1500 and Gil-galad gave it to Círdan before Sauron invaded Eriador in S.A. 1695, but later in the text Gil-galad kept Narya and only gave it to Círdan when he departed from Lindon in the days of the Last Alliance.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>
