**Nan Curunír** was the name given in the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) to the valley in the southern feet of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains) where [Isengard](/wiki/Isengard) stood. The name is taken from *[Curunír](/wiki/Curun%C3%ADr)*, the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name of the [Wizard](/wiki/Wizards "Wizards") [Saruman](/wiki/Saruman), who dwelt there.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nan Curunír* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Saruman's Vale", consisting of <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Nan" title="Nan">nan</a></i> and *[Curunír](/wiki/Curun%C3%ADr)*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

An early form of this name while writing *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* was *Nan Gurunír* which appears as such in the [General Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/General_Map_of_Middle-earth) as published. The name was corrected later in [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes)'s *[A Map of Middle-earth](/wiki/A_Map_of_Middle-earth)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

<b>2001-03: <a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)" title="The Lord of the Rings (film series)"><i>The Lord of the Rings</i> (film series)</a>:</b>

<dl><dd>The valley of Nan Curunír appears in all three movies of <a href="/wiki/Peter_Jackson" title="Peter Jackson">Peter Jackson&#39;s</a> film trilogy.</dd></dl>

<b>2004: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The valley makes appearance as part of Isengard map.</dd></dl>

<b>2006: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Battle_for_Middle-earth_II" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II">The Lord of the Rings: The Battle for Middle-earth II</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The valley and the Ring of Isengard makes appearance once again, though its depiction differs from the first game.</dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online:_Rise_of_Isengard" title="The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard">The Lord of the Rings Online: Rise of Isengard</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Nan Curunír is considered a sub-area of the <a href="/wiki/Dunland" title="Dunland">Dunland</a> region for gameplay purposes. The valley is filled with multiple Orcs, Uruks, Goblins, Wargs and Trolls and several quest chains take place there.</dd></dl>
