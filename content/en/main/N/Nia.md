**nia** is a [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> pronoun meaning "mine"

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*nia* is the dative of personal pronoun <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Ni&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Ni (page does not exist)">ni</a></i> "I".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> From [Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NI&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NI (page does not exist)">NI</a> <span></span>
