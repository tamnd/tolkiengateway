The **Nargil Pass** was a pass over the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) between [Near Harad](/wiki/Near_Harad) and [Nurn](/wiki/Nurn) in the south of [Mordor](/wiki/Mordor).<sup id="cite_ref-NargilPass_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NargilPass-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that one of the great roads of Mordor that ran south to tributary lands, from which the soldiers of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) brought wagon-trains of goods, booty and slaves to supply the armies of Mordor, ran over the Nargil Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Nargil Pass is not mentioned in the narrative of the final published versions of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* and is not mentioned in the maps that were published with *The Lord of the Rings* and with *[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales_of_N%C3%BAmenor_and_Middle-earth)*. The name Nargil pass is mentioned in the earliest draft of the chapter "[The Muster of Rohan](/wiki/The_Muster_of_Rohan)" and the name *Nargul Pass* is mentioned in a later draft of that chapter.

In those drafts it is said that Orcs or the forces of [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron), respectively, used the pass to move from Mordor to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Nargil Pass is spelled "Narghil Pass" on the [First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and "Nargil Pass" on the [1943 Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_1943_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The 1943 Map of The Lord of the Rings").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The Nargil Pass is located in the southern part of the Ephel Dúath east of the headwaters of the river Harnen and east of [Barad-dûr](/wiki/Barad-d%C3%BBr) on the First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-NargilPass_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NargilPass-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Although the [Second Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Second Map of The Lord of the Rings") and the [Third Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](/wiki/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings") do not include a label "Nargil Pass", the contour lines of the Ephel Dúath on those maps indicate a gap in approximately the same location as on the First Map of *The Lord of the Rings* that could be a pass.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nargil* is [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Roman Rausch and Matthew Dinse suggest that Nargil likely contains [lenited](/wiki/Lenition "Lenition") *cîl* ("cleft, pass between hills, gorge") (KIL). The initial element either contains syncopated *narg-* from *NÁRAK* ("tear, rend") or *nar-* from *NAR-* ("flame, fire"), perhaps referring to the colour red. Possible tentative translations could be "Rending Gorge", "Red Pass" or "Fire Pass".<sup id="cite_ref-Sindanoorie_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindanoorie-12">[12]</a></sup>

The earlier name <i><b>Narghil</b></i> could be explained instead as an initial element *narg-* and a possible word for "gorge, pass" that derives from root SKIL, alternate to KIL.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindanoorie_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindanoorie-12">[12]</a></sup>
