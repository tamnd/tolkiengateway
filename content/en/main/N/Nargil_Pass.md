The **Nargil Pass** was a pass over the [Ephel Dúath](../E/Ephel_Dúath.md) between [Near Harad](Near_Harad.md) and [Nurn](Nurn.md) in the south of [Mordor](../M/Mordor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-NargilPass_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NargilPass-1">[1]</a></sup>

It is possible that one of the great roads of Mordor that ran south to tributary lands, from which the soldiers of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) brought wagon-trains of goods, booty and slaves to supply the armies of Mordor, ran over the Nargil Pass.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The Nargil Pass is not mentioned in the narrative of the final published versions of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* and is not mentioned in the maps that were published with *The Lord of the Rings* and with *[Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](../U/Unfinished_Tales_of_Númenor_and_Middle-earth.md)*. The name Nargil pass is mentioned in the earliest draft of the chapter "[The Muster of Rohan](../T/The_Muster_of_Rohan.md)" and the name *Nargul Pass* is mentioned in a later draft of that chapter.

In those drafts it is said that Orcs or the forces of [Sauron](../S/Sauron.md), respectively, used the pass to move from Mordor to the south.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The Nargil Pass is spelled "Narghil Pass" on the [First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](../T/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> and "Nargil Pass" on the [1943 Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](../T/The_1943_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> The Nargil Pass is located in the southern part of the Ephel Dúath east of the headwaters of the river Harnen and east of [Barad-dûr](../B/Barad-dûr.md) on the First Map of *The Lord of the Rings*.<sup id="cite_ref-NargilPass_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NargilPass-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

Although the [Second Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](../T/The_Second_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) and the [Third Map of *The Lord of the Rings*](../T/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) do not include a label "Nargil Pass", the contour lines of the Ephel Dúath on those maps indicate a gap in approximately the same location as on the First Map of *The Lord of the Rings* that could be a pass.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nargil* is [Noldorin](Noldorin.md) but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup> Roman Rausch and Matthew Dinse suggest that Nargil likely contains [lenited](../L/Lenition.md) *cîl* ("cleft, pass between hills, gorge") (KIL). The initial element either contains syncopated *narg-* from *NÁRAK* ("tear, rend") or *nar-* from *NAR-* ("flame, fire"), perhaps referring to the colour red. Possible tentative translations could be "Rending Gorge", "Red Pass" or "Fire Pass".<sup id="cite_ref-Sindanoorie_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindanoorie-12">[12]</a></sup>

The earlier name <i><b>Narghil</b></i> could be explained instead as an initial element *narg-* and a possible word for "gorge, pass" that derives from root SKIL, alternate to KIL.<sup id="cite_ref-Sindanoorie_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sindanoorie-12">[12]</a></sup>
