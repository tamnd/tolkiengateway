<i><b>Nightshade Forests</b></i> is the first EP by [Summoning](/wiki/Summoning), released in [1997](/wiki/1997). It consists of four songs left over from the [Dol Guldur](</wiki/Dol_Guldur_(album)> "Dol Guldur (album)") sessions in [1996](/wiki/1996).

## Track listing {#Track_listing}

1. Mirkwood - 9:45
2. Kortirion Among the Trees - 8:51
3. Flesh and Blood - 7:55
4. Habbanan Beneath the Stars – 7:13
