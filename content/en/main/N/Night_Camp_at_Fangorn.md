**Night Camp at Fangorn** is the eighth scene of *[The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers.md)* and the tenth scene of [*The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers* (extended edition)](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Two_Towers_(extended_edition).md>). This scene was extended in the latter edition.
