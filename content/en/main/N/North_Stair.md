The **North Stair** was an ancient stair cut by the [Gondorians](/wiki/Gondorians)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the days of the great [kings](/wiki/Kings_of_Gondor "Kings of Gondor")<sup id="cite_ref-River_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-2">[2]</a></sup> of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor)<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-3">[3]</a></sup> on the eastern side of the Falls of [Rauros](/wiki/Rauros) that went up from the [Nindalf](/wiki/Nindalf) to the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> to carry boats between the lake of [Nen Hithoel](/wiki/Nen_Hithoel) and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Rauros-foot&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rauros-foot (page does not exist)">Rauros-foot</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-River_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-2">[2]</a></sup> the basin at the foot of the [Falls of Rauros](/wiki/Falls_of_Rauros).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) was camped above [Sarn Gebir](/wiki/Sarn_Gebir), [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) objected to traveling across the lake, pretending that there was no way down past the falls. [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) sternly reminded him of the stairway and the possibility of carrying their boats to the lower river.<sup id="cite_ref-River_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, when the Company was awaiting [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins")'s decision at [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen), Aragorn spoke to the others about the great works of Gondor in the area, including the great Stair beside the falls of Rauros.<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-3">[3]</a></sup>
