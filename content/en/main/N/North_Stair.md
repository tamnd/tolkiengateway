The **North Stair** was an ancient stair cut by the [Gondorians](../G/Gondorians.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> in the days of the great [kings](../K/Kings_of_Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-River_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-2">[2]</a></sup> of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md)<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-3">[3]</a></sup> on the eastern side of the Falls of [Rauros](../R/Rauros.md) that went up from the [Nindalf](Nindalf.md) to the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md)<sup id="cite_ref-UI_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UI-1">[1]</a></sup> to carry boats between the lake of [Nen Hithoel](Nen_Hithoel.md) and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Rauros-foot&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Rauros-foot (page does not exist)">Rauros-foot</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-River_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-2">[2]</a></sup> the basin at the foot of the [Falls of Rauros](../F/Falls_of_Rauros.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) was camped above [Sarn Gebir](../S/Sarn_Gebir.md), [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) objected to traveling across the lake, pretending that there was no way down past the falls. [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) sternly reminded him of the stairway and the possibility of carrying their boats to the lower river.<sup id="cite_ref-River_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-River-2">[2]</a></sup> Later, when the Company was awaiting [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s decision at [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md), Aragorn spoke to the others about the great works of Gondor in the area, including the great Stair beside the falls of Rauros.<sup id="cite_ref-Breaking_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breaking-3">[3]</a></sup>
