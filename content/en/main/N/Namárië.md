**Namárië**, also called **Galadriel's Lament**, **Altariello nainië Lóriendessë** (Quenya for "Galadriel's lament in Lórien"),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and sometimes **Song of the Elves Beyond the Sea**, is the longest [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) text in *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.

The poem was set to music by [Donald Swann](/wiki/Donald_Swann). The sheet music and an audio recording are part of *[The Road Goes Ever On, A Song Cycle](</wiki/The_Road_Goes_Ever_On_(book)> "The Road Goes Ever On (book)")*.

## Poem {#Poem}

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p> Ai! laurië lantar lassi súrinen,<br/>
yéni únótimë ve rámar aldaron!<br/>
Yéni ve lintë yuldar avánier<br/>
mi oromardi lisse-miruvóreva<br/>
Andúnë pella, Vardo tellumar<br/>
nu luini yassen tintilar i eleni<br/>
ómaryo <a href="/wiki/Air%C3%AB" title="Airë">aire</a><a href="/wiki/T%C3%A1ri" title="Tári">tári</a>-lírinen.<br/>
<br/>
Sí man i yulma nin enquantuva?<br/>
<br/>
An sí Tintallë Varda Oiolossëo<br/>
ve fanyar máryat Elentári ortanë,<br/>
ar ilyë tier undulávë lumbulë;<br/>
ar sindanóriello caita mornië<br/>
i falmalinnar imbë met, ar hísië<br/>
untúpa Calaciryo <a href="/wiki/M%C3%ADr%C3%AB" title="Mírë">míri</a> oialë.<br/>
Sí vanwa ná, Rómello vanwa, Valimar!<br/>
<br/>
Namárië! Nai hiruvalyë Valimar.<br/>
Nai elyë hiruva. Namárië!
</p>
</div>

### Translation {#Translation}

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg/300px-Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="214" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/c/cc/Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg/450px-Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/c/cc/Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg/600px-Anna_Kulisz_-_Galadriels_Song.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1000" data-file-height="714"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel&#39;s Song</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Anna_Kulisz" title="Category:Images by Anna Kulisz">Anna Kulisz</a></figcaption></figure>

<div style="font-style:italic; margin-left:20px;" class="poem">
<p>Ah! like gold fall the leaves in the wind, <br/>
long years numberless as the wings of trees! <br/>
The years have passed like swift draughts <br/>
of the sweet mead in lofty halls beyond the West, <br/>
beneath the blue vaults of <a href="/wiki/Varda" title="Varda">Varda</a> <br/>
wherein the stars tremble in the song of her voice, holy and queenly. <br/>
<br/>
Who now shall refill the cup for me? <br/>
<br/>
For now the Kindler, Varda, the Queen of the Stars, <br/>
from Mount Everwhite has uplifted her hands like clouds, <br/>
and all paths are drowned deep in shadow; <br/>
and out of a grey country darkness<br/>
lies on the foaming waves between us,<br/>
and mist covers the jewels of <a href="/wiki/Calacirya" title="Calacirya">Calacirya</a> for ever. <br/>
Now lost, lost to those from the East is <a href="/wiki/Valimar" title="Valimar">Valimar</a>!<br/>
<br/>
Farewell! Maybe thou shalt find Valimar. <br/>
Maybe even thou shalt find it. Farewell!
</p>
</div>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word *namárië* means "farewell" in Quenya.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The word can be analyzed as *[ná-](/wiki/N%C3%A1- "Ná-")* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=M%C3%A1ri%C3%AB&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Márië (page does not exist)">márië</a></i>, being a blessing and meaning literally "be well".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
