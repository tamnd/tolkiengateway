The **Northway** was a road in [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). It is named as such only in the 1960 rewrite of *[The Hobbit](../T/The_Hobbit.md)*, published in *[The History of The Hobbit: Return to Bag-End](../T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit%253A_Return_to_Bag-End.md)*, so it may not be considered canonical.<sup id="cite_ref-HH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HH-1">[1]</a></sup>

The road sprang from the [East Road](../E/East_Road.md) just west of [Frogmorton](../F/Frogmorton.md), crossed [the Water](../T/The_Water.md), and led to [Oatbarton](../O/Oatbarton.md). A side-road branched off just before the Northway reached the [Northfarthing](Northfarthing.md) and stretched to [Dwaling](../D/Dwaling.md) and [Brockenbores](../B/Brockenbores.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On the intersection stood an inn, the [All-Welcome Inn](../A/All-Welcome_Inn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-HH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HH-1">[1]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Northway runs from <a href="../B/Budgeford.md" title="Budgeford">Budgeford</a> through <a class="mw-redirect" href="../B/Brockenborings.md" title="Brockenborings">Brockenborings</a> and <a href="../O/Oatbarton.md" title="Oatbarton">Oatbarton</a>, ending in <a href="../D/Dwaling.md" title="Dwaling">Dwaling</a>.</dd></dl>
