The **Northway** was a road in [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). It is named as such only in the 1960 rewrite of *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, published in *[The History of The Hobbit: Return to Bag-End](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit:_Return_to_Bag-End)*, so it may not be considered canonical.<sup id="cite_ref-HH_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HH-1">[1]</a></sup>

The road sprang from the [East Road](/wiki/East_Road) just west of [Frogmorton](/wiki/Frogmorton), crossed [the Water](/wiki/The_Water "The Water"), and led to [Oatbarton](/wiki/Oatbarton). A side-road branched off just before the Northway reached the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) and stretched to [Dwaling](/wiki/Dwaling) and [Brockenbores](/wiki/Brockenbores).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> On the intersection stood an inn, the [All-Welcome Inn](/wiki/All-Welcome_Inn).<sup id="cite_ref-HH_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HH-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>The Northway runs from <a href="/wiki/Budgeford" title="Budgeford">Budgeford</a> through <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Brockenborings" title="Brockenborings">Brockenborings</a> and <a href="/wiki/Oatbarton" title="Oatbarton">Oatbarton</a>, ending in <a href="/wiki/Dwaling" title="Dwaling">Dwaling</a>.</dd></dl>
