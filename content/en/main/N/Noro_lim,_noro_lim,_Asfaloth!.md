<i><b>Noro lim, noro lim, Asfaloth!</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) phrase cried by [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel) to [Asfaloth](/wiki/Asfaloth) while helping [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) to escape from the [Black Riders](/wiki/Black_Riders). Glorfindel had halted from his horse, telling Frodo to run, but as the hobbit was relucting, Glorfindel commanded the horse in the elf-tongue, making Asfaloth run at once right before the Riders appeared.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The phrase means "run swift, run swift, Asfaloth", from imperative *[nor-](/wiki/Nor- "Nor-")* ("run") and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Lim&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Lim (page does not exist)">lim</a></i> ("quick, swift").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
