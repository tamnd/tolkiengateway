**Nénimë** ([Nínui](Nínui.md) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was the second month of the year in the [Kings' Reckoning](../K/Kings'_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md). In both calendars, *Nénimë* followed the month [Narvinyë](Narvinyë.md), preceded the month of [Súlimë](../S/Súlimë.md), and was 30 days long, lasting from the 32nd through the 61st days of the year. In the [New Reckoning](New_Reckoning.md) it was the eleventh month of the year.

*Nénimë* in the Kings' and Stewards' Reckoning corresponded to [Solmath](../S/Solmath.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [22 January](../_/22_January.md) through [20 February](../_/20_February.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
