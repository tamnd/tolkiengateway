**Nénimë** ([Nínui](/wiki/N%C3%ADnui) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was the second month of the year in the [Kings' Reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning). In both calendars, *Nénimë* followed the month [Narvinyë](/wiki/Narviny%C3%AB), preceded the month of [Súlimë](/wiki/S%C3%BAlim%C3%AB), and was 30 days long, lasting from the 32nd through the 61st days of the year. In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) it was the eleventh month of the year.

*Nénimë* in the Kings' and Stewards' Reckoning corresponded to [Solmath](/wiki/Solmath) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [22 January](/wiki/22_January) through [20 February](/wiki/20_February)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
