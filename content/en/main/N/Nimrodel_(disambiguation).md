**Nimrodel** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel), a [Silvan](/wiki/Silvan_Elves "Silvan Elves") [Elf](/wiki/Elves "Elves") from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) who was lost searching for [her lover](/wiki/Amroth "Amroth").
    - *[Lay of Nimrodel](/wiki/Lay_of_Nimrodel)*, a lay about her loss and her love for [Amroth](/wiki/Amroth), partially sung by [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*.
- [Nimrodel (river)](</wiki/Nimrodel_(river)>), a river of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). It is unclear whether the river or the Elf came first.
