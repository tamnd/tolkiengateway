**Nimrodel** can refer to:

## Middle-earth {#Middle-earth}

- [Nimrodel](Nimrodel.md), a [Silvan](../S/Silvan_Elves.md) [Elf](../E/Elves.md) from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) who was lost searching for [her lover](../A/Amroth.md).
    - *[Lay of Nimrodel](../L/Lay_of_Nimrodel.md)*, a lay about her loss and her love for [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md), partially sung by [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) in *[The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.
- [Nimrodel (river)](<Nimrodel_(river).md>), a river of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md). It is unclear whether the river or the Elf came first.
