## Quenya {#Quenya}

<i><b>nossë</b></i> is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word meaning "kindred, family".<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

In the Quenya of the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies"), *nosse* is glossed as "clan, family, 'house'", derived from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [NŌ](/wiki/ONO#Other_versions "ONO") ("beget").<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

### Cognates {#Cognates}

- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[nos](/wiki/Nos "Nos")*<sup id="cite_ref-PM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PM-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Examples {#Examples}

- *Nos Finrod* ("House of [Finrod](/wiki/Finrod)")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
- *Nos Feanor* ("[House of Feanor](/wiki/House_of_F%C3%ABanor "House of Fëanor")")<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Qenya {#Qenya}

<i><b>nosse</b></i> (or *nossë*) is a [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya) noun meaning "folk, kin, people".<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-6">[6]</a></sup>

### Etymology {#Etymology}

- [Root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [NŌ](/wiki/ONO#Other_versions "ONO") ("become, be born")<sup id="cite_ref-LT1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LT1-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-6">[6]</a></sup>
