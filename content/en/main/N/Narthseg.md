**Narthseg** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elf) of [Artanor](/wiki/Artanor).

## History {#History}

Narthseg belonged to the people of [Artanor](/wiki/Artanor), but later betrayed both Artanor and [Tinwelint](/wiki/Thingol "Thingol") his king by revealing crucial information to [Naugladur](/wiki/Naugladur) lord of [Nogrod](/wiki/Nogrod) which resulted in Tinwelint's death and the sack of Artanor. He was slain in that fighting.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

**Narthseg** is [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish), though it is not glossed. In Gnomish, *narth* means "odour"<sup id="cite_ref-pe11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe11-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:59</sup> and *seg* means "middle point".<sup id="cite_ref-pe11_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pe11-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:67</sup> As words for "centre" are often used to refer to "heart", this may then mean "Odorous Heart" (cf. *Malcamectë*: "Heart of Evil").
