The **New England Tolkien Society** is a defunct Tolkien society in the north-east region of the United States. As of December 2011, it does not currently have a website nor is anything else known about its events or publications aside from those published by its founder Sumner Gary Hunnewell. Sumner Gary Hunnewell has resided in Missouri rather than Maine in New England for many years.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Magazine {#Magazine}

The New England Tolkien Society published a magazine, *Ravenhill*, in the 1980s and 1990s.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
