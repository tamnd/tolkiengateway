**Núneth** was the wife of [Beregar](/wiki/Beregar) and the mother of [Erendis](/wiki/Erendis), who unhappily married [Tar-Aldarion](/wiki/Tar-Aldarion). Núneth and her family lived in the western part of Númenor before Erendis left to join the household of Queen [Almarian](/wiki/Almarian).

Despite knowing her daughter's headstrong nature, Núneth urged her to marry the prince and gave her advice over the years.

When Erendis dismissed the Elven-birds that had dwelt in her garden in [Armenelos](/wiki/Armenelos) they flew to her parents' house and Núneth correctly divined that it was a sign that Aldarion had left his wife.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Núneth* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and means "Woman of the West" (from *[annûn](/wiki/Ann%C3%BBn "Annûn")*, 'West', and <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=-eth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="-eth (page does not exist)">-eth</a></i>, a feminine suffix).
