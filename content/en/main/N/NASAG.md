**NASAG** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") of unknown primary signification, although its derivatives relate to "compulsion, bond".<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nasag&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nasag (page does not exist)">nasag</a></i> &gt; *nazg-* ("compulsion, bond"); <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nazgw%C4%93&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nazgwē (page does not exist)">nazgwē</a></i> ("bondage, durance")<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Naxa&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Naxa (page does not exist)">naxa</a></i> ("bond, fetter"); <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nasque&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nasque (page does not exist)">nasque</a></i> ("bondage, durance")<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nadha&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nadha (page does not exist)">nadha</a></i> ("fetter")<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech): *[nazg](/wiki/Nazg "Nazg")*<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

Before undertaking a revision in the late 1960s, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) had written (probably in 1951) that root NASAG yielded the derivative *nazgwē*, historically shifted to *nasque* ("evil [spectre](/wiki/Spectres "Spectres")").<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>
