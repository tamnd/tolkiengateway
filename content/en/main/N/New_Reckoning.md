[Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md) the [King Elessar](../K/King_Elessar.md)'s **New Reckoning** was the calendar system introduced in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) shortly after the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) and intended to replace the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md). It became the common calendar of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) during the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), except in [The Shire](../T/The_Shire.md) where the use of the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) continued.

The chief feature of the **New Reckoning** calendar was that the year began on the anniversary of the destruction of [the One Ring](../T/The_One_Ring.md).

Its other notable feature was that the birthday of [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md), 30 [Yavannië](../Y/Yavannië.md) in the **New Reckoning** which corresponded with [22 Halimath](../_/22_September.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md), was made a festival in honor of the [Ring-bearer](../R/Ring-bearer.md). In leap-years 30 Yavannië corresponded with 21 Halimath in the Shire Calendar (due to its [Overlithe](../O/Overlithe.md) leap-day at midsummer), so the **New Reckoning** leap-day was inserted between *Yavannië* and the *[Enderi](../E/Enderi.md)*, and Frodo's birthday feast, called *[Cormarë](../C/Cormarë.md)* or "Ringday", was doubled in leap-years.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>
