[Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn) the [King Elessar](/wiki/King_Elessar)'s **New Reckoning** was the calendar system introduced in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) shortly after the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) and intended to replace the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning). It became the common calendar of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) during the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), except in [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire) where the use of the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) continued.

The chief feature of the **New Reckoning** calendar was that the year began on the anniversary of the destruction of [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring").

Its other notable feature was that the birthday of [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins), 30 [Yavannië](/wiki/Yavanni%C3%AB) in the **New Reckoning** which corresponded with [22 Halimath](/wiki/22_September "22 September") in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar), was made a festival in honor of the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearer). In leap-years 30 Yavannië corresponded with 21 Halimath in the Shire Calendar (due to its [Overlithe](/wiki/Overlithe) leap-day at midsummer), so the **New Reckoning** leap-day was inserted between *Yavannië* and the *[Enderi](/wiki/Enderi)*, and Frodo's birthday feast, called *[Cormarë](/wiki/Cormar%C3%AB)* or "Ringday", was doubled in leap-years.<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>
