**Nos Grimsong** is a [Dwarf](../D/Dwarves.md) in Frerin's Court. He was a member of the company of [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) who were led by [Dwalin](../D/Dwalin.md) in T. A. 3016 to investigate Thorin's Gate on [Dáin II](../D/Dáin_Ironfoot.md)'s command.<sup id="cite_ref-return_from_erebor_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return_from_erebor-1">[1]</a></sup> The player was sent to him by [Bolli](../B/Bolli.md) to aid Nos Grimsong with clean up the problems with wild life in Thorin's Gate.<sup id="cite_ref-return_from_erebor_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-return_from_erebor-1">[1]</a></sup> Before Nos can use the player he has to learn more about his class, so Nos sends him to one of the trainers.<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-2">[2]</a></sup> After the player has learnt more from the trainers Nos gives him the task to kill a few lynxes, east of Frerin's Court.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> After the player is finished Nos sends him to [Dori](../D/Dori.md).<sup id="cite_ref-sharpen_your_skills_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sharpen_your_skills-2">[2]</a></sup>
