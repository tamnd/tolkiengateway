**Nori** was one of [Durin's Folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_Folk) and likely dwelt in the northern [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) with [Thorin](/wiki/Thorin) after the latter had settled there.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> He was one of the thirteen Dwarves of [Thorin's company](/wiki/Thorin_and_Company "Thorin and Company") who journeyed to [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain") to challenge the [dragon](/wiki/Dragons "Dragons") [Smaug](/wiki/Smaug). After the death of Smaug, Nori lived with his share of the treasure at the Lonely Mountain.<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

### Family and birth {#Family_and_birth}

Nori's parentage is unknown, although he was a remote kinsman of Thorin and a member of the [House of Durin](/wiki/House_of_Durin).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> He was also the brother of [Dori](/wiki/Dori), and a cousin of [Ori](/wiki/Ori).<sup id="cite_ref-Person_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Person-1">[1]</a></sup> His birth year is also unknown.

However, in [Bilbo Baggins](/wiki/Bilbo_Baggins)' account of the quest to Erebor he stated that the brothers [Fíli](/wiki/F%C3%ADli) and [Kíli](/wiki/K%C3%ADli) were the youngest of the thirteen "by some fifty years",<sup id="cite_ref-Hill_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hill-4">[4]</a></sup> and that when Thorin was missing [Balin](/wiki/Balin) was "the eldest left".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Since Kíli was born in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2864](/wiki/Third_Age_2864 "Third Age 2864") and Balin was born in [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763"),<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-2">[2]</a></sup> this would put Nori's birth between [2763](/wiki/Third_Age_2763 "Third Age 2763") and c. 2814, and he would have been between c. 127 and 178 years old during the quest to Erebor.

### The Quest of Erebor {#The_Quest_of_Erebor}

Nori was one of the five Dwarves in the fourth group to arrive at [Bag End](/wiki/Bag_End). He wore a purple hood. During the evening when Thorin called for music, Nori (along with Dori and Ori) played the flute.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> In Beorn's Halls, when [Gandalf](/wiki/Gandalf) was telling the story of the company's adventures to [Beorn](/wiki/Beorn), Nori and Ori were the second two Dwarves to show up.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> Nori fought in and survived the [Battle of Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_Five_Armies). He was given his share of the treasure by [Dáin Ironfoot](/wiki/D%C3%A1in_Ironfoot), and made his home in Erebor alongside with Dori and Ori.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

### Later life {#Later_life}

Nori lived a wealthy life after the quest to reclaim the Lonely Mountain. His date of death is unknown but he was still alive during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-Meetings_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meetings-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nóri* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It most likely means "Little shaver, small bit of something".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

<dl><dd><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt"></blockquote></dd></dl>

Perhaps the most elusive member of The Company of Thorin Oakenshield, Nori is often in trouble with the dwarvish authorities. Deciding it might be a good time to leave town, he readily joins the Quest for the Lonely Mountain, not realizing the journey ahead may well lead him into far more trouble than he has left behind. Nobody ever quite knows what the quick-witted and wily Nori is up to, except that it’s guaranteed to be dodgy and quite possibly illegal. Despite rarely seeing eye-to-eye with his brothers Dori and Ori, he is nonetheless immensely loyal and will protect them by whatever means possible.

His brother Dori forms a small band of dwarves to rescue him from his folly, including his old companions <a href="/wiki/Bofur" title="Bofur">Bofur</a> and <a href="/wiki/Gl%C3%B3in" title="Glóin">Glóin</a>, and together with the player they follow Nori&#39;s trail across Ered Mithrin.
