**na** is the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) verb for "to be".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

Also, *ná* means "yes, it is so"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Root](/wiki/Sundocarm%C3%AB "Sundocarmë") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NA2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NA2 (page does not exist)">NA2</a><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Forms {#Forms}

Various attested forms of "to be"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

- *ná* "is"
    - *nánye/nanye* "I am"
    - *nalye* "you are" (polite)
    - *natye* "you are" (familiar)
    - *nás "it is"*
    - *náse* "(s)he is"
    - *nalme* "we are"
    - *nát* "they (dual) are"
    - *nar/nár* "they are"
- *náne/né* "was"
    - *anen* "I was"
    - *anel* "you were"
    - *nése* "he was"
    - *anes* "(s)he/it was"
    - *nét* "they (dual) were"
    - *náner/nér* "they were"
- *uva/nauva* "will be"
    - *nauvan* "I will be"
- *anaie* "has been"

- *náve* "being" (infinitive/gerund)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
- *na* "let it be" (imperative)
    - *[nai](/wiki/Nai "Nai")* "be it that"

## Syntax {#Syntax}

*Na* is following the sentece. When used in imperative, it precedes the adjective.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

- *na aire* "be holy"
- *aire na* "it is holy"
