<i><b>Nén</b></i> (nen-) is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) for "water".<sup id="cite_ref-etym_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-etym-1">[1]</a></sup>

See also [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NENE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NENE (page does not exist)">NENE</a>, [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")*.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In earlier [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), the word *[nen](</wiki/Nen_(river_in_Valinor)> "Nen (river in Valinor)")* (with short *e*) means "river, water".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
