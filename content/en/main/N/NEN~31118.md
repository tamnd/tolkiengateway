**NEN** is a [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "water".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](../C/Common_Eldarin.md): *nendā* ("watery, wet")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[nēn](Nén.md)* ("water");<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> *[nenya](Nenya.md)* ("wet");<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> *[nende](Nende.md)*;<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup> *nelle* ("brook")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[nîn](Nîn.md)* ("wet"); *[nĕn](Nen.md)* ("water, lake")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](Noldorin.md): *[nen](Nen.md)*; *nend, nenn* ("watery")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root NENE, given the signification "flow?".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
