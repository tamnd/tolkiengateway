**NEN** is a [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "water".<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Common Eldarin](/wiki/Common_Eldarin): *nendā* ("watery, wet")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[nēn](/wiki/N%C3%A9n "Nén")* ("water");<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> *[nenya](/wiki/Nenya "Nenya")* ("wet");<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup> *[nende](/wiki/Nende "Nende")*;<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup> *nelle* ("brook")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[nîn](/wiki/N%C3%AEn "Nîn")* ("wet"); *[nĕn](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("water, lake")<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin): *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")*; *nend, nenn* ("watery")<sup id="cite_ref-Ety_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the Legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_Legendarium}

In the *Qenya Lexicon* appears the root NENE, given the signification "flow?".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
