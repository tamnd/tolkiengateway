The **Northfarthing** was the cold northern [Farthing](/wiki/Farthings "Farthings") of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire").

## Geography {#Geography}

The Northfarthing is the only one of the four farthings that does not end in the [Three-Farthing Stone](/wiki/Three-Farthing_Stone); its southernmost point lay about eleven miles north of it<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and along its northern border were the high [North Moors](/wiki/North_Moors).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It had fresh and fragrant air; when [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") entered [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) for the first time, the air of the region reminded them of the uplands of the Northfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The North Moors was the only place in the Shire where heavy snow was common.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Geographic features {#Geographic_features}

The farthing contains [Bindbole Wood](/wiki/Bindbole_Wood), a river (perhaps the [Norbourn](/wiki/Norbourn)), and the [Northway](/wiki/Northway) Road that led to the town of [Oatbarton](/wiki/Oatbarton).

Northfarthing was rocky, and settlements like [Hardbottle](/wiki/Hardbottle) were delved in or made from its stones.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup>

A branch of the [Took Family](/wiki/Took_Family) called the [North-tooks](/wiki/North-took_Family "North-took Family") lived in a town called [Long Cleeve](/wiki/Long_Cleeve).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Given the reference to the north (when most Tooks lived in the [Westfarthing](/wiki/Westfarthing)), Long Cleeve was probably located in the Northfarthing.

## History {#History}

The only notable historical event that ever occurred in the Northfarthing was the [Battle of Greenfields](/wiki/Battle_of_Greenfields) ([T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2747](/wiki/Third_Age_2747 "Third Age 2747")).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It was the only battle fought within the boundaries of the Shire until the [Battle of Bywater](/wiki/Battle_of_Bywater).

Around the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring), [Halfred Gamgee](/wiki/Halfred_Gamgee) moved to Northfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> <span></span>
