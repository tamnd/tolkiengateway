The **Northfarthing** was the cold northern [Farthing](../F/Farthings.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md).

## Geography {#Geography}

The Northfarthing is the only one of the four farthings that does not end in the [Three-Farthing Stone](../T/Three-Farthing_Stone.md); its southernmost point lay about eleven miles north of it<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and along its northern border were the high [North Moors](North_Moors.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

It had fresh and fragrant air; when [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) entered [Ithilien](../I/Ithilien.md) for the first time, the air of the region reminded them of the uplands of the Northfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The North Moors was the only place in the Shire where heavy snow was common.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

### Geographic features {#Geographic_features}

The farthing contains [Bindbole Wood](../B/Bindbole_Wood.md), a river (perhaps the [Norbourn](Norbourn.md)), and the [Northway](Northway.md) Road that led to the town of [Oatbarton](../O/Oatbarton.md).

Northfarthing was rocky, and settlements like [Hardbottle](../H/Hardbottle.md) were delved in or made from its stones.<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-5">[5]</a></sup>

A branch of the [Took Family](../T/Took_Family.md) called the [North-tooks](North-took_Family.md) lived in a town called [Long Cleeve](../L/Long_Cleeve.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> Given the reference to the north (when most Tooks lived in the [Westfarthing](../W/Westfarthing.md)), Long Cleeve was probably located in the Northfarthing.

## History {#History}

The only notable historical event that ever occurred in the Northfarthing was the [Battle of Greenfields](../B/Battle_of_Greenfields.md) ([T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [2747](../T/Third_Age_2747.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup> It was the only battle fought within the boundaries of the Shire until the [Battle of Bywater](../B/Battle_of_Bywater.md).

Around the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md), [Halfred Gamgee](../H/Halfred_Gamgee.md) moved to Northfarthing.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> <span></span>
