<i><b>nende</b></i> means "lake"<sup id="cite_ref-PE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE-1">[1]</a></sup> or "pool"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md). It has been suggested that the word would appear as *nendë* in the Quenya form of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
