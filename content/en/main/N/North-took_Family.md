The **North-took** family were the descendants of [Bandobras Took](/wiki/Bandobras_Took), who settled in [Long Cleeve](/wiki/Long_Cleeve)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> presumably in the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> They were therefore a branch of the [Took Family](/wiki/Took_Family).
