<i><b>nwalme</b></i> (earlier pronounced <i><b>ngwalme</b></i> or <i><b>ŋwalme</b></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup>) is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) noun meaning "torment".<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[1]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Nwalme.gif"><img src="/w/images/7/75/Nwalme.gif" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="53" height="65" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="53" data-file-height="65"/></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

*Nwalme* is also the name in Quenya of the twentieth letter of the [Tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar) alphabet.<sup id="cite_ref-Names_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Names-2">[1]</a></sup> It is the fourth letter of the fifth grade or [Tyellë](/wiki/Tyell%C3%AB), which contains "nasal" consonants and it is the fifth letter in the fourth series of consonants. For written Quenya or [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), the fourth series is used for labialized or *kw*-series, the *[Quessetéma](/wiki/Quesset%C3%A9ma)*, and *Nwalme* represents **NGW** or **ŊW**. In [Westron](/wiki/Westron) the fourth series is used for the *k*-series, the *[Calmatéma](/wiki/Calmat%C3%A9ma)*, and this letter represents **NG** or **Ŋ**.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup> The Westron name for this letter is <i><b>Ngá</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The word derives from the [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%91GWAL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="ÑGWAL (page does not exist)">ÑGWAL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>
