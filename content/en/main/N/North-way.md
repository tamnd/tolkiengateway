The **North-way** was the name of a road in the [Pelennor Fields](../P/Pelennor_Fields.md) in [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md).

## Course {#Course}

The North-way ran from the gate of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) to the [north gate](../F/Forannest.md) of the [Rammas Echor](../R/Rammas_Echor.md) where it joined the [Great West Road](../G/Great_West_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On 5 May, [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md) when [Éomer](../_/Éomer.md) and [Éowyn](../_/Éowyn.md)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> rode back from Minas Tirith with the [Rohirrim](../R/Rohirrim.md) and [Elladan](../E/Elladan.md) and [Elrohir](../E/Elrohir.md) on the North-way, the road was lined with people who praised them from the gate of Minas Tirith to the Rammas Echor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> On the evening of Mid-year's day [Elrond](../E/Elrond.md) with his daugther [Arwen](../A/Arwen.md) and his sons Elladan and Elrohir and all the household from [Rivendell](../R/Rivendell.md) and [Galadriel](../G/Galadriel.md) and [Celeborn](../C/Celeborn.md) with many folk from [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) used the North-way to come to the gate of Minas Tirith for the wedding of Arwen with the recently crowned King [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The North-way is called the **North Road** on an unnamed map of the surroundings of Minas Tirith that was drawn by J.R.R. Tolkien during the writing of [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), which is electronically labelled MINAS TIRITH on the website of the Tolkien Estate<sup id="cite_ref-MinasTirithMap_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MinasTirithMap-7">[7]</a></sup>
