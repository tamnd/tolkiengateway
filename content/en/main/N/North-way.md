The **North-way** was the name of a road in the [Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Pelennor_Fields) in [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor).

## Course {#Course}

The North-way ran from the gate of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) to the [north gate](/wiki/Forannest "Forannest") of the [Rammas Echor](/wiki/Rammas_Echor) where it joined the [Great West Road](/wiki/Great_West_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

On 5 May, [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019") when [Éomer](/wiki/%C3%89omer) and [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn)<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> rode back from Minas Tirith with the [Rohirrim](/wiki/Rohirrim) and [Elladan](/wiki/Elladan) and [Elrohir](/wiki/Elrohir) on the North-way, the road was lined with people who praised them from the gate of Minas Tirith to the Rammas Echor.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> On the evening of Mid-year's day [Elrond](/wiki/Elrond) with his daugther [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) and his sons Elladan and Elrohir and all the household from [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) and [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) and [Celeborn](/wiki/Celeborn) with many folk from [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) used the North-way to come to the gate of Minas Tirith for the wedding of Arwen with the recently crowned King [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The North-way is called the **North Road** on an unnamed map of the surroundings of Minas Tirith that was drawn by J.R.R. Tolkien during the writing of [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), which is electronically labelled MINAS TIRITH on the website of the Tolkien Estate<sup id="cite_ref-MinasTirithMap_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MinasTirithMap-7">[7]</a></sup>
