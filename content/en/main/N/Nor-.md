<i><b>nor-</b></i> is a verb meaning "run" or "leap" (of men and animals using legs: *not* of fluids etc.) in both [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) and [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other forms in Quenya {#Other_forms_in_Quenya}

- *norne* = pa.t. "ran"
- *norta-* = "make run, specially used of riding horses or other animals" with form *nortanen* "I rode"
- *norie, norme* = "race, running"

## Other forms in Sindarin {#Other_forms_in_Sindarin}

- *noro* = imperative
- *onur* = pa.t. "ran"
- *northa-* = same as Quenya *norta-*
- *north* = "a riding, a race (of people running), a charge or gallop"
