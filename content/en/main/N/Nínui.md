**Nínui** is the second month of the calendar of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain). It was also called [Nénimë](/wiki/N%C3%A9nim%C3%AB) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Both words mean as much as "Wet"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> - presumably a reference to the late winter rains and their results.
