<i><b>nai</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) word. It can mean perhaps, "may", "maybe", "be it that".<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Verb *[na](Na.md)* and article *[i](../I/I.md)*.

## Example {#Example}

- <i><b>nai</b> elyë hiruva</i> = "maybe even thou shalt find it"<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- <i><b>nai</b> Eru tye mánata</i> = "God bless you"<sup id="cite_ref-Eldarin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eldarin-1">[1]</a></sup>
