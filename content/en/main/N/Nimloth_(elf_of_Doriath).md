**Nimloth** was an elf-maid of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), the daughter of [Galathil](/wiki/Galathil).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-D_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-4">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [497](/wiki/First_Age_497 "First Age 497"),<sup id="cite_ref-D_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-4">[3]</a></sup> she wedded [Dior](/wiki/Dior), King [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol)'s heir, and for a time lived with him by the [Lanthir Lamath](/wiki/Lanthir_Lamath), a waterfall at the base of the [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains). There she gave birth to [Eluréd](/wiki/Elur%C3%A9d) and [Elurín](/wiki/Elur%C3%ADn), her sons, and [Elwing](/wiki/Elwing) her only daughter.

After the murder of King Thingol, she returned to Doriath with her husband and became the queen of Doriath. Their reign was short-lived, however, as in [Yule](/wiki/Yule) of [F.A.](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") [506](/wiki/First_Age_506 "First Age 506")<sup id="cite_ref-Nim_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nim-1">[1]</a></sup> the [Sons of Fëanor](/wiki/Sons_of_F%C3%ABanor) [assaulted](/wiki/Second_Kinslaying "Second Kinslaying") [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth) and slew both her and Dior. Her sons, Eluréd and Elurín, were left in the forest to die by the servants of [Celegorm](/wiki/Celegorm). Elwing, however, escaped to the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion "Mouths of Sirion").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology and names {#Etymology_and_names}

<dl><dd><i>See: <a href="/wiki/Nimloth_(tree)#Etymology" title="Nimloth (tree)">Nimloth (tree)#Etymology</a></i></dd></dl>

The name *Nimloth* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin). It is also the same name as the name of the [White Tree of Númenor](</wiki/Nimloth_(tree)> "Nimloth (tree)"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[5]</a></sup> which means "White Blossom".<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[6]</a></sup>

The earlier name <i><b>Lindis</b></i> is also in Sindarin. According to *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)* it is composed of *[lind](/wiki/Lind "Lind")* ("song") + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=D%C3%AEs&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Dîs (page does not exist)">dîs</a></i> ("bride").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[7]</a></sup>

The name <i><b>Elulin</b></i>, according to *Eldamo*, consists of the elements *[Elu](/wiki/Elu)* (the Sindarin form of *Elwë*) and *lind* ("song").<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The first time Dior's wife was mentioned in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"), she was named <i><b>Lindis</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-D_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-4">[3]</a></sup> An alternative name, <i><b>Elulin</b></i>, was given to her at roughly the same time.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

On the other side of a table with the descendants of Elwë (Thingol), Nimloth is the daughter of Galadhon and the 'sister of Celeborn'. It looks as if the second thought of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was that Nimloth was the daughter of Galathil and the niece of Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-D_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D-4">[3]</a></sup> [Tolkien's](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") later writings offer an alternate ancestry for Celeborn.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup>
