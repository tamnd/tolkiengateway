**Nen Hithoel** was a large lake upon the Great River [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md) amid the [Emyn Muil](../E/Emyn_Muil.md) to the east of [Rohan](../R/Rohan.md).

## Description {#Description}

The lake was approximately 20 miles long from north to south, and 10 miles wide. The lake was formed by a narrow southern outlet and the small island of [Tol Brandir](../T/Tol_Brandir.md), which created a natural dam.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Upon the lake's northern approach from Anduin the [Men](../M/Men.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) carved the huge pillars of the [Argonath](../A/Argonath.md) in the reign of [Rómendacil II](../R/Rómendacil_II.md) to mark the northern boundary of their realm,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> although by the time of the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md) that boundary has long since receded. At the southern end of the lake stood three steep hills. [Amon Hen](../A/Amon_Hen.md), the "Hill of Seeing", was upon the western shore and [Amon Lhaw](../A/Amon_Lhaw.md), the "Hill of Hearing", was upon the east. The third hill formed the island of [Tol Brandir](../T/Tol_Brandir.md). None ever set foot upon the island due to its sheer cliffs that rose directly from the river.<sup id="cite_ref-Break_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Break-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Fellowship of the Ring](../F/Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md) arrived at Nen Hithoel on [25 February](../_/25_February.md), [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3019](../T/Third_Age_3019.md),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and made camp at [Parth Galen](../P/Parth_Galen.md) close to Amon Hen. The fall of [Boromir](../B/Boromir.md) and the breaking of the Fellowship occurred soon after. [Frodo](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md) and [Sam](../S/Samwise_Gamgee.md) took a boat for the eastern shore skirting the southern edge of Tol Brandir,<sup id="cite_ref-Break_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Break-3">[3]</a></sup> [Merry](../M/Meriadoc_Brandybuck.md) and [Pippin](../P/Peregrin_Took.md) were captured by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and after sending Boromir's body over the falls in another boat, [Aragorn](../A/Aragorn.md), [Legolas](../L/Legolas.md) and [Gimli](../G/Gimli.md), calling themselves the *[Three Hunters](../T/Three_Hunters.md)*, set out to track the Orcs and find the captive [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nen Hithoel* is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) for "Mist-cool Water", from *[nen](Nen.md)* ("water") + *[hîth](../H/Hîth.md)* ("mist") + *oel* ("cool").<sup id="cite_ref-Index_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-7">[7]</a></sup> Note that the ([Noldorin](Noldorin.md)) word *[oel](../O/Oel.md)* appeared in the earlier *Etymologies* of the 1930s with the meaning "pool, lake", but this word had changed to (Sindarin) *[ael](../A/Ael.md)* by the time that *The Lord of the Rings* was written, and therefore could not have been an element of the name *Nen Hithoel*. Compare, for example, *[Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md)* with its earlier form *Oelin-uial*.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="../../help/R/References.md" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
