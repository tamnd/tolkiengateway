**Nen Hithoel** was a large lake upon the Great River [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) amid the [Emyn Muil](/wiki/Emyn_Muil) to the east of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan).

## Description {#Description}

The lake was approximately 20 miles long from north to south, and 10 miles wide. The lake was formed by a narrow southern outlet and the small island of [Tol Brandir](/wiki/Tol_Brandir), which created a natural dam.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Upon the lake's northern approach from Anduin the [Men](/wiki/Men) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) carved the huge pillars of the [Argonath](/wiki/Argonath) in the reign of [Rómendacil II](/wiki/R%C3%B3mendacil_II) to mark the northern boundary of their realm,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> although by the time of the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring) that boundary has long since receded. At the southern end of the lake stood three steep hills. [Amon Hen](/wiki/Amon_Hen), the "Hill of Seeing", was upon the western shore and [Amon Lhaw](/wiki/Amon_Lhaw), the "Hill of Hearing", was upon the east. The third hill formed the island of [Tol Brandir](/wiki/Tol_Brandir). None ever set foot upon the island due to its sheer cliffs that rose directly from the river.<sup id="cite_ref-Break_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Break-3">[3]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) arrived at Nen Hithoel on [25 February](/wiki/25_February), [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and made camp at [Parth Galen](/wiki/Parth_Galen) close to Amon Hen. The fall of [Boromir](/wiki/Boromir) and the breaking of the Fellowship occurred soon after. [Frodo](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins "Frodo Baggins") and [Sam](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee "Samwise Gamgee") took a boat for the eastern shore skirting the southern edge of Tol Brandir,<sup id="cite_ref-Break_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Break-3">[3]</a></sup> [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took") were captured by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and after sending Boromir's body over the falls in another boat, [Aragorn](/wiki/Aragorn), [Legolas](/wiki/Legolas) and [Gimli](/wiki/Gimli), calling themselves the *[Three Hunters](/wiki/Three_Hunters)*, set out to track the Orcs and find the captive [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits).<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nen Hithoel* is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) for "Mist-cool Water", from *[nen](/wiki/Nen "Nen")* ("water") + *[hîth](/wiki/H%C3%AEth "Hîth")* ("mist") + *oel* ("cool").<sup id="cite_ref-Index_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Index-7">[7]</a></sup> Note that the ([Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin)) word *[oel](/wiki/Oel "Oel")* appeared in the earlier *Etymologies* of the 1930s with the meaning "pool, lake", but this word had changed to (Sindarin) *[ael](/wiki/Ael "Ael")* by the time that *The Lord of the Rings* was written, and therefore could not have been an element of the name *Nen Hithoel*. Compare, for example, *[Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial)* with its earlier form *Oelin-uial*.<sup class="noprint" style="line-height: 0.4em;">[<a href="/wiki/Help:References" title="Help:References"><i>source?</i></a>]</sup>
