In [Middle-earth Role Playing](/wiki/Middle-earth_Role_Playing), **Ny Chennacatt** was the fortress of [Akhôrahil the Ringwraith](/wiki/Fifth_of_the_Nazg%C3%BBl "Fifth of the Nazgûl") in the [Yellow Mountains](/wiki/Yellow_Mountains), above the ruined city of Chennacatt (which fell to [Ciryatanor](/wiki/Ciryatanor) in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1929&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1929 (page does not exist)">1929</a>). It is the titular subject of the [Nazgûl's Citadel](/wiki/Nazg%C3%BBl%27s_Citadel) supplement.

## History {#History}

Construction began in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_1938&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 1938 (page does not exist)">1938</a>, supervised by Akhôrahil's military leader [Wyatan](/wiki/Army_of_the_Southern_Dragon#Wyatan "Army of the Southern Dragon"), with work being doing alternatively by fine stonemasons from Ciryatanor and Orcs from the Yellow Mountains and Mordor. In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2000&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2000 (page does not exist)">2000</a> Wyatan moved to the fortress, but he was later recalled to Ciryatanor's capital in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2279&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2279 (page does not exist)">2279</a>. After the fall of the Storm King's rule, the fortress was abandoned in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_2285&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 2285 (page does not exist)">2285</a>. In [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Second_Age_3023&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Second Age 3023 (page does not exist)">3023</a>, Orcs moved into the empty fortress.<sup id="cite_ref-citadel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-citadel-1">[1]</a></sup>

Akhôrahil returned in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_1051&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 1051 (page does not exist)">1051</a> with some followers, and subjugated the Orcs who live there. In [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Third_Age_2182&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Third Age 2182 (page does not exist)">2182</a>, after the Army of the Southern Dragon was defeated at old Chennacatt by the army of [Chennacatt](/wiki/S%C3%AErayn#Chennacatt "Sîrayn"), the fortress was ordered to be sealed. Strange lights and foul beasts began to be noticed around [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2400](/wiki/Third_Age_2400 "Third Age 2400") as the demon Magurgoth occupied the ruins, and by [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Fourth_Age_58&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Fourth Age 58 (page does not exist)">58</a> he had gathered a host so large that the fortress formed a threat again.<sup id="cite_ref-citadel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-citadel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> <span></span>
