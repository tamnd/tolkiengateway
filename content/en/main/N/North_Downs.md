The **North Downs** were a range of hills in the northern part of [Eriador](/wiki/Eriador), east of the [Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim) and north of the [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills). From their northernmost point the hills extended in a south-westerly direction. At the southern end was [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain), the ancient city of the [Dúnedain of the North](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain_of_Arnor "Dúnedain of Arnor"), and the northern terminus of the [North-South Road](/wiki/North-South_Road).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the early [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age) the Downs were occupied by [Men](/wiki/Men), as well as [Weather Hills](/wiki/Weather_Hills) and the [Lake Evendim](/wiki/Lake_Evendim).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the year [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [600](/wiki/Second_Age_600 "Second Age 600") [Vëantur](/wiki/V%C3%ABantur) first returned to [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) from [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon hearing that some of their distant kin had come, these [Middle Men](/wiki/Middle_Men) petitioned [Gil-galad](/wiki/Gil-galad) to meet the mariners. Twelve men came and rejoiced to meet the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the kingdom of [Arnor](/wiki/Arnor) was established in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [3320](/wiki/Second_Age_3320 "Second Age 3320")<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the North Downs were incorporated into the realm.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> When Arnor was divided in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [861](/wiki/Third_Age_861 "Third Age 861")<sup id="cite_ref-TA_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-7">[7]</a></sup> the frontier between [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain) and [Rhudaur](/wiki/Rhudaur) was the Weather Hills;<sup id="cite_ref-North_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-8">[8]</a></sup> this frontier may have extended along the eastern edge of the North Downs.

When [Angmar](/wiki/Angmar) attacked Eriador in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [1409](/wiki/Third_Age_1409 "Third Age 1409"), the new King [Araphor](/wiki/Araphor) repelled the enemy from [Fornost Erain](/wiki/Fornost_Erain) and the North Downs, with help from [Cirdan](/wiki/Cirdan).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [1974](/wiki/Third_Age_1974 "Third Age 1974") before the end of winter the [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) came down from Angmar and [captured Fornost](/wiki/Fall_of_Fornost "Fall of Fornost"). King [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) held out on the North Downs until he and some of his guard were driven to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Arriving late, [Eärnur](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnur), the son of King [Eärnil II](/wiki/E%C3%A4rnil_II) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), answered the call for help from Arvedui in [1975](/wiki/Third_Age_1975 "Third Age 1975") with a massive fleet from [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) which disgorged a host marching eastwards. Unaware of his peril the Witch-king marched out to meet the [mighty combined army](</wiki/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor)> "Host of the West (Arnor)") of [Elves](/wiki/Elves) and [Men](/wiki/Men) in the plains between the [Hills of Evendim](/wiki/Hills_of_Evendim) and the North Downs. In the ensuing [Battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost) the forces of Angmar were routed and then destroyed, although the Witch-king himself escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Although the kingdom of Angmar had been defeated and destroyed, so too had Arthedain come to an end. Fornost was abandoned and became a place of fear, acquiring the name Deadmen's Dike.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Other than visits by [Rangers of the North](/wiki/Rangers_of_the_North), the North Downs remained uninhabited for the remainder of the [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age).

In the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age) Men may have returned with the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/High_King" title="High King">High King</a> of the [Reunited Kingdom](/wiki/Reunited_Kingdom) of Arnor and Gondor to Fornost at the North Downs.<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-13">[13]</a></sup> Most likely [King Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn") visited Fornost when he came north to dwell by Lake Evendim for awhile in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [15](/wiki/Fourth_Age_15 "Fourth Age 15").<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
