The **North Downs** were a range of hills in the northern part of [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md), east of the [Hills of Evendim](../H/Hills_of_Evendim.md) and north of the [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md). From their northernmost point the hills extended in a south-westerly direction. At the southern end was [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md), the ancient city of the [Dúnedain of the North](../D/Dúnedain_of_Arnor.md), and the northern terminus of the [North-South Road](North-South_Road.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

In the early [Second Age](../S/Second_Age.md) the Downs were occupied by [Men](../M/Men.md), as well as [Weather Hills](../W/Weather_Hills.md) and the [Lake Evendim](../L/Lake_Evendim.md).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In the year [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [600](../S/Second_Age_600.md) [Vëantur](../V/Vëantur.md) first returned to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) from [Númenor](Númenor.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Upon hearing that some of their distant kin had come, these [Middle Men](../M/Middle_Men.md) petitioned [Gil-galad](../G/Gil-galad.md) to meet the mariners. Twelve men came and rejoiced to meet the Númenóreans.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

When the kingdom of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md) was established in [S.A.](../S/Second_Age.md) [3320](../S/Second_Age_3320.md)<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> the North Downs were incorporated into the realm.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> When Arnor was divided in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [861](../T/Third_Age_861.md)<sup id="cite_ref-TA_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TA-7">[7]</a></sup> the frontier between [Arthedain](../A/Arthedain.md) and [Rhudaur](../R/Rhudaur.md) was the Weather Hills;<sup id="cite_ref-North_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-North-8">[8]</a></sup> this frontier may have extended along the eastern edge of the North Downs.

When [Angmar](../A/Angmar.md) attacked Eriador in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1409](../T/Third_Age_1409.md), the new King [Araphor](../A/Araphor.md) repelled the enemy from [Fornost Erain](../F/Fornost_Erain.md) and the North Downs, with help from [Cirdan](../C/Cirdan.md).<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>

In [1974](../T/Third_Age_1974.md) before the end of winter the [Witch-king](../W/Witch-king.md) came down from Angmar and [captured Fornost](../F/Fall_of_Fornost.md). King [Arvedui](../A/Arvedui.md) held out on the North Downs until he and some of his guard were driven to the north.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup> Arriving late, [Eärnur](../E/Eärnur.md), the son of King [Eärnil II](../E/Eärnil_II.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), answered the call for help from Arvedui in [1975](../T/Third_Age_1975.md) with a massive fleet from [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) which disgorged a host marching eastwards. Unaware of his peril the Witch-king marched out to meet the [mighty combined army](<../H/Host_of_the_West_(Arnor).md>) of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) and [Men](../M/Men.md) in the plains between the [Hills of Evendim](../H/Hills_of_Evendim.md) and the North Downs. In the ensuing [Battle of Fornost](../B/Battle_of_Fornost.md) the forces of Angmar were routed and then destroyed, although the Witch-king himself escaped.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>

Although the kingdom of Angmar had been defeated and destroyed, so too had Arthedain come to an end. Fornost was abandoned and became a place of fear, acquiring the name Deadmen's Dike.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup> Other than visits by [Rangers of the North](../R/Rangers_of_the_North.md), the North Downs remained uninhabited for the remainder of the [Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md).

In the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md) Men may have returned with the <a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="../H/High_King.md" title="High King">High King</a> of the [Reunited Kingdom](../R/Reunited_Kingdom.md) of Arnor and Gondor to Fornost at the North Downs.<sup id="cite_ref-Homeward_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Homeward-13">[13]</a></sup> Most likely [King Elessar](../A/Aragorn.md) visited Fornost when he came north to dwell by Lake Evendim for awhile in [Fo.A.](../F/Fourth_Age.md) [15](../F/Fourth_Age_15.md).<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
