**North End** refers to the northern section of the city of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"). Located here was the [Twenty-first Hall](/wiki/Twenty-first_Hall), which was a important population center for [Balin's Colony](/wiki/Balin%27s_Colony). Also located here was the [Chamber of Mazarbul](/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul). The [Fellowship](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring "Fellowship of the Ring") camped in that section on their journey through Moria.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> <span></span>
