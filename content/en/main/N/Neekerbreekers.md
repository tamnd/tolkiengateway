**Neekerbreekers** were abominable and evil insects dwelling in the [reeds](../R/Reeds.md) and tussocks of the [Midgewater Marshes](../M/Midgewater_Marshes.md).<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

The origin of these creatures is unknown, though they "were evil relatives of the cricket" because of their sound. On [2 October](../_/2_October.md) in [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [3018](../T/Third_Age_3018.md), [Strider](../A/Aragorn.md) led the [Hobbits](../H/Hobbits.md) to [Weathertop](../W/Weathertop.md). While in the [Midgewater Marshes](../M/Midgewater_Marshes.md), the group decided to make camp, though as night fell, most of the group found themselves unable to sleep as a consequence of reportedly hearing thousands of "Neekerbreekers", as Sam called them.<sup id="cite_ref-history_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

"Neekerbreeker" was an "insect-name" that was named so because of their sound, perhaps "like that of a cricket".<sup id="cite_ref-Nomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nomen-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early draft of the chapter "[A Knife in the Dark](../A/A_Knife_in_the_Dark.md)", the creatures known as Neekerbreekers were unnamed and described as being "over-grown cousins of the cricket" because the sound they made was "shriller… and even more persistent" than a cricket.<sup id="cite_ref-nature_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nature-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

It may be possible that these creatures were related to the Gryllidae cricket family.<sup id="cite_ref-name_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-name-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>1982-97: <i><a href="../M/Middle-earth_Role_Playing.md" title="Middle-earth Role Playing">Middle-earth Role Playing</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In the <a href="../_/1994.md" title="1994">1994</a> <i>Creatures of Middle-earth: A Bestiary of Animals and Monsters</i> supplement, Neekerbreekers are &#34;relatives of the mosquito&#34; instead of the cricket and are described as solid meat carnivores that are 1-2 inches tall. Waybread and foul swamps attracts them. An illness called Yellow Rheum can be contracted by Neekerbreekers through bites.<sup id="cite_ref-merp_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-merp-5">[5]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2011: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Card_Game.md" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game">The Lord of the Rings: The Card Game</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>In a card set called <i>The Nîn-in-Eilph</i>, &#34;Neekerbreekers&#34; is the name of an enemy card that can cause a player to deal two damage to a controlled ally if used.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2007: <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd><a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Neekerbreekers" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Neekerbreekers</a> appear in many locations throughout <i><a href="../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online.md" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>. While most appear hostile, one Neekerbreeker named <a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Sniken" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Sniken</a> was taken as a pet by a <a href="../B/Bree-landers.md" title="Bree-landers">Bree-lander</a> named <a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Roderick_Neeker-friend" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Roderik Neeker-friend</a>. Roderick <a class="external text" href="https://lotro-wiki.com/wiki/Quest:Neeker-friend" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">tasks</a> the player character with hunting sickle-fly wings to provide food for Sniken. When Roderick tries feeding the wings to Snicken, his hands are nearly purposefully bitten off.</dd></dl>
