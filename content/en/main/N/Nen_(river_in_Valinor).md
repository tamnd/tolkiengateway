**Nen** was the name of a great river of [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about it, nor is there an equivalent river in the later legendarium.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nen* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya). It means "river, water".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the *[Qenya Lexicon](/wiki/Qenya_Lexicon)* appears the [root](/wiki/Root "Root") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NENE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NENE (page does not exist)">NENE</a> ("flow?"), from which the word *nen* is derived from.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
