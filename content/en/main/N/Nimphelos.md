**Nimphelos** was a huge pearl said to have been the size of a dove's egg (that is, an inch or more in diameter) that was found along the shores of the [Isle of Balar](../I/Isle_of_Balar.md) by the [Falathrim](../F/Falathrim.md). [Círdan](../C/Círdan.md) gave it, along with many lesser pearls, to his friend and ally [Thingol](../T/Thingol.md). When Thingol called on the [Dwarves of Belegost](../D/Dwarves_of_Belegost.md) to aid in the building of [Menegroth](../M/Menegroth.md), Nimphelos was among the rewards he gave them, and the [Lord of Belegost](../L/Lord_of_Belegost.md) was said to have prized the pearl over a mountain of wealth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) and contains the element *[nim](Nim.md)-* ("white").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In a linguistic writing, *nimphelos* is said to be a plant name (mentioned along with *[niphredil](Niphredil.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
