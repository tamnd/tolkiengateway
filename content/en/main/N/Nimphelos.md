**Nimphelos** was a huge pearl said to have been the size of a dove's egg (that is, an inch or more in diameter) that was found along the shores of the [Isle of Balar](/wiki/Isle_of_Balar) by the [Falathrim](/wiki/Falathrim). [Círdan](/wiki/C%C3%ADrdan) gave it, along with many lesser pearls, to his friend and ally [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol). When Thingol called on the [Dwarves of Belegost](/wiki/Dwarves_of_Belegost) to aid in the building of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), Nimphelos was among the rewards he gave them, and the [Lord of Belegost](/wiki/Lord_of_Belegost) was said to have prized the pearl over a mountain of wealth.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) and contains the element *[nim](/wiki/Nim "Nim")-* ("white").<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

In a linguistic writing, *nimphelos* is said to be a plant name (mentioned along with *[niphredil](/wiki/Niphredil "Niphredil")*).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
