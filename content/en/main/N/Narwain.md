**Narwain** ([Narvinyë](/wiki/Narviny%C3%AB) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya)) was the first month of the year in the [Kings'](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning "Kings' Reckoning") and [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning), and the tenth month in the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning). *Narwain* in the Kings' and Stewards' Reckoning corresponded to [Afteryule](/wiki/Afteryule) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [23 December](/wiki/23_December) through [21 January](/wiki/21_January)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Narwain* translates to 'new sun' (*[naur](/wiki/Naur "Naur")* + <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Gwain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Gwain (page does not exist)">gwain</a></i>).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
