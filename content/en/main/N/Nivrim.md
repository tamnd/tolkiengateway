**Nivrim** or the **West March** was a narrow woodland composed of mostly [oaks](/wiki/Oak "Oak"), that lay north of the [Aelin-uial](/wiki/Aelin-uial) and extending to [Taeglin](/wiki/Taeglin). It was the only part of the kingdom of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath) west of the River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion) and was encompassed within the [Girdle of Melian](/wiki/Girdle_of_Melian). [Melian](/wiki/Melian) included this because she loved it in reverence of [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> A guarded bridge crossed Sirion just south of the inflow of [Esgalduin](/wiki/Esgalduin), connecting the land with the rest of Doriath. [Mablung](/wiki/Mablung) and three companions were leading [Nienor](/wiki/Nienor) to this bridge but were assailed by [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) when they rested, and Nienor ran away.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name means "west-march" from [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin) <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nivon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nivon (page does not exist)">nivon</a></i> and *[rim](/wiki/Rim "Rim")*<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> <span></span>
