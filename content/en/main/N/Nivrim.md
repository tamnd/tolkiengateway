**Nivrim** or the **West March** was a narrow woodland composed of mostly [oaks](../O/Oak.md), that lay north of the [Aelin-uial](../A/Aelin-uial.md) and extending to [Taeglin](../T/Taeglin.md). It was the only part of the kingdom of [Doriath](../D/Doriath.md) west of the River [Sirion](../S/Sirion.md) and was encompassed within the [Girdle of Melian](../G/Girdle_of_Melian.md). [Melian](../M/Melian.md) included this because she loved it in reverence of [Ulmo](../U/Ulmo.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> A guarded bridge crossed Sirion just south of the inflow of [Esgalduin](../E/Esgalduin.md), connecting the land with the rest of Doriath. [Mablung](../M/Mablung.md) and three companions were leading [Nienor](Nienor.md) to this bridge but were assailed by [Orcs](../O/Orcs.md) when they rested, and Nienor ran away.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
