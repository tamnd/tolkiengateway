## History {#History}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/e/ef/Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg/190px-Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="190" height="177" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/thumb/e/ef/Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg/285px-Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg 1.5x, /w/images/thumb/e/ef/Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg/380px-Irsanna_-_Narvi_and_Celebrimbor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="745"/></a><figcaption><i>Narvi and Celebrimbor</i> by <a href="/wiki/Category:Images_by_Irsanna" title="Category:Images by Irsanna">Irsanna</a></figcaption></figure>

Narvi lived in [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") during the height of its glory. He formed a friendship with the [Elf](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor")-lord [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor), the master artificer of neighboring [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion). Together they made the mighty [Doors of Durin](/wiki/Doors_of_Durin) that guarded the West-gate of the Dwarven city.<sup id="cite_ref-UT_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-UT-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

He was a very famous Dwarven craftsman.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") used the spelling *Narf\[i\]*.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narfi" title="wikipedia:Narfi">Narfi</a></i> (also spelled *Narvi*), is an [Old Norse](/wiki/Old_Norse) name and character appearing in the *Prose Edda* and the meaning of the name is unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
