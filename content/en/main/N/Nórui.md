**Nórui** is the sixth month of the calendar of the [Dúnedain](/wiki/D%C3%BAnedain). It was also called [Nárië](/wiki/N%C3%A1ri%C3%AB) in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The first element of the name, *Nór-*, is derived from Sindarin *naur* "fire".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The second element *-ui* is a common adjectival ending,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> also present in other Sindarin names of the months.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
