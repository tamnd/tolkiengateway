**Norin** is a [Longbeard](/wiki/Longbeards "Longbeards") [Dwarf](/wiki/Dwarves "Dwarves") and the father of [Farin](</wiki/Farin_(video_game_character)> "Farin (video game character)"). When Farin was born he lived in the [Iron Hills](/wiki/Iron_Hills). It is unknown if he was originally born in [Erebor](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain "Lonely Mountain"), or if he returned after the [Battle of the Five Armies](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Five_Armies). Although he doesn't make an actual appearance in [The Lord of the Rings: War in the North](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_War_in_the_North), he is mentioned in the <a class="external text" href="http://www.warinthenorth.com/the-game/heroes/backstory#dwarfAnchor" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">backstory of Farin</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Backstory_Farin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Backstory_Farin-1">[1]</a></sup>
