**Newbury** was a town in the north-eastern [Buckland](/wiki/Buckland), to the north of [Crickhollow](/wiki/Crickhollow) and next to the [High Hay](/wiki/High_Hay).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Bury*, from the [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/burh#Old_English" title="wiktionary:burh">burh</a></i>, means "fortified enclosure".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> This, combined with Newbury's proximity to the High Hay, perhaps suggests that Newbury was built as a more recent guard against intruders from the [Old Forest](/wiki/Old_Forest).

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

There is a town named <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury" title="wikipedia:Newbury">Newbury</a> in Berkshire, England.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

<b>2007: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Newbury is a small hamlet just past the <a href="/wiki/Buckland_Gate" title="Buckland Gate">Buckland Gate</a>. At which Newbury ends and <a href="/wiki/Bucklebury" title="Bucklebury">Bucklebury</a> begins can be hard to determine.</dd></dl>
