**NGWAW** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "howl".<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[nauro](/wiki/Nauro "Nauro")* ("[werewolves](/wiki/Werewolves "Werewolves")");<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup> *naule* (wolf-howl")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[gaur](/wiki/Gaur "Gaur")* ("werewolf");<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-1">[1]</a></sup> *gaul* ("wolf-howl"); *gaw-* ("howl"); *gawad* ("howling")<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the root ÑGAW, signifying "howl".<sup id="cite_ref-LR_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-2">[2]</a></sup>
