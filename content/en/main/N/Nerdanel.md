**Nerdanel** was the daughter of the [Noldorin](Noldor.md) smith [Mahtan](../M/Mahtan.md) and the wife of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md).

## History {#History}

### Early life {#Early_life}

<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/S/Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/20/Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg/200px-Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="284" class="mw-file-element" srcset="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/20/Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg/300px-Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg 1.5x, https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/thumb/2/20/Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg/400px-Svirina_Vera_-_Nerdanel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="1452"/></a><figcaption><i>Nerdanel</i> by <a href="../../category/I/Images_by_Svirina_Vera.md" title="Category:Images by Svirina Vera">Svirina Vera</a></figcaption></figure>

Nerdanel was a skilful sculptor. She made statues so lifelike that people thought they were real at first, and if they did not know her art, tried to speak to them.<sup id="cite_ref-nerdanel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nerdanel-1">[1]</a></sup> She also created sculptures from her own imaginings, which were "strong and strange but beautiful."<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup> In her youth, Nerdanel loved to wander along the shore or in the hills of [Aman](../A/Aman.md). Whilst wandering, she met Fëanor, and they "were companions in many journeys."<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup>

When Nerdanel married Fëanor, others wondered at his choice for she was not considered among "the fairest of her people."<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:272</sup> For a time, since Fëanor would seek the counsel of none save her, she was able to influence and restrain her prideful husband.<sup id="cite_ref-nerdanel_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nerdanel-1">[1]</a></sup>

### Marriage and motherhood {#Marriage_and_motherhood}

Nerdanel bore Fëanor [seven sons](../S/Sons_of_Fëanor.md): [Maedhros](../M/Maedhros.md), [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md), [Celegorm](../C/Celegorm.md), [Caranthir](../C/Caranthir.md), [Curufin](../C/Curufin.md), [Amrod](../A/Amrod.md), and [Amras](../A/Amras.md). Elves typically have four children or fewer, so the size of their family was particularly noteworthy.<sup id="cite_ref-NotesB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NotesB-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:Note 4</sup> Some inherited her colors, others inherited her character and patience.

When Fëanor was banished from [Túna](../T/Túna.md) for drawing a sword against [his brother](../F/Fingolfin.md), Nerdanel asked for leave to abide with [Indis](../I/Indis.md) during that time.<sup id="cite_ref-Unrest_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Unrest-4">[4]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:279</sup>

However, Fëanor's later deeds deeply grieved her and in the end they became estranged from one another.<sup id="cite_ref-nerdanel_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nerdanel-1">[1]</a></sup>

When Nerdanel, who had since returned to her father's house, learned that her husband and sons were planning to go into exile to [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md), she begged Fëanor to leave with her their youngest sons, Amrod and Amras, or at least one of them. Fëanor's response was that, if she would not follow him, she was an untrue wife for deserting both her husband and her children.<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:354</sup>

Regardless, Nerdanel refused to follow because she heeded the warning of Aulë that: "'It \[the rebellion\] will in the end only lead Fëanor and all your children to death.'"<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup> As predicted she lost all her children in Middle-earth.<sup id="cite_ref-nerdanel_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nerdanel-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

Known as **the wise**, she was strong, free of mind, and filled with the desire for knowledge.<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup> Though she was strong-willed, she was more patient than her husband. She would seek to understand others instead of master them. Her calmer temperament had passed to some of her sons while others were fiery more like their father.<sup id="cite_ref-nerdanel_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nerdanel-1">[1]</a></sup>

Nerdanel is noted as having brown hair and a ruddy complexion,<sup id="cite_ref-VT41_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT41-6">[6]</a></sup> and not considered among "the fairest of her people."<sup id="cite_ref-Feanor_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Feanor-2">[2]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:272</sup> Her father was noted for having hair that was not as dark or black as was that of most of the Noldor, but brown, and had glints of coppery-red in it; three of Nerdanel's children—Maedhros, Amrod, and Amras—had hair of this kind.<sup id="cite_ref-Sons_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sons-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nerdanel* is [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), but it is given no clear meaning or etymology in the published writings of Tolkien.

Editor and linguist [Patrick H. Wynne](../P/Patrick_H._Wynne.md) has suggested that the element *nerd-* in *Nerdanel* perhaps derives from *nerdo* ("large, strong man"), noting that the name "*might refer to her strength of body and mind, and her pursuits of crafts more commonly practiced by men.*" Wynne also suggests that *Istarnië* derives from Quenya *[ista-](../I/Ista-.md)* ("know"), apparently "*referring to her 'desire for knowledge'*".<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
