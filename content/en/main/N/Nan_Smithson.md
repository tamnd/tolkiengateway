**Nan Smithson** was the daughter of [Smith Smithson](/wiki/Smith_Smithson), the title character of the story *[Smith of Wootton Major](/wiki/Smith_of_Wootton_Major)*, and [Nell Smithson](/wiki/Nell_Smithson). When she grew up and married <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Tom_Wright&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Tom Wright (page does not exist)">Tom Wright</a>, she moved to [Wootton Minor](/wiki/Wootton_Minor) and had a son named [Tomling](/wiki/Tomling).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
