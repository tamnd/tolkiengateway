<i><b>nen</b></i> is a [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) noun meaning "water" (being used for a small body of water like a lake, pool, lesser river, or tributary).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)*, [Noldorin](Noldorin.md) *nen* derives from the [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [NEN](NEN~31118.md).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>
