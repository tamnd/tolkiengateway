<i><b>nen</b></i> is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) noun meaning "water" (being used for a small body of water like a lake, pool, lesser river, or tributary).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) *nen* derives from the [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") [NEN](/wiki/NEN).<sup id="cite_ref-LR_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LR-4">[4]</a></sup>

- <i><a href="/wiki/Bruinen" title="Bruinen">Brui<b>nen</b></a> =</i> "Loudwater"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Mirrormere" title="Mirrormere"><b>Nen</b> Cenedril</a></i> = "Mirrormere"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Nen_Hithoel" title="Nen Hithoel"><b>Nen</b> Hithoel</a></i> = "Lake of Mist"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Nen_Lalaith" title="Nen Lalaith"><b>Nen</b> Lalaith</a></i> = "River of Laughter"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Nenuial" title="Nenuial"><b>Nen</b>uial</a></i> = "Lake Evendim"
- <i><a href="/wiki/Sea_of_N%C3%BArnen#Etymology" title="Sea of Núrnen">Núr<b>nen</b></a></i> = "Sad Water"
- <i><a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Ninui" title="Ninui"><b>Nin</b>ui</a></i> = name of the "Wet" month
- <i><a href="/wiki/Swanfleet" title="Swanfleet"><b>Nîn</b>-in-Eilph</a></i> = "Swanfleet"
