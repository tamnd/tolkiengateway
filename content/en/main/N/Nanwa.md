<i><b>nanwa</b></i> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) meaning "existing, actual (true)".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The word is likely derived from the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [roots](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a href="/wiki/ANA" title="ANA">ANA<sup>2</sup></a> and <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%C4%80&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NĀ (page does not exist)">NĀ<sup>2</sup></a>. Compare the near-synonym *[anwa](/wiki/Anwa "Anwa")*.
