**Nightfall** is the fourth track for [Blind Guardian](/wiki/Blind_Guardian)'s Tolkien themed album, *[Nightfall in Middle-Earth](/wiki/Nightfall_in_Middle-Earth)*. It is the lament of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) and his sons for the death of [Finwë](/wiki/Finw%C3%AB) and the theft of the [Silmarils](/wiki/Silmarils), and their [oath](/wiki/Oath_of_F%C3%ABanor "Oath of Fëanor") and rebellion against the [Valar](/wiki/Valar).
