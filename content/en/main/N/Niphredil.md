**Niphredil** was a small white flower that grew first at the moment of [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien)'s birth in [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> It was also found among the golden trees of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien), thickest on [Cerin Amroth](/wiki/Cerin_Amroth), where some pale green varieties could be seen.<sup id="cite_ref-Lothlórien_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lothlórien-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

It was like "a delicate kin of a snowdrop."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Niphredil* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name meaning "little pallor", consisting of *[niphred](/wiki/Niphred "Niphred")* ("pallor") + the ending *[-il](/wiki/-il)*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
