**Nermir** were the [fays](/wiki/Fays "Fays") of the meads (meadows),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[note 1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Ndr_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ndr-2">[1]</a></sup> according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[3]</a></sup>

They arrived to the world with [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB) and [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna), along with many other types of fays.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[4]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nermir* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), likely being the plural form of *nermi* ("a field-spirit");<sup id="cite_ref-Ner_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ner-6">[5]</a></sup> the [Gnomish](/wiki/Gnomish) cognate of *nermi* was <i><b>nermil</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ndr_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ndr-2">[1]</a></sup>

The word *nermi* is derived from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NESE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NESE (page does not exist)">NESE</a> ("give to feed; feed, pasture; graze").<sup id="cite_ref-Ner_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ner-6">[5]</a></sup>
