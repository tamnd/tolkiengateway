**Nárië** ([Nórui](Nórui.md) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was the sixth month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](../K/King's_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md) of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md), following [Lótessë](../L/Lótessë.md), and preceding [Loëndë](../L/Loëndë.md) ([Mid-year's Day](../M/Mid-year's_Day.md)). In the [New Reckoning](New_Reckoning.md) it was the third month of the year, immediately preceding [Cermië](../C/Cermië.md).

*Nárië* in the Kings' Reckoning and in the Stewards' Reckoning corresponded, for the most part, to [Forelithe](../F/Forelithe.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [22 May](../_/22_May.md) through [20 June](../_/20_June.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
