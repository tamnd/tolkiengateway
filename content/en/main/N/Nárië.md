**Nárië** ([Nórui](/wiki/N%C3%B3rui) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was the sixth month of the year in the [King's Reckoning](/wiki/King%27s_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning) of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor), following [Lótessë](/wiki/L%C3%B3tess%C3%AB), and preceding [Loëndë](/wiki/Lo%C3%ABnd%C3%AB) ([Mid-year's Day](/wiki/Mid-year%27s_Day)). In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) it was the third month of the year, immediately preceding [Cermië](/wiki/Cermi%C3%AB).

*Nárië* in the Kings' Reckoning and in the Stewards' Reckoning corresponded, for the most part, to [Forelithe](/wiki/Forelithe) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [22 May](/wiki/22_May) through [20 June](/wiki/20_June)).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
