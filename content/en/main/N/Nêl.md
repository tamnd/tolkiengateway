<i><b>nêl</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> or <i><b>neleð</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> is the number three (3, III) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin).

In [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), the precursor of Sindarin, three is <i><b>neledh</b></i>, which evolved to <i><b>neled</b></i> in later Noldorin. The word derives from [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=NEL&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NEL (page does not exist)">NEL</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-AI_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AI-4">[4]</a></sup>
