**Nulukkizdîn** or **Nulukkhizdīn** was a hall of the [Petty-dwarves](../P/Petty-dwarves.md) on the river [Narog](Narog.md).

## History {#History}

Nulukkizdîn was delved by Dwarves before the [Elves](../E/Elves.md) were exiled from [Valinor](../V/Valinor.md). Over the years, its population waned, and others were hunted to near-extinction by the Elves, who were unfamiliar with the Petty-dwarves. Nulukkizdîn was eventually abandoned, and repopulated by Elves. [Finrod Felagund](../F/Finrod.md) founded [Nargothrond](Nargothrond.md) in its stead. After Nargothrond fell to [Glaurung](../G/Glaurung.md), the last Petty-dwarf, [Mîm](../M/Mîm.md), reclaimed it, but he was slain by [Húrin](../H/Húrin.md).<sup id="cite_ref-S22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S22-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tolkien provided no translations of either **Nulukkizdîn** or **Nulukkhizdīn**. The last element is usually believed to be the [Khuzdul](../K/Khuzdul.md) root for "Dwarf", Kh-Z-D.<sup id="cite_ref-Ardalambion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ardalambion-2">[2]</a></sup> Whether this is a true Khuzdul word or a Petty-dwarvish dialect is unknown, but circumstantial evidence points to the latter option. The [Angerthas Daeron](../A/Angerthas_Daeron.md) and its specific Dwarven variation [Angerthas Moria](../A/Angerthas_Moria.md), do not have a symbol for the letter *Î*.<sup id="cite_ref-Angerthas_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Angerthas-3">[3]</a></sup> Other than in *Nulukkizdîn*, it only appears in *[Khîm](../K/Khîm.md)* and *Mîm*,<sup id="cite_ref-Ardalambion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ardalambion-2">[2]</a></sup> the names of two Petty-dwarves.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md)*, the name is spelled *Nulukkizdîn*; however, [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) has noted that in the original manuscript, his father used the spelling **Nulukkhizdīn**. It replaced an earlier spelling **Nulukkhizidûn**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Tolkien's later notes on the origin of Nargothrond give a different Dwarvish name, **Nar(u)kuthûn**, derived from a Dwarvish name of the [Narog](Narog.md), \**[Naruka](Naruka.md)*. Tolkien includes the words "of Petty-dwarves" between parentheses behind the mention of its Dwarvish origin.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[5]</a></sup> Whether Tolkien ever intended to replace *Nulukkhizdīn* with *Nar(u)kuthûn* is unknown.
