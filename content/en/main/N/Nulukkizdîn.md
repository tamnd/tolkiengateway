**Nulukkizdîn** or **Nulukkhizdīn** was a hall of the [Petty-dwarves](/wiki/Petty-dwarves) on the river [Narog](/wiki/Narog).

## History {#History}

Nulukkizdîn was delved by Dwarves before the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) were exiled from [Valinor](/wiki/Valinor). Over the years, its population waned, and others were hunted to near-extinction by the Elves, who were unfamiliar with the Petty-dwarves. Nulukkizdîn was eventually abandoned, and repopulated by Elves. [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod") founded [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond) in its stead. After Nargothrond fell to [Glaurung](/wiki/Glaurung), the last Petty-dwarf, [Mîm](/wiki/M%C3%AEm), reclaimed it, but he was slain by [Húrin](/wiki/H%C3%BArin).<sup id="cite_ref-S22_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S22-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Tolkien provided no translations of either **Nulukkizdîn** or **Nulukkhizdīn**. The last element is usually believed to be the [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) root for "Dwarf", Kh-Z-D.<sup id="cite_ref-Ardalambion_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ardalambion-2">[2]</a></sup> Whether this is a true Khuzdul word or a Petty-dwarvish dialect is unknown, but circumstantial evidence points to the latter option. The [Angerthas Daeron](/wiki/Angerthas_Daeron) and its specific Dwarven variation [Angerthas Moria](/wiki/Angerthas_Moria), do not have a symbol for the letter *Î*.<sup id="cite_ref-Angerthas_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Angerthas-3">[3]</a></sup> Other than in *Nulukkizdîn*, it only appears in *[Khîm](/wiki/Kh%C3%AEm)* and *Mîm*,<sup id="cite_ref-Ardalambion_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ardalambion-2">[2]</a></sup> the names of two Petty-dwarves.

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, the name is spelled *Nulukkizdîn*; however, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) has noted that in the original manuscript, his father used the spelling **Nulukkhizdīn**. It replaced an earlier spelling **Nulukkhizidûn**.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Tolkien's later notes on the origin of Nargothrond give a different Dwarvish name, **Nar(u)kuthûn**, derived from a Dwarvish name of the [Narog](/wiki/Narog), \**[Naruka](/wiki/Naruka)*. Tolkien includes the words "of Petty-dwarves" between parentheses behind the mention of its Dwarvish origin.<sup id="cite_ref-PE17_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE17-5">[5]</a></sup> Whether Tolkien ever intended to replace *Nulukkhizdīn* with *Nar(u)kuthûn* is unknown.
