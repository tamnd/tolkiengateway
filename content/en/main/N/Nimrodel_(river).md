**Nimrodel** was the small river that rose in the eastern foothills of the [Misty Mountains](/wiki/Misty_Mountains), and flowed east to meet the [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant) on the western borders of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien). It was named after the Elf-maid [Nimrodel](/wiki/Nimrodel), who lived near the river. When the [Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Ring) came to the river they could hear it splashing over a waterfall on their right. There used to be a [bridge](/wiki/Bridge_of_Nimrodel "Bridge of Nimrodel"), but it had long ago disappeared. It was said that the water of the stream was "*healing to the weary*". After passing through [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), the Fellowship crossed and stopped by the Nimrodel to rest.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
