**Nan Elmoth** was a forest in [East Beleriand](/wiki/East_Beleriand) east of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), south of the River [Celon](/wiki/Celon), and north of [Estolad](/wiki/Estolad).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> One of the two roads that the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) took from [Blue Mountains](/wiki/Blue_Mountains) towards Beleriand passed near Nan Elmoth. There the trees grew to be the tallest and darkest in Beleriand, blocking the sun entirely from the forest floor.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

[Melian](/wiki/Melian) met [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) in Nan Elmoth, and stayed motionless for years while the trees grew around them. When they woke up, they founded the realm of Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Nan Elmoth was held to be part of Doriath, and [Eöl](/wiki/E%C3%B6l) the [Dark Elf](/wiki/Dark_Elf) paid Thingol with the black sword [Anglachel](/wiki/Anglachel) as a fee for living there.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> Eöl dwelt with his wife [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel), their son [Maeglin](/wiki/Maeglin), and a household of servants.<sup id="cite_ref-Maeglin_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Maeglin-2">[2]</a></sup> Eöl's house consisted of a smithy and dim halls and was located in the middle of Nan Elmoth around fifteen miles from the northernmost edge where the [Celon](/wiki/Celon) River ran.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

[Robert Foster](/wiki/Robert_Foster) translates the whole [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name as "Valley of star-dusk": <i><a class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" href="/wiki/Nan" title="Nan">nan</a></i> ("valley") + *[el](/wiki/Elen "Elen")* + *moth* ("stardusk").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In [The Second 'Silmarillion' Map](/wiki/The_Second_%27Silmarillion%27_Map), Nan Elmoth is also named as *Gladuial*, meaning "Twilight Wood", from *[glad](/wiki/Glad "Glad")* ("wood") + *[uial](/wiki/Uial "Uial")* ("twilight"). However, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) did not notice that this name was rejected in *[The Lay of Leithian Recommenced](/wiki/The_Lay_of_Leithian_Recommenced)*.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
