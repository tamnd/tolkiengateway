**Norbourn** was a river in the [Northfarthing](/wiki/Northfarthing) of [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire"). Between it and [Brandywine](/wiki/Brandywine) River stretched [Greenfields](/wiki/Greenfields), where the [Battle of Greenfields](/wiki/Battle_of_Greenfields) was fought.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

While the first element of the name means "north", the second element is <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bourn#Etymology_1" title="wiktionary:bourn">bourn</a></i> "brook, stream", also seen names of other water bodies like [Shirebourn](/wiki/Shirebourn), [Upbourn](/wiki/Upbourn) etc.

## Identification {#Identification}

Norbourn is only mentioned in the [detailed index](/wiki/Unfinished_index "Unfinished index") [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) was preparing for *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*, but which was eventually left unfinished. In the map [A Part of the Shire](/wiki/A_Part_of_the_Shire) an unnamed river flows from the north to the [Bywater Pool](/wiki/Bywater_Pool). It might be Norbourn, though it is also possible that Norbourn flowed straight to Brandywine more in the north.
