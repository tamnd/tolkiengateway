**Norbourn** was a river in the [Northfarthing](Northfarthing.md) of [the Shire](../T/The_Shire.md). Between it and [Brandywine](../B/Brandywine.md) River stretched [Greenfields](../G/Greenfields.md), where the [Battle of Greenfields](../B/Battle_of_Greenfields.md) was fought.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

While the first element of the name means "north", the second element is <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bourn#Etymology_1" title="wiktionary:bourn">bourn</a></i> "brook, stream", also seen names of other water bodies like [Shirebourn](../S/Shirebourn.md), [Upbourn](../U/Upbourn.md) etc.

## Identification {#Identification}

Norbourn is only mentioned in the [detailed index](../U/Unfinished_index.md) [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) was preparing for *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*, but which was eventually left unfinished. In the map [A Part of the Shire](../A/A_Part_of_the_Shire.md) an unnamed river flows from the north to the [Bywater Pool](../B/Bywater_Pool.md). It might be Norbourn, though it is also possible that Norbourn flowed straight to Brandywine more in the north.
