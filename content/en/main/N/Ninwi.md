**Ninwi** was a city of [Men](/wiki/Men) in the [Great Lands](/wiki/Great_Lands), according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*. It was identified with the city of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineveh" title="wikipedia:Nineveh">Nineveh</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Nin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nin-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was mentioned in the tale of *[the Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")* by [Littleheart](/wiki/Littleheart), where he compared its [fall](/wiki/Fall_of_Gondolin "Fall of Gondolin") as even being worse than Ninwi's.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Ninwi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), according to [Paul Strack](/wiki/Paul_Strack), but its meaning is unclear. However, it was probably a phonological imitation of *Nineveh*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

An earlier form of the name was *Ninwë*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nin-1">[1]</a></sup>
