**Noel Appleby** was a New Zealand actor. He played [Everard Proudfoot](/wiki/Everard_Proudfoot) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)* and *[The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Return_of_the_King)*.

In [2007](/wiki/2007), he and 14 other actors filed <a class="external text" href="https://us.imdb.com/news/ni0058227/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">a lawsuit</a> against [New Line Cinema](/wiki/New_Line_Cinema) over merchandising royalties,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> but he died before the court made its decision.
