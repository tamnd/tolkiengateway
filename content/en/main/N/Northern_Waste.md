The **Northern Waste** was a vast cold region, in the far north end of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md),<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> beyond the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md), [Mount Gundabad](../G/Gundabad.md) and the [Ered Mithrin](../G/Grey_Mountains.md).

The region was also named **Forodwaith** ([S.](../S/Sindarin.md), "Northern lands, people"), probably after the hardy [Forodwaith](<../F/Forodwaith_(people).md>), who once inhabited it.

## History {#History}

The Northern Waste had always been bitterly cold due to its proximity to [Morgoth](../M/Morgoth.md)'s realm and continued to remain cold into the later ages.<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/A/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/3/35/Angelo_Montanini_-_Northern_Waste.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="253" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="300" data-file-height="253"/></a><figcaption>Angelo Montanini - <i>Northern Waste</i></figcaption></figure>

The region was once inhabited by the [Forodwaith](<../F/Forodwaith_(people).md>), a hardy [Mannish](../M/Men.md) folk accustomed to the cold climate of the Northern Waste, whose name was also applied to the region. In later years the [Lossoth](../L/Lossoth.md), their [remnants](../L/Lossoth.md), lived mostly on the [Cape of Forochel](../C/Cape_of_Forochel.md), but also camped on the southern shores of the Icebay of Forochel near the northern end of the [Blue Mountains](../B/Blue_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Eriador_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Eriador-2">[2]</a></sup>

In [T.A.](../T/Third_Age.md) [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> one of the strongest storms in the history of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) came from the Northern Waste through [Eriador](../E/Eriador.md) into Gondor, tore the ship of [Amroth](../A/Amroth.md) from its moorings near the elven harbour of [Edhellond](../E/Edhellond.md) and blew it out into the [Bay of Belfalas](../B/Bay_of_Belfalas.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

According to [Frodo Baggins](../F/Frodo_Baggins.md)'s [poem for Gandalf](../W/When_evening_in_the_Shire_was_grey.md), [Gandalf](../G/Gandalf.md) had travelled "from northern waste to southern hill".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> It is not known if Frodo meant the Northern Waste that lies north of the Mountains of Angmar and north of the Grey Mountains or some other waste in the north.

[Dragons](../D/Dragons.md) dwelt in the wastes beyond the Grey Mountains.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-6">[6]</a></sup> It is not known if these wastes refer to the Northern Waste in the north of the Grey Mountains or to the [Withered Heath](../W/Withered_Heath.md) in the north-east of the southern and longer eastern branch of the Grey Mountains, which was known for its dragons.<sup id="cite_ref-Party_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-7">[7]</a></sup> After many years, the dragons multiplied and became strong and made [war](../W/War_of_the_Dwarves_and_Dragons.md) against the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Party_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Party-7">[7]</a></sup> Among these dragons were [Cold-drakes](../C/Cold-drakes.md) that drove the [Dwarves of the Grey Mountains](../D/Dwarves_of_the_Grey_Mountains.md) out of their homes.<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-6">[6]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

On the [First Map of The Lord of the Rings](../F/First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), a label "Forodwaith (Northerland)" is written in the area corresponding to the general area of [Arnor](../A/Arnor.md). The comment "Forodwaith (or Eriador)" was added later on [The 1943 Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_1943_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md).<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> This and the absence of the name Eriador from earlier manuscripts suggest that Forodwaith was initially used as a name of the region that would be later called Eriador in the published book.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="../../file/W/Waste_vs_forodwaith.png.md"><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/f/f1/Waste_vs_forodwaith.png" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="278" height="243" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="278" data-file-height="243"/></a><figcaption>The different definitions of &#34;Forodwaith&#34; between the earlier and the later map.</figcaption></figure>

In the [General Map of Middle-earth](../G/General_Map_of_Middle-earth.md) that was drawn by [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) and published with earlier editions of *The Lord of the Rings*, the northern portion of the [Westlands](../W/Westlands.md) is featureless, labelled as "NORTHERN WASTE", with the name "FORODWAITH", in slightly smaller letters, above the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md). These labels could suggest that Forodwaith was a part of the wider Northern Waste, if not a smaller separate region. This nomenclature was carried over to [Pauline Baynes](../P/Pauline_Baynes.md)'s *[A Map of Middle-earth](../A/A_Map_of_Middle-earth.md)*.

Later, Christopher Tolkien realized that the two names certainly seem to have been intended as synonymous, because Forodwaith seems to be explicitly equated with the Northern Waste in one of J.R.R. Tolkien's sketch-maps.<sup id="cite_ref-intro_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-intro-9">[9]</a></sup> The misunderstanding was corrected in his later map known as [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third Age](../T/The_West_of_Middle-earth_at_the_End_of_the_Third_Age.md), where only the name "FORODWAITH" appears, labeling all the blank portion north of the [Mountains of Angmar](../M/Mountains_of_Angmar.md) and north of the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Map_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Map-1">[1]</a></sup> On [The Third Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Third_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) the label "NORTHERN WASTE" north of the Mountains of Angmar and the Grey Mountains has a label "(FORODWAITH)" just below the first label.
