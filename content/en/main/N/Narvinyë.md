**Narvinyë** ([Narwain](Narwain.md) in [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md)) was the first month of the year in the [Kings' Reckoning](../K/Kings'_Reckoning.md) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](../S/Stewards'_Reckoning.md). In both calendars, *Narvinyë* followed the first day of the year, [yestarë](../Y/Yestarë.md), preceded the month of [Nénimë](Nénimë.md), and was 30 days long, lasting from the 2nd through the 31st days of the year. In the [New Reckoning](New_Reckoning.md) it was the tenth month of the year, immediately following [Ringarë](../R/Ringarë.md).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Narvinyë* in the Kings' and Stewards' Reckoning corresponded to [Afteryule](../A/Afteryule.md) in the [Shire Calendar](../S/Shire_Calendar.md) (approximately modern [23 December](../_/23_December.md) through [21 January](../_/21_January.md)).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name means 'new sun' (*[Nar](Nar.md)* + *[vinya](../V/Vinya.md)*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
