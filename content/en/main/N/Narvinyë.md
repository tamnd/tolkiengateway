**Narvinyë** ([Narwain](/wiki/Narwain) in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin)) was the first month of the year in the [Kings' Reckoning](/wiki/Kings%27_Reckoning) and the [Stewards' Reckoning](/wiki/Stewards%27_Reckoning). In both calendars, *Narvinyë* followed the first day of the year, [yestarë](/wiki/Yestar%C3%AB "Yestarë"), preceded the month of [Nénimë](/wiki/N%C3%A9nim%C3%AB), and was 30 days long, lasting from the 2nd through the 31st days of the year. In the [New Reckoning](/wiki/New_Reckoning) it was the tenth month of the year, immediately following [Ringarë](/wiki/Ringar%C3%AB).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

*Narvinyë* in the Kings' and Stewards' Reckoning corresponded to [Afteryule](/wiki/Afteryule) in the [Shire Calendar](/wiki/Shire_Calendar) (approximately modern [23 December](/wiki/23_December) through [21 January](/wiki/21_January)).<sup id="cite_ref-D2_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-D2-1">[1]</a></sup>

The name means 'new sun' (*[Nar](/wiki/Nar)* + *[vinya](/wiki/Vinya "Vinya")*).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
