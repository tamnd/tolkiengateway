**NGUR** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "horror"<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup> or "to die".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *[ñguruk](/wiki/%C3%91guruk "Ñguruk")*<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[gorth](/wiki/Gorth "Gorth")*, *[gorthob](/wiki/Gorthob "Gorthob")*; *[gorgor](/wiki/Gorgor "Gorgor")*<sup id="cite_ref-AC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AC-1">[1]</a></sup>
