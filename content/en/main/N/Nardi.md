**Nardi** was the name of a "flower-[fairy](/wiki/Fays "Fays")" or a "flower-[Vala](/wiki/Valar#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium "Valar")", according to the early version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nar_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nar-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about her, and she doesn't appear in any of the narratives.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nardi* is in [Qenya](/wiki/Qenya), being derived from the root <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=N%E1%B9%9A%C5%98%E1%B9%9A&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NṚŘṚ (page does not exist)">NṚŘṚ</a> ("smell sweet").<sup id="cite_ref-Nar_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nar-1">[1]</a></sup>
