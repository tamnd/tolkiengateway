The **River Narog** was the chief river of West [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), the largest tributary of River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion).

The Narog was eighty leagues in length.<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup> It rose from the **[Pools of Ivrin](/wiki/Pools_of_Ivrin)** in the [Ered Wethrin](/wiki/Ered_Wethrin), flowed south and then southeast, flowing through a gorge in a series of rapids where it crossed the hills of the [Andram](/wiki/Andram) or Long Wall, finally meeting Sirion in the Land of Willows [Nan-tathren](/wiki/Nan-tathren), not far above the Mouths of Sirion. Narog's tributaries were the **River [Ginglith](/wiki/Ginglith)** in the north and the **River [Ringwil](/wiki/Ringwil)** in the **[Taur-en-Faroth](/wiki/Taur-en-Faroth)**.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

Into its western bank, just south of where Ringwil rushed into Narog, was carved the city of [Nargothrond](/wiki/Nargothrond), stronghold of [Finrod Felagund](/wiki/Finrod "Finrod").<sup id="cite_ref-Beleriand_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Beleriand-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)*, the river's name was stated to mean "rushing, violent".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> However, in a later writing by [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien"), the [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name *Narog* was given two possible [Khuzdul](/wiki/Khuzdul) origins: *[Narâg](/wiki/Nar%C3%A2g)* ("black") and *[naruka](/wiki/Naruka "Naruka")* (unknown meaning).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

In [Eriol](/wiki/Eriol)'s [Old English](/wiki/Old_English) translations, Narog is referred as *Hlyda* ("Loudy"). The [Elves of Nargothrond](/wiki/Elves_of_Nargothrond) were named *Hlydingas*.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
