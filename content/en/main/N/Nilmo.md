<i><b>nilmo</b></i> or <i><b>nildo</b></i> is a [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) masculine noun meaning *friend*. For the feminine see *nilde*. See also *nilda* "friendly", and *[nilme](Nilme.md)* "friendship".<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
