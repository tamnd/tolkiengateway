**Nan Dungortheb** was the dreadful valley in northern [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) that ran west to east between the haunted mountains of the [Ered Gorgoroth](/wiki/Ered_Gorgoroth) and the enchanted northern marches of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

After [Ungoliant](/wiki/Ungoliant) fled [Lammoth](/wiki/Lammoth) and the [Balrogs](/wiki/Balrogs) of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) she made her way to the valley below the Ered Gorgoroth. There she bred such horrors that the valley was given the name *Nan Dungortheb*. After she had departed the land was infested with [her offspring](/wiki/Spiders "Spiders").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> Waters that spilled into the valley from the Ered Gorgoroth were defiled, filling the hearts of those that drank of them with madness and despair. All living things other than the spiders avoided the valley, including the [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor) who would only cross it by paths nearest to Doriath.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

When [Aredhel](/wiki/Aredhel), daughter of [Fingolfin](/wiki/Fingolfin), attempted to ride eastward across Nan Dungortheb, her party was separated by the shadows. While she succeeded in reaching [Himlad](/wiki/Himlad), her companions could not find her and were chased away by spiders. Returning to [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), they reported her as lost to [Turgon](/wiki/Turgon).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

[Beren](/wiki/Beren), sorely pressed by the forces of [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth) who sought for him in [Dorthonion](/wiki/Dorthonion), passed over the Ered Gorgoroth and crossed Nan Dungortheb from north to south. He never spoke of his journey through this land lest the horror of it return to his mind.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nan Dungortheb* is translated as "Valley of Dreadful Death",<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> containing the word *nan* ("valley"), and *gor* ("horror")<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

### The Book of Lost Tales {#The_Book_of_Lost_Tales}

In *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)* the glade that would afterward be known as <i><b>Nan Dumgorthin</b></i> ("the land of the dark idols") was in the northward region of [Artanor](/wiki/Artanor) (Doriath) and was a dark land, with dread wandering beneath its lowering trees no less even than in [Tuarfuin](/wiki/Tuarfuin) (Taur-nu-Fuin).<sup id="cite_ref-Tinúviel_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tinúviel-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:48</sup> In *[The Tale of Tinúviel](/wiki/The_Tale_of_Tin%C3%BAviel)*, it was here that [Huan](/wiki/Huan) came upon, and rescued, [Beren](/wiki/Beren) and [Tinúviel](/wiki/Tin%C3%BAviel) as they were fleeing from the pursuit of [Melko's](/wiki/Melko "Melko") [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) after retrieving a [Silmaril](/wiki/Silmaril) and Beren had his hand bitten off by [Karkaras](/wiki/Karkaras).<sup id="cite_ref-Tinúviel_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tinúviel-8">[8]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:48</sup>

The [Gnomish Lexicon](/wiki/Gnomish_Lexicon) defined **Dumgorthin** as "a land of dark forests, East of *Artanor*, where, on a wooded mountain, were hidden idols sacrificed to by some evil tribe of renegade men". **Dum** meaning "secret, not to be spoken, especially of bad things" and **Dungort, dumgort** as "an (evil) idol".<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:31</sup>

### The Lays of Beleriand {#The_Lays_of_Beleriand}

In the *[Lay of the Children of Húrin](/wiki/Lay_of_the_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)*, [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) and [Flinding](/wiki/Gwindor "Gwindor") came upon <i><b>Nan Dungorthin</b></i> in the dim twilight after the accidental murder of [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg). It is described as such:<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>

<div class="poem">
<p>Thus reached they the roots   and the ruinous feet<br/>
of those hoary hills   that Hithlum girdle,<br/>
the shaggy pinewoods   of the Shadowy Mountains.<br/>
There the twain enfolded   phantom twilight<br/>
and dim mazes   dark, unholy,<br/>
in Nan Dungorthin   where nameless gods<br/>
have shrouded shrines   in shadows secret,<br/>
more old than Morgoth   or the ancient lords<br/>
the golden Gods   of the guarded West.<br/>
But the ghostly dwellers   of that grey valley<br/>
hindered nor hurt them,   and they held their course<br/>
with creeping flesh   and quaking limb.<br/>
Yet laughter at whiles   with lingering echo,<br/>
as distant mockery   of demon voices<br/>
there harsh and hollow   in the hushed twilight<br/>
Flinding fancied,   fell, unwholesome<br/>
as that leering laughter   lost and dreadful<br/>
that rang in the rocks   in the ruthless hour<br/>
of Beleg’s slaughter.   ‘Tis Bauglir’s voice<br/>
that dogs us darkly   with deadly scorn’
</p>
</div>

In the <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Unwritten_Cantos&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Unwritten Cantos (page does not exist)">Unwritten Cantos</a></i>, similar to the *Tale of Tinúviel*, a consideration was for Beren and [Lúthien](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien) to be "become lost and bewildered in the dreads of Nan Dungorthin", but now "hunted by phantoms, and snared at last by the great spiders." They would still be rescued by Huan, who would proceed to guide them down Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
