<i><b>noa</b></i> is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word. [Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien "J.R.R. Tolkien") reversed its meaning over the years: (1) one manuscript has "*ekuva nin kare sa noa*", translated as "I 'may' do that tomorrow", where *noa* is "tomorrow"; (2) another manuscript gives *noa* yesterday, from the expression *noa rē* ("former day").<sup id="cite_ref-VT49_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT49-1">[1]</a></sup>

[Helge Fauskanger](/wiki/Helge_Fauskanger) has suggested that the form *noa*, analyzed "basically an adjective" (with the meaning "former" or "yesterday") could be valid [Neo-Quenya](/wiki/Neo-Quenya).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
