**North Ithilien** was the northern part of [Ithilien](/wiki/Ithilien) between the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) and the [Ephel Dúath](/wiki/Ephel_D%C3%BAath) and was somewhat less extensive than its southern counterpart, [South Ithilien](/wiki/South_Ithilien) (lower Ithilien). The refuge of [Henneth Annûn](/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and the [Field of Cormallen](/wiki/Field_of_Cormallen)<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> were to be found in this part of Ithilien.

<dl><dd>North Ithilien is chronologically set after the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields" title="Battle of the Pelennor Fields">Battle of the Pelennor Fields</a>, with the <a href="/wiki/Army_of_the_West" title="Army of the West">Host of the West</a> marching towards the <a href="/wiki/Black_Gate" title="Black Gate">Black Gate</a>. The ranger strongholds <i>Bâr Aroth</i> and <i>Bâr Tarnaher</i> are similarly overrun, while <i>Aelin Veren</i> was only briefly passed by and has since been reclaimed from the Enemy. The secrecy of <a href="/wiki/Henneth_Ann%C3%BBn" title="Henneth Annûn">Henneth Annûn</a> has been abandoned, as it is used to support the Host of the West during its march and would serve no further purpose should its mission fail.</dd></dl>
