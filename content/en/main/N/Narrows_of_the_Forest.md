The **Narrows of the Forest** was the "waist" of [Mirkwood](../M/Mirkwood.md) caused by the indentation of the [East Bight](../E/East_Bight.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> On the western side of the forest there was a matching natural indentation in the borders of the woods, but the East Bight had been produced by the felling of trees by the [Northmen](Northmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Narrows_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narrows-2">[2]</a></sup> Together these two indentations narrowed Mirkwood to less than 100 miles at this location and thus afford a means to traverse the woods.

In the time of [Forthwini](../F/Forthwini.md) of the [Éothéod](../_/Éothéod.md) his realm was troubled by raids by [Wainriders](../W/Wainriders.md) who came through the Narrows as well as up the Vale of the [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md). These incursions prompted Forthwini to send a warning to King [Ondoher](../O/Ondoher.md) that the Wainriders had recovered their strength.<sup id="cite_ref-Narrows_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narrows-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md), the Narrows were populated by [Beornings](../B/Beornings.md) and [Woodmen](../W/Woodmen.md), separating the northern [Woodland Realm](../W/Woodland_Realm.md) from the new region of [East Lorien](../E/East_Lorien.md).
