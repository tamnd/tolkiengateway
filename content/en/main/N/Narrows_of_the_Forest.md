The **Narrows of the Forest** was the "waist" of [Mirkwood](/wiki/Mirkwood) caused by the indentation of the [East Bight](/wiki/East_Bight).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> On the western side of the forest there was a matching natural indentation in the borders of the woods, but the East Bight had been produced by the felling of trees by the [Northmen](/wiki/Northmen).<sup id="cite_ref-Narrows_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narrows-2">[2]</a></sup> Together these two indentations narrowed Mirkwood to less than 100 miles at this location and thus afford a means to traverse the woods.

In the time of [Forthwini](/wiki/Forthwini) of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od) his realm was troubled by raids by [Wainriders](/wiki/Wainriders) who came through the Narrows as well as up the Vale of the [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin). These incursions prompted Forthwini to send a warning to King [Ondoher](/wiki/Ondoher) that the Wainriders had recovered their strength.<sup id="cite_ref-Narrows_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Narrows-2">[2]</a></sup>

In the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age), the Narrows were populated by [Beornings](/wiki/Beornings) and [Woodmen](/wiki/Woodmen), separating the northern [Woodland Realm](/wiki/Woodland_Realm) from the new region of [East Lorien](/wiki/East_Lorien).
