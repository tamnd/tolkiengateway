**Nenuvar** were most likely the [fays](../F/Fays.md) of [lily](../L/Lilies.md)-ponds, according to the early version of the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md) associated with *[The Book of Lost Tales](../T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-Nen_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nen-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nenuvar* is in [Qenya](../Q/Qenya.md), with the word <i><a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=N%C3%A9nuvar&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nénuvar (page does not exist)">nénuvar</a></i> meaning "pool of lilies" in the *[Qenya Lexicon](../Q/Qenya_Lexicon.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-Nen_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Nen-1">[1]</a></sup> being derived from the root <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=NENE&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="NENE (page does not exist)">NENE</a> ("flow?").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
