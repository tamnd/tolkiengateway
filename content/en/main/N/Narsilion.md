**Narsilion** is the "Song of the Sun and Moon", telling of their creation: After the [Two Trees of Valinor](../T/Two_Trees_of_Valinor.md) were poisoned, [Manwë](../M/Manwë.md) commanded [Yavanna](../Y/Yavanna.md) and [Nienna](Nienna.md) to attempt to heal the Trees. Yavanna sang and Nienna wept, but to little avail until at last [Telperion](../T/Telperion.md) bore one silver flower and [Laurelin](../L/Laurelin.md) a single golden fruit. These Yavanna took and gave to [Aulë](../A/Aulë.md). Manwë hallowed them and Aulë and his people made vessels to hold them and preserve their radiance. [Varda](../V/Varda.md) then took the vessels and gave them the power to traverse the lower regions of [Ilmen](../I/Ilmen.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* mentions "the song of the Sun and Moon".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) explains that if his father ever wrote such poem, it has dissapeared.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md), *[Narsil](Narsil.md#Etymology)* means "[Sun](../S/Sun.md) and [Moon](../M/Moon.md)", derived from *[Anar](../A/Anar.md)* ("sun") and *[Isil](../M/Moon.md#Other_names)* ("moon"), and the *–ion* suffix indicates the plural genitive. Thus in full *Narsilion* means "Of the Sun and the Moon". <span></span>
