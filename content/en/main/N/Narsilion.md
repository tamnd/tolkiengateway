**Narsilion** is the "Song of the Sun and Moon", telling of their creation: After the [Two Trees of Valinor](/wiki/Two_Trees_of_Valinor) were poisoned, [Manwë](/wiki/Manw%C3%AB) commanded [Yavanna](/wiki/Yavanna) and [Nienna](/wiki/Nienna) to attempt to heal the Trees. Yavanna sang and Nienna wept, but to little avail until at last [Telperion](/wiki/Telperion) bore one silver flower and [Laurelin](/wiki/Laurelin) a single golden fruit. These Yavanna took and gave to [Aulë](/wiki/Aul%C3%AB). Manwë hallowed them and Aulë and his people made vessels to hold them and preserve their radiance. [Varda](/wiki/Varda) then took the vessels and gave them the power to traverse the lower regions of [Ilmen](/wiki/Ilmen).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The *[Quenta Noldorinwa](/wiki/Quenta_Noldorinwa)* mentions "the song of the Sun and Moon".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) explains that if his father ever wrote such poem, it has dissapeared.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), *[Narsil](/wiki/Narsil#Etymology)* means "[Sun](/wiki/Sun) and [Moon](/wiki/Moon)", derived from *[Anar](/wiki/Anar)* ("sun") and *[Isil](/wiki/Moon#Other_names "Moon")* ("moon"), and the *–ion* suffix indicates the plural genitive. Thus in full *Narsilion* means "Of the Sun and the Moon". <span></span>
