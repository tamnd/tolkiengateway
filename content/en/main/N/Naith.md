The **Naith** of Lórien was a wedge-shaped land between the Rivers [Celebrant](/wiki/Celebrant) and [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin), where [Egladil](/wiki/Egladil), the heartlands of [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) lay, and its capital [Caras Galadhon](/wiki/Caras_Galadhon) was found.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

**Gore** was [Haldir](/wiki/Haldir)'s translation of the [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) name Naith.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) word *naith*, glossed as "angle", is derived from *nek-tē* (root [NEK](/wiki/NEK)). Its [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) form is *nehte*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In *[The Etymologies](/wiki/The_Etymologies)* the word is glossed as "gore" and appears as a [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) word (the procursor of Sindarin), derived from the roots SNAS-/SNAT-. Other words derived from that root have the meaning of "spear-head, point, triangle", including a sketch of an arrow-head.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The English gloss <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/gore#Etymology_3" title="wiktionary:gore">gore</a></i> is a rare word which describes any of a wide range of narrow or pointed items, originally a narrow triangular piece of land.
