The **Naith** of Lórien was a wedge-shaped land between the Rivers [Celebrant](../C/Celebrant.md) and [Anduin](../A/Anduin.md), where [Egladil](../E/Egladil.md), the heartlands of [Lothlórien](../L/Lothlórien.md) lay, and its capital [Caras Galadhon](../C/Caras_Galadhon.md) was found.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

**Gore** was [Haldir](../H/Haldir.md)'s translation of the [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) name Naith.

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md) word *naith*, glossed as "angle", is derived from *nek-tē* (root [NEK](NEK.md)). Its [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md) form is *nehte*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> In *[The Etymologies](../T/The_Etymologies.md)* the word is glossed as "gore" and appears as a [Noldorin](Noldorin.md) word (the procursor of Sindarin), derived from the roots SNAS-/SNAT-. Other words derived from that root have the meaning of "spear-head, point, triangle", including a sketch of an arrow-head.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

The English gloss <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/gore#Etymology_3" title="wiktionary:gore">gore</a></i> is a rare word which describes any of a wide range of narrow or pointed items, originally a narrow triangular piece of land.
