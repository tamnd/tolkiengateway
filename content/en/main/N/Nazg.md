<i><b>nazg</b></i> means "(finger-)ring" or "magic-ring" in the [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-PE19_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*nazg* was an early loan from [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) words derived from [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) [NASAG](NASAG.md).<sup id="cite_ref-PE19_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PE19-2">[2]</a></sup> [Helge Fauskanger](../H/Helge_Fauskanger.md) has also noticed the similarity with [Valarin](../V/Valarin.md) *naškâd* ("ring"), which would probably be the original word, corrupted by Sauron.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>

## Examples {#Examples}

- <i><a href="Nazgûl.md" title="Nazgûl"><b>Naz</b>gûl</a></i> = "Ringwraith"
- <i><a href="../R/Ring-inscription.md" title="Ring-inscription">Ash <b>nazg</b> durbatulûk</a></i> = "One Ring to rule them all"

## Inspiration {#Inspiration}

*nazg* was inspired by the Gaelic (Irish) word *nasc* (Scottish: *nasg*), also meaning ring.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> <span></span>
