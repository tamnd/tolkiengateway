**Northiligand** was the lord of the [people](/wiki/Gondorian "Gondorian") of the [Delta](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin") in the early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*. He is mentioned in a sketch alongside [Benrodir](/wiki/Benrodir) and [Inram](/wiki/Inram) as one of the leaders of [Gondor](/wiki/Gondor) that came to the aid of [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) during the [War of the Ring](/wiki/War_of_the_Ring).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about him, as he appears nowhere else in the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Earlier, his name was spelled <i><b>Nosdiligand</b></i>, though Christopher Tolkien mentioned that a few of the letters are illegible.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to *[Eldamo](/wiki/Eldamo)*, the name *Nosdiligand* is in [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
