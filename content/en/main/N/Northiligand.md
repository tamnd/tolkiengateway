**Northiligand** was the lord of the [people](../G/Gondorian.md) of the [Delta](../E/Ethir_Anduin.md) in the early drafts of *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)*. He is mentioned in a sketch alongside [Benrodir](../B/Benrodir.md) and [Inram](../I/Inram.md) as one of the leaders of [Gondor](../G/Gondor.md) that came to the aid of [Minas Tirith](../M/Minas_Tirith.md) during the [War of the Ring](../W/War_of_the_Ring.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Nothing else is known about him, as he appears nowhere else in the [legendarium](../L/Legendarium.md).

## Etymology {#Etymology}

Earlier, his name was spelled <i><b>Nosdiligand</b></i>, though Christopher Tolkien mentioned that a few of the letters are illegible.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

According to *[Eldamo](../E/Eldamo.md)*, the name *Nosdiligand* is in [Noldorin](Noldorin.md), but its meaning is unclear.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup>
