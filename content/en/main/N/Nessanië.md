**Nessanië** was the daughter of [Hallatan](/wiki/Hallatan) of [Hyarastorni](/wiki/Hyarastorni), a descendant of [Elros](/wiki/Elros) in the sixth generation. Her younger brother was [Hallacar](/wiki/Hallacar), spouse of [Tar-Ancalimë](/wiki/Tar-Ancalim%C3%AB), Queen of [Númenor](/wiki/N%C3%BAmenor).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nessanië* is a [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) name. Its meaning is not glossed, but Paul Strack suggests it is a variation of the name of the Vala [Nessa](/wiki/Nessa).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
