**Norman Forsey** (b. [January 3](/wiki/3_January "3 January"), [1937](/wiki/1937)) is a New Zealand actor born in Cardiff, Wales. He played [Hamfast Gamgee](/wiki/Gaffer_Gamgee "Gaffer Gamgee") in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring)*. He only appeared in the [extended edition](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring_(extended_edition)> "The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (extended edition)").
