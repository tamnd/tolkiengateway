The **North Sea** of [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md) was a sea that was located just to the west of the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Given that the North Sea is just west of the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md), a place which was once a part of the lands to the north of [Beleriand](../B/Beleriand.md), it seems possible that the North Sea was at least partly formed as a result of the [War of Wrath](../W/War_of_Wrath.md).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is unclear, however, if the North Sea was a sea in its own right, a predecessor of the [Belegaer](../B/Belegaer.md), another name for the [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md), or a name for the northern parts of Belegaer.

### Islands {#Islands}

The North Sea lies just to the northeast of the following islands:

- &#8203;
    - [Tol Fuin](../T/Tol_Fuin.md)
    - [Himling](../H/Himling.md)

### Bays and inlets {#Bays_and_inlets}

- [Icebay of Forochel](../I/Icebay_of_Forochel.md)

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The North Sea is not marked on any of the maps that were published with [The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), [Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](../U/Unfinished_Tales.md) or [The Silmarillion](../T/The_Silmarillion.md) and is not mentioned in the text of those works. [J.R.R. Tolkien](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) pencilled in the name on [the First Map of The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md) and [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) included the label at the G 4-5 "coordinates" on [the map that he drew in 1943](../T/The_1943_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md), "though inadvertently omitted on Map I".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
