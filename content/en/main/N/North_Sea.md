The **North Sea** of [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) was a sea that was located just to the west of the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Given that the North Sea is just west of the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel), a place which was once a part of the lands to the north of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), it seems possible that the North Sea was at least partly formed as a result of the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> It is unclear, however, if the North Sea was a sea in its own right, a predecessor of the [Belegaer](/wiki/Belegaer), another name for the [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel), or a name for the northern parts of Belegaer.

### Islands {#Islands}

The North Sea lies just to the northeast of the following islands:

- &#8203;
    - [Tol Fuin](/wiki/Tol_Fuin)
    - [Himling](/wiki/Himling)

### Bays and inlets {#Bays_and_inlets}

- [Icebay of Forochel](/wiki/Icebay_of_Forochel)

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The North Sea is not marked on any of the maps that were published with [The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings), [Unfinished Tales of Númenor and Middle-earth](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales "Unfinished Tales") or [The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion) and is not mentioned in the text of those works. [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) pencilled in the name on [the First Map of The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_First_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The First Map of The Lord of the Rings") and [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) included the label at the G 4-5 "coordinates" on [the map that he drew in 1943](/wiki/The_1943_Map_of_The_Lord_of_the_Rings "The 1943 Map of The Lord of the Rings"), "though inadvertently omitted on Map I".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
