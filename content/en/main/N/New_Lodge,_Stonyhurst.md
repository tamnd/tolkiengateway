**New Lodge, Stonyhurst** is the title of a drawing by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), depicting a garden and guest house on the grounds of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonyhurst_College" title="wikipedia:Stonyhurst College">Stonyhurst College</a>, a Catholic boarding school in <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stonyhurst" title="wikipedia:Stonyhurst">Stonyhurst</a>, Lancashire. It was drawn by Tolkien in August [1947](/wiki/1947).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

The drawing was reproduced as figure 28 in *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist &amp; Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator "J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator")*.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
