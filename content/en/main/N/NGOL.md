**NGOL** is an [Elvish](/wiki/Elvish) [root](/wiki/Sundocarme "Sundocarme") signifying "knowledge, wisdom, lore".<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](/wiki/Primitive_Quendian): *ñgolodō* ("the Wise")<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> or *ngolodō*<sup id="cite_ref-P4k_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P4k-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *[Ñoldo](/wiki/Noldor "Noldor")*,<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> *noldo*<sup id="cite_ref-P4k_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P4k-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *Goldo*<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[Golodh](/wiki/Golodh) (Ngolodh)*<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya): *ñolé* ("long study (of any subject)")<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> or *ñólë* ("lore, knowledge");<sup id="cite_ref-P4k_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P4k-2">[2]</a></sup> *iñgole* ("lore");<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> *ingolmo* ("loremaster")<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](/wiki/Telerin): *góle, engole*
    - [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin): *[gûl](/wiki/G%C3%BBl "Gûl")*
    - [Black Speech](/wiki/Black_Speech): *[gûl](/wiki/G%C3%BBl "Gûl")*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](/wiki/The_Etymologies "The Etymologies") appears the roots ÑGOL, signifying "wise, wisdom, be wise", and ÑGOLOD, signifying "one of the wise folk, Gnome".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety377_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety377-3">[3]</a></sup>
