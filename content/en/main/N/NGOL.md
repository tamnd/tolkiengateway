**NGOL** is an [Elvish](../E/Elvish.md) [root](../S/Sundocarme.md) signifying "knowledge, wisdom, lore".<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Derivatives {#Derivatives}

- [Primitive Quendian](../P/Primitive_Quendian.md): *ñgolodō* ("the Wise")<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> or *ngolodō*<sup id="cite_ref-P4k_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P4k-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *[Ñoldo](Noldor.md)*,<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> *noldo*<sup id="cite_ref-P4k_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P4k-2">[2]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md): *Goldo*<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[Golodh](../G/Golodh.md) (Ngolodh)*<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>

- [Quenya](../Q/Quenya.md): *ñolé* ("long study (of any subject)")<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> or *ñólë* ("lore, knowledge");<sup id="cite_ref-P4k_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-P4k-2">[2]</a></sup> *iñgole* ("lore");<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup> *ingolmo* ("loremaster")<sup id="cite_ref-WJC_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-WJC-1">[1]</a></sup>
    - [Telerin](../T/Telerin.md): *góle, engole*
    - [Sindarin](../S/Sindarin.md): *[gûl](../G/Gûl.md)*
    - [Black Speech](../B/Black_Speech.md): *[gûl](../G/Gûl.md)*

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the [*Etymologies*](../T/The_Etymologies.md) appears the roots ÑGOL, signifying "wise, wisdom, be wise", and ÑGOLOD, signifying "one of the wise folk, Gnome".<sup id="cite_ref-Ety377_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ety377-3">[3]</a></sup>
