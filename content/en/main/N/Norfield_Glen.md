The **Norfield Glen** was a forest within the [hills](/wiki/Rhovanion_Hills "Rhovanion Hills") of [Rhovanion](/wiki/Rhovanion). The Norfield Glen was invented for *[The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Rings_of_Power)* by [Amazon Studios](/wiki/Amazon_Studios).

## History {#History}

In the late [Second Age](/wiki/Second_Age), the Norfield Glen was one of the seasonal havens where a tribe of [Harfoots](/wiki/Harfoots) migrated to in mid-summer due to the edible snails that were said to live there. The Harfoots held their <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Harvest_Festival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Harvest Festival (page does not exist)">Harvest Festival</a> there.
