**Nan** was a [Giant](/wiki/Giant). His<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> sword, [Glend](/wiki/Glend), was known as one of the tallest and longest things upon Earth and it was invoked by [Tinúviel](/wiki/L%C3%BAthien "Lúthien") in her [lengthening spell](/wiki/Lengthening_spell "Lengthening spell").<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
