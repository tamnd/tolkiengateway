**Nan-tathren**, the "**Land of Willows**", was a region in [Methiriad](/wiki/Methiriad). It was located where the River [Narog](/wiki/Narog) met the great River [Sirion](/wiki/Sirion), a little to the north of the [Mouths of Sirion](/wiki/Mouths_of_Sirion).<sup id="cite_ref-S14_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S14-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Famed for its [willows](/wiki/Willows "Willows") and its flowered meadows, Nan-tathren was a desolate land.<sup id="cite_ref-S14_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-S14-1">[1]</a></sup> The region played little part in the history of [Arda](/wiki/Arda), and was perhaps inhabited by [Ents](/wiki/Ents).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> The Land of Willows is said to be the place where [butterflies](/wiki/Butterflies "Butterflies") of all kinds came into the world, and they remained common there during the [First Age](/wiki/First_Age).<sup id="cite_ref-fall_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fall-3">[3]</a></sup><sup class="references" style="margin: 0;line-height: 0.4em; padding-top: 0px !important; font-size: 70%;">:154</sup>

After [Morgoth](/wiki/Morgoth)'s triumph in the [Nirnaeth Arnoediad](/wiki/Nirnaeth_Arnoediad), his [Orcs](/wiki/Orcs) and [wolves](/wiki/Wolves "Wolves") are said to have roamed even the distant southern lands of Nan-tathren and [Ossiriand](/wiki/Ossiriand), causing none of the [Free peoples](/wiki/Free_peoples) in Beleriand to be secure.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

The refugees from [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin), led by [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor), rested in Nan-tathren for a while on their long journey south from their city's ruin. They were healed of their wounds and sorrows by [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo)'s power, which was strong in the land from his presence in the River Sirion. There Tuor sang to his son [Eärendil](/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil) a [song about his encounter with Ulmo](/wiki/The_Horns_of_Ylmir "The Horns of Ylmir"), awakening in both the [sea-longing](/wiki/Sea-longing "Sea-longing").<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>

In the late [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age), [Treebeard](/wiki/Treebeard) remembered Nan-tathren and sang about "*the willow-meads of Tasarinan*" to [Merry](/wiki/Meriadoc_Brandybuck "Meriadoc Brandybuck") and [Pippin](/wiki/Peregrin_Took "Peregrin Took").<sup id="cite_ref-Tree_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tree-6">[6]</a></sup> [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel) later expressed the hope that she and Treebeard might meet there again.<sup id="cite_ref-partings_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-partings-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Nan-tathren* is a [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin) name, meaning "vale of willows" or "land of willows".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other names {#Other_names}

In [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya), the region was called <i><b>Tasarinan</b></i> or <i><b>Nan-tasarion</b></i>,<sup id="cite_ref-Tree_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tree-6">[6]</a></sup> meaning "willow-vale".<sup id="cite_ref-RC_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-RC-8">[8]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In the earliest version of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium") in *[The Book of Lost Tales](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales)*, the 'Land of Willows' is called Tasarinan, being located in roughly the same place as in the later geography of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). [Tuor](/wiki/Tuor) visits it before, as well as after, his sojourn in [Gondolin](/wiki/Gondolin). Its beauty caused Tuor to tarry there longer than [Ulmo](/wiki/Ulmo) had purposed — only leaving when the Vala appeared to him directly and ordered him to seek Gondolin. Following the sack of the city the refugees came to the Land of Willows and stayed there several years, until Tuor's 'sea-longing' drove them on to the Mouths of Sirion.<sup id="cite_ref-fall_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fall-3">[3]</a></sup> Later it becomes the site of the [Battle of Tasarinan](/wiki/Battle_of_Tasarinan).
