**Nils Ivar Agøy** is Professor of Modern History at the University of South-Eastern Norway in Bø i Telemark (Norway),<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> and co-founder of the [Norwegian Tolkien Society](/wiki/Norwegian_Tolkien_Society). He translated *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*, *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)*, *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)* and *[The Children of Húrin](/wiki/The_Children_of_H%C3%BArin)* into Norwegian (Bokmål). He has written many articles on Tolkien and wrote *[Mytenes mann: J.R.R. Tolkien og hans forfatterskap](/wiki/Mytenes_mann "Mytenes mann")* ([2003](/wiki/2003)) and edited the volume *[Between Faith and Fiction: Tolkien and the Powers of His World](/wiki/Between_Faith_and_Fiction "Between Faith and Fiction")* ([1998](/wiki/1998)). He has also given lectures at numerous Tolkien events in recent decades.
