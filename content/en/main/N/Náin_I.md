**Náin I** ([Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) [1832](/wiki/Third_Age_1832 "Third Age 1832") – [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"), lived 149 years) was the son of [Durin VI](/wiki/Durin_VI) and [King of Durin's folk](/wiki/King_of_Durin%27s_folk).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During his time the [Dwarves](/wiki/Dwarves) of [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) lived in [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria"), but had awoken a [Balrog](/wiki/Balrog). The Balrog slew Náin's father and was thereafter known as [Durin's Bane](/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane). Náin ruled for only one year, attempting to hold out against the Balrog but shortly thereafter he also found his death by in [1981](/wiki/Third_Age_1981 "Third Age 1981"), being the last true King of [Khazad-dûm](/wiki/Moria "Moria") until [Durin VII](/wiki/Durin_VII) in the [Fourth Age](/wiki/Fourth_Age).

Under his son [Thráin's](/wiki/Thr%C3%A1in_I "Thráin I") direction, [Durin's folk](/wiki/Durin%27s_folk) fled their ancestral home for safety in [Lonely Mountain](/wiki/Lonely_Mountain) and the [Grey Mountains](/wiki/Grey_Mountains).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Náinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](/wiki/V%C3%B6lusp%C3%A1 "Völuspá")*. It means "Corpselike".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Portrayal in adaptations {#Portrayal_in_adaptations}

<b>2001: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring" title="The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring">The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Written on the wall of <a href="/wiki/Chamber_of_Mazarbul" title="Chamber of Mazarbul">the Chamber of Mazarbul</a> is <a href="/wiki/Khuzdul" title="Khuzdul">Khuzdul</a> text translating to &#34;<a href="/wiki/Durin_VI" title="Durin VI">Durin</a> slain by <a href="/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane" title="Durin&#39;s Bane">Durin&#39;s Bane</a>, and Náin Durin&#39;s son, Durin&#39;s Bane also slew him&#34;.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup></dd></dl>

<b>2008: <i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Online" title="The Lord of the Rings Online">The Lord of the Rings Online</a></i>:</b>

<dl><dd>Prince Náin appears during the fall of <a href="/wiki/Moria" title="Moria">Moria</a> to <a href="/wiki/Durin%27s_Bane" title="Durin&#39;s Bane">Durin&#39;s Bane</a>, in which he becomes the King after his father <a href="/wiki/Durin_VI" title="Durin VI">Durin VI</a> is slain. Inside <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Khazad-d%C3%BBm" title="Khazad-dûm">Khazad-dûm</a>, remnants of the Palace of Nain, built during his short one-year reign, can be found.</dd></dl>
