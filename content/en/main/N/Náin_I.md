**Náin I** ([Third Age](../T/Third_Age.md) [1832](../T/Third_Age_1832.md) – [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md), lived 149 years) was the son of [Durin VI](../D/Durin_VI.md) and [King of Durin's folk](../K/King_of_Durin's_folk.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

During his time the [Dwarves](../D/Dwarves.md) of [Durin's folk](../D/Durin's_folk.md) lived in [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md), but had awoken a [Balrog](../B/Balrog.md). The Balrog slew Náin's father and was thereafter known as [Durin's Bane](../D/Durin's_Bane.md). Náin ruled for only one year, attempting to hold out against the Balrog but shortly thereafter he also found his death by in [1981](../T/Third_Age_1981.md), being the last true King of [Khazad-dûm](../M/Moria.md) until [Durin VII](../D/Durin_VII.md) in the [Fourth Age](../F/Fourth_Age.md).

Under his son [Thráin's](../T/Thráin_I.md) direction, [Durin's folk](../D/Durin's_folk.md) fled their ancestral home for safety in [Lonely Mountain](../L/Lonely_Mountain.md) and the [Grey Mountains](../G/Grey_Mountains.md).<sup id="cite_ref-Durin_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Durin-1">[1]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

*Náinn* is a dwarf from the *[Dvergatal](../V/Völuspá.md)*. It means "Corpselike".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
