The **Noldolantë**, meaning "The Fall of the [Noldor](Noldor.md)", was a lament composed by [Maglor](../M/Maglor.md), a famed minstrel and the second son of [Fëanor](../F/Fëanor.md). The song told of events Maglor himself took part: Fëanor's rebellion against the [Valar](../V/Valar.md), the [Kinslaying](../K/Kinslaying_at_Alqualondë.md) at [Alqualondë](../A/Alqualondë.md), in which Fëanor led the first ever slaying of [Elves](../E/Elves.md) by Elves, and the [Exile of the Noldor](../E/Exile_of_the_Noldor.md) in [Middle-earth](../M/Middle-earth.md).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

The *[Quenta Noldorinwa](../Q/Quenta_Noldorinwa.md)* mentions "the song of the Flight of the Gnomes",<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> which [Christopher Tolkien](../C/Christopher_Tolkien.md) links with the abandoned poem "[The Flight of the Noldoli from Valinor](../T/The_Flight_of_the_Noldoli_from_Valinor.md)", wondering if his father had still intentions of continuing it or write another version.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
