**Nellas** was an [Elf](/wiki/Elves_of_Doriath "Elves of Doriath") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), and a friend and tutor of [Túrin Turambar](/wiki/T%C3%BArin_Turambar) during his boyhood.<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Nellas was an [Elf-maiden](/wiki/Elves "Elves") of [Doriath](/wiki/Doriath), who lived in the woods near [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth). At the bidding of [Melian](/wiki/Melian), she watched over [Túrin](/wiki/T%C3%BArin) whenever he strayed into the woods during his boyhood years. Sometimes they played together or wandered hand in hand. To Túrin, she seemed no more than his own age.<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup>

It was from Nellas' teachings that Túrin learned [Doriathrin](/wiki/Doriathrin), a more archaic and courteous form of [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), as well as the "ways and the wild things of Doriath".<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup>

Eventually however, as Túrin grew to manhood, he turned his attention to warfare, and as Nellas was unwilling to venture under the roofs of stone of [Menegroth](/wiki/Menegroth), Túrin eventually stopped meeting with her altogether. Nonetheless, Nellas continued watching over him, though now she remained hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-Doriath_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Doriath-1">[1]</a></sup>

After Túrin's accidental slaying of [Saeros](/wiki/Saeros),<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> it was Nellas' account that earned him the forgiveness of his King and foster-father [Thingol](/wiki/Thingol) (though he did not know this, having already exiled himself from Doriath).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>

Some time later, when [Beleg](/wiki/Beleg) brought the news of his pardon to Túrin,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> he seemed not to have remembered much about Nellas, leading to Beleg rebuking him.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name *Nellas* is in [Sindarin](/wiki/Sindarin), though its meaning is never glossed. One possible meaning of the name is "Three-leaf", as suggested by [David Salo](/wiki/David_Salo), consisting of the [Noldorin](/wiki/Noldorin) element <i><a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Nel-&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Nel- (page does not exist)">nel-</a></i> ("tri") + Sindarin *[lass](/wiki/Lass "Lass")* ("leaf").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>

For alternative interpretations of the name's meaning, see the entry <b><a class="external text" href="https://www.glyphweb.com/arda/n/nellas.php" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Nellas</a></b> on the *[Encyclopedia of Arda](/wiki/Encyclopedia_of_Arda)*.
