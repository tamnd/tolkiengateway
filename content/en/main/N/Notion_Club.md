The **Notion Club** was a literary discussion group based in [Oxford](../O/Oxford.md).

In <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_2012&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 2012 (page does not exist)">2012</a>, [Howard Green](../H/Howard_Green.md), a School Clerk, found a disordered bundle of papers on the top of one of a number of sacks of waste paper in the basement of the Examination Schools at Oxford. They were loosely tied with a red string and on the outer sheet was inscribed in large Lombardic capitals: **NOTION CLUB PAPERS**. Later, it was discovered that the papers reported approximately one hundred meetings of a certain discussion group. The meetings took place between c. <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_1980&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 1980 (page does not exist)">1980</a> and <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_1990&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 1990 (page does not exist)">1990</a>, and Lowdham was one of its members.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>

During those meetings, many of the members reported having strange dreams, in which it seemed to them that they travelled through space or time.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> During a meeting that took place on [12 June](../_/12_June.md), <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Seventh_Age_1987&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Seventh Age 1987 (page does not exist)">1987</a>, labeled *Night 67*, the <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Great_Storm&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Great Storm (page does not exist)">Great Storm</a> hit <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=England&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="England (page does not exist)">England</a>, and strange clouds in the shapes of [eagles](../E/Eagles.md) hovered above Oxford.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> After that night, [Alwin Arundel Lowdham](../A/Alwin_Arundel_Lowdham.md) and [Wilfrid Trewin Jeremy](../W/Wilfrid_Trewin_Jeremy.md), went missing and were only later heard from by a letter where it was found that they were on a journey across the country.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> and returned to Oxford just one day before the next meeting, which took place on [25 September](../_/25_September.md), 1987.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> <span></span>
