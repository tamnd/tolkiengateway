**Negation in Quenya** is the title of an article by [Bill Welden](/wiki/Bill_Welden), published in [*Vinyar Tengwar* 42](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_42 "Vinyar Tengwar 42") ([2001](/wiki/2001); pp. 32–4).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup>

Welden addresses the issue of how to express negation in [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) by quoting from several [unpublished manuscripts](/wiki/Index:Unpublished_material "Index:Unpublished material") by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien):

- "List of roots and derivatives" - dated December [1959](/wiki/1959).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> The complete manuscript was later published in [*Parma Eldalamberon* 17](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17") ([2007](/wiki/2007)) as "Eldarin Roots and Stems".<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- "Unpublished essay on comparison"" - <sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> Further quotes from this essay appeared in [*Vinyar Tengwar* 47](/wiki/Vinyar_Tengwar_47 "Vinyar Tengwar 47") ([2005](/wiki/2005); p. 30, note 44 and p. 37, note 58). Editor [Patrick H. Wynne](/wiki/Patrick_H._Wynne) suggests that the essay was composed in "*late [1966](/wiki/1966) or afterwards*", by noting Tolkien's reference to a question from [Plotz](/wiki/Dick_Plotz "Dick Plotz") (while preparing an [interview](/wiki/Index:Interviews_with_J.R.R._Tolkien "Index:Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien") for *[Seventeen](/wiki/Seventeen)*) dated November 1966.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> This was later published in full in [*Parma Eldalamberon* 17](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_17 "Parma Eldalamberon 17").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- "Late essay" - likely dating from ca. [1970](/wiki/1970).<sup id="cite_ref-VT42_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-VT42-1">[1]</a></sup> This was later published in full in [*Parma Eldalamberon* 22](/wiki/Parma_Eldalamberon_22 "Parma Eldalamberon 22") as "Late Notes on Verb Structure".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup>
