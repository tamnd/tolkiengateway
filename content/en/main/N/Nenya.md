**Nenya** was one of the [Rings of Power](/wiki/Rings_of_Power), specifically one of the "[Three Rings](/wiki/Three_Rings) for the Elven Kings under the sky". Also known as the **White Ring**, the **Ring of Water**,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-1">[1]</a></sup> and **Ring of Adamant**,<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-2">[2]</a></sup> it was made of *[mithril](/wiki/Mithril "Mithril")* and set with a white stone<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup> of adamant.<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-1">[1]</a></sup>

## History {#History}

Nenya was made by [Celebrimbor](/wiki/Celebrimbor) of [Eregion](/wiki/Eregion) alone,<sup id="cite_ref-Rings_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rings-1">[1]</a></sup> between c. [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1500](/wiki/Second_Age_1500 "Second Age 1500")<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> and c. [1590](/wiki/Second_Age_1590 "Second Age 1590"),<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> along with the other two Elven Rings, [Narya](/wiki/Narya) and [Vilya](/wiki/Vilya). After Celebrimbor discovered that [Sauron](/wiki/Sauron) had forged [the One Ring](/wiki/The_One_Ring "The One Ring") in [S.A.](/wiki/Second_Age "Second Age") [1600](/wiki/Second_Age_1600 "Second Age 1600"),<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup> he went to [Lothlórien](/wiki/Lothl%C3%B3rien) to seek the counsel of [Galadriel](/wiki/Galadriel). They were unwilling to destroy the Rings, so Galadriel advised Celebrimbor to keep them hidden, unused, and dispersed far from Eregion. Celebrimbor followed this counsel, first by giving Nenya to Galadriel. The power of this Ring strengthened and beautified the realm of Lothlórien, but it also increased Galadriel's desire for the [Sea](/wiki/Belegaer "Belegaer") and return to the [West](/wiki/Undying_Lands "Undying Lands").<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>

<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a class="mw-file-description" href="/wiki/File:Ted_Nasmith_-_Galadriel.jpg"><img src="/w/images/thumb/a/a2/Ted_Nasmith_-_Galadriel.jpg/300px-Ted_Nasmith_-_Galadriel.jpg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="460" class="mw-file-element" srcset="/w/images/a/a2/Ted_Nasmith_-_Galadriel.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="359" data-file-height="550"/></a><figcaption><i>Galadriel</i> by <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a></figcaption></figure>

The ring wielded by Galadriel was not normally visible. On [15 February](/wiki/15_February) [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [3019](/wiki/Third_Age_3019 "Third Age 3019"),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup> [Frodo Baggins](/wiki/Frodo_Baggins) saw Nenya on Galadriel's finger, for it could not be hidden from the [Ring-bearer](/wiki/Ring-bearers "Ring-bearers"). [Samwise Gamgee](/wiki/Samwise_Gamgee) told Galadriel he only "saw a star through your fingers".<sup id="cite_ref-Mirror_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mirror-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[note 1]</a></sup>

Nenya's power was preservation, protection, and concealment from evil. After the destruction of the One Ring and the defeat of Sauron, its power faded along with the other Rings of Power. Galadriel bore Nenya on a ship from the [Grey Havens](/wiki/Grey_Havens) into the [West](/wiki/Aman "Aman"), accompanied by the other two Elven Rings and their bearers.<sup id="cite_ref-Havens_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Havens-3">[3]</a></sup> With Nenya gone, the magic and beauty of Lothlórien also faded and it was gradually depopulated, until by the time [Arwen](/wiki/Arwen) came there to die in [Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age") [121](/wiki/Fourth_Age_121 "Fourth Age 121"), it was deserted and in ruins.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[9]</a></sup>

## Etymology {#Etymology}

The name is [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) from *[nén](/wiki/N%C3%A9n "Nén")* ("water")<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[10]</a></sup> + *[-ya](/wiki/-ya)* (adjective suffix).

## Other versions of the legendarium {#Other_versions_of_the_legendarium}

In an early version of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)* Nenya was known as [Ëar](/wiki/E%C3%A4r "Eär"), the [Quenya](/wiki/Quenya) word for Sea. <sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[11]</a></sup>
