**Nathan Clark** (born [December 20](../_/20_December.md), [1974](../_/1974.md) in Wellington) is a Kiwi Graphic Designer who played [Galdor of the Havens](<../G/Galdor_(elf_of_the_Havens).md>) in *[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_The_Fellowship_of_the_Ring.md)*.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-2">[2]</a></sup>

Clark was hired when he attended a casting call in Wellywood.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> As of [December 20](../_/20_December.md), [2004](../_/2004.md), he resides in Wellington.<sup id="cite_ref-Argonath_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Argonath-2">[2]</a></sup> Clark read *[The Lord of the Rings](../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings.md)* five years before [the film trilogy](<../T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series).md>) begun production, and "always thought if they made it into a movie, I'd want to own a copy".
