**Featured artists** on [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) are showcased in the [Main Page](../../main/M/Main_Page.md) as a way to recognise those who kindly allow their art to be used in the wiki. The mention is accompanied by a representative piece of art. A new artist is featured each time the Main Page is updated, being chosen by one of the [admins](../A/Administrators.md). Unlike the featured [articles](Featured_articles.md) and [quotes](Featured_quotes.md), there is no nomination process for the featured artist, as the criteria is not based on taste, but the artist's overall presence throughtout the wiki.
