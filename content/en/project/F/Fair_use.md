At [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) our goal is to provide as much information as possible relating to the works of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). While we attempt to obtain permission for all of the content not created by us, primarily images, sometimes we are unable to contact the owner. If this is the case we believe the limited use of images relating to Tolkien's works with proper credit given to the original owner is considered Fair Use.
