**Featured quotes** on [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) are quotes by, or about, [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) which [the editors of Tolkien Gateway have chosen to feature](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_quotes/Nominations "Tolkien Gateway:Featured quotes/Nominations"). The quotes chosen to highlight a particularly of Tolkien's writings, or for their aesthetic or literary merit. Featured quotes are showcased on the [Main Page](/wiki/Main_Page).

All nominations take place at [Tolkien Gateway:Featured quotes/Nominations](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_quotes/Nominations). The nomination process was formalised by [User:Mith](/wiki/User:Mith) in [2014](/wiki/2014):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Any registered user can nominate an quote for <b>featured</b> status. It should meet these specific criteria:
</p>
<dl><dd><ol><li><i>Preferably, written/spoken by <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">J.R.R. Tolkien</a> himself, either in his fiction or elsewhere (such as in a <a href="/wiki/The_Letters_of_J.R.R._Tolkien" title="The Letters of J.R.R. Tolkien">letter</a>;</i></li>
<li><i>The quote should be no more than 100 words long, and ideally no shorter than 25 words;</i></li>
<li><i>Quotes should be insightful, witty, interesting, powerful, represent some aspect of a story or character, or generally be of interest to a reader;</i></li>
<li><i>Featured quotes should reflect the full range of Tolkien&#39;s writings and not just, for example, </i><a href="/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings" title="The Lord of the Rings">The Lord of the Rings</a><i>.</i></li></ol></dd></dl>
<p><span style="font-style: italic">After nomination, a quote should receive three or more positive votes, with more positive than negative; each user being able to cast one vote. Each entry should begin with <b>Agree</b>, <b>Disagree</b> or <b>Undecided</b> and be signed by the user, and, if they choose, an explanation of their vote.</span>
</p>
</blockquote>

Should a nomination be successful, it is the responsibility of an [administrators](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Administrators "Tolkien Gateway:Administrators") to update the [Main Page](/wiki/Main_Page) and move the nomination to the [archive](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_quotes/Archive "Tolkien Gateway:Featured quotes/Archive").
