**Featured articles** on [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) are considered to be our very best articles, as [judged by its editors](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_articles/Nominations "Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles/Nominations"). They are the articles we take especial pride in, and show an example to the world of our very best work, and as a model to editors for how to write other articles. As such, featured articles are showcased on the [Main Page](/wiki/Main_Page).

To achieve the status of featured article, candidate articles must meet 10 criteria before being reviewed by editors of accuracy, completeness, referencing, length, and style according to our featured article criteria. Since introducing the nomination format in 2008, Tolkien Gateway has had a total of [sixteen featured articles](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_articles/Archive "Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles/Archive") out of [13,644](/wiki/Special:Statistics "Special:Statistics") articles.

All nominations take place at [Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles/Nominations](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_articles/Nominations). The nomination process was formalised by [User:Mith](/wiki/User:Mith) in [2011](/wiki/2011):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Any registered user can nominate an article for <b>featured</b> status. It should be well-written, complete, illustrated, and referenced and meet these specific criteria:
</p>
<dl><dd><ol><li><i>The text is </i>entirely<i> written by TG editors with correct English spelling, grammar and punctuation;</i></li>
<li><i>There are no outstanding <a href="/wiki/Category:Maintenance_templates" title="Category:Maintenance templates">maintenance templates</a> on the article;</i></li>
<li><i>The article is well-referenced throughout, leaving the reader under no illusions as to where to source information themselves;</i></li>
<li><i>The article conforms to TG&#39;s <a href="/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Manual_of_Style" title="Tolkien Gateway:Manual of Style">Standards</a>, as well as those laid out in the relevant <a href="/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Projects" title="Tolkien Gateway:Projects">Project</a>;</i></li>
<li><i>The text is sprinkled with </i>relevant<i> <a href="/wiki/Help:Images" title="Help:Images">images</a> (including captions) of appropriate size - if necessary, including a gallery;</i></li>
<li><i>Articles are at least 5,000 bytes long;</i></li>
<li><i>Where applicable, contains sections of &#34;History&#34;, &#34;Portrayal in adaptations&#34;, &#34;Other versions of the legendarium&#34;, &#34;See also&#34;, &#34;Etymology&#34;, &#34;Genealogy&#34;,  &#34;Other names&#34;, etc;</i></li>
<li><i>Where applicable, </i>full use<i> has been made of relevant templates, e.g.: <a href="/wiki/Category:Disambiguation_and_redirection_templates" title="Category:Disambiguation and redirection templates">disambiguation</a>, <a href="/wiki/Template:Seealso" title="Template:Seealso">see also</a>, <a href="/wiki/Template:Main" title="Template:Main">main</a>, <a href="/wiki/Template:Navigation" title="Template:Navigation">navigation</a>, <a href="/wiki/Template:Pronounce" title="Template:Pronounce">pronounce</a>, <a href="/wiki/Template:Familytree" title="Template:Familytree">familytree</a>, <a href="/wiki/Template:Seq" title="Template:Seq">sequence</a>;</i></li>
<li><i>Where applicable, contains interwiki links;</i></li>
<li><i>Preferably no red-links, but no more than half-a-dozen.</i></li></ol></dd></dl>
<p><i>After nomination, an article should receive five votes with with no objections within a month or so; each user being able to cast one vote.</i>
</p>
</blockquote>

Should a nomination be successful, it is the responsibility of an [administrators](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Administrators "Tolkien Gateway:Administrators") to update the [Main Page](/wiki/Main_Page) and move the nomination to the [archive](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_articles/Archive "Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles/Archive").
