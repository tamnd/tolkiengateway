The **Lord of the Éothéod**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was the hereditary ruler of the [Éothéod](/wiki/%C3%89oth%C3%A9od), the horsemen of the North. The Lords of the Éothéod were descended from the [Kings of Rhovanion](/wiki/King_of_Rhovanion "King of Rhovanion").<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> With the formation of [Rohan](/wiki/Rohan) in [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2510](/wiki/Third_Age_2510 "Third Age 2510"), the title was abandoned in favor of "[King of the Mark](/wiki/King_of_Rohan "King of Rohan")".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>

- [Léod](/wiki/L%C3%A9od), d. [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age") [2501](/wiki/Third_Age_2501 "Third Age 2501")
