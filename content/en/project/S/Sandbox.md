The **Lord of the Éothéod**<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> was the hereditary ruler of the [Éothéod](../../main/_/Éothéod.md), the horsemen of the North. The Lords of the Éothéod were descended from the [Kings of Rhovanion](../../main/K/King_of_Rhovanion.md).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> With the formation of [Rohan](../../main/R/Rohan.md) in [T.A.](../../main/T/Third_Age.md) [2510](../../main/T/Third_Age_2510.md), the title was abandoned in favor of "[King of the Mark](../../main/K/King_of_Rohan.md)".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup>
