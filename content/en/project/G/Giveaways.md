**Tolkien Gateway** periodically holds giveaways. Below is a list of currently active giveaways on the site. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask on the discussion tab of this page.

## July 2023 {#July_2023}

- **Prize:** Three (3) winners will be selected at random and given the chance to choose one of the following:
    - US Residents
        - **[The History of The Hobbit](../../main/T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)** courtesy of [HarperCollins](../../main/H/HarperCollins.md)
        - <b><a class="mw-disambig" href="../../main/T/The_Battle_of_Maldon.md" title="The Battle of Maldon">The Battle of Maldon</a></b> courtesy of [HarperCollins](../../main/H/HarperCollins.md)
        - **[The Fall of Gondolin](../../main/T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin.md)** courtesy of [HarperCollins](../../main/H/HarperCollins.md)
        - **[Tolkien Calendar 2024](../../main/T/Tolkien_Calendar_2024.md)** courtesy of [HarperCollins](../../main/H/HarperCollins.md)
    - Non-US Residents
        - **1-year Membership to the [Tolkien Society](../../main/T/Tolkien_Society.md)**

- **How to enter:** contribute at least one edit to [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) during the month of July, [2023](../../main/_/2023.md). Three (3) random winners will be selected at the end of the month.
- **Rules:** Creating multiple accounts or submitting edits that have no value will result in disqualification.
- The winner will be announced by [August 5th](../../main/A/August_5.md), 2023, and they will be contacted via e-mail. If we are unable to reach the winner by August 31st, another winner will be chosen.

## <strike>June 2023</strike> {#June_2023}

- **Prize:** Your choice of any Middle-earth print from <a class="external text" href="https://donatoarts.com/online-store/secure-store/Middle-earth-c34110463" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Donato Giancola&#39;s store</a> (signed and numbered prints included!)
- **How to enter:** contribute at least one edit to [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) during the month of June, [2023](../../main/_/2023.md). One random winner will be selected at the end of the month.
- **Rules:** Creating multiple accounts or submitting edits that have no value will result in disqualification.
- The winner will be announced by [July 5th](../../main/J/July_5.md), 2023, and they will be contacted via e-mail. If we are unable to reach the winner by July 31st, another winner will be chosen.
