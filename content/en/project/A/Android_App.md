The **Tolkien Gateway Android App** is an app available on Android devices for accessing the Tolkien Gateway website. It offers an additional navigation menu by swiping from the left side of the screen. Future enhancements planned include offline access and a simplified layout.

The app is currently in the beta test phase, which anyone can join by filling out <a class="external text" href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdTIWWLTaQ5VooZ8bD4hwbfTY7wHRt6lFx9wCizAQYYZDCbHQ/viewform" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">this form</a>
