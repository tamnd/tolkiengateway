**Administrators**, also known as **admins** or **sysops** (<a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/sysop" title="wikipedia:sysop"><b>sys</b>tem <b>op</b>erator<b>s</b></a>), are [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) editors who have been granted the technical ability to perform certain tasks, including the ability to [block and unblock users](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:BlockList), [delete and undelete pages](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:Log/delete), and rollback edits.

Administrators are volunteers who use their own *judgement* to exercise their abilities in the best interests of Tolkien Gateway. There are currently 7 administrators.

<h2><span id="Administrators.27_abilities"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Administrators&#39;_abilities">Administrators&#39; abilities</span></h2>

Administrators have the technical ability to perform the following actions, including:

- [block user accounts](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:BlockList) and IP addresses from editing;
- [apply (and remove) page protection](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:Log/protect) to restrict editing of a particular page;
- [delete pages](../D/Deletion_policy.md) and [images](../I/Image_policy.md);
- revert pages quickly via the \[rollback\] function;
- [move a page](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:Log/move) over an already-existing page;
- edit [protected pages](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:ProtectedPages) (including the [Main Page](../../main/M/Main_Page.md));
- view and restore deleted pages;
- hide and delete page revisions;
- change [the interface](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:AllMessages) by editing the MediaWiki namespace (including site-wide [CSS](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/MediaWiki:Common.css) and [JavaScript](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/MediaWiki:Common.js)).

By convention, administrators also normally take responsibility for judging - and, of course, enacting - the outcome of certain discussions, such as deletion and move discussions. This process is fairly informal, however.

## Becoming an administrator {#Becoming_an_administrator}

<div class="tg-hatnote metadata noprint"><div class="tg-hatnote-icon"><span typeof="mw:File"><span><img src="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/images/c/c6/CdxIconArticleDisambiguation.svg" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20"/></span></span></div><div class="tg-hatnote-content">See also: <a href="../R/Requests_for_adminship.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Requests for adminship">Tolkien Gateway:Requests for adminship</a></div></div>

Administrators are appointed following a [request for adminship](../R/Requests_for_adminship.md), a [community meeting](../M/Meetings.md), or by a [bureaucrat](../B/Bureaucrats.md). In a formal request for adminship, Tolkien Gateway has six defined criteria for adminship:

1. They have an account under a screen-name.
2. They have actively contributed for at least six months to the wiki.
3. They have demonstrated they are willing to take on additional responsibilities to make the community better.
4. They have had at least some major article revisions.
5. They have dealings with other users on a regular basis in a fair, restrained, and constructive manner.
6. They have demonstrated an understanding of the community's policies and procedures.

In such requests, voting must be unanimously in favour. Administrators need to have the trust and confidence of the community so administrators are usually those editors with considerable experience (currently, the six administrators are six of the seven [top editors](../T/Top_Editors.md)). Potential administrators also need to be very well-acquainted with Tolkien Gateway's [policies](../P/Policy.md), [style guide](../M/Manual_of_Style.md), and [procedures](../../help/E/Editing.md).
