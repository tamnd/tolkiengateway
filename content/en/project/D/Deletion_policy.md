The **deletion policy** of [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) is relatively straightforward. There are no strict rules regarding this matter and issues are dealt on a case-by-case basis.

If you believe a page needs to deleted as soon as possible, add the template **{{delete}}** to the top of the article. If an administrator agrees it will be [deleted without discussion](#Deletion_without_discussion); if not, they will likely raise the matter on the [talk page](/wiki/Help:Glossary#T "Help:Glossary") or remove the template altogether.

## Deletion after discussion {#Deletion_after_discussion}

If you think a page is not suited for this encyclopaedia, you can discuss the proposed deletion on the [talk page](/wiki/Help:Glossary#T "Help:Glossary"). If there is a consensus in your favour it an [administrator](/wiki/Help:Glossary#A "Help:Glossary") will delete the page.

## Deletion without discussion {#Deletion_without_discussion}

The [administrators](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Administrators "Tolkien Gateway:Administrators") can delete content, pages, articles and files without notice on the following occasions:

- Spam
- [Vandalism](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Vandalism "Tolkien Gateway:Vandalism")
- Copyright violation
- Duplicate files/pages
- Articles not in English
- Obvious advertising or self-promotion
- When a copyright-holder has requested we remove their material
- When an administrator confidently believes that a page falls well outside of our [notability policy](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Notability_policy "Tolkien Gateway:Notability policy")
- Where content is no longer used or useful (such as former templates and categories)
