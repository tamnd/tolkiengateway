## Outcome {#Outcome}

- [Main Page](../../main/M/Main_Page.md)
    - [Administrators](../A/Administrators.md) should be allowed to update the Main Page in order to keep it fresh, so long as there are no Featured Articles/Quotes suitable.
    - In the meantime, active members should participate in the existing [Featured Articles](../F/Featured_articles.md) process.
    - The community will collectively draw up a list of 52 (or more) [Featured Quotes](../F/Featured_quotes.md), so the page can have fresh quotes for an entire year.
- [*The Hobbit* (film series)](<../../main/T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md>)
    - The community recognised the importance of harnessing the films to attract more members.
    - Update our article using <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hobbit_film_project" title="wikipedia:The Hobbit film project">Wikipedia&#39;s</a> as a framework.
    - Creation of a "Hobbit Project" to get *[The Hobbit](../../main/T/The_Hobbit.md)*-related articles up to a high standard.
- [Hobbit](../../main/H/Hobbits.md) articles
    - With regards to female married hobbits, preference shall be made to their maiden name, save where they are more popularly known by married name, especially in the case of [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](../../main/L/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins.md).
    - Numbers shall be placed after names, so for instance, "Isumbras Took III" and "Ponto Baggins II" over "Isumbras III Took" and "Ponto II Baggins".
    - Dates in [Shire-reckoning](../../main/S/Shire-reckoning.md) (using [Template:SR](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Template:SR)) to be used, save for Hobbits who left [the Shire](../../main/T/The_Shire.md) in which case [Third Age](../../main/T/Third_Age.md) dates will be used, with some SR dates included.
    - "Tolman Cotton Senior" to be moved to "Farmer Cotton".
- Technical issues were not discussed due to [Hyarion](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Hyarion)'s absence, although the community noted that the database had been unlocked without CAPTCHA.
- Standard for language articles was no discussed due to lack of time, however, the [discussion can be continued in the forum](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Forums:Diaereses).
