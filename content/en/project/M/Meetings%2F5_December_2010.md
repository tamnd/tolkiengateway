## Outcome {#Outcome}

- [Main Page](/wiki/Main_Page)
    - [Administrators](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Administrators "Tolkien Gateway:Administrators") should be allowed to update the Main Page in order to keep it fresh, so long as there are no Featured Articles/Quotes suitable.
    - In the meantime, active members should participate in the existing [Featured Articles](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_articles "Tolkien Gateway:Featured articles") process.
    - The community will collectively draw up a list of 52 (or more) [Featured Quotes](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Featured_quotes "Tolkien Gateway:Featured quotes"), so the page can have fresh quotes for an entire year.
- [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)")
    - The community recognised the importance of harnessing the films to attract more members.
    - Update our article using <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hobbit_film_project" title="wikipedia:The Hobbit film project">Wikipedia&#39;s</a> as a framework.
    - Creation of a "Hobbit Project" to get *[The Hobbit](/wiki/The_Hobbit)*-related articles up to a high standard.
- [Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") articles
    - With regards to female married hobbits, preference shall be made to their maiden name, save where they are more popularly known by married name, especially in the case of [Lobelia Sackville-Baggins](/wiki/Lobelia_Sackville-Baggins).
    - Numbers shall be placed after names, so for instance, "Isumbras Took III" and "Ponto Baggins II" over "Isumbras III Took" and "Ponto II Baggins".
    - Dates in [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire-reckoning) (using [Template:SR](/wiki/Template:SR)) to be used, save for Hobbits who left [the Shire](/wiki/The_Shire "The Shire") in which case [Third Age](/wiki/Third_Age) dates will be used, with some SR dates included.
    - "Tolman Cotton Senior" to be moved to "Farmer Cotton".
- Technical issues were not discussed due to [Hyarion](/wiki/User:Hyarion "User:Hyarion")'s absence, although the community noted that the database had been unlocked without CAPTCHA.
- Standard for language articles was no discussed due to lack of time, however, the [discussion can be continued in the forum](/wiki/Forums:Diaereses "Forums:Diaereses").
