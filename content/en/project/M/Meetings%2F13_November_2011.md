<tr style="font-size:100%; text-align: center; background-color:#E4F2D9;">
<td>#5
</td>
<td><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn" title="User:KingAragorn">KingAragorn</a>
</td>
<td>I think we all need to be braver and start tackling the bigger, more popular articles. Why don&#39;t we have a &#34;Countdown to The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey&#34;, wherein each month we go down the list of popular pages and collaborate on articles which relate to The Hobbit. So we would make perfect: Gandalf, Sauron (the Necromancer page needs to be merged with Sauron), The Hobbit, Bilbo Baggins, Elrond, Thorin (still needs a bit of expansion in history section), Rivendell, Battle of Five Armies, Gollum, Mirkwood, Dol Guldur, and Thranduil.
</td>
<td>After some discussion it was unanimously accepted. It would begin on <a href="../../main/_/1_December.md" title="1 December">1 December</a>, <a href="../../main/_/2011.md" title="2011">2011</a> and would end 1 December, <a href="../../main/_/2012.md" title="2012">2012</a>. The articles to tackle would be: Gandalf, Sauron (+the Necromancer), The Hobbit, Bilbo Baggins, Elrond, Thorin, Rivendell, Battle of Five Armies, Gollum, Mirkwood, Dol Guldur, and Thranduil.
</td></tr>
<tr style="font-size:100%; text-align: center; background-color:#E4F2D9;">
