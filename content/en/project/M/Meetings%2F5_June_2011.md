<td>#2
</td>
<td><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn" title="User:KingAragorn">KingAragorn</a> and <a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Amroth" title="User:Amroth">Amroth</a>
</td>
<td>Update on technical problems: the spam protection situation; the articles that a too large to edit.
</td>
<td>Mith has prevented spammers from posting content by disallowing uploads, page creation and article movement for users that have been a member for less than 12 hours and have made 10 edits. The bug that meant that some large articles were uneditable has been fixed. reCAPTCHA is a possibility in the future once MediaWiki has been upgraded.
</td></tr>

</td>
<td><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:KingAragorn" title="User:KingAragorn">KingAragorn</a>
</td>
<td>Discussion about <a href="../../main/T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a> articles now that <a class="external text" href="http://www.facebook.com/notes/the-hobbit/the-hobbit-an-unexpected-journey-and-the-hobbit-there-and-back-again-are-announc/206043836097896" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the naming has been confirmed</a>.
</td>
<td>It was agreed that, for now, we should have one article for both films but that in future we will have an article for each film and the duology. The articles <a href="../../main/T/The_Hobbit%253A_An_Unexpected_Journey.md" title="The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey">The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey</a> and <a href="../../main/T/The_Hobbit%253A_The_Battle_of_the_Five_Armies.md" title="The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies">The Hobbit: There and Back Again</a> were set up as redirects to <a href="../../main/T/The_Hobbit_(film_series).md" title="The Hobbit (film series)"><i>The Hobbit</i> (film series)</a>.
</td></tr>
