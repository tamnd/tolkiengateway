**Bureaucrats** are [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) editors who have been granted the technical ability to change individual user rights. In particular, they are able to promote editors to [adminship](../A/Administrators.md) or approve the creation of new [bots](Bots.md).

Bureaucrats are volunteers who use their own *judgement* to exercise their abilities in the best interests of Tolkien Gateway. There are currently 3 bureaucrats.

<h2><span id="Bureaucrats.27_abilities"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Bureaucrats&#39;_abilities">Bureaucrats&#39; abilities</span></h2>

Bureaucrats have the technical ability to perform the following actions:

- Grant (and remove) administrator and bureaucrat [rights](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Special:Log/rights) to other users;
- Approve new [bots](Bots.md) and remove bot user rights.

## Becoming a bureaucrat {#Becoming_a_bureaucrat}

There is currently no defined procedure for an user to become a bureaucrat; previous promotions have been agreed upon in a [community meeting](../M/Meetings.md). However, a first step to becoming a bureaucrat is obtaining the position of an [administrator](../A/Administrators.md).
