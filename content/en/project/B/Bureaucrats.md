**Bureaucrats** are [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway) editors who have been granted the technical ability to change individual user rights. In particular, they are able to promote editors to [adminship](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Administrators "Tolkien Gateway:Administrators") or approve the creation of new [bots](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Bots "Tolkien Gateway:Bots").

Bureaucrats are volunteers who use their own *judgement* to exercise their abilities in the best interests of Tolkien Gateway. There are currently 3 bureaucrats.

<h2><span id="Bureaucrats.27_abilities"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Bureaucrats&#39;_abilities">Bureaucrats&#39; abilities</span></h2>

Bureaucrats have the technical ability to perform the following actions:

- Grant (and remove) administrator and bureaucrat [rights](/wiki/Special:Log/rights "Special:Log/rights") to other users;
- Approve new [bots](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Bots "Tolkien Gateway:Bots") and remove bot user rights.
