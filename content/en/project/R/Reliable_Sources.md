A list of **reliable** and **unreliable sources** which respectively should and shouldn't be used on [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway). The list below covers secondary material only (i.e. not publications by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) or his son [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien)) both published and online.

## Reliable {#Reliable}

### Online {#Online}

- <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkiencalendars.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Compleat Gyde to Tolkien Calendars</a>
- <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkienbooks.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TolkienBooks.net</a>
- <a class="external text" href="https://tolkienbooks.us" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TolkienBooks.us</a>

### Publications {#Publications}

- *[The History of The Hobbit](/wiki/The_History_of_The_Hobbit)* by [John D. Rateliff](/wiki/John_D._Rateliff)
- *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Descriptive Bibliography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Descriptive_Bibliography)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Douglas A. Anderson](/wiki/Douglas_A._Anderson)
- *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull)
- *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Biography](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_A_Biography) by [Humphrey Carpenter](/wiki/Humphrey_Carpenter)*
- *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Companion and Guide](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Companion_and_Guide)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull)
- *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_A_Reader%27s_Companion)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](/wiki/Wayne_G._Hammond) and [Christina Scull](/wiki/Christina_Scull)
- *[The Lord of the Rings: Weapons and Warfare](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings:_Weapons_and_Warfare)* by [Chris Smith](/wiki/Chris_Smith) (for film information)
- *[The Ring of Words](/wiki/The_Ring_of_Words)* by Peter Gilliver, Edmund Weiner and Jeremy Marshall
- *[The Road to Middle-earth](/wiki/The_Road_to_Middle-earth)* by [Tom Shippey](/wiki/Tom_Shippey)
- *[Tolkien's Bag End](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Bag_End)* by Andrew H. Morton
- *[Tolkien's Gedling](/wiki/Tolkien%27s_Gedling)* by Andrew H. Morton and John Hayes
- *[Tolkien and the Great War](/wiki/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War)* by [John Garth](/wiki/John_Garth)
- *[Tolkien and Wales](/wiki/Tolkien_and_Wales)* by Carl Phelpstead
