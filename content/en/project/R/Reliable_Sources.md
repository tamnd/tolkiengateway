A list of **reliable** and **unreliable sources** which respectively should and shouldn't be used on [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md). The list below covers secondary material only (i.e. not publications by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) or his son [Christopher Tolkien](../../main/C/Christopher_Tolkien.md)) both published and online.

### Online {#Online}

- <a class="external text" href="http://www.tolkienbooks.net/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TolkienBooks.net</a>
- <a class="external text" href="https://tolkienbooks.us" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">TolkienBooks.us</a>

### Publications {#Publications}

- *[The History of The Hobbit](../../main/T/The_History_of_The_Hobbit.md)* by [John D. Rateliff](../../main/J/John_D._Rateliff.md)
- *[J.R.R. Tolkien: A Descriptive Bibliography](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_A_Descriptive_Bibliography.md)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](../../main/W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Douglas A. Anderson](../../main/D/Douglas_A._Anderson.md)
- *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien%253A_Artist_and_Illustrator.md)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](../../main/W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../../main/C/Christina_Scull.md)

- *[The Lord of the Rings: A Reader's Companion](../../main/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_A_Reader's_Companion.md)* by [Wayne G. Hammond](../../main/W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) and [Christina Scull](../../main/C/Christina_Scull.md)
- *[The Lord of the Rings: Weapons and Warfare](../../main/T/The_Lord_of_the_Rings%253A_Weapons_and_Warfare.md)* by [Chris Smith](../../main/C/Chris_Smith.md) (for film information)

- *[The Road to Middle-earth](../../main/T/The_Road_to_Middle-earth.md)* by [Tom Shippey](../../main/T/Tom_Shippey.md)
- *[Tolkien's Bag End](../../main/T/Tolkien's_Bag_End.md)* by Andrew H. Morton
- *[Tolkien's Gedling](../../main/T/Tolkien's_Gedling.md)* by Andrew H. Morton and John Hayes
- *[Tolkien and the Great War](../../main/T/Tolkien_and_the_Great_War.md)* by [John Garth](../../main/J/John_Garth.md)
