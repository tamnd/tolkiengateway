**Please note:** as per [Tolkien Gateway's twelfth meeting](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Meetings/27_June_2010 "Tolkien Gateway:Meetings/27 June 2010"), inactive supervisors will be replaced if necessary.

## Adaptations {#Adaptations}

<dl><dd><i>Supervisor: <b><a href="/wiki/User:Ederchil" title="User:Ederchil">Ederchil</a></b></i></dd></dl>

The goal of the project is to create an article for every noteworthy adaptation of Tolkien's works.

- Scene by scene summary of *[The Lord of the Rings](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings)*.
    - Consider using multiple tabs for the infobox data to separate theatrical and extended editions, similar to [May It Be](/wiki/May_It_Be)
- Create an article which details locations in [New Zealand](/wiki/New_Zealand) from [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)").

See proposition at *[Tolkien Gateway:Projects/Adaptations](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Projects/Adaptations)*

## Books {#Books}

<dl><dd><i>Supervisor: <b><a href="/wiki/User:Luotiansha" title="User:Luotiansha">Luotiansha</a></b></i></dd></dl>

The goal of the project is to create an article for every single book published or unpublished which relates to [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his works. Concentration is also on editions, impressions, book covers, as well as the authors.

- Authors: Try and make a list of published work - books, articles - on every author. Try library catalogues, LexisNexis, Google, etc.
- Authors: Include profession (University), motives, image (if available), and other infobox information.
- Books: Change all publishing dates to the first edition dates.
- Books: Write an introduction phrase on all articles.
- Books: Include information on all editions.
- Books: Make a new infobox.
- Books by Tolkien: Include some sort of "Explanation of the Title" section.
- Chapters: chapter synopsis for all of Tolkien's major works. Feature a quote from the chapter at the top of the article.
- Publishers: Consider which publishers need to have articles, and which don't. Minimum of three books?

## Dates {#Dates}

<dl><dd><i>Supervisor: <b><a href="/wiki/User:Mith" title="User:Mith">Mith</a></b></i></dd></dl>

The aim of the Dates is to include all the relevant information on calendars, years and day *within* the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium"). This includes:

- To create a page for every single year of every single [age](/wiki/Ages_of_the_Children_of_Il%C3%BAvatar "Ages of the Children of Ilúvatar") for which we have historical information;
- To bring the existing pages up to standard - especially extending the information on existing calendars;
- To make sure that, where known, all characters, events and locations have relevant dates included;
    - Within [Hobbits](/wiki/Hobbits) and [The Shire](/wiki/The_Shire), making sure that [Shire-reckoning](/wiki/Shire_Calendar "Shire Calendar") is correctly converted to [T.A.](/wiki/Third_Age "Third Age")/[Fo.A.](/wiki/Fourth_Age "Fourth Age");
- Finishing the [Timeline](/wiki/Timeline) and creating other timelines;
- Converting dates in the Gregorian calendar to the various [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) [calendars](/wiki/Category:Calendars "Category:Calendars") correctly.
- Adding images to date articles using [t:event card](/wiki/Template:Event_card "Template:Event card"), identify a way to incorporate these cards into the [Main Page](/wiki/Main_Page).

## Images {#Images}

<dl><dd><i>Supervisor: <b><a href="/wiki/User:KingAragorn" title="User:KingAragorn">KingAragorn</a></b></i></dd></dl>

The goal of the project is to gain permission and upload every piece of Tolkien imagery ever created. From the films to games to 3D illustrations if it is related to Tolkien, it belongs here. Specific aims include:

- Have the largest collection of Tolkien inspired artwork on the world wide web.
- Wherever possible, upload at least one image per article.
- Upload more, higher quality, pictures from [*The Lord of the Rings* (film series)](</wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_(film_series)> "The Lord of the Rings (film series)") and [*The Hobbit* (film series)](</wiki/The_Hobbit_(film_series)> "The Hobbit (film series)").
- <s>Obtain the permission of <a href="/wiki/Ted_Nasmith" title="Ted Nasmith">Ted Nasmith</a>, <a href="/wiki/Alan_Lee" title="Alan Lee">Alan Lee</a>, and <a href="/wiki/John_Howe" title="John Howe">John Howe</a> to host their artwork on Tolkien Gatgeway.</s>
    - <s>Upload all of Ted Nasmith&#39;s artwork.</s>
    - Upload all of Alan Lee's artwork.
    - <s>Upload all of John Howe&#39;s artwork.</s>
- Obtain the permission of artists, such as those on <a class="external text" href="http://deviantart.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">DeviantART</a>, to host their work on Tolkien Gateway.
- Upload all images of all cards from *[The Lord of the Rings Trading Card Game](/wiki/The_Lord_of_the_Rings_Trading_Card_Game)*, including descriptions from <a class="external text" href="http://lotrtcgdb.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">LOTR TCG Database</a>.

## Locations {#Locations}

<dl><dd><i>Supervisor: <b><a href="/wiki/User:KingAragorn" title="User:KingAragorn">KingAragorn</a></b></i></dd></dl>

The goal of the project is to create an article for every single location mentioned in J.R.R. Tolkien's works. Further aims:

- To expand the [Location Portal](/wiki/Portal:Locations "Portal:Locations") to allow users to browse the locations of Arda using maps.
- To fix [Google Maps for Arda](/wiki/User:Eldarion_Telcontar/Google_Maps_for_Arda "User:Eldarion Telcontar/Google Maps for Arda"), or to develop a new extension which allows for the addition of different maps using different layers.
- Create an article which details Tolkien-related locations around Oxford and nearby areas.
- Wherever possible, to include images and maps on all location articles.
- To eradicate all red links from [Index:Locations](/wiki/Index:Locations).
- To ensure that all location articles are fully referenced.

## Music {#Music}

<dl><dd><i>Supervisor: <b><a href="/wiki/User:Hands_of_a_healer" title="User:Hands of a healer">Hands of a healer</a></b></i></dd></dl>

The goal of the project is to create an article for every song and every band/singer who has sung about something inspired by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) and his works. Concentration will be on lyrics, and various other aspects of the information and how it relates to Tolkien. Further aims:

- Verifying and adding [citations](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Manual_of_Style#References_and_notes "Tolkien Gateway:Manual of Style") as needed
- [Rewriting](/wiki/Category:Articles_to_be_rewritten "Category:Articles to be rewritten") articles that are largely [plagiarized](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway:Manual_of_Style#Plagiarism "Tolkien Gateway:Manual of Style")
- Updating [artist](/wiki/Category:Musicians "Category:Musicians")/[band](/wiki/Category:Bands "Category:Bands") information with recent activities
- Adding and updating [infoboxes](/wiki/Category:Infobox_templates "Category:Infobox templates") with as much relevant information as possible
