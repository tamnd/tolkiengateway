<p>Tolkien Gateway is an encyclopedia about <a href="/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien" title="J.R.R. Tolkien">Tolkien</a> that <a href="/wiki/Help:Editing" title="Help:Editing">anyone can edit</a>. Since our launch on <a href="/wiki/3_June" title="3 June">3 June</a> <a href="/wiki/2005" title="2005">2005</a>, we have grown to include <a href="/wiki/Special:Statistics" title="Special:Statistics">13,644</a> articles and <a href="/wiki/Special:NewFiles" title="Special:NewFiles">17,789</a> images on a range of Tolkien-related topics.
</p><p>This Community Portal is designed to help editors find something to work on. It covers a range of ways you can share your Tolkien knowledge with the world including <a href="/wiki/Special:WantedPages" title="Special:WantedPages">creating wanted pages</a>, expanding <a href="/wiki/Category:Stubs" title="Category:Stubs">stub articles</a>, <a href="/wiki/Category:Cleanup" title="Category:Cleanup">cleaning up poorly-written pages</a> and added references to <a href="/wiki/Category:Articles_needing_citation" title="Category:Articles needing citation">articles in need of citations</a>. Some of the work might be a little tedious, but a little goes a long way in continuing to build the world&#39;s largest Tolkien encyclopedia.
</p>
