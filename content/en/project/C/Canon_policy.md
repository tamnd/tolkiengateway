**Canon policy** is [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md)'s policy on how to deal with conflicting [canon](../../main/C/Canon.md). The current policy was summarised by [User:Mith](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith):

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p><b>It is not - and can not - be our place to decide what is canon and what is not</b>. This incurs too many value judgements on our part and opens us up to too much criticism. Certain things are canonical, certain things are not, and there is a lot of grey. The best we can do is admit the situation is grey. For articles in the grey we can have a &#34;Canonicity&#34; section. And that could list, dispassionately, arguments for and against.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Mith" title="User:Mith">User:Mith</a>, <a href="../M/Meetings%252F1_July_2012%252FTranscript.md" title="Tolkien Gateway:Meetings/1 July 2012/Transcript">July 2012</a></cite></p>
</blockquote>

## Background {#Background}

Canon is a concept which cannot be uniformly applied to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [legendarium](../../main/L/Legendarium.md). As [Wayne G. Hammond](../../main/W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md) put it,

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>[...] there are Tolkien&#39;s latest thoughts, his best thoughts, and his published thoughts and these are not necessarily the same.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite><a href="../../main/W/Wayne_G._Hammond.md" title="Wayne G. Hammond">Wayne G. Hammond</a> paraphrasing Jeremy Marshal in <i><a href="../../main/T/Tolkien&#39;s_Legendarium.md" title="Tolkien&#39;s Legendarium">Tolkien&#39;s Legendarium: Essays on The History of Middle-earth</a></i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup></cite></p>
</blockquote>

A consistent Tolkien "[canon](../../main/C/Canon.md)" could only exist if a group or an individual decided what should be considered "canon" and what should not. For [Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md) editors to make such judgements would be for them to project their own views on such matters, thus violating the principles of an encyclopaedia. The search of objectivity can only reveal that Tolkien's thoughts on particular concepts changed over the course time for various reasons.

There are many famous inconsistencies in Tolkien's personal writings. For instance, while working on his legendarium Tolkien considered two very different accounts of the origins of [Celeborn](../../main/C/Celeborn.md): in one version he was a [kinsman](../../main/S/Sindar.md) of [Thingol](../../main/T/Thingol.md) living in [Doriath](../../main/D/Doriath.md), where he met [Galadriel](../../main/G/Galadriel.md);<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup> in another writing, Celeborn was a [Teler](../../main/F/Falmari.md) of [Alqualondë](../../main/A/Alqualondë.md) where he met Galadriel.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup> Since neither of those versions were established in his published works during Tolkien's lifetime, Tolkien Gateway should favour neither version of events and both should be presented and explained to the reader.

## Policy {#Policy}

[Tolkien Gateway](../../main/T/Tolkien_Gateway.md)'s current canon policy was decided at [a community meeting in July 2012](../M/Meetings%252F1_July_2012.md):

- **Tolkien Gateway editors should not judge what is canon and what is not.**
- While the "History" section of an article combines a more coherent narrative, the early or contradictory versions of the subject are discussed on "Other versions of the legendarium" section.
- Only concepts not created by Tolkien (i.e. those created for adaptations) should be considered "non-canon". [Template:Adaptation](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Template:Adaptation) should be applied to these articles.

## Former policy {#Former_policy}

It should be noted that the Tolkien Gateway's former policy was to attempt to reconcile inconsistencies in order to produce a consistent canon.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup> The content of many articles will still reflect this old policy whilst they await a [rewrite](../../category/A/Articles_to_be_rewritten.md).
