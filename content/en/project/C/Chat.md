Tolkien Gateway has its own **chat platform**, held on a <b><a class="external text" href="http://discord.gg/ktX6fhU9FY" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Discord Server</a></b>. <a class="external text" href="http://discord.gg/ktX6fhU9FY" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"><img src="https://discordapp.com/api/guilds/919188348319444993/widget.png" alt="widget.png"/></a>

It is currently open to editors as well as members of the public who wish to help contribute to the wiki, being a place to discuss all matters regarding the wiki and its contents. Wiki talk pages are still useful for matters worthy of being registered, but many discussions, updates, and suggestions are regularly posted on the Discord server.

**Please**, take into account that the primary focus of the server is the Tolkien Gateway wiki and its upkeep; while Tolkien-related conversation is acceptable in the proper channels on the server, those *only* wishing to have non-wiki-related discussions regarding Tolkien and his works should seek out an appropriate forum for this purpose.

As a general rule, users of the Discord server should follow the same [behavior guidelines](../P/Policy.md#Behavior_guidelines) as the wiki:

- Be respectful
- Avoid profanity
- No personal attacks (and move personal debates elsewhere)
- No legal threats
- Be accountable
- No offensive usernames
- Do not bite the newcomers
