[Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway)'s **official chat** or **IRC** (Internet Relay Chat) was an old platform where any editor, user, or members of the public could chat with other like-minded folk about [Tolkien Gateway](/wiki/Tolkien_Gateway), [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien), or anything they wish. The purpose of the IRC was to enable editors to discuss Tolkien Gateway as well as meet fellow Tolkien enthusiasts. As the active users of the wiki decreased, the chat became eventually obsolete and its platform was never updated.

Sign-ups were not required nor do users need to install anything as they can access the chat via Mibbit. <b><a class="external text" href="http://widget.mibbit.com/?server=irc.tolkiengateway.net&amp;channel=%23TolkienGateway" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">To chat now, click here</a></b> or read the instructions below.

### Mibbit {#Mibbit}

<dl><dd><i><a class="external text" href="http://www.mibbit.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Official Mibbit website</a></i></dd></dl>

The easiest way to connect to our IRC was via the online client **Mibbit**. Mibbit required no installation and works on all major Internet browsers, mobiles phones, and even on the Wii's Internet Browser. Simply clicking the link and entering a desired username took you to straight to the channel. <a class="external text" href="http://wbe04.mibbit.com/?server=irc.tolkiengateway.net&amp;channel=%23TolkienGateway" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Join Tolkien Gateway&#39;s IRC via Mibbit now.</a>

### ChatZilla {#ChatZilla}

<dl><dd><i><a class="external text" href="http://chatzilla.hacksrus.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Official ChatZilla website</a></i></dd></dl>

For those who use the Mozilla Firefox browser, a popular choice was the ChatZilla extension which could be used as either a tab in Firefox, or an extra window. It provided greater functionality than Mibbit and, once installed, was easily accessed via Firefox and could be configured to automatically join <a class="external text" href="irc://irc.tolkiengateway.net" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">irc://irc.tolkiengateway.net</a> on opening.

### X-Chat {#X-Chat}

<dl><dd><i><a class="external text" href="http://www.xchat.org" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Official X-Chat website</a></i></dd></dl>

**X-Chat** was an open source IRC client for Linux, Windows and Mac based operating systems. X-Chat could be scripted with plug-ins in a number of programming languages: Perl, Python, Tcl, and Ruby, which were available as external plugins and contain a binary module interface, usually for the C programming language.

On Linux, X-Chat was available through the package managers of most distributions. On Debian-based systems, such as Ubuntu, simply type *sudo apt-get install xchat* in a terminal.

On Windows, the official xchat programme was shareware and required a fee of $20(USD) after its trial period of 30 days. However, there were a number of unofficial builds of xchat which were free for anyone to download. <a class="external text" href="http://silenceisdefeat.org/~b0at/xchat/win32/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Click here for a full list of these unofficial builds.</a> The most popular of these builds was <a class="external text" href="http://www.silverex.info/news/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">SilvereX&#39;s build.</a>
