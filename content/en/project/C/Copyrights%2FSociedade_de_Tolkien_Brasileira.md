FROM Daniel Cossi Jundiaí, SP, Brasil 17th, February, 2012

To Tolkien Gateway

To Whom It may Concern:

My name is Daniel Cossi, and I am hereby providing written consent for authorizing Tolkien Gateway to release and show the Logo and information about Sociedade de Tolkien Brasileira.

I am issuing this letter for granting access to any of the information approved and presented on this website, like our History, Works, Social Midia Links and other information relating to the Sociedade de Tolkien Brasileira which I am aware of its content since 16th February, 2012. The purpose for this access should be limited to the sole purpose of information and spreading the works of STB about J.R.R. Tolkien.

However, I notify that this disclosure of STB information should not be for any other purpose other than this.

Daniel Cossi Sociedade de Tolkien Brasileira Presiden-Founder 17th, February, 2012
