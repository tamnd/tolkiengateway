## Summary {#Summary}

*"Beleriand and Realms of the North"*

Map rendering by [Karolina Węgrzyn](/wiki/User:Sirielle "User:Sirielle"), with [Thangorodrim](/wiki/Thangorodrim), compass rose and [tengwar](/wiki/Tengwar "Tengwar") frame by <a class="external text" href="http://ominously.deviantart.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ominous</a>. Published on-line with <a class="external text" href="http://ominously.deviantart.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ominous</a>'s permission. Based on on maps by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien "Tolkien") in *[HoME XI](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels "The War of the Jewels")*, [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* &amp; [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/Atlas_of_Middle-earth "Atlas of Middle-earth")*, and tips from <a class="external text" href="http://ominously.deviantart.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ominous</a>, without whom the map would be incomplete.
