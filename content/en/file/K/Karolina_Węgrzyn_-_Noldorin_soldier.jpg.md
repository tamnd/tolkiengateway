## Summary {#Summary}

<i><b>Ohtar Noldorinwa (Noldorin Soldier)</b></i>

Nightfall. [Aman](/wiki/Aman) is covered in darkness, the Trees are gone. Fëanorians are approaching [Alqualondë](/wiki/Alqualond%C3%AB)... Noldorin soldier, one of those who followed [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) into exile. [Noldor](/wiki/Noldor), the greatest of the [Elves](/wiki/Elves) in matters of lore and craft. Craftsmen, loremasters, rebellers, kinslayers...
