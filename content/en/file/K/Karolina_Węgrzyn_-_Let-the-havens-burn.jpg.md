<i><b>Let the havens burn</b></i>

[Maedhros](/wiki/Maedhros) son of [Fëanor](/wiki/F%C3%ABanor) arriving at the [Havens of Sirion](/wiki/Havens_of_Sirion):

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Now when first the tidings came to <a href="/wiki/Maedhros" title="Maedhros">Maedhros</a> that <a href="/wiki/Elwing" title="Elwing">Elwing</a> yet lived, and dwelt in possession of the <a href="/wiki/Silmarils" title="Silmarils">Silmaril</a> by the mouths of Sirion, he repenting of the deeds in <a href="/wiki/Doriath" title="Doriath">Doriath</a> withheld his hand. But in time the knowledge of their oath unfulfilled returned to torment him and his brothers, and gathering from their wandering hunting-paths they sent messages to the Havens of friendship and yet of stern demand. Then Elwing and the people of Sirion would not yield the jewel which <a href="/wiki/Beren" title="Beren">Beren</a> had won and <a href="/wiki/L%C3%BAthien" title="Lúthien">Lúthien</a> had worn, and for which <a href="/wiki/Dior" title="Dior">Dior</a> the fair was slain; and least of all while <a href="/wiki/E%C3%A4rendil" title="Eärendil">Eärendil</a> their lord was on the sea, for it seemed to them that in the Silmaril lay the healing and the blessing that had come upon their houses and their ships. And so there came to pass the last and cruellest of the slayings of Elf by Elf; and that was the third of the great wrongs achieved by the accursed oath.
</p>
