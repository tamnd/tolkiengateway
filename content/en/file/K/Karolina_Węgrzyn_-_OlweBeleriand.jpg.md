## Summary {#Summary}

<i><b>Olwë in Beleriand</b></i>

The Age of Stars, Sun yet has to be made. [Olwë](../../main/O/Olwë.md) in [Beleriand](../../main/B/Beleriand.md), looking across the sea towards the Undying Lands. Soon [Teleri](../../main/T/Teleri.md) will follow West.

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Two lords they had, for their numbers were very great: <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/E/Elwë.md" title="Elwë">Elwë</a> <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/S/Singollo.md" title="Singollo">Singollo</a>, which signifies <a class="mw-redirect" href="../../main/G/Greymantle.md" title="Greymantle">Greymantle</a>, and Olwë his brother. The hair of Olwë was long and white, and his eyes were blue; but the hair of Elwë was grey as silver, and his eyes were as stars; he was the tallest of all the Elven-folk.
</p>
