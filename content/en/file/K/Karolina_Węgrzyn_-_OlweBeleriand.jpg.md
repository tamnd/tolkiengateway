## Summary {#Summary}

<i><b>Olwë in Beleriand</b></i>

The Age of Stars, Sun yet has to be made. [Olwë](/wiki/Olw%C3%AB) in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand), looking across the sea towards the Undying Lands. Soon [Teleri](/wiki/Teleri) will follow West.

<p>Two lords they had, for their numbers were very great: <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Elw%C3%AB" title="Elwë">Elwë</a> <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Singollo" title="Singollo">Singollo</a>, which signifies <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/Greymantle" title="Greymantle">Greymantle</a>, and Olwë his brother. The hair of Olwë was long and white, and his eyes were blue; but the hair of Elwë was grey as silver, and his eyes were as stars; he was the tallest of all the Elven-folk.
