## Summary {#Summary}

*A Meal of Cram - Dwarves pause on a journey*, by [Kay Woollard](/wiki/Kay_Woollard), one of the illustrations made for Peter Bolton.

This is part of a diptych: the other piece, depicting Hobbits having a picnic, and called **A meal of pie**, forming a strong contrast with it. The two pieces were separated by the purchase of the second one, and a photo of the second one is not available.
