## Summary {#Summary}

Every year about the time of Bilbo's birthday (September 22), the Tolkien Society organises a weekend event in Oxford, called Oxonmoot. The event culmintates with a moment of respect and reflection on the Sunday in front of Tolkien's grave at Wolvercote cemetery, North Oxford. Here Kay is seen laying a wreath on behalf of Amon Sûl Smial. This photo was taken by Charles Noad in the early 1980s.
