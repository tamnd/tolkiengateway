## Summary {#Summary}

*"Gondolin"*

Gondolin in a fantasy map style by [Karolina Węgrzyn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Sirielle), colored version 2022. Done with Tolkien lore support from <a class="external text" href="http://ominously.deviantart.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ominous</a> and [M.L.](../../main/M/Michał_Leśniewski.md). A result of guessing and constructing based on descriptions from *[The Fall of Gondolin](<../../main/T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)* in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](../../main/T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md)* and the city map from the *[Atlas of Middle-earth](../../main/A/Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)*. Closeups and other variants, with more detailed description of choices made while constructing this map can be found in <a class="external text" href="https://sirielle.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the maps section sirielle.com</a>.

There are also a variations of this map at Tolkien Gateway in the *Category: Images by Karolina Węgrzyn*.
