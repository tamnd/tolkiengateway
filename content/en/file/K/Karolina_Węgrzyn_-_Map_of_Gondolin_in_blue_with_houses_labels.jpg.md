## Summary {#Summary}

*"Gondolin"*

Map by [Karolina Węgrzyn](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/User:Sirielle), with blue coloring and a possible placement of the Houses added. Done again with Tolkien lore support from [Ominous](../../main/J/Jakub_Tymiński.md) and [M.L.](../../main/M/Michał_Leśniewski.md), but this time in large amount it is result of guessing and constructing based on descriptions from *[The Fall of Gondolin](<../../main/T/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter).md>)* in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](../../main/T/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two.md)* and the city map from the *[Atlas of Middle-earth](../../main/A/Atlas_of_Middle-earth.md)*. Especially the placements of the houses are artist's choice, apart the [House of the Harp](../../main/H/House_of_the_Harp.md) and the [House of the King](../../main/H/House_of_the_King.md). More detailed description of choices made is in the <a class="external text" href="https://sirielle.deviantart.com/art/Gondolin-in-blue-more-images-in-description-717296613" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">map&#39;s description at deviantART</a>.

There are is also a variation of this <a class="external text" href="http://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Karolina_Węgrzyn_-_Map_of_Gondolin_in_blue_with_English_labels.jpg" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">map without the houses labeled</a> and the <a class="external text" href="http://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Karolina_Węgrzyn_-_Map_of_Gondolin_in_blue_city_center.jpg" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">city center close up</a>.
