## Summary {#Summary}

The scene describes three members of the staff of a famous smial sharing midday dinner together. From left to right, they are Gaffer Greenthumb, the Gardener, and his friends the Chief Cook and the Head Butler, who regales his friends with the latest misdemeanours of their employers, causing much mirth. The scene is situated near the main garden shed. *The artwork is © Kay Woollard and property of Denis Bridoux*
