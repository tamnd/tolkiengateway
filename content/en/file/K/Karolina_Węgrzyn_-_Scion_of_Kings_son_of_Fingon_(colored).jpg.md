## Summary {#Summary}

Ereinion [Gil-galad](../../main/G/Gil-galad.md).While in the published *[Silmarillion](../../main/S/Silmarillion.md)* he is son of [Fingon](../../main/F/Fingon.md), JRRT's final idea was to make him the son of [Orodreth](../../main/O/Orodreth.md) (who himself got a generation younger and became son of [Angrod](../../main/A/Angrod.md), instead of being his brother). This is rotated and colored version of the original b&amp;w illustration (visible below) to a polemics on his parentage in the jubilee 20th issue of the almanac [Aiglos](<../../main/A/Aiglos_(journal).md>) (a Polish language issue). Done in Painter &amp; Photoshop 2015, colored in 2019.
