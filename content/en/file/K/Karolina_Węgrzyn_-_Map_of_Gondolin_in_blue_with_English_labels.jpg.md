## Summary {#Summary}

*"Gondolin"*

Map rendering by [Karolina Węgrzyn](/wiki/User:Sirielle "User:Sirielle"), with blue coloring. Done again with Tolkien lore support from <a class="external text" href="http://ominously.deviantart.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ominous</a> and [M.L.](/wiki/Micha%C5%82_Le%C5%9Bniewski "Michał Leśniewski"), but this time in large amount it is result of guessing and constructing based on descriptions from *[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")* in *[The Book of Lost Tales Part Two](/wiki/The_Book_of_Lost_Tales_Part_Two)* and the city map from the *[Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/Atlas_of_Middle-earth)*. Closeups and other variants, with more detailed description of choices made while constructing this map can be found in <a class="external text" href="https://sirielle.com" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">the maps section sirielle.com</a>.

There are also a variations of this map at Tolkien Gateway in the *Category: Images by Karolina Węgrzyn*.
