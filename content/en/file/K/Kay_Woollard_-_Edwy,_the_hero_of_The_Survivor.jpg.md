## Summary {#Summary}

Edwy, the hero of [Kay Woollard](/wiki/Kay_Woollard)'s *The Survivor* (included in [*Mallorn* 25](/wiki/Mallorn_25 "Mallorn 25")). "To the help of King Arvedui they sent some archers who never returned; and others went also to the battle in which Angmar was overthrown." (Appendix A)
