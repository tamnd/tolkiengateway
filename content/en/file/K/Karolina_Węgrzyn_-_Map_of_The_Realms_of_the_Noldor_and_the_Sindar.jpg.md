## Summary {#Summary}

*"The Realms of the Noldor and the Sindar"* in [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) in the beginning of the [I Age of Sun](/wiki/First_Age "First Age") and during the [Siege of Angband](/wiki/Siege_of_Angband). This map follows [HoME XII](/wiki/The_Peoples_of_Middle-earth "The Peoples of Middle-earth"), in the published *[Silmarillion](/wiki/Silmarillion)* [Amrod](/wiki/Amrod) and [Amras](/wiki/Amras) would share the same realm.

Map rendering by [Karolina Węgrzyn](/wiki/User:Sirielle "User:Sirielle"), based on on maps by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Tolkien "Tolkien") in [HoME XI](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels "The War of the Jewels"), [Christopher Tolkien](/wiki/Christopher_Tolkien) in *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* &amp; [Karen Wynn Fonstad](/wiki/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad) in *[The Atlas of Middle-earth](/wiki/Atlas_of_Middle-earth "Atlas of Middle-earth")*, and tips from <a class="external text" href="http://ominously.deviantart.com/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Ominous</a>, without whom the map would be incomplete.
