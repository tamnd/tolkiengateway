## Summary {#Summary}

Cropped from [File:Sage - Arda in the Third Age.png](Sage_-_Arda_in_the_Third_Age.png.md) in order to depict the CONTINENT of Middle-earth. All copyrights, permissions and rules that apply to the original file, also apply to this.

The western part are the well-documented [Westlands](../../main/W/Westlands.md). The eastern and southern parts of the continent are not described in [canon](../../main/C/Canon.md); [Karen Fonstad](../../main/K/Karen_Fonstad.md) fleshed them out rendering this [early map sketch](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Ambarkanta_Map_V.png.md) by Tolkien.
