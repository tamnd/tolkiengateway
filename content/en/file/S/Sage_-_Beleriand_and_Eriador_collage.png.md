## Summary {#Summary}

Collage of maps (using that of [Stephen Raw](../../main/S/Stephen_Raw.md) and [Karen Wynn Fonstad](../../main/K/Karen_Wynn_Fonstad.md)'s outlines) to show how Fonstad envisioned the location of [Beleriand](../../main/B/Beleriand.md) (shown submerged) relative to that of [Lindon](../../main/L/Lindon.md)/[Eriador](../../main/E/Eriador.md).
