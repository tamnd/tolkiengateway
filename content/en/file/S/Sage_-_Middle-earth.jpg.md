## Summary {#Summary}

Collage of the maps by Fonstad, edited out all names and letters and other changes to tie the maps together. The Shire is a collage from the regional shire map also by Fonstad, but not very visible in that resolution. Some elements from the original maps of CT were used. The islands were placed according to their respective locations of Beleriand.

AFAIK it's the only map that incorporates the uttermost north of Middle-earth provided in *[The Treason of Isengard](../../main/T/The_Treason_of_Isengard.md), and the only who includes all 3 islands off the coast of M-e and also Harad.*
