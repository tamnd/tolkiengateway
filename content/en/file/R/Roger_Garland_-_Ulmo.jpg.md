## Summary {#Summary}

This painting depicts [The Lord of the Waters](../../main/U/Ulmo.md) appearing in majesty to [Tuor](../../main/T/Tuor.md). Garland brilliantly uses cool colors to emphasize the fact that Ulmo seldom left his watery home, even to join the [Valar](../../main/V/Valar.md) in their councils.
