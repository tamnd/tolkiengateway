## Summary {#Summary}

- **3** – This rating simply means canon except where contradicted elsewhere. It is a neutral. *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion)* as a whole falls into this category. Individual parts of the Silmarillion that are not contradicted elsewhere are **4**s; those contradicted later are **2**s.
