## Summary {#Summary}

My charcoal drawing of the [Barrow-wight](/wiki/Barrow-wights "Barrow-wights"). I really don't prefer charcol as a medium, but I felt in this case it would work best. And no, those aren't neccessarily wings. They are more the wreath of shadow that it can wrap around itself, to cover the pale, bony arms and torso. The head is intentionally indistinct. Just did it less than an hour ago.
