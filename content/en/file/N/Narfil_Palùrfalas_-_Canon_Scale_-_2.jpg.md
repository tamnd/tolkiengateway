## Summary {#Summary}

- **2** – This rating means generally not accepted as canon. On subjects as a whole (such as *[The Fall of Gondolin](</wiki/The_Fall_of_Gondolin_(chapter)> "The Fall of Gondolin (chapter)")*), it means that the details should not be accepted as solid fact, even where not contradicted elsewhere. This mostly is composed of Tolkien’s earlier writings. Found in this category would be such articles as *[Orcobal](/wiki/Orcobal)*.
