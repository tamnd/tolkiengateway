## Summary {#Summary}

A screenshot from the extended edition of The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey. In TA 2894, four-year-old Bilbo Baggins was given a toy sword at the 104th birthday party of his grandfather, The Old Took. This screenshot is in the moment after he swings it at the wizard Gandalf playfully before his mother, Belladonna, stops him and greets Gandalf as an old friend.
