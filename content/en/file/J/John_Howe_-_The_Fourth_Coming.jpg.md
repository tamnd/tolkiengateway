## Summary {#Summary}

Art by [John Howe](/wiki/John_Howe) for France Telecom about the RPG The 4th Coming. Wrongly used in the [fandom](/wiki/Fandom "Fandom") as a representation of the [Dagor Dagorath](/wiki/Dagor_Dagorath), while it resembles more the [War of Wrath](/wiki/War_of_Wrath).
