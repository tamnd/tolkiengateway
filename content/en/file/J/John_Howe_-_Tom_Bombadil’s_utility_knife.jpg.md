<p>Concept art of <a href="/wiki/Tom_Bombadil" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom Bombadil</a>&#39;s utility knife for a <a class="new" href="/w/index.php?title=Syzygy_Forge&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Syzygy Forge (page does not exist)">Syzygy Forge</a> statue.

<div class="tg-quote-container"><div class="tg-section-divider"><span>”</span></div><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r395753">.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider{margin-top:1rem;margin-bottom:0.5rem;color:var(--tg-color-subtle);display:flex;align-items:center;justify-content:space-between}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider:not(:empty){gap:1rem;white-space:nowrap}.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::before,.mw-parser-output .tg-section-divider::after{content:"";height:1px;width:100%;background-color:var(--tg-border-color-base)}</style><blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Obviously, <a href="/wiki/Tom_Bombadil" title="Tom Bombadil">Tom</a> has little need for a weapon, but he may still want something handy for peeling an apple now and then. The blade isn’t meant to feel like a conventional weapon. There’s a subtle elegance to it, but it doesn’t quite resemble the craftsmanship of <a href="/wiki/Elves" title="Elves">Elves</a> or <a href="/wiki/Dwarves" title="Dwarves">Dwarves</a> either. It feels fitting for someone as ancient and mysterious as Tom.
</p>
<p class="tg-quote-footer"><cite>Jordan Costner, Creative Director at Syzygy Forge</cite></p>
