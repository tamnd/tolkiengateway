<td class="description">
<p>This <a href="/wiki/N%C3%BAmen%C3%B3reans" title="Númenóreans">Númenórean</a> tile would have been among such precious artifacts to be saved from the <a href="/wiki/Downfall_of_N%C3%BAmenor" title="Downfall of Númenor">Downfall of Númenor</a> in the ships of <a href="/wiki/Elendil" title="Elendil">Elendil</a> and his sons <a href="/wiki/Isildur" title="Isildur">Isildur</a> and <a href="/wiki/An%C3%A1rion" title="Anárion">Anárion</a> and brought to <a href="/wiki/Middle-earth" title="Middle-earth">Middle-earth</a>, as is told in the <a href="/wiki/Akallab%C3%AAth" title="Akallabêth">Akallabêth</a> (<a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion" title="The Silmarillion">The Silmarillion</a>). This Numenorean tile was used as the centerpiece on the back of the cover of <a class="mw-redirect" href="/wiki/The_J._R._R._Tolkien_Calendar_1974" title="The J. R. R. Tolkien Calendar 1974">The J. R. R. Tolkien Calendar 1974</a> and also in <a href="/wiki/The_Silmarillion_Calendar_1978" title="The Silmarillion Calendar 1978">The Silmarillion Calendar 1978</a>.
