## Summary {#Summary}

Cf. <a class="external text" href="https://tai.tolkienists.org/tai/32/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Tolkien Art Index #32</a>. Published in:

- *[The J.R.R. Tolkien Calendar 1974](/wiki/The_J.R.R._Tolkien_Calendar_1974)* ([1973](/wiki/1973)), back cover
- *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)* ([1995](/wiki/1995)), p. 194, #191
- *[Pictures by J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/Pictures_by_J.R.R._Tolkien)* (second edition, [2021](/wiki/2021)), #47
- *[The Silmarillion](/wiki/The_Silmarillion): Illustrated by J.R.R. Tolkien* ([2022](/wiki/2022)), pp. 36, 63

Inscription in the manuscript says "Winged Sun / House of Finwë". Notice this device was set in a square, unlike [the version in a lozenge](</wiki/File:J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Finw%C3%AB_heraldic_device_(II).jpg> "File:J.R.R. Tolkien - Finwë heraldic device (II).jpg").
