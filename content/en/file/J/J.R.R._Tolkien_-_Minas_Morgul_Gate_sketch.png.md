<p>Minas Morgul must be made more horrible. The usual &#34;goblin&#34; stuff is not good enough here. The Gate shaped like a gaping mouth with teeth and a window like an eye on each side. As S[am]. passes through he feels a horrible shudder. There are <a href="/wiki/Silent_Watchers" title="Silent Watchers">two silent shapes</a> sitting on either side as sentinels. Substitute something of the foll.[owing] sort for p.
