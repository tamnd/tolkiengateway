Sketch of the [Minas Morgul](../../main/M/Minas_Morgul.md) gate by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)

<p>Minas Morgul must be made more horrible. The usual &#34;goblin&#34; stuff is not good enough here. The Gate shaped like a gaping mouth with teeth and a window like an eye on each side.
