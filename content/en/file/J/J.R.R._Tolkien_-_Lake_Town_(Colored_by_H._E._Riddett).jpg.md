## Summary {#Summary}

Originally by [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md), the colour was added by [H.E. Riddett](../../main/H/H.E._Riddett.md) and was made for [The Hobbit Calendar 1976](../../main/T/The_Hobbit_Calendar_1976.md), and has been used in some illustrated editions of the book.
