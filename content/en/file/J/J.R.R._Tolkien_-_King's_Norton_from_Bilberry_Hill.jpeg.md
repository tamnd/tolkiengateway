## Summary {#Summary}

[J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) painted this watercolour on [8 July](/wiki/8_July) [1913](/wiki/1913) at Bilberry Hill.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> The painting was published posthumously as figure 16 on page 21 of *[J.R.R. Tolkien: Artist and Illustrator](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien:_Artist_and_Illustrator)*.

The [front cover](/wiki/File:The_Bovadium_Fragments.jpg "File:The Bovadium Fragments.jpg") of *[The Bovadium Fragments: together with The Origin of Bovadium](/wiki/The_Bovadium_Fragments:_together_with_The_Origin_of_Bovadium)* was designed by Emily Langford and based on *King's Norton from Bilberry Hill*.
