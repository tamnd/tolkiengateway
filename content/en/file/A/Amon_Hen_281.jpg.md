## Summary {#Summary}

Cover art is "[Fangorn and Fimbrethil](../K/Kay_Woollard_-_Fangorn_and_Fimbrethil.jpg.md)" by [Kay Woollard](../../main/K/Kay_Woollard.md).

The artwork was painted on buff paper, but the original reproduction on the magazine cover overemphasized the background.

At the time, the illustration was reproduced as *Amon Hen* cover by its owner without asking for copyright permission. As a consequence the artwork was not ready for publication as it stood.
