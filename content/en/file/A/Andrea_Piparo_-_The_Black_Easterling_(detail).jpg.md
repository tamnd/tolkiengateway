"[The Black Easterling](../../main/K/Khamûl.md)" by [Andrea Piparo](../../category/I/Images_by_Andrea_Piparo.md) for *[War of the Ring: The Card Game](../../main/W/War_of_the_Ring%253A_The_Card_Game.md)*.
