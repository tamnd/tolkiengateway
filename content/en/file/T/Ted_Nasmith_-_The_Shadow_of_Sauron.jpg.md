A painting depicting the [Shadow](../../main/S/Shadow.md) of [Sauron](../../main/S/Sauron.md) after the destruction of the [One Ring](../../main/O/One_Ring.md). Note that the Shadow bears some similarities to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md)'s [unfinished sketch](../J/J.R.R._Tolkien_-_Sauron.jpg.md) of Sauron which Mr. Nasmith possibly used as inspiration.
