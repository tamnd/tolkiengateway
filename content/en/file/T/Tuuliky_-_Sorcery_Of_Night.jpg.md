## Summary {#Summary}

<div class="poem">
<p><i>A night there was when winter died;</i><br/>
<i>then all alone she sang and cried</i><br/>
<i>and danced until the dawn of spring,</i><br/>
<i>and chanted some wild magic thing</i><br/>
<i>that stirred him, till it sudden broke</i><br/>
<i>the bonds that held him, and he woke</i><br/>
<i>to madness sweet and brave despair...</i>
</p>
