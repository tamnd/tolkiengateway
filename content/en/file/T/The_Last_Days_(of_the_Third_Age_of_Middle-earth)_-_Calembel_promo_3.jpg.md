## Summary {#Summary}

Promotional screenshot of [Calembel](/wiki/Calembel) from *[The Last Days (of the Third Age of Middle-earth)](</wiki/The_Last_Days_(of_the_Third_Age_of_Middle-earth)>)*, a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeware" title="wikipedia:Freeware">freeware</a> [total-conversion mod](/wiki/Category:Modifications "Category:Modifications") for the games <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_%26_Blade" title="wikipedia:Mount &amp; Blade">Mount &amp; Blade</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_%26_Blade:_Warband" title="wikipedia:Mount &amp; Blade: Warband">Mount &amp; Blade: Warband</a></i>.

This screenshot depicts the appearance of Calembel in the current releases of the <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_%26_Blade" title="wikipedia:Mount &amp; Blade">Mount &amp; Blade</a></i> and <i><a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_%26_Blade:_Warband" title="wikipedia:Mount &amp; Blade: Warband">Mount &amp; Blade: Warband</a></i> versions of the mod.
