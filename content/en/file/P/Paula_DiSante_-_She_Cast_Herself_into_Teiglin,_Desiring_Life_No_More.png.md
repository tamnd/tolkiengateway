## Summary {#Summary}

"She Cast Herself into Teiglin, Desiring Life No More"

"\[B\]ut Brandir said: 'Hear me to the end! Níniel the beloved is also dead. She cast herself into Teiglin, desiring life no more; for she learned that she was none other than Nienor daughter of Húrin of Dor-lómin, ere her forgetfulness came upon her, and that Turambar was her brother, Túrin son of Húrin.'"

Digital art, Tiffany style.

\--The Silmarillion
