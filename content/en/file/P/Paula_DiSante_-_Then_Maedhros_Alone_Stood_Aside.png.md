hen Maedhros Alone Stood Aside (The Burning of the Ships at Losgar)

Digital art and Photoshop.

"Then Maedhros alone stood aside, but Fëanor caused fire to be set to the white ships of the Teleri. So in that place which was called Losgar at the outlet of the Firth of Drengist ended the fairest vessels that ever sailed the sea, in a great burning, bright and terrible."
