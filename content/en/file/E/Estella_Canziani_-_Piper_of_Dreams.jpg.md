## Summary {#Summary}

*Piper of Dreams* by <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estella_Canziani" title="wikipedia:Estella Canziani">Estella Canziani</a> (1887-1964). Of Italian descent, Canziani was educated and lived in England. She painted mainly wildlife and fairy scenes, as well as illustrating children's books and writing on Italian folklore and costumes. The Museum of London has examples of her work. <a class="external text" href="https://www.medici.co.uk/p/23600/Piper-of-Dreams---E-Canziani--Print" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Purchase image at the Medici Society</a>.
