## Summary {#Summary}

Reconstructed location of [Beleriand](/wiki/Beleriand) at the appropriate scale and position. The reconstructed map is based on a "collage" of several maps by J.R.R. Tolkien and Christopher Tolkien, basically:

- Second Map of Beleriand (with scale grid) from *[The War of the Jewels](/wiki/The_War_of_the_Jewels)*
- Draft Map of the lands of east of the Ered Luin (with scale grid) from *[The Treason of Isengard](/wiki/The_Treason_of_Isengard)*
- Map from *[Unfinished Tales](/wiki/Unfinished_Tales)*
