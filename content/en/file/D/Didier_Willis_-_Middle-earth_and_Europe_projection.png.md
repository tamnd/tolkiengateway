## Summary {#Summary}

According to *Letters* no 294 p. 376, [Hobbiton](/wiki/Hobbiton) and [Rivendell](/wiki/Rivendell) are approximately at the same latitude as [Oxford](/wiki/Oxford) (51-52°N), [Minas Tirith](/wiki/Minas_Tirith) at the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence" title="wikipedia:Florence">Florence</a> (43.7°N) and [Pelargir](/wiki/Pelargir) and the [mouths](/wiki/Ethir_Anduin "Ethir Anduin") of [Anduin](/wiki/Anduin) around the latitude of ancient <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troy" title="wikipedia:Troy">Troy</a> (39.9°N).

According to the annotations provided by [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) to [Pauline Baynes](/wiki/Pauline_Baynes) (*The Guardian*, 23 October 2015), Hobbiton is again approximately at the same latitude as Oxford, and Minas Tirith is about the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenna" title="wikipedia:Ravenna">Ravenna</a> (44.4°N). The bottom of the map is about the latitude of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem" title="wikipedia:Jerusalem">Jerusalem</a>, and [Umbar](/wiki/Umbar) about that of <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus" title="wikipedia:Cyprus">Cyprus</a>. Minas Tirith, being approximately 900 miles east of Hobbiton, is located near <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgrade" title="wikipedia:Belgrade">Belgrade</a> (20.4°E).

These pieces of information might be problematic (geodesic distances do not correspond), unless deformations due to the map projection are assumed.

The reconstructed map below therefore assumes a <a class="extiw" href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambert_conformal_conical_projection" title="wikipedia:Lambert conformal conical projection">Lambert conformal conical projection</a> for Europe (standard parallels 40°N and 56°N, central meridian 12.5°E). [Middle-earth](/wiki/Middle-earth) is then scaled so that to have Hobbiton at 51°N and Minas Tirith at 44°N,20.4°E. The projection "errors" are represented as dashed *horizontal* lines (ignoring the Earth's curvature).

Such a reconstruction remains experimental by nature, and the exercise is limited: Hobbiton would fall in Holland, while Tolkien explicitly stated in *Letters* no 190 that the Shire is "based on rural England and not any other country in the world – least perhaps of any in Europe on Holland, which is topographically wholly dissimilar."
