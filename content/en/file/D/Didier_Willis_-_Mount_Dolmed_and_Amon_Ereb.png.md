## Summary {#Summary}

Annotated collage of maps (using J.R.R. and Christopher Tolkien's maps as initial basis) showing where [Amon Ereb](../../main/A/Amon_Ereb.md) might be (if surviving the submersion of [Beleriand](../../main/B/Beleriand.md)) in [Forlindon](../../main/F/Forlindon.md), and noting a mountain peak slightly aside the main range of the [Blue Mountains](../../main/B/Blue_Mountains.md), approximately at Mount [Dolmed](../../main/D/Dolmed.md)'s location.

Detail from [Didier Willis](../../main/D/Didier_Willis.md), "Du Beleriand aux confins de Rhûn", in [Tolkien, le façonnement d'un monde](../../main/T/Tolkien,_le_façonnement_d'un_monde.md), vol. 2, 2014, p. 198, per author's self authorization.
