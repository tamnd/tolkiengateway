## Summary {#Summary}

Approximate location of [Tol Morwen](../../main/T/Tol_Morwen.md), deduced by superimposing the maps of [Beleriand](../../main/B/Beleriand.md) and [Eriador](../../main/E/Eriador.md).

(Modified) detail from [Didier Willis](../../main/D/Didier_Willis.md), "Du Beleriand aux confins de Rhûn", in [Tolkien, le façonnement d'un monde](../../main/T/Tolkien,_le_façonnement_d'un_monde.md), vol. 2, 2014, p. 198, per author's self authorization.
