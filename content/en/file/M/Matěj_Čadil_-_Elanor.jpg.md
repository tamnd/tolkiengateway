<td class="description">
<blockquote><p>For then next week of Amon Hen Art Challenge &#34;Flora &amp; Fauna⁠&#34; I drew elanor, the beautiful flower growing in Lothlórien, after which Sam Gamgee named his firstborn daughter.
</p><p>In The Lord of the Rings, elanor is desribed as &#34;small golden flowers shaped like stars&#34; and I also generally thought of elanor as a golden flower, as contrasted with the white niphredil, but Tolkien gave a more detailed description in Letter 312, interestingly mentioning silver flowers of elanor as well:⁠
</p><p>&#34;a pimpernel (perhaps a little enlarged) growing sun-golden flowers and star-silver ones on the same plant, and sometimes the two combined.&#34;
</p><p>
Last week, when I was drawing this picture, I was away from home on a holiday, so I couldn&#39;t consult the ultimate source on all things related to the plants in Tolkien&#39;s Legendarium, the book Flora of Middle-earth, at the time. But I read the chapter about elanor now and there is much more interesting information about it based on Tolkien&#39;s quotes.</p></blockquote>
