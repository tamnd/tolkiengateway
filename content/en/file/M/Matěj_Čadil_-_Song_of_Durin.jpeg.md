## Summary {#Summary}

<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>As the Fellowship spends a night in Moria, in response to Sam&#39;s doubtful remark: &#34;What did they do it all for? They didn&#39;t live in these darksome holes surely?&#34; Gimli starts to sing this song.
And the song itself is one of my favourites in The Lord of the Rings. Perhaps inspired by Sam&#39;s own reaction (&#34;I should like to learn it&#34;), I memorised the poem and wanted to make an illustration of it for a long time.
</p>
