<blockquote class="tg-quote noexcerpt">
<p>Winter Hobbit Door (2023). A hobbit hole door in a beautiful snowy winter, decorated for Yule.
Not meant to be Bag End specifically, just a generic Hobbit hole. My Winter in the Shire picture was quite popular, so I thought it would be nice to make another simple wintery Tolkien piece.
