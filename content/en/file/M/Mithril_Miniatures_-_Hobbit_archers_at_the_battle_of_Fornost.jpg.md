## Summary {#Summary}

[Hobbit](/wiki/Hobbits "Hobbits") archers at [the battle of Fornost](/wiki/Battle_of_Fornost "Battle of Fornost") - *[Mithril Miniatures](/wiki/Mithril_Miniatures)*<br>Product description:

<div class="poem">
<p>The Battle of Fornost was the decisive battle between the forces of Angmar, under the Witch-king, and of Eärnur, the Crown Prince of Gondor, in the year TA 1975 that ended the Witch-realm of Angmar. The Shire reportedly sent a company of Hobbit archers to support Gondor.<br/>
<br/>
Only 97 of these figures are produced at this time. This is the 215th release in the Gold Status Fellowship club. This is the August/September 2024 winning Fellowship idea suggested by member &#34;maxtarna&#34;. This is the twenty-eight fully UV resin release. Mithril is offering a choice between primed and unprimed. This release has three figures, no assembly required. It is 32mm scale. The bases have the manufacturer name MITHRIL, the sculptor&#39;s name and creation date and MZ number on the sides of the base with A, B and C to help organise them.
</p>
</div>
