<th scope="row" id="fileinfotpl_desc" class="fileinfo-paramfield" style="background:var(--tg-color-surface-5)">Description<span class="summary fn" style="display:none">Mithril Miniatures - Beregond fighting Troll at the Black Gate.jpg</span></th>
<td class="description">
<blockquote><p>This vignette depicts the opening scene of <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_the_Morannon" title="Battle of the Morannon">the battle of the black gate</a>. The black <a href="/wiki/Trolls" title="Trolls">Trolls</a> of <a href="/wiki/Mordor" title="Mordor">Mordor</a> are unleashed upon <a href="/wiki/Army_of_the_West" title="Army of the West">the armies of Gondor</a>, and serve as shock troops, cutting swathes through the Gondorian ranks. Armed with war-hammers they strike down all before them. Lying dead on the ground are two Gondorian city spearmen, the Troll&#39;s first victims. A Dol Amroth infantryman, whose own unit stands with <a href="/wiki/Beregond" title="Beregond">Beregond</a> and his men at this part of the battle front, is kneeling, also wounded by the great Troll. The awful creature itself has just struck at Beregond, shattering his shield. Beregond, attired in the splendid garb of the <a href="/wiki/Minas_Tirith" title="Minas Tirith">Minas Tirith</a> guards staggers back under the impact while a fearful yet determined <a href="/wiki/Peregrin_Took" title="Peregrin Took">Pippin</a>, also attired as a Minas Tirith guard, attempts to stand his ground.<br/>This is the 72nd release in the Gold Status Fellowship club and was <a href="/wiki/June" title="June">June</a> <a href="/wiki/2011" title="2011">2011</a>&#39;s Winning idea. It was suggested by &#39;barliman&#39;. Vignette measures 44mm in height from bottom of base to the top of the trolls raised buckler and the base measures 83mm in length.</p></blockquote>
