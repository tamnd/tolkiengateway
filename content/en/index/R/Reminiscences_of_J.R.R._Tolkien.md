**Reminiscences of J.R.R. Tolkien** is a page on Tolkien Gateway which collects publications of people recalling their personal experiences of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien). Apart from their literary value, reminiscences might also be useful for scholars researching the life and works of Tolkien.
