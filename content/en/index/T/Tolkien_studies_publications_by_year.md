**Writings by year** is an attempt to chronologically list all published writings relating to [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md) and his works (see also *[Writings by J.R.R. Tolkien](../W/Writings_by_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)*). The list is focused on publication of books, but major journals and important newspaper articles will also be listed (non-scholarly journals and game book series will generally only be listed when they first appear).<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> Non-English titles will be added when these have a description in English on Tolkien Gateway.
