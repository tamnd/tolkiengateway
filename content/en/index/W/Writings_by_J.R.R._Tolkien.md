This is an attempt to list all published writings (including juvenilia, letters, transcribed interviews and statements, etc.) of [J.R.R. Tolkien](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien) appearing in [books](/wiki/Category:Books "Category:Books"), [periodicals](/wiki/Category:Periodicals "Category:Periodicals"), [ephemera](/wiki/Category:Ephemera "Category:Ephemera") and online, in a chronological order of publication.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup> For a list of his major books, see the **[bibliography section on the J.R.R. Tolkien page](/wiki/J.R.R._Tolkien#Bibliography "J.R.R. Tolkien")**.

- *Note 1: For ease of reference (and because many items do not have an exact date of publication), works are sorted alphabetically under each year.*
- *Note 2: The list does not (in general) include reprints or new editions without new content*.
- *Note 3: Publications, with only a vague contribution by Tolkien, or which are questionable, disputed, or with an unknown publication status or similiar, are enclosed with parentheses*.
- *Note 4: Non-English publications are only listed if these contain material published for the first time.*
