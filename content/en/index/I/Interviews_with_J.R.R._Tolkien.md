**Interviews with J.R.R. Tolkien** is a project on Tolkien Gateway to create a chronological list of all known interviews conducted with [J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/J/J.R.R._Tolkien.md).

- Late [1955](../../main/_/1955.md) or early [1956](../../main/_/1956.md): "With Camera and Pen" (by Anthony Price; *Oxford Times*, 27 January 1956)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">[1]</a></sup>
- [1955](../../main/_/1955.md), [June 5](../../main/_/5_June.md): : "Oxford Calling" <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">[2]</a></sup>
- [1957](../../main/_/1957.md): ["Carnival of Books" Interview](../../main/_/1957_%2522Carnival_of_Books%2522_Interview.md)

- [1961](../../main/_/1961.md): [Interview by Jan Broberg](../../main/_/1961_Jan_Broberg.md)
- [1961](../../main/_/1961.md): "[Den besynnerlige professor Tolkien](../../main/D/Den_besynnerlige_professor_Tolkien.md)" (Interview by Lars Gustafsson)
- [1962](../../main/_/1962.md): Interview by John Bowen ([12 December](../../main/_/12_December.md)), for BBC TV's *Bookstand*.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">[3]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">[4]</a></sup>
- [1963](../../main/_/1963.md): Interview by Anthony Curtis (?late October—?early November).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">[5]</a></sup> Extracts published in [*The Sunday Telegraph* (10 November 1963)](<../../main/T/The_Sunday_Telegraph_(10_November_1963).md>), [*I, Palantir* (April 1964)](<../../main/I/I,_Palantir_(April_1964).md>), and [*British Book News* (June 1977)](<../../main/B/British_Book_News_(June_1977).md>).
- [1964](../../main/_/1964.md): [1964 BBC Interview](../../main/_/1964_BBC_Interview.md)
- [1965](../../main/_/1965.md): [1965 BBC Interview](../../main/_/1965_BBC_Interview.md)
- [1966](../../main/_/1966.md): "[The Hobbit-forming World of J.R.R. Tolkien](../../main/T/The_Hobbit-forming_World_of_J.R.R._Tolkien.md)", Henry Resnick, extracts of a more important interview published in 1967 in <a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Niekas_18&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Niekas 18 (page does not exist)"><i>Niekas</i> Issue 18 (Late Spring 1967)</a>
- [1966](../../main/_/1966.md): [Daphne Castell interview](../../main/D/Daphne_Castell.md)
- [1966](../../main/_/1966.md), [July 26](../../main/_/26_July.md): "Writers Talking-1: The Hobbit Man", John Ezard, extracts published in (*Oxford Mail*, 3 August 1966). Slightly different extracts from the interview were published in Ezard's article "Tolkien's Shire" (*Weekend Guardian* 28-29 December 1991)<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">[6]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">[7]</a></sup>
- [1966](../../main/_/1966.md): "Lord of the Hobbits", [William Cater](../../main/W/William_Cater.md) (*Daily Express*, 22 November 1966)<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">[8]</a></sup>
- [1966](../../main/_/1966.md), [November 30](../../main/_/30_November.md): "[The Man Who Understands Hobbits](../../main/T/The_Man_Who_Understands_Hobbits.md)"<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">[9]</a></sup>
- [1967](../../main/_/1967.md): "The Hobbit Man", Philip Norman(*Sunday Times Magazine*, 15 January 1967)
- [1967](../../main/_/1967.md):"<a class="external text" href="http://www.nytimes.com/1967/01/15/books/tolkien-interview.html" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">The Prevalence of Hobbits</a>", Philip Norman (*The New York Times Magazine*,15 January 1967)<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">[10]</a></sup>. Apparently the articles are "*similar but not identical*" to "The Hobbit Man".<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">[11]</a></sup>
- [1967](../../main/_/1967.md): "A Benevolent and Furry-footed People" William Foster (*The Scotsman*, 25 March 1967)
- [1967](../../main/_/1967.md): "<a class="external text" href="http://efanzines.com/Niekas/Niekas-18.pdf#37" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">An Interview with Tolkien</a>", Henry Resnick (<a class="new" href="https://tolkiengateway.net/w/index.php?title=Niekas_18&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" title="Niekas 18 (page does not exist)"><i>Niekas</i> Issue 18 (Late Spring 1967)</a>, 1967)
- [1967](../../main/_/1967.md): "J.R.R. Tolkien Talks about the Discovery of Middle-earth, the Origins of Elvish" (Richard Plotz; *[Seventeen](../../main/S/Seventeen.md)*, 17 January 1967)<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">[12]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">[13]</a></sup>
- [1968](../../main/_/1968.md): "Fireworks for the Author" (Don Chapman 'Anthony Wood', *Oxford Mail*, 9 February 1968)
- [1968](../../main/_/1968.md): "In the Footsteps of the Hobbits" (Keith Brace; *Birmingham Post*, 25 May 1968)
- [1968](../../main/_/1968.md): *[Tolkien in Oxford](../../main/T/Tolkien_in_Oxford.md)*

- [1972](../../main/_/1972.md): "The Lord of the Legends: A Birthday Tribute to the Creator of The Hobbit", Bill Cater [The Sunday Times Magazine (2 January 1972)](<../../main/T/The_Sunday_Times_Magazine_(2_January_1972).md>)
- [1972](../../main/_/1972.md): "An Early History of the Hobbits.” Edinburgh Scotsman (February 5, 1972).
- [1972](../../main/_/1972.md): ?Mid-March: "Tolkien Seeks the Quiet Life in Oxford" (*Oxford Mail*, 22 March 1972)<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">[14]</a></sup>
- [1972](../../main/_/1972.md): “More and More People are Getting the J.R.R. Tolkien Habit.” Los Angeles Times (April 9, 1972), 14,18
- [1973](../../main/_/1973.md): Tolkien interviewed about the Oxford Poetry Chair \[Oxford Poetry Chair: ITV News 25-05-1973\]<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">[15]</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">[note 1]</a></sup>
