The scene of [Dernhelm](../../main/D/Dernhelm.md) confronting the "[Dwimmerlaik](../../main/D/Dwimmerlaik.md)" [Witch-king](../../main/W/Witch-king.md) on the [Fell beast](../../main/F/Fell_beast.md) who is about to finish [Théoden](../../main/T/Théoden.md), the revelation that he is actually [Éowyn](../../main/_/Éowyn.md), and the fulfillment of [Glorfindel](../../main/G/Glorfindel.md)'s prophecy, is a famous instance from the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](../../main/B/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields.md). It has been one of the most favorite scenes among Tolkien artists and one of the most iconic of the [legendarium](../../main/L/Legendarium.md).
