The scene of [Dernhelm](/wiki/Dernhelm) confronting the "[Dwimmerlaik](/wiki/Dwimmerlaik)" [Witch-king](/wiki/Witch-king) on the [Fell beast](/wiki/Fell_beast) who is about to finish [Théoden](/wiki/Th%C3%A9oden), the revelation that he is actually [Éowyn](/wiki/%C3%89owyn), and the fulfillment of [Glorfindel](/wiki/Glorfindel)'s prophecy, is a famous instance from the [Battle of the Pelennor Fields](/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pelennor_Fields). It has been one of the most favorite scenes among Tolkien artists and one of the most iconic of the [legendarium](/wiki/Legendarium "Legendarium").
