This category contains articles relating to the **Kings of [Arthedain](/wiki/Arthedain)** between [Amlaith](/wiki/Amlaith) of [Fornost](/wiki/Fornost), the first King of Arthedain (son of [Eärendur](</wiki/E%C3%A4rendur_(King_of_Arnor)> "Eärendur (King of Arnor)"), the last [King of Arnor](/wiki/Kings_of_Arnor "Kings of Arnor")), and [Arvedui](/wiki/Arvedui) the last King of Arthedain. It was through the Kings of Arthedain that the line of [Isildur](/wiki/Isildur) was preserved all the way to [Aragorn Elessar](/wiki/Aragorn "Aragorn").
